# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.6/10 — The paper contains a substantial and apparently novel population identification result, but its publication case is weakened by a stale verification record, an incompletely specified decoder outside its theorem domain, and a manuscript architecture that obscures the contribution.

The paper establishes topologically generic ratio-law separation and exact population recovery of latent coordinates, target alignment, and the target-permuted DAG from one perfect intervention per scalar latent variable in a positive smooth compact-cube fixed-sign model. It also supplies familywise-valid ancestral discoveries under sample splitting and an explicit first-stage likelihood-ratio error contract, while correctly presenting generated-rank estimation as future work. These are potentially important contributions to nonlinear causal representation learning, but the manuscript requires substantial revision to make its operational domain, identification scope, assumptions, and current verification provenance transparent.

## Strengths
- The central result addresses an important unresolved intervention-count question in arbitrary dimension on a clearly delimited compact, smooth, fixed-sign model class.
- The paper distinguishes the causal-minimal core theorem from the stronger faithful regime used for comparison with prior work.
- The ratio-discrepancy graph, conditional-rank construction, and conditional-independence pruning form a coherent identification strategy with clearly separated logical roles.
- The finite-sample theorem states an honest inference interface: ancestral soundness is familywise valid under an explicit simultaneous first-stage L1 ratio-error event.
- The generated-rank rate is correctly confined to the Limitations and future work section and presented as an open target rather than an attained result.
- The related-work section engages the closest comparators by intervention count, transformation class, support, and target-information requirements.

## Findings
- **[major·prose] Proofs and verification** — The verification record states that the development is "recorded at repository state \texttt{43ec493a}," whereas the authoritative current verification contract identifies commit \texttt{84949d8f5fba5aa328497f983aba7dd77a0e4620}. This makes the manuscript's principal reproducibility pointer stale.
  - *Fix:* Regenerate the verification record and crosswalk from the current certified artifact, report the full current commit hash, and recheck all counts and audit summaries against that same commit.
- **[major·structure] Population decoding** — The manuscript calls \(\mathscr D\) a map "from a law family to a graph and a set of coordinates," but its displayed definition instructs the reader to choose a topological ordering of \(H_D\) and a unique minimal admissible set. Those operations need not exist or be unique for an arbitrary input law family, as the surrounding paragraph itself acknowledges. The mathematical presentation therefore leaves the purported law-only map undefined outside the theorem class, even though the formal implementation is totalized.
  - *Fix:* Either define \(\mathscr D\) explicitly as a partial oracle whose domain is the admissible cover-separated law class, or state the precise law-only fallback conventions used to totalize the ordering and parent selection. Ensure that the algorithm environment and surrounding prose use the same domain.
- **[major·structure] global** — The main narrative is overwhelmed by dozens of numbered bookkeeping definitions, including latent state space, parent sets, d-separation, evaluation-fold notation, and several internal kernel constructions. This makes the central econometric argument difficult to locate and evaluate despite the strength of the headline theorem.
  - *Fix:* Retain in the main text only the model, observable discrepancy, decoder, principal assumptions, and main results. Move routine or proof-internal definitions to an appendix or notation table, and add a concise roadmap explaining the three substantive steps: separation, rank recovery, and parent pruning.
- **[major·prose] Setup and ratio observables** — The fixed own-coordinate derivative-sign restriction is central to both triangular inversion and rank recovery, yet its substantive content receives only a short statement that it "orients each ratio." The paper does not adequately explain how restrictive this condition is, whether the sign vector is known or merely indexes the model class, or why plausible intervention mechanisms satisfy it. Likewise, open density is established conditionally on a nonempty fixed-sign stratum, so its practical interpretation depends on understanding that stratum.
  - *Fix:* Add a focused discussion with representative mechanism classes satisfying the sign condition, clarify whether the decoder requires knowledge of \(s\), and describe open density explicitly as a relative topological result within each nonempty stratum. Keep empirical testability and broader coverage questions in the Limitations and future work section.
- **[minor·prose] Population decoding** — The sentence "on an arbitrary law family the ratio graph may contain cycles and the inclusion-minimal admissible set need not be unique" is a genuine non-coverage statement placed outside the explicitly titled limitations section, contrary to the paper's affirmative contribution-framing contract.
  - *Fix:* State the delivered domain affirmatively, for example: "On the cover-separated model class of \cref{obj:thm:exact-ratio-decoder}, \(H_D\) is acyclic and each pruning problem has a unique inclusion-minimal solution." Move any discussion of arbitrary law families to the Limitations and future work section.
- **[minor·prose] Setup and ratio observables** — The discussion after the observed-world definition says that it packages objects "available to population procedures: common coordinates, environment laws, target labels, and ratio carriers." The target permutation, mixing map, and latent coordinates are generative bookkeeping objects rather than analyst-observed inputs; the decoder is later advertised as using the environment laws alone.
  - *Fix:* Distinguish the generative observed-world witness from the decoder's input. State that \(f\), its inverse, and \(\pi\) encode the data-generating representation, while the population decoder receives the observed law family and derives its ratios from those laws.
- **[minor·prose] abstract** — The sentence "The recovered observed world is identified up to componentwise \(C^2\) coordinate changes and simultaneous relabeling of graph and targets" suppresses an important quantifier restriction: the verified uniqueness comparison ranges over alternative worlds satisfying the same fixed sign vector and the other stated structural conditions.
  - *Fix:* Qualify the sentence as identification within the stated positive smooth causal-minimal fixed-sign model class, and make the same qualification wherever the representation-uniqueness conclusion is summarized.
- **[minor·prose] Generic ratio separation** — The sentence "ratio-law separation is typical" can be read as a probabilistic or measure-theoretic prevalence claim, while the theorem establishes relative open density and a meagre exceptional set in the product \(C^2\) topology.
  - *Fix:* Use "topologically generic" and immediately name the topology and exceptional-set conclusion; avoid unqualified uses of "typical."
- **[minor·statement] Limitations and future work** — Several notation choices impede reproducibility: \(K\) is reused as a generic function domain inside the Hölder assumption despite already denoting the observed evaluation set, and \(\underline N\) appears in the generated-rank frontier before the following paragraph defines it. The structural conditional-ratio CDF is defined with a parent vector \(z\), whereas the exact-decoder theorem subsequently writes the same object as conditioned on the full latent point \(v\).
  - *Fix:* Use a distinct placeholder such as \(D_r\) for each function's domain, define \(\underline N=\min_e N_e\) before its first displayed use, and consistently define the structural CDF either on parent vectors or on full latent points through explicit projection to \(v_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)}\).
- **[minor·structure] Sample-split confidence edges** — The term "confidence edges" can suggest confidence statements for direct causal edges, whereas the verified guarantee assigns every selected arrow an ancestral interpretation and recovers only the transitive closure under the cover margin.
  - *Fix:* Rename the object and section using language such as "ancestral confidence graph" or define "confidence edge" explicitly as an arrow certifying ancestry, and maintain that terminology throughout the abstract and introduction.

## Questions for authors
- Is the sign vector \(s\) intended to be known to the analyst, or does it merely index a model stratum while the decoder remains sign-agnostic?
- What exact fallback conventions in the formal decoder totalize topological-order and parent-set selection on law families outside the cover-separated class?
- Will the public artifact and manuscript crosswalk be regenerated at commit \texttt{84949d8f5fba5aa328497f983aba7dd77a0e4620} before publication?
