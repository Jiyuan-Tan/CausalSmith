\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:thm:simultaneous-confidence-edges}]
Let \(\mathcal H_k\) be the Hilbert space of the Gaussian unit-norm feature map
\(\Phi\).  Write
\[
\underline N=\min_{0\le e\le n}N_e,
\qquad
\delta_N(\alpha)=
\frac{1+\sqrt{2\log\{n(n+1)/\alpha\}}}{\sqrt{\underline N}},
\]
so that
\[
\varepsilon_N(\alpha,\eta)
=
2\sqrt 2\,a_N(\eta)+2\delta_N(\alpha).
\]
For \(e\in\{0,\ldots,n\}\), \(i\in[n]\), and an outcome \(\omega\), set
\[
\widehat\mu_{e i}(\omega)
=
\frac1{N_e}\sum_{r=1}^{N_e}
\Phi\!\left(\widehat R_i(X^{(e)}_r(\omega))\right),
\]
\[
\widetilde\mu_{e i}(\omega)
=
\int \Phi\!\left(\widehat R_i(x)\right)\,dP^e(x),
\qquad
\mu_{e i}
=
\int \Phi\!\left(R_i(x)\right)\,dP^e(x).
\]
Define the evaluation concentration event
\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{emb}}
=
\left\{
\omega:
\forall i\in[n],\ \forall e\in\{0,\ldots,n\},\
\|\widehat\mu_{e i}(\omega)-\widetilde\mu_{e i}(\omega)\|_{\mathcal H_k}
\le \delta_N(\alpha)
\right\}.
\]
The independent-environment sampling assumption supplies the conditional evaluation-sampling hypotheses in \cref{obj:lem:bounded-rkhs-empirical-mean}.  Hence, conditionally on the training fold, \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{emb}}\) has probability at least \(1-\alpha\): for every training-fold measurable event \(B\),
\[
(1-\alpha)\Pr(B)
\le
\Pr(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{emb}}\cap B).
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.ExactID.EID_CrlCoverratioMmdGenericity.bounded_rkhs_empirical_mean

For the bridge from fitted to population embeddings, first note that the Gaussian normalization gives, for all \(a,b\in\mathbb R\),
\[
\|\Phi(a)-\Phi(b)\|_{\mathcal H_k}^2
=
2-2\exp\{-(a-b)^2\}
\le 2(a-b)^2,
\]
and therefore
\[
\|\Phi(a)-\Phi(b)\|_{\mathcal H_k}
\le
\sqrt2\,|a-b|.
\]
On \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}}\), the defining \(L^1(P^e)\) bound gives, for every \(i\) and \(e\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\widetilde\mu_{e i}(\omega)-\mu_{e i}\|_{\mathcal H_k}
&=
\left\|
\int
\left\{
\Phi(\widehat R_i(x))-\Phi(R_i(x))
\right\}
\,dP^e(x)
\right\|_{\mathcal H_k}                                                   \\
&\le
\int
\|\Phi(\widehat R_i(x))-\Phi(R_i(x))\|_{\mathcal H_k}
\,dP^e(x)                                                                 \\
&\le
\sqrt2
\int
|\widehat R_i(x)-R_i(x)|
\,dP^e(x)                                                                 \\
&\le
\sqrt2\,a_N(\eta).
\end{aligned}
\]
The measurability and integrability requirements here are exactly the training-fold and finite-integral clauses in \cref{obj:ass:independent-environment-sampling} and in the definition of \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}}\).
% lean: CausalSmith.ExactID.EID_CrlCoverratioMmdGenericity.meanEmbedding_map_sub_le_l1

Now fix \(\omega\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{emb}}\cap\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}}\) and \(j\ne i\).  The empirical and population discrepancies are
\[
\widehat D_{ji}(\omega)
=
\|\widehat\mu_{j i}(\omega)-\widehat\mu_{0 i}(\omega)\|_{\mathcal H_k},
\qquad
D_{ji}
=
\|\mu_{0 i}-\mu_{j i}\|_{\mathcal H_k}.
\]
By the reverse triangle inequality,
\[
\begin{aligned}
|\widehat D_{ji}(\omega)-D_{ji}|
&\le
\left\|
(\widehat\mu_{j i}-\widehat\mu_{0 i})
-
(\mu_{j i}-\mu_{0 i})
\right\|_{\mathcal H_k}                                                    \\
&\le
\|\widehat\mu_{j i}-\widetilde\mu_{j i}\|_{\mathcal H_k}
+
\|\widetilde\mu_{j i}-\mu_{j i}\|_{\mathcal H_k}                            \\
&\quad+
\|\widehat\mu_{0 i}-\widetilde\mu_{0 i}\|_{\mathcal H_k}
+
\|\widetilde\mu_{0 i}-\mu_{0 i}\|_{\mathcal H_k}                            \\
&\le
2\delta_N(\alpha)+2\sqrt2\,a_N(\eta)
=
\varepsilon_N(\alpha,\eta).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus
\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{emb}}\cap\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}}
\subseteq
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}}.
\]
Applying the conditional bound for \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{emb}}\) to \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}}\cap B\), which is training-fold measurable by \cref{obj:ass:independent-environment-sampling}, yields
\[
(1-\alpha)\Pr(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}}\cap B)
\le
\Pr(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}}\cap\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}}\cap B)
\]
for every training-fold measurable \(B\).  This is the stated conditional probability bound.
% lean: CausalSmith.ExactID.EID_CrlCoverratioMmdGenericity.simultaneous_confidence_edges

Taking \(B=\Omega\) gives
\[
(1-\alpha)\Pr(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}})
\le
\Pr(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}}\cap\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}})
\le
\Pr(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}}).
\]
Since \(\Pr(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{fs}})\ge 1-\eta\) and
\(\alpha,\eta\in(0,1)\),
\[
\Pr(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}})
\ge
(1-\alpha)(1-\eta)
=
1-\alpha-\eta+\alpha\eta
\ge
1-\alpha-\eta.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.ExactID.EID_CrlCoverratioMmdGenericity.simultaneous_confidence_edges

It remains to identify the graph-theoretic consequences on \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}}\).  Fix \(\omega\in\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}}\), and suppose \(j\to i\) is selected by \(\widehat H(\omega)\).  Then \(j\ne i\) and
\[
0<\widehat D_{ji}(\omega)-\varepsilon_N(\alpha,\eta).
\]
If \(\pi(j)\) were not an ancestor of \(\pi(i)\) in \(G\), the structural assumptions
\cref{obj:ass:positive-normalized-smooth-mechanisms,obj:ass:shared-diffeomorphic-mixing,obj:ass:one-perfect-intervention-per-node} imply that the ratio coordinate \(R_i\) has the same law under \(P^0\) and \(P^j\):
\[
(R_i)_\#P^0=(R_i)_\#P^j.
\]
Indeed, in the latent factorization, \(R_i(f(v))\) depends only on \(v_{\pi(i)}\) and the parents of \(\pi(i)\); an intervention at a non-ancestor of \(\pi(i)\) leaves the joint law of these ancestral coordinates unchanged.  Therefore
\[
D_{ji}
=
\left\|
\int\Phi\,d((R_i)_\#P^0)
-
\int\Phi\,d((R_i)_\#P^j)
\right\|_{\mathcal H_k}
=0.
\]
The event \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}}\) then gives
\[
\widehat D_{ji}(\omega)
=
|\widehat D_{ji}(\omega)-0|
\le
\varepsilon_N(\alpha,\eta),
\]
contradicting the selection inequality.  Hence every selected arrow \(j\to i\) satisfies that \(\pi(j)\) is an ancestor of \(\pi(i)\) in \(G\).
% lean: CausalSmith.ExactID.EID_CrlCoverratioMmdGenericity.ratio_nonancestor_zero

Assume now the cover margin
\[
2\varepsilon_N(\alpha,\eta)<D_{ji}
\qquad
\text{whenever }\pi(j)\lessdot_G\pi(i).
\]
The soundness just proved gives the forward inclusion
\[
\operatorname{TC}(\widehat H(\omega))
\subseteq
\operatorname{TC}(G^\pi):
\]
a selected edge \(j\to i\) yields an ancestral chain from \(\pi(j)\) to \(\pi(i)\) in \(G\), and applying \(\pi^{-1}\) to each edge of that chain gives a directed path from \(j\) to \(i\) in \(G^\pi\).

For the reverse inclusion, let \(a\lessdot_G b\) be an ancestral cover and put
\[
j=\pi^{-1}(a),
\qquad
i=\pi^{-1}(b).
\]
The cover relation implies \(a\ne b\), hence \(j\ne i\).  By the margin and the event \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{MMD}}\),
\[
\widehat D_{ji}(\omega)
\ge
D_{ji}-\varepsilon_N(\alpha,\eta)
>
\varepsilon_N(\alpha,\eta),
\]
so \(j\to i\) is selected by \(\widehat H(\omega)\).  Thus every transported ancestral cover of \(G\) is an edge of \(\widehat H(\omega)\).

Finally, every strict ancestral relation in the finite ancestor order of \(G\) factors into covers:
\[
a<_G b
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\exists a=a_0\lessdot_G a_1\lessdot_G\cdots\lessdot_G a_m=b.
\]
This follows by induction on the finite interval
\(\{c:a\le_G c\le_G b\}\): if \(a\lessdot_G b\) there is nothing to prove, while otherwise one chooses an intermediate \(c\) with \(a<_G c<_G b\) and concatenates the two shorter saturated chains.  Hence each edge of \(G^\pi\), and therefore each path in \(G^\pi\), maps to a path in \(\widehat H(\omega)\).  We obtain
\[
\operatorname{TC}(G^\pi)
\subseteq
\operatorname{TC}(\widehat H(\omega)).
\]
Together with the forward inclusion,
\[
\operatorname{TC}(\widehat H(\omega))
=
\operatorname{TC}(G^\pi).
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.ExactID.EID_CrlCoverratioMmdGenericity.isAncestor_transGen_ancestralCover
\end{proof}
