# Title
**One intervention per latent variable: generic identification of nonlinear causal representations on fixed-sign compact strata**

**Contribution statement.** Under positive smooth compact support, causal minimality, fixed own-coordinate derivative signs, and a shared \(C^2\) diffeomorphic observation map, the paper establishes generic likelihood-ratio law separation, constructs a population decoder that recovers latent ranks, target alignment, and the DAG from one unknown-target perfect intervention per scalar latent node, and gives sample-split simultaneous confidence edges conditional on a first-stage ratio-error event.

## section: Introduction
The introduction motivates identification of latent causal variables from multiple environments when intervention targets are unknown, states the econometric problem in terms of observed likelihood-ratio laws under a shared diffeomorphic observation map, and previews the paper's answer: generic ratio-law separation recovers the ancestral order, conditional ranks recover latent coordinates and align targets, and parent pruning recovers the DAG under positivity and causal minimality. It also previews the sample-split confidence-edge theorem and directs readers to the appendix verification note for the Lean checking scope.
