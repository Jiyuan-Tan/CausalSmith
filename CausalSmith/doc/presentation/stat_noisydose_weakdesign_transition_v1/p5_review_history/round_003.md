# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.5/10 — The verified uniform frontier is a substantial theoretical contribution, but conflicting estimator presentations and extensive repetition require substantial revision before publication.

The paper establishes matching orders for pointwise causal estimation and honest connected-interval length under calibrated polynomial design envelopes, bounded outcomes, and known Gaussian measurement error. Its principal contribution is the uniform connection between direct estimation, Gaussian Fourier inversion, and compact-support localization, including both transition regions. Taking the verified results as established, the submission offers a promising theoretical contribution whose exposition and procedure specification need substantial repair.

## Strengths
- The frontier treats sample size and measurement precision jointly and characterizes both transition neighborhoods.
- Estimation and honest interval length are matched over the same calibrated structural class.
- The observable interval has finite-sample coverage, with the wider coverage-level range clearly distinguished from the matched-length frontier.
- The lower bounds compare complete observed samples generated by legal bounded causal alternatives.
- The early related-work section engages competitors' actual rates, losses, and assumptions, and the theorem-local formalization disclosures preserve the external citation boundaries.

## Findings
- **[major·statement] Observable estimation and honest intervals** — The split-sample presentation introduces an additional restriction through the sentence 'The fixed normalization in this construction is' followed by p_min c_lo = 1/16, although the verified estimator and guarantees allow general public K. For example, K=(1/2,1,1) with kappa=0 satisfies the displayed class and calibration conditions. The same presentation leads with projection onto [1/8,7/8] and calls it projection onto the mean range, whereas the full model's range and certified estimator use [0,1]. The final sentence distinguishing the two projections leaves competing procedure descriptions in place.
  - *Fix:* Present the general-K denominator and [0,1] projection as the primary split-sample construction. Label the 1/16 formula explicitly as a reference normalization conditional on that equality. Place any retained [1/8,7/8] construction with the narrower witness profiles and distinguish its role from the estimator certified by \cref{obj:lem:public-error-certificate,obj:thm:observable-upper,obj:thm:finite-honesty}.
- **[major·structure] global** — The central contribution is obscured by repeated constructions and extensive dependency inventories. Setup contains the rank subclass and two lengthy witness constructions before the main frontier; the estimation section repeats score and ratio formulas and inserts the lower-bound packet between the interval and estimator. Several environments end with cross-reference lists covering nearly every object. Statements that the packet definition and witness details are confined to the technical appendix also conflict with their actual placement.
  - *Fix:* Organize the main text around the causal model, frontier, general-K estimator and interval, and a concise converse explanation. Consolidate the witness constructions and move rank-subclass details and the packet definition to the technical appendix. Replace exhaustive dependency lists with the few references needed locally, shorten repeated frontier summaries, and update placement descriptions. Preserve theorem conditions and theorem-local formalization disclosures.
- **[minor·prose] Observable estimation and honest intervals** — The sentence 'The expected-length rate is uniform once the sample size exceeds a threshold depending only on the smoothness and design-decay exponents' understates the permitted parameter dependence. The verified statement permits the threshold to depend on K, beta, kappa, and alpha.
  - *Fix:* State that the threshold may depend on the fixed public class constants, smoothness and design-decay exponents, and noncoverage probability, while remaining uniform over the error scale, schedule space, and model law.
- **[minor·statement] Observable estimation and honest intervals** — The prose specifies an immediate fallback before dictionary evaluation for n=0,1,2, but the numbered algorithm takes an ordered dictionary with scores as an input and unconditionally selects a score minimizer before its final fallback. The score presentation assumes n>=3. The reader-facing evaluation order therefore differs from the total branching function in the verification contract.
  - *Fix:* Make the empty-block check the first operational branch and immediately return the midpoint and full-range interval. Construct the dictionary, evaluate scores, select the entry, and compute empirical moments within the nonempty-block branch.
- **[minor·statement] Setup and assumptions — Structural witnesses and observed-law comparisons** — The synthesized witness-law display defines P_s as the law of a five-coordinate tuple ending with the realized outcome. The structural model has six coordinates, including U, and the verified witness construction retains U. This makes the displayed law a projection of the intended structural law.
  - *Fix:* Append U to the displayed structural tuple and distinguish the six-coordinate structural law from its observed three-coordinate projection. Use the same coordinate convention in the consolidated witness presentation.
- **[minor·citation] Related work** — The Jiang–Ma–Carroll comparison displays algebraic compact-support regression bounds alongside this paper's logarithmic fixed-error frontier. Listing bounded-operator and nonsingularity conditions, followed by 'the two experiments and losses remain distinct,' leaves the decisive separation unclear. Readers need to understand which source premise or uniformity requirement separates those guarantees from the present minimax class.
  - *Fix:* Inspect the cited theorem and conditions and identify the precise separating premise, including its dependence on increasing spline dimension and its coverage of the maintained Gaussian channel. Explain the distinction with accurate source locators and retain the respective losses and quantifiers.
- **[minor·other] Observable estimation and honest intervals; Discussion** — The public certificate establishes an observable mathematical procedure, but its interpretation as a precision benchmark would benefit from numerical evidence about the exact score, selected weights, and interval radii. The large constants and eventual sample-size guarantees make finite-sample informativeness a separate question from the established rate orders.
  - *Fix:* Provide a reproducible numerical implementation of the public scores and inverse weights, documenting integration accuracy and numerical stability. Report selected entries, scores, and radii over a modest grid of sample sizes and error scales for representative calibrated K, beta, and kappa. Interpret these diagnostics within the established theoretical scope.
- **[minor·prose] Observable estimation and honest intervals** — Ordinary prose uses negative framing in 'it is not an upper envelope assumption' and 'no observed outcome enters selection.' Both points have direct affirmative formulations that preserve the construction's scope.
  - *Fix:* Describe 16 as the reference normalization used in tuning, with c_hi supplying the public density-envelope bound. Describe selection as a deterministic function of the public parameters and dictionary order.
- **[nit·prose] Introduction** — The first use of t occurs in the polynomial envelope before prose explicitly names it as the centered latent-dose displacement. The first occurrence of p_min inside K similarly precedes a plain-word identification of its individual role.
  - *Fix:* At first use, name t as the latent dose minus 1/2 and p_min as the minimum stratum probability.

## Questions for authors
- What reader-facing role is intended for the [1/8,7/8] split-sample estimator, given that the certified full-class estimator projects onto [0,1]?
- Which precise operator or uniformity condition separates the Jiang–Ma–Carroll algebraic guarantee from the present fixed-error minimax frontier?
- For representative calibrated public parameters, at what sample sizes does the selected certificate produce informative interval radii?
