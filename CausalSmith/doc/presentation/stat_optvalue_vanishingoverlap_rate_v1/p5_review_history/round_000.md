# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 7.3/10 — The verified uniform minimax characterization is substantial and the core claims are faithful, but attribution of the closest same-target predecessor and the presentation of the attaining construction require revision.

The paper establishes universal minimax squared-error rates for optimal treatment-value estimation with categorical covariates and shrinking overlap, together with an attaining estimator and an exact consistency criterion. Its observed and causal scope is accurately represented by the principal claims. The contribution merits serious consideration, with publication contingent on clearer attribution of the fixed-overlap predecessor and a more focused, transparent exposition.

## Strengths
- The matched bounds characterize the joint dependence on sample size, alphabet size, and overlap with universal constants across the full admissible parameter range.
- The uniformity domain accommodates unknown masses and propensities, null cells, boundary outcome means, and treatment-effect ties.
- Identification and causal completion give a precise connection between the observed statistical problem and causal oracle-value estimation.
- The dedicated early related-work section engages competitors' actual rates, regimes, and inference conditions.
- The weighted approximation and common-mean prior construction provide a substantive methodological contribution, supported by the authoritative verification contract.

## Findings
- **[major·citation] Introduction and Related work** — The closest same-target predecessor remains unattributed. The final related-work paragraph refers to 'the same-target fixed-overlap benchmark' without citing the accepted fixed-overlap Jackson–factorial archive identified in the supplied brief. That archive already establishes the fixed-overlap specialization and uses the same methodological architecture, making its relationship to the present contribution central to assessing novelty.
  - *Fix:* Retrieve the precise bibliographic details and result locator for CausalSmithFixedOverlap2026, cite it explicitly in both contribution positioning and related work, and state its rate and overlap-dependent constants. Explain which armwise sensitivity, weighted-prior, and likelihood arguments deliver universal overlap dependence beyond that predecessor.
- **[minor·prose] Introduction and An attaining estimator** — The introduction says, 'Together with empirical estimation for bounded alphabets and a midpoint statistic in the saturated regime, this construction attains the universal risk bound.' The supplied upper-bound proof selects D_0=2, which makes the empirical branch empty on the stated domain d≥2. The surrounding three-regime explanation therefore gives the empirical branch a role beyond the exhibited attaining calibration.
  - *Fix:* State the exhibited calibration and describe its midpoint and active branches as the construction used for attainment. Present the empirical branch as part of the parameterized estimator definition, with its role distinguished from the exhibited calibration.
- **[minor·prose] An attaining estimator and Limitations and future work** — The estimator is specified mathematically through four-dimensional integrals and an exact conditional average over every auxiliary mark allocation. The explicit sum enumerates 2^k allocations for each k≤n. Readers need a clearer distinction between this deterministic construction's statistical attainment and its numerical evaluation, especially alongside claims that it supports precision assessments.
  - *Fix:* Describe the exact finite sum and integration requirements near the estimator, and discuss computational scaling and numerical approximation in the limitations subsection. Frame the delivered construction as an explicitly specified statistic attaining the theoretical risk benchmark; identify efficient numerical evaluation as a separate research direction.
- **[minor·prose] Observational model and causal interpretation; Weighted approximation and constrained moment priors** — Several promised definitions arrive late. The observed-experiment subsection introduces L_d and says 'This convention fixes the logarithmic factor' before displaying its formula. The scalar appendix says 'We introduce its formula before defining the approximation problem,' yet both approximation and prior-separation definitions use phi_epsilon before its formula appears in the subsequent theorem.
  - *Fix:* Display L_d=log(ed) at its first introduction. Move the existing scalar formula to the opening of the scalar-functional subsection, before the approximation and separation definitions, retaining the verified environments and their labels.
- **[minor·structure] Appendices** — The appendix organization repeatedly states or paraphrases the same conclusions. The identification and upper-bound appendix provides extended summaries, while the detailed proofs appear later; the weighted-separation theorem also repeats the full minimax and consistency conclusions. This organization makes the route from approximation to statistical separation and attainment harder to follow.
  - *Fix:* Consolidate repeated narrative summaries and place the existing proofs adjacent to their relevant appendix discussions. Add a concise dependency roadmap using cleveref, and give the long combined proof descriptive internal divisions for approximation, sparse comparison, dense comparison, fixed-sample transfer, and final consequences. Preserve the verified statements and conditions.
- **[minor·prose] Minimax rates and consistency** — The sentence 'Increasing the cell count raises the risk, with the logarithmic factor moderating this dependence' reads as a claim about the actual minimax risk. The verified characterization directly supports growth of the comparison scale d/log(ed); its constant-factor sandwich does not itself establish a strict increase in the risk.
  - *Fix:* Write that the characterized risk scale increases with the cell count through d/log(ed), holding sample size and overlap fixed. Keep exact monotonicity claims separate from the rate characterization.
- **[nit·prose] Global** — Appendix navigation frequently uses phrases such as 'The appendix develops' and 'The appendix containing' rather than exact structural cleveref targets. Most appendix sections also lack explicit structural labels, leaving readers to locate the relevant material through theorem references.
  - *Fix:* Add labels to the appendix sections and replace navigational appendix mentions with exact \cref or sentence-initial \Cref commands, without manually preceding them with a kind word.

## Questions for authors
