# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 7.2/10 — The verified minimax characterization is a substantial and carefully scoped contribution, while extensive repetition and fragmented organization require substantial editorial revision.

The paper characterizes sharp pointwise CATE absolute-error rates using labeled and outcome-free covariate-treatment records under a known uniform design, fixed overlap, and public Hölder regularity. Its attaining estimator and original-record converse establish an informative acquisition threshold and distinguish strict improvement from supplied-propensity attainment. The principal interpretations faithfully reflect the verified results, and the closest supervised comparison is handled carefully. Publication requires a more economical presentation that makes the statistical contribution and argument easier to follow.

## Strengths
- The uniform characterization across all auxiliary counts answers a clear statistical question and delivers explicit acquisition consequences.
- The original-record lower bound establishes the result within the specified known-uniform class, with a careful boundary between this guarantee and the published supervised benchmark.
- The estimator is fully specified, including role allocation, tuning, guarded inversion, clipping, and measurability.
- The prose consistently preserves fixed-parameter dependence, distinguishes rate comparisons from numerical risk guarantees, and explains the independent sampling requirements for label conversion.
- The dedicated early related-work section engages competitors' targets, information structures, rates, and attainment conditions.

## Findings
- **[major·structure] Introduction; Setup and assumptions; Estimation with outcome-free records** — The central rate formula reaches the reader after a lengthy setup, while the principal minimax theorem follows an extensive estimator specification. The introduction and abstract describe the formula verbally despite its being the paper's central contribution. Repeated assumption explanations and overlapping definitions obscure the relatively simple statistical question and answer.
  - *Fix:* Display the sharp rate in the introduction with its parameter conditions and constant dependence. Present the main minimax characterization immediately after a compact setup, then explain the attaining construction. Consolidate the identifying and regularity restrictions into a concise model description, retaining detailed witness conventions and basis formulas in the technical appendix with precise cross-references.
- **[minor·structure] Upper-bound proofs; Original-record lower bounds; Proofs of the main results** — Proof organization is fragmented. The upper-bound appendix contains auxiliary proofs and short summaries of guarded inversion and the supplied-propensity argument, while their full proofs appear later under a separate heading. Similarly, the original-record appendix presents the certificate and converse, but the certificate's proof appears after the verification note. The roadmap consequently gives an incomplete account of where the main arguments reside.
  - *Fix:* Place the rectangular and supplied-propensity proofs with the upper-bound appendix, the construction certificate proof with the original-record lower bounds, and the acquisition proof with its consequences. Put the verification note after the mathematical appendices and update the roadmap accordingly.
- **[minor·statement] Sharp precision and sample requirements; Acquisition consequences and supervised comparison** — The acquisition proposition repeats the minimax sandwich, rate branches, boundary equality, and supervised comparison already stated elsewhere. Its central sequence equivalences and target-error requirements are embedded in a long omnibus statement, and the broken quantifier lines make the eventual-membership equivalence difficult to parse.
  - *Fix:* Organize the proposition into clearly identified clauses for supplied-propensity attainment, strict improvement, and target precision. Express eventual membership as one complete quantified clause. Refer to the existing sharp characterization and supervised benchmark for their repeated conclusions.
- **[minor·prose] Setup and assumptions — Primitive variables and identifying restrictions** — The sentences calling the control- and treated-response bounds 'specific to the present analysis' make natural probability bounds sound like additional substantive restrictions on a binary-response population. The primitive-class definition already requires the conditional-mean witnesses to take values in [0,1].
  - *Fix:* Describe these bounds as the natural probability-range conditions for the designated binary-response mean witnesses, imposed throughout the cube. Explain their role in defining the contrast range and clipping, and distinguish them explicitly from stronger interior response bounds.
- **[minor·citation] Setup and assumptions** — Several source attributions lack locators, including the citations accompanying uniform design, overlap, Hölder restrictions, identification, and the sampling experiment. This falls short of the requested source-specific citation convention and makes the relevant model restrictions harder to check.
  - *Fix:* Add accurate model-section or assumption locators to these citations, distinguishing attribution of a source's assumptions from attribution of its statistical conclusions. Use textual citation syntax where an author reference is grammatically required.
- **[minor·structure] Acquisition consequences and supervised comparison; Verification note** — The acquisition proposition includes the numerical identity at n=0 and its totalized negative-power convention, while its statistical conclusions concern n≥2. Boolean encodings, zero-division conventions, and the representation handle similarly occupy considerable reader-facing space. These details are clearly disclosed, but their placement interrupts the statistical exposition.
  - *Fix:* Keep statistical statements on their operative sample-size domain and collect representation conventions in the verification note or a compact artifact-conventions paragraph. Preserve the crosswalk and exact correspondence there without repeating those conventions throughout the exposition.

## Questions for authors
- Which parts of the rectangular correction and fixed-size mixture construction constitute the main methodological advances over the closest supervised construction? A concise explanation would sharpen the introduction's contribution statement.
