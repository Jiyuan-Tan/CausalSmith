\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:common-marginal-recipe-target-concentration}]
Fix \(b\in\{0,1\}\).  Define the two exceptional events in the atom space by
\[
\mathsf A_{\mathrm{tar}}
:=
\left\{w:\frac{\Delta}{16}<|r_b(w)-\theta_b|\right\},
\qquad
\mathsf A_{\mathrm{mass}}
:=
\left\{w:\frac{\Delta}{16}<|S(w)-1|\right\}.
\]
We first record the normalization implication used below.  Outside a \(\Gamma\)-null set the recipe gives
\[
r_b(w)=S(w)\tau(P_b(w)),
\qquad
S(w)>0.
\]
Suppose
\[
\frac{\Delta}{4}<|\tau(P_b(w))-\theta_b|,
\qquad
w\notin \mathsf A_{\mathrm{tar}}\cup\mathsf A_{\mathrm{mass}}.
\]
The second condition means
\[
|r_b(w)-\theta_b|\le \frac{\Delta}{16},
\qquad
|S(w)-1|\le \frac{\Delta}{16}.
\]
The raw-center bound supplied by the recipe normalization is
\[
|\theta_b|\le 1.
\]
Hence
\[
|S(w)\theta_b-\theta_b|
=
|(S(w)-1)\theta_b|
\le \frac{\Delta}{16}.
\]
Using \(r_b(w)=S(w)\tau(P_b(w))\), the triangle inequality gives
\[
S(w)|\tau(P_b(w))-\theta_b|
=
|S(w)\tau(P_b(w))-S(w)\theta_b|
\le
\frac{\Delta}{8}.
\]
Also \(|S(w)-1|\le\Delta/16\) and \(\Delta\le2\) imply
\[
S(w)\ge 1-\frac{\Delta}{16}\ge \frac78 .
\]
Therefore
\[
\frac78\,|\tau(P_b(w))-\theta_b|
\le
S(w)|\tau(P_b(w))-\theta_b|
\le \frac{\Delta}{8},
\]
which contradicts \(\Delta/4<|\tau(P_b(w))-\theta_b|\).  Thus, for \(\Gamma\)-almost every \(w\),
\[
\frac{\Delta}{4}<|\tau(P_b(w))-\theta_b|
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
w\in\mathsf A_{\mathrm{tar}}\cup\mathsf A_{\mathrm{mass}} .
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SemisupervisedDiscreteAteAnnotationFrontier.commonMarginal_normalizedTarget_event

By the definition of the branch prior as the pushforward of \(\Gamma\) under \(P_b\), with the two Boolean branches handled separately,
\[
\Pi_b\!\left\{P:\frac{\Delta}{4}<|\tau(P)-\theta_b|\right\}
=
\Gamma\!\left\{w:\frac{\Delta}{4}<|\tau(P_b(w))-\theta_b|\right\}.
\]
The implication above yields
\[
\Pi_b\!\left\{P:\frac{\Delta}{4}<|\tau(P)-\theta_b|\right\}
\le
\Gamma(\mathsf A_{\mathrm{tar}}\cup\mathsf A_{\mathrm{mass}}).
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SemisupervisedDiscreteAteAnnotationFrontier.commonMarginal_prior_target_concentration

We next bound the two exceptional probabilities.  Chebyshev's inequality, applied under \(\Gamma\) with mean
\[
\theta_b=\int r_b(w)\,d\Gamma(w),
\]
and the assumed variance bound
\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\Gamma}(r_b)\le CkBa,
\]
gives
\[
\Gamma(\mathsf A_{\mathrm{tar}})
\le
\frac{CkBa}{(\Delta/16)^2}.
\]
Similarly, since
\[
\int S(w)\,d\Gamma(w)=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_{\Gamma}(S)\le CkBa,
\]
Chebyshev's inequality gives
\[
\Gamma(\mathsf A_{\mathrm{mass}})
\le
\frac{CkBa}{(\Delta/16)^2}.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SemisupervisedDiscreteAteAnnotationFrontier.commonMarginal_target_concentration

Subadditivity of measure gives
\[
\Gamma(\mathsf A_{\mathrm{tar}}\cup\mathsf A_{\mathrm{mass}})
\le
\Gamma(\mathsf A_{\mathrm{tar}})+\Gamma(\mathsf A_{\mathrm{mass}}).
\]
Combining the preceding displays,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Pi_b\!\left\{P:\frac{\Delta}{4}<|\tau(P)-\theta_b|\right\}
&\le
\frac{CkBa}{(\Delta/16)^2}
+
\frac{CkBa}{(\Delta/16)^2}  \\
&=
\frac{512\,CkBa}{\Delta^2},
\end{aligned}
\]
where the final equality uses \(\Delta>0\).  This is the desired bound for the fixed branch \(b\), and hence for both branches.
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SemisupervisedDiscreteAteAnnotationFrontier.commonMarginal_prior_target_concentration
\end{proof}
