\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:fixed-sample-common-marginal-transfer}]
Take
\[
C_{\mathrm{fixed}}=65536,
\qquad
c_{\mathrm{risk}}=\frac1{65536}.
\]
Fix \(0<\epsilon<1/2\) and a positive variance-bound constant \(C_\epsilon\), and set
\[
c_{\epsilon,C_\epsilon}:=\frac{1}{65536\,C_\epsilon}>0.
\]
Let a calibration, a recipe, and all parameters satisfy the hypotheses of the statement.  Put
\[
\bar\theta_b:=\int \theta_b(w)\,dG(w),\qquad b\in\{0,1\},
\]
so that the raw-center separation in the statement is
\[
\Delta=\bar\theta_1-\bar\theta_0.
\]
The recipe's raw prior centers are exactly these two numbers:
\[
\bar\theta_1-\bar\theta_0
=
\int \theta_1(w)\,dG(w)-\int \theta_0(w)\,dG(w).
\]
The Poisson-prior witness in the hypotheses identifies the recipe's labeled and auxiliary intensities as
\[
u=65536\,n,
\qquad
v=65536\,(n+m).
\]
Separately, the concentration-scale hypothesis, the definition of \(c_{\epsilon,C_\epsilon}\), and \(\Delta=\bar\theta_1-\bar\theta_0\) give
\[
kBa
\le
c_{\epsilon,C_\epsilon}\Delta^2
=
\frac{\Delta^2}{65536\,C_\epsilon}
=
\frac{1}{65536\,C_\epsilon}
\left|\bar\theta_1-\bar\theta_0\right|^2.
\]
Together with \(n\ge1\), \(d\ge2\), and \(\operatorname{TV}(M_0,M_1)\le1/8\), these are precisely the hypotheses of \cref{obj:lem:common-marginal-recipe-transfer}, applied with the present calibration, recipe, variance-bound constant \(C_\epsilon\), likelihood-decay parameter \(\rho\), and intensities \(u=65536n\), \(v=65536(n+m)\).  That result yields, for the minimax risk \(R_\epsilon(n,m,d)\) of \cref{obj:def:minimax-risk},
\[
R_\epsilon(n,m,d)
\ge
\frac{1}{65536}
\left|\bar\theta_1-\bar\theta_0\right|^2.
\]
Since \(|\bar\theta_1-\bar\theta_0|^2=\Delta^2\) and \(c_{\mathrm{risk}}=1/65536\), this is exactly
\[
c_{\mathrm{risk}}\Delta^2\le R_\epsilon(n,m,d).
\] % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SemisupervisedDiscreteAteAnnotationFrontier.fixed_sample_common_marginal_transfer
\end{proof}
