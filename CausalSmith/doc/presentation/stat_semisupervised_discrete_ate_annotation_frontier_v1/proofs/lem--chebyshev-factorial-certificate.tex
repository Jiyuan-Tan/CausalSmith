\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:chebyshev-factorial-certificate}]
Choose \(A=7056\), so \(A>1\).

1. Fix an integer \(L\ge2\).  Write
\[
Q_L(z)=T_L(1-2z),
\qquad
H_L(z)=\frac{1-Q_L(z)}{2L^2},
\qquad
E_L(z)=H_L(z)/z,
\qquad
G_L(z)=\{1-E_L(z)\}/z .
\]
Since \(T_L\) has degree \(L\) and \(1-2z\) has degree at most \(1\),
\[
\deg Q_L\le L .
\]
Multiplication by the scalar \((2L^2)^{-1}\) does not increase degree, and subtraction from \(1\) gives
\[
\deg H_L
\le \deg(1-Q_L)
\le \max\{0,\deg Q_L\}
\le L .
\]
Because \(E_L\) is the quotient of \(H_L\) by \(z\), its degree is at most \(L-1\).  Hence
\[
\deg(1-E_L)\le \max\{0,\deg E_L\}\le L-1,
\]
using \(L\ge2\).  Dividing once more by \(z\) gives
\[
\deg G_L\le L-2 .
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SemisupervisedDiscreteAteAnnotationFrontier.chebyshev_factorial_certificate

2. By \cref{obj:lem:explicit-chebyshev-calibration}, for every \(z\in[0,1]\),
\[
0\le E_L(z)\le1,
\qquad
zG_L(z)+E_L(z)=1,
\]
and, for \(z>0\),
\[
E_L(z)\le \frac{1}{L^2z}.
\]
These are exactly the Chebyshev-certificate assertions. 
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SemisupervisedDiscreteAteAnnotationFrontier.explicit_chebyshev_calibration

3. It remains to check the moment assertions with the same constant.  Let
\(a\in\{0,1\}\), \(t>0\), \(B>0\), \(s_0,s_1\ge0\), and \(L\le tB\), and let
\(K_0\sim\operatorname{Poi}(ts_0)\) and \(K_1\sim\operatorname{Poi}(ts_1)\) be independent.  Applying \cref{obj:lem:explicit-chebyshev-calibration} with \(p=s_0+s_1\) gives
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[\widehat W_{a,L}(K_0,K_1)^2\right]
\le
7056^L\left(1+\frac{s_0+s_1}{B}\right)^{2L}.
\]
If in addition \(s_0+s_1\le B\), the same calibration yields
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[\widehat W_{a,L}(K_0,K_1)^2\right]
\le
7056^L .
\]
Since \(A=7056\), these are the two required factorial-lift moment bounds. 
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.SemisupervisedDiscreteAteAnnotationFrontier.chebyshev_factorial_certificate
\end{proof}
