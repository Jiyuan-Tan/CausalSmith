# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.7/10 — The verified overlap-uniform frontier is a worthwhile theoretical contribution, but a misleading benchmark-attainment claim and substantial organizational and definitional problems require revision.

The paper characterizes minimax squared-error risk for a discrete population treatment contrast using independent complete and auxiliary records, uniformly over sample sizes, alphabet size, and treatment overlap. Its strongest contribution is the universal-constant characterization under diminishing overlap, accompanied by original-record attainment, exact resource criteria, and exact-marginal benchmark recovery. Taking the verified results as established, publication requires clearer qualification of benchmark attainment and a substantial repair of the exposition.

## Strengths
- The four-index comparison explicitly separates rare-arm outcome information from pooled treatment–covariate information, with constants uniform over diminishing overlap.
- The results cover arbitrary covariate masses, unrestricted binary outcome regressions, null cells, and unequal sample budgets.
- The exact resource criteria and fixed-parameter convergence to the exact-marginal risk provide useful interpretations of auxiliary information.
- The manuscript carefully distinguishes common marginal distributions under testing priors from the joint information supplied by both observation channels.
- A dedicated early related-work section credits the supervised matching result and fixed-overlap annotation parent.

## Findings
- **[major·prose] Introduction** — The sentence “Attainment of rare-label benchmark order is equivalent to” the displayed conditions incorrectly presents divergence of S_k as necessary for benchmark-order attainment itself. The verified oracle phase explicitly combines benchmark attainment with S_k tending to infinity. For example, a constant two-cell experiment with n=1 and epsilon=1/4 attains the capped benchmark order by the verified benchmark comparison while S_k remains constant.
  - *Fix:* Write “Joint consistency and attainment of rare-label benchmark order are equivalent to” the displayed conditions, or state the benchmark-attainment equivalence conditional on S_k tending to infinity. Use the same qualification when describing this sequence criterion in the abstract.
- **[major·structure] Minimax precision with two information budgets** — The result sequence and its transitions are substantially scrambled. “The next result establishes this benchmark through two laws” introduces the estimator algorithm; “The next comparison relates the uniform rate to three useful reference cases” introduces the frontier theorem; and “we now specify the estimator ... The clipping operation is defined first” introduces the rare-label theorem after both objects have already appeared. These mismatches obstruct the central argument.
  - *Fix:* Present the numerical rate and frontier theorem first, followed by the rare-label floor and benchmark proposition. Move the complete estimator procedure into “An attaining estimator,” with clipping defined immediately before its use. Rewrite each transition against the actual following environment and preserve every environment identifier.
- **[major·structure] Experiment, target, and causal interpretation** — The prose promises to collect the cell quantities “in a common definition,” but their mathematical definitions never appear in the model section. In particular, the target uses mu_aj(P), and later constructions use s_aj(P) and q_aj(P), while their explicit formulas and null-arm convention first appear in appendix material. The section also ends with benchmarks supposedly “defined as follows,” followed by explanatory prose and a new section.
  - *Fix:* Before the target definition, explicitly define p_j(P), s_aj(P), q_aj(P), and mu_aj(P), including the zero convention when s_aj(P)=0. Replace the unfulfilled common-definition promise. Either define the benchmark functions at their introductory passage or direct readers to \cref{obj:prop:benchmark-recovery}.
- **[minor·citation] Related work** — The closest comparisons receive credit, but the discussion gives mostly qualitative descriptions rather than their actual rates and domains. In particular, the fixed-interior supervised comparator's sufficiently-large-sample and alphabet restrictions remain unspecified, and the fixed-overlap annotation parent's rate is left implicit. These details matter for assessing the incremental contribution.
  - *Fix:* Add a compact comparison displaying the supervised conventional upper bound, fixed-overlap supervised matching order with its stated domain, and fixed-overlap two-channel frontier. Identify which constants depend on the fixed overlap floor, then explain the present universal-constant extension. Supply accurate theorem locators and public bibliographic identification for the credited working paper.
- **[minor·prose] Discussion and limitations — Limitations and future work** — The phrase “conservative public tuning” understates the practical meaning of the verified estimator's threshold. The attaining estimator outputs zero whenever n epsilon is below exp(4096), so its uniform order guarantee relies on extraordinarily large comparison constants over all realistic sample sizes. This affects how readers should interpret the annotation-planning discussion.
  - *Fix:* State the threshold explicitly in the limitations subsection and explain that the construction certifies minimax order with proof-oriented constants. Describe the resource conditions as order-based planning benchmarks and distinguish them from calibrated finite-budget recommendations.
- **[minor·citation] Testing and approximation lower bounds** — The standard Pinsker inequality, Markov polynomial inequality, and finite polynomial approximation duality appear as lengthy standalone developments without theorem-local external attribution. The supplied declaration metadata identifies relevant sources and locators, including Wu and Yang's Remark 4 and Appendix E, identity (34), and Pierzchała's Theorem 1.1.
  - *Fix:* Add accurate source citations at the introduction or statement of these standard results. Retain the verified statements and present their detailed derivations as supporting mathematical material, with clear attribution distinguishing standard tools from the new statistical construction.

## Questions for authors

- What public bibliographic entry and exact theorem locator identify the credited fixed-interior supervised matching result?
