# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 7/10 — The verified characterization is a substantial theoretical contribution with careful scope control, but extensive repetition, disrupted ordering, and several concrete presentation errors require substantial editorial revision.

The paper characterizes minimax welfare estimation and globally honest connected-interval length under pure local privacy in a randomized categorical trial, conditional on a published Bernstein approximation result. Its noninteractive attainment and sequential converses establish common statistical orders, and the signed correspondence supplies a matching paired-family total-variation characterization and full-simplex lower bounds. The verified results support the central contribution; publication requires a clearer and considerably more economical presentation.

## Strengths
- The joint characterization of estimation risk, honest expected length, and sequential interaction across the entire stated resource domain is substantive.
- The causal model, privacy domain, sampling assumptions, treatment ties, and confidence-interval criterion are specified precisely.
- The private coordinate-dependence bounds and adaptive higher-order contraction provide distinctive methodological contributions beyond the classical approximation strategy.
- The dedicated early related-work section engages competitors' actual rates and conditions and distinguishes population-optimum estimation from policy regret.
- The manuscript carefully distinguishes matching paired-family results from full-simplex lower bounds and candidly explains the construction's practical calibration in its limitations section.
- The verification disclosures accurately preserve the external Bernstein source boundary.

## Findings
- **[major·structure] Global; Randomized trials, local privacy, and welfare; main theorem sections** — The organization repeatedly interrupts the statistical argument with lengthy packages of conclusions and attainment properties. The signed-experiment subsection announces the symmetric causal construction and then inserts the full resource-rate definition. The following subsection introduces sequence conclusions and paired procedures before the paired model and decision criteria receive their complete definitions. Resource properties subsequently appear again in both main theorems and surrounding prose. These repetitions obscure the hierarchy between model definitions, constructions, central results, and consequences.
  - *Fix:* Present the causal model and decision criteria first, followed by the signed bridge and symmetric family. Introduce the rate function once, retaining only notation in its definition. State the central risk, length, and interaction characterization compactly, and collect the already-established resource consequences in one shared corollary. Move paired-model definitions immediately before the paired result, place its concrete procedure after those definitions, and consolidate repeated attainment and sequence packages. Add a compact regime table to organize the existing consequences while preserving their conditions and quantifiers.
- **[minor·prose] Private messages and the welfare procedure** — The algorithm commentary misidentifies the numbered steps twice. It states, 'Items 1--7 and 9 below specify collection and the reported estimate and interval. Item 8 records converse tuning,' and later again assigns converse tuning to item 8. In the displayed construction, item 8 combines and clips the estimate, item 9 specifies converse tuning, and item 10 outputs the interval. The sentence 'it is an analytical choice and is not an operation performed by participants or the analyst' also uses avoidable negative framing.
  - *Fix:* Correct the operational steps to items 1--8 and 10, and identify item 9 as converse tuning in both explanatory paragraphs. Use affirmative wording such as 'Item 9 specifies the priors used in the lower-bound analysis.'
- **[minor·statement] Matching precision on the paired family** — The paired theorem locates its concrete total-variation procedure through '\cref{obj:def:frontier-handle,obj:thm:uniform-private-value-frontiers}', which specify the welfare construction and its guarantee. The paired construction has different participant allocation, estimator, and interval range and is explicitly specified elsewhere. The current pointer makes the theorem's attainment clause unnecessarily ambiguous.
  - *Fix:* Point the concrete paired attainment clause directly to '\cref{obj:synth_5}', retaining the welfare references only when explaining the shared analytical ingredients. Align the corresponding explanatory references throughout the paired section.
- **[minor·prose] Trial population and welfare** — Several assumption explanations follow the wrong displayed assumption: uniform sampling is discussed after fair randomization, randomized assignment after consistency, consistency after interior means, and interior means after iid sampling. This systematic displacement weakens the motivation of otherwise precise assumptions.
  - *Fix:* Attach each explanatory paragraph directly to the assumption it motivates, or collect the explanations in one clearly ordered paragraph following the complete causal-class definition. Preserve the separate distinction between single-participant restrictions and iid sampling.
- **[minor·prose] Private messages and the welfare procedure; Uniform precision across resource regimes** — The operational overview says 'the pilot determines which treatment contrasts are close to zero,' and the theorem interpretation describes 'the pilot localizing the approximation when resources are large.' Pilot selection operates in the branch requiring both L >= 4096 and t >= d^2 L. The other branches use different decisions, and the n = 2 branch uses constant messages. The overview should carry these gates as faithfully as the displayed construction.
  - *Fix:* Describe block allocation for n > 2 explicitly and qualify pilot localization by both displayed branch conditions. State affirmatively which estimates the remaining branches use. At the first algorithm overview, identify the procedure as a conservative uniform rate-attainment witness, consistent with the later quantitative limitations.
- **[minor·prose] Uniform precision across resource regimes** — The interpretation explains approximation bias and moment variability generically, but leaves the distinctive d^2 private-resource scale largely inside the construction and appendix. Readers receive the frontier formula without a concise explanation connecting the released coordinate magnitude, block noise, and approximation degree to its dimension dependence.
  - *Fix:* Add a short heuristic using the existing construction: explain that the scaled coordinate magnitude is d/delta, the block noise scale is comparable to d^2/t under the stated allocation and privacy range, and polynomial approximation balances that noise against aggregate approximation bias. Use the existing calibration results to explain the logarithmic gain and the roles of the intermediate and dense branches, clearly distinguishing this intuition from the verified bounds.

## Questions for authors
- Can the rate and sequence consequences be consolidated into one shared result so that the paired theorem emphasizes its model, attainment, and transfer implications?
- Which aspects of the adaptive contraction and coordinate-dependence control do the authors regard as the principal methodological advances, and can those be made more prominent in the main-text explanation?
