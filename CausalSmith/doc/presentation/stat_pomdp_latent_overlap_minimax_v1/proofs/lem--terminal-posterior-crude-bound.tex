\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:terminal-posterior-crude-bound}]
Write \(\alpha=\exp(-1/t_0)\) and \(c_0=(1-\alpha)/4\).

1. Since \(t_0>0\), one has \(0<\alpha<1\). Hence
\[
0\le c_0=\frac{1-\alpha}{4}<1,
\qquad
0<1-c_0,
\qquad
0\le 1-c_0\cdot 1 .
\]
For every observed prefix \(o'\), the raw terminal-depth posterior satisfies
\[
0\le \pi_v(o')\le 1.
\]
Indeed, the numerator is a sum of nonnegative filter masses over terminal-depth states, the denominator \(\mathsf M_v(o')\) is positive, and the terminal-depth mass is bounded by the total mass. % lean: signedDepthRawPosterior_mem_Icc

2. First suppose \(Q\le k\). By \cref{obj:lem:terminal-posterior-mature-window},
\[
\pi_v(o)\le \frac{\alpha^Q}{\Lambda_Q(o)} .
\]
Apply \cref{obj:lem:posterior-lower-window-power} with \(b=1\) and \(d=Q\). The hypotheses are exactly those checked above: \(0\le c_0\), \(0\le 1-c_0\cdot 1\), \(Q\le k\), and \(\pi_v(o_{\le i})\le 1\) for every strict prefix. Thus
\[
(1-c_0)^Q\le \Lambda_Q(o).
\]
Since \(\alpha^Q\ge 0\) and \(1-c_0>0\), division by the larger positive denominator gives
\[
\pi_v(o)
\le
\frac{\alpha^Q}{(1-c_0)^Q}
=
\left(\frac{\alpha}{1-c_0}\right)^Q .
\]
% lean: signedDepthRawPosterior_le_ratio_pow

3. It remains to consider \(k<Q\). By \cref{obj:lem:terminal-posterior-initial-window},
\[
\pi_v(o)\le \frac{\alpha^Q}{\Lambda_k(o)} .
\]
Apply \cref{obj:lem:posterior-lower-window-power} with \(b=1\) and \(d=k\). Since \(k\le k\), and the prefix posterior bound \(\pi_v(o_{\le i})\le 1\) holds for every strict prefix, this gives
\[
(1-c_0)^k\le \Lambda_k(o).
\]
Therefore
\[
\pi_v(o)
\le
\frac{\alpha^Q}{(1-c_0)^k}.
\]
Because \(0<1-c_0\le 1\) and \(k<Q\), monotonicity of powers on \([0,1]\) yields
\[
(1-c_0)^Q\le (1-c_0)^k .
\]
Dividing the nonnegative numerator \(\alpha^Q\) by these positive denominators gives
\[
\frac{\alpha^Q}{(1-c_0)^k}
\le
\frac{\alpha^Q}{(1-c_0)^Q}
=
\left(\frac{\alpha}{1-c_0}\right)^Q .
\]
Combining the displayed inequalities proves the claimed bound in the initial-window case as well. % lean: signedDepthRawPosterior_le_ratio_pow
\end{proof}
