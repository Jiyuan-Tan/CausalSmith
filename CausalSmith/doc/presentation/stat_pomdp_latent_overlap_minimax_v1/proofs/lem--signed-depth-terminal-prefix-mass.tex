\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:signed-depth-terminal-prefix-mass}]
1.  Let \(\Gamma_{k+1}\) denote the set of joint-state prefixes
\[
\gamma=(S_1,\ldots,S_{k+1}),
\qquad
S_i\in \{0\}\times\{0,\ldots,2(Q+1)-1\}.
\]
For such a prefix, write \(W_v(\gamma;o)\) for the real chronological weight of the prefix whose observed action/reward word is \(o\). Summing the full trajectory law over all continuations after the cut gives
\[
m_v^Q(o)
=
\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma_{k+1}}
\mathbf 1\{J(S_{k+1}^{\mathrm{hid}})=Q\}\,
W_v(\gamma;o).
\]
This is exactly the fixed-prefix expansion of the terminal-depth event at the cut after \(k\) observed epochs; the hypothesis \(U\ge 1\) ensures that this cut is a valid time in the horizon \(U+k\). % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.terminalPrefixMass_eq_finiteFixedPrefixWeight_sum

2.  For a joint state \(s\in \{0\}\times\{0,\ldots,2(Q+1)-1\}\), define
\[
\Lambda_v(s;o)
:=
\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma_{k+1}}
\mathbf 1\{S_{k+1}=s\}\,W_v(\gamma;o).
\]
Then
\[
\sum_{s}
\mathbf 1\{J(s^{\mathrm{hid}})=Q\}\Lambda_v(s;o)
=
\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma_{k+1}}
\mathbf 1\{J(S_{k+1}^{\mathrm{hid}})=Q\}W_v(\gamma;o).
\]
Indeed, interchange the two finite sums. For each fixed prefix \(\gamma\), the inner sum over \(s\) has exactly one nonzero contribution, namely \(s=S_{k+1}\), and therefore returns the displayed indicator and weight. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.sum_finiteTerminalStatePrefixMass_indicator

3.  Combining the preceding two displays yields
\[
m_v^Q(o)
=
\sum_{s\in \{0\}\times\{0,\ldots,2(Q+1)-1\}}
\mathbf 1\{J(s^{\mathrm{hid}})=Q\}\Lambda_v(s;o).
\]
The observed-state coordinate has the singleton value \(0\), so this sum is
\[
m_v^Q(o)
=
\sum_{h=0}^{2(Q+1)-1}
\mathbf 1\{J(h)=Q\}\Lambda_v((0,h);o).
\]
For the signed-depth filter at the cut after the word \(o\), the unnormalized mass assigned to hidden coordinate \(h\) is the terminal-state prefix mass at the joint state \((0,h)\):
\[
\lambda_v(h;o)
:=
\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma_{k+1}}
\mathbf 1\{S_{k+1}=(0,h)\}\,W_v(\gamma;o).
\]
Thus \(\lambda_v(h;o)=\Lambda_v((0,h);o)\) for every \(h\), and the claimed identity follows. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.signedDepthFilterMass
\end{proof}
