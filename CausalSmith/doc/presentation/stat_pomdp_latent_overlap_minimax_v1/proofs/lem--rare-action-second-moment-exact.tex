\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:rare-action-second-moment-exact}]
Let
\[
S:=\{i\in\{1,\ldots,k\}: k-\ell<i\le k\}.
\]
Reindex each observed prefix \(o\) by its action sequence \(a=(a_1,\ldots,a_k)\) and reward-symbol sequence \(y=(y_1,\ldots,y_k)\). This bijection gives
\[
\sum_o \pi_k^v(o)\mathfrak a_k(o)^2
=
\sum_a\sum_y
\pi_k^v(a,y)
\left(
L^{-(Q-\ell)}
\prod_{i\in S}\mathbf 1\{a_i=1\}
\right)^2 .
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.obsPrefixActionRewardEquiv

For each action sequence \(a\), the product
\[
I_S(a):=\prod_{i\in S}\mathbf 1\{a_i=1\}
\]
is either \(0\) or \(1\). Hence \(I_S(a)^2=I_S(a)\), including the empty-product case \(S=\varnothing\), where \(I_S(a)=1\). Therefore
\[
\sum_o \pi_k^v(o)\mathfrak a_k(o)^2
=
L^{-2(Q-\ell)}
\sum_a I_S(a)\sum_y \pi_k^v(a,y).
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.rareAction_secondMoment_eq

After summing over the reward symbols with the actions fixed, the observed prefix mass factors as the behavior action probability product:
\[
\sum_y \pi_k^v(a,y)
=
\prod_{i=1}^k b(a_i),
\qquad
b(1)=\frac1L,\quad b(0)=1-\frac1L .
\]
This is the reward-summed prefix identity for the signed-depth family of \cref{obj:def:signed-depth-family}. Thus
\[
\sum_o \pi_k^v(o)\mathfrak a_k(o)^2
=
L^{-2(Q-\ell)}
\sum_a
\left(\prod_{i=1}^k b(a_i)\right)
\left(\prod_{i\in S}\mathbf 1\{a_i=1\}\right).
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.sum_rewards_prefixMass_eq_behaviorProduct

It remains to evaluate the action sum. Since the behavior action law is product-form,
\[
\sum_a
\left(\prod_{i=1}^k b(a_i)\right)
\left(\prod_{i\in S}\mathbf 1\{a_i=1\}\right)
=
\prod_{i=1}^k
\sum_{a_i\in\{0,1\}}
b(a_i)\,
\begin{cases}
\mathbf 1\{a_i=1\},& i\in S,\\
1,& i\notin S.
\end{cases}
\]
For \(i\in S\), the inner sum is \(b(1)=1/L\); for \(i\notin S\), it is \(b(0)+b(1)=1\). Hence
\[
\sum_a
\left(\prod_{i=1}^k b(a_i)\right)
\left(\prod_{i\in S}\mathbf 1\{a_i=1\}\right)
=
\left(\frac1L\right)^{|S|}.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.sum_behaviorProduct_mul_trueIndicator

Combining the preceding displays and recalling
\(S=\{i:k-\ell<i\le k\}\) gives
\[
\sum_o \pi_k^v(o)\mathfrak a_k(o)^2
=
L^{-2(Q-\ell)}
\left(\frac1L\right)^{|\{i:\ k-\ell<i\le k\}|},
\]
as claimed.
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.rareAction_secondMoment_eq
\end{proof}
