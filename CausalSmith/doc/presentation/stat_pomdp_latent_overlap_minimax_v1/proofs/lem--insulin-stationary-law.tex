\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:insulin-stationary-law}]
Write
\[
P=P_p^{(T)}
\]
for the transition matrix induced by the policy \(p\) on the finite state space
\(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}\). We verify the hypotheses of the finite-state
contraction fixed-point criterion and then identify the selected fixed point.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Since \(p(\cdot\mid x)\) is a probability law on \(\{0,1\}\) for each
observed grid state \(x\), and since the kernel of \(\mathcal I_{\mathrm{grid}}\)
is a Markov kernel, the policy mixture
\[
P(s,s')
=
\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}} p(a\mid x(s))\,K_T(s,a;s')
\]
is stochastic. Thus, for every probability vector \(\nu\) on
\(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}\),
\[
(\nu P)(s')=\sum_{s\in\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}}\nu(s)P(s,s')
\]
is again a probability vector on \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}\). The initial law specified for \(\mathcal I_{\mathrm{grid}}\) is also a probability
vector. % lean: policyKernel_probabilityVector, insulinEmbeddedInit_probabilityVector

\item By the stochasticity verified in the preceding step, the map
\(\nu\mapsto \nu P\) sends the probability simplex into itself. The displayed
hypothesis of \cref{obj:lem:insulin-stationary-law} gives
\[
\|\nu P-\nu'P\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
\le
\frac12\|\nu-\nu'\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
\]
for all probability vectors \(\nu,\nu'\). The constant \(1/2\) is nonnegative
and strictly smaller than \(1\). Since the probability simplex over the finite
set \(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}\) is complete in total variation distance, the
contraction mapping theorem gives a probability vector \(d\) such that
\[
dP=d .
\]
Equivalently,
\[
d(s')=\sum_{s\in\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}}d(s)P(s,s'),
\qquad s'\in\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}} .
\]
% lean: exists_stationary_of_contraction

\item The selected law \(d_p\) is defined as the stationary law chosen for
\(P\) whenever such a stationary probability vector exists. The preceding step
supplies that existence, so the selected law is itself a probability vector and
satisfies
\[
d_pP=d_p .
\]
Reading this coordinatewise yields
\[
d_p(s')
=
\sum_{s\in\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}}d_p(s)P_p^{(T)}(s,s'),
\qquad s'\in\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}} ,
\]
which is the asserted stationarity equation. % lean: stationaryLaw_isStationary_of_exists
\end{enumerate}
\end{proof}
