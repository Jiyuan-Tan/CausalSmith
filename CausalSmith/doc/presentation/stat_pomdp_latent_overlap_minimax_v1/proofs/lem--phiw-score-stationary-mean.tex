\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:phiw-score-stationary-mean}]
Write \(S_j\) for the joint state at zero-based epoch \(j\), on the same time scale as \(Z_t(k)\). For a policy \(p\in\{b,e\}\) and a function \(\varphi\) on joint states, set
\[
(P_p\varphi)(s)
:=
\sum_{s'} P_p(s,s')\varphi(s'),
\qquad
P_p(s,s')
=
\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}}p(a\mid x_s)\,
K(s,a)\{(y,s''):\ s''=s'\}.
\]
Also write the observable ratio, with its zero-cell convention, as
\[
\rho_j
=
\rho(X_j,A_j)
:=
\begin{cases}
e(A_j\mid X_j)/b(A_j\mid X_j),& b(A_j\mid X_j)>0,\\
0,& b(A_j\mid X_j)=0.
\end{cases}
\]
By \cref{obj:def:model-class}, the model supplies \cref{obj:ass:pomdp-kernel,obj:ass:sequential-ignorability,obj:ass:stationary-start,obj:ass:bounded-reward,obj:ass:policy-overlap}, with \(L=\exp(\zeta)\). Since \(0<\zeta\), \(1\le L\).

Let
\[
\iota:=t-k .
\]
The hypothesis \(k\le t\) makes \(\iota\) a valid epoch, and \(\iota+k=t<T\). We first compute the mean of the score from the state at the beginning of its ratio window:
\[
\int Z_t(k)\,d\mathbb P_M^{\mathrm{obs}}
=
\int (P_e^k g_e)(S_\iota)\,d\mathbb P_M .
\]
Indeed, the score is a function of the observed trajectory, so its observed-law integral is the corresponding full-trajectory integral,
\[
\int Z_t(k)\,d\mathbb P_M^{\mathrm{obs}}
=
\int
Y_t\prod_{j=\iota}^{t}\rho_j
\,d\mathbb P_M .
\]
For any bounded \(\varphi\) and any epoch \(j<T-1\), \cref{obj:ass:sequential-ignorability,obj:ass:pomdp-kernel,obj:ass:policy-overlap} give
\[
\mathbb E_M\!\left[\rho_j\,\varphi(S_{j+1})\mid S_j=s\right]
=
\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}} e(a\mid x_s)
\int \varphi(s')\,K(s,a)(dy,ds')
=
(P_e\varphi)(s).
\]
The same conditional calculation at the terminal reward gives
\[
\mathbb E_M\!\left[\rho_t\,Y_t\mid S_t=s\right]
=
\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}} e(a\mid x_s)
\int y\,K(s,a)(dy,ds')
=
g_e(s).
\]
The zero-cell convention is compatible with the displayed equalities because \(b(a\mid x_s)=0\) and \(e(a\mid x_s)\le Lb(a\mid x_s)\) imply \(e(a\mid x_s)=0\). Iterating the preceding identities backward from \(t\) to \(\iota\), with boundedness supplied by \cref{obj:ass:bounded-reward} and finite state spaces from \cref{obj:def:model-class}, yields the displayed score identity.

It remains to identify the law of \(S_\iota\). For every function \(\varphi\) on joint states,
\[
\int \varphi(S_\iota)\,d\mathbb P_M
=
\sum_s d_b(s)\varphi(s).
\]
At the initial epoch this is \cref{obj:ass:stationary-start}. If the identity holds at epoch \(j\), then \cref{obj:ass:sequential-ignorability,obj:ass:pomdp-kernel} give
\[
\int \varphi(S_{j+1})\,d\mathbb P_M
=
\int (P_b\varphi)(S_j)\,d\mathbb P_M
=
\sum_s d_b(s)\sum_{s'}P_b(s,s')\varphi(s').
\]
The selected behavior stationary law is stationary for \(P_b\), so for every \(s'\),
\[
\sum_s d_b(s)P_b(s,s')=d_b(s'),
\]
and therefore
\[
\int \varphi(S_{j+1})\,d\mathbb P_M
=
\sum_{s'}d_b(s')\varphi(s').
\]
Induction gives the claim at \(\iota\).

Applying the last identity with \(\varphi=P_e^k g_e\) and substituting into the score identity gives
\[
\int Z_t(k)\,d\mathbb P_M^{\mathrm{obs}}
=
\sum_s d_b(s)\,(P_e^k g_e)(s),
\]
which is the asserted equality.
\end{proof}
