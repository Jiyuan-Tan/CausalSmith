\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:observed-path-kl}]
Fix \(t_0>0\), put \(\alpha=\exp(-1/t_0)\), and set \(c_0=(1-\alpha)/4\).

1.  By \cref{obj:lem:signed-depth-bootstrap-factor}, there is a constant \(K_0>0\), depending only on \(t_0\), such that for every \(Q\in\mathbb N\),
\[
\frac{1}{\{1-c_0(\alpha/(1-c_0))^Q\}^Q}\le K_0 .
\]
Fix \(\zeta>0\), \(C>1\), and the two stated model-class hypotheses. Let
\[
B_0:=\frac{4c_0^2K_0^2}{1-c_0^2}.
\]
Since \(0<\alpha<1\), we have \(0<c_0<1\), hence \(1-c_0^2>0\), and therefore \(B_0>0\). % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.observed_path_kl

2.  Fix \(T\in\mathbb N\) and \(Q\ge1\). The refined posterior estimate in \cref{obj:lem:refined-terminal-posterior}, combined with the choice of \(K_0\) in step 1, gives the uniform terminal-depth posterior bound
\[
q_t^v(o)\le K_0\alpha^Q
\]
for every auxiliary horizon \(N\), every \(v\in\{-1,+1\}\), every \(t<N\), and every observed prefix \(o\in\mathcal F_{t-1}^{\mathrm{obs}}\). In particular, for the present horizon \(T\),
\[
q_t^v(o)\le K_0\alpha^Q,
\qquad
v\in\{-1,+1\},\ t<T,\ o\in\mathcal F_{t-1}^{\mathrm{obs}} .
\] % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.terminalPosterior_le_of_bootstrap_factor

3.  Applying \cref{obj:lem:finite-signed-depth-kl} with the posterior bound from step 2 yields the finite observed-word inequality
\[
\operatorname{KL}\!\left(
\mathbb P_{Q,+1}^{\mathrm{fin},T}
\,\middle\|\,
\mathbb P_{Q,-1}^{\mathrm{fin},T}
\right)
\le
\frac{4c_0^2K_0^2}{1-c_0^2}\,
T\,\varepsilon_C^2\,\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}.
\]
The observed-path law is obtained from the finite observed-word law by the deterministic reward decoding map in \cref{obj:def:signed-depth-family}. The data-processing inequality for this embedding gives
\[
\operatorname{KL}\!\left(
\mathbb P_{Q,+1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}
\,\middle\|\,
\mathbb P_{Q,-1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}
\right)
\le
\frac{4c_0^2K_0^2}{1-c_0^2}\,
T\,\varepsilon_C^2\,\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}.
\]
Equivalently, by the definition of \(B_0\),
\[
\operatorname{KL}\!\left(
\mathbb P_{Q,+1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}
\,\middle\|\,
\mathbb P_{Q,-1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}
\right)
\le
B_0\,T\,\varepsilon_C^2\,\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}.
\] % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.signedDepth_finiteObs_klDiv_le

4.  Since \(C>1\), the reset amplitude satisfies
\[
0\le \varepsilon_C=\min\{(C-1)/2,1/2\}\le 1 .
\]
Also \(L=\exp(\zeta)>0\), so \(L^{-Q}\ge0\), and \(\alpha^{2Q}\ge0\). Hence
\[
B_0\,T\,\varepsilon_C^2\,\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}
\le
B_0\,T\,\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}.
\]
Monotonicity of \(x\mapsto \operatorname{ofReal}(x)\) on this real inequality gives
\[
\operatorname{KL}\!\left(
\mathbb P_{Q,+1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}
\,\middle\|\,
\mathbb P_{Q,-1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}
\right)
\le
B_0\,T\,\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}.
\] % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.observed_path_kl

5.  Finally, \cref{obj:lem:signed-depth-conditional-reward-mean} supplies, for each \(v\in\{-1,+1\}\), each \(t<T\), and each observed prefix \(o\in\mathcal F_{t-1}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), the exact identity
\[
\mathbb E_v[Y_t\mid \mathcal F_{t-1}^{\mathrm{obs}}=o]
=
v\,c_0\,\varepsilon_C\,a_t^{(Q)}(o)\,q_t^v(o).
\]
Together with the posterior bound in step 2 and the sharper KL estimate from step 3, this gives all three asserted conclusions:
\[
q_t^v(o)\le K_0\alpha^Q
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{KL}\!\left(
\mathbb P_{Q,+1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}
\,\middle\|\,
\mathbb P_{Q,-1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}
\right)
\le
\frac{4c_0^2K_0^2}{1-c_0^2}\,
T\,\varepsilon_C^2\,\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}.
\] % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.conditionalRewardMean_signedDepth_all
\end{proof}
