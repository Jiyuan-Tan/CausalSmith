\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:insulin-policy-contraction}]
Fix \(T\), a policy \(p\), and probability vectors \(\nu,\nu'\) on
\(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}\).  We prove the displayed total-variation
bound; the \(\ell^1\) formulation follows from
\(\|\eta\|_1=2\|\eta\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\).

1. Let \(\mathsf U(s')=1/360\) be the uniform law on
\(\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}\).  For
\(s=(g,d_1,d_2,x_1,x_2,a_1)\), \(a\in\{0,1\}\), and
\(s'=(g',d'_1,d'_2,x'_1,x'_2,a'_1)\), define the non-refresh transition
weight
\[
\mathsf D(s,a;s')
=
\gamma_{s,a}(g')\,
\pi_d(d'_1)\,
\pi_x(x'_1)\,
\mathbf 1\{d'_2=d_1\}\,
\mathbf 1\{x'_2=x_1\}\,
\mathbf 1\{a'_1=a\},
\]
where \(\pi_d(0)=4/5\), \(\pi_d(78)=1/5\),
\(\pi_x(0)=2/5\), \(\pi_x(31)=2/5\), \(\pi_x(850)=1/5\), and
\[
\gamma_{s,a}(g^\star)
=
\Pr\!\left\{
\operatorname{round}_{\{50,100,150,200,250\}}
\operatorname{clip}_{[50,250]}
\bigl(
10+0.9g+0.1d_1+0.1d_2-0.01x_1-0.01x_2-2a-4a_1+\xi
\bigr)
=g^\star
\right\}
\]
for \(\xi\sim N(0,25)\).  The refresh construction in
\cref{obj:def:insulin-grid} gives, after marginalizing over the reward,
\[
K_T\{(q,s''):\ s''=s'\mid s,a\}
=
\frac{1}{720}+\frac12\,\mathsf D(s,a;s').
\]
Therefore the policy-induced matrix can be written as
\[
P_p^{(T)}(s,s')
=
\frac{1}{720}
+\frac12 R_p(s,s'),
\qquad
R_p(s,s')
=
\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}}p(a\mid x(s))\,\mathsf D(s,a;s').
\]
Equivalently,
\[
P_p^{(T)}(s,s')
=
\Bigl(1-\frac12\Bigr)\mathsf U(s')
+\frac12 R_p(s,s').
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.insulinFinitePolicyKernel_eq

2. For each fixed \(s\), \(R_p(s,\cdot)\) is a probability vector.  Indeed,
all factors in \(\mathsf D(s,a;s')\) are nonnegative.  Also,
\[
\sum_{s'\in\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}}\mathsf D(s,a;s')
=
\sum_{g^\star}\gamma_{s,a}(g^\star)
\sum_{d^\star}\pi_d(d^\star)
\sum_{x^\star}\pi_x(x^\star)
=1,
\]
because the rounded Gaussian cells partition the clipped glucose outcome,
the diet probabilities sum to \(1\), the activity probabilities sum to \(1\),
and the three indicator factors merely select the shifted memory coordinates.
Thus
\[
\sum_{s'\in\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}} R_p(s,s')
=
\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}}p(a\mid x(s))
\sum_{s'\in\mathsf S_{\mathrm{grid}}}\mathsf D(s,a;s')
=
\sum_{a\in\{0,1\}}p(a\mid x(s))
=1.
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.insulinResidual_probabilityVector

3. It remains to apply the common-refresh contraction calculation.  For any
finite state space, any probability vectors \(\eta,\eta'\), and any stochastic
matrix \(R\), a kernel of the form
\[
M_\alpha(s,t)=(1-\alpha)u(t)+\alpha R(s,t),
\qquad \alpha\ge 0,
\]
satisfies
\[
(\eta M_\alpha-\eta'M_\alpha)(t)
=
\alpha\sum_s(\eta(s)-\eta'(s))R(s,t),
\]
since the refresh term contributes
\((1-\alpha)u(t)(\sum_s\eta(s)-\sum_s\eta'(s))=0\).  Hence
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\eta M_\alpha-\eta'M_\alpha\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
&=
\frac12\sum_t
\left|
\alpha\sum_s(\eta(s)-\eta'(s))R(s,t)
\right|  \\
&\le
\alpha\,\frac12\sum_s|\eta(s)-\eta'(s)|
        \sum_t R(s,t) \\
&=
\alpha\|\eta-\eta'\|_{\mathrm{TV}} .
\end{aligned}
\]
With \(\alpha=1/2\), \(u=\mathsf U\), \(R=R_p\), and
\(\eta=\nu,\eta'=\nu'\), this gives
\[
\|\nu P_p^{(T)}-\nu'P_p^{(T)}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
\le
\frac12\|\nu-\nu'\|_{\mathrm{TV}} .
\]
Multiplying by \(2\) yields the stated \(\ell^1\) version.
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.PomdpLatentOverlapMinimax.tvNorm_contraction_of_refresh
\end{proof}
