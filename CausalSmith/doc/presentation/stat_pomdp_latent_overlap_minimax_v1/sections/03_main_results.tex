\section{Main results}

The preceding section defined the observed-data experiment class, the stationary target value, the minimax risk, and the partial-history importance-weighted estimators. This section gives the risk surface as the latent stationary overlap radius varies. The organizing parameters are the normalized distance from unit overlap and the radius-calibrated history depth, where the radius \(C\) is supplied to the estimator as a tuning input.

\begin{definitionv}{def:overlap-distance-radius}[Overlap-distance radius \(q_C\)]
For \(C\ge 1\), define
\[
q_C:=\frac{C-1}{C}.
\]
\end{definitionv}

The radius \(q_C\) is zero at equality of the two stationary laws and increases with the allowed stationary density-ratio bound. It is the scale on which the hidden-state part of the risk is measured.

\begin{definitionv}{def:radius-adaptive-history-depth}[Radius-calibrated history depth \(\kappa_T^{\mathrm{rad}}(C)\)]
For \(T\in\mathbb N\) and \(C\ge 1\), define
\[
\kappa_T^{\mathrm{rad}}(C):=
\begin{cases}
0, & Tq_C^2\le 1,\\[3pt]
\displaystyle
\min\!\left\{
\left\lfloor\frac T2\right\rfloor,
\left\lfloor\frac{\log(Tq_C^2)}{2\log(1/\alpha)+\log L}\right\rfloor
\right\},
& Tq_C^2>1.
\end{cases}
\]
\end{definitionv}

The depth \(\kappa_T^{\mathrm{rad}}(C)\) shortens the history when the radius is close to unit overlap and lengthens it when the latent stationary shift is large enough to make hidden-state memory relevant. The threshold \(Tq_C^2\le1\) is the parametric boundary layer in the theorem below.

For the all-radius theorem it is convenient to index models directly and write their target values without carrying the kernel and policy notation in each display.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_16}[Finite signed-depth observed-word law]
For \(C>1\), \(Q\ge1\), and \(v\in\{-1,+1\}\), let
\[
\mathbb P_{Q,v}^{\mathrm{fin},T}
\]
be the finite observed-word law through horizon \(T\) generated by the signed-depth construction of \cref{obj:def:signed-depth-family}. Its observed state is constant, its latent state is \(H_t=(J_t,U_t)\in\{0,\ldots,Q\}\times\{-1,+1\}\), its behavior and target policies satisfy \(b(1)=1/L\) and \(e(1)=1\), and its reward alphabet is \(\{-1,+1\}\) with
\[
\Pr_v(Y_t=y\mid J_t,U_t,A_t)
=
\frac{1+yvc_0U_t\mathbf 1\{J_t=Q\}}{2}.
\]
The initial latent law is \(d_b(j,u)=w_j(1+u\varepsilon_C L^{-j})/2\), and the resulting finite observation consists of the action-reward word \((A_t,Y_t)_{t=0}^{T-1}\), equivalently the finite observed trajectory with constant observed-state coordinate.
\end{definitionv}

The next theorem is the master characterization. Its constants depend on \(t_0\) and \(\zeta\), while the comparison holds uniformly over every radius \(C\ge1\) and over the finite observed and hidden cardinalities allowed by \cref{obj:def:model-class}.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_14}[Target value of an indexed model]
For an indexed model \(m\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,C)\), write \(K_m\) and \(e_m\) for its joint reward-transition kernel and target policy. The target value of \(m\) is
\[
\theta_m:=\theta(K_m,e_m),
\]
with \(\theta(K,e)\) the stationary target-policy causal value from \(\cref{obj:def:target-value}\).
\end{definitionv}

\Cref{obj:thm:uniform-overlap-frontier} gives a single observable estimator \emph{rule} --- one formula, applied at every radius --- together with a matching minimax surface. The rule is not radius-free: the depth it selects is computed from the supplied value of \(C\), so different radii give different depths and different estimators. The expression for \(\mathfrak r_T(t_0,\zeta,C)\) has a parametric term and a hidden-state term scaled by \(q_C\). The radius-calibrated rule in \cref{obj:def:radius-adaptive-history-depth} implements this balance directly: in the boundary layer it uses depth zero, and away from that layer it increases the observable history length according to the effective radius \(Tq_C^2\).

The lower-bound calculations use the finite observed law induced by the signed-depth construction through horizon \(T\). Naming that law separates the observable experiment used in the converse from the latent construction that generates it.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_10}[Unclipped partial-history average]
For the balanced history depth \(k_T\), define the unclipped partial-history average by
\[
\widehat\theta^{\rm raw}_{k_T}
:=
\frac{1}{T-k_T}\sum_{t=k_T}^{T-1} Z_t(k_T),
\qquad
Z_t(k_T):=
Y_t\prod_{j=t-k_T}^{t}\rho_j .
\]
Here \(\rho_j=e(A_j\mid X_j)/b(A_j\mid X_j)\) on behavior-supported cells, with the zero-cell convention used for \(\rho_j\).
\end{definitionv}

For comparison with the clipped estimator in \cref{obj:def:phiw-estimator}, we also record the unclipped average at the fixed-radius balancing depth. This object is useful for interpreting the bias--variance calculation, while the risk bounds below are stated for the clipped estimator.

\begin{theoremv}{thm:uniform-overlap-frontier}[Uniform overlap frontier]
Fix \(t_0>0\) and \(\zeta>0\).  Let \(R_T(t_0,\zeta,C)\) be the minimax squared-error risk in \cref{obj:def:minimax-risk}, let \(q_C=(C-1)/C\) be the overlap-distance radius in \cref{obj:def:overlap-distance-radius}, and set
\[
\beta=\frac{2}{2+t_0\zeta},
\qquad
\mathfrak r_T(t_0,\zeta,C)
=
T^{-1}+T^{-\beta}q_C^{2(1-\beta)} .
\]
There exist constants \(c_{\mathrm{lo}},c_{\mathrm{hi}}>0\), with
\(c_{\mathrm{lo}}\le c_{\mathrm{hi}}\), and an integer \(T_\star\ge 1\), all depending only on \(t_0\) and \(\zeta\), such that for every \(C\ge 1\) and every \(T\ge T_\star\),
\[
c_{\mathrm{lo}}\mathfrak r_T(t_0,\zeta,C)
\le
R_T(t_0,\zeta,C)
\le
c_{\mathrm{hi}}\mathfrak r_T(t_0,\zeta,C),
\]
and the radius-adaptive partial-history importance-weighted estimator with depth
\(\kappa_T^{\mathrm{rad}}(C)\) from \cref{obj:def:radius-adaptive-history-depth} satisfies
\[
\sup_{m\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,C)}
\mathbb E_m\!\left[
  \bigl(\widehat\theta_{\kappa_T^{\mathrm{rad}}(C)}-\theta_m\bigr)^2
\right]
\le
c_{\mathrm{hi}}\mathfrak r_T(t_0,\zeta,C).
\]
Moreover, for every sequence \(C_T\ge 1\), if \(Tq_{C_T}^2\) is eventually bounded, then there is a constant \(c_{\mathrm{imm}}>0\), depending on the sequence and on \(t_0,\zeta\), such that eventually
\[
\sup_{m\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,C_T)}
\mathbb E_m\!\left[
  \bigl(\widehat\theta_{0}-\theta_m\bigr)^2
\right]
\le
\frac{c_{\mathrm{imm}}}{T}.
\]
\end{theoremv}

Holding the radius at a nonunit value gives the fixed-radius slice of \cref{obj:thm:uniform-overlap-frontier}. The next theorem states that slice in the fixed-depth notation of \cref{obj:def:history-depth}, together with the signed-depth alternatives from \cref{obj:def:signed-depth-family} that witness the converse.

\begin{theoremv}{thm:fixed-c-minimax}[Fixed-overlap minimax rate]
Fix \(t_0,\zeta,C\in\mathbb R\). Assume:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Positive memory exponent.)} \(t_0>0\).
\item \textbf{(Positive overlap exponent.)} \(\zeta>0\).
\item \textbf{(Fixed latent-overlap radius.)} \(C>1\).
\end{itemize}
Let the exponent \(\beta\) be
\[
\beta=\frac{2}{2+t_0\zeta}.
\]
There exist constants \(c_\star,c^\star\in\mathbb R\) and an integer \(T_\star\ge 1\) such that
\[
0<c_\star\le c^\star
\]
and, for every integer \(T\ge T_\star\),
\[
c_\star T^{-\beta}
\le
R_T(t_0,\zeta,C)
\le
c^\star T^{-\beta},
\]
where \(R_T(t_0,\zeta,C)\) is the minimax squared-error risk in \cref{obj:def:minimax-risk}. Moreover, the clipped partial-history importance-weighted estimator \(\widehat\theta_{k_T}\), with \(k_T\) as in \cref{obj:def:history-depth}, satisfies
\[
\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,C)}
\mathbb E_M\!\left[
\{\widehat\theta_{k_T}-\theta(M)\}^2
\right]
\le
c^\star T^{-\beta},
\]
with \(\theta(M)\) the stationary target-policy mean reward in \cref{obj:def:target-value}.

For each integer \(T\ge T_\star\), there is an integer \(Q\ge 1\) such that the lower bound is witnessed by the two signed-depth alternatives \(M_{Q,v}^T\), \(v\in\{-1,+1\}\), generated by \cref{obj:def:signed-depth-family} with one observed state and \(2(Q+1)\) latent states: for every measurable observable-data estimator \(\widehat\theta\), under the usual squared-error integrability conditions for both alternatives,
\[
c_\star T^{-\beta}
\le
\max_{v\in\{-1,+1\}}
\mathbb E_{M_{Q,v}^T}\!\left[
\{\widehat\theta-\theta(M_{Q,v}^T)\}^2
\right].
\]
\end{theoremv}

The exponent \(\beta\) reflects the balance between geometric forgetting and the variance cost of multiplying observable policy ratios. Since \(q_C\) is a positive constant on this slice, the all-radius expression in \cref{obj:thm:uniform-overlap-frontier} reduces to the power \(T^{-\beta}\) up to constants. The estimator achieves that rate, and the two-point construction shows that every observable-data estimator faces the same order of risk over the class.

The unit-overlap boundary is the \(C=1\) slice of the same surface. At this endpoint, the normalized radius is \(q_C=0\), and the risk expression in \cref{obj:thm:uniform-overlap-frontier} is on the \(T^{-1}\) scale.

\begin{theoremv}{prop:unit-overlap-boundary}[Unit overlap boundary]
Let \(t_0>0\) and \(\zeta>0\). At unit latent overlap, \(C=1\), the following assertions hold.

For every trajectory length \(T\), every finite observed and hidden state cardinalities, and every experiment \(M\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,1)\) in the model class of \cref{obj:def:model-class}, the target stationary law \(d_e\) and the behavior stationary law \(d_b\) of the corresponding policy-induced joint-state transition kernels coincide:
\[
d_e=d_b .
\]

Moreover, there is a constant \(c_{\mathrm{imm}}>0\), depending only on \(t_0\) and \(\zeta\), such that for every integer \(T\ge 1\), the worst-case squared risk of the immediate observable estimator \(\widehat\theta_0\) satisfies
\[
\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,1)}
\mathbb E_M\!\left[\bigl(\widehat\theta_0-\theta(K,e)\bigr)^2\right]
\le \frac{c_{\mathrm{imm}}}{T}.
\]
Finally, for the minimax squared-error risk \(R_T(t_0,\zeta,1)\) of \cref{obj:def:minimax-risk}, there are constants \(c_{\mathrm{lo}},c_{\mathrm{hi}}>0\) with \(c_{\mathrm{lo}}\le c_{\mathrm{hi}}\) and an integer \(T_\star\ge 1\) such that, for every \(T\ge T_\star\),
\[
\frac{c_{\mathrm{lo}}}{T}
\le
R_T(t_0,\zeta,1)
\le
\frac{c_{\mathrm{hi}}}{T}.
\]
The hidden-state exponent at these positive scales satisfies
\[
\frac{2}{2+t_0\zeta}<1 .
\]
\end{theoremv}

The boundary result identifies the equality case as an immediate-weighting regime. The coincidence \(d_e=d_b\) gives the stationary invariance needed for depth zero, and the matching minimax bounds place the endpoint on the \(T^{-1}\) scale.

The remaining regime reads \cref{obj:thm:uniform-overlap-frontier} along a deterministic path approaching the unit-overlap boundary. Write the nonnegative excess as \leanref{sym:\delta_T}{\ensuremath{\delta_T}}; then \(C_T=1+\delta_T\) traces a local path into the nonunit region.

\begin{theoremv}{thm:shrinking-overlap-frontier}[Shrinking-overlap frontier]
The following conditions hold:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Fixed smoothness.)} The constants satisfy \(t_0>0\) and \(\zeta>0\).
\item \textbf{(Shrinking radius.)} The deterministic radius excess sequence \((\delta_T)_{T\ge1}\) satisfies \(\delta_T\ge0\) for every \(T\).
\item \textbf{(Local limit.)} The sequence satisfies \(\delta_T\to0\).
\item \textbf{(Rate exponent.)} Let \(\beta=2/(2+t_0\zeta)\).
\item \textbf{(Local rate and depth.)} Define
\[
r_T(\delta)
=
T^{-1}+T^{-\beta}\delta_T^{2(1-\beta)}
\]
and
\[
\kappa_T
=
\begin{cases}
0, & T\delta_T^2\le 1,\\[3pt]
\displaystyle
\min\left\{\left\lfloor T/2\right\rfloor,
\left\lfloor
\frac{\log(T\delta_T^2)}
{2\log(1/\alpha)+\log L}
\right\rfloor\right\}, & T\delta_T^2>1.
\end{cases}
\]
\end{itemize}
Then there exist constants \(c_{\mathrm{loc}},C_{\mathrm{loc}}\) with
\(0<c_{\mathrm{loc}}\le C_{\mathrm{loc}}\), depending only on \(t_0\) and
\(\zeta\), such that, for all sufficiently large \(T\),
\[
c_{\mathrm{loc}}\,r_T(\delta)
\le
R_T(t_0,\zeta,1+\delta_T)
\le
C_{\mathrm{loc}}\,r_T(\delta),
\]
where \(R_T\) is the minimax squared-error risk in
\cref{obj:def:minimax-risk}.  Moreover, the observable partial-history
importance-weighted estimator \(\widehat\theta_{\kappa_T}\) of
\cref{obj:def:phiw-estimator} satisfies
\[
\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,1+\delta_T)}
\mathbb E_M\!\left[
\bigl(\widehat\theta_{\kappa_T}-\theta(M)\bigr)^2
\right]
\le
C_{\mathrm{loc}}\,r_T(\delta)
\]
for all sufficiently large \(T\).  Finally, if there is a constant \(D\ge0\)
such that \(T\delta_T^2\le D\) for all sufficiently large \(T\), then there is
a constant \(c_{\mathrm{imm}}>0\) such that
\[
\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,1+\delta_T)}
\mathbb E_M\!\left[
\bigl(\widehat\theta_{0}-\theta(M)\bigr)^2
\right]
\le
\frac{c_{\mathrm{imm}}}{T}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(T\).
\end{theoremv}

The local rate decomposes into the parametric term and a hidden-state term scaled by the shrinking excess. When \(T\delta_T^2\) remains bounded, the immediate estimator already attains the parametric order. When the product grows, the depth in the theorem increases logarithmically with the effective radius \(T\delta_T^2\), matching the same risk expression.
