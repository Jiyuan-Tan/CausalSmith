# Title
**Latent stationary overlap and minimax off-policy evaluation in finite POMDPs**

**Contribution statement.** The paper characterizes the cardinality-uniform minimax risk surface for bounded-reward, binary-action, single-trajectory finite POMDP off-policy evaluation under latent stationary overlap, with an observable radius-calibrated partial-history estimator attaining the rate and signed-depth alternatives giving the matching converse.
