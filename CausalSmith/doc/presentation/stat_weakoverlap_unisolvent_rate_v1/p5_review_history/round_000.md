# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 7/10 — The verified results deliver a substantial theoretical contribution, but central model-description and comparator-scope errors, together with extensive repetition, require revision before publication.

The paper establishes matching pointwise and expected spatial-supremum minimax rates for treated-response recovery under a global propensity-tail envelope, with an explicit estimator adapting to overlap at known smoothness. Equal-cell conditioning, the ordered information profile, and the treatment-geometry witnesses provide a meaningful advance over the closest comparator. The main contribution is persuasive under the verification contract, but the manuscript needs more accurate class descriptions, sharper attribution of the comparator's adaptation claim, and a substantially more economical presentation.

## Strengths
- The explicit estimator attains pure-power expected spatial-supremum risk uniformly over fixed compact overlap intervals at known smoothness.
- The bounded testing experiment establishes matching pointwise lower order throughout the closed cube, including boundary points.
- Equal-cell weighting and the ordered treated-mass profile give a clear statistical explanation for stable fitting and maximal-error control.
- The thin-strip witness and Gaussian transfer make the enlargement in admissible treatment geometry concrete.
- The dedicated early related-work section engages competitors' actual experiments, losses, rates, and assumptions.

## Findings
- **[major·prose] Related work** — The sentence 'These envelopes impose bounded potential outcomes, intrinsic cube Hölder smoothness, a covariate density lower bound, and a global propensity-tail bound' misdescribes the upper-bound class. The verified model imposes a bounded treated conditional mean and uniformly conditional sub-Gaussian residuals. Bounded potential outcomes characterize the converse subclass and the thin-strip witness.
  - *Fix:* Replace 'bounded potential outcomes' with 'a bounded treated conditional mean and uniformly conditional sub-Gaussian residuals.' Keep the bounded potential-outcome restriction attached explicitly to the testing subclass and geometry witness.
- **[major·prose] Introduction and Related work** — The statements 'Dorn establishes ... an estimator accommodating the unknown overlap exponent' and 'Dorn also establishes adaptation to the overlap exponent' present construction provenance as a theorem-level adaptation guarantee. The supplied source transcription establishes an estimator and probability constants after fixing the source setup, including its exponent; its no-exponent computation sentence is recorded as construction provenance. That distinction matters for the novelty of the present compact-interval uniform guarantee.
  - *Fix:* Attribute to Dorn the description of a count-based estimator computable without the overlap exponent, while stating his verified probability guarantee with its fixed-setup quantifiers. Then distinguish the present result's single estimator and common constants across the prescribed compact exponent interval.
- **[minor·statement] Transfer to fixed-constant Gaussian source envelopes** — The transfer proposition describes R_pt as 'the bounded-response pointwise minimax risk,' although the referenced risk ranges over the full causal class with bounded treated means and conditional sub-Gaussian residuals. The wording can suggest that its infimum and supremum concern the bounded potential-outcome subclass, which is a separate risk in the main theorem.
  - *Fix:* Describe R_pt as the pointwise minimax expected absolute-error risk over the specified causal class. Explain separately that its lower bound is supported by the subclass admitting two-valued potential outcomes. Apply the same precise terminology to surrounding references to the 'bounded-response causal class.'
- **[minor·structure] Global** — The presentation repeatedly restates the same construction, guarantees, and quantifier distinctions. Tensor nodes and template formulas receive overlapping definitions; the selected-mesh analysis is reproduced extensively in the main theorem proof; and the common-regression singleton calculation appears in several formal objects and accompanying discussions. This volume obscures the estimator and ordered-information argument that carry the contribution.
  - *Fix:* Consolidate the template exposition, retain one detailed selected-mesh argument, and make the main theorem proof invoke its established conclusions. Present the common-regression calculation once and cross-reference it elsewhere. Preserve the distinct upper-envelope, geometric-existence, and converse scopes in concise transition paragraphs.
- **[minor·structure] Transfer to fixed-constant Gaussian source envelopes** — The transfer proposition combines envelope membership, an expectation guarantee, a probability consequence, Gaussian geometric witnesses, a common-regression calculation, and a lower bound over another class. Although their scopes are subsequently explained, the long statement makes the transfer contribution difficult to isolate.
  - *Fix:* Organize the presentation into clearly identified components: affine membership and upper recovery, Gaussian geometric witnesses, and converse comparison scope. Place the common-regression and causal-class lower-bound discussion beside the existing source-scope results, using exact cleveref references to preserve traceability.
- **[minor·prose] Related work and Discussion** — The pure-power spatial-supremum result would be easier to interpret alongside the regular-design benchmark supplied in the related-work brief. The current discussion emphasizes the logarithm-free rate without explaining how its fixed finite overlap-envelope domain relates to the usual regular-design supremum rate.
  - *Fix:* Add a short comparison with Stone's regular-design supremum rate, explaining that the present minimax result concerns the larger class indexed by a fixed finite overlap exponent. State that the common constants are uniform on the prescribed compact interval, keeping any discussion of stronger-overlap limits separate from the proved uniformity domain.
- **[minor·prose] Author footnote and Verification note** — The author footnote promises a verification appendix recording 'scope, toolchain, commit,' but the manuscript's verification note supplies neither toolchain versions nor a commit identifier or a usable declaration map. Its blanket statement that 'The formal statements and proofs are machine-checked' also needs to distinguish checked declarations from presentation-level definitions and descriptive future-work text.
  - *Fix:* Include the verification commit, Lean and library versions, artifact location, and a compact mapping from formal objects to declarations. Explicitly identify presentation-level definitions and descriptive future-work entries, while retaining the theorem-local disclosures for external inputs.

## Questions for authors
- Can the comparator discussion distinguish its reported exponent-free computation from the fixed-setup quantifiers of its probability theorem throughout?
- Can the Gaussian transfer and source-family comparison be presented more compactly while preserving their distinct comparison classes?
- What artifact location and toolchain versions should accompany the verification commit so readers can reproduce the recorded checks?
