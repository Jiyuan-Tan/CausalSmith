\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:summary-metric-continuity}]
By \cref{obj:synth_27}, for the fixed dimensions \(d_x\) and \(d_z\) the ambient summary space is
\[
\mathscr S_{\mathrm{all}}
=
(\mathbb R^{d_z\times d_x})^4\times \mathbb R^{d_x}.
\]
Write
\[
s=\bigl(M_0(s),M_1(s),N_0(s),N_1(s),m_X(s)\bigr),
\qquad
q=\bigl(M_0(q),M_1(q),N_0(q),N_1(q),m_X(q)\bigr).
\]
The coordinate map
\[
\iota(s):=\bigl(M_0(s),M_1(s),N_0(s),N_1(s),m_X(s)\bigr)
\]
is continuous for the product topology, and therefore each coordinate projection
\[
s\mapsto M_0(s),\quad s\mapsto M_1(s),\quad
s\mapsto N_0(s),\quad s\mapsto N_1(s),\quad
s\mapsto m_X(s)
\]
is continuous. The same finite-product argument covers the zero-dimensional cases, where the relevant products and sums have their usual singleton or empty interpretation. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.ProxyEffectlawEigencollisionFrontier.dS_continuous

For a pair \((s,q)\), the four matrix-valued difference maps
\[
(s,q)\longmapsto M_0(s)-M_0(q),\quad
(s,q)\longmapsto M_1(s)-M_1(q),
\]
\[
(s,q)\longmapsto N_0(s)-N_0(q),\quad
(s,q)\longmapsto N_1(s)-N_1(q)
\]
are continuous by continuity of the coordinate projections and of subtraction. If \(\Upsilon\in\mathbb R^{d_z\times d_x}\), the associated Euclidean linear operator
\[
\mathbb R^{d_x}\to\mathbb R^{d_z},
\qquad x\longmapsto \Upsilon x,
\]
depends continuously on \(\Upsilon\), because this matrix-to-operator assignment is linear between finite-dimensional normed spaces. Composing with the continuous operator norm gives continuity of
\[
(s,q)\longmapsto \|M_0(s)-M_0(q)\|_{\mathrm{op}},\quad
(s,q)\longmapsto \|M_1(s)-M_1(q)\|_{\mathrm{op}},
\]
\[
(s,q)\longmapsto \|N_0(s)-N_0(q)\|_{\mathrm{op}},\quad
(s,q)\longmapsto \|N_1(s)-N_1(q)\|_{\mathrm{op}} .
\]
% lean: CausalSmith.Stat.ProxyEffectlawEigencollisionFrontier.matrixCLM_continuous

The mean-coordinate difference
\[
(s,q)\longmapsto m_X(s)-m_X(q)
\]
is continuous as a map into \(\mathbb R^{d_x}\). Its Euclidean norm is
\[
\|m_X(s)-m_X(q)\|_2
=
\left(\sum_{i<d_x}\bigl(m_X(s)_i-m_X(q)_i\bigr)^2\right)^{1/2}.
\]
The summand coordinates are continuous, the index set is finite, and the square-root is continuous on the nonnegative range of the sum of squares. Hence
\[
(s,q)\longmapsto \|m_X(s)-m_X(q)\|_2
\]
is continuous. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.ProxyEffectlawEigencollisionFrontier.dS_continuous

Using \cref{obj:def:observable-summary},
\[
d_S(s,q)
=
\|M_0(s)-M_0(q)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\|M_1(s)-M_1(q)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\|N_0(s)-N_0(q)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\|N_1(s)-N_1(q)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\|m_X(s)-m_X(q)\|_2 .
\]
This is a finite sum of continuous real-valued functions on
\(\mathscr S_{\mathrm{all}}\times\mathscr S_{\mathrm{all}}\). Therefore
\[
(s,q)\longmapsto d_S(s,q)
\]
is continuous. % lean: CausalSmith.Stat.ProxyEffectlawEigencollisionFrontier.dS_continuous
\end{proof}
