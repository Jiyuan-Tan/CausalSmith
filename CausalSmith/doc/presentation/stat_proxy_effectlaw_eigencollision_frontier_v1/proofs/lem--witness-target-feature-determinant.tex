\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:witness-target-feature-determinant}]
Fix \(\varepsilon\in\mathbb R\) with \(0\le \varepsilon\le 1/8\).  By \cref{obj:def:target-feature,obj:def:two-class-witness}, the entries of the target-proxy feature matrix are
\[
B_{iu}
=
E_{P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}}}\!\left[X_i\mid U=u\right].
\]
Since \(X=(1,X_2)^\top\), with conditional means \(E[X_2\mid U=1]=1/5\) and \(E[X_2\mid U=2]=4/5\), this gives, in the statement's zero-based coordinate order,
\[
B
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\[0.2em]
1/5&4/5
\end{pmatrix}.
\] % lean: witness_targetFeature
Therefore
\[
B_{00}B_{11}-B_{01}B_{10}
=
1\cdot \frac45-1\cdot \frac15
=
\frac35 .
\] % lean: witness_det_targetFeature
\end{proof}
