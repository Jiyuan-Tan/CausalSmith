\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:stacked-proxy-moment-signal-margin}]
Set
\[
A_0:=\bigl(E_P[Z_i\mid U=u,T=0]\bigr)_{i,u},\qquad
B:=\bigl(E_P[X_i\mid U=u]\bigr)_{i,u},
\]
and
\[
\Gamma_0:=
\operatorname{diag}\left\{
\frac{P(U=u,T=0)}{P(T=0)}:1\le u\le k
\right\}.
\]
Let \(S=\operatorname{range}(B)\subseteq\mathbb R^{d_x}\).  The proxy-rank margin in
\cref{obj:ass:proxy-rank-margin} gives
\[
\sigma_{k-1}(A_0)\ge \sigma_0,\qquad
\sigma_{k-1}(B)\ge \sigma_0 .
\]
Since \(\sigma_0>0\), the maps induced by \(A_0\) and \(B\) are injective.  Hence
\[
\dim S=k .
\]
% lean: stackedProxyMoment_minSingular

The latent-arm positivity condition gives \(P(U=u,T=0)\ge \pi_0\) for every \(u\).  Since
\(0<P(T=0)\le1\), each diagonal entry of \(\Gamma_0\) is at least \(\pi_0\).  Therefore, for every
\(y\in\mathbb R^k\),
\[
\pi_0\|y\|_2\le \|\Gamma_0y\|_2 .
\]
% lean: latentArmWeights_minSingular

Now fix \(x\in S\).  The least-singular-value bound for \(B\), restricted to its range, gives
\[
\sigma_0\|x\|_2\le \|B^\top x\|_2 .
\]
% lean: least_singularValue_mul_norm_le_adjoint_on_range
Applying the preceding diagonal bound to \(y=B^\top x\) gives
\[
\pi_0\|B^\top x\|_2\le \|\Gamma_0B^\top x\|_2 .
\]
% lean: latentArmWeights_minSingular
The least-singular-value bound for \(A_0\) gives
\[
\sigma_0\|\Gamma_0B^\top x\|_2
\le
\|A_0\Gamma_0B^\top x\|_2 .
\]
% lean: least_singularValue_mul_norm_le
Combining these three inequalities,
\[
\pi_0\sigma_0^2\|x\|_2
\le
\|A_0\Gamma_0B^\top x\|_2 .
\]
% lean: stackedProxyMoment_minSingular

By the observable proxy moment factorization in
\cref{obj:lem:observed-proxy-moment-factorization},
\[
M_0(P)=A_0\Gamma_0B^\top .
\]
Thus
\[
\pi_0\sigma_0^2\|x\|_2
\le
\|M_0(P)x\|_2 .
\]
% lean: observedProxyMoment_factorization
For
\[
G:=\operatorname{stack}\bigl(M_0(P),M_1(P)\bigr),
\]
vertical stacking gives the Pythagorean identity
\[
\|Gx\|_2^2=\|M_0(P)x\|_2^2+\|M_1(P)x\|_2^2,
\]
and hence
\[
\|M_0(P)x\|_2\le \|Gx\|_2 .
\]
% lean: observedProxyMoment_norm_le_stackedProxyMoment
Consequently,
\[
\pi_0\sigma_0^2\|x\|_2\le \|Gx\|_2
\qquad\text{for every }x\in S .
\]
% lean: stackedProxyMoment_minSingular

Since \(S\) is a \(k\)-dimensional subspace and \(\pi_0\sigma_0^2\ge0\), the variational
subspace characterization of zero-indexed singular values yields
\[
\pi_0\sigma_0^2
\le
\sigma_{k-1}(G)
=
\sigma_{k-1}\!\left\{\operatorname{stack}\bigl(M_0(P),M_1(P)\bigr)\right\}.
\]
% lean: le_singularValues_of_subspace
\end{proof}
