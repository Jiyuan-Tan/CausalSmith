\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:arm-mass-lower-bound}]
Fix an arm \(t\in\{0,1\}\). The arm event is the disjoint union of its latent-arm cells:
\[
\{T=t\}=\bigsqcup_{u=1}^k \{U=u,T=t\}.
\]
Therefore
\[
P(T=t)=\sum_{u=1}^k P(U=u,T=t).
\] % lean: fullDataArm_real_eq_sum_latentCell
By \cref{obj:ass:latent-arm-positivity}, each summand is at least \(\pi_0\). Hence
\[
P(T=t)
=\sum_{u=1}^k P(U=u,T=t)
\ge \sum_{u=1}^k \pi_0
=k\pi_0 .
\] % lean: arm_mass_lower_of_latentArmPositivity
\end{proof}
