\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:observed-proxy-moment-factorization}]
Fix \(t\in\{0,1\}\).  Write
\[
\mathsf{Arm}_t:=\{T=t\},\qquad
\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}:=\{U=u,T=t\},\qquad
\mathsf{Class}_u:=\{U=u\}.
\]
For every latent class \(u\), latent-arm positivity gives
\[
P(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t})\ge \pi_0>0,
\qquad
P(\mathsf{Class}_u)\ge P(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t})>0.
\]
Also, \cref{obj:lem:arm-mass-lower-bound} gives
\[
P(\mathsf{Arm}_t)\ge k\pi_0>0 .
\]
% lean: latentCell_pos_of_latentArmPositivity, arm_mass_lower_of_latentArmPositivity

We prove the matrix identity entrywise.  Fix coordinates \(1\le i\le d_z\) and \(1\le j\le d_x\).  By \cref{obj:lem:proxy-coordinate-envelopes},
\[
|Z_i|\le L,\qquad |X_j|\le L
\quad\text{\(P\)-almost surely.}
\]
Thus \(Z_iX_j\) is integrable on each \(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}\).  The observed arm pulls back to the full-data arm, so
\[
M_t(P)_{ij}
=
E_{P_O}[Z_iX_j\mid T=t]
=
E_P[Z_iX_j\mid \mathsf{Arm}_t].
\]
% lean: proxy_coordinate_bounds_of_model, conditionalMean_obsArm_eq_fullDataArm

Since \(\mathsf{Arm}_t\) is the disjoint union of the cells
\(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}\), normalized additivity over this finite partition yields
\[
E_P[Z_iX_j\mid \mathsf{Arm}_t]
=
\sum_{u=1}^{k}
\frac{P(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t})}{P(\mathsf{Arm}_t)}
E_P[Z_iX_j\mid \mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}].
\]
% lean: conditionalMean_fullDataArm_eq_sum_latentCell

On a fixed positive cell \(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}\), put
\[
\operatorname{cl}_L(a):=\max\{-L,\min\{a,L\}\}.
\]
The coordinate bounds imply that \(\operatorname{cl}_L(Z_i)=Z_i\) and
\(\operatorname{cl}_L(X_j)=X_j\) almost surely on the normalized restriction to
\(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}\).  Applying reference-proxy separation to the bounded
tests \(z\mapsto \operatorname{cl}_L(z_i)\) and
\((x,y)\mapsto \operatorname{cl}_L(x_j)\), and then removing the clamps, gives
\[
E_P[Z_iX_j\mid \mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}]
=
E_P[Z_i\mid \mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}]\,
E_P[X_j\mid \mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}].
\]
% lean: latentCell_ZX_conditionalMean_factorization

It remains to identify the target-proxy mean in the cell with the latent-class
mean.  Let
\[
f_j(x):=\operatorname{cl}_L(x_j),
\qquad
q_t(y,t'):=\mathbf 1\{t'=t\}.
\]
Target-proxy separation on the positive class \(\mathsf{Class}_u\) gives
\[
E_P[f_j(X)q_t(Y,T)\mid \mathsf{Class}_u]
=
E_P[f_j(X)\mid \mathsf{Class}_u]\,
E_P[q_t(Y,T)\mid \mathsf{Class}_u].
\]
The two factors involving \(q_t\) are ordinary normalized cell masses:
\[
E_P[f_j(X)q_t(Y,T)\mid \mathsf{Class}_u]
=
\frac{P(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t})}{P(\mathsf{Class}_u)}
E_P[f_j(X)\mid \mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}],
\]
and
\[
E_P[q_t(Y,T)\mid \mathsf{Class}_u]
=
\frac{P(\mathsf{Cell}_{u,t})}{P(\mathsf{Class}_u)}.
\]
Because this ratio is positive, cancellation gives
\[
E_P[f_j(X)\mid \mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}]
=
E_P[f_j(X)\mid \mathsf{Class}_u].
\]
Using the same coordinate envelope to remove the clamps on the cell and on the
class,
\[
E_P[X_j\mid \mathsf{Cell}_{u,t}]
=
E_P[X_j\mid \mathsf{Class}_u].
\]
% lean: latentCell_X_conditionalMean_eq_latentClass

Combining the preceding displays and using \cref{obj:def:reference-feature,obj:def:target-feature},
\[
M_t(P)_{ij}
=
\sum_{u=1}^{k}
A_t(i,u)\,
\frac{P(U=u,T=t)}{P(T=t)}\,
B(j,u).
\]
By the definition of \(\Gamma_t\), the right-hand side is exactly
\[
(A_t\Gamma_tB^\top)_{ij}.
\]
Since \(i\) and \(j\) were arbitrary,
\[
M_t(P)=A_t\Gamma_tB^\top .
\]
The arm \(t\) was arbitrary, so the factorization holds for both treatment arms.
% lean: observedProxyMoment_factorization
\end{proof}
