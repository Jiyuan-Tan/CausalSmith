\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:latent-effect-envelope}]
Fix \(u\). By \cref{obj:def:latent-effect},
\[
\tau_u=\mu_{1u}-\mu_{0u}.
\]
Thus the triangle inequality gives
\[
|\tau_u|
=
|\mu_{1u}-\mu_{0u}|
\le
|\mu_{1u}|+|\mu_{0u}|.
\] % lean: latentEffect_abs_le_of_model
Applying \cref{obj:lem:latent-mean-envelope} to the two treatment states,
\[
|\mu_{1u}|\le \frac{L_\tau}{2},
\qquad
|\mu_{0u}|\le \frac{L_\tau}{2}.
\] % lean: latentMean_abs_le_of_model
Therefore
\[
|\tau_u|
\le
\frac{L_\tau}{2}+\frac{L_\tau}{2}
=
L_\tau
=
\frac{4L\sqrt{d_z}}{\sigma_0}.
\] % lean: latentEffect_abs_le_of_model
\end{proof}
