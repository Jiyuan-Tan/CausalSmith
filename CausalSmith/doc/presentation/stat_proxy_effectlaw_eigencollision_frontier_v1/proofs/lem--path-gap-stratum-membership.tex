\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:path-gap-stratum-membership}]
Write \(P=P_{g,h}\).  The hypothesis \(0<g\le 1/4\) gives \(0\le g\).  Also \(|h|\le 1/100\) gives
\[
-\frac1{100}\le h\le \frac1{100},
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
-1\le h\le 1 .
\]
Thus the detailed path construction in \cref{obj:synth_14} applies throughout the tangent-amplitude domain and yields the full-data probability law \(P_{g,h}\). % lean: pathLaw_isProbabilityMeasure

Under the same bounds \(0\le g\le 1/4\) and \(|h|\le1/100\), \cref{obj:lem:path-model-membership} gives
\[
P_{g,h}\in\mathcal M
\]
for \(k=d_x=d_z=2\), \(L=2\), \(\pi_0=1/10\), and \(\sigma_0=1/10\).  This supplies the model restrictions in \cref{obj:def:model-class}, including \cref{obj:ass:bounded-x,obj:ass:latent-arm-positivity,obj:ass:proxy-rank-margin}. % lean: path_ucvmwModel

It remains to check the gap-local conditions.  By the explicit construction in \cref{obj:synth_14}, the latent-class masses are
\[
p_1(P)=\frac25+h,
\qquad
p_2(P)=\frac35-h .
\]
The displacement bound gives
\[
p_1(P)\ge \frac25-\frac1{100}=\frac{39}{100}>0,
\qquad
p_2(P)\ge \frac35-\frac1{100}=\frac{59}{100}>0 .
\]
These are the positive-mass classes entering \cref{obj:def:effect-gap,obj:ass:distinct-effects}. % lean: path_latentMass

Again by \cref{obj:synth_14}, the latent conditional potential-outcome means are
\[
\mu_{01}=\mu_{02}=\frac14,
\qquad
\mu_{11}=\frac12-\frac g2,
\qquad
\mu_{12}=\frac12+\frac g2 .
\]
Using \cref{obj:def:latent-effect}, the corresponding latent effects are
\[
\tau_1(P)=\mu_{11}-\mu_{01}
=\frac14-\frac g2,
\qquad
\tau_2(P)=\mu_{12}-\mu_{02}
=\frac14+\frac g2 .
\]
% lean: path_latentMean
% lean: path_latentEffect

Since \(g>0\), these two effects are distinct, and their distance is
\[
|\tau_1(P)-\tau_2(P)|
=
\left|
\left(\frac14-\frac g2\right)
-
\left(\frac14+\frac g2\right)
\right|
=
|-g|
=
g .
\]
The same value is obtained with the two classes interchanged, while equal-index pairs are excluded by the condition \(\tau_u\ne\tau_v\) in \cref{obj:def:effect-gap}.  Hence
\[
\bigl\{|\tau_u(P)-\tau_v(P)|:
p_u(P)>0,\ p_v(P)>0,\ \tau_u(P)\ne\tau_v(P)\bigr\}
=
\{g\},
\]
and therefore
\[
\delta(P)=g .
\]
% lean: path_effectGap

Consequently,
\[
\frac g2\le \delta(P)\le 2g,
\]
because \(\delta(P)=g\) and \(g>0\).  Also,
\[
\tau_1(P)=\frac14-\frac g2
<
\frac14+\frac g2
=\tau_2(P),
\]
so the positive-mass latent effects take exactly \(2=k\) distinct values.  This is \cref{obj:ass:distinct-effects}. % lean: path_distinctEffects

Combining \(P_{g,h}\in\mathcal M\), the gap domain \(0<g\le1/4\), the displayed gap window, and distinct effects gives
\[
P_{g,h}\in\mathcal M(g)
\]
with \(L=2\), \(\pi_0=1/10\), and \(\sigma_0=1/10\), as required by \cref{obj:def:gap-stratum}. % lean: path_gapStratum
\end{proof}
