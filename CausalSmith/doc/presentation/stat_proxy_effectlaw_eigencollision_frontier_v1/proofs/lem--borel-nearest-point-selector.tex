\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:borel-nearest-point-selector}]
Identify \(\mathbb R^d\) with its standard finite-coordinate Euclidean realization.  Define
\[
f:\mathbb R^d\times\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R,
\qquad
f(s,q):=d_S(s,q).
\]
By the continuity hypothesis, \(f\) is Borel measurable, and for each fixed \(s\) the section
\[
q\longmapsto f(s,q)=d_S(s,q)
\]
is continuous on \(\mathcal K\). % lean: Continuous.measurable

The compact measurable argmin selection theorem for a nonempty compact Euclidean action set gives a map
\[
\Pi:\mathbb R^d\to\mathbb R^d
\]
such that \(\Pi\) is Borel measurable and, for every \(s\in\mathbb R^d\),
\[
\Pi(s)\in\mathcal K,
\qquad
\forall q\in\mathcal K,\quad f(s,\Pi(s))\le f(s,q).
\]
% lean: Causalean.Mathlib.MeasureTheory.borelMeasurable_compact_argmin_selector
Substituting the definition of \(f\) gives
\[
\Pi(s)\in\mathcal K,
\qquad
\forall q\in\mathcal K,\quad d_S(s,\Pi(s))\le d_S(s,q),
\]
which is the asserted selector property. % lean: borelMeasurable_compactLoss_selector
\end{proof}
