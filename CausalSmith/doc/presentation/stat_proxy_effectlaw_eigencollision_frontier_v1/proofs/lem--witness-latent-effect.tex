\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:witness-latent-effect}]
Fix \(0\le\varepsilon\le 1/8\).  For a Boolean variable \(b\), write
\[
\beta_p(b):=
\begin{cases}
p, & b=1,\\
1-p, & b=0,
\end{cases}
\qquad
b^{\mathbb R}:=
\begin{cases}
1, & b=1,\\
0, & b=0.
\end{cases}
\]
For latent class \(r\in\{1,2\}\), put
\[
p_r:=
\begin{cases}
2/5, & r=1,\\
3/5, & r=2,
\end{cases}
\qquad
q_r:=
\begin{cases}
1/2-\varepsilon, & r=1,\\
1/2+\varepsilon, & r=2.
\end{cases}
\]
The range of \(\varepsilon\) puts all Bernoulli parameters used below in \([0,1]\), so the finite weights of \(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}}\) are nonnegative. % lean: witnessWeight_nonneg

The elementary mass assigned to the point indexed by
\((r,t,x,z,y_0,y_1)\in\{1,2\}\times\{0,1\}^5\) is
\[
w_r(t,x,z,y_0,y_1)
=
p_r\,\beta_{p_r}(t)\,\beta_{b_r}(x)\,\beta_{a_{t,r}}(z)\,
\beta_{1/4}(y_0)\,\beta_{q_r}(y_1),
\]
where \(b_1=1/5\), \(b_2=4/5\), and \(a_{t,r}\) is the displayed \(Z_2\)-mean from \cref{obj:def:two-class-witness}.  At this point,
\[
Y(0)=y_0^{\mathbb R},
\qquad
Y(1)=y_1^{\mathbb R}.
\] % lean: witnessWeight

For either \(s\in\{0,1\}\), the latent conditional mean from \cref{obj:def:latent-mean} is the finite conditional average
\[
\mu_{sr}(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}})
=
\left(\sum_{t,x,z,y_0,y_1}w_r(t,x,z,y_0,y_1)\right)^{-1}
\sum_{t,x,z,y_0,y_1}
w_r(t,x,z,y_0,y_1)\,Y(s).
\] % lean: conditionalMean_witness_latentClass

The denominator is
\[
\sum_{t,x,z,y_0,y_1}w_r(t,x,z,y_0,y_1)
=
p_r,
\]
because each Bernoulli factor sums to one over its binary coordinate. % lean: sum_bernoulliMass

For \(s=0\),
\[
\sum_{t,x,z,y_0,y_1}
w_r(t,x,z,y_0,y_1)y_0^{\mathbb R}
=
p_r\sum_{y_0\in\{0,1\}}\beta_{1/4}(y_0)y_0^{\mathbb R}
=
p_r/4,
\]
so
\[
\mu_{0r}(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}})=1/4.
\] % lean: witness_latentEffect

For \(s=1\),
\[
\sum_{t,x,z,y_0,y_1}
w_r(t,x,z,y_0,y_1)y_1^{\mathbb R}
=
p_r\sum_{y_1\in\{0,1\}}\beta_{q_r}(y_1)y_1^{\mathbb R}
=
p_r q_r,
\]
and hence
\[
\mu_{1r}(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}})=q_r.
\] % lean: witness_latentEffect

Using the definition of the latent-class treatment effect in \cref{obj:def:latent-effect},
\[
\tau_r(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}})
=
\mu_{1r}(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}})
-
\mu_{0r}(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}})
=
q_r-\frac14.
\] % lean: latentEffect

Substituting the two values of \(q_r\) gives
\[
\tau_1(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}})
=
\left(\frac12-\varepsilon\right)-\frac14
=
\frac14-\varepsilon,
\qquad
\tau_2(P_{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{wit}})
=
\left(\frac12+\varepsilon\right)-\frac14
=
\frac14+\varepsilon.
\] % lean: witness_latentEffect
\end{proof}
