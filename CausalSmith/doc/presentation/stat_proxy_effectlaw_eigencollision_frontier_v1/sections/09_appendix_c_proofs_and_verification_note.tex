\section{Appendix C: proofs and verification note}

This appendix collects the proof layer for the paper's formal results. The arguments establish the population stability statement in \cref{obj:thm:gap-free-positive-measure-modulus}, the measurable repair and estimation claims in \cref{obj:prop:summary-repair-total-borel,obj:prop:polynomial-net-law-estimator,obj:thm:collision-uniform-root-n}, the confidence and reporting guarantees in \cref{obj:thm:honest-root-n-confidence,obj:thm:cluster-adaptive-report,obj:thm:labeled-weight-upper}, and the local converse statements in \cref{obj:thm:matching-local-lower-bounds,obj:prop:same-class-quotient-minimax,obj:prop:same-class-labeled-minimax,obj:thm:published-vmw-converse-transfer}.

The proof of \cref{obj:thm:gap-free-positive-measure-modulus} uses the compact summary closure from \cref{obj:prop:summary-closure-compact} and the quotient moment representation in \cref{obj:def:summary-closure}. The finite-dimensional moment map gives a Lipschitz modulus from the observable summary coordinates to the \(W_1\) law target on the compact domain. The extension clause follows by completing the admissible image under \(d_S\), with \cref{obj:def:summary-space-repair} and \cref{obj:prop:summary-repair-total-borel} supplying the measurable nearest-summary selection needed to evaluate the extended map at empirical summaries.

The estimation proofs combine this deterministic modulus with concentration for the empirical five-block summary in \cref{obj:def:empirical-summary}. For the repaired estimator, nearest-summary projection keeps the evaluated summary within the same concentration scale as the population summary. For the structured-lattice estimator, \cref{obj:def:structured-lattice-program} and \cref{obj:prop:polynomial-net-law-estimator} give a duplicate-free finite search whose deterministic error is bounded by the empirical summary error and the \(n^{-1/2}\) lattice scale. These two routes yield the joint root-\(n\) tail statement in \cref{obj:thm:collision-uniform-root-n}.

The confidence-set proofs use the same concentration event with the objects in \cref{obj:def:wasserstein-confidence-set}. The summary-inversion set inherits its diameter from the modulus on \(\mathcal K\), while the algorithmic outer set inherits its radius from the deterministic lattice bound. The transport-plan formulation in \cref{obj:synth_10} gives the finite representation used in \cref{obj:thm:honest-root-n-confidence}. The cluster-report argument then applies the atom floor and \(W_1\) transport budget in \cref{obj:def:cluster-report} to associate true atoms with empirical components, yielding the support containment, mass-coverage, and interval-length conclusions in \cref{obj:thm:cluster-adaptive-report}.

For ordered weights, the proof of \cref{obj:thm:labeled-weight-upper} works on the gap-local stratum in \cref{obj:def:gap-stratum}. The positive effect gap converts law-level transport error into an \(\ell_1\) error bound for the effect-ordered mass vector defined in \cref{obj:def:ordered-mass-target} and estimated by \cref{obj:def:labeled-weight-estimator}. This gives the clipped inverse-gap upper rate stated in the theorem.

The lower-bound proofs use the explicit two-class witness in \cref{obj:def:two-class-witness}, its validity certificate in \cref{obj:prop:two-class-witness-valid}, and the local experiments in \cref{obj:def:local-quotient-experiment,obj:def:local-weight-experiment}. Standard two-point testing inequalities \citep{LeCam1986,PolyanskiyWu2019} convert the displayed Kullback--Leibler bounds and target separations into the local risk lower bounds in \cref{obj:thm:matching-local-lower-bounds}. The same comparisons give the same-class minimax rates in \cref{obj:prop:same-class-quotient-minimax,obj:prop:same-class-labeled-minimax}. The transfer statement in \cref{obj:thm:published-vmw-converse-transfer} expresses the same witness-pair logic for comparator classes stated in the published VMW vocabulary \citep{VirkMazaheriWu2026}.
