# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.5/10 — The verified results establish an interesting exact efficiency contribution, but substantial problems in organization, notation, presentation of selector existence, and computational reproducibility require revision.

The paper characterizes exact contrast efficiency for a binary randomized trial under joint local privacy, connects the stationary optimum to sequential local risk, and establishes private-pilot attainment and sharp output-cardinality results. The verified mathematical contribution is substantial within its stated model, and the comparator and prospective application are generally framed accurately. Publication requires a coherent ordering of definitions, consistent indexing, an updated account of measurable selection, and a reproducible method for selecting the release.

## Strengths
- The exact fixed-privacy variance constant and sequential minimax equality provide considerably more information than rate comparisons.
- Nuisance profiling, singular contrast-identifying channels, and arbitrary measurable sequential releases receive explicit treatment.
- The sharp five-output bound and certified three-output and five-output neighborhoods offer distinctive channel-design results.
- Private-pilot attainment combines exact conditional centering, regularity, consistent variance estimation, and pointwise Wald coverage across support changes.
- The dedicated early related-work section engages the closest asymptotic and staircase predecessors, while the application preserves its prospective scope.

## Findings
- **[major·structure] Exact efficiency bound and finite optimization** — The formal environments are extensively displaced from their introductory prose. The promised pattern enumeration is followed by the optimized-value definition; the promised normalization and objective definition is followed by the smooth prior; and the promised localization density is followed by the pattern enumeration. The pilot subsections similarly introduce pilot assumptions, randomized response, local alternatives, and regularity immediately before different objects. This breaks the logical development and leaves essential channel quantities undefined during their use.
  - *Fix:* Reorder the existing environments and their introductions together. Present the ordered patterns and directional objective before the optimized values and oracle; place both pilot-size assumptions, randomized response, and the estimator together; introduce local alternatives and regularity immediately before sequential efficiency; and place the smooth prior with sequential localization. Preserve all anchored identifiers and verified conditions.
- **[major·prose] Trial model and private releases; global notation** — The record alphabet initially assigns x_1=(0,0) through x_4=(1,1), whereas the ordered-pattern and pilot definitions assign x_0=(0,0) through x_3=(1,1). Thus x_1 denotes two different records within the paper. Pattern membership, probability coordinates, and channel formulas depend on this distinction.
  - *Fix:* Adopt zero-based record indexing throughout the manuscript, including the refinement statement and appendix notation. Keep subject indexing one-based if desired, explain that convention once, and translate every record-probability coordinate and pattern-membership expression consistently.
- **[major·prose] Abstract; introduction; private-pilot estimation and Wald inference** — The abstract repeatedly leads with 'Given a measurable strong-saddle selector,' and the introduction and discussion treat the selector entirely as a supplied condition. The current verification contract includes the proved existence declaration exists_measurable_strong_saddle_selector for interior assignment and positive privacy. The presentation therefore makes an established existence result appear to be an unresolved external requirement.
  - *Fix:* State near the first contribution description and alongside the estimator that measurable strong-saddle selectors exist under the stated assignment and privacy conditions. Preserve the attainment theorem's dependence on a fixed selector satisfying its interface, while distinguishing proved existence from the computational task of evaluating a selector. Consolidate the repeated abstract qualification.
- **[major·other] Private-pilot estimation and Wald inference** — The boxed implementation takes the complete strong-saddle selector as an input. The finite optimization characterizes its values, but readers need an executable selection rule, deterministic handling of ties, and numerical certification to reproduce the proposed estimator. This matters particularly where optimal supports change.
  - *Fix:* Provide a reproducible solver or reference implementation for the finite saddle problem, specifying channel-weight selection, tie handling, feasibility checks, profiling stationarity, and optimality tolerances. Validate it against the exact three-output witness, the centered five-output certificate, and the balanced formula. Clearly identify numerical approximations separately from the exact selector covered by the theorem.
- **[minor·prose] Optimal release cardinality; exact efficiency bound and finite optimization** — Several cross-references target an object that does not contain the attributed information. For example, 'The fixed pattern enumeration in \cref{obj:def:staircase-saddle}' points to the optimized-value definition. Other passages attribute pattern vectors and denominators to that same definition.
  - *Fix:* Direct enumeration references to \cref{obj:synth_8}, and references for rays, masses, gradients, feasibility, and the directional objective to \cref{obj:synth_4}. Retain \cref{obj:def:staircase-saddle} for optimized information and variance. Check each reference semantically while preserving exclusive cleveref usage.
- **[minor·citation] Related work** — The distinction between staircase linear utilities and the concave profiled objective is useful, but the contrast-design interpretation would benefit from explicit engagement with the classical optimal-design and experiment-comparison lineage identified in the supplied literature brief.
  - *Fix:* Engage Elfving and Pukelsheim on contrast-specific design and Blackwell on comparison by randomization, with accurate locators. Explain which optimization principles provide background and which binary private-channel cardinality and sequential-attainment conclusions constitute the present contribution.
- **[minor·prose] Related work** — The rate literature is described largely through generic phrases such as 'matching minimax rate bounds' and 'privacy-dependent convergence rate.' Readers cannot directly assess how the exact variance constant strengthens the closest high-privacy rate benchmarks.
  - *Fix:* State the bounded scalar-mean and known-assignment causal benchmark rate min{1,(n epsilon^2)^(-1)}, identifying its model, high-privacy range, and numerical-constant interpretation. Preserve the supplied source locators and distinguish these rate results from the paper's fixed-positive-privacy local asymptotic constant.
- **[minor·prose] Proofs and verification note** — The sentence 'The theorem-local verification notes separately identify published conclusions used as cited dependencies' describes notes absent from the submitted source. Every external_dependencies list in the supplied contract is empty, making this disclosure stale.
  - *Fix:* Replace the sentence with a disclosure matching the current contract: identify the checked main statements and proof receipts, distinguish presentation-synthesized definitions, and provide the artifact revision for reproducibility. Describe ordinary related-work citations separately from formal dependencies.
- **[minor·structure] Global; proofs and verification note** — The main text repeatedly restates the same assumptions, estimator, risk quantifiers, and coverage interpretation across the introduction, results sections, discussion, and proof roadmap. Separate definitions of local alternatives and scaled errors also duplicate the regular-procedure definition. This repetition obscures the distinctive statistical ideas.
  - *Fix:* Consolidate the local-experiment definitions, present the estimator once with a compact implementation summary, and shorten the proof roadmap to the mechanisms linking the results. Preserve formal conditions and sample-size conventions while moving ancillary totalization details into a compact appendix note.
