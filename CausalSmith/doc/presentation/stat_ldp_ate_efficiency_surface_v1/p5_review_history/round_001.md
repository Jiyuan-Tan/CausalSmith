# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 7.1/10 — The verified efficiency and cardinality results constitute a substantial theoretical contribution, but the disrupted exposition and incomplete computational presentation require substantial revision.

The paper characterizes the exact fixed-privacy efficiency constant for a binary-trial treatment-effect contrast, constructs an attaining private-pilot procedure, and establishes sharp stationary release cardinalities on certified regions. Its treatment of nuisance variation, arbitrary measurable sequential releases, and pointwise inference at support changes is valuable. The principal contribution claims faithfully preserve the verified conditions and comparator scope, but the organization and computational presentation currently impede publication.

## Strengths
- The finite nuisance-profiled saddle connects channel design to an exact sequential local risk bound and regular attainment.
- The sharp five-output result and certified three-output neighborhood provide substantive information about contrast-specific mechanism geometry.
- The private-pilot result combines exact conditional centering, regularity, variance consistency, and pointwise Wald coverage under the stated selector and pilot conditions.
- The dedicated early related-work section engages the closest predecessors' objectives, rates, and regularity conditions.
- The Laplace comparison consistently identifies the published release and sample-mean estimator, and the attendance example clearly specifies a prospective binary endpoint.

## Findings
- **[major·structure] Exact efficiency bound and finite optimization** — The private-pilot and sequential-efficiency subsections repeatedly place explanatory paragraphs beside unrelated environments. The paragraph explaining pilot divergence precedes the scaled-error definition; the paragraph explaining the negligible pilot fraction precedes the regular-procedure definition; the randomized-response definition appears under sequential efficiency; and the sentence introducing the regular-class definition precedes the pilot algorithm. This disrupts the dependency order and makes several descriptions misleading.
  - *Fix:* Reorder these subsections into a coherent sequence: local alternatives and scaled errors, regular procedures and the risk criterion, pilot-size assumptions, randomized-response pilot, strong-saddle selector and estimator, then sequential minimax efficiency. Move each explanatory paragraph with its target environment and preserve the verified statements and exact cross-reference labels.
- **[major·other] Private-pilot estimation and Wald inference** — The mathematical estimator is specified through an established measurable selector, but the implementation box leaves numerical selection to a separately specified solver and tie-breaking rule. The manuscript's numerical illustration uses the balanced closed form, so readers receive little computational evidence about selecting the general nuisance-profiled channel or handling optimizer ties.
  - *Fix:* Supply a reproducible reference implementation of the finite saddle calculation with a deterministic selection rule. Document primal feasibility, profiling stationarity, dual optimality checks, tolerances, and behavior at ties; provide representative computed channels and reproduce the certified examples. Explain how numerical approximation relates to the exact selector conditions used by the attainment theorem.
- **[minor·statement] Proofs and verification note — Measurable refinement and information equality** — The refinement lemma writes its pattern masses as a sum over records indexed from one through four, whereas the standing alphabet uses indices zero through three. The lemma therefore introduces an undefined fourth-indexed record and changes the meaning of the existing record symbols without announcing a local convention. The later appendix relabeling does not resolve the reader's initial encounter with the statement.
  - *Fix:* Introduce an explicit lemma-local relabeling immediately before the environment, or consistently reindex the presentation to the standing zero-based alphabet. Apply the same mapping to record probabilities and pattern membership, preserving the verified mathematical claim.
- **[minor·statement] Trial model and private releases — Output information and cardinality** — The initial cardinality convention assigns infinity to output spaces other than finite alphabets equipped with their power-set sigma-field. Later cardinality statements use finite underlying spaces with arbitrary measurable structure, and the appendix introduces outer-measure singleton evaluations. These presentations give different conventions for finite spaces with a coarser sigma-field.
  - *Fix:* State one cardinality convention at its first definition and use it throughout. If the verified arbitrary-measurable-space convention uses outer-measure singleton evaluations, introduce that convention there and explain that ordinary singleton probabilities apply to finite power-set alphabets.
- **[minor·structure] Private-pilot estimation and Wald inference; Proofs and verification note** — The estimator and its conditions are explained repeatedly in the introductory material, the formal algorithm, the boxed algorithm, the inference section, the discussion, and the proof-roadmap section. The proof-roadmap section largely repeats theorem conclusions, while essential statistical intuition receives comparatively little space.
  - *Fix:* Retain one principal algorithm presentation and shorten later recaps to exact cross-references. Condense the proof roadmap around the mechanisms of the argument: directional refinement, nuisance profiling, conditional affine centering, and sequential information accounting. Move technical totalization and indexing conventions to an appropriate appendix where the presentation contract permits.
- **[minor·prose] Related work** — The sentence ending 'the results here optimize one specified contrast rather than assert a global ordering of experiments' explains the contribution through an absent deliverable outside the limitations section.
  - *Fix:* Replace it with an affirmative statement, for example: 'The results characterize contrast-specific efficiency within this experiment-ordering framework.' Preserve the specified contrast and model scope.
- **[minor·citation] Related work; Proofs and verification note** — The manuscript positions staircase mechanisms and private asymptotic efficiency carefully, but gives little bibliographic attribution for nuisance-score projection and the sequential van Trees approach. The appendix also invokes Sion's minimax theorem by name without a source citation. Machine verification and intellectual attribution serve separate purposes.
  - *Fix:* Add the relevant references already identified in the supplied literature context: cite Sion at the minimax invocation, Gill or van Trees at the Bayesian information inequality, and a standard local-asymptotic reference for nuisance projection and the local risk criterion. Retain accurate source locators and distinguish these intellectual antecedents from formal proof dependencies.
