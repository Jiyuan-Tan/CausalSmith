# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 7.3/10 — The verified all-radius characterization is a substantial theoretical contribution with generally faithful claims, while organization, statistical definitions, and attribution require substantial revision.

The paper establishes matching expected-length bounds for honest intervals in a precisely specified homogeneous logistic experiment, including null evaluation and arbitrary shrinking effect radii. Its attaining construction and continuous same-model lower alternatives give the characterization considerable theoretical value. The principal claims faithfully reflect the verified results, but the exposition needs a clearer statistical setup, earlier presentation of the main theorem, and fuller attribution of closely related and inherited results.

## Strengths
- The simultaneous all-radius characterization clearly separates ambient coverage from subclass expected-length evaluation.
- The effect-scaled prognosis contribution and the null-rate transition provide an informative estimand-specific precision result.
- The lower comparisons preserve continuity, native-logit smoothness, and one homogeneous coefficient in every alternative law.
- Finite-sample honesty, rational endpoint realization, and the converse for randomized procedures strengthen the result.
- The dedicated early related-work section carefully distinguishes several competitors' targets, nuisance conditions, and inference criteria.

## Findings
- **[major·structure] Sharp expected length across effect radii; appendices** — The main comparison appears after extensive arithmetic specifications, calibration equations, prior definitions, and comparison-constant selections. Three further frontier statements largely repeat its guarantees. This organization obscures the statistical contribution and makes its supporting machinery difficult to prioritize.
  - *Fix:* Present the main theorem immediately after the model and length objective, followed by the coordinate identity and radius interpretation. Move detailed arithmetic contracts and calibrated-prior definitions to their respective appendices. Consolidate the repeated frontier presentations and replace exhaustive cross-reference inventories with targeted references, preserving the exact labels of retained objects.
- **[major·structure] Model and honest interval criterion** — The statistical setup remains partly implicit. Treatment and outcome notation is introduced after its use in the coordinate estimates; the propensity logit lacks an explicit defining formula; the Hölder seminorm is formally specified much later; and the interval-output space lacks its admissible range and measurable structure. Continuity and interiority of the observed-law carrier are embedded in the prognosis definition rather than established at the beginning of the experiment.
  - *Fix:* Start the section with the record space, independent sampling convention, and observed-law carrier with continuous normalized interior conditional cells. Define arm risks, treatment probability, propensity logit, effect, exponent domain, and Hölder seminorm there. Specify the interval-output space, endpoint conventions, effect-envelope restriction, and Borel structure before defining honest procedures.
- **[major·citation] Related work** — The closest logistic discussion centers on Tan, logistic DML, and DINA, while the supplied literature context also identifies directly relevant doubly robust odds-ratio estimators and a closed-form adjusted estimator for binary exposure. Their relationship to the present homogeneous scalar target needs explicit treatment. Several projection comparisons also describe minimax attainment without displaying the rates needed to assess the present extension.
  - *Fix:* Add a focused comparison with Tchetgen Tchetgen's 2010 and 2013 odds-ratio results, identifying their nuisance specifications and inference guarantees. State the relevant projection-functional rates explicitly and explain how the present contribution combines those estimation ingredients with ambient honesty, all-radius length evaluation, and homogeneous-logistic converses. Verify version-specific locators when adding these comparisons.
- **[major·citation] Introduction; Related work; lower-bound discussion** — The supplied contribution charter credits the known-fair and fixed-(0.3,0.1) problems as supporting antecedents and credits the parametric null-floor reduction to the known-fair problem. The manuscript's attribution leaves these inherited supporting results and the complete-domain extension insufficiently distinguished.
  - *Fix:* Add explicit citations and concise attribution for the known-fair and fixed-exponent supporting results, including the provenance of the parametric null-floor reduction. Identify the complete public-exponent, all-radius characterization as the delivered extension, and distinguish the neighboring rough-design problem by its different experiment.
- **[minor·prose] Global notation** — The symbols b_C and b_S repeatedly change from rank-enclosure endpoints to statistical error radii, prompting numerous explanatory interruptions. The parallel rate names d_n and rho_n and repeated interval aliases add further bookkeeping without a clear reader-facing purpose.
  - *Fix:* Use distinct symbols for rank-enclosure endpoints and coordinate error radii. Choose one rate symbol for explanatory prose and explain any retained formal aliases once in a compact notation paragraph.
- **[minor·prose] Abstract; Introduction; Rational realization and the upper bound** — The phrase 'prescribed finite arithmetic budgets' blurs the distinction between the two prescribed precision levels and engine-specific public recurrence bounds. The verified computational guarantee concerns finite rational evaluation on valid represented inputs; its presentation should make that computational model explicit.
  - *Fix:* Describe the construction as using prescribed rank and endpoint precision levels together with public primitive recurrence bounds on valid named inputs. Keep precision, instruction-count, and practical computational-performance terminology distinct.
- **[minor·prose] Full honest frontier solution** — The sentence 'It receives neither the radius nor unknown nuisance functions' uses ordinary prose negation outside a limitations section to explain the construction.
  - *Fix:* Replace it with an affirmative input description, such as: 'Its inputs are the sample size, public exponent representations, and represented observed records, together with the independently fixed arithmetic engine.' Retain the radius-independence guarantee.
- **[minor·prose] Verification note** — The note refers to theorem-local 'Formalization scope' footnotes and source-proof disclosures, while the supplied verification record lists empty external-dependency sets throughout. The note also provides no concrete revision identifier or retrieval instructions for the formal artifact, leaving its disclosure and reproducibility references difficult to follow.
  - *Fix:* Align the note with the current dependency record and actual rendered footnotes. Add the formal artifact's location, revision identifier, declaration mapping, and concise reproduction instructions. Describe the model and primitive contracts as hypotheses and the construction's admissibility, honesty, and length guarantees as established conclusions.

- What permanent artifact location and revision should readers use to retrieve the formal development and its declaration-to-manuscript mapping?
