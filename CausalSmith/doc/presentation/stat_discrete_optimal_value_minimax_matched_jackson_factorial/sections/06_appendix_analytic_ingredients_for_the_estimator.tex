\section{Appendix: Analytic ingredients for the estimator}

This appendix collects the analytic inputs behind the local polynomial estimator in \cref{obj:def:jackson-factorial-estimator}. The first ingredient is a Jackson approximation certificate for the globally defined cell contribution \(\bar f_\epsilon\) from \cref{obj:def:global-extension}; the second is a pilot/evaluation control statement for the centered factorial lift used to estimate the resulting local polynomial. Together they supply the cellwise bias, variance, and pilot-failure bounds used in \cref{obj:thm:jackson-factorial-upper}.

The approximation step uses the tensor Jackson construction of \cref{obj:def:jackson-kernel}. Standard Jackson smoothing gives polynomial control for nonsmooth functions on rectangles \citep{DeVore1993,JiaoVenkatHanWeissman2015Functionals}; here the certificate is stated in the physical four-cell coordinates needed by the estimator.

\begin{lemmav}{lem:simultaneous-jackson-certificate}[Simultaneous Jackson certificate]
There is a universal constant \(A>0\) such that, for every overlap parameter \(\epsilon\) with \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there is a constant \(C_\epsilon>0\) with the following property. Let:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Degree.)} \(K\ge 2\).
\item \textbf{(Rectangle.)} \(Q_x=\prod_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}[\ell_{\jmath,x},u_{\jmath,x}]\) be a valid pilot-local rectangle as in \cref{obj:def:jackson-kernel}, with \(\ell_{\jmath,x}\ge 0\) and positive radii
\[
r_{\jmath,x}=\frac{u_{\jmath,x}-\ell_{\jmath,x}}{2}>0
\]
for every \(\jmath\in\mathcal J\).
\end{itemize}
Let \(P_{x,K}\) be the tensor Jackson polynomial associated with \(\epsilon\), \(K\), and \(Q_x\) in \cref{obj:def:jackson-kernel}. Then the same polynomial satisfies all of the following conclusions:
\[
\deg_{\jmath}(P_{x,K})\le 2(K-1)
\qquad\text{for every }\jmath\in\mathcal J .
\]
For every \(v\in Q_x\),
\[
\left|P_{x,K}(v)-\bar f_\epsilon(v)\right|
\le
C_\epsilon
\sum_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}
\left\{
\frac{\sqrt{(v_{\jmath}-\ell_{\jmath,x})(u_{\jmath,x}-v_{\jmath})}}{K}
+
\frac{r_{\jmath,x}}{K^2}
\right\}.
\]
There are a finite multi-index set \(\mathcal A_x\) and coefficients \(b_{x,\alpha}\) such that, for every \(v\),
\[
P_{x,K}(v)-\bar f_\epsilon(b_x)
=
\sum_{\alpha\in\mathcal A_x}
b_{x,\alpha}
\prod_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}
\left(\frac{v_{\jmath}-b_{\jmath,x}}{r_{\jmath,x}}\right)^{\alpha_{\jmath}},
\]
with
\[
\alpha_{\jmath}\le 2(K-1)
\qquad\text{for every }\alpha\in\mathcal A_x\text{ and every }\jmath\in\mathcal J,
\]
and
\[
\sum_{\alpha\in\mathcal A_x}|b_{x,\alpha}|
\le
A^K(1+\epsilon^{-1})
\sum_{\jmath\in\mathcal J} r_{\jmath,x}.
\]
\end{lemmav}

\Cref{obj:lem:simultaneous-jackson-certificate} gives one polynomial with the two forms of control needed by the estimator. The pointwise approximation bound adapts to the location of \(v\) inside the pilot rectangle, including boundary and tie configurations. The centered coefficient envelope controls the polynomial expansion around \(b_x\), which is the expansion evaluated by the factorial-moment step in \cref{obj:def:jackson-factorial-estimator}.

The next definitions isolate the event on which the pilot rectangle is well localized and the two pilot-failure terms that enter the cellwise error bounds. They are stated for a single cell because the subsequent lemma applies uniformly over all cells.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_12}[Good-pilot event]
Fix a cell \(x\), four-cell mass vector \(q_x=(q_{\jmath,x})_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}\), Poisson scale \(m>0\), alphabet logarithm \(L_d=\log(ed)\), pilot-radius constant \(H_0\), and pilot counts \((N'_{\jmath,x})_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}\). Put \(c_{\jmath,x}=N'_{\jmath,x}/m\) and \(h_{\jmath,x}=H_0\{\sqrt{c_{\jmath,x}L_d/m}+L_d/m\}\). The coordinatewise good-pilot event used for the pilot rectangle is \[\mathsf G_x:=\bigcap_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}\left\{\left|\frac{N'_{\jmath,x}}{m}-q_{\jmath,x}\right|\le \frac{h_{\jmath,x}}{4}\right\}.\]
\end{definitionv}

\Cref{obj:synth_12} records the self-normalized pilot event generated by the half-widths in \cref{obj:def:jackson-factorial-estimator}. On this event, each coordinate of the true four-cell vector is controlled at the same local scale used to build the Jackson rectangle.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_5}[Pilot-failure contributions]
Fix a cell \(x\), a four-cell mass vector \(q_x\), Poisson scale \(m>0\), and Jackson tuning. Let \(\mathbb P_{m,q_x}^{\mathrm{pil,eval}}\) denote the product-Poisson law of the pilot and evaluation count vectors, and let \(\mathsf G_x\) be the coordinatewise good-pilot event used for the pilot rectangle in the Jackson--factorial construction. With \(T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}\) denoting the clipped cell statistic built from the pilot and evaluation counts, define \[ \mathrm{PF}_{1,x}:=\int_{\mathsf G_x^c}\left|T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)\right|\,d\mathbb P_{m,q_x}^{\mathrm{pil,eval}}, \qquad \mathrm{PF}_{2,x}:=\int_{\mathsf G_x^c}\left(T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)\right)^2\,d\mathbb P_{m,q_x}^{\mathrm{pil,eval}}. \]
\end{definitionv}

The quantities in \cref{obj:synth_5} separate the contribution of pilot mislocalization from the ordinary conditional bias and variance analysis. This separation lets the final cell bounds retain the local mass scale \(p_x\) while remaining uniform over the observed-law class of \cref{obj:def:observed-model-class}.

The factorial step uses the Poisson splitting in \cref{obj:def:jackson-factorial-estimator}. Conditional on the pilot table, centered factorial polynomials estimate centered powers of the true atom masses, and their second moments determine the variance contribution of the local polynomial.

\begin{lemmav}{lem:centered-factorial-pilot-control}[Centered factorial pilot control]
Assume the product experiment satisfies the i.i.d. sampling law in \cref{obj:ass:iid-sampling}.  There is a universal Jackson tuning with self-normalized Poisson pilot-radius constant, polynomial-degree constant \(\kappa=1/100000\), and cutoff \(D_0=3\).  For every overlap parameter \(\epsilon\in(0,1/2)\), there is a constant \(C_\epsilon>0\) such that the following assertions hold for every \(n,d\), every observed law \(\mathbb P\) on \([d]\times\{0,1\}^2\), and every positive sample size \(n\), whenever \(\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) as in \cref{obj:def:observed-model-class}.  Set \(m=n/8\), let \(q_x=(q_{00,x},q_{01,x},q_{10,x},q_{11,x})\) be the four-coordinate cell vector, and let \(p_x\) be the marginal mass of cell \(x\).

In the uncapped marked experiment generated by
\[
M\sim \operatorname{Pois}(n/4)
\]
together with i.i.d. observations from \(\mathbb P\) and independent fair pilot/evaluation marks, the count \(M\) has law \(\operatorname{Pois}(n/4)\).  The induced pilot/evaluation table has law equal to the product, over cells \(x\in[d]\) and coordinates \(\jmath\in\mathcal J\), of two independent Poisson count arrays with coordinate means \(m q_{\jmath,x}\).  For each fixed cell \(x\), the marginal law of the pilot and evaluation counts in that cell is therefore
\[
\bigotimes_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}\operatorname{Pois}(m q_{\jmath,x})
\otimes
\bigotimes_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}\operatorname{Pois}(m q_{\jmath,x}).
\]

For every cell \(x\), every fixed pilot table, every coordinate \(\jmath\in\mathcal J\), every \(z\in\mathbb R\), and every \(h,t\in\mathbb N\), conditional expectation over the evaluation counts satisfies
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[U_h(N_{\jmath,x};z)\mid N'\right]
=
(q_{\jmath,x}-z)^h
\]
and
\[
\mathbb E\!\left[U_h(N_{\jmath,x};z)U_t(N_{\jmath,x};z)\mid N'\right]
=
\sum_{\ell=0}^{\min\{h,t\}}
\binom{h}{\ell}\binom{t}{\ell}\ell!
\left(\frac{q_{\jmath,x}}{m}\right)^\ell
(q_{\jmath,x}-z)^{h+t-2\ell}.
\]

With the same tuning, the clipped Jackson-factorial cell statistic \(T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}\) obeys, uniformly over all cells \(x\in[d]\),
\[
\left|
\mathbb E T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)
\right|
\le
C_\epsilon
\left\{
\sqrt{\frac{p_x}{mL_d}}+\frac{1}{mL_d}
\right\},
\]
\[
\operatorname{Var}(T_x^{\mathrm{JF}})
\le
C_\epsilon d^{1/16}
\left\{
\frac{p_x L_d}{m}+\frac{L_d^2}{m^2}
\right\},
\]
and the pilot-failure contributions satisfy
\[
\mathrm{PF}_{1,x}
\le
C_\epsilon
\left\{
\sqrt{\frac{p_x}{mL_d}}+\frac{1}{mL_d}
\right\},
\qquad
\mathrm{PF}_{2,x}
\le
C_\epsilon d^{1/16}
\left\{
\frac{p_x L_d}{m}+\frac{L_d^2}{m^2}
\right\}.
\]
\end{lemmav}

\Cref{obj:lem:centered-factorial-pilot-control} supplies the cellwise analytic estimates used to aggregate the Jackson--factorial construction. The Poisson splitting clause identifies the product structure of pilot and evaluation counts, the factorial identities give exact first and second conditional moments for the centered monomial lift, and the final bounds control bias, variance, and pilot failure at the local mass scale. Summing these bounds over cells and applying the projection and Rao--Blackwellization steps in \cref{obj:def:jackson-factorial-estimator} yields the upper-risk statement recorded in \cref{obj:thm:jackson-factorial-upper}.
