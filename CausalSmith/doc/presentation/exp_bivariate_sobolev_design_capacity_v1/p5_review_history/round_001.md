# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.7/10 — The verified capacity characterization is a substantial theoretical contribution, but inconsistent norm definitions and extensive structural repetition require major editorial revision before publication.

The paper characterizes minimax scaled Horvitz–Thompson excess variance for a centered, pooled order-two Sobolev class under independent uniform covariates. Its principal contributions are a logarithm-free finite-sample rate with explicit interaction-count dependence, simultaneous attainment across smoothness levels, and an independent-prior converse over the full admissible design class. The verified results support these claims, while the manuscript needs a consistent analytic normalization, a clearer results hierarchy, and fuller positioning against recent nonlinear balancing designs.

## Strengths
- The matched finite-sample characterization explicitly tracks sample size, interaction count, and smoothness, including the endpoint s=1.
- One smoothness-independent assignment law attains the comparison scale while preserving fair treatment probabilities on every original unit.
- The independent coefficient prior respects the fixed-function supremum outside sampling expectation and establishes a converse against every admissible design.
- The causal transfer accurately distinguishes arbitrary square-integrable uniform-covariate outcome laws, their law-specific benchmarks, and the Gaussian calibration.
- The dedicated related-work section engages the closest published rate comparison and carefully distinguishes experimental signing from subset-based integration.
- The discussion clearly identifies the uniform-sampling attainment scope, bounded-density converse, and exact-real arithmetic conventions.

## Findings
- **[major·prose] Setup and assumptions — Canonical components and regularity** — The setup defines N_{p,s} using the Fourier weight (1+||ω||²)^s and an infimum over H^s extensions. The verified reflection statements subsequently define the same symbol using (1+||ω||²/p)^s and an infimum over L¹∩L² extensions with the displayed weighted energy. For pair components, these are different normalizations. Consequently, the manuscript gives two definitions of the unit ball whose numerical constants and prior normalization it reports.
  - *Fix:* Replace the setup definition with the exact normalized extension-infimum definition supplied by the verification contract, including the extension domain and Fourier convention. Use that definition consistently throughout the pooled budget and literature comparison. If the conventional unnormalized norm is retained for comparison, give it a separate symbol and state the precise relationship without treating the two unit balls as identical.
- **[major·structure] Main results; Discussion and extensions; Proof assembly and verification note** — The results hierarchy obscures the contribution. The comprehensive capacity theorem appears under 'An independent-prior converse', whereas the lower-bound theorem appears under 'Causal transfer and prospective precision' immediately after a sentence introducing a Gaussian completion. A second comprehensive characterization appears in the appendix, and the Gaussian completion, variance benchmark, dimension frontier, and sample-size implications are repeatedly restated. The causal-completion definition also contains a cross-reference list covering nearly every object in the paper.
  - *Fix:* Open Main results with the existing capacity characterization, place the independent-prior lower bound under its corresponding subsection, and group the causal transfer and precision implications together. Retain the detailed comprehensive assembly in one location and replace repeated passages with targeted cleveref references. Reduce the causal-completion definition's reference list to the definitions and assumptions needed to specify that construction.
- **[minor·citation] Related work — Randomized balancing** — The recent nonlinear-feature ridge guarantee attributed to Chen (2026) in the supplied related-work brief receives no discussion. Its assignment covariance bound and fixed-schedule actual HT variance guarantee are directly relevant to the paper's construction and precision objective. The current comparison concentrates on GSW and Edge-Walk without assessing this nearby result.
  - *Fix:* Examine Chen's Lemma 4.2 and Proposition 5.1 and add a focused comparison stating the covariance bound, robustness parameter, feature normalization, and fixed-schedule loss. Explain how those conditions relate to the present population criterion and pooled Sobolev class, and identify the precise additional contribution established by the matched capacity theorem.
- **[minor·prose] Discussion and extensions — Causal calibration and relative excess** — The discussion presents the sufficient and necessary inequalities as prospective sample-size comparisons without quantifying their enormous constant gap. At s=1, c_1 is approximately 3.36×10⁻⁷, so the necessary implication applies to relative tolerances below approximately 8.4×10⁻⁸, and the ratio of the displayed upper and lower constants exceeds nine billion. Readers need this calibration to assess the practical meaning of the explicit thresholds.
  - *Fix:* Add a short numerical calibration of the constants and tolerance range. Describe the formulas as conservative finite-sample certificates of the interaction-count scaling, and explain that the Gaussian values fix the outcome scale used for this illustration. Preserve the theorem's exact sufficient and necessary conditions.
- **[minor·prose] Setup and assumptions — Spectral assignment features; Main results — Simultaneous attainment** — The reflection array η is first encoded by signs in {-1,1}, with η=1 selecting X, and later by bits in {0,1}, with η=0 selecting X. Both encodings describe the intended lift, but reusing the same notation without an explicit conversion makes the construction harder to follow.
  - *Fix:* Choose one reflection encoding throughout the reader-facing construction, or use distinct symbols and state the conversion once. Consolidate the repeated lift descriptions around the anchored randomized-reflection definition.
