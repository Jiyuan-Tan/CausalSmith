# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.7/10 — The verified capacity characterization is a substantial theoretical contribution, but inconsistent model normalization and extensive structural duplication require substantial revision.

The paper characterizes minimax scaled Horvitz–Thompson excess variance for centered, pooled Sobolev main and pair effects under independent uniform covariates. Its logarithm-free comparison, uniform smoothness constants, common attaining design, and independent-prior converse establish a meaningful finite-sample benchmark. The principal claims track the verified results closely, including the broader outcome-law interpretation and bounded-density converse. Publication requires a consistent definition of the function class and a substantially clearer presentation of the contribution.

## Strengths
- The matched finite-sample rate explicitly captures interaction count, saturation, and the endpoint s=1.
- One fair assignment kernel attains the comparison simultaneously across the stated smoothness range.
- The independent legal prior respects the fixed-function supremum outside the sampling expectation and supplies a converse against every admissible design.
- The causal interpretation carefully distinguishes law-specific excess variance, Gaussian calibration, and the broader uniform outcome-law class.
- The dedicated early related-work section compares actual rates and sampling budgets, and the limitations section accurately identifies extension and implementation questions.

## Findings
- **[major·prose] Setup and assumptions — Canonical components and regularity** — The setup defines the restriction norm using extensions in H^s and Fourier weight (1+||ω||²)^s, whereas the verified reflection statements use an extension infimum over L¹∩L² with weight (1+||ω||²/p)^s. Both expressions are called N_{p,s}. For pair components, these conventions specify different budget normalizations, making the headline function class and its explicit constants ambiguous.
  - *Fix:* Define N_{p,s} in the setup using the exact extension class and dimension-normalized Fourier weight specified by the verification contract, and use that convention throughout the class definition and literature comparisons. If an alternative conventional norm is retained for background, give it a distinct symbol and clearly distinguish its budget from the theorem's budget.
- **[major·structure] Main results and Proof assembly and verification note** — The two omnibus capacity theorems repeat nearly the entire characterization, including assignment construction, prior legality, causal transfer, and the dimension frontier. These conclusions are then repeatedly restated in discussion and proof assembly. The subsection titled 'An independent-prior converse' begins with the expanded omnibus attainment theorem, and several transitions announce constructions different from the environment that follows. This organization obscures the contribution and makes the results unnecessarily difficult to navigate.
  - *Fix:* Use one headline capacity statement in the main text, followed by focused explanations of attainment, the converse, and causal transfer. Move the expanded omnibus statement to an appendix while retaining its anchor and verified content. Place the full-design lower bound directly under the converse subsection, repair the construction transitions, and compress repeated calibration and proof-assembly summaries.
- **[minor·citation] Related work** — The methodological positioning omits the closely related nonlinear-feature ridge guarantee of Chen (2026), Lemma 4.2 and Proposition 5.1. Its assignment covariance bound and fixed-schedule HT variance interpretation are directly relevant to the present spectral-feature construction. The rounding discussion also needs a comparison with existing iterated randomized rounding.
  - *Fix:* Add a precise comparison with Chen's covariance and ridge approximation guarantees, distinguishing their fixed-schedule criterion from the present population minimax characterization. Examine Bansal (2024) on iterated randomized rounding and identify the specific ordered-prefix guarantee contributed or adapted here, with accurate locators and bibliographic entries.
- **[minor·prose] Appendix — Causal variance identities and class transfer** — The sentence 'The larger-class comparison uses the inclusion recorded in \cref{obj:prop:published-class-inclusion}' points to a proposition that records Gaussian inclusion in the uniform class, admissibility equivalence, and elementary bounds. The inclusion of the uniform class in the bounded-density class is established in the subsequent prognostic-capacity lemma.
  - *Fix:* Replace this reference with \cref{obj:lem:published-prognostic-capacity} and explicitly identify the inclusion Q_{d,s}⊆A_{d,s} being used. Check other references described as supplying the same class-transfer relation.
- **[minor·prose] Setup and assumptions — Spectral assignment features; Main results** — The reflection coins η_{ij} first take values in {-1,1}, with +1 selecting the original coordinate, but the anchored randomized-lift definition uses {0,1}, with 0 selecting the original coordinate. The same symbol consequently denotes two encodings of the construction without an explicit conversion.
  - *Fix:* Choose one coin encoding for all running descriptions and displayed construction formulas. Where the anchored definition uses the other encoding, state the conversion once and distinguish the two arrays by notation.
- **[minor·prose] Discussion and extensions — Causal calibration and relative excess** — The prospective sample-size discussion emphasizes explicit thresholds without adequately interpreting the gap between the upper constant 3072 and the lower constant, approximately 3.35×10^-7 at s=1. Readers should be able to distinguish the established rate-scale calibration from the numerical conservatism of these sufficient and necessary guarantees.
  - *Fix:* Add a concise interpretation of the constant gap and describe the displayed thresholds as conservative theoretical guarantees. Relate the sufficient guarantee to the existing independent-sign upper bound of 4, while preserving the stated tolerance restriction on the necessary implication.

## Questions for authors
- Can the setup be aligned throughout with the dimension-normalized extension-infimum norm used by the verified statements?
- Which features of the ordered boundary-rounding procedure and its prefix guarantee are specific to this construction relative to existing iterated randomized rounding?
