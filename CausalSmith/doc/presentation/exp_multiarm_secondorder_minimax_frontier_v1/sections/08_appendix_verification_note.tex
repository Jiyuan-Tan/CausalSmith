\section{Appendix: Verification note}\label{sec:verification}

This appendix records what is machine-checked, in what environment, and what is not.

Every displayed definition, proposition, lemma and theorem in the paper falls into exactly one of two classes. A \emph{matched} environment renders a Lean declaration in reader-facing mathematics: the displayed statement unfolds the declaration's structures and composite predicates into ordinary notation, with the same hypotheses and the same conclusion, and the proof reproduced in \cref{sec:deferred-proofs} follows the machine-checked proof of that declaration. The rendering is faithful to the declaration's content, not a transcription of Lean source. A \emph{presentation-only} environment introduces notation or packages several matched objects for the reader; it carries no separate mathematical content and is not itself checked. The two lists below are exhaustive.

\paragraph{Environment.} The development is written in Lean~4 against the toolchain \texttt{leanprover/lean4:v4.33.0}, on top of Mathlib and the project's own \texttt{Causalean} library. The results reported here were checked at commit \texttt{715add8e9475f5193526bce0c26baef559a47b68} of the source repository. The source of this development contains no \texttt{sorry} and no \texttt{axiom} declaration, and an axiom audit of all \(26\) declarations listed below reports exactly the three standard Lean axioms --- \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound} --- and nothing else. In particular, the paper contains no statement that is conditional on an unproved input, and no result is imported from a published source as a black box.
