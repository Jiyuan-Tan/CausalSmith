\section{Exact variance geometry}\label{sec:exact-variance}

The setup in \cref{obj:def:random-partition-design,obj:def:group-table,obj:def:pame-estimator} makes the realized groups a sample of disjoint \(M\)-subsets. The variance calculation therefore depends on two finite-population operations: centering each arm table on the uniform \(M\)-slice and averaging a table over groups disjoint from a given group. This section records the exact algebra for those operations and then applies it to the design variance of \(\widehat\tau_n\), the group-level difference-in-means estimator.

We first fix the realized arm counts and the centered table notation used in the covariance formulas.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_9}[Realized arm group counts]
For \(z\in\mathcal Z=\{0,1\}\), let
\[
G_{z,n}=\sum_{g=1}^{G_n}\mathbf 1\{Z_{ng}=z\}
\]
be the number of realized groups assigned to arm \(z\) at row \(n\). Under the complete group-treatment assignment with exactly \(G_{1n}\) treated groups and \(G_{0n}=G_n-G_{1n}\) control groups, this notation gives \(G_{1,n}=G_{1n}\) and \(G_{0,n}=G_{0n}\).
\end{definitionv}

\Cref{obj:synth_9} aligns the random-assignment notation with the fixed arm counts from \cref{obj:def:random-partition-design}. In the balanced complete group assignment used throughout the paper, the realized counts equal the design counts, so later variance expressions may use \(G_{1n}\) and \(G_{0n}\) directly.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_5}[Centered arm table]
For integers \(n\) and \(M\) with \(M\le n\), let
\[
\Omega_{n,M}=\{A\subseteq \{1,\ldots,n\}: |A|=M\}.
\]
Given a deterministic potential-outcome schedule \(Y_{i,n}(z,A)\) for units \(i\in A\), arms \(z\in\mathcal Z\), and groups \(A\in\Omega_{n,M}\), define the centered arm-\(z\) group table by
\[
h_{z,n}^{\circ}(A)
=
h_{z,n}(A)-\mathbb E_n[h_{z,n}]
\]
for each \(A\in\Omega_{n,M}\), where
\[
h_{z,n}(A)=\frac{1}{M}\sum_{i\in A}Y_{i,n}(z,A),
\qquad
\mathbb E_n[h_{z,n}]
=
\frac{1}{|\Omega_{n,M}|}\sum_{B\in\Omega_{n,M}} h_{z,n}(B).
\]
\end{definitionv}

The centered table in \cref{obj:synth_5} isolates the part of an arm-specific group mean that can covary across disjoint realized groups. The centering is with respect to the uniform slice, matching the estimand in \cref{obj:def:pame-estimator} and the ordered-disjoint covariance in \cref{obj:def:kneser-covariance}.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_4}[Uniform-slice norm]
For integers \(M \le n\), let
\[
\Omega_{n,M}:=\{A\subseteq \{1,\ldots,n\}: |A|=M\}.
\]
For any real-valued function \(f:\Omega_{n,M}\to\mathbb R\), define its squared uniform-slice norm by
\[
\|f\|_n^2
:=
\langle f,f\rangle_n
:=
\mathbb E_n\{f(A)^2\},
\]
where \(\mathbb E_n\) denotes averaging over the uniform law on \(\Omega_{n,M}\).
\end{definitionv}

\Cref{obj:synth_4} supplies the norm in which Johnson harmonic components are measured. Because the inner product averages over all candidate groups of size \(M\), these energies describe finite-population variation before the random partition selects the realized disjoint groups.

The next result gives the spectral identity that turns disjointness dependence into degree-by-degree covariance terms. Its constants are the normalized Kneser eigenvalues, obtained from the classical Johnson association-scheme spectrum \citep{Delsarte1973,BrouwerCohenNeumaier1989,BrouwerEtAl2018}; the harmonic decomposition follows the standard slice analysis of Boolean functions \citep{Filmus2016}.

\begin{theoremv}{thm:exact-kneser-identity}[Exact Kneser covariance identity]
Let \(M,n\in\mathbb N\) satisfy \(2\le M\) and \(2M\le n\). Let \(Y_{i,n}(z,A)\) be a deterministic potential-outcome schedule for units \(i\in A\), arms \(z\in\mathcal Z\), and groups \(A\in\Omega_{n,M}\), with group-level arm tables \(h_{z,n}\) as in \cref{obj:def:group-table}. Suppose the following conditions hold.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Johnson projections.)} For each \(k=0,\ldots,M\), \(\Pi_{n,k}\) is a linear projection on functions \(f:\Omega_{n,M}\to\mathbb R\) whose range is the degree-\(k\) Johnson harmonic subspace \(\mathcal H_{n,k}\), which fixes every element of \(\mathcal H_{n,k}\), and whose residual \(f-\Pi_{n,k}f\) is orthogonal to \(\mathcal H_{n,k}\) under the uniform-slice inner product \(\langle u,v\rangle_n=\mathbb E_n[u(A)v(A)]\).
\item \textbf{(Centered orthogonal decomposition.)} For every \(f:\Omega_{n,M}\to\mathbb R\) and \(A\in\Omega_{n,M}\),
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{M}\Pi_{n,k}f(A)=f(A)-\mathbb E_n f(A),
\]
and the projected components are mutually orthogonal: if \(k\ne \ell\), then
\[
\langle \Pi_{n,k}f,\Pi_{n,\ell}g\rangle_n=0
\]
for all \(f,g:\Omega_{n,M}\to\mathbb R\).
\item \textbf{(Kneser spectrum.)} For every \(k=0,\ldots,M\) and every \(f\in\mathcal H_{n,k}\), the unnormalized Kneser adjacency operator has eigenvalue
\[
(-1)^k {n-M-k\choose M-k}.
\]
\end{itemize}
Then the normalized Kneser disjointness operator \(\mathsf K_n\) acts on each projected component by
\[
\mathsf K_n(\Pi_{n,k}f)(A)=\lambda_{n,k}\,\Pi_{n,k}f(A),
\qquad
\lambda_{n,k}=(-1)^k\frac{(M)_k}{(n-M)_k},
\]
for every \(k=0,\ldots,M\), every \(f:\Omega_{n,M}\to\mathbb R\), and every \(A\in\Omega_{n,M}\). Moreover, if \(f,g:\Omega_{n,M}\to\mathbb R\) are centered under the uniform \(M\)-slice, then for a uniform ordered disjoint pair \((A,C)\),
\[
\operatorname{Cov}\{f(A),g(C)\}
=
\sum_{k=1}^{M}\lambda_{n,k}
\langle \Pi_{n,k}f,\Pi_{n,k}g\rangle_n .
\]
Finally, the ordered-disjoint covariance of the arm-difference table satisfies
\[
C_{\tau\tau,n}
=
\sum_{k=1}^{M}\lambda_{n,k}
\bigl\|\Pi_{n,k}(h_{1,n}-h_{0,n})\bigr\|_n^2 .
\]
\end{theoremv}

\Cref{obj:thm:exact-kneser-identity} shows that disjoint group formation has an exact finite-sample diagonalization in Johnson degrees. Each degree contributes its harmonic inner product multiplied by the signed finite-\(n\) factor \(\lambda_{n,k}\), so the covariance retained by sampling groups without replacement is completely summarized by the slice energies of the projected arm tables. Applied to the arm-difference table, the same identity gives \(C_{\tau\tau,n}\), the ordered-disjoint covariance contrast that enters the variance comparison below.

The exact variance formula combines this disjointness covariance with the complete-randomization variation across treatment labels. The definitions in \cref{obj:synth_5,obj:synth_9} make the statement entirely finite-sample: the arm variances \(V_{z,n}\), the ordered-disjoint covariances \(C_{ab,n}\), and the independent-group scale \(R_n\) are all computed from the deterministic row schedule.

\begin{theoremv}{thm:exact-pame-variance}[Exact PAME variance]
Let \(n,M,G_n,G_{1n}\) be natural numbers and let \(G_{0n}=G_n-G_{1n}\). Suppose that:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Feasible partition.)} The two-stage random partition design in \cref{obj:def:random-partition-design} is feasible: \(M G_n\le n\).
\item \textbf{(Group sizes.)} \(2\le M\), \(2\le G_{1n}\), and \(2\le G_{0n}\).
\item \textbf{(Potential outcomes.)} \(Y_{i,n}(z,A)\) is a deterministic potential-outcome schedule, defined for every \(A\in\Omega_{n,M}\), every \(i\in A\), and every \(z\in\mathcal Z\).
\end{itemize}
Set
\[
V_{z,n}
=
\operatorname{Var}_{A\sim\mathrm{Unif}(\Omega_{n,M})}\{h_{z,n}(A)\},
\qquad
C_{ab,n}
=
\mathbb E_{A}\mathbb E_{C\mid C\cap A=\varnothing}
\bigl[h_{a,n}^{\circ}(A)h_{b,n}^{\circ}(C)\bigr],
\]
and
\[
C_{\tau\tau,n}=C_{11,n}+C_{00,n}-2C_{10,n},
\qquad
R_n=\frac{V_{1,n}}{G_{1n}/G_n}+\frac{V_{0,n}}{1-G_{1n}/G_n}.
\]
For the difference-in-means estimator \(\widehat\tau_n\), the finite-population PAME \(\tau_n\), the exact design variance \(\sigma_n^2=\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\tau_n)\), and the scalar equal-group CR2 statistic \(\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}\) in \cref{obj:def:cr2-statistic},
\[
\mathbb E[\widehat\tau_n]=\tau_n,
\]
\[
\sigma_n^2
=
\frac{R_n}{G_n}
+
C_{\tau\tau,n}
-
\frac{C_{11,n}}{G_{1n}}
-
\frac{C_{00,n}}{G_{0n}},
\]
\[
\mathbb E[\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}]
=
\frac{V_{1,n}-C_{11,n}}{G_{1n}}
+
\frac{V_{0,n}-C_{00,n}}{G_{0n}},
\]
and
\[
\mathbb E[\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}]-\sigma_n^2=-C_{\tau\tau,n}.
\]
\end{theoremv}

\Cref{obj:thm:exact-pame-variance} establishes three finite-sample facts for the two-stage design. First, the group difference in means is unbiased for the finite-population PAME. Second, the exact design variance separates an independent-group component, \(R_n/G_n\), from the ordered-disjoint covariance terms induced by the random partition. Third, the scalar equal-group CR2 expectation differs from the exact variance by exactly \(-C_{\tau\tau,n}\). Thus the sign and magnitude of the CR2 discrepancy are governed by the same Kneser covariance contrast characterized in \cref{obj:thm:exact-kneser-identity}.
