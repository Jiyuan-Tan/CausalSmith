# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.5/10 — The paper delivers a substantial and rigorously verified variance-geometry contribution, but central design terminology, the Rademacher construction, object ordering, and closest-literature positioning require significant repair before publication.

The paper derives exact finite-population variance identities for disjoint random group formation, characterizes the dense degree-one correction to equal-group CR2, proves sparse ratio consistency beyond the birthday threshold, and establishes a positive-density one-realization lower bound. These are novel and potentially important results for design-based econometrics with endogenous cluster formation. The formal claims are faithful to the verification contract, but the manuscript currently obscures their scope through an inaccurate full-partition description, incomplete definitions of the lower-bound priors and witness, and an underdeveloped comparison with the closest inference literature.

## Strengths
- The exact Kneser covariance and PAME variance identities provide a clean, genuinely finite-population characterization of dependence from disjoint group formation.
- The dense expansion and CR2 phase frontier isolate an interpretable degree-one Johnson energy and give an exact ratio-consistency criterion under explicit conditions.
- The zero-density result sharply separates variance-ratio consistency from the stronger whole-tuple birthday condition.
- The versioned software identity and eight-unit calculation connect the abstract covariance geometry to an applied CR2 workflow.
- The positive-density lower bound is unusually strong: it covers every measurable one-realization variance statistic through a concrete indistinguishability construction.
- The explicitly titled limitations subsection accurately confines the contribution to variance behavior and identifies distributional inference and variable group sizes as extensions.

## Findings
- **[major·prose] Setup and assumptions** — The prose says, “The finite population \(\mathcal U_n\) is partitioned through the ordered group tuple \(\mathbf A_n\),” although the design assumes only \(MG_n\le n\). When \(MG_n<n\), the realized groups form a partial partition or disjoint packing and leave population units ungrouped. Repeated references to homogeneous random partitions can therefore misstate the sampling mechanism in precisely the sparse and intermediate-density regimes central to the paper.
  - *Fix:* Describe the design throughout as an ordered collection of disjoint groups, or explicitly define it as a partial partition of the grouped subset of size \(N_n=MG_n\). Clarify the status of ungrouped units and reserve “full partition” for \(N_n=n\).
- **[major·statement] One-realization lower bound** — The displayed priors are defined on Boolean coordinates in \(\{0,1\}\), while the proposition, interpretation, and proof use Rademacher signs in \(\{-1,1\}\) and limits such as \(V_{z,n}\to1/M\). The manuscript never defines the map from a Boolean draw to the real potential-outcome schedule, nor the common-arm and independent-arm schedules whose variances appear in the proposition.
  - *Fix:* Define explicitly, before the proposition, \(\xi=2h-1\) and the induced schedules, for example \(Y_i^{\mathrm{same}}(z,A)=\xi_i\) and \(Y_i^{\mathrm{ind}}(z,A)=\xi_{i,z}\). State whether the equality of observed laws holds conditionally on every realized design or after averaging over the full partition-and-treatment design, and use expectation notation that includes all randomized components.
- **[major·structure] Setup and assumptions** — Several objects are used materially before they are defined: \(N_n\) and \(\sigma_n^2\) occur in assumptions, \(G_{0n}\) occurs in the estimator, \(h_{z,n}^{\circ}\) occurs in the Kneser covariance, and \(V_{z,n}\) occurs in the dense-correction definition. Their definitions appear later or only inside theorem statements.
  - *Fix:* Add an initial notation block defining \(N_n=MG_n\), \(G_{0n}=G_n-G_{1n}\), the exact design variance \(\sigma_n^2\), centered tables, and slice arm variances. Reorder the remaining definitions so every displayed object is available before assumptions or formulas depend on it.
- **[major·citation] Related work** — The closest comparison is too compressed. The manuscript says that Fu, Samii, and Wang “study group-formation experiments under a sparse whole-tuple condition,” but it does not explain there that their Section 3 and Theorems 1–2 deliver both asymptotic normality and CR2 ratio consistency, whereas the present beyond-birthday result concerns the variance ratio in the homogeneous fixed-group-size random-partition subcase. The section also omits substantive engagement with the design-based cluster-regression comparison of Su and Ding and the independent-cluster working-model scope of Pustejovsky and Tipton.
  - *Fix:* Add a theorem-level comparison with exact locators: state the competitor's design, assumptions, normality and variance conclusions, then identify affirmatively the present paper's distinct exact identities, dense correction, and variance-only zero-density extension. Discuss Su–Ding and clarify how the random-group covariance channel differs from the working covariance framework behind conventional CR2 inference.
- **[major·structure] Dense and sparse variance behavior** — The main theorem statements repeatedly reproduce the full definitions of Johnson projections, orthogonality, the Kneser spectrum, and the dense class. Because the appendix proves the canonical Johnson and Kneser inputs, this gate-heavy presentation makes the central econometric results appear more conditional and much harder to parse than their substantive content warrants.
  - *Fix:* State reader-facing corollaries in terms of the canonical Johnson decomposition and the previously defined schedule class, with the exact expansion and probability conclusions prominent. Retain the fully expanded verified statements in the formal appendix or supplement and explain once how the canonical lemmas discharge their inputs.
- **[minor·prose] Proofs and auxiliary lemmas** — The verification note says, “The classical Johnson and Kneser spectral facts cited in this appendix ... enter as stated published dependencies.” The current verification record instead marks both spectral lemmas as internally proved, sorry-free declarations with no external dependencies. This is a stale description of the trust boundary.
  - *Fix:* State that the Johnson decomposition and Kneser spectral results are machine-checked internally, with the classical sources cited for attribution and context. Identify only the versioned software semantics as theorem-local external dependencies, consistent with the generated formalization-scope footnote.
- **[minor·statement] Software identity and finite witness** — The definition introduces “the balanced sign vector \(\mathbf a_8=(a_1,\ldots,a_8)\)” without specifying its entries or formally defining balance. The subsequent proof assumes exactly four \(+1\) and four \(-1\) entries, so the numerical witness cannot be reconstructed from the displayed definition alone.
  - *Fix:* Give an explicit vector, such as \((1,1,1,1,-1,-1,-1,-1)\), or define \(a_i\in\{-1,1\}\) with \(\sum_i a_i=0\) and state that the moments are invariant to relabeling.
- **[minor·citation] global** — Many claim-bearing citations lack section, theorem, assumption, equation, or page locators, including the uses of Fu–Samii–Wang for boundedness and growth, Li–Ding for asymptotic conditions, Ohlsson for sampling fractions, and the Johnson/Kneser sources for the spectral formulas. This falls short of the paper's stated locator standard and makes the technical genealogy difficult to audit.
  - *Fix:* Inspect each claim-bearing source and add precise locators. General historical citations may remain grouped, but every attributed condition, theorem, spectrum, or software behavior should point to the supporting location.
- **[minor·structure] global** — The title “Random group formation and cluster-robust variance in dense finite populations” suggests a treatment of cluster-robust variance broadly, while the delivered estimator result concerns the scalar CR2 entry for a tightly specified unweighted, equal-group, intercept-plus-binary-treatment regression.
  - *Fix:* Narrow the title to identify CR2 or equal-group cluster-robust variance, while retaining the broader random-group variance geometry as the first component.
- **[minor·prose] Introduction** — The roadmap uses manual structural references such as “The setup section” and “The exact-variance section” instead of cleveref commands. Similar deictic section references appear elsewhere, despite the manuscript-wide exclusive-cross-reference rule.
  - *Fix:* Add structural labels and replace the roadmap with grouped \(\cref\) references, reproducing each exact label and allowing cleveref to supply the section kind.
- **[minor·statement] Exact variance geometry** — The centered-decomposition condition in the Exact Kneser covariance identity writes \(f(A)-\mathbb E_n f(A)\). The verified object and the paper's earlier definition use the slice mean \(\mathbb E_n f\); the displayed expression is ambiguous because it can be read as applying expectation to the scalar \(f(A)\).
  - *Fix:* Replace \(\mathbb E_n f(A)\) with \(\mathbb E_n f\) consistently.
- **[nit·prose] abstract** — The first displayed asymptotic formula introduces \(G_n\), \(\sigma_n^2\), \(R_n\), and \(E_{\tau,1,n}\) before every symbol has a direct first-use plain-word gloss. The surrounding prose explains the ideas but does not consistently attach each name to its symbol at first appearance.
  - *Fix:* Introduce the formula as involving the row group count \(G_n\), exact design variance \(\sigma_n^2\), independent-group scale \(R_n\), and degree-one arm-contrast energy \(E_{\tau,1,n}\). Apply the same first-use check to \(p_n\) and \(N_n\) in the introduction.

## Questions for authors
- Is “random partition” intended to mean a partial partition of the grouped subset whenever \(N_n<n\), and how are outcomes or assignment status conceptualized for the ungrouped units?
- What is the exact manuscript-level map from the Boolean prior coordinates to the \(\{-1,1\}\)-valued potential-outcome schedules used in the lower-bound proof?
- Can the main text present canonical corollaries with the Johnson and Kneser inputs discharged, leaving the fully expanded verification interfaces to the appendix?
