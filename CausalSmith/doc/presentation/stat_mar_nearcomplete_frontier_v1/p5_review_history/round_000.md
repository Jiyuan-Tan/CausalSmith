# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 7.2/10 — The verified joint arrival-and-dimension characterization is a substantial theoretical contribution, while the manuscript needs substantial editorial consolidation and clearer presentation of the construction's numerical and practical scope.

The paper establishes matching minimax squared-error and fixed-coverage honest connected-interval length rates for a balanced randomized, finite-cell MAR experiment with binary outcomes and surrogates. Its distinctive contribution is the uniform dependence on the arrival deficit, supported by normalized causal priors comparing the complete observed experiment. The principal prose claims faithfully preserve the verified theorem conditions and prior-work boundaries. Publication would benefit from a more focused exposition, explicit numerical interpretation of the proposed procedures, and complete verification provenance.

## Strengths
- The characterization is uniform over sample size, baseline-support bound, arrival floors in [1/2,1], and arbitrary admissible cell masses.
- The normalized converse preserves the complete observed record and the arrival-deficit factor in target separation.
- Estimation and honest expected interval length are evaluated over the same clearly specified population target and statistical experiment.
- The dedicated early related-work section engages the closest competitors' rates, assumptions, and sampling designs.
- The statistical implications accurately explain sampling dominance, saturation, consistency along joint parameter sequences, and complete arrival.
- The limitations section clearly identifies adaptation, weaker arrival floors, computational evaluation, and empirical calibration as extensions.

## Findings
- **[major·structure] Setup and statistical criteria; appendices** — The exposition devotes extensive space to coordinate bookkeeping, elementary range arguments, and repeated explanations of the same constructions. Identification, projection, conditional averaging, and completion of the main comparisons each receive multiple overlapping treatments. This obscures the experiment-specific statistical ideas and substantially weakens readability for an econometrics audience.
  - *Fix:* Consolidate the setup around the observed experiment, causal restrictions, target, and two criteria. Retain one concise explanation of each construction and one proof location for each result. Compress routine finite-space, measurability, range, and supremum bookkeeping while preserving the verified mathematical content; give greater prominence to the missing-mass decomposition and normalized-prior comparison.
- **[major·prose] Minimax estimation and honest inference; Limitations and future work** — The construction's numerical scope deserves substantially more prominence. The polynomial branch requires log(e+n) at least 128, so it begins at n at least e^128-e. The exhibited risk certificate includes C_fall=4e^128; using the displayed C_U makes the confidence interval equal the full target range throughout the small-logarithm regime. The abstract and main discussion correctly claim rate attainment, but readers receive little immediate guidance about what this particular construction delivers at ordinary sample sizes.
  - *Fix:* State the branch threshold and its numerical implication next to the estimator. Describe the interval as a theoretical procedure attaining the minimax order for fixed noncoverage, and identify the consequences of the exhibited certificate for interval width. Expand the existing practical-constants discussion with these concrete facts, preserving all verified rate claims and distinguishing their universal-constant meaning from numerical performance.
- **[minor·prose] Setup and statistical criteria** — The cellwise-quantity and estimator-class definitions contain nearly exhaustive lists of references to later definitions and results. These lists suggest circular dependencies and interrupt otherwise elementary definitions; cellwise conditional probabilities and measurable sample maps can be understood from the already specified spaces and law.
  - *Fix:* Replace each exhaustive reference list with the immediate prerequisites. Define the cellwise quantities from the full-data law and define the estimator class from the observed sample space and target range. Preserve the anchored environment identifiers and use cleveref for the remaining references.
- **[minor·citation] Related work** — The closest discrete-ATE and surrogate-assisted comparisons are handled carefully, but the connection to uniform inference with high-dimensional missing data remains underdeveloped. The related-work brief identifies Robins (1997) and subsequent higher-order influence-function work as directly relevant precedents for the interaction between selection probabilities, nuisance complexity, and uniform precision.
  - *Fix:* Add a concise source-based paragraph explaining how the finite-cell arrival-and-dimension characterization relates to that uniform missing-data literature. Identify the relevant experiment and regularity restrictions, and distinguish the present unrestricted cellwise rate and normalized comparison from the earlier conclusions using accurate source locators.
- **[minor·other] Author footnote; Verification note** — The author footnote promises an appendix recording 'scope, toolchain, commit,' but the supplied verification note gives a narrative inventory without the frozen toolchain, commit identifier, or an object-to-declaration mapping. The verified statements are supported by the supplied contract; the manuscript's reproducibility disclosure should make that provenance available to readers.
  - *Fix:* Add the frozen toolchain information, commit d07574d7eb0a903c2a049c6ae28702de46193ec0, artifact access information, and a compact mapping of formal objects to declaration names and source files. Identify the cellwise-quantity and estimator-class definitions as presentation-level definitions, consistently with the author footnote and supplied verification record.
- **[minor·statement] Setup and statistical criteria — honest connected confidence intervals** — The interval-class definition says that tau(P) 'is the treatment-effect functional in' the observed-data identifying-functional definition. That referenced environment defines Psi(P_O); tau(P) is introduced earlier as the potential-outcome expectation and is connected to Psi by the identification lemma. The wording conflates the population target with its observed-law representation.
  - *Fix:* State tau(P)=E_P[Y(1)-Y(0)] directly in the interval-class definition, then reference the identification lemma for its equality to Psi(P_O) over the admissible law class.

## Questions for authors
- Is the intended contribution primarily a minimax characterization with a theoretical attaining construction? Please make that emphasis explicit alongside the estimator's branch threshold and interval certificate.
- Where can readers access the frozen Lean artifact, toolchain specification, and object-to-declaration mapping?
