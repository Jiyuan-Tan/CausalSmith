\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:good-gram-reproduction}]
Write
\[
K_i=\mathbf 1\{i\in I_2,\ X_i=x,\ 0\le U_i\le1\},
\qquad
G_x=\sum_{i}K_i\,v_\ell(U_i)v_\ell(U_i)^\top ,
\]
where the sum over all sample indices is the same as the sum over \(I_2\), since \(K_i=0\) outside \(I_2\).  Set
\[
N=N_x,\qquad
a=\frac{\lambda_\star N}{2}.
\]
On \(\Omega_x\), \(N>0\), and the assumed \(\lambda_\star>0\) gives \(a>0\).  The good-design inequality is
\[
a\sum_{r=0}^{\ell}b_r^2
\le
b^\top G_x b
\qquad\text{for every }b\in\mathbb R^{\ell+1}.
\]
If \(G_x b=0\), then the right side is zero, so \(\sum_rb_r^2=0\) and \(b=0\).  Thus \(G_x\) is invertible. % lean: coerciveMatrix_isUnit

On \(\Omega_x\), the weights are the equivalent-kernel weights,
\[
w_{ix}=K_i\,e_0^\top G_x^{-1}v_\ell(U_i).
\]
Consequently, for \(0\le j\le\ell\),
\[
\sum_{i\in I_2}w_{ix}U_i^j
=
e_0^\top G_x^{-1}
\left\{\sum_iK_i\,v_\ell(U_i)U_i^j\right\}.
\]
% lean: interceptWeight_eq_equivKernel_of_good

Let \(e_j\) be the \(j\)-th coordinate vector.  Since \(v_\ell(U_i)^\top e_j=U_i^j\),
\[
\sum_iK_i\,v_\ell(U_i)U_i^j
=
\sum_iK_i\,v_\ell(U_i)v_\ell(U_i)^\top e_j
=
G_xe_j .
\]
Therefore
\[
\sum_{i\in I_2}w_{ix}U_i^j
=
e_0^\top G_x^{-1}G_xe_j
=
e_0^\top e_j
=
\begin{cases}
1,&j=0,\\
0,&j\ne0.
\end{cases}
\]
% lean: goodGram_reproduction
\end{proof}
