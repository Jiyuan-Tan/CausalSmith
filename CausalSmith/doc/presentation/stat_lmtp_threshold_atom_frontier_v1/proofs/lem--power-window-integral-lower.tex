\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:power-window-integral-lower}]
Set
\[
m=\delta+\frac h2 .
\]
Then
\[
\delta\le m\le \delta+h,
\qquad
0\le m,
\qquad
\frac{\delta+h}{2}\le m .
\] % lean: integral_rpow_window_lower

For every \(a\in[m,\delta+h]\), the hypotheses \(0\le\delta\) and \(0<h\) give
\[
0\le \frac{\delta+h}{2},
\]
and the bound \((\delta+h)/2\le m\) recorded above, together with \(m\le a\), gives
\[
\frac{\delta+h}{2}\le m\le a .
\]
Together with \(\kappa\ge0\), monotonicity of real powers on nonnegative bases yields
\[
\left(\frac{\delta+h}{2}\right)^\kappa
\le
a^\kappa .
\]
Thus
\[
\int_m^{\delta+h}
\left(\frac{\delta+h}{2}\right)^\kappa\,da
\le
\int_m^{\delta+h} a^\kappa\,da .
\] % lean: integral_rpow_window_lower

The left-hand side is the constant integral over an interval of length \(h/2\):
\[
\int_m^{\delta+h}
\left(\frac{\delta+h}{2}\right)^\kappa\,da
=
\frac h2\left(\frac{\delta+h}{2}\right)^\kappa .
\] % lean: integral_rpow_window_lower

Since \(a^\kappa\ge0\) on \([\delta,\delta+h]\) and
\([m,\delta+h]\subseteq[\delta,\delta+h]\),
\[
\int_m^{\delta+h}a^\kappa\,da
\le
\int_\delta^{\delta+h}a^\kappa\,da .
\] % lean: integral_rpow_window_lower

Combining the window-length bound, the pointwise lower bound on \(a^\kappa\), and the
monotonicity of the window integral gives
\[
\frac h2\left(\frac{\delta+h}{2}\right)^\kappa
\le
\int_\delta^{\delta+h}a^\kappa\,da .
\] % lean: integral_rpow_window_lower
\end{proof}
