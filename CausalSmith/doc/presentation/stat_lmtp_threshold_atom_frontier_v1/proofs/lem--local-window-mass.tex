\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:local-window-mass}]
Let
\[
E_x(\delta,h)
=
\{O=(X,A,Y):X=x,\ A\in[\delta,\delta+h]\}.
\]
Because \(h>0\), for every observation \(O\),
\[
0\le\frac{A-\delta}{h}\le1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A\in[\delta,\delta+h].
\] % lean: scaledDose_mem_Icc_iff

Therefore
\[
\mathbf 1\left\{X=x,\ 0\le\frac{A-\delta}{h}\le1\right\}
=
\mathbf 1_{E_x(\delta,h)}(O).
\] % lean: localWindowWeight_integral_eq

Integrating this identity gives
\[
\int
\mathbf 1\left\{X=x,\ 0\le\frac{A-\delta}{h}\le1\right\}\,dP
=
P(E_x(\delta,h)).
\] % lean: localWindowWeight_integral_eq

The window-support assumption gives
\([\delta,\delta+h]\subseteq[0,1]\). Applying the conditional-density law in
stratum \(x\) to the Borel set \([\delta,\delta+h]\) yields
\[
P(E_x(\delta,h))
=
p_x\int_{[\delta,\delta+h]}\pi_x(a)\,da
=
p_x\int_{\delta}^{\delta+h}\pi_x(a)\,da .
\] % lean: localWindowWeight_integral_eq

The asserted identity follows from the two displayed evaluations of the local window weight. % lean: localWindowWeight_integral_eq
\end{proof}
