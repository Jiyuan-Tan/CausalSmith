\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:prop:pushforward-setup}]
Fix \(x\) and \(\delta\).  By \cref{obj:def:model-class}, the conditional treatment law in stratum \(x\) is represented on \([0,1]\) by the density \(\pi_x\).  By \cref{obj:def:clamp-policy}, the clamp map is measurable and satisfies
\[
d_\delta(a)=\delta\quad\text{for }a\leq\delta,
\qquad
d_\delta(a)=a\quad\text{for }a>\delta .
\]
Splitting the conditional treatment law at \((-\infty,\delta]\) and its complement gives
\[
P\{d_\delta(A)\in\cdot\mid X=x\}
=
P\{A\in\cdot,\ A>\delta\mid X=x\}
+
P\{A\leq\delta\mid X=x\}\,\delta_\delta .
\]% lean: clamp_pushforward_decomposition
Because this conditional treatment law is carried by \([0,1]\), the retained part is
\[
P\{A\in\cdot,\ A>\delta\mid X=x\}
=
P\{A\in\cdot,\ A\in(\delta,1]\mid X=x\}.
\]% lean: clamp_pushforward_decomposition
The same density representation, together with \(0\leq\delta\leq\bar\delta<1\), gives
\[
P\{A\leq\delta\mid X=x\}
=
\int_{[0,\delta]}\pi_x(a)\,da
=
q_x(\delta),
\]% lean: atomMass
where the last equality is the definition of \(q_x(\delta)\) in \cref{obj:def:clamp-functional}.  These identities give the asserted pushforward decomposition.

It remains to bound \(q_x(\delta)\).  The conditional-density and polynomial-thinning conditions in \cref{obj:def:model-class} give integrability of \(\pi_x\) on \([0,\delta]\) and, for Lebesgue-a.e. \(a\in[0,\delta]\),
\[
c_-a^\kappa\leq \pi_x(a)\leq c_+a^\kappa .
\]% lean: PolynomialThinning
Since \(\kappa\geq0\), the function \(a\mapsto a^\kappa\) is integrable on \([0,\delta]\).  Integrating the almost-everywhere inequalities yields
\[
c_-\int_0^\delta a^\kappa\,da
\leq
q_x(\delta)
\leq
c_+\int_0^\delta a^\kappa\,da .
\]% lean: integral_mono_ae
The regime condition \(\kappa\geq0\) implies \(-1<\kappa\), and with \(0\leq\delta\),
\[
\int_0^\delta a^\kappa\,da
=
\frac{\delta^{\kappa+1}}{\kappa+1}.
\]% lean: integral_rpow
Substitution gives
\[
\frac{c_-\,\delta^{\kappa+1}}{\kappa+1}
\leq
q_x(\delta)
\leq
\frac{c_+\,\delta^{\kappa+1}}{\kappa+1},
\]
as required.
\end{proof}
