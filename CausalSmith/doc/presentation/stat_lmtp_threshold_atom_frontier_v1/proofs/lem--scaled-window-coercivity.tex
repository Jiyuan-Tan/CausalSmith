\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:scaled-window-coercivity}]
Put
\[
\rho=\frac{\delta}{h},\qquad
D=\int_0^1(\rho+u)^\kappa\,du,\qquad
N=\int_0^1
\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j u^j\right)^2(\rho+u)^\kappa\,du .
\]
Since \(\delta\ge0\) and \(h>0\), one has \(\rho\ge0\).  The function
\(u\mapsto(\rho+u)^\kappa\) is continuous on \([0,1]\), is nonnegative there, and has value \((\rho+1)^\kappa>0\) at \(u=1\).  Hence it is positive on a neighborhood of \(1\), and the interval-integral positivity criterion gives \(D>0\).

By the definition of \(M_{\ell,\kappa,\rho}\) in \cref{obj:def:local-design-notation}, expanding the quadratic form gives
\[
v^\top M_{\ell,\kappa,\rho}v=\frac{N}{D}.
\]
The uniform coercivity in \cref{obj:lem:shifted-moment-coercivity} supplies the lower boundedness used to compare the defining infimum in \(\lambda_\star\) with the \(\rho\)-term.  The usual rescaling from arbitrary vectors to the Euclidean unit sphere then gives
\[
\lambda_\star\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2
\le
\frac{c_-}{c_+}\,
\frac{N}{D}.
\]
Indeed, for \(v=0\) this is immediate, while for \(v\ne0\) applying the unit-sphere minimum to
\(v/\{\sum_jv_j^2\}^{1/2}\) yields
\[
\inf_{\rho'\ge0}\lambda_{\min}(M_{\ell,\kappa,\rho'})
\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2
\le
v^\top M_{\ell,\kappa,\rho}v .
\]
Multiplying by the nonnegative factor \(c_-/c_+\) and using the displayed identity for \(v^\top M_{\ell,\kappa,\rho}v\) gives the bound
\(\lambda_\star\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2\le(c_-/c_+)\,v^\top M_{\ell,\kappa,\rho}v\).  Since \(c_+D>0\), multiplying by \(c_+D\) gives
\[
\lambda_\star c_+D\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2
\le c_-N .
\]
% lean: lambdaStar_refMomentMatrix_coercive

It remains to transport this inequality from \([0,1]\) to the window.  With the change of variables
\(a=\delta+hu\),
\[
\int_{\delta}^{\delta+h}a^\kappa\,da
=
h\int_0^1(\delta+hu)^\kappa\,du
=
h\,h^\kappa
\int_0^1(\rho+u)^\kappa\,du
=
h\,h^\kappa D,
\]
because \(\delta+hu=h(\rho+u)\) and \(h>0\).  The same substitution gives
\[
\int_{\delta}^{\delta+h}
\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j\left(\frac{a-\delta}{h}\right)^j\right)^2
a^\kappa\,da
=
h\,h^\kappa N .
\]
% lean: lambdaStar_scaled_power_window_coercive

Since \(h\,h^\kappa\ge0\), multiplying the unit-window inequality by this factor and using the two displayed identities gives exactly
\[
\lambda_\star c_+
\left(\int_{\delta}^{\delta+h}a^\kappa\,da\right)
\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2
\le
c_-
\int_{\delta}^{\delta+h}
\left(\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}
v_j\left(\frac{a-\delta}{h}\right)^j\right)^2
a^\kappa\,da .
\]
% lean: lambdaStar_scaled_power_window_coercive
\end{proof}
