\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:local-gram-coercivity}]
Let
\[
        T=[\delta,\delta+h],
        \qquad
        q_v(a)=\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j\left(\frac{a-\delta}{h}\right)^j,
        \qquad
        \phi_j(O)=\mathbf 1\!\left\{0\le\frac{A-\delta}{h}\le1\right\}
        \left(\frac{A-\delta}{h}\right)^j .
\]
The inequalities \(\delta\ge0\) and \(\delta+h\le1\) give \(T\subset[0,1]\).  Since \(h>0\), the condition \(0\le(A-\delta)/h\le1\) is equivalent to \(A\in T\).  Hence \cref{obj:lem:local-window-mass} gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[
 \mathbf 1\!\left\{X=x,\ 0\le\frac{A-\delta}{h}\le1\right\}\right]
 =
 p_x\int_T \pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da .
\]
The left-hand side is \(p_x^{\mathrm{loc}}(\delta,h)\).
% lean: localWindowWeight_integral_eq

For each pair \((j,k)\), \cref{obj:lem:local-gram-entry-integral} gives the corresponding density-weighted local Gram entry.  Summing those identities over \(j\) and \(k\), and expanding \(q_v(a)^2\), yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}\sum_{k=0}^{\ell}
v_jv_k\,
\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\mathbf 1\!\left\{X=x,\ 0\le \frac{A-\delta}{h}\le 1\right\}
\phi_j(O)\phi_k(O)\right]  \\
&\hspace{2em}=
p_x\int_T q_v(a)^2\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da .
\end{aligned}
\]
% lean: localWindow_populationGram_coercive

The stratum-mass condition in \cref{obj:ass:stratum-mass} and \(p_{\min}>0\) give \(p_x\ge0\).  Combining the displayed local mass identity for \(p_x^{\mathrm{loc}}(\delta,h)\) with the upper envelope in \cref{obj:ass:polynomial-thinning} gives
\[
        p_x^{\mathrm{loc}}(\delta,h)
        \le
        p_x\,c_+\int_T a^\kappa\,\mathrm da .
\]
The lower envelope in \cref{obj:ass:polynomial-thinning}, together with \(q_v(a)^2\ge0\), gives
\[
        p_x\,c_-\int_T q_v(a)^2a^\kappa\,\mathrm da
        \le
        p_x\int_T q_v(a)^2\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da .
\]
% lean: localWindow_populationGram_coercive

By \cref{obj:lem:lambda-star-positive}, the assumptions \(\kappa\ge0\), \(c_->0\), and \(c_+>0\) imply \(\lambda_\star>0\); in particular \(\lambda_\star\ge0\).
% lean: lambdaStar_pos

Applying \cref{obj:lem:scaled-window-coercivity} with this \(\delta,h\) and coefficient vector \(v\) gives
\[
 \lambda_\star c_+\!\left(\int_T a^\kappa\,\mathrm da\right)
 \sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2
 \le
 c_-\int_T q_v(a)^2a^\kappa\,\mathrm da .
\]
% lean: lambdaStar_scaled_power_window_coercive

Combining the mass upper bound with \(\lambda_\star\ge0\) and \(\sum_j v_j^2\ge0\), then applying the scaled-window coercivity bound and the lower density-envelope bound, gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\lambda_\star\,p_x^{\mathrm{loc}}(\delta,h)\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2
&\le
p_x\,\lambda_\star c_+\!\left(\int_T a^\kappa\,\mathrm da\right)
\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2 \\
&\le
p_x\,c_-\int_T q_v(a)^2a^\kappa\,\mathrm da \\
&\le
p_x\int_T q_v(a)^2\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da .
\end{aligned}
\]
Using the Gram identity above for the final term proves
\[
\lambda_\star\,p_x^{\mathrm{loc}}(\delta,h)\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}v_j^2
\le
\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}\sum_{k=0}^{\ell}
v_jv_k\,
\mathbb E_P\!\left[
\mathbf 1\!\left\{X=x,\ 0\le \frac{A-\delta}{h}\le 1\right\}
\phi_j(O)\phi_k(O)\right].
\]
% lean: localWindow_populationGram_coercive
\end{proof}
