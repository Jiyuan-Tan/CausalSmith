\begin{proof}[Proof of \cref{obj:lem:bandwidth-balance}]
Put \(H=1-\bar\delta\) and \(p=2\beta+1\).  The hypotheses give \(H>0\) and \(p>0\).  Since \(H^pH^\kappa>0\), choose \(N\) so large that
\[
        N>\frac{1}{H^pH^\kappa}.
\]
Because \(1/(H^pH^\kappa)>0\), this choice gives \(N>0\), and hence every \(n\ge N\) satisfies \(n>0\).  For \(n\ge N\), define
\[
        F_n(h)=n h^p(\delta_n+h)^\kappa,\qquad 0\le h\le H .
\]
The threshold condition gives \(\delta_n\ge0\), so
\[
        F_n(H)
        = nH^p(\delta_n+H)^\kappa
        \ge nH^pH^\kappa
        >1,
\]
because \(H\le \delta_n+H\) and \(t\mapsto t^\kappa\) is nondecreasing on \([0,\infty)\).  For continuity on \([0,H]\), use the real-power convention \(0^0=1\).  If \(\kappa=0\), then \((\delta_n+h)^\kappa=1\) on the whole interval and \(F_n(h)=n h^p\), which is continuous.  If \(\kappa\ne0\), then \(\kappa>0\), and the map \(h\mapsto(\delta_n+h)^\kappa\) is continuous on \([0,H]\) because \(\delta_n+h\ge0\); multiplying by the continuous factor \(n h^p\) gives continuity of \(F_n\).  In both cases
\[
        F_n(0)=n\,0^p(\delta_n+0)^\kappa=0,
\]
since \(p>0\).  Continuity of \(F_n\) on \([0,H]\) therefore gives a point \(\eta_n\in[0,H]\) with
\[
        F_n(\eta_n)=1.
\]
This point is strictly positive, since \(F_n(0)=0\). % lean: infoBandwidth_eventually_balance

The same monotonicity gives strict increase of \(F_n\) on \([0,H]\): if \(0\le a<b\le H\), then \(a^p<b^p\), while
\[
        (\delta_n+a)^\kappa\le(\delta_n+b)^\kappa .
\]
Moreover \(n>0\) by the preceding choice of \(N\), and \(\delta_n+b>0\), so
\[
        n(\delta_n+b)^\kappa>0 .
\]
Multiplying the strict inequality for \(a^p\) and the weak inequality for the second power by this positive factor gives \(F_n(a)<F_n(b)\).  Consequently the crossing set in \cref{obj:def:bandwidth} is exactly
\[
        \{h\in(0,H]:F_n(h)\ge1\}=[\eta_n,H].
\]
Its infimum is \(\eta_n\), so \(h_n=\eta_n\).  Thus, for every \(n\ge N\),
\[
        0<h_n,\qquad h_n\le 1-\bar\delta,\qquad
        n h_n^{2\beta+1}(\delta_n+h_n)^\kappa=1 .
\]
% lean: infoBandwidth_eventually_balance
\end{proof}
