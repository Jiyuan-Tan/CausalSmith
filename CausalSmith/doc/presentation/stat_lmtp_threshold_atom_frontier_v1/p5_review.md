# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.2/10 — The paper delivers a potentially important and rigorously verified minimax frontier, but a contradictory sharp-modulus program, stale verification metadata, understated model scope, and absent finite-sample evidence prevent publication in its current form.

The paper characterizes minimax absolute-risk and uniformly honest expected-length rates for an exact lower-threshold clamp under a finite-stratum Hölder model with polynomial design thinning, then transports the result to a carefully defined causal class and develops a continuity-only frontier. The principal rate, phase diagram, stabilized estimator, honest interval, and causal bridge constitute a coherent and potentially valuable theoretical contribution. Major revision is required because the proposed sharp-constant question is incompatible with its own component-level radius, the reproducibility record names a stale commit, and the practical behavior of the unusually elaborate procedure remains unevaluated.

## Strengths
- The central decomposition of the clamp target into a retained mean and an atom-weighted boundary regression isolates a clear statistical problem.
- The paper establishes matching risk and honest expected-length orders and provides a useful phase diagram covering moving, fixed, and zero thresholds.
- The causal contribution is appropriately formulated as an exact transport through a declared full-data class rather than as a separate unrestricted causal frontier.
- The continuity-only result gives a distinct and interpretable threshold-atom rate, elbow, and fixed-positive-threshold behavior.
- The related-work section engages degenerate-design regression and honest-inference theory at the level of rates and conditions, rather than relying solely on citation lists.
- The formal statements and proof narratives for the main delivered results are aligned with the supplied verification record.

## Findings
- **[major·statement] Discussion, limitations, and open questions** — The sharp-length question is internally incompatible with the object it proposes to calibrate. The manuscript explicitly explains that the radius is unweighted, omits retained-mean and atom-mass uncertainty, and has its natural local-regression scale h_n^β at zero threshold; nevertheless the remark requires its length and 2W_{n,B_n} to match r_n for every regime, including δ_n=0 through the q=0 case. At zero and regular/critical thresholds, the missing atom weight generally makes this component radius much larger than the complete clamp frontier, so the displayed matching limits cannot describe the defined interval. The requirement of one constant c across all phase limits is also unsupported and generally implausible when the relative regular and local contributions vary with the phase limit.
  - *Fix:* Replace the component radius by a modulus for the complete clamp functional, incorporating retained-mean variation, atom weights and their uncertainty, stratum aggregation, and singular-design handling. Then formulate phase-specific calibration constants, potentially depending on the limiting phase ratio. Alternatively, restrict the open question to the component-level regression problem and remove the coverage and full-frontier matching claims.
- **[major·prose] Proofs and verification note** — The manuscript states that the checks were run at commit 6f11a039165a75921bbb6499f3d9bf40f3f404f8, while the authoritative current verification contract identifies commit 84c32d60cb53b2f1cdef917448a45b96b08296ad. The reproduction and archive narrative therefore does not identify the source state supporting the submitted statements.
  - *Fix:* Regenerate the verification note, anchor manifest, declaration excerpts, reproduction commands, and archive metadata from one authoritative commit, and make every commit identifier in the manuscript and supplement agree with that record.
- **[major·other] Discussion, limitations, and open questions** — The paper proposes a nonstandard split-sample estimator, total-Gram stabilization rule, fallback branch, and honest interval, yet supplies only a one-cell exponent calculation. The manuscript itself identifies fallback frequency, realized coverage, and expected length as potentially sensitive to constants and moderate sample size. Without numerical evidence, readers cannot assess whether the proposed procedure is usable in the regimes for which it is advocated.
  - *Fix:* Add a systematic simulation study spanning regular, critical, atom-dominated, fixed-threshold, and near-zero paths. Report risk, coverage, length, Gram-fallback frequency, sensitivity to model constants, and comparisons with unstabilized local polynomial and relevant regular-policy procedures wherever the targets and assumptions permit a fair comparison.
- **[major·prose] abstract and Introduction** — The prose repeatedly describes the density restriction as “lower-tail” thinning or as holding “near the lower endpoint,” but the stated model imposes c_-a^κ ≤ π_x(a) ≤ c_+a^κ almost everywhere on the entire interval [0,1]. This understates a material global restriction and can lead readers to believe the frontier has been proved under a merely local boundary condition.
  - *Fix:* State prominently in the abstract and introduction that the theorem uses a global two-sided polynomial envelope on [0,1]. Explain that its local behavior drives the rates while a theorem under a merely local envelope is outside the delivered result.
- **[minor·statement] Main statistical results** — The displayed minimax theorem writes the estimator risk as an expectation E_P even though the estimator is a function of an n-observation sample and the verified criterion uses the product experiment. As written, the measure on that expectation has the wrong sample space.
  - *Fix:* Replace E_P by E_{P^{⊗n}} in the theorem and check all sample-dependent risk, coverage, and length displays for the same notation.
- **[minor·structure] Estimation and honest intervals** — The presentation claims that each object has one canonical definition but then repeats the estimator and interval as synth_4 and synth_12, references synth_4 before defining it, and says synth_4 is the version used for both risk and length even though synth_4 defines only the estimator. This obscures which displayed procedure each theorem evaluates.
  - *Fix:* Retain one canonical estimator and one canonical interval, or place concise split-indexed aliases immediately after their canonical definitions. Define every alias before its first reference and identify synth_4 with risk and synth_12 with length explicitly.
- **[minor·statement] Main statistical results** — The zero-threshold bullet says stabilized risk and length are “both asymptotic to n^{-1/2},” while the verified conclusion is order comparability (AsympSeq/\asymp), not ratio convergence with constant one. Ordinary mathematical usage makes “asymptotic to” stronger than the delivered result.
  - *Fix:* Write “both are of order n^{-1/2}” or display “both are \asymp n^{-1/2}.” Apply the same terminology consistently throughout the phase discussion.
- **[minor·prose] Related work** — The novelty boundary relative to existing LMTP threshold interventions remains somewhat blurred. The paper acknowledges prior threshold implementations but later describes “the present estimand” as having a different post-policy law without clearly separating the already available exact clamp policy/identification from the new minimax analysis of its moving atom.
  - *Fix:* State explicitly that threshold clamp policies and their causal interpretation belong to the existing modified-policy lineage, while the paper's new contribution is the minimax risk, honest-length frontier, stabilization construction, phase diagram, and continuity-only threshold-atom characterization under the declared model.
- **[minor·prose] Introduction** — The sentences saying split-indexed aliases “add no new content” and that “nothing in [the verification supplement] is needed to read the main results” use contribution-by-negation outside a designated limitations discussion.
  - *Fix:* Rewrite affirmatively, for example: “The split-indexed environments specialize the canonical definitions to a fixed block sequence,” and “The verification supplement contains the supporting design, coercivity, and local-polynomial details; the main text states the results and constructions used by readers.”
- **[nit·prose] abstract** — The symbol h_n first appears in the frontier display without the required plain-language first-use gloss.
  - *Fix:* Add “where h_n is the information-balance bandwidth” immediately before or after the display.

## Questions for authors
- Will the sharp-constant program be rebuilt around a modulus for the complete clamp functional, or restricted to the boundary-regression component it currently defines?
- Which repository commit and archived snapshot should readers treat as authoritative for the submitted manuscript?
- Can the authors provide finite-sample evidence showing when the total-Gram fallback is activated and how coverage and length behave near the two transition scales?
