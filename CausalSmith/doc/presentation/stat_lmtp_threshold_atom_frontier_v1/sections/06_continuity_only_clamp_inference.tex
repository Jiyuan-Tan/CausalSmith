\section{Continuity-only clamp inference}
\label{sec:continuity-only}

\Cref{sec:setup-assumptions,sec:main-statistical-results} impose a Hölder modulus for the regression at the lower threshold. We now record the enlarged continuity-only experiment, which keeps the same density, stratum-mass, and lower-tail thinning restrictions while replacing the Hölder regression member with qualitative continuity.

\begin{definitionv}{def:continuity-model-class}[Continuity-only model class \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\)]
\(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) is the set of laws
\[
P\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X\times[0,1]\times[0,1])
\]
satisfying \cref{obj:ass:conditional-density-law,obj:ass:stratum-mass,obj:ass:polynomial-thinning} and the following continuity condition: for every \(x\in\mathcal X\), an almost-everywhere version of
\[
a\mapsto \mathbb E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=x]
\]
admits a continuous extension to \([0,\bar\delta]\). The continuity extension is required memberwise for each stratum.
\end{definitionv}

The class in \cref{obj:def:continuity-model-class} preserves the design restrictions that govern the amount of mass near the lower endpoint. Its regression condition supplies a selected point value at the clamp threshold through continuity, rather than through a quantitative Hölder modulus.

The corresponding target has the same retained-course plus threshold-atom decomposition as \(\theta_\delta(P)\) in \cref{obj:def:clamp-functional}; the atom value is read from the continuous extension in the continuity-only class.

\begin{definitionv}{def:continuity-clamp-functional}[Continuity clamp target \(\theta_{\delta,\mathrm{cont}}(P)\)]
For \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) and \(0\leq\delta\leq\bar\delta\), with \(q_x(\delta)\) as in \cref{obj:def:clamp-functional} and \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) as in \cref{obj:def:continuity-model-class}, define
\[
 \theta_{\delta,\mathrm{cont}}(P)
 =
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}\right]
 +
 \sum_{x\in\mathcal X}
 p_xq_x(\delta)\mu_{x,\mathrm{cont}}^P(\delta).
\]
\end{definitionv}

The continuity-only scale is determined by the ordinary sampling fluctuation and the amount of mass moved by the lower-threshold clamp.

\begin{definitionv}{def:continuity-frontier}[Continuity frontier \(s_n\)]
Define the continuity-only frontier rate by
\[
s_n=n^{-1/2}+\delta_n^{\kappa+1}.
\]
\end{definitionv}

The estimator and interval make the enlarged model explicit. They use the retained-course mean above the threshold and a fixed midpoint value for the lower-tail atom, with a bounded-sum allowance for the empirical lower-tail mass.

\begin{algorithmv}{def:continuity-fallback-estimator}[Continuity fallback estimator \(\widehat\theta_{n,\mathrm{cont}}\)]
The inputs are the deterministic split blocks \(I_0,I_1\), the threshold \(\delta_n\), and the observations \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\).
\begin{enumerate}
\item Define the retained-course empirical mean
\[
\widehat\nu_n
=
|I_0|^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_0}Y_i\mathbf 1\{A_i>\delta_n\}.
\]
\item For each \(x\in\mathcal X\), define the empirical lower-threshold atom mass
\[
\widehat b_x
=
|I_1|^{-1}\sum_{i\in I_1}\mathbf 1\{X_i=x,A_i\leq\delta_n\}.
\]
\item Output the fixed-regression-fallback estimator
\[
\widehat\theta_{n,\mathrm{cont}}
=
\Pi_{[0,1]}\left\{\widehat\nu_n+\frac12\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}\widehat b_x\right\}.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{algorithmv}

\begin{algorithmv}{def:continuity-honest-interval}[Continuity honest interval \(\mathrm{CI}_{n,\mathrm{cont}}\)]
The inputs are the split blocks \(I_0,I_1\), the level \(\alpha\), the estimator \(\widehat\theta_{n,\mathrm{cont}}\), and the empirical masses \(\widehat b_x\).
\begin{enumerate}
\item For \(j=0,1\), define the split-specific Hoeffding radius
\[
t_{j,\alpha}
=
\sqrt{\frac{\log(4/\alpha)}{2|I_j|}}.
\]
\item Define the empirical total lower-threshold atom mass
\[
\widehat B_n
=
\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}\widehat b_x.
\]
\item Output the Hoeffding interval
\[
\mathrm{CI}_{n,\mathrm{cont}}
=
\left[
\widehat\theta_{n,\mathrm{cont}}-t_{0,\alpha}-t_{1,\alpha}-\frac12\widehat B_n,\,
\widehat\theta_{n,\mathrm{cont}}+t_{0,\alpha}+t_{1,\alpha}+\frac12\widehat B_n
\right]\cap[0,1].
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{algorithmv}

The concentration step for \cref{obj:def:continuity-honest-interval} uses the standard bounded-sum inequality of \citet[Theorem~2]{Hoeffding1963}, applied separately to the retained-course and atom-mass split statistics.

The causal version of the continuity-only analysis uses the same structural ingredients as \cref{obj:def:full-data-model-class}, with the observed margin placed in \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\).

\begin{definitionv}{def:continuity-full-data-model-class}[Continuity full-data class \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\)]
Define \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\) as the collection of full-data law packages \(P^{\mathrm F}\) with a memberwise standard Borel latent-response space \(\mathcal U\), latent variable \(U\), jointly Borel stratum-specific response maps \(g_x\), and jointly measurable potential-outcome process \(\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\}\), such that:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Observed margin.)} The observed \((X,A,Y)\)-margin of \(P^{\mathrm F}\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) in \cref{obj:def:continuity-model-class}.
\item \textbf{(Consistency.)} The package satisfies \cref{obj:ass:consistency}.
\item \textbf{(Exchangeability.)} The package satisfies \cref{obj:ass:exchangeability}.
\item \textbf{(Response continuity.)} The package satisfies \cref{obj:ass:full-data-response-continuity}.
\end{itemize}
The carrier spaces \(\mathcal U\) may vary with \(P^{\mathrm F}\).
\end{definitionv}

For later comparison, the next definition fixes the observed margin of a full-data law as the pushforward onto the observed coordinates.

\begin{definitionv}{def:continuity-frontier-criteria}[Continuity frontier criteria \(R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star\) and \(L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star\)]
For estimators and intervals measurable with respect to \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\), define the continuity-only observed minimax absolute-error risk by
\[
R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star
=
\inf_{\widehat\theta}\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}}
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}
\left|\widehat\theta-\theta_{\delta_n,\mathrm{cont}}(P)\right|.
\]
Define the continuity-only observed minimax honest expected length by
\[
L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star
=
\inf_{C_n:\,\inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}}P^{\otimes n}
\{\theta_{\delta_n,\mathrm{cont}}(P)\in C_n\}\geq1-\alpha}
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}}
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}\!\left[\operatorname{len}(C_n)\right].
\]
For the full-data continuity-only class, define the causal minimax absolute-error risk by
\[
R_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star
=
\inf_{\widehat\psi}\sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}}
\mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}
\left|\widehat\psi-\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})\right|.
\]
Define the continuity-only causal minimax honest expected length by
\[
L_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star
=
\inf_{C_n:\,\inf_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}}
(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}\{\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})\in C_n\}\geq1-\alpha}
\sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}}
\mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}\!\left[\operatorname{len}(C_n)\right].
\]
\end{definitionv}

The decision-theoretic criteria are recorded for both the observed continuity-only class and its full-data counterpart.

\begin{propositionv}{prop:continuity-causal-bridge}[Continuity causal bridge]
Let \(J\in\mathbb N\), let \(\kappa,c_-,c_+,p_{\min},\bar\delta\in\mathbb R\), and let \(P^{\mathrm F}\) be a full-data law package with observed margin \(P\).  Suppose that
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Full-data continuity model.)} \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\) with constants \((\kappa,c_-,c_+,p_{\min},\bar\delta)\), as in \cref{obj:def:continuity-full-data-model-class}.
\item \textbf{(Design constants.)} \(J>0\), \(\kappa\geq0\), \(c_->0\), \(c_-\leq \kappa+1\leq c_+\), \(0<p_{\min}\leq 1/J\), and \(0<\bar\delta<1\).
\end{itemize}
For each \(x\in\mathcal X\) and \(a\in\mathbb R\), define the full-data response mean by
\[
m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)
=
P^{\mathrm F}(X=x)^{-1}
\int_{\{X=x\}} g_x(a,U)\,dP^{\mathrm F}.
\]
Then, for every \(x\in\mathcal X\) and every \(a\in[0,\bar\delta]\),
\[
m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mu_{x,\mathrm{cont}}^P(a).
\]
Moreover, if
\[
\psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F})
=
\int Y\!\left(d_\delta(A)\right)\,dP^{\mathrm F},
\]
then, for every \(\delta\in[0,\bar\delta]\),
\[
\psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F})
=
\theta_{\delta,\mathrm{cont}}(P),
\]
where \(\theta_{\delta,\mathrm{cont}}(P)\) is the continuity-only clamp functional in \cref{obj:def:continuity-clamp-functional}.
\end{propositionv}

The first causal statement aligns the full-data response mean with the observed continuous extension and therefore identifies the causal clamp mean with the continuity-only observed target.

\begin{propositionv}{prop:continuity-observed-margin-surjectivity}[Observed-margin surjectivity]*
Let \(J\in\mathbb N\) and let \(\kappa,c_-,c_+,p_{\min},\bar\delta,\alpha\in\mathbb R\). Suppose that the continuity-regime design constants used in \cref{obj:def:continuity-model-class,obj:def:continuity-full-data-model-class,obj:def:continuity-frontier-criteria} hold for \((J,\kappa,c_-,c_+,p_{\min},\bar\delta)\), and that \(0<\alpha<1/2\).

Then the observed-margin map from \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\) onto \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) is surjective: for every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\), there exists \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\) whose observed margin is \(P\). Moreover, for every \(n\in\mathbb N\) and every \(\delta\in[0,\bar\delta]\), the four continuity-frontier criteria of \cref{obj:def:continuity-frontier-criteria}, evaluated at \((J,n,\kappa,c_-,c_+,p_{\min},\bar\delta,\delta,\alpha)\), satisfy
\[
R_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star
=
R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star,
\qquad
L_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star
=
L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star .
\]
\end{propositionv}

\Cref{obj:prop:continuity-causal-bridge} shows that qualitative continuity turns almost-everywhere regression information into the pointwise threshold value needed after the clamp collapses the lower tail. Under the stated full-data continuity conditions, the structural response mean agrees with the observed continuous extension throughout the threshold envelope, and the causal clamp mean equals the observed continuity clamp target.

The next statement records that the continuity-only full-data class covers the observed continuity-only class exactly at the margin. Its construction uses regular conditional distributions on standard Borel spaces, as supplied by \citet[Theorem~6.3]{Kallenberg2002Foundations}.

\begin{definitionv}{synth_13}[Observed margin of a full-data law]
For a full-data law package $P^{\mathrm F}$ governing $(X,A,Y,U,\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\})$, define $P^{\mathrm F}_{\mathrm{obs}}$ to be the observed-data marginal law of $(X,A,Y)$. Equivalently, if $\pi_{\mathrm{obs}}(X,A,Y,U,\{Y(a)\})=(X,A,Y)$ is the coordinate projection onto $\mathcal X\times[0,1]\times[0,1]$, then $P^{\mathrm F}_{\mathrm{obs}}=(\pi_{\mathrm{obs}})_{\#}P^{\mathrm F}$.
\end{definitionv}

\Cref{obj:prop:continuity-observed-margin-surjectivity} puts the observed and causal continuity-only decision problems on the same footing. Every observed continuity-only law has a full-data representative satisfying the causal restrictions, and the minimax risk and honest expected-length criteria agree exactly for each sample size and threshold in the stated range.

The main continuity-only conclusion now combines the bridge identity, observed-margin surjectivity, and the fallback estimator and interval.

\begin{theoremv}{thm:continuity-only-frontier}[Continuity frontier characterization]*
Fix \(J\in\mathbb N\) and constants \(\kappa,c_-,c_+,p_{\min},\bar\delta,\alpha\in\mathbb R\). Suppose
\[
0<J,
\qquad
0\leq \kappa,
\qquad
0<c_-,
\qquad
c_-\leq \kappa+1\leq c_+,
\qquad
0<p_{\min}\leq \frac{1}{J},
\qquad
0<\bar\delta<1,
\qquad
0<\alpha<\frac12.
\]
Then there exist constants \(c,C\in\mathbb R\) with \(0<c<C\) such that the following statements hold.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{(Uniform frontier bounds.)} For every deterministic threshold sequence \((\delta_n)_{n\geq1}\) with \(\delta_n\in[0,\bar\delta]\) for all \(n\), and for every deterministic sequence \(B_{\bullet}=(B_n)_{n\geq1}\) of admissible three-way split blocks, all sufficiently large \(n\) satisfy the following bounds. With
\[
s_n=n^{-1/2}+\delta_n^{\kappa+1},
\]
and with \(R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star\), \(L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star\), \(R_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star\), and \(L_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star\) as in \cref{obj:def:continuity-frontier-criteria},
\[
c s_n\leq R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star
\leq
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}}
\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}
\left|\widehat\theta_{n,\mathrm{cont}}-\theta_{\delta_n,\mathrm{cont}}(P)\right|
\leq C s_n,
\]
the interval \(\mathrm{CI}_{n,\mathrm{cont}}\) has uniform coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) over \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\), its worst-case expected length is at most \(C s_n\), and
\[
c s_n\leq L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star.
\]
Moreover,
\[
R_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star=R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star,
\qquad
L_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star=L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star,
\]
and the same estimator and interval satisfy
\[
R_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star
\leq
\sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}}
\mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F}_{\mathrm{obs}})^{\otimes n}}
\left|\widehat\theta_{n,\mathrm{cont}}-\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})\right|
\leq C s_n,
\]
with causal coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\) and causal worst-case expected length at most \(C s_n\).

\item \textbf{(Elbow and null-threshold rates.)} For the continuity-only rate in \cref{obj:def:continuity-frontier},
\[
s_n\bigl(\delta_n=n^{-1/(2(\kappa+1))}\bigr)
\asymp n^{-1/2},
\qquad
s_n(0)\asymp n^{-1/2}.
\]

\item \textbf{(Fixed positive thresholds.)} For every deterministic threshold sequence \((\delta_n)_{n\geq1}\) with \(\delta_n\in[0,\bar\delta]\) for all \(n\), every \(\delta_0\in(0,\bar\delta]\), and \(\delta_n\to\delta_0\),
\[
R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star \not\to 0,
\qquad
L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star \not\to 0.
\]
\end{itemize}
\end{theoremv}

\Cref{obj:thm:continuity-only-frontier} characterizes the continuity-only rate for both observed and causal formulations. The fallback estimator and Hoeffding interval attain the rate \(s_n\), while the minimax risk and honest expected length are bounded below at the same order. The honest-length comparison follows the nonparametric confidence-interval lower-bound phenomenon of \citet{Low1997NonparametricConfidenceIntervals}, specialized here to the threshold-atom decision problem.

The rate has a simple interpretation. When \(\delta_n\) is at the elbow \(n^{-1/[2(\kappa+1)]}\), the lower-tail mass term \(\delta_n^{\kappa+1}\) matches \(n^{-1/2}\), so the continuity-only problem has a root-\(n\) scale. At \(\delta_n=0\), the atom term vanishes and the bounded retained-course mean again gives the root-\(n\) scale. For thresholds converging to a fixed positive value, the theorem records persistent minimax risk and honest length, reflecting the positive amount of treatment mass assigned a threshold regression value governed by qualitative continuity.
