# Referee review

**Recommendation:** major_revision
**Overall score:** 6.2/10 — The paper establishes a potentially valuable and formally verified minimax frontier, but substantial revisions are needed to clarify the theorem scope, identify the implemented tuning choices, document reproducibility, streamline the presentation, and establish novelty against the closest literature.

The paper characterizes minimax absolute risk and uniformly honest expected length for an exact lower-threshold clamp under a finite-stratum Hölder model with a global polynomial density envelope, and it derives corresponding phase behavior, causal transport, and a continuity-only frontier. The matched estimation and inference rates, singular-design stabilization, and exact observed-margin lift form a coherent theoretical contribution. Publication consideration requires a substantially leaner manuscript and sharper alignment between its formal scope, prose claims, tuning assumptions, verification disclosures, and related-work positioning.

## Strengths
- The verified results deliver matching minimax absolute-risk and honest expected-length rates for a policy-induced atom coupled to a thinning continuous-treatment design.
- The total-Gram construction is defined on every realized sample and explicitly addresses empty or singular local designs.
- The paper distinguishes the bandwidth edge scale from the overall risk transition and includes a useful one-stratum calibration.
- The observed-margin surjectivity results give an exact decision-theoretic transport to the carefully declared full-data classes.
- The continuity-only theorem identifies an explicit atom-size frontier, its elbow, and the fixed-positive-threshold behavior.

## Findings
- **[major·citation] Related work** — The dedicated literature section covers the broad LMTP, dose-response, degenerate-design, and honest-inference lineages, but its treatment of the closest recent competitors is too thin to support the novelty sentence, "The distinction here is the exact combination of a policy-induced atom, a moving threshold, polynomially thinning treatment support, random local Gram stabilization, and a causal observed-margin lift." In particular, the section needs direct comparison with recent natural-value-policy, positivity/extrapolation, threshold-response, and continuous-dose frontier results, including what targets, density conditions, rates, and inference objects they establish.
  - *Fix:* Expand the early related-work section with substantive comparisons to the closest recent work, including the natural-history LMTP extensions, Koo et al.'s in-support/Lipschitz extrapolation decomposition, conditional and functional MTPs, threshold-response curves and bands, and recent continuous-dose minimax results. State each competitor's positive result and conditions, then locate the exact deterministic clamp frontier within that target-and-assumption map. Present the causal result specifically as an exact transport on the paper-defined full-data class.
- **[major·structure] global** — The paper repeatedly presents the same mathematical objects: the model classes, clamp target, estimator, and interval each receive both synthesized and operative definitions, while theorem statements contain detailed witness constructions that reappear in long proofs. This duplication makes the statistical argument difficult to extract from the formal-verification record and is below the presentation standard of a leading journal.
  - *Fix:* Retain one reader-facing definition for each object, place split-indexed notation immediately after the generic construction without restating the full algorithm, and move formal encodings, exhaustive regime bookkeeping, elementary auxiliary lemmas, and verification-oriented proof detail to a clearly identified supplement. Keep concise theorem statements and proof roadmaps in the main paper while preserving every condition and dependency.
- **[major·statement] Setup and assumptions / Main statistical results** — The setup treats the local-polynomial degree ell as a free smoothness parameter, but the verified procedures and later causal theorem use ell equal to ceil(beta) minus one. The main minimax-risk and honest-length statements do not identify that choice when naming the stabilized estimator. The procedure also depends on beta, kappa, L, c-minus, and c-plus, while the limitations paragraph mentions only a known smoothness radius and thinning exponent.
  - *Fix:* Define ell equal to ceil(beta) minus one before the Hölder condition and use that convention consistently in the model, estimator, interval, and all main theorems. Add a compact inputs paragraph distinguishing model constants required to construct the procedure from constants used only in analysis; explicitly state the knowledge assumptions for beta, kappa, L, c-minus, c-plus, and delta-bar. Update the limitations paragraph accordingly.
- **[major·other] Proofs and verification note** — The author footnote promises that the verification appendix records the "exact scope, toolchain, and commit," whereas the appendix currently supplies only a generic Lean 4 statement. The sentence claiming that Lean "machine-checks each delivered displayed theorem, proposition, lemma, definition, algorithm, and remark" also blurs proof checking, definition typechecking, and the unresolved sharp-constant proposition encoded as a question.
  - *Fix:* Record the exact artifact commit, Lean and dependency versions, build command, artifact location, and a mapping from manuscript labels to declarations. State separately that theorem, proposition, and lemma proofs are checked; definitions and algorithms are formal encodings; and the sharp-constant remark is a typechecked formulation of an unresolved proposition. Retain the theorem-local Kallenberg and Hoeffding dependency disclosures.
- **[minor·prose] abstract / Introduction / Main statistical results** — The prose says that the phase diagram "separates the regular, away-from-boundary, and boundary-layer regimes" and that the theorem identifies those three regimes, but the main theorem is organized by regular, critical, atom-dominated, fixed, and zero thresholds; the near-edge and far-edge bandwidth comparisons appear separately in an appendix lemma. Readers therefore cannot directly match the advertised regime names to delta-edge, delta-crit, h-n, and r-n.
  - *Fix:* Add a main-text table or compact corollary with one row for each design and risk regime, stating the condition on delta-n relative to delta-edge and delta-crit and the resulting orders of h-n and r-n. Alternatively, revise the abstract and introduction to use exactly the categories stated in the theorem.
- **[minor·structure] title** — The plural title "Minimax inference for threshold modified treatment policies" suggests a family of threshold policies, while the results characterize one exact deterministic lower-threshold clamp.
  - *Fix:* Narrow the title to the lower-threshold clamp, for example, "Minimax inference for a lower-threshold modified treatment policy with continuous treatments," unless additional policy classes are formally developed.
- **[minor·prose] abstract / Introduction / Setup and assumptions** — The manuscript repeatedly describes polynomial behavior "near zero" or "near the lower endpoint," while the verified class imposes the two-sided envelope c-minus times a^kappa through c-plus times a^kappa almost everywhere on the entire interval [0,1]. The current language can lead readers to interpret the theorem as requiring only a local regular-variation condition.
  - *Fix:* State prominently that the delivered theorem uses a global two-sided power envelope on [0,1], while explaining that its rate mechanism is driven by the lower endpoint. Reserve a genuinely local-envelope formulation for a separately proved extension.
- **[minor·prose] abstract / Introduction** — Several sentences violate the affirmative contribution-framing rule, including "Under continuity alone, without a quantitative Hölder modulus," "This interaction is absent from regular root-n analyses," and "Thus honest coverage does not alter the rate."
  - *Fix:* Use affirmative formulations: "Under qualitative continuity, the frontier is ..."; "The clamp couples a moving atom weight to moving boundary information"; and "Point estimation and uniformly honest expected length share the rate r-n."
- **[minor·statement] Main statistical results** — The minimax-risk theorem writes the sample risk as an expectation under P even though the estimator is a function of n observations and every surrounding definition and verified criterion uses the product law P^{tensor n}. As printed, the display is measure-theoretically ill-typed.
  - *Fix:* Replace that expectation by the expectation under P^{tensor n} and audit all observed and full-data risk, coverage, and length displays for consistent product-law notation.
- **[minor·prose] Main statistical results** — The zero-threshold guarantee says the stabilized risks and lengths are "asymptotic to" n^{-1/2}, while the verified conclusion is two-sided order equivalence (asymp), not a ratio-one limit.
  - *Fix:* Use "are of order n^{-1/2}" or display the asymp symbol consistently; reserve "asymptotic to" for an explicitly defined usage or a ratio-one result.

## Questions for authors
- Which of beta, kappa, L, c-minus, c-plus, and delta-bar are intended to be known inputs to the proposed estimator and interval, and what operational interpretation should readers assign to that oracle specification?
- Will the Lean artifact, exact commit, dependency lockfile, and reproducible build instructions be publicly available with the submission?
- Is the intended publication claim confined to the exact lower-threshold clamp under the global density envelope, or do the authors intend to add formal results for a broader threshold-policy or locally constrained density class?
