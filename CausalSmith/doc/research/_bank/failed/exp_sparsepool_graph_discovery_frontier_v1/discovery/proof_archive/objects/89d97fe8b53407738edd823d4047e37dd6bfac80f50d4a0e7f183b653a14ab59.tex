The proved results give the explicit upper bound
\[
 m^*\le Cq^{-1}\left\{(2s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{en}{2s+1}\right)
 +\frac{\sigma^2}{\delta^2}(s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{4n^2}{\eta}\right)
 +\log\!\left(\frac4\eta\right)\right\},
\]
obtained by applying the simultaneous-certificate theorem with its noise error and pool-construction error each set to \(\eta/2\).  Every recovery-valid adaptive design also satisfies
\[
 m^*\ge c\frac{\sigma^2\{\log(n-1)+\log(1/\eta)\}}{q\delta^2}.
\]
For probability-one noiseless identification on the degree-one subclass, the separate combinatorial lower bound is
\[
 m\ge\max\{\lceil\log_2(n+2)\rceil,\lceil1/q\rceil\}.
\]
Thus the present proof isolates, but does not close, the factor \(s+1\) between the noisy polynomial-time certificate upper bound and the adaptive information lower bound, as well as the remaining sparse-pool combinatorial gap in noiseless regimes.
