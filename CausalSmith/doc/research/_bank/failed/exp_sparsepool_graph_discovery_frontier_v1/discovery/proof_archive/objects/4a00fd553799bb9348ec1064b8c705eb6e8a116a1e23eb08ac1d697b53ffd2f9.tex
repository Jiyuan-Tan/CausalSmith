For the Dantzig upper bound, apply the simultaneous-certificate theorem with its rowwise noise error parameter equal to \(\eta/2\) and its pool-construction failure parameter equal to \(\eta/2\).  The two logarithms then become \(\log(4n^2/\eta)\) and \(\log(4/\eta)\), and the theorem gives graph error at most \(\eta\) whenever \(m\ge U_{\mathrm D}\), after changing the universal constant.

We verify that a mathematical certified output exists, without asserting an algorithmic running time.  For fixed data and row \(i\), the feasible set
\[
 Q_i=\{u\in\mathcal D_i:\lVert u\rVert_1\le v_i\}
\]
is a nonempty compact polyhedron: the Dantzig constraint is the intersection of \(2n\) affine half-spaces, and \(\lVert u\rVert_1\le v_i\) is the intersection of the finitely many half-spaces \(r^{\mathsf T}u\le v_i\), \(r\in\{-1,1\}^n\).  Write it as \(Q_i=\{u:Cu\le b\}\), and let \(u_* =\theta_i^{\mathrm D}\).  Let \(K\) be the cone generated by the normals of the constraints active at \(u_*\).  We claim that \(-u_*\in K\).  Otherwise, project \(-u_*\) onto the closed finitely generated cone \(K\), and let \(d\) be the nonzero residual.  The projection inequalities and the conic property give \(d^{\mathsf T}k\le0\) for every \(k\in K\), while \(d^{\mathsf T}(-u_*)=\lVert d\rVert_2^2>0\).  Hence every active constraint remains feasible in direction \(d\), every inactive constraint remains feasible for sufficiently small positive steps, and
\[
 \frac12\lVert u_*+td\rVert_2^2-\frac12\lVert u_*\rVert_2^2
 =t u_*^{\mathsf T}d+\frac{t^2}{2}\lVert d\rVert_2^2<0
\]
for sufficiently small \(t>0\), a contradiction.  Thus there is \(\lambda\ge0\), supported on the active constraints, such that \(u_*+C^{\mathsf T}\lambda=0\).  The Lagrange dual value at \(\lambda\) is
\[
 \inf_u\left\{\frac12\lVert u\rVert_2^2+\lambda^{\mathsf T}(Cu-b)\right\}
 =-\frac12\lVert C^{\mathsf T}\lambda\rVert_2^2-\lambda^{\mathsf T}b
 =\frac12\lVert u_*\rVert_2^2,
\]
where the final equality uses complementary slackness and \(C^{\mathsf T}\lambda=-u_*\).  Hence \(u_*\), together with these multipliers, is a certified output with zero primal--dual gap.

The exact solution is measurable in the data.  Indeed, conditional on any of the finitely many possible \(Z\), both stages of the decoder have finitely many active and sign patterns.  On each pattern their solutions and multipliers satisfy fixed linear systems and finitely many polynomial inequalities in \(H_mY_i\); minimum Euclidean norm and strict convexity make the selected primal solution unique.  The resulting finite partition is Borel, so the selected solution and its thresholded graph are measurable.  This proves an admissible Dantzig witness and therefore
\[
 m^*\le\lceil U_{\mathrm D}\rceil.
 \tag{64}
\]
No dimension-only real-arithmetic or bit-complexity guarantee is asserted.

The partially penalized-Lasso lemma supplies a second admissible decoder with error at most \(\eta\) whenever \(m\ge U_{\mathrm L}\).  Hence
\[
 m^*\le\left\lceil\min\{U_{\mathrm D},U_{\mathrm L}\}\right\rceil.
 \tag{65}
\]
In particular, the Lasso witness has no factor \(s+1\) multiplying \(\sigma^2/\delta^2\), although its separate empirical strict-dual-feasibility term is of order \((s+1)q^{-1}\log(8n^3/\eta)\).

The adaptive information lower-bound theorem applies to every recovery-valid admissible pair on its Gaussian subclass, so the definition of \(m^*\) gives
\[
 m^*\ge c\,\frac{\sigma^2\{\log(n-1)+\log(1/\eta)\}}{q\delta^2}.
 \tag{66}
\]
Its noiseless degree-one conclusion gives the separately stated probability-one obstruction, and \(\mathrm{prop{:}fixed\text{-}design\text{-}rank\text{-}obstruction}\) gives \(m\ge2s+2\) for every fixed design with \(\kappa_s(Z)>0\).  Equations (64)--(66) prove every displayed inequality in the theorem.  None of these arguments supplies an adaptive recovery lower bound matching the upper sparse-exploration terms in every regime, and neither upper witness supplies a certified polynomial-time finite-precision implementation.  The final sentence of the theorem therefore records a genuine unresolved gap rather than claiming an all-regime sharp order.
