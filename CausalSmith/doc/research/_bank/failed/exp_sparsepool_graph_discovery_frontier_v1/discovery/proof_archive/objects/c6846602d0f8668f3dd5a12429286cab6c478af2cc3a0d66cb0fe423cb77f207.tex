The already-established theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}sharp\mbox{-}pool\mbox{-}complexity\mbox{-}bracket}\) gives the complete currently justified partial result.  With
\[
a=\frac{\sigma^2}{\delta^2},
\]
it implies
\[
cq^{-1}a\{\log(n-1)+\log(1/\eta)\}
\le m^*(n,B,s,\delta,\sigma,\eta)
\le \left\lceil\min\{U_{\mathrm D},U_{\mathrm L}\}\right\rceil,
\]
where
\[
U_{\mathrm D}=Cq^{-1}\left\{(2s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{en}{2s+1}\right)
+a(s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{4n^2}{\eta}\right)
+\log\!\left(\frac4\eta\right)\right\}
\]
and
\[
U_{\mathrm L}=Cq^{-1}\left\{(s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{8n^3}{\eta}\right)
+a\log\!\left(\frac{8n^2}{\eta}\right)\right\}.
\]
Because \(n\ge8\) and \(0<\eta\le1/4\), the logarithms in the lower bound and in the noise term of \(U_{\mathrm L}\) are comparable up to universal constants.  Hence, whenever \(a\ge s+1\), the lower bound and the Lasso upper bound match up to universal constants, and
\[
m^*(n,B,s,\delta,\sigma,\eta)
\asymp q^{-1}\frac{\sigma^2}{\delta^2}
\{\log n+\log(1/\eta)\}.
\]
For the early-kill specialization \(s=1\) and \(\sigma=0\), the bracket supplies the upper bound
\[
m^*(n,B,1,\delta,0,\eta)
\le
\left\lceil Cq^{-1}\left\{3\log\!\left(\frac{en}{3}\right)
+\log\!\left(\frac4\eta\right)\right\}\right\rceil.
\]
Separately, probability-one recovery on the noiseless degree-one subclass requires
\[
m\ge
\max\!\left\{\left\lceil\log_2(n+2)\right\rceil,
\left\lceil\frac1q\right\rceil\right\},
\]
and every fixed design with positive structured sparse singular value satisfies \(m\ge4\) when \(s=1\).  Thus the noisy regime \(\sigma^2/\delta^2\gtrsim s+1\) is settled, while the low-noise sparse-exploration and certified finite-precision computational regimes remain open.
