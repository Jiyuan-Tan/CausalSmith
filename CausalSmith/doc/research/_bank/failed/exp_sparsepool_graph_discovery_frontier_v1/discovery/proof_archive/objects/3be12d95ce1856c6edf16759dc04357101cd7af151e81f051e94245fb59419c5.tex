Apply \(\mathrm{lem{:}balanced\text{-}pool\text{-}uniform\text{-}concentration}\).  A vector admitted by the definition of \(\kappa_s(Z)\) has at most \(2s+1\) nonzero coordinates, so the first conclusion of that lemma gives
\[
 \kappa_s(Z)^2\ge c_0q.
\]
When \(n\ge9\), every vector admitted by the definition of \(\phi_s(Z)\) satisfies the factor-three cone condition in the same lemma, and hence
\[
 \phi_s(Z)^2\ge c_0q.
\]
Both inequalities hold on one event of probability at least \(1-\rho\).  Renaming the universal constants proves the probability assertion.  By the definition of \(\Pi_{m,B}\), a row is generated by a uniform random permutation of \(\mathcal U\) followed by retaining its first \(B\) entries.  The usual in-place shuffle uses \(O(n)\) randomized elementary operations, so all \(m\) rows are generated in \(O(mn)\) operations.
