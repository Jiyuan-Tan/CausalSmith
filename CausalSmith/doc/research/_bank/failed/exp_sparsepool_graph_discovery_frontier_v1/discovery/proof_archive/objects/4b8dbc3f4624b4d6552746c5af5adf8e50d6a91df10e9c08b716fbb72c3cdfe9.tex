The proved partial result is
\[
 c\,\frac{\sigma^2\{\log(n-1)+\log(1/\eta)\}}{q\delta^2}
 \le m^*(n,B,s,\delta,\sigma,\eta)
 \le\left\lceil\min\{U_{\mathrm D},U_{\mathrm L}\}\right\rceil.
\]
The Dantzig witness has sparse excitation of order \(q^{-1}(2s+1)\log(en/(2s+1))\) and a noise term with factor \(s+1\); the exact partially penalized-Lasso witness removes that factor from the noise term but retains order \(q^{-1}(s+1)\log(8n^3/\eta)\) for empirical strict dual feasibility.  The adaptive Gaussian converse proves only the displayed \(q^{-1}\sigma^2\delta^{-2}\) information term.  The separate noiseless degree-one and fixed-design rank obstructions are also proved, and no dimension-only finite-precision complexity guarantee is proved for either optimizer.
