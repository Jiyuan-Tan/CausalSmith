There are universal constants \(c_0,C_0>0\) with the following property.  Let \(Z\sim\Pi_{m,B}\).  If
\[
 m\ge C_0q^{-1}\left\{(2s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{en}{2s+1}\right)+\log\!\left(\frac2\rho\right)\right\},
\]
then, with probability at least \(1-\rho\), all three conclusions below hold:
\[
 \frac{\lVert Xv\rVert_2^2}{m}\ge c_0q\lVert v\rVert_2^2
 \quad\text{whenever}\quad
 \lVert v\rVert_0\le2s+1,
\]
\[
 \frac{\lVert Xv\rVert_2^2}{m}\ge c_0q\lVert v\rVert_2^2
 \quad\text{whenever there is }S\subseteq\mathcal U, |S|\le s+1,
 \text{ with }\lVert v_{S^c}\rVert_1\le\lVert v_S\rVert_1,
\]
and
\[
 \max_{j\in\mathcal U}\lVert Xe_j\rVert_2^2\le2mq.
\]
If \(n\ge9\), the second conclusion also holds, after decreasing \(c_0\) universally, under the larger cone condition \(\lVert v_{S^c}\rVert_1\le3\lVert v_S\rVert_1\).
