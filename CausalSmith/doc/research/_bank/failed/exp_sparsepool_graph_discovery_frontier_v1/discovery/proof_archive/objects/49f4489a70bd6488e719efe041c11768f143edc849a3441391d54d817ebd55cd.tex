Write \(z=Z_{t\cdot}\).  By uniformity on \(\mathcal Z_B\), permutation symmetry gives
\[
 \mathbb E z_j=p,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(z_j)=p(1-p),
 \qquad j\in\mathcal U.
\]
For distinct \(j,k\), the fixed-cardinality constraint and direct counting give
\[
 \mathbb E(z_jz_k)
 =\frac{B(B-1)}{n(n-1)},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Cov}(z_j,z_k)
 =\frac{B(B-1)}{n(n-1)}-\frac{B^2}{n^2}
 =-\frac{p(1-p)}{n-1}.
\]
Thus the diagonal and off-diagonal entries of the covariance matrix are respectively \(p(1-p)\) and \(-p(1-p)/(n-1)\), whence
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(z)
 =p(1-p)\frac{n}{n-1}
   \left(I_n-\frac1n\mathbf 1_n\mathbf 1_n^{\mathsf T}\right).
\]
Now let \(K=\{j:v_j\ne0\}\), so that \(|K|\le k<n\).  Cauchy--Schwarz on \(K\) yields
\[
 (\mathbf 1_n^{\mathsf T}v)^2
 =\left(\sum_{j\in K}v_j\right)^2
 \le |K|\sum_{j\in K}v_j^2
 \le k\lVert v\rVert_2^2.
\]
Substitution into the covariance formula therefore gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 v^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{Cov}(z)v
 &=p(1-p)\frac{n}{n-1}
   \left(\lVert v\rVert_2^2-\frac{(\mathbf 1_n^{\mathsf T}v)^2}{n}\right)\\
 &\ge p(1-p)\frac{n-k}{n-1}\lVert v\rVert_2^2,
 \end{aligned}
\]
which proves both assertions.
