Apply the balanced-pool uniform concentration lemma.  A vector admitted in the definition of \(\kappa_s(Z)\) has at most \(2s+1\) nonzero coordinates, so its first conclusion gives
\[
 \kappa_s(Z)^2\ge c_0q.
\]
Under \(n\ge9\), every vector admitted in the definition of \(\phi_s(Z)\) satisfies the factor-three cone condition in the lemma, and hence
\[
 \phi_s(Z)^2\ge c_0q.
\]
Both inequalities hold on the same event of probability at least \(1-\rho\); renaming the universal constants proves the probability assertion.  By the definition of \(\Pi_{m,B}\), one row is generated by a uniform random permutation of \(\mathcal U\) followed by retaining its first \(B\) entries.  A standard shuffle uses \(O(n)\) elementary randomized operations, so all \(m\) rows are generated in \(O(mn)\) operations.
