Let \(T_i=\{i\}\cup S_i\).  Directed row sparsity gives \(|T_i|\le s+1\), and additive interference gives
\[
 H_mY_i=X\theta_i+H_m\varepsilon_i,
 \qquad \varepsilon_i=(\varepsilon_{1,i},\ldots,\varepsilon_{m,i})^{\mathsf T}.
 \tag{20}
\]
Apply the balanced-pool concentration lemma with failure probability \(\rho\).  On its event \(E_Z\), simultaneously for every \(i\),
\[
 \frac{\lVert Xv\rVert_2^2}{m}\ge c_0q\lVert v\rVert_2^2
 \quad\text{if}\quad
 \lVert v_{T_i^c}\rVert_1\le\lVert v_{T_i}\rVert_1,
 \qquad
 \max_j\lVert Xe_j\rVert_2^2\le2mq.
 \tag{21}
\]
This invokes the factor-one cone conclusion and therefore remains valid at \(n=8,s=1\).

Use the nonadaptive implementation that draws the independent pool rows before observing outcomes.  Conditional on the complete assignment matrix, the weights in each score are deterministic and the rowwise predictable sub-Gaussian bound may be iterated over time.  Since \(X^{\mathsf T}H_m=X^{\mathsf T}\), for fixed \(i,j\),
\[
 \Pr_{P,d}\!\left(
  |e_j^{\mathsf T}X^{\mathsf T}H_m\varepsilon_i|>\lambda_m\mid Z
 \right)
 \le2\exp\!\left(-\frac{\lambda_m^2}
 {2\sigma^2\lVert Xe_j\rVert_2^2}\right)
 \le\frac{\eta}{n^2}.
 \tag{22}
\]
When \(\sigma=0\), the score is zero almost surely and (22) is read by continuity.  A union bound over all \(n^2\) row-coordinate pairs gives an event \(E_\varepsilon\) of conditional probability at least \(1-\eta\) on which
\[
 \max_{i\in\mathcal U}\lVert X^{\mathsf T}H_m\varepsilon_i\rVert_\infty
 \le\lambda_m.
 \tag{23}
\]
Only scalar bounds for fixed outcome rows were used; contemporaneous dependence between different rows is immaterial to the union bound.

On \(E_Z\cap E_\varepsilon\), (20) and (23) imply \(\theta_i\in\mathcal D_i\).  The feasible set is nonempty for every data realization because any least-squares solution satisfies the normal equations, and the minimum defining \(v_i\) is attained by closedness and coercivity of the \(\ell_1\) objective.  Hence \(\theta_i^{\mathrm D}\) is well defined.  Put \(\Delta_i=\theta_i^{\mathrm D}-\theta_i\).  Minimality of its \(\ell_1\) norm and the support of \(\theta_i\) give
\[
 \lVert(\Delta_i)_{T_i^c}\rVert_1
 \le\lVert(\Delta_i)_{T_i}\rVert_1.
 \tag{24}
\]
Feasibility of both vectors gives
\[
 \lVert X^{\mathsf T}X\Delta_i\rVert_\infty\le2\lambda_m.
 \tag{25}
\]
Using (21), (24), Cauchy--Schwarz, and (25), in that order,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 c_0mq\lVert\Delta_i\rVert_2^2
 &\le\lVert X\Delta_i\rVert_2^2
 =\Delta_i^{\mathsf T}X^{\mathsf T}X\Delta_i\\
 &\le2\lambda_m\lVert\Delta_i\rVert_1
 \le4\lambda_m\sqrt{s+1}\lVert\Delta_i\rVert_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{26}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \lVert\theta_i^{\mathrm D}-\theta_i\rVert_\infty
 \le C_7\sigma\sqrt{\frac{(s+1)\log(2n^2/\eta)}{mq}}.
 \tag{27}
\]
Choose \(C_{\mathrm{cert}}\ge C_7\).  The noise-to-signal term in the sample-size hypothesis then implies
\[
 r_m^2=C_{\mathrm{cert}}^2\sigma^2
 \frac{(s+1)\log(2n^2/\eta)}{mq}<\frac{\delta^2}{4},
 \tag{28}
\]
after one universal enlargement of \(C\).

For a certified feasible \(\widehat\theta_i\), weak duality bounds its objective excess over the unique minimizer by \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{comp}}^2/2\).  The variational inequality at the constrained minimizer and one-strong convexity of \(u\mapsto\lVert u\rVert_2^2/2\) imply
\[
 \frac12\lVert\widehat\theta_i-\theta_i^{\mathrm D}\rVert_2^2
 \le\frac{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{comp}}^2}{2}.
 \tag{29}
\]
Thus its Euclidean and coordinatewise errors are at most \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{comp}}\).  Combining (27)--(29),
\[
 \lVert\widehat\theta_i-\theta_i\rVert_\infty\le R_m,
 \qquad
 \delta-2R_m=\frac34(\delta-2r_m)>0.
 \tag{30}
\]
If \(A_{ij}=0\), then \(|(\widehat\theta_i)_j|\le R_m<\delta-R_m\), so the certificate is \(\mathsf{nonedge}\).  If \(A_{ij}=1\), beta-min and (30) give
\[
 |(\widehat\theta_i)_j|\ge|\Gamma_{ij}|-R_m
 \ge\delta-R_m>R_m,
\]
so it is \(\mathsf{edge}\).  Therefore no certificate is undecided and \(\widehat A=A\).  Finally, \(\Pr(E_Z\cap E_\varepsilon)\ge1-\rho-\eta\), proving the proposed statement.  The proof uses only the validity of the supplied optimization certificate and deliberately makes no complexity claim for finding it.
