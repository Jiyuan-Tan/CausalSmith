Determine \(m^*(n,B,s,\delta,\sigma,\eta)\) up to universal constants in every regime, and construct a polynomial-real-arithmetic-time exact-budget design-decoder pair whose simultaneous edge and nonedge certificates attain that value. In particular, decide whether the factor \(s+1\) multiplying the noise-to-signal term in the simultaneous-certificate theorem is intrinsic to polynomial-time uniform certification or can be removed while preserving the exact-budget constraint.
