The already-established two-sided bracket gives, with \(a=\sigma^2/\delta^2\),
\[
 c q^{-1}a\{\log(n-1)+\log(1/\eta)\}
 \le m^*(n,B,s,\delta,\sigma,\eta)
 \le \left\lceil\min\{U_{\mathrm D},U_{\mathrm L}\}\right\rceil,
\]
where
\[
 U_{\mathrm D}=Cq^{-1}\left\{(2s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{en}{2s+1}\right)
 +a(s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{4n^2}{\eta}\right)
 +\log\!\left(\frac4\eta\right)\right\}
\]
and
\[
 U_{\mathrm L}=Cq^{-1}\left\{(s+1)\log\!\left(\frac{8n^3}{\eta}\right)
 +a\log\!\left(\frac{8n^2}{\eta}\right)\right\}.
\]
Because \(n\ge8\) and \(0<\eta\le1/4\), the logarithms in the lower bound and in the noise term of \(U_{\mathrm L}\) are comparable up to universal constants. Therefore, if \(a\ge s+1\),
\[
 m^*(n,B,s,\delta,\sigma,\eta)
 \asymp q^{-1}\frac{\sigma^2}{\delta^2}
 \{\log n+\log(1/\eta)\}.
\]
For \(s=1\) and \(\sigma=0\), the proved upper bound is
\[
 m^*(n,B,1,\delta,0,\eta)
 \le
 \left\lceil Cq^{-1}\left\{3\log\!\left(\frac{en}{3}\right)
 +\log\!\left(\frac4\eta\right)\right\}\right\rceil.
\]
On the noiseless degree-one subclass, probability-one recovery requires
\[
 m\ge
 \max\!\left\{\left\lceil\log_2(n+2)\right\rceil,
 \left\lceil\frac1q\right\rceil\right\},
\]
and every fixed design with positive structured sparse singular value requires \(m\ge4\). Thus the noisy regime \(\sigma^2/\delta^2\ge s+1\) is settled up to universal constants, whereas the low-noise sparse-exploration order and the certified finite-precision computational regime remain open.
