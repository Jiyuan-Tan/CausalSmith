There exists a universal constant \(c>0\) such that, on the Gaussian-noise subclass of \(\mathcal M_s\), every admissible adaptive exact-budget design and decoder \(d=((\pi_t)_{t=1}^m,g)\) satisfying \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_s}\Pr_{P,d}(g(\mathscr H_m)\ne A)\le\eta\) obeys \(m\ge c\,\sigma^2(\log(n-1)+\log(1/\eta))/(q\delta^2)\). In the noiseless degree-one subclass, every admissible \(d\) that identifies \(A\) uniformly also obeys \(m\ge\max\{\lceil\log_2(n+2)\rceil,\lceil1/q\rceil\}\).
