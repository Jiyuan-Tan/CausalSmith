%%% FIELD feasibility-proof
Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{\beta,\alpha}^{\mathrm{HM}}\).  If \(0<u\leq\delta_0\), the local margin assumption and \(C_0\leq\overline C\) give
\[
 P_X\{0<|\tau_P(X)|\leq u\}\leq C_0u^\alpha
 \leq\overline C u^\alpha.
\]
If \(u>\delta_0\), the probability bound and the definition of \(\overline C\) give
\[
 P_X\{0<|\tau_P(X)|\leq u\}\leq1
 \leq\delta_0^{-\alpha}u^\alpha
 \leq\overline C u^\alpha.
\]
For \(u=0\) the event is empty.  Thus
\[
 P_X\{0<|\tau_P(X)|\leq u\}\leq\overline C u^\alpha
 \quad\text{for every }u\geq0.
\]

By the definitions of participant-welfare loss and \(\pi_P^\star\),
\[
 \mathcal R_T(P,\mathsf D)
 =\mathbb E_{P,\mathsf D}\sum_{t=1}^T
 |\tau_P(X_t)|\mathbf 1\{A_t\ne\pi_P^\star(X_t)\}
\]
is exactly the expected contextual-bandit regret of \(\mathsf D\).  Consequently,
\[
 \rho_{\mathrm{feas}}(T;\mathcal P_{\beta,\alpha}^{\mathrm{HM}})
 =\inf_{\mathsf D}\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\beta,\alpha}^{\mathrm{HM}}}
 \mathcal R_T(P,\mathsf D)
\]
is the minimax regret over the declared Gaussian class.

We next justify the Gaussian transfer without conditioning on adaptively selected pull times.  Fix a Borel bin \(B\subseteq\mathcal X\), an arm \(a\in\mathcal A\), and put
\[
 J_t=\mathbf 1\{X_t\in B,A_t=a\},
 \qquad N_n=\sum_{t=1}^nJ_t.
\]
Let \(\mathcal G_t=\sigma(H_{t-1},X_t,A_t)\), and let \(\mathcal F_t\) be the observed sigma-field after \(Y_t\) is revealed.  Non-anticipation makes \(J_t\) measurable with respect to \(\mathcal G_t\), while the Gaussian-reward assumption gives, for every \(\lambda\in\mathbb R\),
\[
 \mathbb E_{P,\mathsf D}\!\left[
  \exp\!\left\{\lambda\sigma J_t\varepsilon_t
  -\frac{\lambda^2\sigma^2}{2}J_t\right\}
  \middle|\mathcal G_t\right]=1.
\]
Indeed, this is immediate when \(J_t=0\), and when \(J_t=1\) it is the moment-generating identity for a standard Gaussian.  Hence, by iterated conditioning,
\[
 M_n(\lambda)=\exp\!\left\{
 \lambda\sigma\sum_{t=1}^nJ_t\varepsilon_t
 -\frac{\lambda^2\sigma^2}{2}N_n\right\},
 \qquad M_0(\lambda)=1,
\]
is a nonnegative \((\mathcal F_n)\)-martingale.

For an integer \(s\geq1\), let \(S_s=\inf\{n:N_n=s\}\), with \(S_s=+\infty\) if the set is empty.  The time \(S_s\wedge T\) is bounded, so iterating the preceding one-step identity gives
\[
 \mathbb E_{P,\mathsf D}M_{S_s\wedge T}(\lambda)=1.
\]
For \(\lambda>0\), Markov's inequality on the event \(\{S_s\leq T,\ \sigma\sum_{t=1}^{S_s}J_t\varepsilon_t\geq sv\}\) therefore yields
\[
 \mathbb P_{P,\mathsf D}\!\left(
 S_s\leq T,\ \sigma\sum_{t=1}^{S_s}J_t\varepsilon_t\geq sv
 \right)
 \leq\exp\!\left\{-\lambda sv+\frac{\lambda^2\sigma^2s}{2}\right\}.
\]
Taking \(\lambda=v/\sigma^2\), and repeating the argument with \(-\lambda\), proves the predictable optional-skipping bound
\[
 \mathbb P_{P,\mathsf D}\!\left(
 S_s\leq T,\ 
 \left|\frac1s\sum_{j=1}^s\sigma\varepsilon_{S_j}\right|>v
 \right)
 \leq2\exp\!\left\{-\frac{sv^2}{2\sigma^2}\right\}.
\]
No conditional independence of \(\varepsilon_{S_1},\ldots,\varepsilon_{S_s}\) given the selected times is asserted or needed.

Suppose first that \(\alpha>0\).  Apply the explicit two-arm policy in the cited Perchet--Rigollet result.  Its bin and arm indicators are predictable in exactly the sense used above.  Every bounded-noise deviation step in its successive-elimination proof is therefore replaced by the displayed Gaussian bound, with only the confidence-radius constant changed.  The H\"older approximation steps are unchanged because the conditional means are unchanged.  On the simultaneous concentration event, a bin of side \(h\) continues to be explored only when its treatment gap is at most a constant multiple of \(h^\beta\); the global margin envelope proved above then bounds the mass of all such contexts by a constant multiple of \(h^{\alpha\beta}\).  At the terminal resolution
\[
 h_T\asymp T^{-1/(2\beta+d)},
\]
the approximation term is
\[
 T h_T^{\beta(1+\alpha)}
 =T^{(\beta+d-\alpha\beta)/(2\beta+d)}.
\]
The coarser-resolution terms in the cited proof form a geometric sum with this same terminal order, because
\[
 \beta+d-\alpha\beta\geq\beta>0
\]
by \(\alpha\beta\leq d\) and \(\beta>0\).  The optional-skipping tail may be union-bounded at the same calls and levels as in that proof, so its complement contributes only a constant after enlarging the numerical confidence constant.  Thus the cited upper-bound argument gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\beta,\alpha}^{\mathrm{HM}}}
 \mathcal R_T(P,\mathsf D)
 \leq C T^{(\beta+d-\alpha\beta)/(2\beta+d)}.
\]

For completeness, the endpoint \(\alpha=0\) has the same upper order without using any additional margin premise.  Partition \([0,1]^d\) into at most \(C_dh^{-d}\) cubes of side \(h\).  In each cube use a two-arm horizon-aware minimax index rule, restarted on local horizons \(1,2,4,\ldots\), and make the rule ignore the exact within-cube location.  Conditional on a visit to a cube, the arm reward is centered sub-Gaussian with a variance proxy \(v_0\) depending only on \(M\) and \(\sigma\): this follows by multiplying the Gaussian moment-generating function by Hoeffding's elementary bound for the bounded random conditional mean.  For a local epoch of length \(n\), the index
\[
 \widehat\mu_{a,s}
 +\sqrt{\frac{4v_0}{s}
 \max\!\left\{\log\!\left(\frac{n}{2s}\right),0\right\}}
\]
after \(s\) pulls of arm \(a\) has the following direct consequence of the preceding exponential bound: if the two cube-average arm means differ by \(\Delta>0\), then the expected number of inferior pulls is at most
\[
 C\frac{v_0}{\Delta^2}
 \left\{1+\log_+\!\left(\frac{n\Delta^2}{v_0}\right)\right\}.
\]
To see this, split at
\[
 s_\Delta=\left\lceil
 C\frac{v_0}{\Delta^2}
 \left\{1+\log_+\!\left(\frac{n\Delta^2}{v_0}\right)\right\}
 \right\rceil;
\]
after \(s_\Delta\) inferior pulls, selection of that arm forces either an upper deviation of its empirical mean or a lower deviation of the superior empirical mean by at least \(\Delta/2\).  Applying the two one-sided exponential bounds at each possible pull count and summing the resulting geometric tails gives the display.  Multiplication by \(\Delta\), together with the trivial bound \(n\Delta\), gives
\[
 \min\!\left\{n\Delta,
 C\frac{v_0}{\Delta}
 \left(1+\log_+\!\frac{n\Delta^2}{v_0}\right)\right\}
 \leq C\sqrt{v_0n},
\]
because \((1+\log x)/\sqrt{x}\) is bounded on \([1,\infty)\).  Summing the geometric local epochs changes only the constant, so a cube visited \(N(B)\) times incurs expected regret at most \(C\mathbb E\sqrt{N(B)}\) relative to its best cube-average arm.

H\"older continuity adds at most \(CTh^\beta\) when that cube-average comparator is replaced by the contextwise oracle.  Jensen's and Cauchy--Schwarz inequalities give
\[
 \sum_B\mathbb E\sqrt{N(B)}
 \leq\sum_B\sqrt{\mathbb EN(B)}
 \leq\sqrt{C_dh^{-d}T}.
\]
Thus
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\beta,0}^{\mathrm{HM}}}
 \mathcal R_T(P,\mathsf D)
 \leq C\left(Th^\beta+\sqrt{Th^{-d}}\right).
\]
Choosing \(h\asymp T^{-1/(2\beta+d)}\) yields
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\beta,0}^{\mathrm{HM}}}
 \mathcal R_T(P,\mathsf D)
 \leq CT^{(\beta+d)/(2\beta+d)}.
\]

We next transfer the lower bound.  For \(\alpha>0\), take the two-arm hard family underlying the minimax lower statement in the cited Perchet--Rigollet result, and apply a fixed affine contraction so that its mean functions lie in \([-M,M]\) and satisfy the declared H\"older radius.  Replace its bounded arm rewards by Gaussian rewards having those same contracted means and variance \(\sigma^2\).  For neighboring laws with mean differences \(v_a(x)\), the Gaussian likelihood ratio and iterated conditioning give the exact adaptive identity
\[
 \operatorname{KL}\!\left(
 \mathbb P_{P,\mathsf D}^{T}\,\middle\|\,
 \mathbb P_{Q,\mathsf D}^{T}\right)
 =\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}
 \mathbb E_{P,\mathsf D}\sum_{t=1}^T v_{A_t}(X_t)^2.
\]
The source hard family keeps its Bernoulli means in a fixed interior subinterval.  On that interval its one-observation Bernoulli divergence is bounded below by a positive constant times the squared mean difference.  Hence the Gaussian divergence after the fixed contraction is at most a constant multiple of the information in the cited testing construction.  All its neighboring testing errors therefore remain bounded away from zero, whereas participant loss depends only on the unchanged conditional means.  Its separation calculation consequently gives
\[
 \inf_{\mathsf D}\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\beta,\alpha}^{\mathrm{HM}}}
 \mathcal R_T(P,\mathsf D)
 \geq cT^{(\beta+d-\alpha\beta)/(2\beta+d)}.
\]

When \(\alpha=0\), the same order follows directly without treating the margin restriction as void.  Under the uniform context law, place \(m\asymp h^{-d}\) disjoint H\"older bumps, each supported on a cube of side \(h\), with total support volume at most \(\min\{C_0/2,1/2\}\).  Let each bump have height \(ch^\beta\), choose its sign independently, and take the two arm means to be opposite halves of the resulting contrast.  The constant \(c>0\) may be chosen, using \(L>0\) and \(M>0\), so that both mean functions obey the H\"older and boundedness conditions.  Choose the packing constant and the scale constant below so that \(m\geq1\) and \(m\geq c_mh^{-d}\) for every \(T\geq1\), where \(c_m>0\) depends only on \(C_0\) and \(d\).  Since the nonzero-gap set is contained in the union of the bump supports,
\[
 P_X\{0<|\tau_P(X)|\leq u\}
 \leq P_X\{\tau_P(X)\ne0\}\leq C_0
 \quad\text{for every }u\geq0,
\]
so every member of this family satisfies the \(\alpha=0\) margin assumption.

Neighboring sign vectors differ only on one cube, and the preceding Gaussian identity bounds their trajectory divergence by
\[
 CT h^{d+2\beta}.
\]
Choose \(h=c_hT^{-1/(2\beta+d)}\) with \(c_h>0\) small enough that this divergence is at most \(1/8\).  The elementary binary coarse-graining inequality
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(P\|Q)
 \geq p\log\frac pq+(1-p)\log\frac{1-p}{1-q}
 \geq2(p-q)^2,
 \qquad p=P(E),\quad q=Q(E),
\]
shows that the total-variation distance of each neighboring pair is at most \(1/4\).  Thus the sum of the two errors of every sign decision is at least \(3/4\).  Each bump is bounded below by a constant multiple of \(h^\beta\) on an inner cube of volume a constant multiple of \(h^d\).  Averaging over sign vectors and summing over the \(m\asymp h^{-d}\) coordinates therefore gives, for every non-anticipating design,
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\beta,0}^{\mathrm{HM}}}
 \mathcal R_T(P,\mathsf D)
 \geq cT\,m h^{d+\beta}
 \geq cTh^\beta
 =cT^{(\beta+d)/(2\beta+d)}.
\]
The choices of the packing and scale constants above make the construction valid for every \(T\geq1\).  Combining the two cases, and changing the constants for any finite exceptional range in the cited \(\alpha>0\) comparison, proves
\[
 cT^{r_{\beta,\alpha,d}}
 \leq\rho_{\mathrm{feas}}(T;\mathcal P_{\beta,\alpha}^{\mathrm{HM}})
 \leq CT^{r_{\beta,\alpha,d}}.
\]

Finally, when \(d=1\), \(\beta=1\), and \(\alpha=1/5\),
\[
 r_{1,1/5,1}
 =\frac{1+1-1/5}{2+1}=\frac35.
\]
If \(\rho<cT^{3/5}\), then the definition of \(\rho_{\mathrm{feas}}\) implies \(\mathfrak D_T(\rho;\mathcal P_{1,1/5}^{\mathrm{HM}})=\varnothing\).  The empty-design convention in the definition of \(W_{T,\delta}\) therefore gives
\[
 W_{T,\delta}(\rho;\mathcal P_{1,1/5}^{\mathrm{HM}})=+\infty.
\]
