## Assumptions (A)

**A-1 (assumption).** Conditional on the finite population \(\{Y_{gti}:g\in\mathcal G_G,\ i\in\mathcal I_n\}\), the only randomness is generated by \(x_b\) and \(A_{gbi}\).

**A-2 (assumption).** For every unit, \(Y_{gti}\) is invariant to treatment and saturation assignments outside group \(g\).

**A-3 (assumption).** Within group \(g\), the endpoint response depends on the contemporaneous assignment vector only through the unit's own treatment \(d\) and the realized saturation \(q\).

**A-4 (assumption).** For every \(t\leq2B\), if \(h,h'\in\mathcal H_{2B}\) agree through period \(t\), then \(Y_{gti}(h)=Y_{gti}(h')\); assignments after period \(t\) do not affect the period-\(t\) endpoint.

**A-6 (assumption).** Uniformly over \(b\in\mathcal B_B\) and realized \(\mathcal F_{b-1}\), \(c_\sigma\leq\sigma_b^2\leq C_\sigma\).

**A-7 (assumption).** \(B^{-1}\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_B}\Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}\to_P\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}\).
