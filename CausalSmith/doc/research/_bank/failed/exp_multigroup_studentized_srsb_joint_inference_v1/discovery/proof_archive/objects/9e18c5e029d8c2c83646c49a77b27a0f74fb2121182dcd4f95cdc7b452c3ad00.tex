Fix a block \(b\) and condition on \(\mathcal F_{b-1}\).  By the definition of \(\mathcal X_G\), the map \(x\mapsto-x\) is a bijection of \(\mathcal X_G\), and \(P_0(-x)=P_0(x)\).  Since \(Z_b(-x)=-Z_b(x)\), also \(W_b(-x)=-W_b(x)\).  The weight is even, because
\[
 f_{\eta,\kappa}(-w)=\eta+(1-\eta)e^{-\kappa(-w)^2}=f_{\eta,\kappa}(w).
\]
Consequently \(P_{\mathrm{RR},b}(-x\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=P_{\mathrm{RR},b}(x\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})\).  Pairing every \(x\) with \(-x\) gives
\[
 \Pr(x_{gb}=1\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})
 =\Pr(x_{gb}=-1\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\frac12.
\]
The definition of \(Q_{gb}\) proves the two asserted saturation probabilities.

Conditional on \(Q_{gb}=q\), the fixed-count simple random sample includes unit \(i\) with probability \(q\) when \(d=1\) and with probability \(1-q\) when \(d=0\).  Thus, by the preceding first-stage calculation and the definition of \(p_{dq}\),
\[
 \pi_{gbi,dq}
 =\Pr(Q_{gb}=q\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})
   \Pr(A_{gbi}=d\mid Q_{gb}=q,\mathcal F_{b-1})
 =\frac{p_{dq}}2.
\]
Since \(q_L=1/4\) and \(q_H=3/4\), \(p_{dq}\geq1/4\), whence \(\pi_{gbi,dq}\geq1/8\), so every division in the Horvitz--Thompson estimator is valid.

Assumptions \(\texttt{ass:partial-interference}\), \(\texttt{ass:stratified-interference}\), \(\texttt{ass:nonanticipation}\), and \(\texttt{ass:finite-memory-lag-one}\) imply consistency at the endpoint of the held two-period block:
\[
 I_{gbi,dq}Y^{\mathrm{obs}}_{g,2b,i}
 =I_{gbi,dq}Y_{gbi}(d,q).
\]
The potential endpoint on the right is fixed under \(\texttt{ass:design-based-finiteness}\).  Therefore
\[
 \mathbb E\!\left[
   \frac{I_{gbi,dq}Y^{\mathrm{obs}}_{g,2b,i}}{\pi_{gbi,dq}}
   \mathrel{\bigg|}\mathcal F_{b-1}\right]
 =Y_{gbi}(d,q).
\]
Summing this equality with coefficient \(c_{dq}/(Gn)\) proves
\(\mathbb E(\widehat\tau_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\tau_b\).  Iterated expectation and block averaging then give
\[
 \mathbb E(\widehat\tau)
 =\frac1B\sum_{b=1}^B\mathbb E(\widehat\tau_b)
 =\frac1B\sum_{b=1}^B\tau_b=\tau.
\]
