All constants below may depend on the fixed constants \(M\), \(n\), \(q_L\), and \(q_H\), but not on \(G\), \(B\), \(b\), or the realized history.  The fixed contrast coefficients and \(|Y_{gbi}(d,q)|\leq M\) give constants \(L_a,L_e<\infty\) such that
\[
 \|a_{gb}\|\leq L_a,
 \qquad \|e_{gbq}\|\leq L_e
\]
for all indices.  The second inequality also follows uniformly if \(n\) varies through \(4\mathbb N\), because \(p_{dq}\geq1/4\) and every term is an average of bounded endpoints.

Generate a uniform balanced sign vector by first drawing a uniform perfect matching of \(\{1,\ldots,G\}\), and then, independently for each matched pair \(\{r_j,s_j\}\), assigning opposite signs with a Rademacher orientation \(\xi_j\).  Every balanced sign vector is compatible with exactly \((G/2)!\) such oriented matchings, so this construction has law \(P_0\).  Conditional on the matching,
\[
 A_b=G^{-1/2}\sum_{j=1}^{G/2}\xi_j(a_{r_jb}-a_{s_jb}).
\]
For a scalar Rademacher sum, expansion of the fourth power and cancellation of monomials containing an odd power of some \(\xi_j\) give
\[
 \mathbb E\left(\sum_j\xi_jd_j\right)^4
 =\sum_jd_j^4+6\sum_{j<k}d_j^2d_k^2
 \leq3\left(\sum_jd_j^2\right)^2.
\]
Applying this to each coordinate and using norm equivalence in \(\mathbb R^2\) proves \(\mathbb E_0\|A_b\|^4\leq C_A\).

Conditional on \(x_b\), the group terms in \(U_b\) are independent, centered, and bounded by \(2L_e/\sqrt G\).  The same fourth-power expansion, now with expectations in place of Rademacher averages, gives
\[
 \mathbb E(\|U_b\|^4\mid x_b,\mathcal F_{b-1})\leq C_U.
\]
Since \(\|u+v\|^4\leq8(\|u\|^4+\|v\|^4)\), \(\texttt{lem:score-decomposition}\) yields
\[
 \mathbb E_0\|R_b\|^4\leq C_R
\]
for a finite uniform \(C_R\).  Finally,
\[
 \frac{dP_{\mathrm{RR},b}}{dP_0}(x\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})
 =\frac{f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b(x))}{C_b}leq\frac1\eta,
\]
because \(f_{\eta,\kappa}\leq1\) and \(C_b\geq\eta\).  Conditional second-stage laws are identical under the two designs, so likelihood-ratio domination gives
\[
 \mathbb E_{\mathrm{RR}}(\|R_b\|^4\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})
 \leq C_R/\eta.
\]
