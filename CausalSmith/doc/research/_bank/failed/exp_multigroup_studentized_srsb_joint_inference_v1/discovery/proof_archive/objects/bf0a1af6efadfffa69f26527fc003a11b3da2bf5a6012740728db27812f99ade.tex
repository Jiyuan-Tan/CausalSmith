For every sequence of finite-population designs belonging to \(\mathcal M_{G,B}\) along which \(B\to\infty\), with the admissible even value of \(G\) allowed to depend arbitrarily on \(B\) and in particular allowed to remain fixed,
\[
\sqrt{GB}(\widehat\tau-\tau)=B^{-1/2}\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_B}R_b\ \Rightarrow\ N_2(0,\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}).
\]
Thus the root-\(GB\) limit law requires no restriction on \(G/B\); it is derived from martingale centering, the uniform block fourth-moment bound, and predictable covariance stabilization with a positive-definite limit.
