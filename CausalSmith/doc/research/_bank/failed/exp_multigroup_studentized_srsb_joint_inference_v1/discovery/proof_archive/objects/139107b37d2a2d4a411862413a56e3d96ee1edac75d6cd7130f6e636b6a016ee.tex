The structural class does not itself guarantee covariance stabilization or a positive-definite limiting covariance.  The arbitrary-\(G\), \(B\to\infty\) central limit theorem and Wald guarantee therefore apply only on sequences satisfying rerandomization covariance stabilization and positive definiteness.  The projection formula is instead a \(G\to\infty\) approximation and requires complete-randomization, rerandomization, and projection stabilization, though not positive definiteness.  The completed studentizer can be conservative by the nonvanishing average completion, and the population gain matrix \(P\) is not generally observable.  Exact enumeration over \(1{,}536\) assignments at \(G=n=4\) remains a finite diagnostic rather than evidence for either asymptotic regime.
