There exists a deterministic sequence \(\varepsilon_G\downarrow0\) such that, uniformly over blocks, histories, and members of \(\mathcal M_{G,B}\),
\[
\left|\mathbb E_0f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b)-\mathbb Ef_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b^\star)\right|
+\left\|\mathbb E_0[A_bA_b^\top f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b)]-\mathbb E[A_b^\star A_b^{\star\top}f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b^\star)]\right\|
\leq\varepsilon_G,
\]
and
\[
\left\|\mathbb E_0[R_bR_b^\top f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b)]-\mathbb E[R_b^\star R_b^{\star\top}f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b^\star)]\right\|
\leq\varepsilon_G.
\]
The comparison is on the exact covariance range and remains valid when the score covariance is singular; no positive lower bound on either score-coordinate variance is required.
