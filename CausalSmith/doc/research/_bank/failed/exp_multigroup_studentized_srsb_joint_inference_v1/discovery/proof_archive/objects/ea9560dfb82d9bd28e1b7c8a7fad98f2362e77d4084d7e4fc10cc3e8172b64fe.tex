Fix a block and history.  By \(\texttt{lem:score-decomposition}\), under \(P_0\),
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_0(R_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\Sigma_{b,0},
 \qquad \operatorname{Cov}_0(R_b,W_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\beta_b,
 \qquad \operatorname{Var}_0(W_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=1.
\]
The covariance-matched Gaussian pair therefore admits the orthogonal decomposition
\[
 R_b^\star=\beta_bW_b^\star+E_b^\star,
 \qquad E_b^\star\perp W_b^\star,
 \qquad \operatorname{Var}(E_b^\star)=\Sigma_{b,0}-\beta_b\beta_b^\top.     \tag{10}
\]
This remains valid for singular score covariance because it uses only Gaussian orthogonality on the covariance range.

For \(Z\sim N(0,1)\), direct differentiation of
\(\mathbb Ee^{-\kappa Z^2}=(1+2\kappa)^{-1/2}\) gives
\[
 \mathbb E\{Z^2e^{-\kappa Z^2}\}=(1+2\kappa)^{-3/2}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 c_\star:=\mathbb Ef_{\eta,\kappa}(Z)
 =\eta+(1-\eta)(1+2\kappa)^{-1/2},
\]
and
\[
 \frac{\mathbb E\{Z^2f_{\eta,\kappa}(Z)\}}{c_\star}
 =v_{\eta,\kappa}.
\]
Using (10), independence, and the evenness of the tilt, the Gaussian weighted covariance is
\[
 \frac{\mathbb E[R_b^\star R_b^{\star\top}f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b^\star)]}
      {\mathbb Ef_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b^\star)}
 =\Sigma_{b,0}-(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})\beta_b\beta_b^\top.          \tag{11}
\]

Exact centering gives
\[
 \Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}
 =\frac{\mathbb E_0[R_bR_b^\top f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b)]}
        {\mathbb E_0 f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b)}.
\]
The theorem resolving \(\texttt{oeq:weighted-gaussian-transport}\) approximates both numerator and denominator uniformly, and both denominators are at least \(\eta\).  Combining that theorem with (11) proves
\[
 \Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}
 =\Sigma_{b,0}-(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})\beta_b\beta_b^\top+o(1)
\]
uniformly over blocks and histories.  Averaging, using \(\texttt{ass:cr-covariance-stabilization}\) and \(\texttt{ass:projection-stabilization}\), and comparing with the defining limit in \(\texttt{ass:rr-covariance-stabilization}\), gives
\[
 \Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}
 =\Omega_{\mathrm{CR}}-(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})P.
\]
Since \(\kappa>0\), \((1+2\kappa)^{-3/2}<(1+2\kappa)^{-1/2}\), so \(0<v_{\eta,\kappa}<1\).  For any fixed nonzero \(c\), the complete-randomization variance minus the rerandomization variance is
\[
 c^\top(\Omega_{\mathrm{CR}}-\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}})c
 =(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})c^\top Pc.
\]
The matrix \(P\) is positive semidefinite as a limit of averages of \(\beta_b\beta_b^\top\).  The last display is therefore strictly positive if and only if \(c^\top Pc>0\), proving the characterization.
