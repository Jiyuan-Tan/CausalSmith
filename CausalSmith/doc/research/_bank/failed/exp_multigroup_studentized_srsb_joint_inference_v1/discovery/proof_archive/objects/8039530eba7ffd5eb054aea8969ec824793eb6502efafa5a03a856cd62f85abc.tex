Fix a sequence covered by the proposed statement and put
\[
 X_B=\sqrt{GB}(\widehat\tau-\tau),
 \qquad
 \bar D_B=B^{-1}\sum_{b=1}^BD_b.
\]
The separately imposed rerandomization covariance stabilization and positive-definiteness conditions are exactly those required by \(\texttt{thm:joint-design-clt}\) and by the clipping part of \(\texttt{thm:studentizer}\).  Therefore
\[
 X_B\Rightarrow N_2(0,\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}),
 \qquad
 \widehat\Gamma_+
 =\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}+\bar D_B+o_P(1).                    \tag{16}
\]

Fix an arbitrary subsequence.  The uniform bound on \(D_b\) in \(\texttt{thm:studentizer}\) makes the deterministic sequence \(\{\bar D_B\}\) bounded in the finite-dimensional space of symmetric \(2\)-by-\(2\) matrices.  It has a further subsequence such that
\[
 \bar D_B\longrightarrow D_\infty
 \quad\text{for some }D_\infty\succeq0.                  \tag{17}
\]
Along this further subsequence, (16)--(17) imply the joint convergence
\[
 (X_B,\widehat\Gamma_+)\Rightarrow(X,\Gamma_\infty),
 \qquad
 X\sim N_2(0,\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}),
 \quad
 \Gamma_\infty:=\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}+D_\infty.           \tag{18}
\]
No independence assertion is needed in (18), because the second coordinate converges in probability to a deterministic matrix.  The positive-definiteness condition and \(D_\infty\succeq0\) imply that both \(\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}\) and \(\Gamma_\infty\) are positive definite.  Continuity of matrix inversion and of the quadratic-form map at (18) therefore gives
\[
 X_B^\top\widehat\Gamma_+^{-1}X_B
 \Rightarrow X^\top\Gamma_\infty^{-1}X.                  \tag{19}
\]

Write \(X\stackrel d=\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}^{1/2}Z\), where \(Z\sim N_2(0,I_2)\).  Since \(\Gamma_\infty\succeq\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}\succ0\), inversion reverses Loewner order, so
\[
 0\prec
 H:=\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}^{1/2}
       \Gamma_\infty^{-1}
       \Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}^{1/2}
 \preceq I_2.                                               \tag{20}
\]
Diagonalize \(H=O\operatorname{diag}(h_1,h_2)O^\top\), with \(0<h_j\leq1\).  Rotational invariance of \(Z\), coupled using the same two standard-normal coordinates, gives
\[
 X^\top\Gamma_\infty^{-1}X
 \stackrel d=h_1Z_1^2+h_2Z_2^2
 \leq Z_1^2+Z_2^2
 \quad\text{almost surely}.                                \tag{21}
\]
The variable on the right has the \(\chi^2_2\) law, and the variable on the left is a positive weighted sum of two independent squared standard normals, hence has no atom at the positive quantile \(\chi^2_{2,1-\alpha}\).  Applying (19)--(21) at this continuity point yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \liminf_B\Pr_{\mathrm{RR}}\!\left\{
  X_B^\top\widehat\Gamma_+^{-1}X_B
  \leq\chi^2_{2,1-\alpha}\right\}
 &\geq
 \Pr\{Z_1^2+Z_2^2\leq\chi^2_{2,1-\alpha}\}\\
 &=1-\alpha.                                                \tag{22}
 \end{aligned}
\]
By \(\texttt{def:simultaneous-wald-region}\), the event in (22) is exactly \(\{\tau\in\mathcal C_\alpha\}\).  If the asserted liminf failed on the original sequence, a violating subsequence would have the further subsequence just analyzed, contradicting (22).  This proves the result for every \(B\to\infty\) sequence in the structural class satisfying the two separately stated rerandomization covariance conditions, with no restriction on \(G/B\).
