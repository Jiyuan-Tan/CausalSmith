For every block, \(\mathbb E(\widehat\Gamma_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}+D_b\) with \(D_b\succeq0\).  Along every sequence in \(\mathcal M_{G,B}\) with \(B\to\infty\) and arbitrary admissible even \(G\), including fixed \(G\),
\[
\widehat\Gamma=\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}+B^{-1}\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_B}D_b+o_P(1).
\]
The block matrices are uniformly bounded in Frobenius norm.  Every probability limit point of \(\widehat\Gamma\) therefore dominates \(\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}\) in Loewner order; moreover, clipping is inactive with probability tending to one and \(\|\widehat\Gamma_+^{-1}\|=O_P(1)\).
