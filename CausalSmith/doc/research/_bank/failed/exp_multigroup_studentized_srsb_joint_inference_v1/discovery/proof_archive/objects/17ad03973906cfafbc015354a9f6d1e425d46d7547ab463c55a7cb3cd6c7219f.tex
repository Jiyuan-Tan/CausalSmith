Fix a block \(b\) and condition on \(\mathcal F_{b-1}\).  Under \(P_0\), the map \(x\mapsto-x\) preserves \(\mathcal X_G\) and the uniform law, so the definition of \(A_b\) gives \(\mathbb E_0A_b=0\).  By \(\texttt{lem:score-decomposition}\), \(\mathbb E(U_b\mid x_b,\mathcal F_{b-1})=0\); iterated expectation therefore gives \(\mathbb E_0R_b=0\) and \(\operatorname{Cov}_0(U_b,W_b)=0\).  The balanced-sign covariance identity, the unchanged second-stage covariance in that lemma, the definition of \(\beta_b\), and the exact standardization of \(W_b\) yield
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_0(R_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\Sigma_{b,0},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Cov}_0(R_b,W_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\beta_b,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}_0(W_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=1.                       \tag{1}
\]
The division defining \(W_b\) is legitimate because \(\texttt{ass:imbalance-variance-window}\) gives \(\sigma_b^2\geq c_\sigma>0\).

Let \((R_b^\star,W_b^\star)\) be the covariance-matched centered Gaussian pair.  Equation (1) implies \(W_b^\star\sim N(0,1)\).  Define
\[
 E_b^\star=R_b^\star-\beta_bW_b^\star.                                  \tag{2}
\]
Direct covariance algebra using (1) gives
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(E_b^\star,W_b^\star)=0,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(E_b^\star)=\Sigma_{b,0}-\beta_b\beta_b^\top.       \tag{3}
\]
Because the pair in (3) is jointly Gaussian, its characteristic function factors, so \(E_b^\star\) and \(W_b^\star\) are independent.  This argument only inverts the scalar variance \(\operatorname{Var}(W_b^\star)=1\), and therefore remains valid when the score covariance is singular.

For \(Z\sim N(0,1)\), completing the square in the Gaussian density gives
\[
 \mathbb Ee^{-\kappa Z^2}=(1+2\kappa)^{-1/2}.                            \tag{4}
\]
Differentiation under the integral is justified by the integrable envelope \(z^2e^{-z^2/2}\), and differentiating (4) gives
\[
 \mathbb E\{Z^2e^{-\kappa Z^2}\}=(1+2\kappa)^{-3/2}.                   \tag{5}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 c_\star:=\mathbb Ef_{\eta,\kappa}(Z)
 =\eta+(1-\eta)(1+2\kappa)^{-1/2}\geq\eta>0,                            \tag{6}
\]
and the definition \(\texttt{def:projection-gain}\) together with (5)--(6) gives
\[
 \frac{\mathbb E\{Z^2f_{\eta,\kappa}(Z)\}}{c_\star}
 =v_{\eta,\kappa}.                                                       \tag{7}
\]
Using \(R_b^\star=\beta_bW_b^\star+E_b^\star\), independence and centering from (3), and (7), we obtain
\[
 \frac{\mathbb E\{R_b^\star R_b^{\star\top}
                     f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b^\star)\}}
      {\mathbb Ef_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b^\star)}
 =\Sigma_{b,0}-(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})\beta_b\beta_b^\top.                \tag{8}
\]

We next identify the actual soft-design covariance.  Complementation changes \((A_b,W_b)\) to \((-A_b,-W_b)\), while the weight \(f_{\eta,\kappa}\) is even.  Hence \(\mathbb E_{\mathrm{RR}}(A_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=0\).  Conditional centering of \(U_b\) in \(\texttt{lem:score-decomposition}\) then gives \(\mathbb E_{\mathrm{RR}}(R_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=0\).  The Radon--Nikodym ratio in \(\texttt{def:soft-two-stage-design}\) therefore implies
\[
 \Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}
 =\frac{\mathbb E_0\{R_bR_b^\top f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b)
                         \mid\mathcal F_{b-1}\}}
        {\mathbb E_0\{f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b)
                         \mid\mathcal F_{b-1}\}}.                        \tag{9}
\]
Both the denominator in (9) and its Gaussian counterpart in (8) are at least \(\eta\).  Moreover, covariance matching, Cauchy--Schwarz, and \(\texttt{lem:block-moments}\) give
\[
 \left\|\mathbb E\{R_b^\star R_b^{\star\top}
                         f_{\eta,\kappa}(W_b^\star)\}\right\|
 \leq\mathbb E\|R_b^\star\|^2
 =\mathbb E_0\|R_b\|^2
 \leq\{\mathbb E_0\|R_b\|^4\}^{1/2}\leq C_R^{1/2}.                 \tag{10}
\]
If \(N,D\) denote the numerator and denominator in (9), and \(N^\star,D^\star\) those in (8), then (6), (9), and \(f_{\eta,\kappa}\geq\eta\) imply the deterministic ratio bound
\[
 \left\|\frac ND-\frac{N^\star}{D^\star}\right\|
 \leq\frac{\|N-N^\star\|}{\eta}
     +\frac{\|N^\star\|\,|D-D^\star|}{\eta^2}.                       \tag{11}
\]
By \(\texttt{thm:weighted-gaussian-transport}\), both discrepancies on the right of (11) are bounded by a deterministic sequence tending to zero uniformly over blocks and histories.  Equations (8)--(11) prove
\[
 \Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}
 =\Sigma_{b,0}-(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})\beta_b\beta_b^\top+r_{Gb},
 \qquad
 \sup_{b,\mathcal F_{b-1}}\|r_{Gb}\|\longrightarrow0                  \tag{12}
\]
as \(G\to\infty\).  This blockwise argument does not involve \(B\), so it imposes no condition on \(G/B\).

Now take a sequence with \(G\to\infty\) on which exactly the three limit conditions \(\texttt{ass:cr-covariance-stabilization}\), \(\texttt{ass:rr-covariance-stabilization}\), and \(\texttt{ass:projection-stabilization}\) hold.  Averaging (12) over \(b\) yields
\[
 \left\|\frac1B\sum_b\Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}
 -\frac1B\sum_b\Sigma_{b,0}
 +(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})\frac1B\sum_b\beta_b\beta_b^\top\right\|
 \leq\sup_{b,\mathcal F_{b-1}}\|r_{Gb}\|\longrightarrow0.             \tag{13}
\]
The three stated stabilizations and uniqueness of limits in probability applied to (13) imply
\[
 \Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}=\Omega_{\mathrm{CR}}-(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})P.       \tag{14}
\]
Neither \(\texttt{ass:rr-limit-positive}\) nor any relative-growth condition is used.

Finally, \(P\succeq0\).  Indeed, for every fixed \(c\in\mathbb R^2\), each random quantity
\(
 B^{-1}\sum_b(c^\top\beta_b)^2
\)
is nonnegative and converges in probability to the deterministic number \(c^\top Pc\), which therefore cannot be negative.  Since \(\kappa>0\) and \(0<\eta<1\),
\[
 (1+2\kappa)^{-3/2}<(1+2\kappa)^{-1/2}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 0<v_{\eta,\kappa}<1.                                                     \tag{15}
\]
For every fixed \(c\ne0\), (14) consequently gives
\[
 c^\top\Omega_{\mathrm{CR}}c-c^\top\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}c
 =(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})c^\top Pc.                                         \tag{16}
\]
The first factor on the right of (16) is strictly positive and the second is nonnegative.  Therefore soft rerandomization has strictly smaller limiting variance for exactly those contrasts satisfying \(c^\top Pc>0\), as claimed.
