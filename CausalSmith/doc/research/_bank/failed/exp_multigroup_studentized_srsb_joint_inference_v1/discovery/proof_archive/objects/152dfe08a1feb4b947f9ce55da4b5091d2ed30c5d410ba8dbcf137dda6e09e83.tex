By \(\texttt{prop:exact-unbiasedness}\), conditional on \(\mathcal F_{b-1}\), \(\mathbb E(\widehat\tau_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\tau_b\).  Assumptions \(\texttt{ass:nonanticipation}\) and \(\texttt{ass:finite-memory-lag-one}\) ensure that \(\tau_b\), which uses the two-period sustained-exposure endpoints of block \(b\), is a fixed finite-population contrast rather than a function of the current random draw.  Therefore
\[
 \mathbb E(R_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})
 =\sqrt G\{\mathbb E(\widehat\tau_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})-\tau_b\}=0.
\]
Moreover, the definitions of \(\widehat\tau\), \(\tau\), and \(R_b\) give the pathwise identity
\[
 \sqrt{GB}(\widehat\tau-\tau)
 =\frac{\sqrt{GB}}B\sum_{b=1}^B(\widehat\tau_b-\tau_b)
 =B^{-1/2}\sum_{b=1}^BR_b.
\]
