Fix one triangular array in \(\mathcal M_{G,B}\) and a vector \(t\in\mathbb R^2\).  The claim is immediate for \(t=0\), so suppose \(t\neq0\).  Put
\[
 \xi_{bB}=B^{-1/2}t^\top R_b,
 \qquad \mathcal V_B=\sum_{b=1}^B
 \mathbb E(\xi_{bB}^2\mid\mathcal F_{b-1}).
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:martingale-centering}\), \((\xi_{bB},\mathcal F_b)\) is a martingale-difference array and
\[
 \mathcal V_B
 =t^\top\left(B^{-1}\sum_{b=1}^B\Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}\right)t
 \longrightarrow_P t^\top\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}t                 \tag{8}
\]
by \(\texttt{ass:rr-covariance-stabilization}\).  The fourth-moment result gives, for every \(\epsilon>0\),
\[
 \sum_{b=1}^B\mathbb E\!\left[
   \xi_{bB}^2\mathbf1\{|\xi_{bB}|>\epsilon\}
   \mid\mathcal F_{b-1}\right]
 \leq\frac1{\epsilon^2B^2}\sum_{b=1}^B
       \mathbb E\{(t^\top R_b)^4\mid\mathcal F_{b-1}\}
 \leq\frac{C_t}{\epsilon^2B}\longrightarrow0.                 \tag{9}
\]
Thus the conditional Lindeberg condition is derived rather than assumed.

Let \(V_B=\mathbb E\mathcal V_B\).  The fourth-moment bound makes \(\mathcal V_B\) uniformly integrable; hence (8) implies
\[
 V_B\longrightarrow t^\top\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}t,
 \qquad \mathcal V_B/V_B\longrightarrow_P1.                    \tag{10}
\]
Moreover, the unconditional Lindeberg sum in \(\texttt{lem:li-zhao-martingale-clt}\) is bounded, for each fixed threshold, by the expectation of the conditional sum in (9), and is therefore \(O(B^{-1})\).  Applying that cited martingale-array theorem to \(\{\xi_{bB},\mathcal F_b\}\), then using (10), gives
\[
 B^{-1/2}\sum_bt^\top R_b
 \Rightarrow N(0,t^\top\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}t).
\]
The assumption \(\lambda_{\min}(\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}})>0\) makes this limit nondegenerate for \(t\neq0\).  Cram\'er--Wold, whose proof is the equality of all one-dimensional characteristic functions, gives the asserted bivariate Gaussian limit.  Finally, the displayed estimator identity is exactly \(\texttt{lem:martingale-centering}\).

The conclusion is pointwise over triangular arrays.  A uniform-over-class conclusion would require a uniform rate in (8), which is not among the frozen primitive conditions.
