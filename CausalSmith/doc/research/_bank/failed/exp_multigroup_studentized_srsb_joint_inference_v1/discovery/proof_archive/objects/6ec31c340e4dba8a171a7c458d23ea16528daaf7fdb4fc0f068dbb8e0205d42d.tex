For every member of \(\mathcal M_{G,B}\) operated under the predictable soft two-stage design and every block,
\[
\mathbb E(\widehat\Gamma_b\mid\mathcal F_{b-1})=\Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}+D_b,
\qquad D_b\succeq0.
\]
Along every sequence in \(\mathcal M_{G,B}\) with \(B\to\infty\) and arbitrary admissible even \(G\), including fixed \(G\), suppose separately that
\[
B^{-1}\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_B}\Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}\to_P\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}.
\]
Then
\[
\widehat\Gamma=\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}+B^{-1}\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_B}D_b+o_P(1).
\]
Both \(\widehat\Gamma_b\) and \(D_b\) are uniformly bounded in Frobenius norm, and every probability limit point of \(\widehat\Gamma\) dominates \(\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}\) in Loewner order.  If, in addition, \(\lambda_{\min}(\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}})>0\), then clipping is inactive with probability tending to one and \(\|\widehat\Gamma_+^{-1}\|=O_P(1)\).
