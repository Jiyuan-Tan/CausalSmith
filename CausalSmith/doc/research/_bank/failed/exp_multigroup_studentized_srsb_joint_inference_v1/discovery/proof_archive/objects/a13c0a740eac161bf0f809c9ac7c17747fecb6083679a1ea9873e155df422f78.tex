Hudgens--Halloran (2008) and Jiang--Imai--Malani (2023) define and estimate direct and spillover contrasts under static two-stage partial interference.  Bojinov--Rambachan--Shephard (2021) supplies the ordinary no-future-assignment nonanticipation convention for dynamic potential outcomes; the stronger lag-one finite-memory equality used here is an imposed restriction tailored to endpoints after two-period held assignments and is not attributed to that paper.  Yu--Ma--Liu (2025) adds carryover and a minimax timing design, but its first-stage law is fixed rather than predictably reweighted by prior outcomes.  Zhou et al. (2018) optimize sequential covariate balance without spillover or carryover inference.  Li--Zhao (2026) provides the adaptive finite-population martingale template, but not dependent two-stage exposure scores.  Li--Ding--Rubin (2018) and Lu et al. (2023) give static hard-rerandomization projection geometry.  Li--Ding (2017), Shi--Ding (2026), and Tian--Yang--Ding (2025) supply finite-population Gaussian approximation tools, but not radial soft weighting of unbounded score quadratics on singular covariance support.  Jizhou Liu (2026) covers static two-stage covariate-adaptive inference, He--Tchetgen Tchetgen (2026) covers broader static two-stage interference, and Liu--Shaikh--Zhong (2026) covers static saturation randomization tests; these results sharpen the boundary around the outcome-adaptive repeated-block theorem proved here.
