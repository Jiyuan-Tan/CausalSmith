Existing papers separately cover static spillover-and-carryover designs, sequential balance, adaptive martingale inference, and static rerandomization projection geometry.  They do not provide the history-uniform soft-tilt quadratic-moment transport that joins fixed-half group saturations to a joint direct-and-spillover score, nor the resulting separation between arbitrary-\(G\), many-block inference conditions and the \(G\to\infty\) projection conditions.
