As \(G\to\infty\), uniformly over blocks and histories and without any restriction on \(G/B\), \(\Sigma_{b,\mathrm{RR}}=\Sigma_{b,0}-(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})\beta_b\beta_b^\top+o(1)\). Along every sequence with \(G\to\infty\) on which the complete-randomization, soft-rerandomization, and projection covariance stabilizations hold,
\[
\Omega_{\mathrm{RR}}=\Omega_{\mathrm{CR}}-(1-v_{\eta,\kappa})P.
\]
For every fixed contrast \(c\in\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}\), soft rerandomization has strictly smaller asymptotic variance than complete randomization if and only if \(c^\top Pc>0\).
