Put
\[
R_Y=\operatorname{span}\{\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}:\beta_Y\in\mathcal L_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\}.
\]
Fix a class \(\mathfrak C\) satisfying
\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)
\subseteq\mathfrak C
\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A).
\]

Suppose first that \(r_{z^{\circ}}\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\) and \(e_y\in R_Y\).  The first membership supplies \(c\) with
\[
c^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm b}=r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T},
\]
and the second supplies an admissible \(\mu^y\) by \(\texttt{def:terminal-synthesis}\).  For every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak C\), the upper class inclusion gives \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\).  Applying \(\texttt{lem:finite-edge-bow-contraction}\) yields
\[
Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)
=\sum_{\beta_X\in\mathcal L_{X_{\mathrm b}}}
c_{\beta_X}q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y;\mu^y).
\]
By \(\texttt{def:terminally-synthesized-bow-response}\), every term on the right is an explicit finite linear combination of coordinates of \(\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal M)\).  The same lemma shows that the value is independent of the admissible choices of \(c\) and \(\mu^y\).  Therefore, if \(\mathcal M,\mathcal M'\in\mathfrak C\) have equal evidence, applying this same arithmetic functional to both tensors gives
\[
Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)=Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M').
\]
By \(\texttt{def:class-indexed-global-probe-identification}\), this is \(\operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}^{\mathfrak C}(Q_{z^{\circ},y})\).  Notice that this direction used the upper class inclusion and stochastic normalization only, not either strict-positivity assumption.

For necessity of the treatment condition, suppose \(r_{z^{\circ}}\notin\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\).  Let \((w_i)_{i=1}^d\) be an orthonormal basis of \(\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\), with the sum below interpreted as zero if \(d=0\), and define
\[
h=r_{z^{\circ}}-\sum_{i=1}^d(w_i^{\mathsf T}r_{z^{\circ}})w_i.
\]
Then \(h\) is orthogonal to every row of \(A_{\mathrm b}\), so \(A_{\mathrm b}h=0\).  The failed membership gives \(h\neq0\), and orthogonality gives
\[
r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}h=\lVert h\rVert_2^2>0.
\]
Thus \(h\in\ker(A_{\mathrm b})\) satisfies the premise of \(\texttt{lem:bow-positive-twins}\).  That lemma constructs \(\mathcal M_h^+,\mathcal M_h^-\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\) with equal evidence and unequal queries, exactly \(m\) shared-edge source states, and primitive cell floor at least \(1/(2\max\{m,k\})\).  The lower class inclusion puts both witnesses in \(\mathfrak C\), so the implication defining \(\operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}^{\mathfrak C}\) fails.

For necessity of the terminal condition, suppose \(e_y\notin R_Y\).  The lemma \(\texttt{lem:bow-terminal-effect-positive-twins}\) supplies \(\mathcal M,\mathcal M'\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\) with singleton exogenous alphabets, a common uniform treatment mechanism, primitive cell floor at least \(\min\{1/m,1/(2k)\}\), equal evidence, and unequal queries.  Again the lower class inclusion puts the pair in \(\mathfrak C\), so class-indexed identification fails.  These two cases exhaust failure of the conjunction and prove the equivalence and both certified obstructions.

Finally, \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{edge}}\) follows directly by dropping the strict inequalities in \(\texttt{def:primitive-positive-edge-scm-class}\), while \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{edge}}\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{edge}}\) is tautological.  At \(\mathfrak C=\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\), \(\texttt{def:global-probe-identification}\) and \(\texttt{def:class-indexed-global-probe-identification}\) have identical model quantifiers, so the class-indexed predicate is the original \(\operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}\).  Taking instead \(\mathfrak C=\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\) gives the asserted full-class corollary.
