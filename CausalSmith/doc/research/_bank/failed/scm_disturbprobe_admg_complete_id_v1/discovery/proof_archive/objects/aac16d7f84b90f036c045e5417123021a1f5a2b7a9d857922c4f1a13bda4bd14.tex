We verify every part of the counterexample.  Let \(V=\{w,d\}\), let both observed alphabets be \(\{0,1\}\), and let \(G\) have no directed or bidirected edges.  Thus \(\operatorname{dis}_{\mathcal C}(w)=\{w\}\) and \(\operatorname{de}_{\mathcal C}(w)=\{w\}\), so \(w\) satisfies \(\text{ass:fixable-vertex}\).  The one-branch effects satisfy instrument normalization because their sum is \(1\), and the stated \(\psi\)'s are full-support probability states.

First, \(q^{\star}=1\) is attainable.  For example, give each private source a singleton alphabet with mass \(1\), and set both parentless structural kernels equal to the Bernoulli \(1/2\) law.  This model has independent sources, source-mass floor \(1\), and mechanism-cell floor \(1/2\), hence belongs to \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G,\mathcal A)\).  By the definitions of \(W_{\mathcal M}\) and \(\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}\), its sole accessible coordinate is
\[
\begin{aligned}
q^{\star}
&=\sum_{a,z}W_{\mathcal M}(a\mid z)
  \prod_{v\in\{w,d\}}1\cdot\frac12\\
&=\frac14\sum_z\sum_a W_{\mathcal M}(a\mid z)
=\frac14\sum_z1=1,
\end{aligned}
\]
because for every \(z\) the finite kernel \(W_{\mathcal M}(\cdot\mid z)\) is normalized and there are four binary values of \(z\).  The definition of \(\mathcal Q_{\mathcal I}\) therefore gives \(q^{\star}\in\mathcal Q_{\mathcal I}\).

Choose the four entries of the sole column of \(R_{\mathcal C}\) to be the displayed entries of \(k_{\mathcal C}\).  Since \(q^{\star}=1\), the definition of the reconstruction matrix gives exactly that table.  It is strictly positive and sums to \(1\).  Here \(\operatorname{mb}_{\mathcal C}(w)=\varnothing\), so the marginal matrices give
\[
(U_wk_{\mathcal C})(0)=(U_wk_{\mathcal C})(1)=\frac12,
\qquad V_wk_{\mathcal C}=1.
\]
The coordinate-fixing definition consequently gives
\[
\kappa_D(d,w)=\frac{k_{\mathcal C}(d,w)}{1/2}=2k_{\mathcal C}(d,w),
\]
which is the second displayed table.  Its entries are strictly positive and each column sums to \(1\), so \(N_D\kappa_D=\mathbf1\), also as guaranteed by \(\text{prop:one-fixing-coordinate-validity}\).  Because \(\Omega_D=\varnothing\), the equality \(O_D\kappa_D=S_Dq^{\star}\) has no rows and is vacuous.  Every defining condition of \(\mathcal K_D^{\mathrm{alg}}(q^{\star})\) is therefore satisfied.

It remains to prove non-realizability.  Consider any \(\mathcal M'\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G,\mathcal A)\).  There are no edge sources, and the only sources \(U_w,U_d\) are private and independent.  With
\[
 \mu_w(x)=\sum_{u_w}p_w(u_w)f_w(x\mid u_w),
 \qquad
 \mu_d(x)=\sum_{u_d}p_d(u_d)f_d(x\mid u_d),
\]
the independent-source factorization and the empty graph give, equation by equation,
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathcal M'}(w=x,d=t)
&=\sum_{u_w,u_d}p_w(u_w)p_d(u_d)
 f_w(x\mid u_w)f_d(t\mid u_d)\\
&=\left(\sum_{u_w}p_w(u_w)f_w(x\mid u_w)\right)
  \left(\sum_{u_d}p_d(u_d)f_d(t\mid u_d)\right)\\
&=\mu_w(x)\mu_d(t).
\end{aligned}
\]
Strict source and mechanism positivity imply \(\mu_w(x)>0\) and \(\mu_d(t)>0\).  Ordinary fixing of \(w\), using the quotient proved in \(\text{prop:one-fixing-coordinate-validity}\), is therefore
\[
 \frac{P_{\mathcal M'}(w=x,d=t)}{P_{\mathcal M'}(w=x)}
 =\frac{\mu_w(x)\mu_d(t)}{\mu_w(x)}=\mu_d(t),
\]
which is independent of the fixed value \(x\).  Any realizable kernel must consequently satisfy \(\kappa_D(0,0)=\kappa_D(0,1)\).  The constructed certificate violates this equality because \(4/5\ne1/5\), so no \(\mathcal M'\) exists.  A fortiori there cannot be a pair \(\mathcal M,\mathcal M'\) having common observed-node tables and the other properties requested by the open question.

For completeness, we prove sufficiency and the asserted bounds in this instance.  Let \(\kappa_D\) be any strictly positive normalized binary kernel satisfying \(\kappa_D(0,0)=\kappa_D(0,1)=p\).  Column normalization gives \(\kappa_D(1,0)=\kappa_D(1,1)=1-p\), and strict positivity gives \(0<p<1\).  Give both private sources singleton alphabets of mass \(1\), set
\[
 f_d(0)=p,\qquad f_d(1)=1-p,
 \qquad f_w(0)=f_w(1)=\frac12,
\]
and take \(\mathcal M=\mathcal M'\).  The sources are independent, all source masses are at least \(1\), all mechanism cells are at least \(\min\{1/2,p,1-p\}>0\), and the same one-branch calculation gives accessible evidence \(q^{\star}=1\).  Fixing \(w\) yields \(f_d=\kappa_D\), so this is the required shared-mechanism lift.  Finally, a normalized binary kernel given binary \(w\) is parametrized by the two numbers \(\kappa_D(0,0)\) and \(\kappa_D(0,1)\); the realizable subset is cut out by their single equality.  Hence the equality is independent and minimal in this instance.
