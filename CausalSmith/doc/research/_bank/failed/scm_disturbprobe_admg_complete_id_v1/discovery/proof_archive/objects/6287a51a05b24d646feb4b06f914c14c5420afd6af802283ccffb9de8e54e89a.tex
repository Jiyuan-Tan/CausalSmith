At \(X_{\mathrm b}\), each of the first two instruments has the single effect \(\mathbf 1\), and the third has effects \(e_0,e_1\) with \(e_0+e_1=\mathbf 1\).  At \(Y_{\mathrm b}\), the sole instrument has effects \(e_0,e_1\), again summing to \(\mathbf 1\).  Thus instrument normalization holds at both vertices.  The tagged treatment effects contain \(e_0,e_1\), so their span is \(\mathbb R^2\).  The tagged treatment states contain
\[
 \psi^+=\begin{pmatrix}3/4\\1/4\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \psi^-=\begin{pmatrix}1/4\\3/4\end{pmatrix},
\]
whose determinant is \(1/2\ne0\), and hence their span is also \(\mathbb R^2\).  The two terminal effects are \(e_0,e_1\), so the weaker effect-only terminal condition holds for both outcomes.  In addition, the displayed matching states give the unused stronger sanity identities
\[
 (a,z)\longmapsto e_i(a)e_i(z)=\mathbf 1\{a=i,z=i\}=d_i(a,z),
 \qquad i\in\{0,1\}.
\]

By \texttt{def:binary-bow-menu} and \texttt{def:bow-probe-matrix}, the four treatment rows are
\[
 u_+=\mathbf 1\otimes\psi^+,
 \qquad u_-=\mathbf 1\otimes\psi^-,
 \qquad v_0=e_0\otimes\psi^+,
 \qquad v_1=e_1\otimes\psi^+.
\]
They satisfy \(u_+=v_0+v_1\), so their rank is at most three.  If
\[
 \alpha v_0+\beta v_1+\gamma u_-=0,
\]
restriction to the \(a_{\mathrm b}=0\) coordinates gives \(\alpha\psi^++\gamma\psi^-=0\).  Independence of \(\psi^+,\psi^-\) yields \(\alpha=\gamma=0\), and restriction to the \(a_{\mathrm b}=1\) coordinates then gives \(\beta\psi^+=0\), hence \(\beta=0\).  Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{rank}(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger})=3<4.
\]
Direct calculation gives
\[
 e_0=\frac32\psi^+-\frac12\psi^-,
 \qquad
 e_1=-\frac12\psi^++\frac32\psi^-.
\]
Since \(r_i=\mathbf 1\otimes e_i\), it follows that
\[
 r_0=\frac32u_+-\frac12u_-,
 \qquad
 r_1=-\frac12u_++\frac32u_-.
\]
Thus \(r_0,r_1\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger})\).  The two conditions of \texttt{thm:bow-rowspan-iff} now hold for each treatment value and each outcome value, so both binary intervention-response distributions are globally identified throughout the primitive-positive latent-bow class.  Nevertheless the treatment branch map has four coordinates and rank only three, so full treatment-table tomography is impossible.  Identification is therefore query specific and non-tomographic.
