After relabeling \(\mathcal A_{X_{\mathrm b}}=\{0,\ldots,m-1\}\), let \(\delta_i\in\mathbb R^{\mathcal A_{X_{\mathrm b}}}\) be the \(i\)-th coordinate vector, let \(\mathbf 1_X=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\delta_i\), and put \(\rho_i=\tfrac12\delta_i+\tfrac1{2m}\mathbf 1_X\) for \(0\le i<m\).  The finite-alphabet frontier menu \(\mathcal I_{\mathrm b}^{\mathrm{fr}}\) has at \(X_{\mathrm b}\) one instrument with the \(2m-1\) effect-state branches \((\tfrac12\delta_i,\rho_0)\), \(0\le i<m\), and \((\tfrac1{2(m-1)}\mathbf 1_X,\rho_i)\), \(1\le i<m\).  At \(Y_{\mathrm b}\) it has one basis instrument with branches \((e_y,e_y)\), \(y\in\mathcal A_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\).  Its treatment probe matrix \(A_{\mathrm b}^{\mathrm{fr}}\) has rows \(\tfrac12\delta_i\rho_0^{\mathsf T}\), \(0\le i<m\), and \(\tfrac1{2(m-1)}\mathbf 1_X\rho_i^{\mathsf T}\), \(1\le i<m\).
