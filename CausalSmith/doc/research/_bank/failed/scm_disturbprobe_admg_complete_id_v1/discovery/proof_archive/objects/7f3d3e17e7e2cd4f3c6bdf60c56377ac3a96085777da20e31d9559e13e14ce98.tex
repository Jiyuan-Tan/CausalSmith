Let
\[
 R_Y=\operatorname{span}\{\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}:\beta_Y\in\mathcal L_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\}
 \subseteq\mathbb R^{\mathcal A_{Y_{\mathrm b}}},
\]
and suppose \(e_y\notin R_Y\).  Fix any terminal instrument label \(j_Y\).  By \texttt{ass:instrument-normalization},
\[
 \mathbf 1=\sum_{b_Y\in\mathcal B_{Y_{\mathrm b},j_Y}}
 \phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},(j_Y,b_Y)}\in R_Y.
\]
In particular, \(R_Y\) is nonzero.  Choose a matrix \(S\) whose columns form a basis of \(R_Y\).  The columns are linearly independent, so \(S^{\mathsf T}S\) is positive definite and invertible.  Put
\[
 \gamma=e_y-S(S^{\mathsf T}S)^{-1}S^{\mathsf T}e_y.
\]
Then \(\gamma\perp R_Y\), and \(\gamma\ne0\) because \(e_y\notin R_Y\).  Writing \(e_y=S\alpha+\gamma\), where \(S\alpha\perp\gamma\), gives
\[
 \gamma(y)=e_y^{\mathsf T}\gamma=\lVert\gamma\rVert_2^2>0.
\]
Since \(\mathbf 1\in R_Y\) and \(\gamma\perp R_Y\), it follows that
\[
 \sum_{a_{\mathrm t}\in\mathcal A_{Y_{\mathrm b}}}\gamma(a_{\mathrm t})=0.
\]
Let \(L_\gamma=\lVert\gamma\rVert_\infty>0\) and \(t_\gamma=1/(2kL_\gamma)>0\).  For \(\sigma\in\{+1,-1\}\), construct \(\mathcal N^\sigma\) on \(G_{\mathrm b}\) as follows.  Give every private and bidirected-edge source a singleton alphabet with mass one; these masses are strictly positive and factor independently.  Set
\[
 f_{X_{\mathrm b}}^\sigma(a_{\mathrm b}\mid u)=\frac1m,
 \qquad
 f_{Y_{\mathrm b}}^\sigma(a_{\mathrm t}\mid z_{\mathrm b},u)
 =\frac1k+\sigma t_\gamma\gamma(a_{\mathrm t}).
\]
The displayed terminal kernel ignores its allowed parent and source arguments, which is permitted by the bow graph.  Its cells sum to one because \(\sum_{a_{\mathrm t}}\gamma(a_{\mathrm t})=0\), and
\[
 f_{Y_{\mathrm b}}^\sigma(a_{\mathrm t}\mid z_{\mathrm b},u)
 \ge \frac1k-t_\gamma L_\gamma=\frac1{2k}>0.
\]
The treatment cells equal \(1/m>0\).  Thus \texttt{ass:independent-sources}, \texttt{ass:strict-source-positivity}, \texttt{ass:strict-mechanism-positivity}, and \texttt{def:primitive-positive-edge-scm-class} imply
\[
 \mathcal N^+,\mathcal N^-\in
 \mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A),
\]
with primitive cell floor at least \(\min\{1/m,1/(2k)\}\).

The split-port kernel is explicitly
\[
 W_{\mathcal N^\sigma}((a_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t})\mid
 (z_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm t}))
 =\frac1m\left(\frac1k+\sigma t_\gamma\gamma(a_{\mathrm t})\right).
\]
For any accessible branch pair, \texttt{def:probe-evidence-map} and finite factorization of the sums give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\bar q^{+}_{\beta_X,\beta_Y}-\bar q^{-}_{\beta_X,\beta_Y}\\
 &\quad=\frac{2t_\gamma}{m}
 \left(\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}
 \phi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(a_{\mathrm b})
 \psi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(z_{\mathrm b})\right)
 \left(\sum_{a_{\mathrm t},z_{\mathrm t}}
 \phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(a_{\mathrm t})
 \psi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(z_{\mathrm t})
 \gamma(a_{\mathrm t})\right)\\
 &\quad=\frac{2t_\gamma}{m}
 \left(\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}
 \phi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(a_{\mathrm b})
 \psi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(z_{\mathrm b})\right)
 \left(\sum_{a_{\mathrm t}}
 \phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(a_{\mathrm t})\gamma(a_{\mathrm t})\right)
 =0.
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(\sum_{z_{\mathrm t}}\psi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(z_{\mathrm t})=1\), and the last inner product vanishes because \(\gamma\perp R_Y\).  Hence every coordinate agrees and
\[
 \mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal N^+)=\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal N^-).
\]
On the other hand, \texttt{def:atomic-query-contraction} gives
\[
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal N^\sigma)
 =\sum_{a_{\mathrm b}}\frac1m
 \left(\frac1k+\sigma t_\gamma\gamma(y)\right)
 =\frac1k+\sigma t_\gamma\gamma(y),
\]
and therefore
\[
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal N^+)-Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal N^-)
 =2t_\gamma\gamma(y)>0.
\]
These are the required strictly positive same-evidence twins.
