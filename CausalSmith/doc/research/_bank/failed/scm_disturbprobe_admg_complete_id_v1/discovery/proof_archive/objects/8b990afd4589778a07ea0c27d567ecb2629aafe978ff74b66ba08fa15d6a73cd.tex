For every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\) and every normalized separable menu on \(G_{\mathrm b}\), suppose \(e_y\in\operatorname{span}\{\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}:\beta_Y\in\mathcal L_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\}\).  Then every admissible effect synthesis \(\mu^y\) satisfies
\[
q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y;\mu^y)
=\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}A_{\mathrm b}[\beta_X,(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})]
B_{\mathcal M,y}(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}),
\qquad
Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)
=\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}r_{z^{\circ}}[a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}]
B_{\mathcal M,y}(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}).
\]
Consequently, whenever \(c^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm b}=r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}\),
\[
Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)=
\sum_{\beta_X\in\mathcal L_{X_{\mathrm b}}}c_{\beta_X}q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y;\mu^y),
\]
and the right-hand side is invariant under all admissible choices of \(c\) and \(\mu^y\).  These identities use stochastic normalization but no strict positivity.
