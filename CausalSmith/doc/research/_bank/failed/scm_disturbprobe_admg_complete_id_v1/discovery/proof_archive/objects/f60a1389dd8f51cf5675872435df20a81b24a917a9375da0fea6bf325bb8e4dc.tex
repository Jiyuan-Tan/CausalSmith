Assume \(r_{z^{\circ}}\notin\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\), and take the stipulated \(h\in\ker(A_{\mathrm b})\) with \(r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}h\ne0\).  By \texttt{lem:bow-twin-coordinate-sanity}, \(L>0\), so every object in \texttt{def:bow-positive-twin-construction} is well defined.

The shared edge source has exactly \(m\) states, each of mass \(1/m>0\); every private source has one state of mass one.  For each \(u_{\mathrm b}\), the treatment kernel is normalized because
\[
 \sum_{a_{\mathrm b}}
 \left(\frac12\mathbf 1\{a_{\mathrm b}=u_{\mathrm b}\}
 +\frac{1}{2m}\right)=\frac12+\frac12=1,
\]
and every one of its cells is at least \(1/(2m)>0\).  For the outcome kernel, the perturbations at \(y\) and \(y^{\dagger}\) cancel, so its \(k\) cells sum to one.  Moreover,
\[
 |t g(u_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})|
 \le tL=\frac{1}{2k}.
\]
Thus each modified outcome cell is at least \(1/k-1/(2k)=1/(2k)>0\), and every unmodified cell is \(1/k\).  It follows from \texttt{ass:independent-sources}, \texttt{ass:strict-source-positivity}, \texttt{ass:strict-mechanism-positivity}, and \texttt{def:primitive-positive-edge-scm-class} that both \(\mathcal M_h^+\) and \(\mathcal M_h^-\) lie in \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\).

The lower bounds just obtained also give a common primitive-cell floor.  Indeed,
\[
 \min\left\{1,\frac1m,\frac{1}{2m},\frac{1}{2k}\right\}
 =\frac{1}{2\max\{m,k\}}.
\]
Finally, \texttt{lem:bow-twin-coordinate-sanity} proves coordinate by coordinate that all branch-pair evidence differences vanish, while
\[
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M_h^+)-Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M_h^-)
 =2t r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}h\ne0.
\]
Hence the two legal same-observed-alphabet models have identical \(\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}\), unequal targets, the asserted source-cardinality bound, and the asserted positive floor.
