Fix a normalized separable menu on \(G_{\mathrm b}\), an attainable tensor generated by \(\mathcal M\), and \(y\in\mathcal A_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\) such that \(e_y\) belongs to the span of the terminal effect vectors.  By \texttt{def:terminal-synthesis}, an admissible \(\mu^y\) obeys, for every \(a_{\mathrm t}\in\mathcal A_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\),
\[
 \sum_{\beta_Y\in\mathcal L_{Y_{\mathrm b}}}
 \mu^y_{\beta_Y}\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(a_{\mathrm t})
 =e_y(a_{\mathrm t})
 =\mathbf 1\{a_{\mathrm t}=y\}.
\]
This is the asserted pointwise effect synthesis.

The vertex \(Y_{\mathrm b}\) has no directed child in \(G_{\mathrm b}\).  Hence \(Y_{\mathrm b}\notin\operatorname{pa}_{G_{\mathrm b}}(v)\) for both vertices \(v\), and inspection of \texttt{def:split-port-kernel} shows that the terminal input coordinate is absent from every structural-kernel factor.  Thus
\[
 W_{\mathcal M}((a_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t})\mid
 (z_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm t}))
 =W_{\mathcal M}((a_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t})\mid
 (z_{\mathrm b},z'_{\mathrm t}))
\]
for all terminal inputs \(z_{\mathrm t},z'_{\mathrm t}\).  Denote this common value by \(\overline W_{\mathcal M}(a_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t}\mid z_{\mathrm b})\).

By \texttt{lem:split-port-reduction} and \texttt{def:bow-probe-matrix}, every pair of tagged branches satisfies
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \bar q_{\beta_X,\beta_Y}
 ={}&\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t},z_{\mathrm t}}
 \overline W_{\mathcal M}(a_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t}\mid z_{\mathrm b})
 A_{\mathrm b}[\beta_X,(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})]\\
 &\hspace{31mm}\cdot
 \phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(a_{\mathrm t})
 \psi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(z_{\mathrm t}).
\end{aligned}
\]
All alphabets are finite, so the order of summation may be exchanged.  Moreover, each \(\psi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}\) is a probability vector and therefore sums to one.  Using \texttt{def:terminally-synthesized-bow-response} and then the effect-synthesis equation gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y;\mu^y)
 & =\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t}}
 \overline W_{\mathcal M}(a_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t}\mid z_{\mathrm b})
 A_{\mathrm b}[\beta_X,(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})]
 \sum_{\beta_Y}\mu^y_{\beta_Y}
 \phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(a_{\mathrm t})
 \sum_{z_{\mathrm t}}\psi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(z_{\mathrm t})\\
 & =\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t}}
 \overline W_{\mathcal M}(a_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t}\mid z_{\mathrm b})
 A_{\mathrm b}[\beta_X,(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})]
 \mathbf 1\{a_{\mathrm t}=y\}\\
 & =\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}
 A_{\mathrm b}[\beta_X,(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})]
 W_{\mathcal M}((a_{\mathrm b},y)\mid(z_{\mathrm b},y))\\
 & =\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}
 A_{\mathrm b}[\beta_X,(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})]
 B_{\mathcal M,y}(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}).
\end{aligned}
\]
The apparently branch-dependent state vectors have disappeared precisely because terminality made the kernel independent of \(z_{\mathrm t}\).

For the causal query, \texttt{def:atomic-query-contraction} sets \(z_{\mathrm b}=z^{\circ}\), sets the nonintervened terminal input equal to its output, and sets that output to \(y\).  Therefore, by \texttt{def:bow-target-coefficient},
\[
\begin{aligned}
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)
 &=\sum_{a_{\mathrm b}}
 W_{\mathcal M}((a_{\mathrm b},y)\mid(z^{\circ},y))\\
 &=\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}
 r_{z^{\circ}}[a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}]
 B_{\mathcal M,y}(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}).
\end{aligned}
\]
If \(\widetilde\mu^y\) is another solution of the effect-synthesis equations, the preceding calculation applies verbatim and gives the same vector \(A_{\mathrm b}B_{\mathcal M,y}\).  Hence
\[
 q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y;\mu^y)
 =q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y;\widetilde\mu^y)
\]
for every \(\beta_X\).  The common evidence-determined coordinate is therefore well defined as \(q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y)\).
