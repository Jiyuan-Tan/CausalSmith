Set
\[
 R_Y=\operatorname{span}\{\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}:\beta_Y\in\mathcal L_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\}.
\]
First suppose that \(r_{z^{\circ}}\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\) and \(e_y\in R_Y\).  The first membership supplies a finite vector \(c\) such that
\[
 c^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm b}=r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T},
\]
and the second supplies an effect-synthesis vector \(\mu^y\) as in \texttt{def:terminal-synthesis}.  For every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\), \texttt{lem:terminal-synthesis-invariance} gives
\[
 q^{\mathrm b}(y)=A_{\mathrm b}b_y,
 \qquad
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)=r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}b_y,
\]
where \(b_y[a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}]=B_{\mathcal M,y}(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})\).  Hence
\[
\begin{aligned}
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)
 &=c^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm b}b_y\\
 &=\sum_{\beta_X\in\mathcal L_{X_{\mathrm b}}}
 c_{\beta_X}q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y).
\end{aligned}
\]
By \texttt{def:terminally-synthesized-bow-response}, the right-hand side is an explicit arithmetic functional of the accessible tensor.  Its value is independent of the admissible terminal synthesis by \texttt{lem:terminal-synthesis-invariance}, and independent of the admissible treatment coefficient by \texttt{lem:bow-coefficient-invariance}.  Consequently, equality of evidence implies equality of the causal query for every pair of models in the primitive-positive class, which is exactly \(\operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}(Q_{z^{\circ},y})\) by \texttt{def:global-probe-identification}.

For the converse, suppose first that \(r_{z^{\circ}}\notin\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\).  Let \(R_X=\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\).  Treatment-effect normalization and nonemptiness of each branch set imply that some treatment effect is nonzero; its paired state is a probability vector and hence nonzero, so \texttt{def:bow-probe-matrix} gives a nonzero row of \(A_{\mathrm b}\).  Thus \(R_X\ne\{0\}\).  Choose a matrix \(S_X\) whose columns form a basis of \(R_X\), and define
\[
 h=r_{z^{\circ}}-S_X(S_X^{\mathsf T}S_X)^{-1}S_X^{\mathsf T}r_{z^{\circ}}.
\]
Then \(h\perp R_X\), so \(A_{\mathrm b}h=0\); moreover \(h\ne0\) and
\[
 r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}h=\lVert h\rVert_2^2>0.
\]
Thus the hypotheses of the already-established \texttt{lem:bow-positive-twins} hold.  That lemma produces \(\mathcal M_h^+,\mathcal M_h^-\) in the primitive-positive class with identical accessible evidence and unequal \(Q_{z^{\circ},y}\).  The implication in \texttt{def:global-probe-identification} therefore fails.

Suppose instead that \(e_y\notin R_Y\).  The proved \texttt{lem:bow-terminal-effect-positive-twins} produces two primitive-positive bow models with identical accessible evidence and query difference \(2t_\gamma\gamma(y)>0\).  Global identification again fails.  These two cases exhaust the failure of
\[
 r_{z^{\circ}}\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})
 \quad\text{and}\quad e_y\in R_Y.
\]
Together with sufficiency, they establish the exact two-sided criterion, the displayed invariant functional on success, and an explicit positive common-fiber obstruction on either kind of failure.
