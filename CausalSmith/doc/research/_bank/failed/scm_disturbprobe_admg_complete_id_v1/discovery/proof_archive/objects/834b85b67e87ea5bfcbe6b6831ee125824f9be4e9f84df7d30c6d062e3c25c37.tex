First suppose \(r_{z^{\circ}}\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\).  Then there is a finite coefficient vector \(c\) with
\[
 c^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm b}=r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}.
\]
For every model \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\), \texttt{lem:terminal-synthesis-invariance} gives
\[
 q^{\mathrm b}(y)=A_{\mathrm b}b_y,
 \qquad
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)=r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}b_y,
\]
where \(b_y[a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}]=B_{\mathcal M,y}(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})\).  Consequently,
\[
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)
 =c^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm b}b_y
 =\sum_{\beta_X\in\mathcal L_{X_{\mathrm b}}}
 c_{\beta_X}q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y).
\]
The right-hand side is an explicit arithmetic functional of \(\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal M)\).  Therefore two models with the same evidence have the same query, which is precisely \(\operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}(Q_{z^{\circ},y})\) by \texttt{def:global-probe-identification}.  Independence from the terminal synthesis vector follows from \texttt{lem:terminal-synthesis-invariance}, and independence from the choice of \(c\) follows from \texttt{lem:bow-coefficient-invariance}.

Conversely, suppose \(r_{z^{\circ}}\notin\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\).  For completeness, the needed null direction follows directly from finite-dimensional linear algebra.  Let \(R=\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\), choose a basis \(s_1,\ldots,s_d\) of \(R\), and let \(S\) be the matrix with these basis vectors as columns.  Its Gram matrix \(S^{\mathsf T}S\) is positive definite because the columns are independent.  Define
\[
 s=S(S^{\mathsf T}S)^{-1}S^{\mathsf T}r_{z^{\circ}},
 \qquad h=r_{z^{\circ}}-s.
\]
Then \(s\in R\), and \(S^{\mathsf T}h=0\), so \(h\perp R\).  Every row of \(A_{\mathrm b}\) belongs to \(R\), hence \(A_{\mathrm b}h=0\).  Since \(r_{z^{\circ}}\notin R\), one has \(h\ne0\), and
\[
 r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}h=(s+h)^{\mathsf T}h
 =\lVert h\rVert_2^2>0.
\]
Thus \(h\in\ker(A_{\mathrm b})\) has the separation required by \texttt{lem:bow-positive-twins}.  That lemma supplies \(\mathcal M_h^+,\mathcal M_h^-\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\) such that
\[
 \mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal M_h^+)=
 \mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal M_h^-),
 \qquad
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M_h^+)\ne
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M_h^-).
\]
This single attainable common fiber violates the implication in \texttt{def:global-probe-identification}.  Therefore global identification fails.  The two implications prove the equivalence and the asserted functional/certificate alternatives.
