\(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{fin}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G,\mathcal A)\) is the class of all finite canonical edge-source SCMs \(\mathcal M=((p_s)_{s\in\mathcal S_G},(f_v)_{v\in V})\) such that
\[
p_{\mathcal M}^{U}(u)=\prod_{s\in\mathcal S_G}p_s(u_s),\qquad
p_s(u_s)\geq0,\qquad
\sum_{u_s\in\mathcal U_s}p_s(u_s)=1,
\]
for every \(s\in\mathcal S_G\), and, for every vertex \(v\), every admissible parent-input and source context,
\[
f_v(a_v\mid z_{\operatorname{pa}_G(v)},u_v,u_{\operatorname{bi}_G(v)})\geq0,
\qquad
\sum_{a_v\in\mathcal A_v}f_v(a_v\mid z_{\operatorname{pa}_G(v)},u_v,u_{\operatorname{bi}_G(v)})=1.
\]
No strict-positivity condition is imposed.
