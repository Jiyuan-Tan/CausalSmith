Put
\[
R_Y=\operatorname{span}\{\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}:\beta_Y\in\mathcal L_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\}.
\]
First suppose \(r_{z^{\circ}}\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\) and \(e_y\in R_Y\).  The first membership supplies \(c\) with
\[
c^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm b}=r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T},
\]
and the second supplies an effect-synthesis vector \(\mu^y\) by \(\texttt{def:terminal-synthesis}\).  For \(b_y\in\mathbb R^{m^2}\) defined by \(b_y[a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}]=B_{\mathcal M,y}(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})\), \(\texttt{lem:terminal-synthesis-invariance}\) gives, for every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\),
\[
q^{\mathrm b}(y)=A_{\mathrm b}b_y,
\qquad
Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)=r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}b_y.
\]
Consequently
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M)
&=c^{\mathsf T}A_{\mathrm b}b_y\\
&=\sum_{\beta_X\in\mathcal L_{X_{\mathrm b}}}c_{\beta_X}q^{\mathrm b}_{\beta_X}(y).
\end{aligned}
\]
By \(\texttt{def:terminally-synthesized-bow-response}\), this is an explicit arithmetic functional of the accessible evidence tensor.  Its value is independent of the admissible \(\mu^y\) by \(\texttt{lem:terminal-synthesis-invariance}\), and independent of the admissible \(c\) by \(\texttt{lem:bow-coefficient-invariance}\).  Equal evidence therefore gives equal queries for every pair of models in the primitive-positive class.  This is exactly \(\operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}(Q_{z^{\circ},y})\) by \(\texttt{def:global-probe-identification}\).

For necessity of the treatment condition, suppose \(r_{z^{\circ}}\notin\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\).  Let \((w_i)_{i=1}^d\) be an orthonormal basis of \(\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})\), allowing \(d=0\), and define
\[
h=r_{z^{\circ}}-\sum_{i=1}^d(w_i^{\mathsf T}r_{z^{\circ}})w_i.
\]
Then \(h\) is orthogonal to every row of \(A_{\mathrm b}\), hence \(A_{\mathrm b}h=0\).  The failed row-span membership gives \(h\ne0\), and orthogonality gives
\[
r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}h=\lVert h\rVert_2^2>0.
\]
Thus \(h\in\ker(A_{\mathrm b})\) satisfies the hypotheses of \(\texttt{lem:bow-positive-twins}\).  That lemma constructs \(\mathcal M_h^+,\mathcal M_h^-\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\) with identical \(\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}\), unequal \(Q_{z^{\circ},y}\), exactly \(m\) shared-edge source states, and the asserted primitive cell floor.  Hence the implication in \(\texttt{def:global-probe-identification}\) fails.

For necessity of the terminal condition, suppose \(e_y\notin R_Y\).  The already-established \(\texttt{lem:bow-terminal-effect-positive-twins}\), whose hypotheses include \(\texttt{ass:instrument-normalization}\), constructs two members of \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\) with singleton exogenous alphabets, a common uniform treatment mechanism, primitive cell floor at least \(\min\{1/m,1/(2k)\}\), identical evidence, and unequal queries.  Global identification again fails.  Failure of the conjunction is exhausted by these two cases.  Together with sufficiency, this proves the equivalence, the invariant identifying functional on success, and the explicit primitive-positive common-fiber obstruction on either failure side.
