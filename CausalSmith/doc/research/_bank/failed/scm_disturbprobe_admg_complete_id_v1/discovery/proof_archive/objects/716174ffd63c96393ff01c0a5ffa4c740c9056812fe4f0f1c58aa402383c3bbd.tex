For completeness, the front-door equality can be derived directly from the independent-source semantics used in \texttt{lem:split-port-reduction}.  In \(G_{\mathrm f}\), let \(U\) denote the source on \(X_{\mathrm f}\leftrightarrow Y_{\mathrm f}\), and integrate the independent private sources into stochastic kernels \(\alpha(x\mid u)\), \(\mu(m\mid x)\), and \(\gamma(y\mid m,u)\).  The graph and \texttt{ass:independent-sources} give
\[
 P(x,m,y)=\sum_u p(u)\alpha(x\mid u)\mu(m\mid x)\gamma(y\mid m,u).
\]
There is no shared source between \(X_{\mathrm f}\) and \(M_{\mathrm f}\), so \(\mu(m\mid x)=P(m\mid x)\).  If
\[
 \pi_x(u)=\frac{p(u)\alpha(x\mid u)}{P(x)},
\]
then positivity gives
\[
 P(y\mid x,m)=\sum_u\pi_x(u)\gamma(y\mid m,u).
\]
Consequently,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sum_xP(x)P(y\mid x,m)
 &=\sum_{x,u}p(u)\alpha(x\mid u)\gamma(y\mid m,u)\\
 &=\sum_up(u)\gamma(y\mid m,u),
\end{aligned}
\]
because \(\sum_x\alpha(x\mid u)=1\).  Under \(\operatorname{do}(X_{\mathrm f}=x_0)\), the shared source retains its marginal \(p(u)\), the mediator is drawn from \(\mu(\cdot\mid x_0)\), and \(Y_{\mathrm f}\) has no direct \(X_{\mathrm f}\)-parent.  Finite marginalization therefore gives the ordinary front-door functional
\[
 P(Y_{\mathrm f}=y\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{\mathrm f}=x_0))
 =\sum_mP(m\mid x_0)\sum_xP(x)P(y\mid x,m).
\]
For \(y=1\), the inner sums at \(m=0\) and \(m=1\) are respectively
\[
 \frac12\frac18+\frac12\frac38=\frac14,
 \qquad
 \frac12\frac58+\frac12\frac78=\frac34.
\]
Thus
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P(Y_{\mathrm f}=1\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{\mathrm f}=0))
 &=\frac34\frac14+\frac14\frac34=\frac38,\\
 P(Y_{\mathrm f}=1\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{\mathrm f}=1))
 &=\frac14\frac14+\frac34\frac34=\frac58.
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves both the reconstruction and the two exact causal values.
