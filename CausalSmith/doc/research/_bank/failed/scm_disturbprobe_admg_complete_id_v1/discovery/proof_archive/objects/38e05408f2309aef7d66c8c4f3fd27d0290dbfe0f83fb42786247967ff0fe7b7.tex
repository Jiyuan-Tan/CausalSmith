For a fixed input coordinate \(z_{\mathrm b}\), the corresponding block of \(T\) is
\[
 C=\frac{1}{2m}I+\frac{1}{2m^2}J,
 \qquad J=\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Since \(J^2=mJ\), direct multiplication gives
\[
 C(2mI-J)
 =I-\frac{1}{2m}J+\frac{1}{m}J-\frac{1}{2m}J=I,
\]
and the same computation in the reverse order gives \((2mI-J)C=I\).  Thus every block is invertible with inverse \(2mI-J\).  The Kronecker factor \(\mathbf 1\{z_{\mathrm b}=z'_{\mathrm b}\}\) in \texttt{def:bow-positive-twin-construction} makes \(T\) block diagonal with exactly \(m\) such blocks, so \(T\) is an invertible \(m^2\)-by-\(m^2\) matrix.

On the block indexed by \(z_{\mathrm b}\), the displayed definition of \(g\) is precisely
\[
 g(\,cdot\,,z_{\mathrm b})=(2mI-J)h(\,\cdot\,,z_{\mathrm b}).
\]
Consequently \(Tg=h\).  The condition \(r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}h\ne0\) implies \(h\ne0\), and invertibility of \(T\) then implies \(g\ne0\).  Since the array is finite, \(L=\lVert g\rVert_\infty>0\), so \(t=1/(2kL)\) is well defined and positive.

For the distinguished outcome \(y\), the two outcome kernels differ by
\[
 f^+_{Y_{\mathrm b}}(y\mid z_{\mathrm b},u_{\mathrm b})
 -f^-_{Y_{\mathrm b}}(y\mid z_{\mathrm b},u_{\mathrm b})
 =2t g(u_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}).
\]
Using the uniform shared source and the common treatment kernel from \texttt{def:bow-positive-twin-construction}, the response-table difference is therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_y^+(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})-B_y^-(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})
 &=2t\sum_{u_{\mathrm b}}
 \left(\frac{\mathbf 1\{a_{\mathrm b}=u_{\mathrm b}\}}{2m}
 +\frac{1}{2m^2}\right)g(u_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})\\
 &=2t(Tg)(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})
 =2t h(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}).
\end{aligned}
\]
The response-table difference at \(y^{\dagger}\) is the negative of this array, and it is zero at every other outcome cell.

Expand an arbitrary branch-pair evidence coordinate using \texttt{def:probe-evidence-map}.  The bow outcome mechanism does not depend on the terminal output port \(z_{\mathrm t}\), and \(\sum_{z_{\mathrm t}}\psi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(z_{\mathrm t})=1\).  Hence the preceding two nonzero outcome differences yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &[\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal M_h^+)]_{\beta_X,\beta_Y}
 -[\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal M_h^-)]_{\beta_X,\beta_Y}\\
 &\quad=2t\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}
 \phi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(a_{\mathrm b})
 \psi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(z_{\mathrm b})h(a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})
 \bigl[\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(y)
 -\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(y^{\dagger})\bigr]\\
 &\quad=2t(A_{\mathrm b}h)_{\beta_X}
 \bigl[\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(y)
 -\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(y^{\dagger})\bigr]=0,
\end{aligned}
\]
where \(A_{\mathrm b}h=0\) follows from \(h\in\ker(A_{\mathrm b})\).  Finally, \texttt{def:atomic-query-contraction} sums the \(y\)-response table over \(a_{\mathrm b}\) at \(z_{\mathrm b}=z^{\circ}\).  Therefore
\[
 Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M_h^+)-Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal M_h^-)
 =2t\sum_{a_{\mathrm b}}h(a_{\mathrm b},z^{\circ})
 =2t r_{z^{\circ}}^{\mathsf T}h\ne0.
