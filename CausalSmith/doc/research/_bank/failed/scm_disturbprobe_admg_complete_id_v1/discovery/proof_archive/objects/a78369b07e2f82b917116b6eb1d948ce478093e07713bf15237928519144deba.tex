At \(X_{\mathrm b}\), the first and second instruments each have the single effect \(\mathbf 1\), and the third has effects \(e_0,e_1\) with \(e_0+e_1=\mathbf 1\).  Thus \texttt{ass:instrument-normalization} holds separately for all three instruments.  The tagged effects contain \(e_0,e_1\), so their span is \(\mathbb R^2\).  The tagged states contain
\[
 \psi^+=\begin{pmatrix}3/4\\1/4\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \psi^-=\begin{pmatrix}1/4\\3/4\end{pmatrix},
\]
whose two-by-two determinant is \(1/2\ne0\); hence their span is also \(\mathbb R^2\).  At \(Y_{\mathrm b}\), the matching branches have state-effect products
\[
 (a,z)\longmapsto e_i(a)e_i(z)
 =\mathbf 1\{a=i, z=i\}=d_i(a,z),
 \qquad i\in\{0,1\}.
\]
Thus joint terminal target-span holds for both outcomes.

By \texttt{def:binary-bow-menu} and \texttt{def:bow-probe-matrix}, the four treatment rows are
\[
 u_+=\mathbf 1\otimes\psi^+,
 \quad u_-=\mathbf 1\otimes\psi^-,
 \quad v_0=e_0\otimes\psi^+,
 \quad v_1=e_1\otimes\psi^+.
\]
They satisfy \(u_+=v_0+v_1\), so the rank is at most three.  To see that it is exactly three, suppose
\[
 \alpha v_0+\beta v_1+\gamma u_-=0.
\]
Restricting first to the \(a_{\mathrm b}=0\) coordinates gives \(\alpha\psi^++\gamma\psi^-=0\); independence of \(\psi^+,\psi^-\) gives \(\alpha=\gamma=0\).  Restricting to \(a_{\mathrm b}=1\) then gives \(\beta\psi^+=0\), hence \(\beta=0\).  Therefore \(v_0,v_1,u_-\) are independent and
\[
 \operatorname{rank}(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger})=3<4.
\]
Direct arithmetic gives
\[
 e_0=\frac32\psi^+-\frac12\psi^- ,
 \qquad
 e_1=-\frac12\psi^++\frac32\psi^- .
\]
Using \(r_i=\mathbf 1\otimes e_i\), it follows that
\[
 r_0=\frac32u_+-\frac12u_- ,
 \qquad
 r_1=-\frac12u_++\frac32u_- .
\]
Thus \(r_0,r_1\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger})\).  Applying \texttt{thm:bow-rowspan-iff} separately to \(i=0,1\) proves global identification of both intervention responses throughout the primitive-positive latent-bow class.  Since the treatment branch map has four coordinates but rank only three, it is not tomographically recoverable.  The identification is therefore query specific and non-tomographic; no mechanism-recovery claim beyond this latent-bow calculation is used.
