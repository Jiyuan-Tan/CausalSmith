Let
\[
R_Y=\operatorname{span}\{\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}:\beta_Y\in\mathcal L_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\}
\subseteq\mathbb R^{\mathcal A_{Y_{\mathrm b}}},
\]
and suppose \(e_y\notin R_Y\).  Fix any terminal instrument label \(j_Y\), which exists because \(\mathcal J_{Y_{\mathrm b}}\) is nonempty.  By \(\texttt{ass:instrument-normalization}\),
\[
\mathbf 1=\sum_{b_Y\in\mathcal B_{Y_{\mathrm b},j_Y}}
\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},(j_Y,b_Y)}\in R_Y.
\]
Choose a matrix \(S\) whose columns form a basis of \(R_Y\).  Its columns are linearly independent, so \(S^{\mathsf T}S\) is positive definite and invertible.  Define
\[
\gamma=e_y-S(S^{\mathsf T}S)^{-1}S^{\mathsf T}e_y.
\]
Then \(\gamma\perp R_Y\), and \(\gamma\neq0\) because \(e_y\notin R_Y\).  The orthogonal decomposition of \(e_y\) into its projection on \(R_Y\) and \(\gamma\) gives
\[
\gamma(y)=e_y^{\mathsf T}\gamma=\lVert\gamma\rVert_2^2>0.
\]
Since \(\mathbf 1\in R_Y\) and \(\gamma\perp R_Y\),
\[
\sum_{a_{\mathrm t}\in\mathcal A_{Y_{\mathrm b}}}\gamma(a_{\mathrm t})=0.
\]

Let \(L_\gamma=\lVert\gamma\rVert_\infty>0\) and \(t_\gamma=1/(2kL_\gamma)>0\).  For \(\sigma\in\{+1,-1\}\), construct \(\mathcal N^\sigma\) on \(G_{\mathrm b}\) by giving every private and bidirected-edge source a singleton alphabet with mass one and setting
\[
f_{X_{\mathrm b}}^\sigma(a_{\mathrm b}\mid u)=\frac1m,
\qquad
f_{Y_{\mathrm b}}^\sigma(a_{\mathrm t}\mid z_{\mathrm b},u)
=\frac1k+\sigma t_\gamma\gamma(a_{\mathrm t}).
\]
The terminal kernel may ignore its permitted parent and source arguments.  Its cells sum to one because \(\sum_{a_{\mathrm t}}\gamma(a_{\mathrm t})=0\), and
\[
f_{Y_{\mathrm b}}^\sigma(a_{\mathrm t}\mid z_{\mathrm b},u)
\geq\frac1k-t_\gamma L_\gamma=\frac1{2k}>0.
\]
The treatment cells equal \(1/m>0\).  The singleton source masses factor independently and equal one.  Hence \(\texttt{ass:independent-sources}\), \(\texttt{ass:strict-source-positivity}\), \(\texttt{ass:strict-mechanism-positivity}\), and \(\texttt{def:primitive-positive-edge-scm-class}\) give
\[
\mathcal N^+,\mathcal N^-\in
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A),
\]
with primitive cell floor at least \(\min\{1/m,1/(2k)\}\).

Expanding \(\texttt{def:split-port-kernel}\),
\[
W_{\mathcal N^\sigma}((a_{\mathrm b},a_{\mathrm t})\mid(z_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm t}))
=\frac1m\left(\frac1k+\sigma t_\gamma\gamma(a_{\mathrm t})\right).
\]
For any accessible branch pair, \(\texttt{def:probe-evidence-map}\), finite rearrangement of sums, and probability-state normalization give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\bar q^+_{\beta_X,\beta_Y}-\bar q^-_{\beta_X,\beta_Y}
&=\frac{2t_\gamma}{m}
\left(\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}
\phi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(a_{\mathrm b})
\psi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(z_{\mathrm b})\right)
\left(\sum_{a_{\mathrm t},z_{\mathrm t}}
\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(a_{\mathrm t})
\psi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}(z_{\mathrm t})
\gamma(a_{\mathrm t})\right)\\
&=\frac{2t_\gamma}{m}
\left(\sum_{a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b}}
\phi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(a_{\mathrm b})
\psi_{X_{\mathrm b},\beta_X}(z_{\mathrm b})\right)
\left\langle\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y},\gamma\right\rangle
=0.
\end{aligned}
\]
The last equality uses \(\phi_{Y_{\mathrm b},\beta_Y}\in R_Y\) and \(\gamma\perp R_Y\).  Thus all evidence coordinates agree.  On the other hand, \(\texttt{def:atomic-query-contraction}\) gives
\[
Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal N^\sigma)
=\sum_{a_{\mathrm b}}\frac1m
\left(\frac1k+\sigma t_\gamma\gamma(y)\right)
=\frac1k+\sigma t_\gamma\gamma(y),
\]
so
\[
Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal N^+)-Q_{z^{\circ},y}(\mathcal N^-)
=2t_\gamma\gamma(y)>0.
\]
These are the required strictly positive same-evidence twins.
