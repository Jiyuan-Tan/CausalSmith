Write the three tagged treatment branches of the corrected menu as \(\beta_0,\beta_1,\beta_2\), and abbreviate their rows in the column order \((a_{\mathrm b},z_{\mathrm b})\) by
\[
w_0=\tfrac12 e_0\otimes\psi^+,
\qquad
w_1=\tfrac12 e_1\otimes\psi^+,
\qquad
w_2=\tfrac12\mathbf 1\otimes\psi^-.
\]
These are exactly the rows of \(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger}\) by \(\texttt{def:binary-bow-menu}\) and \(\texttt{def:bow-probe-matrix}\).  At \(X_{\mathrm b}\), the sum of the three branch effects is
\[
\tfrac12e_0+\tfrac12e_1+\tfrac12\mathbf 1
=\tfrac12(e_0+e_1)+\tfrac12\mathbf 1
=\mathbf 1,
\]
so the unique treatment instrument is normalized.  At \(Y_{\mathrm b}\), the two branch effects sum to \(e_0+e_1=\mathbf 1\), so the terminal instrument is normalized as well.  The treatment effects \(\tfrac12e_0,\tfrac12e_1\) already span \(\mathbb R^2\).  The treatment states include
\[
\psi^+=\begin{pmatrix}3/4\\1/4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\psi^-=\begin{pmatrix}1/4\\3/4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\det\begin{pmatrix}3/4&1/4\\1/4&3/4\end{pmatrix}=\tfrac12\ne0,
\]
and therefore span \(\mathbb R^2\).  The terminal effects are \(e_0,e_1\), hence also span \(\mathbb R^2\).  Moreover, for \(i\in\{0,1\}\), the matching terminal effect and state obey
\[
(e_i\otimes e_i)(a_{\mathrm t},z_{\mathrm t})
=\mathbf 1\{a_{\mathrm t}=i\}\mathbf 1\{z_{\mathrm t}=i\}
=d_i(a_{\mathrm t},z_{\mathrm t}),
\]
which proves the two asserted terminal identities.

To compute the treatment rank, suppose
\[
\alpha w_0+\beta w_1+\gamma w_2=0.
\]
Restriction to the two coordinates with \(a_{\mathrm b}=0\) gives
\[
\tfrac\alpha2\psi^++\tfrac\gamma2\psi^-=0.
\]
The displayed nonzero determinant implies \(\alpha=\gamma=0\).  Restriction to the coordinates with \(a_{\mathrm b}=1\) then gives \(\tfrac\beta2\psi^+=0\), and \(\psi^+\ne0\) implies \(\beta=0\).  Thus \(w_0,w_1,w_2\) are independent and
\[
\operatorname{rank}(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger})=3<4=m^2.
\]

The two state identities
\[
e_0=\tfrac32\psi^+-\tfrac12\psi^- ,
\qquad
e_1=-\tfrac12\psi^++\tfrac32\psi^-
\]
follow by substituting the displayed coordinates of \(\psi^+\) and \(\psi^-\).  By \(\texttt{def:bow-target-coefficient}\), \(r_i=\mathbf 1\otimes e_i\).  Consequently,
\[
r_0=3w_0+3w_1-w_2,
\qquad
r_1=-w_0-w_1+3w_2,
\]
so \(r_0,r_1\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger})\).  For each \(y\in\{0,1\}\), the terminal effect \(e_y\) lies in the terminal effect span.  The success direction of \(\texttt{thm:bow-rowspan-iff}\), applied for each \(z^{\circ},y\in\{0,1\}\), therefore proves global identification on \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\).  More explicitly, for any admissible terminal synthesis, the theorem's invariant functional may use treatment coefficient vectors \((3,3,-1)\) for \(z^{\circ}=0\) and \((-1,-1,3)\) for \(z^{\circ}=1\).  Finally, the treatment table has \(m^2=4\) coordinates while its probe matrix has rank three, so the linear treatment probe map is not injective and full treatment-table tomography is impossible.  Identification is thus query specific and non-tomographic.
