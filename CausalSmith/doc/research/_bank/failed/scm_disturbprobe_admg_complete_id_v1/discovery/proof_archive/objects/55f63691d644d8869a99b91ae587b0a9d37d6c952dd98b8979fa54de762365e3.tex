Let \(\mathcal I\) be any menu in the class quantified by the theorem.  Its all-query identification premise includes
\[
 \operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}(Q_{0,0})
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}(Q_{1,0}).
\]
Apply the only-if direction of the already-established \(\texttt{thm:bow-rowspan-iff}\) to these two queries.  It gives, respectively,
\[
 r_0\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b})
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 r_1\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b}).
\]
The same theorem's terminal condition is also satisfied because the menu's terminal effects span \(\mathbb R^2\).  The treatment-effect span premise and the two displayed row inclusions are exactly the hypotheses of \(\texttt{thm:binary-bow-universal-rank-lower-bound}\).  Hence
\[
 \operatorname{rank}(A_{\mathrm b})\ge3.
\]
Because each tagged treatment branch contributes exactly one row to \(A_{\mathrm b}\), every menu in the class also satisfies
\[
 \lvert\mathcal L_{X_{\mathrm b}}\rvert\ge3.
\]

For attainment, the corrected \(\texttt{def:binary-bow-menu}\) has exactly one treatment instrument and exactly three tagged branches, with rows
\[
 w_0=\tfrac12e_0\otimes\psi^{+},\qquad
 w_1=\tfrac12e_1\otimes\psi^{+},\qquad
 w_2=\tfrac12\mathbf 1\otimes\psi^{-}.
\]
The proved \(\texttt{thm:binary-bow-query-separation}\) establishes that this menu is normalized, has treatment effect and state spans \(\mathbb R^2\), has terminal effect span \(\mathbb R^2\), has rank three, and identifies every binary atomic bow query throughout \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\).  In particular, its proof gives the explicit certificates
\[
 r_0=3(w_0+w_1)-w_2,
 \qquad
 r_1=-(w_0+w_1)+3w_2.
\]
Thus rank three and three tagged branches are both attained, so the two lower bounds are exact.

For a load-bearing check, remove only treatment-effect completeness.  Use two normalized one-branch treatment instruments with effects \(\mathbf 1,\mathbf 1\) and states \(e_0,e_1\), and retain the terminal basis instrument.  Then \(\texttt{def:bow-probe-matrix}\) gives the two treatment rows
\[
 \mathbf 1\otimes e_0=r_0,
 \qquad
 \mathbf 1\otimes e_1=r_1.
\]
The matrix has rank two, its state span is \(\mathbb R^2\), and the terminal effect span is \(\mathbb R^2\).  Hence \(\texttt{thm:bow-rowspan-iff}\) identifies all four atomic queries, but the treatment effects span only \(\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf 1\}\).  This exact counterexample confirms that treatment-effect completeness is the load-bearing premise excluding rank two.  The argument proves a minimum over the stated class and explicit attainment; it does not assert that rank three alone is sufficient for identification.
