Write \(\mathbf 1=e_0+e_1\) and, using the corrected construction in \(\texttt{def:binary-bow-menu}\), set
\[
 \phi_0=\tfrac14\mathbf 1+\tfrac14e_0=(\tfrac12,\tfrac14)^{\mathsf T},\qquad
 \phi_1=\tfrac14\mathbf 1+\tfrac14e_1=(\tfrac14,\tfrac12)^{\mathsf T},\qquad
 \phi_2=\tfrac14\mathbf 1=(\tfrac14,\tfrac14)^{\mathsf T}.
\]
The three effects are nonnegative and
\[
 \phi_0+\phi_1+\phi_2
 =\tfrac34\mathbf 1+\tfrac14(e_0+e_1)
 =\mathbf 1.
\]
Thus the single treatment instrument is normalized.  The terminal instrument is normalized as well because its two effects obey \(e_0+e_1=\mathbf 1\).  This verifies the normalization hypothesis of \(\texttt{thm:bow-rowspan-iff}\).

The determinant of the matrix having \(\phi_0,\phi_1\) as columns is
\[
 \det\!\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac12&\tfrac14\\[2pt]\tfrac14&\tfrac12\end{pmatrix}
 =\tfrac{3}{16}\ne0,
\]
so the tagged treatment effects span \(\mathbb R^2\).  Likewise, by the definitions of \(\psi^+\) and \(\psi^-\),
\[
 \det\!\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac34&\tfrac14\\[2pt]\tfrac14&\tfrac34\end{pmatrix}
 =\tfrac12\ne0,
\]
so the tagged treatment states span \(\mathbb R^2\).  The terminal effects are \(e_0,e_1\), hence they span \(\mathbb R^2\).  Moreover, for \(i\in\{0,1\}\), the terminal branch with effect and state both equal to \(e_i\) obeys
\[
 (e_i\otimes e_i)(a_{\mathrm t},z_{\mathrm t})
 =\mathbf 1\{a_{\mathrm t}=i\}\mathbf 1\{z_{\mathrm t}=i\}
 =d_i(a_{\mathrm t},z_{\mathrm t}),
\]
which proves the two displayed terminal sanity identities.

By \(\texttt{def:bow-probe-matrix}\), the treatment rows of \(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger}\) are
\[
 w_0=\phi_0\otimes\psi^+,qquad
 w_1=\phi_1\otimes\psi^+,qquad
 w_2=\phi_2\otimes\psi^-.
\]
They are linearly independent.  Indeed, suppose
\[
 \alpha w_0+\beta w_1+\gamma w_2=0.
\]
Because \(\psi^+,\psi^-\) are a basis of \(\mathbb R^2\), there is a linear functional \(\lambda_-\) on the state factor with \(\lambda_-(\psi^+)=0\) and \(\lambda_-(\psi^-)=1\).  Contracting the displayed tensor equality against \(\lambda_-\) gives \(\gamma\phi_2=0\).  Since \(\phi_2\ne0\), \(\gamma=0\).  A functional \(\lambda_+\) satisfying \(\lambda_+(\psi^+)=1\) then gives \(\alpha\phi_0+\beta\phi_1=0\); the nonzero determinant above implies \(\alpha=\beta=0\).  Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{rank}(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger})=3<4,
\]
where four is the number of treatment input--output coordinates.

It remains to check the two query rows.  Put \(u_+=\mathbf 1\otimes\psi^+\) and \(u_-=\mathbf 1\otimes\psi^-\).  Since \(\phi_0+\phi_1=\tfrac34\mathbf 1\) and \(\phi_2=\tfrac14\mathbf 1\),
\[
 u_+=\tfrac43(w_0+w_1),
 \qquad
 u_-=4w_2.
\]
Direct coordinate calculation gives
\[
 e_0=\tfrac32\psi^+-\tfrac12\psi^- ,
 \qquad
 e_1=-\tfrac12\psi^++\tfrac32\psi^- .
\]
Using \(r_i=\mathbf 1\otimes e_i\), as fixed by \(\texttt{def:bow-target-coefficient}\), we therefore obtain the explicit row representations
\[
 r_0=2w_0+2w_1-2w_2,
 \qquad
 r_1=-\tfrac23w_0-\tfrac23w_1+6w_2.
\]
Hence \(r_0,r_1\in\operatorname{row}(A_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger})\).

For each \(z\in\{0,1\}\) and \(y\in\{0,1\}\), the treatment row condition just proved and the terminal effect-span condition satisfy both sides of the success criterion in \(\texttt{thm:bow-rowspan-iff}\).  That theorem therefore yields \(\operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I_{\mathrm b}^{\dagger}}(Q_{z,y})\) throughout \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G_{\mathrm b},\mathcal A)\).  Thus both binary intervention-response distributions are globally identified.  On the other hand, the linear map from the four treatment input--output coordinates to the three accessible treatment branch coordinates has rank three, so it has a nonzero kernel and cannot tomograph an arbitrary four-coordinate treatment table.  The identification is consequently query-specific and non-tomographic.
