For an SCM class \(\mathfrak C\) on \((G,\mathcal A)\),
\[
\operatorname{GID}_{\mathcal I}^{\mathfrak C}(Q_{x,y})
\Longleftrightarrow
\bigl[\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal M)=\mathcal E_{\mathcal I}(\mathcal M')
\Longrightarrow Q_{x,y}(\mathcal M)=Q_{x,y}(\mathcal M')\bigr]
\]
for every \(\mathcal M,\mathcal M'\in\mathfrak C\).
