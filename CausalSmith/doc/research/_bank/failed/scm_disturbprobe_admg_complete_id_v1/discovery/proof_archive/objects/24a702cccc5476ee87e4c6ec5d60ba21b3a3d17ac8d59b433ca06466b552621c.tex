The universal shared-mechanism lift assertion is false for the displayed algebraic certificate.  In particular, take the empty ADMG on two binary random vertices \(w\) and \(d\), put \(D=\{d\}\) and \(H=\varnothing\), give each vertex one one-branch probe with \(\phi\equiv1\) and \(\psi(0)=\psi(1)=1/2\), take \(\Omega_D=\varnothing\), and choose the sole column of \(R_{\mathcal C}\) so that, at \(q^{\star}=1\),
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
k_{\mathcal C}(d,w)&w=0&w=1\\ \hline
d=0&2/5&1/10\\
d=1&1/10&2/5
\end{array}.
\]
Then \(q^{\star}\in\mathcal Q_{\mathcal I}\) and the coordinate fixing image \(\kappa_D=\Phi_w(k_{\mathcal C}(q^{\star}))\), given by
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
\kappa_D(d,w)&w=0&w=1\\ \hline
d=0&4/5&1/5\\
d=1&1/5&4/5
\end{array},
\]
belongs to \(\mathcal K_D^{\mathrm{alg}}(q^{\star})\), but it is not the one-step fixed district kernel of any \(\mathcal M'\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{pp}}^{\mathrm{edge}}(G,\mathcal A)\), irrespective of a shared-mechanism requirement.  For the same binary empty graph, one-branch menu, and evidence \(q^{\star}=1\), a strictly positive normalized candidate kernel has a shared-mechanism lift if and only if it satisfies the additional equality
\[
 \kappa_D(0,0)=\kappa_D(0,1)
\]
is necessary and sufficient for a strictly positive lift.  It is the unique independent equality cutting the realizable kernels out of the two-dimensional space of normalized positive binary kernels given \(w\).  If its common value is \(p\in(0,1)\), a shared lift may be chosen with \(\mathcal M=\mathcal M'\), one state in each private-source alphabet, source-mass floor \(1\), and structural-kernel cell floor \(\min\{1/2,p,1-p\}>0\).
