**Statement.** For the total data maps \((\widehat\theta,\widehat V)\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\), \[\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_4}P\!\left(\left|\sqrt n(\widehat\theta-\theta)-n^{-1/2}\sum_{i=1}^nD(O_i)\right|>\varepsilon\right)\to0,\qquad \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_4}P\!\left(\left|\widehat V/V_P-1\right|>\varepsilon\right)\to0,\] and \[\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_4}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}\left|P\!\left(\frac{\sqrt n(\widehat\theta-\theta)}{\sqrt{\widehat V}}\le z\right)-\Phi(z)\right|\to0.\] The construction uses only \((O_i)_{i=1}^n\) and the known constants defining \(\mathcal P_4\).

### T-block: t2 — For every \(P\in\mathcal P_4^{\circ}\), \(D=D_1-D_0\) is…
**Statement.** For every \(P\in\mathcal P_4^{\circ}\), \(D=D_1-D_0\) is the observed-data canonical gradient of \(\theta\) in the locally unrestricted independent-censoring model, the tangent closure generated by admissible smooth submodels has squared norm \(V_P\), and every regular estimator has asymptotic variance at least \(V_P\); the estimator in thm:uniform-endpoint-efficiency attains this bound.

### T-block: t3 — The constructed law \(P^{\star}\) belongs to…
**Statement.** The constructed law \(P^{\star}\) belongs to \(\mathcal P_4^{\circ}\), and at \(P=P^{\star}\) it satisfies \(E_P|D(O)|^{5/2}<\infty\) and \(E_P|D(O)|^3=\infty\).

### T-block: t4 — For every fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1)\),…
**Statement.** For every fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), \[\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_4}\left|P\{\theta\in\mathcal I_{n,\alpha}\}-(1-\alpha)\right|\to0.\]

### T-block: t5 — The answer is affirmative
**Statement.** The answer is affirmative. The local-cubic handle in def:endpoint-learning-handle has a measurable total completion for which there are constants \(C,c>0\), depending only on the displayed class constants and the fixed polynomial bases, such that for all sufficiently large \(M\), \[ \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_4}P\!\left( \max_{a\in\{0,1\}}\lVert\widehat\lambda_a-\lambda_a\rVert_\infty+ \max_{a\in\{0,1\}}\lVert\widehat g_a-g_a\rVert_\infty+ \lVert\widehat\pi_1-\pi_1\rVert_\infty>C r_M\right) \le C M^{-2}+C J_M^2\exp(-cMh_M^3). \] In particular, \(r_M=O\{(\log M/M)^{1/3}\}\), uniformly over the arbitrary measurable covariate densities allowed by \(\mathcal P_4\).

### T-block: t6 — There are constants \(c_0,c_1>0\), depending only on the…
**Statement.** There are constants \(c_0,c_1>0\), depending only on the displayed primitive-class constants, such that, for every sufficiently large \(M\), \[ \inf_{\widetilde\lambda}\ \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_4} P^{\otimes M}\!\left\{ \max_{a\in\{0,1\}}\lVert\widetilde\lambda_a-\lambda_a\rVert_\infty \ge c_0\left(\frac{\log M}{M}\right)^{1/3} \right\}\ge c_1. \] The infimum ranges over all measurable estimators of the pair of event hazards based on the \(M\) observed data units. Thus the uniform sup-norm exponent in \(\textnormal{thm:primitive-endpoint-concentration}\) is minimax sharp over the same class \(\mathcal P_4\).
