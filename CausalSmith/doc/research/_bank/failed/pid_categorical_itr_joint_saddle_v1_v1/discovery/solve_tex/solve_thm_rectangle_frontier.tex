%%% FIELD statement-rectangle-frontier
For every compatible \(p_x\), \(v_x^{\Box}(p_x)=v_x(p_x)\) if and only if \(\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{val}}(p_x)\neq\varnothing\).  Moreover, simultaneous preservation of the value and complete optimizer correspondence,
\[
 v_x^{\Box}(p_x)=v_x(p_x)\quad\text{and}\quad Q_x^{\Box}(p_x)=Q_x^*(p_x),
\]
holds if and only if \(\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{arg}}(p_x)\) holds.  Both conditions admit finite observable basis certificates.
%%% FIELD proof-rectangle-frontier
Fix a stratum \(x\) and a compatible table \(p_x\).  By Assumption~\texttt{ass:response-fiber-compatibility} and Definitions~\texttt{def:response-fiber} and \texttt{def:action-mean-polytope}, \(\mathcal W_x(p_x)\) is a nonempty compact polytope and its linear image \(\mathcal M_x(p_x)\) is a nonempty compact polytope.  Consequently, every coordinate minimum \(L_{ax}\) and maximum \(U_{ax}\) in Definition~\texttt{def:rectangle} is attained.  Every \(\mu\in\mathcal M_x(p_x)\) therefore satisfies \(L_{ax}\leq\mu_a\leq U_{ax}\) for all \(a\in\mathcal A\), and hence
\[
 \mathcal M_x(p_x)\subseteq\mathcal M_x^{\Box}(p_x).
\tag{1}
\]
This is the set-inclusion step.

Define the two observed-law objective functions
\[
 f(q):=\max_{b\in\mathcal A}h_{\mathcal M_x(p_x)}(e_b-q),
 \qquad
 g(q):=\max_{b\in\mathcal A}h_{\mathcal M_x^{\Box}(p_x)}(e_b-q),
 \qquad q\in\Delta_K.
\tag{2}
\]
By Definitions~\texttt{def:joint-saddle} and \texttt{def:rectangle},
\[
 v_x(p_x)=\min_{q\in\Delta_K}f(q),\quad
 Q_x^*(p_x)=\operatorname*{argmin}_{q\in\Delta_K}f(q),
\quad
 v_x^{\Box}(p_x)=\min_{q\in\Delta_K}g(q),\quad
 Q_x^{\Box}(p_x)=\operatorname*{argmin}_{q\in\Delta_K}g(q).
\tag{3}
\]
Thus \(f\), \(g\), their minima, and their argmin sets are observed-law decision functionals.  Their causal content comes from the sharp response-function projection in Lemma~\texttt{lem:joint-saddle-background}; it is not introduced by (2).

Support functions are monotone under set inclusion: indeed, if \(S\subseteq T\), then for every \(d\),
\[
 h_S(d)=\max_{s\in S}d^\top s\leq\max_{t\in T}d^\top t=h_T(d).
\tag{4}
\]
Applying (4) to (1), first for each \(e_b-q\) and then taking the maximum over \(b\), gives
\[
 f(q)\leq g(q)\quad\text{for every }q\in\Delta_K,
 \qquad
 v_x(p_x)\leq v_x^{\Box}(p_x).
\tag{5}
\]
The functions in (2) are continuous because they are maxima of finitely many affine functions by Lemma~\texttt{lem:joint-saddle-background} and the rectangle calculation below.  Since \(\Delta_K\) is nonempty and compact, all four minimum and argmin objects in (3) are attained and the two argmin sets are nonempty.

We first prove the value certificate.  If \(q_0\in\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{val}}(p_x)\), Definition~\texttt{def:rectangle-frontier} gives \(g(q_0)\leq v_x(p_x)\).  Combining this inequality with (3) and (5) yields
\[
 v_x(p_x)leq v_x^{\Box}(p_x)leq g(q_0)leq v_x(p_x),
\tag{6}
\]
so \(v_x^{\Box}(p_x)=v_x(p_x)\).  Conversely, suppose that the two values are equal and choose \(q^{\Box}\in Q_x^{\Box}(p_x)\), whose existence was just established.  Equations (3) and the assumed equality give
\[
 g(q^{\Box})=v_x^{\Box}(p_x)=v_x(p_x).
\tag{7}
\]
Definition~\texttt{def:rectangle-frontier} then implies \(q^{\Box}\in\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{val}}(p_x)\), so that set is nonempty.  This proves the first equivalence.

We next prove the simultaneous value-and-argmin certificate.  Suppose \(\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{arg}}(p_x)\) holds.  By its definition, every \(q^*\in Q_x^*(p_x)\) belongs to \(\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{val}}(p_x)\).  Hence
\[
 v_x(p_x)=f(q^*)\leq g(q^*)\leq v_x(p_x).
\tag{8}
\]
It follows from (8) and the value equivalence already proved that \(v_x^{\Box}(p_x)=v_x(p_x)\) and \(q^*\in Q_x^{\Box}(p_x)\).  Therefore
\[
 Q_x^*(p_x)\subseteq Q_x^{\Box}(p_x).
\tag{9}
\]
For the reverse inclusion, take \(q^{\Box}\in Q_x^{\Box}(p_x)\).  Equations (3), (5), and equality of the values imply
\[
 v_x(p_x)\leq f(q^{\Box})\leq g(q^{\Box})
 =v_x^{\Box}(p_x)=v_x(p_x).
\tag{10}
\]
Thus \(f(q^{\Box})=v_x(p_x)\), so \(q^{\Box}\in Q_x^*(p_x)\).  Together with (9), this proves equality of the complete optimizer correspondences.

Conversely, assume simultaneous equality of the values and optimizer correspondences.  For every \(q^*\in Q_x^*(p_x)=Q_x^{\Box}(p_x)\),
\[
 g(q^*)=v_x^{\Box}(p_x)=v_x(p_x).
\tag{11}
\]
Equation (11) places every such \(q^*\) in \(\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{val}}(p_x)\).  Hence \(Q_x^*(p_x)\subseteq\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{val}}(p_x)\), which is precisely \(\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{arg}}(p_x)\) by Definition~\texttt{def:rectangle-frontier}.  This proves the second equivalence, including both directions at tied optimal faces.

It remains to expose the finite observable certificates.  For each feasible basis \(\beta\in\mathcal B_x(p_x)\), put
\[
 m_{x,\beta}:=C_\beta\widetilde B_\beta^{-1}\widetilde p_x.
\tag{12}
\]
The feasible-basis representation in Lemma~\texttt{lem:joint-saddle-background} and Definition~\texttt{def:reduced-saddle-lp} gives
\[
 f(q)=\max_{b\in\mathcal A,\,\beta\in\mathcal B_x(p_x)}
 G_{(b,\beta),\cdot}(p_x)q,
\quad
 L_{ax}=\min_{\beta\in\mathcal B_x(p_x)}e_a^\top m_{x,\beta},
\quad
 U_{ax}=\max_{\beta\in\mathcal B_x(p_x)}e_a^\top m_{x,\beta}.
\tag{13}
\]
The last two identities follow by applying the same vertex enumeration to the linear coordinate objectives \(\mu\mapsto\mu_a\).  All lists in (12)--(13) are finite, and every coefficient is a function of the observable table \(p_x\).

For \(q\in\Delta_K\), the coefficient of \(\mu_b\) in \((e_b-q)^\top\mu\) is \(1-q_b\geq0\), while the coefficient of \(\mu_a\) for \(a\neq b\) is \(-q_a\leq0\).  Coordinatewise maximization over the rectangle therefore gives the explicit affine functions
\[
 \gamma_b(q):=(1-q_b)U_{bx}-\sum_{a\neq b}q_aL_{ax},
 \qquad
 g(q)=\max_{b\in\mathcal A}\gamma_b(q).
\tag{14}
\]
Thus \(v_x(p_x)\) is obtained from the finite reduced saddle linear program, and value preservation is certified by feasibility of the finite linear system
\[
 q\geq0,\qquad \mathbf 1^\top q=1,\qquad
 \gamma_b(q)\leq v_x(p_x)\quad(b\in\mathcal A).
\tag{15}
\]
Indeed, (15) is exactly the membership condition \(q\in\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{val}}(p_x)\).

Finally define the observable joint-optimal face
\[
 \mathcal P_x^*(p_x):=left\{q\in\Delta_K:
 G_{(c,\beta),\cdot}(p_x)q\leq v_x(p_x)
 \text{ for every }c\in\mathcal A,
 \ \beta\in\mathcal B_x(p_x)\right\}.
\tag{16}
\]
Equations (3) and (13) show that \(\mathcal P_x^*(p_x)=Q_x^*(p_x)\).  Consequently, optimizer preservation is certified by the following \(K\) finite linear-program inequalities:
\[
 \max_{q\in\mathcal P_x^*(p_x)}\gamma_b(q)\leq v_x(p_x)
 \qquad(b\in\mathcal A).
\tag{17}
\]
Condition (17) is equivalent to \(Q_x^*(p_x)\subseteq\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{val}}(p_x)\), hence to \(\mathcal F_x^{\mathrm{arg}}(p_x)\).  Weak inequalities in (15)--(17) retain all boundary and tie cases.  This completes the finite, observable, operational certificate.
