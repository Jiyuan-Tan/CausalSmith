%%% FIELD ordered-dp-proof
Fix an input \((T,\Sigma,K,\pi)\), and put \(q_\pi=\max_{0\leq \ell\leq c}|B_\ell|\).  For a choice of roots in the first \(\ell\) components of the order, define
\[
D_\ell=\bigcup_{h=1}^{\ell}S_{\pi(h)}(r_{\pi(h)}),
\qquad
A_\ell=D_\ell\cap B_\ell,
\qquad
C_\ell=D_\ell\setminus B_\ell,
\qquad
b_\ell=|C_\ell|.
\]
Thus \((A_\ell,b_\ell)\) is exactly the state of \(\texttt{def:ordered-state}\), and
\[
|D_\ell|=|A_\ell|+b_\ell. \tag{1}
\]
All signatures used below are well defined and positive.  Positive definiteness of \(\Sigma\) gives \(a_u=\Sigma_{uu}>0\) for every \(u\in V\).  For each edge \(uv\), positivity of the corresponding two-by-two principal minor gives
\[
\Sigma_{uu}\Sigma_{vv}-\Sigma_{uv}^{2}>0,
\qquad
b_{v\mid u}=\frac{\Sigma_{uu}\Sigma_{vv}-\Sigma_{uv}^{2}}{\Sigma_{uu}}>0.
\]
Thus every division defining a residual label has a nonzero denominator; rationality of \(\Sigma\) makes every label rational and exactly comparable.

We first prove the transition formula and its sufficiency.  If \(\lambda\in C_\ell\), then \(\lambda\) occurs in a processed component.  If it occurred in any unprocessed component as well, then, by the definition of \(U_j\),
\[
\lambda\in
\left(\bigcup_{h\leq\ell}U_{\pi(h)}\right)
\cap
\left(\bigcup_{h>\ell}U_{\pi(h)}\right)
=B_\ell,
\]
contrary to \(\lambda\in D_\ell\setminus B_\ell\).  Hence
\[
C_\ell\cap U_{\pi(h)}=\varnothing
\quad\text{for every }h>\ell. \tag{2}
\]
Choose \(r\in V(T_{\pi(\ell+1)})\), and write
\[
R=S_{\pi(\ell+1)}(r),
\qquad
W=A_\ell\cup R,
\qquad
D_{\ell+1}=D_\ell\cup R.
\]
By (2), \(C_\ell\cap R=\varnothing\), and therefore
\[
D_{\ell+1}=C_\ell\,\dot\cup\,W. \tag{3}
\]
Moreover, (2) says that every member of \(C_\ell\) is absent from all components strictly after \(\ell+1\), so no such member belongs to \(B_{\ell+1}\).  Intersecting (3) with \(B_{\ell+1}\), and then taking the complement of \(B_{\ell+1}\) in (3), gives respectively
\[
D_{\ell+1}\cap B_{\ell+1}=W\cap B_{\ell+1} \tag{4}
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
|D_{\ell+1}\setminus B_{\ell+1}|
&=|C_\ell|+|W\setminus B_{\ell+1}|\\
&=b_\ell+|W\setminus B_{\ell+1}|. \tag{5}
\end{aligned}
\]
Equations (4) and (5) are precisely the transition specified in \(\texttt{def:ordered-state}\).

They also prove right congruence.  Suppose two root prefixes at cut \(\ell\) have the same pair \((A_\ell,b_\ell)\).  For any common choice of the next root, the right sides of (4) and (5) depend only on that pair and the chosen signature \(R\), so the two extended prefixes have the same next state.  Induction over any fixed suffix-root sequence shows that they have the same state at every subsequent cut.  At the final cut the unprocessed union is empty, whence \(B_c=\varnothing\); consequently their common final state is
\[
(A_c,b_c)=(\varnothing,|D_c|). \tag{6}
\]
Thus two prefixes in the same state admit exactly the same feasible suffix-root sequences.  Notice that (2) is the step that makes the identities of the retired labels in \(C_\ell\) irrelevant; only their number \(b_\ell\) remains relevant.

Initialize the dynamic program at \((A_0,b_0)=(\varnothing,0)\), since both \(D_0\) and \(B_0\) are empty.  Retain a generated state only when
\[
|A_\ell|+b_\ell\leq K. \tag{7}
\]
By (1), condition (7) is exactly \(|D_\ell|\leq K\).  The dictionaries \(D_\ell\) can only grow with \(\ell\), so pruning a state that violates (7) cannot discard a feasible completion.  Conversely, every tuple \(\rho\) with \(|L(\rho)|\leq K\) has \(|D_\ell|\leq K\) at every prefix and, by (4)--(5), follows its exact states without being pruned.  In view of (6), the accepting terminal histories are therefore exactly
\[
\{\rho:|L(\rho)|\leq K\}=\mathcal F_K(\Sigma). \tag{8}
\]
This proves exact feasibility for \(\textsc{K-Fiber}\); by \(\texttt{thm:root-union}\), these tuples correspond bijectively to the claimed causal forest orientations.

For exact multiplicity information, associate to each retained state a number
\[
\nu_\ell(A,b)=
\min\!\left\{2,\#\{\text{root prefixes inducing }(A,b)\}\right\}
\]
and retain that many distinct representative backpointer chains.  Initially \(\nu_0(\varnothing,0)=1\).  At a transition, sum over all predecessor-state and next-root pairs that produce the new state, truncating the sum at two.  Distinct such pairs give distinct extended root prefixes, and
\[
\min\!\left\{2,\sum_t n_t\right\}
=
\min\!\left\{2,\sum_t\min\{2,n_t\}\right\} \tag{9}
\]
for all nonnegative integers \(n_t\).  Hence induction using (9) proves that every stored \(\nu_\ell\) is the exact prefix count truncated at two.  Right congruence proves additionally that, when a state has more than two representatives, any two of them have the same set of feasible suffixes as all the others; retaining only two therefore preserves two-witness recovery, not merely the truncated count.  At cut \(c\), sum \(\nu_c(\varnothing,b)\) over \(0\leq b\leq K\), again truncating at two.  By (8), the result is \(0\), \(1\), or \(2\) according as \(|\mathcal F_K(\Sigma)|\) is zero, one, or at least two.  In the last case the two stored terminal backpointer chains recover two distinct feasible tuples.  This decides \(\textsc{Unique-K-Fiber}\) and solves \(\textsc{Two-K-Fiber}\) exactly.

It remains to establish the stated state and time bounds.  At a fixed cut let \(m=|B_\ell|\).  If \(|A_\ell|=i\), then (7) forces
\[
0\leq i\leq\min(m,K),
\qquad
0\leq b_\ell\leq K-i.
\]
There are \(\binom mi\) choices of \(A_\ell\) and \(K-i+1\) possible values of \(b_\ell\).  Therefore the number of retained states is at most
\[
\sum_{i=0}^{\min(m,K)}\binom mi(K-i+1). \tag{10}
\]
Since \(m\leq q_\pi\), any injection of \(B_\ell\) into a set of cardinality \(q_\pi\) injects every pair counted in (10), preserving \(i\) and \(b_\ell\), into a pair counted by
\[
\mathsf S_{\mathrm{DP}}(q_\pi,K)
=\sum_{i=0}^{\min(q_\pi,K)}\binom{q_\pi}{i}(K-i+1). \tag{11}
\]
This proves the per-cut bound.

The root signatures, the sets \(U_j\), and the cuts \(B_\ell\) are computable by exact rational arithmetic.  There are polynomially many input-derived labels, their numerators and denominators have bit length polynomial in \(N\), and exact equality and finite-set operations on them take \(\operatorname{poly}(N)\) bit operations.  From each retained state there are at most \(|V(T_{\pi(\ell+1)})|\leq p\) outgoing root choices.  The \(c\leq p\) cuts, these outgoing choices, the set operations, the truncated-count operations, and the backpointer operations contribute only a polynomial factor in \(N\).  Together with (11), the total number of bit operations is therefore
\[
\mathsf S_{\mathrm{DP}}(q_\pi,K)\operatorname{poly}(N). \tag{12}
\]
Finally,
\[
\mathsf S_{\mathrm{DP}}(q_\pi,K)
\leq (K+1)\sum_{i=0}^{q_\pi}\binom{q_\pi}{i}
=(K+1)2^{q_\pi}. \tag{13}
\]
If \(q_\pi\geq2\), then \(\binom{q_\pi}{i}\leq q_\pi^i\), and hence
\[
\mathsf S_{\mathrm{DP}}(q_\pi,K)
\leq (K+1)\sum_{i=0}^{K}q_\pi^i
\leq (K+1)^2q_\pi^K. \tag{14}
\]
For \(K=0\) the last quantity is one.  For \(K\geq1\), \(K+1\leq2^K\leq q_\pi^K\), so the right side of (14) is at most \(q_\pi^{3K}\).  Thus (12) is also \(q_\pi^{O(K)}\operatorname{poly}(N)\), completing every asserted bound and algorithmic guarantee.
