All summands are nonnegative, proving the first equivalence.

Suppose every \(a_z\) is positive and \(\Psi(P)=0\). Then every Fourier coefficient of \(g\) vanishes. Let \(F_m\) be the product Fejér kernel on \(\mathbb T^d\). Along its stated quadratic-mean differentiable path, \(\Psi(P_\theta)=\theta w_0\), and its uniform source premise puts \(P_{c/\sqrt n}\) in the fixed source ball for large \(n\). Quadratic-mean differentiability means that, for a score \(\dot\ell\in L^2(P_0)\),
\[
\int\left(\sqrt{dP_\theta}-\sqrt{dP_0}
-\frac{\theta}{2}\dot\ell\sqrt{dP_0}\right)^2=o(\theta^2).
\]
Thus \(H^2(P_{c/\sqrt n}^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n})\le Cc^2\). Choosing \(c\) small and applying the testing--total-variation inequality yields \(\delta_n^\star\ge c'n^{-1/2}\). The asserted uniform Hilbert-valued higher-order upper bound is not proved: the frozen handle does not define its score, empirical Gram stabilization, correction order, or critical value.

%%% FIELD partial-density-frontier
The candidate functional itself has a computable rate. The density band gives \(c_X/K_h\le p_{h,j}\le C_X/K_h\). For \(N\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p)\),
\[
P(N\ge2)\asymp\min\{(np)^2,1\}.
\]
The upper bound follows from \(P(N\ge2)\le E[N(N-1)]/2\); for \(np\le1\), the probability of exactly two successes gives the lower bound, and for \(np\ge1\), binomial monotonicity gives a fixed positive lower bound. Hence
\[
\Gamma_n(h;P_X)\asymp
\begin{cases}
1,&K_h\le n,\\
K_h/n,&K_h\ge n.
\end{cases}
\]
For \(0<t\le d/2\), minimizing the sparse expression
\[
K_h^{-t/d}+K_h^{3/2}/n^2
\]
gives \(K_h\asymp n^{4d/(2t+3d)}\ge n\), and therefore
\[
\rho_n^{\mathrm{occ}}\asymp n^{-1/2}\vee n^{-4t/(2t+3d)}.
\]
Dyadic rounding changes only constants. The proved fuzzy construction gives the distinct lower bound \(\delta_n^\star\gtrsim n^{-2t/(2t+d)}\) for \(0<s\le1/2\). When \(t<d/2\), the occupancy candidate is strictly larger because \(4t/(2t+3d)<2t/(2t+d)\); neither an upper estimator nor a matching occupancy lower mixture is supplied.

%%% FIELD partial-spectral
Only a deterministic fixed-regularization comparison follows. For every \(\lambda\ge\lambda_0>0\) and \(\psi\in\mathcal H\), positivity and the spectral theorem give
\[
\frac{\|\psi\|_{\mathcal H}^2}{\|\Sigma_P\|+\lambda}
\le\langle\psi,(\Sigma_P+\lambda I)^{-1}\psi\rangle_{\mathcal H}
\le\frac{\|\psi\|_{\mathcal H}^2}{\lambda}.
\]
Thus a population resolvent bounded away from singularity is norm-equivalent to identity weighting and cannot change a separation exponent. No criterion for shrinking \(\lambda\) follows because the frozen handle supplies no eigenwise higher-order remainder or empirical-operator error.
