Then, for every \(V_i\in\mathcal Z(M)\cup\mathcal Y(M)\) and every pair \((J,K)\) satisfying either \(J\subseteq\operatorname{An}_{M}(V_i)\) and \(K\subseteq\operatorname{PP}_{M}(V_i)\), or \(V_i\) is an mleaf with a parent \(V_j\), \(J\subseteq\operatorname{An}_{M}(V_i)\setminus\{V_j\}\), and \(K\subseteq\operatorname{PP}_{M}(V_j)\), the rank of \(W^{J}_{K\cup\{V_i\}}\) equals the minimum cardinality of a vertex set meeting every directed path from \(J\) to \(K\cup\{V_i\}\), where a path's initial or terminal vertex may belong to the meeting set.
