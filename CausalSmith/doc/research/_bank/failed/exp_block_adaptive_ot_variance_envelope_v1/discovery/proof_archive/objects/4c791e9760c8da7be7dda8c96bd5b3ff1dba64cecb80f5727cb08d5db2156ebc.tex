For a fixed \(M>0\), define the optional subclass
\[
\mathfrak D_M=\left\{\mathbb D\in\mathfrak D(K,\varepsilon_0,\delta_0,C_R,v_0,(q_n),(\ell_n)):
\sup_{n\ge1}\max_{b\le B_n}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\mathcal F_{n,b-1}}
\max_{a\in\mathcal A}\max_{i\in\mathcal U_{nb}}|R_{nbi}(a)|\le M\right\}.
\]
Thus \(\mathfrak D_M\subseteq\mathfrak D\); the displayed bound is an additional common history-uniform restriction and does not replace any member property of \(\mathfrak D\).
