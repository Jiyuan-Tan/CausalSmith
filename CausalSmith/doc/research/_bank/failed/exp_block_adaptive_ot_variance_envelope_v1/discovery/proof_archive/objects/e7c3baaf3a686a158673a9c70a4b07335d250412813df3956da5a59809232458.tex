For every array in \(\mathfrak D\), \((M_{nj},\mathcal F_{n,j})_{j\le B_n}\) is a martingale and \[E(D_{nb}^2\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1})=w_{nb}^2\left\{\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}\frac{c_a^2S_{nba}^2}{m_{nba}}-\frac1{n_{nb}-1}\int\left(\sum_{a\in\mathcal A}c_ax_a\right)^2d\gamma_{nb}^{\mathrm{true}}\right\},\] where \(\gamma_{nb}^{\mathrm{true}}\in\Gamma_{nb}\) is the joint centered residual coupling in block \(b\). At every \(h\in\mathcal H_{n,b-1}\), the conditional variance range over \(\Gamma_{nb}^{h}\) has endpoints obtained from \(J_{nb}^{h,+}\) and \(J_{nb}^{h,-}\), and both endpoints are attained within that node's convexified coupling set. Evaluating these nodewise endpoints along the realized pre-block histories yields the predictable pathwise containment \(V_n\in[L_n,U_n]\). For any fixed root-to-leaf path, independent selection of feasible couplings at its visited nodes attains both summed endpoints in the rectangular relaxation \(\mathfrak R_n(\boldsymbol\mu_n)\). Thus the interval is exact only nodewise and path by path inside that rectangular convexified relaxation. This theorem does not assert that one globally consistent fixed-potential-outcome adaptive-history completion realizes independently selected optimizers across different nodes. When \(B_n=1\) and \(g_{nbi}(a)=0\), direct substitution gives the static quadratic MOT variance formula of Gao, Ge, and Qian. When \(B_n\ge2\) and counts respond to earlier outcomes, \([L_n,U_n]\) is a sum of pre-block-measurable random endpoints rather than one static MOT functional, and the claim does not condition on \(\mathcal F_{n,B_n}\).
