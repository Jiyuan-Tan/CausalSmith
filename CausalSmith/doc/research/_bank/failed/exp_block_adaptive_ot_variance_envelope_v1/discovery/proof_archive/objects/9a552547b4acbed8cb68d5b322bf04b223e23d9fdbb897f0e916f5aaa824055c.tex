Fix \(K\ge2\) and any contrast \(c\) satisfying \(\sum_ac_a=0\) and \(\sum_ac_a^2=1\). We construct an explicit subclass. Set
\[
 B_n=n,\qquad q_n=2n,\qquad n_{nb}=Kq_n=2Kn,
 \qquad m_{nba}=q_n,qquad w_{nb}=\frac1{B_n}.
 \tag{1}
\]
In each block label the units by \(i=1,\ldots,2Kn\), and set
\[
 x_{nbi}=\begin{cases}-1,&i\le Kn,\\1,&i>Kn,\end{cases}
 \qquad Y_{nbi}(a)=x_{nbi},qquad g_{nbi}(a)=0
 \quad(a\in\mathcal A). \tag{2}
\]
Let the subclass consist of (2) and all arrays obtained by an arbitrary deterministic within-block permutation of this balanced list, using the same permutation in every arm of that block. Every calculation below is invariant to these permutations.

For each block, independently of the past in the conditional-design sense, use the uniform complete randomization with exactly \(q_n\) labels in every arm. Thus the quotas and adjustments are deterministic and hence predictable, consistency defines the observed response, and the only randomness is assignment. Equations (1)--(2) also show that all potential outcomes are fixed before randomization.

We verify membership in a common-parameter admissible class rather than condition on a realized path. Take \(\varepsilon_0=1/(2K)\), \(\delta_0=1\), \(C_R=1\), \(v_0=K\), and \(\ell_n=1/n\). Then \(p_{nba}=1/K\), so
\[
 \varepsilon_0\le p_{nba}\le1-(K-1)\varepsilon_0.
\]
The residuals equal \(x_{nbi}\), so \(n_{nb}^{-1}\sum_i|R_{nbi}(a)|^{4+\delta_0}=1=C_R\). The minimum arm count is \(q_n=2n\to\infty\). Since \(N_n=B_nKq_n\),
\[
 s_n^2=\sum_{b=1}^{B_n}\frac{w_{nb}^2}{n_{nb}}
 =\frac1{B_nKq_n},
 \qquad
 \frac{w_{nb}^2/n_{nb}}{s_n^2}=\frac1{B_n}=\ell_n\longrightarrow0. \tag{3}
\]
The two contrast restrictions hold by the choice of \(c\). Hence every member-property in \(\text{def:admissible-arrays}\), except the two QV properties checked next, has now been verified directly.

The residual mean in every arm and block is zero, and the common finite-population variance is
\[
 S_{nba}^2=S_n^2=\frac{Kq_n}{Kq_n-1}. \tag{4}
\]
The diagonal coupling \((X_1,\ldots,X_K)=(X,\ldots,X)\), with \(X\) uniform on the list in (2), belongs to \(\Gamma_{nb}\). By \(\sum_ac_a=0\), its transport cost is
\[
 E\left(\sum_ac_aX_a\right)^2
 =E\left(X\sum_ac_a\right)^2=0.
\]
The cost is nonnegative, so \(J_{nb}^{-}=0\). The true common-label residual coupling is precisely this diagonal coupling. The conditional-variance identity in \(\text{thm:exact-predictable-ot-envelope}\), together with \(\sum_ac_a^2=1\), therefore gives
\[
 E(D_{nb}^2\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1})
 =U_{nb}=\frac{(S_n^2)}{B_n^2q_n},
 \qquad
 V_n=U_n=\frac{S_n^2}{B_nq_n}. \tag{5}
\]
This quantity is deterministic. From (3)--(5),
\[
 \frac{V_n}{s_n^2}=KS_n^2\ge K=v_0,
 \qquad
 \frac{V_n}{EV_n}=1. \tag{6}
\]
Thus QV growth holds and QV stabilization holds uniformly over the constructed subclass, the latter exactly rather than merely asymptotically.

This construction contains the requested smallest arithmetic witness. When \(K=2\) and \(n=1\), a block list is \(\{-1,-1,1,1\}\). For the unnormalized contrast \((1,-1)\), diagonal pairing has squared contrast zero at every atom, whereas reverse pairing has squared contrast four at every atom; hence the two costs are zero and four. Under the required normalized contrast \((1,-1)/\sqrt2\), both costs are divided by two. This checks the direction and scale without entering the asymptotic argument.

By \(\text{thm:history-uniform-plugin}\), \(\widehat U_n-U_n=o_{\Pr}(s_n^2)\) under the original randomization. Equations (4)--(6) imply \(U_n/s_n^2=KS_n^2\to K>0\), so
\[
 \frac{\widehat U_n}{V_n}=\frac{\widehat U_n}{U_n}\longrightarrow1
 \quad\text{in probability}. \tag{7}
\]
We now verify the martingale CLT directly. The estimator algebra in \(\text{def:aipw-estimator}\), consistency, and \(p_{nba}=m_{nba}/n_{nb}\) give \(\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n=M_{nB_n}\). For an arm sample mean \(X_a=q_n^{-1}\sum_{i:Z_{nbi}=a}x_{nbi}\), expansion by inclusion multiplicities gives, with \(N=Kq_n\), \(A_2=A_4=N\), and \(\pi_j=(q_n)_j/(N)_j\),
\[
 E(q_nX_a)^4
 =\{\pi_1-7\pi_2+12\pi_3-6\pi_4\}A_4
 +3\{\pi_2-2\pi_3+\pi_4\}A_2^2
 \le26N+12N^2.
\]
Thus \(E(X_a^4\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1})\le C_Kq_n^{-2}\). Since \(D_{nb}=B_n^{-1}\sum_ac_aX_a\), \(\sum_ac_a^2=1\), and \((\sum_ac_aX_a)^4\le K\sum_aX_a^4\),
\[
 \sum_{b=1}^{B_n}E D_{nb}^4
 \le \frac{C_K}{B_n^3q_n^2},
 \qquad
 \frac{\sum_bE D_{nb}^4}{V_n^2}
 \le \frac{C_K}{B_n(S_n^2)^2}\longrightarrow0. \tag{8}
\]
For every \(\epsilon>0\), the conditional Lindeberg sum divided by \(V_n\) has expectation at most \(\epsilon^{-2}\sum_bED_{nb}^4/V_n^2\), by \(u^2\mathbf 1\{|u|>x\}\le u^4/x^2\). It therefore converges to zero in probability by Markov's inequality. Together with \(V_n/EV_n=1\) from (6), \(\text{lem:martingale-clt-predictable-variation}\) gives
\[
 T_n:=\frac{\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n}{\sqrt{V_n}}
 \Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1). \tag{9}
\]
Notice also that \(\tau_n=0\), because each unit's causal contrast is \(x_{nbi}\sum_ac_a=0\); the CLT concerns randomization error around this fixed causal estimand.

For fixed \(\rho\in(0,1)\), the shortened interval covers exactly on the event
\[
 |T_n|\le z_{1-\alpha/2}
 \sqrt{(1-\rho)\widehat U_n/V_n}.
\]
To pass to the random threshold, fix \(\zeta>0\) and restrict to the event \(|\widehat U_n/V_n-1|\le\zeta\). The coverage probability is then sandwiched between the probabilities that \(|T_n|\) is at most \(z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{(1-\rho)(1-\zeta)}\) and \(z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{(1-\rho)(1+\zeta)}\), up to \(o(1)\) by (7). Equation (9) and continuity of the Gaussian distribution function, followed by \(\zeta\downarrow0\), yield
\[
 \Pr\!\left\{|\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n|\le z_{1-\alpha/2}
 \sqrt{(1-\rho)\widehat U_n}\right\}
 \longrightarrow
 2\Phi\!\left(z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{1-\rho}\right)-1. \tag{10}
\]
Because \(z_{1-\alpha/2}>0\), \(0<\sqrt{1-\rho}<1\), and \(\Phi(y)-\Phi(x)=(2\pi)^{-1/2}\int_x^y e^{-t^2/2}dt>0\) whenever \(y>x\), the right side of (10) is strictly smaller than \(2\Phi(z_{1-\alpha/2})-1=1-\alpha\).

Finally, let any broader design family contain the constructed array at every \(n\). Its worst-case coverage is no larger than coverage on this member. Taking \(\limsup\) and applying (10) proves the stated subclass minimax certificate. The construction is nonadaptive, so it establishes necessity of the full upper endpoint for uniform inference without purporting to solve the separate cross-history compatibility problem.
