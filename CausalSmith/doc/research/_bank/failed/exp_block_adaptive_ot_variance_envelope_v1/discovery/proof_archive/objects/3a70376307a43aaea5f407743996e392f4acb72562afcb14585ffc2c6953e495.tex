Fix the core's \(K\ge2\) and normalized zero-sum contrast \(c\). We construct a nonadaptive triangular array. Let
\[
B_n=n,qquad q_n=2n,qquad n_{nb}=Kq_n,qquad
m_{nba}=q_n,qquad w_{nb}=B_n^{-1},qquad g_{nbi}(a)=0.
\tag{1}
\]
In every block and every arm use the same fixed list: for exactly \(Kq_n/2=Kn\) common unit labels set \(Y_{nbi}(a)=1\), and for the remaining \(Kn\) labels set \(Y_{nbi}(a)=-1\). Independently across blocks, draw uniformly from assignments realizing the quotas in (1). This is a history-independent special case of predictable complete-block randomization and is one globally consistent fixed-potential-outcome completion.

Every arm list has mean zero and finite-population variance
\[
S_n^2=\frac{Kq_n}{Kq_n-1}.
\tag{2}
\]
The residuals equal the outcomes and are bounded by one, so their \((4+\delta_0)\)-moment average is one. Also \(p_{nba}=1/K\), which satisfies
\(\varepsilon_0\le p_{nba}\le1-(K-1)\varepsilon_0\) for every fixed
\(\varepsilon_0\in(0,1/K)\). Thus the fixed-outcome, consistency, predictable-count, complete-randomization, predictable-adjustment, overlap, contrast, residual-moment, and growing-count conditions hold with common constants, for example \(C_R=1\). Moreover,
\[
s_n^2=\sum_{b=1}^{B_n}\frac{w_{nb}^2}{n_{nb}}
=\frac1{B_nKq_n},qquad
\frac{w_{nb}^2/n_{nb}}{s_n^2}=\frac1{B_n}.
\tag{3}
\]
Hence block leverage holds with \(\ell_n=B_n^{-1}\to0\).

At each block the common labels couple the centered arm coordinates as \((X,\ldots,X)\), with the common centered marginal. Since \(\sum_ac_a=0\), this feasible coupling has cost
\[
\left(\sum_ac_aX\right)^2=X^2\left(\sum_ac_a\right)^2=0.
\tag{4}
\]
Every quadratic cost is nonnegative, so \(J_{nb}^{-}=0\), and the true fixed-potential-outcome coupling is the zero-cost coupling (4). The conditional-variance identity in
\(\text{thm:exact-predictable-ot-envelope}\), followed by \(\sum_ac_a^2=1\), gives
\[
E(D_{nb}^2\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1})
=w_{nb}^2\sum_a\frac{c_a^2S_n^2}{q_n}
=\frac{w_{nb}^2S_n^2}{q_n}.
\tag{5}
\]
The upper endpoint has the same diagonal term and subtracts
\(J_{nb}^{-}/(n_{nb}-1)=0\). Therefore
\[
V_n=U_n=\frac{S_n^2}{B_nq_n},qquad
\frac{V_n}{s_n^2}=KS_n^2\ge K.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus QV growth holds, for instance with \(v_0=1\), and QV stabilization holds identically because \(V_n=E(V_n)\) is deterministic.

The smallest exact stress test is the case \(K=2\), one block, \(q_n=2\), common list
\((-1,-1,1,1)\), and \(c=(1,-1)/\sqrt2\). Across all six equiprobable assignments of two labels to arm one, its sample mean is \(-1,0,1\) with multiplicities \(1,4,1\), and the complementary arm mean is its negative. Thus \(D_{n1}\) is
\(-\sqrt2w_{n1},0,\sqrt2w_{n1}\) with probabilities \(1/6,4/6,1/6\), and
\[
E(D_{n1})=0,qquad E(D_{n1}^2)=\frac{2w_{n1}^2}{3}
=\frac{w_{n1}^2}{2}\frac43.
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(S_n^2=4/3\), (7) exactly equals (5); exhaustive enumeration detects neither bias nor a variance-formula failure.

The sharpened \(\text{thm:history-uniform-plugin}\) applies to this common-parameter array and gives
\[
\frac{|\widehat U_n-U_n|}{s_n^2}\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{in probability}.
\tag{8}
\]
Since (6) has \(U_n/s_n^2=KS_n^2\ge K\),
\[
\frac{\widehat U_n}{U_n}\longrightarrow1
\quad\text{in probability}.
\tag{9}
\]

We verify the CLT primitives rather than assuming a limit law. For a uniform size-\(q_n\) subset \(S\) of a centered list \((x_i)_{i=1}^{Kq_n}\), put \(A_r=\sum_i|x_i|^r\) and
\(\pi_r=(q_n)_r/(Kq_n)_r\). Expanding by equality patterns, exactly as in the fourth-moment calculation for simple random sampling, gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
E\left(\sum_{i\in S}x_i\right)^4
={}&\pi_1A_4-4\pi_2A_4+3\pi_2(A_2^2-A_4)\\
&+6\pi_3(2A_4-A_2^2)+\pi_4(3A_2^2-6A_4).
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
For the present \(\{-1,1\}\) list, \(A_2=A_4=Kq_n\) and \(\pi_r\le K^{-r}\). Dividing by \(q_n^4\), then using
\(|\sum_ac_ax_a|^4\le K\sum_a|x_a|^4\), gives a constant \(C_K<\infty\) such that
\[
E(D_{nb}^4\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1})\le C_Kw_{nb}^4q_n^{-2}.
\tag{11}
\]
This calculation uses only marginal fourth moments and therefore does not assume independence among the arm sample means. With \(\sigma_n^2=V_n=S_n^2/(B_nq_n)>0\), for every \(\epsilon>0\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\frac1{\sigma_n^2}\sum_bE\{D_{nb}^2\mathbf1(|D_{nb}|>\epsilon\sigma_n)
\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1}\}\\
&\quad\le\frac1{\epsilon^2\sigma_n^4}\sum_bE(D_{nb}^4\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1})
\le\frac{C_K}{\epsilon^2B_n(S_n^2)^2}\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
The martingale property follows from the exact-envelope theorem, and QV stabilization follows from (6). The proved
\(\text{lem:martingale-clt-predictable-variation}\) now yields
\[
\frac{\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n}{\sqrt{U_n}}
=\frac{\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n}{\sqrt{V_n}}
\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\tag{13}
\]
The causal estimand is \(\tau_n=0\) on this witness because the potential outcomes agree across arms pointwise and \(\sum_ac_a=0\); it remains a finite-population causal contrast, not a transport functional.

Fix \(\rho\in(0,1)\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and write \(z=z_{1-\alpha/2}>0\). For \(0<\eta<1\), on
\(\{|\widehat U_n/U_n-1|\le\eta\}\) the random threshold below lies between the thresholds obtained from \(1-\eta\) and \(1+\eta\). Equations (9), (13), and continuity of the Gaussian distribution give, first as \(n\to\infty\) and then as \(\eta\downarrow0\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Pr\left\{|\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n|
\le z\sqrt{(1-\rho)\widehat U_n}\right\}\\
&\quad=\Pr\left\{
\left|\frac{\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n}{\sqrt{U_n}}\right|
\le z\sqrt{(1-\rho)\widehat U_n/U_n}\right\}\\
&\quad\longrightarrow2\Phi\left(z\sqrt{1-\rho}\right)-1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]
Because \(z>0\) and \(0<1-\rho<1\), one has \(z\sqrt{1-\rho}<z\). The standard normal density is strictly positive, so
\[
\Phi(z)-\Phi(z\sqrt{1-\rho})
=\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{z\sqrt{1-\rho}}^z e^{-u^2/2}\,du>0.
\]
Hence the limit in (14) is strictly smaller than \(2\Phi(z)-1=1-\alpha\). If a broader design family contains this subclass, its worst-case coverage is no larger than the coverage of this displayed member for every \(n\); taking the upper limit proves the asserted certificate. The witness is nonadaptive and globally consistent, so the argument never selects incompatible optimizers at different adaptive-history nodes.
