The two-arm endpoint identities follow from \(\mathrm{lem:twoarm\text{-}rearrangement\text{-}extrema}\).  To display the sign, write
\[
 \frac12(x-y)^2=\frac12x^2+\frac12y^2-xy.
\]
The marginal square terms are fixed, so equal-rank pairing maximizes \(E(XY)\) and minimizes the quadratic cost, while reverse-rank pairing minimizes \(E(XY)\) and maximizes the cost.

For general fixed \(K\), label every atom even when several labels have the same numerical value.  A coupling of the labeled empirical measures assigns a nonnegative mass \(\pi_{i_1\ldots i_K}\) to each tuple of labels.  Its \(a\)-th marginal is uniform if and only if
\[
 \sum_{(i_{a'})_{a'\ne a}}\pi_{i_1\ldots i_K}=m_{nba}^{-1}
\]
for every \(a\) and \(i_a\).  Thus the feasible arrays in \(\mathrm{def:fixed\text{-}k\text{-}lp}\) are exactly the couplings of the observed empirical margins, and its objective is exactly the quadratic transport cost.  Attainment and equality with the finite Kantorovich dual values now follow from \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}discrete\text{-}mot\text{-}duality}\), for both minimization and maximization (apply the minimization result to the negative cost for the latter).

It remains to prove the paper-specific replication assertion.  The constraint matrix and right-hand side of the finite program are rational.  A nonempty bounded rational polytope has a rational optimal extreme point: Gaussian elimination on the active equalities expresses every extreme point using rational arithmetic.  Let \(\pi\) be such an optimum and choose an integer \(L\) divisible by every \(m_{nba}\) and by every denominator of every \(\pi_{i_1\ldots i_K}\).  Create \(L\pi_{i_1\ldots i_K}\) replicated units of joint type \((i_1,\ldots,i_K)\).  The marginal constraints imply that label \(i_a\) occurs exactly
\[
 \sum_{(i_{a'})_{a'\ne a}}L\pi_{i_1\ldots i_K}=L/m_{nba}
\]
times.  Hence every armwise list is replicated by the same integral factor, and the empirical joint law of the replicated common unit labels is exactly \(\pi\).  Assigning to each replicated unit the corresponding armwise residual values and then choosing fixed potential outcomes and predictable fitted values with their prescribed difference realizes this plan at that single node.  This construction neither preserves the original unreplicated block size nor enforces compatibility with another history node, which proves exactly the stated boundary.

As an arithmetic stress test, take both centered lists to be \(\{-1,-1,1,1\}\).  For the unnormalized contrast \((1,-1)\), equal-rank pairing gives cost \(0\), reverse-rank pairing gives \((4+4+4+4)/4=4\), and therefore \(U_n-L_n=4w_{nb}^2/3>0\) when \(n_{nb}=4\).  The normalized contrast divides both costs and the width by two, exactly as recorded in \(\mathrm{def:pmone\text{-}witness}\).
