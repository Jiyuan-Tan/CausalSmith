For a node \((n,b,h)\), put
\[
 d_{nb}^h=\frac{w_{nb}^2}{s_n^2(n_{nb}-1)}\left[
 \inf_{\gamma\in\mathcal I_{nb}^h}\int\left(\sum_ac_ax_a\right)^2d\gamma-J_{nb}^{h,-}\right].
\]
For \(\Xi_n\in\mathcal C_n(\boldsymbol\mu_n)\), its common-label empirical coupling \(\gamma_{nb}^{\Xi,h}\) belongs to \(\mathcal I_{nb}^h\) by the definitions of \(\mathcal I_{nb}^h\) and \(\mathcal C_n(\boldsymbol\mu_n)\).  Therefore
\[
 e_{nb}^{\Xi,h}=\frac{w_{nb}^2}{s_n^2(n_{nb}-1)}\left[
 \int\left(\sum_ac_ax_a\right)^2d\gamma_{nb}^{\Xi,h}
 -\inf_{\gamma\in\mathcal I_{nb}^h}\int\left(\sum_ac_ax_a\right)^2d\gamma\right]\ge0. \tag{10}
\]
The diagonal terms in \(U_{nb}^h\) and \(Q_{nb}^{\Xi,h}\) cancel, so \(\mathrm{def:history\text{-}tree\text{-}rectangular\text{-}relaxation}\) and \(\mathrm{def:completion\text{-}gap}\) give the exact identity
\[
 \frac{U_{nb}^h-Q_{nb}^{\Xi,h}}{s_n^2}=d_{nb}^h+e_{nb}^{\Xi,h}. \tag{11}
\]
Thus \(A_n=\sup_\varpi\sum_bd_{nb}^{h_b(\varpi)}\), \(C_n=\inf_\Xi\sup_\varpi\sum_be_{nb}^{\Xi,h_b(\varpi)}\), and
\[
 \Delta_n^{\mathrm{tree}}=\inf_\Xi\sup_\varpi\sum_b\{d_{nb}^{h_b(\varpi)}+e_{nb}^{\Xi,h_b(\varpi)}\}. \tag{12}
\]
Because every term in (10) and every \(d_{nb}^h\) is nonnegative, (12) is at least \(A_n\) and at least \(C_n\).  Conversely, for every \(\epsilon>0\), choose a completion whose worst-path sum of the \(e\)'s is at most \(C_n+\epsilon\).  On every path the sum of the \(d\)'s is at most \(A_n\), so (12) is at most \(A_n+C_n+\epsilon\).  Letting \(\epsilon\downarrow0\) proves
\(\max(A_n,C_n)\le\Delta_n^{\mathrm{tree}}\le A_n+C_n\), and the asserted equivalence follows.

By \(\mathrm{def:completion\text{-}gap}\), \(\Delta_n^{\mathrm{node}}=\sup_{b,h}d_{nb}^h\).  Every history node has at least one root-to-leaf extension, so the path through a maximizing node shows \(\Delta_n^{\mathrm{node}}\le A_n\).  Every path contains exactly \(B_n\) nodes and each node loss is at most \(\Delta_n^{\mathrm{node}}\), giving \(A_n\le B_n\Delta_n^{\mathrm{node}}\).  Each \(d_{nb}^h\) is obtained from the finite matching problem over \(\mathcal I_{nb}^h\), and the supremum is over the finite path set, so \(A_n\) is computable.  The optimization defining \(C_n\) exposes exactly the remaining globally consistent completion constraint.

Finally, take \(\mu_{nba}^h=\delta_{0_{\mathbb R}}\) at every node.  The completion with \(Y_{nbi}(a)=g_{nbi}^h(a)=0\) is nonempty and history compatible.  Every feasible coupling is concentrated at the zero vector, so every quadratic cost, every \(d\), and every \(e\) is zero.  Hence both gaps and \(A_n,C_n\) vanish identically.  But every armwise residual is constant across units, every randomized arm mean equals its population mean, and \(D_{nb}=0\) for every block and assignment.  Therefore \(V_n=0\), whereas \(s_n^2>0\) and \(v_0>0\); \(\mathrm{ass:qv\text{-}growth}\) fails.  There is consequently no sequence in the full admissible class, let alone an admissible near-attaining sequence yielding the requested undercoverage.  This is an ex ante fixed completion witness and does not select anything using \(\mathcal F_{n,B_n}\).
