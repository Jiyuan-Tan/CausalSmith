For each \(n\), define, within this statement,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 A_n&=\sup_{\varpi\in\mathcal P_n}\sum_{b=1}^{B_n}
 \frac{w_{nb}^2}{s_n^2(n_{nb}-1)}\left[
 \inf_{\gamma\in\mathcal I_{nb}^{h_b(\varpi)}}\int\left(\sum_ac_ax_a\right)^2d\gamma-J_{nb}^{h_b(\varpi),-}\right],\\
 C_n&=\inf_{\Xi_n\in\mathcal C_n(\boldsymbol\mu_n)}\sup_{\varpi\in\mathcal P_n}
 \sum_{b=1}^{B_n}\frac{w_{nb}^2}{s_n^2(n_{nb}-1)}\left[
 \int\left(\sum_ac_ax_a\right)^2d\gamma_{nb}^{\Xi,h_b(\varpi)}
 -\inf_{\gamma\in\mathcal I_{nb}^{h_b(\varpi)}}\int\left(\sum_ac_ax_a\right)^2d\gamma\right].
 \end{aligned}
\]
Whenever \(\mathcal C_n(\boldsymbol\mu_n)\ne\varnothing\),
\[
 \max\{A_n,C_n\}\le\Delta_n^{\mathrm{tree}}(\boldsymbol\mu_n)\le A_n+C_n,
 \qquad
 \Delta_n^{\mathrm{node}}(\boldsymbol\mu_n)\le A_n\le B_n\Delta_n^{\mathrm{node}}(\boldsymbol\mu_n).
\]
Consequently, \(\Delta_n^{\mathrm{tree}}(\boldsymbol\mu_n)\to0\) if and only if \(A_n\to0\) and \(C_n\to0\).  The quantity \(A_n\) is the computable root-to-leaf accumulation of the single-node multi-index integrality losses, while \(C_n\) is the infimum of the worst-path residual cross-history loss from requiring one fixed potential-outcome array, common unit labels, and one predictable fitting rule.  The decomposition reduces global simultaneous history-tree attainment to controlling \(C_n\), but makes no general claim that \(C_n\to0\).

As a counterexample to inferring nondegenerate admissible attainment from zero gap, suppose for every \(n\) that \(\mathcal C_n(\boldsymbol\mu_n)\ne\varnothing\) and every nodewise centered margin is \(\delta_{0_{\mathbb R}}\).  Then \(A_n=C_n=\Delta_n^{\mathrm{tree}}(\boldsymbol\mu_n)=\Delta_n^{\mathrm{node}}(\boldsymbol\mu_n)=0\), but every completion has \(V_n=0\) and hence violates \(\mathrm{ass:qv\text{-}growth}\).  Thus this global history-tree compatibility obstruction is logically separate from any minimax fixed-fraction upper-endpoint converse on a nonadaptive fixed-quota subclass.
