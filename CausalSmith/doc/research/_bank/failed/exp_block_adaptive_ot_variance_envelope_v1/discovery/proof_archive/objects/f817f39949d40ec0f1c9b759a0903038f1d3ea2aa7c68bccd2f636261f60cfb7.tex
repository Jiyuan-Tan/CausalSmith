We construct the subclass explicitly for the fixed \(K\ge2\) and normalized zero-sum contrast in the core. Let \(B_n=n\), let \(q_n=2n\), and in every block put
\[
n_{nb}=Kq_n,\qquad m_{nba}=q_n,qquad
w_{nb}=B_n^{-1},\qquad g_{nbi}(a)=0.
\tag{23}
\]
Use the same deterministic list in every arm and block: exactly \(Kq_n/2=Kn\) labels have \(Y_{nbi}(a)=1\), the remaining \(Kn\) labels have \(Y_{nbi}(a)=-1\), and the value does not depend on \(a\). Randomize independently from block to block, uniformly over all assignments with the fixed quotas in (23). This is a nonadaptive special case of predictable complete-block randomization.

The lists have mean zero and finite-population variance
\[
S_n^2=\frac{Kq_n}{Kq_n-1}.
\tag{24}
\]
They are bounded by one, their \((4+\delta_0)\)-moment average is one, and \(p_{nba}=1/K\). Thus any fixed \(\varepsilon_0<1/K\), any fixed \(\delta_0>0\), and any \(C_R\ge1\) satisfy overlap and the residual-moment condition. Count growth holds with the displayed \(q_n\). Moreover
\[
s_n^2=\sum_{b=1}^{B_n}\frac{w_{nb}^2}{n_{nb}}
=\frac1{B_nKq_n},
\qquad
\frac{w_{nb}^2/n_{nb}}{s_n^2}=\frac1{B_n},
\tag{25}
\]
so block leverage holds with \(\ell_n=B_n^{-1}\to0\). Fixed potential outcomes, consistency, predictability, and both contrast conditions hold by construction and by the frozen properties of \(c\).

At a node, couple all arm coordinates by the common label. Because the armwise lists are identical and \(\sum_ac_a=0\), this coupling has cost
\[
\left(\sum_ac_aX\right)^2=X^2\left(\sum_ac_a\right)^2=0.
\]
The cost is nonnegative, so \(J_{nb}^{-}=0\). The true common-label coupling is precisely this zero-cost coupling. The exact conditional-variance identity therefore gives
\[
E(D_{nb}^2\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1})
=w_{nb}^2\sum_a\frac{c_a^2S_n^2}{q_n}
=\frac{w_{nb}^2S_n^2}{q_n},
\tag{26}
\]
where contrast normalization was used in the last equality. Hence, deterministically,
\[
V_n=U_n=\frac{S_n^2}{B_nq_n},
\qquad
\frac{V_n}{s_n^2}=KS_n^2\ge K.
\tag{27}
\]
Thus predictable-QV growth holds, for example with \(v_0=1\), and predictable-QV stabilization holds identically because \(V_n=E(V_n)\).

For completeness, the martingale Lindeberg condition also follows directly. Conditional on the past, each arm sample mean is a simple-random-sample mean from the bounded centered list. The fourth-moment calculation (13), with \(m=q_n\), and contrast normalization imply a constant \(C_K\) such that
\[
E(D_{nb}^4\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1})\le C_Kw_{nb}^4q_n^{-2}.
\tag{28}
\]
With \(\sigma_n^2=V_n=S_n^2/(B_nq_n)\), conditional Markov's inequality gives
\[
\frac1{\sigma_n^2}\sum_bE\left[D_{nb}^2
\mathbf1\{|D_{nb}|>\epsilon\sigma_n\}\mid\mathcal F_{n,b-1}\right]
\le\frac{C_K}{\epsilon^2B_n}\longrightarrow0.
\tag{29}
\]
The martingale CLT therefore yields
\[
\frac{\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n}{\sqrt{U_n}}
=\frac{\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n}{\sqrt{V_n}}
\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\tag{30}
\]
The causal estimand is zero here, because the potential outcomes agree across arms pointwise and \(\sum_ac_a=0\); it is nevertheless the same fixed finite-population causal contrast \(\tau_n\), not a newly defined regression functional.

The history-uniform plug-in theorem applies to this admissible subclass. By (20), (25), and (27),
\[
\left|\frac{\widehat U_n}{U_n}-1\right|
\le\frac{|\widehat U_n-U_n|/s_n^2}{U_n/s_n^2}
\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{in probability}.
\tag{31}
\]
For fixed \(\rho\in(0,1)\), (30)--(31), Slutsky's theorem, and continuity of the standard normal distribution give
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Pr\!\left\{|\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n|
\le z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{(1-\rho)\widehat U_n}\right\}\\
&\qquad=\Pr\!\left\{
\left|\frac{\widehat\tau_n-\tau_n}{\sqrt{U_n}}\right|
\le z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{(1-\rho)\widehat U_n/U_n}\right\}\\
&\qquad\longrightarrow
2\Phi\!\left(z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{1-\rho}\right)-1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{32}
\]
Since \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), \(z_{1-\alpha/2}>0\); since \(\rho\in(0,1)\) and \(\Phi\) is strictly increasing, the limit in (32) is strictly below \(2\Phi(z_{1-\alpha/2})-1=1-\alpha\). If a broader family contains this subclass, its worst-case coverage is bounded above at every \(n\) by the coverage of this displayed member. Taking the limsup proves the final assertion. The witness is nonadaptive and globally consistent, so the certificate uses no claim about simultaneous realization of independently selected optimizers on an adaptive history tree.
