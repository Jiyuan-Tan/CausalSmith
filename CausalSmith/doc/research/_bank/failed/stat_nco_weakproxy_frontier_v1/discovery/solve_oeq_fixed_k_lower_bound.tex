%%% FIELD fixed-k-resolution-statement
Fix \(K\).  Suppose that there is one number \(\kappa_K>0\), quantified before \(a\), such that for every \(a\in[a_L,a_U]\) the ambient class \(\mathcal P_K(a)\) contains an \((X,W)\) design with mass function \(q_a\), marginals \(q_{a,X},q_{a,W}\), and conditional-expectation operator \(A_a\) satisfying
\[
\begin{gathered}
q_a(x,w)\ge \kappa_K\quad\text{for every }(x,w)\in\mathcal X_K\times\mathcal W_K,\\
q_{a,X}(x)-\frac{\eta}{K}\ge\kappa_K,
\qquad \frac{1}{\eta K}-q_{a,X}(x)\ge\kappa_K
\quad\text{for every }x\in\mathcal X_K,\\
q_{a,W}(w)-\frac{\eta}{K}\ge\kappa_K,
\qquad \frac{1}{\eta K}-q_{a,W}(w)\ge\kappa_K
\quad\text{for every }w\in\mathcal W_K,
\end{gathered}
\]
and, for every \(g\in\ell^2_{K,W}\) with \(E_{q_a}\{g(W)\}=0\),
\[
(c_{\rm link}+\kappa_K)\|L^{-a}g\|_{\ell^2_{K,W}}
\le \|A_ag\|_{L^2(q_{a,X})}
\le (C_{\rm link}-\kappa_K)\|L^{-a}g\|_{\ell^2_{K,W}}.
\]
Then the transverse handle \(\mathsf H_{\rm tr}\) produces laws \(P_{0,a},P_{t,a}\in\mathcal P_K(a)\) with the same \((X,W)\) design and hence the same displayed slacks, such that \(P_{0,a}\) and \(P_{t,a}\) are strictly positive, \(d(P_{0,a})=0\), and \(d(P_{t,a})=t\) for every \(0<t\le\eta/2\).  Moreover,
\[
D_{\rm KL}(P_{t,a}\|P_{0,a})\le\frac{t^2}{\eta(1-\eta)}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\chi^2(P_{t,a}^{\otimes n},P_{0,a}^{\otimes n})
\le \exp\!\left\{\frac{nt^2}{\eta(1-\eta)}\right\}-1.
\]
Consequently, for every \(\epsilon_{\rm tot}\in(0,1)\), there is a constant \(c>0\), independent of \(a\) and \(n\), for which
\[
\rho^\star_{n,K}(a,\epsilon_{\rm tot})\ge c n^{-1/2}
\quad\text{uniformly over }a\in[a_L,a_U].
\]
Together with \(\mathrm{thm:fixed\mbox{-}k\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}bound}\), this yields \(\rho^\star_{n,K}(a,\epsilon_{\rm tot})\asymp n^{-1/2}\) uniformly in \(a\) for every fixed \(K\) satisfying the common-design premise.
%%% FIELD fixed-k-resolution-proof
Fix \(K\), the single constant \(\kappa_K\) in the premise, and an arbitrary \(a\in[a_L,a_U]\).  Select the asserted design \(q_a\); all expectations through the construction of the two laws are taken under this design.

First, the bridge class is nonempty.  The constant function \(h_{1/2}(w)=1/2\) has DCT coefficients \(\theta_1=1/2\) and \(\theta_j=0\) for \(j\ge2\).  Hence
\[
\sum_{j=1}^K j^{2\beta}\theta_j^2=\frac14<R^2,
\]
where the strict inequality uses \(R>1/2\), and
\[
\eta<h_{1/2}(w)<1-\eta\quad\text{for every }w
\]
uses \(0<\eta<1/2\).  Thus \(h_{1/2}\in\mathcal H_\beta(R,\eta)\) by \(\mathrm{def:bridge\mbox{-}class}\).  In coefficient coordinates the source ellipsoid is closed and bounded in \(\mathbb R^K\), and each box constraint is closed.  Their nonempty intersection is therefore compact.  The linear functional
\[
h\longmapsto E_{q_a}\{h(W)\}
\]
is continuous, so it has a maximizer \(h_a^*\in\mathcal H_\beta(R,\eta)\).  Put
\[
M_a=E_{q_a}\{h_a^*(W)\},
\qquad p_{0,a}(x)=(A_ah_a^*)(x).
\]
The pointwise box constraints and conditional averaging imply
\[
\eta\le p_{0,a}(x)\le1-\eta
\quad\text{for every }x\in\mathcal X_K. \tag{1}
\]

Keep the design \(q_a\) fixed and define the full observed-data laws
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{0,a}(X=x,W=w,Y=y)
&=q_a(x,w)p_{0,a}(x)^y\{1-p_{0,a}(x)\}^{1-y},\\
P_{t,a}(X=x,W=w,Y=y)
&=q_a(x,w)\{p_{0,a}(x)+t\}^y
  \{1-p_{0,a}(x)-t\}^{1-y},
\end{aligned} \tag{2}
\]
for \(0<t\le\eta/2\).  Equation (1) gives
\[
\eta\le p_{0,a}(x)+t\le1-\frac\eta2. \tag{3}
\]
Using \(q_a(x,w)\ge\kappa_K\), equations (1)--(3) show that every atom satisfies
\[
P_{0,a}(x,w,y)\ge\kappa_K\eta,
\qquad P_{t,a}(x,w,y)\ge\frac{\kappa_K\eta}{2}. \tag{4}
\]
Thus both probability tables are strictly positive.  Construction (2) changes only the conditional Bernoulli law of \(Y\) given \(X\).  It leaves the \((X,W)\) mass function, both marginals, and \(A_a\) unchanged.  The cell, marginal, and two link inequalities in the theorem premise therefore verify every member property in \(\mathrm{def:model\mbox{-}class}\), with their displayed slacks, and hence
\[
P_{0,a},P_{t,a}\in\mathcal P_K(a). \tag{5}
\]
This is membership in the ambient class; no claim that an unspecified auxiliary subclass is preserved is needed.

The conditional means under (2) are
\[
b_{0,a}=A_ah_a^*,
\qquad b_{t,a}=A_ah_a^*+t\mathbf 1. \tag{6}
\]
The first equality, feasibility of \(h_a^*\), and \(\mathrm{def:observable\mbox{-}defect}\) give \(d(P_{0,a})=0\).  For every \(h\in\mathcal H_\beta(R,\eta)\), Cauchy--Schwarz in \(L^2(q_{a,X})\), \(\|\mathbf1\|_{L^2(q_{a,X})}=1\), iterated expectation, and maximality of \(h_a^*\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|b_{t,a}-A_ah\|_{L^2(q_{a,X})}
&\ge \left|E_{q_a}\{b_{t,a}(X)-(A_ah)(X)\}\right|\\
&=M_a+t-E_{q_a}\{h(W)\}\\
&\ge t. 
\end{aligned} \tag{7}
\]
For \(h=h_a^*\), equation (6) makes the residual \(t\mathbf1\), whose norm is \(t\).  Combining both directions yields
\[
d(P_{t,a})=t. \tag{8}
\]
In particular, the continuous nonzero functional \(z\mapsto\langle z,\mathbf1\rangle_{L^2(q_{a,X})}\) exposes the face
\[
\left\{A_ah:h\in\mathcal H_\beta(R,\eta),\ E_{q_a}h(W)=M_a\right\}
\]
of the image.  Equation (6) places \(b_{0,a}\) on that face and moves \(b_{t,a}\) in its outward supporting-normal direction.  The face is proper: the strict feasibility of \(h_{1/2}\) permits a sufficiently small positive constant perturbation, so an interior feasible point cannot maximize the displayed expectation.  Thus the construction is genuinely transverse at a boundary null.

By (4), \(P_{t,a}\ll P_{0,a}\).  Direct summation over \(Y\in\{0,1\}\), conditional on \(X\), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\chi^2(P_{t,a},P_{0,a})
&=E_{q_{a,X}}\!\left[
  \frac{t^2}{p_{0,a}(X)}+
  \frac{t^2}{1-p_{0,a}(X)}\right]\\
&=E_{q_{a,X}}\!\left[
  \frac{t^2}{p_{0,a}(X)\{1-p_{0,a}(X)\}}\right]\\
&\le\frac{t^2}{\eta(1-\eta)},
\end{aligned} \tag{9}
\]
where (1) proves the last inequality.  If \(Z=dP_{t,a}/dP_{0,a}\), then \(\log Z\le Z-1\), change of measure, and \(E_{P_{0,a}}Z=1\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
D_{\rm KL}(P_{t,a}\|P_{0,a})
&=E_{P_{t,a}}\log Z\\
&\le E_{P_{t,a}}(Z-1)
=E_{P_{0,a}}\{Z(Z-1)\}\\
&=E_{P_{0,a}}(Z-1)^2
=\chi^2(P_{t,a},P_{0,a})\\
&\le\frac{t^2}{\eta(1-\eta)}. 
\end{aligned} \tag{10}
\]

Under \(\mathrm{ass:iid\mbox{-}sampling}\), product likelihood ratios factor, and therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
1+\chi^2(P_{t,a}^{\otimes n},P_{0,a}^{\otimes n})
&=\{1+\chi^2(P_{t,a},P_{0,a})\}^n\\
&\le\exp\!\left\{n\chi^2(P_{t,a},P_{0,a})\right\}\\
&\le\exp\!\left\{\frac{nt^2}{\eta(1-\eta)}\right\}.
\end{aligned} \tag{11}
\]
Subtracting one proves the asserted product \(\chi^2\) bound.

We finally convert (11) to a minimax testing converse.  For probability laws \(Q\ll P\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
\|Q-P\|_{\rm TV}
=\frac12E_P\left|\frac{dQ}{dP}-1\right|
\le\frac12\sqrt{\chi^2(Q,P)}. \tag{12}
\]
For every possibly randomized test \(0\le\phi\le1\), the variational definition of total variation gives
\[
E_P\phi+E_Q(1-\phi)
=1-\{E_Q\phi-E_P\phi\}
\ge1-\|Q-P\|_{\rm TV}. \tag{13}
\]
Define
\[
c=\min\!\left\{\frac\eta2,
\sqrt{\eta(1-\eta)\log\!\left(1+(1-\epsilon_{\rm tot})^2\right)}\right\}>0,
\qquad t=\frac{c}{\sqrt n}. \tag{14}
\]
Because \(n\ge1\), equation (14) ensures \(0<t\le\eta/2\).  Equations (11)--(14), with \(P=P_{0,a}^{\otimes n}\) and \(Q=P_{t,a}^{\otimes n}\), yield
\[
E_{P_{0,a}^{\otimes n}}\phi+E_{P_{t,a}^{\otimes n}}(1-\phi)
\ge1-\frac{1-\epsilon_{\rm tot}}2
=\frac{1+\epsilon_{\rm tot}}2
>\epsilon_{\rm tot}. \tag{15}
\]
By (5) and (8), the first law is a member of the composite null and the second is an alternative at defect \(t\).  Hence the total error in \(\mathrm{def:minimax\mbox{-}radius}\) exceeds \(\epsilon_{\rm tot}\) for every separation \(r\le t\), because the alternative set \(\{P:d(P)\ge r\}\) then contains \(P_{t,a}\).  It follows that
\[
\rho^\star_{n,K}(a,\epsilon_{\rm tot})\ge\frac{c}{\sqrt n}. \tag{16}
\]
The constant in (14) is independent of \(a\) and \(n\).  Since \(a\) was arbitrary and \(\kappa_K\) was quantified before \(a\), (16) is uniform over \([a_L,a_U]\).  The common-design premise also makes every relevant \(\mathcal P_K(a)\) nonempty.  Combining (16) with \(\mathrm{thm:fixed\mbox{-}k\mbox{-}upper\mbox{-}bound}\) proves the fixed-support equivalence.
