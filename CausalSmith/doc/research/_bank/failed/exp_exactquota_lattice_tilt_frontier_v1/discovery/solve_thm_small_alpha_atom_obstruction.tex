%%% FIELD proof-small-alpha-atom-obstruction
By Lemma \(\texttt{lem:global-compatible-atom-bound}\) and Theorem \(\texttt{thm:deterministic-order-atom-frontier}\), the finite atom thresholds satisfy
\[
0<\delta_\star\le \delta_{\mathrm{ord}}\le 1.
\]
Hence the assumed inequality \(0<\alpha<\delta_\star\) implies
\[
\alpha<\delta_{\mathrm{ord}}.
\]
Fix a compatible arm--outcome table \(H\), meaning \(\mathcal N(H)\ne\varnothing\), and put
\[
\mathfrak O_H=\{\mathcal O(N):N\in\mathcal N(H)\}.
\]
The rule \(\mathfrak C_\alpha\) is deterministic and table-measurable and, by hypothesis, is uniformly design-valid over all deterministic binary schedules in the stated class. Therefore Theorem \(\texttt{thm:deterministic-order-atom-frontier}\), applied using \(\alpha<\delta_{\mathrm{ord}}\), yields both
\[
\mathfrak O_H\subseteq\mathfrak C_\alpha(H)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\widehat{\mathfrak O}_\alpha(H)=\mathfrak O_H.
\]
The assumed containment \(\mathfrak C_\alpha(H)\subseteq\widehat{\mathfrak O}_\alpha(H)\) consequently gives
\[
\widehat{\mathfrak O}_\alpha(H)
=\mathfrak O_H
\subseteq\mathfrak C_\alpha(H)
\subseteq\widehat{\mathfrak O}_\alpha(H),
\]
and thus
\[
\mathfrak C_\alpha(H)=\widehat{\mathfrak O}_\alpha(H).
\]
Because \(H\) was arbitrary, this equality holds at every compatible table. The baseline rule used here is itself uniformly valid by Theorem \(\texttt{thm:exact-weak-order-coverage}\).

For the sharper witness, fix \((H,N)\in\mathfrak J\) and suppose
\[
\mathcal O(N)\notin\mathfrak C_\alpha(H).
\]
The realizability part of Lemma \(\texttt{lem:deterministic-coverage-iff}\), together with the response-type construction \(\texttt{def:response-type}\) under the fixed-schedule and stratified-interference assumptions, supplies a deterministic binary schedule in the stated class whose response-count vector satisfies \(N^\star=N\). Under the exact first- and second-stage randomization assumptions, the exact identity in that lemma gives its design noncoverage probability as
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\Pr_{A\sim\pi_{\exp}}\!\left\{
\mathcal O(N^\star)\notin
\mathfrak C_\alpha(H^{\mathrm{obs}})\right\}\\
&\qquad=
\sum_{\substack{H':(H',N)\in\mathfrak J}}
L_N(H')\,
\mathbf 1\!\left\{
\mathcal O(N)\notin\mathfrak C_\alpha(H')
\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every summand is nonnegative. The summand indexed by the fixed \(H\) is present because \((H,N)\in\mathfrak J\), and its indicator is one by the supposed omission. It follows that
\[
\Pr_{A\sim\pi_{\exp}}\!\left\{
\mathcal O(N^\star)\notin
\mathfrak C_\alpha(H^{\mathrm{obs}})\right\}
\ge L_N(H).
\]
Lemma \(\texttt{lem:global-compatible-atom-bound}\) gives \(L_N(H)\ge\delta_\star\), while the present level satisfies \(\delta_\star>\alpha\). Therefore
\[
\Pr_{A\sim\pi_{\exp}}\!\left\{
\mathcal O(N^\star)\notin
\mathfrak C_\alpha(H^{\mathrm{obs}})\right\}
\ge L_N(H)\ge\delta_\star>\alpha.
\]
This is the asserted schedule-specific atom obstruction and contradicts uniform \((1-\alpha)\)-coverage whenever such an omission occurs.

Finally, suppose that a family indexed by every \(\alpha\in(0,1)\) were uniformly valid, were contained in \(\widehat{\mathfrak O}_\alpha\) at every compatible table, and were strictly contained at some compatible table at every level. Since \(\delta_\star>0\), choose \(\alpha_0\in(0,\delta_\star)\). The equality already proved at level \(\alpha_0\) rules out strict containment at every compatible table, a contradiction. The argument uses no claim about \(\alpha\ge\delta_\star\), so the stated upper-level limitation is preserved.
