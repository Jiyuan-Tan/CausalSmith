The proof separates the causal target, which is the equilibrium response over the observational-law fiber, from the rank-and-ratio functional computed from a mixing representative.

First, \(J_x(C)\) is nonempty. The full-row-rank assumption permits extending the \(p\) rows of \(C\) to the invertible matrix \(T_0\) in the unchanged certificate definition; write \(W_0=T_0^{-1}\). The \((x,x)\) entry of \(T_0W_0=I_n\) is
\[
 1=\sum_{j=1}^n C_{xj}(W_0)_{jx}. \tag{14}
\]
Thus some \(j\) has \(C_{xj}(W_0)_{jx}\ne0\). The inverse-cofactor identity gives
\[
 (W_0)_{jx}=(-1)^{x+j}\frac{\det((T_0)_{-x,-j})}{\det(T_0)}, \tag{15}
\]
so \((T_0)_{-x,-j}\) is nonsingular. Its observed rows other than \(x\), restricted away from column \(j\), are precisely the rows of \(C_{-x,-j}\). Every row subset of a nonsingular square matrix is linearly independent; hence \(\operatorname{rank}(C_{-x,-j})=p-1\). Together with \(C_{xj}\ne0\), the definition of \(J_x(C)\) proves nonemptiness.

For necessity, take any \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak F_x^{\mathrm{law}}(P_X)\). The law-fiber response-rank lemma, whose OICA step uses the exact Appendix B.1 uniqueness result and whose algebraic step canonicalizes the monomial disturbance matrix without changing \(Q\), gives
\[
 m_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}=n,
 \qquad
 j=\bar s_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(x)\in J_x(C),
 \qquad
 \rho_x(\widetilde{\mathcal M})
 =\left(\frac{C_{ij}}{C_{xj}}\right)_{i\in\mathsf O\setminus\{x\}}. \tag{16}
\]
Thus every causal response in the law fiber belongs to \(\mathcal V_x(C)\).

For sufficiency, take a vector \(v\in\mathcal V_x(C)\). By the response-frontier definition there is \(j\in J_x(C)\) with
\[
 v=\left(\frac{C_{ij}}{C_{xj}}\right)_{i\in\mathsf O\setminus\{x\}}. \tag{17}
\]
The canonical exact-witness lemma invokes the unchanged shear-and-matching construction and embeds its output as \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}_j=(n,Q_j,H_j,\varepsilon)\). It proves that this is a member of \(\mathfrak F_x^{\mathrm{law}}(P_X)\) and that its response vector is the right-hand side of (17). Hence every vector in \(\mathcal V_x(C)\) is attained. Combining this with (16) proves the sharp vector-set equality.

Projection onto coordinate \(y\) maps the vector indexed by \(j\) to \(C_{yj}/C_{xj}\). Applying this projection to both sides of the vector equality and invoking the definition of \(\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\) proves the scalar equality. The frontier monomial-invariance lemma proves that these sets do not depend on the permutation or nonzero scaling chosen for the ICA columns, so the displayed rank-and-ratio sets are functionals of \(P_X\) on the stated law class rather than artifacts of one representative.

A target is identified over a model fiber exactly when it is constant on that fiber. The two sharp equalities therefore imply that the full response is identified exactly when \(\mathcal V_x(C)\) has one element, and its \(y\)-coordinate is identified exactly when \(\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\) has one element. If \(\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\) is not a singleton, choose \(j,k\in J_x(C)\) with
\[
 \frac{C_{yj}}{C_{xj}}\ne\frac{C_{yk}}{C_{xk}}. \tag{18}
\]
The two canonical embeddings \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}_j\) and \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}_k\) both have observed law \(P_X\), satisfy observational and postintervention solvability, and have different \(y\)-coordinate responses by (18). They are the promised exact SCM countermodels.

Finally, Gaussian elimination extends \(C\) to \(T_0\) and computes \(W_0\) in \(O(n^3)\) arithmetic operations. For each of at most \(n\) columns, computing the rank of the \((p-1)\)-by-\((n-1)\) deletion matrix costs \(O(n^3)\), so all membership decisions for \(J_x(C)\) cost \(O(n^4)\). For each admissible index, the unchanged certificate uses one \(O(n^2)\) shear, one perfect matching in an \((n-1)\)-by-\((n-1)\) nonzero pattern computable by an augmenting-path method in \(O(n^3)\), and \(O(n^2)\) row normalization. Across at most \(n\) witnesses this is \(O(n^4)\). Forming all response ratios and discarding duplicate vectors or scalar values costs at most \(O(n^2)\) additional arithmetic comparisons. Therefore the overall certificate cost is \(O(n^4)\). Exact zero tests and arithmetic are meant in the real-arithmetic model; when \(C\) is rational, the canonical-witness lemma supplies rational \(Q_j,H_j\). No step invokes a stability restriction. Moment determinacy is not used in this population argument.
