For every \(j\in J_x(C)\), \(\operatorname{Cert}(C,x)\) returns \(\mathcal M_j\in\mathfrak F_x(P_X)\) with \(m_{\mathcal M_j}=n\), \(s_{\mathcal M_j}(x)=j\), and
\[
 \boldsymbol\theta_{-x\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M_j)=\frac{C_{-x,j}}{C_{xj}}.
\]
If \(C\) is rational, its returned matrices \(Q_j\) and \(H_j\) are rational.
