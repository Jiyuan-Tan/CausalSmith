All unlabeled matrix norms below are operator two-norms. Let \(\Delta=\lVert\mathcal T'_r-\mathcal T_r\rVert_F<\epsilon_0\). For each probe \(w\in\{u,v,e_1,\ldots,e_p\}\), contraction against unit vectors and orthogonal compression have norm at most one. Consequently, if \(M_w,M'_w\) are the contracted matricizations, then
\[
 \lVert M'_w-M_w\rVert\le\Delta.
\]

Choose an orthonormal basis \(U\) for \(\operatorname{range}(V_d)\), set \(S=U^{\mathsf T}V_d\), and compress both tensors into this same space:
\[
 A_w=U^{\mathsf T}M_wU,
 \qquad A'_w=U^{\mathsf T}M'_wU.
\]
The lifted-rank margin makes \(S\) invertible, with
\[
 s_{\min}(S)=s_{\min}(V_d)\ge\sigma,
 \qquad \lVert S\rVert\le\lVert V_d\rVert_F=\sqrt n,
 \qquad \operatorname{cond}(S)\le\chi=\frac{\sqrt n}{\sigma}.
\]
Writing
\[
 D_w=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\lambda_j(u^{\mathsf T}c_j)^{q-1}(w^{\mathsf T}c_j)\right),
\]
the tensor factorization gives \(A_w=SD_wS^{\mathsf T}\). In particular, the cumulant and orientation margins imply
\[
 s_{\min}(A_u)
 \ge s_{\min}(S)^2\min_j|\lambda_j(u^{\mathsf T}c_j)^q|
 \ge\kappa\sigma^{q+2}=\eta,
\]
whereas unit columns and \(|\lambda_j|\le\Lambda\) imply
\[
 \lVert A_w\rVert\le \lVert V_d\rVert^2\Lambda\le n\Lambda.
\]
Since \(\Delta<\eta/2\), the elementary Neumann identity
\[
 A'_u=A_u\{I+A_u^{-1}(A'_u-A_u)\}
\]
shows that \(A'_u\) is invertible and \(\lVert(A'_u)^{-1}\rVert\le2/\eta\). The primed exact factorization is
\[
 A'_u=S'D'_u(S')^{\mathsf T},
 \qquad S'=U^{\mathsf T}V'_d.
\]
Every diagonal entry of \(D'_u\) is nonzero; invertibility of \(A'_u\) therefore forces the square matrix \(S'\) to be invertible.

Define \(G_w=A_wA_u^{-1}\) and \(G'_w=A'_w(A'_u)^{-1}\). The inverse identity and the preceding bounds give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \lVert G'_w-G_w\rVert
 &\le \lVert A'_w-A_w\rVert\lVert(A'_u)^{-1}\rVert
   +\lVert A_w\rVert\lVert(A'_u)^{-1}(A_u-A'_u)A_u^{-1}\rVert\\
 &\le\left(\frac2\eta+\frac{2n\Lambda}{\eta^2}\right)\Delta
 =h\Delta.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Exact cancellation of the diagonal factors yields
\[
 G_w=S\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{w^{\mathsf T}c_j}{u^{\mathsf T}c_j}\right)S^{-1},
 \qquad
 G'_w=S'\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\frac{w^{\mathsf T}c'_j}{u^{\mathsf T}c'_j}\right)(S')^{-1}.
\]

We next pair the two spectra without presupposing a label. Let \(\rho_j=v^{\mathsf T}c_j/(u^{\mathsf T}c_j)\), let \(g=\sigma/3\), and let \(\Gamma_j\) be the positively oriented complex circle of radius \(g\) about \(\rho_j\). The gap assumption makes these circles disjoint. For \(z\in\Gamma_j\), diagonalization gives
\[
 \lVert(zI-G_v)^{-1}\rVert\le\frac{\chi}{g}.
\]
For \(G_v(t)=G_v+t(G'_v-G_v)\), \(0\le t\le1\), the condition \(\Delta<\sigma/(6\chi h)\) gives
\[
 \lVert t(G'_v-G_v)(zI-G_v)^{-1}\rVert
 \le \frac{\chi h\Delta}{g}<\frac12.
\]
Thus the Neumann series makes every \(zI-G_v(t)\) invertible and bounds its inverse by \(2\chi/g\). Define entrywise
\[
 P_j(t)=\frac{1}{2\pi\mathrm i}\int_{\Gamma_j}(zI-G_v(t))^{-1}\,dz.
\]
At \(t=0\), direct integration of the displayed diagonalization gives \(P_j(0)=SE_{jj}S^{-1}\), a rank-one projector. The resolvent identity verifies directly that every \(P_j(t)\) is idempotent; it is continuous in \(t\), so its trace, equal to its integer rank, is constantly one. At \(t=1\) the \(n\) disjoint rank-one projectors account for all \(n\) eigenvalues and hence define a permutation pairing of the primed and unprimed components. Relabel the primed components by this permutation and write \(P'_j=P_j(1)\).

The resolvent identity under the same contour bounds yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \lVert P'_j-P_j\rVert
 &\le \frac{1}{2\pi}(2\pi g)
 \frac{\chi}{g}\,h\Delta\,\frac{2\chi}{g}
 =\frac{6\chi^2h}{\sigma}\Delta,\\
 \lVert P_j\rVert&\le\chi,
 \qquad
 \lVert P'_j\rVert\le\lVert P_j\rVert+\lVert P'_j-P_j\rVert<2\chi.
 \end{aligned}
\]
For each coordinate probe, simultaneous diagonalization and cyclicity of trace give
\[
 z_{ij}:=\operatorname{tr}(G_{e_i}P_j)=\frac{c_{ij}}{u^{\mathsf T}c_j},
 \qquad
 z'_{ij}:=\operatorname{tr}(G'_{e_i}P'_j)=\frac{c'_{ij}}{u^{\mathsf T}c'_j}.
\]
Since \(|\operatorname{tr}K|\le n\lVert K\rVert\) and \(\lVert G_{e_i}\rVert\le n\Lambda/\eta\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |z'_{ij}-z_{ij}|
 &\le n\lVert G'_{e_i}-G_{e_i}\rVert\lVert P'_j\rVert
 +n\lVert G_{e_i}\rVert\lVert P'_j-P_j\rVert\\
 &\le nh\left(2\chi+\frac{6n\Lambda\chi^2}{\eta\sigma}\right)\Delta
 =L_z\Delta.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The orientation and unit-normalization conditions give
\[
 \lVert z_j\rVert_2=(u^{\mathsf T}c_j)^{-1}\ge1,
 \qquad
 \lVert z'_j\rVert_2=(u^{\mathsf T}c'_j)^{-1}\ge1,
\]
and \(c_j=z_j/\lVert z_j\rVert_2\), with the same formula for \(c'_j\). For nonzero vectors of norm at least one, adding and subtracting the common denominator shows
\[
 \left\lVert\frac{z'}{\lVert z'\rVert_2}-\frac{z}{\lVert z\rVert_2}\right\rVert_2
 \le2\lVert z'-z\rVert_2.
\]
Hence each paired column obeys \(\lVert c'_j-c_j\rVert_2\le2\sqrt p L_z\Delta\), and summing squared column bounds gives
\[
 \lVert C'_{\pi}-C\rVert_F
 \le2\sqrt{np}L_z\Delta=L_C\Delta.
\]
The projector-trace formulas are exactly the declared map \(\operatorname{OICAInv}\); applying them with each tensor's own range basis changes only the diagonalizing coordinates and returns the same normalized directions. Thus that map recovers the directions attaining the bound.
