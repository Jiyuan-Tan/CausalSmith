For every \(N\) and every nonempty class \(\mathfrak P_{p,n,r}(a,\delta,\sigma,\kappa,\Lambda,M)\) with \(d\ge1\), \(q\ge1\), and \(n\le\binom{p+d-1}{d}\), the iid scalar and response-vector confidence unions satisfy
\[
\inf_{\zeta=(P_X,C,\varepsilon,\lambda)\in\mathfrak P_{p,n,r}}
P_X^{\otimes N}\!\left\{
\begin{array}{l}
\mathcal R_x^{\mathrm{vec}}(C)\subseteq\mathcal U_x^{\mathrm{vec}}
\text{ and }\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\subseteq\mathcal U_{xy}
\text{ for every }x\ne y\in\mathsf O,\\
\text{and, if }2\tau_N<\epsilon_0\text{ and }b_N<\min\{a,\delta\}/2,
\text{ then after one common permutation}\\
\widehat J_x=J_x(C),\quad
d_H(\mathcal U_x^{\mathrm{vec}},\mathcal R_x^{\mathrm{vec}}(C))\le2w_N^{\mathrm{vec}},\quad
d_H(\mathcal U_{xy},\mathcal R_{xy}(C))\le2w_N
\text{ for every }x\ne y
\end{array}\right\}\ge1-\alpha.
\]
By the sharp-frontier theorem these are confidence unions for the full observational-law-fiber causal targets. Uniformly over the same representation-indexed tuples in the informative regime, \(2w_N^{\mathrm{vec}}=O(N^{-1/2})\) and \(2w_N=O(N^{-1/2})\).
