Fix a tuple \(\zeta\) in the displayed regular class. All bounds below are independent of that tuple. Index the \(m=\binom{p+r}{r}-1\) nonconstant raw moments through degree \(r\) by multi-indices \(\beta\), and write \(\mu_\beta=\mathbb E[X^\beta]\). Unit column normalization gives
\[
 \lVert X\rVert_2\le\sum_{j=1}^n|\varepsilon_j|.
\]
Convexity of \(t\mapsto t^{2r}\), followed by the source-moment envelope, yields
\[
 \mathbb E\lVert X\rVert_2^{2r}
 \le n^{2r-1}\sum_{j=1}^n\mathbb E|\varepsilon_j|^{2r}
 \le n^{2r}M\le H.
\]
For \(0<s\le2r\), concavity of \(z\mapsto z^{s/(2r)}\) on \([0,\infty)\) gives
\[
 \mathbb E\lVert X\rVert_2^s
 =\mathbb E\!\left[(\lVert X\rVert_2^{2r})^{s/(2r)}\right]
 \le\left(\mathbb E\lVert X\rVert_2^{2r}\right)^{s/(2r)}
 \le H^{s/(2r)}\le H.
\]
Since \(|X^\beta|\le\lVert X\rVert_2^{|\beta|}\), it follows, for \(1\le|\beta|\le r\), that
\[
 |\mu_\beta|\le H,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(X^\beta)\le\mathbb E\lVert X\rVert_2^{2|\beta|}\le H.
\]
Let \(\bar\mu\) be the vector of the corresponding empirical means before clipping. Independence of the \(N\) observations gives
\[
 \mathbb E\lVert\bar\mu-\mu\rVert_2^2
 =\frac1N\sum_\beta\operatorname{Var}(X^\beta)
 \le\frac{mH}{N}\le\frac{m(1+H)}{N}.
\]
Coordinatewise clipping to \([-B_m,B_m]\) cannot increase distance from \(\mu\), since \(|\mu_\beta|\le H<B_m\). Markov's inequality, applied to the nonnegative squared norm, therefore gives the single simultaneous event
\[
 \mathcal E_N:=\{\lVert\widehat\mu-\mu\rVert_2\le e_N\},
 \qquad
 P_X^{\otimes N}(\mathcal E_N)\ge1-\alpha.
\]

For an ordered tensor coordinate \((i_1,\ldots,i_r)\), the moment--cumulant partition polynomial is
\[
 \operatorname{cum}(X_{i_1},\ldots,X_{i_r})
 =\sum_{\pi\in\mathfrak P_r}(-1)^{|\pi|-1}(|\pi|-1)!
   \prod_{b\in\pi}\mathbb E\!\left[\prod_{k\in b}X_{i_k}\right].
\]
For two moment vectors in the clipping box, telescoping a product with \(|\pi|\) factors shows that its difference is at most
\[
 |\pi|B_m^{|\pi|-1}\lVert\widehat\mu-\mu\rVert_2.
\]
There are \(p^r\) ordered tensor coordinates. Summing their squared bounds proves, on \(\mathcal E_N\),
\[
 \lVert\widehat{\mathcal T}_r-\mathcal T_r\rVert_F
 \le p^{r/2}\sum_{\pi\in\mathfrak P_r}(|\pi|-1)!|\pi|B_m^{|\pi|-1}e_N
 =L_me_N=\tau_N.
\]

The numerical factor set used in the minimum-distance definition is nonempty because the true \((C,\lambda)\) is feasible. It is compact: unit-normalized columns and \(\kappa\le|\lambda_j|\le\Lambda\) give boundedness, while the singular-value, probe-gap, orientation, loading-alternative, and deletion-rank-alternative constraints are closed. The continuous objective therefore attains its minimum; successive minimization of coordinates over compact argmin sets defines the lexicographic tie choice and a Borel measurable estimator. Let
\[
 \widehat{\mathcal T}^{\mathrm{fit}}_r
 =\sum_{j=1}^n\widehat\lambda_j\widehat c_j^{\otimes r}.
\]
On \(\mathcal E_N\), feasibility of the true factor gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \lVert\widehat{\mathcal T}^{\mathrm{fit}}_r-\mathcal T_r\rVert_F
 &\le\lVert\widehat{\mathcal T}^{\mathrm{fit}}_r-\widehat{\mathcal T}_r\rVert_F
 +\lVert\widehat{\mathcal T}_r-\mathcal T_r\rVert_F\\
 &\le2\lVert\widehat{\mathcal T}_r-\mathcal T_r\rVert_F
 \le2\tau_N.
 \end{aligned}
\]

Suppose first that the deterministic informative-regime inequalities \(2\tau_N<\epsilon_0\) and \(b_N<\min\{a,\delta\}/2\) hold. The proof of the local-inverse lemma uses only the numerical restrictions imposed on the fitted factor, so it applies to the true and fitted tensors and supplies one common permutation for which
\[
 \lVert\widehat C-C\rVert_F
 \le L_C\lVert\widehat{\mathcal T}^{\mathrm{fit}}_r-\mathcal T_r\rVert_F
 \le2L_C\tau_N=b_N.
\]
Use this alignment for the remainder of the proof. Both \(C\) and \(\widehat C\) satisfy the loading alternative: each entry is either zero or has magnitude at least \(a\). Since every coordinate difference is less than \(a/2\), one entry cannot be zero while its counterpart is nonzero. Their zero patterns therefore coincide simultaneously for all observed rows and sources.

Similarly, for every \(i\in\mathsf O\) and \(j\in\mathsf S\), the variational formula for the least singular value gives
\[
 \left|s_{\min}(\widehat C_{-i,-j})-s_{\min}(C_{-i,-j})\right|
 \le\lVert\widehat C_{-i,-j}-C_{-i,-j}\rVert
 \le\lVert\widehat C-C\rVert_F<b_N<\delta/2.
\]
Both factors satisfy the deletion alternative that this singular value is either zero or at least \(\delta\), so its zero status also agrees. It follows directly from the definition of the admissible-source set that
\[
 \widehat J_i=J_i(C)\qquad(i\in\mathsf O)
\]
after the one common permutation.

If \(j\in J_x(C)\), the coincident loading pattern and its margin give \(|C_{xj}|\ge a\) and \(|\widehat C_{xj}|\ge a\); unit normalization gives \(|C_{yj}|\le1\). Thus
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \left|\frac{\widehat C_{yj}}{\widehat C_{xj}}-
             \frac{C_{yj}}{C_{xj}}\right|
 &\le \frac{|\widehat C_{yj}-C_{yj}|}{|\widehat C_{xj}|}
 +\frac{|C_{yj}|\,|\widehat C_{xj}-C_{xj}|}
 {|\widehat C_{xj}|\,|C_{xj}|}\\
 &\le(a^{-1}+a^{-2})b_N=w_N.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The bound holds for every ordered observed pair and admissible source on the same event. Hence every point of \(\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\) lies in the corresponding interval of \(\mathcal U_{xy}\). Conversely, every point of that interval is within \(w_N\) of its estimated center, which is within \(w_N\) of the paired true point. Therefore
\[
 \mathcal R_{xy}(C)\subseteq\mathcal U_{xy},
 \qquad
 d_H(\mathcal U_{xy},\mathcal R_{xy}(C))\le2w_N
\]
simultaneously for all \(x\ne y\).

If either informative-regime inequality fails, the definition returns \(\mathcal U_{xy}=[-a^{-1},a^{-1}]\). For \(j\in J_x(C)\), unit normalization and the loading margin give
\[
 \left|\frac{C_{yj}}{C_{xj}}\right|\le a^{-1},
\]
so this fallback contains the whole frontier deterministically. Combining both regimes with \(P_X^{\otimes N}(\mathcal E_N)\ge1-\alpha\), and then taking the infimum over the class, proves the displayed simultaneous probability inequality.

Finally, with \(p,n,r,M,\kappa,\Lambda,\sigma,\delta,a,\alpha\) fixed,
\[
 \tau_N=L_m\sqrt{\frac{m(1+H)}{N\alpha}}=O(N^{-1/2}),
 \qquad
 2w_N=4(a^{-1}+a^{-2})L_C\tau_N=O(N^{-1/2}).
\]
Thus the informative inequalities hold for all sufficiently large \(N\), uniformly over the representation-indexed class, and the asserted root-\(N\) Hausdorff uncertainty follows. The diameter of the causal frontier itself need not shrink; the proved rate concerns the confidence union's Hausdorff excess around that finite target.
