Suppose that \((n,C,\varepsilon)\) is an irreducible ICA representation of \(P_X\), every coordinate of \(\varepsilon\) is non-Gaussian, and \((m_{\mathcal M},A^{\mathcal M},\xi^{\mathcal M})\in\mathfrak I(P_X)\) is any other irreducible ICA representation. Then \(m_{\mathcal M}=n\), every column of \(A^{\mathcal M}\) is proportional to exactly one column of \(C\) and conversely, and every coordinate of \(\xi^{\mathcal M}\) is non-Gaussian. Equivalently, there are an \(n\)-by-\(n\) permutation matrix \(\Pi\) and a nonsingular diagonal matrix \(D\) such that \(A^{\mathcal M}=C\Pi D\).
