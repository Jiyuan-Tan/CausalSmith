Let \(\widetilde C=C\Pi D\), where \(\Pi e_k=e_{\pi(k)}\) for a permutation \(\pi\) of \(\mathsf S\) and \(D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_n)\) with every \(d_k\ne0\). Then \(k\in J_x(\widetilde C)\) if and only if \(\pi(k)\in J_x(C)\), and for every such \(k\),
\[
 \frac{\widetilde C_{-x,k}}{\widetilde C_{xk}}
 =\frac{C_{-x,\pi(k)}}{C_{x,\pi(k)}}.
\]
Consequently \(\mathcal R_x^{\mathrm{vec}}(\widetilde C)=\mathcal R_x^{\mathrm{vec}}(C)\) and \(\mathcal R_{xy}(\widetilde C)=\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\) for every \(y\in\mathsf O\setminus\{x\}\).
