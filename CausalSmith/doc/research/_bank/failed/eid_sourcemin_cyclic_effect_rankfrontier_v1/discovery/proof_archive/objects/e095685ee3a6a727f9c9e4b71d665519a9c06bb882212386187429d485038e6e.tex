For \(P_X=\mathcal L(C\varepsilon)\), let
\[
\mathfrak F_x(P_X)=\left\{\begin{array}{l|l}
\mathcal M=(m_{\mathcal M},Q,H,\xi^{\mathcal M})
&\begin{array}{l}
m_{\mathcal M}\ge p,\quad Q,H\in\mathbb R^{m_{\mathcal M}\times m_{\mathcal M}},\quad
QV=H\xi^{\mathcal M},\\
Q_{ii}=1\ (i\in\mathsf S_{\mathcal M}),\quad H\text{ is monomial},\quad \det(Q)\ne0,\\
A^{\mathcal M}=[Q^{-1}H]_{\mathsf O,:},\quad
\xi^{\mathcal M}_1,\ldots,\xi^{\mathcal M}_{m_{\mathcal M}}\text{ are mutually independent,}\\
\xi^{\mathcal M}_k\text{ is nondegenerate, non-Gaussian, and moment-determinate for every }k,\\
\text{every column of }A^{\mathcal M}\text{ is nonzero and no two are proportional},\\
\mathcal L(A^{\mathcal M}\xi^{\mathcal M})=P_X,\quad
\det(Q_{-x,-x})\ne0
\end{array}
\end{array}\right\}.
\]
Here every matrix deletion by \(-x\) deletes completion row and column \(x\), whereas \((A^{\mathcal M})_{-x,:}\) deletes only the observed row \(x\).
