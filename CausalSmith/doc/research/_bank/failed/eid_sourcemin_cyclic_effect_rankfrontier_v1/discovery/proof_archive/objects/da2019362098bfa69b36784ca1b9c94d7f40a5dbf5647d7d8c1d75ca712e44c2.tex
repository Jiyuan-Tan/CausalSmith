For every \(\mathcal M=(m_{\mathcal M},Q,H,\xi^{\mathcal M})\in\mathfrak F_x(P_X)\), put \(A=A^{\mathcal M}\) and \(k=s_{\mathcal M}(x)\). Then
\[
 A_{xk}\ne0,
 \qquad \operatorname{rank}(A_{-x,-k})=p-1,
 \qquad \boldsymbol\theta_{\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)=\frac{A_{-x,k}}{A_{xk}},
 \qquad \theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)=\frac{A_{yk}}{A_{xk}}.
\]
