For every \(\mathcal M=(m_{\mathcal M},Q,H,\xi^{\mathcal M})\in\mathfrak F_x(P_X)\), one has \(m_{\mathcal M}=n\) and \(A^{\mathcal M}=C\Pi D\) for some permutation matrix \(\Pi\) and nonsingular diagonal matrix \(D\). If \(k=s_{\mathcal M}(x)\), then \(k\in J_x(A^{\mathcal M})\), and
\[
\boldsymbol\theta_{\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)
=\frac{A^{\mathcal M}_{-x,k}}{A^{\mathcal M}_{xk}}
\in\mathcal R_x^{\mathrm{vec}}(C).
\]
For every \(y\ne x\), its \(y\)-coordinate is \(\theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)=A^{\mathcal M}_{yk}/A^{\mathcal M}_{xk}\in\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\).
