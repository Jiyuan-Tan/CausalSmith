The causal target is the set of equilibrium responses over \(\mathfrak F_x^{\mathrm{law}}(P_X)\). The deletion-rank sets are observed-law functionals only after their equality to that target and their invariance to the choice of exact mixing representative have been proved.

First identify the projective directions from the full observational law. The displayed representation \(P_X=\mathcal L(C\varepsilon)\) is among the representations unioned in \(\mathcal D(P_X)\): \(\mathrm{ass:nonzero\text{-}columns}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:distinct\text{-}directions}\) make \(C\) irreducible, while \(\mathrm{ass:independent\text{-}sources}\), \(\mathrm{ass:nondegenerate\text{-}sources}\), and \(\mathrm{ass:nongaussian\text{-}sources}\) give the required source properties. Let \(P_X=\mathcal L(A\xi)\) be any other representation in that union, with \(A\in\mathbb R^{p\times m}\). The hypotheses of \(\mathrm{lem:dai\text{-}irreducible\text{-}oica\text{-}uniqueness\) hold, so there are a permutation matrix \(\Pi\) and a nonsingular diagonal matrix \(D\) such that
\[
 m=n,
 \qquad A=C\Pi D. \tag{1}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \{[a_k]:1\le k\le m\}=\{[c_j]:j\in\mathsf S\}. \tag{2}
\]
Equation (2) holds for every member of the union in \(\mathrm{def:law\text{-}projective\text{-}direction\text{-}functional\), and the reference representation supplies one member. Hence
\[
 \mathcal D(P_X)=\{[c_j]:j\in\mathsf S\}. \tag{3}
\]
This proves uniqueness as a set-valued functional of the full law; it does not assert an evaluation algorithm.

Next prove nonemptiness of the admissible set. Put \(A_x=C_{-x,:}\). By \(\mathrm{ass:full\text{-}row\text{-}rank}\), deleting one row reduces the row rank by exactly one, so \(\operatorname{rank}(A_x)=p-1\). Rank-nullity gives
\[
 \dim\ker(A_x)=n-p+1,
 \qquad
 \dim\ker(C)=n-p. \tag{4}
\]
Because \(\ker(C)\subsetneq\ker(A_x)\), choose \(z\in\ker(A_x)\setminus\ker(C)\). All coordinates of \(Cz\) except possibly coordinate \(x\) vanish; since \(Cz\ne0\),
\[
 0\ne(Cz)_x=\sum_{j=1}^n C_{xj}z_j. \tag{5}
\]
Thus some \(j\) satisfies \(C_{xj}\ne0\) and \(z_j\ne0\). By \(\mathrm{lem:shared\text{-}kernel\text{-}matching\text{-}certificate}\), its matrix \(K_x\) is a basis matrix for \(\ker(A_x)\). Writing \(z=K_xb\), the inequality \(z_j\ne0\) implies that row \(j\) of \(K_x\) is nonzero. The same lemma's equivalence therefore gives \(j\in J_x(C)\), proving
\[
 J_x(C)\ne\varnothing. \tag{6}
\]

For necessity, fix \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak F_x^{\mathrm{law}}(P_X)\). The hypotheses of \(\mathrm{lem:law\text{-}fiber\text{-}response\text{-}rank}\) are precisely the declared irreducibility and source assumptions already listed. That lemma yields
\[
 m_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}=n,
 \qquad
 j=\bar s_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(x)\in J_x(C),
 \qquad
 \rho_x(\widetilde{\mathcal M})
 =\left(\frac{C_{ij}}{C_{xj}}\right)_{i\in\mathsf O\setminus\{x\}}. \tag{7}
\]
Membership \(j\in J_x(C)\), by \(\mathrm{def:admissible\text{-}source\text{-}set}\), states both \(C_{xj}\ne0\) and \(\operatorname{rank}(C_{-x,-j})=p-1\). Thus every response in the law fiber belongs to \(\mathcal V_x(C)\).

For sufficiency, fix \(j\in J_x(C)\). By \(\mathrm{lem:shared\text{-}kernel\text{-}matching\text{-}certificate}\), the declared witness algorithm returns \(\mathcal M_j=(Q_j,H_j)\in\mathfrak F_x(C,\varepsilon)\) with \(s_{\mathcal M_j}(x)=j\). Define
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal M}_j=(n,Q_j,H_j,\varepsilon). \tag{8}
\]
Membership in the fixed-law fiber supplies \((Q_j)_{ii}=1\), monomiality of \(H_j\), \(\det(Q_j)\ne0\), \(\det((Q_j)_{-x,-x})\ne0\), and
\[
 [Q_j^{-1}H_j]_{\mathsf O,:}=C. \tag{9}
\]
The independent, nondegenerate, non-Gaussian source assumptions apply to \(\varepsilon\), and the nonzero-column and distinct-direction assumptions apply to the observed mixing matrix in (9). Moreover,
\[
 \mathcal L([Q_j^{-1}H_j]_{\mathsf O,:}\varepsilon)
 =\mathcal L(C\varepsilon)=P_X. \tag{10}
\]
Equations (8)--(10) verify every condition in \(\mathrm{def:law\text{-}completion\text{-}fiber}\), so \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}_j\in\mathfrak F_x^{\mathrm{law}}(P_X)\).

Because \(H_j\) is monomial and \(s_{\mathcal M_j}(x)=j\), column \(j\) of \(H_j\) has its unique nonzero entry, say \(h_j\ne0\), in row \(x\). Therefore, for every observed \(i\),
\[
 (Q_j^{-1}H_j)_{ij}=(Q_j^{-1})_{ix}h_j. \tag{11}
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:law\text{-}hard\text{-}intervention\text{-}response}\), the response is well defined. Combining that lemma with (9)--(11), for each \(i\in\mathsf O\setminus\{x\}\), gives
\[
 [\rho_x(\widetilde{\mathcal M}_j)]_i
 =\frac{(Q_j^{-1})_{ix}}{(Q_j^{-1})_{xx}}
 =\frac{(Q_j^{-1}H_j)_{ij}}{(Q_j^{-1}H_j)_{xj}}
 =\frac{C_{ij}}{C_{xj}}. \tag{12}
\]
The divisions in (12) are valid: \(h_j\ne0\), and \(j\in J_x(C)\) implies \(C_{xj}\ne0\), hence (11) at \(i=x\) implies \((Q_j^{-1})_{xx}\ne0\). Thus every element of \(\mathcal V_x(C)\) is attained. Together with (7),
\[
 \{\rho_x(\widetilde{\mathcal M}):\widetilde{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak F_x^{\mathrm{law}}(P_X)\}
 =\mathcal V_x(C). \tag{13}
\]

Coordinate projection at \(y\) sends the vector indexed by \(j\) in (13) to \(C_{yj}/C_{xj}\). By \(\mathrm{def:effect\text{-}frontier}\),
\[
 \{[\rho_x(\widetilde{\mathcal M})]_y:\widetilde{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak F_x^{\mathrm{law}}(P_X)\}
 =\mathcal R_{xy}(C). \tag{14}
\]
If \(C'\) is another exact representative obtained by selecting and ordering one nonzero vector from each projective class in (3), then \(C'=C\Pi D\) for a permutation matrix and nonsingular diagonal matrix. Applying \(\mathrm{lem:frontier\text{-}monomial\text{-}invariance}\) gives
\[
 \mathcal V_x(C')=\mathcal V_x(C),
 \qquad
 \mathcal R_{xy}(C')=\mathcal R_{xy}(C). \tag{15}
\]
Thus (13)--(14) are observed-law functionals independent of the representative.

A response is point identified over the completion fiber exactly when it is constant on that fiber. The sharp equalities (13)--(14) therefore imply that the full response vector is identified if and only if \(\mathcal V_x(C)\) is a singleton, and its \(y\)-coordinate is identified if and only if \(\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\) is a singleton. If the latter is not a singleton, choose \(j,k\in J_x(C)\) with
\[
 \frac{C_{yj}}{C_{xj}}\ne\frac{C_{yk}}{C_{xk}}. \tag{16}
\]
The two completions \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}_j\) and \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}_k\) from (8) both have observed law \(P_X\), both are observationally and postintervention solvable by their law-fiber membership, and have distinct \(y\)-coordinate responses by (12) and (16). They are exact same-law SCM countermodels.

Finally, conditional on exact \(C\), \(\mathrm{lem:shared\text{-}kernel\text{-}matching\text{-}certificate}\) computes a single basis extension and inverse, all admissibility decisions from one kernel basis, all leave-one-row matchings from one column-saturating matching and one alternating forest, and materializes every dense pair \((Q_j,H_j)\) in \(O(n^3)\) exact real-arithmetic operations. Forming all coordinate ratios in (12)--(14) and deduplicating at most \(n\) vectors remains within that order. The cited certificate also establishes worst-case output optimality for dense all-witness output. This is conditional on an exact irreducible representative \(C\); (3) is qualitative full-law identification and supplies neither an evaluator nor an end-to-end complexity bound for obtaining \(C\) from \(P_X\). No assumption or construction imposes a spectral-radius or dynamical-stability condition. This proves every assertion.
