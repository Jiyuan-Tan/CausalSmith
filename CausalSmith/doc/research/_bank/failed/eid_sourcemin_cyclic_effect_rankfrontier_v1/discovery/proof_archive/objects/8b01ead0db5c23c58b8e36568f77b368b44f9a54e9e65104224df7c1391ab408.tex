For every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F_x(P_X)\), one has \(m_{\mathcal M}=n\) and \(A^{\mathcal M}=C\Pi D\) for some permutation matrix \(\Pi\) and nonsingular diagonal matrix \(D\). If \(k=s_{\mathcal M}(x)\) and \(j=\pi(k)\) is its matched anchor source, then \(j\in J_x(C)\) and
\[
 \boldsymbol\theta_{-x\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)
 =\frac{A^{\mathcal M}_{-x,k}}{A^{\mathcal M}_{xk}}
 =\frac{C_{-x,j}}{C_{xj}},
 \qquad
 \theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)=\frac{C_{yj}}{C_{xj}}.
\]
