Fix \(\mathcal M\) in the stated fiber and abbreviate \(m=m_{\mathcal M}\). Membership in \(\mathfrak F_x(P_X)\) gives \(Q_{ii}=1\), monomial \(H\), \(\det(Q)\ne0\), and \(\det(Q_{-x,-x})\ne0\). Let \(k=s_{\mathcal M}(x)\) and \(h=H_{xk}\ne0\). Since \(H\) is monomial, its \(k\)-th column has no other nonzero entry, so
\[
 He_k=h e_x. \tag{4}
\]

First derive the hard-intervention response rather than identifying it with an observed quantity by definition. Under \(\operatorname{do}(V_x=t)\), the remaining equilibrium equations are
\[
 Q_{-x,-x}V_{-x}=H_{-x,:}\xi^{\mathcal M}-Q_{-x,x}t.
\]
The postintervention determinant condition permits inversion and differentiation, giving
\[
 \frac{\partial\,\operatorname{Eq}_{\mathcal M}(V_{-x}\mid\operatorname{do}(V_x=t))}{\partial t}
 =-Q_{-x,-x}^{-1}Q_{-x,x}. \tag{5}
\]
The cofactor formula and the two determinant conditions give
\[
 (Q^{-1})_{xx}=\frac{\det(Q_{-x,-x})}{\det(Q)}\ne0. \tag{6}
\]
Taking the rows other than \(x\) in \(QQ^{-1}e_x=e_x\) yields
\[
 Q_{-x,-x}(Q^{-1})_{-x,x}+Q_{-x,x}(Q^{-1})_{xx}=0.
\]
Division is legal by (6), and comparison with (5) proves
\[
 \boldsymbol\theta_{\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)
 =\frac{(Q^{-1})_{-x,x}}{(Q^{-1})_{xx}}. \tag{7}
\]

Set \(T=Q^{-1}H\), so \(T\) is invertible and \(A=T_{\mathsf O,:}\). Equation (4) implies
\[
 T_{:,k}=h(Q^{-1})_{:,x}. \tag{8}
\]
In particular, (6) and \(h\ne0\) imply \(A_{xk}=h(Q^{-1})_{xx}\ne0\). Dividing the observed coordinates of (8) and using (7) gives the vector ratio, and taking coordinate \(y\) gives the scalar ratio.

It remains to prove the deletion rank. From (4), row \(k\) of \(H^{-1}\) is zero off column \(x\) and \((H^{-1})_{kx}=h^{-1}\). Therefore, using \(Q_{xx}=1\),
\[
 (T^{-1})_{kx}=(H^{-1}Q)_{kx}=h^{-1}Q_{xx}=h^{-1}\ne0. \tag{9}
\]
The inverse-cofactor formula applied to (9) shows \(\det(T_{-x,-k})\ne0\). Thus all rows of \(T_{-x,-k}\) are linearly independent. The \(p-1\) observed rows other than \(x\) form the submatrix \(A_{-x,-k}\), so those rows are independent and \(\operatorname{rank}(A_{-x,-k})=p-1\), as claimed.
