On the declared common-order subclass, the proved tensor-pencil inverse recovers all normalized directions with an explicit local modulus \(L_C\), and the proved confidence theorem yields simultaneous coverage and \(O(N^{-1/2})\) Hausdorff uncertainty for every \(\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\). For finitely many moment-determinate non-Gaussian sources, moment determinacy implies only that each non-Gaussian source differs from a Gaussian in some finite moment; it supplies neither a common informative cumulant order, a lifted-rank condition for the union of order-specific components, a cross-order label-alignment margin, nor uniform moment and separation lower bounds. The common-order result is therefore the strongest proved part of the proposed handle.
