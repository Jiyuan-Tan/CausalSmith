For every \(j\in J_x(C)\), the shear-and-matching certificate returns
\[
\mathcal M_j=(n,Q_j,H_j,\varepsilon)\in\mathfrak F_x(P_X),
\qquad A^{\mathcal M_j}=C,
\qquad s_{\mathcal M_j}(x)=j,
\]
and
\[
\boldsymbol\theta_{\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M_j)=\frac{C_{-x,j}}{C_{xj}},
\qquad
\theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M_j)=\frac{C_{yj}}{C_{xj}}
\quad(y\in\mathsf O\setminus\{x\}).
\]
If \(C\) is rational, then the returned \(Q_j\) and \(H_j\) are rational.
