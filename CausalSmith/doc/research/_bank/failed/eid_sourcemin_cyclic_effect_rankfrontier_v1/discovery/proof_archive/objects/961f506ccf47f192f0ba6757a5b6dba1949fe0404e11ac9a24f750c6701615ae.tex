For a fixed normalized full-row-rank representation \(X=C\varepsilon\) with mutually independent, nondegenerate, moment-determinate non-Gaussian sources and pairwise nonproportional mixing columns, put
\[
 A_\nu=\{j:\operatorname{cum}_\nu(\varepsilon_j)\ne0\},
 \qquad R_\nu=|A_\nu|.
\]
There is an exact population inverse which, at every cumulant order \(\nu\ge2n-1\), reshapes \(\operatorname{cum}_\nu(X)\) into blocks of degrees \((n-1,n-1,\nu-2n+2)\), computes a minimal three-factor decomposition, and recovers exactly \(\{[c_j]:j\in A_\nu\}\). For \(R_\nu\ge2\), recovery at that order is already certified whenever \(\nu\ge2R_\nu-1\) by the adaptive block degrees \((R_\nu-1,R_\nu-1,\nu-2R_\nu+2)\); the cases \(R_\nu=0\) and \(R_\nu=1\) are respectively the zero-tensor and rank-one cases. Unioning and exactly deduplicating the recovered projective directions stops after a finite representation-dependent order, recovers \(\{[c_1],\ldots,[c_n]\}\) without cross-order label alignment, and hence recovers \(\mathcal V_x(C)\) and every \(\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\). No finite cumulant cutoff is uniform over the qualitative moment-determinate class. Already for \(p=n=2\), that class contains two fixed separated direction and frontier targets and triangular sequences of observational laws whose \(N\)-sample total-variation distance tends to zero. Consequently neither the direction set nor the causal frontier admits a uniformly consistent estimator on that class, and no frontier-containing confidence-union sequence with uniform coverage strictly greater than one half can have uniformly vanishing Hausdorff error. The frontier map is also discontinuous at vanishing treatment loadings and deletion-rank boundaries. Thus a uniform root-\(N\) heterogeneous-order frontier law does not follow from the qualitative assumptions without quantitative non-Gaussianity, moment, Veronese-conditioning, angular, probe/eigen-gap, deletion-rank, and denominator margins. The existing common-order separated theorem retains its simultaneous coverage and root-\(N\) conclusion.
