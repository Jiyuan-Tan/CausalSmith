First, \(J_x(C)\) is nonempty. Choose the row-basis extension \(T_0\) from the witness construction and write \(W_0=T_0^{-1}\). The \((x,x)\) entry of \(T_0W_0=I_n\) says
\[
 1=\sum_{j=1}^n C_{xj}(W_0)_{jx}.
\]
Thus some \(j\) has both \(C_{xj}\ne0\) and \((W_0)_{jx}\ne0\). For that index,
\[
 \det((T_0)_{-x,-j})=(-1)^{x+j}\det(T_0)(W_0)_{jx}\ne0.
\]
The observed rows of this invertible minor include precisely \(C_{-x,-j}\); hence those \(p-1\) rows are independent and \(j\in J_x(C)\).

For every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F_x(C,\varepsilon)\), the intervention-ratio and necessary-deletion-rank lemmas give
\[
 \theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)
 =\frac{C_{y,s_{\mathcal M}(x)}}{C_{x,s_{\mathcal M}(x)}}
 \in\mathcal R_{xy}(C).
\]
This proves the upper inclusion. Conversely, if \(r_0\in\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\), then by the definition of the frontier there is \(j\in J_x(C)\) with \(r_0=C_{yj}/C_{xj}\). The shear-matching-sufficiency lemma returns \(\mathcal M_j\in\mathfrak F_x(C,\varepsilon)\) having assigned source \(j\); the intervention-ratio lemma then gives
\[
 \theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M_j)=\frac{C_{yj}}{C_{xj}}=r_0.
\]
This is the reverse inclusion and establishes sharpness over the entire frozen fiber. The source assumptions ensure that every returned model uses exactly the supplied mutually independent, nondegenerate, non-Gaussian sources; the construction neither adds nor splits a source. No stability premise is used.

For completeness, a standard-coordinate row-basis extension and one exact inverse cost \(O(n^3)\) arithmetic operations. The equivalence proved in the shear-matching-sufficiency lemma determines every deletion-rank decision by inspecting row \(j\) of \(W_0\), after also checking \(C_{xj}\ne0\), so all indices cost \(O(n^2)\) after the inverse. For each of at most \(n\) admissible indices, the shear is a rank-one update and a perfect matching in an \((n-1)\)-by-\((n-1)\) nonzero pattern can be found by at most \(n-1\) augmentations, each scanning \(O(n^2)\) entries, hence in \(O(n^3)\) operations. Row normalization costs \(O(n^2)\). The total is
\[
 O(n^3)+n\,O(n^3)=O(n^4).
\]
Computing and deduplicating the at most \(n\) ratios costs no more than this bound. Since a witness is constructed for every admissible index, selecting one among the returned witnesses for each distinct ratio certifies every value.
