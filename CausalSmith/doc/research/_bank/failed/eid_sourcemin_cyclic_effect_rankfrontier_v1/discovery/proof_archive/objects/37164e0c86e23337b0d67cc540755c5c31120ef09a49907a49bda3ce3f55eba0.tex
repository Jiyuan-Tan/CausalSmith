Put \(A=Q^{-1}\). Membership in \(\mathfrak F_x(C,\varepsilon)\) gives \(\det Q\ne0\), \(Q_{ii}=1\), and \(\det(Q_{-x,-x})\ne0\). Under the perfect intervention \(V_x=t\), the remaining structural equations are
\[
 Q_{-x,-x}V_{-x}=H_{-x,:}\varepsilon-Q_{-x,x}t.
\]
The postintervention determinant condition therefore gives a unique equilibrium and
\[
 \frac{\partial V_{-x}}{\partial t}=-Q_{-x,-x}^{-1}Q_{-x,x}.
\]
The rows other than \(x\) in \(QA_{:,x}=e_x\) give
\[
 Q_{-x,-x}A_{-x,x}+Q_{-x,x}A_{xx}=0.
\]
The principal-cofactor identity and the two determinant conditions yield
\[
 A_{xx}=\frac{\det(Q_{-x,-x})}{\det Q}\ne0.
\]
Consequently
\[
 -Q_{-x,-x}^{-1}Q_{-x,x}=\frac{A_{-x,x}}{A_{xx}},
 \qquad
 \theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)=\frac{A_{yx}}{A_{xx}},
\]
which also derives the matrix expression in the equilibrium-effect definition from the intervened equations.

Let \(j=s_{\mathcal M}(x)\). The monomial-source condition says that \(h:=H_{xj}\ne0\) and \(H_{ij}=0\) for \(i\ne x\). The fixed-law equality \([AH]_{\mathsf O,:}=C\) then gives, for every observed \(i\),
\[
 C_{ij}=(AH)_{ij}=A_{ix}h.
\]
In particular \(C_{xj}=A_{xx}h\ne0\), so division is legitimate, and
\[
 \theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M)
 =\frac{A_{yx}}{A_{xx}}
 =\frac{A_{yx}h}{A_{xx}h}
 =\frac{C_{yj}}{C_{xj}}.
\]
