Suppose \(A^{\mathcal M}\in\mathbb R^{p\times n}\) and \(A^{\mathcal M}=C\Pi D\), where \(\Pi\) is a permutation matrix and \(D\) is nonsingular diagonal. If \(\pi\) is defined by \(A^{\mathcal M}_{:,k}=D_{kk}C_{:,\pi(k)}\), then for every \(k\in\mathsf S\),
\[
k\in J_x(A^{\mathcal M})\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \pi(k)\in J_x(C),
\qquad
\frac{A^{\mathcal M}_{-x,k}}{A^{\mathcal M}_{xk}}
=\frac{C_{-x,\pi(k)}}{C_{x,\pi(k)}}.
\]
Consequently \(\mathcal R_x^{\mathrm{vec}}(A^{\mathcal M})=\mathcal R_x^{\mathrm{vec}}(C)\) and \(\mathcal R_{xy}(A^{\mathcal M})=\mathcal R_{xy}(C)\) for every \(y\in\mathsf O\setminus\{x\}\).
