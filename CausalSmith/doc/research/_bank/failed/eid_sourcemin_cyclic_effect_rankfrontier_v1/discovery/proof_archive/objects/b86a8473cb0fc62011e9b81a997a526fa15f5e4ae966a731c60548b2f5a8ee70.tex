Let \((C,\lambda)\) and \((C',\lambda')\) be two normalized numerical factor pairs satisfying all cumulant-magnitude, probe-orientation, lifted-rank, pencil-gap, and dimensional restrictions in the definition of \(\mathfrak P_{p,n,r}(a,\delta,\sigma,\kappa,\Lambda,M)\), and let \(\mathcal T_r=\sum_j\lambda_jc_j^{\otimes r}\) and \(\mathcal T'_r=\sum_j\lambda'_j(c'_j)^{\otimes r}\). If \(\lVert\mathcal T'_r-\mathcal T_r\rVert_F<\epsilon_0\), then a permutation \(\pi\) exists such that \(\lVert C'_{\pi}-C\rVert_F\le L_C\lVert\mathcal T'_r-\mathcal T_r\rVert_F\). Thus the local inverse bound applies to the numerical feasible set of the minimum-distance fit, without requiring the fitted factors to be accompanied by a source law.
