Under the population assumptions, the full observational-law fiber \(\mathfrak F_x(P_X)\) is nonempty, every one of its completions has source count \(n\), and
\[
 \{\boldsymbol\theta_{-x\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M):\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F_x(P_X)\}
 =\mathcal R_x^{\mathrm{vec}}(C)
 =\left\{\frac{C_{-x,j}}{C_{xj}}:C_{xj}\ne0,\ \operatorname{rank}(C_{-x,-j})=p-1\right\}.
\]
For every \(y\ne x\), coordinate projection gives
\[
 \{\theta_{y\leftarrow x}(\mathcal M):\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F_x(P_X)\}
 =\mathcal R_{xy}(C).
\]
Every alternative observed mixing matrix in the fiber equals \(C\Pi D\) for a permutation \(\Pi\) and nonsingular diagonal \(D\), so the displayed frontiers are functionals of \(P_X\) on the irreducible class. The algorithm \(\operatorname{Cert}(C,x)\) returns one exact same-law completion for every \(j\in J_x(C)\), and all values and witnesses are certifiable in \(O(n^4)\) real-arithmetic operations.
