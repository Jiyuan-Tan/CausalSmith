Let \(A\in\mathbb R^{p\times m}\) and \(G\in\mathbb R^{p\times k}\) have nonzero pairwise nonproportional columns, and let \(u\in\mathbb R^m\) and \(v\in\mathbb R^k\) have mutually independent, nondegenerate, non-Gaussian components. If \(Au\) and \(Gv\) have the same law, then \(m=k\) and \(G=A\Pi D\) for a permutation matrix \(\Pi\) and a nonsingular diagonal matrix \(D\).
