%%% FIELD proof-uniform-scc-recovery
For a symmetric matrix \(A\) and \(G\in\mathcal F\), put, only within this proof,
\[
\rho_G(A):=\operatorname{dist}_F(A,\mathcal M_G)
=\inf_{B\in\mathcal M_G}\|A-B\|_F.
\]
The finite-candidate assumption makes the minimization over \(G\in\mathcal F\) finite and nonempty.  Moreover, the compact-parameter assumption includes nonemptiness of every \(\Theta_G\), so the model-image definition makes every \(\mathcal M_G\) nonempty.  Thus all the displayed distances are finite, and the minimum-distance selector is well defined after applying the fixed total-order tie breaker.

Fix \(G_0\in\mathcal F\) and \(\Sigma_0\in\mathcal M_{G_0}\), and abbreviate \(H=\widehat G_n\).  On the event
\[
\operatorname{SCC}(H)\ne\operatorname{SCC}(G_0),
\]
the cross-SCC separation assumption, specialized to the fixed point \(\Sigma_0\in\mathcal M_{G_0}\), gives
\[
\delta
\le \inf_{B\in\mathcal M_H}\|\Sigma_0-B\|_F.
\]
For every \(B\in\mathcal M_H\), the triangle inequality gives
\[
\|\Sigma_0-B\|_F
\le \|\Sigma_0-S_n\|_F+\|S_n-B\|_F.
\]
Taking the infimum over \(B\in\mathcal M_H\), using the defining minimizing property of \(\widehat G_n\), and then using \(\Sigma_0\in\mathcal M_{G_0}\), yields, successively,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\delta
&\le \|\Sigma_0-S_n\|_F+\rho_H(S_n)\\
&\le \|\Sigma_0-S_n\|_F+\rho_{G_0}(S_n)\\
&\le 2\|S_n-\Sigma_0\|_F.
\end{aligned}
\]
Consequently,
\[
\bigl\{\operatorname{SCC}(\widehat G_n)\ne\operatorname{SCC}(G_0)\bigr\}
\subseteq
\bigl\{\|S_n-\Sigma_0\|_F\ge\delta/2\bigr\}.
\tag{1}
\]

It remains to bound the probability on the right uniformly.  Write \(\sigma_{ab}=(\Sigma_0)_{ab}\).  Under the iid centered Gaussian sampling assumption,
\[
\mathbb E_{\Sigma_0}(S_n)_{ab}=\sigma_{ab},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_{\Sigma_0}\bigl((S_n)_{ab}\bigr)
=\frac1n\operatorname{Var}_{\Sigma_0}(X_{1a}X_{1b}).
\tag{2}
\]
For completeness, the bivariate Gaussian moment-generating function is
\[
\mathbb E_{\Sigma_0}\exp(sX_{1a}+tX_{1b})
=\exp\!\left\{\frac12\bigl(\sigma_{aa}s^2+2\sigma_{ab}st+\sigma_{bb}t^2\bigr)\right\}.
\]
Differentiating twice in \(s\) and twice in \(t\) at \((s,t)=(0,0)\) gives
\[
\mathbb E_{\Sigma_0}(X_{1a}^2X_{1b}^2)
=\sigma_{aa}\sigma_{bb}+2\sigma_{ab}^2.
\]
Since \(\mathbb E_{\Sigma_0}(X_{1a}X_{1b})=\sigma_{ab}\), equation (2) therefore becomes
\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\Sigma_0}\bigl((S_n)_{ab}\bigr)
=\frac1n\bigl(\sigma_{aa}\sigma_{bb}+\sigma_{ab}^2\bigr).
\tag{3}
\]
Summing (3) over all ordered pairs \((a,b)\in V^2\) proves the exact identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_{\Sigma_0}\|S_n-\Sigma_0\|_F^2
&=\sum_{a,b\in V}\operatorname{Var}_{\Sigma_0}\bigl((S_n)_{ab}\bigr)\\
&=\frac1n\left\{\left(\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_0\right)^2+\|\Sigma_0\|_F^2\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
The covariance-eigenvalue assumption gives \(\lambda_{\max}(\Sigma_0)\le\overline M\).  Because \(\Sigma_0\) is positive definite and has \(p\) eigenvalues, it follows that
\[
\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_0\le p\overline M,\qquad
\|\Sigma_0\|_F^2=\sum_{j=1}^p\lambda_j(\Sigma_0)^2\le p\overline M^2.
\]
Substitution in (4) yields
\[
\mathbb E_{\Sigma_0}\|S_n-\Sigma_0\|_F^2
\le \frac{p(p+1)\overline M^2}{n}.
\tag{5}
\]
The elementary pointwise inequality
\[
\mathbf 1\!\left\{\|S_n-\Sigma_0\|_F^2\ge\delta^2/4\right\}
\le \frac{4}{\delta^2}\|S_n-\Sigma_0\|_F^2
\]
is valid because \(\delta>0\).  Taking expectations, and then applying (1) and (5), gives
\[
\Pr_{\Sigma_0}\!\left\{\operatorname{SCC}(\widehat G_n)\ne\operatorname{SCC}(G_0)\right\}
\le \frac{4p(p+1)\overline M^2}{n\delta^2}.
\]
This probability is also at most \(1\).  The bound is independent of the chosen \(G_0\in\mathcal F\) and \(\Sigma_0\in\mathcal M_{G_0}\); taking the displayed supremum proves
\[
\sup_{G_0\in\mathcal F,\,\Sigma_0\in\mathcal M_{G_0}}
\Pr_{\Sigma_0}\!\left\{\operatorname{SCC}(\widehat G_n)\ne\operatorname{SCC}(G_0)\right\}
\le
\min\!\left\{1,\frac{4p(p+1)\overline M^2}{n\delta^2}\right\}.
\]
For fixed \(p\), \(\mathcal F\), \(\delta>0\), and \(\overline M<\infty\), the second term tends to zero as \(n\to\infty\), which is precisely the asserted uniform SCC consistency.
