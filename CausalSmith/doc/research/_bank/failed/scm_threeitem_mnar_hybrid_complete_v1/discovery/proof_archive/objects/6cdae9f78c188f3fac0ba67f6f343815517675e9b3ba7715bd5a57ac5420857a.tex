Let \(H=G[I_R]\).  A directed graph on three labelled response vertices is determined by a subset of the six possible off-diagonal arrows.  Count acyclic choices by the number \(m\) of edges in the underlying undirected skeleton.  For \(m=0,1,2,3\), respectively, the numbers of labelled acyclic orientations are
\[
1,\qquad {3\choose1}2=6,\qquad {3\choose2}2^2=12,\qquad 2^3-2=6.
\]
For \(m=2\) the skeleton is a forest, so every orientation is acyclic; for \(m=3\) exactly the two directed three-cycles are excluded.  Hence there are
\[
1+6+12+6=25
\]
possible response DAGs.  Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}allowed\text{-}edges}\) permits each of the nine arrows \(X_j\to R_i\) independently, and Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}graph\text{-}class}\) imposes no further condition on them.  Therefore
\[
|\mathfrak G_3|=25\,2^9=12800.
\]

Now forbid the three self-censoring arrows.  For each response DAG there are \(2^6\) choices of the six remaining substantive-to-response arrows, so the resulting set \(\mathcal A\) has cardinality \(25\,2^6=1600\).  For completeness, the orbit-count identity used below follows by double counting
\[
\mathcal T=\{(\sigma,A)\in\mathfrak S_3\times\mathcal A:\sigma A=A\}.
\]
Counting first by \(\sigma\) gives \(|\mathcal T|=\sum_\sigma|\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)|\).  For an orbit \(\mathcal O\) and any representative \(A_0\in\mathcal O\), the number of elements carrying \(A_0\) to a fixed \(A\in\mathcal O\) equals \(|\operatorname{Stab}(A_0)|\), while \(|\mathcal O|\,|\operatorname{Stab}(A_0)|=6\) by the explicit partition of \(\mathfrak S_3\) into those fibres.  Thus every orbit contributes exactly six pairs to \(\mathcal T\), and
\[
|\mathcal A/\mathfrak S_3|=\frac16\sum_{\sigma\in\mathfrak S_3}|\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma)|.
\]
The identity fixes all \(1600\) elements.  Fix a transposition, say \((12)\).  On the six response arrows, the orbit \(\{R_1\to R_2,R_2\to R_1\}\) must be absent in an invariant DAG, while the other two arrow orbits may be chosen as neither, both arrows into \(R_3\), or both arrows out of \(R_3\).  Thus precisely three response DAGs are fixed.  The six non-self substantive arrows form three two-element orbits, giving \(2^3=8\) invariant masks.  Hence every transposition fixes \(3\cdot8=24\) elements of \(\mathcal A\).  Under a three-cycle, the six response arrows form the two directed three-cycle orbits; acyclicity forces both absent.  The six substantive arrows form two orbits, so a three-cycle fixes \(2^2=4\) elements.  The double-count identity now gives
\[
|\mathcal A/\mathfrak S_3|
=\frac{1600+3\cdot24+2\cdot4}{6}
=280.
\]
This proves both asserted counts.
