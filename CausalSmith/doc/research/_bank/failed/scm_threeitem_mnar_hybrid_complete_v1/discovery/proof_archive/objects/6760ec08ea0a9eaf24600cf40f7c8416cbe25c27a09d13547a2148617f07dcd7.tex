Fix \(G\in\mathfrak G_3\).  First suppose that \(\operatorname{Check}_3\) accepts an \(\mathrm{ID}\) certificate \(\gamma\) with terminal expression \(F_G\).  We prove by induction along the finite trace of \(\gamma\) the invariant that every carried rational expression equals, for every \(p\in\mathcal M_+(G)\), the full-law quantity named by its trace label after substituting \(q=C_{\#}p\).

At an observed-coordinate leaf the invariant is exactly Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}observed\text{-}law}\).  It is preserved by finite sums and products by ordinary algebra.  At a quotient node, Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}certificate\text{-}denominator\text{-}safety}\) shows that the checker-certified denominator \(q(A)\) is nonzero, so division preserves the checked equality.  At a graph-factorization node, the checked identity is an instance of Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}graph\text{-}factorization}\).  At a conditional-independence node, substituting that factorization and summing the variables omitted by the node gives the checked conditional equality; all conditioning-event probabilities are nonzero by the denominator lemma.  At a grouped-fixing, intervention-fixing, or odds-ratio node, Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}checker}\) requires the checker to verify the corresponding rational identity from its already verified inputs and outputs.  Clearing its encoded denominators is legitimate by the denominator lemma, and the resulting exact identity therefore preserves the invariant.  These cases exhaust the grammar in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}language}\).

The eight terminal labels are the eight target marginals.  Hence, for every \(x\in\mathcal B\), the terminal invariant and the definition of \(p_X\) give
\[
F_G(C_{\#}p)(x)=\sum_{r\in\mathcal B}p(x,r)=p_X(x).
\]

Now suppose that \(\operatorname{Check}_3\) accepts a \(\mathrm{NONID}\) certificate \((p^{(0)},p^{(1)},x_0)\).  By Definitions \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}language}\) and \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}checker}\), the parser checks normalization of each rational \(64\)-cell probability table, every entry is checked strictly positive, and the displayed conditional tables are checked against Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}graph\text{-}factorization}\) for \(G\).  Therefore \(p^{(0)},p^{(1)}\in\mathcal M_+(G)\).  For each \(w\in\mathcal W\), the checker also verifies the exact rational equality
\[
\sum_{(x,r):C(x,r)=w}p^{(0)}(x,r)
=
\sum_{(x,r):C(x,r)=w}p^{(1)}(x,r),
\]
which is \(C_{\#}p^{(0)}=C_{\#}p^{(1)}\) by Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}observed\text{-}law}\).  Finally, its named-cell check is precisely
\[
\sum_r p^{(0)}(x_0,r)\ne\sum_r p^{(1)}(x_0,r),
\]
or \(p_X^{(0)}(x_0)\ne p_X^{(1)}(x_0)\).  This proves both soundness assertions.
