The direct-association compiler itself has a denominator-safe closed form. Put \(A_i=\operatorname{pa}^X_G(i)\) and \(P_i=\operatorname{pa}^R_G(i)\). On its branch, \(i\notin A_i\) and no \(R_j\), \(j\in A_i\), is a response descendant of \(R_i\). Define the observable event
\[
B_i(x_{A_i})=\{X_{A_i}^*=x_{A_i},\ R_{A_i}=\mathbf1,\ R_{P_i}=\mathbf1\}.
\]
The local Markov property obtained from Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}graph\text{-}factorization}\) permits the nonparent, nondescendant selectors \(R_{A_i\setminus P_i}\) to be added to the conditioning set. Therefore
\[
\lambda_{i,p}(1\mid x_{A_i},\mathbf1_{P_i})
=\Pr_p\{R_i=1\mid B_i(x_{A_i})\}
=\frac{q\{B_i(x_{A_i}),R_i=1\}}{q\{B_i(x_{A_i})\}}.
\]
Both events are nonempty and have positive probability by Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}certificate\text{-}denominator\text{-}safety}\). Evaluating Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}graph\text{-}factorization}\) at \(R=\mathbf1\) gives
\[
q\{X^*=x,R=\mathbf1\}
=p_X(x)\prod_{i=1}^3\lambda_{i,p}(1\mid x_{A_i},\mathbf1_{P_i}).

Substitution identifies \(p_X(x)\). The directive's target-level normalization step is also proved. For any positive unnormalized response weight \(a_r(x)\) satisfying
\[
p(r\mid x)=\frac{a_r(x)}{Z(x)},\qquad Z(x)=\sum_{s\in\mathcal B}a_s(x),
\]
write \(\mathbf 1=(1,1,1)\). The complete-case observed cell obeys
\[
q\{X^*=x,R=\mathbf1\}=p_X(x)\frac{a_{\mathbf1}(x)}{Z(x)}.
\]
Consequently
\[
q\{X^*=x,R=\mathbf1\}
\sum_{r\in\mathcal B}\frac{a_r(x)}{a_{\mathbf1}(x)}
=p_X(x)\frac{a_{\mathbf1}(x)}{Z(x)}
\frac{\sum_r a_r(x)}{a_{\mathbf1}(x)}
=p_X(x).
\]
Thus the \(1/Z(x)\) appearing in the Nabi--Bhattacharya--Shpitser full-law intermediate cancels identically after target projection; no division by a sum is needed. Moreover, once an elementary conditional or odds-ratio factor is written as a product of ratios \(q(A)/q(B)\), inversion of a product can be implemented by successive divisions, every divisor remains a single observed marginal \(q(A)\), and the outer sum over \(r\) is never placed in a denominator. Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}certificate\text{-}denominator\text{-}safety}\) makes every such nonempty-event divisor positive.

What remains unproved is the lower-level statement needed by the theorem: for each of the \(496\) masks, every conditional and odds-ratio factor in the inherited unnormalized weight must be derived from graph factorization, expanded into specified nonempty observed events, and emitted with an exact checker transcript and operation count. The frozen definitions name that compiler but provide neither the mask-indexed weights nor the \(496\) traces. Therefore the cancellation above cannot by itself establish that \(\operatorname{Known}_3\) returns accepted \(\mathcal L_3\) certificates on all \(496\) masks.
