For a direct-index graph and \(a\in\{0,1\}^{A_i}\), define two nonempty observed events
\[
\begin{split}
D_{i,a}&=\{w:w_j=a_j\ (j\in A_i),\ w_j\in\{0,1\}\ (j\in P_i\setminus A_i)\},\\
N_{i,a}&=D_{i,a}\cap\{w:w_i\in\{0,1\}\}.
\end{split}
\]
The direct condition makes each \(R_j\), \(j\in A_i\setminus P_i\), a response non-descendant and non-parent of \(R_i\). The graph factorization therefore gives
\[
\frac{q(N_{i,a})}{q(D_{i,a})}
=p(R_i=1\mid X_{A_i}=a,R_{A_i}=\mathbf1,R_{P_i}=\mathbf1)
=\lambda_{i,p}(1\mid a,\mathbf1).
\]
On the complete-case cell, Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}graph\text{-}factorization}\) then gives
\[
q(X^*=x,R=\mathbf1)
=p_X(x)\prod_{i=1}^{3}\lambda_{i,p}(1\mid x_{A_i},\mathbf1),
\]
and hence the eight explicit formulas
\[
p_X(x)=q(X^*=x,R=\mathbf1)
\prod_{i=1}^{3}q(D_{i,x_{A_i}})
\prod_{i=1}^{3}\frac{1}{q(N_{i,x_{A_i}})},
\qquad x\in\mathcal B.
\]
Each actual division is by the nonempty observed-event marginal \(q(N_{i,x_{A_i}})\); the product is implemented by three successive divisions. This closes the algebraic and divisor portions of the direct compiler.

\frac{1}{Z(x)},\qquad
Z(x)=\sum_{r\in\mathcal B}
\left\{\prod_{i=1}^{3}p(r_i\mid R_{-i}=\mathbf1,X=x)\right\}
\left\{\prod_{i=2}^{3}\operatorname{OR}(r_i,r_{<i}\mid R_{>i}=\mathbf1,X=x)\right\}.
\]
All summands are positive on \(\mathcal M_+(G)\), but \(Z(x)\) is a subtraction-free rational normalizer, not literally \(q(A)\) for a nonempty \(A\subseteq\mathcal W\). It is therefore the first forbidden divisor in a literal full-law trace under Definitions \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}language}\) and \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}checker}\). Marginalizing to \(p_X\) may cancel this divisor in individual graph strata, but neither the frozen core nor the supplied draft gives the required graph-uniform cancellation trace.
