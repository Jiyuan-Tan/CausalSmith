Write the three positive response kernels supplied by Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}graph\text{-}factorization}\) on \(G_{\mathrm{OR}}\) as
\[
\alpha_{z,s}(r_1)=p(r_1\mid X_3=z,R_2=s),\qquad
\beta_{u,t}(r_2)=p(r_2\mid X_1=u,R_3=t),\qquad
\gamma_u(r_3)=p(r_3\mid X_1=u),
\]
and put
\[
J_{st}(u)=\beta_{u,t}(s)\gamma_u(t)
=p(R_2=s,R_3=t\mid X_1=u).
\]
Every displayed factor is strictly positive because the full joint law is strictly positive.

For \(z,s\in\{0,1\}\), summing the factorization over the unshown substantive coordinates and over \(R_1\) where appropriate gives
\[
q(R_1=1,X_3^*=z,R_3=1,R_2=s)
=\alpha_{z,s}(1)\,q(X_3^*=z,R_3=1,R_2=s).
\]
The conditioning denominator is positive by Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}certificate\text{-}denominator\text{-}safety}\), so
\[
a(z,s)=\alpha_{z,s}(1).
\]
Next define the positive quantity
\[
m_s(u)=\sum_{x_2,x_3}p_X(u,x_2,x_3)\alpha_{x_3,s}(1).
\]
Direct summation of the factorization gives, for \(t\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
p(X_1=u,R_1=1,R_2=s,R_3=t)=m_s(u)J_{st}(u).
\]
Consequently
\[
c_s(u)=\frac{J_{s1}(u)}{J_{s0}(u)+J_{s1}(u)}.
\]

Let \(h(u)=\sum_{x_2}p_X(u,x_2,0)>0\).  The two observed reference-slice masses in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}odds\text{-}functional}\) are
\[
\begin{aligned}
q(X_1^*=u,X_3^*=0,R=(1,0,1))&=h(u)\alpha_{0,0}(1)J_{01}(u),\\
q(X_1^*=u,X_3^*=0,R=(1,1,1))&=h(u)\alpha_{0,1}(1)J_{11}(u).
\end{aligned}
\]
Using \(a(0,s)=\alpha_{0,s}(1)\), all positive factors cancel exactly and yield
\[
t(u)=\frac{J_{01}(u)}{J_{11}(u)}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{c_1(u)}+\frac{t(u)}{c_0(u)}
&=\frac{J_{10}(u)+J_{11}(u)}{J_{11}(u)}
 +\frac{J_{01}(u)}{J_{11}(u)}
   \frac{J_{00}(u)+J_{01}(u)}{J_{01}(u)}\\
&=\frac{\sum_{s,t\in\{0,1\}}J_{st}(u)}{J_{11}(u)}
=\frac{1}{J_{11}(u)}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Finally, the complete-case cell satisfies
\[
q(X^*=x,R=\mathbf 1)
=p_X(x)\alpha_{x_3,1}(1)J_{11}(x_1)
=p_X(x)a(x_3,1)J_{11}(x_1).
\]
Substitution into Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}odds\text{-}functional}\) proves
\[
F_{\mathrm{OR}}(C_{\#}p)(x)=p_X(x)
\qquad(x\in\mathcal B).
\]
Each conditional probability just used is a ratio of two observed event marginals.  Expanding \(a\), \(c_s\), and \(t\) therefore expresses the calculation using only sums, products, and divisions by nonempty observed-event probabilities; all are positive by the denominator lemma.  The displayed factorization and cancellation form a finite odds-ratio trace, so Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}checker}\) accepts its encoding.  The remaining algorithmic comparison is exactly the cited Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}guo\text{-}nabi\text{-}odds\text{-}tree\text{-}failure}\).
