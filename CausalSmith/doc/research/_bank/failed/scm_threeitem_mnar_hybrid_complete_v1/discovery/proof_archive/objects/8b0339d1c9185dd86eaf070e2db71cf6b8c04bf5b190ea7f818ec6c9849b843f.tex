Fix \(G\in\mathfrak G_3\), \(p\in\mathcal M_+(G)\), and \(q=C_{\#}p\).  For any \(w\in\mathcal W\), define \(r(w)\in\mathcal B\) by \(r_i(w)=1\) when \(w_i\in\{0,1\}\) and \(r_i(w)=0\) when \(w_i=?\).  Choose \(x(w)\in\mathcal B\) with \(x_i(w)=w_i\) on observed coordinates and, for definiteness, \(x_i(w)=0\) on missing coordinates.  Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}coarsening\text{-}map}\) gives
\[
C(x(w),r(w))=w.
\]
Thus the fibre in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}observed\text{-}law}\) is nonempty.  Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}strict\text{-}positivity}\) gives \(p(x(w),r(w))>0\), and hence
\[
q_w=\sum_{(x,r):C(x,r)=w}p(x,r)\ge p(x(w),r(w))>0.
\]
If \(\varnothing\ne A\subseteq\mathcal W\), choosing any \(w_0\in A\) yields
\[
q(A)=\sum_{w\in A}q_w\ge q_{w_0}>0.
\]

By Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}language}\), a quotient in an \(\mathrm{ID}\) term can be formed only with a denominator represented as \(q(A)\) for an explicitly encoded nonempty event \(A\).  Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}certificate\text{-}checker}\) checks that condition for every input and output divisor of every graph-factorization, conditional-independence, grouped-fixing, intervention-fixing, and odds-ratio constructor.  The inequality just proved therefore applies to every divisor in every checker-accepted \(\mathrm{ID}\) certificate, uniformly over \(p\in\mathcal M_+(G)\).
