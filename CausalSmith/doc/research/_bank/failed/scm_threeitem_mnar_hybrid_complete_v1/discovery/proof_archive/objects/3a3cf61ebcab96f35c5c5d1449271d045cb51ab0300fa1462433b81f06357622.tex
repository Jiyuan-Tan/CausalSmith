Conditional on an accepted rational \(F_G\) satisfying \(F_G(C_{\#}p)=p_X\), the fixed-interior denominator bound makes \(F_G\) twice continuously differentiable on a neighborhood of the compact observed-law image. A uniform Taylor expansion gives
\[
\sqrt n\{F_G(\widehat q)-F_G(q)\}
=D F_G(q)\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}(e_{W_j}-q)+o_p(1).
\]
The finite multinomial central limit theorem yields covariance \(V_G(q)\), plug-in continuity yields \(\widehat V_G\to_pV_G(q)\), and coordinatewise studentization plus the union bound at level \(\alpha/8\) gives the stated Bonferroni region even when the joint covariance is singular. Completion still requires the upstream accepted \(F_G\) supplied by Theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}complete3\text{-}iff}\).
