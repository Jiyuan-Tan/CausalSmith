\(\mathcal L_3\) has two terminal forms. An \(\mathrm{ID}\) term contains eight expressions formed from the coordinates of \(q\) by finite sums, products, and quotients; every quotient denominator is encoded as \(q(A)=\sum_{w\in A}q_w\) for a checker-certified nonempty event \(A\subseteq\mathcal W\). Its trace uses graph factorization, conditional independence, grouped fixing, intervention fixing, or Chen odds-ratio identities, and each constructor records all input and output divisors. A \(\mathrm{NONID}\) term contains two rational \(64\)-cell tables, their graph-factorized conditional tables, exact equality of their \(27\) coarsened cells, strict-positivity checks, and a named cell where their target marginals differ.
