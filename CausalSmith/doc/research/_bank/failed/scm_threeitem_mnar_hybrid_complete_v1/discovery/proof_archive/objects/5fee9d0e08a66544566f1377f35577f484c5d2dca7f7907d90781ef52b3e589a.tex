\(\mathcal L_3\) has two terminal forms. An \(\mathrm{ID}\) term contains eight expressions formed from the coordinates of \(q\) by finite sums, products, and quotients. Every quotient denominator carries a finite recursive subtraction-free positivity trace: a leaf is either a positive rational constant or \(q(A)=\sum_{w\in A}q_w\) for an encoded nonempty event \(A\subseteq\mathcal W\); internal nodes are sums and products of previously certified positive expressions, or quotients whose numerator and denominator have previously certified positive traces. Its identification trace uses graph factorization, conditional independence, grouped fixing, intervention fixing, or Chen odds-ratio identities, and each constructor records every input and output divisor together with its positivity trace. A \(\mathrm{NONID}\) term contains two rational \(64\)-cell tables, their graph-factorized conditional tables, exact equality of their \(27\) coarsened cells, strict-positivity checks, and a named cell where their target marginals differ.
