## Theorems (T)

### T-block: t1 — Suppose \(\Sigma_0\succ0\),…
**Statement.** Suppose \(\Sigma_0\succ0\), \(\operatorname{rank}(\bar Q)=r\), and \(\operatorname{range}(\Delta_e)\subseteq\operatorname{range}(\bar Q)\) for every \(1\le e\le E\), where \(\bar Q=\sum_{e=1}^E\Delta_e\). For every \(T\subseteq\mathcal V\) and ordering \(t\) satisfying \(\operatorname{Cert}(\theta,T,t)\), the construction in \(\text{def:positive-noise-completion}\) yields a model in \(\mathcal M_{d,E,r}\) with target set \(T\), covariance \(\operatorname{Cov}(Y_e)=\Sigma_e\) for all \(e\in\mathcal E\), strictly positive baseline structural-noise variances, and strictly positive stationary measurement-error variances.

### T-block: t2 — For every covariance tuple \(\theta\) satisfying…
**Statement.** For every covariance tuple \(\theta\) satisfying \(\Sigma_0\succ0\), \(\operatorname{rank}(\bar Q)=r\), and \(\operatorname{range}(\Delta_e)\subseteq\operatorname{range}(\bar Q)\) for all \(1\le e\le E\), where \(\bar Q=\sum_{e=1}^E\Delta_e\), one has \(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{causal}}(\theta)=\mathcal F_{\mathrm{LDL}}(\theta)\). Equivalently, a labeled size-\(r\) set is compatible if and only if some ordering has a positive-definite aggregate principal block whose unique unit-lower \(LDL^{\mathsf T}\) factor simultaneously transforms every target-block shift to a diagonal entrywise-nonnegative matrix.

### T-block: t3 — Suppose \(\Sigma_0\succ0\),…
**Statement.** Suppose \(\Sigma_0\succ0\), \(\operatorname{rank}(\bar Q)=r\), and \(\operatorname{range}(\Delta_e)\subseteq\operatorname{range}(\bar Q)\) for every \(1\le e\le E\), where \(\bar Q=\sum_{e=1}^E\Delta_e\). Every reached residual pair \((R_S,(M_{e,S})_{e=1}^{E})\) depends only on \(S\), so the algorithm terminates after visiting at most \(\sum_{k=0}^{r}\binom{d}{k}\) subset states, and the intersection and union of its leaves equal \(\mathcal C(\theta)\) and \(\mathcal P(\theta)\).

### T-block: t4 — Under the independent Gaussian sampling design, uniformly…
**Statement.** Under the independent Gaussian sampling design, uniformly over \(\theta\in\Theta_K\), \(\Pr_{\theta}[\mathcal F_{\mathrm{causal}}(\theta)\subseteq\mathcal F_{\mathrm{out},n}\text{ and }h(\theta)\in\mathcal H_n]\ge1-\alpha\). The region \(R_n\cap\Theta_K\) is nonempty for every sample, and a rule reports a classification only when \(\mathcal H_n\) is a singleton has probability at most \(\alpha\) of reporting a classification different from \(h(\theta)\).

### T-block: t5 — For every \(x>0\), on the event…
**Statement.** For every \(x>0\), on the event \(\lVert\widehat\Sigma-\theta\rVert_w\le x\), every point of \(R_n\) is within weighted distance at most \(\max\{x+c_{\alpha},2x+1\}\) of \(\theta\). Hence, if \(\max\{x+c_{\alpha},2x+1\}<\sqrt{2}m\delta_n(\theta)\), the singleton rule based on \(\mathcal H_n\) reports \(h(\theta)\), and its failure probability is at most \(V_{\max}/x^2\). In particular, \(\sqrt{2}m\delta_n(\theta)>2c_{\alpha}+1\) implies correct reporting with probability at least \(1-\alpha\). For every sequence \(\theta_n\in\Theta_K\) with \(\delta_n(\theta_n)\to\infty\), the rule reports \(h(\theta_n)\) with probability tending to one, uniformly over subclasses on which the infimum of \(\delta_n\) diverges.

### T-block: t6 — The tuple \(\theta^{\pm}\) is generated by an acyclic pos…
**Statement.** The tuple \(\theta^{\pm}\) is generated by an acyclic positive-structural-noise, positive-measurement-noise Gaussian SCM satisfying every member condition of \(\mathcal M_{3,2,2}\) except nonnegative variance shifts, and its true target set is \(\{1,2\}\). For each ordering of that target, the aggregate ordered certificate has unit-lower factor equal to the identity and its transformed second-environment target block contains the diagonal entry \(-1/2\), so \(\operatorname{Cert}\) rejects the true target. The tuple \(\theta^{\mathrm{split}}\) is generated by a member of \(\mathcal M_{3,2,2}\) with true target set \(\{1,2\}\). Its first-environment covariance shift has rank one, strictly less than \(r=2\), whereas its aggregate carrier \(\bar Q\) has rank \(r\), and \(\operatorname{Cert}\) accepts the true target in both orders. Thus signed shifts destroy the nonnegative-diagonal necessity and require strict baseline-variance feasibility in place of nonnegativity, whereas an environment that shifts every target is unnecessary for the aggregate-carrier certificate.

### T-block: t7 — Fix \(C<\infty\)
**Statement.** Fix \(C<\infty\). For any possibly abstaining rule whose probability of an incorrect classification is uniformly at most \(\alpha\) on \(\Theta_K\), there exists \(\kappa=\kappa(C,\alpha,m,M,d,E)>0\) such that along every sequence \(\theta_n\in\Theta_K\) with \(\delta_n(\theta_n)\le C\), the probability of correctly certifying \(h(\theta_n)\) is at most \(1-\kappa\).

### T-block: t8 — Along the legal path \(\theta_{\gamma}\),…
**Statement.** Along the legal path \(\theta_{\gamma}\), \(\mathcal F_{\mathrm{causal}}(\theta_{\gamma})=\{\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{1,4\}\}\) and \(h(\theta_{\gamma})=(\{1\},\{1,2,3,4\})\) for \(\gamma\ne0\), whereas at \(\gamma=0\) all six two-element target sets are compatible and \(h(\theta_0)=(\varnothing,\{1,2,3,4\})\). ### T-block: t9 — When \(r=1\) and…

### T-block: t10 — There is no universal correct-certification envelope dete…
**Statement.** There is no universal correct-certification envelope determined only by \(c=\lim\delta_n(\theta_n)\). For any specified Gaussian tangent geometry \(\mathcal G=(k,g_0,\mathcal A)\), where \(X\sim N(g,I_k)\), \(g_0\) has the focal classification, and \(\mathcal A\) is the opposite-classification tangent set, the geometry-indexed focal-certification value is \[ \beta^{\star}_{\mathcal G}(\alpha) =\sup_{\substack{\phi:\mathbb R^k\to[0,1]\ \mathrm{Borel}\\ \sup_{g\in\mathcal A}\mathbb E_g\phi(X)\le\alpha}} \mathbb E_{g_0}\phi(X). \] The nearest-opposite distance \(c=\inf_{g\in\mathcal A}\lVert g-g_0\rVert_2\) does not determine this value. Explicitly, for \(k=1\), \(g_0=0\), and opposite sets \(\mathcal A_+=[c,\infty)\) and \(\mathcal A_{\pm}=(-\infty,-c]\cup[c,\infty)\), with \(c>0\), \[ \beta^{\star}_{\mathcal G_+}(\alpha)=\Phi(\Phi^{-1}(\alpha)+c), \qquad \beta^{\star}_{\mathcal G_{\pm}}(\alpha)=2\Phi(t_{c,\alpha})-1 <\beta^{\star}_{\mathcal G_+}(\alpha), \] where \(\Phi\) is the standard-normal distribution function and \(t_{c,\alpha}>0\) is the unique solution of \(\Phi(t-c)-\Phi(-t-c)=\alpha\). Thus the handle yields an explicit optimization only after the incident tangent geometry is supplied; neither a one-variable \(\beta^{\star}(c)\) nor one classifier attaining it uniformly over all regular sequences is determined by the frozen core.
