No jointly valid construction with the properties in the question exists for the fixed class \(\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{LP}}\) and the fixed handle \(\mathfrak H_{\mathrm{orth}}(\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{LP}})\). More precisely, there are a law \(P^\circ\in\mathcal M_1(\beta,L,c_0,C_0)\) and a number \(\delta\in(0,1-\log 2)\) such that, for every finite \(C_m>0\) and the unique admissible fit \(\widehat m\in\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{LP}}\),
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}P^{\circ\otimes n}\!\left\{\mathcal G_n(1;C_m,\widehat m)^c\right\}
\ge e^{-\delta}>2e^{-1}.
\]
Consequently, there are no constants \(c_u,C_H,C_R,C_m>0\), measurable statistics \((\widehat H^\perp_{j,b}(\widehat m):(j,b)\in\mathcal Q_d)\), and associated data-dependent radii produced within \(\mathfrak H_{\mathrm{orth}}(\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{LP}})\) for which, uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal M_d(\beta,L,c_0,C_0)\), every \(x\ge1\) with \(u=x+\log(2d)\le c_u n\) satisfies
\[
P^{\otimes n}\!\left(
\mathcal G_n(x;C_m,\widehat m)^c
\cup
\left\{\exists(j,b)\in\mathcal Q_d:
\left|\widehat H^\perp_{j,b}(\widehat m)-H_{j,b}\right|
>C_H\left(\sqrt{\frac{H_{j,b}u}{n}}+\frac{u}{n}+r_n^4\right)
\right\}
\right)
\le 2e^{-x}.
\]
In particular, the complete package requested in the question, including the fourth-order nuisance remainder and joint null-interval coverage, is impossible under this fixed protocol. This result does not rule out other regression estimators, other independence statistics, or other residual-HSIC procedures.
%%% FIELD resolved-uniform-expansion-proof
Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:fixed\text{-}local\text{-}polynomial\text{-}uniform\text{-}expansion\text{-}impossible}\) supplies a fixed law \(P^\circ\in\mathcal M_1(\beta,L,c_0,C_0)\) and \(\delta\in(0,1-\log 2)\) for which, for every finite \(C_m>0\) and the unique fit \(\widehat m\in\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{LP}}\),
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}P^{\circ\otimes n}\!\left\{\mathcal G_n(1;C_m,\widehat m)^c\right\}
\ge e^{-\delta}>2e^{-1}.
\]
The strict final inequality follows from \(\delta<1-\log 2\): exponentiation gives
\[
e^{-\delta}>e^{-(1-\log 2)}=2e^{-1}.
\]

Suppose, toward a contradiction, that the constants, statistics, and radii in the statement exist. Specialize their asserted uniform guarantee to the member law \(P^\circ\), dimension \(d=1\), and \(x=1\). Then
\[
u_1=1+\log 2.
\]
Because \(c_u>0\), the required side condition \(u_1\le c_u n\) holds for every integer \(n\ge \lceil u_1/c_u\rceil\). For such \(n\), define the asserted failure event
\[
\mathcal F_n=
\mathcal G_n(1;C_m,\widehat m)^c
\cup
\left\{\exists(1,b)\in\mathcal Q_1:
\left|\widehat H^\perp_{1,b}(\widehat m)-H_{1,b}\right|
>C_H\left(\sqrt{\frac{H_{1,b}u_1}{n}}+\frac{u_1}{n}+r_n^4\right)
\right\}.
\]
By construction of this union,
\[
\mathcal G_n(1;C_m,\widehat m)^c\subseteq\mathcal F_n.
\]
The purported probability guarantee at \(x=1\) and monotonicity of probability therefore imply
\[
P^{\circ\otimes n}\!\left\{\mathcal G_n(1;C_m,\widehat m)^c\right\}
\le P^{\circ\otimes n}(\mathcal F_n)
\le 2e^{-1}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\). Taking lower limits and invoking the witness bound yields the contradiction
\[
e^{-\delta}
\le
\liminf_{n\to\infty}P^{\circ\otimes n}\!\left\{\mathcal G_n(1;C_m,\widehat m)^c\right\}
\le 2e^{-1}
<e^{-\delta}.
\]
Thus the requested simultaneous probability guarantee cannot hold. Since that guarantee is a necessary conjunct of the proposed construction, adding the stated derivative cancellation, fourth-order remainder bound, or null intervals cannot make the full package exist. The contradiction uses only the compulsory event \(\mathcal G_n\) and the fixed admissible class \(\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{LP}}\), which proves precisely the protocol-qualified negative conclusion and no broader impossibility claim.
