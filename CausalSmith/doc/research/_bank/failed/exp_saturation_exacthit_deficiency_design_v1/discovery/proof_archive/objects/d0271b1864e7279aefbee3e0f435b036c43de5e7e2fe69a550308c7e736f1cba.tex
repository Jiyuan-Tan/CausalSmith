For every realized assignment array, \(P_0\) and its product coupling \(\bar P_0\) have the same marginal law at the single observed assignment in each cluster.  Hence \(Q^{CR}_{\bar P_0,C_C}=Q^{CR}_{P_0,C_C}\).  Conditional on the fixed-count label vector, assumptions \(\text{ass:iid-schedules}\), \(\text{ass:cr-label-schedule-independence}\), and \(\text{ass:cr-exact-slices}\) make the observations independent within the triangular array.

For arbitrary \(h\in\mathbb R^A\), the exponential-family differentiation in (5) remains valid and gives derivative \(h_k\) of \(U_k\).  The conditional observed score is \(\dot\ell_{c,k}^{CR}\); it is centered in stratum \(k\), and disjoint strata give
\[
\frac1C\sum_{c=1}^C
\mathbb E_0[\dot\ell_{c,j}^{CR}\dot\ell_{c,k}^{CR}\mid L]
=\mathbf 1\{j=k\}\frac{C_{C,k}}{C}\frac1{M_k\tau_k^4}
\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}\operatorname{Var}_{P_0}\{W(z)\}
\longrightarrow\mathbf 1\{j=k\}\frac{\alpha_k}{\tau_k^2}. \tag{9}
\]
The limit is \(I_{CR}\), positive definite by \(\alpha_k>0\) and active-slice nondegeneracy.

Apply the bounded log-normalizer expansion (7) within each fixed stratum.  Its linear term is \(h^{\prime}\Delta_C^{CR}\), (9) gives the quadratic term \(-h^{\prime}I_{CR}h/2+o_P(1)\), and the summed cubic remainder is \(O(C^{-1/2}\max_k|h_k|^3)\) on compact sets of representatives.  The maximum normalized increment is \(O(C^{-1/2})\), and (9) is the limiting covariance.  Multiplication of the conditional characteristic functions therefore proves \(\Delta_C^{CR}\Rightarrow N(0,I_{CR})\) and the displayed LAN expansion.

The triangular-array expansion in \(\text{prop:efficient-exact-hit-cr-face}\), applied under \(\bar P_{C,h}\), yields
\[
\sqrt C\{\widetilde U_{C,A}-U_A(P_0)\}\Rightarrow
h+N(0,\Sigma_{CR,A}). \tag{10}
\]
For any finite prior on representatives with quotient norm at most \(M\), joint likelihood-ratio convergence and uniform integrability transfer its Bayes risk to the full Gaussian shift experiment.  The finite-prior separation and equicontinuity argument from the Bernoulli proof, followed by the proved location-invariant reduction, yields \(G_{A,M}(\tau_A^2,\alpha_A)\).  Letting \(M\to\infty\) invokes \(\text{lem:gaussian-quotient-reduction}\).

For the witness, let the observed welfare on each target slice be an independent \(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) bit, copied to all unit outcomes for the realized assignment.  Then \(W\) is that bit, \(\tau_k^2=1/4\), and the fraction \(\alpha_k\) of stratum-\(k\) observations contributes information \(\alpha_k/(1/4)=4\alpha_k\).  This verifies the witness separately from the unrestricted exponential-tilt construction.
