All mechanism values are positive by \(\texttt{thm:mechanism-loss}\).  Squaring the assumed leading-regret comparison and rearranging gives
\[
\frac{C_B}{C_{CR}}\geq
\left\{\frac{R_{B,A}^\star}{R_{CR,A}^\star}\right\}^{2}. \tag{46}
\]
Squaring the mechanism bound gives the next inequality and preserves its equality criterion.  Convexity of \(x\mapsto x^n\) gives, for \(x\in[0,1]\),
\[
x^n+(1-x)^n\geq2(1/2)^n=2^{1-n}. \tag{47}
\]
Every active target count is strictly between zero and \(n\), so for column \(\ell\),
\[
\sum_{k\in A}B_{k\ell}
\leq1-\pi_\ell^n-(1-\pi_\ell)^n\leq1-2^{1-n}. \tag{48}
\]
Maximizing over \(\ell\) gives \(s_{A,\max}\leq1-2^{1-n}\).  Reciprocation is valid because the quantities are positive, and (46)--(48) prove the result.
