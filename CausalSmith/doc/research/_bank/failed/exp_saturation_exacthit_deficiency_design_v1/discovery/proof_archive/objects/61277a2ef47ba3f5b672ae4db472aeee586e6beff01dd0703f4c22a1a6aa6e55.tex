Fix \(A\), \(M<\infty\), a compact representative set \(\mathscr H_{A,M}\subseteq\mathbb R^A\) satisfying
\[
\{[h]:h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}\}
=\{[g]\in H_A:\lVert[g]\rVert_A\leq M\},
\]
and a compact subset of \(\{\alpha\in\Delta_K:\min_{k\in A}\alpha_k>0\}\).  Under \(\mathsf{CR}(\alpha)\), take within each active stratum a prespecified pilot count tending to infinity and equal to \(o(C_{C,k})\), estimate \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\), snap it outward to a prespecified rational covariance partition, compute \(\delta_{\varepsilon,A}\), and apply it to the remaining calibrated estimator.  Uniformly over this compact allocation set and \(h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}\),
\[
\limsup_{C\to\infty}\left\{\sqrt C\,\mathbb E_{Q^{CR}_{\bar P_{C,h},C_C}}
\left[\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_{\delta_{\varepsilon,A}}(\bar P_{C,h})\right]
-U_\varepsilon(v_A,\alpha_A)\right\}\leq0.
\]
A diagonal choice \(\varepsilon_C\downarrow0\) attains the full-ball value \(G_{A,M}(v_A,\alpha_A)\) uniformly on that compact allocation set.  The equality of quotient images above and Gaussian quotient factorization identify the supremum over \(\mathscr H_{A,M}\) with the supremum over the entire closed quotient radius-\(M\) ball.  When this result is invoked in the hierarchical construction, it holds simultaneously over the finitely many retained subsets \(S\subseteq A\), provided the representative set used for each non-singleton \(S\) covers its entire closed quotient ball at the invoked radius; the singleton quotient is trivial.  Global adaptation over every candidate policy requires taking \(A=\mathcal K\) and restricting all candidate shares to a compact subset of the simplex interior.  No positive-rate transfer is asserted as an action-relevant share vanishes.
