For every fixed \(C\), fixed label-count vector, and bounded target-only loss \(\mathcal L\), regime \(\mathsf{CR}(\alpha)\) satisfies
\[
\inf_{\delta\in\mathfrak D_C}\sup_P\mathbb E_P[\mathcal L(U,\delta(\mathcal E_{C,CR}^{\mathrm{full}}(\alpha)))]
=
\inf_{\delta\in\mathfrak D_C}\sup_P\mathbb E_P[\mathcal L(U,\delta(\mathcal E_{C,CR}^{\mathrm{hit}}(\alpha)))].
\]
More generally, the equality holds after replacing the displayed finite deployment action space and its rule class by any standard-Borel decision space and all measurable randomized rules, provided the loss is bounded and target-only; this includes the ranking action of Han--Owusu--Shin.  Here the reduced experiment deletes the randomized label vector after the realized exact-count assignments are recorded.
