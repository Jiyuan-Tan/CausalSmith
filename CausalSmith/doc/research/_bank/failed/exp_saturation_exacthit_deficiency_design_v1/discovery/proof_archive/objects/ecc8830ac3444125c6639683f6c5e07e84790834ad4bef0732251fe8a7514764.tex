Let \(\mathscr H_{A,M}\) be as in the proposed statement.  Its compactness implies \(\sup_{h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}}\lVert h\rVert_2<\infty\).  The covering condition is nonvacuous: since \(D_A\) has full row rank, the linear map
\[
J_A=D_A^{\prime}(D_AD_A^{\prime})^{-1}
\]
is a right inverse of \(D_A\), and \(\{J_Ag:\lVert g\rVert_2\leq M\}\) is one compact representative set with the required quotient image.

Fix \(k\in A\) and \(z\in\mathcal S_k\).  By \(\texttt{lem:exact-hit-factorization}\),
\[
\rho_z(p):=\Pr_p(Z_c=z)=\frac{(Bp)_k}{M_k}
\geq \frac{\min_{\ell\in\mathcal K}B_{k\ell}}{M_k}
=:\rho_{k,0}>0                                                   \tag{81}
\]
uniformly over \(p\in\Delta_K\).  The strict inequality follows from \(0<\pi_\ell<1\) and \(1\leq m_k\leq n-1\), which make every binomial mass \(B_{k\ell}\) positive.  There are only finitely many target cells because \(n\) and \(K\) are fixed.

We first verify pilot consistency rather than assume it.  For a pilot of size \(m=m_C\), the empirical frequency \(\widehat\rho_z\) satisfies
\[
\operatorname{Var}_p(\widehat\rho_z)
=m^{-1}\rho_z(p)\{1-\rho_z(p)\}\leq(4m)^{-1}.
\]
Chebyshev's inequality, obtained by applying Markov's inequality to \((\widehat\rho_z-\rho_z)^2\), and a finite union bound imply uniform convergence of all target-cell frequencies.  Moreover
\[
\Pr_p(N_z=0)=\{1-\rho_z(p)\}^{m}
\leq e^{-m\rho_{k,0}}.                                           \tag{82}
\]
Conditional on \(N_z\), the cell observations are independent, bounded by one, and have the assignment-specific law.  Their conditional sample-mean variance is at most \(1/(4N_z)\); the same bound applies to their bounded squared observations.  On \(N_z\geq m\rho_{k,0}/2\), another application of Chebyshev's inequality therefore proves uniform consistency of every cell mean and second moment.  Equations (81)--(82), followed by a finite union bound, prove uniform consistency of \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\).  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:nondegenerate-active-slices}\), the bound \(\tau_k^2\leq1/4\), and (81) put the resulting covariance matrices in a compact set whose quotient covariances are uniformly positive definite.

Conditional on the pilot, the remaining clusters are independent of the selected receipt.  The ratio expansion underlying \(\texttt{thm:efficient-exact-hit-bernoulli}\), applied on the remaining \(C-m_C\) observations, has influence coordinates
\[
\phi_k^B(O)=\frac{\mathbf1\{Z\in\mathcal S_k\}}{q_k}
\{W(Z)-\mu_Z\}.
\]
They are centered, are uniformly bounded by \(1/\min_{p}(Bp)_k\), have variance \(\tau_k^2/q_k\), and have zero cross-products for distinct target slices.  For every fixed vector \(u\), the third-order Taylor remainder in the characteristic function of \(u^{\prime}\phi_A^B/\sqrt{C-m_C}\) is bounded by a constant times \((C-m_C)^{-3/2}\), uniformly over \(p\) and \(h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}\).  Summing the remainders and using \(m_C/C\to0\) gives the joint Gaussian limit uniformly on these compact index sets.  The local mean calculation in \(\texttt{thm:target-tangent-lan-bernoulli}\) consequently gives
\[
\sqrt C\{\widetilde U_{C,A}-U_A(P_0)\}
\Rightarrow N\{h,\Sigma_{B,A}\}                                 \tag{83}
\]
uniformly over \(p\in\Delta_K\) and \(h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}\).  The bounded exponential tilts also give, uniformly for \(a\in A\),
\[
\sqrt C\{U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_k(P_0)\}=h_k+o(1),
\]
and therefore
\[
\sqrt C\!\left\{\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})
-U_a(\bar P_{C,h})\right\}
=\max_{k\in A}h_k-h_a+o(1).                                     \tag{84}
\]

For fixed \(\varepsilon\), \(\texttt{thm:gaussian-finite-certificate}\) supplies finitely many rational covariance cells and a represented selector on each cell.  Outward snapping assigns a receipt whose cell contains the true covariance, including when the estimate is adjacent to a cell boundary.  The action boundaries of each represented finite-partition selector are finite unions of Gaussian-null affine faces.  Thus (83), conditional independence of pilot and main sample, and (84) imply uniform convergence of its primitive scaled risk to its Gaussian risk.  The fallback event in (82) has exponentially vanishing probability and the scaled loss in (84) is uniformly bounded on the compact representative set, so it contributes \(o(1)\).  The certified Gaussian upper bound now proves the displayed limit-superior inequality.

It remains to identify the domain of that Gaussian bound.  By \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-quotient-reduction}\), every represented selector is location invariant and its Gaussian regret risk factors through \(D_Ah\).  Hence, for its Gaussian risk \(R_{p,\varepsilon}^G\),
\[
\sup_{h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}}R_{p,\varepsilon}^G(h)
=\sup_{[g]\in H_A:\,\lVert[g]\rVert_A\leq M}
  R_{p,\varepsilon}^G(g).                                       \tag{85}
\]
The equality uses both inclusions in the assumed quotient-image identity: the first prevents directions outside the ball and the second represents every point of the ball.  This is the missing coverage step; compactness and an upper radius bound alone would not imply (85).  By the definition of \(G_{A,M}\) and the compact certificate,
\[
G_{A,M}(v_A,(Bp)_A)
\leq\sup_{h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}}R_{p,\varepsilon}^G(h)
\leq U_\varepsilon(v_A,(Bp)_A),
\]
while \(L_\varepsilon\leq G_{A,M}\) and \(U_\varepsilon-L_\varepsilon\leq\varepsilon\).  For tolerance \(1/j\), choose a deterministic sample-size threshold after which the pilot, snapping, Gaussian-approximation, and causal-expansion errors are at most \(1/j\), and let \(j=j(C)\uparrow\infty\) slowly.  The preceding squeeze and (85) prove uniform convergence to the full-ball value \(G_{A,M}\), with \(m_C\to\infty\) and \(m_C/C\to0\).  There are finitely many nonempty \(S\subseteq A\); applying the same construction to a full-ball covering representative set on each non-singleton \(S\), and taking the largest finite error, proves simultaneous transfer.  A singleton has zero-dimensional quotient and exact risk zero.  Finally, \(A=\mathcal K\) together with (81) gives the stated all-candidate Bernoulli scope.
