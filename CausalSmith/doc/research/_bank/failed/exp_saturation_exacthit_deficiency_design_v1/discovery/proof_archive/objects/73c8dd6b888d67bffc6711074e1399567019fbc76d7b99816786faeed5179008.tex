Let the pilot size satisfy \(m_C\to\infty\) and \(m_C/C\to0\).  By \(\texttt{lem:exact-hit-factorization}\), uniformly over \(p\in\Delta_K\),
\[
\Pr_p(Z=z)=\frac{(Bp)_k}{M_k}
\geq\frac{\min_{\ell\in\mathcal K}B_{k\ell}}{M_k}>0,
\qquad z\in\mathcal S_k. \tag{47}
\]
The strict inequality follows from the interior saturations.  Every observed welfare belongs to \([0,1]\).  Cellwise variance bounds and Chebyshev's inequality, followed by a union bound over the fixed finite target cells, show that the pilot cell means, second moments, hit rates, and hence \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\), are uniformly consistent over \(p\).  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:nondegenerate-active-slices}\) and (47) place the quotient covariance in a compact set whose eigenvalues are bounded away from zero and infinity.

Fix \(\varepsilon>0\).  Cover this covariance set by the finite rational terminal cells supplied by \(\texttt{thm:gaussian-finite-certificate}\), using the prescribed boundary convention to assign every estimate.  Conditional on the pilot, the remaining \(C-m_C\) iid records are independent of the selected represented rule.  The bounded characteristic-function expansion used in \(\texttt{thm:efficient-exact-hit-bernoulli}\), and the local mean calculation proved in \(\texttt{thm:target-tangent-lan-bernoulli}\), are uniform over \(p\) by (47) and over compact representative sets because all third moments are uniformly bounded. Conditional weak convergence therefore implies convergence of its bounded compact-parameter regret.  The finite number of receipt cells makes this convergence uniform over the selected rules.

\sqrt C\left\{\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})
-U_a(\bar P_{C,h})\right\}

The factor \(\sqrt C/\sqrt{C-m_C}\to1\), so discarding the pilot does not alter the limiting covariance.  Combining conditional convergence, (48), and the certified upper risk gives the displayed limit superior.
