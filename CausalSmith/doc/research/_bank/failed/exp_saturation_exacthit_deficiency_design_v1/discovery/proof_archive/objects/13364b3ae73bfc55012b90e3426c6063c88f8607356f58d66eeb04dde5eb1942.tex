For every realized assignment array, \(P_0\) and its product coupling \(\bar P_0\) have the same marginal law at the single assignment observed in each cluster.  Hence \(Q^{CR}_{\bar P_0,C_C}=Q^{CR}_{P_0,C_C}\).  Conditional on the fixed-count label vector, the iid schedule, label-schedule independence, and exact-slice assumptions make the observations independent in the triangular array.

For arbitrary \(h\in\mathbb R^A\), the bounded-support tilt calculation (36)--(37) gives derivative \(h_k\) of \(U_k\).  The conditional observed score is \(\dot\ell_{c,k}^{CR}\), centered in stratum \(k\), and
\[
 \frac1C\sum_{c=1}^C\mathbb E_0\{\dot\ell_{c,j}^{CR}\dot\ell_{c,k}^{CR}\mid L\}
 =\mathbf1\{j=k\}\frac{C_{C,k}}C\frac1{M_k\tau_k^4}
 \sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}\operatorname{Var}_{P_0}\{W(z)\}
 \longrightarrow\mathbf1\{j=k\}\frac{\alpha_k}{\tau_k^2}. \tag{41}
\]
This is \(I_{CR}\), positive definite because \(\alpha_k>0\) and \(\tau_k^2\geq\underline\tau^2\) on \(A\).

Applying (39) within each fixed stratum gives linear term \(h'\Delta_C^{CR}\), quadratic term \(-h'I_{CR}h/2+o_P(1)\), and a summed cubic remainder \(O(C^{-1/2}\max_k|h_k|^3)\) on compact sets.  The maximum normalized increment is \(O(C^{-1/2})\); multiplying the conditional characteristic functions and using (41) proves \(\Delta_C^{CR}\Rightarrow N(0,I_{CR})\) and the LAN expansion.  The active ratio expansion in \(\texttt{prop:efficient-exact-hit-cr-face}\), combined with the same likelihood calculation, gives
\[
 \sqrt C\{\widetilde U_{C,A}-U_A(P_0)\}\Rightarrow N\{h,\Sigma_{CR,A}\}. \tag{42}
\]
For each finite prior on representatives in the quotient ball, (41)--(42) and \(\texttt{lem:lan-finite-prior-lower-bound}\) transfer Bayes risk to the full Gaussian shift experiment.  The shift-grid averaging, finite quotient-grid approximation, and inactive-gap argument used above are unchanged, so \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-quotient-reduction}\) yields \(G_{A,M}(\tau_A^2,\alpha_A)\), and then \(G_{A,M}\uparrow G_A\).

For the witness, let the welfare on each target slice be an independent \(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) bit copied to all unit outcomes for that assignment.  Then \(\tau_k^2=1/4\), and (41) gives information \(\alpha_k/(1/4)=4\alpha_k\).  This binary witness lies inside the bounded schedule class and is separate from the unrestricted exponential-tilt path.
