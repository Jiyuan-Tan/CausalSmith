For every fixed \(C\), \(p\), and bounded target-only loss \(\mathcal L\), regime \(\mathsf B(p)\) satisfies \[\inf_{\delta\in\mathfrak D_C}\sup_P\mathbb E_P[\mathcal L(U,\delta(\mathcal E_{C,B}^{\mathrm{full}}(p)))] = \inf_{\delta\in\mathfrak D_C}\sup_P\mathbb E_P[\mathcal L(U,\delta(\mathcal E_{C,B}^{\mathrm{hit}}(p)))].\] The equality persists after deleting \(L_c\) conditional on every realized target assignment. It holds for rules observing the entire outcome vector because the reverse inequality fixes every nontarget assignment marginal at a known distribution and replaces the unidentified schedule coupling by the product of its assignment-specific marginals.
