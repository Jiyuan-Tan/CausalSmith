Let the allocation vectors range over a compact subset of the active interior.  Then some \(\rho>0\) satisfies \(C_{C,k}/C\geq\rho/2\) eventually, uniformly for \(k\in A\).  Under assumptions \(\text{ass:cr-label-vector}\), \(\text{ass:cr-label-schedule-independence}\), and \(\text{ass:cr-exact-slices}\), every target assignment in stratum \(k\) has conditional probability \(1/M_k\), and the stratum contains at least \(\rho C/2\) clusters.  A pilot count tending to infinity and equal to \(o(C_{C,k})\) therefore gives, by bounded Chebyshev inequalities and a finite union bound, a uniformly consistent \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\).  The remaining stratum counts divided by \(C\) still converge uniformly to \(\alpha_k\).

The quotient covariance matrices consequently lie in a compact uniformly positive-definite set.  Snap the pilot estimate to the finite rational partition supplied by \(\text{thm:gaussian-finite-certificate}\).  Conditional on the fixed label vector and pilot observations, the main observations are independent across clusters.  The bounded Lindeberg and local-mean calculations in \(\text{prop:efficient-exact-hit-cr-face}\) and \(\text{thm:target-tangent-lan-cr}\) hold uniformly on the compact allocation and representative sets.  As in the Bernoulli proof, every represented selector boundary is null under every positive-definite Gaussian law, and the finite family of receipt selectors makes conditional risk convergence uniform.  The causal-loss expansion (26) is unchanged.  Thus the displayed upper bound follows; the total pilot fraction vanishes because each within-stratum pilot is \(o(C_{C,k})\).

Applying the same slow diagonal construction with \(\varepsilon=1/j\) gives uniform convergence to \(G_{A,M}(v_A,\alpha_A)\).  If an active share tends to zero, pilot consistency is no longer uniform, quotient covariance leaves every compact positive-definite cell, and the Gaussian value diverges by \(\text{lem:gaussian-information-order}\); hence the proof supplies no boundary or changing-face transfer, exactly as stated.
