Under \(\mathsf{CR}(\alpha)\), for every representative \(h\in\mathbb R^A\), including its common-shift component, the product-coupled path satisfies \(Q^{CR}_{\bar P_0,C_C}=Q^{CR}_{P_0,C_C}\) and
\[
\log\frac{dQ^{CR}_{\bar P_{C,h},C_C}}{dQ^{CR}_{P_0,C_C}}
=h^{\prime}\Delta_C^{CR}-\frac12h^{\prime}I_{CR}h+o_{Q^{CR}_{P_0,C_C}}(1),
\qquad \Delta_C^{CR}\Rightarrow N(0,I_{CR}),
\]
where, conditionally on the fixed-count label vector, \(\dot\ell_{c,k}^{CR}\) is centered in its stratum and \(I_{CR}=\operatorname{diag}(\alpha_k/\tau_k^2:k\in A)\).  Under \(\bar P_{C,h}\),
\[
\sqrt C\{\widetilde U_{C,A}-U_A(P_0)\}\Rightarrow N\{h,\Sigma_{CR,A}\}.
\]
For every measurable randomized rule sequence \(\delta_C\) and every \(M<\infty\),
\[
\liminf_{C\to\infty}\sup_{h\in\mathbb R^A:\ \lVert[h]\rVert_A\leq M}
\sqrt C\,\mathbb E_{Q^{CR}_{\bar P_{C,h},C_C}}
\left[\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_{\delta_C}(\bar P_{C,h})\right]
\geq G_{A,M}(\tau_A^2,\alpha_A),
\]
and \(G_{A,M}\uparrow G_A\).  The independent \(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) target-bit submodel has \(\tau_k^2=1/4\) and information \(4\alpha_k\); it is a witness, not a restriction on the general path.
