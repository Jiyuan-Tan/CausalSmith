For a fixed selector whose discontinuity set has zero probability under every positive-covariance Gaussian shift, the split-sample covariance consistency and the bounded triangular-array CLT imply pointwise, and by compactness uniformly for \(\|h\|\leq M\), convergence of its scaled regret to its Gaussian risk.  A split size tending to infinity but equal to \(o(C)\) preserves the main-sample covariance.  The unclosed step is to verify those stability properties for the unspecified certificate selector and to obtain the claimed global bound \(U_\varepsilon\); that step is exactly the unresolved Gaussian certificate target.
