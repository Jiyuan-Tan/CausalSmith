\frac{C_{C,k}}C\geq\rho/2\qquad(k\in A). \tag{50}

Under \(\texttt{ass:cr-label-vector}\), \(\texttt{ass:cr-label-schedule-independence}\), and \(\texttt{ass:cr-exact-slices}\), each target assignment in stratum \(k\) has conditional probability \(1/M_k\), and (50) gives at least \(\rho C/2\) clusters in that stratum.  A pilot count diverging and equal to \(o(C_{C,k})\) therefore gives, by bounded Chebyshev inequalities and a finite union bound, a uniformly consistent \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\).  Removing the pilot changes each stratum share by \(o(1)\).

The quotient covariance matrices lie in a compact uniformly positive-definite set.  Snap the pilot estimate to the finite rational partition from \(\texttt{thm:gaussian-finite-certificate}\).
