When \(K=2\) and \(A=\mathcal K\), put \(\sigma_k^2=v_k/r_k\).  For the frozen game with \(H_A=\{(d/2,-d/2):d\in\mathbb R\}\), the exact all-rule value is
\[
G_A(v_A,r_A)=\frac{2\kappa}{\sqrt{r_1/v_1+r_2/v_2}},
\]
attained by selecting policy one exactly when \(X_1/\sigma_1^2-X_2/\sigma_2^2\geq0\).  Selecting the larger coordinate instead has worst risk \(\kappa\sqrt{v_1/r_1+v_2/r_2}\), and it is all-rule minimax if and only if \(v_1/r_1=v_2/r_2\).  Within this larger-coordinate rule, the optimized exact-count share is \(\alpha_1^\star=\sqrt{v_1}/(\sqrt{v_1}+\sqrt{v_2})\). If \(B_{11}>B_{12}\) and \(B_{22}>B_{21}\), its Bernoulli optimizer \(t^\star\) equals zero exactly when \(v_1/v_2\leq(B_{22}-B_{21})B_{12}^2/[(B_{11}-B_{12})B_{22}^2]\), equals one exactly when \(v_1/v_2\geq(B_{22}-B_{21})B_{11}^2/[(B_{11}-B_{12})B_{21}^2]\), and otherwise equals
\[
\frac{B_{22}\sqrt{v_1(B_{11}-B_{12})/[v_2(B_{22}-B_{21})]}-B_{12}}{(B_{11}-B_{12})+(B_{22}-B_{21})\sqrt{v_1(B_{11}-B_{12})/[v_2(B_{22}-B_{21})]}}.
\]
For \(n=4\), \((\pi_1,\pi_2)=(1/4,1/2)\), and equal variances, \(B=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}27/64&1/4\\27/128&3/8\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\).  The larger-coordinate benchmark has \(t^\star=0.39383577843\ldots\), regret ratio \(1.26189430297\ldots\), and cluster-count ratio \(1.59237723186\ldots\).  In contrast, the all-rule frozen game maximizes \(q_1/v_1+q_2/v_2\); at equal variances its Bernoulli optimizer is \(t=1\), its regret ratio relative to the exact-count infimum is \(\sqrt{128/81}=1.25707872211\ldots\), and its cluster-count ratio is \(128/81=1.58024691358\ldots\).
