Under exact-slice randomization, \(Z_c\in\mathcal S_{L_c}\) almost surely.  Since the integers \(m_1,\ldots,m_K\) are distinct, the slices are disjoint and
\[
 L_c=k\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \sum_{j=1}^n Z_{c,j}=m_k.
\]
Thus the deleted label vector is a deterministic measurable function of the retained assignment vectors.  Conversely the reduced record is obtained from the full record by forgetting that vector.  The two experiments are therefore isomorphic by parameter-free Markov kernels for every fixed label-count vector and every schedule law \(P\).  Composing a randomized rule with either kernel preserves its risk pointwise in \(P\); taking the supremum over \(P\) and infimum over rules gives the displayed equality.  The target remains the causal exact-slice average because, by schedule independence and uniformity on \(\mathcal S_k\), the conditional mean welfare in stratum \(k\) is
\[
 \mathbb E_P[W(Z_c)\mid L_c=k]=M_k^{-1}\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}\mathbb E_P[W(z)]=U_k.
\]
