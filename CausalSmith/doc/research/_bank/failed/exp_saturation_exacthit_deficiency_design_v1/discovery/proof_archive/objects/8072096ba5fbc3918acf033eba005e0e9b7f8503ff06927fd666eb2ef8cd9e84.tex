Let \(A=\operatorname*{arg\,max}_kU_k(P_0)\), with \(|A|\geq2\), and let every policy in \(A^c\) be separated from the tied active welfare. Set \(\bar P_0=\bigotimes_zP_{0,z}\) and use \(\bar P_{C,h}=\bar P_{C^{-1/2},h}\), \(h\in H_A\). Then \(Q^B_{\bar P_0,p}=Q^B_{P_0,p}\), \(dU_k(\bar P_{t,h})/dt|_{t=0}=h_k\), \(\dot\ell_k^B\in L^2_0(Q^B_{P_0,p})\), and \[\log\frac{d(Q^B_{\bar P_{C,h},p})^{\otimes C}}{d(Q^B_{P_0,p})^{\otimes C}}=h^{\prime}\Delta_C^B-\tfrac12h^{\prime}I_Bh+o_{(Q^B_{P_0,p})^{\otimes C}}(1),\qquad \Delta_C^B\Rightarrow N(0,I_B).\] The active estimator limit is \(X_k=h_k+\sqrt{\tau_k^2/q_k}Z_k\), with covariance \(\Sigma_{B,A}\). For every measurable randomized rule sequence \(\delta_C\), \[\lim_{M\to\infty}\liminf_{C\to\infty}\sup_{h\in H_A:\lVert h\rVert\leq M}\sqrt C\,\mathbb E_{(Q^B_{\bar P_{C,h},p})^{\otimes C}}[\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_{\delta_C}(\bar P_{C,h})]\geq G_A(\tau_A^2,q_A).\] The same bound holds for rules observing the full Bernoulli experiment by nuisance deletion.
