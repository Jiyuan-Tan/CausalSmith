Using the lower-semicontinuous boundary value of \(G_A\), define
\[
R_{B,A}^\star(v_A)=\min_{p\in\Delta_K}G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A),\qquad
R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A)=\min_{a\in\Delta_A}G_A(v_A,a).
\]
Only the active coordinates are supplied to \(G_A\).  Positivity of every entry of \(B\) keeps \((Bp)_A\) in the positive orthant, whereas boundary coercivity makes every minimizer in the exact-count simplex belong to \(\Delta_A^\circ\); allocating mass to inactive exact-count policies is therefore excluded after the active face is selected.
