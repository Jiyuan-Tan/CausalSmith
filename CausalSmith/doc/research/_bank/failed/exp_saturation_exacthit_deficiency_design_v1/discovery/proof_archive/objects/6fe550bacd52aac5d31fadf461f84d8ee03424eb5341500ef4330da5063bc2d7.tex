For \(K=2\), write \(p=(t,1-t)\), \(q_1(t)=B_{12}+(B_{11}-B_{12})t\), and \(q_2(t)=B_{22}-(B_{22}-B_{21})t\).  Define
\[
F_v(t)=\frac{v_1}{q_1(t)}+\frac{v_2}{q_2(t)},\qquad
t^\star=\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{0\leq t\leq1}F_v(t).
\]
This is the exact contrast-variance allocation map for the two-action quotient game.  Its exact-count counterpart minimizes \(v_1/\alpha_1+v_2/(1-\alpha_1)\) over \(0<\alpha_1<1\).
