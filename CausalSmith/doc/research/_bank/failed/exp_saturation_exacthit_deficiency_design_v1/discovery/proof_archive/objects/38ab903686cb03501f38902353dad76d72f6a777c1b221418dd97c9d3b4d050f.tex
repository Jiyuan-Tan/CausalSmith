Let \(C_B,C_{CR}>0\) be cluster budgets for the optimized Bernoulli-label and exact-count designs.  If the Bernoulli design is required to have no larger leading local simple regret than the exact-count design, namely
\[
\frac{R_{B,A}^\star(v_A)}{\sqrt{C_B}}
\leq
\frac{R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A)}{\sqrt{C_{CR}}},
\]
then
\[
\frac{C_B}{C_{CR}}
\geq
\left\{\frac{R_{B,A}^\star(v_A)}{R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A)}\right\}^{2}
\geq\frac1{s_{A,\max}}
\geq\frac1{1-2^{1-n}}.
\]
Equality in the mechanism inequality \(\{R_{B,A}^\star/R_{CR,A}^\star\}^2\geq1/s_{A,\max}\) holds exactly under the support-and-normalized-active-vector criterion in \(\texttt{thm:mechanism-loss}\).
