For a randomized selector \(\delta\), define
\[
R_A(\delta,h)=\mathbb E_h\!\left[\max_{i\in A}h_i-h_{\delta(X)}\right]. \tag{1}
\]
We first prove the subface lower bound.  Fix a nonempty \(S\subseteq A\) and \(M<\infty\).  By the quotient definition in \(\texttt{def:gaussian-face-game}\), every class in the compact \(S\)-game has a representative \(g\) satisfying \(\max_{i\in S}g_i=0\).  If \(\lVert[g]\rVert_S\leq M\), every coordinate differs from the reference coordinate by at most \(M\), and the reference coordinate differs from a maximizing coordinate by at most \(M\); hence \(-2M\leq g_i\leq0\).  Embed this parameter in \(\mathbb R^A\) by
\[
h_i=g_i\quad(i\in S),\qquad h_j=-2M-1\quad(j\in A\setminus S). \tag{2}
\]
Every outside action is strictly dominated.  The diagonal covariance in \(\texttt{def:gaussian-face-game}\) makes \(X_{A\setminus S}\) independent of \(X_S\), and its law in (2) is independent of \(g\).  Thus those outside observations provide only auxiliary randomization, already permitted for an \(S\)-selector.  Replacing every selected outside action by a fixed action in \(S\) cannot increase regret.  Therefore every \(A\)-selector induces an \(S\)-selector whose risk is no larger on (2), and
\[
G_A(v_A,r_A)\geq G_{S,M}(v_S,r_S). \tag{3}
\]
Letting \(M\to\infty\) and invoking \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-quotient-reduction}\) gives \(G_S(v_S,r_S)\leq G_A(v_A,r_A)\).  Taking the maximum of (3) at the prescribed radii gives \(H_{\lambda,T}\leq G_A\).

For the upper construction, write \(X=h+\xi\), where \(\xi\sim N(0,\Sigma)\).  Independently generate \(E\sim N(0,\Sigma)\) and set
\[
X^{\mathrm s}=X+\sqrt{\frac{1-\lambda}{\lambda}}E,
\qquad
X^{\mathrm d}=X-\sqrt{\frac{\lambda}{1-\lambda}}E. \tag{4}
\]
Then
\[
X^{\mathrm s}\sim N(h,\Sigma/\lambda),\qquad
X^{\mathrm d}\sim N(h,\Sigma/(1-\lambda)),
\qquad \operatorname{Cov}(X^{\mathrm s},X^{\mathrm d})=0. \tag{5}
\]
The pair is jointly Gaussian, so the zero cross-covariance in (5) proves independence.  Define the nonempty retained set
\[
\widehat S=\left\{i\in A:X_i^{\mathrm s}\geq\max_{j\in A}X_j^{\mathrm s}-T\right\}. \tag{6}
\]
Conditional on \(\widehat S=S\), feed \(\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,X_S^{\mathrm d}\), not the unscaled decision observation, to the compact \(S\)-selector.  Its covariance is \(\Sigma_{SS}\) and its mean is \(\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,h_S\).  The construction is measurable, randomized, and invariant under a common location shift.

Fix a true maximizing arm \(b\), put \(d_i=h_b-h_i\geq0\), and write \(\xi^{\mathrm s}=X^{\mathrm s}-h\).  Every screening contrast has standard deviation at most
\[
a_\lambda=\sqrt{2/\lambda}\,\bar\sigma. \tag{7}
\]
For \(x>0\), integration of \(-\varphi'(u)=u\varphi(u)\) yields the Mills bound
\[
\Phi(-x)=\int_x^\infty\varphi(u)\,du
\leq x^{-1}\int_x^\infty u\varphi(u)\,du
=\frac{\varphi(x)}x. \tag{8}
\]
If \(b\notin\widehat S\) and \(J\) maximizes \(X^{\mathrm s}\), then \(\xi_J^{\mathrm s}-\xi_b^{\mathrm s}>T+d_J\), while the loss from deleting all maximizers is at most \(d_J\).  For a centered normal variable with standard deviation \(a\leq a_\lambda\), (8) gives
\[
d_i\Pr\{\xi_i^{\mathrm s}-\xi_b^{\mathrm s}>T+d_i\}
\leq a\varphi(T/a)
\leq\frac{a_\lambda}{\sqrt{2\pi}}
       \exp\!\left\{-\frac{T^2}{2a_\lambda^2}\right\}. \tag{9}
\]
The last inequality follows because \(a\mapsto a\exp\{-T^2/(2a^2)\}\) is increasing.  Summing (9) bounds the deletion loss.

If \(i,j\in\widehat S\), then \(|X_i^{\mathrm s}-X_j^{\mathrm s}|\leq T\).  Hence, if \(D=h_j-h_i>2T\) and both are retained,
\[
\xi_i^{\mathrm s}-\xi_j^{\mathrm s}\geq D-T\geq D/2.
\]
Another application of (8) gives
\[
D\Pr\{\xi_i^{\mathrm s}-\xi_j^{\mathrm s}\geq D-T\}
\leq \frac{2a_\lambda}{\sqrt{2\pi}}
       \exp\!\left\{-\frac{T^2}{2a_\lambda^2}\right\}. \tag{10}
\]
The range of a bad retained set is at most the sum of its oriented pairwise gaps exceeding \(2T\).  Summing (9)--(10) over fewer than \(d^2\) relevant indices is bounded by the displayed tail term in the statement.

On the complementary event the true range in \(S=\widehat S\) is at most \(2T\).  Therefore
\[
\left\lVert[\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,h_S]\right\rVert_S
\leq2\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,T\sqrt{|S|-1}\leq M_S. \tag{11}
\]
The screen is independent of the decision observation, so conditioning on \(\widehat S=S\) does not alter its law.  The certified regret in the scaled problem is at most \(G_{S,M_S}+\eta\); undoing the scale multiplies regret by \((1-\lambda)^{-1/2}\).  Averaging over the partition \(\{\widehat S=S\}\) costs \(\max_S(G_{S,M_S}+\eta)\), not a sum.  Combining (9)--(11) proves the upper bound.  The constants depend only on \(d\) and a uniform upper bound for \(\bar\sigma\), so the proof is uniform on compact positive-variance cells and, because there are finitely many subsets, simultaneously over \(S\subseteq A\).  No arm outside the declared \(A\) enters the construction, which proves the stated face qualification.
