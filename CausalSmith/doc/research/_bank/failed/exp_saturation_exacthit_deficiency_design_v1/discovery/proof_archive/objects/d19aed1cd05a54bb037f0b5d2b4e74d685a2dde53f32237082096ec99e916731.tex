For \(z\in\mathcal S_k\),
\[
 \frac{\gamma_k(z)}{\beta_k(z)}
 =\frac{M_k^{-1}}{\pi_k^{m_k}(1-\pi_k)^{n-m_k}}
 =\frac1{B_{kk}}.
\]
Substitution in the published Horvitz--Thompson formula and \(\eta_k=p_kB_{kk}>0\) gives the displayed equality.  Its summands are iid and have expectation
\[
 \eta_k^{-1}\Pr(L=k,Z\in\mathcal S_k)
 \mathbb E[W(Z)\mid L=k,Z\in\mathcal S_k]=U_k,
\]
where conditional uniformity on the slice was established by the factorization lemma.  Thus its exact asymptotic linear coordinate is
\(\phi_k^{HOS}=\eta_k^{-1}{\mathbf 1}\{L=k,Z\in\mathcal S_k\}W(Z)-U_k\).  Since
\[
 \mathbb E[W(Z)^2\mid L=k,Z\in\mathcal S_k]
 =\tau_k^2+M_k^{-1}\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}\mu_z^2
 =\tau_k^2+b_k^2+U_k^2,
\]
direct expansion gives
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(\phi_k^{HOS})
 =\frac{\tau_k^2+b_k^2}{\eta_k}+U_k^2(\eta_k^{-1}-1). \tag{10}
\]

If only nominal-label-\(k\) target hits are retained, each cell \(z\in\mathcal S_k\) has probability \(\eta_k/M_k\).  Repeating equations (2)--(4) with \(q_k\) replaced by \(\eta_k\) proves that
\[
 \phi_k^{nom}=\eta_k^{-1}{\mathbf 1}\{L=k,Z\in\mathcal S_k\}(W(Z)-\mu_Z)
\]
is its canonical gradient and has variance \(\tau_k^2/\eta_k\).  Because \(b_k^2\geq0\), \(U_k^2\geq0\), and \(0<\eta_k\leq1\), equation (10) is at least this efficiency bound.  If \(q_k>\eta_k\) and \(\tau_k^2>0\), division by positive quantities gives
\[
 \tau_k^2/q_k<\tau_k^2/\eta_k\leq\operatorname{Var}(\phi_k^{HOS}).
\]
Finally, \(b_k^2>0\) makes the first excess term \(b_k^2/\eta_k\) strictly positive, separately from the nonnegative participation term \(U_k^2(\eta_k^{-1}-1)\), proving the stated refinement.
