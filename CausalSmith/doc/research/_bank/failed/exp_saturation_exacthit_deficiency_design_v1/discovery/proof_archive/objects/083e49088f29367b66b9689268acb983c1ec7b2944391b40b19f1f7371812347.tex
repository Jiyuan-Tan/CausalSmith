With two arms, \(D_A=(1,-1)\).  By \(\text{lem:gaussian-quotient-reduction}\), every minimax rule may be taken to depend only on
\[
Y=X_1-X_2\sim N(d,s^2),\qquad d=h_1-h_2,\qquad
s^2=\frac{v_1}{r_1}+\frac{v_2}{r_2}. \tag{11}
\]
The location nuisance \(h_1+h_2\) has disappeared from both \(Y\) and the loss, but was not assumed known.  The rule choosing arm one when \(Y\geq0\) has regret
\[
R(d)=|d|\Phi(-|d|/s).
\]
Writing \(x=|d|/s\) gives \(\sup_dR(d)=\kappa s\).  Conversely, put equal mass on the two quotient parameters \(d=\pm sx_\star\), where \(x_\star\) maximizes \(x\Phi(-x)\).  Their likelihood-ratio rule is the sign of \(Y\), and their Bayes regret is \(sx_\star\Phi(-x_\star)=\kappa s\).  Maximum risk dominates Bayes risk, so no randomized rule improves this value.  This proves the exact formula and the asserted rule.

For exact counts, minimizing the squared risk is equivalent to minimizing
\[
f(\alpha)=\frac{v_1}{\alpha}+\frac{v_2}{1-\alpha},\qquad0<\alpha<1.
\]
Now
\[
f'(\alpha)=-\frac{v_1}{\alpha^2}+\frac{v_2}{(1-\alpha)^2},\qquad
f''(\alpha)=\frac{2v_1}{\alpha^3}+\frac{2v_2}{(1-\alpha)^3}>0.
\]
The unique stationary point is \(\alpha_1^\star=\sqrt{v_1}/(\sqrt{v_1}+\sqrt{v_2})\), and divergence at both endpoints makes it the global minimizer.

For Bernoulli labels put \(a=B_{11}\), \(b=B_{12}\), \(c=B_{21}\), \(d_0=B_{22}\), \(A_0=a-b>0\), and \(D_0=d_0-c>0\).  Then
\[
F_v'(t)=-\frac{v_1A_0}{(b+A_0t)^2}+\frac{v_2D_0}{(d_0-D_0t)^2},
\quad
F_v''(t)=\frac{2v_1A_0^2}{(b+A_0t)^3}+\frac{2v_2D_0^2}{(d_0-D_0t)^3}>0. \tag{12}
\]
Thus the signs of \(F_v'(0)\) and \(F_v'(1)\) give exactly the two displayed endpoint inequalities.  In the remaining case, taking the positive square root in \(F_v'(t)=0\) and solving the resulting linear equation gives the displayed interior formula.  Strict convexity proves uniqueness.
