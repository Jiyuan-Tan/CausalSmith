Write \(C_k=C_{C,k}\) and \(a_{C,k}=C_k/C\).  The all-share assumption gives \(a_{C,k}\to\alpha_k>0\), uniformly bounded away from zero for large \(C\).  Conditional on the fixed label vector, the observations are independent and uniform on their assigned slices.  For \(z\in\mathcal S_k\), set
\[
S_z=\sum_{c:L_c=k}\mathbf1\{Z_c=z\}\{W_c(z)-\mu_z\}.
\]
On occupied cells,
\[
\sqrt C(\widetilde U_{C,k}-U_k)
=C^{-1/2}\sum_{c=1}^C\frac{C}{C_k}\mathbf1\{L_c=k\}
\{W_c(Z_c)-\mu_{Z_c}\}+R_{C,k}. \tag{75}
\]
Conditional variance calculations give \(N_z-C_k/M_k=O_P(\sqrt{C_k})\) and \(S_z=O_P(\sqrt{C_k})\), uniformly over bounded schedule laws.  Since the number of cells is fixed and \(C_k\asymp C\), the cell-ratio remainder satisfies \(\max_k|R_{C,k}|=o_P(1)\) uniformly.  Also
\[
\Pr\{\text{some target cell empty}\}
\leq\sum_kM_ke^{-C_k/M_k}\to0. \tag{76}
\]
The conditional covariance of the leading term in (75) is diagonal with entries
\[
\frac{C}{C_k}\frac1{M_k}\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}\operatorname{Var}_P\{W(z)\}
=\frac{\tau_k^2(P)}{a_{C,k}}. \tag{77}
\]
After division by \(\sqrt C\), the summands are uniformly infinitesimal.  A third-order conditional characteristic-function expansion has total remainder \(O(C^{-1/2})\), uniformly over \(P\).  Hence (75)--(77) derive the triangular-array Gaussian approximation from the primitive design.

The empirical covariance is diagonal and, on the regular branch,
\[
\widehat\Sigma_{C,kk}^{(K)}
=\frac{C}{C_k^2}\sum_{c:L_c=k}\{W_c(Z_c)-\overline W_{Z_c}\}^2. \tag{78}
\]
The within-cell least-squares identity and bounded conditional variance bounds show that replacing \(\overline W_{Z_c}\) by \(\mu_{Z_c}\) changes (78) by \(o_P(1)\) uniformly.  The oracle average has mean \(\tau_k^2(P)\) and variance at most \(C_k^{-1}\).  Thus
\[
\widehat\Sigma_C^{(K)}-
\operatorname{diag}(\tau_k^2(P)/a_{C,k}:k\in\mathcal K)\to_P0 \tag{79}
\]
uniformly.

For an arbitrary sequence \((P_C)\) in the nondegenerate class, \(\texttt{lem:weak-compactness-compact-laws}\) supplies a subsequence with \(P_C\Rightarrow P_\star\) on the compact schedule space.  Bounded continuity of \(W(z)\) and \(W(z)^2\) makes all cell means and variances converge.  Equations (75)--(79) then imply
\[
\sqrt C\{\widetilde U_C-U(P_C)\}\Rightarrow
N\{0,\Sigma_{CR}^{(K)}(P_\star)\},\qquad
\widehat\Sigma_C^{(K)}\to_P\Sigma_{CR}^{(K)}(P_\star). \tag{80}
\]
Every pairwise contrast variance is at least \(2\underline\tau^2\).  The studentized Gaussian maximum therefore has a continuous distribution, and its quantile is continuous in covariance on the resulting compact positive-definite set.  The same subsequence contradiction as above gives uniform simultaneous pairwise coverage at level \(1-\gamma\).

On that event, the sets of policies certified strictly above or below \(k\) are subsets of the corresponding true strict-inequality sets.  Hence the inverted endpoints bracket \(r_k^-(U)\) and \(r_k^+(U)\), so \(\operatorname{Rank}_k(U)\subseteq\mathcal R_{C,k}(\gamma)\) for all \(k\), including partial ties.  The full-rank fallback in (76) can only enlarge the set.  This proves the stated uniform coverage.
