Fix \(A\), \(M<\infty\), and a compact representative set \(\mathscr H_{A,M}\subseteq\mathbb R^A\) satisfying
\[
\{[h]:h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}\}
=\{[g]\in H_A:\lVert[g]\rVert_A\leq M\}.
\]
Under \(\mathsf B(p)\), use an independent pilot of size \(m_C\to\infty\) with \(m_C/C\to0\) to estimate \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\), snap it outward to a prespecified rational covariance partition, compute the corresponding compact certificate selector \(\delta_{\varepsilon,A}\), and apply that selector to the calibrated estimator from the remaining clusters.  Uniformly over \(p\in\Delta_K\) and \(h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}\),
\[
\limsup_{C\to\infty}\left\{\sqrt C\,\mathbb E_{(Q^B_{\bar P_{C,h},p})^{\otimes C}}
\left[\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_{\delta_{\varepsilon,A}}(\bar P_{C,h})\right]
-U_\varepsilon(v_A,(Bp)_A)\right\}\leq0.
\]
There is a diagonal choice \(\varepsilon_C\downarrow0\), with pilot fraction still vanishing and pilot size diverging, whose Gaussian risk converges uniformly over \(p\in\Delta_K\) to the full-ball value \(G_{A,M}(v_A,(Bp)_A)\).  The equality of quotient images above and Gaussian quotient factorization identify the supremum over \(\mathscr H_{A,M}\) with the supremum over the entire closed quotient radius-\(M\) ball.  The uniformity over \(p\) uses the primitive bound \(\min_{p\in\Delta_K}(Bp)_k=\min_{\ell\in\mathcal K}B_{k\ell}>0\) for every active \(k\).  When this result is invoked in the hierarchical construction, it holds simultaneously over the finitely many retained subsets \(S\subseteq A\), provided the representative set used for each non-singleton \(S\) covers its entire closed quotient ball at the invoked radius; the singleton quotient is trivial.  Global adaptation over every candidate policy is obtained by taking \(A=\mathcal K\), for which Bernoulli positivity supplies positive information for every candidate.
