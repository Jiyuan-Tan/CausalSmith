For the Bernoulli outer polytope, every active rate is uniformly positive because each entry of \(B\) is positive.  There are only finitely many nonempty faces \(A\), and on any compact variance class bounded away from zero, the homogeneity and noise-addition comparison give the coordinatewise modulus
\[
 (1+\xi)^{-1/2}G_A(v,r)\leq G_A(v,r')\leq(1-\xi)^{-1/2}G_A(v,r)
\]
whenever \((1-\xi)r\leq r'\leq(1+\xi)r\).  This reduces a uniform outer schedule to finitely many positive-rate fixed-face oracles.  The frozen core does not yet prove such an oracle on the unbounded contrast space, nor a uniform coercive truncation for exact-count rates approaching a face boundary, so a single certified schedule cannot yet be constructed.
