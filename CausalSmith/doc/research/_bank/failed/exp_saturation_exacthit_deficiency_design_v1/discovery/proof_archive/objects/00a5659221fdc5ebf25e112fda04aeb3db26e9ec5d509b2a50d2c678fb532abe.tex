Fix \(A\), \(M<\infty\), and a compact set of representative local directions whose quotient norms are at most \(M\).  Under \(\mathsf B(p)\), use an independent pilot of size \(m_C\to\infty\) with \(m_C/C\to0\) to estimate \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\), snap it outward to a prespecified rational covariance partition, compute the corresponding compact certificate selector \(\delta_{\varepsilon,A}\), and apply that selector to the calibrated estimator from the remaining clusters.  Uniformly over \(p\in\Delta_K\) and over that compact local set,
\[
\limsup_{C\to\infty}\left\{\sqrt C\,\mathbb E_{(Q^B_{\bar P_{C,h},p})^{\otimes C}}
\left[\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_{\delta_{\varepsilon,A}}(\bar P_{C,h})\right]
-U_\varepsilon(v_A,(Bp)_A)\right\}\leq0.
\]
There is a diagonal choice \(\varepsilon_C\downarrow0\), with pilot fraction still vanishing and pilot size diverging, whose Gaussian risk converges uniformly to \(G_{A,M}(v_A,(Bp)_A)\).  The uniformity over \(p\) uses the primitive bound \(\min_{p\in\Delta_K}(Bp)_k=\min_{\ell\in\mathcal K}B_{k\ell}>0\) for every active \(k\).  When this proposition is invoked for hierarchical screening, its conclusion holds simultaneously over the finitely many retained subsets \(S\subseteq A\).  Adaptation over every candidate policy requires taking \(A=\mathcal K\), in which case Bernoulli exact-hit positivity supplies positive information for all candidates.
