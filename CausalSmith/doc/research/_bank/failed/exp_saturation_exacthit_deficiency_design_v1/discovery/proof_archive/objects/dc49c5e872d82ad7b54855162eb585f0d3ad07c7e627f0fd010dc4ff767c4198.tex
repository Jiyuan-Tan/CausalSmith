Fix \(A\), positive \(v_A\), and \(M<\infty\). Under \(\mathsf{CR}(\alpha)\), estimate \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\) on a prespecified within-stratum split, compute \(\delta_{\varepsilon,A}\), and apply it to the remaining \(\widetilde U_{C,A}\).
