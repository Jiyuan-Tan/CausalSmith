Condition on a realized label vector with the fixed counts \(C_{C,k}\).  Schedule independence and exact-slice randomization make the cluster records conditionally independent.  For \(z\in\mathcal S_k\),
\[
 \Pr(Z_c=z\mid L_c=k)=M_k^{-1},\qquad
 \mathbb E[W_c(Z_c)\mid L_c=k,Z_c=z]=\mu_z.
\]
Because \(C_{C,k}/C\to\alpha_k>0\), every cell count in stratum \(k\) diverges in probability and the empty-cell fallback is negligible.  The same ratio expansion as in the Bernoulli proof, now with cell frequency \(C_{C,k}/(CM_k)\), gives
\[
 \sqrt C(\widetilde U_{C,k}-U_k)
 =\frac1{\sqrt C}\sum_{c=1}^C\frac{C}{C_{C,k}}{\mathbf 1}\{L_c=k\}
 \{W_c(Z_c)-\mu_{Z_c}\}+o_P(1)
 =\frac1{\sqrt C}\sum_{c=1}^C\phi_{c,k}^{CR}+o_P(1). \tag{8}
\]
For distinct coordinates the summand products vanish.  The conditional variance of coordinate \(k\) in (8) is
\[
 \frac1C\sum_{c=1}^C\mathbb E[(\phi_{c,k}^{CR})^2\mid L]
 =\frac{C}{C_{C,k}}\frac1{M_k}\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}\operatorname{Var}_{P_0}(W(z))
 \longrightarrow\frac{\tau_k^2}{\alpha_k}. \tag{9}
\]
The triangular-array summands divided by \(\sqrt C\) are uniformly bounded by \(\sqrt C/C_{C,k}=O(C^{-1/2})\).  Taylor expansion of the conditional characteristic function, with the third-order remainder bounded by the maximum summand times its total second moment, proves the conditional multivariate Gaussian limit with covariance (9).  That conditional limit is independent of the particular label permutation, so it also holds unconditionally.

Define the empirical covariance by replacing each \(\mu_z\) in (8) by its cell mean and using the fixed coefficient \(C/C_{C,k}\).  Finite-cell conditional laws of large numbers and \(C_{C,k}/C\to\alpha_k\) imply convergence of every diagonal entry to (9); off-diagonal entries are zero.  The bounded fallback has probability tending to zero.  Hence \(\widehat\Sigma_C^{(K)}\to_P\Sigma_{CR}^{(K)}\), proving all assertions.
