Let \(A\) be finite with \(d=|A|\geq2\), let \(v_i,r_i>0\), and write
\[
\Sigma=\Sigma(v_A,r_A)=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_i^2:i\in A),
\qquad \sigma_i^2=v_i/r_i,
\qquad \bar\sigma=\max_{i\in A}\sigma_i.
\]
For every nonempty \(S\subseteq A\), let \(G_{S,M}(v_S,r_S)\) denote the compact quotient game obtained by restricting the Gaussian face-game definition to \(S\), with the singleton value set equal to zero.  Fix \(0<\lambda<1\), \(T>0\), and for every nonempty \(S\subseteq A\) choose \(M_S\) satisfying
\[
M_S\geq2\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,T\sqrt{|S|-1},
\qquad
H_{\lambda,T}(v_A,r_A)=\max_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq A}G_{S,M_S}(v_S,r_S),
\qquad
a_\lambda=\sqrt{2/\lambda}\,\bar\sigma.
\]
If, for each \(S\), a represented invariant selector has risk at most \(G_{S,M_S}(v_S,r_S)+\eta\) on its displayed compact quotient ball, then an explicit randomized invariant selector in the unbounded \(A\)-arm experiment satisfies
\[
H_{\lambda,T}(v_A,r_A)
\leq G_A(v_A,r_A)
\leq \sup_{h\in\mathbb R^A}\mathbb E_h\!\left[\max_{i\in A}h_i-h_{\delta(X)}\right]
\leq
(1-\lambda)^{-1/2}\{H_{\lambda,T}(v_A,r_A)+\eta\}
+\frac{2d^2a_\lambda}{\sqrt{2\pi}}
 \exp\!\left\{-\frac{T^2}{2a_\lambda^2}\right\}.
\]
The construction and bound hold uniformly on every compact cell of positive diagonal variances.  In particular, for every nonempty \(S\subseteq A\),
\[
G_S(v_S,r_S)\leq G_A(v_A,r_A).
\]
