For every fixed face and compact uniformly positive-definite covariance cell, the compact certificate and both primitive transfers are proved.  For Bernoulli outer designs, \((Bp)_k\geq\min_\ell B_{k\ell}>0\), so finitely many facewise compact covariance cells suffice.  A valid pairwise screening calculation is the following: if the true gap of a discarded arm \(j\) from a retained competitor \(k\) is \(d_j>M\), and \(a_{jk}^2=\operatorname{Var}(X_j-X_k)\) is uniformly bounded, then
\[
(M+d_j)\Phi\!\left(-\frac{d_j-M/2}{a_{jk}}\right)
\leq4a_{jk}\varphi\!\left(\frac{M}{2a_{jk}}\right).
\]
Indeed \(d_j-M/2\geq d_j/2\) and \(M+d_j\leq2d_j\).  For \(x>0\), direct integration gives \(\Phi(-x)=\int_x^\infty\varphi(t)dt\leq x^{-1}\int_x^\infty t\varphi(t)dt=\varphi(x)/x\).  This bound and monotonicity of \(\varphi\) on \([0,\infty)\) give the display.  What remains unproved is that a single hierarchical screen controls all simultaneous discard and subset-selection events uniformly over the unbounded quotient parameter space and across changing active faces, and that the resulting selector admits a uniform calibrated-experiment transfer.
