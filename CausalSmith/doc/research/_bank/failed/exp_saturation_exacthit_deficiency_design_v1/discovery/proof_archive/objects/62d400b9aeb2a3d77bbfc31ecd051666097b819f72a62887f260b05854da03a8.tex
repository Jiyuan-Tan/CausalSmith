Let \(\Theta\) and \(\mathcal D\) be nonempty finite sets and let \(d\geq1\) be an integer.  Define the action simplex by \(\Delta_{\mathcal D}=\{v\in[0,1]^{\mathcal D}:\sum_{a\in\mathcal D}v_a=1\}\), and let \(\varpi=(\varpi_\theta)_{\theta\in\Theta}\) be a probability mass function.  For each positive integer \(C\), let \(\mathsf E_C=(\mathsf X_C,\mathcal F_C,\{P_{C,\theta}:\theta\in\Theta\})\) be an experiment whose laws are all dominated by a reference probability measure \(P_{C,0}\) on \((\mathsf X_C,\mathcal F_C)\), and write \(L_{C,\theta}=dP_{C,\theta}/dP_{C,0}\).  Let the limit experiment be a Gaussian-shift experiment on \(\mathbb R^d\), with canonical observation \(G\), laws \(Q_\theta\) all dominated by a reference law \(Q_0\), and likelihood ratios \(L_\theta(G)=dQ_\theta/dQ_0(G)\).  Suppose that: (i) there are measurable statistics \(T_C\colon\mathsf X_C\to\mathbb R^d\) such that, under \(P_{C,0}\),
\[
 \bigl(T_C,(L_{C,\theta})_{\theta\in\Theta}\bigr)
 \ \rightsquigarrow\ 
 \bigl(G,(L_\theta(G))_{\theta\in\Theta}\bigr),
 \qquad G\sim Q_0;
\]
(ii) every likelihood-ratio sequence is uniformly integrable, in the explicit sense that
\[
 \lim_{R\to\infty}\sup_C
 \mathbb E_{P_{C,0}}\!\left[L_{C,\theta}
 \mathbf 1\{L_{C,\theta}>R\}\right]=0
 \quad\text{for every }\theta\in\Theta;
\]
and (iii) nonnegative losses \(\ell_C(\theta,a)\) and \(\ell(\theta,a)\), indexed by \(\Theta\times\mathcal D\), satisfy
\[
 \max_{\theta\in\Theta,\ a\in\mathcal D}
 |\ell_C(\theta,a)-\ell(\theta,a)|\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 \sup_{C,\theta,a}\ell_C(\theta,a)<\infty.
\]
For a measurable randomized rule \(\delta_C\colon\mathsf X_C\to\Delta_{\mathcal D}\), define
\[
 \mathcal R_{C,\theta}(\delta_C)
 =\mathbb E_{P_{C,\theta}}\!\left[
   \sum_{a\in\mathcal D}\delta_{C,a}\ell_C(\theta,a)
 \right],
 \qquad
 \mathcal B_C(\delta_C)=\sum_{\theta\in\Theta}\varpi_\theta
 \mathcal R_{C,\theta}(\delta_C).
\]
Then every sequence \((\delta_C)\) obeys
\[
 \liminf_{C\to\infty}\mathcal B_C(\delta_C)
 \ \geq\ 
 \mathcal B_\infty^*(\varpi)
 :=\inf_{\substack{\delta\colon\mathbb R^d\to\Delta_{\mathcal D}\\
                    \delta\ \mathrm{measurable}}}
 \sum_{\theta\in\Theta}\varpi_\theta
 \mathbb E_{Q_\theta}\!\left[
   \sum_{a\in\mathcal D}\delta_a(G)\ell(\theta,a)
 \right].
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \liminf_{C\to\infty}\max_{\theta\in\Theta}
 \mathcal R_{C,\theta}(\delta_C)
 \ \geq\ \mathcal B_\infty^*(\varpi).
\]
