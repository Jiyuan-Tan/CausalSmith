Let \(R_{\mathrm{full}}\) and \(R_{\mathrm{hit}}\) denote the two minimax risks.  The hit record is a measurable image of the full record, so every hit-rule lifts to a full-rule and
\[
 R_{\mathrm{full}}\leq R_{\mathrm{hit}}. \tag{4}
\]
For each assignment \(z\), write \(F_z\) for the marginal law of \((Y_j(z))_{j=1}^n\) under \(P\).  Because \([0,1]^n\) is standard Borel and \(\mathcal Z_n\) is finite, every collection \((F_z:z\in\mathcal Z_n)\) is realized by the legal product schedule law \(\bigotimes_zF_z\).  Fix known laws \(F_z^0\) off \(\bigcup_k\mathcal S_k\), and let \(\mathcal P_0\) contain the product schedule laws having those nontarget marginals and arbitrary target marginals.  This restriction leaves unrestricted both the possible target vector \(U\) and the target-hit observed laws.

Given any randomized full-data rule \(\delta\), construct a hit-data rule \(\delta^0\) by conditionally drawing every omitted nontarget outcome vector from the known law \(F_{Z_c}^0\), independently across clusters, and applying \(\delta\) to the completed record.  Existence of this parameter-free simulation kernel follows from the standard-Borel condition.  For every \(P\in\mathcal P_0\), the completed record has exactly its full-experiment law, and hence
\[
 \mathbb E_P\mathcal L(U,\delta^0)=\mathbb E_P\mathcal L(U,\delta). \tag{5}
\]
Since \(\mathcal P_0\subseteq\mathcal P\) and its target marginals exhaust the reduced parameter space,
\[
 \sup_P\mathbb E_P\mathcal L(U,\delta)
 \geq\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_0}\mathbb E_P\mathcal L(U,\delta)
 =\sup_P\mathbb E_P\mathcal L(U,\delta^0)
 \geq R_{\mathrm{hit}}. \tag{6}
\]
Taking the infimum over \(\delta\) and combining (4)--(6) proves equality for every bounded target-only loss on an arbitrary standard-Borel action space.

For \(z\in\mathcal S_k\), \(\texttt{lem:exact-hit-factorization}\) supplies the known kernel
\[
 \Pr(L_c=\ell\mid Z_c=z)=p_\ell B_{k\ell}/q_k. \tag{7}
\]
It is parameter-free and well defined because \(q_k>0\).  Thus a label-observing rule can be reproduced after label deletion by simulating (7), while forgetting the label gives the reverse comparison.  This proves label deletion.  A ranking vector is finite and therefore standard Borel, so the Han--Owusu--Shin ranking action is included.
