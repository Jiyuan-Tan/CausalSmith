Let \(A=\operatorname*{arg\,max}_kU_k(P_0)\), with \(|A|\geq2\), and suppose every policy in \(A^c\) is separated below the tied active welfare.  For every representative \(h\in\mathbb R^A\), including its common-shift component, the path \(\bar P_{C,h}=\bar P_{C^{-1/2},h}\) satisfies \(Q^B_{\bar P_0,p}=Q^B_{P_0,p}\), \(dU_k(\bar P_{t,h})/dt|_{t=0}=h_k\), and
\[
\log\frac{d(Q^B_{\bar P_{C,h},p})^{\otimes C}}{d(Q^B_{P_0,p})^{\otimes C}}
=h^{\prime}\Delta_C^B-\frac12h^{\prime}I_Bh+o_{(Q^B_{P_0,p})^{\otimes C}}(1),
\qquad \Delta_C^B\Rightarrow N(0,I_B),
\]
with \(\dot\ell_k^B\in L^2_0(Q^B_{P_0,p})\) and \(I_B=\operatorname{diag}(q_k/\tau_k^2:k\in A)\).  Under \(\bar P_{C,h}\),
\[
\sqrt C\{\widetilde U_{C,A}-U_A(P_0)\}\Rightarrow
N\{h,\Sigma_{B,A}\}.
\]
For every measurable randomized rule sequence \(\delta_C\) and every \(M<\infty\),
\[
\liminf_{C\to\infty}\sup_{h\in\mathbb R^A:\ \lVert[h]\rVert_A\leq M}
\sqrt C\,\mathbb E_{(Q^B_{\bar P_{C,h},p})^{\otimes C}}
\left[\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_{\delta_C}(\bar P_{C,h})\right]
\geq G_{A,M}(\tau_A^2,q_A).
\]
Moreover \(G_{A,M}\uparrow G_A\) as \(M\to\infty\).  The same lower bounds hold for rules observing the full Bernoulli experiment by nuisance deletion.
