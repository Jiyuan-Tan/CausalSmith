For every realized assignment array, only one assignment coordinate of each schedule is observed. Conditional on the fixed-count label vector, \(\texttt{ass:iid-schedules}\), \(\texttt{ass:isolated-clusters}\), \(\texttt{ass:cr-label-schedule-independence}\), and \(\texttt{ass:cr-exact-slices}\) make the observed triangular array independent.

The bounded-support tilt calculation (17)--(20) applies verbatim and proves \(dU_k(\bar P_{t,h})/dt|_{t=0}=h_k\) for every full representative \(h\), including its common-shift coordinate.  The conditional observed score is \(\dot\ell_{c,k}^{CR}\), centered within stratum \(k\).  Disjoint strata and exact-slice uniformity give
\[
\frac1C\sum_{c=1}^C
\mathbb E_0[\dot\ell_{c,j}^{CR}\dot\ell_{c,k}^{CR}\mid L]
=\mathbf1\{j=k\}\frac{C_{C,k}}C
 \frac1{M_k\tau_k^4}\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}
 \operatorname{Var}_{P_0}\{W(z)\}
\longrightarrow\mathbf1\{j=k\}\frac{\alpha_k}{\tau_k^2}. \tag{25}
\]
The limit is positive definite by \(\texttt{ass:cr-shares}\) and \(\texttt{ass:nondegenerate-active-slices}\).

Apply (20) within each fixed stratum.  Its linear term is \(h^{\prime}\Delta_C^{CR}\), (25) gives the quadratic term, and the summed third-order remainder is \(O(C^{-1/2}\max_k|h_k|^3)\) on compact representative sets.  Every normalized score increment is \(O(C^{-1/2})\).  Multiplication of the conditional characteristic functions, using (25), proves
\[
\Delta_C^{CR}\Rightarrow N(0,I_{CR}),\qquad
\log\frac{dQ^{CR}_{\bar P_{C,h},C_C}}{dQ^{CR}_{P_0,C_C}}
=h^{\prime}\Delta_C^{CR}-\frac12h^{\prime}I_{CR}h+o_P(1). \tag{26}
\]
Thus the triangular-array Gaussian limit is derived from bounded increments and stable positive stratum shares.

The asymptotic linear expansion in \(\texttt{prop:efficient-exact-hit-cr-face}\) has cross-moment equal to the identity against the score in (25).  The same joint characteristic-function tilting used in (23)--(24) therefore gives

\Rightarrow N\{h,\Sigma_{CR,A}\}. \tag{27}
\]
