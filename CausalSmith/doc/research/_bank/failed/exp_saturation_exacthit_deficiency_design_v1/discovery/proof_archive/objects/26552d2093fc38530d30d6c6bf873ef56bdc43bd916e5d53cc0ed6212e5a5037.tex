By \(\texttt{def:hit-map}\), \((Bp)_A=s_A(p)a_A(p)\).  Every entry of \(B\) is strictly positive because \(0<\pi_\ell<1\) and \(0<m_k<n\), so \(s_A(p)>0\) and \(a_A(p)\in\Delta_A^\circ\).  The homogeneity in \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-information-order}\) therefore gives
\[
G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)=s_A(p)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p)). \tag{28}
\]
For a fixed nominal label \(\ell\), its active column mass is
\[
\sum_{k\in A}B_{k\ell}
=\Pr\{\operatorname{Bin}(n,\pi_\ell)\in\{m_k:k\in A\}\}. \tag{29}
\]
The event in (29) omits counts zero and \(n\), each of which has positive probability under an interior \(\pi_\ell\).  Consequently
\[
0<s_A(p)\leq s_{A,\max}<1. \tag{30}
\]
Since \(R_{CR,A}^\star\) is the minimum of \(G_A(v_A,a)\) over \(a\in\Delta_A\), equations (28)--(30) imply for every \(p\in\Delta_K\)
\[
G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A). \tag{31}
\]

Both minima are attained.  The image \((B\Delta_K)_A\) is compact and bounded away from every coordinate hyperplane.  The relative-rate modulus in \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-information-order}\) makes \(G_A\) continuous there, so a Bernoulli minimizer exists.  On \(\Delta_A\), the lower-semicontinuous extension attains its minimum.  Its value is finite at the uniform allocation and is \(+\infty\) whenever an action-relevant coordinate vanishes, again by \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-information-order}\); hence every exact-count minimizer is interior.  The positive-rate value is strictly positive, so division by \(R_{CR,A}^\star\) is valid.  Minimizing (31) proves
\[
\frac{R_{B,A}^\star(v_A)}{R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A)}
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}>1. \tag{32}
\]

For equality, let \(p^\star\) be a Bernoulli minimizer.  In
\[
s_A(p^\star)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p^\star))
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p^\star))
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}R_{CR,A}^\star, \tag{33}
\]
equality in (32) forces both nonnegative gaps to vanish.  Strict positivity gives \(s_A(p^\star)=s_{A,\max}\), and the second equality makes \(a_A(p^\star)\) exact-count optimal.  Since \(s_A(p)\) is a convex combination of the column masses in (29), it attains their maximum exactly when \(p\) is supported on maximizing columns.  This proves necessity.  Conversely, a \(p\) with that support and with exact-count-optimal \(a_A(p)\) makes both inequalities in (33) equalities and therefore attains the Bernoulli minimum.  Inactive coordinates never enter the face game, as asserted.
