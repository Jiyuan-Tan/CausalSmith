Fix \(0<\gamma<1\).  On the event that every target assignment cell is occupied, let \(e_{ij}\in\mathbb R^K\) have entries \(1\) and \(-1\) in positions \(i\) and \(j\).  Write
\[
 \widehat s_{ij}^2=e_{ij}'\widehat\Sigma_C^{(K)}e_{ij},
 \qquad
 T_C=\max_{i<j}\frac{\sqrt C\,| (\widetilde U_{C,i}-U_i)-(\widetilde U_{C,j}-U_j)|}{\widehat s_{ij}}.
\]
The max-\(t\) construction uses the conditional \((1-\gamma)\)-quantile \(c_C(\gamma)\) of the same maximum for \(G\sim N(0,\widehat\Sigma_C^{(K)})\), and simultaneously covers every difference when \(T_C\leq c_C(\gamma)\).

The joint CLT and covariance consistency already proved imply \(T_C\Rightarrow T\), where
\[
 T=\max_{i<j}\frac{|e_{ij}'G|}{(e_{ij}'\Sigma_B^{(K)}e_{ij})^{1/2}},
 \qquad G\sim N(0,\Sigma_B^{(K)}).
\]
The denominator is bounded away from zero because \(\tau_i^2\geq\underline\tau^2\) and \(q_i\leq1\).  A maximum of finitely many nondegenerate absolute Gaussian linear forms has a continuous distribution.  Consequently plug-in Gaussian quantiles are valid and
\[
 \liminf_C\Pr_P\{T_C\leq c_C(\gamma)\}\geq1-\gamma. \tag{21}
\]
This assertion is uniform over the displayed class.  Indeed, the conditional outcome laws occupy a finite-dimensional compact simplex because \(n\), \(K\), and the binary outcome spaces are fixed.  If uniformity failed, a violating sequence would have a convergent subsequence.  Bounded characteristic-function expansions prove the triangular CLT along that subsequence, the variance lower bound gives relative covariance consistency, and continuity of the Gaussian maximum gives (21), a contradiction.

On the simultaneous-difference coverage event, a policy \(j\) is counted as certainly above \(k\) only if the lower endpoint for \(U_j-U_k\) is positive.  That certified set is contained in \(\{j:U_j>U_k\}\).  Similarly, the policies certified below \(k\) form a subset of \(\{j:U_j<U_k\}\).  Therefore the inverted lower rank endpoint is no larger than \(r_k^-(U)\), and its upper endpoint is no smaller than \(r_k^+(U)\).  Hence \(\operatorname{Rank}_k(U)\subseteq\mathcal R_{C,k}(\gamma)\) simultaneously for all \(k\) on (21), including every pattern of ties.

Finally, a cell \(z\in\mathcal S_k\) is empty with probability
\[
 (1-q_k/M_k)^C\leq\exp(-Cq_k/M_k).
\]
A union bound over all target cells gives the stated sum.  The full-rank fallback is defined on that event, so it cannot reduce coverage.  Combining this bound with (21) proves the theorem.
