\[
\Pr_p(Z=z)=\frac{(Bp)_k}{M_k}\geq
\frac{\min_{\ell\in\mathcal K}B_{k\ell}}{M_k}>0,

\]
Boundedness, cellwise Chebyshev inequalities, and a finite union bound make pilot cell means, second moments, hit rates, and \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\) uniformly consistent.  Active nondegeneracy and (81) put the quotient covariance in a compact uniformly positive-definite set.

Fix \(\varepsilon\), cover that set by the finite rational terminal cells from \(\texttt{thm:gaussian-finite-certificate}\), and use the recorded boundary convention. uniformly.  Since \(\sqrt C/\sqrt{C-m_C}\to1\), the pilot does not change the limit.  The certificate upper bound and (82) prove the displayed limit superior.
