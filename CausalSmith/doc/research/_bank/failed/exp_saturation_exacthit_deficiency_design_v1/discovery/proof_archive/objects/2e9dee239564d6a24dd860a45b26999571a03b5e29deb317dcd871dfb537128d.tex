Let the pilot size be \(m_C\to\infty\) with \(m_C/C\to0\).  By \(\text{lem:exact-hit-factorization}\), uniformly in \(p\in\Delta_K\),
\[
\Pr_p(Z=z)=\frac{(Bp)_k}{M_k}\geq
\frac{\min_{\ell\in\mathcal K}B_{k\ell}}{M_k}>0,
\qquad z\in\mathcal S_k. \tag{25}
\]
All welfare observations are bounded.  Cellwise Chebyshev inequalities, followed by a finite union bound over the fixed collection of target assignments, show that the pilot cell means, variances, hit rates, and hence \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\), are uniformly consistent over \(p\).  Active-slice nondegeneracy and (25) put the quotient covariance in a compact set with eigenvalues bounded away from zero and infinity.

Fix \(\varepsilon\).  Cover that covariance set by the finite rational terminal cells returned by \(\text{thm:gaussian-finite-certificate}\), with boundary conventions assigning every estimated matrix to one cell.  Conditional on the pilot, the main sample contains \(C-m_C\) independent records and is independent of the selected represented rule.  The bounded triangular-array characteristic-function proof underlying \(\text{thm:efficient-exact-hit-bernoulli}\), and the local mean calculation in \(\text{thm:target-tangent-lan-bernoulli}\), are uniform over \(p\) by (25) and over compact representative sets because all third remainders are bounded.  Since the finite represented selector has only finitely many polyhedral or rectangular boundaries, and a positive-definite Gaussian assigns those boundaries probability zero, conditional convergence gives its Gaussian risk.  The finite number of covariance cells makes the convergence uniform over every selector in the receipt.

Also, uniformly on the stated local set,
\[
\sqrt C\left\{\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_a(\bar P_{C,h})\right\}
=\max_{k\in A}h_k-h_a+o(1), \tag{26}
\]
by the bounded exponential-family Taylor expansion.  Combining conditional convergence, (26), and the certificate's uniform upper bound gives the displayed limit superior.  The factor \(\sqrt C/\sqrt{C-m_C}\to1\), so the vanishing pilot fraction does not change the covariance.

For each integer \(j\), repeat the argument with \(\varepsilon=1/j\) and a rational covariance mesh fine enough for that receipt.  Choose deterministic thresholds \(C_j\) after which the pilot error and main-sample risk error are at most \(1/j\), and let \(j(C)\uparrow\infty\) sufficiently slowly.  Then \(m_C\to\infty\), \(m_C/C\to0\), and
\[
G_{A,M}\leq U_{1/j}\leq L_{1/j}+1/j\leq G_{A,M}+1/j.
\]
This diagonal rule attains \(G_{A,M}(v_A,(Bp)_A)\) uniformly in \(p\) and on the stated compact local set.
