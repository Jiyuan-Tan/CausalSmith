We prove Gaussian certification, outer optimization, and normalized primitive transfer in turn.

\emph{Step 1: unbounded Gaussian certification.}
Fix a terminal covariance cell and write \(d=|A|\).  Compactness and uniform positive definiteness give a computable bound \(\bar\sigma\) on all coordinate standard deviations.  The largest-coordinate rule has, for a best arm \(b\) and gaps \(d_i=h_b-h_i\), risk at most
\[
\sum_{i\ne b}d_i\Phi\!\left(-\frac{d_i}{\sqrt{\sigma_i^2+\sigma_b^2}}\right)
\leq\frac{(d-1)\bar\sigma}{\sqrt\pi}=:H_0. \tag{83}
\]
The Mills bound proved in \(\texttt{lem:hierarchical-gaussian-screening}\) gives the inequality.  Choose rational \(0<\lambda<1\) and rational \(T>0\) so that, for \(a_\lambda=\sqrt{2/\lambda}\bar\sigma\),
\[
\{(1-\lambda)^{-1/2}-1\}H_0\leq\varepsilon/4,\qquad
\frac{2d^2a_\lambda}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-T^2/(2a_\lambda^2)}\leq\varepsilon/4. \tag{84}
\]
These searches terminate as \(\lambda\downarrow0\) and \(T\to\infty\), and directed rational arithmetic certifies them.  For every nonempty \(S\subseteq A\), choose rational
\[
M_S\geq2\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,T\sqrt{|S|-1}. \tag{85}
\]
Use the exact zero value for a singleton; otherwise invoke \(\texttt{thm:gaussian-finite-certificate}\) with error \(\eta\) satisfying \((1-\lambda)^{-1/2}\eta\leq\varepsilon/4\), obtaining \(L_S\leq G_{S,M_S}\leq U_S\) and \(U_S-L_S\leq\eta\).  Define
\[
L^\infty_{\varepsilon,A}=\max_{\varnothing\ne S\subseteq A}L_S,\qquad
U^\infty_{\varepsilon,A}=(1-\lambda)^{-1/2}\max_SU_S
+\frac{2d^2a_\lambda}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-T^2/(2a_\lambda^2)}. \tag{86}
\]
The screening lemma gives the lower and selector upper inequalities.  If \(S_\star\) maximizes \(U_S\), then
\[
\max_SU_S\leq L_{S_\star}+\eta\leq\max_SL_S+\eta.
\]
Together with \(\max_SL_S\leq G_A\leq H_0\), (84)--(86) imply \(U^\infty_{\varepsilon,A}-L^\infty_{\varepsilon,A}<\varepsilon\).  There are only \(2^d-1\) subsets, and the receipt records all split, radius, compact-certificate, tail, and rounding data.  No radial restriction on \(h\) is used.

\emph{Step 2: outer-simplex certification.}
For Bernoulli labels,
\[
(Bp)_k\geq\underline q_k:=\min_{\ell\in\mathcal K}B_{k\ell}>0
\quad(p\in\Delta_K,k\in A). \tag{87}
\]
Partition the explicit constraints \(p_\ell\geq0\), \(\sum_\ell p_\ell=1\) into rational simplex cells.  Linear interval evaluation gives \(q^-\leq Bp\leq q^+\).  By \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-information-order}\), evaluation at \(q^+\) is a cell lower bound, while a feasible rational point supplies an upper incumbent.  If relative coordinate width is at most \(\xi<1\), the unresolved multiplicative variation is bounded by \((1-\xi)^{-1/2}-(1+\xi)^{-1/2}\), which tends uniformly to zero under refinement because of (87).  Branch-and-bound therefore terminates with a width-\(\varepsilon\) value interval, all surviving optimizer cells, and an \(\varepsilon\)-optimal feasible \(p\).

For exact counts, optimize under
\[
a_i\geq0\ (i\in A),\qquad\sum_{i\in A}a_i=1. \tag{88}
\]
A zero active rate has value \(+\infty\) by the information-order lemma.  For positive rates, the subface lower bound and \(\texttt{thm:two-policy-minimax}\) give, for distinct \(i,j\),
\[
G_A(v_A,a)\geq\kappa\sqrt{v_i/a_i+v_j/a_j}\geq\kappa\sqrt{v_i/a_i}. \tag{89}
\]
Let \(\underline v>0\) be the cellwise lower bound and \(\overline U\) a certified finite upper bound at the uniform allocation.  Choose rational
\[
0<\eta_0<\min\left\{\frac1{2d},\frac{\kappa^2\underline v}{4(\overline U+1)^2}\right\}. \tag{90}
\]
If \(a_i<\eta_0\), (89) exceeds \(\overline U\); thus every minimizer lies in the compact polytope (88) with \(a_i\geq\eta_0\).  The positive-cell branch-and-bound above terminates there.  This proves both outer computations without evaluating a positive-rate formula at zero.

\emph{Step 3: normalized primitive transfer.}
Use disjoint prespecified pilot, screening, and decision cluster sets with
\[
m_C+N_C^{\mathrm s}+N_C^{\mathrm d}=C,\quad
m_C\to\infty,\quad m_C/C\to0,\quad
N_C^{\mathrm s}/C\to\lambda,\quad
N_C^{\mathrm d}/C\to1-\lambda. \tag{91}
\]
Under complete randomization, split separately within every active stratum.  The randomization and schedule-independence assumptions give independence of the three outcome arrays conditional on prespecified counts.

Let \(\widetilde U_C^{\mathrm s}\) and \(\widetilde U_C^{\mathrm d}\) be the calibrated estimators formed from the corresponding pieces.  In the Bernoulli regime, every used cell satisfies
\[
\rho_z=q_k/M_k\geq\min_\ell B_{k\ell}/M_k>0,\qquad z\in\mathcal S_k, \tag{92}
\]
while in the certified exact-count interior its limiting frequency is at least \(\eta_0/M_k\).  Expanding each cell ratio, uniformly because the reciprocals in (92) are bounded, gives for \(b\in\{\mathrm s,\mathrm d\}\)
\[
\widetilde U_{C,k}^{b}-U_k
=\frac1{N_C^b}\sum_{c\in b}\frac{\mathbf1\{Z_c\in\mathcal S_k\}}{q_k}
\{W_c(Z_c)-\mu_{Z_c}\}+o_P((N_C^b)^{-1/2}), \tag{93}
\]
with \(q_k\) replaced by the active exact-count share in that regime.  Bounded characteristic-function expansions, equivalently the corresponding compact oracle-transfer results, yield jointly
\[
\sqrt C(\widetilde U_C^{\mathrm s}-U)\Rightarrow N(0,\Sigma/\lambda),\qquad
\sqrt C(\widetilde U_C^{\mathrm d}-U)\Rightarrow N(0,\Sigma/(1-\lambda)), \tag{94}
\]
with independent limits.  The screen uses contrasts of the first statistic, and the compact rule receives
\[
\sqrt{1-\lambda}\,\sqrt C\,\widetilde U_C^{\mathrm d}. \tag{95}
\]
Translation invariance removes the common location.  Under local displacement \(h\), (95) has contrast mean \(\sqrt{1-\lambda}h\) and covariance \(\Sigma\), exactly matching the Gaussian screening construction and its \((1-\lambda)^{-1/2}\) regret rescaling.  Thus both split covariances and the decision normalization are explicit.

Pilot empirical moments are uniformly consistent by boundedness and (92), so outward covariance snapping selects a certified cell or recorded neighbor with probability tending to one.  A binomial lower-tail bound and Hoeffding's inequality conditional on assignments give uniform constants \(c_1,c_2>0\) such that
\[
\Pr\{\sqrt C|\widetilde U_{C,k}^{\mathrm s}-U_k|>x\}
\leq c_1e^{-c_2x^2}+c_1e^{-c_2C},\qquad0\leq x\leq\sqrt C. \tag{96}
\]
The screening lemma's pairwise decomposition then gives a deterministic \(b(T)\downarrow0\) bounding the scaled regret from deleting a best arm or retaining a set with scaled range above \(2T\), up to \(o(1)\).  In particular, coordinates with diverging scaled gaps contribute \(o(1)\).

For uniformity, suppose a violating triangular sequence existed.  The bounded schedule space is compact metric, so \(\texttt{lem:weak-compactness-compact-laws}\) gives a subsequence of schedule laws converging weakly.  Bounded continuous coordinate welfares and their squares make all means and variances converge; pass again so every nonnegative scaled gap converges in \([0,\infty]\).  Infinite-gap coordinates vanish from regret by (96).  On the remaining finite-gap coordinates, (93)--(95) give joint pilot-screen-decision Gaussian convergence.  Finite represented-rule boundaries are Gaussian-null, so the limiting risk is that of the corresponding Gaussian screened selector and is bounded by (86), contradicting the violation.  Thus primitive regret is uniformly bounded by \(U^\infty_{\varepsilon,A}\).

This transfer ranges over retained \(S\subseteq A\).  Taking \(A=\mathcal K\) and using (92) gives global Bernoulli adaptation; exact-count global adaptation also requires all shares in a compact interior.  At a vanishing action-relevant share, only \(+\infty\) is asserted.  Finally, for stage \(j\), use tolerance \(1/j\), choose \(\lambda_j,T_j,\eta_j\) as above, and choose \(C_j\) so all pilot, split-CLT, snapping, and screening errors are at most \(1/j\), with each subsample divergent and pilot fraction at most \(1/j\).  This diagonal schedule combines (86) with the primitive transfer and Step 2, proving the certified noncompact value, design, and implementation.
