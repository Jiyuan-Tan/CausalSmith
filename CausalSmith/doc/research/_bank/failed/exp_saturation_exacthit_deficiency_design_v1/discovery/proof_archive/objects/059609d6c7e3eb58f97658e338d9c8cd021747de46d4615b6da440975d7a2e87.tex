For every tie face \(A\) with \(|A|\geq2\) and positive \(v_A\), define \(s_A(p)=\sum_{k\in A}(Bp)_k\), \(a_A(p)=(Bp)_A/s_A(p)\), and \(s_{A,\max}=\max_\ell\sum_{k\in A}B_{k\ell}\). Then \(G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)=s_A(p)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p))\) and \[\frac{R_{B,A}^\star(v_A)}{R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A)}\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}>1.\] Equality in this lower ratio holds exactly when there exists a Bernoulli optimizer \(p\) supported on columns attaining \(s_{A,\max}\) whose normalized active vector \(a_A(p)\) is exact-count optimal. Inactive coordinates may be zero and are never passed to \(G_A\).
