Let \(\mathscr H_{A,M}\) satisfy the quotient-image identity in the proposed statement.  As in the Bernoulli proof, full row rank of \(D_A\) supplies the explicit compact example \(\{D_A^{\prime}(D_AD_A^{\prime})^{-1}g:\lVert g\rVert_2\leq M\}\), so the condition is consistent.

Let \(\mathcal C\) be the stated compact allocation set.  Continuity of the coordinate maps on \(\mathcal C\) and strict positivity there give
\[
\rho:=\min_{\alpha\in\mathcal C}\min_{k\in A}\alpha_k>0.          \tag{86}
\]
Assumption \(\texttt{ass:cr-shares}\) then implies \(C_{C,k}/C\geq\rho/2\) eventually, uniformly on the corresponding triangular allocation arrays.  Conditional on label \(k\), assumption \(\texttt{ass:cr-exact-slices}\) gives probability \(M_k^{-1}\) to each \(z\in\mathcal S_k\).  Thus every active assignment cell has limiting frequency at least \(\rho/(2M_k)>0\).

Within each active stratum, take a pilot count \(m_{C,k}\to\infty\) with \(m_{C,k}=o(C_{C,k})\).  Conditional on the fixed-count label vector, schedules are independent by \(\texttt{ass:iid-schedules}\) and \(\texttt{ass:cr-label-schedule-independence}\).  The variance of each pilot cell frequency is at most \(1/(4m_{C,k})\), its empty-cell probability is at most \(\exp(-m_{C,k}/M_k)\), and, conditional on the cell count, bounded cell means and second moments have variances at most the reciprocal of four times that count.  Chebyshev's inequality and a finite union bound therefore show that \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\) is uniformly consistent on \(\mathcal C\times\mathscr H_{A,M}\).  The remaining stratum proportions have the same limits because \(m_{C,k}=o(C_{C,k})\).  Equation (86), bounded outcomes, and \(\texttt{ass:nondegenerate-active-slices}\) place the quotient covariance matrices in a compact uniformly positive-definite set.

Conditional on labels and the pilot, the main observations are independent.  The bounded triangular-array expansion in \(\texttt{prop:efficient-exact-hit-cr-face}\) has centered influence coordinates
\[
\phi_{c,k}^{CR}
=\frac{\mathbf1\{L_c=k\}}{\alpha_k}\{W_c(Z_c)-\mu_{Z_c}\},
\]
up to the asymptotically immaterial replacement of \(\alpha_k\) by \(C_{C,k}/C\).  Their limiting variance is \(\tau_k^2/\alpha_k\), and different coordinates have zero products.  The lower bound (86) and boundedness make the sum of the third-order characteristic-function remainders \(O(C^{-1/2})\), uniformly over \(\mathcal C\times\mathscr H_{A,M}\).  Combining this calculation with the local mean expansion in \(\texttt{thm:target-tangent-lan-cr}\) yields
\[
\sqrt C\{\widetilde U_{C,A}-U_A(P_0)\}
\Rightarrow N\{h,\Sigma_{CR,A}\}                                \tag{87}
\]
uniformly on that product set.  The same bounded-tilt Taylor expansion gives, uniformly for \(a\in A\),
\[
\sqrt C\!\left\{\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})
-U_a(\bar P_{C,h})\right\}
=\max_{k\in A}h_k-h_a+o(1).                                     \tag{88}
\]

Snap the pilot estimate outward to the rational partition of \(\texttt{thm:gaussian-finite-certificate}\).  The represented finite-partition selectors have Gaussian-null action boundaries, and the receipt family is finite on the compact covariance and allocation cells.  Conditional weak convergence in (87), pilot independence, (88), and the exponentially small fallback probability therefore transfer each certified Gaussian upper risk uniformly to the primitive exact-count experiment.  This proves the displayed limit-superior bound.  The pilot fraction vanishes in every stratum by construction.

For completeness, the claimed value is the full compact-game value, not a risk restricted to the chosen representatives.  By \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-quotient-reduction}\), the Gaussian risk of every represented selector is constant on common-location cosets and is a measurable function of \(D_Ah\).  The quotient-image identity therefore gives
\[
\sup_{h\in\mathscr H_{A,M}}R_{\alpha,\varepsilon}^G(h)
=\sup_{[g]\in H_A:\,\lVert[g]\rVert_A\leq M}
  R_{\alpha,\varepsilon}^G(g).                                  \tag{89}
\]
The definition of \(G_{A,M}\) and the certificate inequalities squeeze the right side between \(G_{A,M}(v_A,\alpha_A)\) and \(U_\varepsilon(v_A,\alpha_A)\), whose distance from the certified lower bound is at most \(\varepsilon\).  Choosing tolerance \(1/j\), then a common deterministic sample threshold for the uniform pilot, snapping, Gaussian, and causal-loss errors, and finally letting \(j=j(C)\uparrow\infty\) slowly proves the diagonal assertion.

There are finitely many retained subsets \(S\subseteq A\).  For every non-singleton \(S\), repeat (89) with a representative set whose quotient image is the full closed radius-\(M_S\) ball; taking the largest of finitely many errors gives simultaneous transfer.  For a singleton, the quotient and regret are identically zero.  Taking \(A=\mathcal K\) and retaining the lower bound (86) gives the stated global-candidate scope.  If an action-relevant share approaches zero, (86) fails, pilot consistency is no longer uniform, and the covariance leaves every compact positive-definite cell, so no positive-rate transfer has been asserted.
