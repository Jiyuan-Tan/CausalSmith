Fix a face \(A\), a quotient radius \(M<\infty\), and a compact rational cell of positive \((v_A,r_A)\) for which the eigenvalues of \(\Omega_A\) stay in a fixed compact subset of \((0,\infty)\).  For every \(\varepsilon>0\), the deterministic validated calculation in \(\delta_{\varepsilon,A}\) terminates and returns outward-rounded quantities satisfying, throughout every terminal covariance subcell,
\[
L_\varepsilon(v_A,r_A)\leq G_{A,M}(v_A,r_A)\leq U_\varepsilon(v_A,r_A),
\qquad U_\varepsilon(v_A,r_A)-L_\varepsilon(v_A,r_A)\leq\varepsilon.
\]
Every certificate has a complete machine-checkable transcript containing the rational inputs, parameter, observation, and covariance branch cells, working precision, all directed enclosures, the error-budget decomposition, the checker code revision, and a checksum.  Since every entry of \(B\) is positive, finitely many such compact cells cover the Bernoulli outer polytope \((B\Delta_K)_A\); for exact-count designs the same conclusion holds on each compact subset of \(\Delta_A^\circ\), but not uniformly up to its boundary.  For \(n=6\), \(A=\mathcal K\), and \((m_1,m_2,m_3)=(1,2,4)\), exact arithmetic gives
\[
B=\begin{pmatrix}3125/7776&64/243&4/243\\3125/15552&80/243&20/243\\125/15552&20/243&80/243\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its column sums are \(2375/3888\), \(164/243\), and \(104/243\); hence column two is uniquely maximum-mass and its normalized active vector is \((16/41,20/41,5/41)\).  The certified inequalities in this theorem are the compact fixed-face components used by the hierarchical noncompact and outer-simplex computation.
