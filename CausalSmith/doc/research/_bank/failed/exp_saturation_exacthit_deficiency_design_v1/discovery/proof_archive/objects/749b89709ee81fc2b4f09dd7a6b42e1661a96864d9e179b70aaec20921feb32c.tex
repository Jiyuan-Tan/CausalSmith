All quantities are positive by \(\texttt{thm:mechanism-loss}\).  Squaring the assumed leading-regret comparison and rearranging gives
\[
\frac{C_B}{C_{CR}}
\geq\left\{\frac{R_{B,A}^\star}{R_{CR,A}^\star}\right\}^{2}. \tag{34}
\]
Squaring the mechanism bound in that theorem gives the second inequality and preserves its exact equality criterion.  For every \(x\in[0,1]\), convexity of \(x\mapsto x^n\) gives
\[
x^n+(1-x)^n\geq2(1/2)^n=2^{1-n}. \tag{35}
\]
For a column \(\ell\), all active target counts are strictly between zero and \(n\).  Hence (29) and (35), with \(x=\pi_\ell\), imply
\[
\sum_{k\in A}B_{k\ell}
\leq1-\pi_\ell^n-(1-\pi_\ell)^n
\leq1-2^{1-n}. \tag{36}
\]
Taking the maximum over columns gives \(s_{A,\max}\leq1-2^{1-n}\).  Reciprocation is valid because both sides are positive, and combining (34)--(36) proves the result.
