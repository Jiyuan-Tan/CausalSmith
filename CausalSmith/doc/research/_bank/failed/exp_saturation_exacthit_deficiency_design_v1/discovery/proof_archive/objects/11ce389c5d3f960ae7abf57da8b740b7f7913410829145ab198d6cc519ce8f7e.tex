Condition on the fixed-count label vector and restrict attention to \(k\in A\).  For each such \(k\), \(C_{C,k}/C\to\alpha_k>0\), so every active cell count diverges and direct ratio expansion gives
\[
 \sqrt C(\widetilde U_{C,k}-U_k)
 =\frac1{\sqrt C}\sum_{c=1}^C\frac{C}{C_{C,k}}\mathbf1\{L_c=k\}
 \{W_c(Z_c)-\mu_{Z_c}\}+o_P(1). \tag{20}
\]
The active summands are centered and bounded, their cross-products vanish across distinct strata, and their conditional covariance converges to
\[
 \operatorname{diag}(\tau_k^2/\alpha_k:k\in A)=\Sigma_{CR,A}. \tag{21}
\]
The maximum normalized increment is \(O(C^{-1/2})\), so the conditional characteristic-function expansion proves the active Gaussian limit.  Replacing \(\mu_z\) by active cell means and applying the bounded conditional law of large numbers proves \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\to_P\Sigma_{CR,A}\).  The nondegeneracy assumption makes (21) positive definite.  No expression in (20)--(21) involves an inactive stratum, so no result is claimed there.
