Condition on a realized label vector having the fixed counts \(C_{C,k}\).  By schedule independence and exact-slice randomization, the records are conditionally independent and, for \(z\in\mathcal S_k\),
\[
 \Pr(Z_c=z\mid L_c=k)=M_k^{-1},\qquad
 \mathbb E\{W_c(Z_c)\mid L_c=k,Z_c=z\}=\mu_z. \tag{17}
\]
Because \(C_{C,k}/C\to\alpha_k>0\), every cell count in stratum \(k\) diverges in probability and the fallback is negligible.  Expanding the finitely many cell ratios gives
\[
 \sqrt C(\widetilde U_{C,k}-U_k)
 =\frac1{\sqrt C}\sum_{c=1}^C\frac{C}{C_{C,k}}\mathbf1\{L_c=k\}
 \{W_c(Z_c)-\mu_{Z_c}\}+o_P(1)
 =\frac1{\sqrt C}\sum_{c=1}^C\phi_{c,k}^{CR}+o_P(1). \tag{18}
\]
Distinct coordinates have pointwise zero products.  The conditional variance of coordinate \(k\) is
\[
 \frac1C\sum_{c=1}^C\mathbb E\{(\phi_{c,k}^{CR})^2\mid L\}
 =\frac{C}{C_{C,k}}\frac1{M_k}\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}
 \operatorname{Var}_{P_0}\{W(z)\}
 \longrightarrow\frac{\tau_k^2}{\alpha_k}. \tag{19}
\]
After division by \(\sqrt C\), the maximum triangular-array increment is bounded by \(\sqrt C/C_{C,k}=O(C^{-1/2})\).  Taylor expansion of the conditional characteristic functions, with the cubic remainder bounded by this maximum times the total second moment, proves the conditional multivariate Gaussian limit with covariance (19).  The limit does not depend on the label permutation, so it also holds unconditionally.

Replacing each \(\mu_z\) in (18) by its cell mean defines the empirical-score covariance.  Finite-cell conditional laws of large numbers and \(C_{C,k}/C\to\alpha_k\) make every diagonal entry converge to (19); off-diagonal entries are zero.  The fallback probability tends to zero.  Hence \(\widehat\Sigma_C^{(K)}\to_P\Sigma_{CR}^{(K)}\), proving all assertions from the primitive triangular array.
