Let \(\Theta\) and \(\mathsf A\) be nonempty finite sets, let \(\omega=(\omega_\vartheta)_{\vartheta\in\Theta}\) satisfy \(\omega_\vartheta\geq0\) and \(\sum_{\vartheta\in\Theta}\omega_\vartheta=1\), and fix \(\vartheta_0\in\Theta\). Let \(d\geq1\), let \(V\in\mathbb R^{d\times d}\) be positive definite, and let \(g_\vartheta\in\mathbb R^d\), with \(g_{\vartheta_0}=0\). For each integer \(r\geq1\), let \(\{P_{r,\vartheta}:\vartheta\in\Theta\}\) be an experiment on \((\mathsf X_r,\mathcal F_r)\), write \(X_r\) for its canonical observation, suppose \(P_{r,\vartheta}\ll P_{r,\vartheta_0}\), and define \(\Lambda_{r,\vartheta}=dP_{r,\vartheta}/dP_{r,\vartheta_0}\). Suppose that measurable \(T_r:\mathsf X_r\to\mathbb R^d\) satisfy, under \(P_{r,\vartheta_0}\),
\[
 \left(T_r,(\Lambda_{r,\vartheta})_{\vartheta\in\Theta}\right)
 \Rightarrow
 \left(G,(\Lambda_\vartheta(G))_{\vartheta\in\Theta}\right),
 \qquad
 G\sim Q_{\vartheta_0}=N_d(0,V),
\]
where \(Q_\vartheta=N_d(g_\vartheta,V)\) and
\[
 \Lambda_\vartheta(x)=\frac{dQ_\vartheta}{dQ_{\vartheta_0}}(x)
 =\exp\!\left\{g_\vartheta^{\prime}V^{-1}x-
 \frac12g_\vartheta^{\prime}V^{-1}g_\vartheta\right\}.
\]
Suppose also that, for every \(\vartheta\in\Theta\), the likelihood ratios are uniformly integrable under the varying reference laws, meaning
\[
 \lim_{M_0\to\infty}\sup_{r\geq1}
 \mathbb E_{P_{r,\vartheta_0}}\!\left[
   \Lambda_{r,\vartheta}\mathbf 1\{\Lambda_{r,\vartheta}>M_0\}
 \right]=0.
\]
Let \(\ell_r,\ell:\Theta\times\mathsf A\to[0,L]\), where \(0\leq L<\infty\), satisfy
\[
 \max_{\vartheta\in\Theta,\ a\in\mathsf A}
 |\ell_r(\vartheta,a)-\ell(\vartheta,a)|\longrightarrow0.
\]
For a measurable randomized rule \(\delta_r\), a Markov kernel from \((\mathsf X_r,\mathcal F_r)\) to \(\mathsf A\), define
\[
 R_r(\vartheta,\delta_r)
 =\mathbb E_{P_{r,\vartheta}}\!\left[
   \sum_{a\in\mathsf A}\delta_r(a\mid X_r)\ell_r(\vartheta,a)
 \right].
\]
For a measurable randomized rule \(\eta\) from \(\mathbb R^d\) to \(\mathsf A\), define \(R(\vartheta,\eta)\) by the same formula with \(Q_\vartheta\), \(G\), and \(\ell\). Then every sequence \((\delta_r)\) obeys
\[
 \liminf_{r\to\infty}\max_{\vartheta\in\Theta}R_r(\vartheta,\delta_r)
 \ \geq\ 
 \liminf_{r\to\infty}\sum_{\vartheta\in\Theta}
       \omega_\vartheta R_r(\vartheta,\delta_r)
 \ \geq\ 
 \inf_\eta\sum_{\vartheta\in\Theta}
       \omega_\vartheta R(\vartheta,\eta),
\]
where the infimum is over all measurable randomized rules in the Gaussian-shift experiment.
