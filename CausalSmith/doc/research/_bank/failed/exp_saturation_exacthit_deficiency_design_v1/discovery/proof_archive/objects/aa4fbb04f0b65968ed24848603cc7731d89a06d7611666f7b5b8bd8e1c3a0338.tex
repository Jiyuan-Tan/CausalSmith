Fix \(A\), positive \(v_A\), and \(M<\infty\). Under \(\mathsf B(p)\), estimate \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\) on an independent sample split, compute \(\delta_{\varepsilon,A}\), and apply it to the remaining \(\widetilde U_{C,A}\). Uniformly over \(h\in H_A\) with \(\lVert h\rVert\leq M\), its scaled simple regret has limit superior at most \(U_\varepsilon(v_A,(Bp)_A)\); with a vanishing split fraction and \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), it attains the certified Gaussian value.
