For homogeneity, write \(X=h+\operatorname{diag}(v_k/(sr_k))^{1/2}Z\), set \(\widetilde X=\sqrt sX\) and \(\widetilde h=\sqrt sh\), and use the bijection \(\widetilde\delta(\widetilde x)=\delta(\widetilde x/\sqrt s)\).  The transformed experiment has rates \(r_A\), whereas its regret is \(\sqrt s\) times the original regret.  Taking infima and suprema proves
\[
 G_A(v_A,sr_A)=s^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,r_A). \tag{29}
\]
If \(r'_k\geq r_k>0\), add independent coordinatewise Gaussian noise of variance \(v_k/r_k-v_k/r'_k\) to the rate-\(r'_A\) observation.  The resulting law is the rate-\(r_A\) experiment.  Composition with this parameter-free kernel proves
\[
G_A(v_A,r'_A)\leq G_A(v_A,r_A). \tag{30}
\]
Applying (29)--(30) to \((1-\xi)r_A\leq r'_A\leq(1+\xi)r_A\) proves the stated two-sided modulus.

Finiteness follows from the largest-coordinate rule.  If \(i\) is best and \(d_j=h_i-h_j\), then
\[
R(h,\delta_{\max})\leq\sum_{j\ne i}d_j
 \Phi\!\left(-\frac{d_j}{\sqrt{v_i/r_i+v_j/r_j}}\right)
\leq\kappa\sum_{j\ne i}\sqrt{v_i/r_i+v_j/r_j}<\infty. \tag{31}
\]
For positivity, restrict to two arms \(i\ne j\), send all other means to \(-H\), and place equal prior mass on two quotient points having gap \(d\) with opposite signs.  In the quotient experiment, the sign test has error \(\Phi(-d/a_{ij})\), where \(a_{ij}^2=v_i/r_i+v_j/r_j\), and Bayes regret \(d\Phi(-d/a_{ij})\).  Letting \(H\to\infty\) and maximizing at \(d=a_{ij}x\) gives
\[
G_A(v_A,r_A)\geq\kappa a_{ij}>0. \tag{32}
\]
If an action-relevant \(r_i^{(m)}\downarrow0\), choose any other action-relevant \(j\); the right side of (32) diverges.  Thus every positive sequence approaching such a boundary has limit inferior \(+\infty\), and the stipulated lower-semicontinuous boundary value is \(+\infty\).
