Fix \(A\), \(M<\infty\), a compact set of representative local directions whose quotient norms are at most \(M\), and a compact subset of \(\{\alpha\in\Delta_K:\min_{k\in A}\alpha_k>0\}\).  Under \(\mathsf{CR}(\alpha)\), take within each active stratum a prespecified pilot count tending to infinity and equal to \(o(C_{C,k})\), estimate \(\widehat\Sigma_{C,A}\), snap it outward to a prespecified rational covariance partition, compute \(\delta_{\varepsilon,A}\), and apply it to the remaining calibrated estimator.  Uniformly over this compact allocation set and compact local set,
\[
\limsup_{C\to\infty}\left\{\sqrt C\,\mathbb E_{Q^{CR}_{\bar P_{C,h},C_C}}
\left[\max_{k\in A}U_k(\bar P_{C,h})-U_{\delta_{\varepsilon,A}}(\bar P_{C,h})\right]
-U_\varepsilon(v_A,\alpha_A)\right\}\leq0.
\]
A diagonal choice \(\varepsilon_C\downarrow0\) attains \(G_{A,M}(v_A,\alpha_A)\) uniformly on that compact allocation set.  No transfer is asserted as an active share vanishes or the active face changes.
