For every fixed finite \(K\), the routine \(\mathfrak C_K\) yields an arbitrary-\(\varepsilon\) validated certificate in the following extended-value sense.  Fix a declared face \(A\subseteq\mathcal K\) with \(|A|\geq2\) and a compact rational cell of positive \((v_A,r_A)\) on which the quotient covariance \(\Omega_A\) is uniformly positive definite.  A deterministic algorithm enumerates all nonempty subsets \(S\subseteq A\), invokes compact certificates at explicitly computed radii, and returns a represented randomized selector and outward-rounded numbers \(L^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A}\), \(U^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A}\) satisfying throughout every terminal covariance cell
\[
L^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A}
\leq G_A(v_A,r_A)
\leq \sup_{h\in\mathbb R^A}
\mathbb E_h\!\left[\max_{k\in A}h_k-h_{\delta(X)}\right]
\leq U^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A},
\qquad
U^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A}-L^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A}\leq\varepsilon.
\]
For a certified \(0<\lambda<1\) and \(T>0\), the algorithm uses an independent Gaussian split with screening covariance \(\Sigma(v_A,r_A)/\lambda\) and decision covariance \(\Sigma(v_A,r_A)/(1-\lambda)\), retains coordinates within threshold \(T\) of the screening maximum, and feeds \(\sqrt{1-\lambda}\) times the decision observation to the compact selector for the retained subset.  Thus partial-tie limits inside \(A\), such as \((0,-a,-M)\), are handled by a retained lower-dimensional game rather than radial truncation.

The same routine gives certified outer-design computations.  Over \(p\in\Delta_K\), it returns a risk interval of width at most \(\varepsilon\), rational simplex cells containing every minimizer of \(p\mapsto G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)\), and a feasible \(p\) with certified risk within \(\varepsilon\) of the minimum.  Over \(a\in\Delta_A\), it returns the analogous objects for \(a\mapsto G_A(v_A,a)\).  A zero action-relevant coordinate has the proved extended value \(+\infty\); a computable two-arm lower bound excludes a neighborhood of every such boundary before the finite interior calculation.

For primitive implementation, use three disjoint pieces with \(m_C+N_C^{\mathrm s}+N_C^{\mathrm d}=C\): an independent covariance pilot of size \(m_C\to\infty\) with \(m_C/C\to0\), a screening sample of size \(N_C^{\mathrm s}\) with \(N_C^{\mathrm s}/C\to\lambda\), and a decision sample of size \(N_C^{\mathrm d}\) with \(N_C^{\mathrm d}/C\to1-\lambda\).  The calibrated centered statistics have respective limits \(N(0,\Sigma/\lambda)\) and \(N(0,\Sigma/(1-\lambda))\), are asymptotically independent, and the primitive selector feeds \(\sqrt{1-\lambda}\) times the decision statistic to the compact rule.  Uniformly over the Bernoulli outer simplex and over each compact active-interior exact-count allocation set, its asymptotic scaled simple regret is bounded by the certified Gaussian upper value.  This transfer is uniform over the finitely many retained subsets \(S\subseteq A\) and triangular bounded-schedule laws whose calibrated covariance remains in the certified positive-definite cells.  It is not adaptation to candidates outside \(A\); a global all-candidate guarantee takes \(A=\mathcal K\) and requires positive information for every candidate.  Policies separated at a fixed primitive scale are screened with vanishing scaled regret, whereas \(C^{-1/2}\)-separated policies enter the appropriate retained compact game.  At a vanishing action-relevant exact-count share only \(+\infty\) is asserted.  A diagonal choice of tolerances, split sizes, and thresholds transfers the certified noncompact Gaussian value and certified outer design on exactly these domains.
