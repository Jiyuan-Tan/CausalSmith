The homogeneity and coordinatewise information monotonicity are proved in \(\text{lem:gaussian-information-order}\).  Since \((Bp)_A=s_A(p)a_A(p)\) and every entry of \(B\) is positive,
\[
G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)=s_A(p)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p)). \tag{14}
\]
For any nominal label \(\ell\), \(\sum_{k\in A}B_{k\ell}\) is the probability that a \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,\pi_\ell)\) variable belongs to the proper set \(\{m_k:k\in A\}\).  Because \(0<\pi_\ell<1\) and neither zero nor \(n\) is a target count,
\[
0<s_A(p)\leq s_{A,\max}<1. \tag{15}
\]
Moreover \(a_A(p)\in\Delta_A^\circ\).  By the definition of \(R_{CR,A}^\star\), (14)--(15) imply for every \(p\in\Delta_K\)
\[
G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A). \tag{16}
\]

Both optimized values are attained.  For Bernoulli labels, \(p\mapsto(Bp)_A\) has compact image and is bounded away from every active coordinate hyperplane.  The relative-rate modulus in \(\text{lem:gaussian-information-order}\) makes \(G_A\) continuous on that image, so a minimizer exists.  For exact counts, the lower-semicontinuous extension attains its minimum on compact \(\Delta_A\).  Its value is finite at the uniform allocation by the largest-coordinate bound (3), whereas it is \(+\infty\) on the boundary by the zero-rate result.  Hence every exact-count minimizer is in \(\Delta_A^\circ\).  The same two-arm argument gives \(R_{CR,A}^\star>0\), so division is valid.  Taking minima in (16), dividing, and using (15) proves
\[
\frac{R_{B,A}^\star(v_A)}{R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A)}
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}>1. \tag{17}
\]

Suppose equality holds in the weak inequality of (17), and choose a Bernoulli minimizer \(p^\star\).  Both nonnegative gaps in
\[
s_A(p^\star)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p^\star))
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p^\star))
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}R_{CR,A}^\star
\]
must vanish.  Strict positivity of the Gaussian value implies \(s_A(p^\star)=s_{A,\max}\), and the second equality says that \(a_A(p^\star)\) is exact-count optimal.  A convex combination of column masses attains their maximum exactly when it is supported on maximizing columns.  This proves necessity.  Conversely, a \(p\) with that support and an exact-count-optimal normalized active vector turns both inequalities into equalities, so it attains the Bernoulli minimum and gives equality in (17).  No inactive coordinate is supplied to the face game, and therefore an inactive exact-count share may be removed before optimization.
