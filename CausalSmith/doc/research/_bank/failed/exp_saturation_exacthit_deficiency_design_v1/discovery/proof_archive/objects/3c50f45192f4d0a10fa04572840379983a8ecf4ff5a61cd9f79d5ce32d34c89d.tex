For positive \(v_A,r_A\), \(s>0\), and \(r'_A\geq r_A\) coordinatewise,
\[
G_A(v_A,sr_A)=s^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,r_A),\qquad G_A(v_A,r'_A)\leq G_A(v_A,r_A).
\]
If \((1-\xi)r_A\leq r'_A\leq(1+\xi)r_A\) with \(0<\xi<1\), then
\[
(1+\xi)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,r_A)\leq G_A(v_A,r'_A)\leq(1-\xi)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,r_A).
\]
The positive-rate value is finite and strictly positive.  Under the lower-semicontinuous boundary definition, if any action-relevant coordinate \(r_i\) is zero, then \(G_A(v_A,r_A)=+\infty\).
