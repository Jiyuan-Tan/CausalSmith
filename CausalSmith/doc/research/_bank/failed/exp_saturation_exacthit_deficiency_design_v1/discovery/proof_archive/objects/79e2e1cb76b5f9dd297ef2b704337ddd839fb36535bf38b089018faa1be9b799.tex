For every fixed finite \(K\), the routine \(\mathfrak C_K\) gives an arbitrary-\(\varepsilon\) validated certificate in the following extended-value sense.  Fix a declared face \(A\subseteq\mathcal K\) with \(|A|\geq2\) and a compact rational cell of positive \((v_A,r_A)\) on which \(\Omega_A\) is uniformly positive definite.  A deterministic algorithm enumerates every nonempty retained subset \(S\subseteq A\), invokes the compact certificates at explicitly computed radii \(M_S\), and returns a represented randomized selector and outward-rounded numbers satisfying
\[
L^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A}\leq G_A(v_A,r_A)
\leq \sup_{h\in\mathbb R^A}\mathbb E_h\!\left[\max_{k\in A}h_k-h_{\delta(X)}\right]
\leq U^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A},
\qquad U^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A}-L^{\infty}_{\varepsilon,A}\leq\varepsilon.
\]
For every retained non-singleton \(S\), the primitive oracle transfer uses a compact representative set \(\mathscr H_{S,M_S}\subseteq\mathbb R^S\) whose quotient classes cover the full closed quotient radius-\(M_S\) ball; the singleton quotient is trivial and uses its exact zero-risk rule.  The routine makes the independent Gaussian split explicit: the screening observation has covariance \(\Sigma(v_A,r_A)/\lambda\), the decision observation has covariance \(\Sigma(v_A,r_A)/(1-\lambda)\), and the compact selector receives the decision observation multiplied by \(\sqrt{1-\lambda}\).  Coordinates within a fixed threshold of the screening maximum are retained.  Hence partial-tie limits such as \((0,-a,-M)\) enter their retained lower-dimensional game rather than an invalid radial truncation.  Uniformity is over retained subsets of this declared face; global adaptation over every candidate policy requires taking \(A=\mathcal K\) with positive information for every candidate.

The same routine certifies both outer designs.  Over \(p\in\Delta_K\), it returns a risk interval of width at most \(\varepsilon\), rational simplex cells containing every minimizer of \(p\mapsto G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)\), and a feasible \(\varepsilon\)-optimal \(p\).  Over \(a\in\Delta_A\), it returns the analogous objects for \(a\mapsto G_A(v_A,a)\).  A zero action-relevant coordinate has the proved extended value \(+\infty\); a computable two-arm lower bound excludes a whole boundary neighborhood before finite interior branch-and-bound.
