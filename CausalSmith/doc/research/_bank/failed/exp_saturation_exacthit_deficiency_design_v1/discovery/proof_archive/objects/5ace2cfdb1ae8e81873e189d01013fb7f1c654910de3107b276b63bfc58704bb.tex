For every finite \(A\) with \(|A|\geq2\) and every positive \(v_A,r_A\), the all-selector Gaussian value \(G_A(v_A,r_A)\) equals the infimum over location-invariant selectors \(\delta(x+c\mathbf 1_A)=\delta(x)\).  Every such selector factors measurably through \(D_Ax\), and the quotient observation has law \(N(D_Ah,\Omega_A)\).  The same equivalence holds for \(G_{A,M}\).  Moreover \(G_{A,M}\uparrow G_A\) as \(M\to\infty\).
