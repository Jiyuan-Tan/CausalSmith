Let \(H_{c,k}=\mathbf1\{Z_c\in\mathcal S_k\}\) and \(N_k=\sum_cH_{c,k}\).  Since \(N_k/C\to_Pq_k>0\), the fallback is negligible, and ratio expansion gives
\[
 \sqrt C(\widehat U^{\mathrm{raw}}_{C,k}-U_k)
 =\frac1{q_k\sqrt C}\sum_{c=1}^CH_{c,k}\{W_c(Z_c)-U_k\}+o_P(1)
 =C^{-1/2}\sum_{c=1}^C\psi_k(O_c)+o_P(1). \tag{24}
\]
Uniformity of \(Z\) on \(\mathcal S_k\) and total variance give
\[
 \operatorname{Var}\{W(Z)\mid Z\in\mathcal S_k\}
 =\frac1{M_k}\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}\operatorname{Var}_P\{W(z)\}
 +\frac1{M_k}\sum_{z\in\mathcal S_k}(\mu_z-U_k)^2
 =\tau_k^2+b_k^2. \tag{25}
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{Var}(\psi_k)=(\tau_k^2+b_k^2)/q_k\).  Comparing with the efficient bound \(\tau_k^2/q_k\) shows equality exactly when the sum of squares \(b_k^2\) vanishes.

For the witness, the four means average to \(U_1=1/2\).  A Bernoulli welfare bit of mean \(4/5\) or \(1/5\) has variance \(4/25\), so \(\tau_1^2=4/25\), while
\[
 b_1^2=\frac14\{4(3/10)^2\}=\frac9{100},\qquad
 \rho_1^2=\frac4{25}+\frac9{100}=\frac14. \tag{26}
\]
Copying the bit to all unit outcomes under its assignment produces a legal binary schedule, hence also a legal bounded schedule.  The excess before division by \(q_1\) is \(9/100\).
