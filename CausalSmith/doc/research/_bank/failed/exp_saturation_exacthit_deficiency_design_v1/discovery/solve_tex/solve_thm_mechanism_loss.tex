By \(\texttt{lem:gaussian-information-order}\), the Gaussian game is homogeneous and coordinatewise nonincreasing in information.  Since \((Bp)_A=s_A(p)a_A(p)\),
\[
G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)=s_A(p)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p)). \tag{43}
\]
For each label \(\ell\), \(\sum_{k\in A}B_{k\ell}\) is the probability that \(\operatorname{Bin}(n,\pi_\ell)\) belongs to the proper target-count set \(\{m_k:k\in A\}\).  Interior \(\pi_\ell\) and exclusion of counts zero and \(n\) imply
\[
0<s_A(p)\leq s_{A,\max}<1. \tag{44}
\]
Moreover \(a_A(p)\in\Delta_A^\circ\).  The definition of \(R_{CR,A}^\star\) and (43)--(44) give, for every \(p\),
\[
G_A(v_A,(Bp)_A)\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}R_{CR,A}^\star(v_A). \tag{45}
\]

Both minima are attained.  The image \((B\Delta_K)_A\) is compact and bounded away from all active coordinate hyperplanes.  The relative-rate modulus in the information-order lemma makes \(G_A\) continuous there.  On \(\Delta_A\), the lower-semicontinuous extension attains its minimum; it is finite at the uniform allocation and infinite on the boundary, so every exact-count minimizer is in \(\Delta_A^\circ\).  The two-arm lower bound in that lemma makes \(R_{CR,A}^\star>0\).  Taking minima in (45), dividing by this positive value, and using (44) proves the displayed strict ratio.

If equality holds, take a Bernoulli minimizer \(p^\star\).  Both gaps in
\[
s_A(p^\star)^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p^\star))
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}G_A(v_A,a_A(p^\star))
\geq s_{A,\max}^{-1/2}R_{CR,A}^\star
\]
must vanish.  Strict positivity forces \(s_A(p^\star)=s_{A,\max}\), and the second equality makes \(a_A(p^\star)\) exact-count optimal.  A convex combination of column masses reaches their maximum exactly when supported on maximizing columns.  This proves necessity.  Conversely, such support and an exact-count-optimal normalized vector turn both inequalities into equalities, proving sufficiency.  Only active coordinates enter the face game.
