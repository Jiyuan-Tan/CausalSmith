%%% FIELD proof-schur-certificate
Fix a revealed graph \(G\) on the branch \(M_n<n\), and abbreviate
\[
B=B_2(G),\qquad P=P_n,\qquad m_i=m_i(G),\qquad
q_i=q_i(G),\qquad \pi_i=\pi_i(G),\qquad k=k_n.
\]
Let
\[
S_i=\{v\in V_n:\operatorname{dist}_G(i,v)\le 2\}.
\]
The row support of \(B\) is \(S_i\), so \(|S_i|=m_i\).  In a simple
\(d_n\)-regular graph, the vertices at distances zero, one, and two from
\(i\) number at most
\[
1+d_n+d_n(d_n-1)=d_n^2+1=M_n.
\]
Consequently,
\[
q_i=1-\frac{m_i}{n}\ge 1-\frac{M_n}{n}>0,
\qquad
\pi_i=q_i^k>0.
\tag{1}
\]
This proves all positivity needed below.

Introduce seed-augmented estimators
\[
U_1=\frac1k\sum_{\ell=1}^k a_{J_\ell},
\qquad
U_0=\frac1n\sum_{i\in V_n}
\frac{\mathbf 1\{i\in C_G(Z^{\mathrm{seed}})\}b_i}{\pi_i}.
\tag{2}
\]
Every seed \(J_\ell\) is fully treated because the seed-design definition
includes \(N_G[J_\ell]\) in the treated union.  By ANI in
Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}outcome\mbox{-}class}\), its observed outcome is
therefore \(a_{J_\ell}\).  A unit \(i\) is fully control-exposed exactly when
no seed lies in \(S_i\): indeed, \(N_G[J_\ell]\) intersects \(N_G[i]\) if
and only if \(\operatorname{dist}_G(i,J_\ell)\le2\).  Independence and
uniformity of the \(k\) seed draws, together with (1), give
\[
\Pr\{i\in C_G(Z^{\mathrm{seed}})\mid G\}=q_i^k=\pi_i.
\tag{3}
\]
ANI then gives \(y_i(Z^{\mathrm{seed}})=b_i\) on this event.  Hence
\[
\mathbb E(U_1\mid G)=\frac1n\sum_i a_i=\mu_1,
\qquad
\mathbb E(U_0\mid G)=\frac1n\sum_i b_i=\mu_0.
\tag{4}
\]
The treated endpoint second-moment restriction further yields
\[
\operatorname{Var}(U_1\mid G)
=\frac1k\operatorname{Var}(a_{J_1}\mid G)
\le \frac1{kn}\sum_i a_i^2
\le \frac1k.
\tag{5}
\]

For \(i,j\in V_n\), put
\[
h_{ij}=|S_i\cap S_j|=(BB^{\mathsf T})_{ij}.
\]
A single uniform seed avoids \(S_i\cup S_j\) with probability
\(1-(m_i+m_j-h_{ij})/n\).  Thus independence of the seed draws gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\Pr\{i,j\in C_G(Z^{\mathrm{seed}})\mid G\}}{\pi_i\pi_j}
&=\left\{\frac{1-(m_i+m_j-h_{ij})/n}{q_iq_j}\right\}^{k}\\
&=\left\{1+\frac{h_{ij}-m_im_j/n}{nq_iq_j}\right\}^{k}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Because \(B\iota_n=(m_i:i\in V_n)^{\mathsf T}\), the definition
\(P=I_n-\iota_n\iota_n^{\mathsf T}/n\) implies
\[
(BPB^{\mathsf T})_{ij}=h_{ij}-\frac{m_im_j}{n}.
\]
The term added to one in (6) is therefore \(K_{G,ij}\).  Subtracting one
and applying the scalar binomial identity entrywise proves the exact formula
\[
Q_G=\sum_{r=1}^{k}\binom{k}{r}K_G^{\circ r}.
\tag{7}
\]

The matrix \(K_G\) is positive semidefinite, since
\[
K_G=\frac1n(D_q^{-1}BP)(D_q^{-1}BP)^{\mathsf T}.
\tag{8}
\]
If \(H\) and \(L\) are positive semidefinite Gram matrices, say
\(H_{ij}=\langle u_i,u_j\rangle\) and
\(L_{ij}=\langle v_i,v_j\rangle\), then
\[
(H\circ L)_{ij}
=\langle u_i\otimes v_i,u_j\otimes v_j\rangle.
\]
Thus \(H\circ L\) is positive semidefinite.  Iterating this elementary
Gram argument shows \(K_G^{\circ r}\succeq0\) for every \(r\ge1\), and
(7) proves \(Q_G\succeq0\).

We next obtain the stated deterministic certificate.  For any positive
semidefinite matrix \(K\), any symmetric matrix \(X\), and any
\(a\ge\max_iK_{ii}\), the inequalities
\(-\|X\|_{\mathrm{op}}I\preceq X\preceq\|X\|_{\mathrm{op}}I\), the
preceding Schur-product Gram argument, and
\(K\circ I=\operatorname{diag}(K_{ii})\preceq aI\) imply
\[
\|K\circ X\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\le a\|X\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
\tag{9}
\]
Here direct calculation and (1) give
\[
K_{G,ii}
=\frac{m_i-m_i^2/n}{nq_i^2}
=\frac{m_i}{n-m_i}
\le a_0:=\frac{M_n}{n-M_n},
\tag{10}
\]
and
\[
\|K_G\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\le\frac{\|BP\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2}
{n(1-M_n/n)^2},
\qquad
\frac{\|K_G\|_{\mathrm{op}}}{a_0}
\le\frac{\|BP\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2}
{M_n(1-M_n/n)}.
\tag{11}
\]
Applying (9) inductively to the powers in (7), and then using (10)--(11),
yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|Q_G\|_{\mathrm{op}}
&\le \|K_G\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\sum_{r=1}^{k}\binom{k}{r}a_0^{r-1}\\
&=\frac{\|K_G\|_{\mathrm{op}}}{a_0}
\{(1+a_0)^k-1\}\\
&\le
\frac{\|B_2(G)P_n\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2}
{M_n(1-M_n/n)}
\{(1-M_n/n)^{-k}-1\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]

By (6), \(Q_G\) is exactly the covariance matrix of the normalized control
exposure indicators.  The control endpoint second-moment restriction and
(12) therefore imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(U_0\mid G)
&=\frac1{n^2}b^{\mathsf T}Q_Gb\\
&\le\frac{\|Q_G\|_{\mathrm{op}}}{n^2}\|b\|_2^2
\le\frac{\|Q_G\|_{\mathrm{op}}}{n}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
By (4), \(U_1-U_0\) is unbiased for \(\tau_n\).  Combining (5), (12),
(13), and \((x-y)^2\le2x^2+2y^2\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E[(U_1-U_0-\tau_n)^2\mid G]
&\le \frac2k+
\frac{2\|B_2(G)P_n\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2
\{(1-M_n/n)^{-k}-1\}}
{nM_n(1-M_n/n)}\\
&=U(G,k_n).
\end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]

It remains to pass from the seed-augmented estimator to the prescribed
observed-data estimator.  Let
\(\mathcal F=\sigma(G,Z^{\mathrm{seed}},Y^{\mathrm{obs}})\).  The schedule
is fixed, so \(Y^{\mathrm{obs}}\) is a deterministic function of
\((G,Z^{\mathrm{seed}})\) once the schedule is fixed.  Moreover, whenever
\(J_\ell=i\), ANI and the treated union imply
\(y_i(Z^{\mathrm{seed}})=a_i\).  The control term in (2) is already
\(\mathcal F\)-measurable.  The definition of \(w_i\) consequently gives
\[
\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{seed}}
=\mathbb E[U_1-U_0\mid\mathcal F].
\tag{15}
\]
The tower property applied to (15) proves unbiasedness for \(\tau_n\), and
conditional Jensen applied to the square shows that its conditional MSE is
at most (14).

Finally, \(B\), \(m_i\), \(h_{ij}\), \(q_i\), \(\pi_i\), \(K_G\), and
\(Q_G\) are obtained by finitely many matrix and rational operations from
\(G\) and \(k\).  For every supported assignment \(z\), the denominator in
the conditional definition of \(w_i(z;G)\) is positive, and enumerating the
finite set of ordered seed tuples inducing \(z\) computes that weight.
The operator norm in \(U(G,k_n)\) is the square root of the largest
eigenvalue of the rational positive semidefinite matrix
\(BPB^{\mathsf T}\), so it has a certified finite algebraic computation.
This proves all assertions.

%%% FIELD proof-finite-compiler
Use the permitted fixed choice \(c_0=(\log 2)/4\in(0,1)\) in the
seed-design construction.
The initial test compares the integers \(M_n=d_n^2+1\) and \(n\), and hence
uses only the revealed graph and declared design inputs.

Suppose first that \(M_n\ge n\).  By
Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}certified\mbox{-}procedure}\), the compiler forms
none of the inverse-probability objects and deterministically assigns
\(Z=\mathbf 1_n\).  Its report is \(\mu_1\), so its error for
\(\tau_n=\mu_1-\mu_0\) is \(\mu_0\).  Cauchy--Schwarz and the control
endpoint restriction in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}outcome\mbox{-}class}\)
give
\[
\mu_0^2
=\left(\frac1n\sum_i b_i\right)^2
\le\frac1n\sum_i b_i^2
\le1=\overline U(G).
\tag{16}
\]
In particular, this branch covers the smallest admissible regular example
\((n,d_n)=(4,3)\), and no division by a zero exposure probability occurs.

Now suppose \(M_n<n\).  The radius-two counting argument in the proof of
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}schur\mbox{-}certificate}\) gives
\[
q_i(G)\ge1-M_n/n>0,
\qquad
\pi_i(G)=q_i(G)^{k_n}>0
\tag{17}
\]
for every unit.  Put \(x=M_n/n\) and
\(D=(1-x)^{-1}=n/(n-M_n)>1\).  With the chosen \(c_0\), the two quantities
whose floors define the candidate counts have the common form
\[
\frac{\log A}{4\log D},
\qquad A\in\{2,d_n+1\}.
\]
Since the logarithm is strictly increasing, for every nonnegative integer
\(t\),
\[
t\le\frac{\log A}{4\log D}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
D^{4t}\le A.
\tag{18}
\]
Both sides of the last comparison are rational.  Consequently, integer
search using (18), followed by the displayed maximum with one, computes
both candidate counts exactly, including every equality boundary.  There
are exactly \(n^{k_n}\) ordered seed tuples.  Enumerating them, mapping each
tuple to its treated-union assignment, and grouping equal assignments
computes the mass of every supported assignment and the finite conditional
ratios \(w_i(z;G)\).
The same finite graph operations compute \(m_i\), \(h_{ij}\), \(q_i\),
\(\pi_i\), \(K_G\), and \(Q_G\); all their entries are rational.

For completeness, the score comparison and spectral certification can also
be made exact.  Since \(B\) is symmetric and \(P^2=P=P^{\mathsf T}\),
\[
\|BP\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2
=\lambda_{\max}(BPB^{\mathsf T}).
\tag{19}
\]
The matrix on the right of (19) is rational and positive semidefinite.  For
a rational symmetric matrix \(H\), exact symmetric Gaussian elimination
with simultaneous row and column operations reduces \(H\) by rational
congruences to rational one-by-one and two-by-two blocks.  The signs of
those blocks decide \(H\succeq0\) using finitely many rational operations.
It follows that, for rational \(t\), the relation
\(\lambda_{\max}(BPB^{\mathsf T})\le t\) is decidable by applying this test
to \(tI_n-BPB^{\mathsf T}\); equality is detected by its rational
determinant.  Each candidate score is an affine expression in the common
number \(\lambda_{\max}(BPB^{\mathsf T})\), with rational coefficients.
The preceding comparisons therefore select the smaller score exactly,
including the prescribed smaller-index tie rule, and also decide the branch
test \(U(G,k_n)\le1\).

The claimed eigenvalue enclosure for \(Q_G\) is equally finite.  If
\(Q_Gv=\lambda v\) and \(|v_s|=\max_j|v_j|\), then
\[
|\lambda|\le |Q_{G,ss}|+\sum_{j\ne s}|Q_{G,sj}|
\le L_Q:=\max_i\sum_j|Q_{G,ij}|.
\]
Thus \([0,L_Q]\) is already a certified rational enclosure because
\(Q_G\succeq0\).  If a tighter enclosure is requested, rational bisection
and the exact test \(tI_n-Q_G\succeq0\) refine it to any prescribed finite
accuracy.  Thus all steps terminate after finitely many exact operations.

If \(U(G,k_n)\le1\),
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}schur\mbox{-}certificate}\) bounds the worst-case MSE
of the returned seed estimator by
\(U(G,k_n)=\overline U(G)\).  If \(U(G,k_n)>1\), the compiler returns the
all-treated estimator, and (16) bounds its worst-case MSE by
\(1=\overline U(G)\).  Together with the first branch, this proves the
finite-graph risk certificate in every case.

Finally, on the event named in
Open-ended Question~\(\mathrm{oeq{:}boolean\mbox{-}operator}\), namely
\[
U(G_n,k_n)\le C_Ur(n,d_n),
\]
the selected-certificate definition gives directly
\[
\overline U(G_n)
\le U(G_n,k_n)
\le C_Ur(n,d_n).
\tag{20}
\]
This last implication is conditional on the displayed event and does not
assert the unresolved high-probability Boolean-operator estimate.

%%% FIELD proof-finite-honest-confidence
Fix a finite revealed graph \(G\), a schedule
\(y\in\mathcal M_n(G)\), and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\).  If the certified
procedure selects the seed branch, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}schur\mbox{-}certificate}\)
gives
\[
\mathbb E_{\mathcal D_G,y}
\left[(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{cert}}-\tau_n)^2\mid G\right]
\le U(G,k_n)=\overline U(G).
\tag{21}
\]
On either fallback branch, the report is \(\mu_1\), so its error for
\(\tau_n=\mu_1-\mu_0\) equals \(\mu_0\).  By Cauchy--Schwarz and the
control endpoint restriction,
\[
(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{cert}}-\tau_n)^2
=\mu_0^2
\le\frac1n\sum_i b_i^2
\le1=\overline U(G).
\tag{22}
\]
Thus (21) holds for the selected procedure on every branch.  Moreover,
\(\overline U(G)>0\): it equals one on a fallback branch, while on the seed
branch \(U(G,k_n)\ge2/k_n>0\).  Since also \(\alpha>0\), Markov's
inequality applied conditionally on \(G\) to the nonnegative squared error
in (21) yields
\[
\Pr_{\mathcal D_G}\!\left\{
|\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{cert}}-\tau_n|
>\sqrt{\overline U(G)/\alpha}
\,\middle|\,G\right\}
\le\alpha.
\tag{23}
\]

The two endpoint restrictions and Cauchy--Schwarz also give
\[
|\mu_1|\le
\left(\frac1n\sum_i a_i^2\right)^{1/2}\le1,
\qquad
|\mu_0|\le
\left(\frac1n\sum_i b_i^2\right)^{1/2}\le1.
\tag{24}
\]
Hence \(\tau_n=\mu_1-\mu_0\in[-2,2]\).  On the complement of the event
in (23), \(\tau_n\) lies in the untruncated interval centered at
\(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{cert}}\) with radius
\(\sqrt{\overline U(G)/\alpha}\), and (24) places it in \([-2,2]\).
It therefore lies in their intersection
\(\mathcal C_\alpha(G,Z,Y^{\mathrm{obs}})\).  Taking probabilities and
using (23) proves
\[
\Pr_{\mathcal D_G}\!\left\{
\tau_n\in\mathcal C_\alpha(G,Z,Y^{\mathrm{obs}})\mid G
\right\}\ge1-\alpha.
\]
