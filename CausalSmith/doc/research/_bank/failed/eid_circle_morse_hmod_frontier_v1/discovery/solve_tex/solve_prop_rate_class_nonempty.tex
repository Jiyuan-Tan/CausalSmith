Fix \(n\geq 2\), \(k\geq 2\), and \(1\leq m\leq k-1\), as required by Assumptions \(\mathrm{ass:dimension}\), \(\mathrm{ass:smoothness\text{-}order}\), and \(\mathrm{ass:critical\text{-}order}\).  Use the fixed amplitudes \(0<b_1<\cdots<b_n<1\) from Definition \(\mathrm{def:bounded\text{-}rate\text{-}class\text{-}example}\), and put
\[
 I=(1/4,1/3),\qquad B_m=\lVert\gamma_m\rVert_\infty,
 \qquad Z_i=\int_{\mathbb T}\exp\{b_i\gamma_m(t)\}\,d\lambda(t).
\]
Thus \(I\) is a nonempty interval containing no zero.  Since \(|b_i\gamma_m|\leq B_m\) and \(\lambda(\mathbb T)=1\),
\[
 e^{-B_m}\leq Z_i\leq e^{B_m},
 \qquad e^{-2B_m}\leq q_i^1(t)=Z_i^{-1}e^{b_i\gamma_m(t)}\leq e^{2B_m}.
\]
Moreover, \(q_i^1\) is positive, \(2\pi\)-periodic, and normalized relative to \(\lambda\); the same assertions hold for \(q_i^0=1\).  With the empty DAG and \(p_i=1\), Assumptions \(\mathrm{ass:causal\text{-}factorization}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:perfect\text{-}intervention\text{-}family}\) give, respectively,
\[
 p_{\theta_\delta}^{\mathrm{obs}}(x)=1,
 \qquad
 p_{\theta_\delta}^{i,0}(x)=1,
 \qquad
 p_{\theta_\delta}^{i,1}(x)=q_i^1(x_i).
\]
Consequently all complete densities are normalized and, on taking
\[
 c_0=e^{-2B_m},\qquad C_0=e^{2B_m},
\]
they obey Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:density\text{-}bounds}\), uniformly in \(i\) and \(\delta\).  The environment family contains the observational law and the two labelled interventions for every node, so it also satisfies Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:complete\text{-}grouping}\).

We next verify the critical geometry.  Since \(q_i^0=1\),
\[
 r_{\theta_\delta i}(t)=b_i\gamma_m(t)-\log Z_i,
 \qquad
 r_{\theta_\delta i}'(t)=
 \begin{cases}
 b_i\sin t,&m=1,\\
 b_i\sin^m t\cos t,&m\geq2.
 \end{cases}
\]
It follows that
\[
 Z_{\theta_\delta i}=
 \begin{cases}
 \{0,\pi\}\pmod {2\pi},&m=1,\\
 \{0,\pi/2,\pi,3\pi/2\}\pmod {2\pi},&m\geq2.
 \end{cases}
\]
Hence the choice \(J=4\) proves Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:critical\text{-}cardinality}\), and the choice \(\Delta_0=\pi/2\) proves Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:critical\text{-}separation}\).

For the finite-type bound, let \(d=\operatorname{dist}_{\mathbb T}(t,Z_{\theta_\delta i})\).  If \(m=1\), then \(0\leq d\leq\pi/2\) and the elementary chord bound \(\sin d\geq 2d/\pi\) yields
\[
 |r_{\theta_\delta i}'(t)|=b_i\sin d\geq b_1(2/\pi)d.
\]
Suppose \(m\geq2\).  Then \(0\leq d\leq\pi/4\).  If a nearest critical point is a zero of \(\sin t\), then
\[
 |\sin t|=\sin d\geq 2d/\pi,
 \qquad |\cos t|=\cos d\geq 2^{-1/2},
\]
and therefore
\[
 |\sin^m t\cos t|\geq (2/\pi)^m2^{-1/2}d^m.
\]
If a nearest critical point is a zero of \(\cos t\), then
\[
 |\cos t|=\sin d\geq 2d/\pi,
 \qquad |\sin t|=\cos d\geq2^{-1/2}.
\]
Because \(d\leq\pi/4<1\), one has \(d\geq d^m\), and hence
\[
 |\sin^m t\cos t|\geq 2^{-m/2}(2/\pi)d^m.
\]
Define
\[
 a_{\mathrm{prof}}=
 \begin{cases}
 b_1(2/\pi),&m=1,\\
 b_1\min\{(2/\pi)^m2^{-1/2},\,2^{-m/2}(2/\pi)\},&m\geq2.
 \end{cases}
\]
The preceding cases prove
\[
 |r_{\theta_\delta i}'(t)|\geq a_{\mathrm{prof}}
 \operatorname{dist}_{\mathbb T}(t,Z_{\theta_\delta i})^m
\]
for every node, point, and \(\delta\in I\).

The order \(m\) is attained at \(z_i=0\).  Indeed, with
\(\rho=\min\{\Delta_0/4,1\}=\pi/8\), the inequalities
\(|\sin u|\geq(2/\pi)|u|\), \(|\sin u|\leq |u|\), and
\(\cos u\geq\cos(\pi/8)>0\), valid for \(|u|\leq\rho\), give
\[
 a_{\mathrm{att}}|u|^m
 \leq |r_{\theta_\delta i}'(u)|
 \leq |u|^m,
\]
where
\[
 a_{\mathrm{att}}=
 \begin{cases}
 b_1(2/\pi),&m=1,\\
 b_1(2/\pi)^m\cos(\pi/8),&m\geq2.
 \end{cases}
\]
Thus, after setting \(a_0=\min\{a_{\mathrm{prof}},a_{\mathrm{att}}\}>0\), Assumptions \(\mathrm{ass:finite\text{-}type\text{-}profile}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:finite\text{-}type\text{-}attainment}\) both hold with the same margin.

It remains to choose the common regularity constant.  Every \(q_i^1\) is \(C^\infty\), and there are finitely many nodes, so
\[
 M_p=\max_{a\in\mathcal A}\lVert p_{\theta_\delta}^a\rVert_{C^k(\mathbb T^n)}<\infty;
\]
this quantity does not depend on \(\delta\).  For \(\delta\in I\), the displayed inverse in Definition \(\mathrm{def:bounded\text{-}rate\text{-}class\text{-}example}\) verifies directly that \(f_\delta\) is a diffeomorphism, and
\[
 Df_\delta(x)=I_n+\delta\cos x_2\,e_1e_2^{\mathsf T},
 \qquad
 Df_\delta^{-1}(y)=I_n-\delta\cos y_2\,e_1e_2^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Since \(e_1e_2^{\mathsf T}\) is nilpotent and has operator norm one,
\[
 \det Df_\delta=1,
 \qquad
 \lVert Df_\delta\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\leq4/3,
 \qquad
 \lVert Df_\delta^{-1}\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\leq4/3.
\]
The last two inequalities imply that every singular value of \(Df_\delta\) lies in \([3/4,4/3]\).  Also, compactness of \([1/4,1/3]\) and the explicit smooth formulas imply
\[
 M_f=\sup_{\delta\in[1/4,1/3]}
 \bigl(\lVert f_\delta\rVert_{C^k}+\lVert f_\delta^{-1}\rVert_{C^k}\bigr)<\infty.
\]
Choose any
\[
 K\geq\max\{1,M_p,M_f,4/3\}.
\]
This proves Assumptions \(\mathrm{ass:density\text{-}smoothness}\), \(\mathrm{ass:decoder\text{-}smoothness}\), and \(\mathrm{ass:decoder\text{-}conditioning}\); it also supplies the upper constant in Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:finite\text{-}type\text{-}attainment}\).

Finally, \(\det Df_\delta=1\) proves volume preservation.  Each point has exactly one preimage under the displayed inverse and the Jacobian determinant is positive, so the mapping degree is one.  Since \(\delta\neq0\) on \(I\) and \(\partial_{x_2}^2(f_\delta)_1=-\delta\sin x_2\) is not identically zero, every such decoder is nonlinear.  The derivative bounds for \(f_\delta\) and its inverse prove uniform bi-Lipschitzness, while the definition of \(M_f\) proves the asserted uniform \(C^k\) bounds.  We have verified every member condition in Definition \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\), so every \(\theta_\delta\), \(\delta\in I\), belongs to \(\mathcal M_{n,k,m}(c_0,C_0,K,a_0,J,\Delta_0)\), as claimed.
