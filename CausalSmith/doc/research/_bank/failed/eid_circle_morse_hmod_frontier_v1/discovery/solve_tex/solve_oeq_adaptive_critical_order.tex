%%% FIELD oeq-proposed-statement
Characterize the smallest separation from lower-order or plateau profiles under which one procedure is honest over \(m\in\{1,\ldots,k-1\}\) and attains the corresponding fixed-order representing-model diameter. Under the currently proved full-class modulus this diameter is \(\rho_N^{k/((k+n/2)(m+1))}\); the sharper diameter \(\rho_N^{(k+1)/(k+m+1)}\) additionally requires an affirmative resolution of \(\mathrm{oeq:full\text{-}incompressible\text{-}rigidity}\). Prove a matching impossibility result below the separation boundary.

%%% FIELD adaptive-partial-result
For each fixed known \(m\), Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:quotient\text{-}confidence\text{-}set}\) gives an honest quotient-valued set with representing-model diameter of order
\[
\rho_N^{k/((k+n/2)(m+1))}.
\]
The sharper order
\[
\rho_N^{(k+1)/(k+m+1)}
\]
would follow only after an affirmative resolution of \(\mathrm{oeq:full\text{-}incompressible\text{-}rigidity}\). No distance to the union of lower-order classes or to the closure of plateau profiles is defined, so neither an adaptive testing rule nor a matching below-boundary impossibility theorem is determined by the frozen construction.
