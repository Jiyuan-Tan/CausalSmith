Can the handle \(\mathfrak H^{\mathrm{low}}\) exhibit constants \(\eta\in(0,1-2\alpha)\), \(\varepsilon_R\in(0,\min\{R_f,R_q\})\), and \(c_{\mathrm{sep}}>0\), and construct \(P_{0,n},P_{1,n}\in\mathcal M\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), with fixed \(p_x\), fixed positive \(f_x\), and fixed nonidentical asymmetric laws of \(U_1,U_2\) shared across \(j\in\{0,1\}\) and across \(n\), such that only the Bernoulli conditional means \(b_{j,n,x}\) differ; \(1/4\le b_{j,n,x}(a)\le3/4\) for every \(a\in\mathcal A\); \(\sup_x\|f_x\|_{H^{\beta}}\le R_f-\varepsilon_R\) and \(\max_{j\in\{0,1\}}\sup_x\|f_xb_{j,n,x}\|_{H^{\beta}}\le R_q-\varepsilon_R\); \(\operatorname{TV}(((P_{0,n})_{\mathrm{obs}})^{\otimes n},((P_{1,n})_{\mathrm{obs}})^{\otimes n})\le\eta\); and \(\|\mu_{1,n}-\mu_{0,n}\|_{L^{\infty}(I)}\ge c_{\mathrm{sep}}K_n^{1/2-\beta}\)?

The moment condition itself supplies the protected neighborhood that was formerly assumed. Let \(Q\) be either error law and let \(\phi_Q(t)=\int e^{itu}\,dQ(u)\). Since \(q_e>1\), convexity of \(v\mapsto |v|^{q_e}\), the fact that \(Q\) has total mass one, and the error-moment bound give
\[
 \int |u|\,dQ(u)
 \le \left(\int |u|^{q_e}\,dQ(u)\right)^{1/q_e}
 \le M_e^{1/q_e}.
\]
The inequalities \(|1-e^{iv}|\le |v|\) and \(|z|\ge 1-|1-z|\) therefore imply, for every real \(t\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |\phi_Q(t)|
 &\ge 1-|1-\phi_Q(t)|\\
 &\ge 1-\int |1-e^{itu}|\,dQ(u)\\
 &\ge 1-|t|M_e^{1/q_e}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Consequently, with
\[
 \nu_*=(2M_e^{1/q_e})^{-1},
 \qquad c_*=\frac12,
\]
one has \(\inf_{|t|\le\nu_*}|\phi_Q(t)|\ge c_*\), uniformly over the enlarged moment class. Thus no separate spectral-modulus restriction is needed.

Two necessary compatibility conditions for the requested fixed density remain. The support and denominator conditions imply
\[
 1=\int_{\mathcal A}f_x(a)\,da
 \ge \int_I f_x(a)\,da
 \ge \kappa |I|.
\]
Moreover, since \(|\mathcal A|=2B_A\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 1=\int_{\mathcal A}f_x(a)\,da
 \le (2B_A)^{1/2}\|f_x\|_2,
\]
and hence
\[
 R_f\ge \|f_x\|_{H^\beta}\ge \|f_x\|_2
 \ge (2B_A)^{-1/2}.
\]
The displayed ranges of the quantitative constants do not themselves impose either \(\kappa|I|\le1\) or \(R_f\ge(2B_A)^{-1/2}\); when either inequality fails, the requested fixed-density subexperiment cannot exist.

Conditional on compatible strict-interior fixed nuisances, moment annihilation gives analytic attenuation. Put \(h_n=f(b_{1,n}-b_{0,n})\), and suppose that
\[
 \int_{\mathcal A}a^k h_n(a)\,da=0,
 \qquad 0\le k<K_n.
\]
For \(|u|\le\Omega\), Taylor's formula for \(e^{iua}\), the support bound \(|a|\le B_A\), and the preceding cancellations yield
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |\widehat h_n(u)|
 &=\left|\int_{\mathcal A}
     \left(e^{iua}-\sum_{k=0}^{K_n-1}\frac{(iua)^k}{k!}\right)
     h_n(a)\,da\right|\\
 &\le \|h_n\|_1 e^{B_A\Omega}
       \frac{(B_A\Omega)^{K_n}}{K_n!}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
By the marked-channel factorization, the two-dimensional Fourier transform of the observed marked-density perturbation equals
\[
 \widehat h_n(s+t)\phi_1(s)\phi_2(t).
\]
For fixed error transforms supported in bounded frequency intervals, this transform is confined to a fixed square and inherits the factorial bound above. What remains unproved is the simultaneous construction of a packet satisfying the Bernoulli range, strict Sobolev slack, and \(K_n^{1/2-\beta}\) supremum separation, together with a weighted chi-square or Hellinger inequality converting the Fourier attenuation into
\[
 \operatorname{TV}\!\left(((P_{0,n})_{\mathrm{obs}})^{\otimes n},
 ((P_{1,n})_{\mathrm{obs}})^{\otimes n}\right)\le\eta.
\]
