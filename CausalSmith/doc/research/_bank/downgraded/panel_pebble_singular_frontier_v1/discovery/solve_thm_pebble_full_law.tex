Fix \(P\in\mathcal M\), and write \(\mathcal L=\operatorname{col}(\Gamma)\). The conditional moments below exist by \(\texttt{ass:moment-radius}\), and conditioning on cohort \(c\) is legitimate because \(p_c\geq p_{\star}>0\) by \(\texttt{ass:positive-shares}\). By \(\texttt{ass:rank-two-factor-law}\) and \(\texttt{ass:error-mean}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\nu_c
&=\mathbb E_P[J_cY_i(0)\mid C_i=c]\\
&=\mathbb E_P[\Gamma_{S_c}\lambda_i+\varepsilon_{i,S_c}\mid C_i=c]\\
&=\Gamma_{S_c}m_c.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
Similarly, \(\texttt{ass:loading-error-orthogonality}\) makes both cross-covariance terms vanish, and \(\texttt{ass:conditional-homoskedasticity}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
V_c
&=\operatorname{Cov}_P(\Gamma_{S_c}\lambda_i+\varepsilon_{i,S_c}\mid C_i=c)\\
&=\Gamma_{S_c}H_c\Gamma_{S_c}^{\prime}
  +\operatorname{Cov}_P(\varepsilon_{i,S_c}\mid C_i=c)\\
&=\Gamma_{S_c}H_c\Gamma_{S_c}^{\prime}+\sigma_c^2I_{|S_c|}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
By \(\texttt{ass:local-signal-gap}\), the positive-semidefinite first summand in (2) has rank two and its second eigenvalue is at least \(g_{\star}>0\). Consequently the two largest eigenvalues of \(V_c\) strictly exceed \(\sigma_c^2\), while its remaining \(|S_c|-2\) eigenvalues equal \(\sigma_c^2\). Therefore
\[
\Pi_2(V_c)=\Pi_{\operatorname{col}(\Gamma_{S_c})},
\qquad
Q_c=I_{|S_c|}-\Pi_{\operatorname{col}(\Gamma_{S_c})}.
\tag{3}
\]
The entire observed law \(P_O\) determines \(p_c\), \(\nu_c\), and \(V_c\) for every \(c\), since \(p_c>0\). Thus it determines every local plane in (3).

Choose \(B_c\) as in \(\texttt{def:relation-operator}\). Since \(B_c^{\prime}B_c=Q_c\),
\[
\|B_cy\|^2=y^{\prime}Q_cy
\qquad (y\in\mathbb R^{|S_c|}),
\]
and hence \(\ker B_c=\ker Q_c=\operatorname{col}(\Gamma_{S_c})\). Positivity of every \(p_c\) now yields the basis-independent identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal K(P):=\ker\overline R(P)
&=\bigcap_{c=1}^C\ker(B_cJ_c)\\
&=\left\{x\in\mathbb R^T:x_{S_c}\in
\operatorname{col}(\Gamma_{S_c})\text{ for every }c\in[C]\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
In particular, \(\mathcal L\subseteq\mathcal K(P)\), and (4) shows that \(\mathcal K(P)\), despite the nonuniqueness of the row bases \(B_c\), is a functional of \(P_O\).

By \(\texttt{lem:generic-rank-two-coordinate-projection-certificate}\), applied under \(\texttt{ass:fixed-dimensions}\), \(\texttt{ass:deterministic-masks}\), and \(\texttt{ass:generic-factor-rows}\), the local planes \(\operatorname{col}(\Gamma_{S_c})\) determine \(\mathcal L\) uniquely if and only if the anchor multihypergraph from \(\texttt{def:anchor-hypergraph}\) contains a set \(F\) of \(T-2\) triples satisfying
\[
|F'|\leq |V(F')|-2
\qquad\text{for every nonempty }F'\subseteq F.
\tag{5}
\]
By \(\texttt{def:pebble-certificate}\), this is equivalent to \(r_{1,2}(H_3)=T-2\). Such an \(F\) automatically spans \([T]\), because (5) with \(F'=F\) implies
\[
T-2=|F|\leq |V(F)|-2\leq T-2,
\]
so \(|V(F)|=T\).

We next show that uniqueness of the global plane is equivalent to \(\mathcal K(P)=\mathcal L\). If \(\mathcal K(P)=\mathcal L\) and a two-dimensional subspace \(\widetilde{\mathcal L}\) has \((\widetilde{\mathcal L})_{S_c}=\operatorname{col}(\Gamma_{S_c})\) for all \(c\), then (4) gives \(\widetilde{\mathcal L}\subseteq\mathcal K(P)=\mathcal L\); equality follows because both spaces have dimension two. Conversely, suppose that \(\mathcal K(P)\supsetneq\mathcal L\). Choose \(h\in\mathcal K(P)\setminus\mathcal L\) and a basis \(u_1,u_2\) of \(\mathcal L\). For \(z\neq0\), put
\[
\mathcal L_z=\operatorname{span}(u_1+zh,u_2).
\tag{6}
\]
The two displayed generators are linearly independent: otherwise, because \(z\neq0\), one could solve for \(h\in\mathcal L\). Hence \(\dim(\mathcal L_z)=2\), and \(\mathcal L_z\neq\mathcal L\) for the same reason. Equation (4) gives \((\mathcal L_z)_{S_c}\subseteq\operatorname{col}(\Gamma_{S_c})\). At \(z=0\), the restricted pair has rank two for each \(c\), because \(\texttt{ass:local-signal-gap}\) implies \(\operatorname{rank}(\Gamma_{S_c})=2\). For every cohort, select a nonzero two-by-two minor of the restricted pair at \(z=0\). Each selected minor is continuous in \(z\), and \(C<\infty\) by \(\texttt{ass:fixed-dimensions}\); therefore all selected minors remain nonzero for every sufficiently small \(|z|>0\). For such a \(z\),
\[
(\mathcal L_z)_{S_c}=\operatorname{col}(\Gamma_{S_c})
\qquad\text{for every }c\in[C].
\tag{7}
\]
Thus strict inclusion in (4) produces a distinct compatible two-dimensional plane. We have proved
\[
r_{1,2}(H_3)=T-2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal K(P)=\operatorname{col}(\Gamma).
\tag{8}
\]
In particular, certificate failure implies \(\ker\overline R(P)=\mathcal K(P)\supsetneq\operatorname{col}(\Gamma)\), and (6)--(7) supply the asserted plane \(\widetilde{\mathcal L}\).

Suppose now that \(r_{1,2}(H_3)=T-2\). Let \(U\) be any orthonormal basis of the observed-law kernel \(\mathcal K(P)\). By (8) and \(\texttt{ass:factor-normalization}\), there is an orthogonal \(W\in\mathbb R^{2\times2}\) with \(U=\Gamma W\). The local signal gap implies that \(\Gamma_{S_c}\), and hence \(U_{S_c}\), has full column rank. Thus \(U_{S_c}^{\prime}U_{S_c}\) is positive definite. Set \(G_c=\Gamma_{S_c}^{\prime}\Gamma_{S_c}\), which is also positive definite. Using (1), orthogonality of \(W\), and \((W^{\prime}G_cW)^{-1}=W^{\prime}G_c^{-1}W\), we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
U(U_{S_c}^{\prime}U_{S_c})^{-1}U_{S_c}^{\prime}\nu_c
&=\Gamma W(W^{\prime}G_cW)^{-1}W^{\prime}G_cm_c\\
&=\Gamma WW^{\prime}G_c^{-1}WW^{\prime}G_cm_c\\
&=\Gamma m_c.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
The right side is the causal cohort mean, rather than an observed-law definition of it: indeed, \(\texttt{ass:rank-two-factor-law}\) and \(\texttt{ass:error-mean}\) imply
\[
\mathbb E_P[Y_i(0)\mid C_i=c]
=\Gamma\mathbb E_P[\lambda_i\mid C_i=c]
 +\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_i\mid C_i=c]
=\Gamma m_c
=\mu_c(P).
\tag{10}
\]
The left side of (9) is invariant under replacing \(U\) by \(UW_0\) for any orthogonal \(W_0\), as follows either by the same calculation or by cancellation of \(W_0\) and \(W_0^{\prime}\). Since \(P_O\) determines \(\mathcal K(P)\) and \(\nu_c\), (9) is exactly the map in \(\texttt{def:counterfactual-functional}\), and it is determined by the entire observed law. Therefore, if \(P_0,P_1\in\mathcal M\) have \((P_0)_O=(P_1)_O\), the map has the same value under both laws and, by (9)--(10), \(\mu(P_0)=\mu(P_1)\). This proves identification of every cohort-time coordinate when the certificate passes.

It remains to prove that the algebraic conclusion after certificate failure cannot, under the frozen primitives alone, be strengthened to causal-target nonidentification. Consider the allowed fixed specification \(T=4\), \(C=1\), and \(S_1=\{1,2,3\}\). Its anchor hypergraph has one edge, so
\[
r_{1,2}(H_3)=1<T-2=2.
\tag{11}
\]
Let
\[
G_0=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
0&1\\
1&1\\
1&2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\Gamma_0=G_0(G_0^{\prime}G_0)^{-1/2}.
\tag{12}
\]
Because \(G_0^{\prime}G_0\) is positive definite, \(\Gamma_0^{\prime}\Gamma_0=I_2\). The three two-by-two minors made from rows \(1,2,3\) of \(G_0\) are \(1\), \(1\), and \(-1\); right multiplication by the invertible matrix \((G_0^{\prime}G_0)^{-1/2}\) multiplies each by its nonzero determinant. Hence \(\Gamma_{0,S_1}\) has rank two and lies off the determinantal exceptional set for this single anchor triple, as required by \(\texttt{ass:generic-factor-rows}\).

Fix \(v>0\), take \(p_1=1\), set \(v_{\star}=v^{\star}=v\), and, conditional on the sole cohort, let \(\lambda_i\) be uniform on the circle of radius \(\sqrt{2v}\) in \(\mathbb R^2\). Set \(\varepsilon_i=0\) almost surely. Then
\[
\mathbb E[\lambda_i]=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(\lambda_i)=vI_2,
\qquad
\mathbb E\|\lambda_i\|^{4+\delta}=(2v)^{(4+\delta)/2}.
\tag{13}
\]
Choose
\[
M_{\mathrm{mom}}=(2v)^{(4+\delta)/2},
\qquad
0<g_{\star}<\lambda_2\!\left(Gamma_{0,S_1}vI_2\Gamma_{0,S_1}^{\prime}\right),
\qquad
0<p_{\star}<1.
\tag{14}
\]
The eigenvalue on the right of (14) is positive because \(\Gamma_{0,S_1}\) has rank two. Equations (12)--(14) verify normalization, exact rank two, the covariance bounds, the local signal gap, the conditional moment radius, mean-zero errors, loading--error orthogonality, and conditional homoskedasticity with \(\sigma_1=0\). Taking independent copies verifies \(\texttt{ass:iid-units}\), while \(p_1=1\) verifies \(\texttt{ass:positive-shares}\). Thus this certificate-failing frozen-model specification is nonempty.

For these primitive radii, \(q_0=4+\delta\), \(v_{\star}=v^{\star}=v\), and \(M_{\mathrm{mom}}=(2v)^{q_0/2}\). The already-established \(\texttt{lem:tight-moment-radius-forces-zero-means}\) therefore applies to every \(P\) in this fixed specification and gives
\[
m_c=0,
\qquad
\varepsilon_i=0\ \text{ almost surely given }C_i=c,
\qquad
\mu(P)=0.
\tag{15}
\]
Thus the causal target on this nonempty certificate-failing class is the constant observed-law functional zero. At the same time, (8) gives generic nonuniqueness of its factor plane. Hence failure of the pebble certificate alone entails factor-plane ambiguity, but it does not entail two observed-law-equivalent members with distinct causal means. This establishes the final assertion and completes the proof.
