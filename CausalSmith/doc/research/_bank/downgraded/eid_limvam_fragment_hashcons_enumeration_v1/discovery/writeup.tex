\noindent\textit{Specialization:} v1\par

\paragraph{TL;DR.} The observed covariance law supports two exact but different conclusions. Under the published shared-order LiMVAM primitives, unrestricted cross-view disturbance dependence makes every order covariance-compatible and makes common union-support ancestry empty, so pairing contributes neither pruning nor ancestry. Under the component-aligned subclass, the independently defined mechanism fiber is exactly represented by source-to-sink paths of the live \(q\)-DAG. A two-mode immutable-row dynamic program emits each distinct coefficient tuple once; its pairing-informative component-aligned mode runs in \(O((m+|E|)p^3 2^p+p^3 2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}+mp^2N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}})\) ordered exact-real time and computes the nontrivial subclass ancestry intersection. For rational inputs, both modes have deterministic subset-exponential, output-sensitive total bit bounds polynomial in the listed parameters including explicit \(2^p\); these are not output-polynomial guarantees in the conventional input-plus-output sense.

\section{Project justification}

\paragraph{Gap.} The parent calibration frontier had a \(2^p\)-state compatibility recursion but no noncircular exact-once enumeration of distinct numerical coefficient tuples. The previous repair correctly exposed the unrestricted-cross-view all-orders boundary but left the pairing-informative component-aligned coefficient target as an observable order refinement rather than an independent mechanism projection.

\paragraph{Niche.} The note separates the published mechanism fiber from its component-aligned subclass fiber. Within-view normal equations uniquely map any fiber mechanism and order to its immutable modified-Cholesky rows. The published fiber projects onto all orders, whereas the component-aligned fiber projects exactly onto live \(q\)-DAG paths by residual factorization and positive-definite coordinate completion.

\paragraph{Fill.} Source-to-sink pruning makes every retained prefix extendable. Equal immutable original-label fragments at a fixed removed set have identical suffix outputs, unequal fragments have disjoint completion sets, and each bucket charges to at most the final numerical output count. Exact row sorting and integer tries therefore yield exact-once full-lattice and q-pruned enumerators without a real hash oracle or factorial order traversal.

\section{Assumptions}

\begin{assumption}[ass:centered-gaussian-law]\label{ass:centered-gaussian-law}
Under \(P\), for every \(a\in M\), the disturbance vector \(\varepsilon^{(a)}\) has a product probability law whose coordinate marginals are centered and have finite second moments.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (Centered finite-second-moment LiMVAM disturbance independence, \cite{HeurtebiseEtAl2026LiMVAM}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:linear-structural-equations]\label{ass:linear-structural-equations}
For every \(a\in M\), \(X^{(a)}=B^{0,(a)}X^{(a)}+\varepsilon^{(a)}\) almost surely under \(P\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (Linear LiMVAM structural equations, \cite{HeurtebiseEtAl2026LiMVAM}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:shared-topological-order]\label{ass:shared-topological-order}
For every \(a\in M\), simultaneous row and column permutation by \(\pi_0\) makes \(B^{0,(a)}\) strictly lower triangular.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (Shared-order LiMVAM condition, \cite{HeurtebiseEtAl2026LiMVAM}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:component-aligned-disturbances]\label{ass:component-aligned-disturbances}
For distinct \(i,j\in V\) and all \(a,b\in M\), \(\operatorname{Cov}_P(\varepsilon_i^{(a)},\varepsilon_j^{(b)})=0\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Novel.} This condition is imposed only in the component-aligned refinement. It is not part of the published unrestricted-cross-view class; there, off-diagonal cross-view residual covariances are nuisance. The refinement is satisfied by coordinate-aligned repeated sensors with independent coordinate shocks and positive-variance readout errors.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:disturbance-positive-definiteness]\label{ass:disturbance-positive-definiteness}
For every \(j\in V\), \(\Gamma_j\succ0\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Novel.} Strict positive definiteness is natural when every repeated sensor has independent readout noise with positive variance in addition to any shared coordinate shock. It excludes singular common-noise boundaries and keeps the maintained model inside the positive-definite completion cone.
\end{assumption}

\section{Main results}

\begin{lemma}[lem:original-row-invariance]\label{lem:original-row-invariance}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\) and its observed moments. Let \(\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\), and suppose the predecessor set of \(r\) under \(\pi\) is exactly \(A\). Then the original-label row \(B_\pi^{(a)}[r,\cdot]\) equals \(\beta^{(a)}(A,r)\) for every \(a\in M\). Hence that row is invariant to the order within \(A\) and to every suffix after \(r\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put \(L^{0,(a)}=I_p-B^{0,(a)}\). By
\(\texttt{ass:shared-topological-order}\), simultaneous permutation by
\(\pi_0\) makes \(L^{0,(a)}\) unit lower triangular, so it is invertible.
Membership in \(\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\), through
\(\texttt{ass:centered-gaussian-law}\), says that the coordinates of
\(\varepsilon^{(a)}\) are independent and centered; the explicit
nondegeneracy clause in \(\texttt{def:published-limvam-class}\) says their
variances \(d_{ja}\) are strictly positive. Hence
\[
 \Omega^{aa}:=\operatorname{Cov}_P(\varepsilon^{(a)})
 =\operatorname{diag}_{j\in V}(d_{ja})\succ0.
\]
Using \(\texttt{ass:linear-structural-equations}\),
\[
 \Sigma^{aa}=(L^{0,(a)})^{-1}\Omega^{aa}(L^{0,(a)})^{-\mathsf T}\succ0.
 \tag{1}
\]
Indeed, for nonzero \(v\), the quadratic form in (1) equals the strictly
positive quadratic form of \(\Omega^{aa}\) at
\((L^{0,(a)})^{-\mathsf T}v\ne0\). Every principal matrix
\(\Sigma^{aa}[A,A]\) is therefore positive definite and invertible.

By \(\texttt{def:modified-cholesky}\), if the predecessor set of \(r\) is
\(A\), the possibly nonzero part \(c\) of row \(r\) is characterized by the
normal equations
\[
 c\,\Sigma^{aa}[A,A]=\Sigma^{aa}[r,A].
\]
The inverse justified above gives the unique solution
\[
 c=\Sigma^{aa}[r,A]\{\Sigma^{aa}[A,A]\}^{-1}.
\]
By \(\texttt{def:immutable-original-row}\), this is precisely
\(\beta^{(a)}(A,r)[A]\), and both definitions put zero in every column in
\(V\setminus A\). Thus
\[
 B_\pi^{(a)}[r,\cdot]=\beta^{(a)}(A,r).
\]
Reordering the coordinates of \(A\) permutes the normal equations and their
solution compatibly before the coordinates are restored to their original
labels. Suffix variables neither occur in these equations nor occupy a
nonzero column. This proves both invariance assertions.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The immutable marginal regression row is the write-once label shared by the full published lattice and the component-aligned refinement. \textit{Gap.} Recursive residualization does not by itself show that every orderwise coefficient row is a function only of its original-label predecessor set. \textit{Consumer.} The enumerator can cache one exact row identifier and discard the order history that produced it.

\begin{theorem}[thm:qpath-completion-exactness]\label{thm:qpath-completion-exactness}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\Gamma,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) and let \(\mathcal O_G\) be its observed moments for an arbitrary view graph \(G\). A permutation \(\pi\) is a source-to-sink path in \(\mathcal D_G\) if and only if \(\pi\in\Pi_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\). For every such path, each \(Q_{\pi,j}\) has a positive-definite completion obtained as a coordinate principal block of a full residual covariance, and independently assembling these completed blocks yields a component-aligned Gaussian completion with coefficient tuple \(T_\pi\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put \(L^{0,(a)}=I_p-B^{0,(a)}\) and \(\mathcal L^0=\operatorname{diag}_{a\in M}(L^{0,(a)})\).  By \(\texttt{ass:shared-topological-order}\), every \(L^{0,(a)}\), and hence \(\mathcal L^0\), is invertible.  If \(\Omega\) is the covariance of the view-stacked disturbance vector, then \(\texttt{ass:component-aligned-disturbances}\) gives
\[
 \Omega[(a,i),(b,j)]={\bf1}\{i=j\}\Gamma_i[a,b].
 \tag{1}
\]
After grouping indices by \(i\), (1) is \(\operatorname{diag}_{i\in V}(\Gamma_i)\succ0\) by \(\texttt{ass:disturbance-positive-definiteness}\).  The structural equations therefore give
\[
 \Sigma_{\mathrm{full}}
 = (\mathcal L^0)^{-1}\Omega(\mathcal L^0)^{-\mathsf T}\succ0.
 \tag{2}
\]
Thus every \(\Sigma^{aa}\), every principal matrix \(\Sigma^{aa}[A,A]\), and every within-view Schur complement \(C_A^{aa}\) is positive definite.  In particular all inverses in the definitions exist and \(C_A^{aa}[r,r]>0\).

Fix \(A\subseteq V\), \(r\in U_A\), and \(t\in U_A\setminus\{r\}\).  Successive least-squares projection first on \(X_A^{(b)}\) and then on the residual of \(X_r^{(b)}\) is the unique projection on \(X_{A\cup\{r\}}^{(b)}\).  Hence
\[
 Z_{A\cup\{r\},t}^{(b)}
 =Z_{A,t}^{(b)}-
 \frac{C_A^{bb}[t,r]}{C_A^{bb}[r,r]}Z_{A,r}^{(b)}.
\]
Taking covariance with \(Z_{A,r}^{(a)}\), using symmetry of \(C_A^{bb}\), and applying \(\texttt{def:q-root-score}\) yields
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}_P(Z_{A,r}^{(a)},Z_{A\cup\{r\},t}^{(b)})
 =q^{ab}(A,r,t).
 \tag{3}
\]
This also proves that the recursively obtained residual state is the path-independent state of \(\texttt{def:projected-residual-state}\).

Fix an order of \(W=U_A\setminus\{r\}\).  Sequential within-view regression in view \(b\) supplies an invertible unit triangular residualizer \(K^{(b)}\) with \(e_W^{(b)}=K^{(b)}Z_{A\cup\{r\},W}^{(b)}\), while \(e_r^{(a)}=Z_{A,r}^{(a)}\).  Equation (3), coordinate by coordinate, gives
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}_P(e_r^{(a)},e_W^{(b)})
 =\bigl(q^{ab}(A,r,t):t\in W\bigr)(K^{(b)})^{\mathsf T}.
 \tag{4}
\]
Invertibility of \(K^{(b)}\) makes the left side zero exactly when every score on the right is zero.  Exchanging \(a\) and \(b\) supplies the tail-to-root column criterion.  Induction over successive roots, with the one-coordinate suffix as the vacuous base case, proves
\[
 \pi\text{ is a source-to-sink path in }\mathcal D_G
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \operatorname{offdiag}(R_\pi^{ab})=0
 \text{ for every }\{a,b\}\in E.
 \tag{5}
\]
The within-view normal equations separately imply that every \(R_\pi^{aa}\) is diagonal with positive diagonal.

For any candidate \(\pi\), put
\[
 \mathcal L_\pi=\operatorname{diag}_{a\in M}(L_\pi^{(a)}),
 \qquad S_\pi=\mathcal L_\pi\Sigma_{\mathrm{full}}
 \mathcal L_\pi^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Equation (2) and invertibility of \(\mathcal L_\pi\) imply \(S_\pi\succ0\).  Let \(H_{\pi,j}\) be its principal submatrix on \(\{(a,j):a\in M\}\).  Extending a nonzero vector by zeros proves \(H_{\pi,j}\succ0\).  On every observed block,
\[
 S_\pi^{ab}=L_\pi^{(a)}\Sigma^{ab}(L_\pi^{(b)})^{\mathsf T}=R_\pi^{ab},
\]
so \(H_{\pi,j}\) completes \(Q_{\pi,j}\).

Suppose first that \(\pi\) is a path.  By (5), the observed edge blocks \(R_\pi^{ab}\) are diagonal.  Independently for each \(j\), choose a centered Gaussian vector \((\eta_j^{(a)})_{a\in M}\) with covariance \(H_{\pi,j}\), and make these vectors independent across \(j\).  Set \(\eta^{(a)}=(\eta_j^{(a)}:j\in V)\) and \(\widetilde X^{(a)}=(L_\pi^{(a)})^{-1}\eta^{(a)}\).  The resulting disturbance coordinates are independent within each view, component aligned across views, and have positive-definite coordinate covariance blocks \(H_{\pi,j}\).  Moreover, for \(a=b\) or \(\{a,b\}\in E\), diagonality and the completion identities give
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(\widetilde X^{(a)},\widetilde X^{(b)})
 =(L_\pi^{(a)})^{-1}R_\pi^{ab}(L_\pi^{(b)})^{-\mathsf T}
 =\Sigma^{ab}.
 \tag{6}
\]
Thus the constructed Gaussian mechanism belongs to \(\mathfrak F_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\), has order \(\pi\), and has coefficient tuple \(T_\pi\).  By \(\texttt{def:compatible-orders}\), \(\pi\in\Pi_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\).

Conversely, let \(\pi\in\Pi_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\) and take a witnessing mechanism in \(\mathfrak F_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\).  Because \(\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{CA}}\subseteq\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\), this witness lies in \(\mathfrak F_G(\mathcal O_G)\).  The converse normal-equation part of \(\texttt{thm:published-all-orders-covariance-compatible}\) forces its coefficient family to be \(B_\pi\).  Its observed residual blocks are consequently \(R_\pi^{ab}\).  Component alignment makes their off-diagonal coordinate entries zero, and the witnessing matrices \(\widetilde\Gamma_j\succ0\) complete the corresponding \(Q_{\pi,j}\).  Equation (5) now makes \(\pi\) a source-to-sink path.  This proves the equivalence, the stated principal-block completions, and the independent Gaussian completion claim.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The theorem now equates live q-paths with the independently defined component-aligned mechanism-order projection and constructs every forward witness. \textit{Gap.} Local residual zero scores alone do not establish that every path is realized by a valid positive-definite coordinate-noise mechanism. \textit{Consumer.} The q-pruned enumerator receives an exact arbitrary-view-graph path representation on maintained subclass inputs.

\begin{lemma}[lem:live-fragment-right-congruence]\label{lem:live-fragment-right-congruence}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\) and its observed moments \(\mathcal O_G\). Let \(\rho\) and \(\rho'\) be full-lattice prefixes ending at the same removed set \(A\). Then \(F(\rho)=F(\rho')\) if and only if \(\operatorname{Comp}(\rho)=\operatorname{Comp}(\rho')\). Moreover, if \(F(\rho)\ne F(\rho')\), then \(\operatorname{Comp}(\rho)\cap\operatorname{Comp}(\rho')=\varnothing\), and the legal suffix paths depend only on \(A\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Every ordering of \(V\setminus A\) is a legal suffix in the full removed-set lattice, so suffix legality depends only on \(A\).  Write a suffix as \((r_1,\ldots,r_{p-|A|})\), set \(A_0=A\), and set \(A_u=A_{u-1}\cup\{r_u\}\).  By \(\texttt{lem:original-row-invariance}\), it appends the immutable rows
\[
 \beta(A_0,r_1),\ \beta(A_1,r_2),\ \ldots,\
 \beta(A_{p-|A|-1},r_{p-|A|}),
 \tag{7}
\]
which depend only on the fixed observed marginals and the displayed predecessor sets.  Thus the same suffix appends the same rows to \(F(\rho)\) and \(F(\rho')\), proving
\[
 F(\rho)=F(\rho')
 \Longrightarrow
 \operatorname{Comp}(\rho)=\operatorname{Comp}(\rho').
 \tag{8}
\]
Every full-lattice prefix has a suffix, so both completion sets are nonempty.  If \(T\) belongs to their intersection, no assigned row is overwritten and \(\texttt{lem:original-row-invariance}\) shows that the projection of \(T\) onto rows in \(A\) is both \(F(\rho)\) and \(F(\rho')\).  Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{Comp}(\rho)\cap\operatorname{Comp}(\rho')\ne\varnothing
 \Longrightarrow F(\rho)=F(\rho').
 \tag{9}
\]
Equality of the nonempty completion sets implies a nonempty intersection, giving the reverse implication in (8); the contrapositive of (9) gives completion-disjointness.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The published-mode lemma certifies exact merging on the full lattice. \textit{Gap.} A completed-path quotient does not show that a partial numerical key preserves all futures. \textit{Consumer.} The unrestricted boundary can be enumerated without retaining order histories.

\begin{lemma}[lem:projection-output-bound]\label{lem:projection-output-bound}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\) and its observed moments \(\mathcal O_G\). For every \(A\subseteq V\), each retained fragment in \(H_A\) is the projection onto rows indexed by \(A\) of at least one tuple in \(\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)\). Distinct fragments at \(A\) project to disjoint nonempty output subsets, and therefore \(|H_A|\le N_{\mathrm{out}}\), \(\sum_{A\subseteq V}|H_A|\le2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}\), and the number of attempted fragment transitions is at most \(p2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \(\mathcal P_A\) be the set of full-lattice prefixes from \(\varnothing\) to \(A\).  Induction on \(|A|\), using \(\texttt{def:fragment-hashcons-enumerator}\), gives
\[
 H_A=\{F(\rho):\rho\in\mathcal P_A\}.
 \tag{10}
\]
The initialization proves (10) for \(A=\varnothing\).  Every nonempty prefix to \(A'\) has a final edge \(A'\setminus\{r\}\to A'\); by induction its parent fragment is present, and the transition writes exactly \(\beta(A'\setminus\{r\},r)\).  Conversely, every insertion extends a represented prefix by one full-lattice edge.  Exact trie equality removes precisely duplicate fragments.

Fix \(F\in H_A\) and a representing prefix \(\rho\).  Choose any suffix.  The resulting permutation belongs to \(\Pi_G(\mathcal O_G)\) by \(\texttt{thm:published-all-orders-covariance-compatible}\), so its tuple lies in \(\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)\).  By \(\texttt{lem:original-row-invariance}\), every \(T\in\operatorname{Comp}(\rho)\) satisfies
\[
 T\big|_{\text{rows indexed by }A}=F.
 \tag{11}
\]
The nonempty completion sets for unequal fragments are disjoint by \(\texttt{lem:live-fragment-right-congruence}\) and are subsets of \(\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)\).  Hence
\[
 |H_A|\le|\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)|=N_{\mathrm{out}}.
 \tag{12}
\]
Summing (12) over all \(2^p\) subsets gives \(\sum_A|H_A|\le2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}\).  Since each retained fragment is processed once and each subset has at most \(p\) outgoing edges,
\[
 \sum_{A\subseteq V}(p-|A|)|H_A|
 \le p\sum_{A\subseteq V}|H_A|
 \le p2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}.
\]
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The full-lattice projection charge bounds intermediate published fragments by published numerical outputs. \textit{Gap.} The number of compatible orders can be factorial even when the coefficient image is small. \textit{Consumer.} The full-lattice implementation obtains output-sensitive storage and transition bounds.

\begin{theorem}[thm:exact-once-output-sensitive-enumeration]\label{thm:exact-once-output-sensitive-enumeration}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\), and let \(\mathcal O_G\) be its observed moments for an arbitrary view graph \(G\). Then \(\operatorname{Enum}_G(\mathcal O_G)\) emits exactly \(\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)=\{T_\pi:\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\}\), each numerical tuple once. As non-novel post-processing of that exact-once tuple stream, it also computes \(\mathcal A_G(\mathcal O_G)\). In the ordered exact-real model \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{ER}}\), full-lattice construction and pruning, exact lexicographic row interning, integer-fragment trie propagation, decoding, output, and common-ancestry intersection require \(O(\operatorname{poly}(p,m)2^p\max\{1,N_{\mathrm{out}}\})\) total time and space. An explicit time bound is
\[
 O\!\left(mp^3 2^p+p^3 2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}+mp^2N_{\mathrm{out}}\right).
\]
No hash oracle on real numbers, no cross-view residual zero test, and no traversal of all \(p!\) orders is used.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induction on \(|A|\), exactly as in (10), proves that \(H_A\) contains precisely the distinct fragments of full-lattice prefixes ending at \(A\).  Merging equal fragments preserves every continuation by \(\texttt{lem:live-fragment-right-congruence}\).  At \(A=V\), every row is assigned, and \(\texttt{lem:original-row-invariance}\) identifies the decoded fragment of an order \(\pi\) with \(T_\pi\).  The two directions of \(\texttt{thm:published-all-orders-covariance-compatible}\), together with the mechanism-projection definition \(\texttt{def:identified-tuple-set}\), therefore give
\[
 \{\text{decoded members of }H_V\}
 =\{T_\pi:\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\}
 =\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G).
 \tag{13}
\]
For each original row label, lexicographic exact-real sorting assigns one integer identifier exactly to numerically equal viewwise rows.  Trie insertion creates a terminal only on the first occurrence of an integer fragment.  Equation (13) is therefore emitted without omission and exactly once per numerical tuple.

We count ordered exact-real operations.  The full lattice has \(2^p\) vertices and \(p2^{p-1}\) edges; its vacuous forward-backward pruning costs \(O(p2^p)\).  For fixed \(A\) and view \(a\), one dense factorization of \(\Sigma^{aa}[A,A]\), followed by all solves for \(r\notin A\), computes the outgoing rows in \(O(p^3)\) operations.  These matrices are invertible by \(\texttt{lem:original-row-invariance}\), so all rows cost
\[
 O(mp^3 2^p).
 \tag{14}
\]
For fixed row label \(r\), at most \(K_r=2^{p-1}\) edges carry that label.  A lexicographic comparison examines at most \(mp\) real coordinates.  Comparison sorting and adjacent equality interning cost
\[
 O\!\left(mp\sum_{r\in V}K_r\log(K_r+1)\right)
 =O(mp^3 2^p).
 \tag{15}
\]
No real-valued hash is used.  A row identifier has \(O(p)\) bits and a fragment has \(p\) slots, so deterministic binary-trie lookup or insertion costs \(O(p^2)\).  By \(\texttt{lem:projection-output-bound}\), at most \(p2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}\) transitions are attempted, and propagation therefore costs
\[
 O(p^3 2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}).
 \tag{16}
\]
Decoding and emitting \(m\) matrices with \(p^2\) entries costs \(O(mp^2N_{\mathrm{out}})\).

By \(\texttt{prop:published-common-union-support-ancestry-empty}\), published-class common ancestry is always the empty relation.  It is returned in \(O(1)\) time and space and requires no output scan.  Adding (14)--(16) yields the displayed time bound.

For space, the interned rows occupy \(O(mp^2 2^p)\) real cells, the tries occupy \(O(p^2 2^pN_{\mathrm{out}})\) bits or pointers by the projection bound, decoded retained outputs occupy \(O(mp^2N_{\mathrm{out}})\) cells, and the lattice costs \(O(p2^p)\).  The published output is nonempty by \(\texttt{thm:published-all-orders-covariance-compatible}\).  Both total time and space are consequently \(O(\operatorname{poly}(p,m)2^p\max\{1,N_{\mathrm{out}}\})\).  Every loop ranges over subset edges and retained fragments, never over \(p!\) permutations, and no cross-view residual zero is used to screen a published-class order.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The retained theorem gives the published full-lattice exact-once boundary and now returns its known-empty ancestry in constant time. \textit{Gap.} Even though all orders are compatible, their numerical coefficient tuples need not be distinct. \textit{Consumer.} The workflow can report every published-class coefficient mechanism without permutation traversal.

\begin{proposition}[prop:zero-system-sanity]\label{prop:zero-system-sanity}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\). If \(B^{0,(a)}=0\) for every \(a\in M\), then every compatible order induces the same zero tuple. For an empty observed view graph, all \(p!\) orders are compatible, \(N_{\mathrm{out}}=1\), and the full-lattice fragment algorithm retains exactly one fragment in every subset bucket \(H_A\), hence \(2^p\) fragments in total.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Assume \(B^{0,(a)}=0\) for every \(a\in M\).  By \(\texttt{ass:linear-structural-equations}\), \(X^{(a)}=\varepsilon^{(a)}\).  The within-view product condition in \(\texttt{ass:centered-gaussian-law}\) and the positive-variance clause in \(\texttt{def:published-limvam-class}\) give a positive diagonal matrix \(\Sigma^{aa}\).  Thus, for every \(A\subseteq V\) and \(r\notin A\),
\[
 \Sigma^{aa}[r,A]=0,
 \qquad \beta^{(a)}(A,r)=0.
 \tag{17}
\]
By \(\texttt{lem:original-row-invariance}\), every order induces the all-zero tuple.  By \(\texttt{thm:published-all-orders-covariance-compatible}\), every one of the \(p!\) orders is compatible for every \(G\), hence for the empty graph, and
\[
 \Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)=\{0\},
 \qquad N_{\mathrm{out}}=1.
\]
Starting from the unique empty fragment, every transition writes a zero row by (17).  Exact trie insertion retains the resulting identical fragment once in each subset bucket.  There are \(2^p\) buckets, proving the claim.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The zero system certifies a genuine factorial-to-exponential collapse of the full-lattice fragment algorithm. \textit{Gap.} Order enumeration would still list all \(p!\) structural witnesses even though their numerical coefficient output is a singleton. \textit{Consumer.} Implementers obtain a boundary regression test for accidental order traversal or failed row interning.

\begin{theorem}[thm:rational-output-polynomial-bit-enumeration]\label{thm:rational-output-polynomial-bit-enumeration}
Under the premises of \(\texttt{thm:exact-once-output-sensitive-enumeration}\), suppose every entry of the rational input \(\mathcal O_G\) has binary encoding length at most \(L_{\mathrm{bits}}\). The fraction-free immutable-row implementation of \(\operatorname{Enum}_G\), including common-ancestry computation, has deterministic total bit-operation time and bit storage polynomial in \(p\), \(m\), \(L_{\mathrm{bits}}\), \(2^p\), \(N_{\mathrm{out}}\), and the total emitted encoding length. Canonically reduced determinant-ratio certificates decide equality of interned rows, and no superpolynomial intermediate or certificate growth is forced relative to those displayed parameters. Thus the guarantee is subset-exponential and output-sensitive, with the dependence on \(2^p\) explicit; it is not output-polynomial in the conventional input-plus-output sense.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let \(\ell(z)\) denote the binary length of an integer \(z\), and represent each rational input coordinate as \(u/v\), where \(u,v\in\mathbb Z\), \(v>0\), \(\gcd(u,v)=1\), and \(\max\{\ell(u),\ell(v)\}\leq L_{\mathrm{bits}}\).  We first verify that every inverse and division used below is legitimate.  By \(\texttt{def:published-limvam-class}\), for a fixed view \(a\in M\) the disturbance coordinates are mutually independent and have strictly positive variances.  Hence, with
\[
 \Omega^{(a)}=\operatorname{Cov}_P(\varepsilon^{(a)}),
\]
the matrix \(\Omega^{(a)}\) is diagonal and positive definite.  By \(\texttt{ass:linear-structural-equations}\),
\[
 (I_p-B^{0,(a)})X^{(a)}=\varepsilon^{(a)}.
\]
The shared-order condition \(\texttt{ass:shared-topological-order}\) makes \(B^{0,(a)}\) strictly lower triangular after simultaneous permutation, so \(L_0^{(a)}=I_p-B^{0,(a)}\) is invertible.  Taking covariances gives
\[
 \Sigma^{aa}=(L_0^{(a)})^{-1}\Omega^{(a)}(L_0^{(a)})^{-\mathsf T}\succ0.
\tag{1}
\]
Consequently every principal matrix \(\Sigma^{aa}[A,A]\) in \(\texttt{def:immutable-original-row}\) is positive definite and nonsingular.  This also supplies the positivity and rank conditions required for the principal regression solves.

Fix one such solve with \(k=|A|\leq p\), and append its right-hand side to the \(k\times k\) coefficient matrix.  At most \((p+1)^2\) rational entries occur in this augmented matrix.  Let \(d\) be the product of their positive denominators.  Then
\[
 \ell(d)\leq (p+1)^2L_{\mathrm{bits}},
 \qquad
 \ell(dx)=O(p^2L_{\mathrm{bits}})
\tag{2}
\]
for every augmented entry \(x\).  Multiplication of the whole augmented system by the same nonzero scalar \(d\) leaves its solution unchanged.  After any fixed ordering of \(A\), all leading principal minors of the integer-scaled coefficient matrix are positive by (1), so no pivot is needed.

By the cited Bareiss result \(\texttt{lem:bareiss-minor-growth}\), the fraction-free recurrences make exact integer divisions and every transformed entry is a signed bordered minor of the scaled augmented matrix.  If \(K\) is an \(s\times s\) integer matrix with \(|K_{uv}|<2^h\), the Leibniz formula yields
\[
 |\det K|
 \leq \sum_{\sigma\in\mathfrak S_s}
       \prod_{u=1}^s |K_{u,\sigma(u)}|
 < s!\,2^{sh}.
\tag{3}
\]
Using \(s\leq p+1\) and (2), every Bareiss intermediate and every numerator or denominator supplied by the adjugate identity therefore has binary length
\[
 H_0=O(p^3L_{\mathrm{bits}}+p\log p).
\tag{4}
\]
The coefficient determinant is nonzero by (1), so the adjugate identity is applicable.  Dividing numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor and choosing a positive denominator gives a unique rational certificate for every coordinate of \(\beta(A,r)\).  Thus two coordinates are equal if and only if their canonical numerator--denominator pairs agree.  Lexicographic order is also exact: for reduced fractions \(u/v\) and \(u'/v'\) with positive denominators, its scalar comparison is the sign comparison
\[
 uv'\mathrel{\lessgtr}u'v,
\]
whose operands have \(O(H_0)\) bits.  Equality and ordering of viewwise rows are obtained by finitely many such coordinate comparisons.  This proves the claimed deterministic row interning and verifies the certificate handle in \(\texttt{def:fraction-free-bit-handle}\).

Integer addition, multiplication, exact division, comparison, and greatest-common-divisor computation have bit cost polynomial in the operand lengths.  For completeness, Euclid's algorithm has only polynomially many remainder steps: after two consecutive nonterminal steps the positive second operand is less than half its value before those steps, so inputs of at most \(h\) bits require at most \(2h+1\) steps, each on operands of at most \(h\) bits.  Hence all row construction and interning operations have bit cost polynomial in \(p\) and \(L_{\mathrm{bits}}\).

By \(\texttt{thm:exact-once-output-sensitive-enumeration}\), the subsequent deterministic integer-trie computation emits exactly \(\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)\), once per numerical tuple, and uses a number of ordered-exact-real operations and stored cells polynomial in \(p\), \(m\), \(2^p\), and \(N_{\mathrm{out}}\).  Fragment keys consist of at most \(p\) integer row identifiers, so their lengths are logarithmic in a quantity polynomial in the number of subset edges and cannot create rational-expression growth.  Decoding merely copies the canonical certificates already bounded in (4).  Published common ancestry is the empty relation by \(\texttt{prop:published-common-union-support-ancestry-empty}\), so that output requires \(O(1)\) further time and storage.

Multiplying the polynomial operation and cell counts by the polynomial bit cost and height bounds just proved, and adding the total length of the emitted certificates, gives deterministic total bit time and storage polynomial in
\[
 p,\quad m,\quad L_{\mathrm{bits}},\quad 2^p,
 \quad N_{\mathrm{out}},\quad
 \text{and the total emitted encoding length}.
\]
Every regression row is solved directly from an original principal covariance system, so (4) is not recursively compounded along a subset path.  Finally, the displayed parameter list retains \(2^p\).  Since \(2^p\) need not be polynomial in the rational input length plus the output length when, for example, \(N_{\mathrm{out}}=1\), the proved guarantee is subset-exponential and output-sensitive, not output-polynomial in the conventional input-plus-output sense.
This completes the subset-exponential bit-complexity proof.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The published exact-real algorithm has a deterministic rational implementation with a subset-exponential, output-sensitive total bit bound and explicit subset-state dependence. \textit{Gap.} Unit-cost exact-real accounting alone does not control certificate height or justify conventional output-polynomial terminology. \textit{Consumer.} Exact symbolic implementations of the all-orders boundary have a Turing-model guarantee with the subset-state cost stated explicitly.

\begin{theorem}[thm:published-all-orders-covariance-compatible]\label{thm:published-all-orders-covariance-compatible}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\), and let \(\mathcal O_G\) be its observed moments for an arbitrary view graph \(G\). For every \(\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\), the fiber \(\mathfrak F_G(\mathcal O_G)\) contains a centered jointly Gaussian mechanism whose common order is \(\pi\) and whose coefficient family is \(B_\pi\). Conversely, if \((\widetilde P,\widetilde B,\widetilde\varepsilon,\widetilde\pi)\in\mathfrak F_G(\mathcal O_G)\) and \(\widetilde\pi=\pi\), then \(\widetilde B=B_\pi\). Consequently,
\[
 \Pi_G(\mathcal O_G)=\mathfrak S_V,
 \qquad
 \Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)=\{T_\pi:\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\}.
\]
In the Gaussian witnesses, within-view disturbance coordinates are independent and nondegenerate, while off-diagonal cross-view residual covariances are retained as unrestricted nuisance covariances rather than tested for zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Stack the views in a fixed order and write \(\Sigma_{\mathrm{full}}=\operatorname{Cov}_P((X^{(a)})_{a\in M})\).  The argument in \(\texttt{lem:original-row-invariance}\) gives \(\Sigma^{aa}\succ0\), so all modified-Cholesky regressions exist.  Fix \(\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\), and set
\[
 \mathcal L_\pi=\operatorname{diag}_{a\in M}(L_\pi^{(a)}),
 \qquad S_\pi=\mathcal L_\pi\Sigma_{\mathrm{full}}
 \mathcal L_\pi^{\mathsf T}.
 \tag{20}
\]
Each \(L_\pi^{(a)}\) is unit lower triangular after reindexing by \(\pi\), hence invertible, and \(S_\pi\succeq0\).

Let \(e_k^{(a)}\) be the residual from regressing \(X_{\pi(k)}^{(a)}\) on its predecessors \(X_{A_{k-1}}^{(a)}\).  The normal equations give
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}_P(e_k^{(a)},X_{\pi(\ell)}^{(a)})=0
 \quad(\ell<k).
 \tag{21}
\]
Every earlier residual is a linear combination of predecessor variables, so (21) makes distinct residuals uncorrelated.  Their variances are the positive Schur complements
\[
 d_{k,a}=\Sigma^{aa}[\pi(k),\pi(k)]-
 \Sigma^{aa}[\pi(k),A_{k-1}]
 \{\Sigma^{aa}[A_{k-1},A_{k-1}]\}^{-1}
 \Sigma^{aa}[A_{k-1},\pi(k)]>0.
 \tag{22}
\]
Thus every within-view block \(S_\pi^{aa}\) is positive diagonal.

Take an orthogonal diagonalization \(S_\pi=U\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_{mp})U^{\mathsf T}\), with \(\lambda_s\ge0\), and let \(S_\pi^{1/2}=U\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{\lambda_1},\ldots,\sqrt{\lambda_{mp}})U^{\mathsf T}\).  If \(g\) has independent standard normal coordinates, define
\[
 \widetilde\varepsilon=S_\pi^{1/2}g,
 \qquad \widetilde X=\mathcal L_\pi^{-1}\widetilde\varepsilon.
 \tag{23}
\]
Then
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}(\widetilde X)
 =\mathcal L_\pi^{-1}S_\pi\mathcal L_\pi^{-\mathsf T}
 =\Sigma_{\mathrm{full}}.
 \tag{24}
\]
Within each view, (21)--(22) make the disturbance covariance positive diagonal.  The Gaussian characteristic function factors as
\[
 \mathbb E\exp\!\left(i\sum_{j\in V}t_j\widetilde\varepsilon_j^{(a)}\right)
 =\exp\!\left(-\tfrac12\sum_{j\in V}d_{j,a}t_j^2\right)
 =\prod_{j\in V}\exp(-d_{j,a}t_j^2/2),
\]
so the within-view coordinates are independent, centered, and nondegenerate.  Equation (23) supplies the structural equations with coefficients \(B_\pi\) and common order \(\pi\).  Thus the witness belongs to \(\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\), and (24) puts it in \(\mathfrak F_G(\mathcal O_G)\).  This realizes every \(T_\pi\).  All cross-view blocks of \(S_\pi\), including off-diagonal coordinate entries, were retained without restriction.

For the converse, take \((\widetilde P,\widetilde B,\widetilde\varepsilon,\widetilde\pi)\in\mathfrak F_G(\mathcal O_G)\) with \(\widetilde\pi=\pi\).  Fix \(a\), let \(r=\pi(k)\), and set \(A=A_{k-1}\).  Triangularity gives
\[
 \widetilde X_r^{(a)}
 =\widetilde B^{(a)}[r,A]\widetilde X_A^{(a)}
 +\widetilde\varepsilon_r^{(a)},
 \qquad
 \widetilde X_A^{(a)}
 =(I-\widetilde B^{(a)}[A,A])^{-1}
 \widetilde\varepsilon_A^{(a)}.
 \tag{25}
\]
The product disturbance law makes \(\widetilde\varepsilon_r^{(a)}\) independent of \(\widetilde\varepsilon_A^{(a)}\), hence independent of \(\widetilde X_A^{(a)}\).  Centeredness and finite second moments yield zero covariance.  Taking covariance in (25) and using fiber agreement on the marginal block gives
\[
 \Sigma^{aa}[r,A]
 =\widetilde B^{(a)}[r,A]\Sigma^{aa}[A,A].
\]
Positive definiteness permits division, so
\[
 \widetilde B^{(a)}[r,A]
 =\Sigma^{aa}[r,A]\{\Sigma^{aa}[A,A]\}^{-1}
 =B_\pi^{(a)}[r,A].
 \tag{26}
\]
Both rows vanish outside \(A\).  Since \(a\) and \(k\) were arbitrary, \(\widetilde B=B_\pi\).  Taking the order and coefficient projections in \(\texttt{def:compatible-orders}\) and \(\texttt{def:identified-tuple-set}\) proves
\[
 \Pi_G(\mathcal O_G)=\mathfrak S_V,
 \qquad \Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)=\{T_\pi:\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\}.
\]
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The theorem preserves the exact unrestricted-cross-view boundary and the reverse normal-equation uniqueness needed by both fiber projections. \textit{Gap.} Without this boundary, residual cross-view zeros could be misused to discard valid published-class mechanisms. \textit{Consumer.} Analysts can distinguish nuisance cross-view covariance from restrictions that genuinely identify order information.

\begin{lemma}[lem:component-aligned-live-fragment-right-congruence]\label{lem:component-aligned-live-fragment-right-congruence}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\Gamma,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) and its observed moments \(\mathcal O_G\), and let \(\rho,\rho'\) be prefixes in \(\mathcal D_G^{\mathrm{live}}\) ending at the same removed set \(A\).  Then \(F(\rho)=F(\rho')\) if and only if \(\operatorname{Comp}_{\mathrm{CA}}(\rho)=\operatorname{Comp}_{\mathrm{CA}}(\rho')\).  Unequal fragments have disjoint nonempty completion sets, and the legal suffix paths depend only on \(A\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A legal suffix is a path from \(A\) to \(V\) in the fixed DAG \(\mathcal D_G^{\mathrm{live}}\), so legality depends only on \(A\), not on the prefix.  Source-to-sink pruning makes at least one such suffix available.  If a suffix removes \(r_1,\ldots,r_{p-|A|}\), then \(\texttt{lem:original-row-invariance}\) shows that it appends exactly the rows in (7), independently of the prefix.  Equal fragments therefore have equal completion sets.  If a tuple lies in the intersection of two completion sets, its projection onto the already assigned rows \(A\) equals each prefix fragment, so the fragments are equal.  Nonemptiness turns equality of completion sets into a nonempty intersection, proving the reverse implication; contraposition gives disjointness.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} Source-to-sink pruning supplies nonempty completions, and immutable rows make the fragment relation a right congruence on the q-DAG. \textit{Gap.} Dead prefixes make the converse false because unequal fragments can both have empty completion sets. \textit{Consumer.} The pairing-informative enumerator can merge live prefixes safely.

Every fragment in \(H_A^{\mathrm{CA}}\) is the row-\(A\) projection of at least one tuple in \(\Theta_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\).  |H_A^{\mathrm{CA}}|\le N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}},

 H_A^{\mathrm{CA}}
 =\{F(\rho):\rho\text{ is a prefix in }
 \mathcal D_G^{\mathrm{live}}\text{ ending at }A\}.

Summing over \(2^p\) subsets and bounding every outdegree by \(p\) gives the remaining inequalities.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The live-q-DAG projection charge bounds every component-aligned bucket by \(N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}\). \textit{Gap.} Graph path counts do not control numerical tuple projections after prefix merging. \textit{Consumer.} The main q-pruned complexity theorem depends on numerical output size rather than compatible-order count.

\begin{proposition}[prop:published-common-union-support-ancestry-empty]\label{prop:published-common-union-support-ancestry-empty}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{pub}}\) and its observed moments \(\mathcal O_G\).  Then
\[
 \mathcal A_G(\mathcal O_G)=\varnothing.
\]
Thus unrestricted cross-view pairing contributes no common union-support ancestry, and this published-class output is returned in \(O(1)\) time and space.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
By \(\texttt{thm:published-all-orders-covariance-compatible}\), the published coefficient projection is nonempty and satisfies
\[
 \Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)=\{T_\pi:\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\}.
\tag{12}
\]
Fix \(\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\).  In \(\texttt{def:modified-cholesky}\), the only entries that can be nonzero in row \(\pi(k)\) have column labels in \(\{\pi(1),\ldots,\pi(k-1)\}\).  Hence every union-support edge \(u\to v\) of \(\mathcal U(T_\pi)\) points from an earlier vertex to a later vertex in \(\pi\).  Along every directed path the position in \(\pi\) therefore increases strictly.  It follows both that \(\mathcal U(T_\pi)\) is acyclic and that its strict transitive closure contains no diagonal pair.

Now fix distinct \(i,j\in V\).  Choose a permutation \(\pi_{j<i}\) in which \(j\) occurs before \(i\); such a permutation exists because \(V\) is finite and contains both labels.  Equation (12) gives \(T_{\pi_{j<i}}\in\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)\).  Since every directed path in its union-support graph moves forward in \(\pi_{j<i}\), there is no path from the later vertex \(i\) to the earlier vertex \(j\).  Therefore
\[
 (i,j)\notin
 \operatorname{TC}^{+}(\mathcal U(T_{\pi_{j<i}})).
\tag{13}
\]
The definition \(\texttt{def:common-union-support-ancestry}\) intersects these strict transitive closures over all \(T\in\Theta_G(\mathcal O_G)\).  Equation (13) excludes every off-diagonal ordered pair from that intersection, while acyclicity excludes every diagonal pair.  Consequently
\[
 \mathcal A_G(\mathcal O_G)=\varnothing.
\]
The algorithm may therefore return the fixed empty relation without inspecting the tuple stream; writing this predeclared result takes \(O(1)\) time and \(O(1)\) space.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} Reversing any ordered pair inside an admissible published order gives an explicit witness excluding that ancestry pair. \textit{Gap.} A generic stream intersection obscures that unrestricted cross-view pairing identifies no common ancestry at all. \textit{Consumer.} The published boundary reports the empty relation immediately in \(O(1)\).

\begin{proposition}[prop:component-aligned-common-union-support-ancestry]\label{prop:component-aligned-common-union-support-ancestry}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\Gamma,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) and its observed moments \(\mathcal O_G\).  Then \(\mathcal A_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\) is exactly the set of ordered pairs that are ancestors in the union-support DAG of every mechanism in \(\mathfrak F_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\).  Given the exact-once component-aligned tuple stream, it is computed by exact support tests, transitive closure, and intersection in \(O(N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}(mp^2+p^3))\) additional time and \(O(p^2)\) working space.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write \(\mathfrak f=(\widetilde P,\widetilde B,
\widetilde\varepsilon,\widetilde\Gamma,\widetilde\pi)\) for an element of
\(\mathfrak F_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\), and let
\(c(\mathfrak f)=\widetilde B\) denote its coefficient family.  The fixed
data-generating tuple belongs to \(\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) by
\(\texttt{def:component-aligned-limvam-class}\), and it matches its own
observed blocks.  Hence it belongs to
\(\mathfrak F_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\) by
\(\texttt{def:component-aligned-compatibility-fiber}\).  In particular, the
fiber is nonempty.  By \(\texttt{def:identified-tuple-set}\), its coefficient
projection is
\[
 \Theta_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)
 =\{c(\mathfrak f):
       \mathfrak f\in\mathfrak F_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\}.
 \tag{1}
\]
Moreover, \(\texttt{thm:component-aligned-fiber-projection-exactness}\)
identifies this nonempty set with the set of \(T_\pi\) indexed by
source-to-sink paths in \(\mathcal D_G^{\mathrm{live}}\).

We first verify that the word ``ancestor'' in the claim refers to an ordinary
DAG ancestry relation.  If \(T_\pi=(B_\pi^{(a)})_{a\in M}\) is one of the
tuples in the preceding characterization, then
\(\texttt{def:modified-cholesky}\) gives, for
\(r=\pi(k)\),
\[
 B_\pi^{(a)}[r,s]\ne0
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 s\in\{\pi(1),\ldots,\pi(k-1)\}.
 \tag{2}
\]
Thus every edge \(s\to r\) of the union-support graph
\(\mathcal U(T_\pi)\) points strictly forward in the order \(\pi\).  A
directed cycle would therefore give a strict chain of positions ending below
its starting position, which is impossible.  Hence
\(\mathcal U(T_\pi)\) is a DAG, and its ordered ancestor pairs are exactly
\(\operatorname{TC}^{+}(\mathcal U(T_\pi))\).

For any \((i,j)\in V\times V\), the definition
\(\texttt{def:common-union-support-ancestry}\), followed by (1), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 (i,j)\in\mathcal A_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)
 &\Longleftrightarrow
   (i,j)\in
   \bigcap_{T\in\Theta_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)}
        \operatorname{TC}^{+}(\mathcal U(T))                                      \\
 &\Longleftrightarrow
   \forall T\in\Theta_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G),\quad
   (i,j)\in\operatorname{TC}^{+}(\mathcal U(T))                                  \\
 &\Longleftrightarrow
   \forall\mathfrak f\in\mathfrak F_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G),\quad
   (i,j)\in
   \operatorname{TC}^{+}(\mathcal U(c(\mathfrak f))).
 \tag{3}
\end{aligned}
\]
The last equivalence is valid in both directions because (1) says precisely
that \(c\) maps the fiber onto the tuple set; numerical duplicates in that
image do not change a universal statement.  By the DAG conclusion following
(2), the final line of (3) says exactly that \(i\) is an ancestor of \(j\) in
the union-support DAG of every mechanism in the fiber.  This proves the
set-theoretic assertion without identifying the causal target by definition:
the equality follows from the independently defined mechanism projection (1).

It remains to establish the computational bound.  Let the given exact-once
stream be \(T_1,\ldots,T_n\), where the preceding nonemptiness and exactness
give
\[
 n=N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}\geq1,
 \qquad
 \{T_1,\ldots,T_n\}=\Theta_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G).
 \tag{4}
\]
For \(T_\ell=(B_\ell^{(a)})_{a\in M}\), form the Boolean edge relation
\[
 E_\ell=\{(i,j)\in V^2:
              B_\ell^{(a)}[j,i]\ne0
              \text{ for some }a\in M\}.
 \tag{5}
\]
Equation (5) requires at most \(mp^2\) exact zero comparisons and Boolean
updates.  Compute its transitive closure in place by the recurrence
\[
 W_\ell^{(0)}=E_\ell,
 \qquad
 W_\ell^{(k)}=W_\ell^{(k-1)}\cup
 \bigl\{(u,v):
 (u,k)\in W_\ell^{(k-1)},\ (k,v)\in W_\ell^{(k-1)}\bigr\},
 \quad 1\leq k\leq p.
 \tag{6}
\]
The usual path-decomposition induction on the allowed intermediate vertices
shows that \(W_\ell^{(p)}=\operatorname{TC}^{+}(\mathcal U(T_\ell))\): at
stage \(k\), the relation contains exactly the nonempty directed paths whose
internal vertices lie in \(\{1,\ldots,k\}\).  Recurrence (6) uses
\(O(p^3)\) Boolean operations and one \(p\times p\) Boolean matrix.

Initialize \(K_0=\{(i,j)\in V^2:i\ne j\}\), and after processing the
\(\ell\)-th tuple set
\[
 K_\ell=K_{\ell-1}\cap W_\ell^{(p)}.
 \tag{7}
\]
By induction on \(\ell\), (7) gives
\(K_n=\bigcap_{\ell=1}^n
\operatorname{TC}^{+}(\mathcal U(T_\ell))\), which equals
\(\mathcal A_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\) by (3)--(4).  Each intersection
costs \(O(p^2)\), absorbed by \(O(p^3)\) because \(p\geq2\).  Summing (5)--(7)
over all outputs proves the additional-time bound
\[
 O\!\left(N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}(mp^2+p^3)\right).
\]
The edge/closure matrix can be overwritten during (6), and only it and the
accumulator \(K_\ell\) are retained between updates.  Thus, beyond the current
streamed tuple, the working storage is \(O(p^2)\); no earlier tuple is stored.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The proposition isolates the nontrivial ancestry intersection to the pairing-informative mechanism fiber. \textit{Gap.} The published relation is empty and cannot serve as the parent workflow's sharper subclass output. \textit{Consumer.} The workflow receives exactly the ancestry shared by every component-aligned covariance-compatible mechanism.

\begin{theorem}[thm:component-aligned-exact-once-output-sensitive-enumeration]\label{thm:component-aligned-exact-once-output-sensitive-enumeration}
Fix \((P,B^0,\varepsilon,\Gamma,\pi_0)\in\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) and observed moments \(\mathcal O_G\) on an arbitrary view graph \(G\).  Then \(\operatorname{Enum}_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\) emits exactly \(\Theta_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\), once per numerical tuple, and computes \(\mathcal A_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G)\).  In the ordered exact-real model, its total time is
\[
 O\!\left((m+|E|)p^3 2^p
 +p^3 2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}
 +mp^2N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}\right),
\]
and its time and space are \(O(\operatorname{poly}(p,m)2^p\max\{1,N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}\})\).  It uses deterministic exact row interning and integer tries, not a real hash oracle or an enumeration of all \(p!\) orders.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induction on subset size proves that \(H_A^{\mathrm{CA}}\) is exactly the set of distinct fragments of prefixes in \(\mathcal D_G^{\mathrm{live}}\) ending at \(A\).  The induction step follows every live edge and writes its immutable row; merging is safe and complete by \(\texttt{lem:component-aligned-live-fragment-right-congruence}\).  At \(V\), fragments are full tuples, and \(\texttt{thm:component-aligned-fiber-projection-exactness}\) gives
\[
 \{\text{decoded members of }H_V^{\mathrm{CA}}\}
 =\Theta_G^{\mathrm{CA}}(\mathcal O_G).
\]
Exact lexicographic row interning identifies precisely equal numerical rows, and a deterministic binary trie retains precisely one copy of each integer fragment.  This proves exact-once output.

For preprocessing, compute each within-view Schur state and each observed cross-view Schur state directly from \(\mathcal O_G\).  For a fixed subset, dense factorizations and matrix products cost \(O((m+|E|)p^3)\); over all subsets this is \(O((m+|E|)p^3 2^p)\).  The \(q\)-tests cost \(O(|E|p^2 2^p)\), and constructing and pruning the at most \(p2^{p-1}\) edges costs \(O(p2^p)\), both absorbed.  Computing all immutable rows and sorting them by original row label costs \(O(mp^3 2^p)\), also absorbed.

Each integer-fragment trie operation costs \(O(p^2)\).  By \(\texttt{lem:component-aligned-projection-output-bound}\), at most \(p2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) transitions are attempted, giving \(O(p^3 2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}})\) propagation time.  Decoding costs \(O(mp^2N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}})\).  The ancestry computation of \(\texttt{prop:component-aligned-common-union-support-ancestry}\) costs \(O(N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}(mp^2+p^3))\), absorbed by the preceding two terms because \(p,m\ge2\).  This proves the displayed time bound.

State covariances and interned rows occupy \(O((m+|E|)p^2 2^p)\) exact-real cells.  The fragment tries occupy \(O(p^2 2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}})\) bits or pointers by the projection bound, outputs occupy \(O(mp^2N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}})\) cells, and the ancestry accumulator occupies \(O(p^2)\) bits.  The output set is nonempty by \(\texttt{thm:component-aligned-fiber-projection-exactness}\).  These bounds imply the stated polynomial-envelope space bound.  No loop traverses permutations: all propagation is over live subset edges and retained numerical fragments.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} This is the pairing-informative deliverable: exact-once q-pruned enumeration with the explicit \((m+|E|)\)-dependent preprocessing bound. \textit{Gap.} The earlier full-lattice theorem did not exploit valid component-aligned edge information. \textit{Consumer.} Component-aligned multi-view analyses obtain the pruned coefficient frontier and its common ancestry.

\begin{theorem}[thm:rational-component-aligned-subset-exponential-bit-enumeration]\label{thm:rational-component-aligned-subset-exponential-bit-enumeration}
Under the premises of \(\texttt{thm:component-aligned-exact-once-output-sensitive-enumeration}\), suppose every entry of the rational input \(\mathcal O_G\) has binary encoding length at most \(L_{\mathrm{bits}}\). A fraction-free implementation of \(\operatorname{Enum}_G^{\mathrm{CA}}\), including exact \(q\)-DAG construction and component-aligned common-ancestry computation, has a deterministic subset-exponential, output-sensitive total bit bound: its time and storage are polynomial in \(p\), \(m\), \(L_{\mathrm{bits}}\), \(2^p\), \(N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}\), and the total emitted encoding length. This is not output-polynomial in the conventional input-plus-output sense because \(2^p\) remains explicit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We verify first the positivity and height conditions needed by the component-aligned preprocessing.  Fix a maintained mechanism and a view \(a\in M\).  By \(\texttt{ass:component-aligned-disturbances}\), distinct coordinates of \(\varepsilon^{(a)}\) are uncorrelated.  By \(\texttt{ass:disturbance-positive-definiteness}\),
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_P(\varepsilon_j^{(a)})
 =\Gamma_j[a,a]>0
 \qquad (j\in V).
\tag{5}
\]
Thus \(\Omega^{(a)}=\operatorname{Cov}_P(\varepsilon^{(a)})\) is diagonal and positive definite.  The structural equation and shared-order assumptions give, exactly as in (1),
\[
 \Sigma^{aa}=(I_p-B^{0,(a)})^{-1}
 \Omega^{(a)}(I_p-B^{0,(a)})^{-\mathsf T}\succ0.
\tag{6}
\]
Therefore every principal system used in an immutable row or projected residual state is nonsingular.

For \(A\subseteq V\), write
\[
 S_A^{(a)}=\Sigma^{aa}[U_A,A]
 \{\Sigma^{aa}[A,A]\}^{-1},
\]
with the evident zero matrix when \(A=\varnothing\).  The denominator-clearing, Bareiss, determinant bound, and canonical reduction proved in \(\texttt{thm:rational-output-polynomial-bit-enumeration}\) apply verbatim to every coordinate of \(S_A^{(a)}\) and every immutable row \(\beta(A,r)\), because their coefficient matrices are the positive-definite principal matrices in (6).  In particular these coordinates have binary height polynomial in \(p\) and \(L_{\mathrm{bits}}\).

Expanding the covariance of the two residuals in \(\texttt{def:projected-residual-state}\) by bilinearity gives, on every observed diagonal block and oriented edge \((a,b)\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 C_A^{ab}
 ={}&\Sigma^{ab}[U_A,U_A]
 -S_A^{(a)}\Sigma^{ab}[A,U_A]
 -\Sigma^{ab}[U_A,A](S_A^{(b)})^{\mathsf T}\\
 &+S_A^{(a)}\Sigma^{ab}[A,A](S_A^{(b)})^{\mathsf T}.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
This is a direct formula in the original input blocks and two principal-system solutions; no state \(C_{A'}\) for a smaller subset is substituted into it.  Each coordinate of (7) is a sum of at most a polynomial number of products of polynomial-height rationals.  Repeated common-denominator multiplication can increase binary length by at most the number of summands times the largest operand length.  Hence every coordinate of every \(C_A^{ab}\) has binary height polynomial in \(p\) and \(L_{\mathrm{bits}}\), uniformly over the \(2^p\) subsets.

For the division in the root score, the within-view specialization of (7) is the Schur complement
\[
 C_A^{bb}
 =\Sigma^{bb}[U_A,U_A]
 -\Sigma^{bb}[U_A,A]
  \{\Sigma^{bb}[A,A]\}^{-1}
  \Sigma^{bb}[A,U_A].
\tag{8}
\]
The Schur complement of a principal block of the positive-definite matrix in (6) is positive definite: indeed, for nonzero \(y\in\mathbb R^{U_A}\), set
\[
 x_A=-\{\Sigma^{bb}[A,A]\}^{-1}
       \Sigma^{bb}[A,U_A]y,
 \qquad x_{U_A}=y.
\]
Then direct block multiplication yields
\[
 y^{\mathsf T}C_A^{bb}y
 =x^{\mathsf T}\Sigma^{bb}x>0.
\tag{9}
\]
In particular \(C_A^{bb}[r,r]>0\) for every \(r\in U_A\).  The division in \(\texttt{def:q-root-score}\) is therefore valid, and
\[
 q^{ab}(A,r,t)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 C_A^{ab}[r,t]C_A^{bb}[r,r]
 -C_A^{ab}[r,r]C_A^{bb}[r,t]=0.
\tag{10}
\]
Both sides of the integer cross multiplication in (10) have polynomial binary height by (7).  Thus every edge decision in \(\mathcal D_G\), both orientations included, is an exact polynomial-bit-cost decision.  Source-to-sink pruning then uses only the resulting finite Boolean graph.  The maintained-model support and positive-definite-completion validity of this graph are supplied by \(\texttt{thm:qpath-completion-exactness}\); the exact coefficient projection and exact-once output statement are supplied by \(\texttt{thm:component-aligned-exact-once-output-sensitive-enumeration}\).

The latter theorem bounds the number of exact-real operations by
\[
 O\!\left((m+|E|)p^3 2^p
 +p^3 2^pN_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}
 +mp^2N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}\right)
\tag{11}
\]
and bounds storage by a polynomial in \(p\), \(m\), \(2^p\), and \(N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}}\).  Equations (5)--(10) show that every rational arithmetic operation or comparison in the direct Schur-state and \(q\)-DAG preprocessing has polynomial-height operands and polynomial bit cost.  The cited Bareiss lemma and the published rational theorem give the same conclusion for immutable-row certificates.  Fragment propagation uses only canonical integer identifiers.  Exact support tests on decoded rational rows, Boolean transitive closure, and intersection compute component-aligned common ancestry within the last term of (11), without increasing rational height.

Multiplying (11) and the corresponding storage count by the polynomial per-operation bit cost, and adding the total emitted encoding length, proves that total bit time and storage are polynomial in
\[
 p,\quad m,\quad L_{\mathrm{bits}},\quad 2^p,
 \quad N_{\mathrm{out}}^{\mathrm{CA}},\quad
 \text{and the total emitted encoding length}.
\]
Because every state is evaluated directly by (7), the height estimate is not recursively exponentiated through the subset lattice.  The factor \(2^p\) nevertheless remains explicit even when the numerical output set is small.  Thus this is a deterministic subset-exponential, output-sensitive total bit bound, not an output-polynomial bound in the conventional input-plus-output sense.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The rational lift covers direct Schur states, root-score equality, live pruning, tuple output, and subclass ancestry with explicit subset-state dependence. \textit{Gap.} Bareiss control of immutable rows alone does not bound rational root-score DAG preprocessing. \textit{Consumer.} Exact symbolic component-aligned implementations have an explicit subset-exponential, output-sensitive total bit bound.

\section{Interpretation}

Unrestricted cross-view pairing is causally uninformative at the covariance level in this model: every order has a Gaussian covariance witness, and choosing the reverse order for any pair proves that published common ancestry is empty. Component alignment changes the conclusion. Its observed edge blocks prune the order set through the live \(q\)-DAG, and the exact-once stream yields a potentially nonempty common ancestry intersection. Numerical fragment merging can be substantial in both modes because many structural orders may induce the same coefficient tuple.

\section*{Technical internal limitation (diagnostic only)}

The guarantees are total output-sensitive bounds over an explicit \(2^p\) state space, not polynomial delay, not polynomial time in \(p\), and not output-polynomial in the conventional input-plus-output sense. Ordered exact-real arithmetic is not a floating-point stability guarantee; for rational inputs the bit theorems instead control certificate height and total bit cost as polynomials in parameter lists that explicitly include \(2^p\). The \(q\)-DAG theorem requires maintained component-aligned inputs and does not provide polynomial preprocessing for arbitrary off-model nonchordal partial covariances. Gaussianity is used only to construct existential completion witnesses, not assumed of the generating law. The results enumerate exact population coefficient sets, not continuum-valued covariance-region inverses.

\section{Honest scope}

The published shared-order class retains arbitrary cross-view disturbance covariance: pairing supplies neither order pruning nor common ancestry there, although the full-lattice algorithm still removes numerical duplicates without enumerating permutations. The pairing-informative result is exactly the component-aligned subclass with positive-definite coordinate covariance blocks, on an arbitrary observed view graph and at model-generated population moments. It proves \(q\)-path/fiber equivalence, exact-once tuple enumeration, nontrivial ancestry post-processing, ordered-exact-real complexity, and deterministic rational subset-exponential, output-sensitive total bit complexity with explicit \(2^p\) dependence. It does not claim unrestricted LiMVAM identification, conventional output-polynomial complexity, polynomial delay, off-model nonchordal completion, floating-point robustness, or finite enumeration of the parent's covariance-region inverse.
