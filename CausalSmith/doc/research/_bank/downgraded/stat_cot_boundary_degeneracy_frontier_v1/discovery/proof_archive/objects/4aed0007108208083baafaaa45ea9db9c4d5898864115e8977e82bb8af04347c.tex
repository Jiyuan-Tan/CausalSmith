Assume \(L>1\), and put \(a_y=\min\{s_y,1\}\). For every \(d\ge1\), there are constants \(0<c_1<C_1<\infty\), depending only on the fixed class constants, such that
\[
c_1\mathfrak R_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)\le\inf_{\widetilde\theta_n}\sup_{(P_{0,n},P_{1,n})\in\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)}\{E(\widetilde\theta_n-\theta_n)^2\}^{1/2}
\]
\[
\le C_1\min\!\left[\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+r_{n,d}(s_z)+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\right],
\]
where \(\mathfrak R_{n,d}=\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}\}\). The observable upper bound uses \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y^*,J_z^*,n}\), or the zero estimator when \(\rho\lesssim\rho_{Q,n}\). The quadratic and zero-zone lower bounds hold on the explicit sign-paired family in the mixture-check lemma. In the zone \(\rho\gg n^{-1/2}\), extending \(q_{a,t}^{0}\) constantly in \(z\) and comparing \(t=c_0\rho\) with \(t=c_0\rho+c_0n^{-1/2}\) supplies the regular \(\rho n^{-1/2}\) lower bound. The bracket is matched whenever
\[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
=O\{\rho n^{-1/2}+r_{n,d}(s_z)\}.
\]
In this matching region the intrinsic quadratic elbow is \(s_z=d/4\): when \(s_z>d/4\), \(\rho_{Q,n}=n^{-2s_z/(4s_z+d)}\) and \(\rho_{L,n}=n^{(d-4s_z)/(2(4s_z+d))}\) delimit zero, quadratic, mixed, and regular zones; when \(s_z\le d/4\), the quadratic term prevents a positive-variance root-\(n\) rate uniformly over the class. Outside the matching region, the displayed two-sided bracket is the conclusion and its tightness is not asserted.
