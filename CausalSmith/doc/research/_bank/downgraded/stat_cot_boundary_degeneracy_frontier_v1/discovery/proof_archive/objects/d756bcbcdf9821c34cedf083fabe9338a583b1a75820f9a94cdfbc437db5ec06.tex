For \(d\ge1\), choose \(J_z^*\) and \(J_y^*\) by
\[
2^{J_z^*}\asymp n^{2/(4s_z+d)},
\qquad
2^{J_y^*}\asymp n^{4s_z/\{(2s_y+1)(4s_z+d)\}}.
\]
Then
\[
2^{-2s_zJ_z^*}\asymp\frac{2^{dJ_z^*/2}}n
\asymp2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y^*}
\asymp r_{n,d}(s_z),
\]
and
\[
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}
=n^{\{4s_z/(2s_y+1)+2d\}/(4s_z+d)+o(1)}=o(n^2).
\]
