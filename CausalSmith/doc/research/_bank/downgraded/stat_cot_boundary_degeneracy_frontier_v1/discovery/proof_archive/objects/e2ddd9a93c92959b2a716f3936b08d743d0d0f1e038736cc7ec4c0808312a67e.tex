Put \(e=\beta/(2\beta+1)\), \(b=4s_z/(4s_z+d)\), and \(\kappa=2/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)\).  For \(d\ge1\), uniform absorption over the declared local-radius range means
\[
\sup_{0\le\rho\le1}
\frac{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^3+\rho^\kappa\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2}
{\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-b}}=O(1).
\]
This absorption holds, with the strict exponent inequalities forced by the logarithmic pilot factor, if and only if
\[
s_z>\frac{3d}{4}
\quad\text{and}\quad
s_y>
\frac{s_z\!\left[11d+4s_z+\sqrt{(4s_z-d)(100s_z+23d)}\right]}
{2(4s_z-3d)(2s_z+d)}.
\]
Equivalently, the second condition is
\[
(4s_z-3d)(2s_z+d)s_y^2-s_z(11d+4s_z)s_y-12s_z^2>0.
\]
Consequently, throughout exactly this region the observable upper bound in \(\text{thm:conditional-minimax-frontier}\) attains the intrinsic frontier uniformly in \(0\le\rho\le1\).  Thus \(s_z=d/4\) is the intrinsic minimax elbow, whereas uniform sharpness of the present observable pilot construction begins only beyond \(s_z=3d/4\) and the displayed outcome-smoothness threshold.  Outside this region the theorem's pilot-remainder bracket remains the conclusion, and equality of its two sides is not asserted.

There is also a supplemental global estimator on the existing class when \(s_z\ge1\).  Choose an odd \(K_n\asymp\log n\), split each arm into \(K_n\) disjoint blocks of size \(N_n=\lfloor n/K_n\rfloor\), and on every block use cells of side \(N_n^{-1/(d+2)}\).  Project each covariate to its cell center; at each occupied center form the empirical conditional outcome law; optimally couple the two projected empirical covariate marginals in Euclidean distance; and integrate the squared Wasserstein distance between the paired empirical conditional outcome laws.  Let the final estimator be the median of these \(K_n\) block values.  Uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)\),
\[
\left\{E(\widehat\theta_n^{\mathrm{dir,med}}-\theta_n)^2\right\}^{1/2}
\lesssim n^{-1/(d+2)}(\log n)^{1+1/(d+2)}.
\]
Accordingly, for \(s_z\ge1\) the upper side of the minimax bracket may be sharpened to the minimum of \(\rho^2\), the theorem's pilot-remainder envelope, and this direct-COT envelope.  This supplemental bound neither changes the intrinsic converse nor closes the pilot-dominated bracket in general.
