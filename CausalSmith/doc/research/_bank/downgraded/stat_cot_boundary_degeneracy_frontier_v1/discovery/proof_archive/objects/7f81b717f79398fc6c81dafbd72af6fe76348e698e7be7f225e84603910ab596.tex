The observable Gram expansion requires uniform control of a density-dependent ridge Galerkin inverse. After retaining the actual full-minus-projected pilot-error energy, its optimized certified scale is strictly slower than \(n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}\) for every finite positive smoothness pair. The SmoothCOT transfer gives a separate full-slice upper bound only on the fixed-radius positive isotropic class and under fixed compactly supported boundary wavelets of regularity greater than \(\beta_{\mathrm{iso}}\); its exponent does not match the embedded quadratic lower exponent. Full operator-norm covariance estimation additionally requires \(D_{J_y,J_z}\log n=o(m)\) and is used only for an observed spectral proxy, not for the point estimator. None of these results proves a single observable confidence procedure uniformly calibrated across variance degeneracy.
