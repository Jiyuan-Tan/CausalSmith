For fold \(e\), condition on the opposite-fold sigma-field \(\mathscr F_{-e}\). By \(\mathrm{ass{:}two\text{-}arm\text{-}sampling}\), the evaluation observations are independent of \(\mathscr F_{-e}\). Equation (10) in \(\mathrm{lem{:}observable\text{-}pilot\text{-}control}\), the inverse-distribution expansion (11) there, and exact linearity in the common marginal separate the plug-in, the displayed first-order scores, their linear kernel terms, and the conditional mean of the quadratic correction. Averaging the folds gives the exact Hoeffding decomposition
\[
\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n
=\mathbb L_{J_y,J_z,n}+\mathbb U_{J_y,J_z,n}
+B^y_{J_y,n}+B^z_{J_z,n}+R_{J_y,J_z,n}.                          \tag{1}
\]
Here \(\mathbb U\) is the average canonical diagonal-free quadratic term with population kernel \(B_{J_y,J_z,n}\), and \(\mathbb L\) is the oracle first projection. Equations (12)--(18) of the pilot lemma give, uniformly over the model class,
\[
R_{J_y,J_z,n}
=O_P\!\left\{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho_n^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\right\}
+o_P(1)\left\{\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n\right\}. \tag{2}
\]

Let \(H_z(y)=\int_0^y\Delta_n(v,z)dv\) and \(u_z=\mathcal G_{n,z}\Delta_n(\cdot,z)\). Integrating the Neumann equation and using zero conditional mass gives
\[
-\bar q_n(y,z)u_z'(y)=H_z(y),
\qquad
\int_0^1\Delta_nu_z\,dy=\int_0^1\frac{H_z(y)^2}{\bar q_n(y,z)}dy. \tag{3}
\]
Implicit differentiation of the two conditional quantile maps expresses each centered oracle score as a bounded-density multiple of \(H_z\). The lower and upper density bounds in (3) therefore give fixed constants such that
\[
V_{L,n}=n\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb L_{J_y,J_z,n})\asymp\rho_n^2,
\qquad
\operatorname{sd}(\mathbb L_{J_y,J_z,n})\asymp\rho_n/\sqrt n.   \tag{4}
\]
At conditional equality \(H_z=0\), so the derivative and \(\mathbb L\) vanish.

For the canonical projection, independence and diagonal deletion leave only equal or reversed pairs. Direct counting with \(m=n/2\) yields
\[
\begin{split}
\operatorname{Var}(\mathbb U)
={}&\frac{\operatorname{tr}\{(B\Gamma_0)^2\}+\operatorname{tr}\{(B\Gamma_1)^2\}}{m(m-1)}
+\frac{2\operatorname{tr}(B\Gamma_0B\Gamma_1)}{m^2}\\
={}&\frac{4\operatorname{tr}\{(\Gamma^{1/2}B\Gamma^{1/2})^2\}}{n^2}\{1+o(1)\}
=\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2}{n^2}\{1+o(1)\}.                         \tag{5}
\end{split}
\]
The corrected spectrum proposition supplies
\[
|B^y_{J_y,n}|\lesssim2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y},
\qquad |B^z_{J_z,n}|\lesssim2^{-2s_zJ_z}.                         \tag{6}
\]
The theorem's premise turns (2) into little-oh of the sum of the two stochastic scales and the two corrected bias scales. Substitution of (2), (4)--(6) into (1) proves the uniform expansion.

Tail integration of the pilot concentration bound gives fixed fourth moments for every pilot error in (2). Conditional orthogonality of its mean, linear, and canonical parts therefore upgrades (2) to
\[
\|R_{J_y,J_z,n}\|_2
\le C\!\left\{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho_n^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\right\}
+o\!\left\{\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}+\frac A n\right\}.         \tag{7}
\]
At the corrected tuning in \(\mathrm{def{:}anisotropic\text{-}tuning}\),
\[
\frac{A_{J_y^*,J_z^*,n}}n
\asymp\frac{2^{dJ_z^*/2}}n
\asymp2^{-2s_zJ_z^*}
\asymp2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y^*}
\asymp r_{n,d}(s_z).                                               \tag{8}
\]
Moreover,
\[
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}
=n^{\{4s_z/(2s_y+1)+2d\}/(4s_z+d)+o(1)}=o(n^2),                 \tag{9}
\]
because \(4s_z/(2s_y+1)+2d<8s_z+2d=2(4s_z+d)\). Thus the expansion's dimension premise holds at the corrected resolution. Minkowski's inequality applied to (1), using (4)--(9), proves the displayed root-mean-square scale and its absorption sub-regime. No covariance estimate appears in (1)--(9).
