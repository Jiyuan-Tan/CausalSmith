Let
\[
E_{R,n}=C_B\left[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+\mathbf1\{d\ge1\}2^{-s_zJ_z}\}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
\right].
\]
For \(d\ge1\), define the infeasible population proxy
\[
G_n^\circ=\sum_{e=1}^2\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}
\{(\xi_{e,k}+\eta_{k,n})^2-(1+\eta_{k,n}^2)\},
\]
and let \(q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ\) be a \((1-\alpha/2)\)-quantile of \(|G_n^\circ|\).  The mixed spectral theorem and its uniformly nondegenerate normal approximation imply
\[
P\{|n(\Theta^{\mathrm{or}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n)|
\le q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ\}\ge1-\alpha/2-o(1),
\qquad q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ=O(S_n).
\]
The uniform expansion and Markov's inequality then show that the infeasible interval centered at \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}\) with half-width
\[
q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ/n+C_\alpha\{E_{R,n}+r_n^\circ\},
\qquad
r_n^\circ=o\{\rho n^{-1/2}+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n\},
\]
has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) and half-width
\[
O\{E_{R,n}+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n+\rho n^{-1/2}\}.
\]
At \((J_y^*,J_z^*)\), this equals the requested deterministic order.  This proves population calibratability but not an observable confidence interval.

The missing observable step is not supplied by the frozen controls.  For example, take \(d=1\), \(s_y=1/10\), and \(s_z=1\).  Then \(\beta=1/11\), \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}=(\log n/n)^{1/13}\), and
\[
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}\asymp(n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n})^{14/11},
\qquad
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}\log n/m
\asymp n^{25/143}(\log n)^{157/143}\to\infty.
\]
Thus the sufficient covariance-consistency premise fails.  The available operator-norm Hessian error also gives only
\[
\frac{\sqrt{D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{1/10}}
{\|K_{J_y^*,J_z^*,n}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}}
\asymp n^{389/1430}(\log n)^{133/4290}\to\infty,
\]
so relative spectral consistency and common-multiplier coupling cannot be deduced.  For \(d=0\), the exact null chaos does not itself furnish a coupling and anti-concentration argument uniform through local alternatives to the regular Gaussian regime.  The observable calibration question therefore remains open; this is an obstruction in the available proof conditions, not an impossibility theorem.
