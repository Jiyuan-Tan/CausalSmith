The remaining step is a uniform conditional multiplier theorem for the joint linear and diagonal-free quadratic statistic at growing anisotropic rank, including anti-concentration uniformly when the linear variance vanishes.  The frozen handle does not define the multiplier statistic, its conditional quantile, or a certified value of \(C_B\), and the proved population triangular approximation alone does not imply conditional quantile consistency.  One must still show normalized Hilbert--Schmidt consistency of the random pilot Hessian and covariance inside the multiplier law, uniform spectral-tail truncation, conditional Lindeberg replacement, and anti-concentration for the resulting mixed Gaussian polynomial, simultaneously over \(0\le\rho\le1\).  The strongest closed part is the proposed population local triangular law, equality Gaussianization when \(J_z\to\infty\), the separate regular studentized CLT, and the deterministic bias scale \(r_{n,d}(s_z)\); these do not by themselves certify honest coverage.
