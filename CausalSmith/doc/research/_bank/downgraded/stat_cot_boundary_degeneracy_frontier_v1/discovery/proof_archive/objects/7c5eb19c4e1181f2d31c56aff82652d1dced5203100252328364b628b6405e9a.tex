Put \(p_{a,n}(y,z)=q_{a,n}(y,z)r_n(z)\). Products preserve the declared anisotropic H\"older ball up to a constant depending only on \(C,L\). At the pilot resolutions, the tensor wavelet approximation error in supremum norm is bounded by
\[
C\{2^{-s_y\bar J_y}+2^{-s_z\bar J_z}\}\lesssim\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}. \tag{1}
\]
At any point only a fixed number of compactly supported wavelets at each terminal scale are nonzero. Expanding the exponential moment of each bounded centered empirical coefficient gives a quadratic exponential tail up to the bounded-summand cutoff. Taking a union bound over \(O(2^{\bar J_y+d\bar J_z})\) coefficients and applying the compact-support reconstruction bound gives
\[
\max_{a,e}\|\widetilde p_{a,n}^{(-e)}-p_{a,n}\|_\infty
+\max_e\|\widetilde r_n^{(-e)}-r_n\|_\infty
=O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n})                     \tag{2}
\]
uniformly over the model class. The denominators \(r_n\ge c\), the ratio identity
\[
\frac{\widetilde p}{\widetilde r}-\frac p r
=\frac{\widetilde p-p}{\widetilde r}
+p\frac{r-\widetilde r}{r\widetilde r},               \tag{3}
\]
and (2) prove the same rate for the raw conditional pilots. Since the true densities lie in the strict interior of the clipping bounds \(c/2,2C\), (2) implies \(P(\cap_e\mathcal E_n^{(-e)})\to1\). Clipping and normalization are locally Lipschitz on that event and enforce positivity and unit conditional mass on its complement, proving the first two conclusions.

Let \(A_z=P_{J_y}\{-\partial_y(\bar q_n\partial_y)\}P_{J_y}\) and let \(\widehat A_z\) be its pilot analogue. On the zero-mean subspace, \(\|v\|_2\le\|\partial_yv\|_2\), obtained by writing \(v(y)=\int_0^1\{v(y)-v(x)\}dx\), and ellipticity give \(A_z\succeq c_PI\) uniformly. The variational form gives the relative energy bound
\[
|\langle v,(\widehat A_z-A_z)w\rangle|
\le C\|\widehat{\bar q}-\bar q\|_\infty
\langle v,A_zv\rangle^{1/2}\langle w,A_zw\rangle^{1/2}.
\]
The resolvent identity
\[
(\widehat A_z+\lambda_nI)^{-1}-A_z^{-1}
=(\widehat A_z+\lambda_nI)^{-1}
\{A_z-\widehat A_z-\lambda_nI\}A_z^{-1}              \tag{4}
\]
therefore has operator norm \(O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}+\lambda_n)\). Integration against \(\widehat r_n\), using (2), proves the displayed Hessian bound.

Parameterize a pair by its average density \(\bar q\) and discrepancy \(\Delta\). Arm exchange sends \(\Delta\) to \(-\Delta\) without changing \(W_2^2\), so the functional is even in \(\Delta\). The quantile implicit equation used in the cosine proof, twice polarized and integrated, shows on the density set \([c/2,2C]\) that the equality Hessian is Lipschitz in \(\bar q\), while its difference from the Hessian at \((\bar q,\Delta)\) is \(O(\|\Delta\|_\infty)\) as a bilinear form on the pilot error.

The anisotropic interpolation inequality implied by the declared H\"older ball and the zero outcome mass is
\[
\|\Delta_n\|_\infty
\lesssim \rho_n^{\,2/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}.                 \tag{5}
\]
Indeed, if \(A=\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\), H\"older regularity makes an amplitude of order \(A\) persist on an outcome interval of length proportional to \(A^{1/s_y}\) and a covariate rectangle of volume proportional to \(A^{d/s_z}\). Its zero-mass outcome antiderivative has size of order \(A^{1+1/s_y}\) on an outcome interval of length proportional to \(A^{1/s_y}\). Ellipticity and \(r_n\ge c\) therefore give \(\rho_n^2\gtrsim A^{2+3/s_y+d/s_z}\), proving (5), including at support boundaries by one-sided rectangles.

Taylor's identity with integral remainder gives
\[
O_P\{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^3+
\rho_n^{\,2/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\}.                       \tag{6}
\]
The first term is the equality third-order remainder; the second is the unequal-law Hessian mismatch. Replacing the equality Hessian by its ridge pilot contributes \(O_P\{(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}+\lambda_n)\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\}\), which is absorbed because \(\lambda_n=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\). Sample splitting makes the remaining centered operator terms \(o_P(1)\{\rho_n/\sqrt n+A/n\}\). This proves the proposed local-quadratic remainder and its stated negligibility condition.

For the covariance assertion, \(\|\Phi(W)\|_2^2\lesssim D_{J_y,J_z}\), and covariance is uniformly bounded and positive by the spectrum proposition. Applying the same exponential-moment argument to quadratic forms on a fixed net of the unit sphere yields
\[
\max_e\|\widehat\Gamma^{(-e)}-\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_P\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{D_{J_y,J_z}\log n}{m}}+
\frac{D_{J_y,J_z}\log n}{m}\right)=o_P(1).            \tag{7}
\]
On the resulting fixed spectral interval, the matrix square-root map is Lipschitz. Combining (4)--(7) in the definition of \(K\) proves \(\max_e\|\widehat K^{(-e)}-K\|_{\mathrm{op}}=o_P(1)\).
