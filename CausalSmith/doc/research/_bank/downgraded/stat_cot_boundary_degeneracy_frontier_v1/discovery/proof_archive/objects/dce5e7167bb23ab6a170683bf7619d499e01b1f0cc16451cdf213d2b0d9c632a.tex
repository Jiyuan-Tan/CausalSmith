The corrected population result supplies the deterministic envelope
\[
C_B\{2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y}+\mathbf 1\{d\ge1\}2^{-2s_zJ_z}\},

the growing-rank Gaussian-quadratic approximation, forced equality Gaussianization for \(d\ge1\), the separate regular studentized central limit theorem, and the exact \(d=0\) quadratic-process law. It does not define a common-multiplier statistic, a conditional critical value, or an interval. Uniform coverage still requires a conditional multiplier coupling for the joint linear and diagonal-free quadratic terms, normalized Hilbert--Schmidt consistency of the pilot Hessian and covariance in that conditional law, uniform spectral-tail truncation, and anti-concentration that remains valid as the linear variance vanishes. These are not consequences of the proved population bounded-Lipschitz approximation, so honest mixed calibration remains open.
