Definition 3.4, Proposition 3.6, and Theorem 4.3 define and estimate the finite-\(\eta\) mirror-penalized relaxation \(V_{\mathrm{ip}}(\eta)\), which lies between the unconditional and conditional targets and converges to the conditional target only as \(\eta\to\infty\). This is distinct from exact adapted COT and supplies neither equality-local minimax theory nor mixed-regime inference.
