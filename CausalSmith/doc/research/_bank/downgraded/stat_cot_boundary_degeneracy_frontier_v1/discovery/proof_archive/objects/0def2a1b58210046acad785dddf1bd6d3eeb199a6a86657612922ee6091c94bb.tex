\[
q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ/n+C_\alpha(E_{R,n}+r_n^\circ)
\]
has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\), where \(E_{R,n}\) is the displayed deterministic pilot-remainder envelope and \(r_n^\circ=o\{\rho n^{-1/2}+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n\}\). Its half-width is

O\{E_{R,n}+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n+\rho n^{-1/2}\}.
\]
