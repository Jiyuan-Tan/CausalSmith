For \(d\ge1\), abbreviate the deterministic remainder envelope by
\[
E_{R,n}=C_B\left[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-s_zJ_z}\}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
\right].
\]
On any triangular collection satisfying the score-approximation premise of \(\mathrm{thm{:}mixed\text{-}spectral\text{-}transition}\), suppose also that a deterministic \(r_n^\circ=o(S_n/n)\) uniformly dominates in \(L^2\) the little-oh remainder from \(\mathrm{thm{:}uniform\text{-}second\text{-}order\text{-}expansion}\). Define
\[
G_n^\circ=\sum_{e=1}^2\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}
\{(\xi_{e,k}+\eta_{k,n})^2-(1+\eta_{k,n}^2)\},
\]
and let \(q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ\) be the \((1-\alpha/2)\)-quantile of \(|G_n^\circ|\). For a sufficiently large constant \(C_\alpha\), the infeasible interval
\[
\mathcal I_n^\circ=
\left[
\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}
-\frac{q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ}{n}-C_\alpha(E_{R,n}+r_n^\circ),
\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}
+\frac{q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ}{n}+C_\alpha(E_{R,n}+r_n^\circ)
\right]
\]
has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) uniformly over that collection. Indeed, if \(B_n=\sqrt{nV_{L,n}}\), then the variance of the normalized Gaussian proxy is
\[
\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2+B_n^2}{(A_{J_y,J_z,n}+B_n)^2}\in[1/2,1],
\]
so its anti-concentration modulus is uniform. The established bounded-Lipschitz approximations therefore control the oracle term, while Markov's inequality controls the remainder after taking \(C_\alpha\) large enough. Its half-width is
\[
O\!\left(E_{R,n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n+sqrt{\frac{V_{L,n}}n}\right)
\le
O\!\left(E_{R,n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n+\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}\right).
\]
At the corrected resolutions this is the width requested for the population-calibrated interval. It does not furnish an observable interval: the available plug-in spectral control requires \(D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m=o(1)\), which is not implied by the primitive model and corrected tuning. For example, with \(d=1\), \(s_y=1/10\), and \(s_z=1\), one has \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}=(\log n/n)^{1/13}\) and
\[
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}=\{n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\}^{14/11+o(1)},
\qquad
\frac{D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}\log n}{m}
=n^{25/143+o(1)}(\log n)^{1+14/143+o(1)}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
No established result supplies the missing conditional common-multiplier approximation, uniform noncentral spectral-coordinate control, or a \(d=0\) observable quantile coupling across equality, local alternatives, and fixed unequal laws.
