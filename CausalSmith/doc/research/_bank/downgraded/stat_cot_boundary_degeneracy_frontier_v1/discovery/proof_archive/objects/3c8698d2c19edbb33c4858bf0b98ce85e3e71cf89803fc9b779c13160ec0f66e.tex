Let \(d=0\).  Write \(F_a\) for the distribution functions, \(Q_a=F_a^{-1}\), \(D=Q_1-Q_0\), \(\widehat D=\widehat Q_{1,n}-\widehat Q_{0,n}\), and \(P_J=P^Q_{J_y}\).  The one-dimensional increasing-coupling identity gives
\[
\theta_n=\|D\|_2^2,
\qquad
\widehat\theta^0_{J_y,n}=\|P_J\widehat D\|_2^2. \tag{1}
\]
Since \(q_{a,n}\ge c\), every \(Q_a\) is \(c^{-1}\)-Lipschitz.  Under the probability integral transform, \(\widehat Q_{a,n}(u)=Q_a(U_{a,(k)})\), \(k=\lceil nu\rceil\), where \(U_{a,(k)}\) is a uniform order statistic.  Beta-density integration gives, for \(j=1,2,3,4\),
\[
E\{U_{a,(k)}^j\}=\prod_{r=0}^{j-1}\frac{k+r}{n+1+r}. \tag{2}
\]
Expanding centered powers and using \(|k/(n+1)-u|\le2/n\) yields
\[
\sup_{a,u}E|U_{a,(k)}-u|^2\lesssim n^{-1},
\qquad
\sup_{a,u}E|U_{a,(k)}-u|^4\lesssim n^{-2}. \tag{3}
\]
Lipschitz continuity, Tonelli, and Jensen therefore imply
\[
E\|\widehat D-D\|_2^2\lesssim n^{-1},
\qquad E\|\widehat D-D\|_2^4\lesssim n^{-2}. \tag{4}
\]

The identity \(Q_a'=1/(q_{a,n}\circ Q_a)\), the lower density bound, and H\"older composition rules show that \(Q_a\in\mathcal C^{s_y+1}\) uniformly.  Wavelet coefficient decay then gives
\[
\|(I-P_J)D\|_2^2\lesssim2^{-2(s_y+1)J_y}. \tag{5}
\]
With \(E_J=P_J(\widehat D-D)\) and \(B_J=(I-P_J)D\), orthogonality gives
\[
\widehat\theta^0_{J_y,n}-\theta_n
=2\langle P_JD,E_J\rangle+\|E_J\|_2^2-\|B_J\|_2^2. \tag{6}
\]
Equations (4)--(6), Cauchy--Schwarz, and contraction of \(P_J\) imply
\[
\|\widehat\theta^0_{J_y,n}-\theta_n\|_2
\lesssim\|D\|_2n^{-1/2}+n^{-1}+2^{-2(s_y+1)J_y}. \tag{7}
\]
The energy comparison proved in the conditional minimax theorem gives \(\|D\|_2^2=\theta_n\lesssim\rho_n^2\le\rho^2\).  At \(J_y=J_y^0\), the last term in (7) is \(o(n^{-1})\), proving the \(O(\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-1})\) risk.  The zero estimator gives \(O(\rho^2)\), establishing the upper bound.

For the lower bound, compare the fixed-frequency laws at \(t=0\) and \(t'=c_0\min\{\rho,n^{-1/2}\}\).  The explicit-mixture lemma puts them in the class and bounds their product Hellinger distance away from its maximum.  The cosine second variation and continuity of the second derivative give separation
\[
|\Theta(t')-\Theta(0)|\gtrsim\min\{\rho^2,n^{-1}\}. \tag{8}
\]
For \(\rho\ge Kn^{-1/2}\), compare \(t=c_1\rho\) and \(t'=c_1\rho+c_2n^{-1/2}\).  The exact radius \(\rho(t)=|t|/(2\sqrt2\pi)\) puts both points in the class.  Implicit quantile differentiation and \(\Theta''(0)=1/(4\pi^2)\) give
\[
|\Theta(t')-\Theta(t)|\gtrsim\rho n^{-1/2}. \tag{9}
\]
Thresholding any estimator halfway between endpoints and using the Hellinger affinity gives lower bounds by (8) and, in its regime, (9).  The same three-case split as in the conditional theorem, now with \(r=n^{-1}\), yields
\[
\inf_{\widetilde\theta_n}\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,0}}
\{E_P(\widetilde\theta_n-\theta_n)^2\}^{1/2}
\gtrsim\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-1}\}. \tag{10}
\]

At equality under the uniform density, define
\[
u_{a,n}(t)=\sqrt n\{t-\widehat Q_{a,n}(t)\},
\qquad
u_{a,n}^{\mathrm{tr}}(t)=u_{a,n}(t)\mathbf1\{1/n<t<1-1/n\}.
\]
The established del Barrio--Gin\'e--Utzet lemma gives \(u_{a,n}^{\mathrm{tr}}\Rightarrow\mathbb B_a\) in \(L^2(0,1)\).  Formula (2) for the extreme order statistics gives
\[
E\|u_{a,n}-u_{a,n}^{\mathrm{tr}}\|_2^2=O(n^{-2}), \tag{11}
\]
so the same convergence holds without truncation.  Arm independence gives
\[
X_n:=\sqrt n(\widehat Q_{1,n}-\widehat Q_{0,n})
\Rightarrow\mathbb B_0-\mathbb B_1\stackrel d=\sqrt2\,\mathbb B \tag{12}
\]
in \(L^2\).  Since \(J_y^0\to\infty\), the orthogonal projectors \(P^Q_{J_y^0}\) converge strongly to the identity.  Strong convergence of contractions is uniform on compact sets, and tightness in (12) therefore gives \(\|(I-P^Q_{J_y^0})X_n\|_2=o_P(1)\).  Hence
\[
n\widehat\theta^0_{J_y^0,n}=\|P^Q_{J_y^0}X_n\|_2^2
\Rightarrow2\int_0^1\mathbb B(t)^2dt. \tag{13}
\]
The Brownian-bridge covariance kernel is the Green kernel of the Dirichlet operator.  Direct differentiation gives eigenfunctions \(\sqrt2\sin(\pi kt)\) and eigenvalues \((\pi^2k^2)^{-1}\), so
\[
2\int_0^1\mathbb B(t)^2dt
=\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{2}{\pi^2k^2}\xi_{1,k}^2. \tag{14}
\]
Subtracting the stated finite expectation yields the centered chaos.  Balancing \(\rho^2\), \(\rho n^{-1/2}\), and \(n^{-1}\) gives \(\rho_{Q,n}=\rho_{L,n}=n^{-1/2}\), completing the proof.
