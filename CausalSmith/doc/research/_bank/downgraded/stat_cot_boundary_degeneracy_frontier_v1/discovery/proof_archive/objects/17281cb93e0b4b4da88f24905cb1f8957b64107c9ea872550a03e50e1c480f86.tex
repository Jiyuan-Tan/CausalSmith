For \(d\ge1\), conditional squared Wasserstein distance has inverse-elliptic outcome smoothing and covariate-driven growing rank. The explicit Gram-corrected estimator, median SmoothCOT estimator, applicable direct-COT estimator, and zero estimator give an observable upper envelope, while the sign-paired family gives the intrinsic lower frontier \(\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}\}\); the corrected Gram scale is strictly slower than the intrinsic quadratic scale, so the result is an honest bracket rather than a matched rate. On a recognizable fixed-radius positive isotropic scalar slice, and for fixed compactly supported boundary wavelets of regularity greater than \(\beta_{\mathrm{iso}}\), the same constructions yield the additional quadratic-and-zero bracket \(\min\{\rho^2,n^{-4\beta_{\mathrm{iso}}/(4\beta_{\mathrm{iso}}+d)}\}\) below and \(\min\{\rho^2,n^{-a_\tau}(\log n)^{a_\tau}\}\) above. The population growing-rank experiment Gaussianizes for \(d\ge1\), whereas the separate \(d=0\) branch has the exact weighted Brownian-bridge quadratic law and \(n^{-1}\) scale. Uniform observable mixed-regime calibration remains open.
