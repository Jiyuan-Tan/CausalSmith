For \(d\ge1\), define
\[
E_{R,n}(J_y,J_z,\rho)=C_B\left[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-s_zJ_z}\}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
\right].
\]
On every triangular collection satisfying the premises of \(\mathrm{thm{:}mixed\text{-}spectral\text{-}transition}\), let \(G_n^\circ\) be its population Gaussian--quadratic proxy and let \(q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ\) be a \((1-\alpha/2)\)-quantile of \(|G_n^\circ|\). The transition theorem, its Gaussian anti-concentration consequence, the exact expansion in \(\mathrm{thm{:}uniform\text{-}second\text{-}order\text{-}expansion}\), and Markov's inequality show that the infeasible interval centered at \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}\) with half-width
\[
\frac{q_{n,1-\alpha/2}^\circ}{n}
+C_\alpha\{E_{R,n}(J_y,J_z,\rho)+r_n^\circ\},
\qquad
r_n^\circ=o\{\rho n^{-1/2}+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n\},
\]
has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\). Its half-width is
\[
O\!\left\{E_{R,n}(J_y,J_z,\rho)+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n+\rho n^{-1/2}\right\}.
\]
At \((J_y^*,J_z^*)\), this is exactly the envelope requested by the open-ended question.

The currently proved observable controls do not justify replacing the population quantile by a common-multiplier quantile at those resolutions. For the admissible example \(d=1\), \(s_y=1/10\), and \(s_z=1\), one has \(\beta=1/11\), \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}=(\log n/n)^{1/13}\), and
\[
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}\asymp(n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n})^{14/11},
\qquad
\frac{D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}\log n}{m}
\asymp n^{25/143}(\log n)^{157/143}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Thus the sufficient covariance condition in \(\mathrm{lem{:}observable\text{-}pilot\text{-}control}\) fails. Converting its operator-norm Hessian error to Hilbert--Schmidt norm also gives only
\[
\frac{\sqrt{D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}}\,\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{1/10}}
{\|K_{J_y^*,J_z^*,n}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}}
\asymp n^{389/1430}(\log n)^{133/4290}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
This proves failure of the available sufficient route, not impossibility of every confidence procedure. For \(d=0\), the exact equality law is known, but a single observable coupling and anti-concentration argument uniform from null chaos through local alternatives to the regular Gaussian regime is still absent.
