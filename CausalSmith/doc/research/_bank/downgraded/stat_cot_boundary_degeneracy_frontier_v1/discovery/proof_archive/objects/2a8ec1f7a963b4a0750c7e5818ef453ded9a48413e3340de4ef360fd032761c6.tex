Abbreviate \(M=2^{dJ_z}\), \(K=K_{J_y,J_z,n}\), \(\nu_k=\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}\), \(A=A_{J_y,J_z,n}\), \(V=V_{L,n}\), and \(S=A+\sqrt{nV}\). The expansion theorem and the bias premise give
\[
n\{\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}-\theta_n-B^y-B^z\}=n\mathbb L+n\mathbb U+o_P(S). \tag{1}
\]
Indeed, \(\sqrt{nV}\asymp\rho_n\sqrt n\) by the expansion theorem.

Let \(s_{a,n}\) be the exact centered first-order score and let \(s^{(2)}_{a,J_y,J_z,n}\) be its equality-Hessian linearization on the corrected projection. Applying the inverse-cdf identity used in the pilot lemma once more, now with perturbation \(\Delta_n\), gives
\[
\max_a\|s_{a,n}-s^{(2)}_{a,J_y,J_z,n}\|_{L^2(P_{a,n})}
\le C\rho_n\{\|\Delta_n\|_\infty+2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-s_zJ_z}\}.   \tag{2}
\]
The right side is \(o(\rho_n)\) under the hypotheses. For fold \(e\), put
\[
Z_{e,n}=\Gamma^{-1/2}\sqrt m\{\bar\Phi_{1,e}-\bar\Phi_{0,e}-\delta\},
\qquad
G_n=2\sum_{e=1}^2\sqrt m\,\delta^\top B\Gamma^{1/2}Z_{e,n}.          \tag{3}
\]
Then \(EZ_{e,n}=0\), \(E Z_{e,n}Z_{e,n}^\top=I\), and the folds are independent. In the eigenbasis, \(G_n=2\sum_{e,k}\nu_k\eta_{k,n}Z_{e,k}\). Equation (2) and the bias premise imply
\[
\|n\mathbb L-G_n\|_2=o(S),
\qquad \operatorname{Var}(G_n)=8\sum_k\nu_k^2\eta_{k,n}^2=nV+o(S^2). \tag{4}
\]

The corrected spectrum proposition gives \(A\asymp M^{1/2}\), \(\|K\|_{\mathrm{op}}\asymp1\), and \(M\le D=o(n^2)\), whence \(S/n\to0\). The inequality \(2^{-2s_zJ_z}=O(S/n)\) forces \(J_z\to\infty\), and therefore
\[
M\to\infty,
\qquad \frac{\max_k|\nu_k|}{(\sum_k\nu_k^2)^{1/2}}\lesssim M^{-1/2}\to0. \tag{5}
\]
Thus diffuse Gaussianization is forced by the premises.

Index the independent evaluation observations by \(r\). Canonical centering writes \(Q_n=n\mathbb U=\sum_{r<s}h_{n,rs}(W_r,W_s)\). Pairing indices and using compact covariate-wavelet overlap, the bounded one-dimensional Green kernel, and \(\operatorname{tr}(K^2)\asymp M\), gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}(Q_n)=A^2\{1+o(1)\},
\quad \max_r\sum_{s\ne r}Eh_{n,rs}^2\lesssim M/m,
\quad \sum_{r<s}Eh_{n,rs}^4\lesssim M^3/m^2.                         \tag{6}
\]
Moreover,
\[
\frac{\operatorname{tr}(K^4)}{\operatorname{tr}(K^2)^2}
\le\frac{\|K\|_{\mathrm{op}}^2}{\|K\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2}\lesssim M^{-1}. \tag{7}
\]
These bounds follow by expanding the traces: disjoint wavelet supports kill all covariate-index products except coincident or neighboring supports, leaving \(O(M)\), \(O(M^2)\), and \(O(M^3)\) admissible contractions respectively; the outcome sums are finite because their weights are \(2^{-2j_y}\).

Write \(G_n=\sum_r\ell_{n,r}(W_r)\), order the observations, and set
\[
\mathcal D_{n,r}=\ell_{n,r}(W_r)+\sum_{s<r}h_{n,sr}(W_s,W_r).
\]
This is a martingale-difference array with sum \(G_n+Q_n\). The bounded inverse-map score, (5), \(M=o(m^2)\), and (6)--(7) imply, for every \(\epsilon>0\),
\[
\sum_rE[(\mathcal D_{n,r}/S)^2\mathbf1\{|\mathcal D_{n,r}|>\epsilon S\}]\to0, \tag{8}
\]
and direct expansion of paired edges gives
\[
\frac{E[\{\sum_rE(\mathcal D_{n,r}^2\mid\mathcal F_{r-1})-E(G_n+Q_n)^2\}^2]}{S^4}
\lesssim m^{-1}+M/m^2+M^{-1}+o(1)\to0.                                \tag{9}
\]
Conditional Taylor expansion of \(e^{itx}\), with (8) controlling the third-order remainder by truncation and (9) replacing conditional by unconditional variance, yields
\[
E e^{it(G_n+Q_n)/S}
-\exp\!\left[-\frac{t^2}{2S^2}\{A^2+nV+o(S^2)\}\right]\to0.        \tag{10}
\]

For the declared Gaussian proxy set
\[
\mathcal Q_n^G=\sum_{e,k}\nu_k\{(\xi_{e,k}+\eta_{k,n})^2-(1+\eta_{k,n}^2)\}.
\]
With \(c_{e,k}=\nu_k/S\) and \(l_{e,k}=2\nu_k\eta_{k,n}/S\), direct one-dimensional Gaussian integration gives
\[
\log Ee^{it\mathcal Q_n^G/S}
=\sum_{e,k}\left[-itc_{e,k}-\frac12\log(1-2itc_{e,k})
-\frac{t^2l_{e,k}^2}{2(1-2itc_{e,k})}\right].                       \tag{11}
\]
By (4)--(5), the difference between (11) and the exponent in (10) is bounded by
\[
C_t\max_k\frac{|\nu_k|}{S}
\left\{\frac{A^2}{S^2}+\sum_{e,k}l_{e,k}^2\right\}=o(1).           \tag{12}
\]
Tightness is automatic on \(\mathbb R\), and the same characteristic-function calculation applies along every subsequence of the variance ratio in the compact interval \([1/2,1]\). Thus (10)--(12) prove both bounded-Lipschitz approximations. Equation (1) and (4) transfer them to the estimator.

At equality, \(V=0\), \(\eta=0\), and \(\mathbb L=0\), so (5), (11) give \(n\mathbb U/A\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1)\). If \(D\log n/m=o(1)\), the pilot lemma gives \(\|\widehat K-K\|_{\mathrm{HS}}=o_P(\|K\|_{\mathrm{HS}})\). Conditional Gaussian integration then gives
\[
E[\{Z^\top(\widehat K-K)Z-\operatorname{tr}(\widehat K-K)\}^2\mid\text{pilots}]
=2\|\widehat K-K\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2=o_P(S^2),                       \tag{13}
\]
which proves observed-kernel replacement. At a fixed unequal law the exact score is bounded on the compact support. Expanding the characteristic function of its independent sum gives the ordinary linear central limit law; the stated linear-dominance, bias, and remainder hypotheses remove the quadratic and deterministic terms, and \(\widehat V_{L,n}/V_{L,n}\to_P1\) gives the studentized conclusion. Because (5) is forced, no non-Gaussian chaos is used or asserted.
