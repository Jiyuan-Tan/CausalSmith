Assume \(L>1\), put \(a_y=\min\{s_y,1\}\), and fix \(d\ge1\). For every fixed \(0<\tau<\min\{s_y,s_z\}\), put \(a_\tau=\tau/(2\tau+d+1)\). There are constants \(c_1>0\) and \(C_{1,\tau}<\infty\), depending only on the fixed class constants and, for the upper constant, on \(\tau\), such that for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
c_1\mathfrak R_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)\le\inf_{\widetilde\theta_n}\sup_{(P_{0,n},P_{1,n})\in\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)}\{E(\widetilde\theta_n-\theta_n)^2\}^{1/2}
\]
\[
\le C_{1,\tau}\min\!\left\{
\rho^2,
\rho n^{-1/2}+r_{n,d}(s_z)+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2,
n^{-a_\tau}(\log n)^{a_\tau}
\right\}.
\]
When \(s_z\ge1\), a fourth candidate \(n^{-1/(d+2)}(\log n)^{1+1/(d+2)}\) may be inserted in the last minimum. Here
\[
\mathfrak R_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)=\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}\}.
\]
The three nonzero upper candidates are attained, respectively, by the observable Gram-corrected estimator \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y^*,J_z^*,n}\), the median-aggregated SmoothCOT estimator \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{sm,med}}_{\tau,n}\), and, when \(s_z\ge1\), the median-aggregated direct-COT estimator \(\widehat\theta_n^{\mathrm{dir,med}}\); the first entry is attained by the zero estimator. The quadratic and zero-zone lower bounds hold on the explicit sign-paired family in the mixture-check lemma. In the zone \(\rho\gg n^{-1/2}\), extending \(q_{a,t}^{0}\) constantly in \(z\) and comparing amplitudes separated by order \(n^{-1/2}\) supplies the regular \(\rho n^{-1/2}\) lower bound.

The bracket is matched whenever
\[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
=O\{\rho n^{-1/2}+r_{n,d}(s_z)\}.
\]
Uniformly in \(0\le\rho\le1\), neither supplemental global envelope enlarges this matching region: \(a_\tau<\min\{1/2,4s_z/(4s_z+d)\}\), and, when \(s_z\ge1\), \(1/(d+2)<\min\{1/2,4s_z/(4s_z+d)\}\). In the matching region the intrinsic quadratic elbow is \(s_z=d/4\): when \(s_z>d/4\), \(\rho_{Q,n}=n^{-2s_z/(4s_z+d)}\) and \(\rho_{L,n}=n^{(d-4s_z)/(2(4s_z+d))}\) delimit zero, quadratic, mixed, and regular zones; when \(s_z\le d/4\), the quadratic term prevents a positive-variance root-\(n\) rate uniformly over the class. Outside the matching region, the sharpened two-sided bracket above is the conclusion and its tightness is not asserted.
