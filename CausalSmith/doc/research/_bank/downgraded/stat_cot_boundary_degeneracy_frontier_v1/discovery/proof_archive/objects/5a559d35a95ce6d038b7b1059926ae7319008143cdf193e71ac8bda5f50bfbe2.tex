Let \(U_1,\ldots,U_n\) be independent uniform random variables on \((0,1)\), let \(G_n^{-1}\) be their empirical quantile function, set \(u_n(t)=\sqrt n\{t-G_n^{-1}(t)\}\), and define \(u_n^{\mathrm{tr}}(t)=u_n(t)\mathbf 1\{1/n<t<1-1/n\}\).  Then \(u_n^{\mathrm{tr}}\Rightarrow\mathbb B\) weakly in \(L^2(0,1)\), where \(\mathbb B\) is a standard Brownian bridge.
