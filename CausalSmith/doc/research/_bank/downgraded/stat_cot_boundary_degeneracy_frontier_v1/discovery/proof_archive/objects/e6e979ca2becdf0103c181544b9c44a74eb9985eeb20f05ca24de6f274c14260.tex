Put
\[
e=\frac{\beta}{2\beta+1}
=\frac{1}{2+s_y^{-1}+ds_z^{-1}},
\qquad
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}=(\log n/n)^e.
\]
The exact von Mises identity and norm bound in
\(\mathrm{lem{:}observable\text{-}pilot\text{-}control}\) show that the full-minus-projected pilot-error contribution is
\[
O_{L^2}\!\left[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}
\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-s_zJ_z}\}
\right],                                                    \tag{1}
\]
with the sign and additional higher-order terms stated there.  In particular, the true-discrepancy gaps \(B^y_{J_y,n}\) and \(B^z_{J_z,n}\) are not estimator biases.  By
\(\mathrm{prop{:}anisotropic\text{-}spectrum\text{-}bias}\),
\[
\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n\asymp\frac{2^{dJ_z/2}}n.             \tag{2}
\]
Writing \(x=2^{J_z}\), balance the covariate term in (1) with (2):
\[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}x^{-s_z}
=n^{-1}x^{d/2}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x^{s_z+d/2}=n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}.
\]
Thus
\[
2^{J_z^*}\asymp
\{n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\}^{2/(2s_z+d)}.           \tag{3}
\]
Equating the outcome term in (1) to this common scale gives
\[
2^{-(s_y+1)J_y^*}=2^{-s_zJ_z^*},
\qquad
2^{J_y^*}\asymp
\{n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\}^{2s_z/\{(s_y+1)(2s_z+d)\}}. \tag{4}
\]
Substitution into either side of the balance proves that the optimized scale is
\[
n^{-2s_z/(2s_z+d)}
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{d/(2s_z+d)}.                 \tag{5}
\]
Since \(D_{J_y,J_z}\asymp2^{J_y+dJ_z}\), (3)--(4) yield
\[
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}
=\{n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\}^{\kappa+o(1)},
\qquad
\kappa=\frac{2s_z/(s_y+1)+2d}{2s_z+d}.                     \tag{6}
\]
Now \(s_y>0\) implies
\(2s_z/(s_y+1)<4s_z\), so \(\kappa<2\).  Since
\(n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}<n\) eventually, (6) proves
\(D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}=o(n^2)\).

Next,
\[
2+s_y^{-1}+ds_z^{-1}>2+ds_z^{-1}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
e<\frac{s_z}{2s_z+d}.
\]
For \(d>0\), cross-multiplication gives
\[
\frac{s_z}{2s_z+d}<\frac{2s_z}{4s_z+d}.                    \tag{7}
\]
The polynomial exponent in (5) is
\((2s_z+ed)/(2s_z+d)\).  Therefore
\[
\frac{4s_z}{4s_z+d}-\frac{2s_z+ed}{2s_z+d}
=\frac{d\{2s_z-e(4s_z+d)\}}
{(4s_z+d)(2s_z+d)}>0,                                       \tag{8}
\]
where positivity follows from the second inequality in (7).  Restoring the positive logarithmic factor in \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\), (8) proves
\[
\frac{n^{-2s_z/(2s_z+d)}
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{d/(2s_z+d)}}
{n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}}\longrightarrow\infty.                 \tag{9}
\]
Consequently no positive finite pair \((s_y,s_z)\) makes the certified Gram scale uniformly absorbable into the intrinsic quadratic scale.

The remaining candidates are direct consequences of their established estimator lemmas.  Specifically,
\(\mathrm{lem{:}anisotropic\text{-}smoothcot\text{-}median\text{-}envelope}\) gives, for every fixed \(0<\tau<\min\{s_y,s_z\}\),
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)}
\|\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{sm,med}}_{\tau,n}-\theta_n\|_2
\lesssim_\tau n^{-a_\tau}(\log n)^{a_\tau},
\qquad a_\tau=\frac{\tau}{2\tau+d+1}.                    \tag{10}
\]
When \(s_z\ge1\),
\(\mathrm{lem{:}direct\text{-}cot\text{-}median\text{-}envelope}\) gives
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)}
\|\widehat\theta_n^{\mathrm{dir,med}}-\theta_n\|_2
\lesssim n^{-1/(d+2)}(\log n)^{1+1/(d+2)}.                 \tag{11}
\]
The zero-estimator guarantee follows from the energy comparison in
\(\mathrm{thm{:}conditional\text{-}minimax\text{-}frontier}\), and the Gram guarantee follows from
\(\mathrm{thm{:}uniform\text{-}second\text{-}order\text{-}expansion}\) together with (3)--(5).  Selecting before observing the data the smallest deterministic guarantee among these explicit maps proves the certified upper minimum.

Finally, monotonicity of \(x/(2x+d+1)\) and \(\tau<s_z\) imply
\[
a_\tau<\frac{s_z}{2s_z+d+1}
<\min\left\{\frac{4s_z}{4s_z+d},\frac12\right\}.          \tag{12}
\]
If \(s_z\ge1\) and \(d\ge1\), direct cross-multiplication gives
\[
\frac1{d+2}<\min\left\{\frac{4s_z}{4s_z+d},\frac12\right\}. \tag{13}
\]
Equations (1)--(13) prove the optimization, dimension condition, strict Gram gap, both fallback envelopes, and their stated failure to certify the intrinsic quadratic exponent.
