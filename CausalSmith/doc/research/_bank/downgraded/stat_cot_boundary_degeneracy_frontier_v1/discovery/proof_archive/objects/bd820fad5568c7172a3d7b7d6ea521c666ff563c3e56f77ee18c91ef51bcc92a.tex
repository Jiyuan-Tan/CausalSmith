Let \(d\ge1\), put \(a_y=\min\{s_y,1\}\), let \(J_y,J_z\to\infty\), let \(D_{J_y,J_z}=o(n^2)\), and use the observable anisotropic estimator with the declared pilot tuning and studentizer. If
\[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho_n^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
=o\!\left(\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n+2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-2s_zJ_z}\right),
\]
then, uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)\),
\[
\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n=\mathbb L_{J_y,J_z,n}+\mathbb U_{J_y,J_z,n}+B^y_{J_y,n}+B^z_{J_z,n}+o_P\!\left(\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n+2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-2s_zJ_z}\right).
\]
Here \(\operatorname{sd}(\mathbb L_{J_y,J_z,n})\asymp\rho_n/\sqrt n\), \(\operatorname{sd}(\mathbb U_{J_y,J_z,n})=\{1+o(1)\}A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n\), and \(\mathbb L_{J_y,J_z,n}=0\) at conditional equality. At the corrected \((J_y^*,J_z^*)\), the two projection biases and the quadratic fluctuation are of order \(r_{n,d}(s_z)\); the observable root-mean-square scale is
\[
\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}+r_{n,d}(s_z)+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho_n^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2,
\]
and it reduces to \(\rho_n/\sqrt n+r_{n,d}(s_z)\) when the final two terms are of no larger order. No covariance estimate enters this point-estimator expansion.
