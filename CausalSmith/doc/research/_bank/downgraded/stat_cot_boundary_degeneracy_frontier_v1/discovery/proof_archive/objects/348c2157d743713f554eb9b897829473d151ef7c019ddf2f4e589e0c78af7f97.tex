Put \(a_y=\min\{s_y,1\}\).  The Gram-corrected estimator \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y^*,J_z^*,n}=\frac12\sum_{e=1}^2[\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{plug}}^{(-e)}+m^{-1}\sum_{a,i\in I_{a,e}}\widehat s_{a,n}^{(-e)}(W_{a,i,n})+\widehat U_{e,J_y^*,J_z^*,n}]\) has local RMSE at most
\[
\min\!\left\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\right\}
\]
for \(d\ge1\).  Uniformly over \(0\le\rho\le1\), this equals the lower frontier \(\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}\}\) exactly when \(s_z>3d/4\) and
\[
s_y>\frac{s_z\{11d+4s_z+\sqrt{(4s_z-d)(100s_z+23d)}\}}{2(4s_z-3d)(2s_z+d)}.
\]
For \(s_z\ge1\), the median-aggregated blockwise direct-COT estimator in the observable pilot-feasibility corollary additionally has RMSE \(O\{n^{-1/(d+2)}(\log n)^{1+1/(d+2)}\}\), so the available upper envelope is the minimum of this rate, the displayed H\"older-valid pilot-remainder rate, and \(\rho^2\).  Outside the uniform pilot-feasibility region this remains an honest upper bracket, not a matching claim.  For \(d=0\), \(\widehat\theta^0_{J_y^0,n}\) has local RMSE \(\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-1}\}\).
