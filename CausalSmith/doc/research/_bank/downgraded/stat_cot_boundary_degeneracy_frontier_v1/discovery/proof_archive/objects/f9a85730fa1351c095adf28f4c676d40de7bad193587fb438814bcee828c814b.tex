The model has scalar compactly supported outcomes, equal independent randomized-arm samples, a shared positive covariate marginal, positive anisotropic H\"older conditional densities, and known smoothness. For \(d\ge1\), the lower frontier holds over the full declared class, while exact matching by the Gram-corrected estimator is proved in the explicit pilot-feasible region and an honest upper bracket is reported elsewhere. The sign mixture proves only the zero and quadratic lower bounds; the regular lower bound is a distinct fixed-frequency two-point construction. The primary local limit is an \(n\)-dependent Gaussian-quadratic proxy with forced diffuse Gaussianization, not a fixed chaos. The \(d=0\) result uses a separate empirical-quantile estimator and exact null law. Adaptation, multivariate outcomes, observational propensity estimation, high-dimensional observed spectral plug-in without its extra dimension condition, and uniform mixed calibration remain outside the proved scope.
