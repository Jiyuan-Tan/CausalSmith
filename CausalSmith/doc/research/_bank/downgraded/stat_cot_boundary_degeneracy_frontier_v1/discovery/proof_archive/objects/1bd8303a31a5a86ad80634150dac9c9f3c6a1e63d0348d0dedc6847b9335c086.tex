The main anisotropic model has scalar compact outcomes, equal independent randomized-arm samples, a shared covariate marginal, positive H\"older densities, and known smoothness. For \(d\ge1\), the paper gives an intrinsic lower frontier and a deterministic minimum of explicit observable upper guarantees; these generally form an unmatched bracket. The additional recognizable SmoothCOT transfer is confined to the declared fixed-radius positive isotropic slice and requires fixed compactly supported boundary-corrected bases \(\Psi_y\) and \(\Psi_z\) with regularity strictly greater than \(\beta_{\mathrm{iso}}\); it proves only a quadratic-and-zero bracket and no \(\rho n^{-1/2}\) lower term. The population limit theorem is infeasible and triangular, and the \(d=0\) result uses a separate empirical-quantile branch. Adaptation, multivariate outcomes, observational propensity estimation, a high-dimensional observed spectral plug-in, tightness of the nontrivial \(d\ge1\) brackets, and uniform mixed-regime calibration remain open.
