For the opposite-fold estimator and the declared pilot resolutions, uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)\),
\[
\max_e\left[\|\widehat r_n^{(-e)}-r_n\|_\infty+\max_a\|\widehat q_{a,n}^{(-e)}-q_{a,n}\|_\infty\right]=O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}),\qquad P\left(\bigcap_{e=1}^2\mathcal E_n^{(-e)}\right)\to1.
\]
The clipping is inactive on that event, and conditional normalization is exact everywhere. Coercivity and the resolvent identity give
\[
\max_e\|\widehat H_{J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}-H_{J_y,J_z,n}\|_{\mathrm{op}}=O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}+\lambda_n).
\]
The plug-in, ridge, and operator contribution to the second-order expansion is \(O_P\{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^3+\rho_n^{\,2/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\}+o_P(1)\{\rho_n/\sqrt n+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n\}\). Hence it is negligible relative to the displayed expansion scale whenever
\[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^3+\rho_n^{\,2/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2=o\!\left(\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n+2^{-2(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-2s_zJ_z}\right).
\]
If additionally \(D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m=o(1)\), then \(\max_e\|\widehat\Gamma_{J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}-\Gamma_{J_y,J_z,n}\|_{\mathrm{op}}=o_P(1)\) and \(\max_e\|\widehat K_{J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}-K_{J_y,J_z,n}\|_{\mathrm{op}}=o_P(1)\); this extra covariance condition is required only when the population triangular proxy is replaced by its observed spectral plug-in, not for the point estimator.
