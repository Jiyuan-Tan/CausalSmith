Let
\[
E_{R,n}=C_B\left[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+\mathbf 1\{d\ge1\}2^{-s_zJ_z}\}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
\right].
\]
The established population Gaussian--quadratic approximation and the uniform second-order expansion yield an infeasible interval with coverage at least \(1-\alpha-o(1)\) and half-width
\[
O\{E_{R,n}+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n+\rho n^{-1/2}\}.
\]
At \((J_y^*,J_z^*)\), this is the requested deterministic order. Thus population calibratability is proved. Observable calibration is not: for example, when \(d=1\), \(s_y=1/10\), and \(s_z=1\), one has \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}=(\log n/n)^{1/13}\) and
\[
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}\log n/m
\asymp n^{25/143}(\log n)^{157/143}\to\infty,
\]
so the frozen sufficient covariance-consistency condition fails. The available Hessian bound likewise does not imply relative Hilbert--Schmidt spectral consistency. For \(d=0\), the exact null chaos gives neither an observable common-multiplier coupling nor anti-concentration uniformly through local alternatives and into the regular Gaussian regime.
