For \(d\ge1\), it must also account uniformly for the corrected pilot remainder at the deterministic envelope
\[
C_B\left[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}
\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-s_zJ_z}\}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
\right].
\]
The inverse-elliptic quantities \(B^y_{J_y,n}\) and \(B^z_{J_z,n}\) are energy-approximation gaps and are not subtracted from, or added to, the center of the exact-\(\theta_n\) estimator.  The intended target object is \(\mathcal I_n^{\mathrm{mix}}\), with no pretest or variance-threshold switch; this handle does not itself define a multiplier statistic, a critical value, or a confidence interval.
