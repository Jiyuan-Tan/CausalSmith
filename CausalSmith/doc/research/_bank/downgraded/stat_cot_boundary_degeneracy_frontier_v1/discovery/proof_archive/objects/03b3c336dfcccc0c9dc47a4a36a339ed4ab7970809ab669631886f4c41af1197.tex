Put \(p_{a,n}=q_{a,n}r_n\), \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\), and \(\bar D_n=2^{\bar J_y+d\bar J_z}\).  Product stability of anisotropic H\"older classes and the pilot resolutions give
\[
\max_a\|p_{a,n}-p_{a,n}^{\mathrm{proj}}\|_\infty
+\|r_n-r_n^{\mathrm{proj}}\|_\infty
\lesssim2^{-s_y\bar J_y}+2^{-s_z\bar J_z}\lesssim\varepsilon. \tag{1}
\]
For every retained wavelet coefficient, the centered empirical summands are bounded.  Expanding their exponential moments and using \(e^v-1-v\le v^2e^{|v|}/2\), followed by a union bound and finite wavelet overlap, gives uniformly
\[
P\{E_n>C(\varepsilon+x)\}\le C\exp(-cnx^2/\bar D_n),\qquad0\le x\le1, \tag{2}
\]
where \(E_n\) is the maximum, over arms and folds, of the raw joint- and marginal-pilot sup-norm errors.  Since \(\bar D_n\asymp(n/\log n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\), (2) gives \(E_n=O_P(\varepsilon)\), and tail integration gives \(\|E_n\|_{L^k}\lesssim_k\varepsilon\) for every fixed \(k\).

On \(\widetilde r\ge c/2\),
\[
\frac{\widetilde p}{\widetilde r}-\frac pr
=\frac{\widetilde p-p}{\widetilde r}
+p\frac{r-\widetilde r}{r\widetilde r}. \tag{3}
\]
Equations (2)--(3) and the density lower bounds prove the conditional-pilot rate and \(P(\cap_e\mathcal E_n^{(-e)})\to1\).  Conditional normalization is exact everywhere.  Since normalized clipping gives \(c/(4C)\le\widehat r_n^{(-e)}\le4C/c\), orthonormality yields the deterministic Gram bounds.  Moreover,
\[
\|\widehat G-G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le\|\widehat r-r\|_\infty,
\qquad \widehat G^{-1}-G^{-1}=\widehat G^{-1}(G-\widehat G)G^{-1}, \tag{4}
\]
which proves both Gram perturbation assertions.

Let \(A_z=-\partial_y(\bar q_n\partial_y)\), let \(A_z^{\mathrm{op}}=P_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}A_zP_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}\), and use hats for the pilot forms.  The true and clipped pilot bounds give uniform coercivity on the zero-mean space.  The weak forms and (2) imply
\[
\|\widehat A_z^{\mathrm{op}}-A_z^{\mathrm{op}}\|_{H^1/\mathbb R\to H^{-1}}
\lesssim E_n. \tag{5}
\]
The resolvent identity gives an \(O_P(E_n+\lambda_n)\) perturbation in energy norm.  If \(u=A_z^{-1}f\), then \(u'=-F/\bar q_n\), where \(F(y)=\int_0^yf(v)dv\).  Since \(\bar q_n^{-1}\) is uniformly \(a_y\)-H\"older, Galerkin orthogonality and the oversampling rule give, for retained \(f,g\),
\[
|\langle f,\{(A_z^{\mathrm{op}})^{-1}P_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}-A_z^{-1}\}g\rangle|
\lesssim2^{-2a_yJ_y^{\mathrm{op}}}\|f\|_2\|g\|_2
\le\varepsilon^{a_y}\|f\|_2\|g\|_2. \tag{6}
\]
Integrating against \(\widehat r\) and expanding both Gram inverses in (4) proves
\[
\|\widehat B^{(-e)}-B\|_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_P(\varepsilon^{a_y}+\varepsilon+\lambda_n). \tag{7}
\]

Fix a fold \(e\) and condition on its pilot sigma-field.  Define \(d\widehat P_{a,e}^{\dagger}=\widehat q_{a,n}^{(-e)}\widehat r_n^{(-e)}dy\,dz\), \(\dot P_{a,e}=P_{a,n}-\widehat P_{a,e}^{\dagger}\), and
\[
\zeta_e(y,z)=
\frac{r_n(z)\Delta_n(y,z)-\widehat r_n^{(-e)}(z)
(\widehat q_{1,n}^{(-e)}-\widehat q_{0,n}^{(-e)})(y,z)}
{\widehat r_n^{(-e)}(z)}. \tag{8}
\]
All divisions are valid by clipping, and \(\int\zeta_e(y,z)dy=0\).  If \(d_e\) is the retained-moment vector of \(\dot P_{1,e}-\dot P_{0,e}\), the two Gram conjugations give
\[
d_e^\top\widehat B^{(-e)}d_e
=\widehat a_e^{\mathrm{op}}(\widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e,widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e)
=E(\widehat U_{e,J_y,J_z,n}\mid\mathscr F_{-e}). \tag{9}
\]

Along the positive convex path \(\widehat P_e+t\dot P_e\), exact Taylor expansion of the one-dimensional quantile formula gives
\[
\theta_n=\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{plug}}^{(-e)}
+D\Theta_{\widehat P_e}[\dot P_e]
+2\int_0^1(1-t)\mathcal H_{e,t}(\dot P_e,\dot P_e)dt. \tag{10}
\]
The displayed score in \(\mathrm{def{:}anisotropic\text{-}estimator}\) represents the derivative in (10), including the shared-marginal component.  If a distribution has density at least \(c_*>0\), a distribution-function perturbation of size \(E\) moves its inverse by at most \(E/c_*\), and
\[
F(x+w)-F(x)-q(x)w
=\int_0^w\{q(x+t)-q(x)\}dt=O(|w|^{1+a_y}). \tag{11}
\]
Applying (11) to both conditional quantile maps in (10), and writing \(\widehat a_e^0\) for the exact unridged pilot inverse-elliptic form, yields
\[
\left|2\int_0^1(1-t)\mathcal H_{e,t}(\dot P_e,\dot P_e)dt
-\widehat a_e^0(\zeta_e,\zeta_e)\right|
\lesssim E_n^{2+a_y}+\|\Delta_n\|_\infty^{a_y}E_n^2. \tag{12}
\]
The resolvent and oversampling estimates (5)--(7) likewise give
\[
|\widehat a_e^{\mathrm{op}}(\widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e,widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e)
-\widehat a_e^0(\widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e,widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e)|
\lesssim E_n^{2+a_y}+E_n^3. \tag{13}
\]
Subtracting (10) from the conditional mean of the fold estimator and using (9)--(13) proves the sign identity
\[
E(\widehat\theta_{e,J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n\mid\mathscr F_{-e})
=-\{\widehat a_e^0(\zeta_e,\zeta_e)
-\widehat a_e^0(\widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e,widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e)\}+R_{0,e}, \tag{14}
\]
where \(|R_{0,e}|\lesssim E_n^{2+a_y}+\|\Delta_n\|_\infty^{a_y}E_n^2\).  If the pilot is oracle, \(\zeta_e=0\), and (14) vanishes.

On the clipping-inactive event, balanced pilot wavelet estimates have bounded anisotropic H\"older norms in every fixed \(L^k\), while \(\|\zeta_e\|_2=O_{L^k}(\varepsilon)\).  Applying the complement calculation of \(\mathrm{prop{:}anisotropic\text{-}spectrum\text{-}bias}\) to \(\zeta_e\), and using \(\min(x^2,y^2)\le xy\), gives
\[
|\widehat a_e^0(\zeta_e,\zeta_e)
-\widehat a_e^0(\widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e,widehat\Pi_e\zeta_e)|
\lesssim_{L^2}\varepsilon\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-s_zJ_z}\}. \tag{15}
\]
If \(A=\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\), the H\"older modulus preserves amplitude \(A\) on an outcome interval of length \(\asymp A^{1/s_y}\) and a covariate rectangle of volume \(\asymp A^{d/s_z}\); zero outcome mass makes the antiderivative have size \(\asymp A^{1+1/s_y}\).  Since \(\rho_n^2=\int H_n^2/\bar q_n\),
\[
\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\lesssim
\rho_n^{2/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}. \tag{16}
\]
Equations (2), (12)--(16) prove the three deterministic remainder terms.

After subtracting conditional means, the residual first-order coefficient has squared \(L^2(P_0)\oplus L^2(P_1)\) norm bounded by
\[
C\{E_n^{2+2a_y}+\|\Delta_n\|_\infty^{2a_y}E_n^2\}+o_P(\rho_n^2). \tag{17}
\]
Conditional independence divides its empirical variance by \(m\).  Pair counting for the canonical-kernel replacement gives
\[
E[\{U_e^{\mathrm{can}}(\widehat B)-U_e^{\mathrm{can}}(B)\}^2\mid\mathscr F_{-e}]
\le\frac C{m^2}\|\Gamma^{1/2}(\widehat B-B)\Gamma^{1/2}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2. \tag{18}
\]
Levelwise use of (6)--(7) makes (18) \(o_P(A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2/n^2)\).  Since \(\sqrt n\varepsilon\to\infty\), the square roots of (17)--(18) are absorbed by the displayed deterministic terms plus \(o\{\rho_n/\sqrt n+A_{J_y,J_z,n}/n\}\).  Averaging folds proves the asserted \(L^2\) remainder bound and, by (14), excludes \(B^y_{J_y,n}+B^z_{J_z,n}\).

Finally, compact support gives \(\|\Phi(W)\|_2^2\lesssim D_{J_y,J_z}\).  Exponential bounds for scalar quadratic forms on a \(1/4\)-net imply
\[
\max_e\|\widehat\Gamma^{(-e)}-\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_P\!\left(\sqrt{D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m}+D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m\right)=o_P(1). \tag{19}
\]
Uniform positive definiteness makes the square-root map locally Lipschitz.  Equations (7), (18), and (19) prove operator and relative Hilbert--Schmidt consistency of \(\widehat K\).  Since (19) was unused before this last step, the covariance condition is needed only for the observed spectral proxy.
