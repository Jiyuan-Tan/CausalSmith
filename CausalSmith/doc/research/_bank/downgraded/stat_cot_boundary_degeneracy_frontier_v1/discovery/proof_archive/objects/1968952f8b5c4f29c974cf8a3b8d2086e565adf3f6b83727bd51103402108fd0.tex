Put \(a_y=\min\{s_y,1\}\). For \(d\ge1\), use
\[
2^{J_y^*}\asymp n^{4s_z/\{(2s_y+1)(4s_z+d)\}},
\qquad
2^{J_z^*}\asymp n^{2/(4s_z+d)}.
\]
The explicit Gram-corrected estimator \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y^*,J_z^*,n}\) then has local RMSE at most
\[
\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2.
\]
For every fixed \(0<\tau<\min\{s_y,s_z\}\), the explicit median-aggregated aligned wavelet estimator \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{sm,med}}_{\tau,n}\) has RMSE \(O_\tau\{n^{-a_\tau}(\log n)^{a_\tau}\}\), where \(a_\tau=\tau/(2\tau+d+1)\). When \(s_z\ge1\), \(\widehat\theta_n^{\mathrm{dir,med}}\) additionally has RMSE \(O\{n^{-1/(d+2)}(\log n)^{1+1/(d+2)}\}\). Thus the certified upper map is the deterministic minimum of these guarantees and the zero-estimator guarantee \(O(\rho^2)\). Uniformly over \(0\le\rho\le1\), it equals the lower frontier \(\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-4s_z/(4s_z+d)}\}\) throughout exactly the proved pilot-absorption region
\[
s_z>3d/4,
\qquad
s_y>\frac{s_z\{11d+4s_z+\sqrt{(4s_z-d)(100s_z+23d)}\}}{2(4s_z-3d)(2s_z+d)}.
\]
The corrected deterministic projection envelope is \(2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-2s_zJ_z}\). The two global envelopes can sharpen the upper bracket when the pilot term dominates, but their exponents are strictly slower than the intrinsic stochastic exponent and therefore do not independently close it. The sign mixture supplies the zero and quadratic lower bounds; the separate fixed-frequency two-point argument supplies the regular lower bound. For \(d=0\), \(\widehat\theta^0_{J_y^0,n}\) has local RMSE \(\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-1}\}\).
