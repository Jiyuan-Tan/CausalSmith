Given an exact specialized witness \(\omega=(a,y,\theta^0,\theta^1)\) and a finite failed colored-recursion trace \(\mathcal T\), first decode \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) through their selected tables and independent factor weights. Independently scan the recursion states in a fixed deterministic prefix order for a record \((\kappa_*,D,D')\) satisfying every displayed predicate in the definition of \(\Delta\). If such a record is found, prune common ancestors outside \(D'\), retain the candidate minimal forests in \(D,D'\), and color their edges by the licenses stored in \(\kappa_*\); if no such record is found, return failure to certify. The output is therefore either \((\mathrm{failure},\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1)\) or a candidate \((H,\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1)\). This construction does not assert that a qualifying record exists, that \(H\) is a colored hedge, or that the decoded models realize \(H\).
