For a trace state \(\kappa\), let \(r(\kappa)\) be the number of random vertices of its current CADMG, and let \(d(c)\) be the number of missingness indicators assigned by its partial context. Define the nonnegative integer
\[
\mu(\kappa)=r(\kappa)+|\mathcal R|-d(c).
\tag{1}
\]
The number \(n\) in \(\text{def:termination-budget}\) bounds both \(r(\kappa)\) and \(|\mathcal R|\), so \(0\leq\mu(\kappa)\leq2n\). By \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), strict ancestral marginalization, restriction to a proper child of a nontrivial district decomposition, and licensed fixing each reduce \(r(\kappa)\) by at least one. Delayed selection strictly extends \(c\), and therefore increases \(d(c)\) by at least one. None of these transitions increases the other term in (1). Every recursive transition consequently decreases \(\mu\) by at least one, and every recursive operation word has length at most
\[
2n.
\tag{2}
\]

There are at most \(2^n\) observed margins and
\[
c=\prod_{R_Z\in\mathcal R}(|\mathcal X_{R_Z}|+1)
\tag{3}
\]
partial assignments, where the extra value means unassigned. Thus there are at most \(2^n c\) initial traces. At one state, ancestral marginalization and district restriction each have at most \(2^n\) subset choices, fixing has at most \(n(\ell+1)\) vertex-license choices, and strict context extension has at most \(c\) choices. One terminal marker gives the alphabet bound
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1.
\tag{4}
\]
The marker is not a recursive transition and hence does not enlarge the word length in (2). A fixed initial trace and operation word determine the entire literal tuple \((\mathcal H,F,c,q,\pi,\Lambda)\), because every graphical, kernel, and provenance update is deterministic under \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\). It follows from (2)--(4) that
\[
N_{\mathrm{state}}
\leq2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j
=B_{\mathrm{state}}.
\tag{5}
\]
This count keeps every fixing-selection interleaving and the ordered list \(\Lambda\). No fixing is commuted with selection. Under \(\text{lem:colored-adjacent-swap}\), the optional swap optimization merges branches only after replay and literal equality of the complete tuples; any such merge decreases, rather than increases, (5).

We next separate partition construction from trace annotation. By \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), the base input contains exactly one feasibility formula and the finite family of coordinatewise constancy formulas. Its encoding length is at most \(s_{\mathrm{base}}\), and the number of its quantified-plus-free variables is at most
\[
p=2\{g+hK_{\sharp}\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|+1.
\tag{6}
\]
The extra slot in (6) covers the value variable when a unique-value relation is represented. By the definition of \(\operatorname{CAD}(p,s_{\mathrm{base}})\), the fixed signed-subresultant implementation terminates on that input, and both its operations and number of output cells are at most
\[
C_{\mathrm{base}}=\operatorname{CAD}(p,s_{\mathrm{base}}).
\tag{7}
\]
The maximum in the definition exists: there are finitely many binary strings of length at most \(s_{\mathrm{base}}\), and at each projection level a finite polynomial list produces finitely many coefficients and signed subresultants; each nonzero remainder sequence has strictly decreasing degree, and lifting performs finitely many root isolations and sign evaluations over finitely many previously constructed cells.

At most one rational candidate is attached to a visited terminal state for each \((a,y)\). Hence (5) gives
\[
N_{\mathrm{trace}}
\leq|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}}.
\tag{8}
\]
Each candidate is tested on each of the at most \(C_{\mathrm{base}}\) canonical cells, so the number of validation sentences is at most
\[
N_{\mathrm{val}}=N_{\mathrm{trace}}C_{\mathrm{base}}.
\tag{9}
\]
By the definition of \(s_{\mathrm{ann}}\), every validation, including the cell formula, the denominator-support sentence, and the cleared-denominator equality sentence, has encoding length at most \(s_{\mathrm{ann}}\). It uses at most the \(p\) variable slots in (6). The same finite signed-subresultant argument bounds each decision by \(\operatorname{CAD}(p,s_{\mathrm{ann}})\). These decisions only add annotations; by \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) they never refine the partition constructed in (7). Combining (5), (7), and (9) yields
\[
N_{\mathrm{state}}+N_{\mathrm{base}}+N_{\mathrm{validation}}
\leq
B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}
+N_{\mathrm{val}}\operatorname{CAD}(p,s_{\mathrm{ann}})
=B_{\mathrm{tot}}.
\tag{10}
\]

Finally, the canonical partition contains finitely many integer polynomials. Under \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\), rational cells are evaluated by exact rational arithmetic, algebraic cells by their square-free polynomials and isolating intervals, and exact-real cells by the supplied polynomial-sign oracle. Thus cell selection uses finitely many exact sign decisions. The same finite real-algebraic construction yields Thom encodings of the unique value or a witness over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\). These specialization steps terminate but are not included in (10). Equations (1)--(10) prove the displayed bound, and no step supplies or requires a polynomial-time estimate.
