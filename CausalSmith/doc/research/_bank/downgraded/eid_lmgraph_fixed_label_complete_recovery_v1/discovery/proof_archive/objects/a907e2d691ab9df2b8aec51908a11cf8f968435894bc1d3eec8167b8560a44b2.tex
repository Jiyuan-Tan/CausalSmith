Let \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) be compatible with the common finite, strictly positive regular-maximal class, fix \((a,y)\), and let \(\psi_j\) be its unique TCF coordinate cell.  For \(\xi\in\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\), \(\text{lem:da4-recovery}\) gives
\[
f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{1}
\]
The left side depends only on the observed law, so the causal coordinate is constant on the fiber.  By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), \(\psi_j\) is identifying, and \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) yields
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{2}
\]
Combining (1)--(2) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{3}
\]

If \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\) holds, \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\) analogously gives
\[
f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{4}
\]
The same constancy, dichotomy, and soundness argument proves
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (3) and (5) use only the common finite positive domain and therefore establish, respectively, containment of the de Aguas criterion and embedding of the root-control result on that domain.

For strictness of the first containment, fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\).  By \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\), it contains \(A\leftrightarrow Y\).  This bidirected length-one path survives deletion of directed arrows out of \(A\), and no conditioning vertex can block it.  Thus condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) of every proposed de Aguas certificate fails, so
\[
\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G(H,m),A,Y)=\varnothing.
\tag{6}
\]
On the other hand, \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\) gives, for every model in the nonempty fiber,
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_hP_{\mathrm{obs}}(h)
\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathrm{obs}}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)\\
&\qquad\times\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(a'\mid h,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Strict full-data positivity makes every displayed conditional denominator positive.  The right side is an observed-law functional, so the coordinate is constant on the fiber.  The dichotomy lemma and TCF soundness therefore show that the TCF cell carries (7).  Equations (6)--(7) exhibit a whole TCF-identified family outside the de Aguas certificate domain, proving strict class-relative extension.

Every member of the family also contains \(C\to R_W\), so the label-context variable \(R_W\) is not an observed causal root.  The premise of \(\operatorname{RC}\) is therefore inapplicable to this family; this is not a strictness assertion about the complete root-control domain.  When \(H=\{C\}\) and \(m=1\), \(\text{prop:eipfd-star-worked}\) identifies (7) term by term with \(f_a^\star(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\), completing the proof.
