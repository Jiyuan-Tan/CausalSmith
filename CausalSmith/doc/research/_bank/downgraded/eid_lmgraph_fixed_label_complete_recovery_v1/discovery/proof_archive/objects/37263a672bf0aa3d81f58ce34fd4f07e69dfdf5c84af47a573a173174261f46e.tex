For every finite \(\mathcal G\) satisfying the finite-alphabet condition, the history-preserving symbolic compilation \(\operatorname{TCF}(\mathcal G,A,Y,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) visits no more than
\[
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j,
\qquad
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1,
\]
complete provenance-bearing state histories. It generates at most \(N_{\mathrm{term}}=|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}}\) terminal packages. With \(h=|\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)|+|\mathcal V|+|\mathcal R|\), \(g=|\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}|\), \(p=2\{g+hK_{\mathcal G}\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\), and per-package partition-formula encoding length \(s\), set \(S_{\mathrm{tot}}=N_{\mathrm{term}}(s+1)\). The common signed-subresultant CAD and the recursive search together use no more than
\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}=B_{\mathrm{state}}+\operatorname{CAD}(p,S_{\mathrm{tot}})
\]
state visits, projection steps, root-isolation steps, sign evaluations, and output cells. Given \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), selection of the unique partition cell and extraction of a Thom-encoded unique value or witness terminate after finitely many exact sign decisions. This last specialization cost is not included in \(B_{\mathrm{tot}}\). No point-computation claim is made for arbitrary unencoded population reals, and no polynomial-time claim is made.
