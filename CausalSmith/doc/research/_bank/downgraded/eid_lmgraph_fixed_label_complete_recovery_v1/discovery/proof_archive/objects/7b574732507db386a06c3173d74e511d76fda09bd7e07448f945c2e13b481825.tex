For every finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\) and supplied \(A,Y\), the symbolic procedure \(\operatorname{TCF}\) constructs a finite semialgebraic partition \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) of
\[
\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}.
\]
For every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), its unique cell carries a unique value \(f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) if and only if the map \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). On that identifying alternative,
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\]
On the other alternative the cell carries \(\mathsf W_j\); under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), every extracted sample decodes to strictly positive, individual-edge-factorized \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) satisfying
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\]
If the identifying alternative holds for every \(y\) at both \(a=a_0\) and \(a=a_1\), then
\[
\tau_{s_Y}=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\]
This is pointwise semialgebraic completeness over the observed-law fiber; it makes no graphwise colored-hedge existence or sample-to-forest realization claim.
