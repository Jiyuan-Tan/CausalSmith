Given \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), decide the finitely many polynomial signs in \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) using rational arithmetic, algebraic-number arithmetic with the supplied square-free polynomials and isolating intervals, or the supplied exact-real sign oracle. Choose the unique cell containing \(p_o=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o)\). On an identifying cell, represent the unique \(z\) by a Thom encoding over the ordered coefficient field generated by \(\mathbf p\); on a negative cell, represent a sample \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) from \(\mathsf W_j\) by such encodings. For rational or real-algebraic observed cells these coordinates are real algebraic; for an exact-real oracle they need only be algebraic over \(\mathbb Q(\mathbf p)\). No operation on an arbitrary unencoded population real is defined.
