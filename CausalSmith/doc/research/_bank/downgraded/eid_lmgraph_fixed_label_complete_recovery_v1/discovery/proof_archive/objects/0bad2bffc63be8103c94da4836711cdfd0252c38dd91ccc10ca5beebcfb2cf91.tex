For every \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\), every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G(H,m))\), every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), writing \(h\) for a joint value of the always-observed logistics block \(H\),
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=
\sum_hP_{\mathcal M}(h)
\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)
\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(a'\mid h,R_W=0).
\]
Every probability on the right is an observed-law probability.
