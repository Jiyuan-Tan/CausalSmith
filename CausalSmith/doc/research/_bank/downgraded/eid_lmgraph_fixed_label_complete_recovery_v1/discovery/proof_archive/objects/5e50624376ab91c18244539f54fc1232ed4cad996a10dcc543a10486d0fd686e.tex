Fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\), \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G(H,m))\), \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\).  Let \(Y(a)\) denote the outcome obtained by recursively evaluating the structural equations of \(\mathcal M\) after replacing the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\).  Thus \(P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) by \(\text{def:causal-query}\).  By \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\), the endogenous alphabets are finite and every full-data cell has positive probability.  Hence every nonempty marginal cell used below has positive probability, and all displayed conditional distributions are defined.

The graph in \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\) has the always-observed logistics DAG \(H\) as an upstream block whose only edge to the remainder enters \(R_W\).  Under \(\operatorname{do}(A=a)\), the edge \(R_W\to A\) is cut.  The semi-Markovian factorization in \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), with no additional unlisted edge leaving that block, then gives the counterfactual independences
\[
Y(a)\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}R_W\mid H,
\qquad
H(a)=H.
\tag{1}
\]
The second relation is also immediate from acyclicity because \(H\) is pre-treatment and has no parent descended from \(A\).  By the structural-intervention definition of \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}\), total probability, (1), and positivity of \(P(R_W=0\mid H=h)\),
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=\sum_hP_{\mathcal M}(h)
P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),h,R_W=0).
\tag{2}
\]

Now restrict to the context \(R_W=0\).  By \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\), the labeled arrow \(W\to A\) is absent in this context.  In the selected graph, \(B_1\) intercepts every directed path from \(A\) to \(Y\), because the only such route begins the chain
\[
A\to B_1\to\cdots\to B_m\to Y.
\tag{3}
\]
There is no open back-door path from \(A\) to \(B_1\): every edge entering \(B_1\) in the defined family is the arrow from \(A\).  Further, conditioning on \(A,H,R_W\) blocks every back-door path from \(B_1\) to \(Y\).  In particular, the path \(B_1\leftarrow A\leftrightarrow Y\) is blocked at the conditioned noncollider \(A\), as are paths entering \(A\) through \(Z\) before reaching \(W\leftrightarrow Y\).  These are precisely the ordinary front-door graphical conditions conditional on \((H,R_W=0)\).

Therefore the ordinary identification operations whose soundness is supplied by \(\text{lem:ordinary-id-soundness}\) yield, successively,
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),h,R_W=0)
=P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0),
\tag{4}
\]
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid\operatorname{do}(B_1=b),h,R_W=0)
=\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(a'\mid h,R_W=0),
\tag{5}
\]
and, using interception in (3),
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),h,R_W=0)
=\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}
P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid\operatorname{do}(B_1=b),h,R_W=0).
\tag{6}
\]
Substituting (5) into (6), and then the result into (2), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=\sum_hP_{\mathcal M}(h)
\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)
\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(a'\mid h,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Every variable in (7) is always observed, except that \(R_W\) is itself an observed indicator.  No unobserved value of \(W\) or \(Z\) occurs.  By \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\), the joint and conditional probabilities in (7) are therefore probabilities of the observed record \(\mathcal O\).  This proves both the causal equality and observability assertion.
