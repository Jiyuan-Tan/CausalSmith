Let \(r(\kappa)\) denote the number of random vertices in the current CADMG, and let \(d(c)\) denote the number of missingness indicators assigned by the current partial context. Define
\[
\mu(\kappa)=r(\kappa)+|\mathcal R|-d(c).
\tag{1}
\]
Because \(n\) bounds both the current random-vertex count and \(|\mathcal R|\), initially \(0\leq\mu\leq2n\). By the corrected construction in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), a strict ancestral marginalization, passage to a child of a nontrivial district decomposition, or fixing update decreases \(r(\kappa)\) by at least one. A selection update strictly extends the partial context, so it increases \(d(c)\) by at least one. Thus every recursive transition decreases the nonnegative integer \(\mu\) by at least one. It follows from (1) that every operation word has length at most
\[
t=2n.
\tag{2}
\]

There are at most \(2^n\) observed margins and at most \(c\) partial contexts, hence at most \(2^n c\) initial traces. At any state, each of ancestral marginalization and district restriction has at most \(2^n\) subset choices; fixing has at most \(n(\ell+1)\) vertex-license choices; selection has at most \(c\) context-extension choices; and one further symbol records termination. Therefore the operation alphabet has size at most
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1.
\tag{3}
\]
For a fixed initial trace and a fixed operation word, every graphical update and every algebraic update of \(q\), \(\pi\), and \(\Lambda\) is prescribed by \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\). Hence the complete state is determined by that pair. Equations (2) and (3) give
\[
N_{\mathrm{state}}
\leq 2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j
=B_{\mathrm{state}}.
\tag{4}
\]
This count retains all histories and therefore does not assume that fixing commutes with selection or that erasing \(\Lambda\) preserves a state. By \(\text{lem:colored-adjacent-swap}\), the optional canonical replay in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) is attempted only from a common pre-swap state; the corrected procedure merges the branches only after literal equality of the complete replayed tuples. Such merging can only decrease the number in (4).

At most one terminal package is generated for each visited state and each \((a,y)\in\mathcal X_A\times\mathcal X_Y\). Consequently,
\[
N_{\mathrm{term}}
\leq |\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}}.
\tag{5}
\]
Each partition-generating formula sent to the common CAD contains at most two response-vector copies. Each copy has \(g+hK_{\mathcal G}\) coordinates, and \(\mathbf p\) has \(|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\) free coordinates. The number of quantified-plus-free variables is therefore at most
\[
p=2\{g+hK_{\mathcal G}\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|.
\tag{6}
\]
By the corrected size definition, the partition-generating portion of one package has encoding length at most \(s\). Concatenating the portions in (5), using at most one Boolean connective or separator per package, has length at most
\[
S_{\mathrm{tot}}=N_{\mathrm{term}}(s+1).
\tag{7}
\]

It remains to verify that the displayed CAD bound is finite. At each of the at most \(p\) projection levels, the fixed signed-subresultant implementation forms finitely many coefficients, discriminants, and resultants from a finite polynomial set. At the univariate level, every nonzero signed-subresultant remainder has strictly smaller degree than its predecessor, so each sequence terminates and produces only finitely many root-isolation tasks. Lifting performs finitely many sign evaluations over the finitely many cells already constructed. Hence the fixed implementation terminates on each formula with at most \(p\) variables and encoding length at most \(S_{\mathrm{tot}}\). There are only finitely many binary strings of length at most \(S_{\mathrm{tot}}\), so the maximum in the definition of \(\operatorname{CAD}(p,S_{\mathrm{tot}})\) is finite and bounds all projection, isolation, sign-evaluation, and output-cell steps. Combining this with (4) gives
\[
N_{\mathrm{state}}+N_{\mathrm{CAD}}
\leq B_{\mathrm{state}}+\operatorname{CAD}(p,S_{\mathrm{tot}})
=B_{\mathrm{tot}}.
\tag{8}
\]

Finally, the output partition contains finitely many integer polynomials. Rational specialization uses exact integer arithmetic; real-algebraic specialization uses the supplied square-free polynomials and rational isolating intervals; and an exact-real specialization supplies the sign of each polynomial by the definition of \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Thus the unique partition cell is selected after finitely many sign decisions. On an identifying cell, eliminating the response coordinates from \(\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z)\) leaves a univariate semialgebraic relation over the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\); the same finite signed-subresultant and lifting argument isolates its unique \(z\). On a negative cell, the finite CAD sample-point construction yields the Thom-encoded response pair specified in \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\). These point-specialization operations terminate but are not included in (8). Equations (1)--(8) establish only a finite symbolic bound, not a polynomial-time bound.
