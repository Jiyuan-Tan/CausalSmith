Quantifier elimination applied to each \(\mathsf I_{a,y}\) leaves only the coordinates \(\mathbf p\) free. The common sign-invariant CAD in \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\) is finite, its cells are pairwise disjoint, and their union is the feasible projection; hence every compatible observed law belongs to exactly one cell.

Fix that cell and \((a,y)\). Unfolding \(\mathsf I_{a,y}\) gives
\[
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p)
\Longleftrightarrow
\neg\exists\theta^0,\theta^1
\left\{
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;\mathbf p)\wedge
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;\mathbf p)\wedge
q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)
\right\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus (5) holds exactly when \(q_{a,y}\) is constant on the response fiber. The two directions of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) give a surjection in both directions between that response fiber and \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) while preserving \(q_{a,y}=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\). Therefore (5) is equivalent to constancy of the causal coordinate on the lm-SCM fiber. On the true region, \(\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z)\) consequently has one and only one \(z\). On the false region, the unnegated conjunction is precisely \(\mathsf W_j\). Exact specialization chooses the cell by finitely many sign decisions and represents a sample by Thom encodings over the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\). The conclusions about its decoded models are then \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\).
