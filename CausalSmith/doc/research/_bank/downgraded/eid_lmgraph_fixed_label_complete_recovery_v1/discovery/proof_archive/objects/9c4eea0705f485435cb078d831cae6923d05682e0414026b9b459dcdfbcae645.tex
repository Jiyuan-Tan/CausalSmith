Because \(\omega\) satisfies \(\mathsf W_j\), the negative-cell relation in \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) and \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\) gives
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^i;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\quad(i=0,1),
\qquad
q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1).
\tag{1}
\]
Applying \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\) yields
\[
\mathcal M_i\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
P_{\mathcal M_i}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_i}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta^i)
\quad(i=0,1).
\tag{2}
\]
That lemma also proves that the decoded latent law is the product over the individual bidirected-edge and private factors and that every full-data cell is strictly positive. Substitution of (2) into (1) gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{3}
\]

It remains to prove termination and the exact meaning of the tag. The retained history \(\mathcal T\) is finite by hypothesis. It has finitely many prefixes, and the finite graph has finitely many candidate vertex subsets \(D,D'\) at each prefix. Hence the fixed prefix-and-subset order in \(\text{def:certificate-compiler}\) enumerates finitely many triples \((\kappa_*,D,D')\). Every graphical conjunct of \(\Delta\) is decidable by finite ancestor, district, root, and membership computations.

Every stored trace is a rational expression in observed cells and response coordinates. Each denominator tested by the compiler was supported on its recorded pre-restriction trace. By (1), every full-data cell is strictly positive; every marginal or selected support cell used by a legal operation is a finite sum of such positive cells and is therefore nonzero. Multiplication by the finite product of these nonzero denominators consequently preserves the truth value of every trace equality or inequality and reduces it to a polynomial sign test.

Let \(K\) be the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\). By \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\), \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) are represented by Thom encodings over \(K^{\mathrm{rc}}\), not necessarily over \(\overline{\mathbb Q}\). Signed-subresultant evaluation at those encodings reduces every comparison to finitely many signs of integer polynomials at \(\mathbf p\). The interface \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) decides each such sign. Thus every candidate test terminates.

The deterministic finite scan therefore either stops at the first triple satisfying every displayed conjunct of \(\Delta\), in which case it returns \(\operatorname{found}(\kappa_*,D,D')\), or tests every triple and returns \(\operatorname{exhausted}\). In the latter case, by exhaustion of exactly the enumerated list, no enumerated triple passes. In both branches the model components are unchanged from (2), so (2)--(3) prove all model claims. No step identifies a successful record with a colored hedge, a forest realization, or a graphwise recursion failure.
