Finiteness and termination of the symbolic construction are \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\) applied to the corrected procedure and budget. The construction of \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) in \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\), together with \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), gives the finite, pairwise-disjoint partition of the feasible projection and ensures that each compatible observed law belongs to exactly one cell.

Fix such a law, \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\). The dichotomy lemma proves the equivalence
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{the cell has a unique value for }q_{a,y}\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow
q_{a,y}\text{ is constant on }
\{\theta:\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\}\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow
\mathcal M\longmapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
The last equivalence uses both directions of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\), which preserve \(q_{a,y}(\theta)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\). On the true side of (1), \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives, for every \(\mathcal M\) in the fiber,
\[
f_a(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{2}
\]
On the false side of (1), \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) supplies \(\mathsf W_j\). Under exact specialization, \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\) applied to any extracted sample gives the two positive edge-factorized models with their common observed law and unequal causal coordinate.

Finally suppose (2) holds for every \(y\) at \(a=a_0\) and \(a=a_1\). The definition of the causal contrast and substitution of (2) give, equation by equation,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau_{s_Y}
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
  \{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\}\\
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
  \{f_{a_1}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The proof used no implication from a failed graphical branch to a nested district pair and no alignment of an algebraic sample with a colored hedge, so it establishes exactly the pointwise claim and no graphwise converse.
