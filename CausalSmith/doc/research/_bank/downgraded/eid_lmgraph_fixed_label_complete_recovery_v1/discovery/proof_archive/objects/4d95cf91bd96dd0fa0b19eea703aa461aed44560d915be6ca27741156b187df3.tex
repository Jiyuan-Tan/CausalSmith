For a finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\) and supplied \(A,Y\), the graphical recursion has the following implementation-independent dichotomy: it derives a trace functional, or its first failed target-relevant district pair satisfies the invariant \(\Delta=(\kappa_*,D,D')\) of \(\text{thm:qe-failure-compiler}\) and therefore compiles to a colored hedge and a same-observed, different-query positive model pair. The symbolic wrapper \(\operatorname{TCF}\) constructs a finite semialgebraic partition \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) of \(\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}\). For every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), its unique partition cell carries an identifying value for every \(y\) if and only if \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}\) is constant over \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\); otherwise it carries \(\mathsf W_j\). Under an exact specialization, \(\mathsf W_j\) yields \(\omega\), and the independent compiler returns \(H\in\operatorname{CH}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\) and \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) with \(P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and \(Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}\). Thus graphical completeness is a property of the colored recursion and compiler; CAD supplies a finite symbolic fiber decision wrapper.
