By \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\), the history-preserving construction in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) terminates after finitely many complete-state visits and common-CAD operations. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), its cells are finite, pairwise disjoint, and cover exactly
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}
=\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
If \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) is compatible, the forward direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) supplies at least one \(\theta\) with \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Thus its observed-cell vector belongs to (1) and lies in a unique cell.

Fix that law and \((a,y)\). Unfolding \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
&\Longleftrightarrow
\neg\exists\theta^0,\theta^1\bigl[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\\
&\hspace{43mm}\wedge q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)
\bigr]\\
&\Longleftrightarrow
q_{a,y}\text{ is constant on }
\{\theta:\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
The two directions of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) preserve
\[
q_{a,y}(\theta)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{3}
\]
Hence (2) holds if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).

Suppose first that (2) is true, and let \(z=f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) be the value stored by the cell. If the stored representation is a rational trace, \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) stores it only after verifying its equality to \(\mathsf V_{a,y}\) on that cell; otherwise the stored object is the unique-value graph itself. For arbitrary \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), response-fiber equivalence supplies \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) with
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{4}
\]
By the definition of \(\mathsf V_{a,y}\), (4) places \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) on its graph. Uniqueness gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{5}
\]
Thus the equality between the observed functional and the causal coordinate is proved rather than imposed by definition.

If (2) is false, \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) supplies the stored relation \(\mathsf W_j\). Under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), exact specialization supplies a Thom-encoded sample over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\). The terminal package has a finite retained history by \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\). Applying \(\text{thm:qe-failure-compiler}\) yields
\[
\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\quad
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\quad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y),
\tag{6}
\]
with strict full-data positivity and individual-edge product factorization. The same theorem proves termination of the optional checked-record or exhaustion diagnostic.

Finally, suppose (5) holds for every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\) at both \(a_0\) and \(a_1\). The outcome alphabet is finite by \(\text{ass:finite-alphabets}\). By \(\text{def:causal-query}\) and coordinatewise substitution from (5),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau_{s_Y}
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\}\\
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Equations (1)--(7) prove pointwise semialgebraic completeness and the explicit identifying map. They do not assert graphwise colored-hedge completeness.
