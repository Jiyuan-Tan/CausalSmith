Fix that law, \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\). Unfolding \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) gives
\[

\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
Both directions of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) preserve
\[

\tag{3}
\]
Therefore the last condition in (2) holds if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). This is also the equivalence in \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\).

\[

\tag{5}
