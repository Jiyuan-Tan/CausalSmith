Because \(\omega\) satisfies \(\mathsf W_j\), the negative-cell relation in \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) and \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\) gives

\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^i;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\quad(i=0,1),
\qquad

\tag{1}
\]
Applying \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\) to (1) yields
\[
\mathcal M_i\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
P_{\mathcal M_i}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_i}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta^i)
\quad(i=0,1).
\tag{2}
\]
That lemma also proves that each decoded latent law is the product over the individual bidirected-edge and private factors and that every full-data cell is strictly positive. Substituting the last equalities in (2) into the inequality in (1) gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{3}
\]

It remains to prove termination and the meaning of the diagnostic tag. The graph and \(\mathcal T\) are finite by hypothesis, so they have finitely many prefixes and finitely many vertex, district, ancestor, and edge-color candidates at each prefix. Every purely graphical conjunct is decidable by finite graph inspection. The stored traces are rational expressions in observed cells and response coordinates. Each denominator tested by the corrected \(\text{def:certificate-compiler}\) is supported on its pre-restriction trace. It is nonzero because (1) contains strict positivity of every full-data cell, and every required marginal or selected cell is a finite sum of such positive cells. Clearing the finite product of these nonzero denominators turns every trace equality or nonzero test into a polynomial sign test without changing its truth value.

By the corrected \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\), the coordinates of \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) have Thom encodings over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\). Signed-subresultant evaluation at such an encoding reduces every required comparison to finitely many signs of integer polynomials at \(\mathbf p\), and \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) decides those signs. Hence each candidate test terminates. The deterministic finite enumeration in \(\text{def:certificate-compiler}\) therefore either returns the first candidate passing every conjunct or exhausts all candidates. In both branches its model components are exactly those in (2), so (2)--(3) prove all affirmative model claims. Nothing in this argument infers a colored hedge or forest realization from a diagnostic record.
