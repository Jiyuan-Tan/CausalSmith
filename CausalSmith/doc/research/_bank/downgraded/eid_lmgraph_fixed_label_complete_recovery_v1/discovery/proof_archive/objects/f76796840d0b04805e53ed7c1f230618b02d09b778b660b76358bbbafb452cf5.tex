By \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\), the two trace-independent base constructions and the subsequent finite trace-annotation phase terminate.  By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the coordinate cells partition the feasible observed-law projection and the target cells partition its product with the unrestricted real score coordinates.  The construction in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) fixes both partitions before trace enumeration and admits an annotation only after its whole-cell equality sentence has been verified.  Thus trace discovery can neither split nor merge a base cell.

Fix a compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and a real score map \(s_Y\), and write
\[
\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o,
\qquad
\mathbf s_Y=(s_Y(y))_y.
\]
Compatibility makes \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) nonempty.  The dichotomy lemma gives unique coordinate and target cells and proves
\[
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
\mathsf I_\tau(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},\mathbf s_Y)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal M\mapsto\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{2}
\]

Suppose (1) holds.  Its coordinate value relation is nonempty and single-valued.  If a rational trace \(N/D\) passes the whole-cell check, \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) proves \(D(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\neq0\) and \(D(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=N(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\) for every model in the fiber; otherwise the unique-value graph is retained.  In either case \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{3}
\]
Conversely, a common identifying value in (3) makes the right side constant, and (1) holds.  This proves the coordinate iff.

The same argument applies directly to (2), without assuming coordinatewise constancy.  If the target cell retains \(\mathsf V_\tau\), its unique-value semantics and the response-fiber equality in \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) give the causal contrast.  If it has a rational annotation \(N_\tau/D_\tau\), the target whole-cell implication in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) gives
\[
D_\tau(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},\mathbf s_Y)\neq0,
\qquad
D_\tau(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},\mathbf s_Y)\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
=N_\tau(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},\mathbf s_Y)
\tag{4}
\]
for every model in the fiber.  Therefore, exactly when (2) holds,
\[
f_{\tau,k}(P_{\mathrm{obs}};s_Y)=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{5}
\]
This establishes the direct target iff even when some interventional coordinates vary and cancel in the score contrast.

If a coordinate test fails, the coordinate clause of the dichotomy lemma supplies a satisfiable \(\mathsf W_j\) and two strictly positive individual-edge-factorized models in the same observed fiber with different coordinate values.  If the target test fails, its target clause supplies a satisfiable \(\mathsf W_\tau\) and models \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\) satisfying
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_0}\neq\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_1}.
\tag{6}
\]
These are ordinary existential consequences of a nonconstant real response fiber, so no exact encoding is required.  By \(\text{prop:ambient-nonrecovery-transfer}\), each pair also proves nonidentification in the larger published ambient fiber.  If \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) is supplied, coordinate witnesses are represented and \(\text{thm:qe-failure-compiler}\) terminates with a checked divergence record or exhaustion.  If \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\) is supplied, \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) represents the target pair.  No step turns either diagnostic tag into a colored hedge.

Finally, if every coordinate at \(a_0\) and \(a_1\) is constant, finiteness of \(\mathcal X_Y\) permits termwise substitution of (3) into \(\text{def:causal-query}\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\}\\
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Equation (7) is the additional sufficient coordinatewise formula; the iff in (2)--(5) is the direct target criterion.  Equations (1)--(7), together with the finite partitions and termination already established, prove the theorem with no graphwise colored-hedge claim.
