For a finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\) and supplied \(A,Y\), the trace-independent base phase of \(\operatorname{TCF}\) constructs a finite semialgebraic partition
\[
\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}
\quad\text{of}\quad
\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\},
\]
and the colored trace phase only annotates those fixed cells by expressions verified on the whole cell. For every compatible finite/full-positive \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), its unique cell carries \(f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\); in that case
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\]
If the coordinate is not constant, the cell carries \(\mathsf W_j\), and even without \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) there exist strictly positive individual-edge-factorized models \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) with the same observed proxy law and \(Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y)\). Under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), the procedure additionally extracts a represented witness pair, and \(\operatorname{Compile}_{\mathrm{col}}\) terminates with either a checked divergence record or an exhaustion tag. If every coordinate is constant for both \(a_0\) and \(a_1\), then the observed-law functional identifies the causal contrast on the finite/full-positive fiber:
\[
\tau_{s_Y}
=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\]
This is pointwise semialgebraic completeness for the finite/full-positive subclass, together with exact-free ambient nonidentification from every negative cell; it makes no graphwise colored-hedge completeness claim.
