Assume \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\) and that every context graph is identifying.  By \(\text{def:root-control-functional}\), each label-context variable is observed and parentless.  Its structural equation therefore has no endogenous parent, and intervention on \(A\) cannot change its distribution.  Hence, for every context value \(c\),
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(C=c\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))
=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(C=c).
\tag{1}
\]
The recovery theorem \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\) supplies a sound functional in each context and the aggregation identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_cP_{\mathrm{obs}}(C=c)
f^{\mathrm{ID}}_{a,c}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\\
&=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
for every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).  On the finite positive class, every context occurring in a conditional expression is supported; a zero-weight term in the published aggregation, if present syntactically, contributes zero and requires no division.

Because the context variables are roots, restricting their values before the context-specific ID operations cannot remove a parent mechanism or a pre-restriction license needed later.  Thus delayed restriction contributes no additional identifying information in this regime.  More formally, (2) is already an observed-law functional common to the whole fiber, so \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) makes the unique TCF coordinate cell identifying, and \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{3}
\]
The cited root-control result is uniform over its graph class, while TCF tests constancy at each observed law.  Therefore (3) proves the reduction but not the excluded converse for an exceptional law.
