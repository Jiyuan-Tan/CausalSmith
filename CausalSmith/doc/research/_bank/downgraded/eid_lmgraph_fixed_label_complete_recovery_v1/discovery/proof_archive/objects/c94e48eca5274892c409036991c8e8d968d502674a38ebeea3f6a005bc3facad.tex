For the size quantities \((n,\ell,c,h,g,K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G),p_{\mathrm{coord}},p_{\tau},p_{\max},s)\), define
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j.
\]
Here \(2^n c\) bounds the initial observed-margin and partial-context traces, and the operation alphabet bounds ancestral and district subset choices, vertex-license choices, strict context extensions, and one harmless terminal marker. The recursive word length \(2n\) counts every provenance-bearing marginalization, district restriction, fixing, and selection in its actual order.

Let
\[
p_{\mathrm{coord}}
=2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|,
\qquad
p_{\tau}=p_{\mathrm{coord}}+|\mathcal X_Y|,
\qquad
p_{\max}=p_{\tau}.
\]
Let \(s\) be the maximum of the complete binary encoding lengths of the trace-independent coordinate-CAD input, the trace-independent target-CAD input, and any one coordinate or target whole-cell candidate-verification sentence generated by an operation word of length at most \(2n\). The length includes canonical cell formulas, cleared numerators and denominators, polynomial counts, degrees, integer coefficients, variable indices, Boolean connectives, free observed-cell names, and free score-coordinate names. Define
\[
C_{\mathrm{coord}}=\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\mathrm{coord}},s),
\qquad
C_{\tau}=\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\tau},s),
\qquad
C_{\mathrm{base}}=C_{\mathrm{coord}}+C_{\tau},
\]
\[
N_{\mathrm{check}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}
\left\{|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|C_{\mathrm{coord}}+C_{\tau}\right\},
\]
and
\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}
+N_{\mathrm{check}}\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\max}+1,s).
\]
The two base CADs are run once each before trace enumeration. Their combined number of projection, real-root-isolation, sign-evaluation steps and output cells is at most \(C_{\mathrm{base}}\). Each later annotation sentence has at most one response vector, \(z\), the free observed coordinates, and, for a target check, the free score coordinates; hence it has at most \(p_{\max}+1\) variables. Exact coordinate specialization under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) and exact target specialization under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\) are separate finite sign computations.
