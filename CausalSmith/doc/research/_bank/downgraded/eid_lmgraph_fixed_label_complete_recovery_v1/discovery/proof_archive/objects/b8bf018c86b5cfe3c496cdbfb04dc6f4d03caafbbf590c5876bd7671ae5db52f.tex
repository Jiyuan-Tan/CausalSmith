For the size quantities \((n,\ell,c,h,g,K_{\mathcal G},p,s)\), define
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j.
\]
Here \(2^n c\) bounds the initial observed-margin and partial-context traces, and the operation alphabet bounds ancestral and district subset choices, vertex-license choices, strict context extensions, and one harmless terminal marker. The recursive word length \(2n\) counts every provenance-bearing marginalization, district restriction, fixing, and selection in its actual order.

Let
\[
p=2\{g+hK_{\mathcal G}\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|.
\]
Let \(s\) be the maximum of the complete binary encoding length of the single trace-independent base feasibility/coordinate-constancy CAD input and the complete binary encoding length of any one whole-cell candidate-verification sentence generated by an operation word of length at most \(2n\), including its canonical cell formula, cleared numerator and denominator, polynomial counts, degrees, integer coefficients, variable indices, Boolean connectives, and free observed-cell names. Define
\[
C_{\mathrm{base}}=\operatorname{CAD}(p,s),
\qquad
N_{\mathrm{check}}=|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}}C_{\mathrm{base}},
\]
\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}=
B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}
+N_{\mathrm{check}}\operatorname{CAD}(p+1,s).
\]
The first CAD is run once, before trace enumeration. Its number of output cells is at most \(C_{\mathrm{base}}\). Each subsequent annotation sentence uses at most one response vector, \(z\), and \(\mathbf p\), hence at most \(p+1\) variables, and no more than \(N_{\mathrm{check}}\) such sentences are tested. Exact point specialization is a separate finite sign computation under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).
