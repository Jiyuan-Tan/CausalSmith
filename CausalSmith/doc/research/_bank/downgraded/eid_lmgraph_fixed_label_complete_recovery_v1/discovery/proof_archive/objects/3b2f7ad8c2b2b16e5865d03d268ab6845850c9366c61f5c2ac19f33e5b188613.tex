Let \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) have finite endogenous alphabets and mutually independent individual-edge and private factors indexed by \(\mathcal J=\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)\sqcup\mathcal V\sqcup\mathcal R\). For the supplied arbitrary real score map \(s_Y:\mathcal X_Y\to\mathbb R\), write
\[
L_{\mathcal G}=\left|\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\right|,
\qquad
K_{\tau,\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)=L_{\mathcal G}+1.
\]
There is a label-compatible deterministic response representation \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\) in which every factor has support at most the target-only universal upper bound \(K_{\tau,\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\), the factors remain mutually independent, and simultaneously
\[
P_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(V=v,R=r)=P_{\mathcal M}(V=v,R=r)
\quad\text{for every }(v,r),
\qquad
\tau_{s_Y}^{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}.
\]
No rationality or algebraicity condition is imposed on the values of \(s_Y\). The bound preserves the single score contrast and the full-data law; it does not assert simultaneous preservation of every coordinate \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\), and it is not claimed sharp.
