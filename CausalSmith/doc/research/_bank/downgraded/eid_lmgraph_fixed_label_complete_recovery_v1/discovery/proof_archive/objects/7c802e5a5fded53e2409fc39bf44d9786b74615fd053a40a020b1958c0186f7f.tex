On formal input \((\mathcal G,A,Y,\mathbf p)\), start from every observed-trace kernel written as a rational expression in \(\mathbf p\) and recursively apply ancestral marginalization, district decomposition, licensed \(\Phi_v^{\lambda}\) updates, and delayed \(\Sigma_c\) updates. Use fixed total orders for ancestral and district recursion, and recompute \(q\) and \(\pi\) from the unique canonical history represented by \((\mathcal H,F,c,\Lambda)\). Memoize the resulting full trace state. Two branches related by an admissible adjacent swap may be merged only by returning to their common pre-swap state and replaying the two licenses in a fixed canonical order, thereby recomputing the ordered provenance snapshots; branches for which such replay is not legal remain distinct. At each terminal state append the symbolic formulas \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\), \(\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p)\), and \(\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z)\). One common signed-subresultant CAD refines these outputs into \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\), with a rational trace expression verified against the unique-value relation or the unique-value graph itself on identifying cells, and \(\mathsf W_j\) on negative cells. The notation \(\operatorname{TCF}(\mathcal G,A,Y,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) means this symbolic object with the formal names \(p_o=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o)\); selecting a numerical cell is only the separate exact specialization. The procedure neither names nor returns a colored hedge.
