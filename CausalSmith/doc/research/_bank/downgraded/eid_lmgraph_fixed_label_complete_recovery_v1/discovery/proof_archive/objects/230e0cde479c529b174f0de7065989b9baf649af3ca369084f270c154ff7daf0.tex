For every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), every \(a\), and every \(y\), the unique cell containing \((P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\) carries a unique value for \(q_{a,y}\) if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Otherwise it carries \(\mathsf W_j\); under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), a sample from \(\mathsf W_j\) decodes to the positive same-observed, different-query pair of \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\).
