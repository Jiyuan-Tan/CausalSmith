For \(\mathcal G_\star=\mathcal G(\{C\},1)\), apply \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\) with \(H=\{C\}\) and mediator \(B_1\). For every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_+^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G_\star)\), it gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_{c\in\mathcal X_C}P_{\mathcal M}(c)
\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,c,R_W=0)\\
&\quad\times\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid b,a',c,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(a'\mid c,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
Every denominator is nonzero by strict positivity, and the same lemma proves that every probability on the right is an observed-law probability. Replacing it by its \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) value turns (1) exactly into \(f_a^\star(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) from \(\text{def:eipfd-star-functional}\). The proof of the lemma uses the Figure 3(b) trace in \(\text{prop:figure3b-fix-select}\): it records the intervention kernel before selecting \(R_W=0\), applies front-door adjustment through \(B_1\) conditional on \(C\), and finally uses the unselected marginal \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(C)\).

We next check the two comparator obstructions. In every candidate de Aguas certificate, condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) requires the relevant separation in \(\mathcal H_r[\underline A]\). Removing directed arrows out of \(A\) does not remove the bidirected edge \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) present in \(\mathcal G_\star\). That length-one path has no interior vertex and hence cannot be blocked by any proposed \(L_r\) or other admissible conditioning set. Condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) fails for every triple, so
\[
\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G_\star,A,Y)=\varnothing.
\tag{2}
\]
The root-control certificate requires every label-context variable to be parentless. The edge \(C\to R_W\) is present, so \(R_W\) is not a causal root and
\[
\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G_\star,A,Y)\text{ does not hold}.
\tag{3}
\]

Equation (1) holds for every model in every compatible observed-law fiber. Hence each coordinate is constant on that fiber. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), every compatible law lies in an identifying TCF cell. The legal fix-before-select/front-door branch supplies the rational candidate \(f_a^\star\); by \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) it is stored only after its equality to the unique-value relation is verified. Therefore the cell carries exactly \(f_a^\star\), while (2)--(3) prove that neither comparator certificate applies.
