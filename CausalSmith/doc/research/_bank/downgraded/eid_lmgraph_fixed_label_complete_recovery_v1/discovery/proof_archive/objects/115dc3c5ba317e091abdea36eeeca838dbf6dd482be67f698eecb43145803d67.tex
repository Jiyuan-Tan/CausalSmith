By \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\), both trace-independent base constructions and the common finite trace-annotation phase terminate. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the coordinate cells partition the feasible observed-law projection and the target cells partition its product with the unrestricted real score coordinates. The construction in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) fixes both partitions before trace enumeration and admits an annotation only after its whole-cell equality sentence has been verified. Hence trace discovery neither splits nor merges a base cell.

Fix a compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), a score map \(s_Y\), and write \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\), \(\mathbf s_Y=(s_Y(y))_y\). Compatibility makes the fiber nonempty. The dichotomy lemma gives unique coordinate and target cells and proves
\[
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
\mathsf I_{\tau}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},\mathbf s_Y)
\Longleftrightarrow
\mathcal M\mapsto\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{2}
\]

If (1) holds, the coordinate value relation is nonempty and single-valued. If a rational trace expression passes its whole-cell check, \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) proves that its supported denominator is nonzero and that its value equals this unique coordinate; otherwise the value graph itself is retained. Applying \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad
\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{3}
\]
Conversely, an identifying functional common to the fiber forces the right side of (3) to be constant, so (1) holds.

Exactly the same verified-value argument applied to (2) gives
\[
f_{\tau,k}(P_{\mathrm{obs}};s_Y)=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
\quad
\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\tag{4}
\]
if and only if the score contrast is constant. Indeed, if the cell retains \(\mathsf V_{\tau}\), (4) is its unique-value semantics; if it carries a rational annotation \(N_{\tau}/D_{\tau}\), the whole-cell implication in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) gives \(D_{\tau}\neq0\) and \(D_{\tau}\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}=N_{\tau}\) for every compatible target response vector. Thus the target equality is proved directly and is not inferred from coordinatewise recovery.

If (2) fails, \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) supplies a satisfiable \(\mathsf W_{\tau}\) and decodes its two points to
\[
\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_0}\neq\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_1}.
\tag{5}
\]
Fiber membership gives strict full-data positivity, individual-edge factorization, and the common observed proxy law. By \(\text{prop:ambient-nonrecovery-transfer}\), the same pair proves ambient nonidentification. Neither existence nor transfer uses an exact encoding. If \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\) is supplied, \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) represents the pair by Thom encodings. If (1) fails instead, the coordinate clauses of the dichotomy and ambient-transfer proposition give the existing same-observed, different-coordinate pair. Under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), \(\text{thm:qe-failure-compiler}\) additionally terminates with a checked divergence record or an exhaustion tag; no colored hedge is asserted.

Finally, if every coordinate at \(a_0\) and \(a_1\) is constant, finiteness of \(\mathcal X_Y\) permits termwise substitution of (3) into \(\text{def:causal-query}\):
\[
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\tag{6}
\]
Equation (6) is a sufficient coordinatewise route. The iff in (2)--(4) is strictly the direct target criterion and remains valid when some coordinates vary but their score contrast does not. This proves the claimed pointwise completeness without strengthening the model class or claiming a graphwise colored-hedge converse.
