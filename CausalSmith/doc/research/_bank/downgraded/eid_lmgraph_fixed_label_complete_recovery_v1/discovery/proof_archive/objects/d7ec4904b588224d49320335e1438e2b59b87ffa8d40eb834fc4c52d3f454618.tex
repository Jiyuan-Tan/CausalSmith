For each \((a,y)\), \(\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p)\) is a first-order formula over a real closed field whose atoms are polynomial sign conditions. By \(\text{lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination}\), eliminating \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) produces an equivalent quantifier-free formula in \(\mathbf p\). Apply the same result to \(\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\). Finiteness of the graph, the alphabets, the response-table set, and the sharp factor supports makes this a finite family of polynomials. By \(\text{lem:finite-sign-invariant-cad}\), one finite sign-invariant CAD partitions Euclidean space into pairwise-disjoint semialgebraic cells on which every such sign, and hence every Boolean combination of the signs, is constant. Intersecting with feasibility and discarding empty cells gives the canonical finite partition
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}
=\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}
=\bigsqcup_{j=1}^J\{\mathbf p:\psi_j(\mathbf p)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
This uses only the feasibility and constancy formulas in \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\). By the revised \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), it is completed before trace enumeration, so later rational annotations cannot alter (1).

Fix a compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), write \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\), and fix \((a,y)\). Compatibility and the forward direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) imply \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}\), so (1) gives a unique cell. Put
\[
\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
=\{\theta:\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\tag{2}
\]
Unfolding \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
&\Longleftrightarrow
\neg\exists\theta^0,\theta^1
\bigl[\theta^0,\theta^1\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)\bigr]\\
&\Longleftrightarrow
q_{a,y}\text{ is constant on }\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
The two directions of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) give surjective correspondence at the level of query values:
\[
\{q_{a,y}(\theta):\theta\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}
=\{Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y):\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\tag{4}
\]
Indeed, its forward direction puts the query value of every fiber model on the left, and its converse puts every value on the left into a decoded fiber model. Equations (3)--(4) prove the claimed equivalence of constancy.

If (3) is true, feasibility makes \(\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\) nonempty, and
\[
\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},z)
\Longleftrightarrow
\exists\theta\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\,[z=q_{a,y}(\theta)]
\tag{5}
\]
therefore holds for exactly one real \(z\). If (3) is false, ordinary first-order semantics supplies \(\theta^0,\theta^1\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\) with unequal query values. The conjunction asserting precisely these facts is \(\mathsf W_j\), so \(\mathsf W_j\) is satisfiable. Applying the converse direction of response-fiber equivalence separately to \(\theta^0\) and \(\theta^1\) yields
\[
\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta^0)
\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)=Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{6}
\]
Membership in the fiber supplies strict full-data positivity, the exact common observed law, and individual-edge factorization. Neither (3) nor the decoding in (6) uses \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).

If exact input data are additionally supplied, \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) selects the cell and represents a satisfying pair by Thom encodings over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}\). Then \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\) gives the same properties in constructive form. This proves every assertion.
