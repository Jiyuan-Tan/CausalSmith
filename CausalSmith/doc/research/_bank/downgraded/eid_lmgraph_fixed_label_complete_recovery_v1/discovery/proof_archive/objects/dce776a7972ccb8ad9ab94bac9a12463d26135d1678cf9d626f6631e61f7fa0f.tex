For \((n,\ell,c,t,h,g,K_{\mathcal G},p,s)\), define
\[
B_{\mathrm{state}}(n,\ell,c,t)=6^n c\sum_{j=0}^{t}(\ell+1)^j,\qquad B_{\mathrm{tot}}=B_{\mathrm{state}}(n,\ell,c,t)\{1+|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|\operatorname{CAD}(p,s)\}.
\]
The factor \(3^n\) bounds current vertex statuses, \(2^n\) bounds fixed sets, \(c\) bounds partial contexts, and the geometric sum bounds ordered license traces. Each doubled terminal formula has \(2(g+hK_{\mathcal G})\) quantified response variables, \(|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\) free observed-cell variables, and total symbolic encoding length at most \(s\). The fixed CAD eliminates only the response variables, retains the observed-cell parameters, and outputs no more than \(\operatorname{CAD}(p,s)\) cells and elementary steps. This is a symbolic bound; exact point specialization is a separate finite sign computation under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).
