We prove separately the inclusion, its strictness, and the restriction claim.

Let \(\mathcal M^{+}\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\).  By the set-builder construction in Definition \texttt{def:positive-rm-class},
\[
\mathcal M^{+}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\]
Since \(\mathcal M^{+}\) was arbitrary,
\[
\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)
\subseteq
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{1}
\]

We now exhibit why the inclusion is proper.  Start with a compatible finite lm-SCM \(\overline{\mathcal M}\) in the ambient class supplied by the model setup.  Write the treatment equation in this model as
\[
A=f_A\bigl(\operatorname{pa}_A,U_A,U_A^{\leftrightarrow}\bigr),
\tag{2}
\]
where \(U_A\) is the private treatment factor and \(U_A^{\leftrightarrow}\) is the tuple of edge-specific factors incident to \(A\).  Introduce the standard-Borel disjoint union
\[
\mathcal U_A^{0}=\{\bot_A\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}\mathcal U_A
\]
and give the new private factor \(U_A^{0}\) the point-mass law
\[
P(U_A^{0}=\bot_A)=1.
\tag{3}
\]
Replace only (2) by
\[
f_A^{0}(p,u,w)=
\begin{cases}
a_0,&u=\bot_A,\\
f_A(p,u,w),&u\in\mathcal U_A,
\end{cases}
\tag{4}
\]
and call the resulting model \(\mathcal M^{0}\).  All other structural equations, all edge-specific factors, and all proxy equations are left unchanged.  The joint exogenous law is the product of the point mass in (3) and the old laws of the remaining mutually independent factors, so \(\mathcal M^{0}\) is still semi-Markovian.  Equation (4) is measurable.  On every old branch \(u\in\mathcal U_A\) it is the old structural map, while on the added branch it is constant.  Thus it introduces no parent or latent dependence forbidden by \(\mathcal G\), and all old structurally permitted branches remain in the response map.  In particular, every label-specific deletion remains valid.  The unchanged proxy equations retain proxy consistency.  No causal-minimality, faithfulness, or positive-support condition is part of Definition \texttt{def:published-lm-class}.  Hence that definition yields
\[
\mathcal M^{0}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{5}
\]
The endogenous alphabets of \(\overline{\mathcal M}\) were finite and were not changed.

Equations (3)--(4) imply
\[
P_{\mathcal M^{0}}(A=a_0)=1.
\tag{6}
\]
Because \(a_1\ne a_0\), choose a full-data configuration \((v^{1},r^{1})\in\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\) whose \(A\)-coordinate is \(a_1\); all remaining coordinates can be selected from their nonempty state spaces.  Then (6) gives
\[
0\le P_{\mathcal M^{0}}(v^{1},r^{1})
\le P_{\mathcal M^{0}}(A=a_1)=0,
\]
and therefore
\[
P_{\mathcal M^{0}}(v^{1},r^{1})=0.
\tag{7}
\]
The strict-positivity clause of Definition \texttt{def:positive-rm-class} requires every such full-data cell to have positive probability.  Thus
\[
\mathcal M^{0}\notin\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{8}
\]
Combining (5) and (8) with (1) proves
\[
\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)
\subsetneq
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\]
The same argument also verifies the advertised general witness criterion: if a compatible finite \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) has even one cell \((\widetilde v,\widetilde r)\) satisfying \(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(\widetilde v,\widetilde r)=0\), then the strict-positivity clause directly implies \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\notin\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\), so it is a witness to properness.

Finally, Definition \texttt{def:causal-query} gives on every ambient model \(\mathcal M\) the causal law
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=P_{\mathcal M}\bigl(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)\bigr)
\]
and the score-coded contrast
\[
\tau_{s_Y}(\mathcal M)
=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\left\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\right\}.
\tag{9}
\]
For each \(\mathcal M^{+}\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\), inclusion (1) places \(\mathcal M^{+}\) in the domain of these ambient causal maps.  Evaluating the same formulas at \(\mathcal M^{+}\) gives exactly \(Q_a^{\mathcal M^{+}}\) and \(\tau_{s_Y}(\mathcal M^{+})\).  They are therefore the restrictions of the published natural interventional law and its score-coded NATE contrast to \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\).  This is a restriction of causal targets; no equality with an observed-law functional has been assumed or defined in this proposition.
