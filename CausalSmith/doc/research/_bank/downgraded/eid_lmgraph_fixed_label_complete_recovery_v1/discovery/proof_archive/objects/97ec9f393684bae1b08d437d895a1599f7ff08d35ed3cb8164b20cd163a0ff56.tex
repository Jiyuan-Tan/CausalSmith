Fix a model \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{3b}})\) inducing \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}\).  By \(\text{def:figure3b-witness}\), the only directed path from \(R_W\) to \(Y\) is \(R_W\to A\to Y\), and \(R_W\) is incident to no bidirected edge.  Under \(\operatorname{do}(A=a)\), the equation for \(A\) is replaced by the constant \(a\), so that path is cut.  By the mutual independence of the individual-edge and private factors in \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), the exogenous inputs determining \(R_W\) are then independent of those determining \(Y(a)\).  Thus, for each supported \(r\),
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W=r)
=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)).
\tag{1}
\]
Every value of \(R_W\) is supported because \(\text{ass:strict-positivity}\) makes each finite full-data cell positive.

In the context \(R_W=0\), \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\) deletes the input \(W\) from the equation for \(A\).  The resulting treatment is a function only of \(R_W\), \(Z\), and the private factor of \(A\).  In contrast, after setting \(A=a\), the outcome is a function of \(a\), the private factor of \(Y\), the factor on \(W\leftrightarrow Y\), and the factor on \(R_Z\leftrightarrow Y\).  The first collection of unfixed exogenous inputs is disjoint from the second and is independent of it by \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\).  Conditioning on \(R_W=0\), which depends only on its private factor, preserves this independence.  Consequently,
\[
Y(a)\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}A\mid R_W=0.
\tag{2}
\]
Structural consistency follows by evaluating the same acyclic structural equations with \(A\) at its realized value.  Applying it, then (2), then (1), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{3b}}(Y=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
Proxy consistency in \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\) makes all quantities on the observed side of (3) functions of \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}\); strict positivity makes their conditional denominators nonzero.

By \(\text{def:colored-fixing}\), the licensed fixing that derives the kernel \(P(Y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W)\) stores its pre-restriction observed-law expression in \(\Lambda\).  Applying \(\Sigma_{R_W=0}\) afterwards is legal by \(\text{def:context-selection}\), restricts that kernel, and retains the stored entry.  Equation (3) is therefore the value of this fix-then-select trace.  Moreover, (3) holds for every model inducing any compatible observed law on \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{3b}}\).  Hence the coordinate is constant on each observational fiber.  By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the unique TCF coordinate cell is identifying; the trace expression is among the candidates in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), and its graph-uniform equality (3) passes the whole-cell verification.  Thus the cell carries \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}(Y=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)\).

Finally, start instead with \(\Sigma_{R_W=0}\).  Its input has no pre-selection license entry.  Thereafter, ancestral marginalization, district restriction, and fixing operate only on the already restricted kernel, while selection only extends the context; by \(\text{def:colored-fixing}\), a new entry records only the provenance present at the moment of fixing, and by \(\text{def:context-selection}\), selection never inserts an earlier unselected expression.  Induction on the finite operation word therefore shows that every descendant trace lacks the pre-selection provenance used in (1).  This proves the stated closure obstruction without asserting that fixing and selection commute.
