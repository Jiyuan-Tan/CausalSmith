Fix a compatible observed law \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\). If its TCF coordinate cell is negative for \((a,y)\), there exist \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\) with the same observed proxy law and \(Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y)\). If, for a supplied real score map \(s_Y\), its direct target cell is negative, there exist \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\) such that
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_0}\neq\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_1}.
\]
In either case the same pair lies in the larger published ambient set \(\{\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G):P_{\mathcal M}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}\}\), so the corresponding coordinate or score contrast is not identified there. These existential conclusions do not require \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) or \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\); exactness is required only to algorithmically extract a represented pair.
