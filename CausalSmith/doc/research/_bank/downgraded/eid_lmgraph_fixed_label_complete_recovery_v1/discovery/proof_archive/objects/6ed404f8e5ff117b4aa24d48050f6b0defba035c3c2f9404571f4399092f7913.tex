Consider the graph on substantive variables \(A,Y,Z\), with binary \(A,Y\), ternary \(Z\), no missingness indicators or labels, the arrow \(A\to Y\), the edge \(A\leftrightarrow Y\), and isolated \(Z\). Empty labels are regular and maximal. The pair consisting of the \(C\)-forest on \(\{A,Y\}\) rooted at \(Y\) and its subforest on \(\{Y\}\), with empty colors and traces, belongs to \(\operatorname{CH}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\).

We construct two positive models. Let \(U\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) be the factor shared by \(A\) and \(Y\), let \(E_A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/4)\) be private, and set
\[
A=U\mathbin{\oplus}E_A.
\]
Let \(E_Y\) be uniform on \(\{1,\ldots,16\}\), independently of \(U,E_A\), and let \(Z\) be uniform on \(\{0,1,2\}\), independently of all other factors. Put \(t_0=1/4\) and \(t_1=-1/4\). In model \(\mathcal M_i\), define \(Y=1\) precisely for the first
\[
16\left[\frac12+t_i\left\{\mathbf 1(U=A)-\frac34\right\}\right]
\]
values of \(E_Y\). The bracket is \(9/16\) or \(5/16\) for \(i=0\), and \(7/16\) or \(11/16\) for \(i=1\), so this is a well-defined deterministic response table and all conditional probabilities are strictly between zero and one. Both the directed and shared-factor arguments are active.

Because \(U\) is fair, \(A\) is fair and
\[
P(U=A\mid A)=P(E_A=0)=\frac34.
\]
Therefore, in both models,
\[
P_{mathcal M_i}(Y=1\mid A)
=\frac12+t_i\left(\frac34-\frac34\right)=\frac12.
\]
Thus both observed laws are uniform on the twelve cells of \((A,Y,Z)\), so they are equal and strictly positive. Under intervention on \(A=a\), however, \(U\) remains fair, and hence
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_i}(1)
=\frac12+t_i\left(\frac12-\frac34\right)
=\frac12-\frac{t_i}{4}.
\]
The two values are \(7/16\) and \(9/16\). This supplies the required same-observed, different-query pair.

The common observed law assigns probability \(1/3\) to each of the three values of \(Z\). A binary substantive variable has at most two such values and therefore cannot induce this law. More generally, matching an always-observed coordinate requires at least its observed support cardinality. If \(Z\) is partially observed, proxy consistency gives \(Z^{\dagger}=Z\) on \(R_Z=1\), and strict positivity gives
\[
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(Z^{\dagger}=z,R_Z=1)>0
\quad\text{for every }z\in\mathcal X_Z.
\]
Thus the conditional proxy support has size \(|\mathcal X_Z|\). Adding the isolated ternary coordinate changes none of the forest, district, color, or trace data of the hedge, while changing this lower bound from two to three. Hence no alphabet minimum can be a function of the colored hedge structure alone.
