For the size quantities \((n,\ell,c,h,g,K_{\mathcal G},p,s)\), define
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j,
\]
\[
N_{\mathrm{term}}=|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}},
\qquad
S_{\mathrm{tot}}=N_{\mathrm{term}}(s+1),
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{tot}}=B_{\mathrm{state}}+\operatorname{CAD}(p,S_{\mathrm{tot}}).
\]
Here \(2^n c\) bounds the initial observed-margin and partial-context traces; the operation alphabet respectively bounds ancestral and district subset choices, vertex-license choices, strict context extensions, and one harmless terminal marker. The recursive word length \(2n\) counts every provenance-bearing marginalization, district restriction, fixing, and selection in its actual order. The quantity \(s\) bounds the binary encoding length of the partition-generating part of one terminal \((a,y)\)-package, including polynomial counts, degrees, integer coefficients, variable indices, Boolean connectives, and free observed-cell names. One common CAD receives the concatenated input of length at most \(S_{\mathrm{tot}}\), eliminates only the response variables, retains \(\mathbf p\), and is bounded by \(\operatorname{CAD}(p,S_{\mathrm{tot}})\). Exact point specialization is a separate finite sign computation under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).
