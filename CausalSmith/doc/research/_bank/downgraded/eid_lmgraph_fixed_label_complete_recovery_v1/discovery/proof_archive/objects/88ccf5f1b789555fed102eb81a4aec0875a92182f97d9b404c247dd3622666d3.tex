Quantifier elimination returns a quantifier-free sign formula, not a numerical decision at an arbitrary population law. On its true region the value relation is \[\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z):=\exists\theta\left[\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\wedge z=q_{a,y}(\theta)\right],\] which has exactly one \(z\); on its false region the certificate formula is the unnegated doubled conjunction.
