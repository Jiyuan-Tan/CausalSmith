Let
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}
=\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\text{ass:finite-alphabets}\), the graph, full-cell set, observed-cell set, response-table set, and factor-support bound are finite. Hence every atom in the feasibility formula, every \(\mathsf I_{a,y}\), and \(\mathsf I_{\tau}\) is a polynomial sign condition in finitely many variables. Applying \(\text{lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination}\) to the quantified response variables gives equivalent quantifier-free formulas in \(\mathbf p\), or in \((\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\) for the target. Applying \(\text{lem:finite-sign-invariant-cad}\) first to the coordinate family and then to the target family together with the lifted coordinate-cell polynomials gives finite pairwise-disjoint semialgebraic decompositions
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}
=\bigsqcup_{j=1}^{J}\{\mathbf p:\psi_j(\mathbf p)\},
\qquad
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}\times\mathbb R^{\mathcal X_Y}
=\bigsqcup_{k=1}^{K}\{(\mathbf p,\mathbf s):\chi_k(\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\}.
\tag{2}
\]
This is exactly the construction in \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\); the second decomposition refines the lift of the first.

Fix a compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and put \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\). The forward direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) gives a feasible \(\theta\), so \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}\), and (2) gives unique coordinate and target cells. Define
\[
\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
=\{\theta:\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Unfolding \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) yields
\[
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\Longleftrightarrow
q_{a,y}\text{ is constant on }\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{4}
\]
The two directions of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) give the equality of value sets
\[
\{q_{a,y}(\theta):\theta\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}
=\{Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y):\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) prove the coordinatewise equivalence. If (4) fails, the unnegated doubled formula \(\mathsf W_j\) is satisfiable, and the converse direction of response-fiber equivalence decodes its two response vectors to the stated strictly positive, same-observed, different-coordinate models. Under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) and \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\) give represented versions.

We now prove the direct target equivalence without assuming coordinatewise constancy. For the supplied score map set \(\mathbf s_Y=(s_Y(y))_y\). For every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), the forward direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) gives \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\) and preserves every interventional coordinate. Finiteness of \(\mathcal X_Y\) and \(\text{def:causal-query}\) therefore give
\[
t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
=\sum_y s_Y(y)\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\}
=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}.
\tag{6}
\]
Conversely, for every \(\theta\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\), the converse direction of response-fiber equivalence gives \(\mathcal M_{\theta}\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) with \(q_{a,y}(\theta)=Q_a^{\mathcal M_{\theta}}(y)\) for every \((a,y)\). Hence
\[
t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta)=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_{\theta}}.
\tag{7}
\]
Equations (6)--(7) prove
\[
\{t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta):\theta\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}
=\{\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}:\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Unfolding \(\mathsf I_{\tau}\) shows that it holds exactly when the left side of (8) is a singleton, hence exactly when the right side is a singleton. The set is nonempty by compatibility. If constancy fails, ordinary real-field semantics gives \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) satisfying \(\mathsf W_{\tau}\); the converse response-fiber map decodes them to two strictly positive models in the same observed fiber with unequal score contrasts. This existence argument uses no exact encoding. With \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\), \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) selects the joint cell and supplies Thom encodings over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \((\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},\mathbf s_Y)\). This proves every clause.
