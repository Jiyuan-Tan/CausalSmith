Let an exact specialization on a negative partition cell yield \(\omega=(a,y,\theta^0,\theta^1)\) satisfying \(\mathsf W_j\). Decoding the two response vectors gives \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) such that
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\]
The factors in both decoded models retain the individual-edge product structure and every full-data cell is strictly positive.
