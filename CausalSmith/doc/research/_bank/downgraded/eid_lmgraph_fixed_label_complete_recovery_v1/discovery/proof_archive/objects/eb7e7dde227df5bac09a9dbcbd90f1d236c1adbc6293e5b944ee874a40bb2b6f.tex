Let the cell containing \((P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\) be negative for \((a,y)\). By \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) and \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\), the stored witness relation means that the real formula
\[
\exists\theta^0\,\exists\theta^1\,
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)
\right]
\tag{1}
\]
is true. Choose a satisfying pair in the ordinary set-theoretic semantics of (1). This existence step does not require an effective representation of either response vector.

Apply the converse direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) to each vector. It gives \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) with
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta^0)
\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)=Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{2}
\]
By \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\),
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)
=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)
=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{3}
\]
The membership definition \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\) gives the inclusion
\[
\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)
\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{4}
\]
Thus the same two models are elements of the published ambient observed-law set. If \(Q_a(y)\) were identified on that set, it would be constant on it, contradicting (2). This proves ambient nonidentification. The exact-specialization interface in \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) is needed only when one asks the algorithm to represent a satisfying pair by Thom encodings; it is not a premise of the existential inference from (1).
