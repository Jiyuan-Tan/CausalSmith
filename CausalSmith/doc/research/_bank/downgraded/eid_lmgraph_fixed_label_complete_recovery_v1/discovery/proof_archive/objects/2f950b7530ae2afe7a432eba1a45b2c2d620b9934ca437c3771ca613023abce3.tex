Fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\), \(\mathcal M\), \(a\), and \(y\). Strict positivity makes every conditional probability below well defined. The only arrows leaving \(H\) enter \(R_W\), and after intervention on \(A\) every directed path from \(H\) or \(R_W\) to \(Y\) through the treatment equation is cut. The individual-edge factorization supplies no unshown common factor between \(H\cup\{R_W\}\) and the outcome-side variables. Consequently the structural equations give
\[
Y(a)\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}R_W\mid H,
\qquad
P_{\mathcal M}(H=h\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=P_{\mathcal M}(h).
\]
By total probability and the first independence,
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=\sum_hP_{\mathcal M}(h)
P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),h,R_W=0).
\tag{1}
\]

In the selected graph \(R_W=0\), the label semantics deletes \(W\to A\). The vertex \(B_1\) intercepts every directed path from \(A\) to \(Y\); there is no open back-door path from \(A\) to \(B_1\); and conditioning on \(A,H,R_W\) blocks every back-door path from \(B_1\) to \(Y\). In particular, the path through \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) is blocked at \(A\) in the last check, while paths reaching \(Y\) through \(B_m\to Y\) make \(Y\) a collider when paired with \(A\leftrightarrow Y\). Running the ordinary identification steps certified by \(\text{lem:ordinary-id-soundness}\) in this selected graph gives, equation by equation,
\[
P(b\mid\operatorname{do}(a),h,R_W=0)=P(b\mid a,h,R_W=0),
\tag{2}
\]
\[
P(y\mid\operatorname{do}(b),h,R_W=0)
=\sum_{a'}P(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)P(a'\mid h,R_W=0),
\tag{3}
\]
and, because \(B_1\) intercepts all directed \(A\)-to-\(Y\) paths,
\[
P(y\mid\operatorname{do}(a),h,R_W=0)
=\sum_bP(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)P(y\mid\operatorname{do}(b),h,R_W=0).
\tag{4}
\]
Substitution of (3) into (4), followed by substitution into (1), is exactly the displayed formula. The variables \(H,A,B_1,Y,R_W\) are always observed except that \(R_W\) is itself an observed indicator, so proxy consistency shows that every factor on the right is a functional of \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\).
