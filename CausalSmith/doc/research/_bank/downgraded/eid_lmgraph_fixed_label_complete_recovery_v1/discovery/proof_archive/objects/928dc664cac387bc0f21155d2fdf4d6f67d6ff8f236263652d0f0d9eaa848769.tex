Conjoin the simplex formula \(p_o\geq0\), \(\sum_op_o=1\), and the feasible-projection formula \(\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\) with every quantifier-free output of \(\mathsf I_{a,y}\), refine their common sign-invariant CAD, and discard infeasible cells. The remaining pairwise-disjoint formulas \(\psi_1(\mathbf p),\ldots,\psi_J(\mathbf p)\) form the coordinate partition \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\). Each coordinate cell stores, for every \((a,y)\), either \(\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z)\) or \(\mathsf W_j(\mathbf p,a,y,\theta^0,\theta^1)\).

Independently introduce the formal score coordinates \(\mathbf s=(s_y:y\in\mathcal X_Y)\). Conjoin the lifted coordinate-cell formulas with the quantifier-free output of \(\mathsf I_{\tau}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\), refine by one common sign-invariant CAD in \((\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\), and discard points whose \(\mathbf p\)-coordinate is infeasible. The resulting pairwise-disjoint formulas \(\chi_1(\mathbf p,\mathbf s),\ldots,\chi_K(\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\) form
\[
\mathscr P^{\tau}_{\mathcal G,A,Y}
\quad\text{on}\quad
\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}
\times\mathbb R^{\mathcal X_Y}.
\]
This target partition refines the lift of the coordinate partition. Each target cell stores either the unique-value relation \(\mathsf V_{\tau}(\mathbf p,\mathbf s,z)\) or the witness relation \(\mathsf W_{\tau}(\mathbf p,\mathbf s,\theta^0,\theta^1)\). Thus coordinate negativity and target negativity are recorded separately.
