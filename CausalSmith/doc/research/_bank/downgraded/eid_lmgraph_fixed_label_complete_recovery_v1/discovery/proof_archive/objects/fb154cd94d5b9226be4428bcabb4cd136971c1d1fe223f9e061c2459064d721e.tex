Let \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) be compatible with the finite, strictly positive regular-maximal class, so by \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\) the fiber \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) is nonempty. Fix \(a\in\mathcal X_A\) and \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), and let \(\psi_j\) be the unique cell of \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) containing the observed-cell vector of \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\).

First suppose \(\xi\in\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\). By \(\text{lem:da4-recovery}\), for every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\),
\[
f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{1}
\]
The left side of (1) depends only on \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\). Hence \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on the nonempty fiber. The forward implication in \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) therefore says that \(\psi_j\) is identifying for this coordinate. Applying \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) on that cell gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad
\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{2}
\]
Combining (1) and (2) yields
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{3}
\]
This proves containment of the de Aguas criterion on the common finite, full-positive regular-maximal class.

Next suppose \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\) holds. By \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\),
\[
f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad
\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{4}
\]
Exactly as above, (4) makes the query coordinate constant on the fiber; \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) makes \(\psi_j\) identifying, and \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) identifies its value. Thus
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{5}
\]
Equation (5) is an embedding of the published observed-root result after specialization to the present finite, full-positive class. It does not compare the two procedures outside their common domain.

It remains to prove that the containment in (3) is strict. Fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\) and a law \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) compatible with \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G(H,m))\). By \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\), the bidirected edge \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) is present. Deleting directed arrows out of \(A\) does not delete that bidirected edge, so it remains a length-one path in every graph \(\mathcal H_r[\underline A]\) used in condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) of \(\text{def:da4-certificate-functional}\). A length-one path has no nonendpoint that an admissible conditioning set can block. Consequently \(A\) and \(Y\) are not separated as condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) requires, and hence
\[
\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G(H,m),A,Y)=\varnothing.
\tag{6}
\]

On the other hand, \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\) gives, for every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G(H,m),P_{\mathrm{obs}})\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_hP_{\mathrm{obs}}(h)
  \sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathrm{obs}}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)\\
&\qquad\times
  \sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
  P_{\mathrm{obs}}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
  P_{\mathrm{obs}}(a'\mid h,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Every factor on the right of (7) is observed-law measurable by that lemma and proxy consistency built into \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}\). Every conditioning event displayed in (7) has positive probability because strict positivity of every full-data cell implies positivity of each of its nonempty finite marginals. Thus every division in (7) is defined. Since its right side is a functional of \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), (7) again makes the query coordinate constant on the fiber. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) and \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\), the TCF cell is identifying and its functional equals the right side of (7). Together with (6), this supplies a TCF-identified member outside the de Aguas criterion, proving strictness of that class-relative containment.

Finally, \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\) also gives the arrow \(C\to R_W\). Therefore the label-context variable \(R_W\) is not parentless, whereas \(\operatorname{RC}\) in \(\text{def:root-control-functional}\) requires every label-context variable to be an observed causal root. Hence the observed-root theorem is inapplicable to this family. This establishes failure of its premise only, not a strict inclusion claim about its complete published domain. When \(H=\{C\}\) and \(m=1\), \(\text{prop:eipfd-star-worked}\) identifies (7) with \(f_a^\star(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\), proving the stated specialization.
