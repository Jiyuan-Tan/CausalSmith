We prove the three assertions separately.

First, let \(\mathcal M^{+}\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\).  Unpacking the set-builder in Definition \texttt{def:positive-rm-class} gives
\[
\mathcal M^{+}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G),
\qquad
P_{\mathcal M^{+}}(v,r)>0
\quad\text{for every }(v,r)\in
\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R},
\]
as well as the finite-alphabet and regular-maximal conditions appearing in that definition.  The first displayed membership holds for every \(\mathcal M^{+}\) in the left-hand class, and hence
\[
\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)
\subseteq
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{1}
\]

We next construct an element proving that (1) is proper.  The frozen model setup supplies a finite semi-Markovian labeled-missingness SCM \(\overline{\mathcal M}\) compatible with \(\mathcal G\), including the stipulated proxy equations and label-specific mechanism deletions.  Thus Definition \texttt{def:published-lm-class} gives
\[
\overline{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{2}
\]
By the semi-Markovian clause in that definition, the treatment vertex has a private exogenous factor \(U_A\), independent of all other private and edge-specific exogenous factors.  Write its structural equation as
\[
A=f_A\bigl(\operatorname{pa}_A,U_A,U_A^{\leftrightarrow}\bigr),
\]
where \(U_A^{\leftrightarrow}\) denotes the tuple of edge-specific factors incident to \(A\); the arguments among \(\operatorname{pa}_A\) used in each missingness context are those permitted by the labels of \(\mathcal G\).

Form a new private-factor state space
\[
\mathcal U_A^{0}=\{\bot_A\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}\mathcal U_A,
\qquad
P(U_A^{0}=\bot_A)=1,
\]
and replace only the treatment equation by
\[
f_A^{0}\bigl(p,u,w\bigr)
=
\begin{cases}
a_0,&u=\bot_A,\\
f_A(p,u,w),&u\in\mathcal U_A.
\end{cases}
\tag{3}
\]
Call the resulting SCM \(\mathcal M^{0}\).  All other structural equations, all other exogenous laws, and every proxy equation are unchanged.  The new private factor is independent of all the other factors because its law is a point mass and its joint law is taken to be the product of that point mass with their old joint law.  The endogenous, indicator, and proxy state spaces are unchanged, so the finite-state condition is preserved.

It remains to check compatibility rather than infer it from the name of the construction.  On every old response branch \(u\in\mathcal U_A\), equation (3) equals the old equation, so every structural dependence that induces an edge incident to \(A\) in \(\mathcal G\) is retained.  On the new branch \(u=\bot_A\), equation (3) is constant, so it introduces no new dependence.  A parent omitted by a label was absent on every old branch and is also absent on the new constant branch.  Equations at vertices other than \(A\), the edge-specific factors, and the proxy equations were not changed.  Consequently \(\mathcal M^{0}\) induces the same labeled graph \(\mathcal G\), remains semi-Markovian, and satisfies the same proxy and label semantics.  By Definition \texttt{def:published-lm-class},
\[
\mathcal M^{0}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{4}
\]

Because \(P(U_A^{0}=\bot_A)=1\), equation (3) implies
\[
P_{\mathcal M^{0}}(A=a_0)=1.
\tag{5}
\]
The treatment value \(a_1\) is distinct from \(a_0\).  Choose any full-data configuration \((v^{0},r^{0})\in\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\) whose \(A\)-coordinate is \(a_1\); the remaining coordinates can be chosen from their nonempty state spaces.  From (5),
\[
P_{\mathcal M^{0}}(v^{0},r^{0})
\le P_{\mathcal M^{0}}(A=a_1)=0.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal M^{0}\) violates the strict-positivity membership condition in Definition \texttt{def:positive-rm-class}, and therefore
\[
\mathcal M^{0}\notin\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{7}
\]
Equations (4) and (7) prove that the inclusion in (1) is strict.  More generally, if \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) has any cell \((\widetilde v,\widetilde r)\) with \(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(\widetilde v,\widetilde r)=0\), then the same positivity clause immediately gives \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\notin\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\).  Hence every compatible finite lm-SCM with a zero full-data cell is a strictness witness.

Finally, Definition \texttt{def:causal-query} defines, on the ambient model class, the causal maps
\[
\mathcal M\longmapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=P_{\mathcal M}\bigl(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)\bigr)
\]
and
\[
\mathcal M\longmapsto \tau_{s_Y}(\mathcal M)
=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\left\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\right\}.
\]
For every \(\mathcal M^{+}\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\), inclusion (1) puts \(\mathcal M^{+}\) in the domain of these ambient maps, and substitution of \(\mathcal M^{+}\) into the two displays gives exactly \(Q_a^{\mathcal M^{+}}\) and \(\tau_{s_Y}(\mathcal M^{+})\).  They are therefore the restrictions of the published natural interventional law and its score-coded NATE contrast to \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\).  This restriction statement concerns the causal query itself; it does not identify that query with an observed-law functional.
