Under \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\), each context variable is observed and parentless. Consequently intervention on \(A\) does not change the context distribution:
\[
P(C=c\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(C=c).
\]
By \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\), identification in every context graph gives a context functional \(f^{\mathrm{ID}}_{a,c}\) and
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=\sum_cP_{\mathrm{obs}}(C=c)f^{\mathrm{ID}}_{a,c}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
\]
for every model in the fiber. This derivation selects a root context at the outset, so delayed restriction supplies no additional information. The causal coordinate is constant over the fiber; \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) therefore makes the TCF cell identifying, and \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives its stated value. The cited theorem is graphwise, while the TCF test is pointwise in \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\); without an additional uniformity argument it does not imply the deleted converse at each exceptional law.
