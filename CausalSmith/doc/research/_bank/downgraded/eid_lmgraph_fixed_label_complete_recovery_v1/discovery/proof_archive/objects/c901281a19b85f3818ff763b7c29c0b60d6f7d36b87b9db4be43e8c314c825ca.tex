Let \(r(\kappa)\) be the number of random vertices in the current CADMG, and let \(d(c)\) be the number of missingness indicators assigned by the current partial context. Define
\[
\mu(\kappa)=r(\kappa)+|\mathcal R|-d(c).
\tag{1}
\]
The graph is finite by the theorem premise, and \(\text{ass:finite-alphabets}\) makes every enumerated state space finite. Since \(n\) bounds both \(r(\kappa)\) and \(|\mathcal R|\), initially \(0\leq\mu(\kappa)\leq2n\). By the corrected \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), a strict ancestral marginalization, passage to one child of a nontrivial district decomposition, or licensed fixing decreases \(r(\kappa)\) by at least one. A delayed selection strictly extends \(c\), and therefore increases \(d(c)\) by at least one. Each recursive transition thus decreases the nonnegative integer \(\mu\) by at least one. Consequently every recursive operation word has length at most
\[
t=2n.
\tag{2}
\]

There are at most \(2^n\) observed margins and, by the corrected definition of the size parameter \(c\), at most \(c\) partial missingness contexts. Hence there are at most \(2^n c\) initial traces. At a state, ancestral marginalization and district restriction each have at most \(2^n\) subset choices, licensed fixing has at most \(n(\ell+1)\) vertex-license choices, and strict context extension has at most \(c\) choices. Allocating one additional terminal marker gives the harmless alphabet bound
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1.
\tag{3}
\]
The terminal marker is not a recursive transition and is not appended to the words bounded in (2). For a fixed initial trace and recursive operation word, every graphical update and every algebraic update of \(q\), \(\pi\), and \(\Lambda\) is prescribed by \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\). Thus that pair determines the literal complete state. Equations (2)--(3) imply
\[
N_{\mathrm{state}}
\leq 2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j
=B_{\mathrm{state}}.
\tag{4}
\]
This count retains every fixing-selection interleaving and every provenance-bearing history. It neither commutes fixing with selection nor erases \(\Lambda\). The optional adjacent-swap optimization is permitted by the corrected procedure only after replay from a common pre-swap state and literal equality of the two full tuples; \(\text{lem:colored-adjacent-swap}\) supplies the common kernel-level swap comparison but not equality of the raw trace suffixes. Any equality-checked merge can only decrease the number in (4).

At most one package is generated for each visited terminal state and each \((a,y)\in\mathcal X_A\times\mathcal X_Y\). Therefore
\[
N_{\mathrm{term}}
\leq |\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}}.
\tag{5}
\]
Each partition-generating formula has at most two scoped response-vector copies. Each copy contains \(g+hK_{\mathcal G}\) coordinates, and \(\mathbf p\) has \(|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\) free coordinates. Since scoped response names are reused across packages as stipulated by \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), the largest variable index is bounded by
\[
p=2\{g+hK_{\mathcal G}\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|.
\tag{6}
\]
By the corrected definition of the size parameter \(s\), the partition-generating part of one package has encoding length at most \(s\). Concatenating the parts counted in (5), with at most one separator or Boolean connective per package, gives
\[
S_{\mathrm{tot}}=N_{\mathrm{term}}(s+1).
\tag{7}
\]

It remains to prove that the displayed CAD budget is finite. At each of the at most \(p\) projection levels, the fixed signed-subresultant implementation forms finitely many coefficients, discriminants, and resultants from a finite polynomial list. Every nonzero signed-subresultant remainder has strictly smaller degree than its predecessor, so each univariate remainder sequence terminates and creates finitely many root-isolation tasks. Lifting performs finitely many sign evaluations over the finitely many cells already constructed. Hence this fixed implementation terminates on every valid input using at most \(p\) variable slots and at most \(S_{\mathrm{tot}}\) encoded bits. There are finitely many binary strings of length at most \(S_{\mathrm{tot}}\), so the maximum defining \(\operatorname{CAD}(p,S_{\mathrm{tot}})\) exists and bounds all projection, isolation, sign-evaluation, and output-cell steps. Combining this fact with (4) gives
\[
N_{\mathrm{state}}+N_{\mathrm{CAD}}
\leq B_{\mathrm{state}}+\operatorname{CAD}(p,S_{\mathrm{tot}})
=B_{\mathrm{tot}}.
\tag{8}
\]

Finally, \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) contains finitely many integer polynomials. Under \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\), rational inputs use exact integer arithmetic, real-algebraic inputs use the supplied square-free polynomials and isolating intervals, and exact-real inputs use the supplied sign oracle. Thus the unique cell is selected after finitely many sign decisions. The same finite subresultant and lifting construction represents the unique value or a witness by Thom encodings over the real closure of \(\mathbb Q(\mathbf p)\); every comparison of their coefficients reduces to one of the exact sign decisions supplied by \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). These specialization operations terminate but are excluded from (8). Equations (1)--(8) establish a finite symbolic bound and no polynomial-time bound.
