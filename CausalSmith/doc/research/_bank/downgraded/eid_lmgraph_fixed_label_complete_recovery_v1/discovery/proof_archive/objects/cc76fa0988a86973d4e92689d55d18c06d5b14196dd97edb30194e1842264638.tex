Let the quantities in \(\text{def:termination-budget}\) be fixed. For every finite \(\mathcal G\) and finite alphabets, the history-preserving trace enumeration of \(\operatorname{TCF}(\mathcal G,A,Y,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) visits at most
\[
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}\bigl\{2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1\bigr\}^j
\]
literal complete trace states \(\kappa=(\mathcal H,F,c,q,\pi,\Lambda)\), counting every fixing-selection interleaving and provenance-bearing operation history. The trace-independent feasibility and coordinatewise-constancy formulas have one base-CAD cost
\[
C_{\mathrm{base}}=\operatorname{CAD}(p,s_{\mathrm{base}}),
\]
which also bounds the number of canonical cells. There are at most \(N_{\mathrm{trace}}=|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}}\) coordinatewise trace candidates and at most \(N_{\mathrm{val}}=N_{\mathrm{trace}}C_{\mathrm{base}}\) cell-candidate validations. Consequently the canonical partition and every rational annotation are produced within
\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}
+N_{\mathrm{val}}\operatorname{CAD}(p,s_{\mathrm{ann}})
\]
state visits, projection, root-isolation, sign-evaluation steps, and output cells. Given \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), cell selection and Thom-encoded value or witness extraction over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\) terminate after finitely many exact sign decisions. No point computation is claimed for arbitrary unencoded population reals, and no polynomial-time claim is made.
