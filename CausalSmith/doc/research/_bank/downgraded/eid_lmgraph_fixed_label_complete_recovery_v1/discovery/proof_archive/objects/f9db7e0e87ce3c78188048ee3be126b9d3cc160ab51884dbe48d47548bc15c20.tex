Let \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). By \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\) and \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\), \(\mathcal M\) belongs to the published ambient class, has finite endogenous alphabets, has a strictly positive full-data law, and has the mutually independent individual-edge and private factors indexed by
\[
\mathcal J=\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)\sqcup\mathcal V\sqcup\mathcal R.
\tag{1}
\]
The hypotheses needed for finite support reduction are therefore supplied separately by \(\text{ass:finite-alphabets}\), \(\text{ass:acyclic}\), \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), and \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\). Apply \(\text{lem:finite-factor-support-reduction}\). With
\[
L=|\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}|,
\qquad
K_{\sharp}=L+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1),
\tag{2}
\]
it replaces each factor in (1) by one having at most \(K_{\sharp}\) support points, preserves mutual independence, and simultaneously preserves every full-data probability and every interventional outcome coordinate:
\[
P(v,r)=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),
\qquad
P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{3}
\]
Pad each reduced factor by zero-weight states to the common alphabet \(\{1,\ldots,K_{\sharp}\}\), which changes neither equality in (3). Write its weights as \(\rho_{h,k}\). The restricted structural equations form one tuple \(g_*\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\), because the support-reduction lemma preserves every label-required omission. Set
\[
\gamma_{g_*}=1,
\qquad
\gamma_g=0\quad(g\neq g_*).
\tag{4}
\]
Then
\[
\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0,
\quad
\sum_g\gamma_g=1,
\quad
\rho_{h,k}\geq0,
\quad
\sum_{k=1}^{K_{\sharp}}\rho_{h,k}=1.
\tag{5}
\]
By deterministic acyclic evaluation and the product law of the factors, \(\text{def:response-polynomial-fiber}\) and (3)--(5) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
&=\sum_{u}\mathbf 1\{g_*(u)=(v,r)\}
\prod_{h\in\mathcal J}\rho_{h,u_h}
=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),\\
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
&=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Proxy consistency, stated in \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\), permits summation of the first line over precisely the full cells mapping to an observed proxy cell. Since \(\mathcal M\) lies in the observational fiber,
\[
p_o(\theta_{\mathcal M})
=P_{\mathcal M}(\mathcal O=o)
=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o).
\tag{7}
\]
The full-cell positivity clause of \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\) and (6) gives \(p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})>0\) for every \((v,r)\). Equations (5)--(7) are exactly the conjuncts of
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{8}
\]

Conversely, suppose \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Over the reals, \(\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0\) implies \(\gamma_g\in\{0,1\}\). Together with \(\sum_g\gamma_g=1\), this gives a unique \(g_*\) satisfying \(\gamma_{g_*}=1\). For every \(h\in\mathcal J\), let \(U_h\) have distribution
\[
P(U_h=k)=\rho_{h,k},
\qquad 1\leq k\leq K_{\sharp},
\tag{9}
\]
and take all the factors in (9) mutually independent. Use the selected deterministic tables \(g_*\) as the structural equations. Membership \(g_*\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\) ensures that the equations use only graph parents and identify table entries exactly where \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\) requires an omitted parent. Thus the decoded model \(\mathcal M_\theta\) is semi-Markovian with the individual-edge product structure and obeys the label semantics. Its proxy equations obey \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\).

Again by product evaluation,
\[
P_{\mathcal M_\theta}(v,r)
=\sum_u\mathbf 1\{g_*(u)=(v,r)\}
\prod_{h\in\mathcal J}\rho_{h,u_h}
=p_{v,r}(\theta)>0.
\tag{10}
\]
The observed-cell constraints and proxy consistency yield
\[
P_{\mathcal M_\theta}(\mathcal O=o)
=p_o(\theta)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o).
\tag{11}
\]
The finite alphabets are those fixed in the polynomial construction, while regularity and maximality are properties of the input graph. Therefore (10)--(11) and \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\) imply
\[
\mathcal M_\theta\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{12}
\]
Finally, intervention replaces only the selected equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\), so the same finite product calculation gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_\theta}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta).
\tag{13}
\]
Equations (8) and (10)--(13) prove both directions, including full-cell positivity, exact observed-law equality, label compatibility, and preservation of the causal query.
