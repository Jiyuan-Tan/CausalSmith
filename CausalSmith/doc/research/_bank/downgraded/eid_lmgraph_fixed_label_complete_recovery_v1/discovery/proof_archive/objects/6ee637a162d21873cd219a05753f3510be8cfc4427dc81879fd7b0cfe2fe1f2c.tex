For the forward direction, take \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Apply \(\text{lem:finite-factor-support-reduction}\) to all individual-edge and private factors. The resulting factors have alphabets of size at most \(K_{\mathcal G}\), remain mutually independent, and preserve every \(P_{\mathcal M}(v,r)\) and \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\). Pad each factor with zero-weight states and denote its weights by \(\rho_{h,k}\). The restricted structural equations form one tuple \(g_*\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\); set \(\gamma_{g_*}=1\) and \(\gamma_g=0\) for \(g\neq g_*\). Then
\[
\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0,
\quad
\sum_g\gamma_g=1,
\quad
\rho_{h,k}\geq0,
\quad
\sum_k\rho_{h,k}=1.
\]
By deterministic evaluation and independence,
\[
p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
=\sum_{u}\mathbf 1\{g_*(u)=(v,r)\}\prod_h\rho_{h,u_h}
=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),
\]
and the intervention evaluation gives
\[
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\]
The first equality and proxy consistency imply \(p_o(\theta_{\mathcal M})=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o)\). Membership in the positive class gives \(p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})>0\). These are exactly all conjuncts of \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).

Conversely, suppose \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Each equation \(\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0\) implies \(\gamma_g\in\{0,1\}\); together with \(\sum_g\gamma_g=1\), there is exactly one \(g_*\) with \(\gamma_{g_*}=1\). For each factor \(h\), let \(U_h\) have distribution \(P(U_h=k)=\rho_{h,k}\), and take the factors mutually independent. Use the deterministic tables \(g_*\) as structural equations. Since \(g_*\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\), its arguments respect the directed graph and every table identification required by a label. Thus the decoded SCM is compatible with \(\mathcal G\), is semi-Markovian with one factor for each individual bidirected edge and each private vertex, and obeys the label semantics. Its full law is
\[
P_{\mathcal M_\theta}(v,r)
=\sum_u\mathbf 1\{g_*(u)=(v,r)\}\prod_h\rho_{h,u_h}
=p_{v,r}(\theta)>0.
\]
The observed-law conjuncts and proxy consistency give \(P_{\mathcal M_\theta}(\mathcal O=o)=p_o(\theta)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o)\). Since regularity and maximality are properties of the fixed input graph, \(\mathcal M_\theta\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Replacing the equation for \(A\) by \(a\) in the same finite product sum gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_\theta}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta),
\]
which completes the converse.
