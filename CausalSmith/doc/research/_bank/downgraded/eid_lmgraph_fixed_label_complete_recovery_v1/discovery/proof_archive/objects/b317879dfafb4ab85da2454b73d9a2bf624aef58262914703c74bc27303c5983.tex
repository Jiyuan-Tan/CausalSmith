\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=f_a^{\mathrm{ID}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\]
The right-hand side is common to the entire fiber. Hence \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) makes the cell identifying, and \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) makes its value the ordinary ID functional. Graphwise failure is an existential statement over models, whereas the TCF partition tests constancy at one observed law; this proves why the asserted one-way reduction does not justify the deleted converse.
