Let \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) be compatible with the finite, strictly positive regular-maximal class, so \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\neq\varnothing\).  Fix \((a,y)\), and let \(\psi_j\) be the unique TCF cell containing the observed-cell vector.

First take \(\xi\in\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\).  The cited recovery result \(\text{lem:da4-recovery}\) gives, for every \(\mathcal M\) in the fiber,
\[
f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{1}
\]
Because the left side depends only on the observed law, the query coordinate is constant over the fiber.  The equivalence in \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) makes \(\psi_j\) identifying, and \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{2}
\]
Combining (1)--(2) proves
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{3}
\]

If instead \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\) holds, the cited result \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\) gives
\[
f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{4}
\]
The same constancy, dichotomy, and soundness argument now gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{5}
\]
This is an embedding on the common finite, full-positive domain; no implication beyond that common domain has been used.

To prove strictness of (3), fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\).  The graph contains \(A\leftrightarrow Y\).  This edge remains an open length-one path after directed arrows out of \(A\) are deleted, and no admissible conditioning vertex lies on that path.  Thus condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) in every proposed de Aguas certificate fails, so
\[
\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G(H,m),A,Y)=\varnothing.
\tag{6}
\]
On the other hand, \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\) gives for every model in the nonempty fiber
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_hP_{\mathrm{obs}}(h)
\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathrm{obs}}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)\\
&\qquad\times\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(a'\mid h,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Strict full-data positivity implies positivity of every marginal conditioning event in (7), so every division is defined.  The right side is an observed-law functional; therefore the coordinate is constant on the fiber, and \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) plus \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) shows that the TCF cell carries (7).  Equations (6)--(7) exhibit an entire TCF-identified family outside the de Aguas certificate domain, proving the asserted strict class-relative extension.

Finally, every member of this family contains \(C\to R_W\).  Thus \(R_W\) is not an observed causal root, and the premise of \(\operatorname{RC}\) is inapplicable.  This is only a failure of the root-control premise, not a strictness conclusion about its full published domain.  When \(H=\{C\}\) and \(m=1\), \(\text{prop:eipfd-star-worked}\) identifies (7) term by term with \(f_a^\star(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\), completing the proof.
