\(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) is the published ambient class of semi-Markovian labeled-missingness SCMs on the finite vertex graph \(\mathcal G\): endogenous and exogenous state spaces may be measurable categorical or continuous standard Borel spaces, structural maps are measurable, the individual bidirected-edge and private exogenous factors are mutually independent, the proxy equations satisfy proxy consistency, and every labeled mechanism deletion has the semantics encoded by \(\mathcal G\). Finiteness of endogenous alphabets and strict positivity are not part of this ambient definition; they enter only through \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\).
