For a trace state \(\kappa\), let \(r(\kappa)\) be the number of random vertices in its CADMG and let \(d(c)\) be the number of missingness indicators assigned by its partial context. Define
\[
\mu(\kappa)=r(\kappa)+|\mathcal R|-d(c).
\tag{1}
\]
This is a nonnegative integer initially at most \(2n\). Under \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), strict ancestral marginalization, restriction to a proper district child, and fixing each reduce the random set, while delayed selection strictly increases \(d(c)\). Hence every operation word has length at most \(2n\).

There are at most \(2^n\) observed margins and
\[
c=\prod_{R_Z\in\mathcal R}(|\mathcal X_{R_Z}|+1)
\tag{2}
\]
partial contexts. The two subset operations contribute at most \(2^{n+1}\) choices, fixing contributes at most \(n(\ell+1)\), selection contributes at most \(c\), and one terminal marker contributes one. Thus
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j.
\tag{3}
\]
For a fixed initial trace and operation word, every component of \(\kappa=(\mathcal H,F,c,q,\pi,\Lambda)\) is prescribed. Therefore (3) bounds the literal complete states, including every operation order and provenance list. By \(\text{lem:colored-adjacent-swap}\), kernel commutation alone does not erase unequal trace suffixes; the optional canonical-replay merge in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) only decreases (3).

Write \(h=|\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)|+|\mathcal V|+|\mathcal R|\) and \(g=|\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}|\). A doubled coordinate or target formula uses at most \(2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}\) response coordinates and \(|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\) free observed coordinates; the target formula additionally has \(|\mathcal X_Y|\) free score coordinates. Hence their variable counts are bounded by
\[
p_{\mathrm{coord}}=2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|,
\qquad
p_{\tau}=2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|+|\mathcal X_Y|.
\tag{4}
\]
Their complete encodings have length at most \(s\) by \(\text{def:termination-budget}\). The definition of \(\operatorname{CAD}(m,b)\) therefore bounds the two trace-independent base calls by
\[
C_{\mathrm{coord}}=\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\mathrm{coord}},s),
\quad
C_{\tau}=\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\tau},s),
\quad
C_{\mathrm{base}}=C_{\mathrm{coord}}+C_{\tau}.
\tag{5}
\]

Each visited trace offers at most one candidate for each coordinate and coordinate cell, and at most one direct-target candidate for each target cell. Consequently the number of whole-cell tests is at most
\[
N_{\mathrm{check}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}
\left\{|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|C_{\mathrm{coord}}+C_{\tau}\right\}.
\tag{6}
\]
After denominators are cleared, a coordinate test contains at most one response vector, \(z\), and \(\mathbf p\); a target test contains at most one response vector, \(z\), \(\mathbf p\), and \(\mathbf s\). Thus each has at most \(p_{\max}+1\) variables and encoding length at most \(s\). Each is decided within \(\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\max}+1,s)\) steps. Summing (3), (5), and (6) proves
\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}
+N_{\mathrm{check}}\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\max}+1,s).
\tag{7}
\]

Both partitions contain finitely many integer-polynomial signs. The exact observed-law interface decides the coordinate signs, and the augmented exact interface decides the joint observed-law and score signs. Standard finite root isolation from the fixed CAD implementation then supplies the asserted Thom encodings over the corresponding generated ordered field. These specialization steps are finite and separate from (7). No step defines computation on an unencoded real or gives a polynomial bound.
