For every finite \(\mathcal G\) and finite alphabets, the symbolic compilation \(\operatorname{TCF}(\mathcal G,A,Y,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) visits at most \[B_{\mathrm{state}}(n,\ell,c,t)=6^n c\sum_{j=0}^{t}(\ell+1)^j\] trace states, where \(n\) counts vertices, \(\ell\) labels, \(c\) partial contexts, and \(t\leq n\) trace entries. It generates at most \(N_{\mathrm{term}}=|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}}\) doubled terminal formulas. With \(p=2\{g+hK_{\mathcal G}\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\), per-formula encoding length at most \(s\), and \(S_{\mathrm{tot}}=N_{\mathrm{term}}(s+1)\), one common CAD produces \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) within \[B_{\mathrm{state}}+\operatorname{CAD}(p,S_{\mathrm{tot}})\] state transitions, projection, root-isolation, sign-evaluation steps, and output cells. Given \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), cell specialization terminates by finitely many exact sign tests. No point-computation claim is made for arbitrary unencoded population reals, and no polynomial-time claim is made.
