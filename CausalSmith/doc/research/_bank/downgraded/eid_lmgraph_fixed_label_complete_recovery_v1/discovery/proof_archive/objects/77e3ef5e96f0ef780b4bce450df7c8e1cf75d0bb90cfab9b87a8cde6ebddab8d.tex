Fix \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and first take \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). By \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\) and \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\), \(\mathcal M\) has finite endogenous alphabets, its full law is strictly positive, and its exogenous variables consist of mutually independent individual-edge and private factors. Let
\[
L=\left|\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\right|,
\qquad
K_{\mathcal G}=L+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1).
\tag{1}
\]
Applying \(\text{lem:finite-factor-support-reduction}\) sequentially to those factors yields a label-compatible deterministic response representation in which each factor has support at most \(K_{\mathcal G}\), mutual independence is preserved, and simultaneously
\[
P(V=v,R=r)=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),
\qquad
P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\tag{2}
\]
for all \((v,r,a,y)\). The lemma obtains the bound in (1) from the affine dimension \((L-1)+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1)\); no lower bound or sharpness assertion is used here.

Pad a factor having fewer than \(K_{\mathcal G}\) support points by zero-weight states. Let \(\rho_{h,k}\) be the resulting marginal weights. The support reduction preserves the original structural equations on all positive-weight states, and \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\) makes their restricted tables an element \(g_*\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\). Set
\[
\gamma_{g_*}=1,
\qquad
\gamma_g=0\quad(g\neq g_*).
\tag{3}
\]
Equations (3) imply
\[
\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0,
\qquad
\sum_g\gamma_g=1,
\tag{4}
\]
while the factor laws give
\[
\rho_{h,k}\geq0,
\qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{K_{\mathcal G}}\rho_{h,k}=1
\quad\text{for every }h.
\tag{5}
\]
By the product independence in \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), deterministic evaluation of the response tables gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
&=\sum_u\mathbf 1\{g_*(u)=(v,r)\}\prod_h\rho_{h,u_h}
=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),\\
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
&=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y),
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
where the second line uses the intervention equality in (2) and \(\text{def:causal-query}\). Proxy consistency and the first line of (6) imply
\[
p_o(\theta_{\mathcal M})
=\sum_{(v,r):\operatorname{proxy}(v,r)=o}P_{\mathcal M}(v,r)
=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o).
\tag{7}
\]
Strict positivity in \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\) gives \(p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})>0\) for every full cell. Equations (4)--(7) are exactly the conjuncts of \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), proving the forward direction.

Conversely, suppose \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Over \(\mathbb R\), each equation \(\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0\) implies \(\gamma_g\in\{0,1\}\). Together with \(\sum_g\gamma_g=1\), this yields a unique \(g_*\) satisfying \(\gamma_{g_*}=1\). For each \(h\in\mathcal J\), let \(U_h\) have law
\[
P(U_h=k)=\rho_{h,k},
\qquad 1\leq k\leq K_{\mathcal G},
\tag{8}
\]
and construct these finitely many factors independently. Use \(g_*\) as the structural-table tuple. Since \(g_*\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\), the equations respect the directed parents and every table identification imposed by a label. Thus \(\text{def:published-lm-class}\), \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\), and \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\) show that the decoded object \(\mathcal M_\theta\) is an lm-SCM inducing \(\mathcal G\). Its finite response expansion is
\[
P_{\mathcal M_\theta}(v,r)
=\sum_u\mathbf 1\{g_*(u)=(v,r)\}\prod_h\rho_{h,u_h}
=p_{v,r}(\theta)>0.
\tag{9}
\]
The observed-cell equalities in \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}\), proxy consistency, and (9) give
\[
P_{\mathcal M_\theta}(\mathcal O=o)=p_o(\theta)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o).
\tag{10}
\]
Regularity and maximality are properties of the fixed input graph, and (9) supplies strict positivity. Hence (10) and \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\) imply \(\mathcal M_\theta\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Finally, replacing the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\) in the same product expansion gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_\theta}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta).
\tag{11}
\]
Equations (1)--(11) prove both directions without replacing the graph's individual-edge factorization by an unrestricted district latent.
