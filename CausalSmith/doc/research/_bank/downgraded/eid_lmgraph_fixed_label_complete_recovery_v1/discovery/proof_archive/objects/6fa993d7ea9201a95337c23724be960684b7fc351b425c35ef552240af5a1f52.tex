Fix a compatible model \(\mathcal M\) inducing \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}\). In \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{3b}}\), \(R_W\) has only its private exogenous factor and its only directed child is \(A\). After replacing the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\), the structural evaluation of \(Y\) contains no factor appearing in the equation for \(R_W\). Mutual independence of the individual-edge and private factors therefore gives, for every supported \(r\),
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W=r)
=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)).
\tag{6}
\]

When \(R_W=0\), label semantics deletes \(W\to A\). The remaining treatment equation is a function of \(R_W,Z\), and the private factor of \(A\). The counterfactual \(Y(a)\) is a function of \(a\), the private factor of \(Y\), and the factors on \(W\leftrightarrow Y\) and \(R_Z\leftrightarrow Y\). The former inputs are independent of the latter inputs. Hence
\[
Y(a)\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}A\mid R_W=0.
\tag{7}
\]
SCM consistency and (7), followed by (6), give
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{3b}}(Y=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)).
\end{aligned}
\]
Strict positivity justifies both conditionings and divisions.

The licensed fixing step records the pre-selection provenance for the kernel in (6); \(\Sigma_{R_W=0}\) then restricts it and retains that record. Conversely, if restriction is the first operation, the initial trace list is empty and every later provenance expression is a function of the already selected kernel. By the constructions of \(\Phi\) and \(\Sigma\), no later operation inserts the absent unselected provenance, proving the closure claim. Finally, the displayed observed expression has the same value in every model of the fiber. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the unique cell is therefore identifying and carries that value.
