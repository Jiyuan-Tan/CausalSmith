\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\]
The finite scan returns either the first candidate record satisfying every explicitly tested predicate in \(\Delta(\omega,\mathcal T)\), together with its candidate colored forest, or the tag \(\mathtt{no\mbox{-}colored\mbox{-}record}\).
