Let \(\xi\in\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\). The published recovery result \(\text{lem:da4-recovery}\) gives, for every model in the fiber,
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\]
Thus the causal coordinate is constant on the fiber. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) the unique TCF cell is identifying, and by \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) its value is exactly the displayed de Aguas functional. The same argument with \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\) gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
\]
and proves containment of the root-control procedure.

Now fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\). The open length-one path \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) survives deletion of arrows out of \(A\) and cannot be blocked by a conditioning set excluding its endpoints. Therefore de Aguas condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) fails for every proposed \(\xi\). Also \(C\to R_W\) makes the label context variable non-root, violating the root-control hypothesis. On the other hand, \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\) gives exactly
\[
\sum_hP_{\mathrm{obs}}(h)
\sum_bP_{\mathrm{obs}}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)
\sum_{a'}P_{\mathrm{obs}}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(a'\mid h,R_W=0),
\]
so the TCF cell is identifying by the semialgebraic dichotomy and carries this value by soundness. This proves strictness against both procedures on every family member. Setting \(H=\{C\}\) and \(m=1\) reduces the formula term by term to \(f_a^\star\), as proved in \(\text{prop:eipfd-star-worked}\).
