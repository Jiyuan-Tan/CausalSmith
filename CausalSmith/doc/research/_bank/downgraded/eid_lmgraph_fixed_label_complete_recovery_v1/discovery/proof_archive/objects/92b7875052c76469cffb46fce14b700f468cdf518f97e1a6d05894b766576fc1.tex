For every finite \(\mathcal G\) and finite alphabets, the symbolic compilation \(\operatorname{TCF}(\mathcal G,A,Y,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) visits at most
\[
B_{\mathrm{state}}(n,\ell,c,t)=6^n c\sum_{j=0}^{t}(\ell+1)^j
\]
canonical tuples, where \(n\) counts vertices, \(\ell\) labels, \(c\) partial contexts, and \(t\leq n\) trace entries. It generates at most \(|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}}\) doubled terminal formulas. With \(h=|\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)|+|\mathcal V|+|\mathcal R|\), \(g=|\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}|\), \(p=2\{g+hK_{\mathcal G}\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\), and symbolic formula encoding length \(s\), the fixed CAD produces \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) within
\[
B_{\mathrm{state}}\{1+|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|\operatorname{CAD}(p,s)\}
\]
state transitions, QE steps, and output cells. Given \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), cell specialization terminates by finitely many exact sign tests. No point-computation claim is made for arbitrary unencoded population reals, and no polynomial-time claim is made.
