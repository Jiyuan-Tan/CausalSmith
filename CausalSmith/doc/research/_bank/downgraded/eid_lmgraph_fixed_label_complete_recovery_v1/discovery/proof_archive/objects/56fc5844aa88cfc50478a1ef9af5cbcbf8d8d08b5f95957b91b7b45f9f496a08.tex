By \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\), the trace-independent base computation and the subsequent finite trace-annotation computation terminate. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the base cells are pairwise disjoint and cover exactly
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}
=\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
If \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) is compatible, the forward direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) supplies \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) for any \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), with
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{2}
\]
Thus \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\) lies in (1) and belongs to one and only one canonical cell.

Fix \((a,y)\). The dichotomy and response-fiber equivalence give
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow
q_{a,y}\text{ is constant on }
\{\theta:\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}\\
&\quad\Longleftrightarrow
\mathcal M\longmapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
Suppose (3) is true. On this cell, the value relation
\[
\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z)
\Longleftrightarrow
\exists\theta\,[\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)
\wedge z=q_{a,y}(\theta)]
\tag{4}
\]
has exactly one \(z\). The cell functional \(f_{a,j}\) is represented either by this unique-value graph or by a rational trace expression \(n/d\). In the latter case, \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) stores the annotation only after proving throughout the whole cell that \(d\neq0\) and
\[
d(\mathbf p)q_{a,y}(\theta)=n(\mathbf p)
\quad\text{whenever}\quad
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p).
\tag{5}
\]
For arbitrary \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) supplies \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) satisfying (2) and
\[
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{6}
\]
Substitution of (2) and (6) into (4), or into (5) when a rational annotation is stored, proves rather than defines the equality
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad
\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{7}
\]

Suppose instead that (3) is false. The dichotomy says that the stored \(\mathsf W_j\) is satisfiable and, without an exact-input assumption, yields
\[
\begin{gathered}
\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),\\
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\end{gathered}
\tag{8}
\]
Because the models belong to the fiber, every full-data cell is positive and their latent laws retain the product over individual bidirected-edge and private factors. Applying \(\text{prop:ambient-nonrecovery-transfer}\) to (8) shows that the same pair also refutes identification over the larger published ambient observed-law fiber. This is an upward transfer of a negative result; it makes no ambient achievability claim.

If \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) is supplied, \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) represents a satisfying pair for \(\mathsf W_j\) by Thom encodings. The retained operation history is finite by \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\). Therefore \(\text{thm:qe-failure-compiler}\) decodes the pair in (8) and terminates with either a checked divergence record or the exhaustion tag. No clause of that theorem, and no present step, asserts a colored hedge.

Finally, suppose (7) holds for every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\) at both \(a_0\) and \(a_1\). The outcome alphabet is finite by \(\text{ass:finite-alphabets}\). Expanding \(\text{def:causal-query}\) and substituting (7) coordinate by coordinate gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau_{s_Y}
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\}\\
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
Equations (1)--(9) prove the explicit identifying map, the non-exact existential obstruction, the exact extraction contract, and the finite-subclass-to-ambient nonidentification transfer.
