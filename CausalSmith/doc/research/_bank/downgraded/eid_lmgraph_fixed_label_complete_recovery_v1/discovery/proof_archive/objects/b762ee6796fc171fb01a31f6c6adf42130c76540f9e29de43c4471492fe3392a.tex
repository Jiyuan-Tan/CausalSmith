Apply \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\) with \(H=\{C\}\) and \(m=1\).  Its strict-positivity premise is inherited from \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G_\star)\), so every conditional denominator in the displayed functional is positive.  The lemma gives, for every model in the observed-law fiber,
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_{c\in\mathcal X_C}P_{\mathrm{obs}}(c)
\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathrm{obs}}(b\mid a,c,R_W=0)\\
&\qquad\times\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(y\mid b,a',c,R_W=0)
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(a'\mid c,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\text{def:eipfd-star-functional}\), the right side of (1) is exactly \(f_a^\star(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\).  The proof of the cited lemma first retains the interventional kernel conditional on \(R_W\), then invokes the label only in the selected context \(R_W=0\), performs the front-door adjustment through \(B_1\) conditional on \(C\), and finally uses the unselected marginal \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(C)\).  This is the operation order stated in the proposition.

We next exclude a de Aguas certificate.  By \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\), \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) is present in \(\mathcal G_\star\).  Deleting directed arrows out of \(A\) does not delete a bidirected edge.  Hence, in every candidate graph \(\mathcal H_r[\underline A]\), the edge \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) is an open path of length one.  It has no nonendpoint that an admissible conditioning set could block.  Therefore the d-separation required by condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) in \(\text{def:da4-certificate-functional}\) fails for every candidate \((K,H,\{L_r\}_r)\), and
\[
\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G_\star,A,Y)=\varnothing.
\tag{2}
\]
The graph also contains \(C\to R_W\).  Thus the label-context variable \(R_W\) is not parentless, so the observed-causal-root premise in \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G_\star,A,Y)\) fails:
\[
\neg\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G_\star,A,Y).
\tag{3}
\]

Finally, (1) is a functional only of \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), and it equals the causal coordinate for every model in the fiber.  Hence that coordinate is constant on the fiber.  By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the unique TCF cell containing every compatible observed law is identifying for the coordinate.  By \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\), the value carried by that cell equals (1), namely \(f_a^\star(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\).  Together with (2)--(3), this proves every assertion.
