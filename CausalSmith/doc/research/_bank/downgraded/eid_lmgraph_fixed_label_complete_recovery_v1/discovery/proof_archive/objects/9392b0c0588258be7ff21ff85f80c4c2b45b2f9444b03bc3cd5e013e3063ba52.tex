For every finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\) and supplied \(A,Y\), the symbolic wrapper \(\operatorname{TCF}\) constructs a finite semialgebraic partition \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) of
\[
\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}.
\]
For every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), the unique partition cell carries a unique value \(f_a(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) if and only if the map \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\); on the identifying side,
\[
f_a(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\]
Otherwise the cell carries \(\mathsf W_j\), and under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) every extracted sample decodes to the strictly positive, individual-edge-factorized, same-observed pair of \(\text{thm:qe-failure-compiler}\) with unequal \((a,y)\) causal coordinates. If the cell identifies every \((a_i,y)\), where \(i\in\{0,1\}\) and \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), then
\[
\tau_{s_Y}=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)\{f_{a_1}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\]
No implementation-independent graphical-failure dichotomy, colored-hedge existence claim, or alignment of a negative-cell sample with a candidate colored forest is asserted.
