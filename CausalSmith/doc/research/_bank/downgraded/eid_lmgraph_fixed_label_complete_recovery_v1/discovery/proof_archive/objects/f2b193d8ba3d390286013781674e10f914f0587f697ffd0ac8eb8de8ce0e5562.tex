Relative to the common finite, every-full-cell-positive, regular-maximal subclass, TCF strictly extends the de Aguas Proposition 4 sufficient criterion: for every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and every \(\xi\in\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\), the unique TCF cell is identifying and is annotated by \(f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\). For an observed-root-control DAG covered by the Mokhtarian--Jamshidi--Etesami--Kiyavash theorem, its embedding as a finite positive lm-graph has an identifying TCF cell annotated by \(f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\); this is an embedding of that theorem on the common intersection, not an ambient-class extension. Strictness of the class-relative applicability claim is witnessed by every \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\): the edge \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) defeats de Aguas condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\), and \(C\to R_W\) violates the observed-root-control hypothesis.
