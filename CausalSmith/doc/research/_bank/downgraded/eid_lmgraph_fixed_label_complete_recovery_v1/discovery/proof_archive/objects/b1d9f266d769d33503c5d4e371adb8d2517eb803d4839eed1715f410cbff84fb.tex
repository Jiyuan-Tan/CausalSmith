\(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) is the published ambient class of semi-Markovian labeled-missingness SCMs inducing \(\mathcal G\): the endogenous mechanisms are measurable, substantive variables may be categorical or continuous, the individual bidirected-edge and private exogenous factors are mutually independent, the proxy and label semantics hold, and the induced observed-data law has strictly positive mass in the discrete case or a strictly positive density with respect to its stated dominating measure in the continuous case. Finiteness of the endogenous alphabets, positivity of every full-data cell, and regular-maximal labels are not part of this ambient definition.
