Let \(\mathcal J=\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)\sqcup\mathcal V\sqcup\mathcal R\) and let \(K_{\sharp}=|\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}|+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1)\). For \(g\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\) introduce \(\gamma_g\), and for \(h\in\mathcal J\) and \(1\leq k\leq K_{\sharp}\) introduce \(\rho_{h,k}\). For a full cell \((v,r)\), define
\[
p_{v,r}(\theta)=\sum_{g\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}}\sum_{u\in\{1,\ldots,K_{\sharp}\}^{\mathcal J}}\mathbf 1\{g(u)=(v,r)\}\gamma_g\prod_{h\in\mathcal J}\rho_{h,u_h},
\qquad
p_o(\theta)=\sum_{(v,r):\operatorname{proxy}(v,r)=o}p_{v,r}(\theta).
\]
Define \(q_{a,y}(\theta)\) by the same independent-factor sum after replacing the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\). With formal coordinates \(\mathbf p=(p_o)_o\), define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p):={}&
\bigwedge_{g\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}}(\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0)
\ \wedge\ \sum_g\gamma_g=1\\
&\wedge\bigwedge_{h\in\mathcal J}
\left(\bigwedge_{k=1}^{K_{\sharp}}\rho_{h,k}\geq0
\ \wedge\ \sum_{k=1}^{K_{\sharp}}\rho_{h,k}=1\right)\\
&\wedge\bigwedge_{(v,r)}p_{v,r}(\theta)>0
\ \wedge\ \bigwedge_{o\in\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}}p_o(\theta)=p_o.
\end{aligned}
\]
The product over the individual edge and private factors enforces the semi-Markovian independence structure. The one-hot equations encode a finite union over fixed, label-compatible graph-wide structural-table tuples and do not introduce a shared district latent variable.
