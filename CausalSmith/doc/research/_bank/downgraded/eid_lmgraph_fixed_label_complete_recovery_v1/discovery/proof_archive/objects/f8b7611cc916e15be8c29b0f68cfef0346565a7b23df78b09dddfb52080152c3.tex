Fix an identifying cell \(\psi_j\), a compatible specialization \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) in that cell, and \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). By the forward direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\), there is a response vector \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) such that
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\tag{1}
\]
for every \((a,y)\). Strict positivity, inherited from \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_+^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\), guarantees that every denominator in a legal recorded fixing or selection trace is nonzero. The label semantics guarantees that a licensed deletion refers to the mechanism omission encoded by the same label. These facts make each candidate rational trace well defined.

For fixed \((a,y)\), the premise that the cell is identifying means that
\[
\mathsf V_{a,y}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},z)
\Longleftrightarrow
\exists\theta\,[\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge z=q_{a,y}(\theta)]
\tag{2}
\]
has exactly one solution \(z\). The corrected \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) stores a rational trace expression only after checking its equality to (2) on the whole cell; otherwise it stores the unique-value graph (2) itself. Hence, in either representation,
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=z.
\tag{3}
\]
Substitution of \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) from (1) into (2) shows that \(z=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\). Combining this with (3) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\tag{4}
\]
for every model in the observed-law fiber. History preservation and \(\text{lem:colored-adjacent-swap}\) ensure that the compiler never justifies (3) by silently commuting a selection with a fixing: complete tuples are merged only after literal replay and equality checking. Thus (4) applies to every returned representation, not only to a response-function oracle.

Using \(\text{def:causal-query}\), finiteness of \(\mathcal X_Y\), and (4) at the two treatment endpoints,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau_{s_Y}
&=\sum_y s_Y(y)\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\}\\
&=\sum_y s_Y(y)\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]
The argument treats \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) as a mathematical real vector; only later numerical cell selection requires \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).
