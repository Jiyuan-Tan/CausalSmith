For the size quantities \((n,\ell,c,h,g,K_{\sharp},p,s_{\mathrm{base}},s_{\mathrm{ann}})\), define
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j,
\]
\[
C_{\mathrm{base}}=\operatorname{CAD}(p,s_{\mathrm{base}}),
\qquad
N_{\mathrm{trace}}=|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|B_{\mathrm{state}},
\qquad
N_{\mathrm{val}}=N_{\mathrm{trace}}C_{\mathrm{base}},
\]
and
\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}
+N_{\mathrm{val}}\operatorname{CAD}(p,s_{\mathrm{ann}}).
\]
Here \(2^n c\) bounds the initial observed-margin and partial-context traces. The operation alphabet respectively bounds ancestral and district subset choices, vertex-license choices, strict context extensions, and one harmless terminal marker, while the recursive word length \(2n\) counts every provenance-bearing marginalization, district restriction, fixing, and selection in its actual order. The trace-independent base input consists of the feasibility formula and all coordinatewise constancy tests and has encoding length at most \(s_{\mathrm{base}}\). Thus \(C_{\mathrm{base}}\) bounds both the base-CAD work and its number of canonical cells. After trace enumeration, there are at most \(N_{\mathrm{trace}}\) coordinatewise rational candidates and hence at most \(N_{\mathrm{val}}\) cell-candidate validations. Each validation has encoding length at most \(s_{\mathrm{ann}}\), includes the relevant canonical cell, denominator-support sentence, and equality sentence, and may be decided by a separate real-closed-field computation without refining the base partition. Exact point specialization is a separate finite sign computation under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).
