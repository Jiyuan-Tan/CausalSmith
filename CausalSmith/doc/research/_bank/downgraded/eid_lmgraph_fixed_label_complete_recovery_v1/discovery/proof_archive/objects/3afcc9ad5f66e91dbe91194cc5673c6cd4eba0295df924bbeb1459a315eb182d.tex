For a finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\) and supplied \(A,Y\), \(\operatorname{TCF}\) first constructs the trace-independent finite semialgebraic partition
\[
\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}
\quad\text{of}\quad
\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}
\]
and then annotates its fixed cells by validated colored-trace expressions. For every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), its unique cell carries \(f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\); in that case
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\]
If the coordinate is not constant, the cell carries a satisfiable \(\mathsf W_j\), and without assuming \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) there exist strictly positive individual-edge-factorized \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) with the same observed law and different \(Q_a(y)\). Since \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\), the same pair proves nonidentification in the published ambient class. Under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), the pair can be extracted by Thom encodings and the partial diagnostic \(\operatorname{Compile}_{\mathrm{col}}\) terminates with either a checked record or an exhaustion tag. If every coordinate is constant for both \(a_0\) and \(a_1\), then the observed-law functional identifies the causal contrast:
\[
\tau_{s_Y}
=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\]
This is pointwise semialgebraic completeness over the finite full-positive fiber, with an upward ambient nonidentification transfer on negative cells; it makes no graphwise colored-hedge completeness claim.
