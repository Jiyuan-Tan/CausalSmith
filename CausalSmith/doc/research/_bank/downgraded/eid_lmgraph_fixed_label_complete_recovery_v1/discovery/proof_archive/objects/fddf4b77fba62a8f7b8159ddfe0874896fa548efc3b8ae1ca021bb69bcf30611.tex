For a finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\) and supplied \(A,Y\), the graphical recursion has the implementation-independent, graphwise dichotomy that it derives a trace functional or there exists at least one colored hedge \(H\in\operatorname{CH}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\) from a failed target-relevant branch. Independently, the symbolic wrapper \(\operatorname{TCF}\) constructs a finite semialgebraic partition \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) of
\[
\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}.
\]
For every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), its unique partition cell carries a unique value \(f_a(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\); on such a cell the value equals \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) for every model in the fiber. Otherwise the cell carries \(\mathsf W_j\), and under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) every extracted sample decodes to the strictly positive, individual-edge-factorized, same-observed, different-query pair of \(\text{thm:qe-failure-compiler}\). This pair need not realize the graphwise hedge. If the cell identifies every \((a_i,y)\), where \(i\in\{0,1\}\), then
\[
\tau_{s_Y}=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)\{f_{a_1}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\]
Thus the graphwise existential obstruction and the pointwise semialgebraic fiber decision are distinct conclusions: graphwise recursion failure is not equated with pointwise fiber nonconstancy, and a negative-cell sample is not asserted to yield or realize a colored hedge.
