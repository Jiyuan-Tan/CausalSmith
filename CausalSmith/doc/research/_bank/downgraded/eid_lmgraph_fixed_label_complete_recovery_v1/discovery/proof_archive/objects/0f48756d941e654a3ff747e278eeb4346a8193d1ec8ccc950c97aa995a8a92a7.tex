Assume \(\mathcal R=\varnothing\), \(\mathcal V^{\dagger}=\varnothing\), and \(\mathcal L=\varnothing\). Then a partial context has only the empty assignment, no context-selection transition exists, and every label-license list is empty. Erasing these vacuous fields sends a trace state to \((\mathcal H,F,q,\pi)\). By \(\text{def:colored-fixing}\), a fixing transition becomes
\[
q\longmapsto
\frac{q}{q(v\mid\operatorname{mb}_{\mathcal H}(v))},
\tag{1}
\]
which is the ordinary nested fixing update. Ancestral marginalization and district restriction are unchanged.

Induct on the recursive operation word of ordinary \(\operatorname{ID}\). The base word uses the observed kernel \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), which is an initial TCF trace. At an ancestral or district step, TCF has the same branch by \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\). At a fixing step, (1) gives the identical next CADMG, kernel, and rational provenance. Thus every prefix of the ordinary word has an equal TCF trace, and if ordinary \(\operatorname{ID}\) returns \(f_a^{\mathrm{ID}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\), TCF appends the same rational candidate.

By \(\text{lem:ordinary-id-soundness}\), for every compatible model,
\[
f_a^{\mathrm{ID}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{2}
\]
Hence the coordinate is constant on the observed-law fiber. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the unique TCF cell is identifying. The storage rule in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) verifies the candidate against \(\mathsf V_{a,y}\), so (2) implies that the cell carries exactly \(f_a^{\mathrm{ID}}\). The argument establishes the stated one-way reduction only; the pointwise CAD may still refine the ordinary graphwise dichotomy.
