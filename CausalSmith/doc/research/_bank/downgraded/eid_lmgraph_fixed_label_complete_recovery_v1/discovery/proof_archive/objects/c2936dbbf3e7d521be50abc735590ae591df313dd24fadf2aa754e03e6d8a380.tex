Let \(r(\kappa)\) be the number of random vertices of the current CADMG, and let \(d(c)\) be the number of missingness indicators assigned by the current partial context. Define the nonnegative integer
\[
\mu(\kappa)=r(\kappa)+|\mathcal R|-d(c).
\tag{1}
\]
Initially \(r(\kappa)\leq n\) and \(|\mathcal R|-d(c)\leq n\), so \(\mu(\kappa)\leq2n\). By \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), strict ancestral marginalization, passage to a proper district child, and licensed fixing each reduce \(r(\kappa)\) by at least one. A delayed selection strictly extends \(c\), so it increases \(d(c)\) by at least one. No permitted recursive transition increases either summand in (1). Consequently every recursive operation word has length at most
\[
t=2n.
\tag{2}
\]

There are at most \(2^n\) observed margins and
\[
c=\prod_{R_Z\in\mathcal R}(|\mathcal X_{R_Z}|+1)
\tag{3}
\]
partial indicator contexts, hence at most \(2^n c\) initial traces. At a state, the two strict subset operations have at most \(2^n\) choices each, licensed fixing has at most \(n(\ell+1)\) vertex-license choices, and strict context extension has at most \(c\) choices. With one terminal marker,
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1.
\tag{4}
\]
For a fixed initial trace and operation word, every update of \(\mathcal H,F,c,q,\pi,\Lambda\) is prescribed by \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\). Therefore (2)--(4) give
\[
N_{\mathrm{state}}
\leq2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j
=B_{\mathrm{state}}.
\tag{5}
\]
This count preserves every fixing-selection order and the full provenance syntax. The optional optimization in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) merges adjacent-fixing branches only after canonical replay and literal equality. The kernel equality in \(\text{lem:colored-adjacent-swap}\) does not itself identify unequal raw trace suffixes; any verified merge can only reduce (5).

Each doubled constancy formula contains at most \(2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}\) response coordinates, and \(\mathbf p\) contributes \(|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\) free coordinates. \[
p=2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|.
\tag{6}
\]
Its complete encoding length is at most \(s\) by \(\text{def:termination-budget}\). At each projection level the fixed signed-subresultant implementation forms finitely many coefficients, discriminants, and resultants from a finite polynomial list. Every nonzero remainder in a signed-subresultant sequence has smaller degree than its predecessor, so each such sequence terminates. Lifting performs finitely many root-isolation and sign-evaluation operations over the finitely many cells already constructed. Hence the base call terminates. By \(\text{def:termination-budget}\) and the definition of \(\operatorname{CAD}\), its number of projection, isolation, sign-evaluation steps and output cells is at most
\[
C_{\mathrm{base}}=\operatorname{CAD}(p,s).
\tag{7}
\]
In particular, the canonical partition has at most \(C_{\mathrm{base}}\) cells; this count is independent of the number of retained traces.

N_{\mathrm{check}}

N_{\mathrm{total}}
\leq B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}

\tag{9}
