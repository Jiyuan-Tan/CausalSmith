We first establish the de Aguas reduction on the common subclass. Fix \(\xi\in\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\), a compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), and \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). By \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\), \(\mathcal M\) is a finite, every-full-cell-positive, regular-maximal member of the published ambient class. Hence the published recovery statement \(\text{lem:da4-recovery}\), restricted downward to this model, gives
\[
f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{1}
\]
This holds for every model in the finite fiber, so the coordinate is constant there. The certificate conditions in \(\text{def:da4-certificate-functional}\) make the finite sequence of context restrictions, conditional kernel divisions, and marginalizations generating its displayed weighted formula legal. Strict full-cell positivity makes every conditional denominator on the selected support nonzero. Because \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) enumerates every such legal operation word, it discovers \(f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}\) as a rational candidate. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the canonical cell is identifying, and by the validation sentences in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), equation (1) makes that candidate a valid annotation of the whole cell. This proves the class-relative de Aguas containment.

Now take an observed-root-control DAG covered by the Mokhtarian--Jamshidi--Etesami--Kiyavash theorem and embed it as the corresponding lm-graph with finite alphabets and strict full-cell positivity. The root-control result \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_cP_{\mathrm{obs}}(C_{\mathrm{ctx}}=c)
f_{a,c}^{\mathrm{ID}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\\
&=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
for every model in the embedded finite fiber. Rootness ensures that intervention on \(A\) does not change the context weights. Positivity supplies the conditionals in each context. The exhaustive context-restriction and ordinary fixing branches of \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) contain the finite context-specific operation words in (2), and its annotation check validates their aggregate on the canonical cell. Constancy and \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) then prove that this cell is identifying and annotated by \(f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}\). This is only an embedding of the published observed-root-control DAG theorem into the common finite lm intersection.

It remains to prove strictness of the class-relative applicability claim. Fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\). By \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\), \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) is an edge. Removing directed arrows out of \(A\) in every graph \(\mathcal H_r[\underline A]\) does not remove this bidirected edge. Its length-one path has no internal vertex to block, so the d-separation in de Aguas condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) fails for every proposed certificate. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G(H,m),A,Y)=\varnothing.
\tag{3}
\]
The same definition supplies \(C\to R_W\). Hence the relevant context variable is not parentless, and the observed-root-control hypothesis fails. Thus neither published applicability condition covers this family.

Nevertheless, for every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G(H,m))\), \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\) proves
\[

\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
Strict full-cell positivity makes the probability of every conditioning event in (4) positive. The Figure 3(b) submodule and \(\text{prop:figure3b-fix-select}\) first retain the pre-selection intervention trace. The calculus then selects \(R_W=0\), applies the front-door adjustment through \(B_1\) conditional on \(H\), and averages with the unselected marginal \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(h)\). Therefore the exhaustive trace enumeration discovers the right-hand side of (4). Since (4) holds throughout the finite fiber, the semialgebraic dichotomy makes its canonical cell identifying, and the annotation test verifies the displayed rational expression on that whole cell. Equations (3)--(4) prove strict class-relative coverage beyond both applicability conditions. For \(H=\{C\}\) and \(m=1\), \(\text{prop:eipfd-star-worked}\) identifies (4) with \(f_a^\star\). The proof nowhere extends (4) to categorical or continuous ambient models outside \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}\).
