Let
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}
=\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\), the trace-independent base construction and the subsequent finite annotation phase terminate.  By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), their base formulas form a finite pairwise-disjoint semialgebraic partition of (1).  The construction in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) fixes these base cells before trace enumeration and permits a colored trace only to annotate a cell after the displayed whole-cell implication has been verified.  Thus the trace phase neither splits nor merges the partition.

Let \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) be compatible with the finite, strictly full-positive class.  The fiber is nonempty by compatibility.  For any \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), the forward direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) supplies \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) with
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{2}
\]
Hence the observed-cell vector belongs to (1) and lies in a unique partition cell \(\psi_j\).

Fix \((a,y)\).  Unfolding \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
&\neg\exists\theta^0\,\exists\theta^1
\bigl[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\\
&\hspace{35mm}\wedge q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)
\bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
Thus (3) holds exactly when \(q_{a,y}\) is constant on the polynomial fiber.  The two directions of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) map that fiber onto \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) and preserve the coordinate according to
\[
q_{a,y}(\theta)=Q_a^{\mathcal M_\theta}(y),
\qquad
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (3)--(4) prove
\[
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal M\longmapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{5}
\]

Suppose first that (5) holds.  The value relation \(\mathsf V_{a,y}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},z)\) is nonempty by (2) and single-valued by (3).  If no trace annotation passes, the cell stores this unique-value graph.  If a rational expression \(N/D\) passes, its verified whole-cell implication gives \(D(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\neq0\) and \(D(P_{\mathrm{obs}})z=N(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) at the unique value.  In either case the cell carries a well-defined \(f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\).  Applying \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives the explicit identifying equality
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad
\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{6}
\]
Conversely, a cell can carry such an identifying functional only if the right side of (6) is the same for all models in the fiber.  This proves the stated if-and-only-if characterization.

Suppose instead that (5) fails.  Then the unique cell carries the unnegated doubled relation \(\mathsf W_j\) by \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\).  Its satisfiability is a statement over \(\mathbb R\) and does not require an effective encoding of its solutions.  Applying \(\text{prop:ambient-nonrecovery-transfer}\) gives strictly positive, individual-edge-factorized models
\[
\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1
\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G),
\tag{7}
\]
such that
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)
=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)
=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{8}
\]
Equations (7)--(8) are the exact-free obstruction in both the finite/full-positive fiber and, by inclusion, the published ambient class.

If \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) is supplied, \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) selects the unique cell and represents a satisfying pair by Thom encodings over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\).  Choose any finite retained operation history \(\mathcal T\) accompanying the terminal package; its finiteness follows from \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\).  Then \(\text{thm:qe-failure-compiler}\) decodes a pair satisfying (7)--(8) and terminates with either a checked divergence record or the exhaustion tag.  That theorem asserts no colored hedge, and none is used here.

Finally, assume every outcome coordinate is constant for both \(a_0\) and \(a_1\).  The outcome alphabet is finite by \(\text{ass:finite-alphabets}\), so coordinatewise substitution from (6) into \(\text{def:causal-query}\) is legitimate and gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau_{s_Y}
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\}\\
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
This proves the finite/full-positive pointwise completeness claim, its explicit observed-law identifying map, and its same-observed negative certificate, without enlarging the positive identification domain or asserting graphwise colored-hedge completeness.
