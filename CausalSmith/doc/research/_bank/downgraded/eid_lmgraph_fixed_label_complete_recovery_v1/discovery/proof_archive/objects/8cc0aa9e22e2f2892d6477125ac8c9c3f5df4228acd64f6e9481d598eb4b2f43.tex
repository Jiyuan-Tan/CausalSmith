Fix a compatible observed law \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and a coordinate \((a,y)\). If the TCF cell containing \((P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\) is negative for that coordinate, then there exist \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\) such that
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\]
Consequently \(Q_a(y)\) is not identified over the larger published ambient set \(\{\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G):P_{\mathcal M}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}\}\). This existential conclusion does not require \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\); exactness is required only to algorithmically extract a represented witness pair.
