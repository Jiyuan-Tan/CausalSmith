Put
\[
d=\left|\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\right|+
|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|.
\]
We first prove the finite-dimensional support fact needed below. Let \(z:\Omega\to[0,1]^d\) be measurable on a probability space and let \(m=\int z\,d\mu\). If \(S=z(\Omega)\), then \(m\in\operatorname{conv}(S)\). Indeed, let \(K=\overline{\operatorname{conv}(S)}\). If \(m\) is in the relative interior of \(K\), then \(m\in\operatorname{ri}(K)=\operatorname{ri}(\operatorname{conv}(S))\subseteq\operatorname{conv}(S)\). If \(m\) is on the relative boundary, a supporting affine functional \(L\) satisfies \(L(x)\geq L(m)\) for \(x\in K\). Since
\[
0=L(m)-L(m)=\int\{L(z(\omega))-L(m)\}\,d\mu(\omega)
\]
and the integrand is nonnegative, \(L(z)=L(m)\) almost surely. Restricting to this proper affine face lowers the affine dimension. Induction on that dimension proves \(m\in\operatorname{conv}(S)\). Finally, if a representation of \(m\) uses more than \(d+1\) points of \(S\), their augmented vectors \((1,z_i)\in\mathbb R^{d+1}\) are linearly dependent. Moving the weights along a nonzero dependence until one weight becomes zero preserves both their sum and their barycenter. Repetition leaves at most \(d+1\) points. Thus there are \(u_1,\ldots,u_k\in\Omega\) and \(\alpha_i\geq0\), with \(k\leq d+1\), \(\sum_i\alpha_i=1\), and
\[
\int z\,d\mu=\sum_{i=1}^k\alpha_i z(u_i).
\]

Now enumerate the finite factor index set \(\mathcal J\). For a current factor \(U_h\), hold its value equal to \(u\), integrate the deterministic acyclic structural evaluation over all other independent factors, and form the vector
\[
z_h(u)=\left(
P(V=v,R=r\mid U_h=u)_{v,r},
P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),U_h=u)_{a,y}
\right)\in[0,1]^d.
\]
Independence of the factors gives the current full-cell and intervention vector as \(\int z_h,dP_{U_h}\). The support fact replaces \(P_{U_h}\) by a distribution on at most \(d+1\) actual values of \(U_h\) without changing any coordinate of this vector. This replacement preserves independence because only one marginal factor law is changed. Repeating over the finite set \(\mathcal J\) therefore preserves, at every step, all full-cell probabilities and every queried interventional probability.

It remains to check the declared support budget. Let
\[
S_0=\sum_{Z\in\mathcal V\cup\mathcal R}|\mathcal X_Z|,
\qquad
L_0=\prod_{Z\in\mathcal V\cup\mathcal R}|\mathcal X_Z|.
\]
Every multiplier in the definition of \(N_{\mathcal G}\) is at least one, so \(N_{\mathcal G}\geq S_0\). Also \(L_0\leq2^{S_0}\), and \(|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|\leq L_0\). The distinct values \(a_0,a_1\) imply \(N_{\mathcal G}\geq2\). Hence
\[
d+1\leq2L_0+1\leq2^{S_0+2}
\leq2^{N_{\mathcal G}+2}
\leq2^{2^{N_{\mathcal G}}}=K_{\mathcal G}.
\]
Pad each reduced factor with zero-probability states up to \(K_{\mathcal G}\). Restrict the original structural equations to the selected factor values and extend them arbitrarily on padded states. Because the original equations obey every label deletion, the restricted and extended tables can be chosen in \(\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\). Acyclic deterministic evaluation is unchanged on all positive-probability factor states, which proves the two displayed equalities and the lemma.
