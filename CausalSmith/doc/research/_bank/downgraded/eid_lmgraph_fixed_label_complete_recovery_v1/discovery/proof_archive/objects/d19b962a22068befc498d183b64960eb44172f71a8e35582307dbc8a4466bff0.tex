Fix a finite positive regular-maximal graph \(\mathcal G\), a compatible observed law \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), and a coordinate \((a,y)\in\mathcal X_A\times\mathcal X_Y\). Compatibility means that the fiber \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) of \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\) is nonempty. Let \(\psi_j\) be the unique cell of \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) containing the observed-cell vector \((P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\).

First suppose that \(\xi\in\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\). By \(\text{lem:da4-recovery}\), for each \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\),
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{1}
\]
The right-hand side of (1) depends only on \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\). Hence, for any \(\mathcal M,\widetilde{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\),
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=Q_a^{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(y).
\tag{2}
\]
Thus the query coordinate is constant on the fiber. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), \(\psi_j\) is identifying for \((a,y)\). By \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\), its functional obeys
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{3}
\]
Combining (1) and (3) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=f^{\mathrm{DA4}}_{a,\xi}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{4}
\]
This proves containment of the de Aguas criterion on the stated finite/full-positive subclass. Notice that the argument uses the base coordinate-constancy cell, so it does not require a terminal fixing trace to be duplicated in the quantifier-elimination partition. Under \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), a trace-derived rational expression is merely an annotation and is stored only when its equality to the unique-value relation holds throughout the cell.

Second suppose that \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\) holds. The cited observed-root recovery result \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\), specialized to the present finite/full-positive models, gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{5}
\]
Repeating the constancy argument in (2), \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) makes \(\psi_j\) identifying, and \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) then yields
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{6}
\]
By \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\), all models quantified over here lie in the finite strictly positive regular-maximal subclass. Therefore (5)--(6) prove only an embedding of that specialization of the published observed-root theorem; they make no assertion about categorical or continuous models outside the TCF subclass.

It remains to prove that the de Aguas containment is strict. Fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\). By \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\), the bidirected edge \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) is present. In each graph \(\mathcal H_r[\underline A]\) occurring in a proposed certificate from \(\text{def:da4-certificate-functional}\), deletion of directed arrows out of \(A\) does not delete this bidirected edge. The one-edge path \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) has no nonendpoint vertex, and hence no admissible conditioning set can block it. Consequently the d-separation required by Proposition 4 condition \(\mathrm{(v)}\) fails for every proposed certificate, so
\[
\operatorname{DA4}(\mathcal G(H,m),A,Y)=\varnothing.
\tag{7}
\]

Now fix any compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) for this graph and any \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G(H,m),P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). By \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_hP_{\mathcal M}(h)
  \sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)\\
&\qquad\times\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
 P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
 P_{\mathcal M}(a'\mid h,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
Strict positivity from \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\) makes every conditioning event in (8) have positive probability. The last sentence of \(\text{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}\), together with \(P_{\mathcal M}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), therefore permits replacement of every probability in (8) by its observed-law counterpart. Thus
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_hP_{\mathrm{obs}}(h)
  \sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathrm{obs}}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)\\
&\qquad\times\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
 P_{\mathrm{obs}}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
 P_{\mathrm{obs}}(a'\mid h,R_W=0)
=:F_{a,H}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
The expression \(F_{a,H}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) is independent of \(\mathcal M\). Hence (9) proves constancy on the entire fiber. Applying \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) and then \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=F_{a,H}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\tag{10}
\]
Equations (7) and (10) establish strictness relative to the de Aguas criterion on the finite/full-positive subclass. The Figure 3(b) result \(\text{prop:figure3b-fix-select}\) supplies the pre-selection trace used by the expression-discovery route: it derives the intervention kernel before selecting \(R_W=0\); the front-door calculation in (8) then uses \(B_1\) and averages over the unselected marginal of \(H\). The base-cell proof of (10), however, depends on fiber constancy rather than on the existence of that annotation.

Finally, \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\) also supplies \(C\to R_W\). Since \(R_W\) is a label-context variable, it is not parentless, so the observed-root premise \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G(H,m),A,Y)\) fails. This establishes only that the observed-root theorem is inapplicable to this family and is not used as a strictness witness against that theorem. When \(H=\{C\}\) and \(m=1\), \(\text{prop:eipfd-star-worked}\) identifies (9) with \(f_a^\star(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\), completing the worked specialization.
