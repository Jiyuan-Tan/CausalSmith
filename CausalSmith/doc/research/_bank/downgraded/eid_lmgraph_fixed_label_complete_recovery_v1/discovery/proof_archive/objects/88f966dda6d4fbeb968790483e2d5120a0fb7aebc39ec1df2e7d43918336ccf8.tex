Because \(\omega\) satisfies \(\mathsf W_j\), the definitions of the negative-cell relation in \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) and \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\) give

Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{3}
\]

It remains to prove termination of the diagnostic part. The graph and \(\mathcal T\) are finite by hypothesis, so there are finitely many prefixes and finitely many vertex, district, ancestor, and edge-color candidates at each prefix. Purely graphical predicates are decidable by finite graph inspection. The stored traces are rational expressions in observed cells and response coordinates. Every denominator tested by \(\text{def:certificate-compiler}\) is supported on that trace; it is nonzero because (1) includes strict positivity of every full-data cell. After multiplying by the finite product of these nonzero denominators, each trace equality or nonzero test becomes a polynomial sign test.

By the corrected \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\), the coordinates of \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) have Thom encodings over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p\). Signed-subresultant evaluation at a Thom encoding reduces every required sign to finitely many signs of integer polynomials at \(\mathbf p\), which \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) decides. Hence every test terminates. The deterministic scan therefore either finds a first qualifying record or exhausts \(\mathcal T\), exactly producing the two tags prescribed by \(\text{def:certificate-compiler}\). In both branches its model components are the models in (2), so (2)--(3) prove every affirmative model property. The construction and the theorem assert no colored-hedge membership or model-to-forest realization.
