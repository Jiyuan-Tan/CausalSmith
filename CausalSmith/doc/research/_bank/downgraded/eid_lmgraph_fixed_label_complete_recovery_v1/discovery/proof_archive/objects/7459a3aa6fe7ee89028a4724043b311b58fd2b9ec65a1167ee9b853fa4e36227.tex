Fix \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{3b}},P_{\mathrm{3b}})\), a treatment value \(a\), and an outcome value \(y\). In the semi-Markovian structural representation, write \(U_{WY}\) for the factor shared by \(W\) and \(Y\), \(U_{R_ZY}\) for the factor shared by \(R_Z\) and \(Y\), and \(U_T\) for the private factor of vertex \(T\). By \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), these factors are mutually independent except for the explicitly shared endpoints.

After replacing the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\), acyclicity and the edge list in \(\text{def:figure3b-witness}\) give structural representations
\[
Y(a)=g_Y(a,U_Y,U_{WY},U_{R_ZY}),
\qquad
R_W=g_{R_W}(U_{R_W}).
\tag{1}
\]
There is no common factor in the two argument lists. Mutual independence therefore implies
\[
Y(a)\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}R_W.
\tag{2}
\]
Strict positivity gives \(P(R_W=0)>0\), so (2) yields
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y)
=P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid R_W=0).
\tag{3}
\]

In context \(R_W=0\), \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\) removes the \(W\) argument from the equation for \(A\). Thus
\[
A=g_A(0,Z,U_A)\quad\text{when }R_W=0.
\tag{4}
\]
The exogenous variables determining \((Z,U_A)\) are disjoint from and independent of \((U_Y,U_{WY},U_{R_ZY})\). The apparent path \(A\leftarrow Z\to W\leftrightarrow Y\) is blocked at the collider \(W\), and the direct back-door path \(A\leftarrow W\leftrightarrow Y\) is absent in this label context. Equations (1) and (4) therefore give
\[
Y(a)\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}A\mid R_W=0.
\tag{5}
\]
All treatment cells used below have positive probability by strict full-data positivity. From (5), followed by structural consistency of the recursive SCM (on the event \(A=a\), replacing the equation for \(A\) by the same value does not change \(Y\)),
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid R_W=0)
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid A=a,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (3) and (6), and using \(\text{def:causal-query}\), gives
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))
=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W=0)
=P_{\mathrm{3b}}(Y=y\mid A=a,R_W=0).
\tag{7}
\]
The last equality uses \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\): \(A,Y,R_W\) are observed components of the Figure 3(b) record, so this conditional is determined by the observed proxy law.

The graphical branch realizing (7) first applies the licensed fixing updates to the unselected kernel, thereby recording the kernel \(P(Y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W)\) and its provenance, and only then applies \(\Sigma_{R_W=0}\). Both divisions are legal by positivity. The history-preserving \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) enumerates this operation word and retains it literally. Since (7) holds for every model in the fiber, \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) makes the coordinate constant on the response fiber, and the cell containing \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}\) stores the verified functional on the right of (7).

For the last assertion, consider instead the closure whose initial operation is \(\Sigma_{R_W=0}\). Induct on the number of later operations. At length zero, both \(q\) and \(\pi\) are restrictions of the selected law and \(\Lambda\) contains no pre-selection entry. An ancestral marginalization or district restriction preserves this property; a fixing divides two expressions derived from the selected kernel and appends only its current selected provenance; a later selection cannot reconstruct a removed context. Hence every state in this closure has provenance descending from the already selected law, and none contains the pre-selection trace used between (3) and (6). This proves the claimed operation-order obstruction without asserting that fixing and selection commute.
