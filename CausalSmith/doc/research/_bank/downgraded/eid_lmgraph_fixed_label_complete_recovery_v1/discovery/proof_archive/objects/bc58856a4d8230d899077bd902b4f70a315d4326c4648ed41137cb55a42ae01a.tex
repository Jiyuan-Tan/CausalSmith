Assume \(\operatorname{RC}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\). Let \(C_{\mathrm{ctx}}\) denote the finite vector of observed root context variables. Because it has no parents, intervention on \(A\) does not change its distribution. The context-recovery result \(\text{lem:root-control-context-recovery}\) gives, for every compatible model,
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
&=\sum_cP_{\mathrm{obs}}(C_{\mathrm{ctx}}=c)
f_{a,c}^{\mathrm{ID}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\\
&=f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y).
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
The assumed identification of every context graph supplies every \(f_{a,c}^{\mathrm{ID}}\), and positivity supplies the context conditionals on their support.

Since context variables are observed roots, restricting to \(C_{\mathrm{ctx}}=c\) does not discard a parent mechanism needed to form an earlier intervention kernel. The TCF recursion may therefore take the context restriction first, reproduce each ordinary-ID operation word in that context, and average the resulting kernels with the observed root probabilities. This branch supplies the rational candidate on the second line of (1), so delayed restriction is unnecessary in this submodel.

Equation (1) holds for every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\); therefore the coordinate is constant on that fiber. By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the unique TCF cell is identifying. The verified-storage rule of \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), together with (1), shows that its value is exactly \(f_a^{\mathrm{RC}}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) for every \((a,y)\). This proves the reduction and makes no converse claim about exceptional observed laws.
