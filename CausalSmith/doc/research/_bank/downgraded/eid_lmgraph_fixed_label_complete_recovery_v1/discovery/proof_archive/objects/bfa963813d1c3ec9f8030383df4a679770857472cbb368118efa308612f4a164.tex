On a negative cell, the definition of \(\mathsf W_j\) in \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) and \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\) gives the satisfiable formula
\[
\exists\theta^0,\theta^1\,
\bigl[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)
\bigr].
\tag{1}
\]
Choose a satisfying pair in the ordinary semantic sense; this choice does not require an effective representation of the coordinates. The converse direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\), applied twice, decodes models \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) satisfying
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta^0)
\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)=Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{2}
\]
By \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\), each belongs to \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\) and both induce \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\). Unfolding \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\) gives
\[
\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)
\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G),
\tag{3}
\]
so both witnesses lie in the displayed ambient observed-law set. If \(Q_a(y)\) were identified over that set, it would be constant there and hence equal at \(\mathcal M_0\) and \(\mathcal M_1\), contradicting (2). This proves ambient nonidentification. The existence argument uses only the semantics of the satisfiable polynomial formula; \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) enters only through \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) when an algorithmic representation of a satisfying pair is requested.
