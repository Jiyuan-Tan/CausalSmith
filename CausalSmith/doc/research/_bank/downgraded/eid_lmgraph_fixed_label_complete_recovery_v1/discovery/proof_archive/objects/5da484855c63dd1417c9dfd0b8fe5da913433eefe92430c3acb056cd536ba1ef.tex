Given \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), decide the finitely many integer-polynomial signs in \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) using rational arithmetic, algebraic-number arithmetic with the supplied square-free polynomials and isolating intervals, or the supplied exact-real sign oracle. Choose the unique cell containing \(p_o=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o)\). On an identifying cell, represent its unique value \(z\), and on a negative cell choose \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) satisfying \(\mathsf W_j\), by Thom encodings over the real closure of the ordered field generated by the specialized coordinates \(\mathbf p\). These response coordinates are algebraic over that ordered input field; they are real algebraic over \(\mathbb Q\) when the observed cells are rational or real algebraic, but no such claim is made for transcendental exact-real cells. No operation on an arbitrary unencoded population real is defined.
