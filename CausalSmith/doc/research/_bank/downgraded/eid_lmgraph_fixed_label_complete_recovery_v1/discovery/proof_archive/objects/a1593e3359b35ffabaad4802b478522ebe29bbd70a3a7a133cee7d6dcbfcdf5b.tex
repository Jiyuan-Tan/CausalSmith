Because \(\omega\) satisfies \(\mathsf W_j\), \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) and \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\) give

Applying \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\) to (1) yields

That lemma also proves that each decoded latent law is a product over the individual bidirected-edge and private factors and that every full-data cell is strictly positive.  Substitution of (2) into (1) gives

Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{3}
\]

It remains to verify the algorithmic assertions.  The history \(\mathcal T\) and graph \(\mathcal G\) are finite, so there are finitely many prefixes and finitely many choices of \((\kappa_*,D,D')\) examined in the fixed order of \(\text{def:certificate-compiler}\).  Every ancestor, district, root, inclusion, and intervention-incidence conjunct of \(\Delta\) is decidable by inspection of a finite graph.  A stored trace is a rational expression in observed cells and response coordinates.  Each denominator that the compiler clears is supported on its recorded pre-restriction trace.  By the strict full-cell inequalities in (1), every required marginal or selected cell is a finite sum of positive cells and is therefore nonzero.  Multiplying by the finite product of these nonzero denominators consequently turns each trace equality or inequality into an equivalent polynomial sign condition.

By \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\), the coordinates of \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) have Thom encodings over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \(\mathbf p=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\).  The signed-subresultant comparisons stipulated in \(\text{def:certificate-compiler}\) reduce each polynomial comparison at these encodings to finitely many signs at \(\mathbf p\), and \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) decides those signs.  Thus every candidate test terminates.  A finite deterministic enumeration whose individual tests terminate either returns the first triple satisfying every conjunct of \(\Delta\), or reaches its end, in which case no enumerated triple satisfies all conjuncts.  These are precisely the two advertised tags.  In either case the returned models are the pair in (2), so (2)--(3) establish the model claims independently of the tag.  The argument never infers colored-hedge membership, forest realization, or alignment with a graphwise recursion failure.
