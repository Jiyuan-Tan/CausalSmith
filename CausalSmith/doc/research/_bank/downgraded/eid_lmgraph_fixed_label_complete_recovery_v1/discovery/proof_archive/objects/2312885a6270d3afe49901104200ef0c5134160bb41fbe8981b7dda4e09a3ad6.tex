Let \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) have finite endogenous alphabets and the mutually independent individual-edge and private factors indexed by \(\mathcal J=\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)\sqcup\mathcal V\sqcup\mathcal R\). There is a label-compatible deterministic response representation in which every factor has support at most \(K_{\mathcal G}\), the factors remain mutually independent, and, simultaneously for all full cells and all \((a,y)\),
\[
P(V=v,R=r)=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),
\qquad
P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\]
