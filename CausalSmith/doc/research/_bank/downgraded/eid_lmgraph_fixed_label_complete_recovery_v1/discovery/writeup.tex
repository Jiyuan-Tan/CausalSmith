\section*{eid\_\allowbreak{}lmgraph\_\allowbreak{}fixed\_\allowbreak{}label\_\allowbreak{}complete\_\allowbreak{}recovery}

\noindent\textit{Specialization:} v1\par

\paragraph{TL;DR.} Finite labeled-missingness graphs encode context-specific mechanism deletions, but restricting to a missingness context too early can discard an identifying pre-restriction kernel. The procedure \(\operatorname{TCF}(\mathcal G,A,Y,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) first constructs finite semialgebraic coordinate and direct score-contrast partitions from an edge-factorized response-function presentation, and then annotates fixed cells only with trace expressions verified on the whole cell. A coordinate or the declared score-coded NATE is recovered exactly when it is constant on the finite positive observational fiber; otherwise the corresponding negative cell yields two strictly positive same-observed lm-SCMs with different query values. Exact input is needed only to represent such witnesses, after which a finite diagnostic returns either a checked first-divergence record or an exhaustion tag, not a colored hedge. Figure 3(b) supplies the fix-then-select witness. On the common finite positive class, TCF preserves the de Aguas Proposition 4 and Mokhtarian root-control functionals, strictly extends the former on the endogenous-indicator proxy front-door family, and treats the latter as an embedding whose root premise is inapplicable to that family.

\section{Project justification}

\paragraph{Gap.} De Aguas et al. give a sufficient weighted-adjustment criterion and the Figure 3(b) fix-before-select recovery, while Mokhtarian et al. give completeness when observed context variables are causal roots. Neither result supplies a single finite procedure that preserves pre-restriction proxy traces, tests both interventional coordinates and a score contrast directly, and returns positive same-observed countermodels whenever the relevant finite response fiber is nonconstant.

\paragraph{Niche.} For finite strictly positive regular-maximal lm-graphs, individual-edge semi-Markovian response functions turn the observational fiber and intervention queries into a finite polynomial system. Trace-aware colored fixing can then be used as an expression-discovery layer while semialgebraic constancy, rather than a graphwise failure converse, supplies the complete pointwise decision.

\paragraph{Fill.} The note gives a two-way response-fiber equivalence, separate finite semialgebraic partitions for every interventional coordinate and for the declared score contrast, whole-cell verification of trace expressions, exact-free positive countermodel existence on negative cells, an exact-input representation and finite diagnostic compiler, and an explicit terminating CAD budget. It also proves the Figure 3(b) operation-order witness, preserves both published comparator functionals on their common finite positive domain, and establishes strict extension of the de Aguas Proposition 4 criterion through the delayed-selection front-door functional on \(\mathcal G_\star\) and its EIPFD family.

\section{Related work}

De Aguas et al. (2025), Proposition 4 and Figure 3(b), are the first direct comparator: the proposition gives a weighted context-adjustment functional, and the figure exhibits fix-before-select information flow. Mokhtarian et al. (2022), Theorem 1, Corollary 2, and Algorithm 1, provide the second theorem-level comparator: their context-by-context algorithm is complete when context variables are observed causal roots. Theorem \(\text{thm:two-comparator-strict-extension}\) preserves both functionals on the common finite positive subclass, proves strict extension only of the de Aguas Proposition 4 criterion on \(\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\), and records that the root-control premise fails there without claiming strictness over the complete root-control domain. Tikka, Hyttinen, and Karvanen (2019) inform sound finite CSI search, and Bhattacharya et al. (2020) inform recursive removal in missing-data graphs, but neither carries the label-license provenance used here. Richardson et al. (2023) prove ordinary fixing-order invariance; the colored adjacent-swap result preserves the uncolored kernel while retaining order-sensitive provenance and never commutes fixing with selection. Shpitser and Pearl (2006) supply the ordinary ID and hedge background, but this note does not prove a colored-hedge converse. Duarte et al. (2023) motivate response-function polynomialization; the present response-fiber theorem adds the lm-specific individual-edge product structure and strict full-data positivity needed for the exact pointwise dichotomy.

\section{Assumptions}

\begin{assumption}[ass:finite-alphabets]\label{ass:finite-alphabets}
\(|\mathcal X_Z|<\infty\) for every \(Z\in\mathcal V\cup\mathcal R\cup\mathcal V^{\dagger}\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (finite discrete causal model, \cite{ShpitserPearl2006JointID}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:acyclic]\label{ass:acyclic}
The directed part of \(\mathcal G\) is acyclic.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (ADMG acyclicity, \cite{ShpitserPearl2006JointID}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:semimarkovian]\label{ass:semimarkovian}
\(\mathcal M\) has one exogenous factor shared by the two endpoints of each bidirected edge of \(\mathcal G\), one private exogenous factor for each substantive or missingness vertex, and all these factors are mutually independent.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (semi-Markovian SCM compatibility, \cite{ShpitserPearl2006JointID}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:proxy-consistency]\label{ass:proxy-consistency}
For every partially observed \(Z\in\mathcal V\), \(Z^{\dagger}=Z\) when \(R_Z=1\) and \(Z^{\dagger}=\star\) when \(R_Z=0\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (missingness-proxy consistency, \cite{pmlr-v286-aguas25a}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:label-semantics]\label{ass:label-semantics}
Every label \((X,Z,R_X=0)\) in \(\mathcal G\) encodes that the structural mechanism for \(Z\) omits \(X\) in context \(R_X=0\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (labeled-missingness mechanism semantics, \cite{pmlr-v286-aguas25a}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:regular]\label{ass:regular}
The label set of \(\mathcal G\) is regular.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (regular labeled DAG, \cite{MokhtarianJamshidiEtesamiKiyavash2022}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:maximal]\label{ass:maximal}
The label set of \(\mathcal G\) is maximal.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (maximal labeled DAG, \cite{MokhtarianJamshidiEtesamiKiyavash2022}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:strict-positivity]\label{ass:strict-positivity}
\(P_{\mathcal M}(v,r)>0\) for every \((v,r)\in\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (strict positivity for finite causal laws, \cite{TikkaHyttinenKarvanen2019}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{definition}[def:response-polynomial-fiber]\label{def:response-polynomial-fiber}
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Defined object.} \texttt{Edge-\allowbreak{}factorized finite response-\allowbreak{}function polynomial fiber}
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Construction.} Let
\[
L_{\mathcal G}=\left|\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\right|,
\qquad
K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)=L_{\mathcal G}+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1),
\qquad
\mathcal J=\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)\sqcup\mathcal V\sqcup\mathcal R.
\]
The integer \(K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\) is a universal factorwise upper bound, not a claimed sharp support size. For \(g\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\) introduce \(\gamma_g\), and for \(h\in\mathcal J\) and \(1\leq k\leq K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\) introduce \(\rho_{h,k}\). For a full cell \((v,r)\), define
\[
p_{v,r}(\theta)=
\sum_{g\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}}
\sum_{u\in\{1,\ldots,K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}^{\mathcal J}}
\mathbf 1\{g(u)=(v,r)\}\gamma_g\prod_{h\in\mathcal J}\rho_{h,u_h},
\qquad
p_o(\theta)=\sum_{(v,r):\operatorname{proxy}(v,r)=o}p_{v,r}(\theta).
\]
Define \(q_{a,y}(\theta)\) by the same product sum after replacing the selected equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\). With formal observed coordinates \(\mathbf p=(p_o)_o\), set
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p):={}&
\bigwedge_{g\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}}(\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0)
\ \wedge\ \sum_g\gamma_g=1\\
&\wedge\bigwedge_{h\in\mathcal J}
\left(\bigwedge_{k=1}^{K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)}\rho_{h,k}\geq0
\ \wedge\ \sum_{k=1}^{K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)}\rho_{h,k}=1\right)\\
&\wedge\bigwedge_{(v,r)}p_{v,r}(\theta)>0
\ \wedge\ \bigwedge_{o\in\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}}p_o(\theta)=p_o.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus every individual bidirected-edge or private factor has its own simplex and the joint latent law is the product over \(h\). The one-hot equations select one graph-wide tuple of label-compatible deterministic tables; they do not mix structural tables through an additional unrestricted district latent.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Inputs.} \texttt{G}; \texttt{Xspace}; \texttt{pCell}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[def:terminal-qe-test]\label{def:terminal-qe-test}
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Defined object.} \texttt{Symbolic terminal coordinate and target real-\allowbreak{}closed-\allowbreak{}field constancy tests}
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Construction.} For every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\) and \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), use disjoint copies \(\theta^0=(\gamma^0,\rho^0)\) and \(\theta^1=(\gamma^1,\rho^1)\) and eliminate precisely those response variables from
\[
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p):=
\neg\exists\theta^0\,\exists\theta^1
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;\mathbf p)
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;\mathbf p)
\wedge q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)
\right].
\]
The only free variables in this coordinate formula are the \(|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\) coordinates \(\mathbf p\). On its true region define
\[
\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z):=
\exists\theta
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)
\wedge z=q_{a,y}(\theta)
\right],
\]
and on its false region use the unnegated doubled conjunction \(\mathsf W_j(\mathbf p,a,y,\theta^0,\theta^1)\).

For a formal real score vector \(\mathbf s=(s_y:y\in\mathcal X_Y)\), define the target polynomial
\[
t_{\mathbf s}(\theta)
=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_y
\{q_{a_1,y}(\theta)-q_{a_0,y}(\theta)\}
\]
and the direct target-constancy formula
\[
\mathsf I_{\tau}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p,\mathbf s):=
\neg\exists\theta^0\,\exists\theta^1
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;\mathbf p)
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;\mathbf p)
\wedge t_{\mathbf s}(\theta^0)\neq t_{\mathbf s}(\theta^1)
\right].
\]
Its only free variables are \((\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\). On its true region define
\[
\mathsf V_{\tau}(\mathbf p,\mathbf s,z):=
\exists\theta
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)
\wedge z=t_{\mathbf s}(\theta)
\right],
\]
and on its false region define
\[
\mathsf W_{\tau}(\mathbf p,\mathbf s,\theta^0,\theta^1):=
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;\mathbf p)
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;\mathbf p)
\wedge t_{\mathbf s}(\theta^0)\neq t_{\mathbf s}(\theta^1).
\]
Quantifier elimination returns quantifier-free polynomial-sign formulas in the displayed free variables. No rationality or algebraicity is imposed on a supplied score map for the mathematical constancy assertion; represented point extraction is deferred to exact specialization.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Inputs.} \texttt{Compat}; \texttt{qRF}; \texttt{pCell}; \texttt{sY}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[def:symbolic-observed-partition]\label{def:symbolic-observed-partition}
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Defined object.} \texttt{Symbolic observed-\allowbreak{}law coordinate and direct-\allowbreak{}target decision partitions}
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Construction.} Conjoin the simplex formula \(p_o\geq0\), \(\sum_op_o=1\), and the feasible-projection formula \(\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\) with every quantifier-free output of \(\mathsf I_{a,y}\), refine their common sign-invariant CAD, and discard infeasible cells. The remaining pairwise-disjoint formulas \(\psi_1(\mathbf p),\ldots,\psi_J(\mathbf p)\) form the coordinate partition \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\). Each coordinate cell stores, for every \((a,y)\), either \(\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z)\) or \(\mathsf W_j(\mathbf p,a,y,\theta^0,\theta^1)\).

Independently introduce the formal score coordinates \(\mathbf s=(s_y:y\in\mathcal X_Y)\). Conjoin the lifted coordinate-cell formulas with the quantifier-free output of \(\mathsf I_{\tau}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\), refine by one common sign-invariant CAD in \((\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\), and discard points whose \(\mathbf p\)-coordinate is infeasible. The resulting pairwise-disjoint formulas \(\chi_1(\mathbf p,\mathbf s),\ldots,\chi_K(\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\) form
\[
\mathscr P^{\tau}_{\mathcal G,A,Y}
\quad\text{on}\quad
\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}
\times\mathbb R^{\mathcal X_Y}.
\]
This target partition refines the lift of the coordinate partition. Each target cell stores either the unique-value relation \(\mathsf V_{\tau}(\mathbf p,\mathbf s,z)\) or the witness relation \(\mathsf W_{\tau}(\mathbf p,\mathbf s,\theta^0,\theta^1)\). Thus coordinate negativity and target negativity are recorded separately.
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Inputs.} \texttt{Compat}; \texttt{ConstTest}; \texttt{ConstTestTau}; \texttt{pCell}; \texttt{sY}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[def:tcf-procedure]\label{def:tcf-procedure}
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Defined object.} \texttt{Trace-\allowbreak{}aware colored fixing coordinate and direct-\allowbreak{}target procedure}
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Construction.} On formal input \((\mathcal G,A,Y,\mathbf p)\), first construct the response-polynomial feasibility formula \(\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\), all coordinate-constancy formulas \(\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p)\), and the direct target-constancy formula \(\mathsf I_{\tau}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\) with \(\mathbf s\) retained as a formal real vector. Run one signed-subresultant CAD for the coordinate family in \(\mathbf p\), and a second signed-subresultant CAD for the target family in \((\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\) together with the lifted coordinate-cell polynomials. This produces the canonical coordinate cells \(\psi_j\) and target cells \(\chi_k\), with their respective unique-value or witness relations, before any fixing trace is enumerated.

After both base partitions have been fixed, enumerate expression-discovery traces. Start from every supported observed margin and partial-context trace kernel written as a rational expression in \(\mathbf p\). Recursively branch only by strict ancestral marginalization, restriction to a proper child of a nontrivial district decomposition, a licensed \(\Phi_v^{\lambda}\) update, or a delayed \(\Sigma_{c'}\) update that strictly extends the partial context. Retain the complete operation word and memoize only literal complete tuples \(\kappa=(\mathcal H,F,c,q,\pi,\Lambda)\), including the syntax of \(q\), \(\pi\), and every provenance entry in \(\Lambda\). An adjacent-swap optimization may merge branches only after replaying both license orders from their common pre-swap state in a fixed canonical order and verifying literal equality of the complete resulting tuples. Fixing and selection are never commuted.

For a candidate coordinate expression \(r(\mathbf p)=N(\mathbf p)/D(\mathbf p)\), a coordinate cell \(\psi_j\), and \((a,y)\) having a unique relation, decide
\[

\left[
\psi_j(\mathbf p)\wedge\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z)
\Longrightarrow
D(\mathbf p)\neq0\wedge D(\mathbf p)z=N(\mathbf p)
\right].
\]

\left[

\Longrightarrow

\wedge D_{\tau}(\mathbf p,\mathbf s)z=N_{\tau}(\mathbf p,\mathbf s)
\right].
\]

If no candidate passes, retain the corresponding unique-value graph; on a nonconstant coordinate or target cell retain \(\mathsf W_j\) or \(\mathsf W_{\tau}\), respectively. The notation \(\operatorname{TCF}(\mathcal G,A,Y,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) denotes the two symbolic partitions and their verified annotations, with formal names \(p_o=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o)\); a supplied \(s_Y\) selects the target-score section only at mathematical or exact specialization. \section{Main results}

\begin{proposition}[prop:positive-subclass]\label{prop:positive-subclass}
\(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\subsetneq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\). The natural interventional law \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}\) is the restriction to this subclass of the published natural interventional outcome law. For every supplied score \(s_Y\), \(\tau_{s_Y}\) is the scalar contrast obtained by applying \(s_Y\) to that law; when \(s_Y\) is the published numerical outcome coding, it equals the published numerical-outcome NATE, whereas allowing arbitrary \(s_Y\) is a score-coded generalization rather than a claim that every \(\tau_{s_Y}\) is literally the published scalar query. Strictness is witnessed by any compatible finite ambient lm-SCM with a zero full-data cell.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
We prove the class inclusion, exhibit a model witnessing its strictness, and then compare the causal queries.

First let \(\mathcal N\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\).  The set-builder equality in Definition \texttt{def:positive-rm-class} has \(\mathcal N\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) as its first membership condition.  Hence
\[
\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)
\subseteq
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{1}
\]

To prove that (1) is strict, take the declared compatible finite lm-SCM \(\overline{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\).  It has finitely many exogenous factors: one factor for each bidirected edge and one private factor for each substantive or missingness vertex.  For every such factor \(U_j\), choose one point \(u_j^0\) in its nonempty state space and replace its law by the Dirac law \(\delta_{u_j^0}\).  Leave every exogenous state space and every structural and proxy map unchanged, and denote the resulting SCM by \(\mathcal M^0\).  Its joint exogenous law is
\[
P_{mathcal M^0}(d u)=\bigotimes_j\delta_{u_j^0}(d u_j).
\tag{2}
\]
Equation (2) is a product law, so the individual edge factors and private factors remain mutually independent, as required by Definition \texttt{def:published-lm-class}.  Because no structural map, parent argument, shared-factor incidence, or proxy map was changed, \(\mathcal M^0\) has the same acyclic graph, the same bidirected-edge factorization, the same label-specific mechanism deletions, and the same proxy-consistency equations as \(\overline{\mathcal M}\).  Point-mass exogenous laws are allowed in the published ambient class: Definition \texttt{def:published-lm-class} imposes neither exogenous nondegeneracy nor positivity, causal minimality, or faithfulness.  It follows that
\[
\mathcal M^0\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{3}
\]

Under (2), every exogenous factor has a fixed value.  Acyclic evaluation of the unchanged structural equations therefore yields one deterministic full-data configuration \((v^0,r^0)\).  Consequently
\[
P_{mathcal M^0}(V=v^0,R=r^0)=1.
\tag{4}
\]
Write \(a^0\) for the \(A\)-coordinate of \(v^0\).  Since \(a_0\ne a_1\), at least one value \(a^\circ\in\{a_0,a_1\}\) differs from \(a^0\).  All finite state spaces are nonempty, so choose a full-data cell \((v^\circ,r^\circ)\) whose \(A\)-coordinate is \(a^\circ\).  By (4),
\[
0\le P_{mathcal M^0}(v^\circ,r^\circ)
\le P_{mathcal M^0}(A=a^\circ)=0,
\]
and hence
\[
P_{mathcal M^0}(v^\circ,r^\circ)=0.
\tag{5}
\]
Definition \texttt{def:positive-rm-class} requires strict positivity at every full-data cell.  Thus (5) implies
\[
\mathcal M^0\notin\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (1), (3), and (6) prove
\[
\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)
\subsetneq
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{7}
\]
More generally, if a compatible finite \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) has a full-data cell \((\widetilde v,\widetilde r)\) with \(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(\widetilde v,\widetilde r)=0\), the same strict-positivity clause immediately gives \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\notin\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\).  Every such model is therefore a witness to the strictness in (7).

It remains to state the causal-query relationship without identifying the causal target by definition.  For any \(\mathcal N\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\), any \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and the supplied score \(s_Y:\mathcal X_Y\to\mathbb R\), finiteness of \(\mathcal X_Y\) and Definition \texttt{def:causal-query} give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{s_Y}^{\mathcal N}(a)
&:=\mathbb E_{\mathcal N}\!\left[s_Y(Y)\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)\right]\\
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
P_{\mathcal N}\!\left(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)\right)\\
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)Q_a^{\mathcal N}(y).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mu_{s_Y}^{\mathcal N}(a_1)-\mu_{s_Y}^{\mathcal N}(a_0)
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\left\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal N}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal N}(y)\right\}\\
&=\tau_{s_Y}(\mathcal N).
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
The first line of Definition \texttt{def:causal-query} is the natural interventional outcome law on the ambient class.  Restricting its domain along (1) changes no formula, so its restriction at \(\mathcal N\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\) is exactly \(Q_a^{\mathcal N}\).

The published numerical-outcome NATE is the difference of the two interventional means (de Aguas et al., Definition 4, \cite{pmlr-v286-aguas25a}).  On the common finite numerical-outcome setting, let \(\iota_Y:\mathcal X_Y\to\mathbb R\) be that published numerical coding.  Substitution of \(s_Y=\iota_Y\) into (8)--(9) yields, term by term,
\[
\tau_{\iota_Y}(\mathcal N)
=\mathbb E_{\mathcal N}\!\left[\iota_Y(Y)\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a_1)\right]
-\mathbb E_{\mathcal N}\!\left[\iota_Y(Y)\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a_0)\right],
\tag{10}
\]
which is the published numerical-outcome contrast.  For an arbitrary real score \(s_Y\), (9) instead gives the natural contrast for the recoded outcome \(s_Y(Y)\); allowing all such scores is a score-coded generalization of (10), not an assertion that every choice of \(s_Y\) is literally the originally published scalar query.  Equations (8)--(10) concern causal interventional laws only and assert no equality with an observed-law identifying functional.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The inclusion pins the new theorem to the published lm-SCM semantics rather than to a bespoke analogue. \textit{Gap.} De Aguas et al. define the ambient lm-SCM class but do not isolate the finite positive regular-maximal subclass required by an exact recovery calculus. \textit{Consumer.} Applied analyses can interpret every returned \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}\) under the published natural interventional law and every returned \(\tau_{s_Y}\) as its supplied score contrast; the latter equals the published numerical-outcome NATE when the score matches the published numerical coding.

\begin{lemma}[lem:colored-adjacent-swap]\label{lem:colored-adjacent-swap}
Suppose \(\operatorname{Swap}_{\kappa}(u,v)\) holds and both composite colored fixings are legal. After erasing the ordered provenance list \(\Lambda\), the two resulting states have the same CADMG, fixed set, context, normalized kernel, and observed-law provenance. Their two appended trace suffixes are respectively \(\bigl((\lambda_u,\pi),(\lambda_v,\Phi_u^{\lambda_u}\pi)\bigr)\) and \(\bigl((\lambda_v,\pi),(\lambda_u,\Phi_v^{\lambda_v}\pi)\bigr)\); hence the definition does not imply equality of the full trace states up to permutation. Branches may be merged only after replaying the two licenses in a fixed canonical order from their common pre-swap state.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \(E\) erase only the ordered provenance list \(\Lambda\) from a trace-aware state.  Write the initial state as
\[
\kappa=(\mathcal H,F,c,q,\pi,\Lambda).
\tag{1}
\]
The premise that both colored composites are legal supplies fixability at each intermediate CADMG, validity of both licenses, and support of both denominator traces.  In addition, \(\text{ass:strict-positivity}\) makes every conditional kernel appearing in a legal fixing strictly positive on its stated support.  Thus every division below is defined.

Apply \(\text{lem:ordinary-adjacent-fixing-commutation}\) to the CADMG \(\mathcal H\) and normalized kernel \(q\).  Its hypotheses are the two fixability clauses in \(\operatorname{Swap}_{\kappa}(u,v)\), together with the assumed legality of both composites.  It gives
\[
\left(E\Phi_v^{\lambda_v}\Phi_u^{\lambda_u}(\kappa)\right)_{\mathcal H,q}
=
\left(E\Phi_u^{\lambda_u}\Phi_v^{\lambda_v}(\kappa)\right)_{\mathcal H,q}.
\tag{2}
\]
In either order the fixed set is \(F\cup\{u,v\}\), because each fixing adds its vertex and never removes a fixed vertex.  Deleting arrowheads into \(u\) and into \(v\) commutes, and fixing does not alter the context assignment \(c\).  Hence the CADMG, fixed-set, and context components in the two erased states agree.

By \(\text{def:colored-fixing}\), the provenance expression \(\pi\) is transformed by exactly the same two supported conditional-kernel divisions as \(q\).  Equality (2), viewed as the equality obtained after those two divisions, therefore gives equality of the resulting rational observed-law functions after cancellation of their nonzero denominators.  Thus
\[
E\Phi_v^{\lambda_v}\Phi_u^{\lambda_u}(\kappa)
=
E\Phi_u^{\lambda_u}\Phi_v^{\lambda_v}(\kappa)
\tag{3}
\]
in every component retained by \(E\).

It remains to calculate the component erased by \(E\).  Starting from the provenance \(\pi\), the order \(u,v\) appends
\[
\bigl((\lambda_u,\pi),(\lambda_v,\Phi_u^{\lambda_u}\pi)\bigr),
\tag{4}
\]
whereas the order \(v,u\) appends
\[
\bigl((\lambda_v,\pi),(\lambda_u,\Phi_v^{\lambda_v}\pi)\bigr).
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) follow directly from the append rule in \(\text{def:colored-fixing}\).  Merely permuting the two list positions does not replace \(\Phi_u^{\lambda_u}\pi\) by \(\pi\), or \(\Phi_v^{\lambda_v}\pi\) by \(\pi\); hence the hypotheses do not imply equality of the full ordered trace states up to permutation.  Finally, both branches have the same pre-swap state (1) and the same two licenses.  Replaying those licenses from (1) in a fixed canonical order deterministically recomputes one of (4)--(5), while (3) proves equality of all non-list components.  Therefore a branch merge is sound only after that canonical replay, as claimed.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} A local confluence theorem is the algebraic invariant that makes a colored reachable kernel independent of arbitrary implementation order. \textit{Gap.} Richardson et al. prove adjacent-swap invariance inside one CADMG and kernel domain; they do not transport missingness licenses or pre-restriction provenance. \textit{Consumer.} The memoized identification procedure can merge equivalent fixing branches without deleting the Figure 3(b) pre-selection trace.

\begin{proposition}[prop:figure3b-fix-select]\label{prop:figure3b-fix-select}
For every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}\), a legal trace on \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{3b}}\) first derives \(P(Y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W)\) and then applies \(\Sigma_{R_W=0}\), yielding \[P(Y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=P(Y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W=0)=P_{\mathrm{3b}}(Y\mid A=a,R_W=0).\] Hence the unique TCF partition cell containing \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}\) carries this functional. The closure generated by restricting to \(R_W=0\) before any fixing step contains no kernel with the pre-selection provenance needed for the first equality.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Fix a model \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{3b}})\) inducing \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}\).  By \(\text{def:figure3b-witness}\), the only directed path from \(R_W\) to \(Y\) is \(R_W\to A\to Y\), and \(R_W\) is incident to no bidirected edge.  Under \(\operatorname{do}(A=a)\), the equation for \(A\) is replaced by the constant \(a\), so that path is cut.  By the mutual independence of the individual-edge and private factors in \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), the exogenous inputs determining \(R_W\) are then independent of those determining \(Y(a)\).  Thus, for each supported \(r\),
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W=r)
=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)).
\tag{1}
\]
Every value of \(R_W\) is supported because \(\text{ass:strict-positivity}\) makes each finite full-data cell positive.

In the context \(R_W=0\), \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\) deletes the input \(W\) from the equation for \(A\).  The resulting treatment is a function only of \(R_W\), \(Z\), and the private factor of \(A\).  In contrast, after setting \(A=a\), the outcome is a function of \(a\), the private factor of \(Y\), the factor on \(W\leftrightarrow Y\), and the factor on \(R_Z\leftrightarrow Y\).  The first collection of unfixed exogenous inputs is disjoint from the second and is independent of it by \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\).  Conditioning on \(R_W=0\), which depends only on its private factor, preserves this independence.  Consequently,
\[
Y(a)\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}A\mid R_W=0.
\tag{2}
\]
Structural consistency follows by evaluating the same acyclic structural equations with \(A\) at its realized value.  Applying it, then (2), then (1), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{\mathrm{3b}}(Y=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y\mid R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W=0)\\
&=P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a)).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
Proxy consistency in \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\) makes all quantities on the observed side of (3) functions of \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}\); strict positivity makes their conditional denominators nonzero.

By \(\text{def:colored-fixing}\), the licensed fixing that derives the kernel \(P(Y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),R_W)\) stores its pre-restriction observed-law expression in \(\Lambda\).  Applying \(\Sigma_{R_W=0}\) afterwards is legal by \(\text{def:context-selection}\), restricts that kernel, and retains the stored entry.  Equation (3) is therefore the value of this fix-then-select trace.  Moreover, (3) holds for every model inducing any compatible observed law on \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{3b}}\).  Hence the coordinate is constant on each observational fiber.  By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the unique TCF coordinate cell is identifying; the trace expression is among the candidates in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), and its graph-uniform equality (3) passes the whole-cell verification.  Thus the cell carries \(P_{\mathrm{3b}}(Y=y\mid A=a,R_W=0)\).

Finally, start instead with \(\Sigma_{R_W=0}\).  Its input has no pre-selection license entry.  Thereafter, ancestral marginalization, district restriction, and fixing operate only on the already restricted kernel, while selection only extends the context; by \(\text{def:colored-fixing}\), a new entry records only the provenance present at the moment of fixing, and by \(\text{def:context-selection}\), selection never inserts an earlier unselected expression.  Induction on the finite operation word therefore shows that every descendant trace lacks the pre-selection provenance used in (1).  This proves the stated closure obstruction without asserting that fixing and selection commute.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} This is the minimal regression test showing that provenance is identifying information rather than bookkeeping. \textit{Gap.} De Aguas et al. display the functional in Figure 3(b), but no existing calculus represents its fix-before-select derivation as a reusable typed kernel. \textit{Consumer.} The ADHD medication and test-achievement analysis can recover the NATE when treatment assignment changes after a grade-5 pre-test is missing.

\begin{theorem}[thm:response-fiber-equivalence]\label{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}
For every finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\) and every observed law \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), the polynomial presentation is exactly the compatible lm-SCM fiber in both directions. First, for every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), finite support reduction of each mutually independent individual-edge and private latent factor produces \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) such that \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), \(p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r)\), and \(q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) for every \((v,r,a,y)\). Conversely, every \(\theta\) satisfying \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) decodes, using the unique selected table and the product law \(\prod_h\rho_h\), to a model \(\mathcal M_{\theta}\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) with the same full-data cells and \(Q_a^{\mathcal M_{\theta}}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta)\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and first take \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). By \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\) and \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\), \(\mathcal M\) has finite endogenous alphabets, its full law is strictly positive, and its exogenous variables consist of mutually independent individual-edge and private factors. Put
\[
L_{\mathcal G}=\left|\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\right|,
\qquad
K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)=L_{\mathcal G}+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1).
\tag{1}
\]
Applying \(\text{lem:finite-factor-support-reduction}\) sequentially to the finitely many factors yields a label-compatible deterministic response representation in which each factor has support at most \(K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\), mutual independence is preserved, and simultaneously
\[
P(V=v,R=r)=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),
\qquad
P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\tag{2}
\]
for all \((v,r,a,y)\). The support lemma obtains (1) from the affine-dimension bound \((L_{\mathcal G}-1)+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1)\); no lower bound or sharpness assertion is used.

Pad a factor having fewer than \(K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\) supported values by zero-weight states. Let \(\rho_{h,k}\) be the resulting marginal weights. The support reduction preserves the original structural equations on all positive-weight states, and \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\) makes their restricted tables an element \(g_*\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\). Set
\[
\gamma_{g_*}=1,
\qquad
\gamma_g=0\quad(g\neq g_*).
\tag{3}
\]
Then
\[
\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0,
\qquad
\sum_g\gamma_g=1,
\qquad
\rho_{h,k}\geq0,
\qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)}\rho_{h,k}=1.
\tag{4}
\]
By the product independence in \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), deterministic evaluation of the response tables gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
&=\sum_u\mathbf 1\{g_*(u)=(v,r)\}\prod_h\rho_{h,u_h}
=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),\\
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
&=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]
The second equality uses (2) and \(\text{def:causal-query}\). Proxy consistency and the first equality in (5) imply
\[
p_o(\theta_{\mathcal M})
=\sum_{(v,r):\operatorname{proxy}(v,r)=o}P_{\mathcal M}(v,r)
=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o).
\tag{6}
\]
Strict positivity in \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\) gives \(p_{v,r}(\theta_{\mathcal M})>0\) for every full cell. Equations (3)--(6) are exactly the conjuncts of \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), proving the forward direction.

Conversely, suppose \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Over \(\mathbb R\), each equation \(\gamma_g^2-\gamma_g=0\) implies \(\gamma_g\in\{0,1\}\). Together with \(\sum_g\gamma_g=1\), this yields a unique \(g_*\) satisfying \(\gamma_{g_*}=1\). For each \(h\in\mathcal J\), let \(U_h\) have law
\[
P(U_h=k)=\rho_{h,k},
\qquad 1\leq k\leq K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G),
\tag{7}
\]
and construct these finitely many factors independently. Use \(g_*\) as the structural-table tuple. Since \(g_*\in\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}\), the equations respect the directed parents and every table identification imposed by a label. Thus \(\text{def:published-lm-class}\), \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\), and \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\) show that the decoded object \(\mathcal M_\theta\) is an lm-SCM inducing \(\mathcal G\). Its finite response expansion is
\[
P_{\mathcal M_\theta}(v,r)
=\sum_u\mathbf 1\{g_*(u)=(v,r)\}\prod_h\rho_{h,u_h}
=p_{v,r}(\theta)>0.
\tag{8}
\]
The observed-cell equalities in \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}\), proxy consistency, and (8) give
\[
P_{\mathcal M_\theta}(\mathcal O=o)=p_o(\theta)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o).
\tag{9}
\]
Regularity and maximality are properties of the fixed input graph, and (8) supplies strict positivity. Hence (9) and \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\) imply \(\mathcal M_\theta\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\). Finally, replacing the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\) in the same product expansion gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_\theta}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta).
\tag{10}
\]
Equations (1)--(10) prove both directions without replacing the graph's individual-edge factorization by an unrestricted district latent.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[thm:tcf-soundness]\label{thm:tcf-soundness}
For every identifying cell \(\psi_j(\mathbf p)\in\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\), every compatible law \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) satisfying \(\psi_j((P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o)\), every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), the cell's functional satisfies \[f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).\] Consequently, \(\sum_y s_Y(y)\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}=\tau_{s_Y}\) throughout that fiber. This is a mathematical specialization statement and does not require a finite encoding of the population coordinates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix an identifying cell \(\psi_j\), put
\[
\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o):o\in\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}),
\]
and suppose that \(\psi_j(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\) holds.  Fix
\(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).  By
\(\mathrm{def{:}observational\text{-}fiber}\), this model is in
\(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\) and induces
\(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\).  The forward implication of
\(\mathrm{thm{:}response\text{-}fiber\text{-}equivalence}\) therefore supplies a response vector
\(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) satisfying
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta_{\mathcal M};\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\quad\text{and}\quad
q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad
\text{for every }(a,y)\in\mathcal X_A\times\mathcal X_Y.
\tag{1}
\]
In particular, the feasible response fiber over \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}\) is nonempty.  The strict inequalities in
\(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}\) also state that every full-data cell polynomial is positive, as required by
\(\mathrm{ass{:}strict\text{-}positivity}\).

Fix \((a,y)\in\mathcal X_A\times\mathcal X_Y\).  Because \(\psi_j\) is identifying for this coordinate,
\(\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\) is true.  Expanding
\(\mathrm{def{:}terminal\text{-}qe\text{-}test}\), this means
\[
\neg\exists\theta^0\,\exists\theta^1
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge
q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\ne q_{a,y}(\theta^1)
\right].
\tag{2}
\]
Thus any two feasible response vectors have the same \(q_{a,y}\)-value.  By (1), at least one feasible vector exists.  Hence the relation
\[
\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},z)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists\theta\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge z=q_{a,y}(\theta)
\right]
\tag{3}
\]
has exactly one real solution, denoted \(z_{a,y}\).  Substituting \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\) from (1) into (3) gives
\[
z_{a,y}=q_{a,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{4}
\]

It remains to check that either allowed representation of the cell functional evaluates to this unique value.  If the cell stores the graph relation (3), then
\(\mathrm{def{:}symbolic\text{-}observed\text{-}partition}\) and the definition of the successful output give
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=z_{a,y}.
\tag{5}
\]
Otherwise, \(\mathrm{def{:}tcf\text{-}procedure}\) stores a rational trace
\(r(\mathbf p)=N(\mathbf p)/D(\mathbf p)\) only after verifying on the whole cell that
\[
\forall\mathbf p\,\forall z\,
\left[
\psi_j(\mathbf p)\wedge\mathsf V_{a,y}(\mathbf p,z)
\Longrightarrow
D(\mathbf p)\ne0\wedge D(\mathbf p)z=N(\mathbf p)
\right].
\tag{6}
\]
Apply (6) to \(\mathbf p=\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and \(z=z_{a,y}\).  The premises hold by the choice of the cell and (3), so
\[
D(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\ne0,
\qquad
D(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})z_{a,y}=N(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{7}
\]
The first relation in (7) licenses division; the second then yields
\[
r(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})=z_{a,y}.
\tag{8}
\]
The annotated-cell definition of \(f_{a,j}\), together with (5) in the graph-relation case and (8) in the rational case, proves
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)
=z_{a,y}
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\tag{9}
\]
The choices of \(\mathcal M\), \(a\), and \(y\) were arbitrary, so (9) proves the coordinatewise assertion throughout the entire compatible positive fiber, rather than merely at a response-vector oracle.

We record explicitly the direct score-contrast bridge used by the target-only refinement.  For a feasible response vector define the proof intermediate
\[
t_{s_Y}(\theta)
=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\left\{q_{a_1,y}(\theta)-q_{a_0,y}(\theta)\right\}.
\tag{10}
\]
The sum is finite because membership in \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\), as specified by \(\mathrm{def{:}positive\text{-}rm\text{-}class}\), makes \(\mathcal X_Y\) finite.  Applying (1) at \(a_1\) and \(a_0\), and then using \(\mathrm{def{:}causal\text{-}query}\), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
t_{s_Y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\left\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\right\}\\
&=\tau_{s_Y}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently, if a cell satisfies the direct constancy formula
\[
\neg\exists\theta^0\,\exists\theta^1
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;\mathbf p)
\wedge
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;\mathbf p)
\wedge
t_{s_Y}(\theta^0)\ne t_{s_Y}(\theta^1)
\right],
\tag{12}
\]
then at \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}\) its unique-value relation
\[
\exists\theta\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge z=t_{s_Y}(\theta)
\right]
\tag{13}
\]
has the unique solution \(z=\tau_{s_Y}\), by (1), (11), and (12).  The same argument as (6)--(8) shows that any rational annotation certified by the corresponding whole-cell implication also equals \(\tau_{s_Y}\).  This argument treats the finitely many values \(s_Y(y)\) as supplied real coefficients; it neither assumes that they are rational nor requires a finite encoding of them for the mathematical equality.

Finally, the displayed consequence in the theorem follows directly from (9): finiteness permits termwise substitution, so
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\left\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\right\}\\
&\qquad=
\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\left\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\right\}
=\tau_{s_Y},
\end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]
where the last equality is \(\mathrm{def{:}causal\text{-}query}\).  All divisions used above are justified by the nonzero-denominator implication (6), and all conditioning and support needed to construct the response fiber is contained in the positive-fiber equivalence (1).  This completes the proof.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} Every returned expression must be valid for the entire compatible positive lm-SCM class, not merely for the response-function oracle used in testing. \textit{Gap.} Tikka et al. prove soundness for CSI-calculus search and Bhattacharya et al. justify missing-law fixing, but neither result composes the two operations with retained proxy-trace provenance. \textit{Consumer.} Graphical software can expose the returned NATE value as a population estimand without a hidden restrict-first assumption.

\begin{theorem}[thm:tcf-termination]\label{thm:tcf-termination}
Let the quantities in \(\text{def:termination-budget}\) be fixed. For every finite \(\mathcal G\) and finite alphabets, the expression-discovery phase of \(\operatorname{TCF}(\mathcal G,A,Y,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) visits at most
\[
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}
\bigl\{2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1\bigr\}^j
\]
literal complete trace states \(\kappa=(\mathcal H,F,c,q,\pi,\Lambda)\), counting every fixing-selection interleaving and provenance-bearing history. Independently of those traces, the coordinate and direct-target base CADs construct \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) and \(\mathscr P^{\tau}_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) within
\[
C_{\mathrm{base}}
=\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\mathrm{coord}},s)
+\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\tau},s)
\]
projection, root-isolation, sign-evaluation steps, and output cells. At most
\[
N_{\mathrm{check}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}
\left\{|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|
\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\mathrm{coord}},s)
+\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\tau},s)\right\}
\]
coordinate and target whole-cell candidate-equality sentences are subsequently decided, and the total work is bounded by
\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}
+N_{\mathrm{check}}\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\max}+1,s).
\]
Given \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), coordinate-cell selection and represented value or witness extraction terminate after finitely many exact sign decisions. Given \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\), the same is true for the direct-target cell over the ordered field generated by the observed and score coordinates. No point computation is claimed for arbitrary unencoded reals, and no polynomial-time claim is made.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a trace state \(\kappa\), let \(r(\kappa)\) be the number of random vertices in its CADMG and let \(d(c)\) be the number of missingness indicators assigned by its partial context. Define
\[
\mu(\kappa)=r(\kappa)+|\mathcal R|-d(c).
\tag{1}
\]
This is a nonnegative integer initially at most \(2n\). Under \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\), strict ancestral marginalization, restriction to a proper district child, and fixing each reduce the random set, while delayed selection strictly increases \(d(c)\). Hence every operation word has length at most \(2n\).

There are at most \(2^n\) observed margins and
\[
c=\prod_{R_Z\in\mathcal R}(|\mathcal X_{R_Z}|+1)
\tag{2}
\]
partial contexts. The two subset operations contribute at most \(2^{n+1}\) choices, fixing contributes at most \(n(\ell+1)\), selection contributes at most \(c\), and one terminal marker contributes one. Thus
\[
L_{\mathrm{op}}=2^{n+1}+n(\ell+1)+c+1,
\qquad
B_{\mathrm{state}}=2^n c\sum_{j=0}^{2n}L_{\mathrm{op}}^j.
\tag{3}
\]
For a fixed initial trace and operation word, every component of \(\kappa=(\mathcal H,F,c,q,\pi,\Lambda)\) is prescribed. Therefore (3) bounds the literal complete states, including every operation order and provenance list. By \(\text{lem:colored-adjacent-swap}\), kernel commutation alone does not erase unequal trace suffixes; the optional canonical-replay merge in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) only decreases (3).

Write \(h=|\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)|+|\mathcal V|+|\mathcal R|\) and \(g=|\mathcal T_{\mathcal G}|\). A doubled coordinate or target formula uses at most \(2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}\) response coordinates and \(|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|\) free observed coordinates; the target formula additionally has \(|\mathcal X_Y|\) free score coordinates. Hence their variable counts are bounded by
\[
p_{\mathrm{coord}}=2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|,
\qquad
p_{\tau}=2\{g+hK_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}+|\mathcal X_{\mathcal O}|+|\mathcal X_Y|.
\tag{4}
\]
Their complete encodings have length at most \(s\) by \(\text{def:termination-budget}\). The definition of \(\operatorname{CAD}(m,b)\) therefore bounds the two trace-independent base calls by
\[
C_{\mathrm{coord}}=\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\mathrm{coord}},s),
\quad
C_{\tau}=\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\tau},s),
\quad
C_{\mathrm{base}}=C_{\mathrm{coord}}+C_{\tau}.
\tag{5}
\]

Each visited trace offers at most one candidate for each coordinate and coordinate cell, and at most one direct-target candidate for each target cell. Consequently the number of whole-cell tests is at most
\[
N_{\mathrm{check}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}
\left\{|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|C_{\mathrm{coord}}+C_{\tau}\right\}.
\tag{6}
\]
After denominators are cleared, a coordinate test contains at most one response vector, \(z\), and \(\mathbf p\); a target test contains at most one response vector, \(z\), \(\mathbf p\), and \(\mathbf s\). Thus each has at most \(p_{\max}+1\) variables and encoding length at most \(s\). Each is decided within \(\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\max}+1,s)\) steps. Summing (3), (5), and (6) proves
\[
B_{\mathrm{tot}}
=B_{\mathrm{state}}+C_{\mathrm{base}}
+N_{\mathrm{check}}\operatorname{CAD}(p_{\max}+1,s).
\tag{7}
\]

Both partitions contain finitely many integer-polynomial signs. The exact observed-law interface decides the coordinate signs, and the augmented exact interface decides the joint observed-law and score signs. Standard finite root isolation from the fixed CAD implementation then supplies the asserted Thom encodings over the corresponding generated ordered field. These specialization steps are finite and separate from (7). No step defines computation on an unencoded real or gives a polynomial bound.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The finite guarantee counts complete trace histories, both trace-independent CAD partitions, and every coordinate or target annotation validation. \textit{Gap.} The direct score branch adds formal score dimensions and a second base elimination that must be charged explicitly. \textit{Consumer.} Small-graph exact audits can enumerate all traces and compare both the coordinate and target cells with response-function elimination.

\begin{theorem}[thm:qe-failure-compiler]\label{thm:qe-failure-compiler}
For every finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\), every exact specialization \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), and every negative partition-cell sample \(\omega=(a,y,\theta^0,\theta^1)\) satisfying \(\mathsf W_j\), let \(\mathcal T\) be any finite retained operation history accompanying the terminal package. Independently of this diagnostic tag, the decoded models satisfy
\[
\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\]
Both models are strictly positive and retain the product over the individual bidirected-edge and private latent factors. No colored-hedge membership, forest realization, or alignment between the sample and a graphwise recursion failure is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}

\quad(i=0,1),
\qquad
q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1).

\]
Applying \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\) to (1) yields

\begin{gathered}
\mathcal M_i\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
P_{\mathcal M_i}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},\\

\qquad i=0,1.
\end{gathered}
\tag{2}
\]
That lemma also proves that each decoded latent law is a product over the individual bidirected-edge and private factors and that every full-data cell is strictly positive.  Substitution of (2) into (1) gives
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{3}
\]

It remains to verify the algorithmic assertions.  The history \(\mathcal T\) and graph \(\mathcal G\) are finite, so there are finitely many prefixes and finitely many choices of \((\kappa_*,D,D')\) examined in the fixed order of \(\text{def:certificate-compiler}\). The argument never infers colored-hedge membership, forest realization, or alignment with a graphwise recursion failure.

\begin{theorem}[thm:colored-completeness]\label{thm:colored-completeness}
For a finite positive regular-maximal \(\mathcal G\) and supplied \(A,Y\), the trace-independent base phase of \(\operatorname{TCF}\) constructs the finite coordinate partition \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) of \(\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}\) and the finite direct-target partition \(\mathscr P^{\tau}_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) of that feasible projection times \(\mathbb R^{\mathcal X_Y}\). The colored trace phase only annotates fixed cells by expressions verified on the whole cell. For every compatible finite/full-positive \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), every \((a,y)\), and every supplied real score map \(s_Y\), its coordinate cell carries \(f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\) if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), and then
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\]
Its target cell at \((P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\) carries \(f_{\tau,k}(P_{\mathrm{obs}};s_Y)\) if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}\) is constant on the same fiber, and then
\[
f_{\tau,k}(P_{\mathrm{obs}};s_Y)=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\]
This direct iff remains valid when some interventional coordinates vary but their score contrast is constant. If a coordinate or target is not constant, its cell carries \(\mathsf W_j\) or \(\mathsf W_{\tau}\), and without any exact encoding there exist strictly positive individual-edge-factorized models \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) with the same observed proxy law and different values of the corresponding coordinate or score contrast. Under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), coordinate witnesses are represented and \(\operatorname{Compile}_{\mathrm{col}}\) terminates with a checked divergence record or an exhaustion tag; under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\), target witnesses are represented. If every coordinate is constant at \(a_0\) and \(a_1\), the additional sufficient formula
\[
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{f_{a_1,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)-f_{a_0,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)\}
\]
holds. This is pointwise semialgebraic completeness for both the interventional coordinates and the declared score contrast on the finite/full-positive subclass, with exact-free ambient nonidentification from negative cells; it makes no graphwise colored-hedge completeness claim.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By \(\text{thm:tcf-termination}\), the two trace-independent base constructions and the subsequent finite trace-annotation phase terminate.  By \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\), the coordinate cells partition the feasible observed-law projection and the target cells partition its product with the unrestricted real score coordinates.  The construction in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) fixes both partitions before trace enumeration and admits an annotation only after its whole-cell equality sentence has been verified.  Thus trace discovery can neither split nor merge a base cell.

Fix a compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) and a real score map \(s_Y\), and write
\[
\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o,
\qquad
\mathbf s_Y=(s_Y(y))_y.
\]
Compatibility makes \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) nonempty.  The dichotomy lemma gives unique coordinate and target cells and proves
\[
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\tag{1}
\]
and
\[
\mathsf I_\tau(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},\mathbf s_Y)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal M\mapsto\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}
\text{ is constant on }\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{2}
\]

Suppose (1) holds.  Its coordinate value relation is nonempty and single-valued.  If a rational trace \(N/D\) passes the whole-cell check, \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) proves \(D(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\neq0\) and \(D(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=N(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\) for every model in the fiber; otherwise the unique-value graph is retained.  In either case \(\text{thm:tcf-soundness}\) gives
\[
f_{a,j}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})(y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for every }\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{3}
\]
Conversely, a common identifying value in (3) makes the right side constant, and (1) holds.  This proves the coordinate iff.

The same argument applies directly to (2), without assuming coordinatewise constancy.  If the target cell retains \(\mathsf V_\tau\), its unique-value semantics and the response-fiber equality in \(\text{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}\) give the causal contrast.  If it has a rational annotation \(N_\tau/D_\tau\), the target whole-cell implication in \(\text{def:tcf-procedure}\) gives

\end{proof}

\]

\end{aligned}

for every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\).  On the finite positive class, every context occurring in a conditional expression is supported; a zero-weight term in the published aggregation, if present syntactically, contributes zero and requires no division.

\tag{3}
\]
The cited root-control result is uniform over its graph class, while TCF tests constancy at each observed law.  Therefore (3) proves the reduction but not the excluded converse for an exceptional law.

\begin{lemma}[lem:finite-factor-support-reduction]\label{lem:finite-factor-support-reduction}
Let \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) have finite endogenous alphabets and the mutually independent individual-edge and private factors indexed by \(\mathcal J=\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)\sqcup\mathcal V\sqcup\mathcal R\). Write
\[
L_{\mathcal G}=\left|\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}\right|,
\qquad
K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)=L_{\mathcal G}+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1).
\]
There is a label-compatible deterministic response representation in which every factor has support at most the universal upper bound \(K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\), the factors remain mutually independent, and, simultaneously for every full cell \((v,r)\) and every \((a,y)\in\mathcal X_A\times\mathcal X_Y\),
\[
P(V=v,R=r)=P_{\mathcal M}(v,r),
\qquad
P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put
\[
L=L_{\mathcal G},
\qquad
D=(L-1)+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1).
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\text{ass:finite-alphabets}\), these are finite integers, and the displayed definition of \(K_{\mathrm{ub}}\) gives
\[
D+1=L+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1)
=K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G).
\tag{2}
\]

We first prove the finite-dimensional support fact used below. Let \((\Omega,\mathcal A,\mu)\) be a probability space, let \(z:\Omega\to\mathbb R^m\) be bounded and measurable, and suppose that \(z(\Omega)\) lies in an affine subspace of dimension at most \(D\). If \(b=\int z\,d\mu\), then there are points \(u_1,\ldots,u_k\in\Omega\) and weights \(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_k\geq0\), with \(k\leq D+1\), such that
\[
\sum_{i=1}^k\alpha_i=1,
\qquad
b=\sum_{i=1}^k\alpha_i z(u_i).
\tag{3}
\]
Here is a derivation, including the possible nonclosed-range case. Set \(S=z(\Omega)\), \(C=\operatorname{conv}(S)\), and let \(\overline C\) be its Euclidean closure inside its finite-dimensional affine hull. Because \(z\) is bounded, for every \(n\geq1\) a finite Borel partition of a box containing \(S\) into sets of diameter at most \(1/n\), with one selected point of \(S\) from every set meeting \(S\), produces a finite-valued measurable map \(z_n\) satisfying
\[
\|z_n-z\|\leq n^{-1},
\qquad
\int z_n\,d\mu\in C.
\]
Therefore
\[
b=\lim_{n\to\infty}\int z_n\,d\mu\in\overline C.
\tag{4}
\]

We prove \(b\in C\) by induction on \(d=\dim\operatorname{aff}(\overline C)\). The assertion is immediate for \(d=0\). If \(b\in\operatorname{ri}(\overline C)\), choose \(d+1\) points of a sufficiently small relative ball about \(b\) whose simplex contains \(b\) in its relative interior. Approximating these vertices by points of the dense convex set \(C\), sufficiently closely that affine independence and the strictly positive barycentric coordinates of \(b\) persist, puts \(b\) in a simplex contained in \(C\). Hence \(b\in C\).

It remains to treat \(b\) on the relative boundary. Choose \(x_n\in\operatorname{aff}(\overline C)\setminus\overline C\) with \(x_n\to b\), and let \(p_n\) minimize \(p\mapsto\|x_n-p\|\) over the closed convex set \(\overline C\). A minimizer exists because a minimizing sequence can be restricted to a compact ball in the finite-dimensional affine hull. For \(x\in\overline C\), convexity and the minimality of \(p_n\) imply, for \(0<t\leq1\),
\[
0\leq
\|x_n-p_n-t(x-p_n)\|^2-\|x_n-p_n\|^2.
\]
After division by \(t\) and passage to \(t\downarrow0\),
\[
\langle x_n-p_n,x-p_n\rangle\leq0
\qquad(x\in\overline C).
\tag{5}
\]
Since \(b\in\overline C\), minimality also gives \(\|x_n-p_n\|\leq\|x_n-b\|\), and thus \(p_n\to b\). Normalize \(x_n-p_n\) and pass to a convergent subsequence on the unit sphere of the direction space. The limit in (5), after changing sign, is a nonconstant affine functional \(\ell\) satisfying
\[
\ell(x)\geq\ell(b)
\qquad(x\in\overline C).
\tag{6}
\]
Linearity of the integral and (6) yield
\[
0=\ell(b)-\ell(b)
=\int\{\ell(z(u))-\ell(b)\}\,d\mu(u).
\tag{7}
\]
The integrand in (7) is nonnegative, so it is zero \(\mu\)-almost surely. Restricting to this full-measure set preserves \(b\) and puts the range of \(z\) in the proper exposed face
\[
\overline C\cap\{x:\ell(x)=\ell(b)\},
\]
whose affine dimension is strictly below \(d\). The induction hypothesis gives \(b\in C\).

Thus \(b\) is a finite convex combination of points of \(S\). If it uses more than \(D+1\) positive weights, affine dimension at most \(D\) supplies coefficients \(c_i\), not all zero, such that
\[
\sum_i c_i=0,
\qquad
\sum_i c_i z(u_i)=0.
\tag{8}
\]
After changing all signs if necessary, some \(c_i>0\). With
\[
t=\min_{i:c_i>0}\frac{\alpha_i}{c_i},
\qquad
\alpha_i'=\alpha_i-tc_i,
\tag{9}
\]
the new weights are nonnegative, sum to one, have the same barycenter, and at least one formerly positive weight is zero. Repeating (9) proves (3). This derivation uses neither closedness of \(S\) nor an attainment assumption.

We apply (3) factor by factor. By \(\text{def:published-lm-class}\) and \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), the exogenous factors indexed by
\[
\mathcal J=\mathcal E^{\leftrightarrow}(\mathcal G)\sqcup\mathcal V\sqcup\mathcal R
\]
have a product law \(\bigotimes_{h\in\mathcal J}\mu_h\). The index set is finite because \(\mathcal G\) is finite. By \(\text{ass:acyclic}\), the structural equations admit a unique recursive evaluation
\[
\Phi(u)\in\mathcal X_{\mathcal V}\times\mathcal X_{\mathcal R}
\]
at every joint factor state \(u\). For \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), let \(\Phi^a(u)\) be the evaluation after replacing only the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\). This is the intervention in \(\text{def:causal-query}\); in particular, the endogenous missingness equations remain active.

Fix \(h\in\mathcal J\), hold the structural maps and all factor laws other than \(\mu_h\) fixed, and put \(\mu_{-h}=\bigotimes_{j\neq h}\mu_j\). For the model with these current laws, define
\[
\begin{aligned}
z_h(u_h)=\Bigg(&
\left(
\int\mathbf 1\{\Phi(u_h,u_{-h})=(v,r)\}\,d\mu_{-h}(u_{-h})
\right)_{(v,r)},\\
&\left(
\int\mathbf 1\{\Phi_Y^a(u_h,u_{-h})=y\}\,d\mu_{-h}(u_{-h})
\right)_{(a,y)}
\Bigg).
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
The structural maps are measurable by \(\text{def:published-lm-class}\), so Tonelli's theorem makes every coordinate in (10) measurable; all coordinates lie in \([0,1]\). The first block is a probability vector on the \(L\) full cells. For each fixed \(a\), the corresponding outcome block is a probability vector on \(\mathcal X_Y\). Hence every point in the range satisfies the linearly independent equations
\[
\sum_{(v,r)}z_{h,v,r}=1,
\qquad
\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}z_{h,a,y}=1
\quad(a\in\mathcal X_A).
\tag{11}
\]
The range of (10) therefore has affine dimension at most
\[
L+|\mathcal X_A||\mathcal X_Y|-1-|\mathcal X_A|
=(L-1)+|\mathcal X_A|(|\mathcal X_Y|-1)=D.
\tag{12}
\]

Product independence and Tonelli's theorem give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int z_h(u_h)\,d\mu_h(u_h)
=\Big(&\bigl(P(V=v,R=r)\bigr)_{(v,r)},\\
&\bigl(P(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))\bigr)_{(a,y)}\Big)
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
for the current model. In the second block, equality is exactly the structural intervention definition in \(\text{def:causal-query}\). Applying (3) with (12) replaces \(\mu_h\) by an atomic law on at most
\[
D+1=K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)
\tag{14}
\]
actual values of factor \(h\), without changing any coordinate of (13). Only one marginal factor law changes, so the joint exogenous law remains a product law.

Enumerate the finite set \(\mathcal J\) and perform this replacement sequentially, defining (10) with the factor laws current at each step. Equation (13) shows inductively that every replacement preserves the current full-data law and every interventional outcome coordinate. Thus the final model \(\widetilde{\mathcal M}\) satisfies
\[
P_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(V=v,R=r)=P_{\mathcal M}(V=v,R=r)
\quad\text{for all }(v,r),
\tag{15}
\]
and
\[
P_{\widetilde{\mathcal M}}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))
=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
\quad\text{for all }(a,y).
\tag{16}
\]

Relabel the selected support of factor \(h\) as \(\{1,\ldots,k_h\}\), where \(k_h\leq K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\), and restrict the original structural equations to the selected factor values. Finite endogenous and selected exogenous alphabets make these restrictions deterministic response tables. If the common alphabet in \(\text{def:response-polynomial-fiber}\) is desired, pad to \(\{1,\ldots,K_{\mathrm{ub}}(\mathcal G)\}\) with zero-weight states and copy an arbitrary supported state in every incident structural table. Padding changes neither support nor any probability. By \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\), a labeled context makes the original mechanism constant in the omitted parent there; restriction and copying preserve each such table equality. The resulting response representation is therefore label-compatible, and its factors remain mutually independent. Equations (15)--(16) prove the claim.

Because (15) preserves every full-data cell probability, strict full-data positivity is preserved if it was present. Strict positivity, regularity, maximality, overlap, division, inversion, and limiting arguments are not assumptions of the reduction, and no sharpness or support attainment claim is made.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[prop:ambient-nonrecovery-transfer]\label{prop:ambient-nonrecovery-transfer}
Fix a compatible observed law \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\). If its TCF coordinate cell is negative for \((a,y)\), there exist \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\) with the same observed proxy law and \(Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y)\). If, for a supplied real score map \(s_Y\), its direct target cell is negative, there exist \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\) such that
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_0}\neq\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_1}.
\]
In either case the same pair lies in the larger published ambient set \(\{\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G):P_{\mathcal M}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}\}\), so the corresponding coordinate or score contrast is not identified there. These existential conclusions do not require \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) or \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\); exactness is required only to algorithmically extract a represented pair.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\). If the coordinate cell is negative for \((a,y)\), \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) and \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\) make the formula
\[
\exists\theta^0\,\exists\theta^1
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1)
\right]
\tag{1}
\]
true over \(\mathbb R\). Choose a satisfying pair. The converse direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) decodes it to \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) with unequal \(Q_a(y)\) values.

If instead the direct target cell is negative at \(\mathbf s_Y=(s_Y(y))_y\), the formula
\[
\exists\theta^0\,\exists\theta^1
\left[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^0;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^1;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\wedge t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta^0)\neq t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta^1)
\right]
\tag{2}
\]
is true. Apply the converse direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) to both response vectors. It gives
\[
\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}.
\tag{3}
\]
By the definition of \(t_{\mathbf s_Y}\), coordinate preservation in response-fiber equivalence, finite summation, and \(\text{def:causal-query}\), (2) becomes
\[
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_0}=t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta^0)
\neq t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta^1)=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_1}.
\tag{4}
\]
The inclusion \(\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G)\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{lm}}(\mathcal G)\) follows from \(\text{prop:positive-subclass}\). Thus either pair also lies in the corresponding ambient observed-law set. Constancy of the relevant query on that larger set would contradict (1) or (4), proving ambient nonidentification. The choices in (1)--(2) use ordinary real semantics, not an effective encoding. The exact interfaces are required only to output represented points.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} Either a coordinate-level or target-level finite positive countermodel pair is already a countermodel pair in the larger published ambient class. \textit{Gap.} Exact witness representation is algorithmic extra structure and is not a premise of existential ambient nonidentification. \textit{Consumer.} A negative direct NATE cell certifies ambient failure even when no exact numerical extraction oracle is supplied.

\begin{lemma}[lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification]\label{lem:eipfd-frontdoor-identification}
For every \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\), every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G(H,m))\), every \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and every \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\), writing \(h\) for a joint value of the always-observed logistics block \(H\),
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)=
\sum_hP_{\mathcal M}(h)
\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)
\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(a'\mid h,R_W=0).
\]
Every probability on the right is an observed-law probability.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(\mathcal G(H,m)\in\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{EIPFD}}\), \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G(H,m))\), \(a\in\mathcal X_A\), and \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\).  Let \(Y(a)\) denote the outcome obtained by recursively evaluating the structural equations of \(\mathcal M\) after replacing the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\).  Thus \(P_{\mathcal M}(Y(a)=y)=Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) by \(\text{def:causal-query}\).  By \(\text{def:positive-rm-class}\), the endogenous alphabets are finite and every full-data cell has positive probability.  Hence every nonempty marginal cell used below has positive probability, and all displayed conditional distributions are defined.

The graph in \(\text{def:endogenous-proxy-frontdoor-class}\) has the always-observed logistics DAG \(H\) as an upstream block whose only edge to the remainder enters \(R_W\).  Under \(\operatorname{do}(A=a)\), the edge \(R_W\to A\) is cut.  The semi-Markovian factorization in \(\text{ass:semimarkovian}\), with no additional unlisted edge leaving that block, then gives the counterfactual independences
\[
Y(a)\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}R_W\mid H,
\qquad
H(a)=H.
\tag{1}
\]
The second relation is also immediate from acyclicity because \(H\) is pre-treatment and has no parent descended from \(A\).  By the structural-intervention definition of \(Q_a^{\mathcal M}\), total probability, (1), and positivity of \(P(R_W=0\mid H=h)\),
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=\sum_hP_{\mathcal M}(h)
P_{\mathcal M}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),h,R_W=0).
\tag{2}
\]

Now restrict to the context \(R_W=0\).  By \(\text{ass:label-semantics}\), the labeled arrow \(W\to A\) is absent in this context.  In the selected graph, \(B_1\) intercepts every directed path from \(A\) to \(Y\), because the only such route begins the chain
\[
A\to B_1\to\cdots\to B_m\to Y.
\tag{3}
\]
There is no open back-door path from \(A\) to \(B_1\): every edge entering \(B_1\) in the defined family is the arrow from \(A\).  Further, conditioning on \(A,H,R_W\) blocks every back-door path from \(B_1\) to \(Y\).  In particular, the path \(B_1\leftarrow A\leftrightarrow Y\) is blocked at the conditioned noncollider \(A\), as are paths entering \(A\) through \(Z\) before reaching \(W\leftrightarrow Y\).  These are precisely the ordinary front-door graphical conditions conditional on \((H,R_W=0)\).

Therefore the ordinary identification operations whose soundness is supplied by \(\text{lem:ordinary-id-soundness}\) yield, successively,
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),h,R_W=0)
=P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0),
\tag{4}
\]
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid\operatorname{do}(B_1=b),h,R_W=0)
=\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(a'\mid h,R_W=0),
\tag{5}
\]
and, using interception in (3),
\[
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),h,R_W=0)
=\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}
P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid\operatorname{do}(B_1=b),h,R_W=0).
\tag{6}
\]
Substituting (5) into (6), and then the result into (2), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)
=\sum_hP_{\mathcal M}(h)
\sum_{b\in\mathcal X_{B_1}}P_{\mathcal M}(b\mid a,h,R_W=0)
\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}
P_{\mathcal M}(y\mid b,a',h,R_W=0)
P_{\mathcal M}(a'\mid h,R_W=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Every variable in (7) is always observed, except that \(R_W\) is itself an observed indicator.  No unobserved value of \(W\) or \(Z\) occurs.  By \(\text{ass:proxy-consistency}\), the joint and conditional probabilities in (7) are therefore probabilities of the observed record \(\mathcal O\).  This proves both the causal equality and observability assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[lem:negative-cell-model-pair]\label{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}
Let an exact specialization on a negative partition cell yield \(\omega=(a,y,\theta^0,\theta^1)\) satisfying \(\mathsf W_j\). Decoding the two response vectors gives \(\mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) such that
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}},
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\]
The factors in both decoded models retain the individual-edge product structure and every full-data cell is strictly positive.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Unfolding \(\mathsf W_j\) in \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) gives
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta^i;P_{\mathrm{obs}})
\quad(i=0,1),
\qquad
q_{a,y}(\theta^0)\neq q_{a,y}(\theta^1).
\tag{1}
\]
Apply the converse direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) separately to the two compatibility conjuncts.  It decodes \(\theta^i\) to a model \(\mathcal M_i\) satisfying
\[
\mathcal M_i\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}}),
\qquad
Q_a^{\mathcal M_i}(y)=q_{a,y}(\theta^i),
\quad i=0,1.
\tag{2}
\]
The decoding uses the unique one-hot response-table selector and the product measure \(\prod_h\rho_h^i\), so it retains one mutually independent factor for each individual bidirected edge and each private vertex factor.  The equalities \(p_o(\theta^i)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o)\) in \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}\), together with \(\text{def:observational-fiber}\), give
\[
P_{\mathcal M_0}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathcal M_1}(\mathcal O)=P_{\mathrm{obs}}.
\tag{3}
\]
Likewise, the strict inequalities \(p_{v,r}(\theta^i)>0\) in \(\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}\), and the full-cell equality supplied by \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\), prove strict positivity of every full-data cell of both decoded models.  Finally, substituting (2) into the last inequality in (1) yields
\[
Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(y)\neq Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(y).
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) prove all conclusions.  The exact-specialization premise is used to supply the represented points \(\theta^0,\theta^1\), not to establish the decoding identities.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy]\label{lem:semialgebraic-fiber-dichotomy}
The formulas \(\mathscr P_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) form a finite semialgebraic partition of \(\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}\), and the formulas \(\mathscr P^{\tau}_{\mathcal G,A,Y}\) form a finite semialgebraic partition of that feasible projection times \(\mathbb R^{\mathcal X_Y}\), refining the lift of the coordinate partition. For every compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), every \((a,y)\), and every supplied real score map \(s_Y\), the unique coordinate cell carries a unique \(q_{a,y}\)-value if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y)\) is constant on \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), while the unique target cell containing \(((P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o,(s_Y(y))_y)\) carries a unique \(t_{s_Y}\)-value if and only if \(\mathcal M\mapsto\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}\) is constant on that fiber. A negative coordinate cell carries \(\mathsf W_j\) and a negative target cell carries \(\mathsf W_{\tau}\); in either case there exist two strictly positive individual-edge-factorized models in the same observed fiber with different values of the corresponding coordinate or score contrast. These existential pairs do not require exact encodings. Under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), or under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\) for the target, the appropriate pair has a Thom-encoded representation over the real closure of the ordered field generated by the supplied coordinates.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Set
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}
=\{\mathbf p:\exists\theta\,\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\text{ass:finite-alphabets}\), the endogenous cell sets, response-table set, individual-factor index set, and bounded response coordinates in \(\text{def:response-polynomial-fiber}\) are finite.  Therefore feasibility, every \(\mathsf I_{a,y}\), and \(\mathsf I_\tau\) are first-order formulas with finitely many polynomial sign atoms.  Apply \(\text{lem:real-closed-field-quantifier-elimination}\) to the response variables.  This yields equivalent quantifier-free sign formulas in \(\mathbf p\) for the coordinate tests and in \((\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\) for the direct target test.  Applying \(\text{lem:finite-sign-invariant-cad}\) first to the coordinate family and then to the target family together with the lifted coordinate-cell polynomials gives finite pairwise-disjoint semialgebraic decompositions
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}
=\bigsqcup_{j=1}^{J}\{\mathbf p:\psi_j(\mathbf p)\},
\qquad
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{feas}}\times\mathbb R^{\mathcal X_Y}
=\bigsqcup_{k=1}^{K}\{(\mathbf p,\mathbf s):\chi_k(\mathbf p,\mathbf s)\}.
\tag{2}
\]
This is the construction in \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\), and the second decomposition refines the lift of the first.

Fix a compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), put \(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}=(P_{\mathrm{obs}}(o))_o\), and define
\[
\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
=\{\theta:\mathsf C_{\mathcal G}(\theta;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\tag{3}
\]
The forward direction of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) shows that (3) is nonempty, so (2) selects unique coordinate and target cells.  Unfolding \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\) gives
\[
\mathsf I_{a,y}(\mathcal G;\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
q_{a,y}\text{ is constant on }\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}}).
\tag{4}
\]
The two directions of \(\text{thm:response-fiber-equivalence}\) give equality of value sets,
\[
\{q_{a,y}(\theta):\theta\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}
=\{Q_a^{\mathcal M}(y):\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) prove the coordinate equivalence.  If (4) fails, the unnegated doubled relation \(\mathsf W_j\) is satisfiable.  Decoding its two response points by the converse half of response-fiber equivalence produces two models in the same fiber with different coordinates; membership in the fiber supplies strict full-data positivity and the individual-edge product factorization.  This is an existence argument in the real field and does not use an exact encoding.  If \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) is supplied, \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) and \(\text{lem:negative-cell-model-pair}\) additionally represent such a pair.

For the direct target, let \(\mathbf s_Y=(s_Y(y))_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}\).  For every \(\mathcal M\) in the fiber, the forward response-fiber map gives \(\theta_{\mathcal M}\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\) and preserves every interventional coordinate.  Finiteness of \(\mathcal X_Y\), \(\text{def:terminal-qe-test}\), and \(\text{def:causal-query}\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{q_{a_1,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})-q_{a_0,y}(\theta_{\mathcal M})\}\\
&=\sum_{y\in\mathcal X_Y}s_Y(y)
\{Q_{a_1}^{\mathcal M}(y)-Q_{a_0}^{\mathcal M}(y)\}
=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Conversely, for every \(\theta\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\), the converse response-fiber map gives \(\mathcal M_\theta\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) and the same coordinate identities, whence
\[
t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta)=\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_\theta}.
\tag{7}
\]
Thus
\[
\{t_{\mathbf s_Y}(\theta):\theta\in\Theta(\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}})\}
=\{\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}:\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\}.
\tag{8}
\]
Unfolding \(\mathsf I_\tau\) shows that it holds exactly when the nonempty left side of (8) is a singleton, hence exactly when the right side is a singleton.  If it fails, real-field semantics supplies \(\theta^0,\theta^1\) satisfying \(\mathsf W_\tau\).  Applying (7) to these two points yields strictly positive individual-edge-factorized models in the same observed fiber with
\[
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_{\theta^0}}
\neq
\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M_{\theta^1}}.
\tag{9}
\]
No exactness premise occurs in this existence argument.  Under \(\operatorname{Exact}(P_{\mathrm{obs}},s_Y)\), \(\text{def:exact-observed-specialization}\) selects the joint target cell and supplies Thom encodings over the real closure of the ordered field generated by \((\mathbf p_{\mathrm{obs}},\mathbf s_Y)\).  The storage clauses for \(\mathsf W_j\), \(\mathsf W_\tau\), and the unique-value relations follow directly from \(\text{def:symbolic-observed-partition}\).  This proves every clause.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The lemma separates coordinate constancy from direct score-contrast constancy and gives a computable semialgebraic side for each. \textit{Gap.} Coordinatewise identification is sufficient but not necessary for target identification because nonconstant coordinates can cancel under the supplied score. \textit{Consumer.} The ADHD analysis can decide FATE coordinates and NATE directly rather than treating a failed coordinate as a failed scalar target.

\begin{theorem}[thm:binary-witness-observed-support-obstruction]\label{thm:binary-witness-observed-support-obstruction}
The universal binary-witness assertion is false, and the smallest substantive alphabet vector is not determined by the color and district structure of a colored hedge alone. There exist a finite regular-maximal graph \(\mathcal G\), a hedge in \(\operatorname{CH}(\mathcal G,A,Y)\), and a strictly positive compatible \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) for which two models in \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) have different \(Q_a\), but no model matching \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) can make every substantive variable binary. More generally, an always-observed substantive coordinate requires at least the cardinality of its observed support, and a partially observed coordinate \(Z\) requires at least \(|\operatorname{supp}(Z^{\dagger}\mid R_Z=1)|\). Under strict positivity these lower bounds equal the declared substantive alphabet cardinalities.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
We give an explicit finite counterexample and then prove the two support assertions.

Let \(\mathcal V=\{A,Y,Z\}\), let \(\mathcal R=\varnothing\), let \(\mathcal X_A=\mathcal X_Y=\{0,1\}\), and let \(\mathcal X_Z=\{0,1,2\}\). Thus every substantive variable is always observed and \(\mathcal O=(A,Y,Z)\). Let \(\mathcal G\) have the directed edge \(A\to Y\), the bidirected edge \(A\leftrightarrow Y\), the isolated vertex \(Z\), and the empty label set. Its directed part is acyclic, as required by \(\mathrm{ass{:}acyclic}\). Because \(\mathcal R=\varnothing\), there is no missingness context in which a label could occur; consequently the empty label set is regular and maximal, as required by \(\mathrm{ass{:}regular}\) and \(\mathrm{ass{:}maximal}\). Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}proxy\text{-}consistency}\) is vacuous for this graph.

Let \(\mathcal F\) be the \(C\)-forest induced by \(\{A,Y\}\), and let \(\mathcal F'\) be its subforest induced by \(\{Y\}\). The forest \(\mathcal F\) is bidirected-connected, \(A\) has the unique directed child \(Y\), and \(Y\) has no directed child, so its root set is \(\{Y\}\); \(\mathcal F'\) has the same root set. Moreover,
\[
 \mathcal F\cap\{A\}=\{A\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal F'\cap\{A\}=\varnothing.
\]
The common root \(Y\) is an ancestor of \(Y\) after intervention on \(A\), since a vertex is its own ancestor. Take the empty color assignment and the trace family consisting only of the empty trace. This trace family is prefix closed, and the licensing condition is vacuous because no color occurs. Hence, by \(\mathrm{def{:}colored\text{-}hedge}\),
\[
 H=(\mathcal F,\mathcal F',\varnothing,\{\varnothing\})
 \in \operatorname{CH}(\mathcal G,A,Y).
\]

We next construct two compatible models. Let \(U\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) be the exogenous factor shared by \(A\) and \(Y\). Independently, let \(E_A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/4)\), let \(E_Y\) be uniform on \(\{1,\ldots,16\}\), and let \(E_Z\) be uniform on \(\{0,1,2\}\). Put
\[
 A=U\mathbin{\oplus}E_A,
 \qquad
 Z=E_Z,
 \qquad
 t_0=\frac14,
 \qquad
 t_1=-\frac14,
\]
where \(\oplus\) denotes addition modulo two and the Bernoulli parameter is the probability of value one. For \(i\in\{0,1\}\), define
\[
 k_i(u,a)
 =16\left[\frac12+t_i\left\{\mathbf 1(u=a)-\frac34\right\}\right],
\]
and in model \(\mathcal M_i\) set
\[
 Y=\mathbf 1\{E_Y\leq k_i(U,A)\}.
\]
These are deterministic finite response tables because the thresholds are integers: \(k_0(u,a)\) is \(9\) when \(u=a\) and \(5\) otherwise, whereas \(k_1(u,a)\) is \(7\) when \(u=a\) and \(11\) otherwise. For each model, the equation for \(A\) depends on the shared factor \(U\), while the equation for \(Y\) depends on both \(A\) and \(U\), because changing the truth value of \(u=a\) changes the threshold. Thus the directed edge \(A\to Y\) and the individual bidirected-edge factor for \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) are active, and no additional edge is introduced. The factors \(U,E_A,E_Y,E_Z\) are mutually independent, so \(\mathrm{ass{:}semimarkovian}\) holds. The empty label set imposes no response-table restriction.

For positivity, define
\[
 \pi_i(u,a)=\frac{k_i(u,a)}{16}.
\]
The displayed threshold values give \(0<\pi_i(u,a)<1\) for every \(i,u,a\). Moreover, \(P(E_A=e)>0\) for both \(e\in\{0,1\}\). Therefore, for every \((a,y,z)\in\mathcal X_A\times\mathcal X_Y\times\mathcal X_Z\), independence and the structural equations give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P_{\mathcal M_i}(A=a,Y=y,Z=z)
 &=\frac13\sum_{u=0}^1
 \frac12 P(E_A=a\mathbin{\oplus}u)
 \pi_i(u,a)^y\{1-\pi_i(u,a)\}^{1-y}\\
 &>0.
 \end{aligned}
\]
This verifies \(\mathrm{ass{:}strict\text{-}positivity}\) cell by cell. All alphabets are finite, as required by \(\mathrm{ass{:}finite\text{-}alphabets}\). The graph conditions established above and \(\mathrm{def{:}positive\text{-}rm\text{-}class}\) therefore imply
\[
 \mathcal M_0,\mathcal M_1
 \in\mathfrak M_{+}^{\mathrm{rm}}(\mathcal G).
\]

We now compute the observed laws. For either \(a\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
 P_{\mathcal M_i}(A=a)
 =\sum_{u=0}^1\frac12P(E_A=a\mathbin{\oplus}u)
 =\frac12\left(\frac34+\frac14\right)
 =\frac12,
\]
and
\[
 P_{\mathcal M_i}(U=a,A=a)
 =P(U=a,E_A=0)
 =\frac12\cdot\frac34
 =\frac38.
\]
Division is legitimate because \(P_{\mathcal M_i}(A=a)=1/2>0\), and hence
\[
 P_{\mathcal M_i}(U=a\mid A=a)
 =\frac{3/8}{1/2}
 =\frac34.
\]
Using the response equation for \(Y\) and the conditional law of total probability,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P_{\mathcal M_i}(Y=1\mid A=a)
 &=\sum_{u=0}^1
 \left[\frac12+t_i\left\{\mathbf 1(u=a)-\frac34\right\}\right]
 P_{\mathcal M_i}(U=u\mid A=a)\\
 &=\frac12+t_i\left\{P_{\mathcal M_i}(U=a\mid A=a)-\frac34\right\}\\
 &=\frac12.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Since \(Z=E_Z\) is independent and uniform, both models consequently induce
\[
 P_{\mathcal M_i}(A=a,Y=y,Z=z)
 =\frac12\cdot\frac12\cdot\frac13
 =\frac1{12}
\]
for every observed cell. Let \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) denote this common uniform, and hence strictly positive, observed law.

Under \(\operatorname{do}(A=a)\), the structural intervention in \(\mathrm{def{:}causal\text{-}query}\) replaces the equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\) and leaves the exogenous distribution, in particular \(U\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\), unchanged. Therefore
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_a^{\mathcal M_i}(1)
 &=\sum_{u=0}^1
 \left[\frac12+t_i\left\{\mathbf 1(u=a)-\frac34\right\}\right]P(U=u)\\
 &=\frac12+t_i\left(\frac12-\frac34\right)\\
 &=\frac12-\frac{t_i}{4}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
It follows that
\[
 Q_a^{\mathcal M_0}(1)=\frac7{16},
 \qquad
 Q_a^{\mathcal M_1}(1)=\frac9{16}.
\]
By \(\mathrm{def{:}observational\text{-}fiber}\), both models lie in \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal G,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), but their interventional laws differ. Thus the displayed colored hedge has two strictly positive observationally equivalent witnesses with different \(Q_a\).

It remains to prove that no universal binary witness can match this observed law and to establish the general support claim. Let \(W\) be always observed and define
\[
 S_W=\{w:P_{\mathrm{obs}}(W=w)>0\}.
\]
In any model inducing \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), the observed value of \(W\) is its substantive value, so
\[
 S_W\subseteq\mathcal X_W
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 |\mathcal X_W|\geq |S_W|.
\]
For a partially observed substantive coordinate \(W\), \(\mathrm{ass{:}proxy\text{-}consistency}\) gives \(W^{\dagger}=W\) on \(R_W=1\). The required conditioning event has positive probability under \(\mathrm{ass{:}strict\text{-}positivity}\): by \(\mathrm{ass{:}finite\text{-}alphabets}\), summing the finitely many positive full-data cells with \(R_W=1\) gives \(P_{\mathcal M}(R_W=1)>0\). Consequently
\[
 S_W^{\dagger}
 =\operatorname{supp}_{P_{\mathrm{obs}}}(W^{\dagger}\mid R_W=1)
 \subseteq\mathcal X_W,
\]
and hence
\[
 |\mathcal X_W|\geq |S_W^{\dagger}|.
\]

Under \(\mathrm{ass{:}strict\text{-}positivity}\), both inclusions are equalities at the level of supports. Indeed, for every \(w\in\mathcal X_W\), summing the positive full-data cells whose substantive coordinate is \(W=w\) yields
\[
 P_{\mathcal M}(W=w)>0
\]
when \(W\) is always observed. If \(W\) is partially observed, summing the positive full-data cells with \(W=w\) and \(R_W=1\) gives
\[
 P_{\mathcal M}(W=w,R_W=1)>0.
\]
By \(\mathrm{ass{:}proxy\text{-}consistency}\), this equals
\[
 P_{\mathrm{obs}}(W^{\dagger}=w,R_W=1)>0.
\]
Thus every declared substantive value occurs in the conditional proxy support. Conversely, proxy consistency permits no value outside \(\mathcal X_W\) on \(R_W=1\). We have therefore proved
\[
 |S_W|=|\mathcal X_W|
 \quad\text{for always-observed }W,
 \qquad
 |S_W^{\dagger}|=|\mathcal X_W|
 \quad\text{for partially observed }W.
\]

In the constructed common law, \(S_Z=\{0,1,2\}\). Hence every model matching \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) must satisfy \(|\mathcal X_Z|\geq3\), so no such model can make every substantive variable binary. This disproves the universal binary-witness assertion.

Finally, for an arbitrary integer \(m\geq2\), replace \(\mathcal X_Z\) by \(\{0,\ldots,m-1\}\) and take \(E_Z\) uniform on it, leaving the equations for \(A\) and \(Y\) unchanged. The same computation gives two strictly positive models with a common observed law and distinct query values. The forests \(\mathcal F'\subseteq\mathcal F\), their districts and roots, the empty coloring, and the trace family are unchanged because \(Z\) is isolated and belongs to neither forest. On the other hand, the observed support of \(Z\) has cardinality \(m\), so the support argument forces \(|\mathcal X_Z|\geq m\); the displayed construction attains equality. Thus the smallest required \(Z\)-alphabet coordinate is \(m\), while the colored hedge data are fixed. Since \(m\) is arbitrary, the smallest substantive alphabet vector is not determined by the color and district structure of a colored hedge alone. This proves every assertion of the theorem.
\end{proof}

\section{Interpretation}

The observable decision and the causal target remain distinct. On a positive target cell, the unique semialgebraic value or a verified rational trace is proved equal to \(\tau_{s_Y}^{\mathcal M}\) for every model in the observed-law fiber. On a negative target cell, two models have the same proxy law but unequal score contrasts, so no observed-law functional can identify that target on the class. For the ADHD medication and test-achievement consumer, the method can test outcome-distribution FATE coordinates and the score-coded NATE separately; the Figure 3(b) calculation explains why the medication kernel must sometimes be fixed before conditioning on a missing pre-test indicator.

\section*{Technical internal limitation (diagnostic only)}

Completeness is pointwise and semialgebraic on the finite strictly positive regular-maximal subclass. The diagnostic compiler tests candidate first-divergence records but neither tag certifies a colored hedge, realizes a rooted forest, or aligns an algebraic witness with a failed graphical recursion; graphwise colored-hedge completeness therefore remains unproved. Negative finite-subclass witnesses transfer to the larger published ambient class, but positive identification on the finite subclass does not establish ambient identification for continuous or nonpositive models. The explicit CAD and trace bounds prove termination only and may be very large. The adjacent-swap lemma is local, and Figure 3(b) precludes treating fixing and context selection as generally commuting operations.

\section{Honest scope}

The graph, labels, finite alphabets, treatment, outcome, treatment endpoints, and real outcome score are supplied. Theorems range over nonempty observational fibers of finite-state, semi-Markovian, strictly full-data-positive, regular-maximal labeled-missingness SCMs. TCF gives a finite symbolic partition and a complete iff constancy decision for each interventional coordinate and for the declared score contrast; negative cells yield strictly positive individual-edge-factorized same-observed countermodels without any exact representation assumption. Exact observed and score interfaces are required only for algorithmic cell selection, represented value or witness extraction, and the optional finite divergence diagnostic. The procedure returns no colored hedge and makes no graphwise colored-hedge completeness claim. Negative finite witnesses imply nonidentification in the larger published ambient class, while positive finite-subclass results are not asserted for continuous, nonpositive, or merely observed-positive models. Estimation, graph learning, and finite-sample inference are outside scope, and no polynomial-time claim is made.
