For every integer \(k\geq1\), if \(S\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(k,p)\), then the equal-tailed Clopper--Pearson interval \(I^{\mathrm{CP}}_{k,\alpha/2}(S)\) covers \(p\) with probability at least \(1-\alpha/2\).  Writing \(I^{\mathrm{CP}}_{k,\alpha/2}(s)=[\ell,u]\), its boundary conventions are \(\ell=0\) at \(s=0\) and \(u=1\) at \(s=k\); with \(\beta=\alpha/4\), the non-boundary endpoints satisfy \(\mathbb P_{\ell}\{W\geq s\}=\beta\) and \(\mathbb P_u\{W\leq s\}=\beta\) for \(W\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(k,\ell)\) and \(W\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(k,u)\), respectively.
