The polynomial specified in \(\mathrm{def:first\text{-}bin\text{-}alias}\) is
\[
 \phi(u)=1-3u+2u^2,
 \qquad
 \int_0^1u\phi(u)\,du
 =\int_0^1(u-3u^2+2u^3)\,du
 =\frac12-1+\frac12=0.
\]
Furthermore, \(\phi(1)=0\), \(|\phi(u)|\leq1\) for \(u\in[0,1]\), and \(\phi'(u)=-3+4u\), whence \(\sup_{u\in[0,1]}|\phi'(u)|=3\).  Define
\[
 a_\Delta(r)=\Delta\phi(r/\Delta)\mathbf1\{0\leq r<\Delta\}.
\]
Because the two pieces agree at \(r=\Delta\), the preceding derivative bound shows that \(a_\Delta\) is continuous and \(3\)-Lipschitz on \([0,1]\).  The radius map is \(1\)-Lipschitz, since
\[
 \bigl|\lVert x-b\rVert_2-\lVert x'-b\rVert_2\bigr|
 \leq\lVert x-x'\rVert_2.
\]

Consider either law in \(\mathrm{def:first\text{-}bin\text{-}alias}\).  Choose the control anchor \(\theta_0=1/2\) and the treated anchor
\[
 \theta_{1,\pm}=\frac12\pm c_L\Delta
 =\frac12\pm c_La_\Delta(0).
\]
For every treated point \(x\) with radius \(r\leq1/2\), the point-anchor deviation is
\[
 |\mu_{1,\pm}(x)-\theta_{1,\pm}|
 =c_L|a_\Delta(r)-a_\Delta(0)|
 \leq3c_Lr\leq Lr,
\]
where the last inequality follows from \(c_L\leq L/8\).  The corresponding control deviation is zero.  Also
\[
 |c_La_\Delta(r)|\leq c_L\Delta
 \leq\frac1{16}\frac14=\frac1{64},
\]
using \(c_L\leq1/16\) and \(\Delta\leq\Delta_0\leq1/4\); hence all constructed Bernoulli means lie in \([0,1]\).  Thus the Bernoulli and point-anchor member conditions hold.

The construction supplies the known half-plane partition, sharp assignment, consistency, and conditionally Bernoulli potential outcomes.  Uniformity of \(X\) on the unit disc and polar integration give, for every \(t\in\{0,1\}\) and \(0<H\leq1/2\),
\[
 \mathbb P(T=t,R<H)
 =\frac1\pi\int_{\Theta_t}\int_0^H r\,dr\,d\vartheta
 =\frac{H^2}{2}
 \geq c_0H^2,
 \qquad |\Theta_t|=\pi,
\]
by \(c_0\leq1/2\).  Consequently both constructed laws are members of the unknown-design point-anchored class \(\mathcal P_L\).  This verifies membership through an embedded uniform-half-disc subclass only; it does not assert the alias identity for an arbitrary design in \(\mathcal P_L\).

The distribution of \((T,Q_\Delta(R))\) is common to the two signs.  Within the treated first bin, the difference of their joint success masses is
\[
 \frac1\pi\int_{\Theta_1}\int_0^\Delta
 2c_L\Delta\phi(r/\Delta)r\,dr\,d\vartheta
 =2c_L\Delta^3\int_0^1u\phi(u)\,du=0.
\]
Outside this bin the perturbation vanishes, and every control cell is unchanged.  Since \(Y\) is binary, equality of each released cell's total mass and success mass also gives equality of its failure mass.  Hence the entire one-unit laws of \(Z_\Delta=(Y,T,Q_\Delta(R))\) coincide.  Finally, by \(\mu_t(b)=\theta_t\) and \(\phi(0)=1\),
\[
 \tau_{P^{\mathrm{alias}}_{+,\Delta}}(b)=c_L\Delta,
 \qquad
 \tau_{P^{\mathrm{alias}}_{-,\Delta}}(b)=-c_L\Delta,
\]
so their difference is \(2c_L\Delta\), as claimed.
