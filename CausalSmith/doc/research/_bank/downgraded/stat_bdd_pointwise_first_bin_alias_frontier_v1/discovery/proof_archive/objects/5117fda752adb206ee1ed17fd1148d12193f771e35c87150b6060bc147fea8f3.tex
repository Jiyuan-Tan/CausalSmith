Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_L\), \(n\geq256\), \(0<\Delta\leq\Delta_0\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\).  For every event \(B\in\sigma(T,Q_\Delta(R))\) with \(B\subseteq\{T=t\}\) and \(\mathbb P_P(B)>0\),
\[
 Y\mid B\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}\!\left(\mathbb E_P[\mu_t(X)\mid B]\right).
\]
In particular, put \(A_t=\{T=t,Q_\Delta(R)<H_{n,\Delta}\}\).  Then \(A_t=\{T=t,R<H_{n,\Delta}\}\), its probability \(q_t\) is at least \(c_0H_{n,\Delta}^2>0\), and, with
\[
 \nu_t=\mathbb E_P[\mu_t(X)\mid A_t],
\]
one has \(|\nu_t-\mu_t(b)|\leq LH_{n,\Delta}\), \(N_{t,n,\Delta}\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,q_t)\), and
\[
 S_{t,n,\Delta}\mid N_{t,n,\Delta}=k
 \sim\operatorname{Binomial}(k,\nu_t)
 \quad\text{for every }k\in\{0,\ldots,n\}\text{ with }\mathbb P_P(N_{t,n,\Delta}=k)>0.
\]
