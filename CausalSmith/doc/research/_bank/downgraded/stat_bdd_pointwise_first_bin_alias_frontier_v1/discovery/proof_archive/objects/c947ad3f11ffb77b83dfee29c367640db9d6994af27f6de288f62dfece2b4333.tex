For every attainable release law \(Q\), the first-bin side probabilities are positive and the observable conditional means \(\eta_t(Q)=\mathbb E_Q[Y\mid T=t,Q_\Delta(R)=0]\) are defined.  The proved, computable outer endpoint map is
\[
 I_\Delta(Q)\subseteq
 [\underline\theta_1(Q)-\overline\theta_0(Q),
   \overline\theta_1(Q)-\underline\theta_0(Q)],
\]
where
\[
 \underline\theta_t(Q)=\max\{0,\eta_t(Q)-L\Delta\},
 \qquad
 \overline\theta_t(Q)=\min\{1,\eta_t(Q)+L\Delta\}.
\]
Moreover, \(2c_L\Delta\le\omega_\Delta\le4L\Delta\).  This is an outer bound, not a sharp endpoint characterization.
