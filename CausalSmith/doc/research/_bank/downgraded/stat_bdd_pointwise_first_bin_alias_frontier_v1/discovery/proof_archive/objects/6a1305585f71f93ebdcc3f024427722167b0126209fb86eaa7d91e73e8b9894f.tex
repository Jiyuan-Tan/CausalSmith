For each sign, construct \(P^{\mathrm{alias}}_{\pm,\Delta}\) by drawing \(X\) uniformly on \(\mathcal D\), taking \(\mathcal X_{1,P}=\{x\in\mathcal D:e_1^{\top}(x-b)\geq0\}\), assigning \(T=\mathbf1\{X\in\mathcal X_{1,P}\}\), and drawing conditionally independent Bernoulli potential outcomes given \(X\) with
\[
 \mu_{0,\pm}(x)=\frac12,
 \qquad
 \mu_{1,\pm}(x)=\frac12\pm c_L\Delta\phi\!\left(\frac{\lVert x-b\rVert_2}{\Delta}\right)
 \mathbf1\{\lVert x-b\rVert_2<\Delta\}.
\]
Finally set \(Y=TY(1)+(1-T)Y(0)\), where \(\phi(u)=(1-u)(1-2u)\) and \(c_L=\min\{L/8,1/16\}\).
