Let \(\mathfrak H_{n,\Delta}=(P_{0,n,\Delta},P_{1,n,\Delta})\) denote the following pair.  Under both laws, \(X\) is uniform on \(\mathcal D\), \(\mathcal X_{1,P}=\{x\in\mathcal D:e_1^{\top}(x-b)\geq0\}\), assignment is sharp, and the control mean is \(1/2\).  Under \(P_{0,n,\Delta}\) the treated mean is \(1/2\); under \(P_{1,n,\Delta}\) it is
\[
 \frac12+\frac{c_L}{4}\left\{(h_n-\lVert x-b\rVert_2)_+
 +\Delta\phi\!\left(\frac{\lVert x-b\rVert_2}{\Delta}\right)
 \mathbf1\{\lVert x-b\rVert_2<\Delta\}\right\}.
\]
Given \(X\), the potential outcomes are conditionally independent Bernoulli variables with these means, and \(Y=TY(1)+(1-T)Y(0)\).
