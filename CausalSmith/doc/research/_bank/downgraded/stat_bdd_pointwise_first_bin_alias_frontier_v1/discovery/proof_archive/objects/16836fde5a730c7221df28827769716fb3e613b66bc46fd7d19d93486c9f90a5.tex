Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_L\), \(n\geq256\), \(0<\Delta\leq\Delta_0\), and \(t\in\{0,1\}\).  For every event \(B\in\sigma(T,Q_\Delta(R))\) with \(B\subseteq\{T=t\}\) and \(\mathbb P_P(B)>0\), the conditional outcome is supported on \([0,1]\) and
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y\mid B]=\mathbb E_P[\mu_t(X)\mid B].
\]
In particular, put \(A_t=\{T=t,Q_\Delta(R)<H_{n,\Delta}\}\).  Then \(A_t=\{T=t,R<H_{n,\Delta}\}\), its probability \(q_t\) is at least \(c_0H_{n,\Delta}^2>0\), and, with
\[
 \nu_t=\mathbb E_P[\mu_t(X)\mid A_t],
\]
one has \(|\nu_t-\mu_t(b)|\leq LH_{n,\Delta}\) and \(N_{t,n,\Delta}\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,q_t)\).  Conditional on \(N_{t,n,\Delta}=k\geq1\), list the selected outcomes in increasing sample-index order as \(Y_{t,1}^{\star},\ldots,Y_{t,k}^{\star}\).  They are independent and identically distributed, supported on \([0,1]\), and have common mean \(\nu_t\); moreover,
\[
 S_{t,n,\Delta}=\sum_{j=1}^kY_{t,j}^{\star},\qquad
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[\frac{S_{t,n,\Delta}}k\,\middle|\,N_{t,n,\Delta}=k\right]=\nu_t,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}_P\!\left(\frac{S_{t,n,\Delta}}k\,\middle|\,N_{t,n,\Delta}=k\right)\leq\frac1{4k}.
\]
Outside the embedded Bernoulli subclass, the conditional sum need not be binomial.
