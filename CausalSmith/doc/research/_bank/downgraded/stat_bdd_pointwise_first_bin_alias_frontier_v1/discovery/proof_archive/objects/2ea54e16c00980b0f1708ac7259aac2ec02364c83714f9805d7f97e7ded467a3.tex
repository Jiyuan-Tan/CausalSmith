For every \(n\ge256\) and \(0<\Delta\le\Delta_0\), the handle \(\mathfrak H_{n,\Delta}\) admits one same-class baseline--alternative realization with both difficulties present.  Specifically, put \(\kappa=c_L/4\), \(b_h(r)=(h_n-r)_+\),
\[
 a_\Delta(r)=\Delta\phi(r/\Delta)\mathbf 1\{0\le r<\Delta\},
 \qquad
 g_{n,\Delta}(r)=\kappa\{b_h(r)+a_\Delta(r)\}.
\]
There are \(P_{0,n,\Delta},P_{1,n,\Delta}\in\mathcal P_L\) with \(m_{0,0}=m_{0,1}=1/2\), \(m_{1,0}=1/2\), and \(m_{1,1}=1/2+g_{n,\Delta}\), for which
\[
 \operatorname{TV}\!\left(\mathcal L_{P_{0,n,\Delta}}(Z_\Delta^n),
 \mathcal L_{P_{1,n,\Delta}}(Z_\Delta^n)\right)
 \le\frac14<1-2\alpha
\]
and
\[
 \tau_{P_{1,n,\Delta}}(b)-\tau_{P_{0,n,\Delta}}(b)
 =\kappa(h_n+\Delta).
\]
Thus the open question has a positive answer with \(\eta=1/4\) and \(c=c_L/4\), uniformly in \(n\) and \(\Delta\).
