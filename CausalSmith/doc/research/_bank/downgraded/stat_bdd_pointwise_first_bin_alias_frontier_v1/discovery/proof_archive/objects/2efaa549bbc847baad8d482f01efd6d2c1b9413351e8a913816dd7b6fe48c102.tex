Fix \(0<\Delta\le\Delta_0\) and \(Q\in\mathcal Q_\Delta\).  By \(\mathrm{def:released\text{-}identified\text{-}set}\), choose any compatible \(P\in\mathcal P_L\) with \(\mathcal L_P(Z_\Delta)=Q\).  For
\[
 B_t=\{T=t,Q_\Delta(R)=0\}=\{T=t,R<\Delta\},
\]
assumption \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}disc\text{-}design}\), together with \(\Delta\le\Delta_0\le1/4\), gives
\[
 Q(B_t)=\mathbb P_P(B_t)\ge c_0\Delta^2>0.
\]
Thus the observable first-bin conditional means
\[
 \eta_t(Q):=\mathbb E_Q[Y\mid B_t]
\]
are well defined.  The conditional-mean identity in \(\mathrm{lem:conditional\text{-}local\text{-}binomial\text{-}law}\), which follows from sharp assignment and consistency, and the point-anchor envelope give
\[
 |\eta_t(Q)-\theta_t(P)|
 =\left|\mathbb E_P[\mu_t(X)-\theta_t(P)\mid B_t]\right|
 \le L\mathbb E_P[R\mid B_t]
 \le L\Delta.
\]
Consequently every compatible anchor satisfies the computable outer restriction
\[
 \theta_t(P)\in[\underline\theta_t(Q),\overline\theta_t(Q)],
 \quad
 \underline\theta_t(Q)=\max\{0,\eta_t(Q)-L\Delta\},
 \quad
 \overline\theta_t(Q)=\min\{1,\eta_t(Q)+L\Delta\}.
\]
In particular, this proves the release-law-specific outer bound
\[
 I_\Delta(Q)\subseteq
 [\underline\theta_1(Q)-\overline\theta_0(Q),
   \overline\theta_1(Q)-\underline\theta_0(Q)].
\]
For any two laws \(P,P'\) in the same release fiber, the same observable \(\eta_t(Q)\) appears in both inequalities, so
\[
 |\theta_t(P)-\theta_t(P')|\le2L\Delta
\]
and therefore
\[
 |\tau_P(b)-\tau_{P'}(b)|
 \le |\theta_1(P)-\theta_1(P')|+|\theta_0(P)-\theta_0(P')|
 \le4L\Delta.
\]
Taking first the diameter of each fiber and then the supremum over \(Q\) proves \(\omega_\Delta\le4L\Delta\).  Conversely, \(\mathrm{lem:first\text{-}bin\text{-}alias}\) supplies two members of \(\mathcal P_L\) with the same released law and target separation \(2c_L\Delta\).  Their common release law is an element of \(\mathcal Q_\Delta\), hence
\[
 \omega_\Delta\ge2c_L\Delta.
\]
Because \(c_L>0\) and \(\Delta>0\), this fiber is non-singleton.  The conclusion is therefore partial identification of the full-data causal target from the released law, not a redefinition of that target.
