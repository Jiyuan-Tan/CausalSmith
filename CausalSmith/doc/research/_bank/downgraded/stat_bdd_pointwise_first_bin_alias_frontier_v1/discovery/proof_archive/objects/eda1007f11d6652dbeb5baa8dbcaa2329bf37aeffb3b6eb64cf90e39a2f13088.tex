The algebraic null direction is exact:
\[
 \int_0^1u\phi(u)\,du
 =\int_0^1(u-3u^2+2u^3)\,du
 =\frac12-1+\frac12=0.
\]
We next verify class membership rather than taking it from the construction.  Under either law in \(\mathrm{def:first\text{-}bin\text{-}alias}\), uniformity on the unit disc and the half-disc assignment imply, for \(t\in\{0,1\}\) and \(0<H\le1/2\),
\[
 \mathbb P(T=t,R<H)=\frac{H^2}{2}\ge c_0H^2,
\]
where the last inequality uses \(c_0\le1/2\).  Sharp assignment and consistency hold by construction.  On \([0,1]\), \(|\phi(u)|\le1\); hence, since \(c_L\Delta\le(1/16)(1/4)=1/64\), all constructed Bernoulli means lie in \([1/4,3/4]\subset[0,1]\).

For the treated side the boundary anchor is
\[
 \theta_{1,\pm}=\mu_{1,\pm}(b)=\frac12\pm c_L\Delta,
\]
and the control anchor is \(1/2\).  If \(0\le r<\Delta\), then
\[
 |\phi(r/\Delta)-1|
 =3(r/\Delta)-2(r/\Delta)^2
 \le3r/\Delta,
\]
whereas if \(r\ge\Delta\), the perturbation is zero and \(\Delta\le r\).  Thus at every side point of radius at most \(1/2\),
\[
 |\mu_{1,\pm}(x)-\theta_{1,\pm}|
 \le3c_Lr\le Lr,
\]
using \(c_L\le L/8\); the control envelope is immediate.  This verifies every member property of \(\mathrm{def:half\text{-}disc\text{-}class}\), so both laws belong to \(\mathcal P_L\).

It remains to prove observational equivalence.  The laws of \((T,Q_\Delta(R))\) agree.  Conditional on the treated first-bin event, the uniform-disc radial density is \(2r/\Delta^2\) on \([0,\Delta)\).  The two conditional Bernoulli success probabilities after mixing over the latent radius are therefore
\[
 \frac12\pm c_L\Delta\int_0^\Delta\frac{2r}{\Delta^2}\phi(r/\Delta)\,dr
 =\frac12\pm2c_L\Delta\int_0^1u\phi(u)\,du
 =\frac12.
\]
A mixture of Bernoulli variables is Bernoulli with its averaged success probability.  On the control side and on every other radial bin the conditional outcome laws already coincide.  Hence the full released one-unit laws of \(Z_\Delta\) are identical.  Finally,
\[
 \tau_{P^{\mathrm{alias}}_{+,\Delta}}(b)
 -\tau_{P^{\mathrm{alias}}_{-,\Delta}}(b)
 =\theta_{1,+}-\theta_{1,-}=2c_L\Delta,
\]
because the control anchors agree.
