For every \(n\geq256\) and \(0<\Delta\leq\Delta_0\), the answer to the joint-pair question is affirmative.  The pair \((P_{0,n,\Delta},P_{1,n,\Delta})\) realized by \(\mathfrak H_{n,\Delta}\) belongs to \(\mathcal P_L^2\) and, with \(\eta=1/4\) and \(c=c_L/4\), satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{TV}\!\left(\mathcal L_{P_{0,n,\Delta}}(Z_\Delta^n),
 \mathcal L_{P_{1,n,\Delta}}(Z_\Delta^n)\right)\leq\eta<1-2\alpha
\]
and
\[
 \tau_{P_{1,n,\Delta}}(b)-\tau_{P_{0,n,\Delta}}(b)
 =c(h_n+\Delta).
\]
The constants \(c>0\) and \(\eta<1-2\alpha\) are independent of \(n\) and \(\Delta\).
