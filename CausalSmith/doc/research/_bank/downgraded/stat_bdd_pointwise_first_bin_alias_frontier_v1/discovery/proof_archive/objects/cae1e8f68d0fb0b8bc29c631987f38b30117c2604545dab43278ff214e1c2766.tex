Along every sequence with \(0<\Delta\leq n^{-1/4}\), the unknown-design frontier has minimax absolute-error and honest expected-length order \(n^{-1/4}\) and squared-risk order \(n^{-1/2}\).  Along every sequence with \(n^{-1/4}=o(\Delta)\), the corresponding orders are \(\Delta\) and \(\Delta^2\), respectively.
