Fix \(n\) and \(\Delta\) in the stated ranges.  Because \(n\ge256\), \(h_n=n^{-1/4}\le1/4\); also \(\Delta\le\Delta_0\le1/4\).  The calculations in \(\mathrm{lem:first\text{-}bin\text{-}alias}\) show that \(a_\Delta\) is \(3\)-Lipschitz, satisfies \(a_\Delta(0)=\Delta\), and has zero radial-Jacobian integral:
\[
 \int_0^\Delta r a_\Delta(r)\,dr
 =\Delta^3\int_0^1u\phi(u)\,du=0.
\]
The map \(b_h\) is \(1\)-Lipschitz and satisfies \(b_h(0)=h_n\).  Consequently \(g_{n,\Delta}\) is \(4\kappa=c_L\)-Lipschitz.  By the definition of \(c_L\), \(c_L\le L/8<L\).  Also \(|a_\Delta(r)|\le\Delta\), whence
\[
 |g_{n,\Delta}(r)|\le\kappa(h_n+\Delta)
 \le\frac{c_L}{8}\le\frac1{128}<\frac14.
\]
Construct \(P_{0,n,\Delta}\) and \(P_{1,n,\Delta}\) by drawing \(X\) uniformly on the disc, setting \(T\) by the sharp boundary rule, drawing the two potential outcomes conditionally independently given \(X\) as Bernoulli variables with the displayed radial means, and setting \(Y=TY(1)+(1-T)Y(0)\); take iid copies across units.  The preceding Lipschitz and range bounds verify \(\mathrm{ass:radial\text{-}lipschitz}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:bernoulli\text{-}interior\text{-}range}\), while the construction verifies the remaining member properties of \(\mathrm{def:half\text{-}disc\text{-}class}\).  Hence both laws are in \(\mathcal P_L\).  At the boundary,
\[
 \tau_{P_{1,n,\Delta}}(b)-\tau_{P_{0,n,\Delta}}(b)
 =g_{n,\Delta}(0)=\kappa(h_n+\Delta).
\]

We next bound the distance between the released experiments without charging the alias component.  Partition \([0,1]\) into the quantizer cells \(A_j=[j\Delta,\min\{(j+1)\Delta,1\})\) of positive mass and put
\[
 w_j=\int_{A_j}r\,dr,
 \qquad
 \bar b_j=w_j^{-1}\int_{A_j}r b_h(r)\,dr.
\]
Here \(w_j=\mathbb P(T=1,Q_\Delta(R)=j\Delta)\) by \(\mathrm{lem:radial\text{-}grouped\text{-}law}\).  The alias term is supported on \(A_0\) and its weighted integral there is zero; it vanishes on all other cells.  Therefore the conditional treated success probabilities in cell \(j\) are \(1/2\) under \(P_0\) and \(1/2+\kappa\bar b_j\) under \(P_1\).  Every control cell has the same conditional law under the two models.

Let \(Q_k=\mathcal L_{P_k}(Z_\Delta)\).  For a Bernoulli law with success probability \(1/2+x\) relative to one with success probability \(1/2\), direct calculation gives
\[
 \chi^2\!\left(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2+x),
 \operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\right)
 =\frac{x^2}{1/2}+\frac{x^2}{1/2}=4x^2.
\]
Thus, using Jensen's inequality separately in each cell,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 d:=\chi^2(Q_1,Q_0)
 &=4\kappa^2\sum_j w_j\bar b_j^2\\
 &\le4\kappa^2\int_0^1r b_h(r)^2\,dr\\
 &=4\kappa^2\int_0^{h_n}r(h_n-r)^2\,dr
 =\frac{\kappa^2h_n^4}{3}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Independence from \(\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}sampling}\) makes the likelihood ratio a product, so expanding its second moment gives the exact identity
\[
 1+\chi^2(Q_1^{\otimes n},Q_0^{\otimes n})=(1+d)^n.
\]
Since \(nh_n^4=1\), \((1+d)^n\le e^{nd}\le e^{\kappa^2/3}\).  The elementary inequality \(e^x-1\le2x\) for \(0\le x\le1\), together with \(\kappa\le1/64\), yields
\[
 \chi^2(Q_1^{\otimes n},Q_0^{\otimes n})\le\frac{2\kappa^2}{3}.
\]
If \(M=dQ_1^{\otimes n}/dQ_0^{\otimes n}\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives directly
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_1^{\otimes n},Q_0^{\otimes n})
 =\frac12\mathbb E_{Q_0^{\otimes n}}|M-1|
 \le\frac12\{\mathbb E_{Q_0^{\otimes n}}(M-1)^2\}^{1/2}
 \le\frac{\kappa}{\sqrt6}<\frac14.
\]
Finally, \(\alpha<1/4\) implies \(1-2\alpha>1/2>1/4\).  This proves the asserted regime-uniform pair and constants.
