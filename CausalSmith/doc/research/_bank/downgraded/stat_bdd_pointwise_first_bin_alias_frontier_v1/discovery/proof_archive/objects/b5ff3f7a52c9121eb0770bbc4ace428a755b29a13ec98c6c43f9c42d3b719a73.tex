Fix \(n\geq256\), \(0<\Delta\leq\Delta_0\), and \(C\in\mathcal C_{n,\Delta}(\alpha)\).  Apply \(\mathrm{thm:single\text{-}joint\text{-}pair}\), whose uniform-half-disc laws are members of the unknown-design point-anchored superclass, and write
\[
 Q_j=\mathcal L_{P_{j,n,\Delta}}(Z_\Delta^n),
 \qquad
 \vartheta_j=\tau_{P_{j,n,\Delta}}(b),
 \qquad j\in\{0,1\}.
\]
The theorem gives
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_0,Q_1)\leq\frac14,
 \qquad
 \delta:=|\vartheta_1-\vartheta_0|
 =\kappa(h_n+\Delta),
 \qquad
 \kappa=\frac{c_L}{4}>0.
\]
Because \(h_n+\Delta\geq\rho_{n,\Delta}\), it follows that \(\delta\geq\kappa\rho_{n,\Delta}\).

Honesty at these two members gives
\[
 Q_0\{\vartheta_0\in C\}\geq1-\alpha,
 \qquad
 Q_1\{\vartheta_1\in C\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
For every measurable event \(A\), the definition of total variation gives \(|Q_1(A)-Q_0(A)|\leq\operatorname{TV}(Q_0,Q_1)\).  Taking \(A=\{\vartheta_1\in C\}\), and then using the elementary intersection inequality under \(Q_0\), yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_0\{\vartheta_0\in C,\ \vartheta_1\in C\}
 &\geq Q_0\{\vartheta_0\in C\}+Q_0\{\vartheta_1\in C\}-1\\
 &\geq1-2\alpha-\operatorname{TV}(Q_0,Q_1)\\
 &\geq\frac34-2\alpha>0,
 \end{aligned}
\]
where positivity uses \(\alpha<1/4\).  On this event the real interval \(C\) contains both fixed numbers, so its length is at least \(\delta\).  Hence
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_L}\mathbb E_P[\operatorname{length}(C)]
 &\geq\mathbb E_{P_{0,n,\Delta}}[\operatorname{length}(C)]\\
 &\geq\delta\left(\frac34-2\alpha\right)\\
 &\geq\frac{c_L}{4}\left(\frac34-2\alpha\right)\rho_{n,\Delta}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The first inequality is the subclass-to-superclass transfer: the supremum over \(\mathcal P_L\) dominates its value at the explicit embedded member.  Taking the infimum over \(C\in\mathcal C_{n,\Delta}(\alpha)\) proves the lower bound with
\[
 c_{L,\alpha,\Delta_0}
 =\frac{c_L}{4}\left(\frac34-2\alpha\right)>0.
\]
This admissible constant is independent of \(\Delta_0\), which is stronger than required.

By \(\mathrm{thm:total\text{-}honest\text{-}interval}\), the explicit interval \(C_{n,\Delta,\alpha}\) is a closed bounded released-sample-measurable map, has uniform coverage at least \(1-\alpha\), and has worst-case expected length at most \(C_{L,c_0,\alpha,\Delta_0}\rho_{n,\Delta}\).  It therefore belongs to \(\mathcal C_{n,\Delta}(\alpha)\), and taking the infimum on this upper side proves
\[
 \mathfrak W_{n,\Delta}(\alpha)\asymp\rho_{n,\Delta}
\]
with constants uniform over all permitted \(n\) and \(\Delta\).
