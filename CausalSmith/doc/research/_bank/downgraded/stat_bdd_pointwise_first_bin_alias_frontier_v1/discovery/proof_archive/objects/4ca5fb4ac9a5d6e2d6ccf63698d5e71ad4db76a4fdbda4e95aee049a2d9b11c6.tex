Fix \(n\geq256\) and \(0<\Delta\leq\Delta_0\), and abbreviate \(h=h_n\) and \(\kappa=c_L/4\).  Then \(h\leq1/4\) and \(\Delta\leq1/4\).  Since \(\phi(1)=0\) and \(\phi'(u)=-3+4u\), the function
\[
 a_\Delta(r)=\Delta\phi(r/\Delta)\mathbf1\{0\leq r<\Delta\}
\]
is continuous and \(3\)-Lipschitz on \([0,1]\), and it satisfies \(a_\Delta(0)=\Delta\).  The moment identity in \(\mathrm{lem:first\text{-}bin\text{-}alias}\) gives
\[
 \int_0^\Delta ra_\Delta(r)\,dr
 =\Delta^3\int_0^1u\phi(u)\,du=0.
\]
The function \(b_h(r)=(h-r)_+\) is \(1\)-Lipschitz and satisfies \(b_h(0)=h\).  Thus
\[
 g_{n,\Delta}(r)=\kappa\{b_h(r)+a_\Delta(r)\}
\]
is \(4\kappa=c_L\leq L\)-Lipschitz, and
\[
 |g_{n,\Delta}(r)|
 \leq\kappa(h+\Delta)
 \leq\frac{c_L}{8}
 \leq\frac1{128}<\frac12.
\]

Take the laws in \(\mathrm{def:joint\text{-}lower\text{-}handle}\).  Under \(P_{0,n,\Delta}\), choose both anchors equal to \(1/2\).  Under \(P_{1,n,\Delta}\), choose \(\theta_0=1/2\) and
\[
 \theta_1=\frac12+g_{n,\Delta}(0)
 =\frac12+\kappa(h+\Delta).
\]
The range bound puts every conditional Bernoulli mean in \([0,1]\).  For a treated point of radius \(r\leq1/2\),
\[
 |\mu_1(x)-\theta_1|
 =|g_{n,\Delta}(r)-g_{n,\Delta}(0)|
 \leq c_Lr\leq Lr,
\]
and every other point-anchor deviation is zero.  The construction supplies the known sharp half-plane partition, consistency, and conditional Bernoulli laws.  Uniform polar integration gives, on either side and for \(0<H\leq1/2\),
\[
 \mathbb P(T=t,R<H)=\frac{H^2}{2}\geq c_0H^2
\]
because \(c_0\leq1/2\).  Both laws therefore belong to the bounded-outcome point-anchored class \(\mathcal P_L\).  Their target separation is
\[
 \tau_{P_{1,n,\Delta}}(b)-\tau_{P_{0,n,\Delta}}(b)
 =g_{n,\Delta}(0)=\kappa(h+\Delta).
\]

It remains to bound the distance between the released laws.  Partition \([0,1]\) into the positive-mass quantizer cells \(A_j=[j\Delta,\min\{(j+1)\Delta,1\})\), and set
\[
 w_j=\int_{A_j}r\,dr,
 \qquad
 \overline b_j=w_j^{-1}\int_{A_j}rb_h(r)\,dr.
\]
For the uniform disc split into half-discs,
\[
 w_j=\mathbb P(T=1,Q_\Delta(R)=j\Delta).
\]
The alias term is supported in the complete first cell \([0,\Delta)\), where its \(r\)-weighted integral is zero, and vanishes in every other cell.  Thus the treated conditional success probability in cell \(j\) is \(1/2\) under \(P_0\) and \(1/2+\kappa\overline b_j\) under \(P_1\); every control cell agrees.

Let \(Q_k=\mathcal L_{P_k}(Z_\Delta)\).  Direct summation over the two Bernoulli outcomes gives
\[
 \chi^2\!\left(\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2+x),
                    \operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\right)=4x^2.
\]
Consequently, Jensen's inequality within every quantizer cell gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 d:=\chi^2(Q_1,Q_0)
 &=4\kappa^2\sum_jw_j\overline b_j^{,2}\\
 &\leq4\kappa^2\int_0^1rb_h(r)^2\,dr
 =4\kappa^2\int_0^h r(h-r)^2\,dr
 =\frac{\kappa^2h^4}{3}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}sampling}\) makes the likelihood ratio a product.  Expanding its second moment and using \(nh^4=1\),
\[
 1+\chi^2(Q_1^{\otimes n},Q_0^{\otimes n})
 =(1+d)^n
 \leq\exp(nd)
 \leq\exp(\kappa^2/3).
\]
Since \(\kappa=c_L/4\leq1/64\), the elementary bound \(e^x-1\leq2x\) for \(0\leq x\leq1\) yields
\[
 \chi^2(Q_1^{\otimes n},Q_0^{\otimes n})
 \leq\frac{2\kappa^2}{3}.
\]
Writing \(M=dQ_1^{\otimes n}/dQ_0^{\otimes n}\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 \operatorname{TV}(Q_1^{\otimes n},Q_0^{\otimes n})
 =\frac12\mathbb E_{Q_0^{\otimes n}}|M-1|
 \leq\frac12\{\mathbb E_{Q_0^{\otimes n}}(M-1)^2\}^{1/2}
 \leq\frac{\kappa}{\sqrt6}<\frac14.
\]
Finally \(\alpha<1/4\) implies \(1/4<1-2\alpha\).  The same pair therefore has both the order-\(h\) sampling displacement and the exact order-\(\Delta\) alias displacement, while released divergence is charged only for the sampling bump, as claimed.
