For every \(0<\Delta\leq\Delta_0\), every release law \(Q\in\mathcal Q_\Delta\) has positive probability on each first-bin side event \(\{T=t,Q_\Delta(R)=0\}\), and
\[
 2c_L\Delta\leq\omega_\Delta\leq4L\Delta.
\]
Consequently, the full-data causal functional \(\tau_P(b)\) is generally not point identified from a fixed-\(\Delta\) released law, and its worst release-fiber diameter is of order \(\Delta\).
