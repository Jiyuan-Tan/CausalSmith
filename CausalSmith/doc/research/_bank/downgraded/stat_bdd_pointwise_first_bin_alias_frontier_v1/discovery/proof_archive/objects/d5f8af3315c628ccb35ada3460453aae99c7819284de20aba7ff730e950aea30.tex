The polynomial in \(\mathrm{def:first\text{-}bin\text{-}alias}\) is
\[
 \phi(u)=1-3u+2u^2,
 \qquad
 \int_0^1u\phi(u)\,du
 =\frac12-1+\frac12=0.
\]
Moreover, \(\phi(1)=0\), \(|\phi(u)|\le1\) on \([0,1]\), and \(\phi'(u)=-3+4u\), so \(\sup_{0\le u\le1}|\phi'(u)|=3\).  Hence
\[
 r\longmapsto \Delta\phi(r/\Delta)\mathbf 1\{0\le r<\Delta\}
\]
is continuous at \(r=\Delta\) and is \(3\)-Lipschitz on \([0,1]\).  Since \(c_L\le L/8\), each perturbed treated regression is at most \(3c_L\le L\)-Lipschitz; the control regression is constant.  Since \(c_L\le1/16\) and \(\Delta\le\Delta_0\le1/4\),
\[
 \left|c_L\Delta\phi(r/\Delta)\mathbf 1\{r<\Delta\}\right|
 \le \frac1{64}<\frac14,
\]
so all Bernoulli means lie in \([1/4,3/4]\).  Completing the construction with the uniform score law, the sharp assignment, conditionally independent Bernoulli potential outcomes having the displayed radial means, and the consistency equation gives iid full-data laws satisfying each member property in \(\mathrm{def:half\text{-}disc\text{-}class}\).  Thus both alias laws belong to \(\mathcal P_L\).

It remains to compare what the analyst observes.  The two laws have the same distribution of \((T,Q_\Delta(R))\).  In the treated first bin, the difference in the unconditional joint success mass is
\[
 2c_L\Delta\int_0^\Delta r\phi(r/\Delta)\,dr
 =2c_L\Delta^3\int_0^1u\phi(u)\,du=0.
\]
In every other bin the perturbation is zero.  The control means are identical.  The grouped Bernoulli representation in \(\mathrm{lem:radial\text{-}grouped\text{-}law}\), including the fact that a binary mixture is fixed by its success probability, therefore shows that the complete one-unit laws of \(Z_\Delta\) coincide.  Finally, \(\phi(0)=1\), so
\[
 \tau_{P^{\mathrm{alias}}_{+,\Delta}}(b)=c_L\Delta,
 \qquad
 \tau_{P^{\mathrm{alias}}_{-,\Delta}}(b)=-c_L\Delta,
\]
and their difference is \(2c_L\Delta\), as claimed.
