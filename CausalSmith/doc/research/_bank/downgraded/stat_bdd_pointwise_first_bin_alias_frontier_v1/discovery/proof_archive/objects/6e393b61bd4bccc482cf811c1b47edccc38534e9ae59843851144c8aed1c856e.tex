Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_L\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}disc\text{-}design}\), the density of \(X\) with respect to planar Lebesgue measure is \(1/\pi\) on the unit disc centered at \(b\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:sharp\text{-}boundary\text{-}assignment}\), each treatment value selects a half-disc whose angular measure is \(\pi\).  Polar integration around \(b\) therefore gives, for every Borel set \(A\subseteq[0,1]\),
\[
 \mathbb P_P(T=t,R\in A)
 =\frac{1}{\pi}\int_A\int_{\Theta_t}r\,d\vartheta\,dr
 =\int_A r\,dr,
 \qquad |\Theta_t|=\pi.
\]
Taking \(A=[0,1]\) yields \(\mathbb P_P(T=t)=1/2\), and division by this positive probability yields
\[
 f_{R\mid T=t}(r)=2r\mathbf 1\{0\le r\le1\}.
\]
Let \(j\ge0\) be such that \((j+1)\Delta\le1\).  The event \(Q_\Delta(R)=j\Delta\) is, up to a null endpoint, the interval \([j\Delta,(j+1)\Delta)\).  Its conditional probability given \(T=t\) is
\[
 \int_{j\Delta}^{(j+1)\Delta}2r\,dr=(2j+1)\Delta^2>0.
\]
By the consistency member property in \(\mathrm{def:half\text{-}disc\text{-}class}\), on \(T=t\) the observed outcome is \(Y(t)\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:radial\text{-}bernoulli}\) and iterated conditioning, its success probability in this cell is
\[
 \mathbb P_P(Y=1\mid T=t,Q_\Delta(R)=j\Delta)
 =\frac{\int_{j\Delta}^{(j+1)\Delta}2r m_t(r)\,dr}
 {(2j+1)\Delta^2}
 =\bar m_{t,j}.
\]
Because \(Y\) is binary, a mixture of its conditional Bernoulli laws is itself the Bernoulli law determined by this success probability.  This proves every assertion.
