There exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on \(L\) and \(\Delta_0\), such that for every \(n\geq256\) and \(0<\Delta\leq\Delta_0\), \(c\rho_{n,\Delta}\leq\mathfrak R^{(1)}_{n,\Delta}\leq C\rho_{n,\Delta}\) and \(c\rho_{n,\Delta}^2\leq\mathfrak R^{(2)}_{n,\Delta}\leq C\rho_{n,\Delta}^2\). The upper bounds are attained, up to \(C\), by \(\widehat\tau_{n,\Delta}\).
