%%% FIELD sidp-proposed-statement
Let \(\mathcal P\) be an input-free COPAG, let \(A_0,B_0\subseteq V\) be disjoint with \(A_0\neq\varnothing\), and let \(\mathbf M\in\mathfrak M_d^+(\mathcal P)\). Suppose \(\operatorname{sIDP}(\mathcal P;A_0,B_0,P_{\mathrm{obs}})\) returns a proxy kernel \(H(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\), with a continuous version chosen at every application of Rule L2 as required by Theorem 4.14(3). Then, pointwise on the discrete assignment domain,
\[
 H(P_{\mathrm{obs},\mathbf M})
 =P_{\mathbf M}(A_0\mid\operatorname{do}(B_0),S=\mathbf 1)
\]
for every represented \(\mathbf M\in\mathfrak M_d^+(\mathcal P)\). The restriction to the positive discrete class \(\mathfrak M_d^+(\mathcal P)\) is licensed by Remark 4.4(2).
%%% FIELD affine-family-proposed
Fix a terminal role state \(\rho=(\bar X,\bar C)\in\Omega(\mathcal P;Y,X,C)\) and a displayed finite legal role trace
\[
 \pi:\rho_0=(X,C)\longrightarrow\rho_1\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow\rho_\ell=\rho
\]
in \(\Omega(\mathcal P;Y,X,C)\). Let \(a\) be the assignment on \(W=X\mathbin{\dot\cup}C\) determined by \(a_X=x\) and \(a_C=c\), and set \(\bar x=a_{\bar X}\) and \(\bar c=a_{\bar C}\). Fix represented coordinates \(\mathcal M\in[\mathcal P]_{\mathsf M}\) and \(\mathcal G\in[\mathcal M]_{\mathsf G}\), and nested \(R_F\)-rooted forests \(F'\subsetneq F\) satisfying the terminal selected-hedge predicates for the entire terminal intervention \(\bar X\). Choose one normalized local table \(\theta\) of the rational interior base model \(\mathbf M_{\mathrm{base}}\), choose a rational simplex-tangent direction \(v=R_Tq\), and define \(\mathbf M(t)\) by replacing \(\theta\) with \(\theta+t v\) while holding every other local mechanism fixed. The terminal masses of this family are evaluated at the inherited assignments \((y,\bar c;\bar x)\).
%%% FIELD compiler-proposed
The map \(\operatorname{LockCompile}\) retains from the supplied affine family its terminal state \(\rho\), displayed legal trace \(\pi\), represented coordinates \(\mathcal M,\mathcal G\), nested forests \(F'\subsetneq F\), common root set \(R_F\), and inherited terminal assignments. It applies rational row reduction to \(E_T\) and \(b_T\). When the augmented-rank test passes, it selects a rational \(q\in\ker(E_T)\) with \(b_Tq\neq0\), sets \(v=R_Tq\), and chooses a nonzero rational \(t\) with \(0<|t|<\delta\) inside the supplied exact positivity radius \(\delta\). It sets \(\mathbf M_0=\mathbf M(0)\) and \(\mathbf M_1=\mathbf M(t)\), expands their exact rational evaluation tables, and copies \(\rho,\pi,\mathcal M,\mathcal G,F,F'\), and \(R_F\), together with the inherited assignments, tables, and arithmetic trace, into \(\Gamma\).
