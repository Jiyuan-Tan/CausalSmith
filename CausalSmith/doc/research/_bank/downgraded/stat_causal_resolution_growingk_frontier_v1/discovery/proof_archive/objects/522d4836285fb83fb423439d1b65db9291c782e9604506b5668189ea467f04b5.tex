For the frozen positive tilt, summing conditionally over the Rademacher marks leaves \(e_{P_j}(u)=1/2\) and gives
\[
 b_{P_j}(u)=u+\nu_n\tanh\left\{\frac{24\delta_n\nu_nF_{j,1}(u)}{\sigma_{P_0,j}}\right\}.
\]
On the first common cell interval,
\[
 F_{j,1}(0)=-\frac1{2j}+\frac1{2(j+1)}+\frac{g_j}{2}<0.
\]
By right continuity it remains negative on an interval adjacent to zero.  For every \(\delta_n>0\) and \(\nu_n>0\), continuity therefore gives \(b_{P_j}(u)<0\) on a smaller interval of positive probability, violating \(\texttt{ass:bounded-feature}\).  Reversing the tilt gives the proposed inward orientation, but growing-\(K_n\) membership still needs a quantitative condition ensuring that interior jumps are below the \(K_n^{-1}\) cell scale and that perturbed objectives stay in their quadratic basins.
