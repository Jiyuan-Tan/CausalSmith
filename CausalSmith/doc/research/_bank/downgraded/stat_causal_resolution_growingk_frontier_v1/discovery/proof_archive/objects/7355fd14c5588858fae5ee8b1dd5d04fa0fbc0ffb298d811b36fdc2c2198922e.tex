Let \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\) be independent centered vectors in \(\mathbb R^p\).  Suppose, for a constant \(b>0\) and a number \(B_n\geq1\), that
\[
 n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb E X_{ij}^2\geq b,
 \qquad
 n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb E|X_{ij}|^{2+k}\leq B_n^k
 \quad(k=1,2),
\]
for every \(j\), and that \(\mathbb E\exp(|X_{ij}|/B_n)\leq2\) for every \(i,j\).  If \(B_n^2\log^7(pn)/n\to0\), Proposition 2.1 and Corollary 4.2 of Chernozhukov, Chetverikov, and Kato give vanishing Gaussian hyperrectangle approximation error and vanishing conditional empirically centered Gaussian-multiplier hyperrectangle approximation error.  The multiplier conclusion may be taken on events of probability at least \(1-\gamma_n\) for any deterministic \(\gamma_n\downarrow0\) whose logarithm is absorbed by \(\log(pn)\).
