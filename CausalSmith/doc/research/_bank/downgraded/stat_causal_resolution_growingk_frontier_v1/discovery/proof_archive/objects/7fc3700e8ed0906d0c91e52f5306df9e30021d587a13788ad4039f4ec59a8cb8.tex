By \(K_n\to\infty\), \(|J_n|\geq2\) eventually.  The proof of \(\texttt{thm:matched-width-frontier}\) shows that its frontier condition implies \(\delta_nK_n^5\to0\).  Therefore \(\texttt{thm:fano-membership}\), together with \(\nu_n=0\) and \(\underline w\leq1/12\), gives
\[
 \mathcal U_n\subseteq\mathcal P_n^{\mathrm{CQ}}
\]
for every sufficiently large \(n\).  Taking an infimum over the smaller set can only increase the coverage lower bound, so the displayed full-class honesty condition implies
\[
 \inf_{P^{(n)}\in\mathcal U_n}
 \mathbb P_{P^{(n)}}^n\!\left\{
 G_{P^{(n)}}(K)\in[L_{n,K},U_{n,K}]
 \text{ for every }1\leq K<K_n\right\}
 \geq1-\alpha-\eta_n.
\]
This is exactly \(\operatorname{Honest}_\alpha(B_n,\mathcal U_n)\).  Applying the converse half of \(\texttt{thm:matched-width-frontier}\) gives the asserted constants and the law \(P^{(n)}\in\mathcal U_n\).  No upper conclusion transfers, because honesty of the constructed multiplier band has been proved only on \(\mathcal U_n\), not on the larger class.
