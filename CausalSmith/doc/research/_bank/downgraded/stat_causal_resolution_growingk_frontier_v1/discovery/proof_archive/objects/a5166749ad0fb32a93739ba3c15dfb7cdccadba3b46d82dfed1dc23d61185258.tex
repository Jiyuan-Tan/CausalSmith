Let \(p_n=K_n-1\), and for \(1\leq K\leq p_n\) put
\[
 X_{iK}=\Delta\phi_{P^{(n)},K}(O_{n,i})/\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}.
\]
Suppose the row vectors are independent and centered, \(n^{-1}\sum_i\mathbb E(X_{iK}^2)=1\), and there is a common \(B_n\geq1\) such that
\[
 \frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbb E|X_{iK}|^{2+k}\leq B_n^k
 \quad\text{for every }K\text{ and }k\in\{1,2\},
 \qquad
 \mathbb E\exp(|X_{iK}|/B_n)\leq2
 \quad\text{for every }i\text{ and }K.
\]
Set
\[
 D_n=\left\{\frac{B_n^2\log^7(2nK_n)}{n}\right\}^{1/6},
 \qquad \gamma_n=(2nK_n)^{-1}.
\]
Then
\[
 \sup_t\left|\mathbb P\!\left(\max_{K\leq p_n}\left|n^{-1/2}\sum_iX_{iK}\right|\leq t\right)
 -\mathbb P\!\left(\max_{K\leq p_n}|Z_{P^{(n)},K}|\leq t\right)\right|\leq C D_n.
\]
and, with probability at least \(1-\gamma_n\),
\[
 \sup_t\left|\mathbb P\!\left(\max_{K\leq p_n}\left|n^{-1/2}\sum_i\xi_i(X_{iK}-\overline X_K)\right|\leq t\mid O_{n,1},\ldots,O_{n,n}\right)
 -\mathbb P\!\left(\max_{K\leq p_n}|Z_{P^{(n)},K}|\leq t\right)\right|
 \leq C\left\{\frac{B_n^2\log^5(2nK_n)\log^2(1/\gamma_n)}{n}\right\}^{1/6}.
\]
The constant \(C\) is numerical because the variance lower bound equals one.  Hence both errors are \(o(1)\) whenever \(D_n\to0\).  Replacement of the oracle scores by estimated scores is not part of this lemma.
