For \(X_{iK}=\Delta\phi_{P^{(n)},K}(O_{n,i})/\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}\), let \(\mathfrak B_n(P^{(n)})\) be the declared moment--exponential envelope.  The coordinates are independent across \(i\), centered, and have unit variance.  If
\[
 \left\{\frac{\mathfrak B_n(P^{(n)})^2\log^7(2nK_n)}{n}\right\}^{1/6}\longrightarrow0,
\]
then the distribution of \(\max_{K<K_n}|n^{-1/2}\sum_iX_{iK}|\) is uniformly approximated by that of \(\max_{K<K_n}|Z_{P^{(n)},K}|\).  With \(\gamma_n=(2nK_n)^{-1}\) and \(\overline X_K=n^{-1}\sum_iX_{iK}\), the conditional Gaussian-multiplier law based on \(n^{-1/2}\sum_i\xi_i(X_{iK}-\overline X_K)\) has the same uniform approximation property in probability.  This assertion concerns the oracle empirically centered scores only; replacing them by fitted scores requires a separate score-replacement theorem.
