Let
\[
 N_{\mathrm g}=\{n:\nu_nK_n\leq1\},
 \qquad
 N_{\mathrm r}=\{n:\nu_nK_n>1\}.
\]
These sets are disjoint and exhaustive by trichotomy.  Write the first displayed localization premise of the proposed statement as \(a_n\to0\), where
\[
 a_n=
 \mathbf 1\{n\in N_{\mathrm g}\}\delta_nK_n^3
 +\mathbf 1\{n\in N_{\mathrm r}\}\delta_nK_n^5.
\]

Consider first the rows in \(N_{\mathrm g}\).  On those rows, the definition of \(N_{\mathrm g}\) gives
\[
 a_n=\delta_nK_n^3\to0,
 \qquad
 \nu_nK_n\leq1.
\]
Consequently the localization and bounded-noise hypotheses of \(\texttt{prop:positive-noise-lower-transfer}\) hold on every infinite low-noise subsequence, with \(\bar\nu=1\).  The denominator condition \(\underline w\leq1/12\) and the displayed uniform-honesty premise are hypotheses of the proposed theorem itself.  Hence that proposition supplies constants \(c_{0,\mathrm g},c_{1,\mathrm g}>0\) and, for every sufficiently large \(n\in N_{\mathrm g}\), a law \(P_n^{\mathrm g}\in\mathcal U_{n,\mathrm g}\) such that
\[
 \mathbb P_{P_n^{\mathrm g}}^n\!\left\{
 \max_{K\in J_n}
 \frac{\sqrt n\,h_{n,K}}{\sigma_{P_n^{\mathrm g},K}}
 \geq c_{0,\mathrm g}\sqrt{\log|J_n|}
 \right\}\geq c_{1,\mathrm g}.
\]
By \(\texttt{def:piecewise-all-noise-experiment}\), on these rows \(\mathcal U_n^\star=\mathcal U_{n,\mathrm g}\) and \(I_n^\star=J_n\).  The same lower-transfer proposition, through its cited packing theorem, also gives \(P_n^{\mathrm g}\in\mathcal P_n^{\mathrm{CQ}}\).

Now consider the rows in \(N_{\mathrm r}\).  There
\[
 a_n=\delta_nK_n^5\to0,
 \qquad
 \nu_nK_n>1.
\]
In particular \(\inf_{n\in N_{\mathrm r}}\nu_nK_n\geq1\).  The second displayed premise of the proposed theorem means that there are finite constants \(C\) and \(n_0\) such that
\[
 \mathbf 1\{n\in N_{\mathrm r}\}\nu_n\delta_n\leq Cr_n
 \quad\text{for every }n\geq n_0.
\]
Restricting this inequality to \(N_{\mathrm r}\) yields \(\nu_n\delta_n=O(r_n)\).  Thus all localization and nuisance hypotheses of \(\texttt{prop:residual-noise-lower-transfer}\) hold on every infinite high-noise subsequence, with \(\underline\nu=1\).  That proposition supplies constants \(c_{0,\mathrm r},c_{1,\mathrm r}>0\) and, for every sufficiently large \(n\in N_{\mathrm r}\), a law \(P_n^{\mathrm r}\in\mathcal U_{n,\mathrm r}\cap\mathcal P_n^{\mathrm{CQ}}\) such that
\[
 \mathbb P_{P_n^{\mathrm r}}^n\!\left\{
 \max_{K\in J_n^{\mathrm{hi}}}
 \frac{\sqrt n\,h_{n,K}}{\sigma_{P_n^{\mathrm r},K}}
 \geq c_{0,\mathrm r}\sqrt{\log|J_n^{\mathrm{hi}}|}
 \right\}\geq c_{1,\mathrm r}.
\]
By \(\texttt{def:piecewise-all-noise-experiment}\), on these rows \(\mathcal U_n^\star=\mathcal U_{n,\mathrm r}\) and \(I_n^\star=J_n^{\mathrm{hi}}\).

It remains only to put the two branchwise bounds on the claimed common scale.  Since \(K_n\to\infty\), for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 |J_n|=K_n-8\geq K_n/3,
 \qquad
 |J_n^{\mathrm{hi}}|=K_n-\lceil K_n/2\rceil
 =\lfloor K_n/2\rfloor\geq K_n/3.
\]
After increasing the deterministic threshold once more, \(K_n/3\geq\sqrt{K_n}\), and therefore either possible value of \(I_n^\star\) satisfies
\[
 \sqrt{\log|I_n^\star|}
 \geq2^{-1/2}\sqrt{\log K_n}.
\]
Set
\[
 c_0=2^{-1/2}\min(c_{0,\mathrm g},c_{0,\mathrm r}),
 \qquad
 c_1=\min(c_{1,\mathrm g},c_{1,\mathrm r}).
\]
The component constants are uniform within their branches, and the branch thresholds are finite.  If one branch is finite, the other branch therefore proves the assertion eventually.  If both are infinite, take the larger of their two finite thresholds.  Exhaustiveness of \(N_{\mathrm g}\) and \(N_{\mathrm r}\), the preceding cardinality comparison, and the two probability bounds then show that for every sufficiently large \(n\) there exists \(P^{(n)}\in\mathcal U_n^\star\subseteq\mathcal P_n^{\mathrm{CQ}}\) for which
\[
 \mathbb P_{P^{(n)}}^n\!\left\{
 \max_{K\in I_n^\star}
 \frac{\sqrt n\,h_{n,K}}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}
 \geq c_0\sqrt{\log K_n}
 \right\}\geq c_1.
\]
Both invoked propositions are lower-transfer statements for a generic common rectangular band.  No step constructs or validates a feasible positive-noise upper band, so the converse-only conclusion follows.
