Let \(m_n=|J_n|\), so \(m_n\to\infty\).  By \(\texttt{thm:positive-noise-geometric-packing}\), \(\mathcal U_{n,\mathrm g}\subseteq\mathcal P_n^{\mathrm{CQ}}\) eventually.  Restricting the assumed full-class coverage inequality to this finite subclass gives coverage at least \(1-\alpha-\eta_n\) for every member of \(\mathcal U_{n,\mathrm g}\).

Enumerate the nonbase laws as \(P_1,\ldots,P_{m_n}\), put \(P_0=P_{U,\nu_n}^{(n)}\), and abbreviate \(G_a(K)=G_{P_a}(K)\), \(\sigma_a(K)=\sigma_{P_a,K}\), and \(\sigma_0(K)=\sigma_{P_0,K}\).  The packing theorem supplies constants \(a_{\bar\nu},C>0\) and \(\varepsilon_n\to0\) such that
\[
 \max_{a,K}\left|\frac{\sigma_a(K)}{\sigma_0(K)}-1\right|\leq\varepsilon_n,
 \qquad
 \min_{a\ne b}\max_{K\in J_n}\frac{|G_a(K)-G_b(K)|}{\sigma_0(K)}
 \geq a_{\bar\nu}\delta_n,                                             \tag{1}
\]
and
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(P_a^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n})
 \leq Cc_{\mathrm F}^2\log m_n.                                         \tag{2}
\]
Adjoin the declared independent \(V_n\).  Its common law leaves every divergence in (2) unchanged.  The randomized form of \(\texttt{lem:fano-decoder-inequality}\) therefore bounds the average error of every decoder below by
\[
 1-Cc_{\mathrm F}^2-\frac{\log2}{\log m_n}.                              \tag{3}
\]
Choose the fixed \(c_{\mathrm F}\) sufficiently small that (3) is eventually at least \((1+\alpha)/2\).

Given the reported rectangle, select the unique label whose whole gain vector over \(J_n\) lies in it, using label one as a fixed default if there is no unique label.  This decoder is measurable because both the label and coordinate sets are finite.  Put \(c_{0,\bar\nu}=a_{\bar\nu}c_{\mathrm F}/8\) and define
\[
 \mathcal E_{n,a}=\left\{\max_{K\in J_n}
 \frac{\sqrt n\,h_{n,K}}{\sigma_a(K)}
 \geq c_{0,\bar\nu}\sqrt{\log m_n}\right\}.                           \tag{4}
\]
On \(\mathcal E_{n,a}^c\), the variance comparison in (1) and \(\varepsilon_n\to0\) imply, for all large \(n\),
\[
 \max_{K\in J_n}\frac{2h_{n,K}}{\sigma_0(K)}
 <\frac{a_{\bar\nu}}2\delta_n.                                          \tag{5}
\]
Thus the rectangle cannot contain two packing vectors: otherwise rectangularity would make their coordinatewise separation at most \(2h_{n,K}\), contradicting (1).  Coverage and \(\mathcal E_{n,a}^c\) therefore force correct decoding under \(P_a\).  Averaging over labels gives
\[
 \frac1{m_n}\sum_{a=1}^{m_n}P_a^{\otimes n}(\mathcal E_{n,a})
 \geq \frac{1-\alpha}{2}-\eta_n-o(1)
 \geq\frac{1-\alpha}{4}                                                  \tag{6}
\]
eventually, where (3) was used in the first inequality.  At least one summand satisfies (6).  Taking \(c_{1,\bar\nu}=(1-\alpha)/4\) proves the proposition.  Only the full-class coverage premise was restricted to a finite subclass; no upper-band construction or localization argument was used.
