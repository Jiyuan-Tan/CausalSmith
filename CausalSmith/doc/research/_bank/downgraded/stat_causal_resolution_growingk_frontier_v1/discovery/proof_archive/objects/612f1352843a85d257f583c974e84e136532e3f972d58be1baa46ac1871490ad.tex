At the uniform witness with \(\nu_n=0\), exact nuisances, and \(r_n=0\), the frozen score gauge is zero.  Yet a training-fold empirical center is a nondegenerate sample mean.  Conditional on a population-cell count of order \(n/K\), the corresponding oracle-cell center has variance of order \((K^{-2})/(n/K)=1/(nK)\); the empirical quantizer is likewise nonconstant under the continuous uniform sample law.  Thus the fitted nearest-center map, and hence its raw score, differs from the population counterpart with positive probability.  An \(O_P(S^G/\sigma_K)=O_P(0)\) assertion is therefore impossible.  The added \(\delta^C\) and \(Q^C\) terms expose the missing displacement and boundary-switching contributions.  A uniform theorem still needs a noncircular proof that all empirical minimizers enter the local quadratic-growth basins; local curvature and a margin upper bound alone give no uniform global basin gap.
