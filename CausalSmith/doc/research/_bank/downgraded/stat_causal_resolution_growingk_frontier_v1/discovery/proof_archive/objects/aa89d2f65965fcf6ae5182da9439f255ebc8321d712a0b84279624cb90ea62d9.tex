We first check that the frontier condition supplies the growth hypotheses of the two preceding theorems.  At the base law, the exact formulas and the uniform Hessian calculation give
\[
 W_{P_0}(K)\asymp K^{-2},\quad \lambda_{P_0,K}\asymp K^{-3},\quad
 s_{P_0,K}\asymp K^{-1},\quad \sigma_{P_0,K}\asymp K^{-2},
 \quad w_n(P_0)\asymp\sqrt{\log K_n}. \tag{27}
\]
Thus \(\delta^C_{P_0,K,n}\asymp K^2L_n\).  If the validity case in \(Q^C\) were to fail at a diverging resolution, then \(Q^C=1\) and the score term in \(\Xi_n(P_0)\) could not tend to zero.  In the validity case, the uniform margin has \(M=1\) and exponent one, so
\[
 Q^C_{P_0,K,n}\geq c(KL_n)^{1/2}. \tag{28}
\]
Equations (27)--(28) and the two factors of Gaussian width in the score part of \(\Xi_n\) imply
\[
 \Xi_n(P_0)\geq cK_n^{5/2}\log(K_n)L_n^{1/2} \tag{29}
\]
up to replacing \(K_n\) by a fixed fraction of it.  Hence \(\Xi_n(P_0)\to0\) implies \(K_n^5\log^2(K_n)L_n\to0\), which in turn implies both \(K_n^5L_n\to0\) and \(\delta_nK_n^5\to0\), because \(\delta_n\leq C L_n\).  Therefore \(\texttt{thm:fano-membership}\) and \(\texttt{thm:uniform-linearization}\) apply.  An entirely primitive, though conservative, sufficient check for \(\Xi_n\to0\) is
\[
 \frac{\log^7(nK_n)}n\to0,\qquad
 \frac{K_n^3\sqrt{\log K_n}\log(nK_n)}{\sqrt n}\to0,\qquad
 K_n^{5/2}\log K_n\left\{\frac{\log(nK_n)}n\right\}^{1/4}\to0, \tag{30}
\]
as follows by substituting (27)--(28) and the definitions of \(R^G,S^G\) into \(\Xi_n\).

For the upper bound, define the oracle statistic
\[
 T_n^0=\max_{K<K_n}\left|\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n
 \frac{\Delta\phi_{P,K}(O_{n,i})}{\sigma_{P,K}}\right|.
\]
The cited high-dimensional Gaussian and multiplier approximation result applies because the summands are independent, centered by the influence-function definitions, have unit coordinate variances by score nondegeneracy, and satisfy its displayed envelope condition, which is the first term of \(\Xi_n\).  It gives Gaussian approximation of \(T_n^0\) by \(\max_K|Z_{P,K}|\) and multiplier approximation for the oracle empirically centered scores.

The linearization theorem and the remaining terms of \(\Xi_n\) imply
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\left|\frac{\sqrt n\{\widehat G_K-G_P(K)\}}{\widehat\sigma_K}
 -\frac1{\sqrt n}\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{\Delta\phi_{P,K}(O_{n,i})}{\sigma_{P,K}}\right|
 =o_P\{(1+w_n(P))^{-1}\}, \tag{31}
\]
and the analogous conditional replacement of oracle multiplier scores by \(\widehat{\Delta\phi}_{i,K}/\widehat\sigma_K\).  The cited Gaussian anti-concentration inequality shows that a perturbation of size \(o_P\{(1+w_n)^{-1}\}\) changes the Gaussian distribution function by \(o(1)\).  Combining this fact with (31) and the cited oracle approximation gives
\[
 \sup_t\left|P\left(\max_{K<K_n}\frac{\sqrt n|\widehat G_K-G_P(K)|}{\widehat\sigma_K}\leq t\right)
 -P(T_n^{\#}\leq t\mid O_{n,1},\ldots,O_{n,n})\right|=o_P(1) \tag{32}
\]
uniformly over the experiment.  By the quantile-infimum definition, (32) implies simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\alpha-o(1)\).  This is exactly \(\operatorname{Honest}_{\alpha}(B_n^{\#},\mathcal U_n)\).

The Gaussian union bound gives the \((1-\alpha)\)-quantile of \(\max_K|Z_{P,K}|\) at most \(C\sqrt{\log K_n}\).  For the reverse inequality, covariance stability in \(\texttt{thm:fano-membership}\) and the \(0.28\) packing bound imply, eventually, \(R_P(K,L)\leq0.3\) for distinct \(K,L\in J_n\).  Repeating the interpolation comparison in the covariance theorem against \(\sqrt{0.7}\varepsilon_K+\sqrt{0.3}\varepsilon_0\), and using independence plus the Gaussian tail lower bound, shows that its fixed \((1-\alpha)\)-quantile is at least \(c_\alpha\sqrt{\log|J_n|}\).  Hence the Gaussian quantile, the multiplier quantile by (32), and \(w_n(P)\) all have the same order.  Relative variance consistency then proves the asserted standardized half-width formula.

We prove the converse directly.  Write \(m=|J_n|\), \(\theta_j=(G_{P_j}(K):K\in J_n)\), and define the standardized separation metric
\[
 d_\infty(\theta_j,\theta_\ell)=
 \max_{K\in J_n}\frac{|G_{P_j}(K)-G_{P_\ell}(K)|}{\sigma_{P_0,K}}.
\]
The Fano-membership theorem gives \(d_\infty(\theta_j,\theta_\ell)\geq a_0\delta_n\) and
\[
 \frac1m\sum_{j\in J_n}\operatorname{KL}\{P_j^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n}\}
 \leq Cc_{\mathrm F}^2\log m. \tag{33}
\]
Let \(J\) be uniform on \(J_n\), generate the sample from \(P_J^{\otimes n}\), and let \(\widehat J\) be any decoder.  The declared auxiliary variable \(V_n\) may be included in the decoder: it is independent of \(J\) and the sample under every law, so it adds zero mutual information.  If \(Q_j\) denotes the resulting sample-and-randomization law under index \(j\) and \(\overline Q=m^{-1}\sum_jQ_j\), the log-sum inequality gives
\[
 I(J;O^n)=\frac1m\sum_j\operatorname{KL}(Q_j,\overline Q)
 \leq\frac1m\sum_j\operatorname{KL}(Q_j,Q_0). \tag{34}
\]
If \(p_e=P(\widehat J\ne J)\), then the chain rule for entropy and the fact that an error leaves at most \(m-1\) alternatives give
\[
 \log m=H(J)\leq I(J;O^n)+\log2+p_e\log m. \tag{35}
\]
Equations (33)--(35) imply
\[
 \inf_{\widehat J}\frac1m\sum_jQ_j(\widehat J\ne j)
 \geq1-Cc_{\mathrm F}^2-\frac{\log2}{\log m}. \tag{36}
\]
Choose the fixed \(c_{\mathrm F}\) sufficiently small that the right side is eventually larger than \((1+\alpha)/2\).

Now let \(B_n\) be any honest common measurable rectangular band.  Define \(\widehat J\) to be the smallest index \(j\) for which \(\theta_j\in B_n\), using an arbitrary value if there is none.  If \(B_n\) covers \(\theta_j\) and
\[
 \max_{K\in J_n}\frac{h_{n,K}}{\sigma_{P_0,K}}<\frac{a_0\delta_n}{2}, \tag{37}
\]
then the rectangle cannot also contain any \(\theta_\ell\), so \(\widehat J=j\).  Uniform honesty and (36) therefore force, for at least one \(j\),
\[
 P_j^{\otimes n}\left\{\max_{K\in J_n}\frac{h_{n,K}}{\sigma_{P_0,K}}
 \geq\frac{a_0\delta_n}{2}\right\}\geq c_1>0. \tag{38}
\]
The variance comparison in the Fano-membership theorem replaces \(\sigma_{P_0,K}\) by \(\sigma_{P_j,K}\) at a \(1+o(1)\) factor.  Since \(\sqrt n\delta_n=c_{\mathrm F}\sqrt{\log m}\), (38) is the asserted lower bound.  Finally, \(w_n(P)\asymp\sqrt{\log K_n}\) follows from exact covariance packing and covariance stability, proving the matched intrinsic order.
