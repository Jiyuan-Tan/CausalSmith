Set \(\nu_n=0\), suppose \(\underline w\leq1/12\), and let \(K_n=\lfloor n^\varkappa\rfloor\) for a fixed \(0<\varkappa<1/10\).  Use the fixed-fold construction in \(\texttt{def:corrected-profile-estimator}\) with the common known nuisance functions \(e(u)=1/2\), \(\mu_1(u)=u-1/2\), and \(\mu_0(u)=1/2-u\), so that \(r_n=0\).  Uniformly over \(P^{(n)}\in\mathcal U_n\), every selected empirical codebook enters its population quadratic-growth basin and
\[
 \max_{1\leq v\leq V}\max_{K\leq K_n}\|\widehat C_{v,K}-C_{P^{(n)},K}\|_2
 =O_{P^{(n)}}\!\left(\max_{K\leq K_n}\delta^C_{P^{(n)},K,n}\right).
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\frac{|\widehat G_K-G_{P^{(n)}}(K)-(P_{\mathrm{emp},n}-P^{(n)})\Delta\phi_{P^{(n)},K}|}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}
 =O_{P^{(n)}}\!\left(\max_{K<K_n}\frac{R^G_{P^{(n)},K,n}}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}\right),
\]
and
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\left\|
 \frac{\widehat{\Delta\phi}_{K}}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}-
 \frac{\Delta\phi_{P^{(n)},K}}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}
 \right\|_{L_2(P^{(n)})}
 =O_{P^{(n)}}\!\left(\max_{K<K_n}\frac{S^G_{P^{(n)},K,n}}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}\right).
\]
In this explicit regime \(\sup_{P^{(n)}\in\mathcal U_n}\Xi_n(P^{(n)})\to0\).  Consequently,
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\frac{\sqrt n\,|\widehat G_K-G_{P^{(n)}}(K)-(P_{\mathrm{emp},n}-P^{(n)})\Delta\phi_{P^{(n)},K}|}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}
 =o_{P^{(n)}}\!\left(\{1+w_n(P^{(n)})\}^{-1}\right),
\]
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\left|\frac{\widehat\sigma_K}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}-1\right|=o_{P^{(n)}}(1),
\]
and \(\min_{K<K_n}\widehat\sigma_K>0\) with probability tending to one, all uniformly over \(\mathcal U_n\).  This is a known-nuisance, noiseless local-experiment result and makes no positive-noise or infinite-class upper claim.
