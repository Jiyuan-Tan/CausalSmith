Set \(\nu_n=0\), suppose \(\underline w\leq1/12\), and let \(K_n=\lfloor n^\varkappa\rfloor\) for a fixed \(0<\varkappa<1/10\).  Use the known nuisances and fixed-fold estimator specified in \(\texttt{thm:uniform-linearization}\).  Then the band in \(\texttt{def:intrinsic-bootstrap-band}\) satisfies \(\operatorname{Honest}_{\alpha}(B_n^{\#},\mathcal U_n)\).  Uniformly over \(P^{(n)}\in\mathcal U_n\),
\[
 \mathbb P_{P^{(n)}}^n\{\min_{K<K_n}\widehat\sigma_K>0\}\longrightarrow1,
 \qquad
 q_{n,1-\alpha}^{\#}\asymp_{P^{(n)}}w_n(P^{(n)})\asymp\sqrt{\log K_n},
\]
and on that event
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\frac{q_{n,1-\alpha}^{\#}\widehat\sigma_K}{\sqrt n\,\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}
 \asymp_{P^{(n)}}\frac{w_n(P^{(n)})}{\sqrt n}
 \asymp\sqrt{\frac{\log K_n}{n}}.
\]
Conversely, for every sequence of common \(P^{(n)}\)-independent measurable rectangular bands satisfying \(\operatorname{Honest}_{\alpha}(B_n,\mathcal U_n)\), there are constants \(c_0,c_1>0\), depending at most on \(\alpha\) and \(c_{\mathrm F}\), such that for all sufficiently large \(n\), some \(P^{(n)}\in\mathcal U_n\) satisfies
\[
 \mathbb P_{P^{(n)}}^n\left\{\max_{K\in J_n}
 \frac{\sqrt n\,h_{n,K}}{\sigma_{P^{(n)},K}}
 \geq c_0\sqrt{\log|J_n|}\right\}\geq c_1.
\]
Thus the feasible upper radius and the necessary rectangular-band radius match on this explicit known-nuisance, noiseless local experiment.  The theorem does not assert a positive-noise or infinite-primitive-class upper bound; the established positive-noise results remain converse-only.
