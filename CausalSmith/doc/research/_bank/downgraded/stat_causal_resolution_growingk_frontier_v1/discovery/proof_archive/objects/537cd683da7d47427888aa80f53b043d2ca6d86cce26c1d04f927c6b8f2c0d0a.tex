Fix an integer \(V\geq3\).  For each \(n\), fix deterministic evaluation folds \(I_1,\ldots,I_V\) that partition \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\), and, for every \(v\), fix disjoint nuisance-training and codebook-training sets \(N_v,Q_v\subseteq I_v^c\).  Assume that all nonempty sets \(I_v,N_v,Q_v\) have cardinality between two fixed positive multiples of \(n\).  On \(N_v\), fit \(\widehat e_v,\widehat\mu_{0,v},\widehat\mu_{1,v}\), put
\[
 \widehat e_v^{\mathrm{cl}}(x)=
 \min\{1-\epsilon/2,\max(\epsilon/2,\widehat e_v(x))\},
 \qquad
 \widehat b_v(x)=\{\widehat\mu_{1,v}(x)-\widehat\mu_{0,v}(x)+1\}/2,
\]
and define
\[
 \widehat\zeta_v(o)=\frac12\left[
 \frac{a\{y-\widehat\mu_{1,v}(x)\}}{\widehat e_v^{\mathrm{cl}}(x)}
 -\frac{(1-a)\{y-\widehat\mu_{0,v}(x)\}}{1-\widehat e_v^{\mathrm{cl}}(x)}
 \right].
\]
For \(C\in\mathcal C_K\), let \(q_C(u)\) be its smallest nearest center and set
\[
 m_C(o;\widehat\eta_v)=
 \{\widehat b_v(x)-q_C(\widehat b_v(x))\}^2
 +2\{\widehat b_v(x)-q_C(\widehat b_v(x))\}\widehat\zeta_v(o).
\]
To make minimization a total measurable operation despite the jumps of the corrected criterion at empirical Voronoi ties, define the finite ordered grid
\[
 \mathcal C_{K,n}^{\mathrm{grid}}=
 \mathcal C_K\cap\{0,n^{-2},2n^{-2},\ldots,1\}^K.
\]
The fold-specific codebook is the lexicographically smallest ordered element
\[
 \widehat C_{v,K}\in
 \mathop{\operatorname{argmin}}_{C\in\mathcal C_{K,n}^{\mathrm{grid}}}
 |Q_v|^{-1}\sum_{i\in Q_v}m_C(O_{n,i};\widehat\eta_v).
\]
The grid is finite and nonempty, so the argmin is nonempty and the deterministic tie rule is measurable.  If \(v(i)\) is the unique fold containing \(i\), define
\[
 \widehat m_{i,K}=m_{\widehat C_{v(i),K}}(O_{n,i};\widehat\eta_{v(i)}),
 \qquad
 \widehat W_K=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\widehat m_{i,K},
 \qquad
 \widehat d_1=\max\{\widehat W_1,n^{-1}\}.
\]
Put
\[
 \widehat\rho(K)=1-\widehat W_K/\widehat d_1,
 \qquad
 \widehat G_K=\widehat\rho(K+1)-\widehat\rho(K),
 \qquad
 \widehat\phi_{W,i,K}=\widehat m_{i,K}-\widehat W_K,
\]
and define the raw fitted adjacent score by
\[
 \widetilde\psi_{i,K}=
 \frac{\widehat\phi_{W,i,K}-\widehat\phi_{W,i,K+1}}{\widehat d_1}
 -\frac{(\widehat W_K-\widehat W_{K+1})\widehat\phi_{W,i,1}}
 {\widehat d_1^2}.
\]
Finally set \(\overline\psi_K=n^{-1}\sum_i\widetilde\psi_{i,K}\), \(\widehat{\Delta\phi}_{i,K}=\widetilde\psi_{i,K}-\overline\psi_K\), and \(\widehat\sigma_K^2=n^{-1}\sum_i\widehat{\Delta\phi}_{i,K}^2\).  Conditional on the data, draw the declared independent Gaussian multipliers and compute \(T_n^{\#}\), \(q_{n,1-\alpha}^{\#}\), and the intervals \(I_{n,K}\) exactly as in the symbol table.  Thus nuisance fitting, codebook fitting, score evaluation, empirical centering, studentization, and multiplier calibration are all explicit parts of one data-measurable map.
