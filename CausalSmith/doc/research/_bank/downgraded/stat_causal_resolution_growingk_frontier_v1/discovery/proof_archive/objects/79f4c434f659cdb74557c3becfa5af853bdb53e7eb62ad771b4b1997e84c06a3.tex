Let \(m_n=|J_n|\).  Since \(K_n\to\infty\), one has \(m_n\to\infty\) and hence \(m_n\geq2\) eventually.  The sharpened \(\texttt{thm:fano-membership}\), invoked under \(\nu_n=0\), \(\underline w\leq1/12\), and \(\delta_nK_n^3\to0\), gives
\[
 \mathcal U_n\subseteq\mathcal P_n^{\mathrm{CQ}}                                              \tag{1}
\]
for every sufficiently large \(n\).  Restricting the infimum in the displayed full-class coverage hypothesis to the subset (1) yields
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal U_n}P^n\!\left\{G_P(K)\in[L_{n,K},U_{n,K}]
 \text{ for every }K<K_n\right\}\geq1-\alpha-\eta_n.                                  \tag{2}
\]
The probabilities in (2) integrate the independent auxiliary variable \(V_n\), as required by \(\texttt{def:rectangular-band-procedure}\).

Index \(J_n\) as \(\{j_1,\ldots,j_{m_n}\}\), put \(P_a=P_{j_a}^{(n)}\), and write \(P_0=P_{U,0}^{(n)}\), \(G_a=G_{P_a}\), \(\sigma_a(K)=\sigma_{P_a,K}\), and \(\sigma_0(K)=\sigma_{P_0,K}\).  The variance stability, pairwise separation, and divergence conclusions of \(\texttt{thm:fano-membership}\) give constants \(a_0,C_s,C_{\rm KL}>0\) such that
\[
 \varepsilon_n=C_s\delta_nK_n^2=o(1),\qquad
 \max_{a\leq m_n}\max_{K<K_n}\left|\frac{\sigma_a(K)}{\sigma_0(K)}-1\right|
 \leq\varepsilon_n,                                                                     \tag{3}
\]
\[
 \min_{a\ne b}\max_{K\in J_n}\frac{|G_a(K)-G_b(K)|}{\sigma_0(K)}\geq a_0\delta_n,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{KL}(P_a^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n})
 \leq C_{\rm KL}c_{\mathrm F}^2\log m_n.                                               \tag{4}
\]
All denominators are positive by the same membership theorem.

Let \(Q_a=P_a^{\otimes n}\otimes\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]\) and \(Q_0=P_0^{\otimes n}\otimes\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]\), including the declared band randomization.  Common support gives \(Q_a\ll Q_0\), and the common independent tensor factor gives
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(Q_a,Q_0)=\operatorname{KL}(P_a^{\otimes n},P_0^{\otimes n}).          \tag{5}
\]
Applying \(\texttt{lem:fano-decoder-inequality}\) to any decoder \(\widehat J\) yields
\[
 \frac1{m_n}\sum_{a=1}^{m_n}Q_a(\widehat J\ne a)
 \geq1-C_{\rm KL}c_{\mathrm F}^2-\frac{\log2}{\log m_n}.                              \tag{6}
\]
Choose the fixed \(c_{\mathrm F}\) sufficiently small that the right side of (6) is eventually at least \((1+\alpha)/2\), as permitted by its definition.

Construct a measurable decoder from the reported rectangle by selecting the unique \(a\), if one exists, satisfying
\[
 G_a(K)\in[L_{n,K},U_{n,K}]\quad\text{for every }K\in J_n,                              \tag{7}
\]
and using label one otherwise.  Finiteness of the label and coordinate sets and measurability of the band endpoints justify this decoder.  Put \(c_0=a_0c_{\mathrm F}/8\), and under \(Q_a\) define
\[
 \mathcal E_{n,a}=\left\{\max_{K\in J_n}
 \frac{\sqrt n\,h_{n,K}}{\sigma_a(K)}\geq c_0\sqrt{\log m_n}\right\}.                \tag{8}
\]
For large \(n\), (3) gives \(\sigma_a(K)/\sigma_0(K)\leq2\).  On \(\mathcal E_{n,a}^c\),
\[
 \max_{K\in J_n}\frac{2h_{n,K}}{\sigma_0(K)}
 <4c_0\sqrt{\frac{\log m_n}{n}}=\frac{a_0}{2}\delta_n<a_0\delta_n.                    \tag{9}
\]
If both labels \(a\) and \(b\ne a\) satisfied (7), rectangularity would imply \(|G_a(K)-G_b(K)|\leq2h_{n,K}\) for every \(K\in J_n\), contradicting (4) and (9).  Thus coverage of \(G_a\) together with \(\mathcal E_{n,a}^c\) forces correct decoding.  By (2),
\[
 Q_a(\widehat J\ne a)\leq\alpha+\eta_n+Q_a(\mathcal E_{n,a}).                           \tag{10}
\]
Average (10), compare with (6), and use \(\eta_n\to0\) to obtain
\[
 \frac1{m_n}\sum_{a=1}^{m_n}Q_a(\mathcal E_{n,a})\geq\frac{1-\alpha}{4}=:c_1>0          \tag{11}
\]
eventually.  Some summand is at least \(c_1\), which is exactly the asserted event under its corresponding law in \(\mathcal U_n\).  This argument only restricts a full-class honesty premise to a subclass and proves necessity; it supplies no full-class upper band.

For the directed strict-regime check, take
\[
 K_n=\left\lfloor\frac{n^{1/6}}{(\log n)^a}\right\rfloor,qquad a>1/6.
\]
Then \(m_n\asymp K_n\), \(\delta_n\asymp\sqrt{\log n/n}\), and
\[
 \delta_nK_n^3\asymp(\log n)^{1/2-3a}\to0.                                              \tag{12}
\]
By contrast,
\[
 K_n^5L_n\asymp n^{1/3}(\log n)^{1/2-5a}\not\to0                                      \tag{13}
\]
for every fixed \(a\).  Thus (12) verifies a nonvacuous converse regime strictly beyond the displayed empirical-basin condition for the feasible upper theorem; (13) prevents any accidental upper-side claim at this scale.
