Fix \(P=P_j^{(n)}\in\mathcal U_n\), including \(j=0\).  The sharpened \(\texttt{thm:fano-membership}\), invoked under the stated condition \(\delta_nK_n^3\to0\), and \(\texttt{prop:uniform-witness-membership}\) give, uniformly in \(P\) and \(K\leq K_n\),
\[
 cK^{-2}\leq W_P(K)\leq CK^{-2},\qquad
 \lambda_{P,K}\geq cK^{-3},\qquad s_{P,K}\asymp K^{-1},
 \qquad \sigma_{P,K}\asymp K^{-2}.                                                       \tag{1}
\]
They also give a density bounded above and below by fixed positive constants, a validity radius \(c/K\), and \(W_P(1)\geq1/12\).  Thus every division below is justified.  Because \(\nu_n=0\) and the common nuisance functions are inserted exactly, \(\zeta_P=0\), all nuisance errors vanish, and the corrected validation-fold criterion is the empirical squared quantization loss.

We first prove global basin entry.  For every ordered codebook \(C\), the loss \(u\mapsto\ell_C(u)=\min_{c\in C}(u-c)^2\) is continuous, piecewise absolutely continuous, and has derivative bounded by two.  Integration by parts therefore gives, for a training empirical distribution function \(F_N\),
\[
 \sup_{C\in\mathcal C_K}|(P_N-P)\ell_C|
 \leq2\|F_N-F_P\|_\infty.                                                              \tag{2}
\]
To bound the right side without an unmentioned empirical-process premise, choose population quantiles with distribution values \(r/N\), \(0\leq r\leq N\).  Monotonicity bounds the error between consecutive quantiles by the larger endpoint error plus \(N^{-1}\).  The exponential Markov bound for each centered Bernoulli sum and a union bound over these \(N+1\) grid points give
\[
 P\{\|F_N-F_P\|_\infty>x+N^{-1}\}\leq2(N+1)e^{-2Nx^2}.                               \tag{3}
\]
Every training size is at least a fixed multiple of \(n\), and the number of folds is fixed.  Taking \(x=CL_n\) in (2)--(3) gives a simultaneous \(O_P(L_n)\) criterion error.

The completion-of-the-square argument in the proof of \(\texttt{thm:fano-membership}\), transferred by the uniform upper and lower density bounds, gives a population gap \(cK^{-5}\) outside the radius-\(c/K\) basin.  Since \(K_n^5L_n\to0\), (2)--(3) force every empirical minimizer into that basin uniformly over folds, resolutions, and laws.  This step uses the \(K_n^5L_n\) hypothesis; the weaker \(\delta_nK_n^3\) hypothesis is used only for population membership.

Let \(C^\star=C_{P,K}\) and \(d(C)=\|C-C^\star\|_2\).  At \(C^\star\), the one-observation gradient has one nonzero coordinate, equal to \(2(c_r^\star-U)\) on cell \(r\).  The disjoint cells imply, for every \(v\in\mathbb R^K\),
\[
 E_P\!\left[\left\{\left.\frac{d}{dt}\ell_{C^\star+tv}(U)\right|_{t=0}\right\}^2\right]
 \leq CW_P(K)\|v\|_2^2.                                                                \tag{4}
\]
For clarity, the dimension-free local fluctuation follows directly from this disjointness.  If \(g_i\) is the gradient vector and \(\varepsilon_i\) are independent Rademacher signs, then
\[
 E\left\|N^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N\varepsilon_i g_i\right\|_2^2
 =N^{-2}\sum_{i=1}^NE\|g_i\|_2^2\leq CW_P(K)/N.                                      \tag{5}
\]
The exponential Markov inequality applied after revealing the observations one at a time supplies the corresponding \(\sqrt{x/N}\) tail.  Moving a codebook within the basin changes a Voronoi assignment only near a boundary; adjacent squared losses agree at that boundary, so the switching contribution is quadratic.  A dyadic decomposition in \(d(C)\), followed by a union bound over \(K\leq K_n\) and the fixed folds, consequently yields, with probability at least \(1-2e^{-x}\),
\[
 \sup_{d(C)\leq r}|(P_N-P)(\ell_C-\ell_{C^\star})|
 \leq C\left\{r\sqrt{\frac{W_P(K)\{x+\log(nK_n)\}}{n}}
 +\frac{r^2\{x+\log(nK_n)\}}{n}\right\}.                                             \tag{6}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) show explicitly why no factor \(K^{1/2}\) is hidden in (6).

Population quadratic growth, empirical optimality, and (6) imply
\[
 \lambda_{P,K}d(\widehat C_K)^2
 \leq |(P_N-P)(\ell_{\widehat C_K}-\ell_{C^\star})|.
\]
Absorb the quadratic term using \(K_n^5L_n\to0\), and divide by the positive \(\lambda_{P,K}\), to obtain
\[
 d(\widehat C_K)
 =O_P\!\left[\lambda_{P,K}^{-1}
 \left\{\frac{W_P(K)\log(nK_n)}{n}\right\}^{1/2}\right]
 =O_P(\delta^C_{P,K,n})                                                                 \tag{7}
\]
simultaneously.  Substitution of (7) back into (6), and averaging over the fixed folds, gives
\[
 \left|\widehat W_K-W_P(K)-(P_{\mathrm{emp},n}-P)\phi_{W,P,K}\right|
 =O_P\!\left\{\frac{W_P(K)\log(nK_n)}{n\lambda_{P,K}}\right\}
 =O_P(R_{P,K,n}).                                                                        \tag{8}
\]

Write \(\widehat W_a=W_P(a)+Z_a+e_a\), where
\(Z_a=(P_{\mathrm{emp},n}-P)\phi_{W,P,a}\), \(|e_a|=O_P(R_{P,a,n})\), and boundedness gives \(\max_a|Z_a|=O_P(L_n)\).  Since \(W_P(1)\geq1/12\) and \(|Z_1+e_1|=o_P(1)\), the exact algebraic identity
\[
 \frac{x+u}{y+v}-\frac{x}{y}-\frac{u}{y}+\frac{xv}{y^2}
 =-\frac{uv}{y(y+v)}+\frac{xv^2}{y^2(y+v)}                                               \tag{9}
\]
is valid with \(y=W_P(1)\).  Apply (9) at resolutions \(K\) and \(K+1\).  The linear terms combine to \((P_{\mathrm{emp},n}-P)\Delta\phi_{P,K}\); the four remainder types are, respectively, bounded by the four terms in the definition of \(R^G_{P,K,n}\).  Hence
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\frac{|\widehat G_K-G_P(K)-(P_{\mathrm{emp},n}-P)\Delta\phi_{P,K}|}
 {\sigma_{P,K}}
 =O_P\!\left(\max_{K<K_n}\frac{R^G_{P,K,n}}{\sigma_{P,K}}\right).                     \tag{10}
\]

We next compare fitted and oracle scores.  On (7), a point changes its nearest center only if it lies within \(2\delta^C_{P,K,n}\) of a population boundary.  Off that set the residual-map discrepancy is at most \(\delta^C_{P,K,n}\); on it both residuals are bounded by \(2s_{P,K}\).  The margin assumption therefore gives
\[
 \|\{U-q_{\widehat C_K}(U)\}-\{U-q_{P,K}(U)\}\|_{L_2(P)}^2
 \leq(\delta^C_{P,K,n})^2+16M_{P,K}s_{P,K}^2
 \left(\frac{\delta^C_{P,K,n}}{s_{P,K}}\right)^{\alpha_{P,K}}                         \tag{11}
\]
when the validity condition holds; the unit bound applies otherwise.  By definition, both cases are bounded by \((Q^C_{P,K,n})^2\).  Insert (11) into the ratio influence-function formula and use the triangle inequality.  This produces exactly the four codebook terms in \(S^G_{P,K,n}\); the nuisance terms are zero.  Centering by \(\overline\psi_K\) cannot increase the order because its fitted-minus-oracle part is bounded by the same \(L_2\) discrepancy and its oracle part is an empirical mean.  Thus
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\left\|
 \frac{\widehat{\Delta\phi}_{K}}{\sigma_{P,K}}
 -\frac{\Delta\phi_{P,K}}{\sigma_{P,K}}\right\|_{L_2(P)}
 =O_P\!\left(\max_{K<K_n}\frac{S^G_{P,K,n}}{\sigma_{P,K}}\right).                     \tag{12}
\]

Finally, the loss representation, bounded cell radii, and (1) imply
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\|\Delta\phi_{P,K}/\sigma_{P,K}\|_\infty\leq C.                         \tag{13}
\]
Applying the exponential Markov inequality to the bounded squared standardized oracle scores and taking a union bound gives
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}|P_{\mathrm{emp},n}(\Delta\phi_{P,K}/\sigma_{P,K})^2-1|
 =O_P\!\left\{\sqrt{\log(nK_n)/n}\right\}=o_P(1).                                    \tag{14}
\]
For any two score arrays, the difference of their empirical second moments is bounded by the empirical \(L_2\) norm of their difference times the sum of their empirical \(L_2\) norms.  Equations (12)--(14) and the nonnegative score-replacement term in \(\Xi_n(P)\) therefore prove
\[
 \max_{K<K_n}\left|\widehat\sigma_K/\sigma_{P,K}-1\right|=o_P(1).                    \tag{15}
\]
Score nondegeneracy in (1) and (15) imply \(\min_{K<K_n}\widehat\sigma_K>0\) with probability tending to one uniformly.  The two remaining nonnegative terms in \(\Xi_n(P)\) are precisely \((1+w_n)\sqrt nR^G/\sigma\) and \((1+w_n)w_nS^G/\sigma\); (10), (12), and (15) therefore make reoptimization, score replacement, and studentization negligible on the Gaussian anti-concentration scale \((1+w_n)^{-1}\).  This proves every asserted conclusion while retaining the original \(K_n^5L_n\) and \(\Xi_n\) upper-side conditions.
