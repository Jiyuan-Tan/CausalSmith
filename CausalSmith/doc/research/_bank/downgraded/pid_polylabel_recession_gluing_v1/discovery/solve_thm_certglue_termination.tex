\textbf{Technique: effective dovetailing of sound primal and dual semidecisions.}

The extra premise in the theorem is used conditionally; it is not derived from the standing assumptions.  Write \(\mathsf{ECD}(\mathfrak G)\) for the affirmative conclusion specified in \(\text{oeq:effective-compatible-density}\), or for a separately proved package having exactly the same consequences.  Thus there are a computable sequence \(\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)\in\mathbb Q_+\) with \(\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)\downarrow0\), enumerable finite records \(Q_j(K)\in\mathfrak F_{\mathfrak G,j}\), and verifiable outward ledgers such that, for every \(K\in\mathcal C_\eta\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 c\{Q_j(K)\}&\ge 1-\eta-\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j),\\
 r\{Q_j(K)\}&\le R_r(K)+D\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j),\\
 \rho_{\mathrm{led}}\{Q_j(K)\}&\le \Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j).
 \end{split}
 \tag{1}
\]
Here
\[
 c(Q)=\inf_{v\in V}\inf_{h\in D_v}P_h\{v\in Q(W)\},
 \qquad
 r(Q)=\sup_{h\in H_r}E_h\ell\{Q(W)\}.
 \tag{2}
\]
The five simultaneous geometric obligations in that affirmative conclusion ensure that (1) is a single compatible record: the chart, collar, path, translated-tail, weight-variation, integration, covariance, rounding, and repair charges all occur in the ledger of \(\text{def:weighted-error-ledger}\).  This is the only use of \(\mathsf{ECD}(\mathfrak G)\).

At stage \(s\), the search in \(\text{def:certglue-handle}\) enumerates all indices \(j\le s\), all codes of length at most \(s\) for members of \(\mathfrak F_{\mathfrak G,j}\), all rational incidence forests and semantic projections, all rational action probabilities, and the first \(s\) choices of integration and rounding precision.  Each candidate is accepted only after exact rational or real-algebraic geometry checks and outward Gaussian integration give rational numbers
\[
 c^-_{Q,s}\le c(Q),
 \qquad
 r(Q)\le r^+_{Q,s}.
 \tag{3}
\]
The protected-neighborhood integrals, their complements, and every path load are precisely those declared in \(\text{def:weighted-error-ledger}\).  Consequently (3) is sound even on equality faces and unbounded charts; no floating-point sign is used.

Replace \(c^-_{Q,s}\) by its intersection with \([0,1]\), and define
\[
 \rho_{Q,s}=\max\{0,1-\eta-c^-_{Q,s}\},
 \qquad
 q_{Q,s}=\frac{\rho_{Q,s}}{\eta+\rho_{Q,s}}.
 \tag{4}
\]
Because \(0<\eta<1/2\) and \(0\le c^-_{Q,s}\le1\),
\[
 0\le\rho_{Q,s}\le1-\eta<1,
 \qquad \eta+\rho_{Q,s}>0,
 \tag{5}
\]
so the division in (4) is valid.  Moreover, (3)--(4) give \(c(Q)\ge1-\eta-\rho_{Q,s}\).  Apply \(\text{lem:exact-coverage-repair}\) and let \(Q^+=\operatorname{Repair}_{\eta,\rho_{Q,s}}(Q)\).  Then \(Q^+\in\mathcal C_\eta\) and
\[
 M_{\mathfrak G,\eta}(r)
 \le R_r(Q^+)
 \le r^+_{Q,s}+\frac{D\rho_{Q,s}}{\eta+\rho_{Q,s}}.
 \tag{6}
\]
Indeed, the coverage identity underlying the repair is
\[
 \left(1-q_{Q,s}\right)(1-\eta-\rho_{Q,s})+q_{Q,s}=1-\eta,
 \tag{7}
\]
and \(0\le\ell\le D\) gives the risk increment in (6).  Outward rational arithmetic therefore returns a rational \(U_{Q,s}\) no smaller than the last member of (6).

In parallel, enumerate the finite nonnegative atomic dual records supplied by \(\text{thm:attainment-atomic-duality}\).  Their rational weights and rational real-algebraic support encodings permit exact feasibility checks against the finite rational-polyhedral unions \(D_v\), the singleton-label condition, and the quadratic distance inequalities defining \(H_r\).  Refining the outward Gaussian enclosures gives a rational lower value \(l^-_{\nu,s}\) for every processed feasible dual record \(\nu\).  By \(\text{thm:attainment-atomic-duality}\), each such value is a valid lower certificate.  Set
\[
 L_s=\max_{\nu\ \mathrm{processed\ by}\ s}l^-_{\nu,s},
 \qquad
 U_s=\min_{Q\ \mathrm{accepted\ by}\ s}U_{Q,s},
 \tag{8}
\]
with an endpoint left undefined until its finite indexing set is nonempty.  Weak duality and (6) imply
\[
 L_s\le M_{\mathfrak G,\eta}(r)\le U_s
 \tag{9}
\]
whenever both endpoints are defined.

Every fixed stage terminates.  There are only finitely many codes at that stage.  Rational-polyhedral incidence, linear programming, and real-algebraic sign tests terminate.  On a rational box the Gaussian density and each accepted piecewise-rational integrand have computable uniform enclosures away from their finitely described boundaries, while boundary collars have explicitly charged Gaussian mass.  Recursive subdivision therefore drives the box error to zero.  Positive definiteness of \(\Sigma\), from \(\text{ass:rational-positive-covariance}\), supplies an explicit Gaussian tail bound tending to zero as the box expands.  Hence each requested positive enclosure width is reached after finitely many subdivisions.

It remains to prove that the rational stopping test is eventually satisfied.  By \(\text{thm:attainment-atomic-duality}\), there is \(K^*\in\mathcal C_\eta\) with
\[
 R_r(K^*)=M_{\mathfrak G,\eta}(r).
 \tag{10}
\]
Apply (1) to \(K^*\).  Given a rational \(\zeta>0\), sufficiently fine evaluation of the resulting finite record yields
\[
 c^-_{Q_j(K^*),s}\ge c\{Q_j(K^*)\}-\zeta,
 \qquad
 r^+_{Q_j(K^*),s}\le r\{Q_j(K^*)\}+\zeta.
 \tag{11}
\]
Equations (1), (4), and (11) imply
\[
 \rho_{Q_j(K^*),s}\le\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)+\zeta.
 \tag{12}
\]
Using (1), (6), and (10)--(12), and charging at most one further \(\zeta\) for final outward rounding, gives
\[
 U_s\le M_{\mathfrak G,\eta}(r)
 +D\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)+2\zeta
 +\frac{D\{\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)+\zeta\}}
 {\eta+\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)+\zeta}.
 \tag{13}
\]
The denominator is positive because \(\eta>0\).  First let \(j\to\infty\), and then let \(\zeta\downarrow0\).  The right side of (13) tends to \(M_{\mathfrak G,\eta}(r)\).  The final assertion of \(\text{thm:attainment-atomic-duality}\) supplies an enumerated sequence of finite atomic lower certificates converging upward to the same value.  Thus
\[
 L_s\uparrow M_{\mathfrak G,\eta}(r),
 \qquad
 \limsup_{s\to\infty}U_s\le M_{\mathfrak G,\eta}(r).
 \tag{14}
\]
Together with (9), this proves that some finite stage has \(U_s-L_s\le\varepsilon\).

Finally, expose the promised total recurrences.  For \(i\in\{\mathrm{glue},\mathrm{cut},\mathrm{Gauss},\mathrm{LP}\}\), let \(a_i(\mathfrak G,s)\) be the number of operations of type \(i\) performed at stage \(s\), and let \(b(\mathfrak G,s)\) be the largest bit length appearing there.  Define
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 C_i(0)&=a_i(\mathfrak G,0),&
 C_i(s+1)&=C_i(s)+a_i(\mathfrak G,s+1),\\
 B(0)&=b(\mathfrak G,0),&
 B(s+1)&=\max\{B(s),b(\mathfrak G,s+1)\}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{15}
\]
Let
\[
 s_*(\mathfrak G,\varepsilon)
 =\min\{s:L_s,U_s\text{ are defined and }U_s-L_s\le\varepsilon\}.
 \tag{16}
\]
The predicate in (16) is a rational decidable predicate, and (14) proves that its sequential search halts.  Hence \(s_*\) is total recursive under the displayed conditional premise.  Set
\[
 N_i(\mathfrak G,\varepsilon)=C_i\{s_*(\mathfrak G,\varepsilon)\},
 \quad i\in\{\mathrm{glue},\mathrm{cut},\mathrm{Gauss},\mathrm{LP}\},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Bit}(\mathfrak G,\varepsilon)=B\{s_*(\mathfrak G,\varepsilon)\}.
 \tag{17}
\]
Archive at stage \(s_*\) the repaired primal attaining the finite minimum in (8), the atomic dual attaining its finite maximum, every outward enclosure, and the recurrences (15)--(17).  Equations (9), (16), and (17) give the asserted endpoints, exact global honesty, termination, and five-part receipt.  All of these conclusions remain conditional on \(\mathsf{ECD}(\mathfrak G)\).

\textbf{Technique: parameter-independent Le Cam transfer, affine scoring, Gaussian Hellinger comparison, and finite-arrangement stability.}

Put \(D_0=\operatorname{diag}(p_0)\), \(I_0=B'D_0^{-1}B\), and \(\Sigma_0=I_0^{-1}\).  The inverse exists.  Indeed, \(\min_j(p_0)_j\ge\kappa>0\) by \(\text{ass:multinomial-interior}\), and \(B\) has column rank \(d\), so for every \(z\ne0\),
\[
 z'I_0z=\sum_{j\in\mathcal J}\frac{\{(Bz)_j\}^2}{(p_0)_j}>0.
 \tag{18}
\]
Thus \(I_0\succ0\) and \(\Sigma_0\succ0\).

By \(\text{prop:carter-fixed-m-positive-cell}\), the bounded-ratio condition holds uniformly on \(C\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), and Carter's parameter-independent kernel sends the multinomial count experiment to its matched normal count experiment with total-variation error at most
\[
 \varepsilon_{\mathrm{Car},n}
 =C_{2/\kappa}\frac{m\log m}{\sqrt n}.
 \tag{19}
\]
For a matched normal count vector \(Y_n\), apply the parameter-independent affine score
\[
 S_n(Y_n)=\Sigma_0B'D_0^{-1}\frac{Y_n-np_0}{\sqrt n}.
 \tag{20}
\]
At local parameter \(u\), this score is normal with mean and covariance
\[
 \mu_n(u)=\Sigma_0B'D_0^{-1}\sqrt n\{p_n(u)-p_0\},
 \tag{21}
\]
\[
 V_n(u)=\Sigma_0B'D_0^{-1}
 [\operatorname{diag}\{p_n(u)\}-p_n(u)p_n(u)']
 D_0^{-1}B\Sigma_0.
 \tag{22}
\]
Since \(\Sigma_0B'D_0^{-1}B=I_d\),
\[
 \sup_{u\in C}\|\mu_n(u)-u\|_2
 \le \|\Sigma_0B'D_0^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}d_n(C).
 \tag{23}
\]
The local expansion gives \(d_n(C)\to0\), while \(\text{lem:compact-moving-remainder-covariance}\), continuity of \(p\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(p)-pp'\), and compactness give
\[
 \sup_{u\in C}\|V_n(u)-\Sigma_0\|_{\mathrm{op}}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{24}
\]

For positive definite \(V\), define the explicit squared Hellinger distance
\[
 \mathsf H^2(\mu,V;u,\Sigma_0)
 =2-2\frac{|V|^{1/4}|\Sigma_0|^{1/4}}
 {|(V+\Sigma_0)/2|^{1/2}}
 \exp\!\left[-\frac18(\mu-u)'
 \{(V+\Sigma_0)/2\}^{-1}(\mu-u)\right].
 \tag{25}
\]
Completing the square in the product of the two Gaussian densities gives (25).  If \(p\) and \(q\) are any two densities, Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 \frac12\int|p-q|
 =\frac12\int|\sqrt p-\sqrt q|(\sqrt p+\sqrt q)
 \le\left\{\int(\sqrt p-\sqrt q)^2\right\}^{1/2}.
\]
Thus total variation is at most the Hellinger distance in the normalization (25), and we obtain the outward-computable bound
\[
 \operatorname{Err}_{\Sigma,n}(C)
 =\sup_{u\in C}\min\{1,
 \mathsf H(\mu_n(u),V_n(u);u,\Sigma_0)\}.
 \tag{26}
\]
Equations (23)--(24), eventual uniform positive definiteness, and continuity of (25) on the compact parameter set imply
\[
 \operatorname{Err}_{\Sigma,n}(C)\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{27}
\]

Let \(x_n(C)=d_n(C)+\delta_n(C)\).  Apply \(\text{lem:finite-completed-arrangement-stability}\) to the fixed finite completed arrangement and enlarge its finite rational active-set constant, if necessary, to absorb the fixed norm conversions and the equal-covariance Gaussian translation bound.  Then all chart and semantic correspondences on \(C\), including the directed separation-distance comparison for rival semantic unions, contribute at most
\[
 H_Cx_n(C).
 \tag{28}
\]
Equality faces cause no loss because the completed convention assigns every incident label to the face.  The assumed Gaussian-null boundaries of the finite representation allow boundary equalities to be changed without changing any probability.

It remains to charge observations for which an active affine sign can change.  For \(j\in\mathcal J_0^{\mathrm{act}}\), \(a_j\ne0\); (18) therefore gives \(a_j'\Sigma_0a_j>0\).  If \(W_0\sim N_d(h,\Sigma_0)\), the density of \(a_j'W_0\) is bounded, uniformly in \(h\), by \(\{2\pi a_j'\Sigma_0a_j\}^{-1/2}\).  Hence, for every \(s\ge0\),
\[
 \Pr_h\{|a_j'W_0|\le s\}
 \le\min\left\{1,
 \frac{2s}{\sqrt{2\pi}\{a_j'\Sigma_0a_j\}^{1/2}}
 \right\}.
 \tag{29}
\]
The union bound gives
\[
 \mathfrak b_0(s)\le A_0s,
 \qquad
 A_0=\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_0^{\mathrm{act}}}
 \frac{2}{\sqrt{2\pi}\{a_j'\Sigma_0a_j\}^{1/2}}.
 \tag{30}
\]
When the active list is empty, both sides of (30) are zero.  Split the comparison according to the union of the slabs in (29), using width \(s=x_n(C)\).  Off those slabs, the completed active signs agree and (28) applies; on the slabs, both a coverage indicator and the normalized loss lie in \([0,1]\), so their discrepancy is bounded by the slab probability.  Therefore the total geometry charge is at most
\[
 (H_C+A_0)x_n(C).
 \tag{31}
\]

Apply \(\text{lem:bounded-semantic-transfer-by-tv}\) to Carter's kernel and to the Gaussian comparison (26), first with \(A\mapsto\mathbf1\{v\in A\}\) and then with \(A\mapsto\ell_0(A)\).  Both functions take values in \([0,1]\).  Adding (19), (26), and (31) proves that every stated compact semantic-coverage probability and normalized diameter-risk expectation differs from its Gaussian counterpart by at most
\[
 \mathcal E_n^{\mathrm{lin}}(C)
 =C_{2/\kappa}\frac{m\log m}{\sqrt n}
 +(H_C+A_0)x_n(C)+\operatorname{Err}_{\Sigma,n}(C).
 \tag{32}
\]
Finally, \(d_n(C)\to0\) by \(\text{ass:multinomial-local-expansion}\), and \(\delta_n(C)\to0\) by \(\text{lem:compact-moving-remainder-covariance}\).  Together with (27), this gives \(\mathcal E_n^{\mathrm{lin}}(C)\to0\).  Equation (32) keeps the semantic and covariance terms separate and therefore makes no unsupported overall \(O(n^{-1/2})\) assertion.

\textbf{Technique: compatible affine-offset lifting followed by the unconditional Carter--geometry comparison.}

Let \(A_\Psi\) have rows \(\alpha_j'\), \(j\in\mathcal J_0^{\mathrm{act}}\), and put \(L_{\mathrm{ASC}}=\operatorname{lin}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ASC}}-p_0)\).  Every active cut satisfies \(\psi_j(p_0)=0\), so affinity of \(\psi_j\) gives
\[
 c_n=A_\Psi\sqrt n\{p_n(0)-p_0\}.
 \tag{33}
\]
By \(\text{ass:local-family-affine-hull}\), the vector in braces belongs to \(L_{\mathrm{ASC}}\).  The definition of \(\mathcal K_0\) therefore implies
\[
 c_n\in\mathcal K_0\qquad\text{for every }n.
 \tag{34}
\]
Choose rational \(\xi_n\downarrow0\).  The effective moving-offset encoding assumption supplies \(\widehat c_n\in\mathcal K_0\cap\mathbb Q^{|\mathcal J_0^{\mathrm{act}}|}\) with
\[
 \|\widehat c_n-c_n\|_\infty\le\xi_n.
 \tag{35}
\]
Since \(c_n\to c\), equations (35) and \(\xi_n\to0\) imply \(\widehat c_n\to c\).  Thus the two sequences lie eventually in one closed bounded subset \(C_c\) of the finite-dimensional space \(\mathcal K_0\).  It is compact, and \(\text{lem:finite-completed-arrangement-stability}\) supplies the single finite constant
\[
 H_C=H(C,C_c)<\infty.
 \tag{36}
\]
This proves, rather than assumes, the compact-offset premise of \(\text{lem:moving-offset-carter-geometry}\).

We verify that the rational offset object is a fully encoded experiment.  Because \(A_\Psi\) and a rational basis of \(L_{\mathrm{ASC}}\) are rational, Gaussian elimination applied to \(A_\Psi z=\widehat c_n\) returns a rational solution \(z_n\in L_{\mathrm{ASC}}\).  For every fixed \(u\), tangent-image compatibility gives \(Bu\in L_{\mathrm{ASC}}\), hence \(z_n+Bu\in L_{\mathrm{ASC}}\).  By the definition of relative interior, a relative open ball about \(p_0\) in the affine hull of \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ASC}}\) is contained in \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ASC}}\).  Applying this fact to the fixed direction \(z_n+Bu\) gives a number \(t_{n,u}>0\) such that
\[
 p_0+t(z_n+Bu)\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ASC}}
 \qquad(0<t\le t_{n,u}).
 \tag{37}
\]
For an active cut,
\[
 \alpha_j'(z_n+Bu)=\widehat c_{n,j}+a_j'u.
 \tag{38}
\]
The compatible full-law lift in (37) has a feasible optimal completed branch with the signs in (38), and a zero sign belongs to every incident completion.  Therefore \(\bigcup_{v\in V}D_v^0(\widehat c_n)=\mathbb R^d\).

If the active list is empty, \(\text{prop:tangent-fan-global-cover}\) gives one global semantic label, so the separated domain is nonempty for every \(r_0\ge0\).  Otherwise choose a rational vector \(y\) outside the finite union of homogeneous hyperplanes \(a_j'y=0\).  Along \(u=ty\), every sign in (38) is eventually the fixed strict sign of \(a_j'y\); hence there is a unique completed semantic label \(v_y\) for all sufficiently large \(t\).  Every component belonging to a rival semantic label differs in at least one of these finitely many strict signs.  For that component there is a nonzero rational row \(a'\) and constants \(\gamma>0\), \(b\in\mathbb R\) such that its defining inequality implies, for every point \(g\) in the component,
\[
 t\gamma-b\le a'(ty-g)
 \le \|a\|_{\Sigma_0}\|ty-g\|_{\Sigma_0^{-1}}.
 \tag{39}
\]
The divisor is positive because \(a\ne0\) and \(\Sigma_0\succ0\).  There are finitely many rival components, so the minimum of the lower bounds in (39) tends to infinity.  In particular, for all sufficiently large \(t\), \(ty\) has the unique label \(v_y\) and distance at least \(r_0\) from every rival semantic union.  Thus the offset separated domain is nonempty.  Rationality of the offset and the original cuts gives rational polyhedral components; \(\Sigma_0\succ0\) was proved from the rank and positivity conditions.  The rational offset object therefore belongs to \(\mathfrak E\).

For the statistical comparison, \(\text{ass:multinomial-local-expansion}\) and \(\text{lem:compact-moving-remainder-covariance}\) give
\[
 d_n(C)\to0,
 \qquad \delta_n(C)\to0,
 \qquad \operatorname{Err}_{\Sigma,n}(C)\to0.
 \tag{40}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \chi_n(C)=d_n(C)+\delta_n(C)+\xi_n\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{41}
\]
Now apply \(\text{lem:moving-offset-carter-geometry}\), whose compact-offset premise is (36).  For any supplied Gaussian rule and any supplied bound \(\overline\chi_n(C)\ge\chi_n(C)\), it gives
\[
 \Delta_n(C)=C_{2/\kappa}\frac{|\mathcal J|\log|\mathcal J|}{\sqrt n}
 +\operatorname{Err}_{\Sigma,n}(C)
 +\{H_C+2A_0\}\overline\chi_n(C).
 \tag{42}
\]
The mathematical choice \(\overline\chi_n(C)=\chi_n(C)\) is always valid and need not be computable.  Equations (36), (40), and (41) then imply \(\Delta_n(C)\to0\).  This proves the unconditional qualitative compact comparison for every supplied Gaussian rule.

No step above constructs that Gaussian rule.  If the affirmative conclusion specified in \(\text{oeq:effective-compatible-density}\), or a separately proved sufficient package, is supplied, \(\text{thm:certglue-termination}\) applied to the rational offset experiment produces a finite replayable Gaussian rule and its outward ledger.  Composing with Carter's forward kernel adds (42) to the ledger.  The effective moving-remainder and offset-encoding assumptions provide effective rational bounds only for \(\delta_n(C)\) and \(\xi_n\).  A replayable numerical enclosure of (42) additionally requires supplied effective rational upper enclosures for \(d_n(C)\) and \(\operatorname{Err}_{\Sigma,n}(C)\).  This requirement is an input to the accounting, not a consequence of effective compatible density.  Since every supremum in (40)--(42) is over \(C\), the argument gives no global conclusion outside that compact set.

\textbf{Technique: diagonal compact exhaustion, finite-support dual transfer, and exact windowwise repair.}

Let \(B_k=\{u:\|u\|_2\le k\}\).  Fix \(k\in\mathbb N\).  By \(\text{lem:compact-causal-domain}\), the relative-interior, affine-hull, base-convergence, and compact local-expansion premises imply
\[
 B_k\subseteq\mathcal U_n
 \tag{43}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  By \(\text{prop:carter-fixed-m-positive-cell}\), eventually
\[
 \min_{u\in B_k}\min_{j\in\mathcal J}p_{n,j}(u)\ge\kappa/2.
 \tag{44}
\]
For each fixed \(1\le\ell\le k\), zero-offset centering, the local expansion, \(\text{lem:compact-moving-remainder-covariance}\), and \(\text{lem:finite-completed-arrangement-stability}\) make every term in \(\mathscr E_n(\ell)\) tend to zero.  Since there are only finitely many such \(\ell\), (43), (44), and
\[
 \mathscr E_n(\ell)\le1/\ell,
 \qquad 1\le\ell\le k,
 \tag{45}
\]
hold simultaneously for all sufficiently large \(n\).  The definition of \(R_n^{\diamond}\) then gives \(R_n^{\diamond}\ge k\).  Since \(k\) was arbitrary,
\[
 R_n^{\diamond}\longrightarrow\infty.
 \tag{46}
\]
This is a diagonal existence argument; the qualitative hypotheses do not provide an algorithm deciding the eventual indices in (43)--(45).

For all sufficiently large \(n\), put \(k_n=R_n^{\diamond}\ge1\), and let \(\rho_n\) be the complete Carter, score, covariance, offset, boundary, and completed-arrangement comparison charge on \(B_{k_n}\).  In \(\text{def:comparison-window-schedule}\), the finite-arrangement term includes the directed Hausdorff comparison and the separation-restoration displacement required below.  The acceptance inequalities at \(\ell=k_n\) therefore give
\[
 0\le\rho_n\le\mathscr E_n(k_n)\le1/k_n.
 \tag{47}
\]
Thus (46) implies \(\rho_n\to0\).

We first prove the lower bound.  Let \(L<M_{\mathfrak G_0,\eta_0}(r_0)\).  By \(\text{thm:attainment-atomic-duality}\), there is a finite atomic Gaussian dual certificate \(\mu\) of value greater than \(L\).  Its finite support lies in \(B_k\) for some \(k\).  The two directed finite-arrangement comparisons realize every coverage atom \(h\in D_v^0\) by an atom \(h_n\in D_{n,v}\) with \(\|h_n-h\|_{\Sigma_0^{-1}}\to0\).  If \(h\) is on an equality face, a separate copy is used for each dual constraint role; the completed convention retains every incident label.

Risk atoms can also be realized in the separated set.  If \(r_0>0\), the zero-offset cells are conical and distance to each rival cone is positively homogeneous.  For a risk atom \(h\in H_{r_0}\), choose \(t_n\downarrow0\) slowly enough that the extra margin of \((1+t_n)h\) dominates twice the directed Hausdorff error.  The resulting perturbed atom then lies in \(H_{n,r_0}\) and converges to \(h\).  If \(r_0=0\), the completed finite arrangement permits approximation of a singleton-label point by singleton-label chamber points; closedness of the finite rival union gives each chosen chamber point a positive atom-specific distance, after which the same directed comparison places its realization in \(H_{n,0}\).  Because the support of \(\mu\) is finite and (46) holds, all these atoms lie in \(B_{R_n^{\diamond}}\) eventually.  This proves the asserted lawful multinomial realization of every finite atomic Gaussian dual certificate.

Apply Carter's reverse parameter-independent kernel, followed by the score and covariance maps, to an arbitrary window-honest multinomial rule.  By \(\text{lem:bounded-semantic-transfer-by-tv}\), the error in the finite dual objective is at most \(A_\mu\rho_n\), where \(A_\mu<\infty\) is the sum of the absolute coefficients of the fixed finite dual record.  Weak duality then yields
\[
 M_n^{\diamond}\ge L-A_\mu\rho_n.
 \tag{48}
\]
Let \(n\to\infty\), use (47), and then let \(L\uparrow M_{\mathfrak G_0,\eta_0}(r_0)\).  We obtain
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}M_n^{\diamond}
 \ge M_{\mathfrak G_0,\eta_0}(r_0).
 \tag{49}
\]

For the reverse inequality, fix \(a>0\).  Attainment provides \(K^a\in\mathcal C_{\eta_0}\) such that
\[
 \sup_{h\in H_{r_0}}E_h\ell_0\{K^a(W_0)\}
 \le M_{\mathfrak G_0,\eta_0}(r_0)+a.
 \tag{50}
\]
Transfer \(K^a\) through Carter's forward kernel and the affine score map.  The completed-arrangement comparison pairs every \(u\in D_{n,v}\cap B_{k_n}\) with a Gaussian mean carrying label \(v\), and pairs every \(u\in H_{n,r_0}\cap B_{k_n}\) with a restored Gaussian mean in \(H_{r_0}\).  Equal-covariance translation, Carter total variation, covariance replacement, the active-boundary charge, and the restoration term are all included in \(\rho_n\).  Since coverage indicators and \(\ell_0\) take values in \([0,1]\), the transferred rule has window coverage at least \(1-\eta_0-\rho_n\) and window risk at most
\[
 M_{\mathfrak G_0,\eta_0}(r_0)+a+\rho_n.
 \tag{51}
\]

The algebra of \(\text{lem:exact-coverage-repair}\) applies with both infima and suprema restricted to the same window.  Put
\[
 q_n=\frac{\rho_n}{\eta_0+\rho_n}.
 \tag{52}
\]
The denominator is positive because \(\eta_0>0\), and
\[
 (1-q_n)(1-\eta_0-\rho_n)+q_n=1-\eta_0.
 \tag{53}
\]
Mixing with the full label set therefore produces a member of \(\mathcal C_{n,\eta_0}^{\diamond}\).  Since \(0\le\ell_0\le1\), (51)--(53) give
\[
 M_n^{\diamond}
 \le M_{\mathfrak G_0,\eta_0}(r_0)+a+\rho_n
 +\frac{\rho_n}{\eta_0+\rho_n}.
