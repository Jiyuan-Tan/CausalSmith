%%% FIELD carter-statement
Let \(m\ge 2\), \(n\ge 1\), \(R\ge 1\), and
\[
 \Theta_R=\left\{\theta\in(0,1)^m:\sum_{i=1}^m\theta_i=1,
 \ \frac{\max_i\theta_i}{\min_i\theta_i}\le R\right\}.
\]
For \(P_\theta=\operatorname{Multinomial}(n,\theta)\) and the matched Gaussian law
\(Q_\theta=N_m\{n\theta,n(\operatorname{diag}\theta-\theta\theta')\}\), there is a constant \(C_R\), depending only on \(R\), and parameter-independent Markov kernels in both directions such that both uniform total-variation errors are at most
\[
 \varepsilon_{\mathrm{Car}}(n,m,R)=C_R\frac{m\log m}{\sqrt n}.
\]
Equivalently, the Le Cam distance between \(\{P_\theta:\theta\in\Theta_R\}\) and \(\{Q_\theta:\theta\in\Theta_R\}\) is at most \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{Car}}(n,m,R)\).

%%% FIELD carter-cor-statement
Put \(m=|\mathcal J|\). Under \(\text{ass:multinomial-interior}\), \(\text{ass:multinomial-base-convergence}\), and \(\text{ass:multinomial-local-expansion}\), for every compact \(C\subset\mathbb R^d\) there is \(n_C\) such that
\[
 \min_{u\in C}\min_{j\in\mathcal J}p_{n,j}(u)\ge \kappa/2,
 \qquad
 \frac{\max_jp_{n,j}(u)}{\min_jp_{n,j}(u)}\le 2/\kappa
 \quad(n\ge n_C).
\]
Consequently Carter's two kernels apply uniformly to \(\{p_n(u):u\in C\}\) with error
\[
 \varepsilon_{\mathrm{Car},n}=C_{2/\kappa}\frac{m\log m}{\sqrt n}.
\]
The same bound holds after any fixed measurable score or whitening map.

%%% FIELD carter-cor-proof
Let \(M_C=\sup_{u\in C}\|u\|_2<\infty\), and write
\[
 r_n(u)=\sqrt n\{p_n(u)-p_n(0)\}-Bu.
\]
The local expansion gives \(\sup_{u\in C}\|r_n(u)\|_2\to0\). Hence
\[
 \sup_{u\in C}\|p_n(u)-p_0\|_\infty
 \le \|p_n(0)-p_0\|_2+
 \frac{\|B\|_{\mathrm{op}}M_C+\sup_{u\in C}\|r_n(u)\|_2}{\sqrt n}\longrightarrow0,
\]
by \(\text{ass:multinomial-base-convergence}\). Since \(\min_j(p_0)_j\ge\kappa\) by \(\text{ass:multinomial-interior}\), the first displayed inequality follows eventually. Every probability coordinate is at most one, so the ratio is at most \(1/(\kappa/2)=2/\kappa\). Apply \(\text{thm:carter-positive-multinomial-gaussian-comparison}\) with \(R=2/\kappa\). Finally, if \(S\) is a measurable map and \(\mu,\nu\) are probability measures, then
\[
 \|S_\#\mu-S_\#\nu\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
 =\sup_B|\mu(S^{-1}B)-\nu(S^{-1}B)|
 \le \|\mu-\nu\|_{\mathrm{TV}},
\]
which proves the score-map assertion.

%%% FIELD tv-transfer-statement
Let \(\{P_\theta\}\) and \(\{Q_\theta\}\) be experiments with the same parameter set, and let \(T\) be a parameter-independent Markov kernel satisfying
\(\sup_\theta\|TP_\theta-Q_\theta\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\le\epsilon\). If \(K\) is a randomized rule with actions in \(\mathcal A\), then \(K\circ T\) satisfies, for every \(v\in V\),
\[
 \sup_\theta\left|P_\theta\{v\in(K\circ T)(X)\}
 -Q_\theta\{v\in K(Y)\}\right|\le\epsilon,
\]
and for every \(g:\mathcal A\to[0,R]\),
\[
 \sup_\theta\left|E_{P_\theta}g\{(K\circ T)(X)\}
 -E_{Q_\theta}g\{K(Y)\}\right|\le R\epsilon.
\]
In particular the normalized Job Corps diameter loss has error at most \(\epsilon\), and a diameter loss bounded by \(D\) has error at most \(D\epsilon\).

%%% FIELD tv-transfer-proof
For a fixed \(\theta\), composing \(T\) with \(K\) is another Markov kernel, so total variation contracts:
\[
 \|(K\circ T)P_\theta-KQ_\theta\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
 \le \|TP_\theta-Q_\theta\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\le\epsilon.
\]
Apply the defining variational inequality for total variation first to the event \(\{A:v\in A\}\). For \(0\le g\le R\), the layer-cake identity gives
\[
 E_\mu g-E_\nu g
 =\int_0^R\{\mu(g>t)-\nu(g>t)\}\,dt,
\]
and therefore \(|E_\mu g-E_\nu g|\le R\|\mu-\nu\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\). Taking \(g=\ell_0\), whose range is \([0,1]\), or \(g=\ell\), whose range is \([0,D]\), gives the last assertions.

%%% FIELD common-ray-definition
Let \(\mathscr R\) be the finite set of primitive rational ray generators in a common rational simplicial refinement of \(\operatorname{recFan}(\mathfrak G)\). For a Markov kernel \(q\), a ray \(\varrho\in\mathscr R\), and rational \(L>0\), define the one-sided Følner operator
\[
 (\mathsf A_{\varrho,L}q)(w,A)
 =\frac1L\int_0^Lq(w+t\varrho,A)\,dt,
 \qquad A\in\mathcal A.
\]
For a simplicial cone \(\sigma\), let
\[
 \mathsf A_{\sigma,L}=\prod_{\varrho\in\mathscr R(\sigma)}
 \mathsf A_{\varrho,L},
 \qquad q_{\sigma,L}=\mathsf A_{\sigma,L}q_s,
 \qquad q_s=K*\phi_{s^2\Sigma}.
\]
The product uses the same operator for a ray in every incident cone. For a face inclusion \(\tau\preceq\sigma\), the transition is the product over \(\mathscr R(\sigma)\setminus\mathscr R(\tau)\). Compact normal quotients are triangulated by one global rational simplicial complex, and every vertex on a common face is represented by one shared action-probability variable.

%%% FIELD common-ray-lemma-statement
The common-ray Følner operators commute. For every chain \(\tau\preceq\sigma\),
\[
 q_{\sigma,L}
 =\left(\prod_{\varrho\in\mathscr R(\sigma)\setminus\mathscr R(\tau)}
 \mathsf A_{\varrho,L}\right)q_{\tau,L},
\]
and the transition operator is independent of the incidence path. If \(a\ge0\), \(\varrho\in\mathscr R(\sigma)\), and \(q_s(w)\) is an action-probability vector, then
\[
 \|q_{\sigma,L}(w+a\varrho)-q_{\sigma,L}(w)\|_1
 \le 2\min\{1,a/L\}.
\]
For every \(f:\mathcal A\to[0,R_f]\), the corresponding scalar bound is \(R_f\min\{1,a/L\}\). Shared face vertices have exactly identical traces under the global finite-element discretization. These identities give computable \(O(L^{-1})\) translation boundary ratios, but do not assert that \(q_{\sigma,L}=q_{\tau,L}\) on a shared face.

%%% FIELD common-ray-lemma-proof
For two rays \(\varrho_1,\varrho_2\), Tonelli's theorem applies coordinatewise because every coordinate of a Markov kernel is nonnegative and bounded by one. Thus
\[
 \mathsf A_{\varrho_1,L}\mathsf A_{\varrho_2,L}q(w,A)
 =\frac1{L^2}\int_0^L\int_0^L
 q(w+t_1\varrho_1+t_2\varrho_2,A)\,dt_2dt_1
 =\mathsf A_{\varrho_2,L}\mathsf A_{\varrho_1,L}q(w,A).
\]
Induction over the finite ray set proves commutativity. Cancelling the literally shared factors in \(\mathscr R(\tau)\subseteq\mathscr R(\sigma)\) gives the displayed face-transition identity; commutativity makes the product, and hence every composite transition, independent of the chosen chain.

Write \(q=\mathsf A_{\sigma\setminus\{\varrho\},L}q_s\). For \(0\le a\le L\),
\[
 \mathsf A_{\varrho,L}q(w+a\varrho)-\mathsf A_{\varrho,L}q(w)
 =\frac1L\left\{\int_L^{L+a}q(w+t\varrho)\,dt-
 \int_0^aq(w+t\varrho)\,dt\right\}.
\]
Since an action-probability vector has \(\ell^1\)-norm one, the triangle inequality gives \(2a/L\). The trivial distance bound two handles \(a>L\). If \(F_q^f=\sum_Af(A)q_A\in[0,R_f]\), the difference of the two boundary integrals is at most \(R_fa\), giving \(R_fa/L\), with the trivial bound \(R_f\) for \(a>L\). Finally, a global simplicial complex identifies a geometric face vertex only once; using one variable at that vertex makes all incident affine traces equal there by the uniqueness of barycentric interpolation on the face. This last assertion is algebraic and does not identify the cone-interior averages with one another.

%%% FIELD arrangement-stability-statement
For the finite completed affine arrangement defining \(D_v^0(c)\), fix compact sets \(C\subset\mathbb R^d\) and \(C_c\subset\mathcal K_0\). There is a finite computable constant \(H(C,C_c)\) with the following property. If \(c,\widehat c\in C_c\), every active affine inequality is perturbed by at most \(\delta\), and \(u\in C\) has semantic label \(v\) in the perturbed completed system, then there is \(\bar u\in D_v^0(\widehat c)\) such that
\[
 \|u-\bar u\|_{\Sigma_0^{-1}}
 \le H(C,C_c)\{\delta+\|c-\widehat c\|_\infty\}.
\]
Equality faces retain all incident labels. The same finite enumeration gives directed Hausdorff bounds for rival semantic unions and hence changes every separation distance by at most twice the displayed quantity. All constants are obtained by rational active-set enumeration and outward enclosure of \(\Sigma_0\)-norms.

%%% FIELD arrangement-stability-proof
Each completed branch is a finite weak system
\[
 A_bu\le \beta_b+G_bc,
\]
with rational \(A_b,\beta_b,G_b\). For a nonempty system, projection of \(u\) onto it and the Karush--Kuhn--Tucker equations show that the residual is generated by a linearly independent subset \(I\) of active row normals. Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{dist}_{\Sigma_0^{-1}}
 \bigl(u,\{x:A_bx\le\beta_b+G_b\widehat c\}\bigr)
 \le H_b\bigl\|(A_bu-\beta_b-G_b\widehat c)_+\bigr\|_\infty,
\]
where one may take \(H_b\) to be the maximum, over the finitely many linearly independent row subsets \(I\), of an outward rational enclosure of the norm of the corresponding right inverse in the \(\Sigma_0^{-1}\) metric. This is a direct finite active-set proof of the linear error bound.

If \(u\) satisfies the system with right-hand perturbation \(r_b\), \(\|r_b\|_\infty\le\delta\), at offset \(c\), then
\[
 \bigl\|(A_bu-\beta_b-G_b\widehat c)_+\bigr\|_\infty
 \le \delta+\|G_b\|_{\infty\to\infty}\|c-\widehat c\|_\infty.
\]
Empty systems are discarded. When a system becomes feasible only at a boundary value of the offset, its limiting weak equalities occur in every incident completion by construction, so the same point is assigned the same semantic label in at least one retained incident system. Taking the maximum over finitely many branches, offset cells, and semantic labels, and enlarging it to absorb \(\|G_b\|_{\infty\to\infty}\), gives \(H(C,C_c)\). Applying the point-to-set bound in both directions to every component of a rival semantic union yields directed Hausdorff error at most the displayed bound. The inequality
\[
 |\operatorname{dist}(u,E)-\operatorname{dist}(\bar u,\bar E)|
 \le \|u-\bar u\|+d_{\mathrm H}(E,\bar E)
\]
then gives the factor two for separation distances. Rational active sets are finite, and \(\Sigma_0\succ0\), so all inverses used on their ranks and all metric norms have terminating outward rational enclosures.

%%% FIELD escaping-statement
Compact-uniform local expansion does not determine an unbounded-parameter minimax value. Specifically, for every \(\eta\in(0,1/2)\) there are two two-category, two-label multinomial triangular arrays with the same positive base \(p_0\), the same full-rank tangent \(B\), and identical laws on every fixed compact for all sufficiently large \(n\), such that their zero-offset Gaussian tangent experiment has one semantic label and minimax diameter zero, whereas the second array has global honest diameter minimax value at least \(1-2\eta\) for every \(n\). Thus compact comparison cannot imply a global minimax upper bound without an expanding-domain or recession-control premise.

%%% FIELD escaping-proof
Take two categories, labels \(V=\{0,1\}\), discrete label metric, \(p_0=(1/4,3/4)\), and \(B=(1/16,-1/16)'\). For \(q\in(0,1)\), assign truth \(\{0\}\) when \(q<1/2\), truth \(\{0,1\}\) when \(q=1/2\), and truth \(\{1\}\) when \(q>1/2\). This is a completed rational polyhedral label map on the one-dimensional probability simplex. Let
\[
 q_n^A(u)=\frac14+\frac1{16}\operatorname{clip}(u/\sqrt n,-1,1),
 \qquad p_n^A(u)=(q_n^A(u),1-q_n^A(u)).
\]
Define \(p_n^B=p_n^A\) outside \(|u-n|\le\sqrt n\), while on that interval put
\[
 q_n^B(u)=\frac12+\frac{u-n}{8\sqrt n},
 \qquad p_n^B(u)=(q_n^B(u),1-q_n^B(u)).
\]
All coordinates of both arrays are at least \(3/16\). For every fixed compact \(C\), eventually \(C\cap[n-\sqrt n,n+\sqrt n]=\varnothing\) and clipping is inactive on \(C\). Therefore both arrays agree on \(C\) and
\[
 \sup_{u\in C}\left\|\sqrt n\{p_n^A(u)-p_0\}-Bu\right\|_2
 =\sup_{u\in C}\left\|\sqrt n\{p_n^B(u)-p_0\}-Bu\right\|_2=0.
\]
They have the same base, tangent, information, covariance, and every fixed-compact limiting label. Because \(q_n^A(u)\le5/16<1/2\), its sole truth is label zero; the constant action \(\{0\}\) is honest and has diameter zero.

For the second array, \(q_n^B(n)=1/2\). At this parameter, honesty of any rule \(K_n\) implies
\[
 P_{1/2}\{0\in K_n\}\ge1-\eta,
 \qquad P_{1/2}\{1\in K_n\}\ge1-\eta.
\]
The union bound therefore gives
\[
 P_{1/2}\{K_n=\{0,1\}\}
 \ge1-2\eta.
\]
With the discrete metric, the expected diameter is precisely the probability of reporting \(\{0,1\}\). For every fixed randomized rule on the finite count space this probability is a polynomial, hence continuous, in \(q\). Parameters \(u=n+t\), \(t\ne0\), have a unique truth and converge to the boundary law as \(t\to0\). For separation radius zero they belong to the risk domain. Thus
\[
 \sup_{u:\,\mathcal V_n(u)\text{ is a singleton}}
 E_{p_n^B(u)}\ell\{K_n(N_n)\}
 \ge1-2\eta.
\]
Taking the infimum over globally honest \(K_n\) proves the asserted lower bound. The inserted boundary escapes to \(u_n=n\), so no fixed-compact hypothesis detects it.

Define

Let \(p_n(0)=p_0\), put \(m=|\mathcal J|\), and fix a compact \(C\subset\mathbb R^d\) contained in \(\mathcal U_n\) eventually and in one completed positive-support stratum. Fix a Gaussian rule for \(\mathfrak G_0\) whose finite representation has Gaussian-null boundaries. Define

There is a parameter-independent Carter transfer producing \(K_n\) for which every semantic coverage probability and every normalized diameter-risk expectation on \(C\) differs from its Gaussian counterpart by at most

%%% FIELD fixed-proof
Let \(D_n=\operatorname{diag}\{p_n(0)\}\), \(I_n=B'D_n^{-1}B\), and \(\Sigma_n=I_n^{-1}\). Positivity on \(C\) and the Carter error follow from \(\text{cor:carter-fixed-m-positive-cell}\). If \(Y_n\) has Carter's matched normal law at \(p_n(u)\), apply the fixed affine score map
\[
 S_n(Y_n)=\Sigma_nB'D_n^{-1}\frac{Y_n-np_n(0)}{\sqrt n}.
\]
It has law \(N_d\{\mu_n(u),V_n(u)\}\), where
\[
 \mu_n(u)=\Sigma_nB'D_n^{-1}\sqrt n\{p_n(u)-p_n(0)\},
\]
\[
 V_n(u)=\Sigma_nB'D_n^{-1}
 [\operatorname{diag}\{p_n(u)\}-p_n(u)p_n(u)']D_n^{-1}B\Sigma_n.
\]
Because \(\Sigma_nB'D_n^{-1}B=I_d\),
\[
 \sup_{u\in C}\|\mu_n(u)-u\|_2
 \le \|\Sigma_nB'D_n^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}d_n(C)\longrightarrow0.
\]
The covariance conclusion in \(\text{lem:compact-moving-remainder-covariance}\), base convergence, and the compact expansion imply \(\sup_{u\in C}\|V_n(u)-\Sigma_0\|_{\mathrm{op}}\to0\).

For positive definite \(V\), define
\[
 \mathsf H^2(\mu,V;u,\Sigma_0)=2-2
 \frac{|V|^{1/4}|\Sigma_0|^{1/4}}
 {|(V+\Sigma_0)/2|^{1/2}}
 \exp\left[-\frac18(\mu-u)'\{(V+\Sigma_0)/2\}^{-1}(\mu-u)\right].
\]
Direct integration of the product of the two Gaussian densities gives their Hellinger affinity and hence total variation at most \(\min\{1,\mathsf H\}\). Set
\[
 \operatorname{Err}_{\Sigma,n}(C)=
 \sup_{u\in C}\min\{1,\mathsf H(\mu_n(u),V_n(u);u,\Sigma_0)\}.
\]
The preceding mean and covariance limits, compactness, and eventual uniform positive definiteness give \(\operatorname{Err}_{\Sigma,n}(C)\to0\).

The exact slack identity and \(\text{lem:finite-completed-arrangement-stability}\) imply that a finite-sample semantic label at \(u\in C\) is an incident Gaussian label at some \(\bar u\) with
\[
 \|u-\bar u\|_{\Sigma_0^{-1}}
 \le H_C\{d_n(C)+\delta_n(C)\}
\]
whenever \(d_n(C)+\delta_n(C)\le\tau\); equality labels are retained on all incident faces. Gaussian shift total variation is at most one half of this Mahalanobis distance, after enlarging \(H_C\). For separated-risk points, the directed Hausdorff part of the same lemma and continuity of Gaussian convolutions give the same modulus; the protected boundary contribution is bounded by \(\mathfrak b_0(\tau)\). Finally apply \(\text{lem:bounded-semantic-transfer-by-tv}\) first to Carter's kernel and then to the two Gaussian laws. This yields the displayed sum for coverage indicators and, because \(0\le\ell_0\le1\), for diameter risk. The established compact remainder lemma gives \(d_n(C),\delta_n(C)\to0\); therefore the indicator in \(\operatorname{Err}_{\mathrm{geom}}\) vanishes for fixed \(\tau>0\), and \(\mathfrak b_0(\tau)\downarrow0\), proving the iterated limit.

Conditional on effective compatibility density, the effective moving-remainder assumption, and effective moving-offset encoding, suppose \(p_n(0)\to p_0\), \(p_n(u)-p_0\in\operatorname{lin}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ASC}}-p_0)\), \(c_n\to c\in\mathcal K_0\), and \(\delta_n(C)=\max_j\sup_{u\in C}|e_{n,j}(u)|\to0\) on every rational compact polytope \(C\).
