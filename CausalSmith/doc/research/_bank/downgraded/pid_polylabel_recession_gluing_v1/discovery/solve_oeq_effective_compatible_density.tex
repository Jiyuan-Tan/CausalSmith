Do the standing assumptions defining \(\mathfrak E\) permit the following input-computable construction?  Given \(\mathfrak G\in\mathfrak E\) and an accuracy index \(j\in\mathbb N\), the construction must output a finite rational core-and-recession library on one common rational subdivision of \(\operatorname{recFan}(\mathfrak G)\), with all semantic projections, incidence maps, chart meshes, protected stars, partitions of unity, and outward error enclosures, and it must certify the following sufficient obligations simultaneously:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every canonical section used by a chart preserves membership in each active \(D_v\), membership in \(H_r\), and the declared protected star;
\item every local action kernel receiving positive gluing weight is safe for the coverage or separated-risk mean under consideration;
\item on every simultaneous collar support, incident charts have either exactly equal traces or an explicit Gaussian-weighted discrepancy bound;
\item all guard-region and normal-base tail bounds hold uniformly over translated means;
\item if \(N_j\) is the computed number of charged refined cells, then the construction gives computable rational schedules \(L_j,B_j>0\) and \(\delta_j>0\) for which every accumulated chart, collar, path, tail, weight-variation, integration, covariance, rounding, and repair term tends to zero and \(N_j/L_j\to0\).
\end{enumerate}
As the resulting sufficient target, these certificates should produce finite classes \(\mathfrak F_{\mathfrak G,j}\) and a computable modulus \(\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)\downarrow0\) such that every \(K\in\mathcal C_\eta\) has some \(\widetilde K_j\in\mathfrak F_{\mathfrak G,j}\) with semantic-label coverage at least \(1-\eta-\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)\), separated risk at most \(R_r(K)+D\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)\), and \(\rho_{\mathrm{led}}\leq\Omega_{\mathfrak G}(j)\).  The listed obligations are sufficient conditions to be proved, not an if-and-only-if characterization, and no refinement-independent constant is presumed to bound the growing incidence ledger.

Starting from the single ancestor \(q_s=K*\phi_{s^2\Sigma}\), \(\mathrm{lem:common\mbox{-}ray\mbox{-}folner\mbox{-}coherence}\) proves that, for every face inclusion \(\tau\preceq\sigma\),
\[
q_{\sigma,L}
=
\left(
\prod_{\varrho\in\mathscr R(\sigma)\setminus\mathscr R(\tau)}
\mathsf A_{\varrho,L}
\right)q_{\tau,L},
\]
independently of the incidence path, together with the bounds
\[
\left\|q_{\sigma,L}(w+a\varrho)-q_{\sigma,L}(w)\right\|_1
\leq 2\min\{1,a/L\}
\]
and
\[
\left|F^f_{\sigma,L}(w+a\varrho)-F^f_{\sigma,L}(w)\right|
\leq R_f\min\{1,a/L\}.
\]
The established smoothing, one-chart averaging, and weighted path results separately give
\[
R_f\min\{1,s^2\sqrt{d/8}\},
\qquad
\frac{R_fk}{T\sqrt{2\pi}},
\qquad
\sum_e\Gamma_{C,e}^f+\frac{R_f}{2}\omega(h)
\]
for each fixed finite library. These identities certify path-independent transitions and the stated componentwise errors only. They do not prove simultaneous safety on collar supports, tails uniform over translated means, or a refinement-count schedule whose accumulated ledger vanishes.
