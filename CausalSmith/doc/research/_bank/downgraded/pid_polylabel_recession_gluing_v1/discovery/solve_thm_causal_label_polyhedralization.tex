Put \(L=\operatorname{lin}(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ASC}}-p_0)\).  By
\(\text{ass:causal-base-relative-interior}\),
\(\text{ass:tangent-image-compatible}\), and
\(\text{lem:relative-interior-tangent-lift}\), for every
\(u\in\mathbb R^d\) there is \(t_u>0\) such that
\[
 p_0+tBu\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{ASC}}
 \qquad(0<t\leq t_u).
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\text{lem:compatible-observed-law-polytope}\) and
\(\text{prop:fiber-polytope-equivalence}\), every law in (1) has a
nonempty finite response-type fiber, so all of the finitely many branch
operations and endpoint programs in \(\mathscr L_0\) admit feasible
optimal symbolic completions.

Choose a sequence \(t_k\downarrow0\).  Since the set of symbolic branch
and primal--dual optimal completions is finite, a subsequence has one
constant completion \(b\).  If
\(\psi(p)=\alpha_\psi'p+\beta_\psi\in\mathscr L_0\) and
\(\psi(p_0)\ne0\), continuity implies that the sign along this
subsequence is its frozen base sign.  If \(\psi(p_0)=0\), affinity gives
the exact identity
\[
 \psi(p_0+t_kBu)=t_k\alpha_\psi'Bu.
\tag{2}
\]
Consequently the constant completion chooses the weak tangent sign
prescribed by \(\alpha_\psi'Bu\).  If
\(\alpha_\psi'Bu=0\), both weak inequalities hold, which is precisely
the stipulated incidence convention.  Passing the finite weak linear
inequalities and the finite primal--dual feasibility and optimality
equalities to the limit shows that \(b\in\mathcal B_0\) is a feasible
optimal completion at \(u\).  Thus \(u\in P_{v,b}^0\) for the semantic
projection \(v\) of \(b\).  This is also the conclusion of
\(\text{lem:compatible-lift-completed-cover}\), and hence
\[
 \bigcup_{v\in V}D_v^0=\mathbb R^d.
\tag{3}
\]
Identity (2) proves directly that a zero tangent slack belongs to every
incident weak completion.

We next prove the moving-offset assertion.  Let \(A_\Psi\) be the
matrix with rows \(\alpha_j'\) for
\(j\in\mathcal J_0^{\mathrm{act}}\).  Since every active cut satisfies
\(\psi_j(p_0)=0\), the definition of \(c_n\) gives
\[
 c_n=\sqrt n\,A_\Psi\{p_n(0)-p_0\}.
\tag{4}
\]
By \(\text{ass:local-family-affine-hull}\), the vector in braces lies
in \(L\).  Therefore (4) proves
\(c_n\in A_\Psi L=\mathcal K_0\); offset compatibility is derived rather
than assumed.

Suppose now that \(c_n\to c\in\mathcal K_0\), that the moving
remainders converge uniformly on compact sets as stated, and that a
compact set \(C\) has positive distance from every hyperplane
\(c_j+a_j'u=0\).  When the active index set is nonempty, compactness,
finiteness, and the nonzero normal condition give
\[
 \delta_C
 :=\min_{u\in C}\min_{j\in\mathcal J_0^{\mathrm{act}}}
       |c_j+a_j'u|>0.
\tag{5}
\]
When the active set is empty, the following sign argument is vacuous.
Convergence of \(c_n\) and compact-uniform convergence of the
remainders imply that, eventually,
\[
 \max_j|c_{n,j}-c_j|
 +\max_j\sup_{u\in C}|e_{n,j}(u)|
 <\frac{\delta_C}{2}.
\tag{6}
\]
Using the defining identity
\[
 \zeta_{n,j}(u)=c_{n,j}+a_j'u+e_{n,j}(u),
\tag{7}
\]
the reverse triangle inequality and (5)--(6) show that
\(\zeta_{n,j}(u)\) and \(c_j+a_j'u\) have the same strict sign,
simultaneously for every \(u\in C\) and every active \(j\).

For completeness, if \(\psi(p_0)\ne0\), then
\(\text{ass:multinomial-base-convergence}\) and
\(\text{ass:multinomial-local-expansion}\) give
\[
 \sup_{u\in C}\|p_n(u)-p_0\|_2
 \leq \|p_n(0)-p_0\|_2
 +n^{-1/2}\sup_{u\in C}\|Bu\|_2+o(n^{-1/2})
 \longrightarrow0.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus every nonzero base sign is also eventually its frozen sign,
uniformly on \(C\).  A zero-base cut with zero tangent normal is, by
\(\text{def:label-fan}\), a fixed tie included in all of its incident
completions.  The complete finite cut list therefore has exactly the
same branch signs for the multinomial rule and the offset arrangement
on \(C\).  By \(\text{def:multinomial-label-regions}\),
\[
 \mathcal V_n(u)=\{v:u\in D_v^0(c)\}
 \qquad\text{for every \(u\in C\)}
\tag{9}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  The compact-domain lemma ensures
simultaneously that \(C\subseteq\mathcal U_n\), so every label on the
left side of (9) is lawful.

Finally suppose that
\(\mathcal J_0^{\mathrm{act}}=\varnothing\).  Every retained tangent
cell is then all of \(\mathbb R^d\).  The equivalence step in the
definition of the semantic map identifies patterns with the same
completed tangent region and retains only one representative, so there
is one global semantic label, say \(v_0\).  The deterministic rule
reporting \(\{v_0\}\) has coverage one at every mean and
\(\ell(\{v_0\})=\delta(v_0,v_0)=0\).  Since diameter loss is
nonnegative, its honest minimax value is therefore zero.

Assume first that
\(\mathcal J_0^{\mathrm{act}}\ne\varnothing\).  Every active normal
\(a_j\) is a nonzero rational vector.  A finite union of the proper
hyperplanes \(\{u:a_j'u=0\}\) has empty interior, so its complement
contains a nonempty full-dimensional open chamber.  Rational points
are dense in that chamber.  By
\(\text{prop:tangent-fan-global-cover}\), every point of the chamber
has a feasible optimal completion.  All active signs are constant on
the chamber, and the complete cut list includes every branch,
feasibility, optimality, and semantic assertion inequality.  Hence its
strict sign pattern determines one completed tangent component.
Patterns with identical completed regions have only their canonical
semantic representative.  It follows that the chamber is contained
in a unique semantic component \(P_{v,b}^0\).

Choose a rational point in this strict chamber.  Every nonredundant
defining inequality of its component has strictly positive rational
slack there.  The active arrangement is homogeneous, so multiplying
this point by a sufficiently large positive rational number makes
every such slack at least \(1\).  Therefore the rational linear
program used to define \(i_*\), which imposes
\(0\leq\gamma\leq1\), has optimum \(\gamma=1\) for at least one
component.  The canonical finite search consequently finds a positive
margin component and returns its lexicographically first rational
optimal basic solution \(u_*\).  This point is in a strict chamber of
the selected component.  If the active list is empty instead, the
prescribed choice \(u_*=0\) lies in the unique component
\(\mathbb R^d\).  In both cases, with
\(v_*=\operatorname{pr}_V(i_*)\),
\[
 \{v:u_*\in D_v^0\}=\{v_*\}.
\tag{10}
\]

Consider any retained rival component
\[
 P_{v,b}^0=\{g:A_{v,b}^0g\leq b_{v,b}^0\},
 \qquad v\ne v_*.
\tag{11}
\]
It cannot contain \(u_*\), by (10).  Since (11) is exactly the
intersection of its displayed closed halfspaces, at least one
displayed row \(i\) has the positive rational gap
\[
 \gamma_{v,b,i}
 :=(A_{v,b}^0u_*-b_{v,b}^0)_i>0.
\tag{12}
\]
That row is nonzero.  By \(\text{ass:multinomial-interior}\), every
coordinate of \(p_0\) is positive; by the declared rank condition on
\(B\), \(Bx\ne0\) for \(x\ne0\).  Therefore
\[
 x'I_0x
 =x'B'\operatorname{diag}(p_0)^{-1}Bx
 =\|\operatorname{diag}(p_0)^{-1/2}Bx\|_2^2>0
 \qquad(x\ne0).
\tag{13}
\]
Thus \(I_0\succ0\) and \(\Sigma_0=I_0^{-1}\succ0\).  In particular,
\(\|(A_{v,b}^0)_{i\cdot}\|_{\Sigma_0}>0\).

For every \(g\in P_{v,b}^0\), its \(i\)-th inequality and (12) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \gamma_{v,b,i}
 &\leq (A_{v,b}^0)_{i\cdot}(u_*-g)\\
 &\leq
 \|(A_{v,b}^0)_{i\cdot}\|_{\Sigma_0}
 \|u_*-g\|_{\Sigma_0^{-1}},
 \end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]
where the second step is Cauchy--Schwarz after inserting
\(\Sigma_0^{1/2}\Sigma_0^{-1/2}\).  If the rational norm enclosure in
the certificate gives
\[
 s_{v,b,i}\geq
 \|(A_{v,b}^0)_{i\cdot}\|_{\Sigma_0}>0,
\]
then (14), equivalently
\(\text{lem:violated-row-mahalanobis-separation}\), yields
\[
 \inf_{g\in P_{v,b}^0}
 \|u_*-g\|_{\Sigma_0^{-1}}
 \geq
 \frac{\gamma_{v,b,i}}{s_{v,b,i}}
 =\rho_{v,b}>0.
\tag{15}
\]
There are finitely many rival components.  Hence the minimum defining
\(\rho_0\) is positive; if there is no rival component, the included
value \(1\) gives \(\rho_0=1\).  Since
\(r_0=\min\{r_{\mathrm{req}},\rho_0\}\), taking the minimum in (15)
over the finitely many components of every rival semantic union gives
\[
 \inf_{v\ne v_*}\inf_{g\in D_v^0}
 \|u_*-g\|_{\Sigma_0^{-1}}
 \geq\rho_0\geq r_0.
\tag{16}
\]
Equations (10) and (16) show \(u_*\in H_{r_0}\), including the allowed
case \(r_0=0\).  The chamber search, linear programs, row gaps, and
finite minima use rational arithmetic.  Since \(\Sigma_0\) is rational
positive definite, a proposed rational upper enclosure \(s\) is
checked exactly by the rational inequality
\(s^2\geq (A_{v,b}^0)_{i\cdot}\Sigma_0
(A_{v,b}^0)_{i\cdot}'\), with positivity checked separately.
Thus all stated receipts are finite exact rational certificates, and
no causal margin assumption was used.

%%% FIELD proof-causal-label-polyhedralization
Fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{ASC}}\), put \(p=p(P)\), and write
\[
 \mathsf C
 :=\{D^{\mathrm{pot}}(1)>D^{\mathrm{pot}}(0)\},
 \qquad
 \mathsf A
 :=\{S^{\mathrm{pot}}(0)=S^{\mathrm{pot}}(1)=1\}.
\]
By known assignment probabilities, conditional randomization, and
treatment, selection, and outcome consistency, for
\(a,z\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
 \frac{p_{x,z,a,1,y_k}}{\pi_z(x)}
 =
 P\{X=x,D^{\mathrm{pot}}(z)=a,S^{\mathrm{pot}}(a)=1,
       Y^{\mathrm{pot}}(a)=y_k\}.
\tag{17}
\]
The division is valid because
\(\text{ass:instrument-stratum-positivity}\) gives
\(\pi_z(x)\geq\pi_*>0\).  With binary treatments,
\(\text{ass:no-defiers}\) implies the pointwise identities
\[
 \mathbf 1\{D^{\mathrm{pot}}(1)=1\}
 -\mathbf 1\{D^{\mathrm{pot}}(0)=1\}
 =\mathbf 1_{\mathsf C},
\tag{18}
\]
and
\[
 \mathbf 1\{D^{\mathrm{pot}}(0)=0\}
 -\mathbf 1\{D^{\mathrm{pot}}(1)=0\}
 =\mathbf 1_{\mathsf C}.
\tag{19}
\]
Substituting (17)--(19) into the definition of \(c_{axk}(p)\) proves
the identifying equality
\[
 c_{axk}(p)
 =P\{X=x,\mathsf C,S^{\mathrm{pot}}(a)=1,
       Y^{\mathrm{pot}}(a)=y_k\}.
\tag{20}
\]
Thus the selected-complier submeasure is identified from the observed
law; equation (20) is a consequence of the maintained restrictions,
not a definition of the causal quantity.

Within a stratum \(x\) having positive complier probability,
\(\text{ass:weak-selection-monotonicity}\) makes the two
selected-complier selection events nested.  If \(s(x)=1\), the event
\(\{S^{\mathrm{pot}}(0)=1\}\) is the smaller one; if \(s(x)=-1\), the
event \(\{S^{\mathrm{pot}}(1)=1\}\) is the smaller one.  The same
conclusion is trivial in a stratum with zero complier probability.
Summing (20) over \(k\) therefore gives
\[
 m_x=\min\{q_{0x},q_{1x}\}
 =P(X=x,\mathsf C,\mathsf A),
 \qquad
 M_{\mathrm{ASC}}=P(\mathsf C,\mathsf A)\geq m_*>0.
\tag{21}
\]
The final inequality is
\(\text{ass:aggregate-target-positivity}\); it is the positivity
condition that makes every conditional quantile below well defined.

For an arbitrary compatible law, define
\[
 R_{ax}(j)
 :=P\{X=x,\mathsf C,\mathsf A,
       Y^{\mathrm{pot}}(a)\leq y_j\}.
\tag{22}
\]
By (20), this measure is obtained from the selected-complier measure
of total mass \(q_{ax}\) by retaining a submeasure of total mass
\(m_x\).  At most \(q_{ax}-m_x\) units of its mass below \(y_j\) can
be deleted, and neither the retained mass below \(y_j\) nor its total
mass can exceed \(C_{ax}(j)\) and \(m_x\), respectively.  Hence
\[
 [C_{ax}(j)-q_{ax}+m_x]_+
 \leq R_{ax}(j)
 \leq\min\{m_x,C_{ax}(j)\},
\tag{23}
\]
or equivalently
\[
 L_{ax}(j)\leq R_{ax}(j)\leq U_{ax}(j).
\tag{24}
\]
Summing (24) over the finite set \(\mathcal X\), dividing by the
strictly positive \(M_{\mathrm{ASC}}\) in (21), and applying the
finite-grid generalized inverse yields
\[
 Q_a^-(t)\leq Q_{a,P}(t)\leq Q_a^+(t)
 \qquad(a\in\{0,1\},\ t\in\mathcal T).
\tag{25}
\]
Indeed, the upper cumulative envelope \(U\) crosses the threshold no
later than the true cumulative distribution, while the lower envelope
\(L\) crosses no earlier.  The defining sets are nonempty because at
\(j=J\) both envelopes sum to \(M_{\mathrm{ASC}}\) and \(t<1\).
Thus (25) is an observed-law marginal outer bound; it does not identify
the two latent marginal quantiles separately.

By \(\text{prop:fiber-polytope-equivalence}\), optimization over
\(\Xi(p)\) neither adds nor omits a member of \(\mathcal F(p)\).
The lower- and upper-endpoint attainment propositions therefore give,
separately for every \(t\in\mathcal T\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_{1,P_t^-(p)}(t)-Q_{0,P_t^-(p)}(t)
 &=\underline\Delta(t;p),\\
 Q_{1,P_t^+(p)}(t)-Q_{0,P_t^+(p)}(t)
 &=\overline\Delta(t;p),
 \end{aligned}
\tag{26}
\]
where both marginal quantiles on either line are generated by the same
compatible law.  For every \(P\in\mathcal F(p)\), the definitions of
infimum and supremum imply
\[
 \underline\Delta(t;p)
 \leq Q_{1,P}(t)-Q_{0,P}(t)
 \leq\overline\Delta(t;p).
\tag{27}
\]
Collecting (27) over the finite index set \(\mathcal T\) proves
\[
 \Theta_{\mathrm{ASC}}(p)
 \subseteq
 \prod_{t\in\mathcal T}
 [\underline\Delta(t;p),\overline\Delta(t;p)].
\tag{28}
\]
Attainment in (26) is coordinatewise and may use different compatible
laws at different \(t\) and at the two endpoints.  Thus (28) is an
outer rectangle and no rectangle-equality assertion has been made.

For the tangent conclusion, fix \(P_0\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{ASC}}\)
and use the identical observed base \(p_0=p(P_0)\).  Under
\(\text{ass:causal-base-relative-interior}\) and
\(\text{ass:tangent-image-compatible}\),
\(\text{lem:relative-interior-tangent-lift}\) proves that every
direction \(Bu\) has a compatible small positive lift; no ambient
simplex neighborhood is invoked.  The proposition
\(\text{prop:tangent-fan-global-cover}\) then supplies closed rational
cells satisfying
\[
 \bigcup_{v\in V}D_v^0=\mathbb R^d.
\tag{29}
\]
Since \(p_0\in\Delta^\circ(\mathcal J)\) and \(B\) has column rank
\(d\), for every \(x\ne0\),
\[
 x'B'\operatorname{diag}(p_0)^{-1}Bx
 =\|\operatorname{diag}(p_0)^{-1/2}Bx\|_2^2>0.
\tag{30}
\]
Thus \(I_0\succ0\) and \(\Sigma_0=I_0^{-1}\succ0\).  For rational
\((p_0,B)\), both \(I_0\) and \(\Sigma_0\) are rational.  Proposition
\(\text{prop:job-corps-radius-nonempty}\) supplies the certified point
\(u_*\in H_{r_0}\), so the separated domain is nonempty.

The finite rational component description and semantic projection in
\(\text{def:label-fan}\), the global cover (29), positive-definite
rational covariance (30), the declared finite rational metric and
honesty level, and the nonempty separated domain verify the respective
membership conditions for the sample-size-free encoded Gaussian
experiment.  Hence \(\mathfrak G_0\in\mathfrak E\).  Every tangent
inequality used here is the affine directional slack obtained from a
compatible lift at \(p_0\); at no point was \(p_0+Bu\) treated as a
probability law.
