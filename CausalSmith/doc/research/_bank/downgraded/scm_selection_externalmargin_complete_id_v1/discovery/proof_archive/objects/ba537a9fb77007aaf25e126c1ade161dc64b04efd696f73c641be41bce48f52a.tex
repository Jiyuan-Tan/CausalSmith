Let \(S\) be terminal in \(\mathcal G\), suppose no bidirected edge of \(\mathcal G\) is incident to \(S\), and put \(G_V=\mathcal G[V]\).  Fix \(T_0\subseteq V\) and a target cell \((x,y)\in\mathcal I\).  Suppose a finite recursive semi-Markovian SCM \(N\) compatible with \(G_V\) has a finite independent response representation \(\mathcal R^{V}\), a coordinate \(U_0\) with active support \(\mathcal J_0\), strictly positive masses \(u_j\), substantive rows
\[
 c_v(j)=P_{\mathcal R^{V}}(V=v\mid U_0=j),
 \qquad
 h_{x,y}(j)=P_{\mathcal R^{V}}(Y=y\mid\operatorname{do}(X=x),U_0=j),
\]
and a vector \(\beta\in\mathbb R^{\mathcal J_0}\) such that
\[
 u^{\mathsf T}c_v>0\quad\text{for every }v,qquad
 \beta^{\mathsf T}\mathbf 1=0,qquad
 \beta^{\mathsf T}c_v=0\quad\text{for every }v,qquad
 \beta^{\mathsf T}h_{x,y}\neq0.
\]
These displayed conditions are the positive ordinary active-coordinate hedge-witness hypothesis.  Let \(\pi_S(1\mid p)\in(0,1)\) be a common terminal selection kernel for every cell \(p\) of \(\operatorname{pa}_{\mathcal G}(S)\), implemented by a finite exogenous response coordinate independent of \(\mathcal R^{V}\), and extend \(N\) by this same kernel.  Define
\[
 q_S(v)=
 \frac{\pi_S(1\mid v_{\operatorname{pa}_{\mathcal G}(S)})u^{\mathsf T}c_v}
 {\sum_{v'}\pi_S(1\mid v'_{\operatorname{pa}_{\mathcal G}(S)})u^{\mathsf T}c_{v'}},
 \qquad
 q_0(t)=\sum_{v:v_{T_0}=t}u^{\mathsf T}c_v.
\]
Then, for every \(\epsilon\) with \(0<\epsilon\leq\min_v q_S(v)\), this extension gives a compatible input \((q_S,q_0)\in\mathcal C_{\epsilon}(\mathcal G,T_0)\).  Moreover there are \(\eta>0\), a represented baseline \((M,\mathcal R,U_0)\in\mathfrak R_{\epsilon,\eta}^{\circ}(\mathcal G,T_0;q_S,q_0)\), and the same active direction \(\beta\in\Gamma_{(M,\mathcal R,U_0)}^{\perp}\) with \(\beta^{\mathsf T}h_{x,y}\neq0\).  Consequently the projective perturbation constructs two finite graph-compatible models \(M_1,M_2\in\mathfrak F_{\epsilon}(\mathcal G,T_0;q_S,q_0)\) that agree exactly on \(q_S,q_0\), have a common positive response-atom lower bound, and satisfy \(\psi_{x,y}(M_1)\neq\psi_{x,y}(M_2)\).
