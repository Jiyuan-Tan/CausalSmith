Qiu et al. (2026) consider a Euclidean covariate, a binary treatment, and a real-valued outcome observed through a selected observational law, together with an external unbiased covariate margin. Under unconfoundedness and treatment overlap, their Appendix B.2 Condition 2 is a pairwise distinguishability condition on specified classes of propensity scores, covariate--potential-outcome laws, and selection laws; their Theorem B.5 states that the full-population scalar average treatment effect is identifiable from the selected law and the unbiased covariate margin if and only if those classes satisfy that condition. The theorem's conclusion concerns that scalar average treatment effect, not every cell of an interventional outcome distribution or the complete interventional table. Their setup assumes continuous covariate--outcome laws with densities for exposition and includes continuous probability-family examples.
