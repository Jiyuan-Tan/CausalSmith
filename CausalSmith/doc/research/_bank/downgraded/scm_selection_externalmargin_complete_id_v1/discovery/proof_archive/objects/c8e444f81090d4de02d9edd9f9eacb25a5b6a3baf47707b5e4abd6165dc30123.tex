Let \(\mathcal G\) be any finite selection ADMG on \(V\cup\{S\}\), put \(G_V=\mathcal G[V]\), fix \(T_0\subseteq V\), and fix \((x,y)\in\mathcal I\). If \(\psi_{x,y}\) is identifiable from \((P_M(V\mid S=1),P_M(T_0))\) over the full class of positive finite recursive semi-Markovian models compatible with \(\mathcal G\), then \(\psi_{x,y}\) is identifiable from \(P_N(V)\) over the positive finite substantive model class compatible with \(G_V\). If \(S\) is terminal and \(T_0=V\), the converse also holds, without any restriction on bidirected edges incident to \(S\); hence on that boundary the two identification statements are equivalent. Moreover, for every \(\epsilon>0\), every compatible input \((q_S,q_0)\in\mathcal C_{\epsilon}(\mathcal G,V)\), and every ordinarily identifiable cell, \(\operatorname{EXT\text{-}ID}\) returns a finite arithmetic expression \(F_{x,y}\in\mathcal A(q_S,q_0)\) satisfying
\[
 F_{x,y}(q_S,q_0)=\psi_{x,y}(M)
\]
for every \(M\in\mathfrak F_{\epsilon}(\mathcal G,V;q_S,q_0)\). No converse from a bare \(\operatorname{FAIL}\) is asserted.
