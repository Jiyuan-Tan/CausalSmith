We prove the assertion by induction on the number of transitions from the initialized state in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}extid\text{-}state\text{-}machine}\).

At initialization, the selected key indexed by a full cell \(v\) has annotation
\[
 q_S(v)=P_M(V=v\mid S=1),
\]
where the equality follows from membership of \(M\) in the exact fiber.  If \(T_0\neq\varnothing\), the population key indexed by \(t\) has annotation
\[
 q_0(t)=P_M(T_0=t).
\]
These are precisely the kernels named by their selected and population source tags.  The initialized keys belong to \(\mathscr K_{\mathcal G}\), and each is assigned once with its prescribed initialization backpointer.

Assume the invariant before one transition.  For a marginalization transition, the induction hypothesis and finite additivity give, cell by cell,
\[
 \sum_{h\in\mathcal X_H}
 P_M(A=a,H=h\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,S_s)
 =P_M(A=a\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,S_s).
\]
For a conditioning transition, its required strict denominator condition and the induction hypothesis give
\[
 \frac{P_M(A=a,H=h\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,S_s)}
 {\sum_{a'\in\mathcal X_A}P_M(A=a',H=h\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,S_s)}
 =P_M(A=a\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,H=h,S_s).
\]
For a chain transition, the conditional product rule gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &P_M(A=a\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,S_s)\\
 &\qquad{}\times
 P_M(H=h\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,A=a,S_s)\\
 &=P_M(A=a,H=h\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,S_s).
 \end{aligned}
\]
All denominators invoked here are positive by the eligibility condition in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}probability\text{-}moves}\).

For any population or selected graphical-fixing transition, the relevant equality is exactly one of the sound do-calculus equivalences in Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}do\text{-}calculus\text{-}rules}\).  Its source tag is retained, and its conditioning cell is required to be positive.  For margin extension, Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}margin\text{-}extension}\) gives at every eligible \((t,z)\)
\[
 P_M(T_0=t,Z=z)
 =P_M(Z=z\mid T_0=t,S=1)q_0(t),
\]
which is the stored product.  Finally, for population storage the graphical premise \(A\perp\!\!\!\perp S\mid B,C\) in \(G_{\overline B}\), the positive selected conditioning cell, and Rule 1 of Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}do\text{-}calculus\text{-}rules}\) imply
\[
 P_M(A=a\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c,S=1)
 =P_M(A=a\mid\operatorname{do}(B=b),C=c).
\]
Thus every possible transition stores the model kernel named by its output key.

The transition definitions require their complete finite input-key sets to be present and their output to lie in \(\mathscr K_{\mathcal G}\).  Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}extid\text{-}state\text{-}machine}\) further requires that output to be absent, adds it once with one annotation and one backpointer, and preserves every old entry.  Therefore uniqueness and the inclusion \(K_\sigma\subseteq\mathscr K_{\mathcal G}\) are preserved.  The list of move tags shows directly that probability and graphical-fixing moves retain \(s\), while the two source-bridge moves are the only transitions with selected input and population output.  This completes the induction.
