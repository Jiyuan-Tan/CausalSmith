By the meaning of recursive semi-Markovian compatibility in \(\mathrm{ass{:}semi\text{-}markovian}\), expand \(\mathcal G\) to its canonical latent DAG \(\widetilde{\mathcal G}\): the main variables are \(V\cup\{S\}\), every disturbance is exogenous, and the independent disturbance coordinates encode exactly the bidirected and private exogenous inputs permitted by \(\mathcal G\).  This expansion is merely the defining latent-DAG realization of the ADMG and changes no structural equation or counterfactual variable.

The main-variable state spaces are finite by \(\mathrm{ass{:}finite\text{-}state}\).  Apply Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}finite\text{-}disturbance\text{-}full\text{-}law\text{-}reduction}\) to \(\widetilde{\mathcal G}\).  It replaces every disturbance cardinality by a sufficiently large finite one without changing the full data law.  In particular, it preserves the joint factual law \(P_M(V,S)\), the marginal \(P_M(T_0)\), and the joint law of every counterfactual outcome under \(\operatorname{do}(X=x)\), hence preserves \(\psi_{x,y}(M)\).

For each finite disturbance coordinate, two atom labels that induce the same deterministic response map at all of their graph-permitted children can be merged.  Because every observed parent configuration and every child state space is finite, there are only finitely many such response maps.  After merging, the independent disturbance law remains a product of finite coordinate-weight vectors, and the acyclic structural recursion is a deterministic function of their joint assignment.  Enumerating the finitely many nonempty support subsets of these finite response-map sets gives the claimed finite inventory of support strata.  Finally, if \(P_M(S=1)>0\), equality of \(P_M(V,S)\) gives
\[
 P_M(V=v\mid S=1)=\frac{P_M(V=v,S=1)}{\sum_{v'}P_M(V=v',S=1)},
\]
with a common positive denominator, so the selected conditional is preserved as well.  This proves the adaptation.
