For brevity set
\[
 A_0=u^{\mathsf T}a_+,
 \qquad N_v=u^{\mathsf T}a_v,
 \qquad \delta_+=\beta^{\mathsf T}a_+,
 \qquad \delta_v=\beta^{\mathsf T}a_v.
\]
The law of total probability over the positive-support atoms \(j\in\mathcal J_0\), using independence of \(U_0\) from the other exogenous coordinates in \(\mathcal R\), gives
\[
 P_M(V=v,S=1)=\sum_j u_j a_v(j)=N_v,
 \qquad P_M(S=1)=\sum_j u_j a_+(j)=A_0>0.
\]
Thus \(q_S(v)=N_v/A_0\).  The identical calculation for the margin and intervened response gives
\[
 q_0(t)=P_M(T_0=t)=u^{\mathsf T}b_t,
 \qquad
 \psi_{x,y}(M)=u^{\mathsf T}h_{x,y}.
\]

Because \(\Gamma_{(M,\mathcal R,U_0)}\) is a finite-dimensional subspace and \(h_{x,y}\notin\Gamma_{(M,\mathcal R,U_0)}\), subtracting the orthogonal projection of \(h_{x,y}\) onto \(\Gamma_{(M,\mathcal R,U_0)}\) produces a nonzero \(\beta\in\Gamma_{(M,\mathcal R,U_0)}^\perp\) with
\[
 \beta^{\mathsf T}h_{x,y}=\lVert\beta\rVert_2^2>0.
\]
Orthogonality to \(\mathbf 1\), to each \(b_t\), and to each selected cross-product row gives
\[
 \beta^{\mathsf T}\mathbf 1=0,
 \qquad \beta^{\mathsf T}b_t=0,
 \qquad A_0\delta_v-N_v\delta_+=0
 \quad\text{for every }t,v.
\]

The prescribed step-size bound is well defined because \(\beta\neq0\).  For every active atom,
\[
 u_j^\pm
 \geq u_j-\lvert\tau\rvert\lVert\beta\rVert_\infty
 \geq\eta/2>0,
 \qquad
 \sum_j u_j^\pm=1\pm\tau\beta^{\mathsf T}\mathbf 1=1.
\]
Hence both vectors are probability vectors on exactly the unchanged positive support \(\mathcal J_0\).  Every other exogenous atom retains mass at least \(\eta\), so every atom in each perturbed representation has mass at least \(\eta/2\).

Since \(A_0>0\), at least one \(a_+(j)>0\).  All \(u_j^\pm\) are positive and all \(a_+(j)\) are nonnegative, so
\[
 (u^\pm)^{\mathsf T}a_+=A_0\pm\tau\delta_+>0.
\]
For each \(v\), the cross-product equality above yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \frac{(u^\pm)^{\mathsf T}a_v}{(u^\pm)^{\mathsf T}a_+}
 &=\frac{N_v\pm\tau\delta_v}{A_0\pm\tau\delta_+}\\
 &=\frac{N_v}{A_0}=q_S(v),
 \end{aligned}
\]
where the middle equality follows after cross multiplication from \(A_0\delta_v=N_v\delta_+\).  Similarly,
\[
 (u^\pm)^{\mathsf T}b_t=u^{\mathsf T}b_t=q_0(t).
\]
Thus both perturbed models reproduce the two observed tables exactly, and in particular preserve every selected lower bound \(q_S(v)\geq\epsilon\).

Only the atom masses of one independent exogenous coordinate are changed; the deterministic response maps, their parent arguments, and every other independent exogenous law are unchanged.  Therefore both induced SCMs remain recursive, semi-Markovian, and compatible with \(\mathcal G\).  The preceding positivity facts place their represented triples in \(\mathfrak R_{\epsilon,\eta/2}^{\circ}(\mathcal G,T_0;q_S,q_0)\).  Finally,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \psi_{x,y}(M_1)-\psi_{x,y}(M_2)
 &=(u+\tau\beta)^{\mathsf T}h_{x,y}
 -(u-\tau\beta)^{\mathsf T}h_{x,y}\\
 &=2\tau\beta^{\mathsf T}h_{x,y}\neq0.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Each query is a convex combination of entries of \(h_{x,y}\in[0,1]^{\mathcal J_0}\), so it remains in \([0,1]\).  This proves every assertion.
