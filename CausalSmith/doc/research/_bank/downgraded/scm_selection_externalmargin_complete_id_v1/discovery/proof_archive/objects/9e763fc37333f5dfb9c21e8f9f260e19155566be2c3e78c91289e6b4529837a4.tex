The projective theorem completely reduces the desired witness to one finite-dimensional construction: for each failed exact fiber, it suffices to find one represented baseline and one active exogenous coordinate for which \(h_{x,y}\notin\Gamma_{(M,\mathcal R,U_0)}\).  The resolved three-node gate supplies such a baseline explicitly for \(\mathcal G_\star\), with \(q_S\) uniform, \(q_0\) uniform, \(\eta=1/4\), \(\beta=(0,0,1,-1)^{\mathsf T}\), and \(\beta^{\mathsf T}h_{1,1}=1\).  What remains unproved is the universal extraction of this active-coordinate separation from an arbitrary terminal key-closure obstruction.
