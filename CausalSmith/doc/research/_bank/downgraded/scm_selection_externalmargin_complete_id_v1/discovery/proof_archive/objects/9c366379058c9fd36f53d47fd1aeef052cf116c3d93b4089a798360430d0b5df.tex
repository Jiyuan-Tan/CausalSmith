Let \(U_W\) and \(U_A\) be independent fair binary coordinates, and let \(U_0\) be independent of them with active state space \(\mathcal J_0=\{1,2,3,4\}\) and masses \(u_j=1/4\).  Define
\[
 W_\star=U_W,
 \qquad A_\star=U_A,
\]
and, for \(j\in\mathcal J_0\), define
\[
 f_{Y_\star}(a,j)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&j=1,\\
 1,&j=2,\\
 a,&j=3,\\
 1-a,&j=4.
 \end{cases}
\qquad
 f_S(w,y,j)=
 \begin{cases}
 1,&j\in\{1,2\},\\
 0,&j\in\{3,4\}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Every structural function uses only a subset of its displayed parents in \(\mathcal G_\star\), and the only shared exogenous coordinate is \(U_0\), shared by \(Y_\star\) and \(S\).  Hence the SCM is recursive, semi-Markovian, and compatible with the directed and bidirected edges of \(\mathcal G_\star\).  Each coordinate state space is exactly its positive support, and every atom has mass at least \(1/4\).

Selection occurs exactly for types \(1,2\), so
\[
 P_M(S=1)=\frac12.
\]
Conditional on selection, \(W_\star\) and \(A_\star\) remain independent fair coins, while types \(1,2\) make \(Y_\star\) respectively \(0,1\) with equal conditional probability.  For every \((w,a,y)\in\{0,1\}^3\), therefore,
\[
 P_M(W_\star=w,A_\star=a,Y_\star=y,S=1)
 =\frac12\cdot\frac12\cdot\frac14=\frac1{16},
\]
and division by \(P_M(S=1)=1/2\) gives
\[
 q_S(w,a,y)=\frac18.
\]
Also \(q_0(w)=P_M(W_\star=w)=1/2\).  Thus \(q_S(v)\geq1/16\), the input belongs to \(\mathcal C_{1/16}(\mathcal G_\star,T_0)\), and the represented baseline belongs to \(\mathfrak R_{1/16,1/4}^{\circ}(\mathcal G_\star,T_0;q_S,q_0)\).

Order the active types as above and put
\[
 \beta=(0,0,1,-1)^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Integrating over the independent fair \(U_W,U_A\) gives
\[
 a_+=(1,1,0,0)^{\mathsf T},
 \qquad b_0=b_1=\frac12\mathbf 1.
\]
For a selected full cell with \(y=0\),
\[
 a_v=(1/4,0,0,0)^{\mathsf T},
\]
and for a selected full cell with \(y=1\),
\[
 a_v=(0,1/4,0,0)^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Since \(u^{\mathsf T}a_+=1/2\) and \(u^{\mathsf T}a_v=1/16\), every selected cross-product row is a scalar multiple of \((1,-1,0,0)^{\mathsf T}\).  Consequently \(\beta\) is orthogonal to \(\mathbf 1\), each \(b_t\), and every selected cross-product row, and therefore
\[
 \beta\in\Gamma_{(M,\mathcal R,U_0)}^\perp.
\]
For \((x,y)=(1,1)\), the intervention-response row is
\[
 h_{1,1}=(0,1,1,0)^{\mathsf T},
 \qquad
 \beta^{\mathsf T}h_{1,1}=1.
\]
Thus \(h_{1,1}\notin\Gamma_{(M,\mathcal R,U_0)}\).

Take \(\tau=1/8=(1/4)/(2\lVert\beta\rVert_\infty)\).  Theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}projective\text{-}nullspace\text{-}certificate}\) applies, with perturbed mass vectors
\[
 u^+=(1/4,1/4,3/8,1/8),
 \qquad
 u^-=(1/4,1/4,1/8,3/8).
\]
They preserve the displayed selected joint law and \(W_\star\)-margin, while direct evaluation gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P_{M_1}(Y_\star=1\mid\operatorname{do}(A_\star=1))
 &=u_2^++u_3^+=\frac58,\\
 P_{M_2}(Y_\star=1\mid\operatorname{do}(A_\star=1))
 &=u_2^-+u_3^-=\frac38.
 \end{aligned}
\]
If \(\operatorname{EXT\text{-}ID}\) returned an arithmetic functional at this input, Theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}extid\text{-}sound\text{-}termination}\) would force that one value to equal both \(5/8\) and \(3/8\), a contradiction.  The state machine terminates and has only the two terminal outputs specified in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}extid\text{-}state\text{-}machine}\), so its output here is \(\operatorname{FAIL}\).  This proves the claimed exact interior witness.
