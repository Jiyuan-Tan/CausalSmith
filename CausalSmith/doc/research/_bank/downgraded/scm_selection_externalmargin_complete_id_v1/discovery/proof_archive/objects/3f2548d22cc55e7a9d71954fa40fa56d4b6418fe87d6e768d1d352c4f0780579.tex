Every recursive semi-Markovian SCM compatible with a finite-state selection ADMG \(\mathcal G\) has, with respect to the full joint law of all factual and counterfactual variables on \(V\cup\{S\}\), an equivalent graph-compatible representation with finitely many independent exogenous coordinates of finite cardinality.  The representation can be written using a finite graph- and state-space-determined inventory of deterministic response maps and a product of finite coordinate-weight simplices.  It preserves \(P_M(V,S)\), every margin \(P_M(T_0)\), and every intervention cell \(\psi_{x,y}(M)\); when \(P_M(S=1)>0\), it therefore also preserves \(q_S^M=P_M(V\mid S=1)\).
