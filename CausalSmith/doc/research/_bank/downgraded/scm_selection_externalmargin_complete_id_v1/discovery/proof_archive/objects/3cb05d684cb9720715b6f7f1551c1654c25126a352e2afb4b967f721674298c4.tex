At fixed nonrestrictive finite disturbance cardinalities, a canonical graph-compatible response point \(\vartheta\) selects one of the finitely many active-support strata of deterministic response maps allowed by \(\mathcal G\).  On each of its finitely many independent exogenous coordinates, indexed by \(\ell\), it has a nonempty active set \(\mathcal J_\ell\) and weights \(w_{\ell j}>0\) satisfying \(\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_\ell}w_{\ell j}=1\).  For an exogenous assignment \(\omega\in\prod_\ell\mathcal J_\ell\), let the acyclic recursion determine \(V_\vartheta(\omega),S_\vartheta(\omega)\), and the intervened value \(Y_{\vartheta,x}(\omega)\).  Define the explicit multi-affine polynomials
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 p_\vartheta(v,1)
 &=\sum_{\omega:\,V_\vartheta(\omega)=v,\,S_\vartheta(\omega)=1}
      \prod_\ell w_{\ell,\omega_\ell},\\
 m_\vartheta(t)
 &=\sum_{\omega:\,(V_\vartheta(\omega))_{T_0}=t}
      \prod_\ell w_{\ell,\omega_\ell},\\
 g_{\vartheta,x,y}
 &=\sum_{\omega:\,Y_{\vartheta,x}(\omega)=y}
      \prod_\ell w_{\ell,\omega_\ell},
 \qquad
 \rho_\vartheta=\sum_vp_\vartheta(v,1).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The formula \(\operatorname{RF}_{x,y}(\mathcal G,T_0;q_S,q_0)\) is the following finite disjunction over ordered pairs of canonical active-support strata: there exist response points \(\vartheta^{(1)},\vartheta^{(2)}\) in the chosen strata and one \(\eta>0\) such that, for \(i=1,2\),
\[
 w^{(i)}_{\ell j}\geq\eta,qquad
 \sum_{j\in\mathcal J^{(i)}_\ell}w^{(i)}_{\ell j}=1,qquad
 \rho_{\vartheta^{(i)}}>0,
\]
for every coordinate \(\ell\) and active atom \(j\), and
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 p_{\vartheta^{(i)}}(v,1)&=q_S(v)\rho_{\vartheta^{(i)}}
 &&\text{for every }v,\\
 m_{\vartheta^{(i)}}(t)&=q_0(t)
 &&\text{for every }t,\\
 g_{\vartheta^{(1)},x,y}&\neq g_{\vartheta^{(2)},x,y}.&&
 \end{aligned}
\]
All support choices, deterministic response maps, sums, and products in this formula are finite and graph-determined.  Thus \(\operatorname{RF}_{x,y}\) is an explicit finite semialgebraic feasibility formula in the two observed tables; it is a specification of the witness constraints, not an assertion that an exhaustive solver run has been executed.
