## Lemmas (L)

**L-1 (For every feasible treated-count table…).** For every feasible treated-count table \(0\le r_{q,a}\le q\), including boundary counts and menus constant in \(a\), every joint design \(D\in\Delta(\mathcal X)\) and measurable rule \(\delta\) can be averaged over \(\prod_{g\in\mathcal G}\mathfrak S_{s_g}\) and then conditioned on the assignment to produce a design-rule pair whose worst-case risk is no larger, whose saturation-label law may retain arbitrary cross-cluster dependence, and whose treated subset in each cluster is uniform conditional on its saturation label and independent across clusters conditional on the label vector. Consequently imposing this conditional uniformity does not change \(R^\star\).

**L-2 (Fix \(g\in\mathcal G\) and \(a\in\mathcal A\), put…).** Fix \(g\in\mathcal G\) and \(a\in\mathcal A\), put \(q=s_g\), and suppose \(q\ge 3\) and \(1\le r_{q,a}<q\). Let \[ h=\max\{r_{q,a},q-r_{q,a}\}, \qquad z_\star=\begin{cases} 1,&r_{q,a}\ge q-r_{q,a},\\ 0,&r_{q,a}<q-r_{q,a}. \end{cases} \] There are \(h+1\) distinct coordinates \(e_j=(g,i_j,z_\star,a)\) such that every \(h\)-element subcollection, and hence every pair, is contained in \(\mathcal O(X)\) for some \(X\in\mathcal X\), whereas the full \(h+1\)-element collection is contained in no \(\mathcal O(X)\). Consequently, the compatibility complex \[ \bigl\{T:T\subseteq\mathcal O(X)\text{ for some }X\in\mathcal X\bigr\} \] is not the independent-set complex of any pairwise conflict graph. The exact orbit kernel \(H\) preserves this higher-order obstruction in the precise sense that its coefficients aggregate only feasible exact-count local observations and contain no term obtained by replacing their revealed-coordinate sets with a pairwise closure.
