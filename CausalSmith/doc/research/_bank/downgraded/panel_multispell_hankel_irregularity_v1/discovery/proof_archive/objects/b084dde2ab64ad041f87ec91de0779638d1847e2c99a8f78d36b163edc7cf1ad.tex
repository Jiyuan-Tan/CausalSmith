For the finite-support law, separation from \(\mathcal Z\) and finiteness of the mover support give \(\sup H_k<\infty\). At every mover support point \(M\) has full column rank, and therefore the distributed-lag equation and conditional-mean-zero restriction imply
\[
E[e_k'M(X)^+(\Delta Y-\Gamma)\mid X]
=E[e_k'M(X)^+M(X)B\mid X]
=E[B_k\mid X].
\]
Averaging over movers identifies \(\tau_k(P^{\mathrm{fs}})\). Bounded support and the assumed moments give finite score variance, so independence gives variance \(O(G^{-1})\) for the sample average. Chebyshev's inequality with a fixed variance bound gives an honest interval of radius and expected length \(O(G^{-1/2})\).

For \(K=0,T=2\), \(M(x)=(x)\), \(H_0(x)=|x|^{-1}\) off zero, and \(\mathcal Z=\{0\}\). Thus a density bounded above and away from zero near zero gives
\[
P(H_0>C\mid X\ne0)\asymp C^{-1},
\qquad
\mathcal J_{\Sigma,0,s}(C)
\asymp\int_{|x|<c/C}|x|^s\,dx
\asymp C^{-(s+1)}.
\]
The nearest retained anchor lies at distance comparable to \(C^{-1}-|x|\), so direct integration gives the same order for \(\mathcal D_{\Sigma,0,s}(C)\). Setting \(s=1\) proves \((\alpha,m,\zeta,\ell)=(1,0,2,0)\). Since \(\overline V(C)\lesssim C\) and the corrected bias is at most a constant times \(C^{-2}\), the choice \(C=G^{1/5}\) gives
\[
G^{-1}\overline V(C)+\overline{\mathfrak B}(C)^2
\lesssim G^{-4/5}.
\]
Without extrapolation, the omitted tail has worst-case upper bias \(O(C^{-1})\), and \(C=G^{1/3}\) gives \(O(G^{-2/3})\). Positive lower bounds of orders \(C^{-2}\) and \(C^{-1}\) on the respective uniform bias envelopes make these balances two-sided; without that richness, the established zero-coefficient witness precludes equality uniformly over the frozen class.

Finally, the early-kill proposition gives \((\alpha,m)=(2,0)\) for both \(K=1,T=5\) coordinates. The critical second-moment integral is \(O(\log C)\), and the proposed matched-frontier theorem gives \(R_{G,\Sigma,k}^{\star}\lesssim G^{-1}\log G\). If a nonzero frontier modulus forces the cutoff to diverge, the variance lower bound and the same integral give the reverse order. These are precisely the qualifications in the proposed statement.
