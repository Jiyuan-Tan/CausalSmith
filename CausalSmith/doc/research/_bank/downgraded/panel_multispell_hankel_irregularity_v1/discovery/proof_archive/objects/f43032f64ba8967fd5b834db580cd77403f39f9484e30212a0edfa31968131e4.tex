The total-measurability assertion and the deterministic inequalities \(\overline{\mathfrak B}_{\Sigma,k,s}(C)\le C_{\Sigma,s}L_s\mathcal D_{\Sigma,k,s}(C)\le C'_{\Sigma,s}L_s\mathcal J_{\Sigma,k,s}(C)\) follow from the selector lemma. The generated-\(\Gamma\) error in the directly truncated average is also at most a constant multiple of \(\{\overline V_{\Sigma,k}(C)/G\}^{1/2}\) by Cauchy--Schwarz and the regular-\(\Gamma\) lemma. The unresolved step is the asserted uniform local-polynomial fitting rate. At a smooth codimension-one \(K=1,T=4\) frontier with \(d=3\), \(s=1\), \(\alpha=1\), and \(\zeta=2\), the oracle scale has \(C\asymp G^{1/5}\), whereas the frozen bandwidth is \(h\asymp G^{-3/25}(\log G)^{1/5}\), larger than the anchor normal scale \(C^{-1}=G^{-1/5}\). For a conditional mean affine in the active normal coordinate, the one-sided local-constant fit has bias of order \(h\); multiplying by tail mass \(C^{-1}\) gives \(G^{-8/25}(\log G)^{1/5}\), which is larger than \(R_{G,\Sigma,k}^{\star 1/2}\asymp G^{-2/5}\). The proposed certified-radius cap repairs this explicit crossing bias, but a uniform proof still needs an anisotropic occupancy and integrated-Riesz bound at all higher-rank and boundary cells; those properties are not consequences of the current normal-link record.
