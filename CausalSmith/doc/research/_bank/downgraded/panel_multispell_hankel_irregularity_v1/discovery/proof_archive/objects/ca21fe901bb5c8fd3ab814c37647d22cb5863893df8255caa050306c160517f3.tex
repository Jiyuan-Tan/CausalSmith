The construction has three literal reductions. First, let \(P^{\mathrm{fs}}\) be a separate law with independent groups, the distributed-lag equation and conditional-mean-zero restriction, positive stayer mass, bounded moments, and finitely many eligible mover histories, every one separated from \(\mathcal Z\). This discrete law is not an element of the absolutely continuous class \(\mathcal P_\Sigma\). Under \(P^{\mathrm{fs}}\), \(H_k\) is bounded on the mover support, the untrimmed Moore--Penrose score identifies \(\tau_k(P^{\mathrm{fs}})\), and the tuning-free finite-support estimator of deChaisemartinDHa2025 has squared risk of order \(G^{-1}\) and Wald expected length of order \(G^{-1/2}\) under nondegenerate score variance. Second, inside the continuous scalar class with \(K=0\), \(T=2\), mover density positive at zero, and \(H_0(x)\asymp|x|^{-1}\), \[\mathcal J_{\Sigma,0,s}(C)\asymp\mathcal D_{\Sigma,0,s}(C)\asymp\int_{|x|<c/C}|x|^s\,dx\asymp C^{-(s+1)}.\] Hence \((\alpha_{\Sigma,0},m_{\Sigma,0},\zeta_{\Sigma,0,1},\ell_{\Sigma,0,1})=(1,0,2,0)\), \(C_{G,\Sigma,0}^{\star}\asymp G^{1/5}\), and \(R_{G,\Sigma,0}^{\star}\asymp G^{-4/5}\) under first-order Whitney extrapolation. Disabling that correction gives bias order \(C^{-1}\), cutoff \(G^{1/3}\), and risk \(G^{-2/3}\), the GrahamPowell2012 scalar slow-mover benchmark under \(C=1/h\). Third, if \(K=1\), \(T=5\) near \(x^\star\), then \(Z_k(z)\) has its first positive pole at \(z=2\) with order one for both targets; the selector-distance bound therefore retains \(R_{G,\Sigma,k}^{\star}\asymp G^{-1}\log G\).
