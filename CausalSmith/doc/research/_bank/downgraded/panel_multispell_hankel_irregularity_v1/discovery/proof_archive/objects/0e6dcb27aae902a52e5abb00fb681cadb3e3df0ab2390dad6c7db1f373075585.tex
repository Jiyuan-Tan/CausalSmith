For every full-column-rank history \(x\), \[H_k(x)^2=e_k'(M(x)'M(x))^{-1}e_k=\frac{A_k(x)}{Q(x)}.\] At a smooth rank-\((p-1)\) point \(x_0\in\mathcal Z\) with unit right-kernel vector \(v_0\), target activity \(e_k'v_0\ne0\) implies \(H_k(x)\asymp |e_k'v_0|/\sigma_p(M(x))\) on a sufficiently small conic neighborhood. On any incident exceptional divisor for which the valuation of \(A_k\) is at least that of \(Q\), the globally defined \(H_k\) remains locally bounded; this is target-inactive cancellation.
