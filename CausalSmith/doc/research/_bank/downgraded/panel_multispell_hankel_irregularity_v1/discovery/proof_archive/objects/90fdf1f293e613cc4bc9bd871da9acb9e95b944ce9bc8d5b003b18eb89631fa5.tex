On \(\{Q>0\}\), conditional mean zero and full column rank give
\[
e_k'M(X)^+(\Delta Y-\Gamma)=B_k+e_k'M(X)^+\varepsilon.
\]
The \(q\)-moment bound, the conditional variance upper bound, and \(H_k=\|(M')^+e_k\|_2\) imply
\[
\overline V_{\Sigma,k}(C)
\le C_0\left(1+\int_0^C2u\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_\Sigma}P\{H_k(X)>u\mid X\ne0\}\,du\right).
\]
The tail atlas therefore gives
\[
\overline V_{\Sigma,k}(C)\lesssim
\begin{cases}
1,&\alpha_{\Sigma,k}>2,\\
(\log C)^{m_{\Sigma,k}+1},&\alpha_{\Sigma,k}=2,\\
C^{v_{\Sigma,k}}(\log C)^{m_{\Sigma,k}},&0<\alpha_{\Sigma,k}<2.
\end{cases}
\]
The selector lemma and the density upper bound give
\[
\overline{\mathfrak B}_{\Sigma,k,s}(C)
\lesssim C^{-\zeta_{\Sigma,k,s}}(\log C)^{\ell_{\Sigma,k,s}}.
\]

If \(\alpha_{\Sigma,k}>2\), the tail integral shows \(\overline V_{\Sigma,k}(\infty)<\infty\); choosing \(C=\infty\) gives \(R^star\lesssim G^{-1}\). If \(\alpha_{\Sigma,k}=2\), choose a polynomial cutoff from the corrected grid large enough that its squared bias is no larger than \(G^{-1}(\log G)^{m_{\Sigma,k}+1}\). Its variance has that order as well.

For \(0<\alpha_{\Sigma,k}<2\), set \(v=v_{\Sigma,k}\), \(\zeta=\zeta_{\Sigma,k,s}\), \(m=m_{\Sigma,k}\), and \(\ell=\ell_{\Sigma,k,s}\). Balancing the two displayed bounds gives
\[
C^{v+2\zeta}=G(\log C)^{2\ell-m},
\]
so a geometric-grid point within a factor \(a_0\) of
\[
C_G=G^{1/(v+2\zeta)}(\log G)^{(2\ell-m)/(v+2\zeta)}
\]
is available under the corrected degree cap. Substitution yields
\[
G^{-1}C_G^v(\log C_G)^m
\asymp C_G^{-2\zeta}(\log C_G)^{2\ell}
\asymp
G^{-2\zeta/(v+2\zeta)}
(\log G)^{2(\zeta m+\ell v)/(v+2\zeta)}.
\]
Since \(C_{G,\Sigma,k}^{\star}\) minimizes the criterion, this proves the proposed one-sided phase bound. No step supplies a lower bound for the supremum bias envelope or controls the frozen local fit, so the proof deliberately makes neither claim.
