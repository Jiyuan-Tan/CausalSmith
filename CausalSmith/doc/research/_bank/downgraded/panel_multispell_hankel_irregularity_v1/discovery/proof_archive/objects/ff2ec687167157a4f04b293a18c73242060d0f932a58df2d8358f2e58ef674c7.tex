Let \(\mathcal X_{\mathrm m}\) be the finite mover support under \(P^{\mathrm{fs}}\). Separation from \(\mathcal Z\) gives \(Q(x)>0\) for every \(x\in\mathcal X_{\mathrm m}\). Hence each \(M(x)\) has full column rank, and finiteness gives
\[
\overline H_k^{\mathrm{fs}}:=\max_{x\in\mathcal X_{\mathrm m}}H_k(x)<\infty.
\]
The distributed-lag equation and conditional-mean-zero restriction imply, for every eligible mover history,
\[
E_{P^{\mathrm{fs}}}[e_k'M(X)^+(\Delta Y-\Gamma)\mid X]
=E_{P^{\mathrm{fs}}}[e_k'M(X)^+M(X)B\mid X]
=E_{P^{\mathrm{fs}}}[B_k\mid X].
\]
Averaging this equality conditional on \(X\ne0\) identifies \(\tau_k(P^{\mathrm{fs}})\) from the oracle untrimmed score.

It remains to verify feasibility when \(\Gamma\) is unknown. On a training fold, use the total estimator from \(\text{def:gamma-estimator}\). Because \(M(0)=0\), one has \(\Pi(M(0))=I_r\), and therefore
\[
E_{P^{\mathrm{fs}}}[\Pi(M(X))]\succeq P^{\mathrm{fs}}(X=0)I_r.
\]
The independent-group and fixed second-moment conditions permit the argument of \(\text{lem:regular-gamma}\), including its threshold fallback, and yield
\[
\|\widehat\Gamma-\Gamma\|_{L^2(P^{\mathrm{fs}})}\le C G^{-1/2}.
\]
On an independent evaluation fold with \(n_{mathrm m}>0\) movers, the score change caused by estimating \(\Gamma\) is
\[
T_\Gamma=-\left\{n_{\mathrm m}^{-1}\sum_{g:X_g\ne0}e_k'M(X_g)^+\right\}(\widehat\Gamma-\Gamma).
\]
The bracketed vector has Euclidean norm at most \(\overline H_k^{\mathrm{fs}}\). Fold independence thus gives
\[
\|T_\Gamma\|_{L^2(P^{\mathrm{fs}})}
\le \overline H_k^{\mathrm{fs}}\|\widehat\Gamma-\Gamma\|_{L^2(P^{\mathrm{fs}})}
=O(G^{-1/2}).
\]
The oracle score has finite variance because \(\overline H_k^{\mathrm{fs}}<\infty\) and \(B\) and \(\varepsilon\) have bounded second moments. Since the mover probability is positive, the inverse binomial moment is \(O(G^{-1})\), while the event \(n_{\mathrm m}=0\) has exponentially small probability. Thus the oracle evaluation average has root mean square \(O(G^{-1/2})\). Combining it with the displayed generated-trend bound proves squared risk \(O(G^{-1})\) for the cross-fitted feasible estimator. If \(C_{\mathrm{fs}}/G\) is its fixed uniform mean-squared-error bound, then
\[
\mathcal C_{G,k}^{\mathrm{fs}}
=\left[\widehat\tau_k^{\mathrm{fs}}-\sqrt{C_{\mathrm{fs}}/(\eta G)},
\widehat\tau_k^{\mathrm{fs}}+\sqrt{C_{\mathrm{fs}}/(\eta G)}\right]
\]
has coverage at least \(1-\eta\) by Markov's inequality applied to squared error, and its expected length is \(2\sqrt{C_{\mathrm{fs}}/(\eta G)}=O(G^{-1/2})\).

For \(K=0,T=2\), \(M(x)=(x)\), \(H_0(x)=|x|^{-1}\) off zero, and \(\mathcal Z=\{0\}\). Thus a density bounded above and away from zero near zero gives
\[
P(H_0>C\mid X\ne0)\asymp C^{-1},
\qquad
\mathcal J_{\Sigma,0,s}(C)
\asymp\int_{|x|<c/C}|x|^s\,dx
\asymp C^{-(s+1)}.
\]
The nearest retained anchor lies at distance comparable to \(C^{-1}-|x|\), so direct integration gives the same order for \(\mathcal D_{\Sigma,0,s}(C)\). Setting \(s=1\) proves \((\alpha,m,\zeta,\ell)=(1,0,2,0)\). Since \(\overline V(C)\lesssim C\) and the corrected bias is at most a constant times \(C^{-2}\), the choice \(C=G^{1/5}\) gives
\[
G^{-1}\overline V(C)+\overline{\mathfrak B}(C)^2
\lesssim G^{-4/5}.
\]
Without extrapolation, the omitted tail has worst-case upper bias \(O(C^{-1})\), and \(C=G^{1/3}\) gives \(O(G^{-2/3})\). Positive lower bounds of orders \(C^{-2}\) and \(C^{-1}\) on the respective uniform bias envelopes make these balances two-sided; without that richness, the established zero-coefficient witness precludes equality uniformly over the frozen class.

Finally, the early-kill proposition gives \((\alpha,m)=(2,0)\) for both \(K=1,T=5\) coordinates. The critical second-moment integral is \(O(\log C)\), and the proposed matched-frontier theorem gives \(R_{G,\Sigma,k}^{\star}\lesssim G^{-1}\log G\). If a nonzero frontier modulus forces the cutoff to diverge, the variance lower bound and the same integral give the reverse order. These are precisely the qualifications in the proposed statement.
