Take \(T=2\), \(K=0\), \(S=[2,3]\), and let the mover density be one on \(S\). Fix \(0<\pi<1\), set \(\underline\pi=\overline\pi=\pi\), set \(\underline\sigma=\overline\sigma=\sigma>0\), and set \(C_q=(1-\pi)\sigma^q\). The signature is certified because \(M(x)=(x)\) has full rank throughout \(S\), which is a rational interval. A member of the class is obtained by taking \(P(X=0)=\pi\), the stated uniform mover law, \(B=0\), and, conditionally on moving, \(\varepsilon=\sigma\) or \(-\sigma\) with equal probabilities, while \(\varepsilon=0\) for stayers.

For any law in this same fixed-envelope class, the conditional variance lower bound and Jensen's inequality give
\[
E_P[|\varepsilon|^q\mid X=x]
\ge \{E_P[\varepsilon^2\mid X=x]\}^{q/2}
\ge\sigma^q
\]
for mover-almost every \(x\). Hence
\[
C_q\ge E_P[|B|^q+|\varepsilon|^q]
\ge E_P|B|^q+(1-\pi)\sigma^q=C_q+E_P|B|^q.
\]
Thus \(B=0\) almost surely for every law in the class, so \(\tau_0=0\). The constant estimator zero has zero squared risk and the singleton interval \([0,0]\) has coverage one and length zero. Therefore
\[
\operatorname{Risk}_{G,0}(\Sigma)=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{Len}_{G,0}(\Sigma,\eta)=0.
\]
On the other hand \(H_0(x)=x^{-1}\le1/2\) on \(S\), so every cutoff in the grid leaves the score untrimmed and the bias envelope is zero. The conditional variance equality gives
\[
V_{P,0}(C)=\sigma^2\int_2^3x^{-2},dx=\frac{\sigma^2}{6}
\]
for every grid cutoff, and hence
\[
R_{G,\Sigma,0}^{\star}=\frac{\sigma^2}{6G}>0.
\]
This is the claimed nonempty certified witness.
