In the distributed-lag reduced-form experiment of de Chaisemartin and D'Haultf\oe{}uille, the lag order obeys \(K<T-1\), \(E[\lVert B\rVert_2^2]<\infty\), and
\[
\Delta Y=\Gamma+MB+\varepsilon,
\qquad E[\varepsilon\mid M]=0.
\]
Their design condition is invertibility of \(E[\Pi(M)]\). For every coordinate \(k\) with \(P\{e_k\in\operatorname{Im}(M')\}>0\), they define the component-specific eligible mean
\[
\overline\beta_k=E[B_k\mid e_k\in\operatorname{Im}(M')]
\]
and Theorem 4 identifies it by
\[
\overline\beta_k
=\lim_{C\uparrow\infty}
E\!\left[e_k'M^+(\Delta Y-\Gamma)
\mathrel{\big|}e_k\in\operatorname{Im}(M'),\ \lVert(M')^+e_k\rVert_2\le C\right].
\]
Eligibility is coordinate specific. Section 4.2 also permits overidentified designs in which the projector condition holds without a stayer atom; in particular, it gives a no-long-enough-stayer example for \(K=1\) and \(T=5\), and explains why such cases may occur when \(T>2(K+1)\).
