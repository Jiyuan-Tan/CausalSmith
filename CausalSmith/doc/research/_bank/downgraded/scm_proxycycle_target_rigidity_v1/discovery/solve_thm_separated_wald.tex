Let \(H\) be a compact subset of \(\mathfrak R_{\varepsilon}\).  All uniform probability statements below are over observable laws \(\zeta\in H\).  Under \(\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}multinomial\text{-}sampling}\), the reduced selected-table coordinates are sample means of bounded categorical vectors.  In design A with estimated margins, the reduced margin coordinates are sample means of an independent bounded categorical vector, and \(\mathrm{ass:margin\text{-}sample\text{-}scaling}\) gives \(n/n_m\to\lambda\in(0,\infty)\).  Consequently, for some finite constant \(C_H\),
\[
\sup_{\zeta\in H}
 \mathbb E_{\zeta}\!\left[\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2^2\right]
 \le \frac{C_H}{n}.
\tag{1}
\]
When the margins are known, their coordinates are nonrandom and the corresponding summand in (1) is zero.  Markov's inequality applied to (1) implies, for every \(\delta>0\),
\[
\sup_{\zeta\in H}
\mathbb P_{\zeta}\!\left(\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2>\delta\right)
\le \frac{C_H}{n\delta^2},
\qquad
\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2=O_{\mathbb P}(n^{-1/2})
\ \text{uniformly on }H.
\tag{2}
\]

The covariance of the selected-table part of
\(\sqrt n(\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta)\) is
\(\operatorname{diag}(p^-)-p^-p^{-\top}\).  Independence of the margin sample and the equality \(n/n_m\to\lambda\) give the additional block
\(\lambda\{\operatorname{diag}(m^-)-m^-m^{-\top}\}\) in design A; that block is absent for known margins.  These are exactly the blocks defining \(\Omega_{\zeta}\).  Thus \(\mathrm{lem:multinomial\text{-}clt\text{-}and\text{-}delta}\) gives, at every \(\zeta\in H\),
\[
\sqrt n(\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta)
\ \Rightarrow\ N(0,\Omega_{\zeta}).
\tag{3}
\]
The convergence is uniform on \(H\).  To verify this rather than assume it, let \(\zeta_n\) be any sequence in \(H\).  Compactness gives a subsequence converging to some \(\zeta_\star\in H\).  Write \(\Omega^{(p)}_{\zeta_n}\) for the selected-table block of \(\Omega_{\zeta_n}\).  For a centered selected-table categorical summand \(\xi_{n,k}\), uniformly for fixed \(u\),
\[
\mathbb E_{\zeta_n}
 \exp\!\left\{i u^\top\xi_{n,k}/\sqrt n\right\}
=1-\frac{u^\top\Omega^{(p)}_{\zeta_n}u}{2n}
O(n^{-3/2}).
\tag{4}
\]
The same expansion holds for the independent margin block after its deterministic relative-rate rescaling.  Raising (4) to the appropriate sample sizes, multiplying the independent characteristic functions, and using \(n/n_m\to\lambda\) yields the characteristic function of \(N(0,\Omega_{\zeta_n})\), with a remainder tending to zero along the subsequence.  The subsequence criterion for uniform convergence then proves the asserted uniform version of (3).  Since the plug-in covariance map is polynomial in the empirical probabilities and \(n/n_m\to\lambda\), (2) and the continuous-mapping part of \(\mathrm{lem:multinomial\text{-}clt\text{-}and\text{-}delta}\) also give
\[
\widehat\Omega_n\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}\Omega_{\zeta}
\quad\text{uniformly on }H.
\tag{5}
\]

We next prove selection of a valid chart.  By \(\mathrm{def:separated\text{-}rigid\text{-}stratum}\), for each \(\zeta\in H\) the incident set
\[
\Gamma(\zeta)
=\{\chi:d_{\chi}(\zeta)=0,\ \eta_{\chi}(\zeta)\ge\varepsilon\}
\tag{6}
\]
is nonempty.  Distance to a closed set is one-Lipschitz, so every \(\chi\in\Gamma(\zeta)\) satisfies
\[
d_{\chi}(\widehat\zeta_n)
\le \|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2.
\tag{7}
\]
There are only finitely many atlas labels.  On the incident chart domains, their normalized slack functions have the continuous local extensions required by \(\mathrm{def:separated\text{-}rigid\text{-}stratum}\).  Compactness of \(H\), finiteness of the labels, and \(\eta_{\chi}(\zeta)\ge\varepsilon\) therefore yield a common \(\delta_H>0\) such that
\[
\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2<\delta_H
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\eta_{\chi}(\widehat\zeta_n)\ge\varepsilon/2
\quad\text{for every }\chi\in\Gamma(\zeta).
\tag{8}
\]
Since \(t_n=n^{-1/4}\), equations (2), (7), and (8) give
\[
\sup_{\zeta\in H}
\mathbb P_{\zeta}\!\left(
\text{no }\chi\in\Gamma(\zeta)\text{ passes both selector inequalities}
\right)
\le
\sup_{\zeta\in H}
\mathbb P_{\zeta}\!\left(
\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2>\min(t_n,\delta_H)
\right)
=O(n^{-1/2})=o(1).
\tag{9}
\]
More explicitly, one may choose any one member of the nonempty finite set \(\Gamma(\zeta)\); (7), (8), and the event
\(\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2\le \min(t_n,\delta_H)\) show that this member passes both selector inequalities.

Conversely, the definition of \(\mathfrak R_{\varepsilon}\) places the closure of every first-order-incompatible chart at distance at least \(\varepsilon\) from \(\zeta\).  The reverse one-Lipschitz inequality gives
\[
d_{\chi}(\widehat\zeta_n)
\ge d_{\chi}(\zeta)-\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2
\ge\varepsilon-\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2.
\tag{10}
\]
On the event \(\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2<\varepsilon/2\), the right side of (10) exceeds \(t_n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Hence no incompatible chart can pass the selector.  The exhaustive certification in \(\mathrm{thm:full\text{-}rigidity\text{-}atlas}\), together with (8)--(10) and the lexicographic rule in \(\mathrm{def:wald\text{-}estimator}\), proves
\[
\sup_{\zeta\in H}
\mathbb P_{\zeta}\!\left(
\widehat\chi_n=\bot
\ \text{or }\widehat\chi_n
\text{ is not an incident first-order-compatible rigid chart}
\right)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{11}
\]

On the event complementary to (11), rigidity and the atlas theorem give
\[
F_{\widehat\chi_n}(\zeta)=\tau,
\qquad
J_{\widehat\chi_n}(\zeta)=J(\zeta),
\tag{12}
\]
where \(J(\zeta)\) denotes the common Jacobian of all incident first-order-compatible charts.  The twice continuously differentiable extensions required in \(\mathfrak R_{\varepsilon}\), the finiteness of the atlas, and compactness of \(H\) give a common finite bound \(M_H\) for the operator norms of their Hessians on a fixed neighborhood of \(H\).  Applying the fundamental theorem of calculus twice along the segment from \(\zeta\) to \(\widehat\zeta_n\), then using (2) and (12), gives, uniformly on \(H\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\tau_n-\tau
&=J(\zeta)(\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta)+R_n,\\
\|R_n\|_2
&\le \frac{M_H}{2}\|\widehat\zeta_n-\zeta\|_2^2,
\qquad
\sqrt n\,R_n=o_{\mathbb P}(1).
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
Combining (3) and (13) proves
\[
\sqrt n(\widehat\tau_n-\tau)
\ \Rightarrow\
N\!\left(0,J(\zeta)\Omega_{\zeta}J(\zeta)^\top\right)
=N(0,V_{\tau}),
\tag{14}
\]
with the same compact-uniform interpretation as (3).

Continuity of the chart derivatives on their extensions, (2), and (5) show that the raw sandwich matrix
\[
\widetilde V_n
:=
J_{\widehat\chi_n}(\widehat\zeta_n)
\widehat\Omega_n
J_{\widehat\chi_n}(\widehat\zeta_n)^\top
\]
satisfies
\[
\widetilde V_n\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}V_{\tau}
\quad\text{uniformly on }H.
\tag{15}
\]
By the nonsingularity condition in \(\mathrm{def:separated\text{-}rigid\text{-}stratum}\),
\(\lambda_{\min}(V_{\tau})\ge\varepsilon\).  For every unit vector \(x\),
\[
x^\top\widetilde V_nx
=x^\top V_{\tau}x+x^\top(\widetilde V_n-V_{\tau})x
\ge\varepsilon-\|\widetilde V_n-V_{\tau}\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
Thus (15) implies
\[
\mathbb P_{\zeta}\!\left(
\lambda_{\min}(\widetilde V_n)>\varepsilon/2\right)\longrightarrow1
\quad\text{uniformly on }H.
\tag{16}
\]
Thus \(\widehat\gamma_n=1\) with probability tending to one.  On that event \(\widehat V_n=\widetilde V_n\), so (15) also proves
\[
\widehat V_n\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}V_{\tau}
\quad\text{uniformly on }H.
\tag{17}
\]

For any fixed nonzero scalar-contrast vector \(h\in\mathbb R^2\), the denominator below is bounded away from zero because
\(h^\top V_{\tau}h\ge\varepsilon\|h\|_2^2\).  Slutsky's lemma, (14), and (17) give
\[
\frac{\sqrt n\,h^\top(\widehat\tau_n-\tau)}
{\{h^\top\widehat V_nh\}^{1/2}}
\ \Rightarrow\ N(0,1).
\tag{18}
\]
Therefore the usual two-sided normal Wald interval for \(h^\top\tau\) has asymptotic coverage \(1-\alpha\).  Likewise,
\[
n(\widehat\tau_n-\tau)^\top
\widehat V_n^{-1}
(\widehat\tau_n-\tau)
\ \Rightarrow\ \chi^2_2.
\tag{19}
\]
Because \(\tau\in[0,1]^2\), intersecting the ellipse with \([0,1]^2\) does not alter the event that the true \(\tau\) belongs to it.  The \(\chi^2_2\) distribution is continuous at its \(1-\alpha\) quantile, so (19) proves
\[
\mathbb P_{\zeta}\{\tau\in W_n\}\longrightarrow1-\alpha
\tag{20}
\]
for the elliptical branch, uniformly on \(H\).  The compact-uniform assertions in (14), (18), and (20) also follow directly by contradiction: a sequence violating uniformity has a convergent subsequence in \(H\), while the bounded triangular-array expansion (4), the uniform Taylor remainder (13), and the uniform eigenvalue bound (16) establish the claimed limit along that subsequence.

Finally, suppose no chart is certified by the selector.  By \(\mathrm{def:wald\text{-}estimator}\), \(\widehat\chi_n=\bot\).  The total-map definitions give
\[
F_{\bot}(\widehat\zeta_n)=(0,0),
\qquad
J_{\bot}(\widehat\zeta_n)=0.
\]
The raw sandwich matrix is then zero and is not positive definite, hence
\(\widehat\gamma_n=0\).  The fallback clauses in the same definition now give, identically rather than asymptotically,
\[
\widehat\tau_n=(0,0),\qquad
\widehat V_n=I_2,\qquad
W_n=[0,1]^2.
\]
All covariance-consistency and Wald-coverage conclusions above used the event in (11); accordingly, none is asserted for this fallback branch.
