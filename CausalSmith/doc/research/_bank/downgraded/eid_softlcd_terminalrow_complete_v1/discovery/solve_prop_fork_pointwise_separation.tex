%%% FIELD proof-prop-fork-pointwise-separation
Fix \(T>0\) with \(T\ne 1\).  Denote by \(\theta_T\) and \(\theta'_T\), respectively, the true and candidate parameter points specified by \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{fork}}(T)\), and let \(\mathcal R_T\) and \(\{\Sigma_T^{(k)}\}_{k\in K}\) be the aligned factorization datum and covariance tuple generated by \(\theta_T\).

First consider covariance compatibility.  By Proposition \(\mathrm{prop{:}fork\text{-}rational\text{-}collision}\), \(\theta'_T\in\mathcal M_{2,4}(G'_{\mathrm{fork}})\): in particular, its mixing matrix has full row rank and nonzero pairwise noncollinear columns, its baseline coefficients and change rows have the exact supports prescribed by \(G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\), every changed edge is retained, and all of its baseline and intervention variances are positive.  The same proposition gives, context by context,
\[
 \Sigma_{\theta'_T}^{(k)}=\Sigma_{	heta_T}^{(k)}=\Sigma_T^{(k)}
 \qquad(k\in K).
\]
It remains to check the closure condition in the definition of \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{cov}}\).  The true actual rows displayed in \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{fork}}(T)\) are
\[
 h_1^{\mathsf T}=0,\qquad
 h_2^{\mathsf T}=(1,0,0,0),\qquad
 h_3^{\mathsf T}=(3,1,0,0),\qquad
 h_4^{\mathsf T}=(1,1,0,0).
\]
Therefore Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}recovered\text{-}closure}\) gives
\[
 \mathcal C(\mathcal R_T)
 =\{1\to2,\,1\to3,\,2\to3,\,1\to4,\,2\to4\}.
\]
Each displayed loading column \(a_k\) is nonzero.  Consequently the rank-one equation used in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}recovered\text{-}closure}\) determines its row uniquely: if \(a_ku^{\mathsf T}=a_kw^{\mathsf T}\), then a nonzero coordinate of \(a_k\) gives \(u=w\).  Hence the displayed rows are indeed the rows recovered from \(\mathcal R_T\).  This relation is both \(\overline G_{\mathrm{fork}}\) and \(\overline {G'_{\mathrm{fork}}}\), by direct transitive closure of the two edge sets in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}fork\text{-}witness}\).  Thus the legal witness \(\theta'_T\), the displayed covariance equalities, and Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}observable\text{-}classes}\) imply
\[
 G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\in
 \mathcal G_{\mathrm{cov}}
 \bigl(\mathcal R_T,\{\Sigma_T^{(k)}\}_{k\in K}\bigr).
\]

We next prove exclusion from the soft graph set.  Suppose, to the contrary, that
\(G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\in\mathcal G_{\mathrm{soft}}(\mathcal R_T)\).  By Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}observable\text{-}classes}\), there would then be a legal parameter point for \(G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\) whose aligned population factorization represents \(\mathcal R_T\).  Proposition \(\mathrm{prop{:}observable\text{-}quotient}\) permits us to choose the common permutation and rational projective scaling representative in which the target labels and loading lines agree with the displayed true representative; it also guarantees that neither soft membership nor the support tests change under this choice.  The rank-one update equation in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}recovered\text{-}closure}\) then recovers the displayed rows of \(H\).

For column \(j=1\) of the candidate graph, one has
\[
 C_1=\operatorname{ch}_{G'_{\mathrm{fork}}}(1)=\{2\},
 \qquad
 R_1=\operatorname{de}_{G'_{\mathrm{fork}}}(1)\setminus C_1=\{3,4\}.
\]
Write \(x=\Lambda'^{(0)}_{21}\).  Since \(U=I_4-\Lambda'^{(0)}\), the first coordinate of the candidate change row at target \(k\) is
\[
 \delta'_{k1}=(h_k^{\mathsf T}U)_1=H_{k1}-xH_{k2}.
\]
In \(G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\), vertex \(1\) is not a parent of either \(3\) or \(4\).  Exact change support therefore requires \(\delta'_{31}=\delta'_{41}=0\).  Equivalently, the affine block of Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}affine\text{-}block}\) must satisfy
\[
 H[R_1,C_1]x=H[R_1,1],
 \qquad\text{that is,}\qquad
 \begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}x
 =\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
No real \(x\) solves these two equations: the augmented minor is
\[
 \det\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\1&1\end{pmatrix}=-2\ne0.
\]
Hence no legal \(G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\)-parameter can represent \(\mathcal R_T\), contradicting the assumed soft membership.  Consequently,
\[
 G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\notin\mathcal G_{\mathrm{soft}}(\mathcal R_T).
\]
Proposition \(\mathrm{prop{:}observable\text{-}quotient}\) transports both the covariance witness and the nonzero augmented minor through the simultaneous monomial orbit.  Combining the two membership conclusions proves, modulo \(\mathfrak M_q\),
\[
 G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\in
 \mathcal G_{\mathrm{cov}}
 \bigl(\mathcal R_T,\{\Sigma_T^{(k)}\}_{k\in K}\bigr)
 \setminus\mathcal G_{\mathrm{soft}}(\mathcal R_T).
\]

Finally, this conclusion is pointwise.  Proposition \(\mathrm{prop{:}observable\text{-}quotient}\) explicitly separates this quotient-valued pointwise functional from the chamberwise graph functional in the absence of a generic-to-pointwise constancy theorem.  The witness above supplies one covariance twin for each value of \(T\), and the affine determinant supplies soft exclusion at those data, but neither statement establishes relative density of the complete legal branch union in every full-dimensional true sign and variance chamber.  Thus the argument yields no membership assertion for \(G'_{\mathrm{fork}}\) in \(\mathsf{Cov}_2(G_{\mathrm{fork}})\), and hence none in \(\mathsf{Frontier}_2(G_{\mathrm{fork}})\), as claimed.
