Write (j=(j_0,j_1,j_2)inmathbb F_2^3) for the binary digits of (jin{0,ldots,7}), with (j_0) the least significant bit.  By the random-phase affine Boolean block construction in (mathrm{def{:}golay	ext{-}block	ext{-}law}), for any finite list (j_1,ldots,j_m) (repetitions allowed),
\[
 \prod_{r=1}^m W_{j_r}
 =\sigma^m(-1)^{\sum_{r=1}^m\phi_8(j_r)}
   \prod_{k=0}^2(-1)^{\alpha_k\sum_{r=1}^m(j_r)_k}.
\]
All exponents in the last product are read in (mathbb F_2).  The sign (sigma) and bits (alpha_0,alpha_1,alpha_2) are mutually independent and uniform.  Consequently,
\[
 \mathbb E[\sigma^m]=\frac{1+(-1)^m}{2}=\mathbf 1\{m\text{ is even}\},
 \qquad
 \mathbb E[(-1)^{\alpha_k s}]=\frac{1+(-1)^s}{2}=\mathbf 1\{s=0\}
 \quad(s\in\mathbb F_2).
\]
Taking expectations in the preceding product and using independence therefore gives
\[
 \mathbb E\!\left[\prod_{r=1}^mW_{j_r}\right]
 =\mathbf 1\{m\text{ is even}\}
  \mathbf 1\!\left\{\bigoplus_{r=1}^m j_r=0\right\}
  (-1)^{\sum_{r=1}^m\phi_8(j_r)}.
\]
This is zero for odd (m), and for even (m) it is exactly the coefficient asserted in the lemma (and in (mathrm{def{:}fourth	ext{-}tensor	ext{-}certificate})).

For the stated fourth moment, (0\oplus1\oplus2\oplus3=0).  Moreover, from (phi_8(j)=j_0j_1+j_1j_2),
\[
 (\phi_8(0),\phi_8(1),\phi_8(2),\phi_8(3))=(0,0,0,1).
\]
The even-moment formula thus yields
\[
 \mathbb E[W_0W_1W_2W_3]=(-1)^{0+0+0+1}=-1.
\]

It remains to verify the finite covariance calculation rather than infer it from complementarity.  The phase-sign row and the three affine-mask rows are
\[
 b:=((-1)^{\phi_8(j)})_{j=0}^7=(+,+,+,-,+,+,-,+),
\]
\[
 r_0=(+,-,+,-,+,-,+,-),\qquad
 r_1=(+,+,-,-,+,+,-,-),\qquad
 r_2=(+,+,+,+,-,-,-,-).
\]
Hence the row indexed by (alpha=(alpha_0,alpha_1,alpha_2)) and (sigma=1) is the componentwise product
\[
 b\,r_0^{\alpha_0}r_1^{\alpha_1}r_2^{\alpha_2}.
\]
For (alpha=000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111), direct componentwise multiplication gives, in that order, precisely the eight (W)-rows displayed in (mathrm{def{:}l8	ext{-}golay	ext{-}audit}).  Applying the definition
\[
 C_{8,h}(w)=\sum_{t=1}^{8-h}w_tw_{t+h},\qquad h=1,2,3,
\]
with the harmless reindexing of the displayed block coordinates from (0,ldots,7) to (1,ldots,8), gives the corresponding exact list
\[
 \begin{split}
 c_{000}&=(-1,0,3),&c_{001}&=(1,0,-3),\\
 c_{010}&=(1,0,1),&c_{011}&=(-1,0,-1),\\
 c_{100}&=(1,0,-3),&c_{101}&=(-1,0,3),\\
 c_{110}&=(-1,0,-1),&c_{111}&=(1,0,1).
 \end{split}
\]
These sums are also the three rightmost columns of the displayed audit table.  Multiplication of an entire row by (sigma=-1) leaves every product (w_tw_{t+h}), and hence every (C_{8,h}), unchanged.  Since (alpha) is uniform on eight masks and independent of (sigma), the definition of (M_8) in (mathrm{def{:}block	ext{-}covariance	ext{-}objects}) gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 M_8
 &=\frac18\sum_{\alpha\in\mathbb F_2^3}c_\alpha c_\alpha^{\top}\\
 &=\frac18
 \begin{pmatrix}
 8&0&-8\\
 0&0&0\\
 -8&0&40
 \end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}
 1&0&-1\\
 0&0&0\\
 -1&0&5
 \end{pmatrix}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Finally, for (d=3), the same definition states (H_3=\operatorname{Diag}(1,2,3)).  Entrywise addition therefore yields
\[
 M_8+H_3=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 2&0&-1\\
 0&2&0\\
 -1&0&8
 \end{pmatrix},
\]
which proves every assertion.
