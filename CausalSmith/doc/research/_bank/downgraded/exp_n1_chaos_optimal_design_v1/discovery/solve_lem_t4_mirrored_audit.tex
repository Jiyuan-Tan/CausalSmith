Set \(T=4\).  By \(\text{def:mirrored-edge-law}\), \(m_4=1\), so if we write \(a=A_1\), \(b=B\), and \(s=X_1\), then \(a,b,s\) are mutually independent uniform signs and
\[
 (Z_1,Z_2,Z_3)=(a,b,-a).
\]
The recursion \(X_{t+1}=X_tZ_t\) in the same definition and the identity \(a^2=1\) give, successively,
\[
 X_1=s,\qquad X_2=sa,\qquad X_3=sab,\qquad X_4=(sab)(-a)=-sb.
\]
Thus
\[
 X=(s,sa,sab,-sb).
\]
Each triple \((a,b,s)\in\{-1,+1\}^3\) has probability \(2^{-3}=1/8\).  Substitution of the eight triples into the last display gives exactly the eight paths in \(\text{def:t4-mirrored-audit}\), including their displayed probabilities.

By \(\text{def:lag-one-objects}\) and the defining relation \(Z_t=X_tX_{t+1}\) from \(\text{def:mirrored-edge-law}\),
\[
 S_4=\sum_{t=1}^{3}X_tX_{t+1}=\sum_{t=1}^{3}Z_t=a+b-a=b=B.
\]
This equality is pointwise, hence holds in every row of the displayed table.  Because \(B\in\{-1,+1\}\), it also gives pointwise \(S_4^2=B^2=1\), and therefore
\[
 \mathbb E_{D_4^{\mathrm{mir}}}[S_4^2]=1.
\]

It remains to audit every off-diagonal entry of the second-moment matrix.  For \(1\le i<j\le4\), repeated use of \(X_{t+1}=X_tZ_t\) and \(X_i^2=1\) yields
\[
 X_iX_j=\prod_{r=i}^{j-1}Z_r.
\]
Since independent uniform signs have mean zero, the six off-diagonal expectations are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E[X_1X_2]&=\mathbb E[a]=0,
 &\mathbb E[X_2X_3]&=\mathbb E[b]=0,
 &\mathbb E[X_3X_4]&=\mathbb E[-a]=0,\\
 \mathbb E[X_1X_3]&=\mathbb E[ab]=\mathbb E[a]\mathbb E[b]=0,
 &\mathbb E[X_2X_4]&=\mathbb E[-ab]=-\mathbb E[a]\mathbb E[b]=0,
 &\mathbb E[X_1X_4]&=\mathbb E[-a^2b]=-\mathbb E[b]=0.
\end{aligned}
\]
All four diagonal entries equal \(\mathbb E[X_i^2]=1\).  Symmetry of \(XX^{\top}\) now proves
\[
 \mathbb E_{D_4^{\mathrm{mir}}}[XX^{\top}]=I_4.
\]

Finally, holding \((a,b)\) fixed and replacing \(s\) by \(-s\) sends the formula \(X=(s,sa,sab,-sb)\) to \(-X\).  The two values of \(s\) have equal conditional probability, so for every \(B_0\subseteq\{-1,+1\}^4\),
\[
 D_4^{\mathrm{mir}}(B_0)=D_4^{\mathrm{mir}}(-B_0).
\]
Together with the already proved identities \(\mathbb E[X_iX_j]=0\) for every \(i<j\), the two membership conditions in \(\text{def:finite-design-class}\) give
\[
 D_4^{\mathrm{mir}}\in\mathfrak D_4.
\]
Moreover, \(T=4\) is even, so \(\text{def:lag-one-objects}\) gives \(a_4=1\); hence the pointwise calculation above reads \(\mathbb E[S_4^2]=1=a_4\), which is precisely the claimed finite audit of the even-horizon value.
