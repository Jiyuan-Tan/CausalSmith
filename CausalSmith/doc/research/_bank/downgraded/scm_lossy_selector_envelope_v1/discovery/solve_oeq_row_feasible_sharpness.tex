%%% FIELD definition-response-boundary-singleton
Fix \(\mathcal C=\mathcal X=\lbrace 1,2\rbrace\), a boundary sequence satisfying \(0<\kappa_n\leq 1/4\), and constants
\[
0<a<\lambda<\min\lbrace 1/2,-\log\alpha\rbrace.
\]
For row \(n\), let the deployment margins be known without error and equal to \(p_n=r_n=(1/2,1/2)\).  Let \(W_{n,1},\ldots,W_{n,n}\) be independent response-study cell labels, each uniform on \(\mathcal C\times\mathcal X\), and, conditionally on the labels, let the responses be independent with
\[
Y_{n,i}\mid W_{n,i}=(c,x)\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\lambda/n).
\]
Thus \(\pi_{n,cx}=1/4\) and \(\mu_{n,cx}=\lambda/n\) for all \((c,x)\).  If \(\overline Y_{n,cx}\) is the within-cell response proportion, set to zero when the cell is empty, define
\[
\widehat\mu_{n,cx}=\left(1-\frac{2a}{n}\right)\overline Y_{n,cx}+\frac{a}{n},
\qquad
\widehat\beta_n=(p_n,r_n,\widehat\mu_n),
\]
and let \(G_n^\star\) be the empirical-influence multiplier draw for these response estimators with zero margin coordinates.  Denote the resulting row law by \(P_n^{\circ,0}\), the triangular-array experiment by \(\mathbb P^0=(P_n^{\circ,0})_{n\geq1}\), and the singleton experiment class by
\[
\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}=\lbrace\mathbb P^0\rbrace.
\]

%%% FIELD theorem-response-boundary-statement
\textbf{Response-boundary failure under bounded-Lipschitz approximation.}
Under \(\mathrm{def:response\text{-}boundary\text{-}singleton}\), \(\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}\subseteq\mathfrak P_{\kappa}^{0}\).  The primitive and conditional multiplier bounded-Lipschitz approximations hold uniformly over this singleton class:
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P\in\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}}
\sup_{\lVert f\rVert_{\mathrm{BL}}\leq1}
\left|
E_{\mathbb P}f\!\left(\sqrt n(\widehat\beta_n-\beta_n)\right)
-E f(G_{\mathbb P,n})
\right|\longrightarrow0,
\]
and, for every \(\epsilon>0\),
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P\in\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}}
\Pr_{\mathbb P}\!\left(
\sup_{\lVert f\rVert_{\mathrm{BL}}\leq1}
\left|
E_{\mathbb P}^{\star}f(G_n^\star)-E f(G_{\mathbb P,n})
\right|>\epsilon
\right)\longrightarrow0.
\]
Nevertheless, the same \(\mathbb P^0\), selected independently of the numerical-delta schedule, satisfies
\[
\exists\,\mathbb P^0\in\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}\quad
\forall\,t=(t_n)_{n\geq1}\ \text{with }t_n>0,qquad
\limsup_{n\to\infty}
\Pr_{\mathbb P^0}\!\left(
[L(\beta_n),U(\beta_n)]\subseteq\mathrm{CI}_n(t)
\right)
\leq 1-\exp(-\lambda)<1-\alpha.
\]
Thus bounded-Lipschitz Gaussian and multiplier approximation, even uniformly over a singleton experiment class, does not by itself imply critical-value or coverage validity when response variance collapses.

%%% FIELD theorem-response-boundary-proof
For every row of \(\mathbb P^0\), the construction gives
\[
\min_{c\in\mathcal C}p_{n,c}=\min_{x\in\mathcal X}r_{n,x}=\frac12,
\qquad
\min_{(c,x)\in\mathcal C\times\mathcal X}\pi_{n,cx}=\frac14.
\]
Because \(0<\kappa_n\leq1/4\), both margin floors in \(\mathrm{def:uniform\text{-}experiment}\) hold.  The observations are independent and the supports are fixed by construction.  Hence
\[
\mathbb P^0\in\mathfrak P_{\kappa}^{0},
\qquad
\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}=\lbrace\mathbb P^0\rbrace
\subseteq\mathfrak P_{\kappa}^{0}.
\tag{1}
\]

Let
\[
D_n(\mathbb P)=
\sup_{\lVert f\rVert_{\mathrm{BL}}\leq1}
\left|
E_{\mathbb P}f\!\left(\sqrt n(\widehat\beta_n-\beta_n)\right)
-E f(G_{\mathbb P,n})
\right|
\]
and
\[
D_n^\star(\mathbb P)=
\sup_{\lVert f\rVert_{\mathrm{BL}}\leq1}
\left|
E_{\mathbb P}^{\star}f(G_n^\star)-E f(G_{\mathbb P,n})
\right|.
\]
The conclusion of \(\mathrm{lem:vanishing\text{-}variance\text{-}all\text{-}zero\text{-}certificate}\), applied to the row laws in \(\mathrm{def:response\text{-}boundary\text{-}singleton}\), gives
\[
D_n(\mathbb P^0)\longrightarrow0
\quad\text{and}\quad
D_n^\star(\mathbb P^0)\longrightarrow_{\mathbb P^0}0.
\tag{2}
\]
Since \(\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}\) has exactly one element, (2) implies
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P\in\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}}D_n(\mathbb P)
=D_n(\mathbb P^0)\longrightarrow0,
\tag{3}
\]
and, for every \(\epsilon>0\),
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P\in\mathfrak Q_{\kappa}}
\Pr_{\mathbb P}\!\left(D_n^\star(\mathbb P)>\epsilon\right)
=
\Pr_{\mathbb P^0}\!\left(D_n^\star(\mathbb P^0)>\epsilon\right)
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (3) and (4) are precisely the two asserted singleton-uniform bounded-Lipschitz approximations.

It remains to calculate coverage.  Define the all-zero event
\[
A_n=\bigcap_{i=1}^{n}\lbrace Y_{n,i}=0\rbrace.
\]
Every response has success probability \(\lambda/n\), independently of its cell label, so
\[
\Pr_{\mathbb P^0}(A_n)
=\left(1-\frac{\lambda}{n}\right)^n
\longrightarrow\exp(-\lambda).
\tag{5}
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:vanishing\text{-}variance\text{-}all\text{-}zero\text{-}certificate}\), on \(A_n\) all true costs equal \(\lambda/n\), all estimated costs equal \(a/n\), and the multiplier critical value is zero for every positive value of \(t_n\).  Because every coupling in \(\Gamma(p_n,r_n)\) has total mass one, these statements give
\[
L(\beta_n)=U(\beta_n)=\frac{\lambda}{n},
\qquad
F(\widehat\beta_n)=\left(\frac{a}{n},\frac{a}{n}\right),
\qquad
q_{1-\alpha,n}^\star=0
\quad\text{on }A_n.
\tag{6}
\]
Therefore, for every deterministic schedule with \(t_n>0\),
\[
\mathrm{CI}_n(t)=\left[\frac{a}{n},\frac{a}{n}\right]
\quad\text{on }A_n.
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(a<\lambda\), (6) and (7) show that, with
\[
E_n(t)=\left\lbrace[L(\beta_n),U(\beta_n)]
\subseteq\mathrm{CI}_n(t)\right\rbrace,
\]
one has \(A_n\subseteq E_n(t)^c\).  Consequently, by (5),
\[
\limsup_{n\to\infty}\Pr_{\mathbb P^0}(E_n(t))
\leq
\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1-\Pr_{\mathbb P^0}(A_n)\right)
=1-\exp(-\lambda).
\tag{8}
\]
The experiment \(\mathbb P^0\) did not depend on \(t\), so (8) holds simultaneously for every deterministic positive schedule.  Finally, \(\lambda<-\log\alpha\) implies \(\exp(-\lambda)>\alpha\), and hence
\[
1-\exp(-\lambda)<1-\alpha.
\tag{9}
\]
Combining (1), (3), (4), (8), and (9) proves the theorem.  No rare-margin rate is used: the obstruction is the collapse of response variance and the resulting failure of quantile regularity.

\textbf{Repaired rare-margin sharpness question.}
Fix a boundary sequence with \(\kappa_n\downarrow0\), \(n\kappa_n\to\infty\), and \(\limsup_{n\to\infty}n\kappa_n^3<\infty\).  First determine a minimal faithful uniform regularity condition on a subclass of \(\mathfrak P_{\kappa}^{0}\) that excludes the response-boundary counterexample by controlling response interiority, nondegenerate response variance, relative variance consistency, or Gaussian anti-concentration.  The condition must be stated in primitive terms and must not assume the desired quantile or coverage conclusion.  Only on the resulting repaired subclass, and after the companion sufficiency question is posed on that same subclass, determine whether \(n\kappa_n^3\) is a sharp threshold for rare-margin optimal-face switching by establishing or refuting
\[
\forall t=(t_n)_{n\geq1}\ \text{with }t_n>0,
\quad
\exists\mathbb P^{(t)}\in\mathfrak P_{\kappa}^{0}
\ \text{satisfying the selected regularity condition}:
\quad
\liminf_{n\to\infty}
\Pr_{\mathbb P^{(t)}}\!\left(
[L(\beta_n),U(\beta_n)]\subseteq\mathrm{CI}_n(t)
\right)<1-\alpha.
\]
Use the two schedule-dependent branches in \(\mathcal H_{\mathrm{unif}}\) on the \(2\times2\) array with \(p_n=r_n=(\kappa_n,1-\kappa_n)\) and \(\Delta_n\) zero or local at rate \((n\kappa_n)^{-1/2}\).  Any negative conclusion must exhibit an actual critical-value or coverage distortion caused by rare-margin face switching rather than response-variance collapse.  The question concerns only this paired numerical-delta procedure and makes no minimax impossibility claim for other confidence procedures.

%%% FIELD row-sharpness-partial
The all-zero response construction gives one fixed experiment \(\mathbb P^0\), independent of \(t\), for which both singleton-uniform bounded-Lipschitz approximation statements hold but
\[
\forall t=(t_n)_{n\geq1}\ \text{with }t_n>0,qquad
\limsup_{n\to\infty}
\Pr_{\mathbb P^0}\!\left(
[L(\beta_n),U(\beta_n)]\subseteq\mathrm{CI}_n(t)
\right)
\leq1-\exp(-\lambda)<1-\alpha.
\]
This proves response-boundary failure but does not determine the rare-margin \(n\kappa_n^3\) threshold after response nondegeneracy is imposed.
