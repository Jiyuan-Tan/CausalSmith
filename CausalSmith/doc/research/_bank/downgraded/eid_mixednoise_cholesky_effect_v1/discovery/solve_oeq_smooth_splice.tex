%%% FIELD smooth-splice-resolution-statement
The smooth-splice question has an affirmative answer.  The witnesses of \(\mathrm{thm:smooth\text{-}splice\text{-}pair}\) are Borel laws \(P_{0,N},P_{1,N}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{col}}\subseteq\mathcal M_\star\) for every sufficiently large \(N\), and, with \(s_N=1/(600\log N)\), they satisfy
\[
 \Gamma(P_{0,N})=\Gamma(0),\qquad
 \Gamma(P_{1,N})=\Gamma(s_N),\qquad
 \left\|P_{0,N}^{\otimes N}-P_{1,N}^{\otimes N}\right\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Their compatible effect fibers are the exact singletons
\[
 \Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P_{0,N}),\preceq)=\{b(0)\},\qquad
 \Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P_{1,N}),\preceq)=\{b(s_N)\}.
\]

%%% FIELD smooth-splice-resolution-proof
Invoke \(\mathrm{thm:smooth\text{-}splice\text{-}pair}\).  Its quantifier supplies an integer \(N_0\) such that, for each \(N\ge N_0\), the stated Borel laws belong to \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{col}}\subseteq\mathcal M_\star\).  The same theorem fixes
\[
 s_N={1\over600\log N},
\]
so \(s_N\asymp(\log N)^{-1}\), and gives, respectively,
\[
 \Gamma(P_{0,N})=\Gamma(0),\qquad
 \Gamma(P_{1,N})=\Gamma(s_N),
\]
and
\[
 \left\|P_{0,N}^{\otimes N}-P_{1,N}^{\otimes N}\right\|_{\mathrm{TV}}longrightarrow0.
\]
It also asserts that both witnesses have a one-dimensional unit-variance source representation with full column rank, loading norm less than three, common exponent-two ray envelopes \(2/5\) and \(60\), eighth moment at most \(50000000\), and the displayed covariance as greatest Gaussian divisor.  Its \(m=6\) construction proves the two analytic facts demanded by the question: the inverse-Fourier correction is dominated globally by the positive Student-tail component, and its derivatives through order six obey the weighted-Plancherel bound that makes the product total variation vanish.  Thus none of the membership conditions is inferred merely from the conclusion of an asymptotic comparison.

Finally, the theorem gives
\[
 \Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P_{0,N}),\preceq)=\{b(0)\},\qquad
 \Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P_{1,N}),\preceq)=\{b(s_N)\}.
\]
These are precisely the covariance, indistinguishability, class-membership, greatest-divisor, and exact-collision clauses requested by the open-ended question.  Therefore the answer is affirmative.
