%%% FIELD proof-thm-laplace-boundary
Fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{Lap}}\).  Let
\[
 \mathcal I_4=\left\{r=(r_1,\ldots,r_p)\in\mathbb N_0^p:
 1\le |r|:=\sum_{i=1}^p r_i\le 4\right\}.
\]
Stars and bars gives
\(\lvert\mathcal I_4\rvert=\binom{p+4}{4}-1=d_4\), in agreement with
the definition of \(d_4\).  For \(r\in\mathcal I_4\), define
\[
 Z_r=\prod_{i=1}^p X_i^{r_i},\qquad
 \mu_r=\mathbb E_P Z_r,\qquad
 \overline Z_r=\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^N\prod_{i=1}^p X_{ni}^{r_i}.
\]
Put \(B=\sqrt{V_8}\), and let
\(\widehat\mu_r=\operatorname{clip}_{[-B,B]}(\overline Z_r)\), as
prescribed by \texttt{def:laplace-region}.  Since
\(\lvert Z_r\rvert\leq\lVert X\rVert^{|r|}\), the elementary inequality
\(z^{2|r|}\leq 1+z^8\) for \(z\geq0\) and \(1\leq |r|\leq4\), the
eighth-moment assumption \texttt{ass:eighth-moment}, and the definition
\(V_8=1+M_8\) imply
\[
 \mathbb E_P Z_r^2
 \leq \mathbb E_P\lVert X\rVert^{2|r|}
 \leq 1+\mathbb E_P\lVert X\rVert^8
 \leq 1+M_8=V_8.                                      \tag{1}
\]
By Cauchy--Schwarz, \(\lvert\mu_r\rvert\leq
(\mathbb E_PZ_r^2)^{1/2}\leq B\).  Projection onto the interval
\([-B,B]\) cannot increase distance from \(\mu_r\in[-B,B]\), and hence
\[
 \lvert\widehat\mu_r-\mu_r\rvert
 \leq\lvert\overline Z_r-\mu_r\rvert.                 \tag{2}
\]
The independent-sampling assumption \texttt{ass:iid} gives
\[
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes N}}(\overline Z_r-\mu_r)^2
 =\frac{\operatorname{Var}_P(Z_r)}{N}
 \leq\frac{V_8}{N}.                                    \tag{3}
\]
Summing (2)--(3) over the \(d_4\) indices yields
\[
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes N}}\lVert\widehat\mu-\mu\rVert_2^2
 \leq\frac{d_4V_8}{N}.                                 \tag{4}
\]
Consequently, Jensen's inequality and Markov's inequality applied to the
nonnegative random variable
\(\lVert\widehat\mu-\mu\rVert_2^2\), respectively, give
\[
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes N}}\lVert\widehat\mu-\mu\rVert_2
 \leq\sqrt{\frac{d_4V_8}{N}},                           \tag{5}
\]
and, using the declared definition
\(r_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}=\sqrt{d_4V_8/(N\alpha)}\),
\[
 P^{\otimes N}\!\left\{
 \lVert\widehat\mu-\mu\rVert_2>r_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}
 \right\}
 \leq
 \frac{d_4V_8/N}{(r_N^{\mathrm{Lap}})^2}
 =\alpha.                                               \tag{6}
\]

We next identify the population value of the reconstruction map.  The
centered standardized Laplace variable \(H_{\mathrm{Lap}}\) has density
\(h\mapsto 2^{-1/2}\exp(-\sqrt2\lvert h\rvert)\).  Symmetry gives
\(\mathbb EH_{\mathrm{Lap}}=0\), while direct integration using
\(\int_0^\infty h^m e^{-ah}\,dh=m!a^{-(m+1)}\) gives
\[
 \mathbb EH_{\mathrm{Lap}}^2
 =\sqrt2\frac{2!}{(\sqrt2)^3}=1,
 \qquad
 \mathbb EH_{\mathrm{Lap}}^4
 =\sqrt2\frac{4!}{(\sqrt2)^5}=6.                       \tag{7}
\]
Thus its fourth cumulant is \(6-3(1)^2=3\).
By \texttt{ass:laplace-source},
\(U=a^{\mathrm{Lap}}H_{\mathrm{Lap}}\).  Membership in the model in
\texttt{def:laplace-model} also retains the declared additive decomposition
\(X=U+G_{\Gamma(P)}\), in which \(U\) and the centered Gaussian vector are
independent.  Expanding the fourth joint cumulant by multilinearity, every
mixed term between the two independent summands is zero, and the fourth
joint cumulant of the Gaussian summand is zero.  Therefore (7) implies, for
every \(i\in V\),
\[
 \kappa_{TTTT}(X)=3(a_T^{\mathrm{Lap}})^4,
 \qquad
 \kappa_{iTTT}(X)=3a_i^{\mathrm{Lap}}(a_T^{\mathrm{Lap}})^3. \tag{8}
\]
The treatment-loading assumption \texttt{ass:treatment-loading} states
\(\lvert a_T^{\mathrm{Lap}}\rvert\geq a_{\min}>0\).  Hence
\(\kappa_{TTTT}(X)\geq3a_{\min}^4>(3/2)a_{\min}^4\), so the positive
floor in \(F_{p,M_8,a_{\min}}\) is inactive at \(\mu\) and every division
below has a strictly positive denominator.  The definitions of the
coordinates of \(a(\mu)\), followed by (8), give
\[
 a_T(\mu)=\lvert a_T^{\mathrm{Lap}}\rvert,
 \qquad
 a_i(\mu)
 =\frac{3a_i^{\mathrm{Lap}}(a_T^{\mathrm{Lap}})^3}
 {3\lvert a_T^{\mathrm{Lap}}\rvert^3}
 =\operatorname{sgn}(a_T^{\mathrm{Lap}})a_i^{\mathrm{Lap}}. \tag{9}
\]
In particular,
\[
 a(\mu)a(\mu)^\top
 =a^{\mathrm{Lap}}(a^{\mathrm{Lap}})^\top.              \tag{10}
\]
Independence in the additive model, centering, and the unit variance in (7)
also give
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}_P(X)
 =a^{\mathrm{Lap}}(a^{\mathrm{Lap}})^\top+\Gamma(P).    \tag{11}
\]
By (10)--(11), the matrix before spectral clipping in
\(F_{p,M_8,a_{\min}}(\mu)\) is \(\Gamma(P)\).  Assumptions
\texttt{ass:spectral-lower} and \texttt{ass:spectral-upper} put its spectrum
in \([\lambda,\Lambda]\), so clipping fixes it and
\[
 F_{p,M_8,a_{\min}}(\mu)=\Gamma(P).                     \tag{12}
\]

The construction in \texttt{def:laplace-region} is precisely
\(\widehat\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}
=F_{p,M_8,a_{\min}}(\widehat\mu)\).  Both \(\mu\) and
\(\widehat\mu\) lie in
\([-\sqrt{V_8},\sqrt{V_8}]^{d_4}\).  The already established global
Lipschitz result \texttt{lem:laplace-global-lipschitz} and (12) thus imply,
pointwise in the sample,
\[
 \lVert\widehat\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}-\Gamma(P)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq C_{\mathrm{Lap}}\lVert\widehat\mu-\mu\rVert_2.   \tag{13}
\]
Combining (5) and (13), and then taking the supremum over the model, proves
the explicit uniform bound
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{Lap}}}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes N}}
 \lVert\widehat\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}-\Gamma(P)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq C_{\mathrm{Lap}}\sqrt{\frac{d_4V_8}{N}}.          \tag{14}
\]
All quantities on the right are fixed model constants apart from \(N\), so
(14) is the first asserted uniform \(O(N^{-1/2})\) conclusion.  Notice that
no loading-norm bound has been used.

Spectral clipping puts \(\widehat\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}\) in the same
spectral interval \([\lambda,\Lambda]\) as \(\Gamma(P)\).  Applying the
already established result \texttt{lem:fiber-lipschitz} to (13), followed by
(5), gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{Lap}}}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes N}}
 d_H\!\left(
 \Theta_{TY}(\widehat\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{Lap}},\preceq),
 \Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P),\preceq)
 \right)\\
 &\hspace{18mm}\leq
 L_\beta C_{\mathrm{Lap}}\sqrt{\frac{d_4V_8}{N}}.
                                                               \tag{15}
 \end{aligned}
\]
This proves the second asserted uniform \(O(N^{-1/2})\) conclusion.

It remains to establish finite-sample whole-fiber coverage.  Define
\[
 \mathcal E_N=
 \left\{\lVert\widehat\mu-\mu\rVert_2
 \leq r_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}\right\},
 \qquad
 R_N=L_\beta C_{\mathrm{Lap}}r_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}.
\]
On \(\mathcal E_N\), (13) and \texttt{lem:fiber-lipschitz} imply
\[
 d_H\!\left(
 \Theta_{TY}(\widehat\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{Lap}},\preceq),
 \Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P),\preceq)
 \right)\leq R_N.                                      \tag{16}
\]
The exact representation in \texttt{prop:ideal-fiber}, applied once to each
of the two positive-definite matrices in (16), is
\[
 \Theta_{TY}(H,\preceq)=\{\beta_J(H):J\in\mathcal J\}. \tag{17}
\]
For any \(\theta\in\Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P),\preceq)\), the directed
true-to-estimated term in the Hausdorff bound (16), together with (17),
therefore supplies some \(J\in\mathcal J\) such that
\[
 \left\lvert
 \theta-\beta_J(\widehat\Gamma_N^{\mathrm{Lap}})
 \right\rvert\leq R_N.                                 \tag{18}
\]
This argument uses distance to the entire finite set and neither labels nor
matches its branches.  Thus (18) remains valid on exact effect-collision
strata, without any swap margin.

We verify that the terminal intersection with
\([-\Lambda/\lambda,\Lambda/\lambda]\) in
\(C_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}\) cannot remove a true effect.  Fix
\(J\in\mathcal J\) and put \(S=D\cup J\); by the definitions of \(D\),
\(A\), and \(\mathcal J\), one has \(T\notin S\).  If \(Y\in S\), the
definition of partial covariance gives
\(\gamma_{YT\mathbin{\cdot}S}(\Gamma(P))=0\).  If \(Y\notin S\), the
Schur complement of \(\Gamma(P)_{SS}\) is positive definite.  Cauchy--Schwarz
for that conditional covariance matrix gives
\[
 \left\lvert\gamma_{YT\mathbin{\cdot}S}(\Gamma(P))\right\rvert
 \leq
 \sqrt{
 \gamma_{YY\mathbin{\cdot}S}(\Gamma(P))
 \gamma_{TT\mathbin{\cdot}S}(\Gamma(P))}.
                                                               \tag{19}
\]
For any coefficient vector \(b\in\mathbb R^S\), let \(b_S\in\mathbb R^p\)
denote its extension by zero outside \(S\).  Because \(T\notin S\), the
spectral lower bound yields
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_P\!\left(X_T-b^\top X_S\right)
 \geq\lambda\lVert e_T-b_S\rVert_2^2
 \geq\lambda.
\]
Minimizing the left side over \(b\) is the Schur-complement variational
formula, and hence
\[
 \gamma_{TT\mathbin{\cdot}S}(\Gamma(P))\geq\lambda.     \tag{20}
\]
Choosing the zero predictor and using the spectral upper bound similarly
gives
\[
 \gamma_{YY\mathbin{\cdot}S}(\Gamma(P))
 \leq\Gamma(P)_{YY}\leq\Lambda.                         \tag{21}
\]
Equations (19)--(21), and the zero numerator in the case \(Y\in S\), imply
in all cases
\[
 \lvert\beta_J(\Gamma(P))\rvert
 \leq\sqrt{\frac{\Lambda}{\lambda}}
 \leq\frac{\Lambda}{\lambda},                          \tag{22}
\]
where the last inequality uses the declared strict bounds
\(0<\lambda<\Lambda\).  Thus every true element represented in (17) lies
inside the terminal clipping interval.  Combining (18), (22), and the
definition of \(C_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}\) in
\texttt{def:laplace-region} proves, on \(\mathcal E_N\),
\[
 \Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P),\preceq)\subseteq C_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}. \tag{23}
\]
By (6), \(P^{\otimes N}(\mathcal E_N)\geq1-\alpha\) for every
\(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{Lap}}\).  Taking the infimum in (23) therefore
gives
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{Lap}}}
 P^{\otimes N}\!\left\{
 \Theta_{TY}(\Gamma(P),\preceq)\subseteq C_N^{\mathrm{Lap}}
 \right\}\geq1-\alpha                                  \tag{24}
\]
for every positive integer \(N\), which is the third assertion.

Finally, the construction is measurable.  Empirical raw moments are Borel
functions; coordinatewise interval projection, cumulant polynomials, the
maximum with a positive constant, the fourth root, and reciprocal powers on
the positive-floor domain are continuous.  Spectral projection is
continuous, and inversion is continuous on matrices bounded below by
\(\lambda I\).  The family \(\mathcal J\) is finite, so a finite union of
closed intervals with Borel endpoints, followed by intersection with a
fixed closed interval, is a measurable random compact set.  The same bound
as (22), applied to the spectrally clipped estimate, also shows that its
centers lie in the fixed interval, so this compact set is nonempty.  Hence
all estimators, events, and probabilities used above are well defined.
