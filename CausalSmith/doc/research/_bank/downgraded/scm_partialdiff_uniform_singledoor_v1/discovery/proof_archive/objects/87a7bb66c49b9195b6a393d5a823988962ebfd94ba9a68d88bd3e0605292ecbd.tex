For every \(B\ge1\), put
\[
K_B=2B^2+4,
\qquad
\kappa_B=K_B^{-2},
\]
and define
\[
\mathcal M_{p,B}^{\mathrm{df}}
=\left\{M\in\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}:
\max_{e\in\mathbb E}\max_{u,v\in\mathbb V}|B_{e,uv}|\le B,
\quad
\kappa_B I_p\preceq\Sigma_{M,e}\preceq K_BI_p
\ \text{for every }e\in\mathbb E\right\}.
\]
For \(s\in\Omega_p\), let
\[
\Psi_B(s)
=\overline{\{\tau(M'):M'\in\mathfrak F(s)\cap
\mathcal M_{p,B}^{\mathrm{df}}\}},
\]
and project the same paired exact Wishart region through this restricted fiber:
\[
\widehat{\mathcal C}^{B}_{n,\gamma}
=\overline{\bigcup_{s:\operatorname{vech}^{-1}(s)in
\mathcal R_{n,\gamma}}\Psi_B(s)}.
\]
Then, for every integer \(p\ge3\), every \(n_1,n_2>p\), and every \(0<\gamma<1/2\),
\[
\inf_{M\in\mathcal M_{p,B}^{\mathrm{df}}}
\Pr_M\{\tau(M)\in\widehat{\mathcal C}^{B}_{n,\gamma}\}
\ge1-\gamma.
\]
Moreover, with the infimum below taken over all possibly randomized, \(\mathcal Z_n\)-measurable closed-set-valued rules \(D\) satisfying the displayed uniform coverage constraint, and with the extended-diameter convention \(\operatorname{diam}(\varnothing)=0\),
\[
\sqrt2 B(1-2\gamma)
\le
\inf_{\substack{D:\ 
 \inf_{M\in\mathcal M_{p,B}^{\mathrm{df}}}
 \Pr_M\{\tau(M)\in D\}\ge1-\gamma}}
\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_{p,B}^{\mathrm{df}}}
\mathbb E_M[\operatorname{diam}(D)]
\le
\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_{p,B}^{\mathrm{df}}}
\mathbb E_M[\operatorname{diam}(\widehat{\mathcal C}^{B}_{n,\gamma})]
\le2\sqrt6 B.
\]
Consequently the bounded projected-Wishart union is finite-sample constant-factor minimax for expected diameter, with factor at most \(2\sqrt3/(1-2\gamma)\).  This assertion is neither exact minimax optimality nor attainment of the minimax infimum, and it asserts no rate in the sample sizes.
