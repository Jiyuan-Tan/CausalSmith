Fix an integer \(p\ge3\), an admissible total sample size \(N\), and first take \(\mathcal C=\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\).  Set \(b=1\).  For every \(a\neq0\), global honesty supplies the two coverage inequalities required by \(\text{thm:honesty-noncontraction-lower-bound}\).  Under the common sample law \(Q_{N,a}\) of the corresponding \(p\)-dimensional witnesses, that theorem gives
\[
 Q_{N,a}\!\left\{\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\ge\sqrt2|a|\right\}\ge1-2\gamma.
\]
For any nonnegative extended random variable \(T\) and \(t>0\), the pointwise inequality \(T\ge t\mathbf 1\{T\ge t\}\) and monotonicity of integration imply
\[
 \mathbb E[T]\ge t\Pr\{T\ge t\}.
\]
Apply this identity with \(T=\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\) and \(t=\sqrt2|a|\).  Since either witness belongs to \(\mathcal C\) and has sample law \(Q_{N,a}\),
\[
 \sup_{M\in\mathcal C}\mathbb E_M\!\left[\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\right]
 \ge \sqrt2|a|(1-2\gamma).
\]
The assumption \(\gamma\in(0,1/2)\) makes \(1-2\gamma>0\).  The model class places no upper bound on \(|a|\), and the witness remains positive definite for every finite \(a\), so letting \(|a|\to\infty\) proves that the supremum is \(+\infty\).

For \(\mathcal C=\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{BD}}\), the same argument invokes \(\text{thm:anchor-class-honesty-noncontraction}\), which supplies the identical tail inequality for every \(a\neq0\).  Thus every globally honest procedure has infinite worst-case expected diameter for either class.  The collection over which the infimum is taken is nonempty: the constant closed-set rule \(D_N=\mathbb R^3\) is sample-measurable and has coverage one, although its extended diameter is infinite.  Since every member of this nonempty collection has worst-case expected diameter \(+\infty\), its infimum is \(+\infty\).

Finally, for every declared bounded tangent experiment and every \(R\in(0,\infty)\), \(\text{thm:local-minimax-attainment-characterization}\) proves \(v_R<\infty\) and produces an attaining randomized confidence rule.  Combining that finite attained local value with the global conclusion above proves the asserted elbow for every \(p\ge3\).
