For every integer \(p\ge3\) and every \((a,b)\) in the chain--fork parameter space, the construction in \(\text{def:chain-fork-witness}\) gives two models \(M^{\mathrm{ch}},M^{\mathrm{fk}}\in\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\) with the same law pair
\[
 \bigl(\mathcal N(0,\Sigma^{\mathrm{cf}}(a,b)\oplus I_{p-3}),
 \mathcal N(0,\Sigma^{\mathrm{cf}}(a,b)\oplus I_{p-3})\bigr).
\]
Here \(I_0\) denotes the empty covariance block.  The models have the same empty difference DAG, the same complete equality ledger, and the same empty population LDiffPC output, but \(\tau(M^{\mathrm{ch}})=(a,a,0)\) and \(\tau(M^{\mathrm{fk}})=(0,0,0)\).  For every ordered pair of distinct vertices and every eligible conditioning set, each model satisfies diff-faithfulness.  Therefore every procedure \((D_N)\) having coverage at least \(1-\gamma\) for both models satisfies
\[
 \Pr\{\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\ge\sqrt2|a|\}\ge1-2\gamma
\]
for every \(N\).
