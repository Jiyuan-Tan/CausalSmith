Fix \(b\in\mathbb B_p\). The finite enumeration in \(\text{thm:finite-branch-representation}\) fixes, in each environment \(e\), a predecessor set \(P_{e,b}\) for \(Y\), the position of \(X\), and whether the \(X\to Y\) coefficient is constrained to be zero. If it is constrained to zero, extend that coordinate by the constant zero function. Otherwise \(X\in P_{e,b}\), and the Cholesky predecessor regression gives
\[
 \alpha_{e,b}(\Sigma_e)
 =e_{X,P_{e,b}}^{\mathsf T}
   \Sigma_{e,P_{e,b}P_{e,b}}^{-1}
   \Sigma_{e,P_{e,b}Y}
 =\frac{q_{e,b}(\Sigma_e)}
        {\det(\Sigma_{e,P_{e,b}P_{e,b}})},
 \tag{1}
\]
where \(e_{X,P_{e,b}}\) is the coordinate vector selecting \(X\) and \(q_{e,b}\) is a polynomial, by the adjugate identity \(A^{-1}=\operatorname{adj}(A)/\det(A)\). For every \(s\in\Omega_p\), the matrix \(\Sigma_e\) is positive definite. Its principal submatrix \(\Sigma_{e,P_{e,b}P_{e,b}}\) is therefore positive definite, and hence
\[
 \det(\Sigma_{e,P_{e,b}P_{e,b}})>0.
 \tag{2}
\]
Thus the right side of (1) is a rational function with nowhere-vanishing denominator on the open set \(\Omega_p\), so it is \(C^\infty\) there. The branch triple is
\[
 \theta_b(s)=
 \bigl(\alpha_{1,b}(\Sigma_1),
       \alpha_{2,b}(\Sigma_2),
       \alpha_{1,b}(\Sigma_1)-\alpha_{2,b}(\Sigma_2)\bigr),
 \tag{3}
\]
and is therefore \(C^\infty\) on \(\Omega_p\).

Now fix \(s_0\in\overline{\mathcal S_b}\cap\Omega_p\) and a compact set \(K\subset\mathbb R^d\). Openness of \(\Omega_p\) implies that \(s_0+t u\in\Omega_p\) for all \(u\in K\) and all sufficiently small \(|t|\). Continuity of the first derivative on a compact neighborhood gives
\[
 \sup_{u\in K}\|\theta_b(s_0+t u)-\theta_b(s_0)\|=O(|t|),
 \tag{4}
\]
and continuity of the derivative, through the integral remainder formula, gives
\[
 \sup_{u\in K}
 \frac{\|\theta_b(s_0+t u)-\theta_b(s_0)
 -tD\theta_b(s_0)u\|}{|t|}=o(1).
 \tag{5}
\]
Equations (4)--(5) are the asserted uniform continuity and first-order Taylor properties. They were derived from positive definiteness and the rational branch representation, not imposed as a separate regularity assumption.
