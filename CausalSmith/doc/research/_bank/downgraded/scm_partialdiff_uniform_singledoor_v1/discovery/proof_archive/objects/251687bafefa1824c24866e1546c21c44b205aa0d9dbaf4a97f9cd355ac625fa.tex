Fix an integer \(p\ge3\) and nonzero \(a,b\).  By \(\text{thm:honesty-noncontraction-lower-bound}\), the corrected chain and fork witnesses are members of \(\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\), have the same complete sample law, and have target vectors at Euclidean distance \(\sqrt2|a|\).  The inclusion
\[
 \mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\subseteq\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{BD}}
\]
from \(\text{lem:gaussian-subclass-of-anchor}\) puts both witnesses in the full anchor class.  The displayed uniform-coverage premise therefore gives coverage at least \(1-\gamma\) at each witness.  Applying the two-model conclusion of \(\text{thm:honesty-noncontraction-lower-bound}\) under their common law gives
\[
 \Pr\!\left\{\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\ge\sqrt2|a|\right\}\ge1-2\gamma
\]
for every admissible \(N\).  Neither the inclusion nor the same-law argument imposes a bound on \(|a|\), and \(p\ge3\) was arbitrary.
