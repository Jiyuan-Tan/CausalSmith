The observed-law partial-identification map is \(s\mapsto\Psi(s)\), and inference projects the exact paired covariance region through that map: \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\gamma}=\overline{\bigcup_{s:\operatorname{vech}^{-1}(s)\in\mathcal R_{n,\gamma}}\Psi(s)}\). On the bounded subclass, the corresponding map is \(s\mapsto\Psi_B(s)\) and the projection is \(\widehat{\mathcal C}^{B}_{n,\gamma}\).
