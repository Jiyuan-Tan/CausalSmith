Fix \(M\in\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\) and put \(s=s(M)\).  Positive innovation variances and acyclicity imply that both covariance matrices represented by \(s\) are positive definite, so every partial-regression denominator occurring in \(\mathcal L(s)\) is positive.  By \(\text{def:exact-ledger}\), the ledger therefore supplies perfect equality information for every ordered pair and conditioning set.  The baseline-parent containment and modularity assumptions identify the typed difference graph used here with the difference graph to which \(\text{lem:ldiffpc-population-soundness}\) applies, while \(\text{ass:diff-faithfulness}\) supplies that result's faithfulness premise.  Hence
\[
 \operatorname{skel} H(s)=\operatorname{skel}D(M)
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \{u\to v:u\to v\text{ is oriented in }H(s)\}
 \subseteq E_D(M).
\]
Thus \(D(M)\) has exactly the skeleton required by \(\text{def:completion-fiber}\) and agrees with every orientation recorded by \(H(s)\).  Since membership in \(\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\) is already given and \(s(M)=s\) by construction, all three clauses of that definition hold, so \(M\in\mathfrak F(s)\).  Finally, \(\text{def:identified-effect-set}\) gives
\[
 \tau(M)\in\{\tau(M'):M'\in\mathfrak F(s)\}
 \subseteq\overline{\{\tau(M'):M'\in\mathfrak F(s)\}}=\Psi(s).
\]
This is the claimed retention of the fixed causal target; no regression functional has been substituted for \(\tau(M)\).
