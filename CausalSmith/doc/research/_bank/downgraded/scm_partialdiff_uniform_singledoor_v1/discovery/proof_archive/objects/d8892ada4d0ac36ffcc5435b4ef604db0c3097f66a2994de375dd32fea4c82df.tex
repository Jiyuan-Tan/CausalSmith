For every integer \(p\ge3\) and every \((a,b)\in\mathbb R^2\) with \(a\neq0\) and \(b\neq0\), choose \(W\in\mathbb V\setminus\{X,Y\}\) and enumerate the remaining vertices as \(\mathbb V\setminus\{X,Y,W\}=\{U_1,\ldots,U_{p-3}\}\).  When \(p=3\), this list is empty and every appended equation and order relation below is vacuous.  Use coordinate order \((X,Y,W,U_1,\ldots,U_{p-3})\).  In each environment of \(M^{\mathrm{ch}}\), define
\[
 X=\varepsilon_X,\qquad Y=aX+\varepsilon_Y,\qquad W=bY+\varepsilon_W,
 \qquad U_j=\varepsilon_{U_j}\quad(1\le j\le p-3),
\]
where all innovations are mutually independent centered Gaussians with variance one.  In each environment of \(M^{\mathrm{fk}}\), define
\[
 Y=\eta_Y,\qquad X=\frac{a}{a^2+1}Y+\eta_X,\qquad W=bY+\eta_W,
 \qquad U_j=\eta_{U_j}\quad(1\le j\le p-3),
\]
where all innovations are mutually independent centered Gaussians, with variance vector
\[
 \bigl((a^2+1)^{-1},a^2+1,1,\underbrace{1,\ldots,1}_{p-3}\bigr)
\]
in coordinate order \((X,Y,W,U_1,\ldots,U_{p-3})\).  Thus every \(U_j\) is independent of \((X,Y,W)\), has no incident edge, and has the same standard-Gaussian mechanism in both environments.  Within each paired SCM take the two environment coefficient matrices and innovation-variance vectors to be identical, take the displayed DAG and coefficient matrix as \((G^*,B^*)\), and take \(\mathcal I_1=\mathcal I_2=\varnothing\).  Use common orders obtained by appending \(U_1,\ldots,U_{p-3}\) to \(X\prec Y\prec W\) for \(M^{\mathrm{ch}}\) and to \(Y\prec X\prec W\) for \(M^{\mathrm{fk}}\).
