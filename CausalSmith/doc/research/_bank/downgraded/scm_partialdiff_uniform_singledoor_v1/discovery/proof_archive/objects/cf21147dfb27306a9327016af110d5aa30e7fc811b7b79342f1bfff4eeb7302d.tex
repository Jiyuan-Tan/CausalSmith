Fix \(s_0\) with \(\Gamma(s_0)=\{\tau_0\}\) and \(R\in(0,\infty)\).  Put \(K_R=\bigcup_{(b,h)\in\Lambda_R}\{\eta_b(h)\}\) and let \(\mathcal H_R\) be the compact hyperspace of closed subsets of \(K_R\), including the empty set.  If \(\varphi_h\) is the \(\mathcal N(h,V_0)\) density, then \(v_R<\infty\), the infimum defining \(v_R\) is attained, and it has the explicit semi-infinite linear-program characterization
\[
\begin{aligned}
v_R=\min_{t,Q}\quad &t\\
\text{subject to}\quad
&Q(\,\cdot\mid y)\in\mathcal P(\mathcal H_R)\text{ measurably in }y,\\
&\int_{\mathbb R^d}\!\int_{\mathcal H_R}
  {\bf1}\{\eta_b(h)\in C\}\,Q(dC\mid y)\varphi_h(y)\,dy
  \ge1-\gamma,\quad (b,h)\in\Lambda_R,\\
&\int_{\mathbb R^d}\!\int_{\mathcal H_R}
  \operatorname{diam}(C)\,Q(dC\mid y)\varphi_h(y)\,dy
  \le t,\quad (b,h)\in\Lambda_R.
\end{aligned}
\]
After the coverage-preserving and diameter-nonincreasing truncation \(C\mapsto C\cap K_R\), the attaining \(\mathcal H_R\)-valued randomized confidence rules are exactly the measurable uniform-randomizer realizations of the minimizing kernels \(Q\), up to null sets.  In particular, every unrestricted attaining rule has an \(\mathcal H_R\)-valued truncation represented by a minimizing kernel, although the unrestricted rule itself need not be supported on \(\mathcal H_R\).  This local characterization does not by itself settle finite-sample dominance: changing any proposed pullback at one fixed sample size leaves all its local asymptotic limits unchanged while it can destroy coverage or diameter dominance at that sample size.
