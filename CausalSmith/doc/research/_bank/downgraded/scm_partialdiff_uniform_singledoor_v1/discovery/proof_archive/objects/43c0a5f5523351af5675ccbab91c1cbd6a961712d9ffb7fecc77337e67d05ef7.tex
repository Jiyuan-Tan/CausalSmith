Fix \(M\in\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\) and let
\[
 E_M=\{\boldsymbol\Sigma_M\in\mathcal R_{n,\gamma}\}.
\]
By \(\text{lem:wishart-pivot}\), \(\Pr_M(E_M)=1-\gamma\).  On \(E_M\), the covariance coordinate \(s(M)\) is one of the coordinates over which \(\text{def:confidence-union}\) takes its union.  By \(\text{lem:true-completion-retention}\), the fixed causal target, rather than a selected regression surrogate, satisfies \(\tau(M)\in\Psi(s(M))\).  Consequently
\[
 E_M\subseteq
 \{\tau(M)\in\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\gamma}\},
\]
because taking the outer closure can only enlarge the union.  It follows that
\[
 \Pr_M\{\tau(M)\in\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\gamma}\}
 \ge \Pr_M(E_M)=1-\gamma.
\]
The calculation holds for every member of the same class \(\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\), so taking the infimum over \(M\) preserves the inequality.  No step conditions on a selected ledger or branch.  In particular, the argument uses no lower bound on any equality, conditional-independence, or completion-separation margin, and it remains valid along arbitrary sequences on which all such nonzero margins tend to zero jointly.
