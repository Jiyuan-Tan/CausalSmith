Let \(\mathcal C\) be either \(\mathcal M_3^{\mathrm{df}}\) or \(\mathcal M_3^{\mathrm{BD}}\).  For every \(N\), every confidence procedure satisfying
\[
 \inf_{M\in\mathcal C}\Pr_M\{\tau(M)\in D_N\}\ge1-\gamma
\]
obeys
\[
 \sup_{M\in\mathcal C}\mathbb E_M\!\left[\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\right]=\infty.
\]
Consequently the infimum of this worst-case expected diameter over all such globally honest procedures is infinite.  By contrast, for every declared bounded tangent experiment with \(R\in(0,\infty)\), \(\text{thm:local-minimax-attainment-characterization}\) gives \(v_R<\infty\) and attainment.  Thus the theory has a global-unbounded versus bounded-local elbow.
