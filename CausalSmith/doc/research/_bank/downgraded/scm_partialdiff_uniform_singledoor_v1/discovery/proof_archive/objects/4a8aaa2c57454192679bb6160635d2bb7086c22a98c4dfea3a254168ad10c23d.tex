Define \(\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{BD}}\) to be the class of baseline-indexed paired linear acyclic SCMs \(M\) for which \(\text{ass:linear-structural-equations}\), \(\text{ass:baseline-parent-containment}\), \(\text{ass:modular-interventions}\), and \(\text{ass:diff-faithfulness}\) hold, the coordinates \((\varepsilon_{e,v})_{v\in\mathbb V}\) are mutually independent for each \(e\in\mathbb E\), and the full exogenous-vector laws are invariant across environments:
\[
 \operatorname{Law}(\varepsilon_1)=\operatorname{Law}(\varepsilon_2).
\]
No Gaussian restriction is imposed in this definition.
