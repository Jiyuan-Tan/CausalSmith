For \(p=3\) and \((a,b)\in\mathbb R^2\) with \(a\neq0\) and \(b\neq0\), use coordinate order \((X,Y,W)\).  In each environment of \(M^{\mathrm{ch}}\), define the chain \(X\to Y\to W\) by
\[
 X=\varepsilon_X,\qquad Y=aX+\varepsilon_Y,\qquad W=bY+\varepsilon_W,
\]
where the three innovations are mutually independent centered Gaussians with variance vector \((1,1,1)\).  In each environment of \(M^{\mathrm{fk}}\), define the fork \(X\leftarrow Y\to W\) by
\[
 Y=\eta_Y,\qquad X=\frac{a}{a^2+1}Y+\eta_X,\qquad W=bY+\eta_W,
\]
where the three innovations are mutually independent centered Gaussians with variance vector \(((a^2+1)^{-1},a^2+1,1)\) in coordinate order \((X,Y,W)\).  Within each paired SCM take the two environment coefficient matrices and innovation-variance vectors to be identical, take the displayed DAG and coefficient matrix as \((G^*,B^*)\), take \(\mathcal I_1=\mathcal I_2=\varnothing\), and use respectively the common orders \(X\prec Y\prec W\) and \(Y\prec X\prec W\).
