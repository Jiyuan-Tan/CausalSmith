For every \((a,b)\) in the chain--fork parameter space, the construction in \(\text{def:chain-fork-witness}\) gives two models \(M^{\mathrm{ch}},M^{\mathrm{fk}}\in\mathcal M_3^{\mathrm{df}}\) with the same law pair \((\mathcal N(0,\Sigma^{\mathrm{cf}}(a,b)),\mathcal N(0,\Sigma^{\mathrm{cf}}(a,b)))\), the same empty difference DAG, the same complete equality ledger, and the same empty population LDiffPC output, but \(\tau(M^{\mathrm{ch}})=(a,a,0)\) and \(\tau(M^{\mathrm{fk}})=(0,0,0)\). Therefore every procedure \((D_N)\) having coverage at least \(1-\gamma\) for both models satisfies \(\Pr\{\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\ge\sqrt2|a|\}\ge1-2\gamma\) for every \(N\).
