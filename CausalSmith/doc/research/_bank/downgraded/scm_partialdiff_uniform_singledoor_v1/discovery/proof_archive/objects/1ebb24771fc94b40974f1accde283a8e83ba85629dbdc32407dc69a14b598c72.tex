Under the stated singleton condition, the unions in \(\text{def:boundary-limit-sets}\) contain only the branch \(b_0\).  With \(y=h+\mathbb Z_0\), the zeroth-order definition therefore gives
\[
 \mathcal K_h^{(0)}=
 \begin{cases}
   \{\theta_{b_0}(s_0)\},&
   T_{b_0}(s_0)\cap\mathcal A_\gamma(y)\ne\varnothing,\\
   \varnothing,&
   T_{b_0}(s_0)\cap\mathcal A_\gamma(y)=\varnothing.
 \end{cases}
\]
The first-order definition and \(J=D\theta_{b_0}(s_0)\) similarly give
\[
 \mathcal K_h^{(1)}
 =J\bigl(T_{b_0}(s_0)\cap\mathcal A_\gamma(y)\bigr)
 =\mathcal P_\gamma(y;b_0,s_0)
\]
by \(\text{def:regular-product-score-image}\).

If \(s_0\) is an ambient-interior point of \(\mathcal S_{b_0}\), then for every \(u\in\mathbb R^d\) and all sufficiently small \(t>0\), \(s_0+tu\in\mathcal S_{b_0}\).  The definition of the tangent cone gives \(T_{b_0}(s_0)=\mathbb R^d\).  Since \(\mathcal A_\gamma(y)=\mathcal A_{1,\gamma}(y_1)\times\mathcal A_{2,\gamma}(y_2)\) and \(J=(J_1,J_2)\),
\[
 J\mathcal A_\gamma(y)
 =\{J_1u_1+J_2u_2:u_e\in\mathcal A_{e,\gamma}(y_e)\}
 =J_1\mathcal A_{1,\gamma}(y_1)
    \oplus J_2\mathcal A_{2,\gamma}(y_2).
\]
If \(\mathcal A_{e,\gamma}(y_e)=y_e+E_{e,\gamma}\), linearity yields
\[
 J\mathcal A_\gamma(y)
 =Jy+J_1E_{1,\gamma}\oplus J_2E_{2,\gamma}.
\]
A Minkowski sum of two linear ellipsoid images need not be an ellipsoid: for example, a horizontal segment plus a vertical segment is a rectangle.  Thus no joint Wald-ellipsoid identity is available in general.

Finally, on every branch the target definition gives \((\theta_b)_3=(\theta_b)_1-(\theta_b)_2\).  Differentiating this identity at \(s_0\) shows that the third row of \(J\) is the first row minus the second.  Therefore \(\operatorname{rank}(J)\le2\), without any additional rank assumption.
