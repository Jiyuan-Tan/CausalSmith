For every \(b\in\mathbb B_p\), the rational predecessor-regression map \(\theta_b\) on \(\mathcal S_b\) has a \(C^\infty\) extension to all of \(\Omega_p\). More precisely, each environment-specific nonzero direct-effect coordinate is a polynomial divided by a positive-definite principal minor of the corresponding covariance matrix, coordinates fixed to zero extend as zero, and the third coordinate is the difference of the first two. Consequently every \(\theta_b\) is continuously differentiable, with a uniform first-order Taylor expansion on compact local-coordinate sets, at every \(s_0\in\overline{\mathcal S_b}\cap\Omega_p\).
