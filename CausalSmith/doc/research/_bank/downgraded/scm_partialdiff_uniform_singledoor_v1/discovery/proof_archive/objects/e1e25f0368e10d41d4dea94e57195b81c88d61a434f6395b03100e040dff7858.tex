Let \(C\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) be closed and semialgebraic, and let \(E\subseteq\mathbb R^d\) be compact, full-dimensional, semialgebraic, and regular closed, meaning \(E=\overline{\operatorname{int}E}\).  Outside a semialgebraic Lebesgue-null set of translations \(y\),
\[
 C\cap(y+E)=\overline{C\cap(y+\operatorname{int}E)}.
\]
At every such \(y\), locally Painleve--Kuratowski convergence \(C_n\to C\), Hausdorff convergence \(y_n+E_n\to y+E\), and a common compact bound on the intersections imply Fell convergence \(\overline{C_n\cap(y_n+E_n)}\to C\cap(y+E)\), with Hausdorff convergence whenever the limiting intersection is nonempty.
