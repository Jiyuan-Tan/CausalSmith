The Gaussian boundary class is a subclass of the full Bystrova--Devijver anchor class:
\[
 \mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\subseteq\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{BD}}.
\]
In particular, membership in \(\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{df}}\) entails invariance of the full exogenous distribution, not merely equality of its coordinate variances.
