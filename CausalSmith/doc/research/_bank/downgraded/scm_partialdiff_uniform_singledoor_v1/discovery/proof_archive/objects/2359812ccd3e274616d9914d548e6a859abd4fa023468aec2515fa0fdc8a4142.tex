Let \((M_N)\) be a local model sequence with \(s(M_N)=s_0+h/\sqrt N+o(N^{-1/2})\) in a true branch \(b_0\). Under tangent-cone regularity, \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\gamma}\Rightarrow\mathcal K_h^{(0)}\) in the Fell topology. If \(\Gamma(s_0)=\{\tau_0\}\), then \(\sqrt N(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\gamma}-\tau_0)\Rightarrow\mathcal K_h^{(1)}\) in the Fell topology. The limit is derived from the paired Gaussian covariance central limit theorem, the exact Wishart acceptance bodies, and the rational branch representation; branch smoothness is a derived conclusion rather than an assumption.
