Fix \((a,b)\) in the stated parameter space.  By \(\text{thm:honesty-noncontraction-lower-bound}\), the chain and fork witnesses belong to \(\mathcal M_3^{\mathrm{df}}\), have the same sample law, and have target vectors separated by \(\sqrt2|a|\).  By \(\text{lem:gaussian-subclass-of-anchor}\), both witnesses also belong to \(\mathcal M_3^{\mathrm{BD}}\).  Uniform coverage over \(\mathcal M_3^{\mathrm{BD}}\) therefore supplies coverage at least \(1-\gamma\) at each of these two models.  Applying the final two-model conclusion of \(\text{thm:honesty-noncontraction-lower-bound}\) under their common law gives
\[
 \Pr\{\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\ge\sqrt2|a|\}\ge1-2\gamma
\]
for every \(N\), as required.
