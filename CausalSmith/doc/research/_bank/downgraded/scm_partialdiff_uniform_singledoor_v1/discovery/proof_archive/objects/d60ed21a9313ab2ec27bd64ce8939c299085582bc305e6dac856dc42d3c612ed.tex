For every integer \(p\ge3\) and every \((a,b)\) in the chain--fork parameter space, let \((D_N)\) be a confidence procedure satisfying
\[
 \inf_{M\in\mathcal M_p^{\mathrm{BD}}}
 \Pr_M\{\tau(M)\in D_N\}\ge 1-\gamma
\]
for every \(N\).  Then, under the common sample law of the \(p\)-dimensional chain and fork witnesses in \(\text{def:chain-fork-witness}\),
\[
 \Pr\{\operatorname{diam}(D_N)\ge\sqrt2|a|\}\ge1-2\gamma
\]
for every \(N\).  Thus, in every dimension \(p\ge3\), the same-law noncontraction bound applies to uniform honesty over the full, not necessarily Gaussian, Bystrova--Devijver anchor class.
