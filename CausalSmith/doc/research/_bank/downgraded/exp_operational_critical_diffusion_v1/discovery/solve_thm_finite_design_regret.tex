Put
\[
 S_N(p,\gamma)=V_{0,N}(p)+V_{1,N}(p,\gamma),
 \qquad
 S(p,\gamma)=V_0(p)+V_\gamma(p).
\]
These quantities are nonnegative because each summand is a stationary Poisson energy by the definitions of \(V_{0,N}\), \(V_{1,N}\), \(V_0\), and \(V_\gamma\).  The compact parameter and staffing domains are supplied by Assumptions \(\mathrm{rate\mbox{-}membership}\) and \(\mathrm{staffing\mbox{-}window}\).  Therefore the uniform energy convergence in Theorem \(\mathrm{diffusion\mbox{-}poisson}\) gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 r_N
 &:={\sup}_{p\in\mathcal P,\,|\gamma|\leq G}
       |S_N(p,\gamma)-S(p,\gamma)| \\
 &\leq {\sup}_{p\in\mathcal P}|V_{0,N}(p)-V_0(p)|
   +{\sup}_{p\in\mathcal P,\,|\gamma|\leq G}
       |V_{1,N}(p,\gamma)-V_\gamma(p)|
 \longrightarrow 0 .                                      \tag{1}
\end{aligned}
\]
For \(x,y\geq0\), if \(x\geq y\), then
\[
 (\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y})^2
 =x+y-2\sqrt{xy}
 \leq x-y=|x-y|,
\]
because \(\sqrt{xy}\geq y\); interchanging \(x\) and \(y\) proves in all cases that
\[
 |\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y}|\leq\sqrt{|x-y|}.                       \tag{2}
\]
By Definitions \(\mathrm{design\mbox{-}objective}\) and \(J_N\), the terms \(c_M\gamma\) cancel when the objectives are subtracted.  Equations (1)--(2) consequently imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta_N
 &:={\sup}_{p\in\mathcal P,\,|\gamma|\leq G}
       |J_N(p,\gamma)-J(p,\gamma)| \\
 &\leq c_I z_\alpha\sqrt{r_N}
 \longrightarrow0 .                                       \tag{3}
\end{aligned}
\]
The multiplier in (3) is finite: Assumption \(\mathrm{information\mbox{-}cost\mbox{-}positive}\) gives \(0<c_I<\infty\), while Assumption \(\mathrm{test\mbox{-}size}\) gives \(1-\alpha/2\in(1/2,1)\), and hence \(z_\alpha=\Phi^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)\in(0,\infty)\).  Thus \(\Delta_N=o(1)\) uniformly in \(p\) and \(\gamma\).

Theorem \(\mathrm{certified\mbox{-}capacity\mbox{-}design}\) supplies the model-specific sound interval-oracle pair from the stipulated rational input name.  Under the present theorem's conditional premise that this sound oracle is supplied and that the certificate algorithm is run with it, Theorem \(\mathrm{oracle\mbox{-}conditional\mbox{-}capacity\mbox{-}certificate}\) applies.  For each fixed \(p\in\mathcal P\), write
\[
 \mu(p)=\inf_{|\gamma|\leq G}J(p,\gamma).
\]
The returned certificate and incumbent satisfy
\[
 L_\epsilon\leq\mu(p)
 \leq J(p,\gamma_\epsilon)
 \leq U_\epsilon,
 \qquad
 U_\epsilon-L_\epsilon\leq\epsilon,                        \tag{4}
\]
and \(\gamma_\epsilon\in[-G,G]\).  It follows equation by equation from (4) that
\[
 J(p,\gamma_\epsilon)-\mu(p)
 \leq U_\epsilon-L_\epsilon
 \leq\epsilon.                                               \tag{5}
\]

The definition of \(\Delta_N\) yields, for every \(|\gamma|\leq G\),
\[
 J_N(p,\gamma)\geq J(p,\gamma)-\Delta_N.
\]
Taking the infimum over the same nonempty compact staffing interval gives
\[
 \inf_{|\gamma|\leq G}J_N(p,\gamma)
 \geq \mu(p)-\Delta_N.                                      \tag{6}
\]
Likewise, the certified feasibility of \(\gamma_\epsilon\) and the definition of \(\Delta_N\) give
\[
 J_N(p,\gamma_\epsilon)
 \leq J(p,\gamma_\epsilon)+\Delta_N.                        \tag{7}
\]
Subtracting (6) from (7), and then using (5), proves
\[
\begin{aligned}
 J_N(p,\gamma_\epsilon)
 -\inf_{|\gamma|\leq G}J_N(p,\gamma)
 &\leq J(p,\gamma_\epsilon)-\mu(p)+2\Delta_N \\
 &\leq\epsilon+2\Delta_N.                                  \tag{8}
\end{aligned}
\]
Because the same \(\Delta_N\) controls every \(p\in\mathcal P\), taking the supremum in (8) over \(p\) and applying (3) proves the asserted uniform bound \(\epsilon+o(1)\).  The recommendation and every inequality involving \(\gamma_\epsilon\) remain conditional on the supplied sound oracle and its returned certificate, exactly as claimed.
