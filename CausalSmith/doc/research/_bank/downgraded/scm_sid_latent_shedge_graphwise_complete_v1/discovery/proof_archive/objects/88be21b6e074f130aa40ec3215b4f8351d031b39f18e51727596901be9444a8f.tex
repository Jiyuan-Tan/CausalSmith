Fix \(H\subseteq N_S\).  Suppose first that \(u,v\in H\) are joined by a bidirected path \(\gamma\) in \(G^S[H\cup A_S\cup\{S\}]\).  List, in their order along \(\gamma\), the vertices of \(\gamma\) that lie in \(H\):
\[
  h_0=u,h_1,\ldots,h_r=v.
\]
The internal vertices of the subpath from \(h_{j-1}\) to \(h_j\) lie outside \(H\), and every vertex of \(\gamma\) lies in \(H\cup A_S\cup\{S\}\).  Since \(H\subseteq N_S\) and \(A_S\cap N_S=\varnothing\), those internal vertices lie in \(A_S\cup\{S\}\).  By \(\mathrm{def:suppression\text{-}projection}\), \(h_{j-1}\leftrightarrow h_j\) is therefore an edge of \(\Pi_S(G^S)[H]\).  Hence \(u\) and \(v\) are bidirected-connected in the projection.

Conversely, suppose \(u=h_0,\ldots,h_r=v\) is a bidirected path in \(\Pi_S(G^S)[H]\).  For each projected edge \(h_{j-1}\leftrightarrow h_j\), \(\mathrm{def:suppression\text{-}projection}\) supplies a bidirected path in \(G^S\) whose internal vertices lie in \(A_S\cup\{S\}\).  Concatenating these finitely many paths gives a bidirected walk from \(u\) to \(v\) contained in \(H\cup A_S\cup\{S\}\).  Deleting closed subwalks gives a bidirected path in the same induced graph.  Thus the two bidirected-connectivity relations on \(H\) coincide.  Districts are the equivalence classes of bidirected connectivity, while \(\operatorname{SComp}_{G^S}(H)\) is defined in \(\mathrm{def:s\text{-}component}\) by intersecting the original districts with \(H\).  The asserted equality of partitions follows.
