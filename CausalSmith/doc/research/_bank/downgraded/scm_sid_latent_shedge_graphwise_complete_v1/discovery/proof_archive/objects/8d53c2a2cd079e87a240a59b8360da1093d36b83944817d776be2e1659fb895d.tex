Let \(F_0\subset F_1\) be finite nested common-\(R\)-rooted \(C\)-forests in a semi-Markovian ADMG, with \(C\cap X_N=\varnothing\), \(V(F_1)\cap X_N\ne\varnothing\), and \(R\subseteq\operatorname{An}_{G[N_S\setminus X_N]}(Y_N)\). The positive finite-state completeness construction of Kivva et al. supplies an odd integer \(\kappa>4\), finite state spaces, and two semi-Markovian SCMs using the forest incidence that have the same strictly positive observational distribution but, at a common treatment value, have different postintervention distributions on \(R\).
