Let \(\widetilde M\) be a recursive augmented SCM with finite endogenous signature \(\widetilde\sigma\) on \(V\cup\{S\}\), whose declared parent interfaces and shared-exogenous incidences induce an augmented ADMG contained in \(G^S\). The completion \(\operatorname{Exactify}^{\mathrm{fin}}_{G^S}(\widetilde M)\) adds each missing parent in \(\operatorname{Pa}_{G^S}(v)\) to the declared interface of \(v\) and makes the structural function constant in that coordinate. For every listed bidirected edge not already witnessed by shared exogenous incidence, it adds a fresh independent nondegenerate finite latent factor incident to its endpoints and makes both structural functions constant in that factor. It introduces no other interface or shared incidence and leaves all original state spaces, exogenous laws, and structural values unchanged.
