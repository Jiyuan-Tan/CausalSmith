Assume \(\operatorname{NativeForestTrace}(G^S;X,Y)\) and let \((C,T)\) be a reachable terminal failure. By \(\mathrm{lem:shedge\text{-}projects\text{-}to\text{-}hedge}\), together with the actual-incidence clause of \(\mathrm{def:native\text{-}forest\text{-}trace}\), there are nested common-\(R\)-rooted C-forests \(F_0\subset F_1\) with
\[
 V(F_0)=C,\qquad V(F_1)=T,
\]
all their directed and bidirected edges belonging to \(G^S[N_S]\), and
\[
 C\cap X_N=\varnothing,\qquad
 T\cap X_N\ne\varnothing,\qquad
 R\subseteq\operatorname{An}_{G^S[N_S\setminus X_N]}(Y_N).
\]
These are exactly the hypotheses of \(\mathrm{lem:kivva\text{-}positive\text{-}finite\text{-}forest\text{-}countermodels}\), applied to the semi-Markovian graph \(F_1\). Hence there are an odd \(\kappa>4\), a finite signature \(\widetilde\sigma_T\), two finite semi-Markovian models \(\overline M_0,\overline M_1\) using only the edges of \(F_1\subseteq G^S[T]\), and a common treatment value \(z^\dagger\in\mathcal X_{X_N\cap T}\), such that
\[
 P_{\overline M_0}(T=t)=P_{\overline M_1}(T=t)>0
 \quad\text{for every }t\in\mathcal X_T,
\]
while their postintervention laws on \(R\) at \(z^\dagger\) differ.

For each \(q\in X_N\cap T\), choose a permutation of its finite alphabet that maps \(z_q^\dagger\) to a distinguished symbol \(0\), and conjugate the structural table for \(q\) and every table receiving \(q\) by that permutation, in both models. This is a common bijective relabeling of endogenous states. Therefore it preserves equality of corresponding observational atoms, strict positivity of every atom, and inequality of the corresponding postintervention distributions. After relabeling the common intervention is
\[
 x_{N,T}^\dagger=0_{X_N\cap T}.
\]
The two resulting probability measures on the finite set \(\mathcal X_R\) are unequal. Therefore there exists \(r^\dagger\in\mathcal X_R\) for which their point masses differ. Defining
\[
 \delta=
 P_{\overline M_0}(R=r^\dagger\mid\operatorname{do}(X_N\cap T=0))
 -P_{\overline M_1}(R=r^\dagger\mid\operatorname{do}(X_N\cap T=0))
\]
gives \(\delta\ne0\). The alphabet description is inherited from the cited Kivva construction: the forest coordinates are based on \(\{0,\ldots,\kappa\}\), and all other coordinates are finite products. No step performs a binary reduction, chooses rational tables, or constructs an effective encoding.
