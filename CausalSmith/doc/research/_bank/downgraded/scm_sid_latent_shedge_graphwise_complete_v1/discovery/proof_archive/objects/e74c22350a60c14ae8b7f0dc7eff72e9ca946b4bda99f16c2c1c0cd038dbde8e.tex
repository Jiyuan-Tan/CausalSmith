Fix \(D_i\in\mathcal D_S(D)\) and a terminal call at \((C,T)\), where \(C=D_i\), and fix \((F_0,F_1,R,\overline M_0,\overline M_1,x_{\mathrm{bin}})\in\mathsf{ProjHedgeInput}(G^S;X,Y;C,T)\). Let \(\widetilde E_0,\widetilde E_1\in\operatorname{RawCertSCM}(G^S,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}})\) be candidate rational full-graph extensions satisfying
\[
 P_{\llbracket\widetilde E_0\rrbracket,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}^S(v)
 =P_{\llbracket\widetilde E_1\rrbracket,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}^S(v)>0
 \quad\text{for every }v\in\{0,1\}^{V}.
\]
Suppose their causal targets \(\Theta_{x_{\mathrm{bin}},\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}(\llbracket\widetilde E_r\rrbracket)\), \(r\in\{0,1\}\), have factorizations with the same outer selected conditional \(p^S\) and the same factors \(q_j\) for \(D_j\ne C\), and use local factors \(q_C^{(0)}\) and \(q_C^{(1)}\) in the \(C\)-position. Define
\[
 \Delta(y)=\sum_{w,d_{D\setminus Y_N}}
 p^S(y_S,w\mid x_S)
 \bigl(q_C^{(0)}-q_C^{(1)}\bigr)
 \prod_{D_j\ne C}q_j.
\]
Then the two candidate full targets differ if and only if \(\Delta(y)\ne0\) for at least one \(y\in\mathcal X_Y\). If this nonvanishing condition holds, then \(E_r=\operatorname{Exactify}_{G^S}(\widetilde E_r)\), \(r\in\{0,1\}\), belong to \(\operatorname{CertSCM}(G^S,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}})\), retain the common positive selected law and unequal targets, and satisfy \(\mathsf{Check}_{\mathrm{NONID}}(G^S,X,Y,x_{\mathrm{bin}},E_0,E_1)=\mathrm{accept}\). If \(\Delta(y)=0\) for every \(y\), these exactifications have equal targets and the checker's target-separation test rejects. In particular, \(q_C^{(0)}\ne q_C^{(1)}\) alone is insufficient for a district certificate.
