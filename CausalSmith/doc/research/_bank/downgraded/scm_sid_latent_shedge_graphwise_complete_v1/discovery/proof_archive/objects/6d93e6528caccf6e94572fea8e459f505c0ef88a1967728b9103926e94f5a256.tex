The note defines \(\operatorname{NativeForestTrace}\), proves its inclusion in the published exact-graph setting, and establishes success if and only if graphwise finite-signature identification on that subclass. Its failure proof uses Kivva et al.'s positive finite multistate separator, a randomized-response transmission with bound \(|\delta|-2\{1-(1-\epsilon)^L\}>0\), and inert finite-state exactification. It also proves projection geometry, the exact Eq. 23 contraction obstruction, recursion bounds, and certificate-checker soundness, while preserving universal completeness and total compilation as open questions.
