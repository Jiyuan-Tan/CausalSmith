Let \(H\) be a finite semi-Markovian graph, let \(Z\) and \(O\) be sets of observed vertices, and let \(F\) and \(F'\) be \(R\)-rooted C-forests. If \(V(F)\cap Z\ne\varnothing\), \(V(F')\cap Z=\varnothing\), \(F'\subseteq F\), and \(R\subset\operatorname{An}_{H_{\overline Z}}(O)\), then \(F,F'\) form a hedge for \(P_z(O)\), and \(P_z(O)\) is not identifiable from the observational law in \(H\).
