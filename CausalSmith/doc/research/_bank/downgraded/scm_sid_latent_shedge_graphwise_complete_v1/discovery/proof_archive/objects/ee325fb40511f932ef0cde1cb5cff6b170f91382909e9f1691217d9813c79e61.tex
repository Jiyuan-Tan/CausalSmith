\(\operatorname{NativeForestTrace}(G^S;X,Y)\) holds when: (i) \(G^S\) is a finite acyclic directed mixed graph on \(V\cup\{S\}\), \(S\) has no children, \(X\cap Y=\varnothing\), and \(Y\ne\varnothing\); (ii) the initial selected d-separation check of published Algorithm 1 passes; and (iii) for every \(D_i\in\mathcal D_S(D)\) and every reachable terminal failed call \((C,T)\) on the chain rooted at the unique \(T_i^{(0)}\in\operatorname{SComp}_{G^S}(N_S)\) containing \(D_i\), one has \(C=D_i\subsetneq T\subseteq T_i^{(0)}\), both \(G^S[C]\) and \(G^S[T]\) are bidirected-connected, and there exist \(R=C\) and nested common-\(R\)-rooted \(C\)-forests \(F_0\subset F_1\) with vertex sets \(C\) and \(T\), respectively, such that every directed and bidirected edge of both forests is an actual edge of \(G^S[N_S]\), \(C\cap X_N=\varnothing\), \(T\cap X_N\ne\varnothing\), and \(R\subseteq\operatorname{An}_{G^S[N_S\setminus X_N]}(Y_N)\). Compatibility on such an input remains the exact induced-graph compatibility of \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\); the predicate restricts graph traces, not mechanisms on a fixed graph.
