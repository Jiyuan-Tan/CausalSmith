For every finite signature \(\sigma\), fix \(D_i\in\mathcal D_S(D)\), let \(T_i^{(0)}\) be the unique member of \(\operatorname{SComp}_{G^S}(N_S)\) containing \(D_i\), and consider the call chain of \(\operatorname{sID\text{-}Single}(D_i,T_i^{(0)},Q_{P_\sigma}^S[T_i^{(0)}])\). If this chain fails at \((C,T)\), then \(C=D_i\), \(C\subsetneq T\subseteq T_i^{(0)}\), \(\operatorname{SComp}_{G^S}(C)=\{C\}\), \(\operatorname{SComp}_{G^S}(T)=\{T\}\), and \(\operatorname{An}_{G^S[T]}(C)=T\), so \(\operatorname{SHedge}_{G^S}(C,T)\). Moreover, \(\Pi_S(G^S)[C]\) and \(\Pi_S(G^S)[T]\) are single districts, \(\operatorname{An}_{\Pi_S(G^S)[T]}(C)=T\), and one can construct \(R=C\) and nested common-\(R\)-rooted \(C\)-forests \(F_0\subset F_1\) with vertex sets \(C\) and \(T\) satisfying \(C\cap X_N=\varnothing\), \(T\cap X_N\ne\varnothing\), and \(R\subseteq\operatorname{An}_{\Pi_S(G^S)[N_S\setminus X_N]}(Y_N)\). This graph-theoretic conclusion does not assert the existence of a strictly positive rational projected countermodel pair.
