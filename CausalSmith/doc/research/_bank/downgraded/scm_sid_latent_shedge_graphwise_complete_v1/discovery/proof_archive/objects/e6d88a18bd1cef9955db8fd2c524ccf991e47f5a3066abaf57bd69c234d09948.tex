Fix \(\sigma_{EI}\), \(G^{EI}\), \(\mathcal O\), and \(a\) as in the statement, and abbreviate
\[
 \mathfrak M=\mathfrak M_{EI,+}(G^{EI},\sigma_{EI}),
 \qquad
 \mathcal F=\mathcal F_{EI}(\mathcal O;G^{EI}).
\]
We first verify that every conditional law used below is well defined.  If \(N\in\mathfrak M\), then, by \(\mathrm{ass:exposure\text{-}selector\text{-}positivity}\), \(\mathrm{ass:imaging\text{-}selector\text{-}positivity}\), and \(\mathrm{def:two\text{-}selector\text{-}model\text{-}class}\), for each \(r\in\{E,I\}\) and each substantive cell \(v\),
\[
 P_N(V_{EI}=v,S_r=1)>0.
\]
Because the signature is finite and its product state space is nonempty, summation over all substantive cells gives
\[
 P_N(S_r=1)
 =\sum_{v\in\prod_{k\in V_{EI}}\mathcal X_k}
   P_N(V_{EI}=v,S_r=1)>0.
\]
Thus both components of \(\operatorname{Obs}_{EI}(N)\), as defined in \(\mathrm{def:tian\text{-}observation\text{-}map}\), exist.  Moreover,
\[
 P_N(A=a,S_I=1)
 =\sum_{v:v_A=a}P_N(V_{EI}=v,S_I=1)>0.
\]
The model is recursive by \(\mathrm{ass:two\text{-}selector\text{-}exact\text{-}compatibility}\) and \(\mathrm{def:two\text{-}selector\text{-}model\text{-}class}\).  For any exogenous realization at which the observational solution has \(A=a\), replacing the structural equation for \(A\) by the constant \(a\) leaves the entire solution unchanged: this follows by induction along a topological ordering, since the replacement agrees with the observational value at \(A\), and every later structural function receives the same parent values and the same exogenous inputs.  Consequently
\[
 \{A=a,S_I=1\}_{\mathrm{obs}}
 \subseteq
 \{S_I=1\}_{\operatorname{do}(A=a)},
\]
and hence
\[
 P_N(S_I=1\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a))
 \ge P_N(A=a,S_I=1)>0.
\]
Therefore \(\Theta^I_a(N)=P_N(Y\mid\operatorname{do}(A=a),S_I=1)\), with intervention performed before conditioning as specified by the symbol \(\Theta^I_a\), is also well defined.

We now separate the causal target from the observation map.  Point identification of \(\Theta^I_a\) from the two Tian margins over \(\mathfrak M\) means the following property:
\[
 \mathsf{Id}_{EI}(a)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{for all }N_0,N_1\in\mathfrak M,
 \quad
 \operatorname{Obs}_{EI}(N_0)=\operatorname{Obs}_{EI}(N_1)
 \Longrightarrow
 \Theta^I_a(N_0)=\Theta^I_a(N_1).
\]
This line is the definition of identification relative to the stated observation map; it does not define the causal target, which was defined independently by intervention followed by conditioning.

Suppose that the audit exhibits \(M_{EI,0},M_{EI,1}\in\mathcal F\) with
\[
 \Theta^I_a(M_{EI,0})\ne\Theta^I_a(M_{EI,1}).
\]
Unfolding \(\mathrm{def:tian\text{-}feasibility\text{-}fiber}\) separately for \(j=0,1\) yields
\[
 M_{EI,j}\in\mathfrak M
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 \operatorname{Obs}_{EI}(M_{EI,j})=\mathcal O.
\]
In particular,
\[
 \operatorname{Obs}_{EI}(M_{EI,0})
 =\mathcal O
 =\operatorname{Obs}_{EI}(M_{EI,1}),
\]
whereas their causal targets differ.  The pair therefore falsifies the universal implication defining \(\mathsf{Id}_{EI}(a)\), so
\[
 \neg\mathsf{Id}_{EI}(a).
\]
This proves nonidentification from the two observed margins over the positive exact-graph two-selector class.

The same membership statements also prove \(\mathcal F\ne\varnothing\).  Furthermore, by \(\mathrm{ass:two\text{-}selector\text{-}exact\text{-}compatibility}\) and \(\mathrm{def:two\text{-}selector\text{-}model\text{-}class}\), each displayed model has exactly the parent interfaces of \(G^{EI}\), and two distinct endogenous mechanisms share an exogenous input if and only if their vertices are joined by a bidirected edge of \(G^{EI}\).  Thus permitted edges are not being silently deleted or added.  By \(\mathrm{def:tian\text{-}imaging\text{-}law\text{-}set}\), the full imaging-selected laws compatible with the two margins are exactly
\[
 \Gamma_I(\mathcal O;G^{EI})
 =\{P_N(V_{EI}\mid S_I=1):N\in\mathcal F\},
\]
which is the set recorded by the audit.

Finally, unfold \(\mathrm{def:tian\text{-}feasibility\text{-}audit}\).  Its nonidentification branch is enabled only by two displayed models in the same fiber whose \(\Theta^I_a\)-values differ.  If no such pair is displayed, that definition permits only an unmet-assumptions report identifying additional fusion or selected-instrument assumptions, or an inconclusive output.  A one-selector \(\operatorname{CertSCM}\) pair with a common law \(P(V\mid S=1)\) does not, merely from that fact, instantiate the two quantifiers in \(\mathsf{Id}_{EI}(a)\): its members have not thereby been shown to lie in \(\mathfrak M\), and equality of one common selected law has not thereby been shown to equal the ordered two-selector observation \(\mathcal O\).  Separate verification that both realized models belong to the same \(\mathcal F_{EI}(\mathcal O;G^{EI})\) is therefore a necessary condition for that pair to serve as a certificate for the Tian margins.  This proves every asserted branch of the proposition.
