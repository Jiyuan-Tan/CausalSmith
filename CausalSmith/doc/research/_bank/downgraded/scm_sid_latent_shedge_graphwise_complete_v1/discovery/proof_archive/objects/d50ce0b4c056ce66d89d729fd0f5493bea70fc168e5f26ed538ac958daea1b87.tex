Assume throughout that \(\operatorname{NativeForestTrace}(G^S;X,Y)\) holds.  By \(\mathrm{lem:native\text{-}forest\text{-}trace\text{-}subclass}\), \(G^S\) is an admissible finite childless-selector augmented ADMG for published Algorithm~1, and, for each finite signature \(\sigma\), its compatible models are exactly the members of \(\mathfrak M_+(G^S,\sigma)\).

First suppose that Algorithm~1 succeeds.  Fix a finite signature \(\sigma\), an intervention value \(x\in\prod_{v\in X}\mathcal X_v\), and a model \(M\in\mathfrak M_+(G^S,\sigma)\).  By \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:selected\text{-}cell\text{-}positivity}\),
\[
 P_M(V=v,S=1)>0
 \qquad\text{for every }v\in\prod_{j\in V}\mathcal X_j.
\]
Consequently, every marginal cell of the selected law that occurs as a denominator in the outer conditional or in a recursively extracted selected kernel is a finite sum of positive cells and is therefore positive.  In particular,
\[
 P_M(X_S=x_S,S=1)
 =\sum_{v:\,v|_{X_S}=x_S}P_M(V=v,S=1)>0,
\]
so the outer conditional at \(X_S=x_S\) is defined.  The same summation argument applies to every conditioning configuration used by the successful recursive factors evaluated at \(X_N=x_N\).  Moreover, \(\mathrm{lem:target\text{-}support}\) gives
\[
 P_M(S=1\mid\operatorname{do}(X=x))>0,
\]
so the intervention-before-conditioning target is defined.  The hypotheses of \(\mathrm{lem:published\text{-}latent\text{-}sid\text{-}soundness}\) therefore apply, and, pointwise for every \(y\in\prod_{v\in Y}\mathcal X_v\),
\[
 \Phi_{23,x}^{\sigma}(P_{M,\sigma}^S)(y)
 =\Theta_{x,\sigma}(M)(y).
\]
This is precisely the displayed success conclusion, with the evaluation conventions fixed by \(\mathrm{def:eq23\text{-}functional}\).

To deduce graphwise identification, take arbitrary \(M_0,M_1\in\mathfrak M_+(G^S,\sigma)\) satisfying
\[
 P_{M_0,\sigma}^S=P_{M_1,\sigma}^S.
\]
Applying the preceding equality first to \(M_0\) and then to \(M_1\) yields
\[
 \Theta_{x,\sigma}(M_0)
 =\Phi_{23,x}^{\sigma}(P_{M_0,\sigma}^S)
 =\Phi_{23,x}^{\sigma}(P_{M_1,\sigma}^S)
 =\Theta_{x,\sigma}(M_1).
\]
Because \(\sigma\), \(x\), \(M_0\), and \(M_1\) were arbitrary within one common signature, \(\mathrm{def:graphwise\text{-}sid}\) implies \(\operatorname{Ident}^{\mathrm{fin}}_S(G^S;X,Y)\).

Conversely, suppose that Algorithm~1 does not succeed.  Clause~(ii) of \(\mathrm{def:native\text{-}forest\text{-}trace}\) says that its initial selected d-separation check passes.  Hence nonsuccess occurs, if at all, in a single-component recursive chain.  By \(\mathrm{lem:sid\text{-}graph\text{-}recursion\text{-}termination}\), the chain rooted at each \(T_i^{(0)}\) has at most \(|T_i^{(0)}|\) invocations, every chain has depth at most \(|N_S|\), and the total number of single-component invocations is at most
\[
 B_0=\sum_{(D_i,T_i^{(0)})\in\mathcal I_0}|T_i^{(0)}|<\infty.
\]
Therefore a finite execution that does not return the success branch reaches an actual terminal failed call \((C,T)\).  The hypotheses of \(\mathrm{lem:native\text{-}forest\text{-}selected\text{-}extension}\) now hold.  That lemma supplies one finite multistate signature \(\widetilde\sigma\), one value \(x^\dagger\in\mathcal X_X\), models
\[
 M_0,M_1\in\mathfrak M_+(G^S,\widetilde\sigma),
\]
and an event \(A_Y\subseteq\mathcal X_Y\) such that, pointwise on \(\mathcal X_V\),
\[
 P_{M_0,\widetilde\sigma}^S(v)
 =P_{M_1,\widetilde\sigma}^S(v)>0,
\]
whereas
\[
 \Theta_{x^\dagger,\widetilde\sigma}(M_0)(A_Y)
 \ne
 \Theta_{x^\dagger,\widetilde\sigma}(M_1)(A_Y).
\]
The latter inequality implies that the two finite probability distributions \(\Theta_{x^\dagger,\widetilde\sigma}(M_0)\) and \(\Theta_{x^\dagger,\widetilde\sigma}(M_1)\) are unequal, while the preceding pointwise equality says that their selected observational laws coincide.  Thus this single within-\(\widetilde\sigma\) pair violates the implication quantified in \(\mathrm{def:graphwise\text{-}sid}\), and
\[
 \neg\operatorname{Ident}^{\mathrm{fin}}_S(G^S;X,Y).
\]
We have proved that success implies identification and that failure implies nonidentification; equivalently, Algorithm~1 succeeds if and only if \(\operatorname{Ident}^{\mathrm{fin}}_S(G^S;X,Y)\) holds.  The converse used the terminal selected extension only under \(\operatorname{NativeForestTrace}(G^S;X,Y)\); it makes no assertion about virtual-edge traces, initial-check failures, binary or rational witnesses, or an executable failure compiler.
