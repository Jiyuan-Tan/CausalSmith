Let \(L\) be the vector of the three independent fair latent bits. A bijective affine map \(l\mapsto Al+c\) on \(\mathbb F_2^3\) sends the uniform law on \(\mathbb F_2^3\) to the uniform law. Therefore, under the intervention,
\[
 P(Y=y,Z_1=z_1,Z_2=z_2)=\frac18
\]
for every \((y,z_1,z_2)\). In particular, \(Y\) is fair and independent of \((Z_1,Z_2)\). If the selector is a function of \((Z_1,Z_2)\) and an exogenous input independent of \(L\), then \(Y\) is also independent of \(S\). Positivity of the selection denominator permits conditioning, and hence
\[
 P(Y=1\mid\operatorname{do}(X_\star=x_\star),S=1)
 =P(Y=1\mid\operatorname{do}(X_\star=x_\star))=\frac12.
\]
The conclusion applies to each member of any proposed pair in this affine class, so their targets cannot differ.
