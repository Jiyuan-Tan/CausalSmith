Fix \(\sigma\), \(M\in\mathfrak M_+(G^S,\sigma)\), and \(x\in\mathcal X_X\).  Because every substantive state space is nonempty, choose \(z\in\mathcal X_{V\setminus X}\), and put \(v=(x,z)\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:selected\text{-}cell\text{-}positivity}\),
\[
  P_M(V=v,S=1)>0. \tag{1}
\]
Let \(U\) denote the complete exogenous input and let
\[
  A_v=\{u:V_M(u)=v,\ S_M(u)=1\}.
\]
Equation (1) says \(P_U(A_v)>0\).  We claim that every \(u\in A_v\) also gives \(S=1\) after \(\operatorname{do}(X=x)\).  Indeed, order the endogenous vertices topologically, which is possible by the recursiveness clause of \(\mathrm{ass:augmented\text{-}scm\text{-}compatibility}\).  At a vertex in \(X\), the intervention assigns the value \(x\), which is its already realized value on \(A_v\).  At a vertex outside \(X\), if all preceding parents retain their observational values, its unchanged structural function, evaluated at the same exogenous input, retains its observational value.  Induction over the topological order therefore leaves every nonintervened endogenous variable, in particular \(S\), unchanged.  Thus
\[
  A_v\subseteq\{u:S_{M,x}(u)=1\}.
\]
The intervention does not alter the law of \(U\), so
\[
  P_M(S=1\mid\operatorname{do}(X=x))
  =P_U\{S_{M,x}(U)=1\}
  \ge P_U(A_v)>0. \tag{2}
\]
The denominator in the intervention-before-conditioning definition of \(\Theta_{x,\sigma}(M)\) is therefore positive by (2), proving the claim.
