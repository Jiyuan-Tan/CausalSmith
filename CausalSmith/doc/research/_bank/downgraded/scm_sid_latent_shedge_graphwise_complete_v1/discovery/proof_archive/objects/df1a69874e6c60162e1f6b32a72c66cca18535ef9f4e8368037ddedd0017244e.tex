Let \(H\) be a finite semi-Markovian causal graph and let \(Z,O\) be disjoint observed vertex sets. Under the positive-model definition \(P_M(h)>0\) for every observed assignment \(h\), ordinary Algorithm 1 is sound and complete. When it returns failure for \(P_z(O)\), the constructive necessity argument may be carried out with an odd integer \(\kappa>4\) and common finite state spaces, and it gives two semi-Markovian structural equation models inducing \(H\), with the same full-support observational law, but with different distributions \(P_z(O)\) at some common value \(z\). The construction uses \(\{0,\ldots,\kappa\}\)-valued forest coordinates and finite product coordinates; Proposition 4 combines the failed component kernels, and the supplementary proof of Proposition 3 transfers the resulting kernel disagreement to the stated causal effect.
