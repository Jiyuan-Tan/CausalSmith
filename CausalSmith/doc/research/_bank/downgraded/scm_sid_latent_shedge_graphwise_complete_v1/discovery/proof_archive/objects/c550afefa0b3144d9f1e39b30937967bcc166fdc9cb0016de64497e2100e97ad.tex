The suppression projection converts the terminal S-Hedge into ordinary hedge geometry: \(C\) and \(T\) are single projected districts, their directed ancestry is unchanged, and explicit nested common-root forests satisfy the treatment/outcome incidence conditions. For overlapping virtual edges, however, independently replacing each projected latent by a collider-chain realization changes the posterior coupling of shared ancestor mechanisms after conditioning on \(S=1\). The handle definition only specifies the selected-atom equations to be solved; it does not prove that their rational feasible set meets the relative interior or retains the target contrast. The positive binary two-edge base case is itself still open, although a support-deficient \(1/2\)-versus-\(5/8\) pair and two exact obstruction lemmas are available.
