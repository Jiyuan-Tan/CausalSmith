Let \(E\in\operatorname{RawCertSCM}(G^S,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}})\).  Write its original mutually independent latent factors as \(U_1,\ldots,U_m\).  The map \(\operatorname{Exactify}_{G^S}\) makes two kinds of additions, as specified in \(\mathrm{def:exactification\text{-}map}\).

First, every missing observed parent \(u\in\operatorname{Pa}_{G^S}(v)\) is added to the declared interface of \(v\), but the extended table is constant in that new coordinate.  Second, for every still-unwitnessed edge \(v\leftrightarrow w\), a fresh fair factor \(F_{vw}\) with scope \(\{v,w\}\) is added, and both incident tables are constant in \(F_{vw}\).  No other interface or scope is added.  Since the raw incidence graph is contained in \(G^S\), every old parent and every pair in an old latent scope is allowed by \(G^S\).  Consequently the completed parent interfaces are exactly \(\operatorname{Pa}_{G^S}(v)\), every listed bidirected edge is witnessed, and no unlisted pair shares a factor.  All new factors are independent of one another and of \((U_1,\ldots,U_m)\).  The rational normalization, deterministic-table completeness, and strict selected-cell positivity requirements of \(\mathrm{def:certscm\text{-}encoding}\) are therefore satisfied once law preservation below is established.  Hence the completed string belongs to \(\operatorname{CertSCM}(G^S,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}})\).

For law preservation, fix an old joint latent assignment \(u=(u_1,\ldots,u_m)\), a joint assignment \(f\) of all fresh factors, and a topological order of the directed part of \(G^S\).  Induction in that order shows
\[
  (V,S)_{\operatorname{Exactify}_{G^S}(E)}(u,f)=(V,S)_E(u), \tag{3}
\]
because each added coordinate is ignored by the extended structural table.  Therefore, for every event \(A\) in the finite state space,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P_{\llbracket\operatorname{Exactify}_{G^S}(E)\rrbracket}(A)
 &=\sum_u\sum_f
   \left\{\prod_{j=1}^m p_j(u_j)\right\}
   \left\{\prod_{e}2^{-1}\right\}
   \mathbf 1\{(V,S)_E(u)\in A\}\\
 &=\sum_u\left\{\prod_{j=1}^m p_j(u_j)\right\}
   \mathbf 1\{(V,S)_E(u)\in A\}
 =P_{\llbracket E\rrbracket}(A), \tag{4}
\end{aligned}
\]
where the middle equality uses \(\sum_f\prod_e2^{-1}=1\).  In particular, (4) preserves every selected atom and hence its strict positivity.

Replace the structural tables at vertices in \(X\) by the constants \(x_{\mathrm{bin}}\).  The same topological induction gives the analogue of (3) under this intervention, because the only additional coordinates at nonintervened vertices are still ignored and both models use the same intervention at vertices in \(X\).  Repeating (4) yields equality of the complete postintervention laws.  The raw type has strictly positive selected observational cells.  Applying the same topological consistency argument to any observational cell with \(X=x_{\mathrm{bin}}\) shows directly that its postintervention probability of \(S=1\) is positive; equality of the postintervention laws gives the same conclusion for the exactified encoding.  Division of the equal joint masses by the equal positive normalizer gives
\[
 \Theta_{x_{\mathrm{bin}},\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}(\llbracket E\rrbracket)
 =\Theta_{x_{\mathrm{bin}},\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}
   (\llbracket\operatorname{Exactify}_{G^S}(E)\rrbracket).
