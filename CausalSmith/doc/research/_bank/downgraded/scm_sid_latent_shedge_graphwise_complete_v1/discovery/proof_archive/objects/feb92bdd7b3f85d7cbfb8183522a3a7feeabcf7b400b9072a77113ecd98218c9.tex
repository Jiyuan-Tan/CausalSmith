Its chain rooted at \(T_i^{(0)}\) has at most \(|T_i^{(0)}|\) invocations, the recursion depth is at most \(\max_i|T_i^{(0)}|\le|N_S|\), and the total number of single-component invocations is at most \(B_0\).
