Fix an actual terminal call for \(D_i=C\) and two candidate full-graph extensions that use kernels \(q_C^{(0)}\) and \(q_C^{(1)}\) in the \(C\)-position, the same kernels \(q_j\) for every \(D_j\ne C\), and the same outer selected conditional \(p^S\). Their pointwise Eq. 23 product contrast is
\[
 \Delta(y)=\sum_{w,d_{D\setminus Y_N}}
 p^S(y_S,w\mid x_S)
 \bigl(q_C^{(0)}-q_C^{(1)}\bigr)
 \prod_{D_j\ne C}q_j.
\]
The two constructed full targets differ if and only if \(\Delta(y)\ne0\) for at least one outcome value \(y\). Strict positivity of \(p^S\) and of the common factors, together with \(q_C^{(0)}\ne q_C^{(1)}\), does not by itself imply this nonvanishing condition.
