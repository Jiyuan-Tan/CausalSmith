Universal graphwise completeness is not proved. The smallest two-virtual-edge binary problem has only a support-deficient separator with targets \(1/2\) and \(5/8\); independent smoothing and full-rank affine fair-bit constructions are no-go subclasses. Shared or branching selection ancestors couple all selected atoms and their normalizer, and a local kernel contrast may lie in the nullspace of Eq. 23 marginalization. Exact-rational inverse lifting, an initial-failure compiler, and total \(\mathsf{sID}^{\pm}\) compilation remain open. The proved native countermodels are finite and positive but may be multistate and nonrational.
