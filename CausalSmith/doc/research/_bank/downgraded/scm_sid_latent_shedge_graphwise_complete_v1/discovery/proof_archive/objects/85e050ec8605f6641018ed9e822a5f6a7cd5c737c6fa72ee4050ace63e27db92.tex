Known-case regression corollary. If \(G^S\) has no bidirected edges and \(S\) has no children, then for every \(H\subseteq N_S\), \(\operatorname{SComp}_{G^S}(H)\) is the singleton partition of \(H\), and no recursive S-Hedge exists. Consequently the recursive S-Hedge failure branch of \(\mathsf{sID}^{\pm}\) cannot occur. The latent-paper criterion reduces to its initial selected d-separation condition, which the published paper states is consistent with the fully observed S-ID criterion. Whenever the latent Algorithm 1 succeeds, its returned \(\Phi_{23,x}^{\sigma}\) identifies \(\Theta_{x,\sigma}\) by published soundness. This corollary does not assert byte-for-byte equality of the two papers' displayed formulas.
