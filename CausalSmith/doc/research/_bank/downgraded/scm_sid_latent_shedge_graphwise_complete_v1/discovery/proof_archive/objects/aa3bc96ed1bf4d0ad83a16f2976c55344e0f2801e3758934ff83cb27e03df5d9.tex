Assume that the checker accepts \((E_0,E_1)\).  Its initial validation, by \(\mathrm{def:certificate\text{-}checker}\), verifies the complete rational latent tables, their product-factor interpretation, all structural tables, and exact equality of the induced graph with \(G^S\).  Therefore \(\mathrm{lem:certscm\text{-}ambient\text{-}inclusion}\) gives
\[
  \llbracket E_i\rrbracket\in\mathfrak M_+(G^S,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}),
  \qquad i=0,1. \tag{8}
\]
For a binary substantive atom \(v\), let
\[
 a_i(v)=P_{\llbracket E_i\rrbracket}(V=v,S=1),
 \qquad z_i=\sum_v a_i(v).
\]
Acceptance requires \(a_i(v)>0\) for every \(v\).  Hence \(z_i>0\), and it also requires
\[
 a_0(v)z_1=a_1(v)z_0\qquad\text{for every }v. \tag{9}
\]
Dividing (9) by the positive number \(z_0z_1\) yields
\[
 P^S_{\llbracket E_0\rrbracket,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}(v)
 =\frac{a_0(v)}{z_0}
 =\frac{a_1(v)}{z_1}
 =P^S_{\llbracket E_1\rrbracket,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}(v)>0. \tag{10}
\]
After replacing the tables at \(X\) by \(x_{\mathrm{bin}}\), the checker computes
\[
 b_i(y)=P_{\llbracket E_i\rrbracket}
   (Y=y,S=1\mid\operatorname{do}(X=x_{\mathrm{bin}})),
 \qquad q_i=\sum_y b_i(y).
\]
Acceptance requires \(q_0q_1>0\) and, for some \(y\),
\[
 b_0(y)q_1\ne b_1(y)q_0. \tag{11}
\]
Thus both intervention-before-conditioning distributions are defined, and division of (11) by \(q_0q_1\) gives
\[
 \Theta_{x_{\mathrm{bin}},\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}(\llbracket E_0\rrbracket)(y)
 =\frac{b_0(y)}{q_0}
 \ne\frac{b_1(y)}{q_1}
 =\Theta_{x_{\mathrm{bin}},\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}}}(\llbracket E_1\rrbracket)(y). \tag{12}
\]

It remains to justify the stated operation bound.  Table and normalization validation costs at most a constant multiple of \(L(E_0)+L(E_1)\); checking all vertex pairs costs \(O(|V|^2)\).  For model \(i\), each observational or postintervention pass enumerates \(R(E_i)\) latent assignments, evaluates at most \(|V|+1\) endogenous tables in topological order, and updates an atom table.  Inspecting all substantive atoms costs \(O(2^{|V|})\), and inspecting all outcome atoms costs \(O(2^{|Y|})\le O(2^{|V|})\).  Since \(R(E_i)\ge1\), all these terms are bounded, after increasing one universal implementation constant \(c\), by
\[
 c\left\{|V|^2+L(E_0)+L(E_1)
 +2^{|V|}\bigl(R(E_0)+R(E_1)\bigr)(|V|+|Y|+1)\right\}.
\]
Every loop has a finite explicitly encoded range, so the checker terminates.  Equations (8), (10), and (12) prove its semantic soundness.
