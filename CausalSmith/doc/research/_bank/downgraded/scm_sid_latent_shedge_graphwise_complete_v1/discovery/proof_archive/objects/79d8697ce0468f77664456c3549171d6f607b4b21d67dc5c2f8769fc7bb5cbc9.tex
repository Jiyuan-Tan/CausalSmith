Fix a terminal call \((C,T)\). By \(\mathrm{lem:shedge\text{-}projects\text{-}to\text{-}hedge}\), the suppression graph supplies nested ordinary hedge forests in \(\Pi_S(G^S)\). This conclusion is purely graph-theoretic: when a forest edge \(u\leftrightarrow v\) is virtual, it gives only a bidirected path from \(u\) to \(v\) with internal vertices in \(A_S\cup\{S\}\), not a compatible structural realization of the projected shared latent.

There is nevertheless a complete computable reduction of the remaining task. For a proposed certificate \(E_j\), let \(\mathcal U_j\) be its finite joint latent support, let
\[
 \lambda_j(u)=\prod_{\ell=1}^{m(E_j)}p_{j\ell}(u_\ell),
\]
and let \(F_j(u)\) and \(F^{x}_{j}(u)\) be, respectively, the deterministic observed vector before intervention and after replacing the equations for \(X\) by \(x_{\mathrm{bin}}\). Define
\[
 a_j(v)=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U_j}\lambda_j(u)
 \mathbf 1\{F_j(u)|_V=v,\ F_j(u)|_S=1\},
 \qquad z_j=\sum_v a_j(v),
\]
and
\[
 b_j(y)=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U_j}\lambda_j(u)
 \mathbf 1\{F^x_j(u)|_Y=y,\ F^x_j(u)|_S=1\},
 \qquad q_j=\sum_y b_j(y).
\]
By \(\mathrm{def:certificate\text{-}checker}\), after its syntax and exact-incidence tests the checker accepts exactly when
\[
 a_j(v)>0\quad(j=0,1;\ v\in\{0,1\}^V),
 \qquad a_0(v)z_1=a_1(v)z_0\quad(v\in\{0,1\}^V),
\]
\[
 q_0q_1>0,
 \qquad b_0(y)q_1\ne b_1(y)q_0
 \quad\hbox{for some }y\in\{0,1\}^Y.
\]
The first line is precisely strict selected-cell positivity and equality of the two normalized selected laws, because \(z_0,z_1>0\). The second line is precisely a named atomic-event contrast between the intervention-before-conditioning targets. These are exactly the checker-visible constraints. The inverse-lift handle must additionally impose the finite rational equalities that identify the appropriate restrictions of these tables with the common projected observational kernel and the two carried local kernels \(\overline Q^{(j)}_{C,x_N}\). Thus the desired inverse lift is equivalent to producing finite rational tables satisfying the displayed constraints, those record-preservation equalities, and the original latent-scope and parent-interface restrictions. Once a raw pair satisfies them, \(\mathrm{lem:exactification\text{-}preserves\text{-}laws}\) permits completion of missing allowed incidences without changing any displayed quantity.

For fixed \((G^S,X,Y)\), existence of such a rational certificate is semidecidable. Enumerate \(x_{\mathrm{bin}}\) and all pairs of finite rational table strings in increasing total encoded length, and run \(\mathsf{Check}_{\mathrm{NONID}}\) on each pair. Every individual run terminates by the explicit operation bound in \(\mathrm{def:certificate\text{-}checker}\), and the enumeration reaches every member of \(\operatorname{CertSCM}(G^S,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}})^2\). Hence it halts with an accepted pair if and only if such a pair exists. What is missing is the mathematical existence theorem, not a checker or a search procedure conditional on existence.

The frozen results do not provide that existence theorem. In particular, \(\overline Q^{(0)}_{C,x_N}\ne\overline Q^{(1)}_{C,x_N}\) cannot replace the final inequality above: by \(\mathrm{lem:local\text{-}contrast\text{-}survives\text{-}eq23}\), summation over \(w\) and \(D\setminus Y_N\) can cancel the local contrast. Independent construction along each suppressed path also does not compose at a shared or branching internal ancestor, because the same observed mechanism then occurs in several path constraints while the exogenous factors must remain mutually independent. The smallest unresolved specialization is \(G_\star^S\): a universal constructor would in particular produce the accepted positive binary pair asked for by \(\mathrm{oeq:positive\text{-}binary\text{-}two\text{-}virtual\text{-}edge\text{-}separation}\). The available support-deficient pair fails the inequalities \(a_j(v)>0\), while \(\mathrm{lem:independent\text{-}smoothing\text{-}kills\text{-}early\text{-}contrast}\) and \(\mathrm{lem:affine\text{-}fair\text{-}bit\text{-}early\text{-}target\text{-}half}\) exclude only two restricted response families. Therefore neither a positive universal construction nor a negative impossibility certificate follows from the frozen core.
