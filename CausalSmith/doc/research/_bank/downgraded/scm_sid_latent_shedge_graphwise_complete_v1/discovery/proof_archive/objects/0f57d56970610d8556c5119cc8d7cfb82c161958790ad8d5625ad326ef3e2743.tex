Whenever published Algorithm 1 reaches its single-component recursion, every call chain terminates. A recursive step with fixed target \(C=D_i\) replaces \(T\) by the S-component \(T'\) containing \(D_i\) inside \(A=\operatorname{An}_{G^S[T]}(D_i)\), where \(T'\subseteq A\subsetneq T\). Hence \(\rho\) decreases strictly. The chain rooted at \(T_i^{(0)}\) has at most \(|T_i^{(0)}|\) invocations, its depth is at most \(|N_S|\), and the total number of single-component invocations is at most \(B_0\).
