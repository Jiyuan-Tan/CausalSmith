Take \(E\in\operatorname{CertSCM}(G^S,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}})\).  Its directed structural tables can be evaluated in a topological order because the directed part of \(G^S\) is acyclic.  Thus their interpretation \(\llbracket E\rrbracket\) is a recursive augmented SCM.  By \(\mathrm{def:certscm\text{-}encoding}\), the joint latent mass is
\[
  p(u_1,\ldots,u_m)=\prod_{j=1}^m p_j(u_j),
\]
so the latent factors are mutually independent; the definition imposes no two-mechanism restriction on a scope \(I_j\).  The same definition gives, for every endogenous vertex \(v\),
\[
  P_v=\operatorname{Pa}_{G^S}(v), \tag{5}
\]
and, for every pair of distinct vertices \(v,w\),
\[
 v\leftrightarrow w\in G^S
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists j\;\{v,w\}\subseteq I_j. \tag{6}
\]
Equations (5) and (6) are exactly the parent-interface and shared-exogenous clauses of \(\mathrm{ass:augmented\text{-}scm\text{-}compatibility}\).  Finally, membership in the certificate type requires
\[
 P_{\llbracket E\rrbracket}(V=z,S=1)>0
 \quad\text{for every }z\in\{0,1\}^{V}, \tag{7}
\]
which is \(\mathrm{ass:selected\text{-}cell\text{-}positivity}\) for the binary signature.  Recursiveness, (5), (6), and (7) are precisely the membership clauses of \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\).  Hence \(\llbracket E\rrbracket\in\mathfrak M_+(G^S,\sigma_{\mathrm{bin}})\).
