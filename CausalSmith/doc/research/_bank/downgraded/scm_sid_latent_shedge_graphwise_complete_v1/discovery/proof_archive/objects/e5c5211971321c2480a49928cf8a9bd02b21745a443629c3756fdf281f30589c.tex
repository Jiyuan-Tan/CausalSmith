The target of every call on the chain is fixed at \(C=D_i\) by \(\mathrm{def:success\text{-}or\text{-}certificate\text{-}algorithm}\). At a terminal failure,
\[
 C\subsetneq T\subseteq T_i^{(0)},\qquad
 \operatorname{SComp}_{G^S}(C)=\{C\},\qquad
 \operatorname{SComp}_{G^S}(T)=\{T\},\qquad
 \operatorname{An}_{G^S[T]}(C)=T.
\]
These are exactly the clauses of \(\operatorname{SHedge}_{G^S}(C,T)\) in \(\mathrm{def:s\text{-}hedge}\).

Apply \(\mathrm{lem:projection\text{-}components}\) with \(H=C\) and \(H=T\). The two singleton S-component identities imply that \(\Pi_S(G^S)[C]\) and \(\Pi_S(G^S)[T]\) each have one district. Projection preserves all and only directed edges with endpoints in \(N_S\), so
\[
 \operatorname{An}_{\Pi_S(G^S)[T]}(C)
 =\operatorname{An}_{G^S[T]}(C)=T.
\]
Choose a bidirected spanning tree \(K_0\) of \(\Pi_S(G^S)[C]\). Starting from \(K_0\), repeatedly attach a vertex outside the current set by the first edge on a bidirected path to that set; this gives a spanning tree \(K_1\) of \(\Pi_S(G^S)[T]\) with \(K_1[C]=K_0\). Put \(R=C\). Give \(F_0\) vertex set \(C\), bidirected tree \(K_0\), and no directed edges. For \(t\in T\setminus C\), define
\[
 d(t)=\min\{\ell:\text{a directed path of length }\ell
 \text{ runs from }t\text{ to }C\text{ in }G^S[T]\}.
\]
The ancestral equality makes \(d(t)\) finite. Choose a next vertex \(c(t)\) on a shortest path and put \(t\to c(t)\) in \(F_1\). Since \(d(c(t))=d(t)-1\), there is no directed cycle; vertices outside \(C\) have one child and vertices in \(C\) have none. With bidirected tree \(K_1\), \(F_1\) is an \(R\)-rooted \(C\)-forest and \(F_1[C]=F_0\).

From \(D=\operatorname{An}_{G^S[N_S\setminus X_N]}(Y_N)\) and \(C=D_i\subseteq D\),
\[
 C\cap X_N=\varnothing,\qquad
 R=C\subseteq\operatorname{An}_{G^S[N_S\setminus X_N]}(Y_N)
 =\operatorname{An}_{\Pi_S(G^S)[N_S\setminus X_N]}(Y_N).
\]
If \(T\cap X_N=\varnothing\), every \(t\in T\) reaches \(C\), and every vertex of \(C\) reaches \(Y_N\), inside \(N_S\setminus X_N\). Thus \(T\subseteq D\). Since \(T\) is one S-component containing the S-component \(D_i=C\) of \(D\), maximality of \(D_i\) gives \(T\subseteq C\), contrary to \(C\subsetneq T\). Hence \(T\cap X_N\ne\varnothing\).

If \(\operatorname{NativeForestTrace}(G^S;X,Y)\) holds, its terminal-trace clause supplies forests with the same vertex, root, and incidence properties whose edges all belong to \(G^S[N_S]\). This proves the native conclusion. No step constructs a causal model.
