Fix \((D_i,T_i^{(0)})\). Every call keeps \(C=D_i\). Recursion occurs only when
\[
 C\subsetneq A:=\operatorname{An}_{G^S[T]}(C)\subsetneq T.
\]
The next enclosure is the S-component \(T'\) of \(A\) containing \(C\), whence
\[
 \varnothing\ne C\subseteq T'\subseteq A\subsetneq T,\qquad |T'|\le|T|-1.
\]
Thus each step replaces a positive entry \(|T|\) of \(\rho\) by a strictly smaller one. Equivalently, \(|T|\) decreases strictly on each chain, excluding an infinite chain. If \(|T_i^{(0)}|=m\), there are at most \(m-1\) decreases and at most \(m\) invocations. Since \(T_i^{(0)}\subseteq N_S\), depth is at most \(|N_S|\). Summing and using \(\mathrm{def:initial\text{-}call\text{-}budget}\),
\[
 \#\{\text{single-component invocations}\}
 \le\sum_{(D_i,T_i^{(0)})\in\mathcal I_0}|T_i^{(0)}|=B_0.
\]
Finiteness of \(G^S\) makes all bounds finite.
