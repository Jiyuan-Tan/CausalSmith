An exact rational support-deficient pair is known: both models have \(D=B\), \(Y=B\oplus Z_2\), and the same selected law on their common support, while at \(\operatorname{do}(B=0,D=0)\) their selected targets are \(1/2\) and \(5/8\). It fails strict selected-cell positivity. Independent smoothing forces equality of the relevant outcome kernels, and full-rank affine fair-bit codings force both targets to \(1/2\).
