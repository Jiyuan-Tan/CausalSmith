%%% FIELD proof-restricted-uniform-corollary
Put \(m=|\mathcal A|\) and \(L=|\mathrm{LIT}|\).  Theorem~\(\mathrm{Explicit\ oracle\ enumeration\ frontier}\) supplies a deterministic algorithm for the uniform certificates accepted by \(\operatorname{Acc}\).  These are exactly the restricted uniform certificates quantified over in the present statement; the word ``restricted'' records that scope and does not change the feasible set.  The cited theorem gives, simultaneously,
\[
 \#\{\text{distinct queries to }\mathcal O_{\mathrm{src}}\}\leq 2^m,
\]
\[
 T,\;S_{\mathrm{oracle}}
 \leq 2^{O(m+L)}\operatorname{poly}(N_{\mathrm{in}}+N_{\mathcal O}),
\]
where \(T\) is running time and \(S_{\mathrm{oracle}}\) is oracle-answer space.  It also states that the returned certificate is the lexicographically first optimum when \(\operatorname{Opt}<+\infty\), whereas a final infinite value certifies \(\operatorname{Opt}=+\infty\).  These are every algorithmic and output clause of the present theorem.  Consequently the positive alternative in the oracle-search question holds for the restricted uniform problem.  Since the invoked theorem asserts only this upper bound, the corollary asserts no oracle lower bound.
