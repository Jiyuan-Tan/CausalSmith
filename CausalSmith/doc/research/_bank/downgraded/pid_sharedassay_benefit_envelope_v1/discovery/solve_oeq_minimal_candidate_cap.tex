%%% FIELD oeq-proposed-statement
Determine \(M^{\star}\) only after explicitly specifying an admissible evaluation/query model, including the available inputs, permissible symbolic preprocessing and dominance tests, the information returned by a channel evaluation, and the charged cost.  Within that model, prove either a universal reduction below the ten-point complete map or a matching worst-case lower bound, while preserving the already-proved strict-model bound transfer and exact endpoint-attainment criterion.
%%% FIELD oeq-partial-result
The finite-envelope theorem gives a direct exhaustive procedure using at most ten evaluations of the declared candidate map, and at most eight when the informativeness cut is absent.  Ten distinct candidate incidences can occur even in the strictly compatible region.  Indeed, set
\[
[u_L,u_U]=[0,2/5],\qquad [v_L,v_U]=[1/2,1],\qquad
\kappa=3/10,\qquad (q_0,q_1)=(21/50,12/25),\qquad \theta=16.
\]
Then
\[
\min(u_U,q_0,q_1)=2/5,qquad
\max(v_L,q_0,q_1)=1/2,
\]
so \(D=[0,2/5]\times[1/2,1]\cap\{v-u\geq3/10\}\).  The line \(v-u=3/10\) meets \(v=1/2\) at \((1/5,1/2)\) and meets \(u=2/5\) at \((2/5,7/10)\).  Hence the five vertices of \(D\) are
\[
(0,1/2),\quad(1/5,1/2),\quad(2/5,7/10),\quad(2/5,1),\quad(0,1).
\]
Moreover,
\[
q_0+q_1=9/10,qquad \sqrt{\theta}\lvert q_1-q_0\rvert
=4(3/50)=6/25,
\]
and therefore the two stationary sums are \(33/50\) and \(57/50\).  Intersecting the corresponding lines \(u+v=33/50\) and \(u+v=57/50\) with the axis-aligned edges of \(D\) gives exactly the four feasible contacts
\[
(0,33/50),\quad(4/25,1/2),\quad(2/5,37/50),\quad(7/50,1).
\]
On the informativeness edge, the equations \(v-u=3/10\) and \(u+v=9/10\) have the unique solution \((u,v)=(3/10,3/5)\).  These ten points are pairwise distinct.  Finally, every one is strictly compatible because every \((u,v)\in D\) obeys
\[
u\leq2/5<21/50=\min(q_0,q_1),
\qquad
v\geq1/2>12/25=\max(q_0,q_1).
\]
Thus the ten-incidence configuration preserves strict compatibility.  This calculation is not a query lower bound: without a declared rule for symbolic preprocessing, dominance tests, oracle responses, and cost, an exact algorithm may discard a candidate without evaluating \(b_{\theta}\) there.
