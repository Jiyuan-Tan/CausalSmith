Fresh maximality and substrate audit

The current paper is still not class-maximal. Its own proved substrate yields
a third exact regime that lies strictly outside both existing recovery
domains: stable, rank-deficient feedback with zero diagonal, including
reciprocal pairs, under a principal-log condition only on the observable
active image.

This is not speculative. The stable covariance lemma already proves, without
acyclicity,

  S=im(Gamma_1)=im(A),  Gamma_2=T Gamma_1,
  Gamma_1=J^2 K,  and  Z:=K-K^T=AD-DA^T,

with J invertible and S invariant under A, B, T, and J. Consequently the same
O,R,F construction used by the DAG theorem identifies F as T restricted to
S for every stable member, not only for a DAG. The current full-rank theorem
uses S=R^p and therefore leaves this rank-deficient information unused.

A nonempty strict witness is a four-label model whose first three labels form
a stable directed 3-cycle and whose fourth label is excitation-isolated. Its
A is singular and cyclic, so it belongs to neither the DAG branch nor the
full-rank branch. It has zero diagonal and satisfies the restricted strip for
an open range of amplitudes and bin widths. Reciprocal rank-deficient examples
are also admitted by the construction below. A direct independent numerical
check on a stable 3-cycle-plus-isolated-label example recovered beta, K, and A
to errors below 3e-15; this is diagnostic only, while the algebraic proof path
below supplies the actual justification.

Proof path

Let r=rank(Gamma_1)>0, choose an orthonormal O for S=im(Gamma_1), let
R=O^T Gamma_1, and define

  F=O^T Gamma_2 R^T (R R^T)^{-1}.

The stable semigroup lemma gives F as the coordinate matrix of T|S. On the
image-defined domain with spec(Delta B|S) in |Im z|<pi, set

  L=Delta^{-1} Log(F)=B|S.

Because A maps the ambient space into S, its induced map on R^p/S is zero.
In a basis adapted to S, A therefore has block form [A_S,*;0,0], so
tr(A_S)=tr(A). Nonnegative excitation and diag(A)=0 give tr(A)=0. Hence the
unknown scalar shift is identified directly on the image:

  beta=-tr(L)/r,              A_S=L+beta I_r.

Both L and F-I_r are invertible by stability. Put

  H=L(F-I_r)^{-1}=J_S^{-1},
  K=O H^2 R,
  Z=K-K^T,
  D=diag(m/Delta),
  P=O O^T,
  W=O^T D^{-1} O.

It remains to recover the action of A off S; no orientation assumption is
needed. Write C=AD. Since im(C)=S, PC=C, and since Z=C-C^T,

  C(I-P)=Z(I-P).

The known restriction A O=O A_S is equivalent to C D^{-1}O=O A_S. Decompose
D^{-1}O=O W+(I-P)D^{-1}O and use the preceding identity to obtain

  C O=(O A_S-Z(I-P)D^{-1}O)W^{-1}.

As D and W are positive definite, every inverse exists, and therefore

  C=(O A_S-Z(I-P)D^{-1}O)W^{-1}O^T+Z(I-P),
  A=C D^{-1},
  mu=(I-A/beta)m/Delta.

These are functions only of (m,Gamma_1,Gamma_2). Under O -> OU, all coordinate
objects transform by orthogonal similarity and the displayed ambient C is
unchanged, proving basis invariance. Applying the same reconstruction to any
structural representative proves representative independence. The theorem
strictly adds singular cyclic and singular reciprocal networks; when the
model is a DAG, its restricted spectrum is {-beta} and the strip is automatic
for every Delta, while the existing nilpotent-polynomial theorem remains the
clean all-Delta statement. When A is full rank, the current Lyapunov/trace-
square theorem remains the more general branch because it also permits
self-excitation.

Laundering and open-question conclusion

The proposed extension uses only identities already proved in this paper plus
the same explicitly cited principal-log theorem. It does not borrow a richer
observation operator, assume the target through an observable-domain
condition, or relabel a cited theorem as novel. It is therefore a genuine
same-observation class extension rather than literature or observation
laundering.

Do not claim that the extension settles either sealed OEQ. The chain and
transitive-triangle one-lag fibers still concern (m,Gamma_1), not the two-lag
inverse. The positive-delta_n boundary frontier still requires local
experiments and an honest attaining procedure. No root-n frontier should be
asserted without that work. Apart from synchronizing the two stale
underclaiming prose sentences and optionally consolidating the overlapping
empty-graph propositions, no cosmetic refinement counts as material.
