1. Dimensions and orientation are correct.

   Pwh = (Hwh)^2 R is r×p.

   Cwh_+(t) = O exp(Lwh t) Pwh is p×p.

   Its transpose is
       Pwh^T exp(Lwh^T t) O^T,
   so
       RcalWh(x)
       = Pwh^T (x I_r - Lwh^T)^(-1) O^T
   is p×p. The resolvent exists for every x>0 because Lwh is Hurwitz.

   The candidate equations
       M O = O(Lwh+xI_r),
       M[Dwh+RcalWh(x)] = Kwh
   have dimensions p×r and p×p respectively.

2. True-pair existence is correct.

   From C_+(t)=exp(Bt)K,
       RcalWh(beta)
       = K^T(2 beta I_p-A^T)^(-1).

   Transposing K=A(2 beta I_p-A)Q gives
       K^T=Q(2 beta I_p-A^T)A^T.
   Since A^T commutes with 2 beta I_p-A^T,
       RcalWh(beta)=QA^T.

   The Lyapunov identity
       BQ+QB^T=-D
   with B=A-beta I gives
       D+QA^T=(2 beta I_p-A)Q.
   Hence
       D+RcalWh(beta)=(2 beta I_p-A)Q,
       A[D+RcalWh(beta)]=K.

   Q is positive definite and 2 beta I_p-A is invertible under rho(A/beta)<1, so beta belongs to OmegaWh. Invariance of im(A) gives
       AO=O(Lwh+beta I_r).
   Thus the true pair satisfies both candidate equations.
