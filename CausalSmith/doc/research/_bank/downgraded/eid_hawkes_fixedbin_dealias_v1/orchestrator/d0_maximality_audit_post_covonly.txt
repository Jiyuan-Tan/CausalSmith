Define the proper active-block class as follows.  There is a nonempty proper observed-label subset I⊂{1,…,p}, with ordered coordinate selector E_I∈R^{p×r}, 1≤r<p, such that

   A = E_I A_I E_I^T,        det(A_I)≠0,

all entries of A_I are nonnegative, ρ(A_I)<β, μ>0, and spec(Δ(A_I-βI_r)) lies in the principal strip.  Equivalently, labels outside I have neither incoming nor outgoing excitation, although their independent Poisson innovations remain observed.  Define the mean-plus-two-lag image and covariance-only image as the actual population images of this class.  This image definition is the same honest totality device used by the existing three regimes; it is not an observable-membership claim.

On either image,

   S := im(Γ1) = im(A) = span{e_i:i∈I}.

Thus I is itself observation-determined: it is the unique coordinate support of S.  Let E=E_I and form

   F := E^T Γ2 Γ1^† E,
   L := Δ^{-1}Log(F),
   J_I := ∫_0^Δ exp(Lu)du,
   K_I := J_I^{-1}(E^TΓ1E)J_I^{-T}.

Because B is block diagonal after ordering I first,

   B = diag(L,-βI_{p-r}),       L=A_I-βI_r,

and the strip assumption gives F=exp(LΔ) and hence the displayed principal logarithm exactly.  Moreover J_I is invertible and K_I is the active block of K.

For the mean-plus-two-lag observation put λ=m/Δ, D_I=diag(λ_I), and let Q_I be the unique Lyapunov solution

   LQ_I + Q_IL^T = -D_I.

Stability makes Q_I positive definite.  Block diagonality of B and D implies that the full Q is block diagonal, so the active block obeys the same Hawkes identity as in the full-rank proof:

   K_I = A_I(2βI_r-A_I)Q_I

   β = sqrt( (1/r) tr(K_I Q_I^{-1}+L²) ).

The radicand is β²>0 on the population image.  Recovery is then

   μ = (I_p-A/β)λ.
