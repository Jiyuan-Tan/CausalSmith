MATHEMATICAL AUDIT OF THE SUBMITTED BUNDLE

The following mathematical checks pass and may be retained in a corrected resubmission.

1. The proposed I_restricted is a genuine population-moment image. It is the image of stable common-exponential Hawkes laws satisfying A != 0, diag(A)=0, and the principal-log strip only for Delta(A-beta I) restricted to im(A). The condition is structural, not an arbitrary observable gate encoding the answer. A != 0 ensures rank(A)=rank(Gamma_1)=r>=1. Cycles and reciprocal edges are not excluded.

2. Phi_restricted is total on that image. For any representative, stationarity gives lambda=m/Delta>0 and hence D invertible. The semigroup identities give S=im(Gamma_1)=im(K)=im(A). If O is an orthonormal basis of S and Gamma_1=OR, then R has full row rank, so RR^T is positive definite. The restricted strip defines the principal logarithm. Stability makes the recovered restricted generator L Hurwitz and invertible; F-I is invertible because every eigenvalue of F has modulus below one. W=O^T D^{-1}O is positive definite. The trace identity below gives beta=-tr(L)/r>0. Thus every inverse and logarithm appearing in the formula is defined.

3. F is exactly the coordinate matrix of T restricted to S. Writing TO=OF_0 and Gamma_1=OR gives Gamma_2=T Gamma_1=OF_0R, and therefore
   O^T Gamma_2 R^T(RR^T)^{-1}=F_0.
   The restricted principal-log lemma then gives L=O^T(A-beta I)O.

4. The beta trace formula is valid. Complete O to an orthogonal basis. From im(A)=S, the lower block row of A is zero; consequently tr(A)=tr(O^TAO). Since diag(A)=0, tr(A)=0. Therefore
   tr(L)=tr(O^TAO)-r beta=-r beta,
   so beta=-tr(L)/r. This argument does not assume ambient invertibility of A.

5. The H/K/Z/C reconstruction is algebraically correct. On S,
   J_S=L^{-1}(F-I), hence H=L(F-I)^{-1}=J_S^{-1}.
   Since K has columns in S and R=J_S^2K_S, K^sharp=OH^2R=K. Thus Z=K-K^T=AD-DA^T. Let C_0=AD and P=OO^T. Since im(C_0)=S, PC_0=C_0 and C_0(I-P)=Z(I-P). Also C_0D^{-1}O=AO=OA_S. Decomposing D^{-1}O=OW+(I-P)D^{-1}O yields
   C_0OW=OA_S-Z(I-P)D^{-1}O,
   and hence exactly
   C_0={OA_S-Z(I-P)D^{-1}O}W^{-1}O^T+Z(I-P).
   The dimensions are coherent: the braced factor is p by r, W^{-1} is r by r, and the result is p by p.

6. The formulas are basis invariant. Under O'=OU for orthogonal U, R, F, L, A_S, H, and W transform by the expected left/right orthogonal actions, while P, K, and Z are invariant. The braced C factor transforms on the right by U and its product with W^{-1}O^T is unchanged. Therefore C, A, beta, and mu do not depend on O.
