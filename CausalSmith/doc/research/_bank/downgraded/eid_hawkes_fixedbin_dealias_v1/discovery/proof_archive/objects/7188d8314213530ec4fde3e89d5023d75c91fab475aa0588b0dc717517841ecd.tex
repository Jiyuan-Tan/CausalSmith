Every fixed law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{DAG},+}\) has finite bin-count moments of every order. Fix \(p\), \(\Delta\), and \(\kappa\in(0,1/2)\). Uniformly over \(P_{\theta}\in\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)\), the total progeny of any immigrant cluster is dominated by a depth-\(p-1\) Poisson branching tree whose moment-generating functions obey \(g_0(z)=e^z\) and \(g_d(z)=\exp[z+\overline\nu\{g_{d-1}(z)-1\}]\). For every integer \(u\in\{1,\ldots,16\}\), differentiating the stationary Poisson-cluster generating functional and using the duration bound in \(\text{lem:finite-depth-geometric-mixing}\) gives \[\operatorname{cum}_u(\lVert X_0\rVert_1)\leq\overline\mu\left[\Delta g_{p-1}^{(u)}(0)+2c_{\mathrm{mix}}^{-1}\{C_{\mathrm{mix}}g_{p-1}^{(2u)}(0)\}^{1/2}\right].\] The complete moment--cumulant formulas through order sixteen therefore yield the named finite constant \(M_8=M_8(p,\Delta,\kappa)\), the bound \(\sup_{\theta:P_{\theta}\in\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)}\mathbb E_{\theta}\lVert X_0\rVert_2^8\leq M_8\), and \(\sup_{\theta:P_{\theta}\in\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)}\mathbb E_{\theta}\lVert X_0\rVert_2^{16}<\infty\).
