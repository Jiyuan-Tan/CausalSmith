Fix \(\gamma\in\mathfrak J_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}\), choose an arbitrary admissible structural representative \((\beta,A,\mu,I,E_I,A_I)\), and let \(m\) be its population bin mean.  By the two image definitions,
\[
 (m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\in\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}.
\tag{1}
\]
Applying \(\text{thm:isolated-invertible-block-two-lag-recovery}\) to (1) proves that \(S=\operatorname{im}(\Gamma_1)\) uniquely determines \(I\) and its ordered selector, and that every inverse and logarithm in the positive-lag part of \(\text{def:isolated-invertible-block-covariance-only-inverse}\) exists.  It also gives
\[
 L^0=L_I=A_I-\beta I_r,\qquad J^0=J_I,\qquad K^0=K_I.
\tag{2}
\]
The block calculations (1)--(3) in the proof of that theorem, together with \(\text{lem:stable-covariance-semigroup}\), show that \(K=E_IK_IE_I^{\mathsf T}\) and that the active subspace is invariant under \(B\).  Hence, for \(t>0\),
\[
 \widetilde C_{+}^{\mathrm{ib}}(t)
 =E_Ie^{L^0t}K^0E_I^{\mathsf T}
 =e^{Bt}K=C_{+}(t).
\tag{3}
\]
This curve extends continuously to \(t=0\), so the integral in the covariance-only definition is finite.  Substitution of (3) into \(\text{lem:zero-lag-bin-covariance}\) yields
\[
 D^0=\Delta^{-1}\!\left[\Gamma_0-
 \int_0^\Delta(\Delta-t)\{C_{+}(t)+C_{+}(t)^{\mathsf T}\}\,dt\right]
 =D,
\tag{4}
\]
and therefore \(\lambda^0=\operatorname{diag}(D)=\lambda\) and \(m^0=\Delta\lambda=m\).  In particular, the triple passed to \(\Phi_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}\) is exactly the triple in (1), so
\[
 \Psi_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib},0}(\gamma)
 =\Phi_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)
 =(\beta,A,\mu)
\tag{5}
\]
by \(\text{thm:isolated-invertible-block-two-lag-recovery}\).  Equations (2)--(5), and the basis-invariance part of that theorem, prove totality, coordinate invariance, and representative independence.  Finally, each witness law proved there has a zero-lag covariance as well as two positive-lag covariances and satisfies the identical structural restrictions in \(\text{def:isolated-invertible-block-stable-covariance-image}\); its covariance triple therefore belongs to \(\mathfrak J_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}\).
