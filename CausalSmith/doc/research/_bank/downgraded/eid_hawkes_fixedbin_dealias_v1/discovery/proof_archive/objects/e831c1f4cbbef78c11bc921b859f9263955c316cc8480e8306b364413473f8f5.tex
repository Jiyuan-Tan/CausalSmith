Let \(\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}\) be the population moment image
\[
 \left\{(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2):\begin{array}{l}
 \text{there exist }(\beta,A,\mu)\text{ inducing a law in }\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\text{ and a set }I\subsetneq\{1,\ldots,p\},\\
 1\leq r=|I|\leq p-1,\quad E_I=(e_{i_1},\ldots,e_{i_r})\text{ for }i_1<\cdots<i_r\text{ the elements of }I,\\
 A=E_IA_IE_I^{\mathsf T},\quad A_I\in\mathbb R^{r\times r},\quad A_I\geq0,\quad \det(A_I)\neq0,\quad \rho(A_I)<\beta,\\

\]

This is an image-defined domain: no claim is made that membership in it can be certified from an arbitrary observed triple beyond the recovery statements proved on the image.
