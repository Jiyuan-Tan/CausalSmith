For every input on which the rank-\(r\) inverse is evaluated, let \(Z^*=K^*-(K^*)^{\mathsf T}\).  The positive-part edge formulas imply the algebraic identity
\[
 \lambda_j^*A_{ij}^*-\lambda_i^*A_{ji}^*
 =\max\{Z_{ij}^*,0\}-\max\{-Z_{ij}^*,0\}=Z_{ij}^*,
\]
for every \(i<j\).  Hence \(\zeta\circ\Phi_{\Delta,r}\) equals the signed jump recovered before the positive parts are taken.  On a fixed-rank neighborhood this latter map is a composition of the smooth rank projector and pseudoinverse, restricted matrix multiplication and inversion, the scalar logarithm on \(q\in(0,1)\), and a finite matrix polynomial.  It is therefore Fr\'{e}chet differentiable even when an edge is absent, although \(\Phi_{\Delta,r}\) itself need not be.

The uniform conditioning bounds established in the proposed proof of \(\text{thm:rank-adaptive-estimation}\) bound this derivative and its local modulus of continuity.  Taylor's formula, the uniform raw-moment Gaussian approximation, and \(P(\widehat r_n=r)\to1\) uniformly therefore give
\[
 \sup_\theta d_{\mathrm{BL}}\left(
 \mathcal L_\theta\left[\sqrt n\{\zeta(\widehat\theta_n)-\zeta(\theta)\}\right],
 \mathcal N\{0,W(\theta)\}\right)\to0,
\]
with \(W\) equal to the direct composite-Jacobian covariance in the proposed statement.  The corrected estimator \(\widehat W_n\) uses this same smooth representative.  Uniform HAC consistency, derivative continuity, and \(\widehat\xi_n\to\xi\) imply \(\|\widehat W_n-W(\theta)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\to0\) uniformly in probability.

The Gaussian maximum used for calibration is nondegenerate uniformly in at least one direction.  Indeed the class is nonempty and the edge gap gives some coordinate \(|\zeta_{ij}(\theta)|\geq\kappa\).  If both \(\Gamma_1\) and \(\Gamma_2\) are multiplied by \(1+\varepsilon\), the restricted transition, \(q\), and \(H\) are unchanged, while \(K^*\) and \(Z^*\) are multiplied by \(1+\varepsilon\).  Thus the derivative in this uniformly bounded input direction equals \(\zeta(\theta)\).  The variance gap \(\Omega\succeq\kappa I\) and the uniform moment envelope consequently give a common positive lower bound on at least one diagonal entry of \(W(\theta)\), while all entries are uniformly bounded.  In fixed dimension, the distribution functions of \(\max_{i<j}|G_{ij}|\), \(G\sim\mathcal N(0,W(\theta))\), are therefore uniformly continuous at their \((1-\alpha)\)-quantiles; this follows directly by conditioning on a nondegenerate coordinate and covering the bounded covariance set by finitely many operator-norm balls.  Consistency of \(\widehat W_n\) then implies consistency of the plug-in quantile.

The uniform Gaussian approximation and this quantile consistency show that the corrected rectangular intervals satisfy
\[
 \liminf_n\inf_\theta P_\theta\{\zeta_{ij}(\theta)\in I_{ij,n}\text{ for all }i<j\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
On this event, for each pair a positive true score makes the true arrow an admissible sign, a negative score makes the reverse arrow admissible, and a zero score makes absence admissible.  Since the true support is acyclic, it belongs to the sign-inversion set \(\mathcal C_n(1-\alpha)\).  The graph-coverage inequality follows deterministically.
