For a law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\), define its complete nonsingular conditional-response matrix by
\[
 g(t)=
 \begin{cases}
 C_{+}(t)D^{-1},&t>0,\\
 C_{+}(-t)^{\mathsf T}D^{-1},&t<0,\\
 0,&t=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
The value at zero is immaterial for convolution and integration.  The normalization is on the right because \(C_{+}(t)_{ij}=\operatorname{Cov}(dN_i(t),dN_j(0))/dt^2=g^{ij}(t)\lambda_j\).
