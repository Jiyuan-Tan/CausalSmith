Write \(C_d\) for the total progeny of an actual cluster with at most \(d\) generations remaining, counting its root.  By \(\text{lem:finite-depth-geometric-mixing}\), every child follows a directed edge of \(G(A)\).  Hence \(\text{ass:acyclic-support}\) implies that a lineage has at most \(p-1\) edges.  The integrated number of children of an event of type \(j\) is Poisson with mean \(\sum_i A_{ij}/\beta\).  On \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)\), \(\text{ass:decay-margin}\) gives \(\beta\geq\underline\beta\), while \(\text{ass:parameter-envelope}\) gives \(\sum_iA_{ij}\leq\sqrt p\lVert A\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\overline a\).  Thus this offspring mean is at most \(\overline\nu=\overline a/\underline\beta\).  Independence of the Poisson offspring processes and monotone coupling with enlarged Poisson processes construct dominating progeny variables \(T_d\) such that \(C_d\preceq T_d\) and
\[
 T_0=1,
 \qquad
 T_d\mathrel{\stackrel{d}{=}} 1+\sum_{a=1}^{P_{\overline\nu}}T_{d-1,a},
 \qquad 1\leq d\leq p-1,
\tag{1}
\]
where \(P_{\overline\nu}\) is Poisson with mean \(\overline\nu\), the variables \(T_{d-1,a}\) are independent copies of the dominating depth-\(d-1\) tree, and they are independent of \(P_{\overline\nu}\).  Conditional expectation in (1) proves inductively, for every \(z\geq0\),
\[
 \mathbb E e^{zT_d}
 = \exp\!\left[z+\overline\nu\{g_{d-1}(z)-1\}\right]
 =g_d(z),
 \qquad g_0(z)=e^z.
\tag{2}
\]
Each \(g_d\) is an iterated exponential and is finite on a neighborhood of zero.  Differentiating (2) at zero yields
\[
 \mathbb E T_d^u\leq g_d^{(u)}(0)<\infty
 \quad\text{for every fixed integer }u\geq1.
\tag{3}
\]

Let \(Z_s\) be the total number of points in \([0,\Delta]\) belonging to a cluster whose immigrant occurs at time \(s\).  The immigrant processes of the \(p\) types are independent Poisson processes.  Partitioning the immigrant-time axis into bounded intervals, using additivity of cumulants for independent compound-Poisson sums, and then applying monotone convergence gives, for the total bin count \(H=\lVert X_0\rVert_1\),
\[
 \operatorname{cum}_u(H)
 =\sum_{j=1}^p\mu_j\int_{-\infty}^{\Delta}
       \mathbb E_j(Z_s^u)\,ds.
\tag{4}
\]
This is a derivation of the Poisson-cluster cumulant identity: on a bounded immigrant interval the logarithm of the compound-Poisson moment-generating function is the immigrant intensity times \(\mathbb E(e^{zZ_s}-1)\), whose \(u\)-th derivative at zero is \(\mathbb E Z_s^u\); the nonnegative integrands permit the limit to the whole half-line.  In particular, every cumulant in (4) is nonnegative.

Immigrants with \(s>\Delta\) contribute nothing.  If \(0\leq s\leq\Delta\), then \(Z_s\leq T_{p-1}\).  If \(s<0\), the event \(Z_s>0\) implies \(D_{\mathrm{cl}}>-s\).  Therefore Cauchy--Schwarz, (3), and the duration bound in \(\text{lem:finite-depth-geometric-mixing}\) give, for every integer \(u\geq1\),
\[
 \mathbb E_j Z_s^u
 \leq
 \left\{\mathbb E T_{p-1}^{2u}
 P_{\theta}(D_{\mathrm{cl}}>-s)\right\}^{1/2}
 \leq
 \{C_{\mathrm{mix}}g_{p-1}^{(2u)}(0)\}^{1/2}
 e^{c_{\mathrm{mix}}s/2}.
\tag{5}
\]
Moreover \(\sum_j\mu_j\leq\sqrt p\lVert\mu\rVert_2\leq\overline\mu\) by \(\text{ass:parameter-envelope}\).  Substitution of (3)--(5) into (4), followed by integration over \(( -\infty,0)\) and \([0,\Delta]\), proves for every \(1\leq u\leq16\),
\[
 \operatorname{cum}_u(H)
 \leq\overline\mu\left[
 \Delta g_{p-1}^{(u)}(0)
 +2c_{\mathrm{mix}}^{-1}
 \{C_{\mathrm{mix}}g_{p-1}^{(2u)}(0)\}^{1/2}
 \right].
\tag{6}
\]
At \(u=16\), the tail step in (5) uses exactly \(g_{p-1}^{(32)}(0)\).

For a random variable with finite moments, its \(v\)-th moment is the complete Bell polynomial \(B_v\) in its first \(v\) cumulants.  Here this identity follows directly by differentiating \(\exp\{\sum_{u\geq1}\operatorname{cum}_u(H)z^u/u!\}\) through order \(v\).  Every coefficient of \(B_v\) is a nonnegative integer, and the cumulants in (4) are nonnegative.  Applying (6) in \(B_8\) gives the declared finite constant \(M_8(p,\Delta,\kappa)\).  Applying it in the complete Bell polynomial \(B_{16}\) gives a finite bound depending only on \(p,\Delta,\kappa\) for \(\mathbb E H^{16}\).  Since \(\lVert X_0\rVert_2\leq H\),
\[
 \sup_{\theta:P_\theta\in\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)}
 \mathbb E_\theta\lVert X_0\rVert_2^8\leq M_8,
 \qquad
 \sup_{\theta:P_\theta\in\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)}
 \mathbb E_\theta\lVert X_0\rVert_2^{16}<\infty.
\tag{7}
\]
For a fixed law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{DAG},+}\), the same argument uses its finite offspring means, positive decay, finite immigrant rate, and the law-dependent geometric duration bound supplied by \(\text{lem:finite-depth-geometric-mixing}\).  Since (3) holds at every fixed order, (4)--(6) then give every fixed bin-count moment.
