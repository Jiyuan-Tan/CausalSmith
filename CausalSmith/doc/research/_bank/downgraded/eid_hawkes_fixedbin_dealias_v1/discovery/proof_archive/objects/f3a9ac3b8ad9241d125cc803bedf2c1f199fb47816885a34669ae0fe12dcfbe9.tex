For \(\eta=(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\in\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}\), set
\[
 \lambda^\dagger=m/\Delta,\quad D^\dagger=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda^\dagger),\quad
 T^\dagger=\Gamma_2\Gamma_1^{-1},\quad
 B^\dagger=\Delta^{-1}\operatorname{Log}(T^\dagger),
\]
\[
 J^\dagger=(B^\dagger)^{-1}(T^\dagger-I_p),\quad
 K^\dagger=(J^\dagger)^{-2}\Gamma_1,\quad
 Q^\dagger=\int_0^\infty e^{B^\dagger u}D^\dagger e^{(B^\dagger)^{\mathsf T}u}\,du,
\]
and
\[
 s^\dagger=p^{-1}\operatorname{tr}\!\left\{K^\dagger(Q^\dagger)^{-1}+(B^\dagger)^2\right\},\quad
 \beta^\dagger=(s^\dagger)^{1/2},\quad
 A^\dagger=B^\dagger+\beta^\dagger I_p,\quad
 \mu^\dagger=(I_p-A^\dagger/\beta^\dagger)\lambda^\dagger,
\]
where the square root is the positive real square root. Define the domain-restricted map
\[
 \Phi^{\mathrm{fr}}_{\Delta,p}:\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}longrightarrow
 \mathbb R_+\times\mathbb R^{p\times p}\times\mathbb R^p,
 \qquad
 \Phi^{\mathrm{fr}}_{\Delta,p}(\eta)=(\beta^\dagger,A^\dagger,\mu^\dagger).
\]
