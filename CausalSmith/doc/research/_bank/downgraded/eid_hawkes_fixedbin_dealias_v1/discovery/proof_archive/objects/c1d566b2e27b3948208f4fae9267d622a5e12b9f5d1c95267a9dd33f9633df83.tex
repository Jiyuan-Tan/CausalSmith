For every \(\gamma=(\Gamma_0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\in\mathfrak J_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}\), every operation in \(\text{def:full-rank-covariance-only-inverse}\) is well defined and independent of the admissible structural representative.  For every such representative,
\[
 T^0=T,\quad B^0=B,\quad J^0=J,\quad K^0=K,
 \quad \widetilde C_{+}^{\mathrm{fr}}(t)=C_{+}(t)\ (0<t\leq\Delta),
 \quad D^0=D,\quad m^0=m,
\]
and
\[
 \Psi_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr},0}(\Gamma_0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)
 = (\beta,A,\mu).
\]
Thus three covariance matrices identify the stable full-rank principal-strip branch without a supplied mean.  The domain is strictly non-DAG: for every \(p\geq2\) and \(\Delta>0\), it contains the stationary law with \(\beta=1\), \(\mu=\mathbf 1_p\), and \(A=\tfrac12 I_p\), whose graph has a self-loop at every label.
