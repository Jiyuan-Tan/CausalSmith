Every law in \(\overline{\mathcal M}_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{DAG}}\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{exp,st}}\) by \(\text{lem:stable-covariance-semigroup}\): acyclicity under the loop-inclusive convention makes a simultaneous permutation of \(A\) strictly triangular, hence \(A^p=0\) and \(\operatorname{spr}(A/\beta)=0<1\).  Applying that lemma gives \(Q\succ0\), \(C_+(t)=e^{Bt}K\), both formulas for \(K\), \(\Gamma_k=T^{k-1}J^2K\), and
\[
 \operatorname{im}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{im}(K)=\operatorname{im}(A),
 \qquad \operatorname{rank}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{rank}(A).
\]
The stable lemma proves the image equality without dividing by \(A\), selecting eigenvectors, or imposing distinct poles, connectivity, or structural rank.  Its conclusions therefore apply unchanged to singular matrices, disconnected graphs, repeated poles, diamonds, and proportional rows, which proves the target.
