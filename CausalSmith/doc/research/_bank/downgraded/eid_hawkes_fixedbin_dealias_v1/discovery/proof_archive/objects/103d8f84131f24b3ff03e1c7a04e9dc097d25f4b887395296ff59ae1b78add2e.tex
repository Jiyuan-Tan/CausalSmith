Let \(N\) be a stationary \(p\)-dimensional linear Hawkes process with positive causal kernel matrix \(\phi\), suppose every entry of \(\phi\) is in \(L^1(\mathbb R_+)\), and suppose the spectral radius of the matrix \((\int_0^\infty\phi^{ij}(t)\,dt)_{i,j}\) is strictly less than one.  Define its nonsingular conditional-response density by
\[
 g^{ij}(t)\,dt
 =\mathbb E\{dN_i(t)\mid dN_j(0)=1\}
  -\mathbf 1_{\{i=j\}}\delta_0(dt)-\lambda_i\,dt.
\]
Then \(\phi\) is the unique matrix function \(\chi\) whose entries are causal and in \(L^1(\mathbb R_+)\) and which solves
\[
 g(t)=\chi(t)+(\chi*g)(t),\qquad t>0,
\]
where \((\chi*g)(t)=\int_0^\infty\chi(s)g(t-s)\,ds\) with matrix multiplication.
