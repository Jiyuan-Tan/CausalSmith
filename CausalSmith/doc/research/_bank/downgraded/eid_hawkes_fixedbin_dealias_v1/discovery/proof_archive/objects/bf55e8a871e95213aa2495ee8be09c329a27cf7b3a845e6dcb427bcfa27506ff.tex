Fix \(\gamma\in\mathfrak J_{\Delta,p}^{0,+,\mathrm{rs}}\).  By \(\text{def:restricted-rank-stable-covariance-image}\), choose an arbitrary admissible structural representative \((\beta,A,\mu)\), and denote its population bin mean by \(m\).  Then \(\eta=(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\) belongs to \(\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{0,+,\mathrm{rs}}\).  The conclusions of \(\text{thm:restricted-rank-stable-two-lag-recovery}\), applied to this \(\eta\), show that \(r\geq1\), \(R\) has full row rank, the principal logarithm of \(F\) exists, and \(L^\sharp\) and \(F-I_r\) are invertible.  Hence all positive-lag operations in \(\text{def:restricted-rank-covariance-only-inverse}\) are defined.  That theorem also gives
\[
 L^\sharp=O^{\mathsf T}(B|_S)O,\qquad
 K=O(H^\sharp)^2R,\qquad S=\operatorname{im}(K),
\tag{1}
\]
where the first equality denotes the coordinate matrix of \(B|_S\).  Since the columns of \(K\) lie in the \(B\)-invariant space \(S\), (1) and \(\text{lem:stable-covariance-semigroup}\) give
\[
 \widetilde C_{+}^{\mathrm{rs}}(t)
 =Oe^{L^\sharp t}(H^\sharp)^2R
 =e^{Bt}K=C_{+}(t),
 \qquad 0<t\leq\Delta.
\tag{2}
\]

This also proves basis invariance explicitly.  If \(O'=OU\) is another orthonormal basis of \(S\), with \(U\) orthogonal, then coordinate representations of the same operators give
\[
 R'=U^{\mathsf T}R,\qquad
 F'=U^{\mathsf T}FU,\qquad
 L'=U^{\mathsf T}L^\sharp U,\qquad
 H'=U^{\mathsf T}H^\sharp U.
\tag{3}
\]
Substitution in the reconstructed curve yields
\[
 O'e^{L't}(H')^2R'
 =OU\,U^{\mathsf T}e^{L^\sharp t}U
   U^{\mathsf T}(H^\sharp)^2U\,U^{\mathsf T}R
 =Oe^{L^\sharp t}(H^\sharp)^2R.
\tag{4}
\]
Thus \(D^0\), \(m^0\), and the input passed to the already basis-invariant map \(\Phi_{\Delta,p}^{0,+,\mathrm{rs}}\) do not depend on \(O\).

The integrand in (2) extends continuously to zero.  Applying \(\text{lem:zero-lag-bin-covariance}\) and substituting (2) gives
\[
 D^0=D=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)\succ0,\qquad
 \lambda^0=\lambda,\qquad m^0=\Delta\lambda=m.
\tag{5}
\]
Therefore \((m^0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)=\eta\) belongs to the mean-plus-two-lag image domain, so the final operation is total.  By \(\text{thm:restricted-rank-stable-two-lag-recovery}\),
\[
 \Psi_{\Delta,p}^{0,+,\mathrm{rs},0}(\gamma)
 =\Phi_{\Delta,p}^{0,+,\mathrm{rs}}(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)
 =(\beta,A,\mu).
\tag{6}
\]
The left side of (2), (5), and (6) is a function only of \(\gamma\), the known \(\Delta\), and an inessential basis; since the representative was arbitrary, these equations prove representative independence and uniqueness of the recovered structural triple.
