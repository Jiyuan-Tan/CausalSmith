Let \(T_d\) be total progeny when at most \(d\) generations remain.  The offspring-count domination in \(\text{lem:finite-depth-geometric-mixing}\) gives \(T_0=1\) and the stochastic recursion
\[
 T_d\preceq1+\sum_{a=1}^{P_{\overline\nu}}T_{d-1,a},
\]
where \(P_{\overline\nu}\) is Poisson with mean \(\overline\nu\) and the variables on the right are independent.  Taking moment-generating functions proves inductively
\[
 E(e^{zT_d})\leq g_d(z),\quad g_0(z)=e^z,\quad
 g_d(z)=\exp[z+\overline\nu\{g_{d-1}(z)-1\}].
\]
Every \(g_d\) is finite in a neighborhood of zero, so \(E(T_d^u)\leq g_d^{(u)}(0)\) for every fixed \(u\).

For an immigrant at time \(s\), let \(Z_s\) be its cluster's number of points in \([0,\Delta]\).  The Poisson-cluster generating functional implies, by differentiating at zero, that the \(u\)-th cumulant of \(\|X_0\|_1\) is the immigrant-rate integral of \(E(Z_s^u)\).  This identity is also obtained directly by partitioning immigrant time into bounded intervals, using additivity of cumulants for independent compound-Poisson sums, and applying monotone convergence.  Immigrants after \(\Delta\) contribute zero.  For \(0\leq s\leq\Delta\), \(Z_s\leq T_{p-1}\).  For \(s<0\), the event \(Z_s>0\) implies \(D_{\mathrm{cl}}>-s\), and Cauchy--Schwarz with the duration bound gives
\[
 E(Z_s^u)\leq\{E(T_{p-1}^{2u})P(D_{\mathrm{cl}}>-s)\}^{1/2}
 \leq\{g_{p-1}^{(2u)}(0)C_{\mathrm{mix}}\}^{1/2}e^{c_{\mathrm{mix}}s/2}.
\]
Since the total immigrant rate is at most \(\overline\mu\), integration over \(( -\infty,0)\) and \([0,\Delta]\) yields, for \(1\leq u\leq8\),
\[
 \operatorname{cum}_u(\|X_0\|_1)\leq\overline\mu\left[\Delta g_{p-1}^{(u)}(0)+2c_{\mathrm{mix}}^{-1}
 \{C_{\mathrm{mix}}g_{p-1}^{(2u)}(0)\}^{1/2}\right].
\]
The eighth moment is the complete Bell polynomial in the first eight cumulants.  Substituting these eight explicit upper bounds defines a finite \(M_8(p,\Delta,\kappa)\).  Finally \(\|X_0\|_2\leq\|X_0\|_1\), proving the asserted uniform eighth-moment bound.  With parameters fixed, the same finite-depth recursion has finite constants of every order, proving all fixed-law moments.
