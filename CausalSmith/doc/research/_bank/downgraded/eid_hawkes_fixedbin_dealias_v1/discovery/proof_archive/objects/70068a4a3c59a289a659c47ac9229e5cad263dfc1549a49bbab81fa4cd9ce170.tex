The rank strata and the complete fork fiber are settled by \(\text{prop:fork-one-lag-fiber}\): only \(s=1\) occurs for a fork, its one-lag fiber is a singleton exactly at the unique \(\beta_*\) satisfying \(e^{\beta_*\Delta}=1+2\beta_*\Delta\), and otherwise it has one observationally distinct alias on the opposite side of \(\beta_*\).  Only \(s=2\) can occur for a chain or transitive triangle.  Their exact injectivity loci and the existence or absence of relatively open alias regions remain unresolved.
