For every law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\), and hence for every law in \(\overline{\mathcal M}_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{DAG}}\),
\[
 \Gamma_0=\Delta D+\int_0^\Delta(\Delta-t)
 \{C_{+}(t)+C_{+}(t)^{\mathsf T}\}\,dt,
 \qquad m=\Delta\lambda.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 D=\Delta^{-1}\!\left[\Gamma_0-
 \int_0^\Delta(\Delta-t)
 \{C_{+}(t)+C_{+}(t)^{\mathsf T}\}\,dt\right],
 \qquad \operatorname{diag}(D)=\lambda\in(0,\infty)^p.
\]
