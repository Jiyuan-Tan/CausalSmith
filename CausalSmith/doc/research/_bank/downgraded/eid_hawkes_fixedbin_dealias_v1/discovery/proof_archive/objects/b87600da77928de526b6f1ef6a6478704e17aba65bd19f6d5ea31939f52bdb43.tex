The equivalence \(\Gamma_1=0\Longleftrightarrow A=0\) follows immediately from \(\operatorname{rank}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{rank}(A)\) in \(\text{lem:covariance-semigroup}\).  If \(A=0\), then \(\text{ass:hawkes-intensity}\) reduces to \(\ell_i(t)=\mu_i\).  For disjoint labeled intervals, the conditional survivor formula obtained by integrating this deterministic hazard is exponential with rate equal to the sum of the corresponding \(\mu_i\)'s, and the conditional label probability at a jump is \(\mu_i/\sum_j\mu_j\).  Iterating these two conditional laws shows that the superposed process is homogeneous Poisson and its labels are independent identically distributed marks with those probabilities.  Poisson thinning therefore gives independent homogeneous Poisson coordinates of rates \(\mu_i\).  The resulting law contains no occurrence of \(\beta\).  Thus every \(\beta>0\), with the same \(\mu\) and \(A=0\), induces the same observable law, so the decay is not identified on precisely this stratum.
