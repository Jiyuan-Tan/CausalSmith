For every law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{exp,st}}\), \(B\) is Hurwitz, \(Q\succ0\), \(C_{+}(t)=e^{Bt}K\) for \(t>0\), \(K=A(2\beta I_p-A)Q\), \(K-K^{\mathsf T}=AD-DA^{\mathsf T}\), and \(\Gamma_k=T^{k-1}J^2K\) for every \(k\geq1\). Moreover, \(J\) is invertible, \(\operatorname{im}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{im}(K)=\operatorname{im}(A)\), and \(\operatorname{rank}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{rank}(A)\). Every law in \(\overline{\mathcal M}_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{DAG}}\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{exp,st}}\), so these conclusions include singular, disconnected, repeated-pole, diamond, and proportional-row acyclic cases.
