Let the law belong to \(\overline{\mathcal M}_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{DAG}}\).  By \(\text{ass:nonnegative-excitation}\), an entry of \(A^d\) is positive only if the support graph contains a directed walk of length \(d\).  If \(d\geq p\), such a walk repeats a vertex and therefore contains a directed cycle.  This contradicts \(\text{ass:acyclic-support}\), under which a self-loop is already a cycle of length one.  Hence
\[
 A^p=0,qquad \rho(A)=0<\beta,
\tag{1}
\]
where the strict inequality uses \(\text{ass:positive-decay}\).  All remaining membership conditions of \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\) are exactly those in \(\text{def:closure-class}\).  Thus the law belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\).

Apply \(\text{lem:stable-covariance-semigroup}\).  It gives, with the same definitions of \(B,T,J,Q,K\),
\[
 Q\succ0,\quad C_+(t)=e^{Bt}K,\quad K=A(2\beta I_p-A)Q,
\]
\[
 K-K^{\mathsf T}=AD-DA^{\mathsf T},\quad
 \Gamma_k=T^{k-1}J^2K,quad
 \operatorname{im}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{im}(K)=\operatorname{im}(A),
\]
and therefore \(\operatorname{rank}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{rank}(A)\).  Equation (1) also shows that every eigenvalue of \(B\) equals \(-\beta\); its algebraic multiplicity, Jordan structure, and the rank of \(A\) were never restricted.  Relabeling into topological order permits arbitrary disconnected blocks, while diamonds and proportional rows merely change the entries and rank of the nilpotent matrix.  The displayed conclusions therefore include singular, disconnected, repeated-pole, diamond, and proportional-row cases.
