The frozen Gaussian and rank-threshold results are asymptotic and uniform only on \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)\), where the smallest nonzero singular value and every nonzero signed edge are bounded below. They therefore do not imply the finite-sample coverage required by \(\mathfrak H_{n,\kappa_0}(\alpha)\) when rank changes, edges vanish, or \(A=0\). The added empty-boundary proposition gives an exact obstruction only at \(\delta=0\). No frozen premise supplies a local asymptotic experiment or a quantitative total-variation, Hellinger, or likelihood-ratio modulus for binned Hawkes laws indexed by \(\delta\); without such a modulus, neither matching testing lower bounds nor a rank-union confidence construction with sharp positive-\(\delta_n\) diameter and inclusion loss follows.
