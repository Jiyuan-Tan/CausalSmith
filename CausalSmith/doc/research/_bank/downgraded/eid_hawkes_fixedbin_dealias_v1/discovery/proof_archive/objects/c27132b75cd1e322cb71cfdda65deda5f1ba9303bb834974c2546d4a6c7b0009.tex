By \(\text{prop:empty-graph-equivalence}\), the two empty-graph parameters induce the same law \(P_0\), because they have the same baseline \(\mu\) and their intensities contain no occurrence of the decay.  Membership of \(\mathfrak U_n\) in \(\mathfrak H_{n,\kappa_0}(\alpha)\) implies, for \(a\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
 P_0\{G(0)\in\mathcal G_n,\ \theta_a\in\mathcal T_n\}\geq1-\alpha.
\tag{1}
\]
Dropping the graph event in (1) gives \(P_0\{\theta_a\in\mathcal T_n\}\geq1-\alpha\).  The union bound therefore yields
\[
 P_0\{\theta_0,\theta_1\in\mathcal T_n\}\geq1-2\alpha.
\tag{2}
\]
On the event in (2), the definitions of \(\operatorname{diam}_\Theta\) and \(\rho_\Theta\) give
\[
 \operatorname{diam}_\Theta(\mathcal T_n)
 \geq\rho_\Theta(\theta_0,\theta_1)
 =1\wedge|\beta_0-\beta_1|.
\]
This proves the first claim.  Under the additional separation, the lower bound equals one; since \(\rho_\Theta\leq1\), every set has diameter at most one, proving the last assertion.
