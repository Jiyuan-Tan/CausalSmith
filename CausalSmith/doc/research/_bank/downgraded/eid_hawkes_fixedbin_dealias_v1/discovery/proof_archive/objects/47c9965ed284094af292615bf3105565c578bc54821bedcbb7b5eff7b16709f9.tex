Let \(\mathfrak J_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{WH}}\) be the population covariance image
\[
 \left\{(\Gamma_0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2):\begin{array}{l}
 \text{there exists }(\beta,A,\mu)\text{ inducing a law in }\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}},\\
 A\neq0,\qquad
 \operatorname{spec}\!\left(\left.\Delta(A-\beta I_p)\right|_{\operatorname{im}(A)}\right)
 \subset\{z\in\mathbb C:|\operatorname{Im}z|<\pi\},\\
 \text{and }\Gamma_0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2\text{ are that law's population covariances}
 \end{array}\right\}.
\]
This is the covariance-only image of exactly the same nonempty stable restricted-strip regime as \(\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{WH}}\).
