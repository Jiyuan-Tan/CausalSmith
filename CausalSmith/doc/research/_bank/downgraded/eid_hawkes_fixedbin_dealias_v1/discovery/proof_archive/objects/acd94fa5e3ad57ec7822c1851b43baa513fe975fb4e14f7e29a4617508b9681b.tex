Let \(\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}\) be the population moment image
\[
 \left\{(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2):\begin{array}{l}
 \text{there exists }(\beta,A,\mu)\text{ inducing a law in }\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}},\\
 \det(A)\neq0,\quad
 \operatorname{spec}\{\Delta(A-\beta I_p)\}\subset\{z\in\mathbb C:|\operatorname{Im}z|<\pi\},\\
 \text{and }m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2\text{ are that law's population moments}
 \end{array}\right\}.
\]
This is an image-defined domain, not an additional observable assumption.
