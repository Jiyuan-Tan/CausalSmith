Fix \(p\geq2\), \(\Delta>0\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and \(\kappa_0\in(0,1/2)\). Suppose that there are \(\mu_0\in(0,\infty)^p\) and distinct \(\beta_0,\beta_1>0\) such that, for \(s\in\{0,1\}\), \(\theta_s=(\beta_s,0,\mu_0)\in\overline\Theta_{\Delta,p}\) and \(P_{\theta_s}\in\overline{\mathcal M}_{\Delta,p}(\kappa_0)\). Put \(d_{01}=1\wedge|\beta_1-\beta_0|\). Then \(P_{\theta_0}=P_{\theta_1}=:P_0\), and, for every \(n\geq3\) and every \(\mathfrak U_n=(\mathcal G_n,\mathcal T_n)\in\mathfrak H_{n,\kappa_0}(\alpha)\),
\[
 P_0\!\left\{\operatorname{diam}_{\Theta}(\mathcal T_n)\geq d_{01}\right\}
 \geq (1-2\alpha)_+ .
\]
Consequently, for every \(n\geq3\),
\[
 R_{\Theta,n,\kappa_0}(0)
 \geq (1-2\alpha)_+\{1\wedge|\beta_1-\beta_0|\}.
\]
