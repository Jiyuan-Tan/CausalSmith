The proposed uniform rank-adaptive result gives a uniform root-\(n\) tight bound for \(\|\widehat A_n-A\|_{\max}\).  It remains only to expose the signal margin in amplitude units.  Acyclicity gives the finite stationary-mean expansion
\[
 \lambda=\sum_{d=0}^{p-1}(A/\beta)^d\mu.
\]
On the margin class, \(\beta\geq\underline\beta\), \(\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\kappa^{-1}\), and \(\|\mu\|_2\leq\kappa^{-1}\).  Hence
\[
 \max_j\lambda_j\leq\Lambda_\kappa:=\kappa^{-1}\sum_{d=0}^{p-1}(\kappa^{-1}/\underline\beta)^d<\infty.
\]
For every true edge \(j\to i\), acyclicity makes \(Z_{ij}=A_{ij}\lambda_j\), so the edge gap yields \(A_{ij}\geq\kappa/\Lambda_\kappa\).  For every absent directed edge, \(A_{ij}=0\).  Therefore, for all sufficiently large \(n\), on the event
\[
 \|\widehat A_n-A\|_{\max}<\min\{a_n,\kappa/(2\Lambda_\kappa)\},
\]
there are neither false inclusions nor false exclusions.  Uniform root-\(n\) tightness and \(\sqrt n a_n\to\infty\) make the complement probability tend uniformly to zero, which proves the proposition.
