Fix \(\eta\in\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{0,+,\mathrm{rs}}\) and choose an arbitrary admissible representative \((\beta,A,\mu)\).  Put \(B=A-\beta I_p\), \(T=e^{B\Delta}\), and use the remaining notation of \(\text{lem:stable-covariance-semigroup}\).  That lemma gives
\[
 B\text{ is Hurwitz},\quad D\succ0,\quad J\text{ is invertible},
 \quad \Gamma_1=J^2K,\quad \Gamma_2=T\Gamma_1,
\tag{1}
\]
\[
 S=\operatorname{im}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{im}(K)=\operatorname{im}(A),
 \quad r=\operatorname{rank}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{rank}(A).
\tag{2}
\]
The image-domain condition \(A\neq0\) and (2) imply \(r\geq1\).  Since \(A\operatorname{im}(A)\subseteq\operatorname{im}(A)\), the space \(S\) is invariant under \(A\), hence under \(B\), \(T\), and \(J\).

Choose the basis \(O\) prescribed by the inverse and put \(R=O^{\mathsf T}\Gamma_1\).  Equation (2) implies \(\Gamma_1=OR\).  Because \(\Gamma_1\) has rank \(r\) and \(O\) has \(r\) orthonormal columns, \(R\) has row rank \(r\); therefore \(RR^{\mathsf T}\succ0\) and its inverse exists.  Invariance gives a unique \(F_0\in\mathbb R^{r\times r}\) such that \(TO=OF_0\).  From (1),
\[
 \Gamma_2=T\Gamma_1=TO R=OF_0R.
\]
Multiplication by \(O^{\mathsf T}\) on the left and by the right inverse \(R^{\mathsf T}(RR^{\mathsf T})^{-1}\) on the right proves
\[
 F=O^{\mathsf T}\Gamma_2R^{\mathsf T}(RR^{\mathsf T})^{-1}=F_0.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus \(F\) represents \(T|_S\).  Define
\[
 L_0=O^{\mathsf T}BO,\quad A_{S,0}=O^{\mathsf T}AO.
\]
Then \(BO=OL_0\), \(AO=OA_{S,0}\), and
\[
 L_0=A_{S,0}-\beta I_r,\quad F=e^{\Delta L_0}.
\tag{4}
\]
The restricted-strip condition in \(\text{def:restricted-rank-stable-moment-image}\), (3)--(4), and \(\text{lem:principal-log-strip}\), applied in dimension \(r\), imply that the principal logarithm exists and
\[
 L^\sharp=\Delta^{-1}\operatorname{Log}(F)=L_0.
\tag{5}
\]
The restriction of a Hurwitz matrix to an invariant subspace is Hurwitz, so \(L^\sharp=L_0\) is invertible.  By spectral mapping, every eigenvalue of \(F=e^{\Delta L_0}\) has modulus strictly less than one; hence \(F-I_r\) is invertible.

Complete \(O\) to an orthogonal matrix \(\mathcal U=(O,O_\perp)\).  Because \(\operatorname{im}(A)=S\),
\[
 \mathcal U^{\mathsf T}A\mathcal U
 =\begin{pmatrix}A_{S,0}&*\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{6}
\]
The image definition imposes \(\operatorname{diag}(A)=0\), so \(\operatorname{tr}(A)=0\).  Taking traces in (6), and then in (4), yields
\[
 \operatorname{tr}(A_{S,0})=\operatorname{tr}(A)=0,
 \quad \operatorname{tr}(L_0)=-r\beta.
\tag{7}
\]
Since \(r>0\), (5)--(7) prove
\[
 \beta^\sharp=-\operatorname{tr}(L^\sharp)/r=\beta>0,
 \quad A_S^\sharp=L^\sharp+\beta^\sharp I_r=A_{S,0}.
\tag{8}
\]

Let \(J_S=O^{\mathsf T}JO\), the coordinate matrix of \(J|_S\).  Integrating \(e^{Bu}O=Oe^{L_0u}\), and using the invertibility of \(L_0\), gives
\[
 J_S=\int_0^\Delta e^{L_0u}\,du=L_0^{-1}(F-I_r).
\tag{9}
\]
Equations (5) and (9) imply
\[
 H^\sharp=L^\sharp(F-I_r)^{-1}=J_S^{-1}.
\tag{10}
\]
The columns of \(K\) lie in \(S\) by (2), so write \(K=OK_S\), where \(K_S=O^{\mathsf T}K\).  From (1), invariance, and \(\Gamma_1=OR\),
\[
 R=O^{\mathsf T}\Gamma_1=O^{\mathsf T}J^2OK_S=J_S^2K_S.
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently, by (10)--(11),
\[
 K^\sharp=O(H^\sharp)^2R=OJ_S^{-2}J_S^2K_S=OK_S=K.
\tag{12}
\]
The antisymmetric-jump identity in \(\text{lem:stable-covariance-semigroup}\) now gives
\[
 Z^\sharp=K-K^{\mathsf T}=AD-DA^{\mathsf T}.
\tag{13}
\]

The mean identity follows directly from stationarity:
\[
 m=\mathbb E\{N(\Delta)-N(0)\}=\Delta\lambda.
\tag{14}
\]
Thus \(\lambda^\sharp=\lambda\) and \(D^\sharp=D\succ0\), so \(D^\sharp\) is invertible.  Also, for every nonzero \(x\in\mathbb R^r\),
\[
 x^{\mathsf T}W^\sharp x
 =(Ox)^{\mathsf T}D^{-1}(Ox)>0,
\tag{15}
\]
because \(D^{-1}\succ0\) and \(O\) has full column rank.  Hence \(W^\sharp\succ0\) and is invertible.

It remains to verify the nontrivial block reconstruction.  Put \(P=OO^{\mathsf T}\) and \(C_0=AD\).  Since \(D\) is invertible, \(\operatorname{im}(C_0)=\operatorname{im}(A)=S\); hence
\[
 PC_0=C_0,\quad (I_p-P)C_0=0.
\tag{16}
\]
By (13), \(Z^\sharp=C_0-C_0^{\mathsf T}\).  Multiplying this identity on the right by \(I_p-P\), and using \(C_0^{\mathsf T}(I_p-P)=\{(I_p-P)C_0\}^{\mathsf T}=0\), gives
\[
 C_0(I_p-P)=Z^\sharp(I_p-P).
\tag{17}
\]
Furthermore, (8) gives \(AO=OA_S^\sharp\), so
\[
 C_0D^{-1}O=AO=OA_S^\sharp.
\tag{18}
\]
Since
\[
 D^{-1}O=PD^{-1}O+(I_p-P)D^{-1}O
 =O\{O^{\mathsf T}D^{-1}O\}+(I_p-P)D^{-1}O
 =OW^\sharp+(I_p-P)D^{-1}O,
\]
substitution into (18), followed by (17), yields
\[
 C_0O W^\sharp
 =OA_S^\sharp-Z^\sharp(I_p-P)D^{-1}O.
\tag{19}
\]
Right multiplication by \((W^\sharp)^{-1}\) is valid by (15).  Combining (16)--(19),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 C_0
 &=C_0P+C_0(I_p-P)\\
 &=\{OA_S^\sharp-Z^\sharp(I_p-P)D^{-1}O\}
   (W^\sharp)^{-1}O^{\mathsf T}+Z^\sharp(I_p-P)
 =\mathsf C^\sharp.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{20}
\]
Therefore
\[
 A^\sharp=\mathsf C^\sharp D^{-1}=AD D^{-1}=A.
\tag{21}
\]
Taking expectations in the nonnegative Hawkes intensity and using monotone convergence, stationarity, and \(\beta>0\),
\[
 \lambda=\mu+\int_0^\infty Ae^{-\beta u}\lambda\,du
 =\mu+(A/\beta)\lambda.
\tag{22}
\]
Equations (8), (14), (21), and (22) imply
\[
 \mu^\sharp=(I_p-A^\sharp/\beta^\sharp)\lambda^\sharp=\mu.
\tag{23}
\]

We next prove basis invariance.  Let \(O'=OU\) for an orthogonal \(U\in\mathbb R^{r\times r}\).  Direct substitution gives
\[
 R'=U^{\mathsf T}R,\quad F'=U^{\mathsf T}FU.
\tag{24}
\]
The principal logarithm is preserved by similarity, by uniqueness of the principal logarithm, so
\[
 L^{\sharp\prime}=U^{\mathsf T}L^\sharp U,\quad
 \beta^{\sharp\prime}=\beta^\sharp,\quad
 A_S^{\sharp\prime}=U^{\mathsf T}A_S^\sharp U,\quad
 H^{\sharp\prime}=U^{\mathsf T}H^\sharp U.
\tag{25}
\]
Equations (24)--(25) show \(O'(H^{\sharp\prime})^2R'=O(H^\sharp)^2R\), so \(K^\sharp\) and \(Z^\sharp\) are invariant.  Also
\[
 P^{\sharp\prime}=P^\sharp,\quad
 W^{\sharp\prime}=U^{\mathsf T}W^\sharp U.
\tag{26}
\]
Writing
\[
 V_0=OA_S^\sharp-Z^\sharp(I_p-P^\sharp)D^{-1}O,
\]
the corresponding primed bracket is \(V_0U\).  Therefore
\[
 V_0U(U^{\mathsf T}W^\sharp U)^{-1}U^{\mathsf T}O^{\mathsf T}
 =V_0(W^\sharp)^{-1}O^{\mathsf T},
\]
while the second term \(Z^\sharp(I_p-P^\sharp)\) is unchanged.  Hence \(\mathsf C^\sharp\), \(A^\sharp\), and \(\mu^\sharp\) are basis invariant.  Since the original representative was arbitrary and the recovered output is a basis-invariant function only of \(\eta\) and \(\Delta\), equations (3)--(23) force every admissible representative to be the same recovered triple.  This proves representative independence and exact identification of \(G(A)\), because the labels are observed and \(G(A)=\{j\to i:A_{ij}>0\}\).

Finally, the displayed witnesses satisfy every condition strictly.  For the four-dimensional example, the nonzero block is a weighted three-cycle with eigenvalues \(\varepsilon_\Delta\), \(\varepsilon_\Delta e^{2\pi i/3}\), and \(\varepsilon_\Delta e^{4\pi i/3}\).  Thus \(\rho(A)=\varepsilon_\Delta\leq1/2<\beta=1\), its rank is three, and on its image
\[
 \max_{z\in\operatorname{spec}(\Delta B|_S)}|\operatorname{Im}z|
 =\Delta\sqrt3\,\varepsilon_\Delta/2\leq\pi/4<\pi.
\]
It has a directed cycle and one isolated coordinate.  For the three-dimensional reciprocal example, the nonzero block has eigenvalues \(1/2\) and \(-1/2\), so \(\rho(A)=1/2<1\), its rank is two, and the spectrum of \(\Delta B|_S\) is real for every \(\Delta>0\).  It contains both directions of the pair and one isolated coordinate.  Both matrices are nonnegative, nonzero, and zero-diagonal, and both baseline vectors are strictly positive.  To see existence rather than merely formal parameter admissibility, use the immigration--birth construction with independent rate-one immigrants and offspring kernel \(Ae^{-u}\): the expected total progeny matrix is \(\sum_{h\geq0}A^h=(I_p-A)^{-1}\), finite because \(\rho(A)<1\), so every cluster is finite almost surely; stationary superposition over immigrants is locally finite, is simple because offspring delays have continuous densities, and has the displayed Hawkes intensity.  Hence both witnesses induce laws in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\) and their moment triples belong to \(\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{0,+,\mathrm{rs}}\).
