The identical-law pair at \(A=0\) was converted into the sharp two-point coverage argument in \(\text{prop:empty-graph-honest-diameter-obstruction}\). Extrapolating a root-\(n\) elbow from \(\text{thm:rank-adaptive-estimation}\) or \(\text{thm:simultaneous-graph-coverage}\) fails because their uniform differentiability and rank-selection arguments use fixed rank and edge margins that disappear along the required sequences. Exact-event-time local-alternative results cannot be substituted for the coarsened binned-count experiment. A vacuous procedure exists, but it does not determine the frontier.
