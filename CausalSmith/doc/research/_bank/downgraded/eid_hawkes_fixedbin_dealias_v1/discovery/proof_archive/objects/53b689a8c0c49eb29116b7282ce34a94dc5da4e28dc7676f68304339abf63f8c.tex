For every \(\eta=(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\in\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{WH}}\), every operation in \(\text{def:wiener-hopf-two-lag-inverse}\) is well defined, independent of the orthonormal basis \(O\), and independent of the admissible structural representative.  For every such representative \((\beta,A,\mu)\),
\[
 r=\operatorname{rank}(A)\geq1,\quad S=\operatorname{im}(A),\quad
 F=[T|_S]_O,\quad L^{\mathrm{WH}}=[B|_S]_O,\quad
 H^{\mathrm{WH}}=[J|_S]_O^{-1},
\]
\[
 K^{\mathrm{WH}}=K,\quad C_{+}^{\mathrm{WH}}(t)=C_{+}(t)\quad(t>0),\quad
 D^{\mathrm{WH}}=D,\quad g^{\mathrm{WH}}=g.
\]
Writing \(P^{\mathrm{WH}}=(H^{\mathrm{WH}})^2R\), the response transform is finite dimensional:
\[
 \mathcal R^{\mathrm{WH}}(x)
 =(P^{\mathrm{WH}})^{\mathsf T}(xI_r-(L^{\mathrm{WH}})^{\mathsf T})^{-1}O^{\mathsf T},
 \qquad x>0.
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \mathcal R^{\mathrm{WH}}(\beta)
 =K^{\mathsf T}(2\beta I_p-A^{\mathsf T})^{-1}=QA^{\mathsf T},
\]
\[
 D+\mathcal R^{\mathrm{WH}}(\beta)=(2\beta I_p-A)Q,
 \qquad A[D+\mathcal R^{\mathrm{WH}}(\beta)]=K,
 \qquad AO=O(L^{\mathrm{WH}}+\beta I_r).
\]
The pair \((\beta,A)\) is the unique \((x,M)\in(0,\infty)\times\mathbb R^{p\times p}\) satisfying
\[
 MO=O(L^{\mathrm{WH}}+xI_r),\qquad
 M[D+\mathcal R^{\mathrm{WH}}(x)]=K.
\]
Consequently \(\beta\in\Omega^{\mathrm{WH}}\), the scalar equation
\[
 \mathcal A^{\mathrm{WH}}(x)O=O(L^{\mathrm{WH}}+xI_r),\qquad x\in\Omega^{\mathrm{WH}},
\]
has the unique solution \(\beta^{\#}=\beta\), and \(A^{\#}=A\), \(\mu^{\#}=\mu\).  Hence
\[
 \Phi_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{WH}}(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)=(\beta,A,\mu).
\]
Thus the mean and two positive fixed-bin covariance lags identify the entire stable nonzero common-exponential Hawkes mechanism, and hence its labeled graph, whenever the principal-log strip holds only on \(S=\operatorname{im}(A)\); no full-rank, zero-diagonal, coordinate-support, isolated-label, acyclicity, or invertible-active-block condition is required.  The inverse consists of finite-dimensional matrix operations and one uniquely characterized positive scalar root.
