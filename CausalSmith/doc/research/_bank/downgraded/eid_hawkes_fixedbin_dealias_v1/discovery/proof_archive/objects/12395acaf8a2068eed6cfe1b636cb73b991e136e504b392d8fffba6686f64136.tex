For every \(\eta=(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\in\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}\), every operation in \(\text{def:isolated-invertible-block-two-lag-inverse}\) is well defined.  If \((\beta,A,\mu,I,E_I,A_I)\) is any structural representative in \(\text{def:isolated-invertible-block-stable-moment-image}\), then
\[
 S=\operatorname{im}(\Gamma_1)=\operatorname{im}(A)
   =\operatorname{span}\{e_i:i\in I\},\qquad
 I=\{i:e_i\in S\},\qquad r=|I|=\operatorname{rank}(\Gamma_1),
\]
so the active label set and its ordered selector are unique.  Writing
\[
 L_I=A_I-\beta I_r,\quad
 J_I=\int_0^\Delta e^{L_Iu}\,du,\quad
 D_I=E_I^{\mathsf T}DE_I,\quad
 Q_I=\int_0^\infty e^{L_Iu}D_Ie^{L_I^{\mathsf T}u}\,du,\quad
 K_I=A_I(2\beta I_r-A_I)Q_I,
\]
the recovered quantities satisfy
\[
 F^\circ=e^{L_I\Delta},\quad L^\circ=L_I,\quad J^\circ=J_I,\quad
 K^\circ=K_I=(\beta^2I_r-L_I^2)Q_I,\quad Q^\circ=Q_I\succ0,\quad s^\circ=\beta^2>0,
\]
and
\[
 \Phi_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)=(\beta,A,\mu).
\]
The output is unchanged if the active space is represented by any orthonormal basis rather than the canonical ordered selector, and it is independent of the admissible structural representative.  For every \(\Delta>0\), the domain contains the law with \(p=2\), \(\beta=1\), \(A=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,0)\), and \(\mu=\mathbf 1_2\), and it also contains the law with \(p=3\), \(\beta=1\), \(\mu=\mathbf 1_3\), and
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}1/4&1/4&0\\1/4&1/2&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Thus this singular branch includes self-excitation and, in the second witness, an invertible reciprocal self-exciting active block, while retaining an isolated observed label.
