The proved proposition \(\text{prop:empty-graph-honest-diameter-obstruction}\) shows that every honest procedure has, under the common empty-network law, parameter-set diameter at least \(1\wedge|\beta_1-\beta_0|\) with probability at least \((1-2\alpha)_+\), and hence gives the displayed constant lower bound for \(R_{\Theta,n,\kappa_0}(0)\) for every \(n\). At the opposite, purely feasibility level, the deterministic procedure
\[
 \mathfrak U_n^{\mathrm{all}}=(\mathfrak G_p,\overline\Theta_{\Delta,p})
\]
has coverage one and therefore belongs to \(\mathfrak H_{n,\kappa_0}(\alpha)\). Directly from \(\ell_{\mathrm{inc}}\) and the bound \(\rho_{\Theta}\leq1\), it gives
\[
 R_{G,n,\kappa_0}(\delta)\leq|\mathfrak G_p|-1,
 \qquad
 R_{\Theta,n,\kappa_0}(\delta)\leq1
\]
whenever \(\mathcal M_{\kappa_0}(\delta)\) is nonempty. These bounds certify feasibility and the empty-fiber obstruction but do not characterize the positive-separation rates.
