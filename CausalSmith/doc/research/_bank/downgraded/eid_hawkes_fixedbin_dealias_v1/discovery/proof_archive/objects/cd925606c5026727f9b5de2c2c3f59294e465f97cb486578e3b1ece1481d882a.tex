For \(p=3\), every labeled fork support has \(\operatorname{rank}(A)=\operatorname{rank}(\Gamma_1)=1\), whereas every labeled directed-chain support and every labeled three-node diamond support, meaning the transitive triangle in \(\mathfrak G_3^{\mathrm{cfd}}\), has rank two; consequently all other rank-support strata are empty.  Fix a fork and relabel it as \(1\to2\) and \(1\to3\), with \(A_{21}=a>0\) and \(A_{31}=b>0\).  Put \(x=a/\beta\), \(y=b/\beta\), and \(h(\beta)=(1-e^{-\beta\Delta})^2/\beta\).  On this fork stratum, \((m,\Gamma_1)\) identifies \(x\), \(y\), and \(h(\beta)\).  The function \(h\) has a unique maximizer \(\beta_\star=z_\star/\Delta\), where \(z_\star>0\) is the unique positive solution of \(e^{z_\star}=1+2z_\star\).  The within-support one-lag fiber is a singleton exactly when \(\beta=\beta_\star\), and otherwise consists of exactly two parameter points, one on each side of \(\beta_\star\).  The two points induce different laws because their second positive covariance lags differ.  Thus each of \(\{\theta\in\Theta_{1,G_0}:\beta<\beta_\star\}\) and \(\{\theta\in\Theta_{1,G_0}:\beta>\beta_\star\}\) is a nonempty relatively open one-lag alias region for every labeled fork \(G_0\).
