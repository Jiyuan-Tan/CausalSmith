Fix \(\gamma\in\mathfrak J_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}\).  By \(\text{def:full-rank-stable-covariance-image}\), choose an arbitrary admissible structural representative \((\beta,A,\mu)\), and let \(m\) be its population bin mean.  Then \(\eta=(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\) belongs to \(\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}\).  By \(\text{thm:full-rank-stable-two-lag-recovery}\), \(\Gamma_1\) is invertible, the principal logarithm exists, \(B\) and \(J\) are invertible, and the observable operations in the first line of \(\text{def:full-rank-covariance-only-inverse}\) obey
\[
 T^0=T,\qquad B^0=B,\qquad J^0=J,\qquad K^0=K.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus every inverse and logarithm in that line is defined.  By (1) and \(\text{lem:stable-covariance-semigroup}\),
\[
 \widetilde C_{+}^{\mathrm{fr}}(t)=e^{B^0t}K^0=e^{Bt}K=C_{+}(t)
 \qquad(0<t\leq\Delta).
\tag{2}
\]
The integrand extends continuously to \(t=0\), so the finite-interval integral defining \(D^0\) exists.  Substituting (2) into \(\text{lem:zero-lag-bin-covariance}\) gives
\[
 D^0=D=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)\succ0,\qquad
 \lambda^0=\lambda,\qquad m^0=\Delta\lambda=m.
\tag{3}
\]
Consequently \((m^0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)=\eta\in\mathfrak I_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}\), so the last line of the covariance-only map is total.  Equations (3) and \(\text{thm:full-rank-stable-two-lag-recovery}\) imply
\[
 \Psi_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr},0}(\gamma)
 =\Phi_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)
 =(\beta,A,\mu).
\tag{4}
\]
All quantities on the left of (1)--(4) are functions only of \(\gamma\) and the known \(\Delta\).  Since the representative was arbitrary, (1)--(4) hold for every representative and force all representatives to equal the same recovered triple.  This proves representative independence.

For strictness, fix \(p\geq2\) and \(\Delta>0\), and take \(\beta=1\), \(\mu=\mathbf 1_p\), and \(A=\tfrac12 I_p\).  Then \(A\geq0\), \(\det(A)=2^{-p}\neq0\), \(\rho(A/\beta)=1/2<1\), and
\[
 \operatorname{spec}\{\Delta(A-\beta I_p)\}=\{-\Delta/2\}
 \subset\{z:|\operatorname{Im}z|<\pi\}.
\tag{5}
\]
The immigration--birth construction exists here directly: immigrants of each label form independent rate-one Poisson processes, and every event has same-label offspring with intensity \(\tfrac12e^{-u}\,du\).  The expected number of generation-\(h\) descendants is \(2^{-h}\), so the expected total cluster size is \(\sum_{h\geq0}2^{-h}=2<\infty\); hence every cluster is finite almost surely and the stationary superposition is locally finite.  Continuous offspring delays make it simple, and summing the independent offspring intensities gives the stated Hawkes conditional intensity.  Thus this parameter triple induces a law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\), and its covariance triple belongs to \(\mathfrak J_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{fr}}\).  Since \(A_{ii}=1/2>0\), its graph has self-loop cycles and is not a DAG.
