Fix a law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\).  Equation (2) in the proof of \(\text{lem:stable-covariance-semigroup}\) gives \(\lambda_j>0\) for every \(j\), so conditioning on an infinitesimal type-\(j\) event is well normalized.  Let \(\bar g\) denote the nonsingular conditional-response density used in \(\text{lem:bacry-muzy-wiener-hopf-uniqueness}\).  For \(t>0\), there is no diagonal Dirac atom between times zero and \(t\).  Stationarity and \(\mathbb P\{dN_j(0)=1\}=\lambda_j\,dt+o(dt)\) therefore give, entry by entry,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 C_{+}(t)_{ij}\,dt^2
 &=\mathbb E\{dN_i(t)dN_j(0)\}-\lambda_i\lambda_j\,dt^2\\
 &=\lambda_j\,dt\left[
   \mathbb E\{dN_i(t)\mid dN_j(0)=1\}-\lambda_i\,dt
  \right]
 =\lambda_j\bar g^{ij}(t)\,dt^2.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
Thus \(C_{+}(t)=\bar g(t)D\), and right multiplication by \(D^{-1}\) is valid because \(D\succ0\).  Hence
\[
 \bar g(t)=C_{+}(t)D^{-1},\qquad t>0.
\tag{2}
\]
For \(t<0\), covariance symmetry and stationarity give
\[
 \operatorname{Cov}\{dN(t),dN(0)\}
 =C_{+}(-t)^{\mathsf T}\,dt^2.
\tag{3}
\]
Repeating (1) with the negative lag and retaining the time-zero coordinate in the conditioning column yields
\[
 \bar g(t)=C_{+}(-t)^{\mathsf T}D^{-1},\qquad t<0.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) are exactly the two branches in \(\text{def:complete-hawkes-response}\).  Values at \(t=0\) are irrelevant to the entrywise-\(L^1\) equivalence class and to convolution, proving the claim.
