For every law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\), the nonsingular conditional-response density in \(\text{lem:bacry-muzy-wiener-hopf-uniqueness}\) agrees almost everywhere with \(g\) in \(\text{def:complete-hawkes-response}\).  Explicitly,
\[
 C_{+}(t)_{ij}=g^{ij}(t)\lambda_j\quad(t>0),
\]
and consequently
\[
 g(t)=C_{+}(t)D^{-1}\quad(t>0),\qquad
 g(t)=C_{+}(-t)^{\mathsf T}D^{-1}\quad(t<0).
\]
