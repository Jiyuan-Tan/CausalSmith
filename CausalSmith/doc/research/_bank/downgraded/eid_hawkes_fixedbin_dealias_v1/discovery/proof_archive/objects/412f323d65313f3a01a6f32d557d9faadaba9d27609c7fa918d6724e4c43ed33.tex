For every fixed law in \(\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{DAG},+}\), \(\widehat\xi_n\to\xi\) and \(\widehat\eta_n=\mathcal C(\widehat\xi_n)\to\eta=(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\) in probability. Fix \(p\), \(\Delta\), and \(\kappa\in(0,1/2)\). The derived geometric-mixing and sixteenth-bin-count bounds imply that \(\Omega(\theta)\) is finite uniformly over \(P_{\theta}\in\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)\) and \[\sup_{\theta:P_{\theta}\in\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)}d_{\mathrm{BL}}\left(\mathcal L_{\theta}\{\sqrt n(\widehat\xi_n-\xi(\theta))\},\mathcal N\{0,\Omega(\theta)\}\right)\longrightarrow0.\] Because \(\widehat\eta_n=\mathcal C(\widehat\xi_n)\) and \(\eta=\mathcal C(\xi)=(m,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\), the same uniform statement holds for \(\sqrt n(\widehat\eta_n-\eta(\theta))\) with covariance \(D\mathcal C_{\xi(\theta)}\Omega(\theta)D\mathcal C_{\xi(\theta)}^{\mathsf T}\). If \(b_n\to\infty\) and \(b_n=o(n^{1/2})\), then for every \(\varepsilon>0\), \[\sup_{\theta:P_{\theta}\in\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}(\kappa)}P_{\theta}\{\lVert\widehat\Omega_n-\Omega(\theta)\rVert_{\mathrm{op}}>\varepsilon\}\longrightarrow0.\]
