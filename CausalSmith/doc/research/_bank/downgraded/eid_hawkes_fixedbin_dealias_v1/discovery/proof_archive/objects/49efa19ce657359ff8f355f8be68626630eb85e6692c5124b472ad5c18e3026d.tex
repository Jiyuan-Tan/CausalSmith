Let \(\mathfrak J_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{ib}}\) be the population covariance image
\[
 \left\{(\Gamma_0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2):\begin{array}{l}
 \text{there exist }(\beta,A,\mu)\text{ inducing a law in }\mathcal M_{\Delta,p}^{\mathrm{stab}}\text{ and a set }I\subsetneq\{1,\ldots,p\},\\
 1\leq r=|I|\leq p-1,\quad E_I=(e_{i_1},\ldots,e_{i_r})\text{ for }i_1<\cdots<i_r\text{ the elements of }I,\\
 A=E_IA_IE_I^{\mathsf T},\quad A_I\in\mathbb R^{r\times r},\quad A_I\geq0,\quad \det(A_I)\neq0,\quad \rho(A_I)<\beta,\\
 \operatorname{spec}\{\Delta(A_I-\beta I_r)\}\subset\{z\in\mathbb C:|\operatorname{Im}z|<\pi\},\\
 \text{and }\Gamma_0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2\text{ are that law's population covariances}
 \end{array}\right\}.
\]
The inequalities \(A_I\geq0\) are entrywise.  This is a population image under \((\Gamma_0,\Gamma_1,\Gamma_2)\), not an observable gate imposed on arbitrary covariance triples.
