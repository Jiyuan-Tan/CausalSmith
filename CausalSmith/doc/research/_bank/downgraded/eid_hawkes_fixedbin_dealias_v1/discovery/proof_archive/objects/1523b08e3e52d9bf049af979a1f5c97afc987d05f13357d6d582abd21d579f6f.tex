Fix \(n\geq3\). Let \(\theta_s=(\beta_s,0,\mu_0)\), \(s\in\{0,1\}\), have a common strictly positive baseline \(\mu_0\), distinct decays \(\beta_0\neq\beta_1\), and satisfy \(P_{\theta_s}\in\overline{\mathcal M}_{\Delta,p}(\kappa_0)\). Put \(d_{01}=1\wedge|\beta_1-\beta_0|\). Then \(P_{\theta_0}=P_{\theta_1}=:P_0\), and every \(\mathfrak U_n=(\mathcal G_n,\mathcal T_n)\in\mathfrak H_{n,\kappa_0}(\alpha)\) satisfies
\[
 P_0\!\left\{\operatorname{diam}_{\Theta}(\mathcal T_n)\geq d_{01}\right\}\geq(1-2\alpha)_+.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 R_{\Theta,n,\kappa_0}(0)\geq d_{01}(1-2\alpha)_+.
\]
