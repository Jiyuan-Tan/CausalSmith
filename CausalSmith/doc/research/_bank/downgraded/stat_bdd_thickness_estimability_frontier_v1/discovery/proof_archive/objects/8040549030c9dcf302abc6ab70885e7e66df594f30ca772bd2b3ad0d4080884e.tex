For each \(t\in\{0,1\}\), the residual \(\varepsilon_t=Y-g_{t,P}(X)\) is conditionally centered and conditionally \(\sigma\)-sub-Gaussian with fixed known proxy \(\sigma\), in the sense that \(\mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_t\mid X=z]=0\) and \(\mathbb E_P[\exp(\lambda\varepsilon_t)\mid X=z]\le\exp(\sigma^2\lambda^2/2)\) for every \(\lambda\in\mathbb R\) and for \(P_X\)-almost every \(z\in\mathcal X_{t,P}\).
