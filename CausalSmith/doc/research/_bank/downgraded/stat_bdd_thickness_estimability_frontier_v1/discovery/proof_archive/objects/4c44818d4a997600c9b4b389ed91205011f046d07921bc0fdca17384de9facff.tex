Fix \(\kappa\in(2,\overline\kappa]\), use the circular inside--outside assignment and constants certified by \(\mathrm{lem:circular\text{-}witness\text{-}membership}\), and suppose \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\).  Put \(m(h)=c_mh^\kappa\) and declare
\[
 \mathsf J_{-}(h,u)=\mathbf 1\{u\ge C_mh^\kappa\},
 \qquad 0<h\le h_0,\quad 0\le u\le1.
\]
Then
\[
 \mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\cup
 \mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}
 \subseteq \mathcal P(m,\mathsf J_{-}),
\]
and there are constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 c\,r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}
 \le R_n\{\mathcal P(m,\mathsf J_{-})\}
 \le C\,r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}.
\]
In particular,
\[
 \frac{r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 {r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}}
 =(\log n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Therefore \(\mathrm{oeq:arbitrary\text{-}modulus\text{-}frontier}\) has a negative answer as stated: a class constrained only by a lower mass modulus and a lower thin-site packing declaration cannot both retain the pervasive subclass and recover the isolated polynomial frontier when the declared multiplicity is one.  To formulate an arbitrary-modulus result that distinguishes the two regimes, one must add an upper thin-site packing restriction or use an intrinsic two-sided profile; under the frozen two-argument class, the requested package of conclusions cannot hold.
