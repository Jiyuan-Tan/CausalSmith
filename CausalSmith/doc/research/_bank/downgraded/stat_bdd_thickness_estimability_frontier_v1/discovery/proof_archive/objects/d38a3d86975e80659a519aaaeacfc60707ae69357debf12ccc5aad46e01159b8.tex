For fixed score values \(X_1,\ldots,X_n\), let \(\mathscr S_n\) be the collection of nonempty index sets
\[
 \{i:X_i\in\mathcal A_t,\ d(X_i,x)\le h\},\qquad t\in\{0,1\},\quad x\in\mathcal B,\quad h\in\mathcal H_n.
\]
Then
\[
 |\mathscr S_n|\le 2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj.
\]
If \(\mathscr S_n\ne\varnothing\), then under \(\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}sampling}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:gaussian\text{-}errors}\), conditionally on the scores, if \(\varepsilon_i=Y_i-g_{T_i,P}(X_i)\), then for every \(z>0\)
\[
 \Pr\left\{\max_{S\in\mathscr S_n}\frac{|\sum_{i\in S}\varepsilon_i|}{\sigma\sqrt{|S|}}>z\ \middle|\ X_1,\ldots,X_n\right\}
 \le2|\mathscr S_n|e^{-z^2/2},
\]
and the conditional expectation of the displayed maximum is at most \(\sqrt{2\log\{2|\mathscr S_n|\}}+1\).
