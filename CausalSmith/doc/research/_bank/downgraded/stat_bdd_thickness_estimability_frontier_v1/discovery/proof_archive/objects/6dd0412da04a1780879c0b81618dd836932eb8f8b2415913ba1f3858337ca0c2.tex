Suppose \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\). Put \(M_n=2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj\), let \(N=N_{t,n}(x,\widehat h_{t,n}(x))\), and define
\[
B_{t,n}(x)=
\begin{cases}
L\widehat h_{t,n}(x)+\sigma\sqrt{2\log(2M_n/\gamma)/N},&N>0,\\
L,&N=0.
\end{cases}
\]
Then the data-measurable band
\[
\mathcal C_n(x)=\left[\widehat\tau_n(x)-B_{0,n}(x)-B_{1,n}(x),\ 
\widehat\tau_n(x)+B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)\right]
\]
satisfies, for every declared nonempty triangular union \(\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)\),
\[
\inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)}P\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
There is a constant \(C<\infty\), independent of \(n\), \(P\), and \(\kappa\in(2,\overline\kappa]\), such that uniformly over \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) the band also satisfies \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}\mathbb E_P[\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)]\le C r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) for all sufficiently large \(n\). Moreover, for any \(\epsilon_n\ge0\), every band with simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma-\epsilon_n\) over a class containing laws \(P_0,P_1\) obeys, for every \(x\in\mathcal B\),
\[
\mathbb E_{P_0}W_n(x)\ge
\frac{|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|}{2}
\left[1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-
\operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})\right]_+.
\]
Thus finite-sample global honesty, pervasive-rate global width, and a computable full-observed-law two-point width obstruction hold. This is a partial resolution: no uniform local-oracle width ratio and no necessary-and-sufficient criterion based only on score-law total variation are asserted.
