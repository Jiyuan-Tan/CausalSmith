Apply \(\mathrm{thm:global\text{-}honest\text{-}band\text{-}and\text{-}width\text{-}obstruction}\) with its displayed value of \(M_n\) and its displayed side radii \(B_{t,n}(x)\).  Its first conclusion gives data measurability and, for each \(P\in\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)\),
\[
P\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
Taking the infimum over the nonempty union preserves the same lower bound.  Because each \(B_{t,n}(x)\ge0\), the displayed interval has diameter \(2\{B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)\}\); hence \(\mathrm{def:band\text{-}half\text{-}width}\) gives
\[
W_n(x)=B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x).
\]
The pervasive-width clause of the cited theorem therefore yields
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
\mathbb E_P\left[\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)\right]
\le C r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}.
\]

For completeness, the last clause of \(\mathrm{thm:global\text{-}honest\text{-}band\text{-}and\text{-}width\text{-}obstruction}\) is obtained by setting \(A_j=\{\tau_{P_j}(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\}\).  Coverage and the definition of total variation give
\[
P_0(A_0)\ge1-\gamma-\epsilon_n,
\qquad
P_0(A_1)\ge P_1(A_1)-\operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})
\ge1-\gamma-\epsilon_n-operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n}).
\]
The union bound consequently gives
\[
P_0(A_0\cap A_1)
\ge1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n}).
\]
On \(A_0\cap A_1\), the interval contains both target values, so \(W_n(x)\ge|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|/2\).  Multiplication by the positive part of the preceding probability lower bound and expectation under \(P_0\) prove the displayed obstruction.  These deductions establish exactly the three positive conclusions in the statement and do not invoke either of the two explicitly excluded, sharper assertions.
