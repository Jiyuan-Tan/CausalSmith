It remains open whether one can construct a band honest over an arbitrary declared nonempty triangular union \(\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)\) whose pointwise expected half-width is uniformly within a constant factor of \(r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)\), and whether every honest band obeys the matching local-oracle ratio lower bound.  If such adaptation is impossible, it also remains open to give the requested exact necessary-and-sufficient characterization, in terms of pairs of profile-restricted components, by uniform score-law distinguishability.
