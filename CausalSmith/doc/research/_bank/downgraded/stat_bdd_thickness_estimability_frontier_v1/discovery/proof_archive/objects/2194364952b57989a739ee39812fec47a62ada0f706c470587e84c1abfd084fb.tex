By \(\mathrm{def:polynomial\text{-}horn}\), a vertical section at inward coordinate \(u\) has length \(2u^{\kappa-1}\). Since \(B(0,h)\subset\{0<u<h\}\times[-h,h]\), direct integration of these nonnegative section lengths gives
\[
 \operatorname{Leb}_2(\mathsf H_{\kappa}\cap B(0,h))
 \le 2\int_0^h u^{\kappa-1}\,du=\frac{2}{\kappa}h^{\kappa}.
\]
For \(0<u<h/2\), one has \(h^2-u^2\ge3h^2/4\). Because \(\kappa>2\), for all sufficiently small \(h<h_0\), \(u^{\kappa-1}\le(h/2)^{\kappa-1}\le\sqrt3h/2\). Hence the entire section \(\{|v|\le u^{\kappa-1}\}\) lies in \(B(0,h)\), and therefore
\[
 \operatorname{Leb}_2(\mathsf H_{\kappa}\cap B(0,h))
 \ge2\int_0^{h/2}u^{\kappa-1}\,du
 =\frac{2^{1-\kappa}}{\kappa}h^{\kappa}.
\]
Taking \(c_\kappa=2^{1-\kappa}/\kappa\) and \(C_\kappa=2/\kappa\) proves both inequalities and shows that the exponent is \(\kappa\), rather than \(\kappa-1\).
