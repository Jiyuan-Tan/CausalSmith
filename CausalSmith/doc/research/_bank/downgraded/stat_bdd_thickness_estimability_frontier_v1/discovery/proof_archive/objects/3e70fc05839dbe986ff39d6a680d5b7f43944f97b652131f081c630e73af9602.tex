Suppose \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\), and put
\[
 M_n=2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj.
\]
For \(N=N_{t,n}(x,\widehat h_{t,n}(x))\), define
\[
 B_{t,n}(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 L\widehat h_{t,n}(x)+\sigma\sqrt{2\log(2M_n/\gamma)/N},&N>0,\\
 L,&N=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
The explicit band
\[
 \mathcal C_n(x)=
 \left[
 \widehat\tau_n(x)-B_{0,n}(x)-B_{1,n}(x),
 \widehat\tau_n(x)+B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)
 \right]
\]
is data measurable and satisfies, for every declared nonempty triangular union,
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)}
 P\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\}
 \ge 1-\gamma.
\]
For each admissible polynomial component it obeys
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)
 \le C_\gamma(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}.
\]
Whenever \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\ne\varnothing\), every band with coverage at least \(1-\gamma-\epsilon_n\), where \(\epsilon_n\downarrow0\), satisfies the matching global lower bound and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)
 \ge c_\gamma(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)},
\]
as well as
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B}
 \frac{\mathbb E_PW_n(x)}{r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)}
 \ge c_\gamma.
\]
Moreover, for any two laws \(P_0,P_1\) in an honesty class and every \(x\in\mathcal B\), simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma-\epsilon_n\) implies
\[
 \mathbb E_{P_0}W_n(x)
 \ge
 \frac{|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|}{2}
 \left[
 1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-
 \operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})
 \right]_+.
\]
These established conclusions give global achievability, the pervasive local-ratio converse, and a general obstruction expressed through the full observed laws. They do not yield the requested uniformly local oracle-width ratio on an arbitrary triangular union or an exact criterion expressed only through score-law total variation.
