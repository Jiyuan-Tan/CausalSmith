By \(\mathrm{def:polynomial\text{-}horn}\), a vertical section of \(\mathsf H_{\kappa}\) at inward coordinate \(u\) has length \(2u^{\kappa-1}\). Since \(B(0,h)\subset\{0<u<h\}\times[-h,h]\), Tonelli's theorem for the nonnegative section indicator gives
\[
 \operatorname{Leb}_2\{\mathsf H_{\kappa}\cap B(0,h)\}
 \le 2\int_0^h u^{\kappa-1}\,du
 =\frac{2}{\kappa}h^{\kappa}.
\]
For \(0<u<h/2\), one has \(h^2-u^2\ge3h^2/4\). Because \(\kappa>2\), for all sufficiently small \(h<h_0\),
\[
 u^{\kappa-1}\le(h/2)^{\kappa-1}\le \sqrt{3}h/2.
\]
Hence the entire section \(\{|v|\le u^{\kappa-1}\}\) lies in \(B(0,h)\), and another application of Tonelli gives
\[
 \operatorname{Leb}_2\{\mathsf H_{\kappa}\cap B(0,h)\}
 \ge 2\int_0^{h/2}u^{\kappa-1}\,du
 =\frac{2^{1-\kappa}}{\kappa}h^{\kappa}.
\]
Thus one may take \(c_{\kappa}=2^{1-\kappa}/\kappa\) and \(C_{\kappa}=2/\kappa\). Both are positive and finite, and the power is \(\kappa\), not \(\kappa-1\).
