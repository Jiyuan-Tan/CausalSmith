Under \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\), put
\[
 M_n=2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj
\]
and, for \(N=N_{t,n}(x,\widehat h_{t,n}(x))\), define
\[
 B_{t,n}(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 L\widehat h_{t,n}(x)+
 \sigma\sqrt{2\log(2M_n/\gamma)/N},&N>0,\\
 L,&N=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Then
\[
 \mathcal C_n(x)=
 \left[\widehat\tau_n(x)-B_{0,n}(x)-B_{1,n}(x),
       \widehat\tau_n(x)+B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)\right]
\]
is data measurable and, by
\(\mathrm{thm:global\text{-}honest\text{-}band\text{-}and\text{-}width\text{-}obstruction}\),
satisfies finite-sample simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma\) on
every declared nonempty \(\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)\).  On every polynomial
base component its global expected-supremum half-width is at most
\(C_\gamma(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}\).  If the corresponding pervasive
subclass is nonempty, every honest band has the matching global lower
bound and obeys
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_\kappa^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \sup_{x\in\mathcal B}
 \frac{\mathbb E_PW_n(x)}{r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)}\ge c_\gamma.
\]
For any two laws \(P_0,P_1\) in an honesty class, the same theorem proves
the pointwise full-observed-law obstruction
\[
 \mathbb E_{P_0}W_n(x)\ge
 \frac{|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|}{2}
 \left[1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-
 \operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})\right]_+.
\]
These are the strongest conclusions presently derived from the frozen
premises.  They do not establish a uniformly local oracle-width upper
ratio over an arbitrary triangular union, its matching lower ratio when
no pervasive component is present, or an exact necessary-and-sufficient
criterion involving only score-law total variation.
