Fix \(\kappa\in(2,\overline\kappa]\), an assignment geometry satisfying \(\mathrm{ass:global\text{-}boundary\text{-}entropy}\) and \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\), and constants for which \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\) and \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) are nonempty; \(\mathrm{lem:circular\text{-}witness\text{-}membership}\) supplies an explicit such specification. The two classes share the scalar lower modulus \(c_mh^{\kappa}\), but there are constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), independent of \(n\), such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\), \(c r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\le R_n(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}})\le C r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\) and \(c r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\le R_n(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}})\le C r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\). Thus no expected-sup minimax frontier is a functional of the common scalar modulus alone, and the frontier separates according to the declared thin-site multiplicity profile.
