Fix the circular assignment and constants in the statement. Let \(P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\). The base-class member properties include sharp assignment, consistency, global boundary entropy, Lipschitz side means, bounded side traces, Gaussian errors, and
\[
 q_{t,P}(x,h)\ge c_mh^\kappa=m(h)
\]
for all \(x\in\mathcal B\), \(t\in\{0,1\}\), and \(0<h\le h_0\). Thus every member property of \(\mathcal P(m,\mathsf J_{-})\) except the lower-profile condition is immediate from \(\mathrm{def:base\text{-}thickness\text{-}class}\) and \(\mathrm{def:modulus\text{-}profile\text{-}class}\).

For \(u<C_mh^\kappa\), \(\mathsf J_{-}(h,u)=0\le\mathsf J_P(h,u)\). For \(u\ge C_mh^\kappa\), the thin set defining \(\mathsf J_P(h,u)\) contains the thin set at level \(C_mh^\kappa\); therefore the packing profile is nondecreasing in its second argument. The isolated thin-site property gives
\[
 \mathsf J_P(h,u)\ge\mathsf J_P(h,C_mh^\kappa)\ge1
 =\mathsf J_{-}(h,u). \tag{74}
\]
Hence \(P\in\mathcal P(m,\mathsf J_{-})\). If instead \(P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\), the same argument applies, because
\[
 \mathsf J_P(h,C_mh^\kappa)\ge c_Jh^{-1}>0,
\]
and the integer-valued packing number is consequently at least one. We have proved
\[
 \mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\cup
 \mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}
 \subseteq\mathcal P(m,\mathsf J_{-}). \tag{75}
\]
The circular witness-membership lemma makes both subclasses, and hence the containing class, nonempty.

Minimax risk is monotone under class inclusion: if \(\mathcal Q\subseteq\mathcal P\), then for each estimator \(\widetilde\tau_n\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P}\mathbb E_P\|\widetilde\tau_n-\tau_P\|_\infty
 \ge
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal Q}\mathbb E_P\|\widetilde\tau_n-\tau_P\|_\infty,
\]
and taking the infimum over the same estimators preserves the inequality. Applying this to the pervasive inclusion in (75), and then using the refreshed exact-Gaussian result \(\mathrm{thm:thickness\text{-}entropy\text{-}separation}\), gives, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 R_n\{\mathcal P(m,\mathsf J_{-})\}
 \ge R_n(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}})
 \ge c\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{1/(\kappa+2)}. \tag{76}
\]

For the reverse inequality, apply the upper display of \(\mathrm{thm:arbitrary\text{-}modulus\text{-}two\text{-}sided\text{-}bracket}\) with \(m(h)=c_mh^\kappa\). Put
\[
 r_n=D\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{1/(\kappa+2)},
\]
where \(D\ge1\) is fixed. For all sufficiently large \(n\), the dyadic grid contains \(h_n\) with \(r_n\le h_n<2r_n\), \(2h_n\in\mathcal H_n\), and \(2h_n\le h_0\). Moreover,
\[
 \frac{nm(h_n)}{\log(e+n/h_n)}
 \ge c\frac{n r_n^\kappa}{\log n}
 =cD^\kappa\left(\frac n{\log n}\right)^{2/(\kappa+2)}longrightarrow\infty. \tag{77}
\]
Thus the bandwidth is eligible for every fixed \(A\) eventually. Since \(nh_n^{\kappa+2}\asymp D^{\kappa+2}\log n\),
\[
 Lh_n+\sigma\sqrt{\frac{\log(e+n/h_n)}{nc_mh_n^\kappa}}
 \le C r_n. \tag{78}
\]
The bracket theorem supplies one clipped, data-driven estimator with maximal risk bounded by the right side of (78); therefore the infimum defining minimax risk obeys
\[
 R_n\{\mathcal P(m,\mathsf J_{-})\}
 \le C\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{1/(\kappa+2)}. \tag{79}
\]
Combining (76) and (79) proves the claimed two-sided rate.

Finally, the two named rates satisfy the exact algebraic identity
\[
 \frac{r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}{r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}}
 =\frac{(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}}{n^{-1/(\kappa+2)}}
 =(\log n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}\longrightarrow\infty. \tag{80}
\]
Thus a class imposing only the lower mass modulus and the declared lower packing profile with value one still contains the pervasive subclass and necessarily has the pervasive logarithmic rate. It cannot recover the isolated frontier without an upper-profile or intrinsic two-sided-profile restriction, which proves the stated no-go conclusion.
