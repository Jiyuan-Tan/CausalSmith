Suppose \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\). Put \(M_n=2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj\), let \(N=N_{t,n}(x,\widehat h_{t,n}(x))\), and define
\[
 B_{t,n}(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 L\widehat h_{t,n}(x)+\sigma\sqrt{2\log(2M_n/\gamma)/N},&N>0,\\
 L,&N=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Then the explicit band
\[
 \mathcal C_n(x)=
 \left[\widehat\tau_n(x)-B_{0,n}(x)-B_{1,n}(x),
 \widehat\tau_n(x)+B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)\right]
\]
is data measurable and, for every declared nonempty triangular union \(\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)\), satisfies the finite-sample guarantee
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)}P\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
Fix an admissible \(\kappa\).  For every fixed \(\gamma\in(0,1)\), there is \(C_\gamma<\infty\), independent of \(n\), such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)
 \le C_\gamma(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\). For the remaining lower-bound conclusions only, further specialize to the circular inside--outside assignment and the fixed class constants certified by \(\mathrm{lem:circular\text{-}witness\text{-}membership}\); in particular, \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) is nonempty. If \(\gamma\) is smaller than one quarter of the universal numerical risk constant furnished by \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}information\text{-}testing}\), there is \(c_\gamma>0\), independent of \(n\), such that every data-measurable band sequence with simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma\) over \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) obeys, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)
 \ge c_\gamma(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}.
\]
Every band honest over \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}\) obeys the same lower bound. Thus, under this certified circular specification, the displayed band attains the matched global expected-sup half-width frontier on the pervasive and base classes.

Independently of the preceding small-\(\gamma\) restriction, for any \(\gamma\in(0,1)\) and \(\epsilon_n\ge0\), every band with simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma-\epsilon_n\) over a class containing two laws \(P_0,P_1\) obeys, for every \(x\in\mathcal B\),

 \mathbb E_{P_0}W_n(x)\ge\frac{|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|}{2}
 \left[1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-
 \operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})\right]_+.
\]
