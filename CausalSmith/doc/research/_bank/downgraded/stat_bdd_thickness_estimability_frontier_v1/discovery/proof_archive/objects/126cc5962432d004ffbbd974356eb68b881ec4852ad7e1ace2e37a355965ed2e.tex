For each \(t\in\{0,1\}\), define \(\varepsilon_t=Y-g_{t,P}(X)\) on \(\{X\in\mathcal X_{t,P}\}\). For \(P_X\)-almost every \(z\in\mathcal X_{t,P}\), the conditional residual is centered and has fixed known proxy \(\sigma\):
\[
 \mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_t\mid X=z]=0,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P[\exp(\lambda\varepsilon_t)\mid X=z]
 \le \exp(\sigma^2\lambda^2/2)
 \quad\text{for every }\lambda\in\mathbb R.
\]
