Suppose \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\), and put
\[
 M_n=2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj.
\]
For \(N=N_{t,n}(x,\widehat h_{t,n}(x))\), define
\[
 B_{t,n}(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 L\widehat h_{t,n}(x)+\sigma\sqrt{2\log(2M_n/\gamma)/N},&N>0,\\
 L,&N=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
The established theorem \(\mathrm{thm:global\text{-}honest\text{-}band\text{-}and\text{-}width\text{-}obstruction}\), using \(\mathrm{lem:disk\text{-}pattern\text{-}sub\text{-}gaussian\text{-}control}\), proves that the explicit data-measurable band
\[
 \mathcal C_n(x)=
 \left[
 \widehat\tau_n(x)-B_{0,n}(x)-B_{1,n}(x),
 \widehat\tau_n(x)+B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)
 \right]
\]
satisfies, for every declared nonempty triangular union,
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)}
 P\!\left\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)
 \text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\right\}\ge 1-\gamma.
\]
Thus coverage concerns the identified causal trace contrast \(\tau_P\), whereas \(\mathcal C_n\) is the sample-measurable inferential object.  For every fixed admissible \(\kappa\) and fixed \(\gamma\in(0,1)\), the same theorem proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}}
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)\right]
 \le C_\gamma\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{1/(\kappa+2)}.
\]
If \(\gamma\) is smaller than one quarter of the universal numerical risk constant in \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}information\text{-}testing}\), every band honest over \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) obeys
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)\right]
 \ge c_\gamma\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{1/(\kappa+2)}.
\]
When \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\ne\varnothing\), the same lower bound holds for bands honest over \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}\).  Finally, without the small-\(\gamma\) restriction, every band having simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma-\epsilon_n\) over a class containing two full observed laws \(P_0,P_1\) satisfies, for every \(x\in\mathcal B\),
\[
 \mathbb E_{P_0}W_n(x)
 \ge
 \frac{|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|}{2}
 \left[
 1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-
 \operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})
 \right]_+.
\]
This closes finite-sample global honesty, the pervasive global expected-sup width frontier, and the general two-full-law pointwise obstruction.  It does not prove a constant bound for \(\mathbb E_P[W_n(x)]/r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)\), its matching local-ratio converse, or an exact necessary-and-sufficient criterion expressed only through score-law total variation.
