The arbitrary-modulus theorem is a proved two-sided bracket: its lower profile may be slack, so an upper thin-site packing envelope is needed to distinguish isolated from pervasive thinning exactly. The globally honest band calibrates all disk patterns and therefore uses \(\log M_n\asymp\log n\); this need not match \(\log\{eK_P(x,h)/\gamma\}\) at an isolated site. The geometry-general Fano construction closes the pervasive global-width converse and the pervasive pointwise-ratio lower side, but not uniform local-oracle achievability on arbitrary triangular unions. Finally, score-law distinguishability alone does not control total variation between full observed laws, so it cannot by itself be an exact honest-adaptation criterion.
