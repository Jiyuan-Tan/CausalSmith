The accepted-bank note \texttt{stat\_bdd\_uniform\_log\_penalty\_v1} establishes its logarithmic expected-sup frontier under regular-density/common-map conditions and therefore does not cover vanishing two-sided boundary mass or distinguish an isolated thin site from \(\asymp h^{-1}\) pervasive thin sites. Gaiffas2007SharpSupNorm gives an unknown-density, spatially normalized univariate sup-norm estimator under a positive continuous design density, Gaiffas2009Uniform treats finitely many univariate density zeros, and AntoniadisPenskySapatinas2014 studies known-design global \(L^2\) loss; none gives this same-modulus isolated-versus-pervasive expected-sup separation for a two-sided causal boundary.
