Fix \(\kappa\in(2,\overline\kappa]\), suppose \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\), fix an assignment geometry satisfying \(\mathrm{ass:global\text{-}boundary\text{-}entropy}\), and suppose \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\ne\varnothing\). For every fixed \(\gamma\in(0,1)\), every sequence \(\epsilon_n\downarrow0\), and every data-measurable band sequence satisfying
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 P\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\}
 \ge1-\gamma-\epsilon_n,
\]
there is \(c_\gamma>0\), independent of \(n\), such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)
 \ge c_\gamma r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}},
 \qquad
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}
 \frac{\mathbb E_PW_n(x)}{r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)}
 \ge c_\gamma.
\]
Every band honest over \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}\) obeys the first conclusion, and it obeys the second conclusion whenever widths are compared over its pervasive subclass. These conclusions hold for every such assignment geometry; the circular laws are needed only as explicit nonemptiness witnesses.
