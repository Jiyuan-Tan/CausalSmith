Under \(\mathrm{ass:gaussian\text{-}errors}\), define \(\mu_{t,P}(E)=P_X(E\cap\mathcal A_t)\), and let \(\mathsf R_P\) and \(N_{t,P}\) be the conditional outcome kernel and side-specific null sets furnished by that assumption. For each \(t\in\{0,1\}\) and every \(z\in\mathcal X_{t,P}\setminus N_{t,P}\), the residual \(\varepsilon_t=Y-g_{t,P}(X)\) is conditionally integrable and satisfies
\[
 \mathbb E_P[\varepsilon_t\mid X=z]=0,\qquad
 \mathbb E_P[\exp(\lambda\varepsilon_t)\mid X=z]\le \exp(\sigma^2\lambda^2/2)\quad\text{for every }\lambda\in\mathbb R,
\]
and
\[
 \mathbb E_P[|\varepsilon_t|\mid X=z]\le \sigma\sqrt{2\pi}.
\]
Consequently \(\mathbb E_P[Y\mid X=z]=g_{t,P}(z)\) at every such \(z\); in particular, all these conclusions hold \(\mu_{t,P}\)-almost everywhere on \(\mathcal X_{t,P}\).
