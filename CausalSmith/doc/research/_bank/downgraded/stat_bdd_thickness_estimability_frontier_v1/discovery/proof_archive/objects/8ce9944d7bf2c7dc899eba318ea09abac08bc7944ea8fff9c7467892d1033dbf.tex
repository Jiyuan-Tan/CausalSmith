Suppose \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\), and put
\[
 M_n=2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj.
\]
For \(N=N_{t,n}(x,\widehat h_{t,n}(x))\), define
\[
 B_{t,n}(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 L\widehat h_{t,n}(x)+\sigma\sqrt{2\log(2M_n/\gamma)/N},&N>0,\\
 L,&N=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
The already established theorem \(\mathrm{thm:global\text{-}honest\text{-}band\text{-}and\text{-}width\text{-}obstruction}\) proves that
\[
 \mathcal C_n(x)=
 \left[\widehat\tau_n(x)-B_{0,n}(x)-B_{1,n}(x),
 \widehat\tau_n(x)+B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)\right]
\]
is data measurable and satisfies
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)}
 P\!\left\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)
 \text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\right\}\ge 1-\gamma
\]
for every declared nonempty triangular union.  For every fixed admissible \(\kappa\) and fixed \(\gamma\in(0,1)\), the same theorem gives
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}}
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)\right]
 \le C_\gamma\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{1/(\kappa+2)}.
\]
If \(\gamma\) is smaller than one quarter of the universal numerical risk constant in \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}information\text{-}testing}\), every band honest over \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)\right]
 \ge c_\gamma\left(\frac{\log n}{n}\right)^{1/(\kappa+2)}.
\]
The same lower bound holds for bands honest over \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}\) whenever \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\ne\varnothing\).  Finally, for arbitrary \(\gamma\in(0,1)\), if a band has simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma-\epsilon_n\) over a class containing \(P_0\) and \(P_1\), then, for every \(x\in\mathcal B\),
\[
 \mathbb E_{P_0}W_n(x)\ge
 \frac{|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|}{2}
 \left[1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-
 \operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})\right]_+.
\]
Thus finite-sample global honesty, the matched pervasive global expected-sup width, and a general full-observed-law two-point width obstruction are closed.  What remains unresolved is the uniformly local ratio to \(r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)\), its matching local ratio lower bound, and an exact criterion stated solely through score-law distinguishability.
