Choose an arbitrary \(P^\circ\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\),
whose existence is assumed, and retain its score marginal and assignment
geometry.  On an extension carrying independent
\(Z_0,Z_1\sim\mathcal N(0,1)\), define \(Q_0\) by
\(Y(t)=\sigma Z_t\) and the consistency equation.  Sharp assignment and
consistency hold by construction.  Boundary entropy, polynomial lower
mass, and pervasive packing depend only on the retained score law and
geometry.  Both regressions and traces are zero, and the observed-outcome
kernel is exactly centered Gaussian with variance \(\sigma^2\).  Thus
\[
 Q_0\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}.
 \tag{77}
\]

Put \(r_n=(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}\), fix
\(a_\gamma=(1-\gamma)/4\), and let \(h=b_\gamma r_n\), with the fixed
positive \(b_\gamma\) chosen below.  Eventually \(h\le h_0\).  The
pervasive thin-site condition and the definition of \(\mathsf J_{Q_0}\)
provide an \(h\)-separated set \(\{x_1,\ldots,x_M\}\) with
\[
 M\ge\lfloor c_Jh^{-1}\rfloor,
 \qquad a_{Q_0}(x_j,h)\le C_mh^\kappa.
 \tag{78}
\]
Choose \(t_j\) attaining the minimum, so
\[
 q_{t_j,Q_0}(x_j,h)\le C_mh^\kappa.
 \tag{79}
\]
Set \(\eta=\min\{L/6,1\}\), \(\Delta=\eta h\), and
\(\phi_j(z)=(1-3d(z,x_j)/h)_+\).  Retain the score law and independent
Gaussian noises, and define \(Q_j\), for \(j\ge1\), by
\[
 \begin{cases}
 Y(1)=\Delta\phi_j(X)+\sigma Z_1,\quad Y(0)=\sigma Z_0,&t_j=1,\\
 Y(1)=\sigma Z_1,\quad Y(0)=-\Delta\phi_j(X)+\sigma Z_0,&t_j=0.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{80}
\]
The perturbed side mean is \(3\eta\)-Lipschitz, with
\(3\eta\le L/2<L\), and \(h<h_0<1\) gives
\(\Delta\le\eta<L\).  All score conditions are unchanged and the
centered residual kernel remains \(\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)\).  Therefore
\[
 Q_j\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}},
 \qquad 0\le j\le M.
 \tag{81}
\]
This membership conclusion uses only the frozen member conditions, not
circular geometry.

The tents have radius \(h/3\), whereas their centers are
\(h\)-separated.  Continuity of the side means supplies their boundary
traces, and hence
\[
 \tau_{Q_0}(x_k)=0,
 \qquad \tau_{Q_j}(x_k)=\Delta\mathbf1\{j=k\}.
 \tag{82}
\]
The equal-variance Gaussian likelihood calculation, (79), and
\(0\le\phi_j\le1\) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{KL}(Q_j^{\otimes n},Q_0^{\otimes n})
 &=\frac{n\Delta^2}{2\sigma^2}
   \int_{\mathcal A_{t_j}}\phi_j(z)^2\,dP_X^\circ(z)\\
 &\le\frac{C_m\eta^2}{2\sigma^2}nh^{\kappa+2}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{83}
\]
Independent sampling justifies the product experiment and additivity in
(83).  Since \(nh^{\kappa+2}=b_\gamma^{\kappa+2}\log n\), whereas (78)
implies
\[
 \log M\ge\frac{1}{2(\kappa+2)}\log n
 \tag{84}
\]
eventually, choose \(b_\gamma>0\), independently of \(n\), so that
\[
 \frac1{M+1}\sum_{j=0}^M
 \operatorname{KL}(Q_j^{\otimes n},Q_0^{\otimes n})
 \le a_\gamma\log M.
 \tag{85}
\]
The explicit entropy-decoding bound then gives, for every decoder
\(\widehat J\),
\[
 \frac1{M+1}\sum_{j=0}^M Q_j^{\otimes n}\{\widehat J\ne j\}
 \ge p_n:=
 \frac{\log(M+1)-a_\gamma\log M-\log2}{\log M}.
 \tag{86}
\]
Since \(M\to\infty\),
\[
 p_n\longrightarrow1-a_\gamma=\frac{3+\gamma}{4}>\gamma,
 \tag{87}
\]
and therefore \(p_n-\gamma-\epsilon_n\ge(1-\gamma)/2\) eventually.

For the global conclusion put
\(W_n^*=\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)\).  From the reported intervals at
\(x_1,\ldots,x_M\), let \(\widehat J\) be the least code index whose
vector \((\tau_{Q_j}(x_1),\ldots,\tau_{Q_j}(x_M))\) lies in their
Cartesian product, using zero if none qualifies.  On simultaneous
coverage under \(Q_j\), code \(j\) qualifies.  If also
\(W_n^*<\Delta/2\), no two codes qualify, because two codes differ by
\(\Delta\) in some coordinate while each interval has diameter below
\(\Delta\).  Markov's inequality therefore gives
\[
 Q_j^{\otimes n}\{\widehat J\ne j\}
 \le\gamma+\epsilon_n+
 \frac{2\mathbb E_{Q_j}W_n^*}{\Delta}.
 \tag{88}
\]
If an expectation is infinite the conclusion is immediate.  Otherwise,
average (88) and use (86)--(87) to obtain
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \mathbb E_P\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)
 \ge\frac\Delta2(p_n-\gamma-\epsilon_n)
 \ge\frac{\eta b_\gamma(1-\gamma)}4r_n.
 \tag{89}
\]

For the pointwise conclusion, define a decoder to equal zero if every
\(\mathcal C_n(x_k)\) contains zero, and otherwise to equal the smallest
\(k\) for which zero is absent.  Under \(Q_0\), simultaneous coverage
implies correct decoding.  Under \(Q_j\), (82), simultaneous coverage,
and \(W_n(x_j)<\Delta/2\) imply that every inactive interval contains
zero while the active interval contains \(\Delta\) and excludes zero.
Consequently,
\[
 Q_0^{\otimes n}\{\widehat J\ne0\}\le\gamma+\epsilon_n,
 \qquad
 Q_j^{\otimes n}\{\widehat J\ne j\}
 \le\gamma+\epsilon_n+
 \frac{2\mathbb E_{Q_j}W_n(x_j)}\Delta.
 \tag{90}
\]
Average (90), compare with (86), and use (87).  For at least one
\(j\ge1\),
\[
 \mathbb E_{Q_j}W_n(x_j)
 \ge\frac{M+1}{2M}\Delta(p_n-\gamma-\epsilon_n)
 \ge c_\gamma r_n.
 \tag{91}
\]

For all sufficiently large \(n\), the dyadic grid contains
\(\overline h\) with \(h\le\overline h<2h\).  Polynomial lower mass,
retained by every \(Q_j\), gives
\[
 q_{t,Q_j}(x_j,\overline h)\ge c_m\overline h^\kappa,
 \qquad t\in\{0,1\}.
 \tag{92}
\]
Every \(\overline h\)-separated set counted by
\(\mathsf J_{Q_j}(\overline h,a_{Q_j}(x_j,\overline h))\) is a subset
of the whole boundary.  Global boundary entropy and the definition of
\(K_P\) imply
\[
 K_{Q_j}(x_j,\overline h)
 \le1\vee C_{\mathcal B}\overline h^{-1}.
 \tag{93}
\]
Insert \(\overline h\) in the oracle infimum.  Equations (92)--(93),
\(\overline h\asymp r_n\), and \(nr_n^{\kappa+2}=\log n\) yield
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 r_{n,Q_j}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x_j)
 &\le2L\overline h+
 \sigma\sqrt{\frac{\log\{e(1\vee C_{\mathcal B}\overline h^{-1})/
 \gamma\}}n
 \{q_{0,Q_j}(x_j,\overline h)^{-1}
   +q_{1,Q_j}(x_j,\overline h)^{-1}\}}\\
 &\le C_\gamma r_n.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{94}
\]
Combining (91) and (94) proves the ratio bound.  Finally,
\(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\subseteq
\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{base}}\), so a band honest over the base
class is honest over the pervasive subclass and its supremum is no
smaller.  This proves the stated transfer without any use of circular
geometry.
