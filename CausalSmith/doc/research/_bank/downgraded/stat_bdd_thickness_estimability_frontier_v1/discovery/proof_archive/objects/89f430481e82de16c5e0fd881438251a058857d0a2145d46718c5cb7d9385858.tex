The explicit globally calibrated band in \(\mathrm{thm:global\text{-}honest\text{-}band\text{-}and\text{-}width\text{-}obstruction}\) has finite-sample simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma\) on every declared nonempty triangular union and global expected-supremum half-width \(O\{(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}\}\) on every polynomial base component. The geometry-general construction in \(\mathrm{lem:geometry\text{-}general\text{-}pervasive\text{-}band\text{-}width\text{-}obstruction}\) proves, for every fixed \(\gamma\in(0,1)\), the matching global lower bound on every nonempty pervasive component and its base class, without circular specialization. It also proves the partial pointwise converse
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}
 \frac{\mathbb E_PW_n(x)}{r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)}\ge c_\gamma
\]
for every band with coverage \(1-\gamma-o(1)\) on a nonempty pervasive component, and hence on any triangular union containing that component. The full-observed-law two-point inequality in the global-band theorem remains valid for every geometry. What is not proved is a band attaining the local ratio uniformly on an arbitrary triangular profile union, the matching local-ratio converse for every possible union, or an exact necessary-and-sufficient criterion stated only through score-law total variation.
