The established global calibration controls all nonempty empirical disk patterns simultaneously and therefore pays \(\log M_n\asymp\log n\).  At an isolated thin site, however, \(K_P(x,h)\) can remain bounded, so the oracle logarithm \(\log\{eK_P(x,h)/\gamma\}\) can be of constant order.  The resulting global radius does not prove a constant bound for \(\mathbb E_P[W_n(x)]/r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)\).  Conversely, the available two-law inequality is expressed in \(\operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})\) for the full observed laws.  The frozen assumptions do not provide an implication replacing this distance by \(\operatorname{TV}(P_{0,X}^{\otimes n},P_{1,X}^{\otimes n})\), because the conditional outcome laws may also differ.  Hence neither the local ratio converse nor an exact score-law criterion follows from the declared primitives.
