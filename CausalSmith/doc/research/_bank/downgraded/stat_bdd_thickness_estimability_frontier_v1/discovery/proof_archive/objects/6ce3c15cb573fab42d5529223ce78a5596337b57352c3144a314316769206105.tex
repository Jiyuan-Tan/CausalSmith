Apply \(\mathrm{thm:global\text{-}honest\text{-}band\text{-}and\text{-}width\text{-}obstruction}\) with its displayed \(M_n\) and side radii \(B_{t,n}(x)\). Its first conclusion gives data measurability and, for each \(P\in\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)\),
\[
 P\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
Taking the infimum over the nonempty union preserves this bound. Since each \(B_{t,n}(x)\ge0\), the displayed interval has diameter \(2\{B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)\}\). Hence \(\mathrm{def:band\text{-}half\text{-}width}\), as used by the preceding theorem, gives
\[
 W_n(x)=B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x).
\]
Its pervasive-width conclusion therefore yields
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}}
 \mathbb E_P\left[\sup_{x\in\mathcal B}W_n(x)\right]
 \le C r_{n,\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}.
\]

For the last clause, let \(A_j=\{\tau_{P_j}(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\}\). Coverage and total variation give
\[
 P_0(A_0)\ge1-\gamma-\epsilon_n,
\]
and
\[
 P_0(A_1)\ge P_1(A_1)-\operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})
 \ge1-\gamma-\epsilon_n-\operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n}).
\]
The union bound consequently gives
\[
 P_0(A_0\cap A_1)
 \ge1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-\operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n}).
\]
On \(A_0\cap A_1\), the interval contains both target values, so \(W_n(x)\ge|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|/2\). Multiplication by the positive part of the probability lower bound and expectation under \(P_0\) prove the displayed obstruction. These deductions establish exactly the three positive conclusions in the statement and do not invoke either explicitly excluded sharper assertion.
