The map \(z\mapsto d(z,x_j)\) is one-Lipschitz, so \(\psi_{j,h}=(1-d(\,\cdot\,,x_j)/h)_+\) is \(h^{-1}\)-Lipschitz. Therefore each perturbed side regression in \(\mathrm{def:fano\text{-}tent\text{-}family}\) is \(L/4\)-Lipschitz. Its absolute trace is at most \(Lh/4\le L\), and changing the outcome regressions leaves the score law, all local masses, and every packing profile unchanged. The Gaussian-error, sharp-assignment, and consistency conditions follow directly from the construction. The witness-membership lemma therefore places every pervasive perturbation in \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) and both isolated perturbations in \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\), for sufficiently small \(h\).

Maximality of the \(3h\)-separated set implies that its \(3h\)-arcs cover \(S^1\), while its \(3h/2\)-arcs are disjoint. Comparing their total arclength with \(2\pi\) yields
\[
 c h^{-1}\le |\Xi_h^{\mathrm{perv}}|\le C h^{-1}.
\]
Let \(a=Lh/4\). Conditional on \(X=z\), the Kullback--Leibler divergence between two full potential-outcome vectors in the pervasive family is
\[
 \frac{a^2}{\sigma^2}\{\psi_{j,h}(z)-\psi_{k,h}(z)\}^2.
\]
The integrand vanishes outside \(B(x_j,h)\cup B(x_k,h)\). The local-coordinate calculation in the witness-membership proof gives
\[
 \int_{B(x_j,h)} f_\kappa^{\mathrm{perv}}(z)\,dz\le Ch^\kappa,
\]
and the corresponding polar calculation at \(x_\star\) gives \(\int_{B(x_\star,h)}f_\kappa^{\mathrm{iso}}(z)\,dz\le Ch^\kappa\). Hence, in either family, the one-observation divergence is at most \(CL^2h^{\kappa+2}/\sigma^2\). Independent sampling makes product divergences additive, proving the claimed \(CnL^2h^{\kappa+2}/\sigma^2\) bound. The observed-data divergence is no larger, because marginalization cannot increase the log-sum expression defining relative entropy.
