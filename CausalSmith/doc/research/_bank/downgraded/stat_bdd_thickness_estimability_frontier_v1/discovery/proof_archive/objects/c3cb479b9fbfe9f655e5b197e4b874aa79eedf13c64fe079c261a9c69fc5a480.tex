Fix \(0<h\le h_0/6\).  A maximal \(3h\)-separated subset of the unit
circle is a \(3h\)-net.  Comparing disjoint arcs of chordal radius
\(3h/2\) and covering arcs of chordal radius \(3h\) with the circle's
total arclength gives constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) such that
\[
 c h^{-1}\le |\Xi_h^{\mathrm{perv}}|\le C h^{-1}.
 \tag{11}
\]

The score law of every pervasive alternative in
\(\mathrm{def:fano\text{-}tent\text{-}family}\) is that of
\(P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv},0}\).  Thus all score-dependent member
conditions proved in \(\mathrm{lem:circular\text{-}witness\text{-}membership}\)
are unchanged.  Since \(z\mapsto(1-d(z,x_j)/h)_+\) is
\(h^{-1}\)-Lipschitz, each displayed side regression has Lipschitz
constant at most \(L/4\).  Moreover \(h<h_0<1\), so its absolute value
and its boundary trace are at most \(Lh/4<L\).  Conditional on \(X=z\),
the observed response under alternative \(j\) is Gaussian with variance
\(\sigma^2\) and mean
\[
 \mu_j(z)=\frac{Lh}{4}\{\mathbf1_{\mathcal A_1}(z)
              -\mathbf1_{\mathcal A_0}(z)\}\psi_{j,h}(z).
 \tag{12}
\]
After subtracting the side mean, the same regular conditional kernel has
the centered \(\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)\) moment generating function.
Sharp assignment and consistency hold by construction.  Hence every
pervasive alternative belongs to
\(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\).  The identical argument with
the isolated score law and \(x_1=x_{\star}\), using the isolated
membership conclusion of the circular-witness lemma, puts both isolated
laws in \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\).

We next derive the information bound rather than treating it as part of
the construction.  If two normal distributions have common variance
\(\sigma^2\) and means \(a,b\), direct integration of their log-density
ratio gives Kullback--Leibler divergence \((a-b)^2/(2\sigma^2)\).
Conditioning on \(X\) and using that all alternatives have the same score
law therefore gives
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(\mathbb P_{\kappa,j,h}^{\mathrm{perv}},
                    \mathbb P_{\kappa,k,h}^{\mathrm{perv}})
 =\frac{L^2h^2}{32\sigma^2}
   \int(\psi_{j,h}-\psi_{k,h})^2 f_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}.
 \tag{13}
\]
For \(j\ne k\), the \(3h\)-separation makes the radius-\(h\) tent
supports disjoint; the same upper bound below also covers \(j=0\) or
\(k=0\).  Since \(0\le\psi_{j,h}\le1\), the integral in (13) is at most
the pervasive score mass of the union of two radius-\(h\) balls.  The
two one-sided bounds in the circular-witness lemma make this at most
\(C h^{\kappa}\).  Thus
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(\mathbb P_{\kappa,j,h}^{\mathrm{perv}},
                    \mathbb P_{\kappa,k,h}^{\mathrm{perv}})
 \le C L^2h^{\kappa+2}/\sigma^2.
 \tag{14}
\]
For the displayed \(n\)-fold product laws, the product density ratio
factorizes, so its logarithm is the sum of the one-observation log density
ratios.  Taking expectations proves
\[
 \operatorname{KL}((\mathbb P_{\kappa,j,h}^{\mathrm{perv}})^{\otimes n},
                    (\mathbb P_{\kappa,k,h}^{\mathrm{perv}})^{\otimes n})
 \le CnL^2h^{\kappa+2}/\sigma^2.
 \tag{15}
\]
For the isolated pair, the same calculation uses
\(q_{t,P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso},0}}(x_{\star},h)\le Ch^{\kappa}\)
from the circular-witness lemma and yields
\[
 \operatorname{KL}((\mathbb P_{\kappa,1,h}^{\mathrm{iso}})^{\otimes n},
                    (\mathbb P_{\kappa,0,h}^{\mathrm{iso}})^{\otimes n})
 \le CnL^2h^{\kappa+2}/\sigma^2.
 \tag{16}
\]

The tents are continuous, so the trace definition and the side-regression
formulas in \(\mathrm{def:fano\text{-}tent\text{-}family}\) give, at an
active center \(x_j\),
\[
 \theta_{1,P}(x_j)=Lh/4,
 \qquad \theta_{0,P}(x_j)=-Lh/4,
 \qquad \tau_P(x_j)=Lh/2.
 \tag{17}
\]
Equations (11), (15), (16), and (17) prove every asserted property.
