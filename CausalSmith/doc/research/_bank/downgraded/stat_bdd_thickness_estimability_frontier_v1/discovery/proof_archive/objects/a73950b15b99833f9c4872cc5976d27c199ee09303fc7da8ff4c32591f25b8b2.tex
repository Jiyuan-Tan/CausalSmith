Put \(a=Lh/4\).  The reverse triangle inequality makes \(z\mapsto d(z,x_j)\) one-Lipschitz, so \(\psi_{j,h}=(1-d(z,x_j)/h)_+\) is \(h^{-1}\)-Lipschitz and each perturbed side regression in \(\mathrm{def:fano\text{-}tent\text{-}family}\) is \(L/4\)-Lipschitz.  Its absolute trace is at most \(Lh/4\le L\) for sufficiently small \(h\).  The perturbation changes neither the score law nor any local-mass or packing property, and its residual about the new observed conditional mean is exactly \(\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)\), hence satisfies the conditional kernel moment-generating-function bound in \(\mathrm{ass:gaussian\text{-}errors}\).  Sharp assignment and consistency hold by the construction.  Therefore \(\mathrm{lem:circular\text{-}witness\text{-}membership}\) and the two subclass definitions put every pervasive perturbation in \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv}}\) and both isolated perturbations in \(\mathcal P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso}}\).

Maximality of the \(3h\)-separated set implies that arcs of Euclidean radius \(3h\) about its points cover \(S^1\), whereas arcs of radius \(3h/2\) are disjoint.  For sufficiently small \(h\), each such arc has arclength bounded above and below by fixed multiples of its Euclidean radius.  Comparing total arclength with \(2\pi\) gives
\[
 c h^{-1}\le |\Xi_h^{\mathrm{perv}}|\le C h^{-1}. \tag{1}
\]

Conditional on \(X=z\), two Gaussian coordinates with common variance \(\sigma^2\) and means \(\mu,\nu\) have relative entropy
\[
 \operatorname{KL}\{\mathcal N(\mu,\sigma^2),\mathcal N(\nu,\sigma^2)\}
 =\frac{(\mu-\nu)^2}{2\sigma^2}, \tag{2}
\]
as follows by integrating their log likelihood ratio.  For alternatives \(j,k\), the mean difference vanishes outside \(B(x_j,h)\cup B(x_k,h)\) and is bounded in absolute value by \(a\) on each potential-outcome coordinate.  The local-mass calculations certified by \(\mathrm{lem:circular\text{-}witness\text{-}membership}\) give
\[
 P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{perv},0}\{X\in B(x_j,h)\}\le Ch^\kappa,
 \qquad
 P_{\kappa}^{\mathrm{iso},0}\{X\in B(x_{\star},h)\}\le Ch^\kappa. \tag{3}
\]
Conditioning on \(X\), applying (2) to the independent Gaussian coordinates, and using (3) yields in either family
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(\mathbb P_{\kappa,j,h},\mathbb P_{\kappa,k,h})
 \le \frac{Ca^2h^\kappa}{\sigma^2}
 \le \frac{CL^2h^{\kappa+2}}{\sigma^2}. \tag{4}
\]
The observed-data divergence is no larger by marginalization.  Under \(\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}sampling}\), likelihood ratios factor, and Fubini's theorem gives additivity of relative entropy for the product experiments.  Hence
\[
 \operatorname{KL}(\mathbb P_{\kappa,j,h}^{\otimes n},
 \mathbb P_{\kappa,k,h}^{\otimes n})
 \le \frac{CnL^2h^{\kappa+2}}{\sigma^2}. \tag{5}
\]
Finally, \(\psi_{j,h}(x_j)=1\).  The trace formulas in \(\mathrm{def:fano\text{-}tent\text{-}family}\) therefore give
\[
 \theta_{1,P}(x_j)=\frac{Lh}{4},\qquad
 \theta_{0,P}(x_j)=-\frac{Lh}{4},\qquad
 \tau_P(x_j)=\frac{Lh}{2}.
\]
Together with (1) and (5), these are all the asserted properties.
