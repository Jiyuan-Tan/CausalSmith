Suppose \(\mathcal A_0=\mathbb R^2\setminus\mathcal A_1\). Put \(M_n=2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj\), let \(N=N_{t,n}(x,\widehat h_{t,n}(x))\), and define
\[
 B_{t,n}(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 L\widehat h_{t,n}(x)+\sigma\sqrt{2\log(2M_n/\gamma)/N},&N>0,\\
 L,&N=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Then the explicit band
\[
 \mathcal C_n(x)=
 \left[\widehat\tau_n(x)-B_{0,n}(x)-B_{1,n}(x),\ 
 \widehat\tau_n(x)+B_{0,n}(x)+B_{1,n}(x)\right]
\]
is data measurable and, for every declared nonempty triangular union \(\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)\), satisfies the finite-sample guarantee
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n(\Lambda_n)}P\{\tau_P(x)\in\mathcal C_n(x)\text{ for every }x\in\mathcal B\}\ge1-\gamma.
\]
In particular, uniformly over \(\mathcal P_\kappa^{\mathrm{perv}}\), \(\mathbb E_P\sup_x W_n(x)\le C(\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}\) for all sufficiently large \(n\). Moreover, for any \(\epsilon_n\ge0\), every band with simultaneous coverage at least \(1-\gamma-\epsilon_n\) over a class containing two laws \(P_0,P_1\) obeys, for every \(x\in\mathcal B\),
\[
 \mathbb E_{P_0}W_n(x)\ge\frac{|\tau_{P_1}(x)-\tau_{P_0}(x)|}{2}
 \left[1-2\gamma-2\epsilon_n-
 \operatorname{TV}(P_0^{\otimes n},P_1^{\otimes n})\right]_+.
\]
Thus the frozen assumptions yield an explicit honest global band and a computable two-law width obstruction. They do not by themselves establish the sharper uniform ratio to \(r_{n,P}^{\mathrm{loc}}(x)\): the global calibration uses \(\log M_n\asymp\log n\), whereas \(\log\{eK_P(x,h)/\gamma\}\) can be bounded at an isolated thin site. Nor does the displayed two-law argument turn score-law total variation alone into an exact necessary-and-sufficient adaptation criterion without a same-class outcome perturbation whose full observed-law distance is controlled. This is a partial resolution of the honest-adaptation question.
