For a disk with center \(c\in\mathbb R^2\) and radius \(r\), membership of a fixed point \(x_i\) is equivalent to
\[
 2c^{\mathsf T}x_i+s\ge\|x_i\|_2^2,
 \qquad s=r^2-\|c\|_2^2.
\]
Thus every disk trace is a sign pattern induced by \(n\) affine planes in the three-dimensional parameter \((c_1,c_2,s)\).  A trace realized by a closed disk can be made strict by increasing its radius by less than the positive minimum distance to the finitely many excluded points, so boundary equalities add no patterns.

Let \(A(n,v)\) be the largest number of open cells cut out by \(n\) affine hyperplanes in \(\mathbb R^v\).  Adding the last hyperplane preserves every old cell and splits at most \(A(n-1,v-1)\) cells, because the induced arrangement on that hyperplane has dimension \(v-1\).  Hence
\[
 A(n,v)\le A(n-1,v)+A(n-1,v-1),
 \qquad A(0,v)=A(n,0)=1.
\]
Induction using Pascal's identity gives
\[
 A(n,3)\le\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj. \tag{39}
\]
Restricting the parameter to genuine disks, fixing a radius, restricting centers to \(\mathcal B\), or intersecting every range with the fixed set of indices satisfying \(X_i\in\mathcal A_t\) cannot increase the number of traces.  Multiplying (39) by the two sides and by \(|\mathcal H_n|\) proves
\[
 |\mathscr S_n|\le2|\mathcal H_n|\sum_{j=0}^3\binom nj.
\]

By \(\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}sampling}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:gaussian\text{-}errors}\), conditionally on the scores, the residuals occurring in any member of \(\mathscr S_n\) are independent \(\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)\).  Consequently, for each nonempty \(S\),
\[
 \frac{\sum_{i\in S}\varepsilon_i}{\sigma\sqrt{|S|}}\sim\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
For \(Z\sim\mathcal N(0,1)\), completing the square in the exponential-moment bound gives \(\Pr(|Z|>z)\le2e^{-z^2/2}\).  A union bound, which does not require independence between the different set-indexed sums, therefore gives
\[
 \Pr\left\{\max_{S\in\mathscr S_n}\frac{|\sum_{i\in S}\varepsilon_i|}{\sigma\sqrt{|S|}}>z\mid X_1,\ldots,X_n\right\}
 \le2|\mathscr S_n|e^{-z^2/2}.
\]
Finally, integrating this tail from \(z_0=\sqrt{2\log\{2|\mathscr S_n|\}}\), and using \(\int_{z_0}^{\infty}e^{-z^2/2}\,dz\le z_0^{-1}e^{-z_0^2/2}\) when \(z_0>0\), bounds the conditional expectation by \(z_0+1\), as claimed.
