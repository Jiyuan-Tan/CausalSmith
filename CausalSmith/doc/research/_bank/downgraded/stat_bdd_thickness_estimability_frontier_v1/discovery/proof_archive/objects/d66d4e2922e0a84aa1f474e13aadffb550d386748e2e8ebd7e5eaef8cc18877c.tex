The causal target is the difference of the unique Lipschitz traces of the two observed-side regression classes on \(\operatorname{supp}(P_X|_{\mathcal A_t})\), hence an observed-law functional even at null boundary points. A scalar mass order does not determine expected-supremum difficulty: isolated and pervasive polynomial thinning have rates \(n^{-1/(\kappa+2)}\) and \((\log n/n)^{1/(\kappa+2)}\). A count-qualified estimator attains the pervasive estimation frontier without knowing \(\kappa\). The explicit confidence band has finite-sample simultaneous coverage on every declared nonempty triangular union; whenever the pervasive class is nonempty, its global expected-supremum half-width is matched for every fixed \(\gamma\in(0,1)\) on any admissible complementary geometry. A full-observed-law two-point inequality and a geometry-general pervasive pointwise-ratio converse are proved, while uniform local-oracle width adaptation and an exact score-law criterion remain open.
