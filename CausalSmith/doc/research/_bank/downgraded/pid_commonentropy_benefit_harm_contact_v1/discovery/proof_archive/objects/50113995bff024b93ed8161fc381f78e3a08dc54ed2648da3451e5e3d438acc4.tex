On an interior multinomial law, \(\sqrt N(\widehat P_N-P)\) has the usual centered Gaussian limit with covariance \(\operatorname{diag}(p)-pp^{\mathsf T}\).  If, in addition, the radial map were proved uniformly Hadamard directionally differentiable with derivative \(\dot\rho_{P,\kappa}(v;\cdot)\), and if radial reconstruction were locally exact uniformly in \(v\), the directional delta method would give the conditional conclusion
\[
 \sqrt N\,d_{\mathrm H}\bigl(\Theta_\kappa(\widehat P_N),\Theta_\kappa(P)\bigr)
 \ \Longrightarrow\ 
 \sup_{v\in\mathbb S^1}\left|\dot\rho_{P,\kappa}(v;Z)\right|,
\]
where \(Z\) is that Gaussian vector.  Neither differentiability premise follows from the frozen core, and at a disappearing mass the term \(-x\log x\) is not differentiable at zero, so this conditional regular-stratum calculation does not determine the contact normalization or a uniformly valid resampling law.
