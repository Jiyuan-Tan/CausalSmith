The entropy-nonincreasing six-state compression recorded for \(\texttt{thm:three-state-reduction}\) makes the attainable set the continuous image of a compact finite-dimensional feasible set and therefore compact.  Consequently, finite inner point nets and outer unions of balls with arbitrarily small Hausdorff gap exist abstractly.  Moreover, any compact parameter box disjoint from the feasible fiber has a strictly positive minimum of the continuous feasibility residual.  These compactness facts do not provide a finite procedure that finds exact feasible net points, exact witness sections over included positive-dimensional cells, or positive residual bounds uniformly at equality and support-changing strata.
