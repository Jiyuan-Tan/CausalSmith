Let \(R=(Y(0),Y(1))\). The proof uses a support-pattern obstruction for a mixture of product distributions and then embeds it in the causal response-type program. Define the balanced reverse-channel law \(P^\dagger\) by
\[
p^\dagger_{00}=p^\dagger_{11}=\frac1{20},
\qquad
p^\dagger_{01}=p^\dagger_{10}=\frac9{20},
\tag{1}
\]
and put
\[
\theta^\dagger=\left(0,\frac9{10}\right),
\qquad
\kappa^\dagger=h\!\left(\frac9{10}\right)+\frac1{10}\log2.
\tag{2}
\]
Every summand in (2) is nonnegative and at least one is positive, so \(\kappa^\dagger>0\). Moreover,
\[
10\kappa^\dagger
=9\log\frac{10}{9}+\log20
=\log\!\left(20\left(\frac{10}{9}\right)^9\right).
\tag{3}
\]
The exact integer inequality
\[
20\cdot10^9=20{,}000{,}000{,}000
<396{,}718{,}580{,}736=2^{10}9^9
\]
and strict monotonicity of the logarithm imply from (3) that \(10\kappa^\dagger<10\log2\). Hence \(0<\kappa^\dagger<\log2\), as required by the declared budget range.

First construct a three-state witness. Index its states by \(00,10,11\), and take
\[
(\pi_{00},\pi_{10},\pi_{11})
=\left(\frac1{20},\frac9{10},\frac1{20}\right),
\qquad
(e_{00},e_{10},e_{11})=\left(0,\frac12,1\right),
\tag{4}
\]
with the response distribution at state \(ij\) degenerate on response type \((i,j)\). Equivalently, take \(W=R\). Conditional on \(W\), the response type is constant, so \(A\perp R\mid W\). Under the causal completion \(Y=R_A\), the state \(00\) contributes mass \(1/20\) to factual cell \((A,Y)=(0,0)\), state \(10\) contributes masses \(9/20\) to cells \((0,1)\) and \((1,0)\), and state \(11\) contributes mass \(1/20\) to cell \((1,1)\). Thus all four factual cells equal (1). The benefit and harm coordinates are
\[
B=0,
\qquad
H=\pi_{10}=\frac9{10},
\tag{5}
\]
and the latent entropy is
\[
-2\frac1{20}\log\frac1{20}
-\frac9{10}\log\frac9{10}
=h\!\left(\frac9{10}\right)+\frac1{10}\log2
=\kappa^\dagger.
\tag{6}
\]
Equations (4)--(6), or directly \(\texttt{lem:factor-program-equivalence}\), therefore show
\[
\theta^\dagger\in
\Phi_3(\mathcal F_{3,\kappa^\dagger}(P^\dagger)).
\tag{7}
\]

We next prove that no two-state witness is possible at this budget. Consider any factual-compatible law with the coordinates in (2), and let \(J\) be its joint law of \((A,R)\). Since all response-type masses are nonnegative, \(B=0\) and \(H=9/10\) imply
\[
J(R=01)=0,
\qquad J(R=10)=\frac9{10},
\qquad J(R=00)+J(R=11)=\frac1{10}.
\tag{8}
\]
Consistency and the two diagonal factual cells give
\[
J(0,00)=p^\dagger_{00}=\frac1{20},
\qquad
J(1,11)=p^\dagger_{11}=\frac1{20}.
\tag{9}
\]
The two quantities in (9) already sum to the last quantity in (8). Nonnegativity therefore forces
\[
J(1,00)=J(0,11)=0.
\tag{10}
\]
Using the remaining factual cells and (10) now gives
\[
J(0,10)=p^\dagger_{01}=\frac9{20},
\qquad
J(1,10)=p^\dagger_{10}=\frac9{20}.
\tag{11}
\]
Consequently, (8)--(11) force the complete joint table
\[
J(0,00)=\frac1{20},\quad
J(0,10)=\frac9{20},\quad
J(1,10)=\frac9{20},\quad
J(1,11)=\frac1{20},
\tag{12}
\]
with every other cell equal to zero.

Suppose, toward a contradiction, that (12) has an exact product-mixture factorization with at most two positive-mass states. Write the state masses as \(\lambda_w\), the propensities as \(e_w\), and the conditional response probabilities as \(s_w(r)\). Positivity of \(J(0,00)\) produces a state \(w_0\) with \(\lambda_{w_0}s_{w_0}(00)>0\). Since
\[
0=J(1,00)=\sum_w\lambda_w e_w s_w(00),
\]
and every summand is nonnegative, \(e_{w_0}=0\). Likewise, positivity of \(J(1,11)\) produces a state \(w_1\) with \(\lambda_{w_1}s_{w_1}(11)>0\), while
\[
0=J(0,11)=\sum_w\lambda_w(1-e_w)s_w(11)
\]
forces \(e_{w_1}=1\). Thus \(w_0\neq w_1\). With at most two positive states these are all the states, and \(W\) determines \(A\). The observed law (1) is balanced, so
\[
\lambda_{w_0}=P^\dagger(A=0)=\frac12,
\qquad
\lambda_{w_1}=P^\dagger(A=1)=\frac12.
\]
Every such two-state factorization consequently has entropy
\[
-\frac12\log\frac12-\frac12\log\frac12=\log2
>\kappa^\dagger,
\tag{13}
\]
contradicting its membership in \(\mathcal F_{2,\kappa^\dagger}(P^\dagger)\). Hence
\[
\theta^\dagger\notin
\Phi_2(\mathcal F_{2,\kappa^\dagger}(P^\dagger)).
\tag{14}
\]

Finally, every two-state feasible tuple can be padded by a third zero-mass state; this changes neither its entropy, its factual cells, nor its benefit--harm image. Therefore
\[
\Phi_2(\mathcal F_{2,\kappa^\dagger}(P^\dagger))
\subseteq
\Phi_3(\mathcal F_{3,\kappa^\dagger}(P^\dagger)),
\]
and (7), (14) make the inclusion strict. The zero-pattern argument is the causal response-type embedding of the product-mixture support obstruction in \(\citet[Section V, Example 2 and Appendix A]{KumarLiElGamal2014}\); the information-theoretic obstruction is not claimed as new here, while the factual-compatible benefit--harm embedding has been verified explicitly above.
