Let the auxiliary range over every finite alphabet. Define
\[
G_P(A;Y)=\inf_{\substack{m\geq1,\ \widetilde P\in\mathcal P(\mathcal A\times\mathcal Y\times\{1,\ldots,m\}):\\ \widetilde P_{A,Y}=P,\ A\perp_{\widetilde P}Y\mid U}}
\left\{-\sum_{u=1}^m\widetilde P(U=u)\log\widetilde P(U=u)\right\}.
\]
For binary \(A\) and \(Y\), the binary specialization of the finite-cardinality theorem permits the convention \(1\leq m\leq2\) without changing the value; the resulting finite union of compact feasible sets attains the infimum, so \(G_P(A;Y)\) is a minimum. Zero-mass auxiliary states are allowed, with \(0\log0=0\).
