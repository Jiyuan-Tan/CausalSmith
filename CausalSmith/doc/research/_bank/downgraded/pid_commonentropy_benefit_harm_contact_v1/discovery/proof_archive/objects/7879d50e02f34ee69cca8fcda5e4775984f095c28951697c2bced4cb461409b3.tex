The technique is an exact product-mixture reduction followed by causal completion.  We prove necessity and sufficiency separately.

For necessity, suppose \((0,0)\in\Theta_\kappa(P)\), and choose its witness \(Q\in\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\).  Since the four response-type probabilities are nonnegative,
\[
 Q\{Y(0)\neq Y(1)\}=B(Q)+H(Q)=0.
\]
Consequently there is a binary random variable \(V\) such that \(V=Y(0)=Y(1)\) almost surely.  Conditional exchangeability gives \(A\perp V\mid U\), while consistency gives \(Y=V\) almost surely.  Therefore \(A\perp Y\mid U\).  Factual compatibility and the entropy budget show that the \((A,Y,U)\)-marginal of \(Q\) is admissible in \(\texttt{def:common-entropy}\), whence
\[
 G_P(A;Y)\leq \operatorname{Ent}_Q(U)\leq\kappa.
\]

For sufficiency, suppose \(G_P(A;Y)\leq\kappa\).  By \(\texttt{lem:binary-common-entropy-cardinality}\), it is enough to use at most three auxiliary states.  The corresponding parameter space
\[
 \Delta_3\times[0,1]^3\times[0,1]^3
\]
for the masses and the two binary conditional probabilities is compact; the product-mixture map and Shannon entropy, extended by \(-0\log0=0\), are continuous.  The fiber over \(P\) is closed and nonempty, for example by taking \(U=A\).  Hence the infimum is attained by a product-mixture extension \(\widetilde P\) with at most three states and entropy \(G_P(A;Y)\).

For each positive-mass state define
\[
 e_u=\widetilde P(A=1\mid U=u),\qquad
 s_u=\widetilde P(Y=1\mid U=u),
\]
and set
\[
 r_{00\mid u}=1-s_u,qquad r_{11\mid u}=s_u,qquad
 r_{01\mid u}=r_{10\mid u}=0.
\]
Choose arbitrary conditional values at zero-mass states.  Conditional independence under \(\widetilde P\) makes the factual equations in \(\mathcal F_{3,\kappa}(P)\) hold, and the entropy is at most \(\kappa\).  Thus \(\Phi_3(\pi,e,r)=(0,0)\).  The constructive reverse direction of \(\texttt{lem:factor-program-equivalence}\) supplies a law \(Q\in\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\) with \(Y(0)=Y(1)\) almost surely.  Hence \((0,0)\in\Theta_\kappa(P)\), completing the equivalence and the support-three causal completion.
