The proof uses Fr\'echet response-type algebra at zero budget and an explicit factual-cell split at high budget.

First set \(\kappa=0\) and take \(Q\in\mathfrak M_0(P)\). By \(\texttt{ass:entropy-budget}\), \(\operatorname{Ent}_Q(U)\leq0\). Every summand \(-Q(U=u)\log Q(U=u)\) is nonnegative, so the finite-alphabet entropy can vanish only when exactly one latent state has probability one. Hence \(U\) is \(Q\)-almost surely constant. The conditional independence in \(\texttt{ass:latent-exchangeability}\) therefore becomes
\[
A\perp (Y(0),Y(1)).
\]
Treatment positivity ensures that both \(\mu_0\) and \(\mu_1\) are defined. For each \(a\in\{0,1\}\), independence, consistency, and factual compatibility give
\[
Q\{Y(a)=1\}
=Q\{Y(a)=1\mid A=a\}
=Q(Y=1\mid A=a)
=\mu_a.
\]
Write \(x=Q\{Y(0)=1,Y(1)=1\}\). The four response-type probabilities must then be
\[
r_{11}=x,\qquad
r_{01}=\mu_1-x,\qquad
r_{10}=\mu_0-x,\qquad
r_{00}=1-\mu_0-\mu_1+x.
\]
Their nonnegativity is equivalent to
\[
\max\{0,\mu_0+\mu_1-1\}\leq x\leq\min\{\mu_0,\mu_1\}.
\]
Since \(B(Q)=r_{01}\) and \(H(Q)=r_{10}\), it follows that
\[
B(Q)-H(Q)=\mu_1-\mu_0,
\]
\[
0\leq B(Q)\leq\min\{\mu_1,1-\mu_0\},\qquad
0\leq H(Q)\leq\min\{\mu_0,1-\mu_1\}.
\]
By \(\texttt{def:zero-budget-segment}\), this proves \(\Theta_0(P)\subseteq\mathcal S_0(P)\).

Conversely, fix \((b,h)\in\mathcal S_0(P)\) and define a response-type law by
\[
r_{01}=b,\qquad r_{10}=h,\qquad
r_{11}=\mu_1-b=\mu_0-h,\qquad
r_{00}=1-\mu_0-b=1-\mu_1-h.
\]
The equalities follow from \(b-h=\mu_1-\mu_0\). The coordinate bounds in \(\texttt{def:zero-budget-segment}\) make every displayed probability nonnegative, and direct summation gives \(\sum_{i,j}r_{ij}=1\). Let \(U\) be constant, draw \((Y(0),Y(1))\) from this law, draw \(A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p_1)\) independently of the response type, and set \(Y=Y(A)\). Then \(A\perp(Y(0),Y(1))\mid U\), \(\operatorname{Ent}_Q(U)=0\), and the two response margins are
\[
Q\{Y(0)=1\}=r_{10}+r_{11}=\mu_0,
\qquad
Q\{Y(1)=1\}=r_{01}+r_{11}=\mu_1.
\]
Independence and consistency therefore reproduce \(P(A=a,Y=y)=p_{ay}\) for all \(a,y\). This constructs a member of \(\mathfrak M_0(P)\) with \((B(Q),H(Q))=(b,h)\). Thus \(\mathcal S_0(P)\subseteq\Theta_0(P)\), and
\[
\Theta_0(P)=\mathcal S_0(P).
\]
This is sharp because every point of the segment has just been attained by a Fr\'echet coupling of the two Bernoulli margins.

We next prove the high-budget assertion. For any \(Q\in\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\), split the benefit event according to treatment and use consistency and factual compatibility:
\[
\begin{aligned}
B(Q)
&=Q\{A=0,Y(0)=0,Y(1)=1\}
  +Q\{A=1,Y(0)=0,Y(1)=1\}\\
&\leq Q(A=0,Y=0)+Q(A=1,Y=1)\\
&=p_{00}+p_{11}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The same argument for harm gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
H(Q)
&=Q\{A=0,Y(0)=1,Y(1)=0\}
  +Q\{A=1,Y(0)=1,Y(1)=0\}\\
&\leq Q(A=0,Y=1)+Q(A=1,Y=0)\\
&=p_{01}+p_{10}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Because both coordinates are probabilities, \(\Theta_\kappa(P)\subseteq\mathcal R(P)\) for every \(\kappa\).

For the reverse inclusion, fix \((b,h)\in\mathcal R(P)\) and put
\[
b_0=\min\{b,p_{00}\},\qquad b_1=b-b_0,
\qquad
h_0=\min\{h,p_{01}\},\qquad h_1=h-h_0.
\]
The rectangle restrictions imply
\[
0\leq b_0\leq p_{00},\quad 0\leq b_1\leq p_{11},
\qquad
0\leq h_0\leq p_{01},\quad 0\leq h_1\leq p_{10}.
\]
Indeed, if \(b>p_{00}\), then \(b_1=b-p_{00}\leq p_{11}\), while otherwise \(b_1=0\); the argument for \(h_1\) is identical. Define the joint masses of \((A,Y(0),Y(1))\), with columns indexed by the response types \(00,01,10,11\), by
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 &00&01&10&11\\ \hline
A=0&p_{00}-b_0&b_0&h_0&p_{01}-h_0\\
A=1&p_{10}-h_1&b_1&h_1&p_{11}-b_1.
\end{array}
\]
All entries are nonnegative, and their sum is \(\sum_{a,y}p_{ay}=1\). Set \(Y=Y(A)\) and \(U=A\). In the row \(A=0\), the masses of response types with \(Y(0)=0\) and \(Y(0)=1\) are respectively \(p_{00}\) and \(p_{01}\); in the row \(A=1\), the masses with \(Y(1)=0\) and \(Y(1)=1\) are respectively \(p_{10}\) and \(p_{11}\). Thus consistency produces exactly the factual law \(P\). Moreover,
\[
B(Q)=b_0+b_1=b,
\qquad
H(Q)=h_0+h_1=h.
\]
Conditional on \(U\), treatment is deterministic, so \(A\perp(Y(0),Y(1))\mid U\), including when some displayed masses vanish. Finally,
\[
\operatorname{Ent}_Q(U)=h\bigl(Q(A=1)\bigr)=h(p_1).
\]
Hence this law belongs to \(\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\) whenever \(\kappa\geq h(p_1)\). It follows that \(\mathcal R(P)\subseteq\Theta_\kappa(P)\) in that regime. Combining the inclusions proves
\[
\Theta_\kappa(P)=\mathcal R(P)\qquad\text{for every }\kappa\geq h(p_1).
\]
The construction attains every pair in the rectangle simultaneously, so this is a sharp joint set and its coordinate projections are the sharp factual benefit and harm intervals.
