The no-individual-effect point obeys the exact equivalence
\[
(0,0)\in\Theta_\kappa(P)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad G_P(A;Y)\leq\kappa.
\]
Here \(G_P(A;Y)\) optimizes over all finite auxiliary alphabets. When these equivalent conditions hold, its minimum is attained by an exact product-mixture factor with at most two positive latent states, and the explicit diagonal completion gives a causal witness \(Q\in\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\) whose \(U\) also has at most two positive states and for which \(B(Q)=H(Q)=0\) and \(Y(0)=Y(1)\) almost surely. Conversely, every causal origin witness induces a feasible product-mixture factor with the same latent entropy.
