The fixed support-three representation reduces the problem to sensitivity of one compact constrained image, but it does not make that image regular.  On a full-support law, write \(p_{a+}=p_{a0}+p_{a1}\) and let \(z=(z_{ay})\) be a tangent direction with \(\sum_{a,y}z_{ay}=0\).  Direct differentiation of \(\mu_a=p_{a1}/p_{a+}\) gives
\[
\dot\mu_a(P;z)=\frac{z_{a1}p_{a+}-p_{a1}(z_{a0}+z_{a1})}{p_{a+}^2}.
\tag{1}
\]
Put \(\delta=\mu_1-\mu_0\), \(\ell=\max\{0,\delta\}\), and \(u=\min\{\mu_1,1-\mu_0\}\).  The midpoint definition of \(\theta_c(P)\) and \(\texttt{def:zero-budget-segment}\) give
\[
\theta_c(P)=\left(\frac{\ell+u}{2},\frac{\ell+u}{2}-\delta\right).
\tag{2}
\]
With \(\dot\delta=\dot\mu_1-\dot\mu_0\), the elementary directional derivatives are
\[
\dot\ell=
\begin{cases}
\dot\delta,&\delta>0,\\
0,&\delta<0,\\
\max\{0,\dot\delta\},&\delta=0,
\end{cases}
\qquad
\dot u=
\begin{cases}
\dot\mu_1,&\mu_0+\mu_1<1,\\
-\dot\mu_0,&\mu_0+\mu_1>1,\\
\min\{\dot\mu_1,-\dot\mu_0\},&\mu_0+\mu_1=1.
\end{cases}
\tag{3}
\]
Therefore
\[
\dot\theta_c(P;z)=
\left(\frac{\dot\ell+\dot u}{2},
\frac{\dot\ell+\dot u}{2}-\dot\delta\right).
\tag{4}
\]
If the joint map \(P\mapsto(\theta_c(P),\rho_\kappa(\cdot;P))\) and the radial reconstruction map are proved uniformly Hadamard directionally differentiable, the fixed-direction boundary perturbation is necessarily
\[
\dot\theta_c(P;z)+\dot\rho_{P,\kappa}(v;z)v,
\tag{5}
\]
not merely \(\dot\rho_{P,\kappa}(v;z)v\).  For multinomial sampling, \(\sqrt N(\widehat P_N-P)\) has the usual centered Gaussian limit \(Z\) with covariance \(\operatorname{diag}(p)-pp^{\mathsf T}\), but (5) alone does not identify the Hausdorff derivative: matching points across directions is part of the unproved radial reconstruction derivative.  In particular, the Hausdorff limit cannot be asserted to be \(\sup_v|\dot\rho_{P,\kappa}(v;Z)|\).  At a disappearing latent mass, the right derivative of \(-x\log x\) is unbounded, so neither the contact normalization nor a resampling rule uniform over support changes follows from the regular calculation.
