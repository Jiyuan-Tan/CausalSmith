Assume treatment positivity, (0<p_1<1), as required by (  exttt{ass:treatment-positivity}), and fix (kappain[0,log 2]).  (No division by (p_1) is needed below, so the reduction itself in fact remains valid at the treatment-boundary laws.)  We prove the two set inclusions.

First take (  heta=(b,h)inTheta_kappa(P)).  By (  exttt{def:identified-set}), there is a law (Qinmathfrak M_kappa(P)) such that
\[
b=B(Q),qquad h=H(Q).
\]
Put (R=(Y(0),Y(1))), whose alphabet is the four-element set (mathcal T={0,1}^2), and let (J=Q_{A,R}).  By (  exttt{def:model-fiber}), (  exttt{ass:latent-exchangeability}), and (  exttt{ass:entropy-budget}), the original finite auxiliary (U) satisfies
\[
A\perp Rmid U,qquad operatorname{Ent}_Q(U)leqkappa.
\]
Thus (U) is an exact product-mixture factor for the fixed joint law (J).  Define, only for this proof,
\[
G_J(A;R)
=
\inf_{\substack{\widetilde J_{A,R}=J,\ W\ {
m finite}:\\ A\perp_{\widetilde J}R\mid W}}
\operatorname{Ent}_{\widetilde J}(W).
\]
Using the extension induced by (Q) and (U) in this infimum gives
\[
G_J(A;R)leq operatorname{Ent}_Q(U)leqkappa.   ag{1}
\]
This is the fixed-joint reduction step: unlike a moment compression, it retains the complete law of ((A,R)).

Apply the published finite-alphabet common-entropy cardinality theorem (  exttt{lem:finite-common-entropy-cardinality}) to the alphabets (mathcal A={0,1}) and (mathcal T={0,1}^2).  It gives an attaining extension (J') of (J) and a factor (W) such that
\[
J'_{A,R}=J,qquad A\perp_{J'}Rmid W,qquad
|operatorname{supp}(W)|leq 2^{\min\{2,4\}}-1=3,qquad
\operatorname{Ent}_{J'}(W)=G_J(A;R).   ag{2}
\]
Combining (1) and (2) shows (operatorname{Ent}_{J'}(W)leqoperatorname{Ent}_Q(U)leqkappa).  This is entropy-minimizing product-mixture compression applied to a fixed complete joint law, not directly to the six displayed moments from the earlier partial argument.

We next give the causal reconstruction explicitly.  Index the support of (W) by at most three values and pad with zero-mass values if necessary.  For (w=1,2,3), define
\[
\pi_w=J'(W=w),qquad
e_w=J'(A=1mid W=w),qquad
r_{ijmid w}=J'(R=(i,j)mid W=w).
\]
When (pi_w=0), choose (e_win[0,1]) and (r_{cdotcdotmid w}inDelta_4) arbitrarily; all products containing that state then vanish.  Hence (piinDelta_3), (ein[0,1]^3), (r_{cdotcdotmid w}inDelta_4), and
\[
-\sum_{w=1}^3pi_wlogpi_w
=operatorname{Ent}_{J'}(W)leqkappa,   ag{3}
\]
where (0log0=0).  Conditional independence in (2), preservation of (J), consistency (  exttt{ass:consistency}), and factual compatibility (  exttt{ass:factual-compatibility}) give, for every (a,yin{0,1}),
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{w=1}^3\pi_w e_w^a(1-e_w)^{1-a}
 \sum_{i,j=0}^1 r_{ijmid w}
 \mathbf 1\{(1-a)i+aj=y\}\\

\sum_{\substack{i,j\in\{0,1\}:\\(1-a)i+aj=y}}

\sum_{\substack{i,j\in\{0,1\}:\\(1-a)i+aj=y}}

\end{aligned}
\]
Equations (3) and (4), together with the simplex conditions, are exactly the constraints of (  exttt{def:finite-factor-program}).  Therefore ((pi,e,r)inmathcal F_{3,kappa}(P)).  Moreover, because (J') preserves the full (J),
\[

&=\left(\sum_{w=1}^3\pi_w r_{01mid w},

&=\bigl(J(R=(0,1)),J(R=(1,0))\bigr)\\
&=(B(Q),H(Q))=(b,h)=\theta.   ag{5}

Equivalently, one can define the reconstructed full-data law directly by drawing ((A,R,W)sim J') and setting (Y=Y(A)); (2)--(4) verify conditional exchangeability, consistency, factual compatibility, and the entropy budget.  Thus (5) supplies the announced causal witness and proves
\[
\Theta_kappa(P)subseteqPhi_3(\mathcal F_{3,kappa}(P)).   ag{6}
\]

Conversely, (  exttt{lem:factor-program-equivalence}), applied with (m=3), constructs from every ((pi,e,r)inmathcal F_{3,kappa}(P)) a law in (mathfrak M_kappa(P)) with the same benefit--harm projection.  Hence

\Phi_3(\mathcal F_{3,kappa}(P))subseteqTheta_kappa(P).   ag{7}

The two constructive inclusions (6) and (7) prove the stated equality.  In the forward construction, (1)--(3) additionally prove that the new three-state factor has Shannon entropy no larger than that of the original witness.
