If the radial map is proved uniformly Hadamard directionally differentiable with derivative \(\dot\rho_{P,\kappa}(v;\cdot)\), and if the moving-center radial reconstruction is proved locally exact uniformly in \(v\), the directional delta method yields the conditional regular-stratum conclusion
\[
\sqrt N\,d_{\mathrm H}\bigl(\Theta_\kappa(\widehat P_N),\Theta_\kappa(P)\bigr)
\ \Longrightarrow\ 
\sup_{v\in\mathbb S^1}\left|\dot\rho_{P,\kappa}(v;Z)\right|.
\]
Neither differentiability premise follows from compactness or the three-state reduction.
