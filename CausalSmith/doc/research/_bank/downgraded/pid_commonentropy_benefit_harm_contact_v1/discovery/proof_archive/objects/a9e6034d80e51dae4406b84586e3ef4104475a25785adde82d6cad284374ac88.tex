The proof uses an exact product-mixture reduction in the necessity direction and an explicit causal completion in the sufficiency direction.  Throughout, \(A\) and \(Y\) take values in \(\{0,1\}\), \(P\) is an arbitrary probability law on \(\{0,1\}^2\), and \(\kappa\in[0,\log 2]\).  No positivity, overlap, rank, division, or limiting condition is used; in particular, observed cells and latent masses may vanish, and the entropy convention is \(0\log 0=0\).

First suppose \((0,0)\in\Theta_\kappa(P)\).  By \texttt{def:identified-set}, there is a law \(Q\in\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\) such that \(B(Q)=H(Q)=0\).  Since the two off-diagonal response events are disjoint and exhaust the event of unequal potential outcomes, the definitions of \(B(Q)\) and \(H(Q)\) give
\[
Q\{Y(0)\neq Y(1)\}
=Q\{Y(0)=0,Y(1)=1\}+Q\{Y(0)=1,Y(1)=0\}
=B(Q)+H(Q)=0.
\]
Consequently the binary random variable \(V:=Y(0)\) satisfies \(V=Y(0)=Y(1)\), \(Q\)-almost surely.  Fix a latent value \(u\) with \(Q(U=u)>0\).  The conditional exchangeability condition in \texttt{ass:latent-exchangeability} implies, after summing its factorization over the unused potential-outcome coordinate,
\[
Q(A=a,V=y\mid U=u)
=Q(A=a\mid U=u)Q(V=y\mid U=u)
\qquad(a,y\in\{0,1\}).
\]
By \texttt{ass:consistency} and the almost-sure equality of the two potential outcomes,
\[
Y=Y(A)=V\qquad Q\text{-almost surely}.
\]
Substitution into the preceding conditional factorization therefore yields
\[
Q(A=a,Y=y\mid U=u)
=Q(A=a\mid U=u)Q(Y=y\mid U=u).
\]
Thus \(A\perp Y\mid U\) under the \((A,Y,U)\)-marginal \(\widetilde P:=Q_{A,Y,U}\).  The factual-compatibility condition \texttt{ass:factual-compatibility} gives \(\widetilde P_{A,Y}=P\), while \texttt{ass:entropy-budget} gives
\[
\operatorname{Ent}_{\widetilde P}(U)=\operatorname{Ent}_{Q}(U)\leq\kappa.
\]
The auxiliary alphabet is finite by \texttt{def:model-fiber}.  Hence \(\widetilde P\) is feasible in the infimum defining \(G_P(A;Y)\) in \texttt{def:common-entropy}, and therefore
\[
G_P(A;Y)\leq \operatorname{Ent}_{\widetilde P}(U)
=\operatorname{Ent}_{Q}(U)\leq\kappa.
\]
This proves necessity and, importantly, maps a causal origin witness to a feasible observed-law factorization rather than identifying the two objects by definition.

Conversely, suppose \(G_P(A;Y)\leq\kappa\).  Apply \texttt{lem:binary-common-entropy-cardinality} to the two binary alphabets \(\mathcal A\) and \(\mathcal Y\).  The cited binary cardinality result gives an entropy-minimizing extension \(\widetilde P\) of \(P\) such that
\[
A\perp_{\widetilde P}Y\mid U,
\qquad
\bigl|\operatorname{supp}_{\widetilde P}(U)\bigr|\leq2,
\qquad
\operatorname{Ent}_{\widetilde P}(U)=G_P(A;Y)\leq\kappa.
\]
This is where finite attainment is used.  It is a published product-mixture fact, not an assumption about the causal law.

Define a full-data probability mass function on \((A,Y(0),Y(1),Y,U)\) by the explicit diagonal completion
\[
Q(a,i,j,y,u)
:=\widetilde P(a,y,u)\,\mathbf 1\{i=y\}\,\mathbf 1\{j=y\},
\qquad a,i,j,y\in\{0,1\}.
\]
Nonnegativity is immediate, and summing over \(a,i,j,y,u\) gives one because \(\widetilde P\) is a probability law.  Summing over \(i,j,u\) gives
\[
Q(A=a,Y=y)=\widetilde P(A=a,Y=y)=p_{ay},
\]
so \texttt{ass:factual-compatibility} holds.  The indicators in the construction imply
\[
Q\{Y(0)=Y(1)=Y\}=1,
\]
and hence \(Y=Y(A)\), proving \texttt{ass:consistency}.

For every \(u\) of positive mass, conditional independence under \(\widetilde P\) and the defining display for \(Q\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
&Q\bigl(A=a,Y(0)=i,Y(1)=j\mid U=u\bigr)\\
&\quad=\mathbf 1\{i=j\}\,
\widetilde P(A=a,Y=i\mid U=u)\\
&\quad=\widetilde P(A=a\mid U=u)\,
\mathbf 1\{i=j\}\widetilde P(Y=i\mid U=u)\\
&\quad=Q(A=a\mid U=u)\,
Q\bigl(Y(0)=i,Y(1)=j\mid U=u\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus \(A\perp(Y(0),Y(1))\mid U\), which proves \texttt{ass:latent-exchangeability}.  Conditional kernels at zero-mass states are irrelevant and may be assigned arbitrarily.  Because the \(U\)-marginal is unchanged,
\[
\operatorname{Ent}_{Q}(U)=\operatorname{Ent}_{\widetilde P}(U)\leq\kappa,
\]
so \texttt{ass:entropy-budget} holds.  The support of \(U\) has at most two points and is finite.  All defining conditions in \texttt{def:model-fiber} have now been checked equation by equation, so \(Q\in\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\).  Finally, the diagonal construction gives
\[
B(Q)=Q\{Y(0)=0,Y(1)=1\}=0,
\qquad
H(Q)=Q\{Y(0)=1,Y(1)=0\}=0.
\]
By \texttt{def:identified-set}, \((0,0)\in\Theta_\kappa(P)\).  This proves sufficiency constructively.  It also proves the stronger support conclusion that the origin witness needs at most two positive latent states; in particular it satisfies the theorem's stated bound of at most three states.  Combining the two directions establishes
\[
(0,0)\in\Theta_\kappa(P)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
G_P(A;Y)\leq\kappa.
\]
