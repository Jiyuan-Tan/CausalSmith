Put \(R=(Y(0),Y(1))\).  The technique is direct finite-kernel factorization: we prove the two inclusions by explicit constructions.

First let \(Q\in\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\) and suppose that the support of \(U\) has size at most \(m\).  Relabel its positive-mass states by a subset of \(\{1,\ldots,m\}\), pad with zero-mass states if necessary, and define, for every positive-mass state,
\[
 \pi_u=Q(U=u),\qquad e_u=Q(A=1\mid U=u),\qquad
 r_{ij\mid u}=Q(R=(i,j)\mid U=u).
\]
At a zero-mass state choose \(e_u\in[0,1]\) and \(r_{\cdot\cdot\mid u}\in\Delta_4\) arbitrarily.  Conditional exchangeability, namely \(\texttt{ass:latent-exchangeability}\), gives, for every positive-mass state,
\[
 Q(A=a,R=(i,j),U=u)
 =\pi_u e_u^a(1-e_u)^{1-a}r_{ij\mid u}.
\]
By consistency \(\texttt{ass:consistency}\) and factual compatibility \(\texttt{ass:factual-compatibility}\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 p_{ay}
 &=Q(A=a,Y=y)\\
 &=\sum_{u=1}^m\sum_{i,j=0}^1
 \pi_u e_u^a(1-e_u)^{1-a}r_{ij\mid u}
 \mathbf 1\{(1-a)i+aj=y\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Moreover, the entropy restriction \(\texttt{ass:entropy-budget}\) is exactly
\[
 -\sum_{u=1}^m\pi_u\log\pi_u=\operatorname{Ent}_Q(U)\leq\kappa,
\]
with the stipulated convention at zero.  Hence \((\pi,e,r)\in\mathcal F_{m,\kappa}(P)\).  Direct summation also gives
\[
 B(Q)=\sum_{u=1}^m\pi_u r_{01\mid u},\qquad
 H(Q)=\sum_{u=1}^m\pi_u r_{10\mid u},
\]
so the pair generated by \(Q\) is \(\Phi_m(\pi,e,r)\).

Conversely, let \((\pi,e,r)\in\mathcal F_{m,\kappa}(P)\).  On the finite space \(\{1,\ldots,m\}\times\{0,1\}\times\{0,1\}^2\), put
\[
 Q\{U=u,A=a,R=(i,j)\}
 =\pi_u e_u^a(1-e_u)^{1-a}r_{ij\mid u},
\]
and define \(Y=(1-A)Y(0)+AY(1)\).  The displayed masses are nonnegative and sum to one.  Their factorization proves \(A\perp R\mid U\), the definition of \(Y\) proves consistency, and the factual equations in \(\texttt{def:finite-factor-program}\) prove \(Q_{A,Y}=P\).  The entropy is \(-\sum_u\pi_u\log\pi_u\leq\kappa\), and the two response-type sums are precisely \(\Phi_m(\pi,e,r)\).  Thus \(Q\in\mathfrak M_\kappa(P)\) and realizes the proposed pair.  Taking the union over all finite \(m\), as required by \(\texttt{def:model-fiber}\) and \(\texttt{def:identified-set}\), proves the final equality and the asserted constructive equivalence.
