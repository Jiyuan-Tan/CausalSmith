The strongest unconditional result available from the frozen primitives is non-effective finite Hausdorff approximability.  By \(\texttt{thm:three-state-reduction}\),
\[
\Theta_\kappa(P)=\Phi_3(\mathcal F_{3,\kappa}(P)).
\]
The ambient product \(\Delta_3\times[0,1]^3\times(\Delta_4)^3\) is compact.  The factual equalities are closed, and \(x\mapsto-x\log x\), extended by zero at \(x=0\), is continuous; hence \(\mathcal F_{3,\kappa}(P)\) and its continuous image \(\Theta_\kappa(P)\) are compact, including zero-mass and active-entropy strata.  For a finite closed-cell grid of \([0,1]^2\) with cell diameter at most \(\eta\), let \(K^+\) be the union of cells meeting \(\Theta_\kappa(P)\), and select one point of \(\Theta_\kappa(P)\) in each retained cell to form the zero-dimensional complex \(K^-\).  Then
\[
K^-\subseteq\Theta_\kappa(P)\subseteq K^+,
\qquad d_{\mathrm H}(K^-,K^+)\leq\eta,
\]
and every selected vertex has an exact three-state SCM witness.  For a closed cell \(C\) disjoint from the image, minimize on the compact ambient parameter space the continuous residual
\[
R_C(\pi,e,r)=
\max_{a,y}|F_{ay}(\pi,e,r)-p_{ay}|
+\left[-\sum_u\pi_u\log\pi_u-\kappa\right]_+
+\operatorname{dist}(\Phi_3(\pi,e,r),C),
\]
where \(F_{ay}\) is the factual-cell map in \(\texttt{def:finite-factor-program}\).  Its minimum is attained and is strictly positive, because a zero would be a feasible parameter whose image lies in \(C\).  This supplies existential exact witnesses and positive separation margins, but not an effective procedure for producing them or certifying the positive minima from arbitrary real input data.
