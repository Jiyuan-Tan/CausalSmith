%%% FIELD resolution-proof
Fix \(x_0\in[0,1]^d\), \(n\ge2\), and a parameter tuple with \(S\le S_\star\).  Apply \(\mathrm{lem:closed\mbox{-}regime\mbox{-}fixed\mbox{-}point\mbox{-}component\mbox{-}mixtures}\) at this \(x_0\), this \(n\), and this tuple.  That result supplies two finite priors \(\Pi_+\) and \(\Pi_-\), supported on \(\mathcal P(d,\alpha,\beta,\gamma,L)\), and constants and tuning quantities depending only on the fixed tuple and uniformly in \(x_0\).  Its target-separation conclusion is exactly
\[
 |\theta_+-\theta_-|\ge c\rho_n. \tag{1}
\]
For \(\varepsilon\in\{+,-\}\), define the fixed-product mixture
\[
 \mathsf M_\varepsilon
 =\int P_O^{\otimes n}\,d\Pi_\varepsilon(P). \tag{2}
\]
The same result states that the conditional sign mixtures agree on every singleton component and, uniformly for every component size \(r\ge2\), obey
\[
 Q_{1,+}=Q_{1,-},
 \qquad
 H^2(Q_{r,+},Q_{r,-})\le Ca^2b^2r^4(9/2)^r. \tag{3}
\]
Its component-factorization and spanning-tree conclusion gives, for the mixtures in (2),
\[
 H^2(\mathsf M_+,\mathsf M_-)
 \le Cn^2h^d\ell^da^2b^2
 \le\frac14. \tag{4}
\]
Finally, the invoked result places every prior atom in the same class \(\mathcal P(d,\alpha,\beta,\gamma,L)\), retains the exact uniform covariate marginal on the full cube, and obtains (4) directly for \(P_O^{\otimes n}\), without an occupancy-event restriction or a Poissonized experiment.  Equations (1)--(4) are all the requested parts of the all-\(x_0\), fixed-\(n\) component-mixture certificate, so the answer to the open-ended question is affirmative.
