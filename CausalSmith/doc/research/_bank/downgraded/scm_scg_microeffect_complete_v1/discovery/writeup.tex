\noindent\textit{Specialization:} v1\par

\paragraph{TL;DR.} The full sound-and-complete lag-robust fixing theorem remains open, but the terminating three-way certified procedure now has two imported graph-native success regions in addition to its existing concrete and saturated-ID rules. After the Assaad--Devijver--Gaussier--Gössler--Meynaoui causally sufficient adjustment test, it deterministically checks every nonempty macro-mediator set against Assaad's 2025 four-condition latent-confounded SCG front-door criterion, only for \(\gamma\in\{0,L\}\), and returns the source's explicit nested finite-cylinder functional for the first passing set. The local proof establishes the required full-time-ADMG class inclusion and positivity of every denominator; no necessity or generic-family inclusion is claimed. The verified binary lag-switch pair remains the direct failure certificate, all other undecided inputs return \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\), and the conservative transition bound is \(6+2^{n-2}+3^{n\{\gamma+(n-1)L+1\}}\). Every success supports the existing cylinder plug-in and HAC delta-method inference under the stated positivity, mixing, bandwidth, and variance conditions.

\section{Project justification}

\paragraph{Gap.} Published SCG adjustment covers a substantial causally sufficient region, and Assaad (2025), Theorem 1 covers a selected latent-confounded front-door region, but neither gives a sound-and-complete decision rule for lag-specific micro effects over the present fixed-alphabet stationary refinement class. Fixed-graph hedges likewise do not automatically preserve time homogeneity, exact summary, positivity, equality of the entire observed process law, and unchanged coordinate alphabets under a refinement switch.

\paragraph{Niche.} The attainable contribution is certified partial identification with graph-native abstention. The procedure faithfully imports both the published causally sufficient adjustment region and the published latent-confounded SCG front-door region, adds saturated-ID successes for acyclic loop-free directed summaries, and gives a direct cyclic lag-switch nonidentification pair. Precisely the two displayed lag-switch refinements each admit their own refinement-specific identifying functional; no assertion is made that every refinement in the full class is individually identifiable. Remaining latent-confounded cyclic cases require the still-open offset-sensitive normal form and direct fixed-alphabet witness construction.

\paragraph{Fill.} The ordered procedure recognizes one proved outside-front-door success, one proved positive stationary lag-switch failure, the explicit 2024 adjustment branch, and then, for \(\gamma\in\{0,L\}\), at most \(2^{n-2}-1\) nonempty mediator sets under Assaad's 2025 SCG front-door criterion. A first passing set returns the displayed two-stage cylinder functional; otherwise the procedure uses a memoized ordinary-ID call on at most \(n\{\gamma+(n-1)L+1\}\) ancestral roles when the directed summary is acyclic and loop-free. All remaining cases return \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\), preserving the honest absence of a completeness claim.

\section{Related work}

Assaad (2025), Theorem 1 supplies the four-condition SCG front-door criterion for selected micro-query lags under latent confounding and its common two-stage do-free formula. The present procedure imports that result explicitly: it enumerates macro-mediator sets, constructs the source's mediator block and two temporal adjustment blocks, and proves that every local bounded-lag stationary semi-Markovian SCM induces a candidate full-time ADMG in the source class. Assaad, Devijver, Gaussier, Gössler, and Meynaoui (2024) separately give sufficient graphical conditions and finite adjustment sets under causal sufficiency; that criterion remains the preceding branch. Ferreira and Assaad treat macro effects and linear direct effects, while Yvernes, Devijver, and Gaussier characterize common adjustment under causal sufficiency. Jahn, Karnik, and Schulman bound identification for one specified periodic fine graph; Jaber and coauthors decide a finite graph class represented by one PAG; Shpitser and Pearl prove ID soundness and completeness for one finite ADMG; and Richardson, Robins, Evans, and Shpitser develop fixed-ADMG fixing and intrinsic sets. These imported results do not supply the offset-sensitive cross-refinement normal form or fixed-alphabet rejection construction required for completeness. The note's local claims are therefore the typed stationary transfers, the certified ordered combination, and the explicit lag-switch obstruction, not novelty of either published sufficient criterion.

\section{Assumptions}

\begin{assumption}[ass:refinement-span]\label{ass:refinement-span}
\(E_{\to}(R)\subseteq\{i_{t-\ell}\to j_t:0\le\ell\le L\}\ \wedge\ E_{\leftrightarrow}(R)\subseteq\{i_t\leftrightarrow j_{t+\delta}:|\delta|\le L\}\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (bounded-lag time-series causal graph, \cite{JahnKarnikSchulman2025}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:instantaneous-acyclicity]\label{ass:instantaneous-acyclicity}
\((V,\{i\to j:i_t\to j_t\in E_{\to}(R)\})\in\operatorname{DAG}(V)\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (acyclic instantaneous slice, \cite{Assaad2025MicroSCGFrontDoor}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:exact-summary]\label{ass:exact-summary}
\(\operatorname{summary}(R)=H\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (summary-graph compatibility by temporal abstraction, \cite{Assaad2025MicroSCGFrontDoor}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:finite-alphabet]\label{ass:finite-alphabet}
\(2\le|\mathcal A_i|<\infty\quad\forall i\in V\).
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (finite categorical observed state spaces, \cite{ShpitserPearl2008}).

\section{Main results}

\begin{lemma}[lem:stationary-lift]\label{lem:stationary-lift}
Fix \(\mathcal A\) satisfying \(2\le|\mathcal A_i|<\infty\) for every \(i\in V\).  Let \(M_0\) and \(M_1\) be two directly specified SCMs with refinements in \(\mathfrak R(H,L)\), observed alphabets exactly \(\mathcal A\), semi-Markovian roots, time-homogeneous equations, strictly stationary observed laws, and positive finite cylinders.  If \(P_{M_0}(C_{\mathcal A})=P_{M_1}(C_{\mathcal A})\) for every finite cylinder and \(\theta_{M_0}(y;x)\ne\theta_{M_1}(y;x)\), then \(M_0,M_1\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\), \(P_{M_0}=P_{M_1}\) as measures on \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\), and \(\operatorname{UID}(H,L,\mathcal A,X,Y,\gamma,x,y)=0\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put \(\mu=P_{M_0}\) and \(\nu=P_{M_1}\).  By hypothesis, the refinement of each \(M_j\), \(j\in\{0,1\}\), lies in \(\mathfrak R(H,L)\).  Hence \(\mathrm{def:refinement\text{-}class}\), together with \(\mathrm{ass:refinement\text{-}span}\), \(\mathrm{ass:instantaneous\text{-}acyclicity}\), and \(\mathrm{ass:exact\text{-}summary}\), supplies the required bounded span, acyclic instantaneous slice, and exact summary.  The remaining hypotheses supply the common alphabet family \(\mathcal A\), \(\mathrm{ass:finite\text{-}alphabet}\), \(\mathrm{ass:semi\text{-}markovian}\), \(\mathrm{ass:time\text{-}homogeneous}\), \(\mathrm{ass:strict\text{-}stationarity}\), and \(\mathrm{ass:cylinder\text{-}positivity}\).  Therefore \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\) gives
\[
 M_0,M_1\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A).
\]

It remains to pass from cylinder equality to equality of measures.  Let \(\mathscr E\) be the algebra of finite unions of finite cylinders in \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\).  Given finitely many cylinders, take the union of their finite coordinate supports.  The cells on that common support form a finite disjoint cylinder partition.  Finite additivity and the assumed equality on cylinders therefore give
\[
 \mu(E)=\nu(E)\qquad(E\in\mathscr E).
\]
Define
\[
 \mathscr D=\{B\in\sigma(\mathscr E):\mu(B)=\nu(B)\}.
\]
The class \(\mathscr D\) contains \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\), is closed under complements because \(\mu\) and \(\nu\) are probability measures, and is closed under countable disjoint unions by countable additivity.  Thus \(\mathscr D\) is a lambda-system.  The algebra \(\mathscr E\) is a pi-system and is contained in \(\mathscr D\).  For completeness, the pi--lambda conclusion follows directly as follows.  The smallest lambda-system containing \(\mathscr E\) is closed under intersections: first fix one member of \(\mathscr E\) and apply lambda-system closure in the other argument, and then reverse the two arguments.  A lambda-system closed under finite intersections is a sigma-field, because it is closed under complements and arbitrary countable unions after disjointification.  Hence that smallest lambda-system is \(\sigma(\mathscr E)\), and so \(\mathscr D=\sigma(\mathscr E)\).  Since finite cylinders generate the product sigma-field on the common path space,
\[
 P_{M_0}=P_{M_1}\quad\text{as measures on }\Omega_{\mathcal A}.
\]

Finally, the assumed inequality
\[
 \theta_{M_0}(y;x)\ne\theta_{M_1}(y;x)
\]
and the whole-law equality just proved furnish a counterexample to the implication quantified over compatible model pairs in \(\mathrm{def:uniform\text{-}identification}\).  Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{UID}(H,L,\mathcal A,X,Y,\gamma,x,y)=0.
\]
No alphabet enlargement or model gluing occurs: the argument only verifies the two directly specified models on the fixed input alphabet family.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} This is now a direct final-model certificate rather than an alphabet-changing lifting theorem. \textit{Gap.} A generic multi-role fixed-alphabet lift remains unavailable. \textit{Consumer.} Sound failure branches must construct final SCMs and then invoke this verifier.

\begin{proposition}[prop:acyclic-summary-reduction]\label{prop:acyclic-summary-reduction}
Fix \(\mathcal A\).  If the directed part of \(H\) is acyclic and has no directed self-loop, let \(G^\star\) contain every permitted directed lag and bidirected offset, and put \(A^\star=\operatorname{an}_{G^\star}(\{X_{-\gamma},Y_0\})\).  Then \(A^\star\subseteq V\times[-\gamma-(n-1)L,0]\) and \(|A^\star|\le n(nL+1)\).  If ordinary ID on the finite ADMG \(G^\star[A^\star]\) returns \(F\), then \(F\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A)\) and \(F(P_M)=\theta_M(y;x)\) for every \(M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\).  Failure of ID on the saturated ADMG is left undecided and is not asserted to be complete for uniform identification.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every directed edge of \(G^\star\) is of the form \(i_{t-\ell}\to j_t\), where \(0\leq\ell\leq L\), and its coordinate projection is an edge \(i\to j\) of \(H^{\to}\).  Thus the coordinate projection of a directed path in \(G^\star\) is a directed walk in \(H^{\to}\).  No coordinate can occur twice on such a walk: consecutive repetition would be a directed self-loop, while nonconsecutive repetition would contain a directed cycle.  Both are excluded by the hypothesis on \(H^{\to}\).  A directed path ending at either query vertex therefore has at most \(n-1\) edges.

Tracing such a path backward decreases time by an amount in \(\{0,\ldots,L\}\) at each edge.  The two terminal times are \(-\gamma\) and \(0\), so
\[
 A^\star:=\operatorname{an}_{G^\star}(\{X_{-\gamma},Y_0\})
 \subseteq V\times[-\gamma-(n-1)L,0].
\]
The interval on the right has \(\gamma+(n-1)L+1\) integer times.  Since \(0\leq\gamma\leq L\),
\[
 |A^\star|
 \leq n\{\gamma+(n-1)L+1\}
 \leq n(nL+1).
\]

Fix \(M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\), and write \(R\) for its refinement.  By \(\mathrm{ass:refinement\text{-}span}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:exact\text{-}summary}\), each directed edge and each bidirected edge of \(R\) is among the corresponding permitted lag or offset edges placed in \(G^\star\).  Hence \(R\subseteq G^\star\).  Since \(A^\star\) is ancestral in \(G^\star\), no observed vertex outside \(A^\star\) is a directed parent of a vertex in \(A^\star\).  Restricting the structural equations to \(A^\star\) consequently leaves every equation on \(A^\star\) unchanged.  Under \(\mathrm{ass:semi\text{-}markovian}\), a pairwise exogenous root whose second observed child is removed becomes a private root, and the retained roots remain mutually independent.  The restricted equations thus define a finite semi-Markovian SCM compatible with the supergraph \(G^\star[A^\star]\).  Moreover, every directed ancestor of either query vertex lies in \(A^\star\), so recursive substitution in the structural equations shows that restriction preserves the distribution of \(Y_0\) after replacing the equation for \(X_{-\gamma}\) by the constant \(x\).

Suppose ordinary ID on \(G^\star[A^\star]\) returns \(F\).  Applying \(\mathrm{lem:ordinary\text{-}id\text{-}soundness}\) to the restricted SCM gives
\[
 F(P_M|_{A^\star})
 =P_M(Y_0=y\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{-\gamma}=x))
 =\theta_M(y;x).
\]
The set \(A^\star\) is finite.  Therefore every joint cell and every marginal in the returned expression is a finite-cylinder probability on \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\).  Marginalization over \(\mathcal A\) uses finite sums, the recursive factorization uses finite products, and each conditional-kernel denominator is the probability of a finite cylinder.  Such a denominator is positive by \(\mathrm{ass:cylinder\text{-}positivity}\).  Hence \(\mathrm{def:cylinder\text{-}language}\) yields
\[
 F\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A).
\]
Because \(M\) was arbitrary, the identifying equality is uniform over \(\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\).  Only soundness on an ID success was used; an ID failure on the saturated graph therefore has no implication here.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} A successful saturated ordinary-ID call gives an explicit uniform finite-cylinder functional. \textit{Gap.} Failure of saturated ID is not proved equivalent to uniform nonidentification. \textit{Consumer.} Acyclic EasyRCA queries can be accepted on success and otherwise abstain.

\begin{proposition}[prop:lag-switch-collision]\label{prop:lag-switch-collision}
For the fixed binary family in \(\mathrm{def:lag\text{-}switch\text{-}regression}\), the two models belong to the same \(\mathfrak M(H,1,\mathcal A)\), have exact common summary \(1\to2\), \(2\to1\), \(2\to3\), no bidirected edges, the same strictly positive entire stationary observed law as measures on \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\), and separately identifiable refinement-specific effects, but \(P_{M_0}(O_{3,0}=1\mid\operatorname{do}(O_{1,-1}=0))=13/50\) and \(P_{M_1}(O_{3,0}=1\mid\operatorname{do}(O_{1,-1}=0))=1/2\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write \(A_t=O_{1,t}\), \(B_t=O_{2,t}\), and \(C_t=O_{3,t}\), and define
\[
 b_s(d)=s^d(1-s)^{1-d},\qquad s\in(0,1),\quad d\in\{0,1\}.
\]
All observed variables are binary by \(\mathrm{def:lag\text{-}switch\text{-}regression}\).  Substitution in model zero and model one, respectively, gives
\[
 B_t=(R_t\wedge B_{t-1})\oplus U_t\oplus N_t,
 \qquad
 A_t=(R'_t\wedge A_{t-1})\oplus U'_t\oplus N'_t.
\]
When \(R_t=0\), respectively \(R'_t=0\), the right-hand side forgets the preceding state.  The reset probability is \(1/2\), independently over time.  Therefore, for each fixed time, there is almost surely a reset at a finite time in its past.  Starting from the most recent reset constructs the unique causal two-sided solution; any two causal solutions agree after that reset.  The construction commutes with integer shifts, and the primitive noise law is shift invariant, so the resulting observed law is strictly stationary.

Let \(\mathcal H^{\mathrm{obs}}_{t-1}\) be the observed history through time \(t-1\).  In model zero the independent fair bit \(U_t\) makes \(A_t\) conditionally fair given this history.  Since \(B_t=A_t\oplus N_t\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P_{M_0}(A_t=a,B_t=b\mid\mathcal H^{\mathrm{obs}}_{t-1})
 &=P_{M_0}(A_t=a\mid\mathcal H^{\mathrm{obs}}_{t-1})P(N_t=a\oplus b)\\
 &=\frac12 b_q(a\oplus b).
 \end{aligned}
\]
In model one, \(U'_t\) makes \(B_t\) conditionally fair, and \(A_t=B_t\oplus N'_t\), yielding the same conditional mass.  Thus in both models the pairs \((A_t,B_t)\) are independent over time with common mass
\[
 \pi(a,b)=\frac12 b_q(a\oplus b).
\]
The last structural equation gives in both models
\[
 P(C_t=c\mid(A_s,B_s)_{s\in\mathbb Z},(C_s)_{s<t})
 =b_r(c\oplus B_{t-1}).
\]
Consequently a completely specified observed cylinder on an integer interval \([u,v]\) has, under either model, probability
\[
 \sum_{b_{u-1}\in\{0,1\}}\frac12
 \prod_{t=u}^{v}\pi(a_t,b_t)b_r(c_t\oplus b_{t-1}).
\]
Every arbitrary finite cylinder is obtained by taking a containing integer interval and summing this expression over unspecified cells.  The formula is the same in both models, so all finite-cylinder probabilities agree.  Because \(q=1/5\) and \(r=1/10\), every factor in every completely specified cylinder probability is strictly positive; hence every finite cylinder has positive probability.

We next verify the exact graphs.  In model zero the structural tables genuinely depend on the observed parents in
\[
 B_{t-1}\to A_t,\qquad A_t\to B_t,\qquad B_{t-1}\to C_t,
\]
whereas in model one they genuinely depend on
\[
 A_{t-1}\to B_t,\qquad B_t\to A_t,\qquad B_{t-1}\to C_t.
\]
For example, the first lagged dependence in either model is exposed on the positive-probability root event where its corresponding \(R\)-bit equals one.  Each instantaneous slice contains only one of \(A_t\to B_t\) and \(B_t\to A_t\), and hence is acyclic.  Every directed lag is zero or one.  All primitive noise sequences are mutually independent and each primitive noise has one observed child, so there is no bidirected edge.  Translation of the displayed edge templates gives in both cases the exact common summary
\[
 1\to2,\qquad 2\to1,\qquad 2\to3,
\]
and no other directed or bidirected edge.  The equations use the same table at every time.  Thus the span, instantaneous acyclicity, exact-summary, fixed finite-alphabet, semi-Markovian, time-homogeneity, strict-stationarity, and cylinder-positivity requirements in \(\mathrm{def:refinement\text{-}class}\) and \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\) all hold, giving
\[
 M_0,M_1\in\mathfrak M(H,1,\mathcal A).
\]

Under \(\operatorname{do}(A_{-1}=0)\) in model zero, the relevant equations reduce to
\[
 B_{-1}=N_{-1},\qquad C_0=N_{-1}\oplus E_0.
\]
Independence yields
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P_{M_0}(C_0=1\mid\operatorname{do}(A_{-1}=0))
 &=q(1-r)+(1-q)r\\
 &=q+r-2qr
 =\frac15+\frac1{10}-\frac2{50}
 =\frac{13}{50}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
In model one, \(A_{-1}\) is a child of \(B_{-1}\).  Replacing the equation for \(A_{-1}\) therefore leaves the fair distribution of \(B_{-1}\), and hence the distribution of its child \(C_0\), unchanged.  Thus
\[
 P_{M_1}(C_0=1\mid\operatorname{do}(A_{-1}=0))
 =\frac12(1-r)+\frac12r=\frac12.
\]

The two known refinements also admit different refinement-specific observed-law functionals.  Indeed, in every semi-Markovian SCM with the first displayed refinement, there is no shared root, the only observed parent of \(B_{-1}\) is \(A_{-1}\), and the only observed parent of \(C_0\) is \(B_{-1}\).  Independence of the private roots and recursive substitution through \(A_{-1}\to B_{-1}\to C_0\) therefore give
\[
 \sum_{b\in\{0,1\}}P(C_0=1\mid B_{-1}=b)P(B_{-1}=b\mid A_{-1}=0).
\]
In every semi-Markovian SCM with the second displayed refinement there is no directed path from \(A_{-1}\) to \(C_0\), so recursive substitution shows that replacing the equation for \(A_{-1}\) leaves the outcome distribution invariant; the corresponding functional is \(P(C_0=1)\).  Cylinder positivity makes the conditional expression well defined.  Thus each displayed refinement is individually identifiable even though the two functionals disagree on their common observational law.

All premises of \(\mathrm{lem:stationary\text{-}lift}\) have now been verified, including equality on every finite cylinder and unequal target effects.  That lemma gives
\[
 P_{M_0}=P_{M_1}\quad\text{as measures on }\Omega_{\mathcal A},
\]
and completes the fixed-alphabet collision certificate.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The pair is a complete direct fixed-alphabet rejection certificate with exact effects. \textit{Gap.} It is one collision, not a grammar for all rejecting states. \textit{Consumer.} It is the certified procedure's explicit failure regression test.

\begin{theorem}[thm:tid-dichotomy]\label{thm:tid-dichotomy}
For every feasible \((H,L,\mathcal A,X,Y,\gamma,x,y)\), the certified partial procedure \(\operatorname{TID\text{-}SCG}\) in \(\mathrm{def:transducer\text{-}handle}\) terminates after at most
\[
 B_\star(n,L,\gamma)=6+2^{n-2}+3^{n\{\gamma+(n-1)L+1\}}
\]
transitions and emits exactly one of \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F)\), \(\operatorname{FAIL}(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{lag}}(\mathcal A))\), or \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\).  Every success has \(F\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A)\) and \(F(P_M)=\theta_M(y;x)\) for all \(M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\).  Every failure encodes two final models \(M_0,M_1\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\) on the fixed alphabets such that \(P_{M_0}=P_{M_1}\) on \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\) and \(\theta_{M_0}(y;x)\ne\theta_{M_1}(y;x)\).  Consequently success implies \(\operatorname{UID}=1\), failure implies \(\operatorname{UID}=0\), and no converse is claimed.  In its defined order, the procedure returns the displayed success on \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{out}}\), the displayed failure on the binary lag-switch query in \(\mathrm{def:lag\text{-}switch\text{-}regression}\), returns \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}})\) when neither special branch matches and \(\mathsf{Adj}_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(H,X,Y,\gamma)\) holds, next returns \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W})\) for the first enumerated nonempty \(\mathbb W\subseteq V\setminus\{X,Y\}\) satisfying \(\mathsf{FD}_{\mathrm{A25}}(H,\mathbb W,X,Y,\gamma,L)\), then uses one saturated ordinary-ID call when the directed part of \(H\) is acyclic and loop-free, and returns \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\) in every residual case.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set
\[
N_\star=n\{\gamma+(n-1)L+1\}.
\]
Feasibility gives distinct coordinates \(X,Y\in V\), so \(n\geq 2\), and it gives \(0\leq\gamma\leq L\).  The first two rules in \(\mathrm{def:transducer\text{-}handle}\) compare the input with fixed finite templates.  The adjustment rule and each front-door candidate test use ancestry, descendant, simple-path, blocking, and directed-cycle searches in the finite graph \(H\).  The front-door search is entered only for \(\gamma\in\{0,L\}\) and examines the
\[
2^{|V\setminus\{X,Y\}|}-1=2^{n-2}-1<2^{n-2}
\]
nonempty subsets of \(V\setminus\{X,Y\}\) in the prescribed order.  The temporal sets produced by either imported branch are finite, and their product alphabets are finite by \(\mathrm{ass:finite\text{-}alphabet}\).

Suppose the saturated-ID rule is reached.  The rule's graphical premise and \(\mathrm{prop:acyclic\text{-}summary\text{-}reduction}\) give
\[
A^\star\subseteq V\times[-\gamma-(n-1)L,0].
\]
The integer interval on the right contains \(\gamma+(n-1)L+1\) time indices, whence
\[
|A^\star|\leq n\{\gamma+(n-1)L+1\}=N_\star.
\]
By \(\mathrm{def:transducer\text{-}handle}\), an ID memo state assigns to each vertex of \(A^\star\) one of the three labels absent, random, or fixed, and no state is revisited.  Therefore at most
\[
3^{|A^\star|}\leq 3^{N_\star}
\]
ID memo transitions occur.  Counting one initialization and the five ordered top-level rule evaluations contributes at most six further transitions.  Thus the total number of transitions is at most
\[
6+(2^{n-2}-1)+3^{N_\star}
\leq 6+2^{n-2}+3^{N_\star}
=B_\star(n,L,\gamma).
\]
Every test is deterministic, a return ends the procedure immediately, and the last rule returns \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\) in every residual case.  Hence the procedure terminates and emits exactly one of the three stated tags.

We next prove the semantics of every success.  If the first rule matches, the input is exactly \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{out}}\).  By \(\mathrm{prop:outside\text{-}witness\text{-}identification}\),
\[
F_{\mathrm{out}}(P_M)=\theta_M(1;0)
\qquad
\text{for every }M\in\mathfrak M(H_{\mathrm{out}},1,\mathcal A^{\mathrm{bin}}).
\]
The same proposition and \(\mathrm{def:frontdoor\text{-}outside\text{-}witness}\) display \(F_{\mathrm{out}}\) as a finite sum of observed conditional probabilities.  Each conditional is the ratio of a joint-cylinder probability to its conditioning-cylinder probability.  The denominator is strictly positive by \(\mathrm{ass:cylinder\text{-}positivity}\).  Consequently \(\mathrm{def:cylinder\text{-}language}\) yields \(F_{\mathrm{out}}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A^{\mathrm{bin}})\).

If the adjustment rule returns, its test establishes \(\mathsf{Adj}_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(H,X,Y,\gamma)\).  Therefore \(\mathrm{prop:scg\text{-}adjustment\text{-}transfer}\) gives
\[
F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A),
\qquad
F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(P_M;y,x)=\theta_M(y;x)
\quad
\text{for every }M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A).
\]
If the front-door rule returns on its first passing set \(\mathbb W\), the tested premise is precisely \(\mathsf{FD}_{\mathrm{A25}}(H,\mathbb W,X,Y,\gamma,L)\).  Hence \(\mathrm{prop:assaad\text{-}frontdoor\text{-}transfer}\) gives
\[
F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A),
\qquad
F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}(P_M;y,x)=\theta_M(y;x)
\quad
\text{for every }M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A).
\]
Finally, if the saturated ordinary-ID rule returns \(F\), its premise states that the directed part of \(H\) is acyclic and loop-free.  Applying \(\mathrm{prop:acyclic\text{-}summary\text{-}reduction}\) gives
\[
F\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A),
\qquad
F(P_M)=\theta_M(y;x)
\quad
\text{for every }M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A).
\]
These are all success-producing rules, so every success has the asserted uniform identifying semantics.

There is only one failure-producing rule.  When the binary lag-switch template with \(x=0\) and \(y=1\) matches, \(\mathrm{def:lag\text{-}switch\text{-}regression}\) supplies the rational structural tables and rational noise laws forming the returned certificate.  By \(\mathrm{prop:lag\text{-}switch\text{-}collision}\), the two encoded models obey
\[
M_0,M_1\in\mathfrak M(H,1,\mathcal A),
\qquad
P_{M_0}=P_{M_1}\quad\text{as measures on }\Omega_{\mathcal A},
\]
and
\[
\theta_{M_0}(1;0)=\frac{13}{50}
\neq\frac12=\theta_{M_1}(1;0).
\]
Thus every failure has exactly the claimed fixed-alphabet certificate semantics.

It remains to relate these operational conclusions to \(\operatorname{UID}\).  Suppose \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F)\) is returned, and take arbitrary \(M_0,M_1\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\) satisfying \(P_{M_0}=P_{M_1}\) on \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\).  Equality of these measures implies equality of every cylinder probability entering the finite expression \(F\); all denominators in that expression are positive by \(\mathrm{ass:cylinder\text{-}positivity}\).  Success soundness therefore gives
\[
\theta_{M_0}(y;x)=F(P_{M_0})=F(P_{M_1})=\theta_{M_1}(y;x).
\]
Since the two models were arbitrary, \(\mathrm{def:uniform\text{-}identification}\) yields \(\operatorname{UID}(H,L,\mathcal A,X,Y,\gamma,x,y)=1\).  On a failure, the certified pair instead has equal observational laws and unequal target values, so it falsifies the implication quantified in \(\mathrm{def:uniform\text{-}identification}\), yielding \(\operatorname{UID}(H,L,\mathcal A,X,Y,\gamma,x,y)=0\).  No conclusion follows from \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\), so no converse has been established.  The stated order and output of all branches is exactly the order defining \(\operatorname{TID\text{-}SCG}\) in \(\mathrm{def:transducer\text{-}handle}\), completing the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[thm:cylinder-hac]\label{thm:cylinder-hac}
Fix \(\mathcal A\) and condition on \(\operatorname{TID\text{-}SCG}(H,L,\mathcal A,X,Y,\gamma,x,y)=\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F)\). For one stationary trajectory in \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\), geometric beta mixing and boundedness of the indicators of the finitely many returned cylinders \(C_{\mathcal A,j}\) imply the moment and mixing hypotheses used for Newey--West HAC consistency. With modified Bartlett weights \(1-k/(h_T+1)\), \(h_T\to\infty\), and \(h_T=o(T^{1/4})\), \(\widehat\Omega_F\overset p\to\Omega_F\), \(\sqrt T(\widehat\theta_F-\theta_M(y;x))\Rightarrow N(0,v_F)\), \(\widehat v_F\overset p\to v_F\), and \(P_M\{\theta_M(y;x)\in[\widehat\theta_F\pm z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{\widehat v_F/T}]\}\to1-\alpha\). The limit is invariant under algebraically equivalent expressions in \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A)\) after redundant simplex coordinates are removed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Condition on
\[
 \operatorname{TID\text{-}SCG}(H,L,\mathcal A,X,Y,\gamma,x,y)
 =\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F).
\]
Success soundness in \(\mathrm{thm:tid\text{-}dichotomy}\) gives the identifying equality
\[
 F(p_F)=\theta_M(y;x),
 \qquad M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A).
\]
This is the required link between the causal target and the observed-law functional; it is not part of the definition of \(F\).

Let \(a_-\) and \(a_+\) be the smallest and largest time offsets in the finite supports of the cylinders in \(\mathcal C_{F,\mathcal A}\), and put \(w=a_+-a_-\).  Then \(Z_t\) is measurable with respect to the observed block from time \(t+a_-\) through time \(t+a_+\), and
\[
 \lVert Z_t\rVert_2\leq\sqrt{d_F}.
\]
Thus all moments of the finite-dimensional indicator vector are finite.  Strict stationarity of \(P_M\) makes \((Z_t)\) strictly stationary.  For \(k>w\), monotonicity of absolute-regularity coefficients under measurable maps and restriction of sigma-fields gives
\[
 \beta_Z(k)\leq\beta_M(k-w)
 \leq C_\beta\rho^{k-w},
\]
using \(\mathrm{ass:geometric\text{-}beta\text{-}mixing}\).  Hence the indicator process is geometrically beta mixing.  By \(\mathrm{ass:cylinder\text{-}positivity}\), every population denominator of the rational expression \(F\) is strictly positive.  The empirical-denominator event consequently has probability tending to one, as asserted in \(\mathrm{lem:valid\text{-}cylinder\text{-}hac\text{-}delta}\).

The kernel and bandwidth hypotheses in that lemma are precisely
\[
 K(u)=(1-|u|)\mathbf 1\{|u|\leq1\},\qquad
 h_T\to\infty,\qquad h_T/T^{1/4}\to0,
\]
from \(\mathrm{ass:hac\text{-}kernel}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:hac\text{-}bandwidth}\).  The remaining scale premise is \(v_F>0\), which is \(\mathrm{ass:variance\text{-}nondegeneracy}\).  The indicator boundedness and geometric mixing verified above provide the stated moment and dependence conditions.  Omitting a fixed number of shifts at the two trajectory boundaries changes \(\widehat p_F\) by \(O(T^{-1})\); for the HAC sum it changes the result by \(O(h_T/T)=o(1)\), since the summands are bounded and \(h_T=o(T^{1/4})\).

All premises of \(\mathrm{lem:valid\text{-}cylinder\text{-}hac\text{-}delta}\) are now satisfied.  Applying it and substituting \(F(p_F)=\theta_M(y;x)\) gives
\[
 \widehat\Omega_F\overset p\longrightarrow\Omega_F,
\]
\[
 \sqrt T\{\widehat\theta_F-\theta_M(y;x)\}
 \Rightarrow N(0,v_F),
 \qquad
 \widehat v_F\overset p\longrightarrow v_F,
\]
and
\[
 P_M\!\left\{\theta_M(y;x)\in
 \left[\widehat\theta_F\pm
 z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{\widehat v_F/T}\right]\right\}
 \longrightarrow1-\alpha.
\]
The same lemma states that algebraically equivalent cylinder expressions have the same limit after coordinates made redundant by the probability simplex are removed.  Every inferential conclusion is therefore conditional on a sound success output; none assigns a causal estimand to an \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\) output.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} Every sound successful cylinder functional receives a proved single-trajectory limit law and interval. \textit{Gap.} Inference is conditional on identification and cannot resolve an unknown query. \textit{Consumer.} Certified EasyRCA scores obtain HAC uncertainty quantification.

\begin{proposition}[prop:static-reduction]\label{prop:static-reduction}
Fix \(\mathcal A\).  When \(L=0\), every feasible refinement is the translation closure of one finite static ADMG on \(V\), and \(\gamma=0\).  The first two lag-one special branches of \(\operatorname{TID\text{-}SCG}(H,0,\mathcal A,X,Y,0,x,y)\) cannot match.  If \(\mathsf{Adj}_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(H,X,Y,0)\) holds, the procedure returns \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}})\), which is uniformly valid.  Otherwise the procedure enumerates the nonempty sets \(\mathbb W\subseteq V\setminus\{X,Y\}\); if one satisfies \(\mathsf{FD}_{\mathrm{A25}}(H,\mathbb W,X,Y,0,0)\), it returns the uniformly valid \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W})\) for the first such set.  If no such set passes, it makes one ordinary-ID call on the relevant ancestral subgraph.  If ID succeeds, it returns \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F)\), where \(F\) is the ordinary-ID functional on the common product alphabet and satisfies \(F(P_M)=\theta_M(y;x)\) for every \(M\in\mathfrak M(H,0,\mathcal A)\); if ID fails, it returns \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\).  No fixed-alphabet stationary hedge lift is claimed on the ID-failure branch.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Assume \(L=0\).  Feasibility includes \(0\leq\gamma\leq L\), and hence
\[
\gamma=0.
\]
Let \(R\in\mathfrak R(H,0)\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:refinement\text{-}span}\), every directed edge of \(R\) has the form \(i_t\to j_t\), and every bidirected edge has the form \(i_t\leftrightarrow j_t\).  Because \(R\) is a stationary refinement, translation invariance gives, for every \(k\in\mathbb Z\),
\[
i_t\to j_t\in E_{\to}(R)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
i_{t+k}\to j_{t+k}\in E_{\to}(R),
\]
with the analogous equivalence for bidirected edges.  It follows that \(R\) is the translation closure of its finite time-zero mixed graph.  By \(\mathrm{ass:exact\text{-}summary}\), that time-zero graph has exactly the coordinate adjacencies of \(H\), and by \(\mathrm{ass:instantaneous\text{-}acyclicity}\) its directed part is acyclic.  Thus it is a finite static ADMG on \(V\).

This also proves that the directed part of \(H\) is acyclic and has no directed self-loop.  Indeed, at lag zero any directed summary cycle through distinct coordinates would be a directed cycle in the time-zero slice, and any directed summary self-loop would be a directed self-loop in that slice; either case contradicts \(\mathrm{ass:instantaneous\text{-}acyclicity}\).

The first two rules of \(\mathrm{def:transducer\text{-}handle}\) require their displayed lag-one inputs, so neither can match when \(L=0\).  If \(\mathsf{Adj}_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(H,X,Y,0)\) holds, the next rule returns \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}})\), and \(\mathrm{prop:scg\text{-}adjustment\text{-}transfer}\) gives
\[
F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A),
\qquad
F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(P_M;y,x)=\theta_M(y;x)
\quad
\text{for every }M\in\mathfrak M(H,0,\mathcal A).
\]

Suppose the adjustment test fails.  Since \(\gamma=0\in\{0,L\}\), the Assaad front-door enumeration is eligible and examines the finitely many nonempty sets \(\mathbb W\subseteq V\setminus\{X,Y\}\).  For the first set satisfying \(\mathsf{FD}_{\mathrm{A25}}(H,\mathbb W,X,Y,0,0)\), \(\mathrm{def:transducer\text{-}handle}\) returns \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W})\), while \(\mathrm{prop:assaad\text{-}frontdoor\text{-}transfer}\) proves
\[
F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A),
\qquad
F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}(P_M;y,x)=\theta_M(y;x)
\quad
\text{for every }M\in\mathfrak M(H,0,\mathcal A).
\]

Suppose no candidate passes.  The acyclic and loop-free property proved above activates the saturated ordinary-ID rule.  With \(L=\gamma=0\), \(\mathrm{prop:acyclic\text{-}summary\text{-}reduction}\) gives
\[
A^\star\subseteq V\times[0,0].
\]
Because the maximal refinement at lag zero is the translation closure of the common static ADMG, \(G^\star[A^\star]\) is precisely its relevant ancestral subgraph.  If ordinary ID returns \(F\), the same proposition yields
\[
F\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A),
\qquad
F(P_M)=\theta_M(y;x)
\quad
\text{for every }M\in\mathfrak M(H,0,\mathcal A).
\]
If ID fails, \(\mathrm{def:transducer\text{-}handle}\) returns \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\).  Neither that definition nor \(\mathrm{thm:tid\text{-}dichotomy}\) turns this failure into a fixed-alphabet stationary witness pair, so no stationary hedge-lift conclusion is asserted.  These cases exhaust the ordered procedure and establish every claim.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} At zero lag the success computation is exactly ordinary ID. \textit{Gap.} ID failure returns unknown because the fixed-alphabet stationary hedge lift is unproved. \textit{Consumer.} Static users recover valid ordinary-ID successes without an unsafe failure claim.

\begin{proposition}[prop:easyrca-abstention]\label{prop:easyrca-abstention}
For any finite collection of EasyRCA lag-specific transmission queries on one fixed coordinate set and alphabet family \(\mathcal A\), rank only queries for which the certified partial \(\operatorname{TID\text{-}SCG}\) returns \(\operatorname{SUCCESS}(F)\).  Under the hypotheses of \(\mathrm{thm:cylinder\text{-}hac}\), if their population scores are separated by a fixed positive margin, the plug-in ranking is consistent.  A \(\operatorname{FAIL}\) output carries its directly constructed fixed-alphabet lag-switch certificate, and an \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\) output records an unresolved query; both are excluded rather than assigned a causal score.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let the successful queries in the fixed finite collection be indexed by \(1\leq j\leq m\), with returned functionals \(F_j\).  A collection with at most one successful query has a trivial ordering, so suppose \(m\geq2\).  By \(\mathrm{thm:tid\text{-}dichotomy}\), each is a finite-cylinder observed-law functional and satisfies
\[
 \theta_j=F_j(p_{F_j})
\]
throughout its compatible model class.  Under the hypotheses of \(\mathrm{thm:cylinder\text{-}hac}\), each plug-in estimator is consistent.  Equivalently, \(\mathrm{prop:finite\text{-}margin\text{-}plugin\text{-}ranking}\) applies and gives the desired ordering.  Explicitly, for every \(j\),
\[
 \widehat\theta_{F_j}-\theta_j\overset p\longrightarrow0.
\]
Because \(m\) is fixed and finite, the union bound gives
\[
 \max_{1\leq j\leq m}|\widehat\theta_{F_j}-\theta_j|
 \overset p\longrightarrow0.
\]
If
\[
 \Delta=\min_{j\ne k}|\theta_j-\theta_k|>0,
\]
then on the event where the preceding maximum is smaller than \(\Delta/2\), every pairwise sign is preserved:
\[
 \operatorname{sign}(\widehat\theta_{F_j}-\widehat\theta_{F_k})
 =\operatorname{sign}(\theta_j-\theta_k).
\]
The probability of this event tends to one, proving consistency of the ranking restricted to successes.

On a \(\operatorname{FAIL}\) output, \(\mathrm{thm:tid\text{-}dichotomy}\) supplies two directly constructed final models on the fixed alphabet family with the same entire observed-process law and unequal causal scores.  Hence the observed law cannot select a unique causal rank for that query.  An \(\operatorname{UNKNOWN}\) output carries neither an identifying functional nor a nonidentification certificate.  Excluding both tags therefore ranks exactly the certified causal scores and preserves the distinct meanings of certified failure and unresolved abstention.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The consumer contract now separates causal scores, certified nonidentification, and unresolved abstention. \textit{Gap.} Unknown queries cannot yet be ranked causally. \textit{Consumer.} Operators see why each excluded lag is failed or unresolved.

\begin{lemma}[lem:ordinary-id-soundness]\label{lem:ordinary-id-soundness}
For every finite acyclic directed mixed graph \(\mathcal G\), whenever the recursive ID algorithm returns an expression for \(P_{\mathbf x}(\mathbf y)\), that expression equals the interventional distribution in every semi-Markovian SCM compatible with \(\mathcal G\).
\end{lemma}

For the input \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{out}}\), the family \(\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{FD}}(H_{\mathrm{out}},1,\mathcal A^{\mathrm{bin}},1,2,1)\) is empty, while for every \(M\in\mathfrak M(H_{\mathrm{out}},1,\mathcal A^{\mathrm{bin}})\),

Here \(V=\{X,Y\}\), so the required nonempty mediator set in \(\mathrm{def:micro\text{-}frontdoor\text{-}family}\) cannot exist.  Fix \(M\in\mathfrak M(H_{\mathrm{out}},1,\mathcal A^{\mathrm{bin}})\).  Exact summary says that the only possible observed parents of \(Y_0\) are \(X_{-1}\) and \(X_0\), with at least one of those arguments active, and that \(X_t\) has no observed parent.  g(u,v)=P_M\{f_Y(u,v,U_{Y,0})=1\},

 P_M(Y_0=1\mid X_{-1}=u,X_0=v)=g(u,v).

\begin{lemma}[lem:valid-cylinder-hac-delta]\label{lem:valid-cylinder-hac-delta}
Fix \(\mathcal A\) and \(M\), and let \(F\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A)\) use finitely many cylinders.  Assume \(F(p_F)=\theta_M(y;x)\), strict stationarity, cylinder positivity, \(\beta_M(k)\le C_\beta\rho^k\), the Bartlett kernel, \(h_T\to\infty\), \(h_T=o(T^{1/4})\), and \(v_F>0\).  Define \(\widehat\theta_F\) and \(\widehat v_F\) on the event that their empirical denominators are positive, and arbitrarily off that event.  Then that event has probability tending to one,
\[
 \widehat\Omega_F\overset p\longrightarrow\Omega_F,\qquad
 \sqrt T(\widehat\theta_F-\theta_M(y;x))\Rightarrow N(0,v_F),\qquad
 \widehat v_F\overset p\longrightarrow v_F,
\]
and
\[
 P_M\!\left\{\theta_M(y;x)\in
 \left[\widehat\theta_F\pm z_{1-\alpha/2}\sqrt{\widehat v_F/T}\right]\right\}
 \longrightarrow1-\alpha.
\]
Algebraically equivalent cylinder expressions have the same limit after redundant simplex coordinates are removed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let \([a_j,b_j]\) be the smallest integer interval containing the time indices in cylinder \(j\), after placing that cylinder at shift zero, and put \(w=\max_j(b_j-a_j)<\infty\).  The vector \(Z_t\) is measurable with respect to a finite observed block of width at most \(w\).  For \(k>w\), restriction of sigma-fields in the definition of absolute regularity gives
\[
 \beta_Z(k)\le\beta_M(k-w)\le C_\beta\rho^{k-w},
\]
while \(\beta_Z(k)\le1\) for the finitely many \(k\le w\).  Since each coordinate of \(Z_t\) lies in \([0,1]\), the total-variation definition of \(\beta\) gives, for bounded scalar functions \(G,H\) of blocks separated by \(k\),
\[
 |\operatorname{Cov}(G,H)|
 \le4\lVert G\rVert_\infty\lVert H\rVert_\infty\beta_Z(k).
\]
Consequently every entry of \(\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}\operatorname{Cov}(Z_0,Z_k)\) is absolutely summable, \(\Omega_F\) is finite, and
\[
 E\lVert\widehat p_F-p_F\rVert^2=O(T^{-1}).
\]
Omitting the fixed boundary required by the cylinders changes the mean by \(O(T^{-1})\), so \(\widehat p_F-p_F=O_p(T^{-1/2})\).

We derive the required multivariate central limit theorem by blocking.  Fix \(c\) and put \(W_t=c^{\mathsf T}(Z_t-p_F)\).  Let \(b_T=\lfloor T^{1/5}\rfloor\), let \(s_T=\lceil d\log T\rceil\) with \(d>2/|\log\rho|\), and partition \(1,\ldots,T\) into big blocks of length \(b_T\) separated by gaps of length \(s_T\).  Absolute covariance summability shows that the variance of the gap sum divided by \(T\) is \(O(s_T/b_T)=o(1)\).  If \(m_T\) is the number of big blocks, iterative use of the total-variation definition bounds the difference between their joint characteristic function and the product of their marginal characteristic functions by
\[
 2(m_T-1)\beta_Z(s_T)
 \le C\frac{T}{b_T}\rho^{s_T-w}=o(1).
\]
It remains to analyze independent variables with the big-block-sum marginals.  Absolute covariance summability gives
\[
 \frac1T\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_{t=1}^T W_t\right)
 \longrightarrow \sigma_c^2:=c^{\mathsf T}\Omega_Fc,
\]
and removing the gaps preserves this limit.  Each centered big-block sum is bounded by \(Cb_T\).  Taylor expansion of its characteristic function has total third-order remainder at most
\[
 \frac{Cb_T}{T^{3/2}}\sum_{j=1}^{m_T}\operatorname{Var}(B_{T,j})
 =O(b_TT^{-1/2})=o(1).
\]
The quadratic terms converge to \(-u^2\sigma_c^2/2\), while their maximum is \(O(b_T/T)=o(1)\).  Their product therefore converges to \(\exp(-u^2\sigma_c^2/2)\).  Restoring the negligible gaps proves
\[
 T^{-1/2}\sum_{t=1}^T W_t\Rightarrow N(0,\sigma_c^2).
\]
Because \(c\) was arbitrary, the characteristic-function identity yields
\[
 \sqrt T(\widehat p_F-p_F)\Rightarrow N(0,\Omega_F).
\]

For HAC consistency, write \(U_t=Z_t-p_F\), \(\Gamma_k=E(U_tU_{t-k}^{\mathsf T})\), and
\[
 \widetilde\Gamma_k=T^{-1}\sum_{t=k+1}^TU_tU_{t-k}^{\mathsf T}.
\]
For any matrix entry, products indexed by \(t\) and \(t+r\) are bounded.  If \(r>k+w\), their observed blocks are separated by at least \(r-k-w\), so the covariance inequality applies; for the remaining \(k+w+1\) separations use the bound by one.  Uniformly for \(0\le k<T\),
\[
 E\lVert\widetilde\Gamma_k-E\widetilde\Gamma_k\rVert
 \le C\sqrt{\frac{k+w+1}{T}}.
\]
Therefore
\[
 E\sum_{k=0}^{h_T}\lVert\widetilde\Gamma_k-E\widetilde\Gamma_k\rVert
 \le C\frac{h_T^{3/2}}{\sqrt T}=o(1),
\]
because \(h_T=o(T^{1/4})\).  Also \(E\widetilde\Gamma_k=(1-k/T)\Gamma_k\).  The Bartlett weights converge to one at each fixed lag and are bounded by one.  Since \(\sum_k\lVert\Gamma_k\rVert<\infty\), truncating first at a fixed lag and then sending that lag to infinity gives
\[
 \widetilde\Gamma_0+
 \sum_{k=1}^{h_T}K(k/(h_T+1))
 (\widetilde\Gamma_k+\widetilde\Gamma_k^{\mathsf T})
 \overset p\longrightarrow\Omega_F.
\]
With \(d_T=\widehat p_F-p_F\), replacing \(U_t\) by \(U_t-d_T\) changes each empirical lag covariance by at most \(C(\lVert d_T\rVert+\lVert d_T\rVert^2)\).  Summing through \(h_T\) gives \(O_p(h_TT^{-1/2})=o_p(1)\).  Thus \(\widehat\Omega_F\overset p\to\Omega_F\).

Every denominator of \(F\) is positive at \(p_F\).  Since there are finitely many denominators, they remain positive on a neighborhood of \(p_F\).  Consistency of \(\widehat p_F\) makes the empirical-domain event have probability tending to one.  On this neighborhood the rational map is twice continuously differentiable, and Taylor expansion gives
\[
 \sqrt T\{F(\widehat p_F)-F(p_F)\}
 =\nabla F(p_F)^{\mathsf T}\sqrt T(\widehat p_F-p_F)+o_p(1).
\]
Using \(F(p_F)=\theta_M(y;x)\) yields the stated normal limit with
\[
 v_F=\nabla F(p_F)^{\mathsf T}\Omega_F\nabla F(p_F).
\]
Continuity of \(\nabla F\), consistency of \(\widehat p_F\), and HAC consistency imply \(\widehat v_F\to_pv_F\).  Because \(v_F>0\), studentization gives the asserted Wald coverage.

Finally, remove linearly redundant cylinder cells and use coordinates on the relative interior of the resulting probability simplex.  Algebraically equivalent rational expressions agree on an open subset of those coordinates, so their gradients agree.  Before reduction, their gradient difference is normal to the affine support of \(Z_t-p_F\) and is therefore annihilated by \(\Omega_F\).  Their linear limits and quadratic variance forms coincide.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[prop:finite-margin-plugin-ranking]\label{prop:finite-margin-plugin-ranking}
For a fixed finite family of valid cylinder identifying functionals \(F_1,\ldots,F_m\), suppose each trajectory obeys the hypotheses of \(\mathrm{lem:valid\text{-}cylinder\text{-}hac\text{-}delta}\), and let \(\theta_j=F_j(p_{F_j})\).  If \(\Delta=\min_{j\ne k}|\theta_j-\theta_k|>0\), then the ordering induced by \(\widehat\theta_{F_1},\ldots,\widehat\theta_{F_m}\) equals the population ordering with probability tending to one.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For every fixed \(j\), \(\mathrm{lem:valid\text{-}cylinder\text{-}hac\text{-}delta}\) gives \(\widehat\theta_{F_j}\to_p\theta_j\).  Since \(m<\infty\), the union bound yields
\[
 P\!\left(\max_{1\le j\le m}|\widehat\theta_{F_j}-\theta_j|\ge\Delta/3\right)
 \le\sum_{j=1}^mP\!\left(|\widehat\theta_{F_j}-\theta_j|\ge\Delta/3\right)
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
On the complementary event, \(\theta_j<\theta_k\) implies
\[
 \widehat\theta_{F_j}<\theta_j+\Delta/3
 \le\theta_k-2\Delta/3
 <\widehat\theta_{F_k}.
\]
Every pairwise comparison, and hence the full ranking, is then correct.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[lem:assaad-scg-adjustment]\label{lem:assaad-scg-adjustment}
Consider a directed summary causal graph \(H\) obtained from an acyclic full-time causal graph of a dynamic structural causal model with maximal lag bounded by \(L\), causal sufficiency, and consistency throughout time.  For the singleton query \(P(Y_0=y\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{-\gamma}=x))\), if \(\mathsf{Adj}_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(H,X,Y,\gamma)\) holds, then the effect equals \(P(Y_0=y)\) when \(X\notin\operatorname{Anc}_{H^{\to}}(Y)\); when \(X\in\operatorname{Anc}_{H^{\to}}(Y)\), the set \(\mathcal A_{\gamma}^{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(H,L,X)\) is a valid adjustment set and
\[
 P(Y_0=y\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{-\gamma}=x))
 =\sum_a P(Y_0=y\mid X_{-\gamma}=x,
 \mathcal A_{\gamma}^{\mathrm{ADGGM}}=a)
 P(\mathcal A_{\gamma}^{\mathrm{ADGGM}}=a).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proposition}[prop:scg-adjustment-transfer]\label{prop:scg-adjustment-transfer}
Fix \(\mathcal A\).  If \(\mathsf{Adj}_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(H,X,Y,\gamma)\) holds, then \(F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A)\) is well defined and, for every \(M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\),
\[
 F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(P_M;y,x)=\theta_M(y;x).
\]
This conclusion includes eligible cyclic or self-looped directed summaries; it does not assert that the published sufficient graphical condition is necessary.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\), and let \(R\in\mathfrak R(H,L)\) be its refinement.  We first verify that \(M\) lies in the class to which \(\mathrm{lem:assaad\text{-}scg\text{-}adjustment}\) applies.

Every directed edge of \(R\) has the form \(i_{t-\ell}\to j_t\) with \(0\le\ell\le L\), by \(\mathrm{ass:refinement\text{-}span}\).  If a directed cycle existed in the full-time graph, the sum of its nonnegative time increments would be zero.  Every edge on that cycle would therefore have lag zero, contradicting \(\mathrm{ass:instantaneous\text{-}acyclicity}\).  Hence the full-time directed graph is acyclic.

The predicate \(\mathsf{Adj}_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}\) includes
\(E_{\leftrightarrow}(H)=\varnothing\).  Suppose an exogenous root of \(M\) had two distinct observed children.  By \(\mathrm{ass:semi\text{-}markovian}\), that shared root would induce a bidirected edge between those two temporal vertices of \(R\); by \(\mathrm{ass:exact\text{-}summary}\), its coordinate pair would induce a bidirected edge, including a bidirected self-loop when the coordinates agree, in \(H\).  This contradicts \(E_{\leftrightarrow}(H)=\varnothing\).  Thus every exogenous root is private to one observed vertex and the full-time model is causally sufficient.

By the definition of a stationary refinement, translation of time preserves every directed relation in \(R\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:time\text{-}homogeneous}\), the translated structural equation is the same equation after relabelling its shifted arguments.  These two facts give the source's consistency-through-time setup.  The lag bound is \(L\), and \(\mathrm{ass:exact\text{-}summary}\) gives exactly the directed SCG \(H^{\to}\).  Consequently
\[
 M\in\mathscr C_{\mathrm{ours}}(H,L,\mathcal A)
 \subseteq \mathscr C_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(H,L),
\]
where the right-hand side denotes the causally sufficient, time-consistent dynamic-SCM class in the cited result.  This is a class inclusion, not an identification assumption.

There are two cases.  If
\(X\notin\operatorname{Anc}_{H^{\to}}(Y)\), the first conclusion of
\(\mathrm{lem:assaad\text{-}scg\text{-}adjustment}\) yields
\[
 \theta_M(y;x)=P_M(Y_0=y)
 =F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(P_M;y,x).
\]
If \(X\in\operatorname{Anc}_{H^{\to}}(Y)\), the predicate
\(\mathsf{Adj}_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}\) says exactly that neither listed obstruction holds.  The second conclusion of the cited lemma therefore gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \theta_M(y;x)
 &=\sum_{a\in\mathfrak a_{\gamma}}
 P_M\bigl(Y_0=y\mid X_{-\gamma}=x,
 \mathcal A_{\gamma}^{\mathrm{ADGGM}}=a\bigr)
 P_M\bigl(\mathcal A_{\gamma}^{\mathrm{ADGGM}}=a\bigr)\\
 &=F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}(P_M;y,x),
 \end{split}
\]
where the second equality expands each conditional probability as the cylinder ratio in \(\mathrm{def:scg\text{-}adjustment\text{-}certificate}\).

It remains to check typing and support.  The set
\(\mathcal A_{\gamma}^{\mathrm{ADGGM}}\) contains at most \(n(L+1)\) temporal vertices, so \(\mathfrak a_{\gamma}\) is finite by \(\mathrm{ass:finite\text{-}alphabet}\).  For every \(a\in\mathfrak a_{\gamma}\), \(\mathrm{ass:cylinder\text{-}positivity}\) gives
\[
 P_M\bigl(C_{\mathcal A}(\mathcal A_{\gamma}^{\mathrm{ADGGM}}\cup
 \{(X,-\gamma)\},(a,x))\bigr)>0.
\]
Thus every displayed division is defined.  All numerator, denominator, and marginal terms are probabilities of finite cylinders in the common space \(\Omega_{\mathcal A}\); the expression uses only a finite sum, products, and ratios with positive denominators.  By \(\mathrm{def:cylinder\text{-}language}\),
\(F_{\mathrm{ADGGM}}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A)\).  Since \(M\) was arbitrary, the equality holds uniformly over \(\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\).
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} This is the explicit class-inclusion proof that makes the published adjustment result a sound branch of the local procedure. \textit{Gap.} The graphical criterion is imported from Assaad--Devijver--Gaussier--Gössler--Meynaoui (2024); the local work is the typed stationary semi-Markovian transfer and finite-cylinder compilation. \textit{Consumer.} Eligible EasyRCA lag queries on causally sufficient cyclic or self-looped summaries receive a certified adjustment score instead of an unknown tag.

\begin{lemma}[lem:assaad-scg-frontdoor]\label{lem:assaad-scg-frontdoor}
Let \(H\) be an SCG induced by a full-time acyclic directed mixed graph of a stationary dynamic structural causal model whose directed temporal lag is bounded by \(L\), and let the query lag satisfy \(\gamma\in\{0,L\}\).  For a nonempty \(\mathbb W\subseteq V\setminus\{X,Y\}\), if \(\mathsf{FD}_{\mathrm{A25}}(H,\mathbb W,X,Y,\gamma,L)\) holds and the observational distribution is strictly positive, then
\[
 P(Y_0=y\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{-\gamma}=x))
 =F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}(P;y,x).
\]
The result is sufficient only: it does not assert necessity of the four graphical conditions.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proposition}[prop:assaad-frontdoor-transfer]\label{prop:assaad-frontdoor-transfer}
Fix the alphabet family \(\mathcal A\), and let \(\varnothing\ne\mathbb W\subseteq V\setminus\{X,Y\}\).  If \(\mathsf{FD}_{\mathrm{A25}}(H,\mathbb W,X,Y,\gamma,L)\) holds, then \(F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A)\) is well defined and, for every \(M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\),
\[
 F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}(P_M;y,x)=\theta_M(y;x).
\]
This is a sufficiency transfer of Assaad (2025), Theorem 1.  It neither identifies the certificate with \(\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{FD}}(H,L,\mathcal A,X,Y,\gamma)\) nor claims necessity.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(\mathbb W\) satisfying the premise, take an arbitrary \(M\in\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\), and let \(R\in\mathfrak R(H,L)\) be the refinement underlying \(M\), as provided by \(\mathrm{def:model\text{-}class}\) and \(\mathrm{def:refinement\text{-}class}\).  We first verify, one hypothesis at a time, that the cited theorem applies to this model.

By \(\mathrm{ass:refinement\text{-}span}\), every directed edge of \(R\) has a nonnegative time increment not exceeding \(L\).  If the full-time directed graph contained a directed cycle, the sum of the time increments around that cycle would be zero.  Nonnegativity would then force every edge on the cycle to have lag zero.  Translation invariance would place the same directed cycle in an instantaneous slice, contradicting \(\mathrm{ass:instantaneous\text{-}acyclicity}\).  Thus the directed part of \(R\) is acyclic, so \(R\) is a full-time ADMG of the kind used by the cited theorem.

By \(\mathrm{ass:semi\text{-}markovian}\), the exogenous roots of \(M\) are mutually independent and each has at most two observed children.  Latently projecting such a pair-root model gives precisely directed observed-parent edges and pairwise bidirected edges.  This local pair-root class is therefore a subclass of the cited latent-confounded dynamic-SCM class; no causal-sufficiency restriction is being imposed.  By \(\mathrm{ass:exact\text{-}summary}\), summarizing this full-time ADMG gives exactly \(H\), and by \(\mathrm{ass:refinement\text{-}span}\), \(L\) is a valid upper bound for the source's \(\gamma_{\max}\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:time\text{-}homogeneous}\), integer time shifts use the same structural equations with shifted arguments.  This is the source's stationarity, or consistency-through-time, requirement.  Finally, the definition of \(\mathsf{FD}_{\mathrm{A25}}\) supplies the source query regime \(\gamma\in\{0,L\}\) and each of its four SCG conditions verbatim.  Consequently the candidate full-time graph and model satisfy every graphical and structural premise of \(\mathrm{lem:assaad\text{-}scg\text{-}frontdoor}\).

It remains to verify the support premise and the cylinder compilation.  The sets \(\mathbb F_{\mathbb W}\), \(\mathbb B^x_{\mathbb W}\), and \(\mathbb B^f_{\mathbb W}\) are finite because \(V\), \(L\), and \(\gamma\) are finite.  Their product alphabets are finite by \(\mathrm{ass:finite\text{-}alphabet}\).  For every assignment appearing in the first bracket, \(\mathrm{ass:cylinder\text{-}positivity}\) gives
\[
 p_{P_M}(X_{-\gamma}=x,\mathbb B^x_{\mathbb W}=b^x)>0,
\]
and for every assignment appearing in the second bracket it gives
\[
 p_{P_M}(\mathbb F_{\mathbb W}=f,
 \mathbb B^f_{\mathbb W}=b^f,X_{-\gamma}=x')>0.
\]
Moreover, every joint assignment on any finite source time window is itself a cylinder assignment, so \(\mathrm{ass:cylinder\text{-}positivity}\) supplies the cited theorem's strict-positivity premise.  Thus every displayed ratio is defined.  The elementary conditional-probability identity, with these positive denominators, yields
\[
 \frac{p_{P_M}(\mathbb F_{\mathbb W}=f,X_{-\gamma}=x,
 \mathbb B^x_{\mathbb W}=b^x)}
 {p_{P_M}(X_{-\gamma}=x,\mathbb B^x_{\mathbb W}=b^x)}
 =P_M(\mathbb F_{\mathbb W}=f\mid
 X_{-\gamma}=x,\mathbb B^x_{\mathbb W}=b^x)
\]
and
\[
 \frac{p_{P_M}(Y_0=y,\mathbb F_{\mathbb W}=f,
 \mathbb B^f_{\mathbb W}=b^f,X_{-\gamma}=x')}
 {p_{P_M}(\mathbb F_{\mathbb W}=f,
 \mathbb B^f_{\mathbb W}=b^f,X_{-\gamma}=x')}
 =P_M(Y_0=y\mid\mathbb F_{\mathbb W}=f,
 \mathbb B^f_{\mathbb W}=b^f,X_{-\gamma}=x').
\]
Substituting these two identities into the definition of \(F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}\) gives exactly the two brackets in Equation (1) of the cited theorem.  Hence \(\mathrm{lem:assaad\text{-}scg\text{-}frontdoor}\) implies
\[
 F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}(P_M;y,x)
 =P_M(Y_0=y\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{-\gamma}=x))
 =\theta_M(y;x),
\]
where the last equality is the definition of \(\theta_M\), rather than a definition of the observed-law functional.

The displayed expression contains only finitely many cylinder probabilities, finite sums and products, and ratios whose denominators have just been shown positive.  Therefore \(\mathrm{def:cylinder\text{-}language}\) gives \(F_{\mathrm{A25},\mathbb W}\in\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cyl}}(\mathcal A)\).  Since \(M\) was arbitrary, the equality holds uniformly over \(\mathfrak M(H,L,\mathcal A)\).  The argument invokes only the stated sufficient criterion and proves no converse or relation to the separate generic family \(\mathfrak F_{\mathrm{FD}}\).
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} This proposition proves the class inclusion, positivity check, and finite-cylinder compilation needed to make the imported Assaad branch a sound success rule. \textit{Gap.} The four graphical conditions are sufficient, not necessary, and this proposition makes no claim about the separate generic front-door family. \textit{Consumer.} An EasyRCA query passing the published SCG front-door test receives the explicit nested cylinder functional rather than an unresolved tag.

\section{Interpretation}

A success tag is a positive causal certificate. It may arise from the concrete outside-front-door rule, the imported causally sufficient adjustment rule, the imported Assaad latent-confounded SCG front-door rule, or a successful saturated ordinary-ID call; in every case the returned finite-cylinder arithmetic functional equals the lag-specific intervention probability in every compatible SCM. A failure tag remains a negative certificate consisting of two fixed-alphabet stationary SCMs with the same entire observational process law and different targets. An unknown tag is neither. EasyRCA ranks only successful queries and reports the provenance of the functional; it displays failures and unresolved cases separately rather than converting either into a low transmission score.

\section*{Technical internal limitation (diagnostic only)}

The two imported SCG branches are only sufficient. The adjustment branch excludes bidirected summaries, while the Assaad front-door branch is restricted to \(\gamma\in\{0,L\}\), a passing macro-mediator set, and its stated cycle condition. In the remaining region, a finite rejection witness may contain several temporal roles of the same observed coordinate; independent-shift replication then attempts to place several role values into one coordinate-time variable, while product-slot repairs enlarge the fixed alphabet. The procedure therefore constructs only its displayed failure pair directly and returns unknown elsewhere. Strict stationarity and bounded structural lag do not make the observed process finite-order Markov, so transition-kernel truncation and semantic gluing of refinement-specific formulas still cannot establish completeness.

\section{Honest scope}

The note proves sound partial certification, not a sound-and-complete TID-SCG dichotomy. Both published branches are imported coverage: the 2024 rule applies under causal sufficiency and its stated sufficient graphical condition, while the 2025 rule applies only in its stated query-lag regime and when a nonempty macro-mediator set passes all four SCG front-door conditions. No necessity, novelty, or equality with the note's unproved generic front-door family is claimed for either criterion. The acyclic saturated-ID branch is sound but not complete for uniform identification, the concrete outside-family witness remains separate, and the direct failure branch covers only the verified binary lag-switch query. Latent-confounded cyclic cases outside these rules remain open. The two displayed lag-switch refinements each have a refinement-specific identifying functional, but that fact is not generalized to every refinement. The HAC theorem applies only after a sound success and additionally requires cylinder positivity, geometric beta mixing, the Bartlett kernel, \(h_T\to\infty\), \(h_T=o(T^{1/4})\), and \(v_F>0\).
