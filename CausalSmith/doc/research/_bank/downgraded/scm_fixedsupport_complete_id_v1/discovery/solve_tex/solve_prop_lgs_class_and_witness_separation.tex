Define the restriction set
\[
\mathcal R_H
:=
\left\{\theta\in\Theta(G^+):
\operatorname{supp}_{p_\theta}(S)=H,
\quad
p_\theta(c)>0\text{ for every }c\in\mathcal C_H^{\mathrm{adm}}(G^+)
\right\}.
\]
All the displayed probabilities are well defined because the alphabets are finite by \(\text{ass:finite-categorical}\), and \(p_\theta\) is the law obtained from the recursive equations and latent-root marginalization.  Let \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)\) denote the parameters satisfying the common recursive selector-gated syntax: the prescribed recursive order, the declared independent-root factorization, the known selector multiplexers, and the full canonical response-table parameterization.  By the declared meaning of \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)\),
\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)=\mathcal C_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+).
\]
By \(\text{def:model-class}\), whose member conditions include \(\text{ass:exact-selector-support}\) and \(\text{ass:admissible-cell-interior}\) in addition to precisely that common syntax,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Theta_H
&=\left\{\theta\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+):
\operatorname{supp}_{p_\theta}(S)=H,
\ p_\theta(c)>0\text{ for every }c\in\mathcal C_H^{\mathrm{adm}}(G^+)
\right\}\\
&=\mathcal C_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)\cap\mathcal R_H\\
&=\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)\cap\mathcal R_H.
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves both the asserted equality and \(\Theta_H\subseteq\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)\).  If \(\theta_0\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)\) violates either restriction defining \(\mathcal R_H\), then \(\theta_0\notin\mathcal R_H\), and hence
\[
\theta_0\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)\setminus\Theta_H.
\]
Thus the inclusion is strict whenever the premise in the proposition holds.

Next, the declared definition of the support-exact global twin relation is
\[
\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}
=
\left\{(\theta^+,\theta^-)\in\Theta_H^2:
O_H(\theta^+)=O_H(\theta^-),\quad
T_{a,H}(\theta^+)\ne T_{a,H}(\theta^-)
\right\}.
\]
It follows immediately that
\[
\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}
\subseteq \Theta_H^2
\subseteq \mathfrak M_{\mathrm{LGS}}(G^+)^2,
\]
where the second inclusion is the Cartesian square of the inclusion proved above.  This is also the typing guaranteed for every negative certificate by \(\text{thm:certificate-soundness}\) and \(\text{def:certificate}\): such a certificate supplies a pair in \(\Theta_H^2\), equality of the complete observation maps, and unequal targets.

Let \(w=(\theta^+,\theta^-)\in\mathcal W_{\mathrm{LGS}}^{(5)}\).  We verify the three stated exclusion conditions separately.

First, suppose one coordinate, denoted \(\theta^\varepsilon\) with \(\varepsilon\in\{+,-\}\), assigns positive selector mass outside \(H\).  Then there is an \(s_0\notin H\) such that
\[
p_{\theta^\varepsilon}(S=s_0)>0.
\]
If \(w\) belonged to \(\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}\), then \(\theta^\varepsilon\in\Theta_H\); but \(\text{ass:exact-selector-support}\) would give
\[
p_{\theta^\varepsilon}(S=s_0)>0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
s_0\in H,
\]
contradicting \(s_0\notin H\).  Hence \(w\notin\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}\).

Second, suppose that for one coordinate \(\theta^\varepsilon\) there is a cell \(c_0\in\mathcal C_H^{\mathrm{adm}}(G^+)\) with \(p_{\theta^\varepsilon}(c_0)=0\).  Membership of \(w\) in \(\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}\) would again imply \(\theta^\varepsilon\in\Theta_H\), whereas \(\text{ass:admissible-cell-interior}\) would require
\[
p_{\theta^\varepsilon}(c_0)>0,
\]
a contradiction.  Thus \(w\notin\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}\).

Third, suppose equality fails on a coordinate of \(O_H\).  Since \(O_H\) is indexed by \(\mathcal C_H^{\mathrm{adm}}(G^+)\), this means that some \(c_0\in\mathcal C_H^{\mathrm{adm}}(G^+)\) satisfies
\[
O_H(\theta^+)(c_0)\ne O_H(\theta^-)(c_0).
\]
Therefore \(O_H(\theta^+)\ne O_H(\theta^-)\), contrary to the observation-equality predicate in the definition of \(\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}\).  Hence \(w\notin\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}\).

The definition of \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{LGS}}^{(5)}\) does not add any of these exact-support, interior-positivity, or complete-\(O_H\)-equality predicates.  Consequently membership in the published failure relation alone supplies no implication of membership in \(\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}\).  More concretely, if \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{LGS}}^{(5)}\) contains any pair satisfying one of the three exclusion conditions, that pair lies in
\[
\mathcal W_{\mathrm{LGS}}^{(5)}\setminus\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}},
\]
and therefore \(\mathcal W_{\mathrm{LGS}}^{(5)}\not\subseteq\mathcal W_H^{\mathrm{global}}\), as claimed.
