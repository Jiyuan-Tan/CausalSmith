Fix a successful execution of Lee--Ghassami--Shpitser Algorithms 1--2 that uses only selector values in \(H\) and only denominators supported on \(\mathcal C_H^{\mathrm{adm}}(G^+)\).  By \(\textnormal{lem:lgs-case-refinement}\), every existential choice in that execution is represented by a finite decidable candidate set in the input-determined machine, and the machine enumerates the set in its fixed canonical order.  Hence, whenever the published branch predicate has a witness, the enumerated set has a least passing witness.  The machine therefore cannot take the associated exhaustion failure and cannot inspect a selector value outside \(H\).

We compare the two executions recursively over their district tasks.  The factor-kernel conclusion of \(\textnormal{lem:lgs-case-refinement}\) applies at each context, computed-closure, fixing, and Algorithm--2 case: after each displayed predicate has passed, the canonically selected machine factor agrees with the corresponding published factor on the normalized compatible image, independently of the valid fixing order.  The same lemma covers the terminal district product and ancestor marginalization.  Induction over the finite published recursion therefore gives
\[
 F_H\{O_H^N(\eta)\}=F_H^{\mathrm{LGS}}\{O_H^N(\eta)\}
 =T_{a,H}^N(\eta),
 \qquad \eta\in\mathcal I_H,
\tag{1}
\]
where \(F_H^{\mathrm{LGS}}\) denotes any support-licensed successful published output.  The first equality is extensional: no equality of the two executions' syntactic presentations, context choices, or denominator lists is claimed.

Every division actually made by the machine is entered in its step-indexed ledger.  The hypotheses of the theorem place every compatible observed law in the admissible-cell interior, so \(\textnormal{lem:step-indexed-ledger-positivity}\) yields
\[
 O_H(\Theta_H)\subseteq\mathfrak D_H.
\tag{2}
\]
In particular, all divisions used to evaluate the left side of (1) are legal at a compatible law.  For arbitrary \(\theta\in\Theta_H\), put \(\eta=\nu_H(\theta)\).  Normalization equivalence gives
\[
 O_H^N\{\nu_H(\theta)\}=O_H(\theta),
 \qquad
 T_{a,H}^N\{\nu_H(\theta)\}=T_{a,H}(\theta).
\tag{3}
\]
Substitution of (3) into (1) gives
\[
 F_H\{O_H(\theta)\}=T_{a,H}(\theta),
 \qquad \theta\in\Theta_H.
\tag{4}
\]
Gate reinsertion reverses the normalized context substitutions by \(\textnormal{thm:normalization-equivalence}\).  Equations (1)--(4) therefore also show that the returned circuit is extensionally equal on \(O_H(\Theta_H)\) to every support-licensed published output.  Equation (2) certifies the actual machine divisors, including intermediate divisors later cancelled.  The proof neither equates distinct branch ledgers nor introduces any selector context outside \(H\).

By \(\textnormal{prop:input-determinacy}\), the initial state \(\operatorname{init}_H(I)\) is unique and \(\operatorname{next}_I\) is a total deterministic function of the finite input and current state.  By \(\textnormal{lem:nonresidual-progress}\), every reachable state whose result is \(\operatorname{Running}\) and whose selected work task is nonresidual has a unique successor; hence no such state is stuck.

We first prove local soundness.  For \(s\in H\), \(\textnormal{lem:context-construction-soundness}\) identifies the mechanically selected graph \(G[s]\) and the initialized kernel with evaluation of the known selector gates at \(s\), without introducing a value outside \(H\).  For a positive current kernel \(q\), \(\textnormal{lem:truncated-factorization-identity}\) gives
\[
 \operatorname{TF}_{\prec}(q,B)
 =\prod_{X\in B}q\{X\mid\operatorname{pre}_{\prec}(X)\},
\tag{1}
\]
with every conditioning denominator equal to its actual marginal.  If the case function has computed a valid sequence \(\pi\), then \(\textnormal{lem:computed-fixing-soundness}\) gives, at every fixing step,
\[
 q^{\mathrm{new}}
 =\frac{q^{\mathrm{old}}}
 {q^{\mathrm{old}}\{W\mid\operatorname{nd}_G(W),\operatorname{fixed}(G)\}},
\tag{2}
\]
together with the corresponding removal of incoming arrowheads and the exact cross-multiplied identity.  No use of (2) precedes computation of a valid sequence.  For a passing Algorithm--2 triple, \(\textnormal{lem:hybrid-kernel-soundness}\) shows that the product in (1), followed by the computed fixing sequence, equals the required district kernel.  When all district factors have returned, \(\textnormal{lem:district-assembly-soundness}\) gives
\[
 \sum_{Y^*\setminus Y}\prod_{D^*}q_{D^*}
 =T_{a,H}^N
\tag{3}
\]
on every compatible normalized model.  Finally, \(\textnormal{lem:step-indexed-ledger-positivity}\) gives strict positivity of every divisor in (1)--(2) on the compatible image.  Thus each displayed cross-multiplication is reversible there, and all context, factorization, fixing, hybrid, factor-storage, and Join transitions preserve the represented observation--target relation.

The residual transition is also sound.  A projection candidate \(P/D\) passes only if the verifier establishes componentwise, modulo the retained constraints,
\[
 D\,t_{a,H}^N-P(o_H^N)=0,
\tag{4}
\]
and every factor of \(D\) is registered as positive.  Evaluating (4) at \(\eta\in\mathcal I_H\) and dividing by \(D\{O_H^N(\eta)\}>0\) proves
\[
 F_H\{O_H^N(\eta)\}=T_{a,H}^N(\eta).
\tag{5}
\]
A contrast candidate passes only if its two rational affine parameter maps satisfy the primitive simplex constraints, the retained equalities, selector mass positive exactly on \(H\), strict positivity of every admissible cell, equality of every coordinate of \(o_H^N\), and a target contrast with nonzero constant sign.  By \(\textnormal{thm:normalization-equivalence}\), gate reinsertion maps both normalized parameters into \(\Theta_H\) and preserves their observation and target maps.  Hence an accepted contrast is a genuine support-exact global twin.  If all residual candidates fail, the machine emits \(\operatorname{ResidualPending}\); neither that result nor an LGS failure tag is assigned causal content.

It remains to establish termination.  Give the control phases their declared natural-number codes, ordered so that every listed control successor has a smaller code.  For a task rank \(r_I(\tau)=(n,c,f,k)\), define the ordinal code
\[
 \rho\{r_I(\tau)\}
 =\omega^3n+\omega^2c+\omega f+k<\omega^4.
\tag{6}
\]
Lexicographic descent of \((n,c,f,k)\) is equivalent to descent of (6); hence the task-rank order is well founded.  For a finite work multiset \(M\), define
\[
 \Omega(M)=\mathop{\#\!\sum}_{\tau\in M}
 \omega^{\rho\{r_I(\tau)\}},
\tag{7}
\]
where \(\#\!\sum\) is the finite natural sum.  If one task is removed, or is replaced by finitely many tasks whose ranks are all strictly smaller, the coefficient of the largest affected exponent in the Cantor normal form of (7) decreases, every larger coefficient is unchanged, and all new summands have smaller exponents.  Therefore \(\Omega(M)\) strictly decreases.  This directly proves well-foundedness of the finite-multiset order defining \(\mu_H\).

We now inspect the exhaustive nonresidual cases of \(\operatorname{next}_I\).  A Start task or district split replaces its parent by proper-ambient district children, or by equal-ambient administrative tasks at a lower control level.  Ancestor restriction decreases the random ambient size unless it merely changes phase, in which case it decreases the control level.  A fixing step decreases the number \(f\) of remaining vertices, and its last step invokes a lower-control continuation.  Rejecting an Algorithm--2 candidate decreases \(k\); accepting one enters lower-control hybrid fixing.  Factor storage and completed Join remove work.  Consequently every nonterminal nonresidual successor satisfies
\[
 \mu_H(\zeta_{m+1})<\mu_H(\zeta_m).
\tag{8}
\]
The residual generators produce finite lists from the residual payload.  Their deterministic verification is therefore a terminating finite computation: it returns the first accepted candidate, if one exists, and otherwise returns \(\operatorname{ResidualPending}\).

An infinite run would either contain infinitely many nonresidual transitions, contradicting the well-founded strict descent (8), or reach a residual task after finitely many transitions, after which the finite residual verification terminates.  Thus every run is finite.  Its last state cannot be a running nonresidual state by nonresidual progress.  By the exhaustive result tags and the residual rule, its result is exactly one formula, one verifier-accepted global twin, or one \(\operatorname{ResidualPending}\).  Determinism makes this endpoint unique.  Equations (1)--(5) establish the required local identities and certificate soundness along the run, while (8) proves the asserted decrease and termination.

 C=\{S,A,Y\}\supsetneq D^*.
