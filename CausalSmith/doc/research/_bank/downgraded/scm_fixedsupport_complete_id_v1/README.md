GROUNDING: Published SS-ID interleaves selection correction, district factorization, and fixing. The first changed proof step is to substitute support-constant selector gates while retaining the exact parameter image, then derive a residual rational-rule-or-common-contrast alternative whose twin compiler preserves all contexts simultaneously.

Prove soundness, completeness over every residual branch, preservation of valid published positive derivations, and termination by a decreasing finite state measure. A marked support-hole ancestor may terminate in II but must supply the legal pair. Projection may organize elimination but may not enlarge the model class.
