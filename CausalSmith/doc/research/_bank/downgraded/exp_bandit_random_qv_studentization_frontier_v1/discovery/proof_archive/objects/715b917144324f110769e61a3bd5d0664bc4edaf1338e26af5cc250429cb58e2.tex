Let \(T_\ell\to\infty\) and \(P_\ell\in\mathfrak P_{T_\ell}\) be a sequence along which the law of \(V_J\) under \(P_\ell\) converges weakly to a probability law \(\mu\) on the compact covariance set in Theorem 1, and let \(V\sim\mu\). Fix nonzero \(c\) and deterministic positive-definite \(\Sigma\). Then
\[
 \lim_{\ell\to\infty}P_\ell\{\tau\in C_{T_\ell}^{\Sigma}(c,\alpha)\}=1-\alpha
 \quad\text{for every }\alpha\in(0,1)
\]
if and only if
\[
 c^\top Vc=c^\top\Sigma c\qquad\mu\text{-almost surely}.
\]
In particular, if along the sequence \(p_{\ell j}:=P_\ell(J=j)\to p_j^0>0\) and the basin covariance under \(P_\ell\), denoted \(V_{\ell j}\), converges to \(W_j\) for every \(j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\), then every-level validity holds if and only if \(c^\top W_jc=c^\top\Sigma c\) for every basin \(j\). The necessity uses all nominal levels and makes no claim from validity at only one level.
