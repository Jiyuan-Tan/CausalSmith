Put
\[
 Q_T=T^{-1}\sum_{t=k_T+1}^T E_P[X_{T,t}^2\mid\mathcal G_{T,t-1}].
\]
Fix \(\eta>0\) and partition \([v_-,v_+]\) into finitely many Borel intervals \(I_r\) of diameter at most \(\eta\). The events \(B_r=\{\lambda_T\in I_r\}\) belong to \(\mathcal G_{T,k_T}\). Choose \(\lambda_r\in I_r\). For every cell of positive probability, let \(P_r=P(\,\cdot\mid B_r)\). Because \(B_r\in\mathcal G_{T,k_T}\subseteq\mathcal G_{T,t-1}\) for \(t>k_T\), the defining martingale-difference equality remains valid under \(P_r\):
\[
 E_{P_r}[X_{T,t}\mid\mathcal G_{T,t-1}]
 =\frac{E_P[\mathbf 1_{B_r}X_{T,t}\mid\mathcal G_{T,t-1}]}{P(B_r\mid\mathcal G_{T,t-1})}=0.
\]
Here the displayed ratio is read only on \(B_r\), where its denominator is one; equivalently, the equality follows directly by testing against bounded \(\mathcal G_{T,t-1}\)-measurable functions under the conditional law.

Define
\[
 d_r=E_{P_r}|Q_T-\lambda_T|+\eta,
 \qquad q_r=E_{P_r}Q_T,
 \qquad s_r^2=Tq_r.
\]
Since \(|\lambda_T-\lambda_r|\le\eta\) on \(B_r\),
\[
 |q_r-\lambda_r|\le d_r,
 \qquad
 \sum_rP(B_r)d_r\le\Delta_T+\eta.
\]
Call a cell good when \(d_r\le v_-/2\). The total probability of the bad cells is at most \(2(\Delta_T+\eta)/v_-\). On a good cell, \(q_r\ge v_-/2\), and the normalized predictable quadratic variation in the cited Mourrat bound is \(V_r^2=Q_T/q_r\). Therefore
\[
 E_{P_r}|V_r^2-1|
 \le q_r^{-1}E_{P_r}|Q_T-q_r|
 \le \frac{4d_r}{v_-}.
\]
The increments are bounded by \(L\), the number of tail increments is at most \(T\), and \(s_r^2\ge Tv_-/2\). Apply the cited quantitative bound \(\mathrm{lem:mourrat\text{-}bounded\text{-}martingale\text{-}clt}\) with \(p=1\) under \(P_r\). It gives a common constant \(C\) such that
\[
 \sup_z\left|P_r\left\{T^{-1/2}\sum_{t>k_T}X_{T,t}\le z\right\}
 -\Phi\left(\frac z{\sqrt{q_r}}\right)\right|
 \le C\left\{\frac{\log T}{\sqrt T}+\left(d_r+T^{-1}\right)^{1/3}\right\}
\]
on every good cell. The normal scale family is uniformly Lipschitz in its variance on \([v_-/2,2v_+]\): indeed, with \(\phi\) the standard normal density,
\[
 \sup_z\left|\frac{\partial}{\partial q}\Phi\left(\frac z{\sqrt q}\right)\right|
 =\sup_u\frac{|u|\phi(u)}{2q}\le C_{v_-}.
\]
Consequently, on a good cell,
\[
 \sup_z\left|\Phi\left(\frac z{\sqrt{q_r}}\right)
 -E_{P_r}\Phi\left(\frac z{\sqrt{\lambda_T}}\right)\right|
 \le C_{v_-}d_r.
\]
After multiplying by \(P(B_r)\), summing, bounding each bad-cell discrepancy by one, and using concavity of \(x^{1/3}\), we obtain
\[
 \sup_z\left|P\left\{T^{-1/2}\sum_{t>k_T}X_{T,t}\le z\right\}
 -E_P\Phi\left(\frac z{\sqrt{\lambda_T}}\right)\right|
 \le C\left\{\frac{\log T}{\sqrt T}+\left(\Delta_T+\eta+T^{-1}\right)^{1/3}
 +\Delta_T+\eta\right\}.
\]
Taking, for example, \(\eta=T^{-1}\) proves the assertion. Every constant in the display depends only on \(L,v_-,v_+\), which also proves the stated uniformity.
