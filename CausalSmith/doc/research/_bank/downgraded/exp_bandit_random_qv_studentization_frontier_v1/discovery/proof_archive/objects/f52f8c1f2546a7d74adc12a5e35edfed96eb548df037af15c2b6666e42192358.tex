For the class in Definition~\(\mathrm{def:saco\text{-}early\text{-}stabilized\text{-}aipw\text{-}class}\), let \(S_n,Q_n,\widehat\theta_n,\widehat V_n,\theta_{0,n}\), and \(N_n=n_{\mathrm{eff}}\) have the meanings given there.  Then the centered AIPW scores are martingale differences in deterministic scored-set order, and there is a finite constant \(C_X\), depending only on the common overlap and outcome/nuisance fourth-moment envelopes, such that
\[
\sup_{n,\mathbb P_n,s}E_{\mathbb P_n}|X_{n,s}|^4\le C_X.
\]
For every \(\zeta>0\),
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_n\in\mathfrak C_n^{\mathrm{Saco,es}}}
\mathbb P_n\left(\left|\frac{Q_n}{N_n\Lambda_n}-1\right|>\zeta\right)\longrightarrow0,
\]
and
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_n\in\mathfrak C_n^{\mathrm{Saco,es}}}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}
\left|\mathbb P_n\left\{\frac{S_n}{\sqrt{Q_n}}\le z\right\}-\Phi(z)\right|\longrightarrow0,
\]
where the score ratio is set to zero when \(Q_n=0\).  If
\[
Z_n^{\mathrm{feas}}=
\begin{cases}
\sqrt{N_n}(\widehat\theta_n-\theta_{0,n})/\sqrt{\widehat V_n},&\widehat V_n>0,\\
0,&\widehat V_n=0,
\end{cases}
\]
then
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_n\in\mathfrak C_n^{\mathrm{Saco,es}}}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}
\left|\mathbb P_n\{Z_n^{\mathrm{feas}}\le z\}-\Phi(z)\right|\longrightarrow0.
\]
Consequently, for every fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), the interval
\[
\mathcal I_n(\alpha)=\left[\widehat\theta_n\ \pm\ \Phi^{-1}(1-\alpha/2)\sqrt{\widehat V_n/N_n}\right]
\]
satisfies
\[
\sup_{\mathbb P_n\in\mathfrak C_n^{\mathrm{Saco,es}}}
\left|\mathbb P_n\{\theta_{0,n}\in\mathcal I_n(\alpha)\}-(1-\alpha)\right|\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus the valid direct repair is Saco's published enumerated AIPW/DML class intersected with early measurable suffix-QV stabilization, not the variance-growth-only source class.
