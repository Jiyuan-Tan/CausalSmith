For fixed \(\varepsilon,B,D_1,D_2,h_-,L_\Pi,\omega_-,\kappa,s_\Theta,\rho\) and common sequences \((r_T,\delta_T)_{T\ge1}\), define \(\mathfrak P_T=\{P:\text{at horizon }T,\ P\text{ satisfies every condition in }\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}full\text{-}data},\ldots,\mathrm{ass:basin\text{-}nondegeneracy}\text{ with these same fixed envelopes and moduli}\}\). The random element \(\eta_\infty\) is only required to be terminally measurable and \(\mathsf H\)-valued. No finite support and no pointwise convergence of \(\eta_t\) to \(\eta_\infty\) are imposed; all path approximation is supplied by the uniform Cesaro inequality in \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}attraction\text{-}modulus}\).
