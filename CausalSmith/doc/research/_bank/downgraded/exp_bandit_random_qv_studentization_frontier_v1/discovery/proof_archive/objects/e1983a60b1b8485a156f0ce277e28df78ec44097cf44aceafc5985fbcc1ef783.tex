Fix a prespecified \(c\in\mathbb R^d\setminus\{0\}\) and a common deterministic sequence \((\omega_{c,T})_{T\ge1}\) satisfying \(\omega_{c,T}>0\) and \(\sqrt T\,\omega_{c,T}\to\infty\). For the projected predictable-QV-growth class \(\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)\) formed with this sequence, the exact-root event is uniformly asymptotically certain and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}P(E_T^c)\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 \sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)+H^{-1}T^{-1/2}S_T
 =O_P(T^{-1/2})
\]
uniformly. Moreover,
\[
 T^{-1}([S]_T-\langle S\rangle_T)=O_P(T^{-1/2}),\qquad
 \widehat\Omega_T-T^{-1}[S]_T=O_P(T^{-1/2}),\qquad
 \widehat H_T-H=O_P(T^{-1/2})
\]
uniformly over that class. Although no growth is required outside the direction \(H^{-\top}c\) and \(T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T\) need not converge, the contrast-specific truncation at \(\omega_{c,T}/2\) is inactive:
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}P\{\mathcal E_T(c)^c\}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}
 \left|P\{T_T^{\mathrm{rqv}}(c)\le z\}-\Phi(z)\right|
 \longrightarrow0,
\]
and, for every fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1)\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}
 \left|P\{\tau\in I_T^{\mathrm{rqv}}(c,\alpha)\}-(1-\alpha)\right|
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
The constant-floor result is the explicit special case \(\omega_{c,T}\equiv\omega_c>0\): whenever
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}
 P\!\left\{c^\top H^{-1}(T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T)H^{-\top}c<\omega_c\right\}
 \longrightarrow0,
\]
the preceding conclusions hold with truncation threshold \(\omega_c/2\). The full predictable-QV-growth class \(\mathfrak Q_T\) satisfies this constant projected condition simultaneously for all \(\|c\|_2=1\) with \(\omega_c=\omega_-/(KD_1)^2\). Thus the pivot and coverage conclusions hold with an additional supremum over all unit contrasts on \(\mathfrak Q_T\). Finally, every finite-tail-attractor class \(\mathfrak P_T\) satisfies the constant projected condition for each fixed nonzero \(c\) and every
\[
 0<\omega_c<\frac{\omega_-\|c\|_2^2}{(KD_1)^2},
\]
so the same contrast-specific realized-QV pivot and Wald coverage are further corollaries.
