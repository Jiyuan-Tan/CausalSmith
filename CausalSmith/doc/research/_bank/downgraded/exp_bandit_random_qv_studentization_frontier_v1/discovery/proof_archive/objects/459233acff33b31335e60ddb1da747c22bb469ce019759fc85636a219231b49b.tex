Set \(\widehat\Omega_T=T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^T\widehat\xi_t\widehat\xi_t^\top\) and
\[
 \mathcal E_T=\left\{\sigma_{\min}(\widehat H_T)\ge h_-/2,
 \ \lambda_{\min}(\widehat\Omega_T)\ge\omega_-/2\right\}.
\]
Define \(\widehat V_T=\widehat H_T^{-1}\widehat\Omega_T\widehat H_T^{-\top}\) on \(\mathcal E_T\) and \(\widehat V_T=I_d\) on \(\mathcal E_T^c\). Here \(\omega_-\) is the common predictable-QV growth constant for \(\mathfrak Q_T\), or any certified positive lower-growth constant when this construction is used on a subclass; no terminal covariance is part of the construction.
