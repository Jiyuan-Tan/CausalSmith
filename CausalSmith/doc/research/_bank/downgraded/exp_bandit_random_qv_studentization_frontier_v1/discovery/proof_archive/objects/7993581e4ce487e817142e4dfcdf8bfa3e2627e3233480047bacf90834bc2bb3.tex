The bounded experiment in \(\mathrm{prop:endogenous\text{-}qv\text{-}self\text{-}normalization\text{-}obstruction}\), after relabeling its second arm as control, is an exact specialization of the hypotheses of Saco, arXiv:2602.15559v1, Theorem~5.14, with the deterministic scored set equal to all \(T\) times, logged executed propensities, zero predictable nuisance fits, \(Y_t(0)=Y_t(1)=1\), and \(\theta^*=0\). It has bounded fourth moments, uniform overlap, and predictable quadratic variation \(\langle S\rangle_T\ge4T\), but \(S_T/\sqrt{[S]_T}\) converges along \(T=2n\) to the noncentered, non-Gaussian limit displayed in that proposition. Consequently the variance-growth-only Studentized central limit conclusion asserted in Saco, Theorem~5.14, is false.
