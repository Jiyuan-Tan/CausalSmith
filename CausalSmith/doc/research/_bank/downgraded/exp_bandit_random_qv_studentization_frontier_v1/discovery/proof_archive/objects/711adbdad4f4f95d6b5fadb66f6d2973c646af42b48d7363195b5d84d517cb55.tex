For the finite-attractor class, the common derivative and bounded-IPW envelopes imply
\[
 \|H\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le KD_1,
 \qquad \omega_-I_d\preceq\Omega_J\preceq(KB^2/\varepsilon)I_d
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
There is a finite constant \(C\), independent of \(T\) and \(P\), such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}E_P\!\left[\left\|T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T-\Omega_J\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}\right]
 \le C(r_T+\delta_T).
\]
The preliminary estimator is uniformly consistent, \(G_T(\theta^*)=O_P(T^{-1/2})\), and \(\partial_\theta G_T(\theta^*)-H=O_P(T^{-1/2})\) uniformly. Together with \(D_2\rho/\varepsilon<h_-/4\), these bounds imply that, with probability tending uniformly to one, there is a unique empirical root in the closed \(\rho\)-ball about \(\theta^*\), it is \(O_P(T^{-1/2})\) from \(\theta^*\), and it is the unique member of \(\mathcal Z_T\). Hence \(\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}P(E_T^c)\to0\), and
\[
 \sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)+H^{-1}T^{-1/2}S_T=O_P(e_T)
\]
uniformly. Moreover, for every basin \(j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}\sup_{\|c\|_2=1}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}
 \left|P\!\left\{c^\top\sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)\le z\mid J=j\right\}
 -\Phi\!\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{c^\top V_jc}}\right)\right|\longrightarrow0.
\]
Equivalently, the convergence is basin-stable: with the supremum also over all functions \(\psi:\{1,\ldots,m\}\to[-1,1]\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}\sup_{\|c\|_2=1}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}\sup_{\|\psi\|_\infty\le1}
 \left|E_P\!\left[\psi(J)\mathbf 1\!\left\{c^\top\sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)\le z\right\}\right]
 -\sum_{j=1}^mp_j\psi(j)\Phi\!\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{c^\top V_jc}}\right)\right|\longrightarrow0.
\]
Finally,
\[
 \frac{\omega_-}{(KD_1)^2}I_d\preceq V_j
 \preceq\frac{KB^2}{\varepsilon h_-^2}I_d
 \qquad(j=1,\ldots,m).
\]
No pointwise convergence of the policy state is used.
