Let \(F:\mathbb R^r\rightrightarrows\mathbb R^r\) have closed graph, nonempty compact convex values, and linear growth. In the terminology of Bena\"im, Hofbauer, and Sorin, every bounded perturbed solution of \(\dot x\in F(x)\) is an asymptotic pseudotrajectory, and its limit set is internally chain transitive.
