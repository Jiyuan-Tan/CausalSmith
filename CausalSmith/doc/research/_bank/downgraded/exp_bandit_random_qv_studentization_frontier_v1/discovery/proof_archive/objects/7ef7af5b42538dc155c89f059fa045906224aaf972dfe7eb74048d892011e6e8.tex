For each \(n\), let \((X_{n,t},\mathcal G_{n,t})_{t=1}^{N_n}\) be a scalar martingale-difference array, let \(0\le h_n<N_n\) be deterministic, and suppose \(|X_{n,t}|\le L<\infty\) almost surely. Let \(\lambda_n\) be positive and \(\mathcal G_{n,h_n}\)-measurable, and let \(\nu_n>0\) be deterministic with \(\lambda_n\ge\nu_n\) almost surely. Define
\[
 S_n=\sum_{t=1}^{N_n}X_{n,t},\qquad
 Q_n=\sum_{t=1}^{N_n}X_{n,t}^2,\qquad
 A_{n,+}^2=\sum_{t=h_n+1}^{N_n}E[X_{n,t}^2\mid\mathcal G_{n,t-1}].
\]
If
\[
 N_n\nu_n\longrightarrow\infty,\qquad
 \frac{h_n}{N_n\nu_n}\longrightarrow0,\qquad
 \frac{A_{n,+}^2}{N_n\lambda_n}\longrightarrow1
 \quad\text{in probability},
\]
then
\[
 \frac{Q_n}{N_n\lambda_n}\longrightarrow1
 \quad\text{in probability},\qquad
 \frac{S_n}{\sqrt{Q_n}}\Rightarrow N(0,1).
\]
Both conclusions are uniform over any triangular collection for which the increment bound and the two deterministic limits are common and the displayed predictable-variation convergence is uniform. The early measurability of \(\lambda_n\) and the negligible-prefix condition are essential: arbitrary terminal random normalization is not covered.
