For fixed \(m\), \(\bar\eta_1,\ldots,\bar\eta_m\), \(s_\eta\), \(p_-\), \(\varepsilon,B,D_1,D_2,h_-,L_\Pi,\omega_-,\kappa,s_\Theta,\rho\), and common sequences \((r_T,\delta_T)_{T\ge1}\), define \(\mathfrak P_T\) to be the set of laws \(P\) that, at horizon \(T\), satisfy every condition in \(\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}full\text{-}data},\ldots,\mathrm{ass:basin\text{-}nondegeneracy}\) with these common objects, envelopes, and moduli, where \(\mathrm{ass:finite\text{-}tail\text{-}selection}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}attraction\text{-}modulus}\) have their finite-attractor meanings. Thus \(\eta_\infty=\bar\eta_J\), every one of the \(m\) separated basins has mass at least \(p_-\), and the policy path is approximated by its selected attractor only through the uniform Cesaro inequality. Pointwise convergence of \(\eta_t\) is not imposed.
