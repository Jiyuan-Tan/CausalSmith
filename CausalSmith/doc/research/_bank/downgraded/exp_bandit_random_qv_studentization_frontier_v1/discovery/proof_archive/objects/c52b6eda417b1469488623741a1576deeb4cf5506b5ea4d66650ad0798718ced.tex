Let \(T_\ell\to\infty\) and \(P_\ell\in\mathfrak P_{T_\ell}\) be a sequence along which the law of \(V_J\) under \(P_\ell\) converges weakly to a probability law \(\mu\) on the compact covariance set in Theorem 1. Let \(V\sim\mu\). Fix nonzero \(c\) and deterministic positive-definite \(\Sigma\). Then
\[
 \lim_{\ell\to\infty}P_\ell\{\tau\in C_{T_\ell}^{\Sigma}(c,\alpha)\}=1-\alpha
 \quad\text{for every }\alpha\in(0,1)
\]
if and only if
\[
 c^\top Vc=c^\top\Sigma c\qquad\mu\text{-almost surely}.
\]
If, along the same subsequence, \(p_{\ell,j}=P_\ell(J=j)\to p_j^0\) and \(V_{\ell,j}\to W_j\) for every \(j\), then every \(p_j^0\ge p_->0\), \(\mu=\sum_{j=1}^mp_j^0\delta_{W_j}\), and the equivalent basin-invariance condition is
\[
 c^\top W_jc=c^\top\Sigma c\qquad\text{for every }j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}.
\]
The necessity identifies the projected variance law of the centered Gaussian scale mixture from coverage at every nominal level; it makes no claim from validity at only one level.
