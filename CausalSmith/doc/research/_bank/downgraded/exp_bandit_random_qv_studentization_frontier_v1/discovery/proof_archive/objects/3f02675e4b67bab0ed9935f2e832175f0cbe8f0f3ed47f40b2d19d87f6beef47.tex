For fixed \(\varepsilon,B,D_1,D_2,h_-,\omega_-,\kappa,s_\Theta,\rho\), fixed compact \(\Theta\), and common deterministic sequences \((r_T,\delta_T)_{T\ge1}\), let \((\mathfrak Q_T)_{T\ge1}\) be any sequence of law classes such that, for every horizon \(T\) and every \(P\in\mathfrak Q_T\), the law \(P\) satisfies
\[
 \mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}full\text{-}data},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:sequential\text{-}randomization},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}overlap},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:bounded\text{-}score},
\]
\[
 \mathrm{ass:smooth\text{-}z\text{-}map},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}root\text{-}separation},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:root\text{-}interior},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:jacobian\text{-}nonsingular},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:vanishing\text{-}modulus}.
\]
The sequence \((\mathfrak Q_T)_{T\ge1}\), as a whole, is required to satisfy \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}predictable\text{-}qv\text{-}growth}\), with the supremum taken over the chosen subclass \(\mathfrak Q_T\) at horizon \(T\). Thus \(\mathfrak Q_T\) is not stipulated to be the maximal class of every law satisfying the pointwise base conditions. In this definition, \(r_T\) and \(\delta_T\) enter only through the numerical approximation tolerance in \(\mathrm{def:ipw\text{-}z\text{-}estimator}\); no state-attraction interpretation is imposed. In particular, membership does not require \(m\), \(J\), \(\eta_\infty\), fixed attractors, attractor separation, basin masses, Cesaro attraction, convergence of \(T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T\), or a terminal covariance.
