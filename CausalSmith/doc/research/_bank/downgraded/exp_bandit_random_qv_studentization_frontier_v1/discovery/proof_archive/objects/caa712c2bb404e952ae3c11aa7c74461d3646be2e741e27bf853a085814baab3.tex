Classical Aldous--Eagleson stable martingale theory supplies the stable and mixed-normal limit apparatus. This theorem uniformly derives the random basin covariance from finite separated attractors and a common Cesaro modulus, constructs an early \(\mathcal F_{k_T}\)-measurable decoder \(J_T\) to avoid conditioning the martingale on terminal \(J\), and carries the limit through uniform IPW--Z exact-root linearization.
