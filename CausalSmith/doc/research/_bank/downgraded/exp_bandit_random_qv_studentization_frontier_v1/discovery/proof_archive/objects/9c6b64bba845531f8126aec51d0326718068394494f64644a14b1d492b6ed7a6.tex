Write \(Z^{\mathrm{GX}}_t=(\beta_1,\beta_2,q_1,q_2)^\top\) for the current augmented state. Its induced armwise update is

The next clipped-UCB action uses \(\beta_aX+|X|\sqrt{q_a}\), so \(Z^{\mathrm{GX}}_t\), rather than its two-dimensional slope projection, is the time-homogeneous Markov state. Here is the actual one-step finite expectation under the bounded law in Definition~\(\textnormal{def:guoxu-recursion}\). Let \(\mu_1=1/2\), \(\mu_2=1/12\), let \(p_{t,1}(1)=u_t\), \(p_{t,1}(-4)=v_t\), and let \(p_{t,2}(x)=1-p_{t,1}(x)\). For every coordinate \(i\in\{1,2,3,4\}\), every \(\epsilon>0\), and every initialized law \(P_{\eta_0}\), the coordinatewise dyadic tail estimate proved in equation (12) is
