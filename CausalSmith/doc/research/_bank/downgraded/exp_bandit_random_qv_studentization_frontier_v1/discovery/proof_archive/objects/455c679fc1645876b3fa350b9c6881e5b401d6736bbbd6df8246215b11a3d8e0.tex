A single deterministic matrix \(\Sigma\) gives asymptotic coverage \(1-\alpha\) for every \(\alpha\in(0,1)\) and every nonzero contrast \(c\in\mathbb R^d\) if and only if \(V_j=\Sigma\) for every \(j\in\mathcal J\). Equivalently, \(V_J\) is almost surely deterministic. The implication from all projected equalities to matrix equality follows by polarization.
