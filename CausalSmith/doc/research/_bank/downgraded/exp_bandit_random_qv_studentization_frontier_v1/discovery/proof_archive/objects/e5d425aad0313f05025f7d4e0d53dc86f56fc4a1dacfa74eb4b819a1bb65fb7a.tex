For each \(P\in\mathfrak P_T\), write \(\mathcal J_P=\{j:P(J=j)>0\}\), \(p_{P,j}=P(J=j)\), and let \(V_{P,j}\) denote the value of \(V_j\) under \(P\). The common derivative and bounded-IPW envelopes imply \(\|H\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le KD_1\) and \(\Omega_j\preceq(KB^2/\varepsilon)I_d\) for every \(j\in\mathcal J_P\). There is a finite constant \(C\), independent of \(T\) and \(P\), such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}E_P\left[\left\|T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T-\Omega_J\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}\right]
 \le C(r_T+\delta_T).
\]
The preliminary estimator is uniformly consistent, \(G_T(\theta^*)=O_P(T^{-1/2})\), and \(\partial_\theta G_T(\theta^*)-H=O_P(T^{-1/2})\) uniformly. Together with \(D_2\rho/\varepsilon<h_-/4\), these bounds imply that, with probability tending uniformly to one, there is a unique empirical root in the closed \(\rho\)-ball about \(\theta^*\), it is \(O_P(T^{-1/2})\) from \(\theta^*\), and it is the unique member of \(\mathcal Z_T\). Hence \(\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}P(E_T^c)\to0\), and
\[
 \sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)+H^{-1}T^{-1/2}S_T=O_P(e_T)
\]
uniformly. The strongest label-free distributional conclusion implied by these primitives is the \(P\)-specific finite-mixture approximation
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}\sup_{\|c\|_2=1}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}
 \left|P\left\{c^\top\sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)\le z\right\}
 -\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_P}p_{P,j}\Phi\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{c^\top V_{P,j}c}}\right)\right|\longrightarrow0.
\]
For every sequence \(P_T\in\mathfrak P_T\), there is a subsequence \(T_\ell\to\infty\), a fixed set \(\mathcal J_0\), and limits \(p_j^\infty\) and positive-definite \(V_j^\infty\) such that \(\mathcal J_{P_{T_\ell}}=\mathcal J_0\), \(p_{P_{T_\ell},j}\to p_j^\infty\), and \(V_{P_{T_\ell},j}\to V_j^\infty\) for every \(j\in\mathcal J_0\). Along this subsequence, every fixed contrast has the centered Gaussian-mixture limit with weights \(p_j^\infty\) and projected variances \(c^\top V_j^\infty c\), omitting zero limiting weights. If two positive limiting weights have unequal projected variances, this limit is not Gaussian. In general, the class primitives do not imply stable convergence relative to \(\sigma(J)\), even when all basin covariance matrices are equal.
