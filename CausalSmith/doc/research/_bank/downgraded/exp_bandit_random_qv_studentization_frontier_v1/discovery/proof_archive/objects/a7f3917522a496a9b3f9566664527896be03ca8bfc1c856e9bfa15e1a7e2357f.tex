Suppose first that one deterministic positive-definite \(\Sigma\) gives the asserted coverage for every nonzero \(c\) and every nominal level. Apply \(\mathrm{thm:contrastwise\text{-}deterministic\text{-}sandwich\text{-}iff}\) to each of the finitely many contrasts
\[
 \mathcal C_0=\{e_r:1\le r\le d\}\cup
 \{e_r+e_s:1\le r<s\le d\}. \tag{1}
\]
For each \(c\in\mathcal C_0\),
\[
 c^\top(V-\Sigma)c=0\qquad\mu\text{-almost surely}. \tag{2}
\]
Because \(\mathcal C_0\) is finite, the intersection of the probability-one events in (2) still has probability one. On that intersection put \(A=V-\Sigma\), which is symmetric. Taking \(c=e_r\) gives \(A_{rr}=0\). For \(r<s\), taking \(c=e_r+e_s\) and using the diagonal equalities gives
\[
 0=(e_r+e_s)^\top A(e_r+e_s)=2A_{rs}. \tag{3}
\]
Every entry of \(A\) vanishes, so \(V=\Sigma\) \(\mu\)-almost surely.

Conversely, if \(V=\Sigma\) \(\mu\)-almost surely, then \(c^\top Vc=c^\top\Sigma c\) almost surely for every nonzero \(c\). The contrastwise theorem gives coverage \(1-\alpha\) for every such \(c\) and every \(\alpha\in(0,1)\). This proves both directions and the point-mass formulation. Under the labelwise limits, the limiting law is \(\sum_jp_j^0\delta_{W_j}\) with every \(p_j^0>0\); it is the point mass at \(\Sigma\) if and only if \(W_j=\Sigma\) for every basin \(j\).
