The fixed states \(\bar\eta_1,\ldots,\bar\eta_m\in\mathsf H\) satisfy \(\|\bar\eta_j-\bar\eta_k\|_2\ge s_\eta\) whenever \(j\ne k\). There is a \(\{1,\ldots,m\}\)-valued random label \(J\), measurable with respect to \(\sigma(\bigcup_{t\ge1}\mathcal F_t)\), such that \(\eta_\infty=\bar\eta_J\) almost surely and \(P(J=j)\ge p_-\) for every \(j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\).
