Let \(T_\ell\to\infty\) and \(P_\ell\in\mathfrak P_{T_\ell}\) be a sequence or subsequence for which the \(P_\ell\)-specific mixture parameters in Theorem 1 have a fixed support \(\mathcal J_0\), \(p_{P_\ell,j}\to p_j^\infty\), and \(V_{P_\ell,j}\to V_j^\infty\) for every \(j\in\mathcal J_0\). Put \(\mathcal J_\infty=\{j\in\mathcal J_0:p_j^\infty>0\}\). Fix nonzero \(c\) and deterministic positive-definite \(\Sigma\). Then
\[
 \lim_{\ell\to\infty}P_\ell\{\tau\in C_{T_\ell}^{\Sigma}(c,\alpha)\}=1-\alpha
 \quad\text{for every }\alpha\in(0,1)
\]
if and only if \(c^\top V_j^\infty c=c^\top\Sigma c\) for every \(j\in\mathcal J_\infty\). The necessity identifies the law of the limiting finite centered Gaussian scale mixture from coverage at every nominal level; it is not a one-level variance-matching claim.
