The common derivative and bounded-IPW envelopes imply
\[
 \|H\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le KD_1,
 \qquad \omega_-I_d\preceq\Omega_\infty\preceq(KB^2/\varepsilon)I_d
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
There is a finite constant \(C\), independent of \(T\) and \(P\), such that
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}E_P\!\left[\left\|T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T-\Omega_\infty\right\|_{\mathrm{op}}\right]
 \le C(r_T+\delta_T).
\]
The preliminary estimator is uniformly consistent, \(G_T(\theta^*)=O_P(T^{-1/2})\), and \(\partial_\theta G_T(\theta^*)-H=O_P(T^{-1/2})\) uniformly. Together with \(D_2\rho/\varepsilon<h_-/4\), these bounds imply that, with probability tending uniformly to one, there is a unique empirical root in the closed \(\rho\)-ball about \(\theta^*\), it is \(O_P(T^{-1/2})\) from \(\theta^*\), and it is the unique member of \(\mathcal Z_T\). Hence \(\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}P(E_T^c)\to0\), and
\[
 \sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)+H^{-1}T^{-1/2}S_T=O_P(e_T)
\]
uniformly. The strongest terminal-representative-free distributional conclusion implied by these primitives is
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}\sup_{\|c\|_2=1}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}
 \left|P\left\{c^\top\sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)\le z\right\}
 -E_P\Phi\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{c^\top V_\infty c}}\right)\right|\longrightarrow0.
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \frac{\omega_-}{(KD_1)^2}I_d\preceq V_\infty
 \preceq\frac{KB^2}{\varepsilon h_-^2}I_d\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
Thus, for every sequence \(P_T\in\mathfrak P_T\), the laws of \(V_\infty\) have a weakly convergent subsequence with a limiting law \(\mu\) supported on the displayed compact covariance set. Along that subsequence, every fixed nonzero contrast has the centered Gaussian scale-mixture limit \(\sqrt{c^\top Vc}\,Z_1\), where \(V\sim\mu\), \(Z_1\sim N(0,1)\), and \(V\) and \(Z_1\) are independent. This limit is Gaussian if and only if \(c^\top Vc\) is \(\mu\)-almost surely constant. In general, the class primitives do not imply stable convergence relative to \(\sigma(\eta_\infty)\), even when \(V_\infty\) is deterministic.
