Fix \(K=2\), draw \(X_t\) independently and uniformly from \(\{-4,1\}\), and set \(Y_t(1)=1/2+\sigma_1R_{t,1}\) and \(Y_t(2)=1/12+\sigma_2R_{t,2}\), where the \(R_{t,a}\) are independent centered Rademacher innovations and \(\sigma_1,\sigma_2>0\) are arbitrary fixed constants declared as part of this bounded analogue; no numerical innovation variances are attributed to Guo and Xu. Keep their no-intercept working model, ridge constant \(\lambda=1\), unit-precision ridge initialization, and updates \(\Phi^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,a}=\Phi^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t-1,a}+\mathbf 1\{A_t=a\}X_t^2\), \(b^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,a}=b^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t-1,a}+\mathbf 1\{A_t=a\}X_tY_t\), and \(\widehat\beta^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,a}=b^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,a}/\Phi^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,a}\). For \(\eta_0\in\mathsf K_{\mathrm{GX}}\), initialize \(Z^{\mathrm{GX}}_0=\iota_{\mathrm{GX}}(\eta_0)=(\eta_{0,1},\eta_{0,2},1,1)^\top\), equivalently \(\Phi^{\mathrm{GX}}_{0,a}=1\) and \(b^{\mathrm{GX}}_{0,a}=\eta_{0,a}\); the published initialization is the included case \(\eta_0=(0,0)^\top\). At occasion \(t\), break every exact index tie in favor of arm \(1\), maximize \(\widehat\beta^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t-1,a}X_t+|X_t|\sqrt{q^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t-1,a}}\), and then assign the maximizing arm with probability \(0.99\) and the other arm with probability \(0.01\). The process of interest is the slope projection \((\widehat\beta^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,1},\widehat\beta^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,2})^\top\) of \(Z^{\mathrm{GX}}_t\), not a slope-only Markov chain.
