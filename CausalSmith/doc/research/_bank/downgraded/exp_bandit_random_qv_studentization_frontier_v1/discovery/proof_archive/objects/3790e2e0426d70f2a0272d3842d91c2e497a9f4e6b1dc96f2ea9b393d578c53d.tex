Write \(Z^{\mathrm{GX}}_t=(\beta_1,\beta_2,q_1,q_2)^\top\) for a current augmented state. Its induced armwise update is \(q_a^+=q_a/(1+\mathbf 1\{A=a\}q_aX^2)\) and \(\beta_a^+=(\beta_a+\mathbf 1\{A=a\}q_aXY(a))/(1+\mathbf 1\{A=a\}q_aX^2)\). The next clipped-UCB action uses \(\beta_aX+|X|\sqrt{q_a}\), so \(Z^{\mathrm{GX}}_t\), rather than its two-dimensional slope projection, is the time-homogeneous Markov state. For stochastic-approximation analysis, put \(D_{t,a}=\Phi^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,a}\), \(N_{t,a}=b^{\mathrm{GX}}_{t,a}\), and, for \(t\ge1\), \(\Psi_t=(D_{t,1}/t,D_{t,2}/t,N_{t,1}/t,N_{t,2}/t)\). Analyze the exact \(1/(t+1)\) recursion for \(\Psi_t\) on an eventually compact region with both normalized Gram coordinates bounded away from zero. At the equal-slope surface, retain every limiting conditional-mean mode generated by the square-root UCB bonus: although the bonus is \(O(t^{-1/2})\), it can make the same arm greedy at both contexts inside an \(O(t^{-1/2})\) switching layer. Thus a closed convexified limiting graph must include these same-arm modes as well as the two opposite-context modes. Any differential-inclusion reduction must verify the closed-graph, compact-convex-value, boundedness, vanishing-perturbation, and martingale-tail conditions for this normalized recursion. Compact-uniform sequences \(r_T(K_0),\delta_T(K_0)\to0\) require a separate quantitative hitting or escape estimate and may not be inferred from pointwise convergence by a finite-cover argument.
