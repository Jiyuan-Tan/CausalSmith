Let \((X_{n,t},\mathcal G_{n,t})_{t=1}^{N_n}\) be a scalar martingale-difference triangular array, where \(N_n\) and \(h_n\) are deterministic integers satisfying \(0\le h_n<N_n\), and suppose \(|X_{n,t}|\le L<\infty\) almost surely. Let \(\lambda_n\) be a positive \(\mathcal G_{n,h_n}\)-measurable random variable, and let \(\nu_n>0\) be deterministic with \(\lambda_n\ge\nu_n\) almost surely. Define
\[
 S_n=\sum_{t=1}^{N_n}X_{n,t},\qquad
 Q_n=\sum_{t=1}^{N_n}X_{n,t}^2,\qquad
 A_{n,+}^2=\sum_{t=h_n+1}^{N_n}E[X_{n,t}^2\mid\mathcal G_{n,t-1}].
\]
If
\[
 N_n\nu_n\longrightarrow\infty,qquad
 \frac{h_n}{N_n\nu_n}\longrightarrow0,qquad
 \frac{A_{n,+}^2}{N_n\lambda_n}\longrightarrow1
 \quad\text{in probability},
\]
then
\[
 \frac{Q_n}{N_n\lambda_n}\longrightarrow1
 \quad\text{in probability},\qquad
 P(Q_n>0)\longrightarrow1,qquad
 \frac{S_n}{\sqrt{Q_n}}\Rightarrow N(0,1).
\]
All conclusions are uniform over a collection of arrays if \(L\) is common and each displayed convergence premise is uniform over that collection.
