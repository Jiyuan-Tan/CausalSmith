Let \(F:\mathbb R^r\rightrightarrows\mathbb R^r\) satisfy the standing differential-inclusion hypotheses. If \(V:\mathbb R^r\to\mathbb R\) is a continuous Lyapunov function for \(\Lambda\), and \(V(\Lambda)\) has empty interior, then every internally chain-transitive set \(L\) is contained in \(\Lambda\) and \(V\) is constant on \(L\).
