Fix a prespecified \(c\in\mathbb R^d\setminus\{0\}\) and an early-stabilized projected class \(\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)\) with deterministic \(k_T\), early-measurable scale \(\Lambda_T\), and lower scale \(\omega_{c,T}\) as in \(\mathrm{def:projected\text{-}qv\text{-}growth\text{-}ipwz\text{-}class}\). Then the exact-root event is uniformly asymptotically certain and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}P(E_T^c)\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 \sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)+H^{-1}T^{-1/2}S_T
 =O_P(T^{-1/2})
\]
uniformly. Moreover,
\[
 T^{-1}([S]_T-\langle S\rangle_T)=O_P(T^{-1/2}),\qquad
 \widehat\Omega_T-T^{-1}[S]_T=O_P(T^{-1/2}),\qquad
 \widehat H_T-H=O_P(T^{-1/2})
\]
uniformly. With \(a=H^{-\top}c\), the directional realized variation obeys the sharper relative conclusion
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}
 P\!\left\{\left|\frac{a^\top[S]_Ta}{T\Lambda_T}-1\right|>x\right\}
 \longrightarrow0\qquad\text{for every }x>0.
\]
No predictable-variation growth or convergence is required outside the direction \(H^{-\top}c\). The contrast-specific truncation is inactive,
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}P\{\mathcal E_T(c)^c\}
 \longrightarrow0,
\]
and the natural information condition \(T\omega_{c,T}\to\infty\) is sufficient for
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}
 \left|P\{T_T^{\mathrm{rqv}}(c)\le z\}-\Phi(z)\right|
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Consequently, for every fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1)\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)}
 \left|P\{\tau\in I_T^{\mathrm{rqv}}(c,\alpha)\}-(1-\alpha)\right|
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Every finite-tail-attractor class \(\mathfrak P_T\) is covered for each fixed nonzero \(c\): an explicit early scale is obtained by choosing \(k_T=\lceil T\max\{T^{-1/2},\sqrt{r_T}\}\rceil\), selecting the nearest attractor from the first \(k_T\) policy states, and using its directional basin variance. Thus the same realized-QV pivot and Wald coverage hold uniformly over \(\mathfrak P_T\). There is no corollary from a lower predictable-QV floor alone.
