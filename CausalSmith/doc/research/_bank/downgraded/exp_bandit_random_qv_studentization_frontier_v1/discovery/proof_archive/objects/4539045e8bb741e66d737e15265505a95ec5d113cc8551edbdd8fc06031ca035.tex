Fix a prespecified \(c\in\mathbb R^d\setminus\{0\}\), the common objects \(\varepsilon,B,D_1,D_2,h_-,\kappa,s_\Theta,\rho,\Theta\), deterministic tolerance sequences \((r_T,\delta_T)_{T\ge1}\), deterministic integers \(0\le k_T<T\), and deterministic numbers \(\omega_{c,T}>0\) satisfying
\[
 T\omega_{c,T}\longrightarrow\infty,
 \qquad
 \frac{k_T}{T\omega_{c,T}}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Let \((\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c))_{T\ge1}\) be any chosen sequence of law classes such that every \(P\in\mathfrak Q_T^{\mathrm{proj}}(c)\) satisfies
\[
 \mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}full\text{-}data},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:sequential\text{-}randomization},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}overlap},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:bounded\text{-}score},
\]
\[
 \mathrm{ass:smooth\text{-}z\text{-}map},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}root\text{-}separation},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:root\text{-}interior},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:jacobian\text{-}nonsingular},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:vanishing\text{-}modulus}.
\]
For each member law there must exist a positive random scale \(\Lambda_T\), measurable with respect to \(\mathcal F_{k_T}\), such that \(\Lambda_T\ge\omega_{c,T}\) almost surely and the chosen class sequence satisfies \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}projected\text{-}predictable\text{-}qv\text{-}growth}\). No information-growth condition is imposed in a direction orthogonal to \(H^{-\top}c\), and neither finite attractors nor convergence of the full matrix \(T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T\) is required.
