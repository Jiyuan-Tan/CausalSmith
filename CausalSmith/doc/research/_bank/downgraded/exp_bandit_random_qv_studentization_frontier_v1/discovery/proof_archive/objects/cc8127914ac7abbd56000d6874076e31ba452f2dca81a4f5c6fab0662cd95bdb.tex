Let \((X_{n,t},\mathcal G_{n,t})_{t=1}^{k_n}\) be a scalar martingale-difference array, \(S_n=\sum_tX_{n,t}\), \(Q_n=\sum_tX_{n,t}^2\), and \(A_n^2=\sum_tE[X_{n,t}^2\mid\mathcal G_{n,t-1}]\). If \(A_n^2\to\infty\) in probability, \(P(Q_n>0)\to1\), \(Q_n/A_n^2\to1\) in probability, and for some \(\delta>0\),
\[
 A_n^{-(2+\delta)}\sum_tE[|X_{n,t}|^{2+\delta}]\to0,
\]
then \(S_n/\sqrt{Q_n}\Rightarrow N(0,1)\).
