There exist a fixed integer \(m\ge1\), fixed deterministic states \(\bar\eta_1,\ldots,\bar\eta_m\in\mathsf H\), and a \(\sigma(\bigcup_{t\ge1}\mathcal F_t)\)-measurable label \(J\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\) such that \(\eta_\infty=\bar\eta_J\) almost surely, \(\min_{j\ne k}\|\bar\eta_j-\bar\eta_k\|_2\ge s_\eta\) when \(m\ge2\), and \(P(J=j)\ge p_-\) for every \(j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\). The constants \(m\), \(s_\eta>0\), and \(p_->0\), and the attractor states are common across horizons and member laws.
