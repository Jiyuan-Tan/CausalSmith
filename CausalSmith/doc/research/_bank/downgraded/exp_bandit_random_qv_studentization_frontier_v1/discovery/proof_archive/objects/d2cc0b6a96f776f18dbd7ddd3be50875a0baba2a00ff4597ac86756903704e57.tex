\]

 \mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}full\text{-}data},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:sequential\text{-}randomization},\quad

 \mathrm{ass:bounded\text{-}score},

 \mathrm{ass:smooth\text{-}z\text{-}map},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}root\text{-}separation},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:root\text{-}interior},\quad
 \mathrm{ass:jacobian\text{-}nonsingular},\quad

For each member law, the class includes a positive \(\mathcal F_{k_T}\)-measurable variable \(\Lambda_T\) satisfying \(\Lambda_T\ge\omega_{c,T}\) almost surely, and the chosen class sequence satisfies \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}projected\text{-}predictable\text{-}qv\text{-}growth}\).
