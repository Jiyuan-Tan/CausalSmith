Let
\[
 D_t=\xi_t\xi_t^\top-E_P[\xi_t\xi_t^\top\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}].
\]
Each matrix entry of \(D_t\) is a martingale difference. Since the bounded-score and overlap assumptions give \(\|\xi_t\|_2\le B/\varepsilon\), martingale orthogonality and fixed \(d\) imply
\[
 E_P\left\|T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^TD_t\right\|_{\mathrm F}^2\le C/T.
\]
The operator norm is bounded by the Frobenius norm, so
\[
 T^{-1}([S]_T-\langle S\rangle_T)=O_P(T^{-1/2})
\tag{16}
\]
uniformly.

On the high-probability exact-root event proved in \(\mathrm{thm:basin\text{-}stable\text{-}ipwz}\), \(\|\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*\|_2=O_P(T^{-1/2})\). The derivative and overlap bounds give
\[
 \|\widehat\xi_t-\xi_t\|_2
 \le\frac{D_1}{\varepsilon}\|\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*\|_2.
\]
Both scores have norm at most \(B/\varepsilon\); hence
\[
 \|\widehat\xi_t\widehat\xi_t^\top-\xi_t\xi_t^\top\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le\frac{2BD_1}{\varepsilon^2}\|\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*\|_2.
\]
After averaging and combining this inequality with (16) and the predictable-QV bound from \(\mathrm{thm:basin\text{-}stable\text{-}ipwz}\), we obtain
\[
 \widehat\Omega_T-\Omega_J
 =O_P(T^{-1/2}+r_T+\delta_T)=O_P(e_T)
\tag{17}
\]
uniformly. Likewise, smoothness gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \|\widehat H_T-H\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 &\le\|\partial_\theta G_T(\theta^*)-H\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 +\frac{D_2}{\varepsilon}\|\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*\|_2\\
 &=O_P(T^{-1/2})=O_P(e_T).
\end{aligned}
\tag{18}
\]
The fallback event has uniformly vanishing probability, so restoring it changes none of these uniform order statements.

By (18) and \(\sigma_{\min}(H)\ge h_-\), \(\sigma_{\min}(\widehat H_T)\ge h_-/2\) with probability tending uniformly to one. Regime nondegeneracy and (17) similarly imply \(\lambda_{\min}(\widehat\Omega_T)\ge\omega_-/2\) with probability tending uniformly to one. Therefore
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}P(\mathcal E_T^c)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{19}
\]
On \(\mathcal E_T\),
\[
 \widehat H_T^{-1}-H^{-1}=\widehat H_T^{-1}(H-\widehat H_T)H^{-1},
\]
and \(\|\widehat H_T^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le2/h_-\). Expanding the two inverse factors and the middle covariance factor in the sandwich, and using the common covariance upper bound from \(\mathrm{thm:basin\text{-}stable\text{-}ipwz}\), gives
\[
 \widehat V_T-V_J=O_P(e_T)
\tag{20}
\]
uniformly. The identity-matrix branch makes the statistic total at finite \(T\) and, by (19), is entered with uniformly vanishing probability.

Fix \(\|c\|_2=1\), put \(a=H^{-\top}c\), and set \(U_t=a^\top\xi_t\). The covariance and Jacobian bounds in \(\mathrm{thm:basin\text{-}stable\text{-}ipwz}\) give
\[
 (KD_1)^{-1}\le\|a\|_2\le h_-^{-1},
 \qquad |U_t|\le B/(\varepsilon h_-).
\tag{21}
\]
Moreover, its predictable-QV bound and regime nondegeneracy imply, with probability tending uniformly to one,
\[
 A_T^2:=\sum_{t=1}^TE_P[U_t^2\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}]
 =a^\top\langle S\rangle_Ta
 \ge\frac{\omega_-}{2(KD_1)^2}T.
\tag{22}
\]
Equation (16) also gives
\[
 a^\top([S]_T-\langle S\rangle_T)a=O_P(\sqrt T),
\]
so \(a^\top[S]_Ta/A_T^2\to_P1\) uniformly and \(P\{a^\top[S]_Ta>0\}\to1\) uniformly. The fourth-moment condition in \(\mathrm{lem:saco\text{-}self\text{-}normalized\text{-}martingale\text{-}clt}\) holds with \(\delta=2\): by (21)--(22),
\[
 \sum_{t=1}^TE_P|U_t|^4\le T\left(\frac{B}{\varepsilon h_-}\right)^4,
 \qquad A_T^4\ge C_-T^2
\]
outside an event of uniformly vanishing probability. Every premise of that cited martingale theorem has now been derived, and it yields
\[
 \frac{a^\top S_T}{\sqrt{a^\top[S]_Ta}}\Rightarrow N(0,1).
\tag{23}
\]
This convergence is uniform in \(P\) and unit \(c\): otherwise a violating sequence \((P_T,c_T)\) would itself be a triangular array satisfying the same common increment bound, variance-growth bound, QV-ratio convergence, and fourth-moment condition, contradicting the cited theorem.

The exact-root linearization and the feasible covariance expansions give
\[
 \sqrt T\,c^\top(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)
 =-T^{-1/2}a^\top S_T+O_P(e_T)
\tag{24}
\]
and
\[
 c^\top\widehat V_Tc=T^{-1}a^\top[S]_Ta+O_P(e_T)
\tag{25}
\]
uniformly. By (17), (19), and regime nondegeneracy, the denominator in (25) is bounded away from zero with probability tending uniformly to one. Division is legitimate there, and (23)--(25) give
\[
 T_T^{\mathrm{rqv}}(c)
 =-\frac{a^\top S_T}{\sqrt{a^\top[S]_Ta}}+o_P(1)
\]
uniformly. For any \(\eta>0\), the same two-sided event inclusion just displayed bounds the Kolmogorov distance after adding the \(o_P(1)\) remainder by its pre-remainder distance, \(P\{|R_T|>\eta\}\), and \(\sup_z\{\Phi(z+\eta)-\Phi(z-\eta)\}\). The first two terms vanish uniformly and the last tends to zero with \(\eta\), proving the asserted uniform Kolmogorov convergence. Consequently
\[
 P\{|N(0,1)|\le z_{1-\alpha/2}\}=1-\alpha,
\]
which gives the corresponding realized-QV Wald coverage at every fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1)\).

For comparison with the cited Saco result, the mapping is only at the martingale-score level. The i.i.d.-full-data assumption corresponds to superpopulation arrivals; the logged policy definition together with sequential randomization gives logging integrity and no anticipation; uniform overlap gives executed overlap; the scored set here is deterministically \(\{1,\ldots,T\}\); bounded \(g\) gives the required score moments; and (22) derives variance growth. Predictable nuisance construction and nuisance stability are vacuous here because the oracle score entering (23) has no fitted outcome-regression nuisance. Saco's Theorem 5.14 is nevertheless an AIPW/DML mean theorem with its own outcome-moment and feasible-nuisance hypotheses, whereas the present result is for a smooth IPW-Z root with an estimated Jacobian and event-truncated matrix sandwich. The classes are therefore not literally nested. What is proved here is uniform verification of the corresponding score-level self-normalization conditions over the finite-Cesaro-regime class, not a strict estimator-class tightening. Neither this comparison nor any preceding proof step invokes pointwise convergence of the policy state.
