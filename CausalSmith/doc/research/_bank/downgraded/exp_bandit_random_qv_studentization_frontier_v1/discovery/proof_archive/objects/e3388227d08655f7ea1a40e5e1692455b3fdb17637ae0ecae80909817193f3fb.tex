Pointwise self-normalized results do not justify Gaussian inference when an early score can endogenously select later information. Saco treats a distinct logged-propensity martingale AIPW/DML setup; Shen and coauthors impose directional stability for a different estimator and class; Guo--Xu study stabilized scaled inverse propensities; and Leiner, Dunn, and Ramdas use flexible variance stabilization for stationary-policy off-policy targets. None of these classes has been proved identical to, a subclass of, or a superclass of the present early-stabilized projected IPW--Z class, so no wholesale inclusion transfer is asserted.
