For the common envelopes defining \(\mathfrak P_T\), the existing derivative and bounded-IPW envelopes imply the nonprimitive upper bounds \(\|H\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le KD_1\) and \(\Omega_j\preceq(KB^2/\varepsilon)I_d\) for every \(j\in\mathcal J\). There is a finite constant \(C\), independent of \(T\) and \(P\), such that \(\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}E_P[\|T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T-\Omega_J\|_{\mathrm{op}}]\le C(r_T+\delta_T)\). The preliminary estimator is uniformly consistent, and bounded-IPW martingale concentration gives \(G_T(\theta^*)=O_P(T^{-1/2})\) and \(\partial_\theta G_T(\theta^*)-H=O_P(T^{-1/2})\) uniformly. Together with \(D_2\rho/\varepsilon<h_-/4\), these bounds imply by a quantitative inverse-function argument that, with probability tending uniformly to one, there is a unique empirical root in the closed \(\rho\)-ball about \(\theta^*\), it lies at distance \(O_P(T^{-1/2})\) from \(\theta^*\), and it belongs to \(\mathcal Z_T\). Hence \(\sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}P(E_T^c)\to0\), \(G_T(\widehat\theta_T)=0\) on \(E_T\), and the fallback \(\widehat\theta_T=\widetilde\theta_T\) occurs only on a uniformly negligible event. With \(e_T=T^{-1/2}+r_T+\delta_T\), uniformly over \(P\in\mathfrak P_T\), the exact-root linearization is \(\sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)+H^{-1}T^{-1/2}S_T=O_P(e_T)\). Moreover, for every unit vector \(c\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathfrak P_T}\sup_{\|c\|_2=1}\sup_{z\in\mathbb R}\left|P\{c^\top\sqrt T(\widehat\theta_T-\theta^*)\le z\}-\sum_{j\in\mathcal J}p_j\Phi\!\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{c^\top V_jc}}\right)\right|\to0.
\]
Thus every convergent subsequence of the finite mixture parameters has the corresponding centered Gaussian-mixture limit. If two positive-mass limiting basins have different values of \(c^\top V_jc\), that limit is non-Gaussian.
