Saco's Theorem~5.14 asserts realized-QV and feasible Studentized normality under its enumerated AIPW/DML assumptions plus predictable-QV growth, but the audited bounded two-regime specialization satisfies those assumptions and has a noncentered non-Gaussian self-normalized limit.  The missing premise is not boundedness: the source outcome and nuisance fourth moments already transfer to an \(L^4\) AIPW-score envelope.  What fails is temporal separation between the information used to select a variance regime and the score mass normalized by that regime.  Guo--Xu and Shen and coauthors supply adjacent stable-policy and directional-stability results, Han studies an orthogonal SDE-invariant Thompson-sampling regime, Leiner--Dunn--Ramdas allow unstable logging for a stationary evaluation-policy target, and Zhang--Janson--Murphy study pooled longitudinal adaptive sampling in a different asymptotic regime.  Existing stochastic-approximation results do not supply the required uniform attraction or early-information modulus for the Guo--Xu recursion.
