Let \(p\in[1,\infty)\), \(\gamma>0\), and let \((X_i,\mathcal G_i)_{i=1}^n\) be a scalar martingale-difference sequence with \(n\ge2\) and \(\max_i\|X_i\|_\infty\le\gamma\). Put \(s^2=\sum_{i=1}^nE[X_i^2]\), \(V^2=s^{-2}\sum_{i=1}^nE[X_i^2\mid\mathcal G_{i-1}]\), and \(D=\sup_x|P\{s^{-1}\sum_iX_i\le x\}-\Phi(x)|\). There is a constant \(C_{p,\gamma}\) such that
\[
D\le C_{p,\gamma}\left\{\frac{n\log n}{s^3}+\left(\|V^2-1\|_p^p+s^{-2p}\right)^{1/(2p+1)}\right\}.
\]
