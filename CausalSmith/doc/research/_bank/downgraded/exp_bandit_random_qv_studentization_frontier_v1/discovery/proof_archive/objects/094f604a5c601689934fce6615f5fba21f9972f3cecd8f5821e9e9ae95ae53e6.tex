For every \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/6)\), the bounded two-state witness \(P^{\dagger}\) belongs at every \(T\ge2\) to a class \(\mathfrak P_T\) with common choices \(B=2\), \(D_1=D_2=1\), \(h_-=1\), \(L_\Pi=1/12\), \(\omega_-=1\), \(\kappa(u)=u\), \(s_\Theta=1\), \(\rho=\varepsilon/8\), \(r_T=1/(2T)\), and \(\delta_T=0\). The terminal representative satisfies \(P^{\dagger}(\eta_\infty=0)=2/5\) and \(P^{\dagger}(\eta_\infty=1)=3/5\), and
\[
 V_\infty=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)\mathbf1\{\eta_\infty=0\}
 +\operatorname{diag}(1,3)\mathbf1\{\eta_\infty=1\}.
\]
Hence, for \(\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)\) and \(c=(1,0)^\top\), \(c^\top V_\infty c=c^\top\Sigma c=1\) almost surely although \(V_\infty\) is nondegenerate. Thus contrastwise deterministic-sandwich validity can hold while matrixwise invariance fails, even inside the bounded terminal-representative class.
