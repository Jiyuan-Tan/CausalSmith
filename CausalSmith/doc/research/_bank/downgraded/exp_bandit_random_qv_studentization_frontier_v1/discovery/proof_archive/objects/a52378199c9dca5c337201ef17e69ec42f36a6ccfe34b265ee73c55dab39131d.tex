In the setting of Theorem 3, suppose the limiting covariance law has finite support \(\mu=\sum_{j=1}^r p_j\delta_{W_j}\), where \(r<\infty\), every \(p_j>0\), \(\sum_jp_j=1\), and every \(W_j\) is positive definite. For a fixed nonzero \(c\), the deterministic sandwich \(\Sigma\) has asymptotic coverage \(1-\alpha\) at every \(\alpha\in(0,1)\) if and only if \(c^\top W_jc=c^\top\Sigma c\) for every \(j\). The same \(\Sigma\) is valid at every level for every nonzero contrast if and only if \(W_j=\Sigma\) for every \(j\).
