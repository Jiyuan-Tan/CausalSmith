For fixed \(\varepsilon,B,D_1,D_2,h_-,\omega_-,\kappa,s_\Theta,\rho\), fixed compact \(\Theta\), and common deterministic sequences \((r_T,\delta_T)_{T\ge1}\), define
\[
 \mathfrak Q_T=\left\{P:
 \begin{array}{l}
 P\text{ satisfies }\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}full\text{-}data},
 \mathrm{ass:sequential\text{-}randomization},
 \mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}overlap},
 \mathrm{ass:bounded\text{-}score},\\
 \mathrm{ass:smooth\text{-}z\text{-}map},
 \mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}root\text{-}separation},
 \mathrm{ass:root\text{-}interior},
 \mathrm{ass:jacobian\text{-}nonsingular},
 \mathrm{ass:vanishing\text{-}modulus}
 \end{array}\right\}.
\]
The sequence of classes \((\mathfrak Q_T)_{T\ge1}\) is additionally required to satisfy \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}predictable\text{-}qv\text{-}growth}\). In this definition, \(r_T\) and \(\delta_T\) enter only through the numerical approximation tolerance in \(\mathrm{def:ipw\text{-}z\text{-}estimator}\); no state-attraction interpretation is imposed. In particular, membership does not require \(m\), \(J\), \(\eta_\infty\), fixed attractors, attractor separation, basin masses, Cesaro attraction, convergence of \(T^{-1}\langle S\rangle_T\), or a terminal covariance.
