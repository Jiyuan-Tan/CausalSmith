Let \(0<a\le b<\infty\), and let \((U_n)\) be random symmetric \(d\)-by-\(d\) matrices satisfying \(aI_d\preceq U_n\preceq bI_d\) almost surely. There are a subsequence \((n_k)\) and a Borel probability measure \(\mu\), supported on \(\mathcal K_{a,b}=\{u=u^\top:aI_d\preceq u\preceq bI_d\}\), such that \(E[f(U_{n_k})]\to\int f(u)\,d\mu(u)\) for every continuous \(f:\mathcal K_{a,b}\to\mathbb R\).
