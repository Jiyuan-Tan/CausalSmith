Saco's Theorem 5.14 gives a pointwise realized-QV pivot for a distinct logged-propensity martingale AIPW/DML setup, and Appendix A records a self-normalized martingale CLT under its variance-ratio and Lyapunov premises. The present paper does not transfer that theorem from a predictable-QV floor; it proves an early-measurable normalization lemma and verifies its premises for bounded adaptive IPW--Z scores. Guo and Xu prove Gaussian IPW--Z limits after scaled inverse propensities stabilize; their no-intercept two-mode example motivates the dynamics question but does not certify the present finite-attractor class. Shen and coauthors study deterministic directional stability, while Leiner, Dunn, and Ramdas study stationary-evaluation-policy off-policy M-estimation with flexible variance stabilization. These four classes are not established as identical or nested with the present class. Borkar supplies local lock-in tools, Mourrat supplies bounded-martingale approximation ingredients, and Qian and coauthors together with Liao and coauthors define the HeartSteps causal-excursion consumer. The finite Gaussian-mixture identification results support the separate deterministic-sandwich converses.
