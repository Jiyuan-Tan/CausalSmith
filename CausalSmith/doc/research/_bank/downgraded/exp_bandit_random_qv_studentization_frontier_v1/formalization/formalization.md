## Assumptions (A)

**A-1 (assumption).** The vectors \(W_1,\ldots,W_T\) are independent and identically distributed under \(P\).

**A-2 (assumption).** Conditional on \(X_t\) and \(\mathcal F_{t-1}\), the action \(A_t\) is drawn from the logged distribution \(\pi_t(\cdot\mid X_t,\mathcal F_{t-1})\) independently of \((Y_t(a))_{a\in\mathcal A}\).

**A-3 (assumption).** For every \(a\in\mathcal A\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and \(\eta\in\mathsf H\), the policy satisfies \(\Pi(a\mid x,\eta)\ge\varepsilon\).

**A-4 (assumption).** The common envelope satisfies \(\sup_{a\in\mathcal A,\theta\in\Theta}\|g(a,X_t,Y_t(a);\theta)\|_2\le B\) almost surely.

**A-5 (assumption).** The map \(\theta\mapsto g(a,x,y;\theta)\) is continuously differentiable on a common open neighborhood of \(\operatorname{conv}(\Theta)\), with \(\|\partial_\theta g(a,x,y;\theta)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le D_1\) there, and is twice continuously differentiable on the closed \(\rho\)-ball about \(\theta^*\), with \(\|\partial_\theta^2 g(a,x,y;\theta)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le D_2\) throughout that ball.

**A-6 (assumption).** For every \(u>0\), \(\inf_{\theta\in\Theta:\|\theta-\theta^*\|_2\ge u}\|M_P(\theta)\|_2\ge\kappa(u)\).

**A-7 (assumption).** The causal root satisfies \(\operatorname{dist}(\theta^*,\partial\Theta)\ge s_\Theta\).

**A-8 (assumption).** The population Jacobian satisfies \(\sigma_{\min}(H)\ge h_-\).

**A-9 (assumption).** The common policy modulus satisfies \(\sup_{a\in\mathcal A,x\in\mathcal X}|\Pi(a\mid x,\eta)-\Pi(a\mid x,\eta')|\le L_\Pi\|\eta-\eta'\|_2\) for all \(\eta,\eta'\in\mathsf H\).

**A-10 (assumption).** There exists a \(\sigma(\bigcup_{t\ge1}\mathcal F_t)\)-measurable \(\{1,\ldots,m\}\)-valued random variable \(J\) such that \(\eta_\infty=\bar\eta_J\) almost surely.

**A-11 (assumption).** The member law obeys \(P\{T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^T\|\eta_{t-1}-\bar\eta_J\|_2>r_T\}\le\delta_T\).

**A-12 (assumption).** The common deterministic error envelope satisfies \(e_T=T^{-1/2}+r_T+\delta_T\to0\).

**A-13 (assumption).** The terminal covariance satisfies \(\Omega_\infty\succeq\omega_-I_d\) almost surely.

**A-14 (assumption).** For all distinct \(j,k\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\), \(\|\bar\eta_j-\bar\eta_k\|_2\ge s_\eta\).

**A-15 (assumption).** For every \(j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\), \(P(J=j)\ge p_-\).
