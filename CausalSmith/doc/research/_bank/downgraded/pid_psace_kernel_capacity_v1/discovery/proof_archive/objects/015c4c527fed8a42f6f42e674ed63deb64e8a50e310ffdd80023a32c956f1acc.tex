For rational empirical marginals and rational numerical radii \(\varepsilon_{ga}(\alpha),\kappa,d\), \(\mathsf{ProfileTV}\) has a face-complete exact completion by cylindrical algebraic decomposition.  For each \(g\), it returns the real-valued part of \(\mathcal I_{1-\alpha,g}(d)\) as a finite union of closed intervals and isolated real-algebraic points, with an attaining \((p,\mathbf K)\) for every boundary point, and it returns whether \(\bot_{\mathrm{inc}}\) is present.  It returns \(\mathcal D_{1-\alpha}^{\mathrm{all}}\cap[0,1]\) as a finite union of real-algebraic points and intervals with exact open/closed endpoint flags, and returns whether \(+\infty\) is present.  The algorithm terminates after finitely many sign-invariant cells for every rational input, including empty confidence regions, tied active cuts, compatibility boundaries, and sign-radius boundaries.
