Let \(P\) be a nonempty polyhedron and let \(a,b\) be affine functionals such that \(b(v)>0\) on \(P\).  The substitution \(t=1/b(v)\) and \(y=tv\) converts minimization or maximization of \(a(v)/b(v)\) over \(P\) into a linear program with normalization \(b(y,t)=1\), preserves objective values, and recovers every optimizer by \(v=y/t\).
