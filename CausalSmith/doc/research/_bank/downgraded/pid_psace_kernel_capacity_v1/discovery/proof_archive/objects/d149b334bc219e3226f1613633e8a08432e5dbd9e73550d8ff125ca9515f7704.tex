Fix \(g\in\mathcal G\) and \(a\in\{0,1\}\).  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:arm-overlap}\) makes conditioning on \(G=g,A=a\) well defined.  For every \(b\in\mathcal X\), consistency gives
\[
 \Pr\{X^{\mathrm{obs}}=b\mid G=g,A=a\}
 =\Pr\{X(a)=b\mid G=g,A=a\}.
\]
By \(\texttt{ass:randomization}\), the right side is
\(\Pr\{X(a)=b\mid G=g\}\).  Taking \(a=0\) and using the definition of \(\mu_g^0\) proves \(\pi_{g0}^{\mathrm{obs}}(b)=\mu_g^0(b)\); taking \(a=1\) and using the definition of \(\nu_g\) proves \(\pi_{g1}^{\mathrm{obs}}(b)=\nu_g(b)\).  Since this holds in every coordinate \(b\), the two asserted vector equalities follow.
