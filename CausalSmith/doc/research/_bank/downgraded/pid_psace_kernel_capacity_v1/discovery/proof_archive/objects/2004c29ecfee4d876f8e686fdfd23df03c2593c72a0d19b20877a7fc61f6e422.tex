Let \(p^0=(\mu_g^0,\nu_g)_{g\in\mathcal G}\).  By \(\texttt{lem:observed-arm-identification}\), these are the true observed-arm probability vectors.  Conditional on the sample sizes, apply \(\texttt{lem:weissman-multinomial-l1}\) separately to each of the \(2m\) independent multinomial empirical laws.

Put \(C_4=2^4-2\).  If
\(\sqrt{2n_{ga}^{-1}\log(C_4,2m/\alpha)}<2\), the cited inequality gives
\[
 \Pr\!\left\{\|\widehat p_{ga}-p^0_{ga}\|_1>
 \varepsilon_{ga}(\alpha)\mid(n_{g'a'})_{g',a'}\right\}
 \le C_4\exp\{-n_{ga}\varepsilon_{ga}(\alpha)^2/2\}
 =\frac{\alpha}{2m}.
\]
If the square root is at least two, then \(\varepsilon_{ga}(\alpha)=2\) and the failure event is empty because the \(\ell_1\) distance between two probability vectors is at most two.  A union bound, which does not require independence, therefore yields
\[
 \Pr\!\left\{
 \|\widehat p_{ga}-p^0_{ga}\|_1\le\varepsilon_{ga}(\alpha)
 \text{ for every }g,a
 \right\}\ge1-\alpha.
\]
The inequality holds conditionally on the sample sizes and hence also unconditionally by averaging.  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:positive-first-stage}\) says that the true vectors also obey every first-stage inequality in \(\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}^{\kappa}\).  Thus
\[
 \Pr\{p^0\in\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}^{\kappa}\}\ge1-\alpha.
\]

On this one event, \(\texttt{lem:projection-set-inclusion}\) simultaneously includes every augmented true fixed-radius fiber and the true all-trial radius in their projections.  If the true class at radius \(d\) is compatible, \(\texttt{thm:sharp-psace-endpoints}\) identifies its real fiber with the attained interval
\([L_g^{\mathrm{pr}}(d),U_g^{\mathrm{pr}}(d)]\); hence the interval hull of the real-valued portion of the projected fiber contains both endpoints.  If it is incompatible, the included value is \(\bot_{\mathrm{inc}}\).  The sign-radius inclusion uses the extended real line and therefore includes \(+\infty\) without alteration.

Every step is an event inclusion or a finite union bound.  It invokes neither differentiability of an optimum nor uniqueness of a cut.  It is consequently unchanged when several cuts tie, when \(d=0\) or \(d=1\), at a compatibility boundary, or in the extended-real no-reversal case.
