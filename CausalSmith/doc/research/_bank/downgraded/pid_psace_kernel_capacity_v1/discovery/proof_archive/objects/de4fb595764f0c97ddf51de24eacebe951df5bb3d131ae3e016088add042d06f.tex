We give the algorithm and prove its output exact.  Treat the numerical radii appearing in the input as rational numbers.  Membership of \(p\) in \(\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}^{\kappa}\) is a finite system of rational linear equalities and inequalities after replacing each absolute value in an \(\ell_1\) constraint by nonnegative auxiliaries.  The set is compact because it is closed and contained in \((\Delta_4)^{2m}\).

For fixed \(g,d\), form the first-order formula with one free real variable \(t\),
\[
\begin{split}
 Q_{g,d}(t)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \exists p,\mathbf K:\;&
 p\in\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}^{\kappa},\quad
 \mathbf K\in\mathfrak K_d(p_{\cdot0},p_{\cdot1}),\\
 &N_g^{\mathrm{pr}}(\mathbf K;p)-tD_g^{\mathrm{pr}}(\mathbf K;p)=0.
\end{split}
\]
All constraints are polynomial equalities or weak inequalities with rational coefficients.  In particular, each marginal-reproduction term \(p_{g0}(x)k_{gx}(z)\) is bilinear, and the last equality has finite polynomial degree; no linear relaxation is being treated as exact.  The first-stage identity proved in \(\texttt{lem:first-stage-denominator}\), applied with the candidate marginals \(p\), gives \(D_g^{\mathrm{pr}}\ge\kappa>0\).  Hence \(Q_{g,d}(t)\) holds exactly for the real-valued part of \(\mathcal I_{1-\alpha,g}(d)\).

The quantified domain in \((p,\mathbf K)\) is closed and bounded.  On it the ratio \(N_g^{\mathrm{pr}}/D_g^{\mathrm{pr}}\) is continuous because the denominator is at least \(\kappa\).  Its image is therefore compact.  The effective quantifier-elimination result \(\texttt{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), applied to \(Q_{g,d}\), returns a quantifier-free sign condition in \(t\).  Isolating its finitely many real algebraic boundary roots gives a finite union of closed intervals and isolated points.  At an included boundary, exact sample-point lifting through the cylindrical decomposition returns a witness \((p,\mathbf K)\); compactness proves that every boundary is included and attained.  Separately eliminate quantifiers in the sentence
\[
 B_d\ \Longleftrightarrow\
 \exists p\in\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}^{\kappa}\;
 \neg\exists\mathbf K\in\mathfrak K_d(p_{\cdot0},p_{\cdot1}).
\]
This decides exactly whether \(\bot_{\mathrm{inc}}\) occurs.

For the sign radius, let
\[
 F_g(p,r)\ \Longleftrightarrow\
 \exists\mathbf K:\ \mathbf K\in\mathfrak K_r(p_{\cdot0},p_{\cdot1}),\quad
 N_g^{\mathrm{pr}}(\mathbf K;p)\le0,
\]
and put \(F_{\mathrm{all}}(p,r)=\bigvee_{g\in\mathcal G}F_g(p,r)\).  For each fixed \(p\), compactness of the row variables implies that if \(F_{\mathrm{all}}(p,r)\) holds for some \(r\in[0,1]\), its least such \(r\) is attained.  Therefore the formula
\[
\begin{split}
 R(r)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \exists p:\;&p\in\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}^{\kappa},\quad
 F_{\mathrm{all}}(p,r),\quad 0\le r\le1,\\
 &\forall s\,[0\le s<r\ \Longrightarrow\ \neg F_{\mathrm{all}}(p,s)]
\end{split}
\]
defines exactly \(\mathcal D_{1-\alpha}^{\mathrm{all}}\cap[0,1]\).  Quantifier elimination and one-dimensional root isolation return its exact finite union of points and intervals.  Unlike the PSACE image, this value set need not be asserted closed; the decomposition records each endpoint's inclusion flag exactly.  The extended-real marker is decided by
\[
 I_\infty\ \Longleftrightarrow\
 \exists p\in\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}^{\kappa}\;
 \neg F_{\mathrm{all}}(p,1),
\]
because radius one is the largest allowed rowwise total-variation distance.

These formulas also cover an empty confidence region, in which case all existential projections are empty.  Cylindrical algebraic decomposition terminates on every finite rational polynomial family and keeps cells on which one or more polynomials vanish.  Thus active-cut ties and boundary contacts are represented as zero-sign cells rather than removed by a generic-position assumption.  To complete the handle in \(\texttt{def:profiling-handle}\), its McCormick boxes may be used only for preliminary pruning; on every surviving box, or simply at the root box, the exact formulas above are passed to cylindrical algebraic decomposition.  Finite exact termination follows from \(\texttt{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), and the returned lifted witnesses and sign conditions certify every claimed endpoint and marker.
