Fix \(g\) and \(\mathbf K\in\mathfrak K_d(\mu^0,\nu)\), and put
\(J_g(x,z)=\mu_g^0(x)k_{gx}(z)\).  By \(\texttt{def:robust-kernel-class}\), \(J_g\ge0\), its row marginal is \(\mu_g^0\), and its column marginal is \(\nu_g\).  For binary \(s_x,s_z\), the pointwise identity
\[
 \mathbf 1\{s_x=0,s_z=1\}-\mathbf 1\{s_x=1,s_z=0\}
 =s_z-s_x
\]
holds in all four cases.  Multiplication by \(J_g(x,z)\) and summation therefore give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 D_g^{\mathrm{pr}}(\mathbf K)-R_g^{\mathrm{rev}}(\mathbf K)
 &=\sum_{x,z}(s_z-s_x)J_g(x,z)\\
 &=\sum_{z:s_z=1}\nu_g(z)-\sum_{x:s_x=1}\mu_g^0(x)
 =\Delta_g^S.
\end{aligned}
\]
Nonnegativity of \(J_g\) gives \(R_g^{\mathrm{rev}}(\mathbf K)\ge0\).  Hence
\(D_g^{\mathrm{pr}}(\mathbf K)\ge\Delta_g^S\), and Assumption \(\texttt{ass:positive-first-stage}\) supplies \(\Delta_g^S\ge\kappa>0\).  This proves the identity and the uniform denominator bound.
