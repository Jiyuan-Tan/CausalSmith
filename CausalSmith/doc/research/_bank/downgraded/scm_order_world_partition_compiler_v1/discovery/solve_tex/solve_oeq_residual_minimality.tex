%%% FIELD residual-minimality-resolution-statement
For every \(h\in\mathbb N_{+}\), under the declared existential-support residual,
\[
\rho(h)=B_h=\operatorname{Bell}(h).
\]
Consequently, complete-prefix equality partitions are worst-case state-minimal, and the existing single-conjunction compiler upper bound \(B_h3^{(h-1)/3}\operatorname{poly}(N_{\mathrm{in}})\) is unchanged. This is a state-minimality conclusion for the declared residual, not a running-time lower bound or a claim about all reduced multi-valued decision diagrams.
%%% FIELD residual-minimality-resolution-proof
The conclusion is exactly the characterization established by \texttt{thm:residual-partition-minimality}: that theorem proves \(\rho(h)=B_h=\operatorname{Bell}(h)\), proves worst-case state-minimality of the complete-prefix equality partitions under the declared existential-support residual, and records that the upper bound \(B_h3^{(h-1)/3}\operatorname{poly}(N_{\mathrm{in}})\) remains valid. The asserted resolution therefore follows by direct invocation of \texttt{thm:residual-partition-minimality}.
