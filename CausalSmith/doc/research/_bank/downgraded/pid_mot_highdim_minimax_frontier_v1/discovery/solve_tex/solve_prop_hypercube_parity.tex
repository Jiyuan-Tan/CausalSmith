%%% FIELD proof-hypercube-parity
We use a pointwise lattice-parity lower bound and an explicit marginal-preserving coupling.

First suppose that \(K\) is even.  Let \(U_1,\ldots,U_{K/2}\) be random vectors with law \(H_d\), and define
\[
 Z_{2r-1}=U_r,
 \qquad
 Z_{2r}=-U_r,
 \qquad 1\le r\le K/2.
\]
(The vectors \(U_r\) may, for example, be independent.)  The map \(u\mapsto-u\) is a bijection of \(\{-1,1\}^d/\sqrt d\), so central symmetry of the uniform law gives \(-U_r\sim H_d\).  Hence every \(Z_k\) has marginal law \(H_d\), and the joint law \(\gamma_{\mathrm{even}}\) of \((Z_1,\ldots,Z_K)\) belongs to \(\Gamma(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})\).  Moreover,
\[
 \sum_{k=1}^K Z_k
 =\sum_{r=1}^{K/2}(U_r-U_r)=0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
Since the squared Euclidean norm is nonnegative and \(K\ge3\), the definition of \(L\) and the admissibility of \(\gamma_{\mathrm{even}}\) imply
\[
 0\le L(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})
 \le \int K^{-2}\left\|\sum_{k=1}^Kz_k\right\|_2^2
 \,d\gamma_{\mathrm{even}}=0.
\]
Thus \(L(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})=0\) for even \(K\).

Now suppose that \(K\) is odd.  Fix an arbitrary \(\gamma\in\Gamma(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})\), and let \((Z_1,\ldots,Z_K)\) have law \(\gamma\).  Because each of the finitely many marginals is supported on \(\{-1,1\}^d/\sqrt d\), simultaneously for every \(j\in\{1,\ldots,d\}\) and \(k\in\{1,\ldots,K\}\) we may write
\[
 Z_{k,j}=\frac{\varepsilon_{jk}}{\sqrt d},
 \qquad \varepsilon_{jk}\in\{-1,1\},
\]
almost surely.  For each fixed coordinate \(j\), the integer
\[
 S_j:=\sum_{k=1}^K\varepsilon_{jk}
\]
is a sum of an odd number of odd integers, hence is odd.  In particular \(S_j\ne0\) and \(S_j^2\ge1\).  Therefore the pointwise support argument gives
\[
 \left\|\sum_{k=1}^K Z_k\right\|_2^2
 =\sum_{j=1}^d\left(\frac{S_j}{\sqrt d}\right)^2
 =\frac1d\sum_{j=1}^d S_j^2
 \ge1.
\]
Integrating, multiplying by the positive number \(K^{-2}\), and then taking the infimum over all admissible \(\gamma\) yields
\[
 L(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})\ge K^{-2}.
\]

It remains to show attainability, rather than merely an outer lower bound.  Define the finite set
\[
 \mathcal A_K
 :=\left\{e=(e_1,\ldots,e_K)\in\{-1,1\}^K:
 \left|\sum_{k=1}^K e_k\right|=1\right\}.
\]
This set is nonempty because \(K\) is odd.  Independently for \(j=1,\ldots,d\), draw \(E_j=(E_{j1},\ldots,E_{jK})\) uniformly from \(\mathcal A_K\), and put
\[
 Z_k:=\frac{(E_{1k},\ldots,E_{dk})}{\sqrt d},
 \qquad k=1,\ldots,K.
\]
For any fixed \(j,k\), the fixed-point-free bijection \(e\mapsto-e\) of \(\mathcal A_K\) interchanges the events \(e_k=1\) and \(e_k=-1\).  Consequently
\[
 \Pr(E_{jk}=1)=\Pr(E_{jk}=-1)=\frac12.
\]
The rows \(E_1,\ldots,E_d\) are independent, so for every fixed arm \(k\) and every \(a=(a_1,\ldots,a_d)\in\{-1,1\}^d\),
\[
 \Pr\bigl((E_{1k},\ldots,E_{dk})=a\bigr)
 =\prod_{j=1}^d\Pr(E_{jk}=a_j)=2^{-d}.
\]
Thus each \(Z_k\) has law \(H_d\).  The resulting joint law \(\gamma_{\mathrm{odd}}\) therefore lies in \(\Gamma(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})\).  By the definition of \(\mathcal A_K\), for every realization and every \(j\),
\[
 \left|\sum_{k=1}^K E_{jk}\right|=1,
\]
and hence
\[
 \left\|\sum_{k=1}^K Z_k\right\|_2^2
 =\frac1d\sum_{j=1}^d
 \left(\sum_{k=1}^K E_{jk}\right)^2
 =1.
\]
Using this admissible coupling in the definition of \(L\) gives
\[
 L(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})
 \le \int K^{-2}\left\|\sum_{k=1}^Kz_k\right\|_2^2
 \,d\gamma_{\mathrm{odd}}
 =K^{-2}.
\]
Together with the preceding lower bound, this proves \(L(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})=K^{-2}\) for odd \(K\).  Finally, \(K=3\) and \(d=5\) satisfy the declared ranges, so the odd case gives \(L(\mathbf P^{\mathrm{hyp}})=3^{-2}=1/9\).  No rank, overlap, conditioning, boundary-limit, or asymptotic argument is used; the only division is by the positive quantities \(K^2\) and \(d\).
