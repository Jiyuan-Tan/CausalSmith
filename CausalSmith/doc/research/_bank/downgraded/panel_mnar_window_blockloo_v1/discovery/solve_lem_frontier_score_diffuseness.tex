%%% FIELD proof-frontier-score-diffuseness
Fix a compact set \(\mathcal K\subset[1/2,1]\times(0,1]\times[0,1]\), and put
\[
 b_*=\min\{b:(a,b,c)\in\mathcal K\}>0.
\]
All constants below are uniform over \((a,b,c)\in\mathcal K\) and \(P\in\mathcal P_n(a,b,c)\).  This uniformity follows because the constants in the cohort-growth, window-growth, and frontier-variance assumptions are locally uniform over compact exponent sets, while the constants in the balanced-growth, incoherence, and restricted-Gram assumptions are fixed.

We first retain the normalized-factor part of the already established result \(\mathrm{lem:score\mbox{-}diffuseness}\), which does not use the fixed-fraction cohort assumption.  For completeness, write
\[
 u_i=D^{-1/2}x_i,
 \qquad
 v_t=D^{-1/2}f_t.
\]
Because \(D\) is positive definite by exact rank, these vectors are well defined.  From \(X=UD^{1/2}\), \(F=VD^{1/2}\), incoherence, and balanced growth,
\[
 \max_{i\in I}\lVert u_i\rVert_2
 \le \sqrt{\frac{\mu r}{N}}
 \le Cn^{-1/2},
 \qquad
 \max_{t\in J}\lVert v_t\rVert_2
 \le \sqrt{\frac{\mu r}{T}}
 \le Cn^{-1/2}.
\]
Moreover, the normalized donor and preperiod Gram bounds give
\[
 \sum_{j\in C(t)}u_ju_j^{\top}\succeq c_AI_r,
 \qquad
 \sum_{s\in S}v_sv_s^{\top}\succeq c_BI_r.
\]
Thus both matrices are invertible.  Defining the donor and preperiod tangent coefficients by
\[
 a_{ij,t}
 =u_i^{\top}\!\left(\sum_{k\in C(t)}u_ku_k^{\top}\right)^{-1}u_j,
 \qquad
 b_{t,s}
 =v_t^{\top}\!\left(\sum_{u\in S}v_uv_u^{\top}\right)^{-1}v_s,
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz and the two inverse bounds yield
\[
 \sup_{i\in G,\,t\in W,\,j\in C(t)}|a_{ij,t}|\le \frac{C}{n},
 \qquad
 \sup_{t\in W,\,s\in S}|b_{t,s}|\le \frac{C}{n}.
\]

Let \(\mathcal D=I\setminus G\).  Under the common-start design, \(C(t)=\mathcal D\) for every \(t\in W\).  Expanding the same complete three-term tangent score used in \(\mathrm{lem:score\mbox{-}diffuseness}\) inside the definition of \(L\), the coefficient of each noise coordinate is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 w^{(1)}_{jt}
 &=\frac{1}{gH}\sum_{i\in G}a_{ij,t},
 &&(j,t)\in\mathcal D\times W,\\
 w^{(2)}_{is}
 &=\frac{1}{gH}\sum_{t\in W}b_{t,s},
 &&(i,s)\in G\times S,\\
 w^{(3)}_{js}
 &=-\frac{1}{gH}\sum_{i\in G}\sum_{t\in W}a_{ij,t}b_{t,s},
 &&(j,s)\in\mathcal D\times S.
 \end{aligned}
\]
The three supports are pairwise disjoint: \(G\cap\mathcal D=\varnothing\), and \(S\cap W=\varnothing\) follows from \(\max S<\min W\).  The coefficient bounds therefore imply
\[
 \max_{j\in I,\,s'\in J}|w_{js'}|
 \le C\max\left\{\frac{1}{nH},\frac{1}{ng},\frac{1}{n^2}\right\}.
\]
Balanced growth and \(S\subset J\) show that the three support cardinalities are at most \(CnH\), \(Cgn\), and \(Cn^2\), respectively.  Consequently,
\[
 \sum_{j\in I}\sum_{s'\in J}w_{js'}^2
 =\sum_{\mathcal D\times W}(w^{(1)}_{jt})^2
  +\sum_{G\times S}(w^{(2)}_{is})^2
  +\sum_{\mathcal D\times S}(w^{(3)}_{js})^2
 \le C\left\{\frac{1}{nH}+\frac{1}{ng}+\frac{1}{n^2}\right\}.
\]

Set
\[
 A_n=\frac{1}{nH},
 \qquad
 B_n=\frac{1}{ng}.
\]
The frontier variance-scale assumption supplies the strictly positive lower bound
\[
 s^2\ge c_v(A_n+B_n).
\]
The window-growth assumption and \(c\le1\) give \(H\le Cn^c\le Cn\), whence \(n^{-2}\le CA_n\).  Division by the preceding positive variance bound is therefore legitimate and gives
\[
 s^{-2}\sum_{j\in I}\sum_{s'\in J}w_{js'}^2
 \le C\frac{A_n+B_n+n^{-2}}{A_n+B_n}
 \le C.
\]

For the maximal weight, \(s\ge c\sqrt{A_n+B_n}\) implies separately
\[
 \frac{A_n}{s}\le C\sqrt{A_n},
 \qquad
 \frac{B_n}{s}\le C\sqrt{B_n},
 \qquad
 \frac{n^{-2}}{s}
 \le C\frac{n^{-2}}{\sqrt{A_n}}
 =C\frac{\sqrt{nH}}{n^2}
 \le \frac{C}{n}.
\]
Since \(H\ge c_Hn^c\ge c_H\) and \(g\ge c_gn^b\ge c_gn^{b_*}\) uniformly on \(\mathcal K\), it follows that
\[
 \sup_{(a,b,c)\in\mathcal K}\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_n(a,b,c)}
 \frac{\max_{j\in I,\,s'\in J}|w_{js'}|}{s}
 \le C\left\{n^{-1/2}+n^{-(1+b_*)/2}+n^{-1}\right\}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Together with the preceding uniform squared-weight bound, this proves both asserted diffuseness conclusions.
