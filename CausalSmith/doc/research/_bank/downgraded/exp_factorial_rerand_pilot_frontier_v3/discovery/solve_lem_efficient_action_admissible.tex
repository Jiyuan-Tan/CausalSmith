Condition on an arbitrary realization of the pilot sigma-field \(\mathcal O_m\).  By \(\mathrm{def{:}efficient\text{-}action\text{-}handle}\), the plug-in rule evaluates the empirical oracle construction on \(T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}\) and applies the normalization \(\mathcal N\).  Its unnormalized matrix is therefore of the form
\[
U_m=\mathcal B^{\mathsf T}W\mathcal B.
\]
Because \(W\in\mathbb S_{++}^{F}\), for every \(v\in\mathbb R^{Fp}\),
\[
v^{\mathsf T}U_m v=(\mathcal Bv)^{\mathsf T}W(\mathcal Bv)\geq 0,
\]
so \(U_m\in\mathbb S_{+}^{Fp}\).  The definition of \(\mathcal N\) gives two exhaustive cases.  If \(U_m=0\), then \(\widehat G_m=\mathcal N(U_m)=0\).  If \(U_m\neq0\), positive semidefiniteness implies \(\operatorname{tr}(U_m)>0\), and hence
\[
\widehat G_m=\mathcal N(U_m)=\frac{U_m}{\operatorname{tr}(U_m)}\in\mathbb S_{+}^{Fp},
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(\widehat G_m)=1.
\]
Thus \(\widehat G_m\) satisfies \(\mathrm{ass{:}admissible\text{-}action}\), and \(\mathrm{def{:}action\text{-}space}\) yields
\[
\widehat G_m\in\mathcal H_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}}.
\]
Conditional on \(\mathcal O_m\), the set \(T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}=S_m^c\) and the matrix \(\widehat G_m\) are fixed.  Applying \(\mathrm{def{:}exact\text{-}mixture}\) with \(T=T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}\) and \(H=\widehat G_m\) gives
\[
\frac{1}{\lvert\Omega_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}}\rvert}
\sum_{z\in\Omega_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}}}
a_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}},\widehat G_m}(z)=\alpha.
\]
This is the asserted exact acceptance mass.  Moreover, an acceptance probability is nonnegative, so for every \(z\in\Omega_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}}\),
\[
p_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}},\widehat G_m}(z)
=\frac{1}{\lvert\Omega_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}}\rvert}
\left\{\eta+\frac{1-\eta}{\alpha}
a_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}},\widehat G_m}(z)\right\}
\geq\frac{\eta}{\lvert\Omega_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}}\rvert}>0,
\]
where the final strict inequality uses \(\eta\in(0,1)\) and the nonempty finite balanced support \(\Omega_{T_{n,m}^{\mathrm{main}}}\).  The argument holds for every pilot realization on which the rule is defined; consequently all three conclusions hold almost surely.  No symmetry of \(\widehat G_m\), no asymptotic approximation, and no efficiency assertion is used.
