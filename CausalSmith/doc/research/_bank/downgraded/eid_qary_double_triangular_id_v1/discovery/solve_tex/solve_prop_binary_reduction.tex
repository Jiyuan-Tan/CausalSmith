Fix \((G_{\mathrm{bin}},P_{\mathrm{bin}})\in
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{LG},1}^{\mathrm{bin}}(K,J)\).  By
Definition~\textnormal{def:binary-rank-comparator-class}, the binary
measurement-graph, Markov, positive-latent-support,
measurement-nondegeneracy, and double-triangular assumptions hold.  Apply
Lemma~\textnormal{lem:binary-triangular-view-nonsingularity} separately to
the two disjoint triangular views.  It gives
\[
 \operatorname{rank}\bigl((M_{\mathrm{bin}})_a\bigr)=2^K,
 \qquad a\in\{1,2\}.
\]
Each \((M_{\mathrm{bin}})_a\) is a square matrix of order \(2^K\), so both
matrices are nonsingular.  The cited lemma uses the measurement-graph,
Markov, measurement-nondegeneracy, and double-triangular assumptions, and
does not use faithfulness or positive latent support.

Positive latent support states that
\((\pi_{\mathrm{bin}})_h>0\) for every
\(h\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{bin}}^K\).  Consequently
\(D:=\operatorname{diag}(\pi_{\mathrm{bin}})\) is nonsingular, with
\[
 D^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}
 \left(\bigl((\pi_{\mathrm{bin}})_h^{-1}\bigr)_
 {h\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{bin}}^K}\right).
\]
By the definition of \((T_{12})_{\mathrm{bin}}\),
\[
 (T_{12})_{\mathrm{bin}}
 =(M_{\mathrm{bin}})_1D(M_{\mathrm{bin}})_2^{\mathsf T}.
\]
All three factors are nonsingular, and direct multiplication verifies that
\[
 (T_{12})_{\mathrm{bin}}^{-1}
 =(M_{\mathrm{bin}})_2^{-\mathsf T}D^{-1}
  (M_{\mathrm{bin}})_1^{-1}.
\]
It follows that
\[
 \operatorname{rank}\bigl((T_{12})_{\mathrm{bin}}\bigr)=2^K.
\]

We now distinguish the structural latent dimension \(K\) from the
observed-law functional \((K_{\min})_{\mathrm{bin}}\).  The present model is
a binary measurement-model realization with \(K\) latent coordinates, so
Definition~\textnormal{\((K_{\min})_{\mathrm{bin}}\)} gives
\[
 (K_{\min})_{\mathrm{bin}}((P_X)_{\mathrm{bin}})\leq K.
\]
For the reverse inequality, let an arbitrary binary measurement-model
realization of the same observed law have \(L\) latent coordinates
\(\widetilde H\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{bin}}^L\), latent mass vector
\(\widetilde\pi\), and view tables
\[
 \widetilde M_a(x,h)
 :=\widetilde P\bigl((X_{\mathrm{bin}})_{(S_a)_{\mathrm{bin}}}=x
          \mid \widetilde H=h\bigr),
 \qquad
 \widetilde M_a\in\mathbb R^{2^K\times 2^L}.
\]
The measurement-graph restriction and Markov factorization in such a
realization imply conditional independence of distinct observed vertices
given the full latent vector.  Since the two view sets are disjoint, for
every pair of view configurations \((x_1,x_2)\), the law of total probability
therefore gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (T_{12})_{\mathrm{bin}}(x_1,x_2)
 &=\sum_{h\in\mathcal Q_{\mathrm{bin}}^L}
   \widetilde P\bigl((X_{\mathrm{bin}})_{(S_1)_{\mathrm{bin}}}=x_1
                   \mid\widetilde H=h\bigr)
   \widetilde\pi_h
   \widetilde P\bigl((X_{\mathrm{bin}})_{(S_2)_{\mathrm{bin}}}=x_2
                   \mid\widetilde H=h\bigr) \\
 &=\bigl[\widetilde M_1\operatorname{diag}(\widetilde\pi)
          \widetilde M_2^{\mathsf T}\bigr](x_1,x_2).
 \end{aligned}
\]
Thus the image of \((T_{12})_{\mathrm{bin}}\) is contained in the image of
the \(2^K\)-by-\(2^L\) matrix \(\widetilde M_1\), whence
\[
 \operatorname{rank}\bigl((T_{12})_{\mathrm{bin}}\bigr)
 \leq\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde M_1)\leq 2^L.
\]
Combining this inequality with the already proved rank \(2^K\) yields
\(2^K\leq2^L\), hence \(K\leq L\).  This holds for every realization in
the defining minimum.  Together with the upper bound furnished by the true
realization, it proves
\[
 (K_{\min})_{\mathrm{bin}}((P_X)_{\mathrm{bin}})=K.
\]

The scope of the comparison with Lee and Gu is separate from the foregoing
derivation.  By Lemma~\textnormal{lem:lee-gu-blcm-scope}, their BLCM permits
arbitrary observed variables on nondegenerate separable metric spaces, has
binary latent coordinates, and includes faithfulness among its standing
assumptions.  Binary response spaces are nondegenerate separable metric
spaces.  Moreover, double triangularity supplies at least one observed child
for every latent coordinate.  Hence, after adding binary faithfulness, a
member of \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{LG},1}^{\mathrm{bin}}(K,J)\) satisfies the
published standing BLCM conditions, and the cited
Lemma~\textnormal{lem:lee-gu-theorem-one-binary-rank} applies on that faithful
intersection.  It cannot serve as the justification on all of
\(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{LG},1}^{\mathrm{bin}}(K,J)\), because faithfulness is
not in the definition of that reduced class.  The preceding matrix argument
is instead a direct proof of the reduced-assumption binary-response
strengthening.

Finally fix
\((G_{\mathrm{bin}},P_{\mathrm{bin}})\in
\mathcal M_{\mathrm{LG},2}^{\mathrm{bin}}(K,J)\).  By
Definition~\textnormal{def:binary-identification-comparator-class}, this model
belongs to the first comparator class and additionally satisfies binary
faithfulness, third-view coverage, and the subset condition.  The first part
of the proof identifies \(K\) from \((P_X)_{\mathrm{bin}}\).  Third-view
coverage is precisely
\[
 \forall k\in\{1,\ldots,K\}\quad
 \exists j\in(S_3)_{\mathrm{bin}}
 \quad (\Gamma_{\mathrm{bin}})_{jk}=1,
\]
so every column of the residual block is nonempty.  All hypotheses of
Lemma~\textnormal{lem:lee-gu-theorem-two-binary-identification} are therefore
satisfied.  That cited result identifies \(\Gamma_{\mathrm{bin}}\), the
complete-data law \(P_{\mathrm{bin}}\), and the latent graph
\(\Lambda_{\mathrm{bin}}\), up to latent-coordinate permutation, binary
state flips, and latent Markov equivalence.

The identified complete-data law determines
\[
 (\pi_{\mathrm{bin}})_h=P_{\mathrm{bin}}(H_{\mathrm{bin}}=h)
\]
and, for every \(j\), \(x\), and \(h\),
\[
 (p_{\mathrm{bin}})_j(x\mid h)
 =\frac{P_{\mathrm{bin}}((X_{\mathrm{bin}})_j=x,
                         H_{\mathrm{bin}}=h)}
        {(\pi_{\mathrm{bin}})_h}.
\]
The denominator is strictly positive by binary positive latent support.
Coordinate permutations and binary state flips constitute
\(\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{bin}}=\mathfrak S_K\ltimes(\mathfrak S_2)^K\), and
the remaining ambiguity in the latent graph is exactly its Markov equivalence
class.  Therefore \((P_X)_{\mathrm{bin}}\) determines
\[
 \mathcal R_{\mathrm{bin}}((P_X)_{\mathrm{bin}})
 =\bigl[\Gamma_{\mathrm{bin}},\pi_{\mathrm{bin}},p_{\mathrm{bin}},
        [\Lambda_{\mathrm{bin}}]_{\mathrm{MEC}}\bigr]
 \quad\text{modulo }\mathfrak G_{\mathrm{bin}},
\]
which is the claimed binary-response subclass of the published Theorem~2
conclusion.

Let \(q_{\mathrm{bin}}=2\).  On
\(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{LG},1}^{\mathrm{bin}}(K,J)\), as defined by the binary
measurement graph, Markov factorization, positive latent support, binary
measurement nondegeneracy, and binary double triangularity, both
\((M_{\mathrm{bin}})_1\) and \((M_{\mathrm{bin}})_2\) are nonsingular,
\(\operatorname{rank}((T_{12})_{\mathrm{bin}})=2^K\), and

\(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{LG},2}^{\mathrm{bin}}(K,J)\), the added faithfulness,
third-view-coverage, and subset conditions identify
\(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{bin}}((P_X)_{\mathrm{bin}})\) modulo binary coordinate
and state relabeling and the latent Markov equivalence class.  This second
comparator class is a binary-response subclass of the published Theorem~2
class.
