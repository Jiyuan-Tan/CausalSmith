## Lemmas (L)

**L-1 (Fix a positive integer \(q\)).** Fix a positive integer \(q\). Let \(\Gamma_n\) and \(\Gamma\) be positive-semidefinite \(q\times q\) matrices with unit diagonal and \(\Gamma_n\to\Gamma\). If \(Z_n\sim N(0,\Gamma_n)\) and \(Z\sim N(0,\Gamma)\), allowing every matrix to be singular, then the distribution function of \(\max_{1\leq k\leq q}|Z_k|\) is continuous and strictly increasing on \([0,\infty)\), and the \(u\)-quantiles of \(\max_k|Z_{n,k}|\) converge to its \(u\)-quantile for every \(u\in(0,1)\).

## Theorems (T)

### T-block: t1 — For every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{face}}\) satisfying \…
**Statement.** For every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{face}}\) satisfying \(\texttt{ass:no-date-one-absorption}\), the conclusions of \(\texttt{thm:active-frontier-recovery}\) upgrade throughout \(\mathcal F(p)\) to \[ \pi_j^d=\alpha_j^d>0,\qquad \tau_j=\eta_j=\nu_j/\pi_j^1. \] Thus the same formulas recover the full type masses, the complete component law of \((Z,X_0,X_1,X_2,X_3)\) in every cohort, every full conditional type effect, and the aggregate \(\tau\), in the common descending signature order. The result requires neither time homogeneity nor a date-\(4\) common untreated kernel and permits the rank-deficient common absorbing row.

### T-block: t2 — For every regular no-date-\(1\)-absorption base law…
**Statement.** For every regular no-date-\(1\)-absorption base law \(P^\circ\), every \(0<\varepsilon<1\), and distinct labels \(h,k\), the two laws \(P_\varepsilon^A,P_\varepsilon^B\) in \(\texttt{def:outside-face-pair}\) belong to \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{face}}\), induce the same observed law, scale every active slice and active mass by \(1-\varepsilon\), and leave \(K^d\) unchanged. Between the two allocations they have the same \(\alpha_j^d\), \(\nu_j\), \(\eta_j\), and \(\tau\), but different full type shares; their full conditional effects obey \(\tau_j=\nu_j/\pi_j^1\) and therefore differ for every reallocated type having nonzero contribution. Hence initial-absorber allocation is a generic obstruction to full-share and full-conditional-effect identification, while the active targets remain identified. The exact specialization \(\omega^\star\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{face}}\), satisfies \(\texttt{ass:no-date-one-absorption}\), and has \[ K^0=K^1=\begin{pmatrix}29/5&-12/5\\8/5&1/5\end{pmatrix}, \qquad (\rho_1^d,\rho_2^d)=(5,1), \] \[ \tau_1=(1/25,0,-1/25),\quad \tau_2=(-1/40,-1/40,1/20),\quad \tau=(3/400,-1/80,1/200), \] \[ \alpha_j^d=1/2,\quad \nu_1=(1/50,0,-1/50),\quad \nu_2=(-1/80,-1/80,1/40),\quad \eta_j=\tau_j. \] For \(P^A,P^B\), the full shares are \((3/5,2/5)\) and \((2/5,3/5)\), respectively, and \[ \tau_1^A=(2/75,0,-2/75),\quad \tau_2^A=(-1/40,-1/40,1/20), \] \[ \tau_1^B=(1/25,0,-1/25),\quad \tau_2^B=(-1/60,-1/60,1/30). \] Both laws have \(\alpha_1^d=\alpha_2^d=2/5\), \[ \nu_1=(2/125,0,-2/125),\quad \nu_2=(-1/100,-1/100,1/50), \] the same at-risk effects as \(\omega^\star\), and \(\tau=(3/500,-1/100,1/250)\).

### T-block: t3 — For fixed \(m\) and known \(J\), the generalized map…
**Statement.** For fixed \(m\) and known \(J\), the generalized map \(\Phi\) extends real analytically to a relative open neighborhood of every regular \(p\). In the saturated finite-multinomial model on its actual absorption-legal support stratum, \[ G_p(s)=J_\Phi(p)(e_s-p) \] is the canonical gradient of \(\Phi(p)\), \(\Sigma(p)\) is the information bound, and the empirical-cell plug-in attains that bound at every relative-interior law on the stratum.

### T-block: t4 — For fixed \(m,J,\mathcal L,\kappa\), uniformly over…
**Statement.** For fixed \(m,J,\mathcal L,\kappa\), uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal P_\kappa\), \[ P(\mathcal E_\kappa)\to1,\qquad \sqrt n(\widehat\theta-\theta_J)=n^{-1/2}\sum_{i=1}^nG_p(O_i)+o_P(1), \qquad \widehat\Sigma-\Sigma(p)=o_P(1). \] Allowing \(\Sigma(p)\) and its correlation matrix to be singular while retaining the buffered reported diagonal, \[ \inf_{P\in\mathcal P_\kappa}P\{\theta_{J,\ell}\in I_{\ell,n} \text{ for every }\ell\in\mathcal L\}\longrightarrow1-\alpha. \]

### T-block: t5 — Fix \(m=|\mathcal A|\) and a known \(J\) with…
**Statement.** Fix \(m=|\mathcal A|\) and a known \(J\) with \(2\leq J\leq m\). For every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{face}}\), the anchor-star recovery map is well-defined and \[ \Phi(p)=\theta_J=(\nu_1^\top,\ldots,\nu_J^\top,\tau^\top)^\top \in\mathbb R^{(J+1)(m+1)}. \] Throughout \(\mathcal F(p)\), in the common descending cycle-product order, the formulas recover \(\alpha_j^d>0\), \(s_j^d\), every active row of \(Q_j\) and \(R_j^d\), the complete unnormalized component law of \((Z,X_0,X_1,X_2,X_3)\) on \(\{X_1\in\mathcal A\}\), \[ \nu_j=s_j^1Q_j(R_j^1-R_j^0),\qquad \eta_j=\nu_j/\alpha_j^1, \qquad \tau=\sum_{j=1}^J\nu_j. \] Moreover \(\pi_j^d\geq\alpha_j^d>0\) is derived from anchor-star nonsingularity, rather than assumed. Four anchor slices determine the spectral basis, while the \(2m-1\) observable anchor-star slices transport recovery to all active states. Units with \(X_1=e\) have zero treatment effect by no anticipation and absorption. Without the no-date-\(1\)-absorption condition, the theorem does not identify \(\pi_j^d\) or the full conditional \(\tau_j\). No time homogeneity is required. Only four waves \(X_0,\ldots,X_3\) are required; if a fifth wave is observed it may be marginalized out.
