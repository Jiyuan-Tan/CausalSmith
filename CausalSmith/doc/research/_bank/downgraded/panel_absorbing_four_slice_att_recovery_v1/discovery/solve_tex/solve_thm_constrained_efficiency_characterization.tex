For any supplied \(C^1\) chart \(p(\zeta)\) on a fixed positive support \(\mathcal S_0\), let
\[
 W=\operatorname{diag}\{p_s:s\in\mathcal S_0\},\qquad
 A=\frac{\partial p(\zeta)}{\partial\zeta^\top},\qquad
 S=W^{-1}A.
\]
For a differentiable path \(\zeta_t\) through \(\zeta\), direct differentiation gives \(\dot p=A\dot\zeta\) and hence the score \(h=W^{-1}\dot p=S\dot\zeta\). If the chart has locally constant rank and its image is the whole chosen stratum, its score space is therefore \(\operatorname{col}(S)\). For any reported linear direction \(a\), put \(g_a(s)=a^\top G_p(s)\). The weighted least-squares projection and its squared residual are the computable quantities
\[
 \Pi_{\operatorname{col}(S)}g_a
 =W^{-1}A(A^\top W^{-1}A)^\dagger A^\top g_a,
\]
\[
 \left\|(I-\Pi_{\operatorname{col}(S)})g_a\right\|_{L_2(P)}^2
 =g_a^\top Wg_a-(A^\top g_a)^\top(A^\top W^{-1}A)^\dagger(A^\top g_a).
\]
Consequently the residual is nonzero exactly when the last display is positive, equivalently when \(g_a\notin\operatorname{col}(W^{-1}A)\). This closes the projection calculation conditional on a valid full-stratum chart, but the construction and rank certification of that chart remain open.
