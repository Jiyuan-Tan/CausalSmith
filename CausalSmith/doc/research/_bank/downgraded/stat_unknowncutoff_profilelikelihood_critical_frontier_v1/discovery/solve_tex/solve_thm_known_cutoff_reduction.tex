Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_n\) satisfying the two hypotheses in the statement, and let
\[
\mathscr N_P=\sigma\{N_1(c_P),\ldots,N_{K_n}(c_P)\}.
\]
By \(\text{def:known-cutoff-threshold}\), \(\mathcal G_c=\{c_P\}\), so \(|\mathcal G_c|=1\).  Substitution into the threshold in \(\text{def:four-cell-calibration}\) gives, equation by equation,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Gamma_n(\delta_n)
&=3\sum_{k=1}^{K_n}\log\{N_k(c_P)+1\}
  +\log\frac{|\mathcal G_c||\mathcal G_\vartheta|}{\delta_n}\\
&=3\sum_{k=1}^{K_n}\log\{N_k(c_P)+1\}
  +\log\frac{|\mathcal G_\vartheta|}{\delta_n}\\
&=\Gamma_n^{\mathrm{kc}}(\delta_n).
\end{aligned}
\]
The hypotheses \(\theta_P\in\mathcal G_\vartheta\) and that the certified outer program contains the true bin averages imply
\((c_P,\theta_P)\in\mathcal G_c\times\mathcal G_\vartheta\) and verify precisely the two premises of \(\text{prop:association-unrestricted-calibration}\).  That proposition and the displayed threshold identity therefore yield
\[
P\!\left\{\Delta_n(c_P,\theta_P)>\Gamma_n^{\mathrm{kc}}(\delta_n)\mid\mathscr N_P\right\}
\le \frac{\delta_n}{|\mathcal G_\vartheta|}
\le\delta_n,
\]
where the last inequality follows from the nonemptiness of the finite grid \(\mathcal G_\vartheta\).

By the definition of \(\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\) in \(\text{def:known-cutoff-grid-rule}\), on the complementary event
\[
\Delta_n(c_P,\theta_P)\le\Gamma_n^{\mathrm{kc}}(\delta_n)
\]
one has
\[
\theta_P\in\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{kc}}
\subseteq\operatorname{hull}(\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{kc}}).
\]
Consequently the conditional coverage probabilities of both the inverted grid set and its connected hull are at least \(1-\delta_n/|\mathcal G_\vartheta|\), hence at least \(1-\delta_n\).

Let \(F_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\) be the union of the certified fallback branches listed in \(\text{def:worked-four-cell-grid}\).  Proposition \(\text{prop:wald-late-identification}\) gives
\[
\theta_P=E_P\!\left[Y(1)-Y(0)\mid R=C,X=c_P\right]\in\Theta;
\]
and the conditional causal contrast is well defined by the positivity premise already discharged in that proposition.  Hence \(\theta_P\in I_n^{\mathrm{kc}}=\Theta\) on \(F_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\).  On \((F_n^{\mathrm{kc}})^c\), \(\text{def:known-cutoff-grid-rule}\) and the preceding acceptance implication show that the same conclusion holds whenever the true-label deviance does not exceed its threshold.  Pointwise,
\[
\{\theta_P\notin I_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\}
\subseteq
\{\Delta_n(c_P,\theta_P)>\Gamma_n^{\mathrm{kc}}(\delta_n)\}.
\]
Conditioning on \(\mathscr N_P\) and using the calibrated bound gives
\[
P\{\theta_P\in I_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\mid\mathscr N_P\}
\ge1-\frac{\delta_n}{|\mathcal G_\vartheta|}
\ge1-\delta_n.
\]
Taking expectations with respect to \(\mathscr N_P\) proves the corresponding unconditional bound for \(\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\), its hull, and \(I_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\); in particular,
\[
P\{\theta_P\in I_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\}\ge1-\delta_n.
\]
For completeness, each count vector has finite support, and \(\Delta_n(c_P,\vartheta)\), the certified fallback indicators, and the lexicographic tie rule in \(\text{def:worked-four-cell-grid}\) are deterministic extended-real functions of that vector.  Therefore \(\mathcal A_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\) and \(I_n^{\mathrm{kc}}\) are measurable; the latter is connected because it is either \(\Theta\) or a connected hull.

The uniformity assertion is inherited from \(\text{prop:association-unrestricted-calibration}\): its multinomial type bound permits zero joint cells and every binary association, and no residual-variance lower bound was invoked anywhere above.  Finally, the cutoff is supplied and \(\mathcal G_c\) is a singleton throughout, so neither the construction nor its guarantee uses a cutoff-recovery condition.
