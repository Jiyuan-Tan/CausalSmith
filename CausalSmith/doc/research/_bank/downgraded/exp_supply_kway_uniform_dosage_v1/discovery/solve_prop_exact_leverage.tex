%%% FIELD proof-exact-leverage
By \(\textnormal{ass:supply-range}\) and \(\textnormal{def:uniform-dosage}\),
\[
 0<u=\min\{L/p,1/2\}\le \frac12<1,
\]
so \(d^\star=u\mathbf 1_p\) is an interior dosage.  In particular, the positive definiteness conclusion of \(\textnormal{lem:trace-identity}\) applies to \(\Sigma_k(d^\star)\).

We now make the triangular change of basis explicit in order to evaluate the quadratic form in \(\textnormal{def:maximum-leverage}\).  Put
\[
 m=2u-1,
 \qquad
 s=\sqrt{4u(1-u)}>0,
 \qquad
 g_i(x_i)=\frac{x_i-m}{s},
 \qquad
 \psi_T(x)=\prod_{i\in T}g_i(x_i)
 \quad(T\in\mathcal I_k).
\]
Under the product randomization at \(d^star\), the coordinates \(X_i\) are independent and satisfy
\[
 \mathbb E_{d^star}X_i=m,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_{d^star}(X_i-m)^2=s^2.
\]
Consequently \(\mathbb E_{d^star}g_i(X_i)=0\) and \(\mathbb E_{d^star}g_i(X_i)^2=1\).  Independence then gives, for \(T,U\in\mathcal I_k\),
\[
 \mathbb E_{d^star}\{\psi_T(X)\psi_U(X)\}
 =\prod_{i\in T\cap U}\mathbb E_{d^star}g_i(X_i)^2
   \prod_{i\in T\triangle U}\mathbb E_{d^star}g_i(X_i)
 =\mathbf 1\{T=U\}.
\]

For each \(T\in\mathcal I_k\), expansion of the product gives
\[
 \psi_T(x)
 =s^{-|T|}\prod_{i\in T}(x_i-m)
 =s^{-|T|}\sum_{R\subseteq T}(-m)^{|T\setminus R|}\phi_R(x).
\]
Because \(R\subseteq T\) implies \(R\in\mathcal I_k\), this defines a square matrix \(A\), indexed by \(\mathcal I_k\), such that \(\psi(x)=A\phi(x)\).  Ordering subsets by nondecreasing cardinality makes \(A\) triangular, and its diagonal entry indexed by \(T\) is \(s^{-|T|}>0\); hence \(A\) is invertible.  The preceding orthonormality calculation and the definition of \(\Sigma_k\) yield
\[
 I_q
 =\mathbb E_{d^star}\{\psi(X)\psi(X)^{\mathsf T}\}
 =A\Sigma_k(d^star)A^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Thus
\[
 \Sigma_k(d^star)^{-1}=A^{\mathsf T}A,
\]
and therefore, for every \(x\in\{-1,1\}^p\),
\[
 \phi(x)^{\mathsf T}\Sigma_k(d^star)^{-1}\phi(x)
 =\|A\phi(x)\|_2^2
 =\sum_{T\in\mathcal I_k}\psi_T(x)^2.
\]

For each coordinate, direct substitution gives
\[
 g_i(x_i)^2
 =\begin{cases}
 \dfrac{1-u}{u},&x_i=1,\\[6pt]
 \dfrac{u}{1-u},&x_i=-1.
 \end{cases}
\]
Since \(0<u\le1/2\),
\[
 \frac{u}{1-u}\le\frac{1-u}{u}.
\]
Every term \(\psi_T(x)^2=\prod_{i\in T}g_i(x_i)^2\) is a product of nonnegative coordinate factors.  Hence all terms in their sum are simultaneously maximized by \(x=\mathbf 1_p\).  The definition of \(\mu^\star\) now gives
\[
 \mu^\star
 =\sum_{T\in\mathcal I_k}\prod_{i\in T}\frac{1-u}{u}
 =\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom pj\left(\frac{1-u}{u}\right)^j,
\]
where the last equality groups the subsets \(T\) by cardinality and uses that exactly \(\binom pj\) subsets of \([p]\) have size \(j\).  This is the asserted exact maximum.

%%% FIELD statement-exact-leverage
At the optimal dosage, the exact maximum whitened leverage is
\[
\mu^\star=\sum_{j=0}^{k}\binom pj\left(\frac{1-u}{u}\right)^j.
\]
