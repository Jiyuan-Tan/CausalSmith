Corollary. For every \(p_*\in[\epsilon,1-\epsilon]\), \((\ell_{m,p_*},u_{m,p_*})\in\mathcal E_m\). For every \(b\in\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\), the extrema defining \(I_{LW,m}^{\cap}(b)\) are attained and \([L_m(b),U_m(b)]\subseteq I_{LW,m}^{\cap}(b)=\bigcap_{p_*\in[\epsilon,1-\epsilon]}I_{LW,m,p_*}(b)\). If \(m\ge2\), there is one nonempty relatively open set \(\mathcal R\subseteq\operatorname{relint}(\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon})\) such that, for every \(b\in\mathcal R\), \(L_m(b)>\max_{p_*\in[\epsilon,1-\epsilon]}\ell_{m,p_*}^{\top}b\) and \(U_m(b)<\min_{p_*\in[\epsilon,1-\epsilon]}u_{m,p_*}^{\top}b\). More generally, every homogeneous, constant-coefficient order-\(m\) population-envelope pair obtained from Lee--Weidner display (14) after setting the binary outcome limits to zero and one belongs to \(\mathcal E_m\) and is covered by the contact-hull theorem. The optimized interval above is the intersection of the scalar-reference, \(b\)-only transplant; no inclusion comparison with their full observed-covariate, covariate-dependent procedure is asserted.
