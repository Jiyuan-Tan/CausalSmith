Fix a cell coordinate \(k=(a,y)\).  Expanding a multinomial count into its \(m\) indicator summands gives the first two factorial-moment identities
\[
 \sum_{c\in\mathcal I_m}\frac{c_k}{m}B_c^{(m)}(q)=q_k,
 \qquad
 \sum_{c\in\mathcal I_m}\frac{c_k(c_k-1)}{m(m-1)}B_c^{(m)}(q)=q_k^2,
\]
where the second identity uses \(m\geq2\).  Let \(b=g_m(q^*)\) and \(\nu\in\mathcal F_{m,\epsilon}(b)\).  Applying these two linear combinations to the fiber equations yields
\[
 \int q_k\,d\nu=q_k^*,
 \qquad
 \int q_k^2\,d\nu=(q_k^*)^2.
\]
Therefore
\[
 \int(q_k-q_k^*)^2\,d\nu=0,
\]
so \(q_k=q_k^*\) almost surely.  Repeating for all four cells gives \(q=q^*\) almost surely and \(\nu=\delta_{q^*}\).  Thus the fiber is the claimed singleton and its causal image is \(L_m(b)=U_m(b)=f(q^*)\).
