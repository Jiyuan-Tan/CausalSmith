For every fixed \(m\geq1\) and rational \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), the boundary \(\partial\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\) admits a finite semialgebraic stratification on each stratum of which the indicator of \(L_m(b)=U_m(b)\), the affine dimension \(0\) or \(1\) of \(\Theta_{m,\epsilon}(b)\), and the ordered pair of minimum lower- and upper-endpoint support sizes are constant.  Both minimum sizes lie in \(\{1,\ldots,N_m+1\}\).  The construction \(\mathcal A_{m,\epsilon}\), completed by the quantified endpoint and support-size tests in the proof, is a terminating CAD procedure that returns defining formulas for all nonempty strata and endpoint witnesses of the certified minimum sizes.
