By lem:scm-moment-reduction, under every \((\mu,\mathcal M)\in\mathfrak M_{m,\epsilon}\), the cluster counts are iid with categorical law \(b\), so \(Z\sim\operatorname{Multinomial}(n,b)\); moreover \(b\in\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\).  Equation (5) in thm:finite-complexity-inference, applied at the true \(b\), gives
\[
 \Pr_{\mathcal M}\{b\notin\mathcal R_{n,\alpha}(Z)\}
 =\Pr_{\mathcal M}\{P_n(b;Z)\leq\alpha\}\leq\alpha.
\]
On the complementary event, the true \(b\) is one of the candidates in \(\mathcal R_{n,\alpha}(Z)\cap\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\).  The defining union for \(\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}(Z)\) therefore contains its entire sharp fiber image:
\[
 [L_m(b),U_m(b)]\subseteq\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}(Z).
\]
Taking probabilities proves the displayed \(1-\alpha\) guarantee.  The argument used no law-specific constant and applies to every member of \(\mathfrak M_{m,\epsilon}\), so the coverage is uniform.  It did not invoke relative interior or positive width, and therefore also covers boundary laws whose fiber is a singleton.
