For every finite \(m\geq1\) and every \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), let \(d_m(\epsilon)\) be the uniform approximation modulus in the preceding definition.  Then
\[
 \max_{b\in\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}}\{U_m(b)-L_m(b)\}
 =\sup_{b\in\operatorname{relint}(\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon})}\{U_m(b)-L_m(b)\}
 =2d_m(\epsilon),
\]
and
\[
 0<d_m(\epsilon)\leq(1-2\epsilon)^m.
\]
Consequently every feasible fiber has width at most \(2(1-2\epsilon)^m\).  For every \(0<\delta\leq2\), the integer plate-size condition
\[
 m\geq\frac{\log(2/\delta)}{-\log(1-2\epsilon)}
\]
guarantees \(U_m(b)-L_m(b)\leq\delta\) simultaneously for all \(b\in\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\).
