Fix \((\ell,u)\in\mathcal E_m\), \(b\in\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\), and \(\nu\in\mathcal F_{m,\epsilon}(b)\).  The envelope inequalities and moment equations imply
\[
 \ell^{\top}b=\int\ell\,d\nu
 \leq\int f\,d\nu
 \leq\int u\,d\nu=u^{\top}b.
\]
Taking the infimum and supremum proves \([L_m(b),U_m(b)]\subseteq[\ell^{\top}b,u^{\top}b]\).

Let \(T_{\ell}=\{q\in K_{\epsilon}:\ell(q)=f(q)\}\).  It is compact.  If \(b\in\operatorname{conv}g_m(T_{\ell})\), an atomic probability measure supported on \(T_{\ell}\) belongs to the fiber and has objective \(\ell^{\top}b\), so \(L_m(b)=\ell^{\top}b\).  Conversely, the sharp primal--dual theorem supplies an attaining lower measure \(\nu\).  Equality implies
\[
 0=\int(f-\ell)\,d\nu.
\]
The integrand is nonnegative, so \(\nu(T_{\ell})=1\), and its moment barycenter lies in \(\operatorname{conv}g_m(T_{\ell})\).  This proves the lower equivalence.  Replacing \(f-\ell\) by \(u-f\) proves the upper equivalence with \(T_u=\{q:u(q)=f(q)\}\).

Assume \(m\geq2\).  By the finite-plate nonpolynomial lemma, neither contact set is all of \(K_{\epsilon}\).  Choose \(q^*\notin T_{\ell}\).  If \(g_m(q^*)\in\operatorname{conv}g_m(T_{\ell})\), a measure supported on \(T_{\ell}\) would have moment vector \(g_m(q^*)\), contradicting the singleton-fiber conclusion of the boundary Dirac-regression proposition.  Hence the lower contact hull is a proper subset of the moment body.  It is closed because it is the convex hull of a compact subset of finite-dimensional space.  The same argument applies to the upper contact hull.

A proper closed convex subset of a compact convex set cannot contain the latter's relative interior, whose closure is the whole set.  Therefore the complement of each contact hull contains a nonempty relatively open subset of \(\operatorname{relint}(\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon})\).  On the lower complement the weak inequality is strict, \(L_m(b)>\ell^{\top}b\); on the upper complement it is strict, \(U_m(b)<u^{\top}b\).  The two relatively open subsets need not coincide.
