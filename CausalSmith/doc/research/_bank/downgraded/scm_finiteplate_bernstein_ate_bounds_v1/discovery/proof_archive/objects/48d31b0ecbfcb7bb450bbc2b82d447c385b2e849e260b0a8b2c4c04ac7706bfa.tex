For every finite \(m\geq1\) and every \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), let \(d_m(\epsilon)\) be the uniform approximation modulus in the preceding definition.  Put \(\rho=1-2\epsilon\) and
\[
 E_m(\epsilon)=\frac{1}{T_m(1/\rho)}
 =\operatorname{sech}\!\left(m\operatorname{arcosh}(1/\rho)\right),
\]
where \(T_m\) is the degree-\(m\) Chebyshev polynomial of the first kind.  Then
\[
 \max_{b\in\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}}\{U_m(b)-L_m(b)\}
 =\sup_{b\in\operatorname{relint}(\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon})}\{U_m(b)-L_m(b)\}
 =2d_m(\epsilon),
\]
and
\[
 0<d_m(\epsilon)\leq E_m(\epsilon)\leq\rho^m.
\]
Moreover, \(E_m(\epsilon)<\rho^m\) whenever \(m\geq2\).  Consequently every feasible fiber has width at most
\[
 2\operatorname{sech}\!\left(m\operatorname{arcosh}\!\left(\frac{1}{1-2\epsilon}\right)\right).
\]
For every \(0<\delta\leq2\), the integer plate-size condition
\[
 m\geq
 \frac{\operatorname{arcosh}(2/\delta)}
 {\operatorname{arcosh}(1/(1-2\epsilon))}
\]
guarantees \(U_m(b)-L_m(b)\leq\delta\) simultaneously for all \(b\in\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\).
