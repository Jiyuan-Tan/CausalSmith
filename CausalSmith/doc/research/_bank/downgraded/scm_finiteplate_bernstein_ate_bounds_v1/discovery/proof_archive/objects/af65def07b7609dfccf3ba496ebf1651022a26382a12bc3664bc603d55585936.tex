Fix \((\mu,\mathcal M)\in\mathfrak M_{m,\epsilon}\) and a value \(q\in K_{\epsilon}\) at which the stipulated conditional laws are defined.  Write \(r=p(q)=q_{10}+q_{11}\).  By observed-cell calibration,
\[
 \Pr(A_{ij}=1\mid Q_i=q)=q_{10}+q_{11}=r,
 \qquad
 \Pr(A_{ij}=0\mid Q_i=q)=1-r.
\]
The definition of \(K_{\epsilon}\) gives \(r\geq\epsilon>0\) and \(1-r\geq\epsilon>0\), so both following divisions are legitimate.  Consistency and latent ignorability give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E\{Y_{ij}(1)\mid Q_i=q\}
 &=\mathbb E\{Y_{ij}(1)\mid A_{ij}=1,Q_i=q\}\\
 &=\Pr(Y_{ij}=1\mid A_{ij}=1,Q_i=q)=\frac{q_{11}}{r},\\
 \mathbb E\{Y_{ij}(0)\mid Q_i=q\}
 &=\mathbb E\{Y_{ij}(0)\mid A_{ij}=0,Q_i=q\}\\
 &=\Pr(Y_{ij}=1\mid A_{ij}=0,Q_i=q)=\frac{q_{01}}{1-r}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Consequently the conditional causal contrast is \(f(q)\).  Since \(Q_i\sim\mu\) by the environment-law assumption, iterated expectation yields the cluster-equally-weighted causal target
\[
 \mathbb E\{Y_{ij}(1)-Y_{ij}(0)\}
 =\int_{K_{\epsilon}}\left\{\frac{q_{11}}{p(q)}-\frac{q_{01}}{1-p(q)}\right\}\,d\mu(q)
 =\int f\,d\mu=\tau(\mu).
\]
Conditional iid sampling and consistency make the observed pairs \(O_{ij}\) iid conditional on \(Q_i=q\), and calibration gives their four probabilities as \(q\).  Hence
\[
 \Pr(C_i=c\mid Q_i=q)
 =\frac{m!}{\prod_{a,y}c_{ay}!}\prod_{a,y}q_{ay}^{c_{ay}}
 =B_c^{(m)}(q).
\]
Integrating with respect to the environment law proves
\[
 \Pr(C_i=c)=\int B_c^{(m)}(q)\,d\mu(q).
\]

Conversely, fix any \(\mu\in\mathcal P(K_{\epsilon})\).  Independently over clusters draw \(Q_i\sim\mu\).  Given \(Q_i=q\), independently over \(j\), draw \(A_{ij}\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(r)\), \(Y_{ij}(0)\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(q_{01}/(1-r))\), and \(Y_{ij}(1)\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(q_{11}/r)\), taking these three variables mutually independent, and impose consistency to define \(Y_{ij}\).  Positivity on \(K_{\epsilon}\), together with \(q_{01}\leq1-r\) and \(q_{11}\leq r\), makes both Bernoulli means elements of \([0,1]\).  Conditional mutual independence gives latent ignorability and the conditional-iid plate condition.  Direct multiplication gives
\[
 \begin{array}{ll}
 \Pr(A=1,Y=1\mid q)=r(q_{11}/r)=q_{11},
 &\Pr(A=1,Y=0\mid q)=r-q_{11}=q_{10},\\
 \Pr(A=0,Y=1\mid q)=(1-r)(q_{01}/(1-r))=q_{01},
 &\Pr(A=0,Y=0\mid q)=1-r-q_{01}=q_{00}.
 \end{array}
\]
Thus all defining assumptions of \(\mathfrak M_{m,\epsilon}\) hold, and this construction has exactly the asserted conditional cells, count law, and causal target.
