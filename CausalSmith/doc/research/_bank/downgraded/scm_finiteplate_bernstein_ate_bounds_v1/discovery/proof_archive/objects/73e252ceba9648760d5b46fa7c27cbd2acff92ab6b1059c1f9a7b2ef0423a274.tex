Fix \(m\) and rational \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), and put \(N=N_m\).  Let \(\Gamma\subset\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\times[-1,1]\) be the attainable-pair set.  By the affine-dependence reduction already proved, \((b,t)\in\Gamma\) if and only if there exist \(N+1\) locations and weights satisfying the polynomialized system (1) in thm:exact-cad-recovery.  Hence \(\Gamma\) is compact and semialgebraic by lem:cad-quantifier-elimination.  The same finite-atom formula without the objective characterizes \(\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\), and its relative boundary inside \(\sum_cb_c=1\) is semialgebraic because relative interior and closure are first-order definable.

The graphs of the endpoints are described without any continuity premise by
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 t=L_m(b)&\Longleftrightarrow (b,t)\in\Gamma
 \ \wedge\ \forall s\bigl((b,s)\in\Gamma\Rightarrow t\leq s\bigr),\\
 t=U_m(b)&\Longleftrightarrow (b,t)\in\Gamma
 \ \wedge\ \forall s\bigl((b,s)\in\Gamma\Rightarrow s\leq t\bigr).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
These are semialgebraic relations.  Thus the point-identification predicate \(L_m(b)=U_m(b)\) is semialgebraic.  Since every nonempty fiber image is an interval, its affine dimension is zero exactly under that predicate and is one otherwise.

For \(e\in\{L,U\}\) and \(1\leq k\leq N+1\), let \(E_{e,k}(b)\) be the first-order predicate that there are at most \(k\) atom locations and nonnegative weights satisfying the moment equations and attaining the endpoint selected by (7).  Zero weights permit the phrase ``at most.''  Compactness makes endpoint attainment automatic, and the \(N+1\)-atom reduction gives \(E_{e,N+1}(b)\) for every feasible \(b\).  Therefore the minimum support size equals \(k\) precisely on the semialgebraic set
\[
 E_{e,k}(b)\wedge\neg E_{e,k-1}(b),
 \qquad E_{e,0}(b)\equiv\mathrm{false}.
\tag{8}
\]
Intersect the relative boundary with the finitely many truth-value combinations of the point-identification predicate and the lower and upper predicates (8), discard empty combinations, and apply sign-invariant CAD cell decomposition to each.  The resulting finite cells are semialgebraic strata on which all requested records are constant.  The cited CAD lemma guarantees termination.  Its algebraic sample points, together with witnesses in the existential formulas \(E_{e,k}\), supply endpoint measures, while the negated \(E_{e,k-1}\) certifies minimality.  This completes the proposed atlas for rational \(\epsilon\); no assertion that all boundary fibers are wide is made, consistently with prop:boundary-dirac-regression.
