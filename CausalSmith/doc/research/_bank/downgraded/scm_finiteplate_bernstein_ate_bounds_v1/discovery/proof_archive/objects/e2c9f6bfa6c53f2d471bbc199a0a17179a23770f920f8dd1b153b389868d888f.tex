Write \(r=p(q)\), \(y_1=q_{11}/r\), \(y_0=q_{01}/(1-r)\), \(w_1=w_m(r,p_*)\), and \(w_0=w_m(1-r,1-p_*)\).  Positivity gives \(r,1-r,p_*,1-p_*>0\), and \(0\leq y_0,y_1\leq1\).  For odd \(m\),
\[
 1-w_m(r,p_*)=(1-r)\left(\frac{p_*-r}{p_*}\right)^{m-1}\geq0,
\]
because \(m-1\) is even; for even \(m\),
\[
 1-w_m(r,p_*)=\left(\frac{p_*-r}{p_*}\right)^m\geq0.
\]
The same conclusions hold after replacing \((r,p_*)\) by \((1-r,1-p_*)\).  Direct simplification of the two proposed envelopes gives
\[
 \ell_{m,p_*}=y_1w_1-y_0w_0-(1-w_0),
 \qquad
 u_{m,p_*}=y_1w_1-y_0w_0+(1-w_1).
\]
Since \(f=y_1-y_0\),
\[
 f-\ell_{m,p_*}=y_1(1-w_1)+(1-y_0)(1-w_0)\geq0,
\]
and
\[
 u_{m,p_*}-f=(1-y_1)(1-w_1)+y_0(1-w_0)\geq0.
\]

It remains to verify that these are observable degree-\(m\) polynomials.  In both parity cases \(w_m(0,p_*)=0\), so the polynomial \(w_m(r,p_*)\) is divisible by \(r\), and its quotient has degree at most \(m-1\).  Likewise \(w_m(1-r,1-p_*)\) is divisible by \(1-r\).  Thus every displayed term defining \(\ell_{m,p_*}\) and \(u_{m,p_*}\) is a polynomial of total degree at most \(m\) in the four cells.  On the simplex, every such polynomial is a degree-\(m\) Bernstein linear combination, by homogenizing with \((\sum_{a,y}q_{ay})^{m-d}=1\).  Hence both envelopes lie in \(V_m\), and the pointwise inequalities prove \((\ell_{m,p_*},u_{m,p_*})\in\mathcal E_m\).

Applying thm:sharp-envelope-domination gives the asserted interval inclusion for every feasible \(b\).  When \(m\geq2\), its two proper-contact-hull conclusions give a nonempty relatively open set of strict lower improvement and, separately, a possibly different nonempty relatively open set of strict upper improvement.
