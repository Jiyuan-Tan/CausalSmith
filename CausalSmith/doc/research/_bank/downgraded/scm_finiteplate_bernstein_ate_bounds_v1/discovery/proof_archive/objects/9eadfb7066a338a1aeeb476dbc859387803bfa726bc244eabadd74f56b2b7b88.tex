The paper derives the sharp primal--dual interval, proves an iff Bernstein-span identification frontier and strict width throughout the relative interior, and constructs finite-latent endpoint SCMs. A generic contact-hull theorem characterizes equality for every valid degree-\(m\) envelope; its Lee--Weidner corollary dominates the intersection over all scalar references and proves simultaneous strict improvement of both endpoints on one nonempty relatively open interior set. Exact multinomial inversion gives a one-dimensional semialgebraic confidence set with terminating CAD recovery, and a finite CAD atlas classifies boundary identification and minimum endpoint support sizes for rational \(\epsilon\).
