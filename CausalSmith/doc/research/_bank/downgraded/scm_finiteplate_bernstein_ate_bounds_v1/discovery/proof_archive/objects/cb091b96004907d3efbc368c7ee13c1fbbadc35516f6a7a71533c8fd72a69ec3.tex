Let \(H_{m,\epsilon}:=\operatorname{conv}\{(g_m(q),f(q)):q\in K_{\epsilon}\}\). The set \(H_{m,\epsilon}\) is compact and equals
\[
 \left\{\left(\int g_m\,d\nu,\int f\,d\nu\right):
 \nu\in\mathcal P(K_{\epsilon})\right\}.
\]
Every pair in \(H_{m,\epsilon}\) has a representing probability measure supported on at most \(N_m+1\) points of \(K_{\epsilon}\), and therefore has a compatible hierarchical SCM in \(\mathfrak M_{m,\epsilon}\) with at most \(N_m+1\) latent cluster laws. Consequently, for every \(b\in\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon}\), the sharp fiber \(\Theta_{m,\epsilon}(b)\) is a nonempty attained compact interval \([L_m(b),U_m(b)]\), and both endpoints have such \(N_m+1\)-atom witnesses. If \(b\in\operatorname{relint}(\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon})\), then \(D_m(b)=(L_m(b),U_m(b))\) with both dual extrema attained; each endpoint then has a representing measure, and hence a compatible hierarchical SCM, supported on at most \(N_m={m+3\choose3}\) latent laws. If an optimal dual polynomial has contact image of affine dimension \(r\), its associated endpoint admits at most \(r+1\) atoms.
