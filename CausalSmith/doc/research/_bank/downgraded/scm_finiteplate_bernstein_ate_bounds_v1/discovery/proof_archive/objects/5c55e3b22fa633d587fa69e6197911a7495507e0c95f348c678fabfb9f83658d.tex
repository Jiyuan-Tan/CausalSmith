For every \(b\in\operatorname{relint}(\mathcal B_{m,\epsilon})\), the sharp identified set equals the attained interval \([L_m(b),U_m(b)]\), and \(D_m(b)=(L_m(b),U_m(b))\) with both dual extrema attained. Each endpoint is generated by a measure supported on at most \(N_m={m+3\choose3}\) points of \(K_{\epsilon}\), hence by a compatible model in \(\mathfrak M_{m,\epsilon}\) with at most \(N_m\) latent cluster laws. If an optimal dual polynomial has contact image of affine dimension \(r\), its associated endpoint admits at most \(r+1\) atoms.
