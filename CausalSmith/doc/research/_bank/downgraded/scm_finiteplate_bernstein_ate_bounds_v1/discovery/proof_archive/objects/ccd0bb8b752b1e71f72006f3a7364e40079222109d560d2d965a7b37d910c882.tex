Exact CAD recovery and the boundary atlas are terminating for rational inputs but may have high worst-case computational cost. The named-comparator result concerns only the homogeneous, constant-coefficient, \(b\)-only scalar-reference transplant and its intersection; it makes no dominance claim over Lee and Weidner's full observed-covariate, covariate-dependent procedure.
