All products below are well typed by the dimensions in the proposition.  From the target-row factorization and the unit-factor-span premise,
\[
y=v_n^\top U_0=w^\top VU_0=w^\top M.
\]
Thus \(y\) is a linear combination of the rows of \(M\), so \(y\in\operatorname{row}(M)\).

Assumption~\ref{ass:exact-rank} gives \(\operatorname{rank}(M)=r\).  Hence rank-\(r\) truncation leaves the matrix unchanged, \(M_r=M\), and therefore \(M_r^+=M^+\).  The Moore--Penrose identity \(MM^+M=M\), together with \(y=w^\top M\), implies
\[
yM^+M=w^\top MM^+M=w^\top M=y.
\]
The post/pre time-factor-span premise separately gives
\[
B=VU_1=VU_0A=MA.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\begin{aligned}
yM_r^+B
&=yM^+B\\
&=yM^+MA\\
&=yA\\
&=v_n^\top U_0A\\
&=v_n^\top U_1.
\end{aligned}
\]
The last row is the latent untreated target-post survival row under the factor representation assumed in the proposition.  Thus its equality to the observed-law spectral functional is derived from the two span premises and exact rank, rather than imposed as a definition of the causal target.

We next verify every witness claim from Definition~\ref{def:rational-witness}.  Its displayed donor-pre matrix satisfies
\[
\det(M)=\frac45\frac3{10}-\frac35\frac35
=\frac6{25}-\frac9{25}
=-\frac3{25}\ne0.
\]
Therefore \(M\) is invertible, \(\operatorname{rank}(M)=2=r\), and \(M_r^+=M^{-1}\).  Moreover,
\[
\left(\frac12,\frac12\right)M
=\left(
\frac12\left(\frac45+\frac35\right),
\frac12\left(\frac35+\frac3{10}\right)
\right)
=\left(\frac7{10},\frac9{20}\right)
=y.
\]
Right multiplication by \(M^{-1}\) yields
\[
yM^{-1}=\left(\frac12,\frac12\right).
\]
Set
\[
d=2,\qquad V=I_2,\qquad U_0=M,\qquad U_1=B,
\qquad A=M^{-1}B,\qquad v_n^\top=yM^{-1}.
\]
Then
\[
VU_0=M,\qquad v_n^\top U_0=yM^{-1}M=y,
\qquad VU_1=B,\qquad U_0A=MM^{-1}B=B=U_1.
\]
Taking \(w=v_n\), which is well typed because \(d=N_0=2\), also gives
\[
w^\top V=v_n^\top I_2=v_n^\top.
\]
Thus both factor equations and both span premises in the proposition hold for the witness.  Its spectral value is
\[
\begin{aligned}
yM_r^+B
&=yM^{-1}B\\
&=\left(\frac12,\frac12\right)
\begin{pmatrix}7/10&2/5\\1/2&1/5\end{pmatrix}\\
&=\left(
\frac12\left(\frac7{10}+\frac12\right),
\frac12\left(\frac25+\frac15\right)
\right)\\
&=\left(\frac35,\frac3{10}\right)=:\theta^0.
\end{aligned}
\]

It remains to check that this row is genuinely the witness's untreated potential target-post survival row.  The finitely supported laws in Definition~\ref{def:rational-witness} are valid probability laws: every displayed survival pair \((a,b)\), including \(\theta^0\), obeys
\[
\frac15\le b<a\le\frac45,
\qquad a-b\ge\frac15.
\]
For \(\epsilon_x\in\{-1,1\}\), the three event-time atom probabilities are therefore bounded below respectively by \(3/20\), \(7/40\), and \(7/40\), and their sum is
\[
1-a-\frac{\epsilon_x}{20}
+a-b+\frac{\epsilon_x}{40}
+b+\frac{\epsilon_x}{40}=1.
\]
At the temporally ordered two-point grid of the construction, the conditional survival probabilities of the target untreated potential time, for which \((a,b)=(3/5,3/10)\), are
\[
\begin{aligned}
P^\star\!\left(T^0_{\mathrm{post},i}>t_{c1}\mid X_{ci}=x\right)
&=\left(a-b+\frac{\epsilon_x}{40}\right)
+\left(b+\frac{\epsilon_x}{40}\right)
=a+\frac{\epsilon_x}{20},\\
P^\star\!\left(T^0_{\mathrm{post},i}>t_{c2}\mid X_{ci}=x\right)
&=b+\frac{\epsilon_x}{40}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Because \(P^\star(X_{ci}=0)=P^\star(X_{ci}=1)=1/2\) and \(\epsilon_0+\epsilon_1=0\), the law of total probability gives
\[
\left(
P^\star(T^0_{\mathrm{post},i}>t_{c1}),
P^\star(T^0_{\mathrm{post},i}>t_{c2})
\right)
=(a,b)
=\left(\frac35,\frac3{10}\right)
=\theta^0.
\]
Thus the target's latent untreated post-period row is \(v_n^\top U_1=yM^{-1}B=\theta^0\).

For completeness, the full-data augmentation does not alter any observed margin.  In each of the five non-target-post cells, the observed event time is the stipulated untreated potential event time.  In the target factual-post cell, consistency sets the observed time equal to \(T^1_{\mathrm{post},i}\), whose marginal survival row is \(s_1\); the newly attached \(T^0_{\mathrm{post},i}\) does not enter \(U_{ci}=\min(T_{ci},C_{ci})\) or \(\Delta_{ci}=\mathbf 1\{T_{ci}\le C_{ci}\}\).  Mutual conditional independence of \(T^0_{\mathrm{post},i}\), \(T^1_{\mathrm{post},i}\), and \(C_{ci}\) given \(X_{ci}\) implies in particular conditional independent censoring for the factual observed time.  The censor-time atom probabilities are all at least \(1/5\), since they are \(1-\gamma_{1x}\), \(\gamma_{1x}-\gamma_{2x}\), and \(\gamma_{2x}\) for the two displayed pairs \((\gamma_{1x},\gamma_{2x})=(4/5,1/2)\) and \((3/5,2/5)\).  Hence censoring positivity holds on the fixed two-point horizon.  This also shows directly that the causal embedding is compatible with the witness's unchanged six observed laws.

No limiting argument is used in this proposition: \(N_0\), \(T_0\), \(J\), and \(r\) are fixed, and all inversions are justified above by either Assumption~\ref{ass:exact-rank} in the general argument or the nonzero determinant in the witness argument.
