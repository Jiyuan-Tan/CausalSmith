%%% FIELD target-statement
For every fixed cell and all its fixed coordinates jointly, the estimator in Definition~\ref{def:cell-estimator} satisfies
\[
\sqrt K(\widehat s_c-s_c)=K^{-1/2}\sum_{i=1}^K\phi_c(O_{ci})+o_P(1).
\]
The conditional second-order remainder is \(O_P(a_Kb_K)+o_P(K^{-1/2})\), no first-order event- or censor-nuisance term remains, and the foldwise plug-in influence vectors are \(L_2(P_c)\)-consistent. Stacking over the finite set \(\mathcal C\) gives the same expansion for \((\widehat y,\widehat M,\widehat B,\widehat s_1)\) with influence \(\Phi_i\).
%%% FIELD ass-relative-survival-bv-condition
For every \(K\), \(c\in\mathcal C\), and \(1\le f\le L\), conditional on the training sample outside \(\mathcal V_{cf}\), the path \(u\mapsto R_{cf}(u\mid X_{ci})\) is of bounded variation on \([0,\max_{1\le\ell\le d_c}t_{c\ell}]\) for \(P_c\)-almost every \(X_{ci}\), almost surely over the training sample.
%%% FIELD ass-relative-survival-variation-rate-condition
The foldwise relative-survival variations satisfy
\[
\max_{c\in\mathcal C}\max_{1\le f\le L}\max_{1\le\ell\le d_c}
\left\|V_{cf\ell}(X_{ci})\right\|_{L_2(P_c)}=O_P(a_K).
\]
%%% FIELD conditional-partial-result
The following calculation proves the target conditional on Assumptions~\ref{ass:event-origin-support}, \ref{ass:learner-origin-normalization}, \ref{ass:relative-survival-bv}, and \ref{ass:relative-survival-variation-rate}; the last two assumptions are proposed in this round and therefore are not silently treated as frozen premises.

Fix \(c\in\mathcal C\), \(1\le \ell\le d_c\), and a fold \(f\), and abbreviate \(t=t_{c\ell}\), \(Q=Q_c\), \(G=G_c\), \(\bar Q=\widehat Q_{c,-f}\), and \(\bar G=\widehat G_{c,-f}\).  Let \(\mathcal T_{cf}\) be the training-sample sigma-field.  Assumption~\ref{ass:event-origin-support} gives \(Q(0\mid X)=1\), and Assumption~\ref{ass:learner-origin-normalization} gives \(\bar Q(0\mid X)=1\).  All statements below concerning \(\bar Q\) and \(\bar G\) are conditional on \(\mathcal T_{cf}\).

Put
\[
N(u)=\mathbf 1\{U\le u,\Delta=1\},
\qquad
Y(u)=\mathbf 1\{U\ge u\}.
\]
By Assumption~\ref{ass:conditional-censoring}, the definition \(\Delta=\mathbf 1\{T\le C\}\), and the left-continuous convention \(G(u\mid X)=P(C\ge u\mid X)\),
\[
E\{dN(u)\mid X\}=Q(u-\mid X)G(u\mid X)\Lambda(du\mid X),
\qquad
E\{Y(u)\mid X\}=Q(u-\mid X)G(u\mid X).
\]
The absence of an event atom at zero, supplied by Assumption~\ref{ass:event-origin-support}, makes the integration interval \((0,t]\) agree with Definition~\ref{def:aipcw-score}.  Taking the conditional expectation of that definition and applying Tonelli separately to the positive and negative finite hazard integrals yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
E\{\psi_t(O;\bar Q,\bar G)\mid X,\mathcal T_{cf}\}
&=\bar Q(t\mid X)\left[
1+\int_{(0,t]}
\frac{Q(u-\mid X)G(u\mid X)}{\bar Q(u\mid X)\bar G(u\mid X)}
(\bar\Lambda-\Lambda)(du\mid X)
\right].
\end{aligned}
\]
Here and below, the displayed integral with respect to \(\bar\Lambda-\Lambda\) denotes the difference of the two finite Stieltjes integrals with the same predictable integrand.

Define \(R(u\mid X)=Q(u\mid X)/\bar Q(u\mid X)-1\), using the declared \(0/0=1\) convention.  The product-integral quotient rule gives
\[
dR(u\mid X)=
\frac{Q(u-\mid X)}{\bar Q(u\mid X)}
(\bar\Lambda-\Lambda)(du\mid X).
\]
For completeness, at a jump \(u\), the product-integral equations \(Q(u)=Q(u-)\{1-\Delta\Lambda(u)\}\) and \(\bar Q(u)=\bar Q(u-)\{1-\Delta\bar\Lambda(u)\}\) imply
\[
\Delta\!\left(\frac Q{\bar Q}\right)(u)
=\frac{Q(u-)}{\bar Q(u)}
\{\Delta\bar\Lambda(u)-\Delta\Lambda(u)\};
\]
on the continuous part the ordinary quotient rule gives the same signed differential.  Because \(R(0\mid X)=0\), integration from zero to \(t\) gives
\[
Q(t\mid X)=\bar Q(t\mid X)\left[
1+\int_{(0,t]}
\frac{Q(u-\mid X)}{\bar Q(u\mid X)}
(\bar\Lambda-\Lambda)(du\mid X)
\right].
\]
Subtracting this identity from the preceding score mean proves the exact orthogonality identity
\[
\mathfrak R_{cf\ell}(X)
:=E\{\psi_t(O;\bar Q,\bar G)\mid X,\mathcal T_{cf}\}-Q(t\mid X)
=\bar Q(t\mid X)
\int_{(0,t]}
\left\{\frac{G(u\mid X)}{\bar G(u\mid X)}-1\right\}
dR(u\mid X).
\]
In particular, setting either nuisance equal to its truth makes the right-hand side zero, so there is no first-order event- or censor-nuisance term.

Let
\[
A_{cf\ell}(X)=
\sup_{0\le u\le t}
\left|\frac1{\bar G(u\mid X)}-\frac1{G(u\mid X)}\right|.
\]
Since \(0\le \bar Q(t\mid X)\le1\), \(0\le G(u\mid X)\le1\), and Assumption~\ref{ass:relative-survival-bv} makes \(dR\) a finite signed Stieltjes measure,
\[
\begin{aligned}
|\mathfrak R_{cf\ell}(X)|
&\le A_{cf\ell}(X)
|dR(\cdot\mid X)|((0,t])\\
&=A_{cf\ell}(X)V_{cf\ell}(X).
\end{aligned}
\]
Assumption~\ref{ass:censor-estimability} gives \(\|A_{cf\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}=O_P(b_K)\), while Assumption~\ref{ass:relative-survival-variation-rate} gives \(\|V_{cf\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}=O_P(a_K)\).  Cauchy--Schwarz, conditional on \(\mathcal T_{cf}\), therefore yields the required second-order bound
\[
\left|P_c\{\psi_t(\bar Q,\bar G)-\psi_t(Q,G)\}\right|
\le \|A_{cf\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}
\|V_{cf\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}
=O_P(a_Kb_K).
\]
This is the load-bearing use of the proposed variation-rate assumption.  Assumption~\ref{ass:nuisance-product-rate} turns the last quantity into \(o_P(K^{-1/2})\).

We next control the cross-fitted empirical term.  For \(0\le u\le t\), put
\[
r_t(u\mid X)=\frac{Q(t\mid X)}{Q(u\mid X)},
\qquad
\bar r_t(u\mid X)=\frac{\bar Q(t\mid X)}{\bar Q(u\mid X)},
\]
again using \(0/0=1\).  These paths are nondecreasing in \(u\) and take values in \([0,1]\).  The same jump calculation as above gives
\[
d\bar r_t(u\mid X)=\bar r_t(u\mid X)\bar\Lambda(du\mid X).
\]
Consequently Definition~\ref{def:aipcw-score} can be rewritten, for \(w=t\wedge U\), as
\[
\psi_t(O;\bar Q,\bar G)
=\bar Q(t\mid X)
-\mathbf 1\{U\le t,\Delta=1\}
\frac{\bar r_t(U\mid X)}{\bar G(U\mid X)}
+\int_{(0,w]}\frac1{\bar G(u\mid X)}\,d\bar r_t(u\mid X).
\]
Define
\[
D_{Q,cf\ell}(X)=
\sup_{0\le u\le t}|\bar r_t(u\mid X)-r_t(u\mid X)|.
\]
Assumption~\ref{ass:event-estimability} gives \(\|D_{Q,cf\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}=O_P(a_K)\), and \(0\le D_{Q,cf\ell}\le1\).  Assumption~\ref{ass:censor-positivity} makes \(G^{-1}\) nondecreasing with supremum at most \(g^{-1}\).  Splitting the last Stieltjes integral as
\[
\int\frac1{\bar G}\,d\bar r_t-
\int\frac1G\,dr_t
=\int\left(\frac1{\bar G}-\frac1G\right)d\bar r_t
+\int\frac1G\,d(\bar r_t-r_t)
\]
and applying Stieltjes integration by parts to the second term gives a constant \(C_g<\infty\), depending only on \(g\), such that
\[
\left|
\int_{(0,w]}\frac1{\bar G}\,d\bar r_t-
\int_{(0,w]}\frac1G\,dr_t
\right|
\le A_{cf\ell}(X)+C_gD_{Q,cf\ell}(X).
\]
Indeed, the first term is bounded by \(A_{cf\ell}\) because the total variation of the nondecreasing path \(\bar r_t\) is at most one; the boundary terms and the integral against \(d(G^{-1})\) in the second term are bounded by a constant times \(D_{Q,cf\ell}\), because \(G^{-1}\) has total variation at most \(g^{-1}-1\).  The baseline and event terms in the rewritten score satisfy the same bound.  Hence
\[
\|\psi_t(\bar Q,\bar G)-\psi_t(Q,G)\|_{L_2(P_c)}
\le C_g\{
\|D_{Q,cf\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}+
\|A_{cf\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}\}
=O_P(a_K+b_K)=o_P(1),
\]
where the last equality uses Assumptions~\ref{ass:event-rate-vanishes} and \ref{ass:censor-rate-vanishes}.

Let \(n_{cf}=|\mathcal V_{cf}|\), let \(\mathbb P_{cf}\) be the empirical measure on that validation fold, and abbreviate the fitted and true scores by \(\widehat\psi_{cf\ell}\) and \(\psi_{c\ell}\).  Since the folds partition the \(K\) observations, Definition~\ref{def:cell-estimator} and \(P_c\psi_{c\ell}=S_c(t)\) give the exact decomposition
\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat s_{c\ell}-S_c(t)
&=K^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^K\phi_{c\ell}(O_{ci})\\
&\quad+\sum_{f=1}^L\frac{n_{cf}}K
(\mathbb P_{cf}-P_c)
(\widehat\psi_{cf\ell}-\psi_{c\ell})\\
&\quad+\sum_{f=1}^L\frac{n_{cf}}K
P_c(\widehat\psi_{cf\ell}-\psi_{c\ell}).
\end{aligned}
\]
By Assumption~\ref{ass:cell-iid}, validation observations are independent of \(\mathcal T_{cf}\) and are conditionally i.i.d. with law \(P_c\).  Conditional Chebyshev and the preceding \(L_2(P_c)\) convergence therefore imply
\[
(\mathbb P_{cf}-P_c)
(\widehat\psi_{cf\ell}-\psi_{c\ell})
=o_P(n_{cf}^{-1/2})=o_P(K^{-1/2}),
\]
because \(L\) is fixed and the folds are approximately equal by Assumption~\ref{ass:fixed-panel}.  The bias term in the exact decomposition is \(O_P(a_Kb_K)=o_P(K^{-1/2})\) by the Cauchy--Schwarz bound above.  It follows that
\[
\sqrt K\{\widehat s_{c\ell}-S_c(t)\}
=K^{-1/2}\sum_{i=1}^K\phi_{c\ell}(O_{ci})+o_P(1).
\]

The number of folds, cells, and coordinates is fixed by Assumption~\ref{ass:fixed-panel}.  A finite union bound therefore makes every displayed rate simultaneous over \(c\), \(f\), and \(\ell\).  Assumption~\ref{ass:cross-cell-independence} then permits the cell influences with common within-cell index \(i\) to be stacked as \(\Phi_i\), and extracting the target-pre, donor-pre, donor-post, and factual-post blocks gives the asserted joint expansion for \((\widehat y,\widehat M,\widehat B,\widehat s_1)\).

Finally define the foldwise centered plug-in influence coordinate by
\[
\widehat\phi_{cf\ell}(O)=
\widehat\psi_{cf\ell}(O)-\widehat s_{c\ell}.
\]
Assumption~\ref{ass:influence-moment} supplies finite second moments, so the empirical average of the true influence is \(O_P(K^{-1/2})\).  The expansion just proved gives \(|\widehat s_{c\ell}-S_c(t)|=o_P(1)\); hence
\[
\|\widehat\phi_{cf\ell}-\phi_{c\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}
\le
\|\widehat\psi_{cf\ell}-\psi_{c\ell}\|_{L_2(P_c)}
+|\widehat s_{c\ell}-S_c(t)|
=o_P(1).
\]
Another finite union bound proves joint \(L_2(P_c)\)-consistency of all plug-in influence vectors.  Thus every conclusion of the target follows once the four explicitly named support, normalization, bounded-variation, and variation-rate premises are part of its frozen dependency spine.
