Let \(g(x)=\sqrt{x_+}\). The contact endpoint theorem gives the following conditional limit for the ordinary centered upper endpoint:
\[
 \mathcal O_{h,z}
 =\mathcal L\{g(h+z+Z^*)-g(h+z)\}, \tag{1}
\]
whereas its sampling limit, centered at the population endpoint, is
\[
 \mathcal S_h
 =\mathcal L\{g(h+Z)-\sqrt h\}. \tag{2}
\]
For the retained algorithm, the corresponding conditional centered limit is
\[
 \mathcal R_{h,z}
 =\mathcal L\{g(h+z+Z^*)-g(h+z)\mid
 Z^*>-(h+z)\}. \tag{3}
\]
These kernels are not constant in the first-stage realization \(z\). Put
\[
 f(x)=\min\{(-x)_+,1\}.
\]
This function is bounded by one and has Lipschitz constant one. At \(h=0\), the law in (2) is supported on \([0,\infty)\), so \(\int f\,d\mathcal S_0=0\). At \(z=1\), the variable in (1) equals \(-1\) whenever \(Z^*\leq-1\); hence
\[
 \int f\,d\mathcal O_{0,1}
 \geq P(Z^*\leq-1)=\Phi(-1/2)>0. \tag{4}
\]
For (3), on the retained event \(-1<Z^*<-1/2\), the centered endpoint belongs to \((-1,-1+1/\sqrt2)\), and therefore
\[
 \int f\,d\mathcal R_{0,1}
 \geq (1-1/\sqrt2)
 \frac{P(-1<Z^*<-1/2)}{P(Z^*>-1)}>0. \tag{5}
\]
Normal probabilities and the integrals in (1)--(3) are continuous in \((h,z)\) near \((0,1)\); the conditioning probability in (3) is bounded away from zero there. Thus (4)--(5) imply that there are \(h_0>0\), \(\rho>0\), and \(\varepsilon>0\) such that, for
\[
 H_0=[0,h_0]\subseteq[0,\bar H],
\]
after reducing \(h_0\) if necessary, both bounded-Lipschitz distances from (2) exceed \(2\varepsilon\) whenever \(h\in H_0\) and \(z\in[1-\rho,1+\rho]\). The probability
\[
 \pi_0=P\{Z\in[1-\rho,1+\rho]\}
\]
is strictly positive. Uniform conditional convergence in the contact endpoint theorem and the triangle inequality now give, for any \(0<\pi<\pi_0\),
\[
 \inf_{h\in H_0}P_{n,h}\!\left\{
 d_{\mathrm{BL}}(\widehat{\mathcal O}_{n,h},\mathcal S_h)
 >\varepsilon\right\}\geq\pi+o(1), \tag{6}
\]
and the identical assertion for the centered version of \(\widehat{\mathbb L}_n^{\mathrm{ret}}\).

Equation (6), coordinate projection, and the uniformly negligible zero-retention fallback establish the asserted positive-probability bounded-Lipschitz separation for both endpoint-pair laws. Hence uniform conditional weak consistency fails on \(H_0\). No conclusion about coverage at any preassigned percentile level is drawn, because bounded-Lipschitz separation does not by itself locate the distributional discrepancy at that level.
