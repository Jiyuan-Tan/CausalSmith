Let \((G,s,c)\) be any finite simple bow-free singleton-target instance in the stated class.  Thus \(c(v)\in\mathbb Q_{>0}\) for every \(v\in V^{-}\), \(r(G,s)\leq 3\), and \(\mathcal C(G,s)\neq\varnothing\).  Choose \(R_0\in\mathcal C(G,s)\).  By Definitions \(\mathrm{def{:}singleton\mbox{-}hedges}\) and \(\mathrm{def{:}minimal\mbox{-}residual\mbox{-}clutter}\), there is a hedge \(F\) such that \(\{s\}\subsetneq F\) and \(R_0=F\setminus\{s\}\), so \(R_0\neq\varnothing\).  Finiteness and positivity give
\[
 m_0:=\min_{v\in R_0}c(v)>0.
\]
For every vector \(y\) feasible for the relaxation in Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}covering\mbox{-}lp}\), nonnegativity of \(y\), the constraint indexed by \(R_0\), and the definition of \(m_0\) imply
\[
 \sum_{v\in V^{-}}c(v)y_v
 \geq \sum_{v\in R_0}c(v)y_v
 \geq m_0\sum_{v\in R_0}y_v
 \geq m_0>0.
\]
Hence \(\operatorname{LP}(G,s,c)>0\), so the displayed ratio is well defined.  By Theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}rank\mbox{-}three\mbox{-}rounding}\), there is a feasible intervention \(A_t\) satisfying
\[
 \sum_{v\in A_t}c(v)\leq 2\operatorname{LP}(G,s,c).
\]
Definition \(\mathrm{def{:}mcid\mbox{-}objective}\) minimizes over all feasible interventions, whence
\[
 \operatorname{OPT}(G,s,c)
 \leq \sum_{v\in A_t}c(v)
 \leq 2\operatorname{LP}(G,s,c).
\]
Division by the strictly positive LP value proves
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{OPT}(G,s,c)}{\operatorname{LP}(G,s,c)}\leq 2
\]
for every instance in the full weighted class.

For the matching lower bound, fix \(n\geq2\), equip \(K_n\) with any total order, form its private-guard expansion \(G_{K_n}\), and assign unit cost to every vertex in \(V^{-}\).  Theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}private\mbox{-}guard\mbox{-}realization}\) shows that this is a finite simple bow-free singleton-target instance, that its bidirected subgraph is a tree of radius at most two, and that
\[
 \mathcal C(G_{K_n},s)
 =\bigl\{\{x_u,x_v,z_e\}:e=\{u,v\}\in E(K_n)\bigr\}.
\]
Because \(E(K_n)\neq\varnothing\), the same theorem gives \(r(G_{K_n},s)=3\).  Theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}objective\mbox{-}preserving\mbox{-}hardness}\) gives
\[
 \operatorname{OPT}(G_{K_n},s,c)=\tau(K_n).
\]
Any set of at most \(n-2\) vertices omits two vertices and therefore fails to cover their edge, whereas deleting any one vertex leaves a cover of all edges.  Consequently \(\tau(K_n)=n-1\).

Set \(y_{x_u}=1/2\) for every \(u\in U\) and \(y_{z_e}=0\) for every \(e\in E(K_n)\).  The displayed description of the clutter makes every covering constraint equal to \(1/2+1/2+0=1\), so this vector is feasible and has unit-cost objective \(n/2\).  Conversely, for any feasible \(y\), summing the constraint
\[
 y_{x_u}+y_{x_v}+y_{z_e}\geq1
\]
over all \(e=\{u,v\}\in E(K_n)\) counts each \(x_u\) exactly \(n-1\) times and yields
\[
 (n-1)\sum_{u\in U}y_{x_u}+\sum_{e\in E(K_n)}y_{z_e}
 \geq \binom n2.
\]
After division by \(n-1>0\),
\[
 \sum_{u\in U}y_{x_u}
 +\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{e\in E(K_n)}y_{z_e}
 \geq \frac n2.
\]
Since \(n\geq2\) and every \(y_{z_e}\geq0\), the unit-cost objective obeys
\[
 \sum_{u\in U}y_{x_u}+\sum_{e\in E(K_n)}y_{z_e}
 \geq \frac n2.
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{LP}(G_{K_n},s,c)=n/2\), and therefore
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{OPT}(G_{K_n},s,c)}
 {\operatorname{LP}(G_{K_n},s,c)}
 =\frac{n-1}{n/2}=2-\frac2n.
\]
These unit-cost radius-two-tree instances belong to the full weighted class.  Their ratios converge to two, so the supremum is at least two; the preceding universal upper bound shows that it is exactly two.

Finally assume \(\mathsf{UGC}\), fix \(\epsilon>0\), and suppose for contradiction that a polynomial-time \((2-\epsilon)\)-approximation exists on the narrower subclass of unit-cost instances with nonempty clutter, rank at most three, and a radius-two bidirected tree.  Given any finite simple source graph \(H\) with at least one edge, construct \(G_H\) and assign unit costs.  By Theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}private\mbox{-}guard\mbox{-}realization}\), the resulting instance lies in that subclass.  If the assumed algorithm returns a feasible intervention \(A\), the polynomial-time guard-replacement map in Theorem \(\mathrm{thm{:}objective\mbox{-}preserving\mbox{-}hardness}\) returns a vertex cover \(C_A\) with \(|C_A|\leq |A|\); the same theorem gives \(\operatorname{OPT}(G_H,s,c)=\tau(H)\).  Therefore
\[
 |C_A|
 \leq |A|
 \leq (2-\epsilon)\operatorname{OPT}(G_H,s,c)
 =(2-\epsilon)\tau(H).
\]
For an edgeless \(H\), the empty set is an optimal vertex cover, so this construction together with that trivial case would be a polynomial-time \((2-\epsilon)\)-approximation for Minimum Vertex Cover on all finite simple graphs.  This contradicts Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}ugc\mbox{-}vertex\mbox{-}cover\mbox{-}hardness}\).  Hence, under \(\mathsf{UGC}\), no such approximation algorithm exists on the stated narrower subclass.
