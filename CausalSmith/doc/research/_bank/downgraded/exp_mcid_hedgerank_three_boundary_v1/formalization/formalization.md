## Theorems (T)

### T-block: t1 — Every \(F\in\mathsf{Hdg}(G,s)\) has \(|F|\geq4\)
**Statement.** Every \(F\in\mathsf{Hdg}(G,s)\) has \(|F|\geq4\). A four-vertex \(F\) is a hedge if and only if the unordered pairs of \(D[F]\) form a spanning path oriented toward \(s\) and \(B[F]\) is the complementary spanning path. ### T-block: t2 — For every ordered finite simple graph \(H=(U,E)\), the gr…
**Statement.** For every ordered finite simple graph \(H=(U,E)\), the graph \(G_H\) is bow-free and acyclic, its bidirected subgraph is a tree of radius at most two centered at \(s\), and \(\mathcal C(G_H,s)=\{\{x_u,x_v,z_e\}:e=\{u,v\}\in E\}\). Hence \(r(G_H,s)=3\) when \(E\neq\varnothing\), and \(r(G_H,s)=0\) otherwise.

### T-block: t3 — Assign unit cost to every vertex of \(G_H\)
**Statement.** Assign unit cost to every vertex of \(G_H\). Every vertex cover \(C\subseteq U\) maps to the intervention \(\{x_u:u\in C\}\) with the same cost, and every feasible intervention maps in polynomial time, by replacing each selected \(z_e\) with an endpoint of \(e\), to a vertex cover of no larger cost. Therefore \(\operatorname{OPT}(G_H,s,c)=\tau(H)\). The reduction is an \(L\)-reduction with constants one, so rank-three MCID is \(\mathsf{NP}\)-hard and \(\mathsf{APX}\)-hard even with unit costs and a radius-two bidirected tree; the \(\mathsf{APX}\)-hardness remains for source graphs of maximum degree three.

### T-block: t4 — Given a supplied total order on \(V\), under…
**Statement.** Given a supplied total order on \(V\), under \(r(G,s)\leq3\), every nonempty \(R\in\mathcal C(G,s)\) has size three and meets both \(P_s\) and \(T_s\). For an optimal \(y\) of the natural covering relaxation, every \(A_t\) is feasible for \(0<t<1/2\), and some breakpoint choice satisfies \(\sum_{v\in A_t}c(v)\leq2\operatorname{LP}(G,s,c)\leq2\operatorname{OPT}(G,s,c)\). Such a choice is found in polynomial time by the promised-rank enumerator using the supplied order.

### T-block: t5 — The supremum of…
**Statement.** The supremum of \(\operatorname{OPT}(G,s,c)/\operatorname{LP}(G,s,c)\) over all positive-rational-cost finite simple bow-free singleton-target instances with \(r(G,s)\leq3\) and \(\mathcal C(G,s)\neq\varnothing\) equals two. ### T-block: t6 — Given a supplied total order on \(V\) and a fixed promise…
**Statement.** Given a supplied total order on \(V\) and a fixed promised rank bound \(r_0\), all inclusion-minimal residual hedges can be output exactly once, in the lexicographic order induced by the supplied order, in time \(|V|^{r_0+O(1)}\) and polynomial space.
