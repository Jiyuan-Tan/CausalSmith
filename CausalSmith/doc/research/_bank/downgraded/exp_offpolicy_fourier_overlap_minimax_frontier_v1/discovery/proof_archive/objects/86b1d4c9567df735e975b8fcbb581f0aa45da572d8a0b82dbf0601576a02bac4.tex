Every \(M=\sum_r\lambda_r(v_r\otimes v_r)\in\mathfrak M(K)\) is the second moment of the genuine symmetric prior assigning mass \(\lambda_r/2\) to each of \(v_r\) and \(-v_r\).  For that prior, writing \(T=\theta(\mathbf V)\), \(Y_z=N_z\mathbf V\), and \(M=\mathbb E[\mathbf V\otimes\mathbf V]\), symmetry gives zero means and
\[
 \mathbb E[\operatorname{Var}(T\mid Y_z)]
 =\inf_{g\ \mathrm{Borel}}\mathbb E[(T-g(Y_z))^2]
 \le\inf_{a_z}\mathbb E[(T-a_z^{\mathsf T}Y_z)^2]
 =\psi_z(M).
\]
Thus the moment optimizer can be realized by a genuine prior, but this elementary realization supplies the inequality in the wrong direction for the required converse; what remains is a dimension-free lower comparison for a suitably chosen decomposition.
