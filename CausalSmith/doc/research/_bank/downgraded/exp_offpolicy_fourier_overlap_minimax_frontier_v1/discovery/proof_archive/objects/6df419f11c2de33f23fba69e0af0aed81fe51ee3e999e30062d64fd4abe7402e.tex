Fix \(p\ne q\) in \([\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\) and \(n\ge2\).  Put
\[
 A_{p,q}=(1-p)q^2+p(1-q)^2,
 \qquad
 C_{p,q}=\frac{(1-q)^2}{1-p}+\frac{q^2}{p}.
\]
Source and target overlap imply \(0<p,q<1\).  Therefore \(A_{p,q}>0\) and \(C_{p,q}<\infty\), and both constants are independent of \(n\).  By \texttt{lem:hub-frontier-rate-sandwich},
\[
 \frac{n-1}{n^2}A_{p,q}
 \le R_n^{\star}\le F_n
 \le\frac{1+(n-1)C_{p,q}}{n^2}.
\]
For \(n\ge2\), \((n-1)/n^2\ge1/(2n)\) and
\(
 [1+(n-1)C_{p,q}]/n^2\le(1+C_{p,q})/n
\).  Hence
\[
 \frac{A_{p,q}}{2n}
 \le R_n^{\star}\le F_n
 \le\frac{1+C_{p,q}}{n}.
\]
The first and third terms are positive finite multiples of \(n^{-1}\), so this one display proves both
\[
 R_n^{\star}=\Theta(n^{-1})
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 F_n=\Theta(n^{-1}).
\]
No universal comparison between \(F_n\) and \(R_n^{\star}\) is used here.

By \texttt{lem:hub-loomba-eckles-envelope}, if
\(
 c=(q-p)^2/[p(1-p)]
\), then
\[
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)}{n}
 =1+nc=\Theta(n).
\]
In particular, the published spectral-overlap envelope is \(\Omega(1)\) and does not converge to zero, whereas the exact linear frontier and unrestricted minimax risk both converge to zero at rate \(n^{-1}\).  This proves the claimed strict rate separation on the genuinely overlapping hub family.
