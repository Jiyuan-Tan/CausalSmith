For every admissible fixed instance, write \(\operatorname{vert}(K_i)\) for the vertex set of the local polytope \(K_i\).  The exact linear frontier is the finite convex epigraph program
\[
\begin{aligned}
 F={}&\min_{\substack{t\in\mathbb R\\ a\in(\mathbb R^{V_n})^{\Omega_n}}}t\\
 \text{subject to }{}&
 \sum_{z\in\Omega_n}p_\pi(z)
 \left[
 \sum_{i\in V_n}(a_z)_i v_i(z_{\Gamma_i})
 -\frac1n\sum_{i\in V_n}\sum_{x\in X_i}p_{\pi',i}(x)v_i(x)
 \right]^2\le t
 \quad\text{for every }v\in\prod_{i\in V_n}\operatorname{vert}(K_i).
\end{aligned}                                                                     \tag{P}
\]
Its explicit numeric inputs are the tables \(p_\pi(z)\), \(p_{\pi',i}(x)\), the restrictions \(z_{\Gamma_i}\), and the vertex-evaluation tables \(\{(v_i(x))_{x\in X_i}:v_i\in\operatorname{vert}(K_i)\}\), all computed from the known \(\pi,\pi',\Gamma_i,\mathcal S_i\).  Program \((P)\) has exactly \(n2^n+1\) real decision variables and \(\prod_{i\in V_n}\lvert\operatorname{vert}(K_i)\rvert\) listed convex quadratic constraints.  An optimum exists, its weight component may be taken to be \(a^{\star}\), and there is a matching moment certificate \(M\in\mathfrak M(K)\) such that
\[
 F=\mathcal D=
 \sum_{z\in\Omega_n}p_\pi(z)\psi_z(M).                                         \tag{D}
\]
When \(F>0\), \(M\) may be chosen to be the minimum-rank optimizer \(M^{\star}\) from \(\mathrm{def:adaptive-rank-fiber-complexity}\).
