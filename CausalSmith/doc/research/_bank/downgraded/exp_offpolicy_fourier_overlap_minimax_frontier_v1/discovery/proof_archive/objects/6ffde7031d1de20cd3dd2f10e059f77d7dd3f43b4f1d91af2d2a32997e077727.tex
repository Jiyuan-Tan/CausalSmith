Determine whether there exists one numerical constant \(c_0>0\), independent of the population size, graph, source and target policies, Fourier supports, selector system, and triangular array, such that every admissible fixed instance satisfies \(c_0F\le R^{\star}\). Equivalently, determine whether one \(c_0\) makes \(\mathsf C(c_0,\mathfrak A)\) true for every \(\mathfrak A\in\mathsf{Adm}\). One sufficient, but not logically necessary, route through \(\mathfrak H_{\mathrm{prior}}\) is to construct for every instance a symmetric genuine law \(\nu\in\mathcal P(K)\) satisfying \(\mathcal B(\nu)\ge c_0\mathcal D\). A negative resolution instead requires an explicit admissible growing family for which \(R^{\star}/F\to0\).
