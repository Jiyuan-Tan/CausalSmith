The established selector duality and certificate proposition give, for every genuine prior \(\nu\) and every feasible linear rule \(a\),
\[
 \operatorname{LB}(\nu)\le R^{\star}\le F=\mathcal D\le\operatorname{UB}(a),
\]
and the clipped minimizing incidence estimator has worst-case risk at most \(F\).  A dimension-free inequality \(\mathcal B(\nu)\ge c_0\mathcal D\) for some genuine prior would complete the lower comparison, but it has not been derived.
