For product-Bernoulli source and target policies, the retained-support spectral transport weight is
\[
 w_{mathcal S_i}^{\pi,\pi'}(z)
 =\sum_{U\in\mathcal S_i}\Delta_U^{\pi,\pi'}
   \chi_{i,U}^{\pi}(z_{\Gamma_i}),
\]
and it transports the target expectation of every function in \(H_i\).  If square-integrable unit summands depend respectively only on the assignment coordinates in \(\Gamma_i\), if
\[
 D=\max_{i\in V_n}
 \left|\left\{k\in V_n\setminus\{i\}:\Gamma_i\cap\Gamma_k\ne\varnothing\right\}\right|,
\]
and if their second moments are all at most \(B\), then the variance of their average is at most \(B(D+1)/n\).
