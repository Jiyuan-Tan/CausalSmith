Fix \(n\ge 2\) and \(p,q\in[\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\).  Define
\[
 A_{p,q}=(1-p)q^2+p(1-q)^2,
 \qquad
 C_{p,q}=\frac{(1-q)^2}{1-p}+\frac{q^2}{p}.
\]
The overlap assumptions imply \(p,1-p,q,1-q\ge\varepsilon>0\).  Hence every denominator in \(C_{p,q}\) is positive, and
\[
 A_{p,q}
 \ge \varepsilon\{q^2+(1-q)^2\}
 \ge \frac{\varepsilon}{2}>0,
 \qquad
 C_{p,q}
 \le \frac{q^2+(1-q)^2}{\varepsilon}
 \le \frac1\varepsilon.
\]
Here the middle lower bound uses
\(q^2+(1-q)^2=2(q-1/2)^2+1/2\), and the last upper bound uses
\(q^2+(1-q)^2=1-2q(1-q)\le1\).

By \(\texttt{lem:hub-frontier-rate-sandwich}\),
\[
 \frac{n-1}{n^2}A_{p,q}
 \le R_n^{\star}\le F_n
 \le \frac{1+(n-1)C_{p,q}}{n^2}.
\]
Since \((n-1)/n^2\ge1/(2n)\), \(1\le1/\varepsilon\), and \(n\ge2\), the preceding display gives the uniform bounds
\[
 \frac{\varepsilon}{4n}
 \le R_n^{\star}\le F_n
 \le \frac{1+(n-1)/\varepsilon}{n^2}
 \le \frac1{\varepsilon n}.
\]
Thus both \(R_n^{\star}\) and \(F_n\) are bounded above and below by positive finite multiples of \(n^{-1}\); in particular,
\[
 R_n^{\star}=\Theta(n^{-1}),
 \qquad
 F_n=\Theta(n^{-1}).
\]
This argument includes \(p=q\); no division by \(q-p\) has been used.

Now put
\[
 c_{p,q}=\frac{(q-p)^2}{p(1-p)}.
\]
The denominator is positive by \(\texttt{ass:source-overlap}\).  The exact calculation in
\(\texttt{lem:hub-loomba-eckles-envelope}\) yields
\[
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)}{n}
 =1+n c_{p,q}.
\]
If \(p=q\), then \(c_{p,q}=0\), so the envelope equals one.  For either
\(X_n\in\{R_n^{\star},F_n\}\), the uniform frontier bounds imply
\[
 \varepsilon n
 \le \frac1{X_n}
 \le \frac{4n}{\varepsilon}.
\]
Multiplying by the envelope, which is one in this case, proves
\[
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)/n}{R_n^{\star}}
 =\Theta(n),
 \qquad
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)/n}{F_n}
 =\Theta(n).
\]

If \(p\ne q\) is fixed, then \(c_{p,q}>0\).  For every \(n\ge1\),
\[
 n c_{p,q}\le 1+n c_{p,q}\le n(1+c_{p,q}).
\]
Combining this with the reciprocal bounds gives, for either permitted \(X_n\),
\[
 \varepsilon c_{p,q}n^2
 \le
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)/n}{X_n}
 \le
 \frac{4(1+c_{p,q})}{\varepsilon}n^2.
\]
Consequently both ratios are \(\Theta(n^2)\) for fixed \(p\ne q\).

As the smallest-row boundary check, at \(n=2\) the four assignments all have positive source mass by \(\texttt{ass:source-overlap}\), and the two established lemmas specialize to
\[
 \frac{A_{p,q}}4\le R_2^{\star}\le F_2\le\frac{1+C_{p,q}}4,
 \qquad
 \frac{B_2^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_2^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)}2=1+2c_{p,q}.
\]
Thus the smallest admissible hub row has neither a zero-risk nor a division boundary that could invalidate the comparison.  Finally, by \(\texttt{def:hub-family}\), the hub neighborhood \(\Gamma_1=V_n\) intersects every peripheral neighborhood \(\Gamma_i=\{i\}\), so the family is genuinely overlapping for every \(n\ge2\).  The displayed bounds and exact identity prove all assertions.
