Fix \(n\ge2\) and abbreviate \(p=p_n\), \(q=q_n\), and \(d_n=q_n-p_n\).  Because both policy sequences lie in \([\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\),
\[
 p,1-p,q,1-q\ge\varepsilon.
\]
For the two quantities in \(\texttt{lem:hub-frontier-rate-sandwich}\), the same elementary estimates as in the fixed-policy theorem give
\[
 (1-p)q^2+p(1-q)^2
 \ge\varepsilon\{q^2+(1-q)^2\}
 \ge\frac\varepsilon2
\]
and
\[
 \frac{(1-q)^2}{1-p}+\frac{q^2}{p}
 \le\frac{(1-q)^2+q^2}{\varepsilon}
 \le\frac1\varepsilon.
\]
Substitution into that lemma, followed by \((n-1)/n^2\ge1/(2n)\), gives
\[
 \frac{\varepsilon}{4n}
 \le R_n^{\star}\le F_n\le\frac1{\varepsilon n}.
\]
These constants are independent of the particular sequences, proving the two uniform \(\Theta_{\varepsilon}(n^{-1})\) assertions.

Next, \(\texttt{lem:hub-loomba-eckles-envelope}\), applied row by row, gives the exact equality
\[
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)}n
 =1+\frac{n d_n^2}{p_n(1-p_n)}.
\]
Source overlap implies
\[
 \varepsilon(1-\varepsilon)
 \le p_n(1-p_n)\le\frac14.
\]
The upper bound follows from
\(p_n(1-p_n)=1/4-(p_n-1/2)^2\le1/4\); the lower bound follows because the concave function \(t(1-t)\) takes its minimum on \([\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\) at an endpoint.  Therefore
\[
 1+4n d_n^2
 \le
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)}n
 \le
 1+\frac{n d_n^2}{\varepsilon(1-\varepsilon)}.
\]
For either \(X_n\in\{R_n^{\star},F_n\}\), inversion of the positive frontier bounds is legitimate and yields
\[
 \varepsilon n\le X_n^{-1}\le\frac{4n}{\varepsilon}.
\]
Multiplying the last two displays proves
\[
 \varepsilon n(1+4n d_n^2)
 \le
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)/n}{X_n}
 \le
 \frac{4n}{\varepsilon}
 \left(1+\frac{n d_n^2}{\varepsilon(1-\varepsilon)}\right).
\]
The lower and upper expressions are constant multiples, depending only on \(\varepsilon\), of \(n+n^2d_n^2\).  This proves the claimed uniform order.

Finally, if \(|d_n|\le n^{-1/2}\), then
\[
 n\le n+n^2d_n^2\le2n,
\]
so each ratio is \(\Theta_{\varepsilon}(n)\).  If \(|d_n|\ge n^{-1/2}\), then
\[
 n^2d_n^2\le n+n^2d_n^2\le2n^2d_n^2,
\]
so each ratio is \(\Theta_{\varepsilon}(n^2d_n^2)\).  The two regimes meet at \(|q_n-p_n|=n^{-1/2}\), establishing the stated elbow without any constants depending on the policy sequences.
