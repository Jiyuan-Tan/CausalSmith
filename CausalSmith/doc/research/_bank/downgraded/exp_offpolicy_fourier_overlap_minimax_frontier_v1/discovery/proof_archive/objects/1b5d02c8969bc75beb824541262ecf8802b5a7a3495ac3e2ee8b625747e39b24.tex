The selector reduction gives an exact finite convex primal and the exact moment value
\[
 F=\mathcal D.
\]
Thus exhaustive enumeration of assignments and vertices is an exact finite algorithm, while every feasible weight rule gives \(F\le\operatorname{UB}(a)\) and every genuine prior gives \(\operatorname{LB}(\nu)\le R^{\star}\le F\).  These are certified general-graph bounds.  They do not imply a running time \(\operatorname{poly}(L_{\mathrm{in}})2^{O(w)}\): the squared global target residual creates cross-bag terms and an unrestricted coefficient \(a_z\) may depend on all coordinates of \(z\).
