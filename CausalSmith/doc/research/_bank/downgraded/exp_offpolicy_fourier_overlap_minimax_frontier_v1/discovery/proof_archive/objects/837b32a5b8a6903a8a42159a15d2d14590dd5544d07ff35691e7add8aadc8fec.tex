For every \(n\ge2\) on \(\mathcal H_n^{\mathrm{hub}}\),
\[
 \frac{n-1}{n^2}\bigl((1-p)q^2+p(1-q)^2\bigr)
 \le R_n^{\star}\le F_n
 \le \frac{1+(n-1)\bigl((1-q)^2/(1-p)+q^2/p\bigr)}{n^2}.
\]
In particular, for fixed \(p,q\in[\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\), both \(F_n\) and \(R_n^{\star}\) are \(\Theta(n^{-1})\).
