First, \(R^{\star}\le F\).  Indeed, for every weight rule \(a\), the map \((z,y)\mapsto a_z^{\mathsf T}y\) is Borel, so it is feasible in \texttt{def:unrestricted-frontier}.  Taking its worst-case risk and then the infimum over \(a\) gives
\[
 R^{\star}\le
 \inf_a\sup_{v\in K}\sum_zp_{\pi}(z)
 (a_z^{\mathsf T}N_zv-\theta(v))^2=F.
\]
Also, for a fixed feasible \(a\), \texttt{def:linear-frontier} and \texttt{def:primal-certificate} give directly
\[
 F\le \sup_{v\in K}\sum_zp_{\pi}(z)
 (a_z^{\mathsf T}N_zv-\theta(v))^2=\operatorname{UB}(a).
\]

Now fix a genuine prior \(\nu\in\mathcal P(K)\), let \(\mathbf V\sim\nu\), and fix an arbitrary Borel estimator \(\delta\).  The target is bounded: by \texttt{ass:bounded-schedules}, \texttt{ass:target-product-policy}, \texttt{def:local-target-mass}, and \texttt{def:target-value},
\[
 |\theta(v)|
 \le \frac1n\sum_{i\in V_n}\sum_{x\in X_i}
 p_{\pi',i}(x)|v_i(x)|
 \le \frac1n\sum_{i\in V_n}1=1.
\]
Thus all conditional second moments below exist.  Conditional on \(N_z\mathbf V\), the elementary squared-error decomposition is
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\mathbb E_{\nu}\!\left[
   (\delta(z,N_z\mathbf V)-\theta(\mathbf V))^2
   \mid N_z\mathbf V\right]\\
 &\quad=\operatorname{Var}_{\nu}(\theta(\mathbf V)\mid N_z\mathbf V)
 +\left(\delta(z,N_z\mathbf V)
 -\mathbb E_{\nu}[\theta(\mathbf V)\mid N_z\mathbf V]\right)^2\\
 &\quad\ge \operatorname{Var}_{\nu}(\theta(\mathbf V)\mid N_z\mathbf V).
\end{aligned}
\]
For each \(\delta\), its supremum over \(K\) is at least its average under \(\nu\).  Taking expectations in the last display and then averaging over the source assignment therefore gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in K}\sum_zp_{\pi}(z)
 (\delta(z,N_zv)-\theta(v))^2
 \ge \sum_zp_{\pi}(z)\mathbb E_{\nu}
 [\operatorname{Var}_{\nu}(\theta(\mathbf V)\mid N_z\mathbf V)]
 =\operatorname{LB}(\nu).
\]
Taking the infimum over all Borel \(\delta\) proves \(\operatorname{LB}(\nu)\le R^{\star}\).

By \texttt{lem:selector-reduction-and-dual}, a minimizing rule \(a^{\star}\) exists.  For any \(s\in[-1,1]\) and \(t\in\mathbb R\), projection onto the interval satisfies
\[
 |\operatorname{clip}(t)-s|\le |t-s|.
\]
The preceding bound on the target gives \(\theta(v)\in[-1,1]\).  Applying the projection inequality pointwise with \(t=(a_z^{\star})^{\mathsf T}N_zv\), summing over \(z\), and then taking the supremum over \(v\in K\) yields
\[
 \sup_{v\in K}\sum_zp_{\pi}(z)
 (\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{inc}}(z,N_zv)-\theta(v))^2
 \le F.
\]
Combining the displayed inequalities proves every assertion.
