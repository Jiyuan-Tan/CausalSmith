As a rowwise corollary of the instance-adaptive risk certificate, let \(\mathfrak A\in\mathsf{Adm}\) and let \(r_n,\rho_{n,z},S_{n,\mathrm{fib}},m_{n,\mathrm{fib}},\kappa_{n,\mathrm{fib}}\), and \(\kappa_n\) be the quantities in \(\mathrm{def:adaptive-rank-fiber-complexity}\).  Then: \(\mathrm{(a)}\) \(F_n\to0\) implies \(R_n^{\star}\to0\); \(\mathrm{(b)}\) in every fixed row, \(R_n^{\star}=0\) if and only if \(F_n=0\); \(\mathrm{(c)}\) \(\kappa_nR_n^{\star}\to0\) implies \(F_n\to0\); and \(\mathrm{(d)}\) on every subfamily satisfying \(\sup_n\kappa_n<\infty\), one has \(R_n^{\star}\to0\) if and only if \(F_n\to0\).  Clause \(\mathrm{(d)}\) is conditional on that subfamily property and does not add a condition to \(\mathsf{Adm}\).
