The proved instance-adaptive theorem gives, for every admissible instance,
\[
 \max\left\{\frac{F}{r_{\star}},m_{\mathrm{fib}}\right\}
 \le R^{\star}\le F=\mathcal D\le S_{\mathrm{fib}},
 \qquad
 \frac{F}{\min\{r_{\star},\kappa_{\mathrm{fib}}\}}\le R^{\star}.
\]
The rank construction is a genuine symmetric ellipsoid prior with exact ambiguity \(F/r_{\star}\), and each fiber construction is a genuine symmetric two-point prior with ambiguity at least \(p_{\pi}(z)\rho_z^2\). Thus the remaining gap is only whether these instance-dependent factors can be replaced by one positive numerical constant, or whether a growing admissible counterexample makes \(R^{\star}/F\to0\).
