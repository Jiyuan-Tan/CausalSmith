Fix \(n\ge 2\) and \(p,q\in[\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\).  Since \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/2)\), \texttt{ass:source-overlap} gives \(0<p<1\), and hence
\[
 \Delta=\frac{q-p}{\sqrt{p(1-p)}}
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 c=\Delta^2=\frac{(q-p)^2}{p(1-p)}
\]
are well defined, with \(c\ge0\).  No strict inequality between \(p\) and \(q\) is used.

Let \(Z\) have the source law in \texttt{ass:source-product-design}, and abbreviate
\[
 \chi_j=\frac{Z_j-p}{\sqrt{p(1-p)}}.
\]
The two Bernoulli values give
\[
 \mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_j]
 =\frac{p(1-p)+(1-p)(-p)}{\sqrt{p(1-p)}}=0
\]
and
\[
 \mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_j^2]
 =\frac{p(1-p)^2+(1-p)p^2}{p(1-p)}=1.
\]
For \(j\ne k\), product independence from \texttt{ass:source-product-design} and the first identity imply
\[
 \mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_j\chi_k]
 =\mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_j]\mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_k]=0.
\]
These are precisely the singleton characters of \texttt{def:local-fourier-character} on the homogeneous source policy.

By \texttt{def:hub-family},
\[
 \mathcal S_1=\{\varnothing\}\cup\{\{j\}:j\in V_n\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal S_i=\{\varnothing,\{i\}\}\quad(2\le i\le n).
\]
For a singleton, \texttt{def:loomba-eckles-spectral-envelope} gives
\(
 \Delta_{\{j\}}^{\pi,\pi'}=(q-p)/\sqrt{p(1-p)}=\Delta
\), while the empty-set loading is one.  Therefore the transport formula, equivalently \texttt{lem:loomba-eckles-spectral-overlap-envelope}, yields
\[
 W_1^{\mathrm{LE}}
 =1+\Delta\sum_{j=1}^n\chi_{1,\{j\}}^{\pi},
 \qquad
 W_i^{\mathrm{LE}}
 =1+\Delta\chi_{i,\{i\}}^{\pi}\quad(2\le i\le n).
\]
Using the three moment identities above term by term,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_{\pi}\!\left[(W_1^{\mathrm{LE}})^2\right]
 &=1+2\Delta\sum_{j=1}^n\mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_j]
   +\Delta^2\sum_{j=1}^n\mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_j^2]
   +2\Delta^2\sum_{1\le j<k\le n}\mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_j\chi_k]\\
 &=1+n\Delta^2=1+nc,
\end{aligned}
\]
and, for \(2\le i\le n\),
\[
 \mathbb E_{\pi}\!\left[(W_i^{\mathrm{LE}})^2\right]
 =1+2\Delta\mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_i]
   +\Delta^2\mathbb E_{\pi}[\chi_i^2]
 =1+c.
\]

It remains to verify exactness of the bounded-class envelope.  For every \(v_i\in K_i\), \texttt{def:local-evaluation-polytope} gives \(|v_i(x)|\le1\) at every local cell.  Hence, pointwise in \(Z\),
\[
 \bigl(v_i(Z_{\Gamma_i})W_i^{\mathrm{LE}}(Z)\bigr)^2
 \le (W_i^{\mathrm{LE}}(Z))^2,
\]
and consequently
\[
 \sup_{v_i\in K_i}\mathbb E_{\pi}\!\left[
   \bigl(v_i(Z_{\Gamma_i})W_i^{\mathrm{LE}}(Z)\bigr)^2
 \right]
 \le \mathbb E_{\pi}\!\left[(W_i^{\mathrm{LE}})^2\right].
\]
Conversely, the constant schedule \(v_i\equiv1\) is the empty-set character.  Each support displayed above contains \(\varnothing\), so \texttt{def:local-evaluation-polytope} implies \(v_i\equiv1\in K_i\); it attains equality in the preceding display.  Thus
\[
 \sup_{v_i\in K_i}\mathbb E_{\pi}\!\left[
   \bigl(v_i(Z_{\Gamma_i})W_i^{\mathrm{LE}}(Z)\bigr)^2
 \right]
 =\mathbb E_{\pi}\!\left[(W_i^{\mathrm{LE}})^2\right].
\]
Because \(n\ge2\) and \(c\ge0\), \(1+nc\ge1+c\).  Taking the maximum over units in \texttt{def:loomba-eckles-spectral-envelope} therefore proves
\[
 B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}=1+nc.
\]

For the overlap degree, \texttt{def:hub-family} gives \(\Gamma_1=V_n\) and \(\Gamma_i=\{i\}\) for \(i\ge2\).  Hence \(\Gamma_1\cap\Gamma_k=\{k\}\ne\varnothing\) for every \(k\ne1\).  The hub has \(n-1\) intersecting partners, and no unit can have more, so \texttt{def:loomba-eckles-spectral-envelope} gives
\[
 D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}=n-1.
\]
Combining the two exact formulas,
\[
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)}{n}
 =\frac{(1+nc)n}{n}
 =1+n\frac{(q-p)^2}{p(1-p)}.
\]
If \(p=q\), then \(c=0\), so this quantity equals one for every \(n\).  If \(p\ne q\) is fixed, source overlap makes \(c\in(0,\infty)\) independent of \(n\), and the quantity is \(\Theta(n)\).  As the smallest-instance check, \(n=2\) gives \(B_2^{\mathrm{LE}}=1+2c\), \(D_2^{\mathrm{LE}}=1\), and the same envelope value \(1+2c\), including the boundary value one when \(p=q\).
