The exact rank-prior identity, the fiber two-point lower certificate, the norm-extension upper certificate, all boundary cases, and the complexity inequalities are proved in \(\mathrm{lem:rank-fiber-genuine-prior-certificates}\). The equality \(F=\mathcal D\) is also independently supplied by \(\mathrm{lem:selector-reduction-and-dual}\), while \(R^{\star}\le F\) and the clipped-estimator conclusion are supplied by \(\mathrm{prop:primal-dual-certificates}\). Combining these declared conclusions gives every displayed inequality and the zero case exactly as stated.
