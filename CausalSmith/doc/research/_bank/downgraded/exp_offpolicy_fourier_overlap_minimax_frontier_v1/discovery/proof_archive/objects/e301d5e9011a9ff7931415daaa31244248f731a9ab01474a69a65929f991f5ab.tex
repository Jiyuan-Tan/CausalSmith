The proved proposition \(\mathrm{prop:fully-expanded-frontier-program}\) gives an exact finite convex quadratic program with \(n2^n+1\) scalar variables, \(\prod_i|\operatorname{vert}(K_i)|\) vertex constraints, an attaining weight rule, and an attained equal-valued moment certificate.  The proved proposition \(\mathrm{prop:raw-incidence-bag-moment-obstruction}\) supplies a treewidth-one additive star on which ordinary overlap-consistent edge-bag laws fail to determine \(\langle\ell,M\ell\rangle_E\).  Together these show exact finite computability and rule out raw edge-bag moment state, but they neither construct a \(\operatorname{poly}(L_{\mathrm{in}})2^{O(w)}\) compressed algorithm nor preclude a separator state augmented by a compressed global target sum.
