For every admissible array, the proved upper comparison gives \(R_n^{\star}\le F_n\) row by row, and hence
\[
 F_n\longrightarrow0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_n^{\star}\longrightarrow0.
\]
The reverse implication is exactly the missing consequence of a single row-uniform lower comparison \(c_0F_n\le R_n^{\star}\) with \(c_0>0\).
