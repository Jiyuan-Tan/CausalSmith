Fix a row \(n\).  For each \(i\in V_n\), let \(\mathcal V_i=\operatorname{vert}(K_i)\) and \(r_i=|\mathcal V_i|\).  Given the explicit local vertex tables \(\mathcal V_i\), the source masses \(p_{\pi}(z)\), and the computable arrays \(N_ze\) and \(\theta(e)\) for \(z\in\Omega_n\) and \(e\in\prod_{i\in V_n}\mathcal V_i\), the frontier is the exact finite convex quadratic epigraph program
\[
 F=\min_{\substack{a_z\in\mathbb R^{V_n},\ z\in\Omega_n\\ t\in\mathbb R}}t
 \quad\text{subject to}\quad
 \sum_{z\in\Omega_n}p_{\pi}(z)
 \bigl(a_z^{\mathsf T}N_ze-\theta(e)\bigr)^2\le t
 \quad\text{for every }e\in\prod_{i\in V_n}\mathcal V_i.
\]
Before structural compression this program has exactly \(n2^n+1\) scalar decision variables and \(\prod_{i\in V_n}|\operatorname{vert}(K_i)|\) convex quadratic constraints.  Its minimum is attained, and its weight component may be chosen to be \(a^{\star}\).  Moreover, there exists \(\widetilde M^{\star}\in\mathfrak M(K)\), which may be chosen as the defined minimum-rank optimizer \(M^{\star}\) when \(F>0\), such that
\[
 \sum_{z\in\Omega_n}p_{\pi}(z)\psi_z(\widetilde M^{\star})
 =\mathcal D=F,
\]
so \(\widetilde M^{\star}\) is a matching moment certificate for the fully expanded primal program.
