Let \((p_n)_{n\ge2}\) and \((q_n)_{n\ge2}\) be arbitrary sequences in \([\varepsilon,1-\varepsilon]\), and instantiate the row-\(n\) member of \(\mathcal H_n^{\mathrm{hub}}\) at source probability \(p_n\) and target probability \(q_n\).  Then, uniformly over \(n\ge2\),
\[
 \frac{\varepsilon}{4n}
 \le R_n^{\star}\le F_n\le\frac1{\varepsilon n}.
\]
Moreover, for either \(X_n\in\{R_n^{\star},F_n\}\),
\[
 \varepsilon n\bigl(1+4n(q_n-p_n)^2\bigr)
 \le
 \frac{B_n^{\mathrm{LE}}(D_n^{\mathrm{LE}}+1)/n}{X_n}
 \le
 \frac{4n}{\varepsilon}
 \left(1+\frac{n(q_n-p_n)^2}{\varepsilon(1-\varepsilon)}\right).
\]
Consequently \(R_n^{\star}=\Theta_{\varepsilon}(n^{-1})\), \(F_n=\Theta_{\varepsilon}(n^{-1})\), and both displayed envelope-to-frontier ratios are
\[
 \Theta_{\varepsilon}\!\left(n+n^2(q_n-p_n)^2\right).
\]
The local-policy elbow is \(|q_n-p_n|\asymp n^{-1/2}\): if \(|q_n-p_n|\le n^{-1/2}\), each ratio is \(\Theta_{\varepsilon}(n)\), whereas if \(|q_n-p_n|\ge n^{-1/2}\), each is \(\Theta_{\varepsilon}(n^2(q_n-p_n)^2)\).  All constants hidden by \(\Theta_{\varepsilon}\) depend only on \(\varepsilon\).
