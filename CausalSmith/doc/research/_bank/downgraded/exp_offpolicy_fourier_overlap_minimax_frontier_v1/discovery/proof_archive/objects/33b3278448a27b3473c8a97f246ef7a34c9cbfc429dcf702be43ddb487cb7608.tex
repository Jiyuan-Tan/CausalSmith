For every admissible array, \(R_n^{\star}\le F_n\) for every \(n\).  Hence
\[
 F_n\longrightarrow0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R_n^{\star}\longrightarrow0.
\]
The reverse implication would follow from a row-uniform inequality \(c_0F_n\le R_n^{\star}\) with \(c_0>0\), but that is exactly the unresolved universal prior-rounding step.
