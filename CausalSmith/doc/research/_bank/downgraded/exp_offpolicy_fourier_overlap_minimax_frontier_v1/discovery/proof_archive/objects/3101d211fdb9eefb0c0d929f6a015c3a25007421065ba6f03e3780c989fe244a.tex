For every fixed admissible instance, the established primal--dual certificate proposition gives, for every genuine prior \(\nu\in\mathcal P(K)\) and every feasible weight rule \(a\),
\[
 \mathcal B(\nu)=\operatorname{LB}(\nu)
 \le R^{\star}\le F=\mathcal D\le \operatorname{UB}(a).
\]
It also gives
\[
 \sup_{v\in K}\sum_{z\in\Omega_n}p_{\pi}(z)
 \bigl(\widehat\theta_{\mathrm{inc}}(z,N_zv)-\theta(v)\bigr)^2\le F.
\]
Consequently, if a single numerical \(c_0>0\), chosen independently of the instance, and a genuine prior \(\nu\in\mathcal P(K)\) satisfied \(\mathcal B(\nu)\ge c_0\mathcal D\), then
\[
 c_0F=c_0\mathcal D\le\mathcal B(\nu)\le R^{\star}\le F.
\]
Applying this same constant separately to every row of every \(\mathfrak A\in\mathsf{Adm}\) would give \(\mathsf C(c_0,\mathfrak A)\). Thus all parts of the target other than the uniform genuine-prior lower bound are established, and that lower bound is sufficient for the residual claim.
