For every admissible fixed instance, if \(F>0\) there is a symmetric genuine probability law on \(K\) whose posterior-ambiguity certificate is exactly \(F/r_{\star}\). For every \(z\in\Omega_n\), there is a symmetric genuine two-point law \(\nu_z\in\mathcal P(K)\) satisfying \(\mathcal B(\nu_z)\ge p_{\pi}(z)\rho_z^2\), and there is a coefficient vector \(a_z\in\mathbb R^{V_n}\) satisfying
\[
 \sup_{v\in K}\left\lvert a_z^{\mathsf T}N_zv-\theta(v)\right\rvert=\rho_z.
\]
Consequently,
\[
 \max\left\{\frac{F}{r_{\star}},m_{\mathrm{fib}}\right\}
 \le R^{\star}\le F=\mathcal D\le S_{\mathrm{fib}},
 \qquad
 \frac{F}{\kappa}\le R^{\star}\le F,
\]
and
\[
 1\le\kappa_{\mathrm{fib}}
 \le\left\lvert\{z\in\Omega_n:\rho_z>0\}\right\rvert
 \le 2^n
\]
whenever \(m_{\mathrm{fib}}>0\), with the stated convention when \(m_{\mathrm{fib}}=0\). In the latter case \(R^{\star}=F=\mathcal D=S_{\mathrm{fib}}=0\). The clipped incidence estimator has worst-case risk at most \(F\).
