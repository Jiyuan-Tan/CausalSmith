For every admissible fixed finite-population instance, the unrestricted and linear minimax risks obey the instance-adaptive certificate
\[
 \max\left\{\frac{F}{r_{\star}},m_{\mathrm{fib}}\right\}
 \le R^{\star}\le F=\mathcal D\le S_{\mathrm{fib}},
 \qquad
 \frac{F}{\kappa}\le R^{\star}\le F.
\]
The factors \(r_{\star}\) and \(\kappa_{\mathrm{fib}}\) are respectively the minimum rank of a dual optimizer and the effective number of assignment fibers carrying nonzero ambiguity, with \(1\le\kappa_{\mathrm{fib}}\le|\{z:\rho_z>0\}|\le2^n\) when \(m_{\mathrm{fib}}>0\). If \(m_{\mathrm{fib}}=0\), all four values \(R^{\star},F,\mathcal D,S_{\mathrm{fib}}\) are zero. The clipped incidence estimator has worst-case risk at most \(F\). This is an instance-adaptive comparison and does not assert a universal numerical comparison constant.
