For the fixed ambient \((\pi,\pi_{\mathrm{base}},\tau)\),
\[
 \Theta_i^{\mathrm{CI}}(K,\tau)
 =\{\Phi_i(\mathcal M\mid\tau):\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{CI}}(K)\},
\]
where the selected transition posterior kernel is
\[
 q_{t,x_t}^{\mathrm{CI}}=\delta_{y_t},
 \qquad
 q_{t,x}^{\mathrm{CI}}=K_t(\cdot\mid x)quad(x\ne x_t).
\]
The notation concerns only the transition-response component of the present externally randomized-policy, deterministic-reward class.
