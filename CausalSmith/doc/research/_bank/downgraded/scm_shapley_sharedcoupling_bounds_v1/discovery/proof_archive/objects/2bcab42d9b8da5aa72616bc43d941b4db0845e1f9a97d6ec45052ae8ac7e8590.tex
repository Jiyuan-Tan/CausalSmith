Fix \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{CI}}(K)\).  At a time \(t\), \(\text{ass:within-time-row-independence}\) makes every \(R_t(x)\), \(x\ne x_t\), independent of the factual coordinate \(R_t(x_t)\).  Since \(p_t>0\), conditioning on \(R_t(x_t)=y_t\) therefore gives
\[
 \Pr\{R_t(x)=y\mid R_t(x_t)=y_t\}
 =\Pr\{R_t(x)=y\}=K_t(y\mid x)
\]
for \(x\ne x_t\), using \(\text{ass:transition-marginals}\) through \(\text{def:counterfactual-independent-subclass}\).  Consistency on the conditioned coordinate gives \(q_{t,x_t}=\delta_{y_t}\).  Independence across times, inherited from \(\mathfrak M(K)\), makes the full-trajectory posterior kernel uniquely equal to \(q^{\mathrm{CI}}\) in \(\text{def:counterfactual-independent-target}\).

This kernel is feasible in \(\mathcal B(K,\tau)\): for \(x\ne x_t\),
\[
 p_tq_{t,x}^{\mathrm{CI}}(y)=p_tK_t(y\mid x)\le K_t(y\mid x),
\]
and its factual row is the required point mass.  Every member of the counterfactually independent subclass therefore has the same Markov recursion and the same credit \(\Phi_i(q^{\mathrm{CI}})\), proving
\[
 \Theta_i^{\mathrm{CI}}(K,\tau)=\{\Phi_i(q^{\mathrm{CI}})\}.
\]
Because \(q^{\mathrm{CI}}\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)\), \(\text{thm:sharp-shared-interval}\) gives \(\Phi_i(q^{\mathrm{CI}})\in\Theta_i(K,\tau)\), which proves the asserted subset relation.
