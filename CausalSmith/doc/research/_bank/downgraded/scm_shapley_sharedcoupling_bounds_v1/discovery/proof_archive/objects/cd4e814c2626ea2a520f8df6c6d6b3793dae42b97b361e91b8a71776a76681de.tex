Fix \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M(K)\).  For \(t\in[H]\), define
\[
 E_t=\{R_t(x_t)=y_t\},\qquad E=\bigcap_{t=1}^H E_t,
 \qquad B=\bigcap_{t=1}^H\{A_t=a_t\}.
\]
By \(\texttt{ass:fixed-initial-state}\), \(S_1=s_1\) almost surely.  On \(B\), the transition equation in \(\texttt{ass:markov-structural-equations}\) shows inductively that \(E_t\) is equivalent to \(S_{t+1}=y_t=s_{t+1}\), once the displayed state has been reached.  Therefore the factual trajectory event is exactly
\[
 \{\tau\}=B\cap E. \tag{1}
\]
Because \(R_t\) is a deterministic function of \(U_t\), \(\texttt{ass:independent-time-disturbances}\) makes \(R_1,\ldots,R_H\) mutually independent.  Let \(\lambda_t\) be the law of \(R_t\).  Conditional on a response-table tuple \((r_1,\ldots,r_H)\) satisfying \(E\), the displayed state path is fixed.  The equations \(A_t=G_t(S_t,V_t)\), the independence of \(V_1,ldots,V_H\) from all response tables, and the policy marginal identity in \(\texttt{ass:factual-policy-exogeneity}\) give
\[
 \Pr(B\mid S_1=s_1,R_1=r_1,ldots,R_H=r_H,E)
 =c_\tau,
 \qquad
 c_\tau:=\prod_{t=1}^H\pi_t(a_t\mid s_t). \tag{2}
\]
Every factor in \(c_\tau\) is positive by \(\texttt{ass:factual-action-positivity}\).  Thus the action likelihood in (2) is positive and, crucially, constant in the table tuple.  For every set \(D\) of table tuples, (1)--(2) imply
\[
 \Pr\{(R_1,\ldots,R_H)\in D,\tau\}
 =c_\tau\Pr\{(R_1,\ldots,R_H)\in D,E\}. \tag{3}
\]
By \(\texttt{ass:transition-marginals}\),
\[
 \Pr(E_t)=K_t(y_t\mid x_t)=p_t>0, \tag{4}
\]
where positivity is \(\texttt{ass:factual-positivity}\).  Independence of the time blocks gives \(\Pr(E)=\prod_t p_t\).  Dividing (3) by \(\Pr(\tau)=c_\tau\prod_t p_t>0\) yields the exact cancellation and posterior factorization
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{Law}(R_1,\ldots,R_H\mid\tau)
 &=\operatorname{Law}(R_1,\ldots,R_H\mid E)\\
 &=\bigotimes_{t=1}^H\operatorname{Law}(R_t\mid E_t).
 \end{aligned} \tag{5}
\]
This proves explicitly that a known policy kernel alone is not being used to infer exogeneity; equation (5) uses the independent coin construction in \(\texttt{ass:factual-policy-exogeneity}\).

Define
\[
 q_{t,x}(y)=\Pr\{R_t(x)=y\mid E_t\}.
\]
Then \(q_{t,x_t}=\delta_{y_t}\).  For \(x\ne x_t\), the multiplication rule, event inclusion, and \(\texttt{ass:transition-marginals}\) give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 p_tq_{t,x}(y)
 &=\Pr\{R_t(x_t)=y_t,R_t(x)=y\}\\
 &\le \Pr\{R_t(x)=y\}=K_t(y\mid x).
 \end{aligned} \tag{6}
\]
Each row is a probability mass function because (4) permits conditioning.  Equations (6) and the factual point mass are exactly \(\texttt{def:posterior-row-fiber}\), so \(q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)\).  By (5), a coalition path using the fresh action coins specified in \(\texttt{ass:factual-policy-exogeneity}\) queries at time \(t\) the coordinate \(R_t(S_t,A_t)\) with conditional marginal \(q_{t,S_t,A_t}\); those action coins are independent of the table tuple.  Hence every coalition return is evaluated by the same posterior Markov kernel \(q\).

Conversely, fix \(q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)\).  At time \(t\), draw \(Z_t\) with law \(K_t(\cdot\mid x_t)\) and set \(R_t(x_t)=Z_t\).  If \(p_t<1\), define for every \(x\ne x_t\)
\[
 h_{t,x}(y)=\frac{K_t(y\mid x)-p_tq_{t,x}(y)}{1-p_t}. \tag{7}
\]
The numerator is nonnegative by the fiber constraint, and
\[
 \sum_y h_{t,x}(y)=\frac{1-p_t}{1-p_t}=1, \tag{8}
\]
so \(h_{t,x}\in\Delta(\mathcal S)\).  Conditional on \(Z_t=y_t\), draw all nonfactual coordinates independently with respective laws \(q_{t,x}\); conditional on \(Z_t\ne y_t\), draw them independently with respective laws \(h_{t,x}\).  This defines one joint table law \(\lambda_t\), not a law indexed by coalitions, and
\[
 \Pr\{R_t(x)=y\}=p_tq_{t,x}(y)+(1-p_t)h_{t,x}(y)=K_t(y\mid x). \tag{9}
\]
The factual coordinate has its required marginal by construction.  If \(p_t=1\), the inequalities \(q_{t,x}(y)\le K_t(y\mid x)\) and equality of the two total masses force \(q_{t,x}=K_t(\cdot\mid x)\); use that row in the sole branch and omit (7).

Draw the constructed tables independently over \(t\), take \(U_t=R_t\), and set \(F_t(x,U_t)=U_t(x)\).  This gives the transition equation, independent transition disturbances, and marginals required by the three transition assumptions.  Set \(S_1=s_1\).  On a further product space, choose independent factual coins \(V_t\), independent of all tables, and finite inverse-transform maps \(G_t\) realizing \(\pi_t\); for every coalition evaluation use fresh coins \(V_t^C\), independent of the tables, to realize \(\alpha_t^C\).  This completes the model under \(\texttt{ass:fixed-initial-state}\) and \(\texttt{ass:factual-policy-exogeneity}\), and factual-action and transition positivity give \(\Pr(\tau)=c_\tau\prod_t p_t>0\).  Thus the model belongs to the repaired class in \(\texttt{def:compatible-scm-class}\), equation (5) applies, and its posterior row kernel is precisely the prescribed \(q\).  The single table law \(\lambda_t\) is shared by all nonfactual rows and all coalition worlds, completing both directions.
