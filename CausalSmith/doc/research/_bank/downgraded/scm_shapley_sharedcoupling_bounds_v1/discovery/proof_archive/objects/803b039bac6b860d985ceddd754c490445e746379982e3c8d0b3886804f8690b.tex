For every \(i\in[H]\), \(\Theta_i(K,\tau)=[L_i(K,\tau),U_i(K,\tau)]\). Both endpoints are attained by compatible SCMs in \(\mathfrak M(K)\). Each endpoint has a witness whose posterior row \(q_{t,x}\) is a vertex of its capped-simplex factor for every \(t\in[H]\) and \(x\in\mathcal X\), and the witness is returned as a compact conditional response-table sampler rather than an exponentially long mass table.
