After substituting the unique feasible row into every singleton posterior-row factor, define
\[
 J=\{t\in[H]:p_t<1\}
\]
and
\[
 m=\#\left\{(t,x)\in[H]\times\mathcal X:
 x\ne x_t\ \text{and}\ 
 \left\{v\in\Delta(\mathcal S):p_tv(y)\le K_t(y\mid x)
 \ \text{for every }y\in\mathcal S\right\}
 \text{ is not a singleton}\right\}.
\]
Thus \(m\) is exactly the number of non-singleton row selectors remaining in either fixed-sign circuit.
