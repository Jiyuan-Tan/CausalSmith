Under the rational-input and \(R>0\) hypotheses of \(\texttt{thm:optimization-preserving-compiler}\), let \(J\) and \(m\) be as in \(\texttt{def:active-ambiguity-count}\).  Then
\[
 m\le |J|\bigl(|\mathcal X|-1\bigr).
\]
After singleton substitution, the order that keeps the global selector \(W\) live, eliminates the Markov state-action path backward in path order, sums \(W\), and then maximizes the remaining row selectors is legal and satisfies
\[
 w_{\mathrm c}(\preceq)\le m+3.
\]
With this order, \(\mathsf{Compile}_i\) returns \(L_i(K,\tau)\) and \(U_i(K,\tau)\) exactly in
\[
 O\!\left(H^2N d^{m+4}\right)
\]
rational arithmetic operations, with the same common-SCM backtracking guarantee.  For fixed state, action, and nonglobal alphabets, this cost is polynomial in the horizon whenever \(|J|=O(\log H)\).
