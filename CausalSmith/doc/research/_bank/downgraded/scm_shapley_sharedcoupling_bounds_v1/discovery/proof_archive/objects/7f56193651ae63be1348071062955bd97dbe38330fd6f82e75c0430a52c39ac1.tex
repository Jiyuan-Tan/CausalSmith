For every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M(K)\), conditioning on \(\tau\) induces a kernel \(q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)\). Conversely, every \(q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)\) is induced by one compatible response-table law \(\lambda_t\) at each time, shared by all nonfactual rows and all coalition worlds; the timewise laws can be sampled independently to form a member of \(\mathfrak M(K)\).
