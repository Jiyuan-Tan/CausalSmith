Fix a stage \(t\notin J\).  By definition, \(p_t=1\).  For any nonfactual row \(x\ne x_t\), \(\texttt{def:posterior-row-fiber}\) gives
\[
 q_{t,x}(y)\le K_t(y\mid x)\quad\text{for every }y\in\mathcal S,
 \qquad
 \sum_yq_{t,x}(y)=\sum_yK_t(y\mid x)=1.
 \tag{1}
\]
Every difference \(K_t(y\mid x)-q_{t,x}(y)\) is nonnegative and their sum is zero, so every difference is zero.  Thus every nonfactual row at \(t\notin J\) is the singleton \(\{K_t(\cdot\mid x)\}\); the factual row is always the singleton \(\{\delta_{y_t}\}\).  At a stage in \(J\), at most the \(|\mathcal X|-1\) nonfactual rows can remain non-singleton.  The definition of \(m\) therefore yields
\[
 m\le\sum_{t\in J}(|\mathcal X|-1)=|J|(|\mathcal X|-1).
 \tag{2}
\]

Substitute every singleton selector by its unique row.  Write \(\mathcal D_t\) for the remaining decisions belonging to stage \(t\), so \(\sum_t|\mathcal D_t|=m\).  Use the explicit circuit constructed in \(\texttt{thm:optimization-preserving-compiler}\).  Its time-\(t\) policy, transition, and reward factors have scope contained in
\[
 \{W,S_t,A_t,S_{t+1}\}\cup\mathcal D_t.
 \tag{3}
\]
Eliminate in the backward order
\[
 A_H,S_{H+1},A_{H-1},S_H,\ldots,A_1,S_2,
 \tag{4}
\]
with a fixed initial state removed by its point-mass factor.  A direct backward induction shows that, before processing stage \(t\), the accumulated future message has scope contained in \(\{W,S_{t+1}\}\cup\bigcup_{u>t}\mathcal D_u\).  Combining it with (3) and eliminating the next variable in (4) leaves the same form with boundary state \(S_t\).  Consequently every path bucket contains \(W\), at most all \(m\) active decisions, and at most four nonglobal path variables.  Since the definition of constrained width excludes the global variable and subtracts one from the number of nonglobal live variables,
\[
 w_{\mathrm c}(\preceq)\le (m+4)-1=m+3
 \tag{5}
\]
for all path buckets.  After (4), the \(W\)-bucket contains no path variable and at most the \(m\) decisions.  After summing \(W\), successive decision buckets also contain at most those \(m\) variables, so (5) bounds every bucket.

All chance variables in (4), followed by \(W\), are summed before any row selector is maximized.  Hence every chance variable sharing a current factor with a decision has already been summed, which is exactly legality of \(\preceq\).  Applying \(\texttt{thm:optimization-preserving-compiler}\) and (5) gives
\[
 O\!\left(H^2N d^{w_{\mathrm c}(\preceq)+1}\right)
 \subseteq O\!\left(H^2N d^{m+4}\right).
 \tag{6}
\]
Singleton substitution does not change the objective: it merely inserts the unique feasible value.  The target theorem's backpointers therefore recover the joint assignment of the remaining selectors; restoring the unique singleton rows and applying its gluing sampler yields one compatible endpoint SCM shared across all worlds.

Finally, suppose \(|J|\le c\log H\) for a constant \(c\), while \(|\mathcal S|\), \(|\mathcal A|\), and \(d\) are fixed.  Then \(|\mathcal X|\) is fixed and (2) gives
\[
 d^{m+4}\le d^4H^{c(|\mathcal X|-1)\log d}.
\]
The explicit circuit has \(N=O(H|\mathcal X|)\) variables and factors for fixed alphabets, while the \(2H^2\)-state global selector is already accounted for by the leading \(H^2\) in (6).  Thus (6) is polynomial in \(H\), proving the stated sparse-stochastic tractability claim.
