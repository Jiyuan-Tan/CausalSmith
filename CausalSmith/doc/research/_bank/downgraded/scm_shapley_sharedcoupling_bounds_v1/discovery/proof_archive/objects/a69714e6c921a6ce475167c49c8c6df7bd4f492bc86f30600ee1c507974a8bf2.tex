For fixed ambient \((\pi,\pi_{\mathrm{base}},\tau)\),
\[
 \mathfrak M_{\mathrm{CI}}(K)=\left\{\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M(K):
 (R_t(x))_{x\in\mathcal X}\text{ is a mutually independent family for every }t\in[H]\right\}.
\]
Equivalently, under the transition-marginal constraint, its time-\(t\) response-table law is
\[
 \lambda_t=\bigotimes_{x\in\mathcal X}K_t(\cdot\mid x).
\]
The superscript \(\mathrm{CI}\) here records mutual independence only of transition-response coordinates indexed by state-action rows.  It does not assert equivalence to, or inclusion of, any external full SCM class or its policy-response, reward-response, or joint-counterfactual mechanisms.
