Work on the fixed discrete stratum \(H=2\), \(\mathcal S=\mathcal A=\{0,1\}\), \(\tau=(0,0,0,0,0)\), and \(i=1\).  Write \(\theta=(K,r,\pi,\pi_{\mathrm{base}})\).  The interior center \(\theta^\star\) has \(K_t(y\mid s,a)=1/2\) for all cells, \(r_1((s,a),y)=0\), \(r_2((s,a),y)=y\), \(\pi_t(1\mid s)=1/64\) for every \(t,s\), \(\pi_{\mathrm{base},1}(1\mid s)=1/4\), and \(\pi_{\mathrm{base},2}(1\mid s)=63/64\) for every \(s\).  All complementary action probabilities are determined by normalization.  Set \(\rho=1/1024\) and, with probability vectors ordered by \(0,1\), set
\[
\begin{aligned}
d_{\mathrm{pr}}(\theta,\theta^\star)=\max\Bigl\{
&\max_{t,x}\|K_t(\cdot\mid x)-(1/2,1/2)\|_1,
 \max_{x,y}|r_1(x,y)|,
 \max_{x,y}|r_2(x,y)-y|,\\
&\max_{t,s}\|\pi_t(\cdot\mid s)-(63/64,1/64)\|_1,\\
&\max_s\|\pi_{\mathrm{base},1}(\cdot\mid s)-(3/4,1/4)\|_1,
 \max_s\|\pi_{\mathrm{base},2}(\cdot\mid s)-(1/64,63/64)\|_1
\Bigr\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Define \(\mathcal N_\rho(\theta^\star)\) to be the set of all valid kernel, reward, and policy primitives on this stratum satisfying \(d_{\mathrm{pr}}(\theta,\theta^\star)<\rho\).  Apart from the probability-simplex constraints, no continuous cell is held fixed.  For \(\theta\in\mathcal N_\rho(\theta^\star)\), define
\[
 \mu_\tau(\theta)=\prod_{t=1}^2\pi_t(0\mid0)K_t(0\mid(0,0)),
\]
\[
 s_\pi(\theta)=\min_{t,s,a}\min\{\pi_t(a\mid s),\pi_{\mathrm{base},t}(a\mid s)\},
 \qquad
 s_K(\theta)=\min_{t,x,y}K_t(y\mid x),
\]
\[
 V_{\mathrm{fd}}(\theta)=\min_{x\in\mathcal X}
 \operatorname{Var}_{Y\sim K_2(\cdot\mid x)}\{r_2(x,Y)\},
\]
and the lower and upper excess widths of the internal relaxation by
\[
 \Delta_1^-(\theta)=L_1(K,\tau)-\inf\mathcal I_1^{\mathrm{ind}}(K,\tau),
 \qquad
 \Delta_1^+(\theta)=\sup\mathcal I_1^{\mathrm{ind}}(K,\tau)-U_1(K,\tau).
\]
