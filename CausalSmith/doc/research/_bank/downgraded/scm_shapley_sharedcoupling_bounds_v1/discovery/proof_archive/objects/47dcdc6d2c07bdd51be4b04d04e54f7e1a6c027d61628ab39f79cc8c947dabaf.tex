Fix \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{CI}}(K)\).  The repaired subclass inherits the fixed initial state, the externally randomized factual and coalition policies, factual-action positivity, and the transition assumptions from \(\mathfrak M(K)\).  Therefore the cancellation in \(\texttt{lem:posterior-row-projection}\) gives
\[
 \operatorname{Law}(R_1,\ldots,R_H\mid\tau)
 =\bigotimes_{t=1}^H\operatorname{Law}(R_t\mid R_t(x_t)=y_t). \tag{1}
\]
At time \(t\), \(\texttt{ass:within-time-row-independence}\) makes the vector of all coordinates \(R_t(x)\), \(x\ne x_t\), independent of \(R_t(x_t)\).  Since \(p_t>0\), conditioning on the factual coordinate and using \(\texttt{ass:transition-marginals}\) gives, for \(x\ne x_t\),
\[
 \Pr\{R_t(x)=y\mid R_t(x_t)=y_t\}
 =\Pr\{R_t(x)=y\}=K_t(y\mid x). \tag{2}
\]
On the conditioned coordinate itself the law is \(\delta_{y_t}\).  Equations (1)--(2) make the posterior kernel uniquely equal to \(q^{\mathrm{CI}}\) in \(\texttt{def:counterfactual-independent-target}\).

This kernel is feasible in \(\mathcal B(K,\tau)\), since its factual row is the required point mass and, for \(x\ne x_t\),
\[
 p_tq_{t,x}^{\mathrm{CI}}(y)=p_tK_t(y\mid x)\le K_t(y\mid x).
\]
The fresh policy coins are independent of the response tables, so every model in the subclass evaluates every coalition by the same Markov recursion with \(q^{\mathrm{CI}}\).  Consequently
\[
 \Theta_i^{\mathrm{CI}}(K,\tau)=\{\Phi_i(q^{\mathrm{CI}})\}.
\]
Feasibility of \(q^{\mathrm{CI}}\) and \(\texttt{thm:sharp-shared-interval}\) yield \(\Phi_i(q^{\mathrm{CI}})\in\Theta_i(K,\tau)\), proving the asserted inclusion.  This reduction selects only the transition-row coupling inside the explicitly externally randomized, deterministic-reward model; it does not claim to remove the full policy- or reward-counterfactual-independence scheme of the published model.
