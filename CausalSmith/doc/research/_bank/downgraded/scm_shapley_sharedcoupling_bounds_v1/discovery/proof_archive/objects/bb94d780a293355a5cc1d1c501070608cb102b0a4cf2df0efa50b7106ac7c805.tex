On a finite product extension of the transition probability space, the complete family \(\{V_t:t\in[H]\}\cup\{V_t^C:t\in[H],\ C\subseteq[H]\}\) is mutually independent and jointly independent of \((S_1,U_1,\ldots,U_H)\), with each \(V_t\in\mathcal A^{\mathcal S}\) satisfying \(\Pr\{V_t(s)=a\}=\pi_t(a\mid s)\) for every \(s\in\mathcal S\) and \(a\in\mathcal A\) and entering the factual equation \(A_t=G_t(S_t,V_t)=V_t(S_t)\), and with each \(V_t^C\in\mathcal A^{\mathcal S}\) satisfying \(\Pr\{V_t^C(s)=a\}=\alpha_t^C(a\mid s)\) for every \(s\in\mathcal S\) and \(a\in\mathcal A\) and generating the state-contingent action sample \(A_t=V_t^C(S_t)\) in coalition world \(C\).
