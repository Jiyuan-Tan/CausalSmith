Fix \(t\in[H]\), and abbreviate \(E_t=\{R_t(x_t)=y_t\}\).  By \(\text{ass:transition-marginals}\), \(\Pr(E_t)=K_t(y_t\mid x_t)=p_t\), which is positive by \(\text{ass:factual-positivity}\).  For a compatible SCM define
\[
 q_{t,x}(y)=\Pr\{R_t(x)=y\mid E_t\}.
\]
Then \(q_{t,x_t}=\delta_{y_t}\).  If \(x\ne x_t\), the multiplication rule and monotonicity of probability give, for every \(y\in\mathcal S\),
\[
 p_tq_{t,x}(y)
 =\Pr\{R_t(x_t)=y_t,R_t(x)=y\}
 \le \Pr\{R_t(x)=y\}
 =K_t(y\mid x),
\]
where the last equality is \(\text{ass:transition-marginals}\).  Thus the induced array belongs to \(\mathcal B(K,\tau)\) by \(\text{def:posterior-row-fiber}\).

Conversely, fix \(q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)\).  At time \(t\), first draw a variable \(F_t\) with law \(K_t(\cdot\mid x_t)\), and put \(R_t(x_t)=F_t\).  Suppose first that \(p_t<1\).  For every \(x\ne x_t\), define
\[
 h_{t,x}(y)=\frac{K_t(y\mid x)-p_tq_{t,x}(y)}{1-p_t}.
\]
The numerator is nonnegative by \(\text{def:posterior-row-fiber}\), and
\[
 \sum_{y\in\mathcal S}h_{t,x}(y)
 =\frac{1-p_t}{1-p_t}=1.
\]
Conditional on \(F_t=y_t\), draw the coordinates \(R_t(x)\), \(x\ne x_t\), independently with respective laws \(q_{t,x}\).  Conditional on \(F_t\ne y_t\), draw them independently with respective laws \(h_{t,x}\).  The particular value of \(F_t\ne y_t\) need not enter this second draw.  For every nonfactual row,
\[
 \Pr\{R_t(x)=y\}
 =p_tq_{t,x}(y)+(1-p_t)h_{t,x}(y)
 =K_t(y\mid x).
\]
If \(p_t=1\), the inequalities \(q_{t,x}(y)\le K_t(y\mid x)\), together with equality of both coordinate sums to one, imply \(q_{t,x}=K_t(\cdot\mid x)\); use that law in the only, factual branch and omit the residual branch.  In either case the resulting random table has a law \(\lambda_t\) with all the required marginals and with conditional row marginals \(q_{t,x}\) given \(E_t\).  Notice that this is one joint table law: it is not reselected by a coalition or by the row later queried.

Sample the constructed tables independently over \(t\).  This enforces \(\text{ass:independent-time-disturbances}\), while table lookup supplies the equations in \(\text{ass:markov-structural-equations}\); the displayed marginal calculation supplies \(\text{ass:transition-marginals}\).  Hence the construction is in \(\mathfrak M(K)\) by \(\text{def:compatible-scm-class}\).  Because the events \(E_t\) belong to independent time blocks,
\[
 \Pr\{R_1\in dr_1,\ldots,R_H\in dr_H\mid E_1\cap\cdots\cap E_H\}
 =\prod_{t=1}^H\Pr\{R_t\in dr_t\mid E_t\}.
\]
Under the specified policy interventions, initial-state and action-randomization factors are fixed and do not alter these environment-table likelihoods.  A counterfactual path at time \(t\) queries exactly the coordinate \(R_t(S_t,A_t)\); earlier states are functions of earlier, conditionally independent blocks.  Its conditional transition law is therefore \(q_{t,S_t,A_t}\).  Thus every coalition world is evaluated by the same posterior Markov kernel \(q\), completing both directions.
