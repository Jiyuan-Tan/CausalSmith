Let
\[
 \mathcal E=\{K\in\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}(\widehat K)\}.
\]
The assumed simultaneous coverage gives \(\Pr_K(\mathcal E)\ge1-\alpha\) under \(\text{ass:fixed-row-multinomial-sampling}\).  On \(\mathcal E\), every prespecified trajectory having positive probability under \(K\) makes the true kernel admissible in the defining optimizations for \(L_i^\alpha\) and \(U_i^\alpha\).  Hence, directly from \(\text{def:confidence-fiber-endpoints}\),
\[
 L_i^\alpha\le L_i(K,\tau)\le U_i(K,\tau)\le U_i^\alpha.
\]
The event \(\mathcal E\) depends only on the transition kernel, not on \(i\), \(\tau\), or the specified policy pair.  The same event therefore proves all asserted containments simultaneously; no additional union bound over queries is needed.  By \(\text{thm:sharp-shared-interval}\), these are containments of the whole population identified sets, not merely of selected point values.

For the geometric assertion, write the rowwise product confidence region as \(\prod_{t,x}\mathcal C_{t,x}\), and set
\[
 p_t^- =\min_{k\in\mathcal C_{t,x_t}}k(y_t),
 \qquad
 p_t^+ =\max_{k\in\mathcal C_{t,x_t}}k(y_t).
\]
The proposed statement assumes that the row polytopes are nonempty and that \(p_t^+>0\) for every \(t\), precisely the conditions needed for a nonempty positive-evidence confidence fiber.  If \(p_t^->0\), define, for \(x\ne x_t\),
\[
 \mathcal Q_{t,x}
 =\{q_x\in\Delta(\mathcal S):
       \exists k_x\in\mathcal C_{t,x}\text{ such that }
       p_t^-q_x(y)\le k_x(y)\text{ for all }y\}.
\]
This is the projection of an explicitly given bounded polyhedron in \((q_x,k_x)\), hence is an explicit polytope.  If \(q_x\) is feasible for some common factual probability \(p_t\ge p_t^-\), it is feasible at \(p_t^-\) with the same \(k_x\).  Conversely, choose a factual confidence row attaining \(p_t^-\) and, independently for each alternate row, a witnessing \(k_x\).  Product structure of the confidence region then supplies a single kernel \(K'\), with the same \(p_t^-\) for every alternate row.  Thus the union of posterior fibers is exactly
\[
 \{q_{t,x_t}=\delta_{y_t}\}\times
 \prod_{x\ne x_t}\mathcal Q_{t,x}
\]
at time \(t\), and the product over times is exact as well.

Suppose instead that \(p_t^-=0<p_t^+\), and put
\[
 J_{t,x}=\{y\in\mathcal S:\exists k\in\mathcal C_{t,x}
                    \text{ with }k(y)>0\}.
\]
For every positive factual value \(p\), feasibility \(pq_x(y)\le k_x(y)\) forces \(q_x(y)=0\) when \(y\notin J_{t,x}\).  This proves that the closure is contained in the simplex supported on \(J_{t,x}\).  Conversely, by convexity of \(\mathcal C_{t,x}\), averaging one witness for each member of the finite set \(J_{t,x}\) gives a single \(k_x^*\in\mathcal C_{t,x}\) positive on all of \(J_{t,x}\).  For any \(q_x\) supported there, all inequalities \(pq_x(y)\le k_x^*(y)\) hold for sufficiently small \(p>0\).  There are finitely many alternate rows, so one sufficiently small \(p\) works for all of them.  The projection of the convex factual-row polytope onto its \(y_t\)-coordinate is the interval \([0,p_t^+]\), so that common positive \(p\) is attainable.  Letting \(p\downarrow0\) proves that the closure is the claimed product of support simplices.  The same reasoning independently at each time completes the product-fiber characterization.

If \(p_t^+=0\), the set of confidence kernels satisfying the positive-evidence restriction is empty at that time, and its closure is empty rather than a support simplex.  This explicit countercase is why the positive-maximum clause in the proposed statement is necessary.
