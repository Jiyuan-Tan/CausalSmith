For every \(i\in[H]\), let \(K\) and \(K'\) satisfy \(K_t(y_t\mid x_t)\ge\kappa\) and \(K'_t(y_t\mid x_t)\ge\kappa\) for every \(t\in[H]\). Then
\[
 \max\!\left\{|L_i(K,\tau)-L_i(K',\tau)|,
                 |U_i(K,\tau)-U_i(K',\tau)|\right\}
 \le \frac{4DH}{\kappa}\,\delta(K,K').
\]
Define the certified positivity-preserving radius by \(r_{\mathrm{pos}}(\kappa)=\kappa/2\). If only \(K_t(y_t\mid x_t)\ge\kappa\) is initially known and \(\delta(K,K')<r_{\mathrm{pos}}(\kappa)\), then \(K'_t(y_t\mid x_t)>\kappa/2\) for every \(t\), and, for each \(E_i\in\{L_i,U_i\}\),
\[
 E_i(K,\tau)-\frac{8DH}{\kappa}\delta(K,K')
 \le E_i(K',\tau)
 \le E_i(K,\tau)+\frac{8DH}{\kappa}\delta(K,K').
\]
On the coverage event \(K\in\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}(\widehat K)\), let
\[
 r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K)
 =\sup_{K'\in\mathcal C_{1-\alpha}(\widehat K)}\delta(K,K').
\]
If \(K_t(y_t\mid x_t)\ge\kappa\) for every \(t\) and \(r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K)<\kappa/2\), then
\[
 L_i(K,\tau)-\frac{8DH}{\kappa}r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K)
 \le L_i^{\alpha}\le L_i(K,\tau)
\]
and
\[
 U_i(K,\tau)
 \le U_i^{\alpha}
 \le U_i(K,\tau)+\frac{8DH}{\kappa}r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K).
\]
Consequently, the plug-in endpoints are uniformly consistent on classes with \(\kappa\)-positive factual cells whenever \(\delta(\widehat K,K)\) converges uniformly to zero in probability. The displayed confidence-fiber brackets also give uniform convergence on any sequence of coverage events for which \(r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K)\) converges uniformly to zero.
