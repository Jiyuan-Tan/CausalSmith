Write \(\delta=\delta(K,K')\), \(p=K_t(y_t\mid x_t)\), and \(p'=K'_t(y_t\mid x_t)\).  Then \(|p-p'|\le\delta\), and by hypothesis \(p,p'\ge\kappa\).  Fix a nonfactual row \(x\ne x_t\), abbreviate \(k=K_t(\cdot\mid x)\), \(k'=K'_t(\cdot\mid x)\), and take \(q\) in its \(K\)-fiber.  Truncate it to
\[
 a(y)=\min\{q(y),k'(y)/p'\},
 \qquad d_0=1-\sum_ya(y).
\]
Since \(pq(y)\le k(y)\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 d_0
 &=\frac1{p'}\sum_y\bigl(p'q(y)-k'(y)\bigr)_+\\
 &\le\frac{|p'-p|+\|k-k'\|_1}{p'}
 \le\frac{2\delta}{\kappa}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Indeed, coordinatewise \(p'q-k'=(p'-p)q+(pq-k)+(k-k')\), the middle term is nonpositive, the positive parts of the first term sum to at most \(|p'-p|\sum_yq(y)\), and those of the last sum to at most \(\|k-k'\|_1\).

The remaining upper-cap capacity is
\[
 \sum_y\{k'(y)/p'-a(y)\}=1/p'-1+d_0\ge d_0.
\]
Redistribute mass \(d_0\) within these capacities to obtain \(q'\in\Delta(\mathcal S)\) satisfying \(p'q'(y)\le k'(y)\).  Removed and added masses are both \(d_0\), so
\[
 \|q-q'\|_1\le2d_0\le\frac{4\delta}{\kappa},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{TV}(q,q')\le\frac{2\delta}{\kappa}.
\]
The factual posterior rows agree exactly at \(\delta_{y_t}\).  Applying the construction to every row and time maps any \(q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)\) to a \(q'\in\mathcal B(K',\tau)\) with
\[
 \max_{t,x}\operatorname{TV}(q_{t,x},q'_{t,x})
 \le\frac{2\delta}{\kappa}.
\]
The same construction with \(K,K'\) reversed gives the other Hausdorff direction.

Fix a coalition policy.  Couple its two Markov paths sequentially by maximal coupling whenever their histories still agree.  Conditional on agreement through time \(t\), the probability of a mismatch at the next transition is at most \(2\delta/\kappa\); the action kernel is identical in the two models.  A union bound over \(H\) transitions yields path-law total variation at most \(2H\delta/\kappa\).  Every complete return lies in \([\underline G,\overline G]\), an interval of width \(D\), so the layer-cake identity after subtracting \(\underline G\), or directly the coupling inequality, gives
\[
 |g_C(q)-g_C(q')|\le\frac{2DH\delta}{\kappa}.
\]
The Shapley weights are nonnegative and sum to one: grouping by \(k=|C|\),
\[
 \sum_{C\subseteq[H]\setminus\{i\}}\omega_H(C)
 =\sum_{k=0}^{H-1}{H-1\choose k}\frac{k!(H-k-1)!}{H!}=1.
\]
Consequently
\[
 |\Phi_i(q)-\Phi_i(q')|
 \le\sum_C\omega_H(C)
   \bigl(|g_C(q)-g_C(q')|+|g_{C\cup\{i\}}(q)-g_{C\cup\{i\}}(q')|\bigr)
 \le\frac{4DH\delta}{\kappa}.
\]

Match a maximizer over the \(K\)-fiber into the \(K'\)-fiber to obtain \(U_i(K,\tau)\le U_i(K',\tau)+4DH\delta/\kappa\), and reverse the kernels for the opposite inequality.  The identical two comparisons for minimizers prove the asserted bound for \(L_i\).  Attainment and the causal interpretation of these extrema are supplied by \(\text{thm:sharp-shared-interval}\).

For the consistency statement, let a class have true factual cells at least \(\kappa\) and suppose \(\delta(\widehat K,K)\to0\) uniformly in probability.  On the event \(\delta(\widehat K,K)<\kappa/2\), the empirical factual cells are at least \(\kappa/2\); applying the proved bound with positivity constant \(\kappa/2\) makes both plug-in endpoint errors tend uniformly to zero.  For a shrinking confidence fiber, assume its maximal row discrepancy from \(K\) tends uniformly to zero on its coverage event.  Eventually every member has factual cells at least \(\kappa/2\), and the same bound holds uniformly over every kernel in the fiber.  Taking its infimum and supremum therefore makes the confidence-fiber endpoints converge uniformly to the population endpoints.
