For \(i\in[H]\), define the internally constructed coalitionwise relaxation
\[
 \mathcal I_i^{\mathrm{ind}}(K,\tau)
 =\left[
 \sum_{C\subseteq[H]\setminus\{i\}}\omega_H(C)
 \left\{\inf_{q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)}g_C(q)
       -\sup_{q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)}g_{C\cup\{i\}}(q)\right\},
 \sum_{C\subseteq[H]\setminus\{i\}}\omega_H(C)
 \left\{\sup_{q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)}g_C(q)
       -\inf_{q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)}g_{C\cup\{i\}}(q)\right\}
 \right].
\]
Thus every distinct coalition return is optimized over its own copy of the posterior fiber before the signed Shapley sum is formed.  This relaxation is defined internally for comparison; its definition does not attribute this operation to an external estimator or workflow.
