Call a simplified endpoint circuit \(\mathfrak F_i^\sigma\) a fully active chain minor of length \(k\) if a factor-graph minor contains \(k\) distinct non-singleton row selectors, one at each of \(k\) active stages, linked successively by summed Markov chance variables, with every transition factor genuinely depending on its selector and the two adjacent chain variables.  If \(k=|J|\), then every legal sum-before-max order induces a clique on these selectors before the first of them is maximized and hence satisfies
\[
 w_{\mathrm c}(\preceq)\ge |J|-1.
\]
Such fully active minors occur for every horizon in binary finite Markov SCMs with strictly interior transition and policy probabilities.  Therefore the ordinary fully stochastic growing-horizon class admits \(w_{\mathrm c}(\preceq)=\Omega(|J|)=\Omega(H)\), so neither bounded nor logarithmic constrained width holds uniformly on that class.
