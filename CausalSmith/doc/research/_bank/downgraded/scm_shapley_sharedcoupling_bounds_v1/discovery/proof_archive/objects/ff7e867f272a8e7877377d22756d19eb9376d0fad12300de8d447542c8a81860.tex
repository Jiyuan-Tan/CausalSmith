Put \(\delta=\delta(K,K')\).  Fix \(t\), write \(p=K_t(y_t\mid x_t)\), \(p'=K'_t(y_t\mid x_t)\), and take a nonfactual row \(x\ne x_t\).  Let \(k=K_t(\cdot\mid x)\), \(k'=K'_t(\cdot\mid x)\), and let \(q\) be feasible for the \(K\)-fiber.  Define
\[
 a(y)=\min\{q(y),k'(y)/p'\},
 \qquad d_0=1-\sum_ya(y).
\]
Division is valid because \(p'\ge\kappa>0\).  Since \(pq(y)\le k(y)\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 d_0
 &=\frac1{p'}\sum_y\bigl(p'q(y)-k'(y)\bigr)_+\\
 &\le\frac{|p'-p|+\|k-k'\|_1}{p'}
 \le\frac{2\delta}{\kappa}.
 \end{aligned} \tag{1}
\]
For the middle inequality, decompose \(p'q-k'=(p'-p)q+(pq-k)+(k-k')\); the middle term is coordinatewise nonpositive, while the positive parts of the other two terms total at most \(|p'-p|\) and \(\|k-k'\|_1\).  Both are at most \(\delta\) by its definition.

The unused upper-cap capacity is
\[
 \sum_y\{k'(y)/p'-a(y)\}=1/p'-1+d_0\ge d_0.
\]
Distribute mass \(d_0\) within those capacities and call the added vector \(b\).  Then \(q'=a+b\) is in the \(K'\)-row factor and
\[
 \|q-q'\|_1\le\|q-a\|_1+\|b\|_1=2d_0
 \le\frac{4\delta}{\kappa},
 \qquad \operatorname{TV}(q,q')\le\frac{2\delta}{\kappa}. \tag{2}
\]
The factual posterior rows agree at \(\delta_{y_t}\).  Applying this construction to every nonfactual row and time maps any \(q\in\mathcal B(K,\tau)\) to a \(q'\in\mathcal B(K',\tau)\) with the uniform row bound (2).  Interchanging \(K,K'\) gives the reverse fiber approximation.

Fix a coalition policy and couple its paths under \(q,q'\).  Both start at \(s_1\) by \(\texttt{ass:fixed-initial-state}\).  While the states agree, use the same fresh external action coin, which is licensed by \(\texttt{ass:factual-policy-exogeneity}\), and maximally couple the two next-state rows.  Equation (2) bounds the conditional probability of a first next-state mismatch by \(2\delta/\kappa\) at every transition.  A union bound over \(H\) transitions therefore bounds the complete-path mismatch probability by \(2H\delta/\kappa\).  Equal paths have equal deterministic returns, while unequal complete returns differ by at most \(D\).  Hence, for every coalition,
\[
 |g_C(q)-g_C(q')|\le\frac{2DH\delta}{\kappa}. \tag{3}
\]
The weights are nonnegative, and their total is
\[
 \sum_{C\subseteq[H]\setminus\{i\}}\omega_H(C)
 =\sum_{k=0}^{H-1}{H-1\choose k}\frac{k!(H-k-1)!}{H!}=1. \tag{4}
\]
Thus \(\texttt{def:shapley-coordinate}\) and (3) give
\[
 |\Phi_i(q)-\Phi_i(q')|
 \le\frac{4DH\delta}{\kappa}. \tag{5}
\]

By \(\texttt{thm:sharp-shared-interval}\), both extrema are attained and represent the causal identified-set endpoints in the repaired class.  Map a maximizer over the \(K\)-fiber into the \(K'\)-fiber and apply (5); then reverse the kernels.  This proves the two-sided upper-endpoint bound.  Applying the same argument to minimizers proves
\[
 \max\left\{|L_i(K,\tau)-L_i(K',\tau)|,
 |U_i(K,\tau)-U_i(K',\tau)|\right\}
 \le\frac{4DH}{\kappa}\delta(K,K'). \tag{6}
\]

By definition, \(r_{\mathrm{pos}}(\kappa)=\kappa/2\).  If only the \(K\)-cells are initially at least \(\kappa\) and \(\delta(K,K')<\kappa/2\), then
\[
 K'_t(y_t\mid x_t)\ge K_t(y_t\mid x_t)-\delta(K,K')>\kappa/2.
\]
Both kernels consequently satisfy the first part with common lower bound \(\kappa/2\), so for \(E_i\in\{L_i,U_i\}\),
\[
 |E_i(K',\tau)-E_i(K,\tau)|
 \le\frac{8DH}{\kappa}\delta(K,K'). \tag{7}
\]

On the coverage event, the true kernel belongs to the explicit confidence product.  If \(r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K)<\kappa/2\), every kernel in that product is within the certified radius, has positive factual cells, and obeys (7).  Taking the infimum of its lower endpoints and the supremum of its upper endpoints, while using the true kernel as a feasible point, gives
\[
 L_i(K,\tau)-\frac{8DH}{\kappa}r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K)
 \le L_i^\alpha\le L_i(K,\tau)
\]
and
\[
 U_i(K,\tau)\le U_i^\alpha
 \le U_i(K,\tau)+\frac{8DH}{\kappa}r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K).
\]
The empty-fiber fallback is irrelevant on this event because the confidence product contains the positive-evidence true kernel.

Finally, on \(\delta(\widehat K,K)<\kappa/2\), equation (7) with \(K'=\widehat K\) bounds each plug-in error by \(8DH\delta(\widehat K,K)/\kappa\).  Uniform convergence of this discrepancy in probability therefore implies uniform plug-in consistency.  The two confidence displays give uniform confidence-endpoint convergence on any coverage events for which \(r_{\mathcal C}(K,\widehat K)\) converges uniformly to zero.  No differentiability, normality, or adaptive-sampling assertion is used.
