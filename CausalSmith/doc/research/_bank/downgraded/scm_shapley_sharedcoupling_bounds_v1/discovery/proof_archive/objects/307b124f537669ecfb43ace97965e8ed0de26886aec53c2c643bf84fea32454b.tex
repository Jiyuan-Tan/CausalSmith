If \(t\notin J\), then \(p_t=1\).  For every nonfactual row, \(\texttt{def:posterior-row-fiber}\) gives
\[
 q_{t,x}(y)\le K_t(y\mid x)\quad\forall y,
 \qquad \sum_yq_{t,x}(y)=\sum_yK_t(y\mid x)=1.
\]
All coordinate differences \(K_t(y\mid x)-q_{t,x}(y)\) are nonnegative and sum to zero, so the row is the singleton \(K_t(\cdot\mid x)\).  The factual row is always a singleton.  At a time in \(J\), at most \(|\mathcal X|-1\) nonfactual rows remain non-singleton.  Therefore
\[
 m\le |J|(|\mathcal X|-1). \tag{1}
\]

Substitute every singleton selector and let \(\mathcal D_t\) be the remaining selectors at time \(t\), so \(\sum_t|\mathcal D_t|=m\).  In the explicit circuit of \(\texttt{thm:optimization-preserving-compiler}\), every stage-\(t\) policy, transition, and reward factor has scope contained in
\[
 \{W,S_t,A_t,S_{t+1}\}\cup\mathcal D_t. \tag{2}
\]
Eliminate the chance path backward in the order
\[
 A_H,S_{H+1},A_{H-1},S_H,\ldots,A_1,S_2. \tag{3}
\]
A backward induction shows that before stage \(t\) the accumulated future message has scope within \(\{W,S_{t+1}\}\cup\bigcup_{u>t}\mathcal D_u\).  Combining it with (2) and eliminating the next variable in (3) leaves the analogous message with boundary state \(S_t\).  Thus a path bucket contains \(W\), at most all \(m\) decisions, and at most four nonglobal path variables.  Since \(W\) is excluded from constrained width and the definition subtracts one,
\[
 w_{\mathrm c}(\preceq)\le m+3. \tag{4}
\]
After the path is eliminated, the \(W\)-bucket and all decision buckets obey the same bound.  All path variables and then \(W\) are summed before any row selector is maximized, so the order is legal.

The compiler theorem and (4) give
\[
 O\!\left(H^2N d^{w_{\mathrm c}(\preceq)+1}\right)
 \subseteq O\!\left(H^2N d^{m+4}\right). \tag{5}
\]
Singleton substitution changes no objective value.  Its backpointers recover the remaining joint selector assignment; restoring the unique rows and applying the repaired gluing lemma, including the fixed initial state and independent policy coins, gives one compatible endpoint SCM shared across all worlds.

If \(|J|\le c\log H\) while \(|\mathcal S|\), \(|\mathcal A|\), and \(d\) are fixed, (1) gives
\[
 d^{m+4}\le d^4H^{c(|\mathcal X|-1)\log d}.
\]
For fixed alphabets the explicit circuit has \(N=O(H|\mathcal X|)\); the \(2H^2\)-state global selector is already represented by the leading \(H^2\) in (5).  Hence (5) is polynomial in \(H\).
