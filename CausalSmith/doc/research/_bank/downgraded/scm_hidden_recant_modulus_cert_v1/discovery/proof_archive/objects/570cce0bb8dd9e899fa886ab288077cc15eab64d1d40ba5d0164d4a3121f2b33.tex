\(q^W=(2/3,1/6,2/3)\).  In

treatment arm, both mediator values occur at each hidden state, and all observed

displayed bridge-denominator supports hold.

two.  The two columns of \(P(H\mid W)\) are likewise nonproportional and have
equal first and third coordinates, so \((1,0,-1)^\top\) is a left-null vector and
the rank is exactly two.
