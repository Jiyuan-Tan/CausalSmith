The proposed universal full-law converse is false.  Specifically, modify either
SCM in \(\mathrm{def{:}failure\text{-}artifact}\) by setting
\(Y_{a,h,m,w}=0\) for every \((a,h,m,w)\), and let \(P\) be the resulting
observed law.  Then \(P\) is induced by a member of
\(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{WBC}}^+\),
\(\ell(P)\notin\operatorname{row}(B(P))\), and yet every
\(\mathcal S\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{WBC}}^+\) satisfying
\(P_{\mathcal S}^O=P\) has \(\psi(\mathcal S)=0\).  Thus failed observable
row span does not by itself imply causal nonidentification; deterministic-zero
observed outcomes form an explicit observable rescue subclass, although no
maximal rescue subclass is claimed.
