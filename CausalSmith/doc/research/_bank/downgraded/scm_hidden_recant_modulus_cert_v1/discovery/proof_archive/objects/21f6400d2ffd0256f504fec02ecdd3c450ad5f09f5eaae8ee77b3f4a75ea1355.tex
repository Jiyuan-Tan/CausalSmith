Fix a map \(f:\mathcal X\to\mathcal X\), an observed law \(P\) satisfying the premise, and an arbitrary \(\widetilde{\mathcal S}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{WBC}}^{+}\) with \(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}^O=P\). All probabilities in the first part of the proof are under \(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}\). The arm-zero denominator condition in \(\mathrm{ass{:}a0\text{-}bridge\text{-}denominators}\) implies \(P(A=0)>0\). Hence the observed-law premise is equivalent to
\[
P\bigl(Y=f(W),A=0\bigr)=P(A=0).
\tag{1}
\]
Define
\[
h_{0,f}(w,m)=f(w),\qquad h_{1,f}(w)=f(w),
\]
and let \(x_f=(f(0),f(1),f(0),f(1),f(0),f(1))^{\top}\) be their coordinate vector in the ordering fixed by \(\mathrm{def{:}coupled\text{-}bridge\text{-}matrix}\).

We first prove latent bridge validity. Fix \((h,m)\in\mathcal H\times\mathcal X\) with \(P(H_0=h)>0\) and \(P(M_{1,h}=m)>0\). By \(\mathrm{ass{:}latent\text{-}a0\text{-}transport\text{-}support}\),
\[
P(H=h,M=m,A=0)>0.
\]
Since the complement of the event in (1) has probability zero inside \(\{A=0\}\), it also has probability zero inside this positive-probability subevent. Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E[Y\mid H=h,M=m,A=0]
&=\mathbb E[f(W)\mid H=h,M=m,A=0]\\
&=\sum_{w\in\mathcal X}h_{0,f}(w,m)
  P(W=w\mid H=h,M=m,A=0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
This is exactly the first latent bridge equation in \(\mathrm{def{:}latent\text{-}bridge\text{-}set}\), on every cell on which that definition requires it.

Next fix \(h\in\mathcal H\) with \(P(H_0=h)>0\). The condition in \(\mathrm{ass{:}latent\text{-}a1\text{-}transport\text{-}support}\) gives \(P(H=h,A=1)>0\), so the following conditional laws are defined. Finite marginalization gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{w,m}h_{0,f}(w,m)P(W=w,M=m\mid H=h,A=1)
&=\sum_w f(w)P(W=w\mid H=h,A=1)\\
&=\sum_wh_{1,f}(w)P(W=w\mid H=h,A=1).
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
Thus the second latent bridge equation is tautological, and (2)--(3) prove \(x_f\in\mathcal H_{\mathrm{lat}}(\widetilde{\mathcal S})\).

We next verify the observed bridge equations rather than inferring them from latent ones. For every \((z,m)\in\mathcal X^2\), \(\mathrm{ass{:}a0\text{-}bridge\text{-}denominators}\) makes \(P(A=0,Z=z,M=m)>0\). Applying (1) on this subevent yields
\[
\mathbb E_P[Y\mid Z=z,M=m,A=0]
=\sum_wh_{0,f}(w,m)P(W=w\mid Z=z,M=m,A=0).
\tag{4}
\]
For every \(z\in\mathcal X\), \(\mathrm{ass{:}a1\text{-}bridge\text{-}denominators}\) makes \(P(A=1,Z=z)>0\), and marginalization yields
\[
\sum_{w,m}h_{0,f}(w,m)P(W=w,M=m\mid Z=z,A=1)
=\sum_wh_{1,f}(w)P(W=w\mid Z=z,A=1).
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (4)--(5), together with \(\mathrm{def{:}coupled\text{-}bridge\text{-}matrix}\) and \(\mathrm{def{:}observed\text{-}bridge\text{-}fiber}\), give
\[
B(P)x_f=b(P),\qquad x_f\in\mathcal H_{\mathrm{obs}}(P).
\tag{6}
\]

We have proved that \(x_f\) lies in the latent--observed intersection for the arbitrary compatible model \(\widetilde{\mathcal S}\). The causal equality in \(\mathrm{lem{:}latent\text{-}bridge\text{-}identifies}\), whose hypotheses are the consistency, sequential, cross-world, proxy-exclusion, support, and overlap properties included in \(\mathrm{def{:}wbc\text{-}class}\), now gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\psi(\widetilde{\mathcal S})
&=\ell(P)^{\top}x_f\\
&=\sum_{w\in\mathcal X}f(w)P(W=w\mid A=0)\\
&=\mathbb E_P[f(W)\mid A=0].
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
The second equality uses the definition of \(\ell(P)\). Since \(\widetilde{\mathcal S}\) was arbitrary, (7) proves point identification on the entire observed-law fiber. No row-space, rank, inversion, completeness, or positivity of an individual value of \(W\) or \(Y\) was used.

It remains to certify that the rescue can occur nondegenerately while row span fails. From the two SCMs \(\mathcal S^{\oplus}\) and \(\mathcal S^{\ominus}\) in \(\mathrm{def{:}failure\text{-}artifact}\), form \(\overline{\mathcal S}^{\oplus}\) and \(\overline{\mathcal S}^{\ominus}\) by retaining every structural kernel except that the outcome potentials are set to
\[
Y_{0,h,m,w}=w,
\qquad
Y_{1,h,m,w}=\mathbf 1\{U_Y\le 1/2\}.
\tag{8}
\]
These models are compatible with \(\mathcal G_{\mathrm{WBC}}\): the right sides in (8) use only allowed parents and the already declared exogenous shock. All five consistency conditions hold by structural evaluation. The mediator potentials depend only on \(U_M\), so \(\mathrm{ass{:}sequential\text{-}mediator}\) follows from independence of the shocks. For \(a=0\), the pair \(\{Y_{0,H_0,m,W_{H_0}},H_0\}=\{W_{H_0},H_0\}\) depends only on \((U_H,U_W)\); for \(a=1\), it depends only on \((U_H,U_Y)\). Since \(A\) depends only on the independent shock \(U_A\), \(\mathrm{ass{:}sequential\text{-}treatment}\) holds. Conditional on \((H,A)\), the fixed-\(m\) outcome \(Y_{A,H,m,W_H}\) depends on \(U_W\) in arm zero and on \(U_Y\) in arm one, whereas the observed mediator depends on \(U_M\); this proves \(\mathrm{ass{:}sequential\text{-}outcome}\). The same separation of \(U_M\) from \((U_H,U_W,U_Y)\) proves \(\mathrm{ass{:}cross\text{-}world}\). Independence of \(U_Z\) from \((U_M,U_W,U_Y)\) conditional on \((H,A)\) proves \(\mathrm{ass{:}z\text{-}proxy\text{-}exclusion}\), and independence of \(U_W\) from \((U_A,U_Z,U_M)\) conditional on \(H\) proves \(\mathrm{ass{:}w\text{-}proxy\text{-}exclusion}\).

The support conditions are unchanged from \(\mathrm{def{:}failure\text{-}artifact}\). Explicitly, both treatment probabilities and both proxy probabilities equal \(1/2\); every coordinate of the arm-specific hidden-state distributions is positive; \(P(M_{0,h}=1)=1/2\); and every displayed value of \(P(M_{1,h}=1)\) lies strictly between zero and one. Consequently all observed denominators and both target-relevant latent transport-overlap conditions hold. With \(f(w)=w\), equations (2)--(6) apply to each modified SCM and establish the latent-valid observed bridge intersection required by \(\mathrm{ass{:}latent\text{-}valid\text{-}bridge\text{-}existence}\). Hence both modified SCMs belong to \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{WBC}}^{+}\).

The two modified models have a common observed law. In arm zero their \((H,Z,W,M)\) kernels coincide and both obey \(Y=W\). In arm one the treatment-one outcome remains an independent Bernoulli one-half variable, and the equality of the \((Z,W,M)\) mixtures for the two treatment-one mediator kernels is exactly the annihilation calculation recorded in \(\mathrm{def{:}failure\text{-}artifact}\). Denote this common law by \(P\). Since \(B(P)\) and \(\ell(P)\) depend only on \((A,Z,W,M)\), they equal the displayed matrix and loading in that definition. For
\[
v=(0,0,0,0,1,-1)^{\top},
\]
direct multiplication gives
\[
B(P)v=0,
\qquad
\ell(P)^{\top}v=\frac{7}{12}-\frac{5}{12}=\frac16\ne0.
\tag{9}
\]
Equation (6) shows \(b(P)\in\operatorname{col}(B(P))\). Therefore \(\mathrm{lem{:}null\text{-}annihilator}\) and (9) imply
\[
\ell(P)\notin\operatorname{row}(B(P)).
\tag{10}
\]
Nevertheless the outcome is nondegenerate in arm zero, because the exact hidden-state and proxy probabilities in \(\mathrm{def{:}failure\text{-}artifact}\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=1\mid A=0)
&=P(W=1\mid A=0)\\
&=\frac12\frac14+\frac13\frac12+\frac16\frac34
=\frac5{12},
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
and hence \(P(Y=0\mid A=0)=7/12\). Applying (7) with \(f(w)=w\) proves that every member of \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{WBC}}^{+}\) inducing this \(P\) has target \(5/12\), despite (10).

Finally, replace all outcome potentials in either SCM of \(\mathrm{def{:}failure\text{-}artifact}\) by zero. The preceding membership verification simplifies, \(h_{0,f}=h_{1,f}=0\) with \(f\equiv0\), and \((A,Z,W,M)\), hence \(B(P)\), \(\ell(P)\), and (9)--(10), are unchanged. Equation (7) gives \(\psi=0\) for every compatible class member inducing that observed law. This is exactly the former deterministic-zero result, now obtained as the constant proxy-measurable corollary rather than the headline.
