All structural equations in \(\mathrm{def{:}failure\text{-}artifact}\) are
functions of mutually independent uniforms.  Thus consistency holds by
construction; treatment independence follows because \(U_A\) is independent of
the potential outcomes; each \(M_{a,h}\) is a function only of \(U_M\), proving
the mediator and cross-world independences; the fixed-\(m\) outcome is a function
of \((U_H,U_W,U_Y)\) and is conditionally independent of the observed mediator;
and the two proxy exclusions follow from the separate \(U_Z\) and \(U_W\).
Every hidden-state probability, every proxy success probability, every mediator
success probability, and both treatment probabilities are strictly between zero
and one.  This proves both latent transport supports and the observed denominator
supports.
