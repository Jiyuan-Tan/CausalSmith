Take \(\mathcal S^{\oplus}\) from
\(\mathrm{def{:}failure\text{-}artifact}\), retain all of its structural kernels
except the outcome kernel, and set every potential outcome \(Y_{a,h,m,w}\) equal
to zero.  Call its observed law \(P\) within this proof.  Replacing the outcome
kernel by a constant preserves all five consistency conditions, the treatment,
mediator, outcome, and cross-world independence conditions, both proxy
exclusions, and every support condition; the support conditions involve only
\((A,H,Z,W,M)\).  The zero pair \(h_0=h_1=0\) satisfies both latent bridge
equations and all observed bridge equations.  Consequently the modified SCM
belongs to \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{WBC}}^+\).

The distributions of \((A,Z,W,M)\) are unchanged from the failure artifact, so
\(B(P)\) and \(\ell(P)\) are exactly its displayed matrix and loading, while
\(b(P)=0\).  For \(v=(0,0,0,0,1,-1)^\top\), direct multiplication gives
\[
 B(P)v=0,
 \qquad
 \ell(P)^\top v=\tfrac7{12}-\tfrac5{12}=\tfrac16\ne0.
\]
The null-annihilator lemma therefore gives
\(\ell(P)\notin\operatorname{row}(B(P))\).

It remains to show that this law cannot support two different target values.
Let \(\widetilde{\mathcal S}\in\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{WBC}}^+\) be arbitrary with
\(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}^O=P\).  Then
\(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}(Y=0)=1\).  Fix \(h\) with
\(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}(H_0=h)>0\) and \(m\) with
\(P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}(M_{1,h}=m)>0\).  The latent arm-zero transport
support gives
\[
 P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}(H=h,M=m,A=0)>0.
\]
On this event, consistency identifies the observed outcome with
\(Y_{0,h,m,W_h}\), and hence
\[
 P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}(Y_{0,h,m,W_h}=1
 \mid H=h,M=m,A=0)=0.
\]
Sequential outcome independence permits removal of \(M=m\) from the
conditioning set.  Treatment consistency rewrites \(H=h\) as \(H_0=h\) in arm
zero, and sequential treatment independence then permits removal of \(A=0\).
Therefore
\[
 P_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}(Y_{0,h,m,W_h}=1\mid H_0=h)=0.
 \tag{9}
\]
The alphabets are finite.  For every \(m\) that can be taken by \(M_{1,h}\),
(9) holds; a value with zero marginal probability cannot occur.  Hence, on
\(\{H_0=h\}\),
\(Y_{0,h,M_{1,h},W_h}=0\) almost surely.  Summing over all \(h\) in the support of
\(H_0\) yields
\[
 \psi(\widetilde{\mathcal S})
 =\mathbb E_{\widetilde{\mathcal S}}
   [Y_{0,H_0,M_{1,H_0},W_{H_0}}]=0.
\]
Thus every compatible class member has the same causal value despite failed row
span, which refutes the universal converse by an explicit full-SCM witness.
