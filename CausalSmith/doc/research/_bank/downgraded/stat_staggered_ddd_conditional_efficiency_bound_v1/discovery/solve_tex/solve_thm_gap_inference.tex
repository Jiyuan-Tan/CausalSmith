For a declared comparator rule \(z\in\{\mathrm{OVS},\mathrm{const}\}\), let \(b_z(P)\) denote the law-dependent loading obtained from \(\Omega(P)\) by the corresponding Moore--Penrose KKT rule; it is not held fixed as the law varies.  Its loss relative to the published OVS bound is
\[
\Delta_{\mathrm{cmp},z}(P)
=\operatorname{Var}_P\{b_z(P)'\Psi_P(O)\}-V_{\mathrm{OVS}}(P).
\tag{G1}
\]
For pointwise inference, suppose \(X\) has fixed support \(\{x_1,\ldots,x_D\}\).  Define the observable finite vector \(U(O)\) by stacking, separately, all coordinates
\[
U_d^X=\mathbf 1\{X=x_d\},
\qquad
U^{(0)}_{d,a,s}=\mathbf 1\{X=x_d,G=a,S=s\},
\tag{G2}
\]
\[
U^{(1,u)}_{d,a,s}=U^{(0)}_{d,a,s}\Delta Y_u,
\qquad
U^{(2,u,v)}_{d,a,s}=U^{(0)}_{d,a,s}\Delta Y_u\Delta Y_v,
\tag{G3}
\]
for every support point, every retained cohort--eligibility cell \((a,s)\in\mathcal G\times\{0,1\}\), and \(u,v\in\{2,\ldots,T\}\).  Let \(d_\mu\) be the number of stacked coordinates and \(\mu(P)=E_P[U(O)]\).  The separate coordinates \(U_d^X\) reconstruct every support mass exactly.  The remaining coordinates reconstruct all conditional cell probabilities, first moments, and second cross-moments, hence reconstruct \(p,m,\pi,\theta,\Psi,\Omega,Z^{\mathrm{OVS}},K_{\mathrm{OVS}},r_{\mathrm{OVS}}\).

Let \(b^{\mathrm{ref}}\) put weight \(\omega_k\) on \((k,c_0(k))\) and zero on the other score coordinates.  For every moment vector in the overlap domain define
\[
F_z(\mu)=b_z(\mu)'\Omega(\mu)b_z(\mu)
-\left\{b^{\mathrm{ref}\prime}\Omega(\mu)b^{\mathrm{ref}}
-\sum_{d=1}^D P_\mu(X=x_d)
r_{\mathrm{OVS},\mu}(x_d)'K_{\mathrm{OVS},\mu}(x_d)^\dagger
r_{\mathrm{OVS},\mu}(x_d)\right\}.
\tag{G4}
\]
Let \(F_z^\sharp\) denote the locally selected-rank branch of \(F_z\).  At the true moment define
\[
\xi_z(O)=DF_z^\sharp\{\mu(P)\}'[U(O)-\mu(P)].
\tag{G5}
\]
Every inverse in (G4), including an indefinite comparator KKT inverse, is operationally replaced by the spectral inverse that retains exactly eigenvalues satisfying \(|\lambda|>\tau_n\).  Denote the resulting map by \(F_{z,\tau_n}\).  With \(\widehat\mu=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nU(O_i)\), define
\[
\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{cmp}}=F_{z,\tau_n}(\widehat\mu).
\tag{G6}
\]
Let \(\widehat d_z\) be the analytic derivative of the selected-rank branch of \(F_z\) at \(\widehat\mu\), put
\[
\widehat\xi_{z,i}=\widehat d_z'\{U(O_i)-\widehat\mu\},
\qquad
\widehat V_{\Delta,\mathrm{cmp}}=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\widehat\xi_{z,i}^2,
\tag{G7}
\]
and set
\[
T_{\Delta,\mathrm{cmp}}
=\frac{\sqrt n\{\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{cmp}}-\Delta_{\mathrm{cmp},z}(P)\}}
{\sqrt{\widehat V_{\Delta,\mathrm{cmp}}}}.
\tag{G8}
\]
