%%% FIELD rank-handle-construction
For the published normal system, form cross-fitted spectral projectors of \(\widehat K_{\mathrm{OVS}}^{-\ell}(x)\) and regularized candidate coefficients from \(\widehat r_{\mathrm{OVS}}^{-\ell}(x)\) over a threshold grid whose deterministic scale is \(\lambda_n\).  Compare the candidate projections across adjacent retained eigenspaces by a Lepski-type stability rule and calibrate critical values jointly over the retained-rank candidates.  This handle acts on the published canonical-projection coefficient
\[
\alpha_{\mathrm{OVS},P}(x)=K_{\mathrm{OVS},P}(x)^\dagger r_{\mathrm{OVS},P}(x),
\]
not on the aggregate loading \(b^\star\) or the aggregate contrast operator \(H\).  A uniform theorem based on the handle must specify a class of local law sequences \((P_n)\), quantitative envelopes for the positive eigenvalues of \(K_{\mathrm{OVS},P_n}(X)\) and for all nuisance and conditional-moment errors relative to \(\lambda_n\), and a nonvacuity criterion stated through interval length or local power.  At every fixed finite-support law with separated positive rank, the selected center and interval are required to be asymptotically equivalent to the existing \(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and its Wald interval.  Rank changes of the comparator Moore--Penrose KKT systems are outside this handle and belong to a separate efficiency-gap inference extension.
%%% FIELD rank-oeq-statement
Does there exist a confidence procedure based on the rank-adaptive spectral handle for \(K_{\mathrm{OVS},P}(X)\) over an explicitly specified class of local law sequences \((P_n)\) such that: (i) its coverage for \(\tau_\omega(P_n)\) is uniformly asymptotically at least \(1-\alpha\); (ii) a declared expected-length, diameter, or local-power criterion rules out the vacuous interval \(\mathbb R\); (iii) the class gives quantitative envelopes for the smallest positive eigenvalue of \(K_{\mathrm{OVS},P_n}(X)\), for covariate sets of positive \(P_{n,X}\)-probability on which rank changes, and for nuisance and conditional-moment errors relative to \(\lambda_n\); and (iv) at every fixed finite-support law whose positive spectrum is separated, the procedure is asymptotically equivalent to \(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}\) and its existing Wald interval?  This question concerns the published canonical-projection operator only; rank changes in the comparator KKT systems are a separate efficiency-gap inference problem.
%%% FIELD rank-open-partial
Fix a conditional-mean interior law induced by the global OVS model.  Suppose that \(X\) has fixed finite support with every support mass bounded below, all first stages are training-cell averages, sampling is independent and identically distributed with fixed \(T\), retained-cell overlap and the unconditional and global conditional fourth-moment envelopes hold, cross-fitting uses a fixed number of folds, and \(V_{\mathrm{OVS}}>0\).  Because the support of \(X\) is finite, at this fixed law the positive eigenvalues of \(K_{\mathrm{OVS},P}(x)\), when present, have a positive minimum over support points.  Proposition~\ref{prop:ovs-normal-finite-support-rates} then gives consistent rank recovery and root-\(n\) estimation of \(K_{\mathrm{OVS},P}\) and \(r_{\mathrm{OVS},P}\) on the recovered-rank event.  With the existing deterministic threshold \(\tau_n=n^{-a}\), \(0<a<1/4\), Theorem~\ref{thm:crossfit-attainment} gives the pointwise expansion and Wald limit for \(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}\).  The proposed rank-adaptive procedure is required to agree asymptotically with that estimator and interval at every such fixed law; no uniform statement is obtained as the positive spectrum approaches zero or the rank changes.
