Order the eigenvalues of the symmetric matrix \(A\) by decreasing absolute value, breaking equal-absolute-value ties by decreasing signed value.  Within every remaining repeated eigenspace, choose the orthonormal basis obtained by applying Gram--Schmidt to the nonzero vectors in the ordered list of projections of \(e_1,\ldots,e_n\) onto that eigenspace.  Let \(\widehat\Psi_r(A)\) contain the first \(r\) resulting orthonormal eigenvectors and define
\[
 \widehat\Pi_r(A)=\widehat\Psi_r(A)\widehat\Psi_r(A)^\top.
\]
This deterministic tie rule makes \(A\mapsto\widehat\Pi_r(A)\) a fixed Borel-measurable sample map.
