Assume \(L>0\), and write
\[
 c_{n,d}=n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4},
 \qquad
 g(h)=Lh^2+c_{n,d}h^{-3/2},
 \qquad
 \ell=d^{-1},
 \qquad
 u=d^{-1/2}.
\tag{64}
\]
By \({\rm ass:log\text{-}degree}\), \(d\to\infty\), so at every sufficiently large index \(d\geq4\) and \({\rm def:bandwidth\text{-}set}\) gives \(\mathcal H_{n,d}=[\ell,u]\).  From \({\rm def:optimal\text{-}bandwidth}\),
\[
 h_0^7=\frac{L^{-2}}{n\sqrt d}.
\tag{65}
\]
Since \(c_{n,d}^2=(n\sqrt d)^{-1}\), positive square roots in (65) give
\[
 Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}.
\tag{66}
\]

If \(h_0\leq\ell\), then \(g(h)\geq L\ell^2\) for every \(h\in[\ell,u]\), while monotonicity and (66) give
\[
 c_{n,d}\ell^{-3/2}
 \leq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 =Lh_0^2\leq L\ell^2.
\tag{67}
\]
Evaluation at \(\ell\) proves
\[
 Ld^{-2}\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\leq2Ld^{-2}.
\tag{68}
\]
If \(\ell<h_0<u\), then for \(h\geq h_0\) the first term of \(g(h)\) is at least \(Lh_0^2\), and for \(h\leq h_0\) its second term is at least \(c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\).  Evaluation at \(h_0\) gives
\[
 Lh_0^2\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\leq2Lh_0^2,
 \qquad
 Lh_0^2=L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}.
\tag{69}
\]
If \(h_0\geq u\), then
\[
 g(h)\geq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}=\sqrt{d/n}
\tag{70}
\]
for every admissible \(h\), while (66) and \(u\leq h_0\) imply \(Lu^2\leq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}\).  Evaluation at \(u\) yields
\[
 \sqrt{d/n}\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\leq2\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{71}
\]
By \({\rm def:frontier\text{-}envelope}\),
\[
 \mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 =\sqrt{d/n}+\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h).
\tag{72}
\]
Adding \(\sqrt{d/n}\) to (68)--(71) proves the three asserted analytic equivalences.

At every index with nonempty bandwidth set, \({\rm thm:honest\text{-}upper}\) supplies a measurable estimator with worst-case absolute risk at most \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\), and an interval meeting the uniform coverage constraint with worst-case expected length at most \(2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\).  Inserting these procedures into the infima in \({\rm def:minimax\text{-}criteria}\) gives
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{73}
\]
Along every sequence satisfying the hypotheses of \({\rm thm:unrestricted\text{-}supported\text{-}converse}\), that theorem, now proved while retaining the independent uniform latent types and the original admissible kernel, gives a constant \(c>0\) such that
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
\tag{74}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Finally, \({\rm thm:conditional\text{-}isolate\text{-}wall}\) proves pointwise that every graph-only conditional-bias radius over the same class satisfies
\[
 b(A)\geq\frac Bn\sum_i\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}.
\tag{75}
\]
Equations (68)--(75) prove the analytic phases and the honest bracket
\[
 c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 \leq R^*_{n,d,L}\leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 \leq\Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{76}
\]
Neither \({\rm def:radii}\) nor \({\rm thm:honest\text{-}upper}\) supplies \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\lesssim\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\).  Therefore the three phases are a tuning geometry and minimax lower benchmark, not an achieved honest expected-length frontier, exactly as asserted.
