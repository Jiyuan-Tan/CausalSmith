The supported-envelope optimization is completely determined.  Put
\[
 c_{n,d}=n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4},\qquad
 g(h)=Lh^2+c_{n,d}h^{-3/2}.
\]
For \(L>0\), the definition of \(h_0\) is equivalent to
\[
 Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}.
\]
Let \(\ell=d^{-1}\) and \(u=d^{-1/2}\).  If \(h_0\leq\ell\), then
\[
 g(\ell)\leq2L\ell^2,
 \qquad
 g(h)\geq Lh^2\geq L\ell^2\quad(h\in[\ell,u]),
\]
so \(\inf g\asymp Ld^{-2}\).  If \(\ell<h_0<u\), then \(g(h_0)=2Lh_0^2\), while for every \(h\), either \(h\geq h_0\) and \(Lh^2\geq Lh_0^2\), or \(h\leq h_0\) and \(c_{n,d}h^{-3/2}\geq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\).  Hence
\[
 \inf g\asymp Lh_0^2=L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}.
\]
If \(h_0\geq u\), then
\[
 c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}=\sqrt{d/n},\qquad
 Lu^2\leq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2},
\]
and monotonicity of the stochastic term on \([\ell,u]\) yields \(\inf g\asymp\sqrt{d/n}\).  Adding the graph floor in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) proves all three displayed supported-unit phases.
