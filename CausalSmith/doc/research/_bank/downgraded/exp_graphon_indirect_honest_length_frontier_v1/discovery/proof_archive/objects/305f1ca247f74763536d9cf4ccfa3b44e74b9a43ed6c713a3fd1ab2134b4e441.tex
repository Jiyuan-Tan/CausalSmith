Assume \(L>0\) and write
\[
 c_{n,d}=n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4},\qquad
 g(h)=Lh^2+c_{n,d}h^{-3/2},\qquad
 \ell=d^{-1},\qquad u=d^{-1/2}.
\tag{29}
\]
For all sufficiently large indices in the declared logarithmic-degree sequence, \(d\ge4\), and \({\rm def:bandwidth-set}\) gives \(\mathcal H_{n,d}=[\ell,u]\).  By \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\),
\[
 h_0^7=\frac{L^{-2}}{n\sqrt d}.
\]
Since \(c_{n,d}^2=(n\sqrt d)^{-1}\), positive square roots give
\[
 Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}.
\tag{30}
\]

If \(h_0\le\ell\), then \(g(h)\ge L\ell^2\) for every \(h\in[\ell,u]\), while monotonicity and (30) give
\[
 c_{n,d}\ell^{-3/2}\le c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\le L\ell^2.
\]
Evaluation at \(\ell\) proves
\[
 Ld^{-2}\le\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\le2Ld^{-2}.
\tag{31}
\]
If \(\ell<h_0<u\), then for \(h\ge h_0\) the first term of \(g(h)\) is at least \(Lh_0^2\), and for \(h\le h_0\) its second term is at least \(c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\).  Evaluation at \(h_0\) gives
\[
 Lh_0^2\le\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\le2Lh_0^2,
 \qquad
 Lh_0^2=L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}.
\tag{32}
\]
If \(h_0\ge u\), then
\[
 g(h)\ge c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}=\sqrt{d/n}
\]
for every admissible \(h\), while (30) and \(u\le h_0\) imply \(Lu^2\le c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}\).  Evaluation at \(u\) yields
\[
 \sqrt{d/n}\le\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\le2\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{33}
\]
By \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\), \(\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}=\sqrt{d/n}+\inf_hg(h)\).  Adding \(\sqrt{d/n}\) to (31)--(33) proves the three analytic equivalences.

At every index with nonempty bandwidth set, \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\) supplies one measurable estimator with worst-case absolute risk at most \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\), and one interval meeting the uniform coverage constraint with worst-case expected length at most \(2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\).  Inserting these procedures into the two infima in \({\rm def:minimax-criteria}\) gives
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\le\mathfrak u_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \le2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{34}
\]
Along every sequence satisfying the hypotheses of \({\rm thm:unrestricted-supported-converse}\), that theorem gives a constant \(c>0\), independent of \(n\) and \(d\), for which
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\ge c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)\ge c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
\tag{35}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Finally, \({\rm thm:conditional-isolate-wall}\) proves pointwise that every graph-only conditional-bias radius over the same class satisfies
\[
 b(A)\ge\frac Bn\sum_i\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}.
\tag{36}
\]
Equations (31)--(36) prove the analytic phases and the honest two-sided bracket
\[
 c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 \le R^*_{n,d,L}\le\mathfrak u_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 \le\Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \le2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\]
Neither the corrected variance definition nor \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\) supplies \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\lesssim\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\).  Thus the final scope sentence follows without labeling the bracket as matched.
