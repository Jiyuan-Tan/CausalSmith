Fix \(i\in\mathcal V_n\) with \(N_i=N\geq1\), fix \(h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}\), and condition on \(A\) and the preassignment response functions.  By \({\rm ass:bernoulli-design}\),
\[
 M_i\mid A\sim{\rm Binomial}(N,1/2),\qquad V_i=N^{-1}M_i-1/2.
\]
If \(N\) is even, the nearest nonzero lattice points are \(\pm N^{-1}\); if \(N\) is odd, they are \(\pm(2N)^{-1}\).  Each has positive binomial mass.  Thus \({\rm def:lattice-radius}\) implies
\[
 P\{0<|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\mid A\}>0,
\]
and \({\rm def:discrete-score}\) gives \(\mu_{2,i}(h)>0\).

The involution \(M_i\mapsto N-M_i\) preserves binomial mass and sends \(V_i\) to \(-V_i\).  The truncation indicator is even and the score is odd.  Therefore
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}\mid A]=0,
\]
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i\mid A]
 =\frac{\mathbb E_Z[V_i^2\mathbf1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]}{\mu_{2,i}(h)}=1,
\]
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i^2\mid A]
 =\frac{\mathbb E_Z[V_i^3\mathbf1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]}{\mu_{2,i}(h)}=0,
\]
and
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}^2\mid A]
 =\frac{\mathbb E_Z[V_i^2\mathbf1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]}{\mu_{2,i}(h)^2}
 =\mu_{2,i}(h)^{-1}.
\]

Fix \(a\in\{0,1\}\).  Taylor's formula at \(1/2\) and \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\) give
\[
 f_i(a,1/2+v)=f_i(a,1/2)+\partial_xf_i(a,1/2)v
 +\frac12\partial_x^2f_i(a,1/2)v^2+R_i(a,v),
 \qquad |R_i(a,v)|\leq \frac L6|v|^3.
\]
Multiplication by \(q_{i,h}\), conditional expectation, and the three preceding moments yield
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}f_i(a,1/2+V_i)\mid A]-\partial_xf_i(a,1/2)
 =\mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}R_i(a,V_i)\mid A].
\]
On the support of the score, \(|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\), whence
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \left|\mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}R_i(a,V_i)\mid A]\right|
 &\leq \frac{L}{6\mu_{2,i}(h)}
 \mathbb E_Z[|V_i|^4\mathbf1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]\\
 &\leq \frac{Lh_i(h)^2}{6\mu_{2,i}(h)}
 \mathbb E_Z[V_i^2\mathbf1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]
 =\frac L6h_i(h)^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every division is justified by the strict positivity already proved.
