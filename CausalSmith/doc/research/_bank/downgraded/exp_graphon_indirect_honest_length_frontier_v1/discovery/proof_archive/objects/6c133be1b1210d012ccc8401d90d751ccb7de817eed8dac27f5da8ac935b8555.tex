Let \(D=(A,Z,(Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}})_{i\in\mathcal V_n})\). Define \(R^*_{n,d,L}=\inf_{\widetilde\theta}\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}\mathbb E_P|\widetilde\theta-\theta_n|\), where the infimum ranges over all real-valued measurable estimators \(\widetilde\theta=\widetilde\theta(D)\). Define \(\Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)=\inf_{\mathcal C}\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}\mathbb E_P[u(D)-\ell(D)]\), where the infimum ranges over data-measurable closed intervals \(\mathcal C(D)=[\ell(D),u(D)]\) with finite measurable endpoints \(\ell(D)\leq u(D)\) and satisfying \(\inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal C(D)\}\geq1-\alpha\). For every such interval, \(\operatorname{length}\{\mathcal C(D)\}=u(D)-\ell(D)\).
