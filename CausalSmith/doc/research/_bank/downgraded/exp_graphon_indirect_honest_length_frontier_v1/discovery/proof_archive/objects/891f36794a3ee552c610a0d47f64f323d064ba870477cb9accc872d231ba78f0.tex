Along every triangular-array sequence for which, at every sufficiently large index, \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) contains at least one law having \((U_i)_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}{\rm Unif}[0,1]\), \(C_{\log}\log n\leq d=o(n)\), and \(B,L,r,\kappa,\underline\lambda,b_\psi,\alpha,C_{\log}\) are fixed, there is a constant \(c>0\), independent of \(n\) and \(d\), such that, for every sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}.
\]
The two minimax criteria still range over the whole class \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\); the uniform-latent member is only a least-favourable witness.  No equal-block compatibility inequalities are required.  Keeping the graphon kernel \(W\) of any such member fixed gives
\[
 N_i\mid U_i=u\sim {\rm Binomial}\!\left(n-1,\frac dn\int_0^1W(u,v)\,dv\right)
\]
for almost every \(u\), and the row lower bound and kernel upper bound make its conditional mean uniformly comparable to \(d\).  On this graph submodel, explicit linear, local \(C^3\)-bump, and degree-lattice response pairs give, respectively, the graph, interior, and lattice terms in the displayed frontier for both minimax absolute risk and minimax honest expected length.
