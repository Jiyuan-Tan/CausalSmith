Assume \(L>0\), and put
\[
 c_{n,d}=n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4},\qquad
 g(h)=Lh^2+c_{n,d}h^{-3/2},\qquad
 \ell=d^{-1},\qquad u=d^{-1/2}.
\tag{1}
\]
By \({\rm ass:log\text{-}degree}\), \(d\to\infty\) along the triangular array.  Thus \(d\geq4\) eventually, and \({\rm def:bandwidth\text{-}set}\) gives \(\mathcal H_{n,d}=[\ell,u]\).  From \({\rm def:optimal\text{-}bandwidth}\),
\[
 h_0^7=\frac{L^{-2}}{n\sqrt d}.
\tag{2}
\]
Since \(c_{n,d}^2=(n\sqrt d)^{-1}\), positivity and (2) imply
\[
 Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}.
\tag{3}
\]
If \(h_0\leq\ell\), then \(g(h)\geq L\ell^2\) for every admissible \(h\), whereas
\[
 c_{n,d}\ell^{-3/2}
 \leq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 =Lh_0^2\leq L\ell^2.
\]
Evaluation at \(\ell\) gives
\[
 Ld^{-2}\leq\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)\leq2Ld^{-2}.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(\ell<h_0<u\), then for \(h\geq h_0\), \(Lh^2\geq Lh_0^2\), while for \(h\leq h_0\), \(c_{n,d}h^{-3/2}\geq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\).  Evaluation at \(h_0\) gives
\[
 Lh_0^2\leq\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)\leq2Lh_0^2,
 \qquad
 Lh_0^2=L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}.
\tag{5}
\]
If \(h_0\geq u\), then every admissible \(h\) satisfies \(g(h)\geq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}=\sqrt{d/n}\), while
\[
 Lu^2\leq Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 \leq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}.
\]
Evaluation at \(u\) proves
\[
 \sqrt{d/n}\leq\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)
 \leq2\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (4)--(6) with
\[
 \mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 =\sqrt{d/n}+\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)
\tag{7}
\]
proves all three asserted equivalences, with constants independent of \(n,d\).

Fix an index at which both \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\) and \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) are nonempty.  By \({\rm thm:honest\text{-}upper}\), the observed-data estimator \(\widehat\theta_{\widehat h(A)}\) satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{\widehat h(A)}-\theta_n|
 \leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{8}
\]
It is an admissible competitor in \({\rm def:minimax\text{-}criteria}\), hence
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{9}
\]
The same theorem states that the endpoint-clipped interval \(\mathcal C_{\widehat h(A)}\) is a data-measurable nonempty closed interval, is uniformly honest after integration, and obeys
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_{\widehat h(A)})]
 \leq2\sqrt{2\log(2/\alpha)}\,\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{10}
\]
It is therefore an admissible interval competitor in \({\rm def:minimax\text{-}criteria}\), so
\[
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \leq2\sqrt{2\log(2/\alpha)}\,\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{11}
\]

Along every sequence covered by \({\rm thm:unrestricted\text{-}supported\text{-}converse}\), that theorem supplies a constant \(c>0\), independent of \(n,d\), such that eventually
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}.
\tag{12}
\]
Under the two compatibility inequalities in \({\rm thm:minimax\text{-}lower}\), its explicit block response pairs are members of the maintained class, so their lower bounds transfer upward to that class.  The unrestricted theorem proves (12) directly without the block compatibility conditions.  By the nonnesting qualification in \({\rm def:model\text{-}class}\), and because \(\theta_n\) differs from the finite indirect-effect target in LiWager2022RandomGraph, neither argument transfers automatically to that source regime.

Finally, \({\rm thm:conditional\text{-}isolate\text{-}wall}\) gives, on every realized graph,
\[
 b(A)\geq\frac Bn\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}
\tag{13}
\]
for every uniformly valid graph-only conditional-bias certificate of the stated kind.  Equations (9), (11), and (12) are the conservative lower--upper bracket.  Its lower side is \(\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\), whereas its upper side is \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\).  No declared result bounds the latter by the former, so (4)--(7) describe tuning geometry and a lower benchmark, not a sharp minimax rate or an achieved honest expected-length frontier.
