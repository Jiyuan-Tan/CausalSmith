Fix \(i\in\mathcal V_n\) with \(N_i=N\geq 1\), fix \(h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}\), and condition on \(A\) and on the preassignment response functions.  By \({\rm ass:bernoulli-design}\),
\[
 M_i\mid A\sim {\rm Binomial}(N,1/2),\qquad
 V_i=N^{-1}M_i-1/2.
\]
If \(N\) is even, the two lattice points adjacent to zero are \(\pm N^{-1}\); if \(N\) is odd, the two points closest to zero are \(\pm(2N)^{-1}\).  Their binomial probabilities are strictly positive.  Hence \({\rm def:lattice-radius}\) implies that the event
\[
 \{0<|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}
\]
has strictly positive conditional probability.  Consequently, by \({\rm def:discrete-score}\),
\[
 \mu_{2,i}(h)=\mathbb E_Z\!left[V_i^2\mathbf 1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A\right]>0,
\]
so every division in the definition of \(q_{i,h}\) is valid.

The map \(M_i\mapsto N-M_i\) preserves the \({\rm Binomial}(N,1/2)\) mass and maps \(V_i\) to \(-V_i\).  Thus the conditional law of \(V_i\) is symmetric.  Since the truncation indicator is even and \(q_{i,h}\) is odd, this symmetry and \({\rm def:discrete-score}\) give, equation by equation,
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}\mid A]=0,
\]
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i\mid A]
 =\frac{\mathbb E_Z[V_i^2\mathbf 1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]}{\mu_{2,i}(h)}=1,
\]
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i^2\mid A]
 =\frac{\mathbb E_Z[V_i^3\mathbf 1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]}{\mu_{2,i}(h)}=0,
\]
and
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}^2\mid A]
 =\frac{\mathbb E_Z[V_i^2\mathbf 1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]}{\mu_{2,i}(h)^2}
 =\mu_{2,i}(h)^{-1}.
\]

It remains to establish the approximation bound.  Fix \(a\in\{0,1\}\).  Taylor's formula at \(1/2\), together with \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\), gives for every \(v\in[-1/2,1/2]\)
\[
 f_i(a,1/2+v)
 =f_i(a,1/2)+\partial_xf_i(a,1/2)v
 +\frac12\partial_x^2f_i(a,1/2)v^2+R_i(a,v),
 \qquad |R_i(a,v)|\leq \frac L6|v|^3.
\]
Multiplying by \(q_{i,h}\), taking conditional expectation, and applying the three moment equations above yields
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}f_i(a,1/2+V_i)\mid A]-\partial_xf_i(a,1/2)
 =\mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}R_i(a,V_i)\mid A].
\]
On the support of \(q_{i,h}\), \(|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\).  Therefore
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \left|\mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}R_i(a,V_i)\mid A]\right|
 &\leq \frac{L}{6\mu_{2,i}(h)}
 \mathbb E_Z\!\left[|V_i|^4\mathbf 1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A\right]\\
 &\leq \frac{Lh_i(h)^2}{6\mu_{2,i}(h)}
 \mathbb E_Z\!\left[V_i^2\mathbf 1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A\right]\\
 &=\frac L6h_i(h)^2.
 \end{aligned}
\]
This proves every asserted moment and the stated unitwise bias bound.
