Conditionally on \(A\), put \(X=Z-\tfrac12\mathbf1_n\), \(P_A=\widehat\Pi_r(A)\), and \(C_A=P_AA\).  For every \(h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}\), define
\[
 \widetilde\gamma_{i,h}=q_{i,h}-4(C_AX)_i.
\]
Thus only the bandwidth-free first Hoeffding projection is spectrally balanced.  Define the graph-observable certificates
\[
 \widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{pc},h}
 =\frac{2B}{n}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}|(C_A)_{ii}|
 +\frac{B}{n}\sum_{i:N_i>0}\frac1{N_i}
       \sum_{j:A_{ij}=1}|(C_A)_{ij}|
\]
and
\[
 \widehat V_{\mathrm{pc},h}
 =\frac{B^2}{n}\sum_{i:N_i>0}\mu_{2,i}(h)^{-1}.
\]
The latter is the conservative conditional-Hoeffding certificate obtained by retaining the exact third-and-higher score remainder and applying a final Euclidean Cauchy--Schwarz bound; it is not asserted to have order \(d/n\).
