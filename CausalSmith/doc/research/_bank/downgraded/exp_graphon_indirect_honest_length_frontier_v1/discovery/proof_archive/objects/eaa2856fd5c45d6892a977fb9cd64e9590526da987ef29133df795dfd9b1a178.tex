Let \(\mathcal G=\sigma(A,(f_i)_{i\in\mathcal V_n})\).  The theorem replacing \({\rm oeq:uniform-feasible-pc}\) gives
\[
 |\mathbb E_P(\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n\mid\mathcal G)|\leq\overline b_h(A),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}_P(\widehat\theta_h\mid\mathcal G)\leq\overline s_h(A)^2.
\]
Conditional Cauchy--Schwarz therefore gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P[|\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n|\mid\mathcal G]
 \leq\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A).
\]
At \(h=h^\dagger\), take expectations and then the supremum over \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\).  By \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\), the right side is exactly \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\), proving the risk assertion.

Conditional Chebyshev gives
\[
 P\!\left(
 |\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n|>
 \overline b_h(A)+\frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal G\right)\leq\alpha.
\]
By \({\rm ass:response-envelope}\) and \({\rm def:target}\), \(|\theta_n|\leq B\).  Thus intersecting the untruncated interval with \([-B,B]\) cannot remove \(\theta_n\).  Integrating the conditional inequality proves coverage at least \(1-\alpha\).  Finally,
\[
 \operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_h)
 \leq2\left\{\overline b_h(A)+\frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}\right\}.
\]
At \(h=h^\dagger\), its worst-case expectation is at most
\(2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\).  The pointwise untruncated half-length is precisely the displayed certificate, and the intersection can only shorten it.
