Assume \(L>0\), and write
\[
 c_{n,d}=n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4},
 \qquad g(h)=Lh^2+c_{n,d}h^{-3/2},
 \qquad \ell=d^{-1},
 \qquad u=d^{-1/2}.
\tag{1}
\]
By \({\rm ass:log-degree}\), \(d\to\infty\), so at every sufficiently large index \(d\geq4\) and \({\rm def:bandwidth-set}\) gives \(\mathcal H_{n,d}=[\ell,u]\).  From \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\),
\[
 h_0^7=\frac{L^{-2}}{n\sqrt d}.
\tag{2}
\]
Because \(c_{n,d}^2=(n\sqrt d)^{-1}\), taking positive square roots in (2) gives
\[
 Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}.
\tag{3}
\]

If \(h_0\leq\ell\), then \(g(h)\geq L\ell^2\) for every \(h\in[\ell,u]\), while monotonicity and (3) imply
\[
 c_{n,d}\ell^{-3/2}\leq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 =Lh_0^2\leq L\ell^2.
\]
Evaluation at \(\ell\) therefore proves
\[
 Ld^{-2}\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\leq2Ld^{-2}.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(\ell<h_0<u\), then for \(h\geq h_0\) the first term of \(g(h)\) is at least \(Lh_0^2\), whereas for \(h\leq h_0\) the second is at least \(c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\).  Evaluation at \(h_0\) gives
\[
 Lh_0^2\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\leq2Lh_0^2,
 \qquad Lh_0^2=L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}.
\tag{5}
\]
If \(h_0\geq u\), then \(g(h)\geq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}=\sqrt{d/n}\) for all admissible \(h\).  Moreover, (3) and \(u\leq h_0\) imply \(Lu^2\leq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}\).  Evaluation at \(u\) yields
\[
 \sqrt{d/n}\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)\leq2\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{6}
\]
Adding the graph term \(\sqrt{d/n}\) in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) to (4)--(6) proves the three asserted analytic equivalences.

At every index with nonempty bandwidth set, \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\) supplies a measurable estimator with worst-case absolute risk at most \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\), and an interval satisfying uniform coverage with worst-case expected length at most \(2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\).  Inserting those procedures into \({\rm def:minimax-criteria}\) gives
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{7}
\]
Along every sequence satisfying \({\rm thm:unrestricted-supported-converse}\), that theorem gives a fixed \(c>0\) such that
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
\tag{8}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Finally, \({\rm thm:conditional-isolate-wall}\) proves pointwise that every graph-only conditional-bias radius over the repaired class satisfies
\[
 b(A)\geq\frac Bn\sum_i\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}.
\tag{9}
\]
Equations (4)--(9) prove the advertised lower--upper bracket.  Neither \({\rm def:radii}\) nor \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\) bounds \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\) by a constant multiple of \(\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\); therefore the three phases are only the tuning geometry and supported minimax lower benchmark, exactly as claimed.
