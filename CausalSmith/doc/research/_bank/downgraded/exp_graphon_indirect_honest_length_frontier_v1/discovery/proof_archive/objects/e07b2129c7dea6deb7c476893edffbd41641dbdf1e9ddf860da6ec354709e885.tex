Fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), and put
\[
 \mathcal G=\sigma\!\left(A,(f_i)_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\right),
 \qquad
 E_h=\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n,
 \qquad
 m_h=\mathbb E_P(E_h\mid\mathcal G).
\]
The bandwidth \(h^\dagger\) in \({\rm def:exact-optimal-bandwidth}\) is fixed by the model class and is therefore not a sample-dependent choice.  By \({\rm thm:explicit-pc-conditional-certificate}\), for every \(h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}\),
\[
 |m_h|\leq\overline b_h(A),
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P\!\left[(E_h-m_h)^2\mid\mathcal G\right]
 =\operatorname{Var}_P(E_h\mid\mathcal G)
 \leq\overline s_h(A)^2.
\tag{1}
\]
All quantities on the right of (1) are nonnegative by \({\rm def:radii}\).  Conditional Cauchy--Schwarz and (1) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_P(|E_h|\mid\mathcal G)
 &\leq |m_h|+\mathbb E_P(|E_h-m_h|\mid\mathcal G)\\
 &\leq |m_h|+
 \left\{\mathbb E_P[(E_h-m_h)^2\mid\mathcal G]\right\}^{1/2}\\
 &\leq\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A).
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
Taking expectations in (2), setting \(h=h^\dagger\), and then taking the supremum over \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), the definition of \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\) in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) yields
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}-\theta_n|
 \leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus the first asserted \(\lesssim\) bound holds, in fact with constant one.

For coverage, if \(\overline s_h(A)>0\), conditional Chebyshev and (1) imply
\[
 P\!\left(
 |E_h-m_h|>\frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal G
 \right)\leq\alpha.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(\overline s_h(A)=0\), (1) gives \(E_h=m_h\) almost surely conditionally on \(\mathcal G\), so (4) remains true with left side zero.  The triangle inequality and \(|m_h|\leq\overline b_h(A)\) therefore give
\[
 P\!\left(
 |\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n|>
 \overline b_h(A)+\frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal G
 \right)\leq\alpha.
\tag{5}
\]
Membership of \(P\) in \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), through the response-envelope member property of \({\rm def:model-class}\), gives \(|\partial_x f_i(a,1/2)|\leq B\).  Hence \({\rm def:target}\) gives
\[
 |\theta_n|
 \leq\frac1{2n}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}
 \left\{|\partial_x f_i(1,1/2)|+|\partial_x f_i(0,1/2)|\right\}
 \leq B.
\tag{6}
\]
Consequently intersecting the raw interval in (5) with \([-B,B]\) cannot remove \(\theta_n\).  Integrating (5), taking \(h=h^\dagger\), and then taking the infimum over the class proves
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal C_{h^\dagger}\}\geq1-\alpha.
\tag{7}
\]

Intersection with \([-B,B]\) can only shorten an interval.  Therefore \({\rm def:honest-interval}\) gives, pointwise in the realized graph and outcomes,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_h)
 &\leq2\left\{\overline b_h(A)+
          \frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}\right\}\\
 &\leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}
       \{\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
At \(h=h^\dagger\), expectation, supremum over \(P\), and \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) now yield
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_{h^\dagger})]
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{9}
\]
Because \(\alpha\) is fixed by the theorem's setup, (9) is the asserted \(\lesssim\) claim.  Finally, before the harmless intersection with \([-B,B]\), \({\rm def:honest-interval}\) shows that the half-length is exactly
\(\overline b_{h^\dagger}(A)+\overline s_{h^\dagger}(A)/\sqrt\alpha\).  This is the stated pointwise certificate, and its definitions in \({\rm def:radii}\) include every realized degree and the isolate term.
