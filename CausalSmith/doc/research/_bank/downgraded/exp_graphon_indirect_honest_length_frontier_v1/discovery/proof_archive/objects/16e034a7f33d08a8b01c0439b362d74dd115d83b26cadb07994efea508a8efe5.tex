There is a constant \(c>0\) such that \(R^*_{n,d,L}\ge c\{\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}+\omega^{\mathrm{tail}}_{n,d,L}\}\) and \(\Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)\ge c\{\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}+\omega^{\mathrm{tail}}_{n,d,L}\}\) for all sufficiently large \(n\). Compatible members of the same class furnish the degree-lattice term, the interior smooth-bump term, the nonlinear honest-length term, the graph floor, and the tail modulus in \({\rm lem:tail-testing-modulus}\). No lower-bound term of order \(B\pi_{0,n}\) is asserted.
