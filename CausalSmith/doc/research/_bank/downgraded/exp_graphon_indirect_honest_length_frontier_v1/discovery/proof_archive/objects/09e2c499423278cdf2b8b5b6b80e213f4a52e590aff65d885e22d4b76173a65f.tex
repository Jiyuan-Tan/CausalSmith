Put
\[
 c_{n,d}=n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4},
 \qquad
 g(h)=Lh^2+c_{n,d}h^{-3/2},
 \qquad
 \ell=d^{-1},
 \qquad
 u=d^{-1/2}.
\]
For \(L>0\), \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\) implies
\[
 Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}.
\tag{23}
\]
If \(h_0\leq\ell\), then for every \(h\in[\ell,u]\), \(g(h)\geq Lh^2\geq L\ell^2\).  Moreover, (23), \(h_0\leq\ell\), and monotonicity of the two summands give
\[
 c_{n,d}\ell^{-3/2}
 \leq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 =Lh_0^2
 \leq L\ell^2.
\]
Hence
\[
 L\ell^2\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)
 \leq g(\ell)\leq2L\ell^2,
\tag{24}
\]
so the infimum is comparable to \(Ld^{-2}\).

If \(\ell<h_0<u\), then (23) yields \(g(h_0)=2Lh_0^2\).  For any \(h\), either \(h\geq h_0\), in which case \(Lh^2\geq Lh_0^2\), or \(h\leq h_0\), in which case
\(c_{n,d}h^{-3/2}\geq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\).  Therefore
\[
 Lh_0^2\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)
 \leq2Lh_0^2.
\tag{25}
\]
Direct substitution of \(h_0=(L^{-2}/(n\sqrt d))^{1/7}\) gives
\[
 Lh_0^2=L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}.
\tag{26}
\]

If \(h_0\geq u\), then for all \(h\leq u\), monotonicity gives \(c_{n,d}h^{-3/2}\geq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}\).  At \(u\), (23) and \(u\leq h_0\) give
\[
 Lu^2\leq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2},
 \qquad
 c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}=\sqrt{d/n}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \sqrt{d/n}\leq\inf_{h\in[\ell,u]}g(h)
 \leq g(u)\leq2\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{27}
\]
Adding the graph term \(\sqrt{d/n}\) in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) to (24)--(27) proves the three displayed phase relations.

For the decision-theoretic upper bounds, the conditional Cauchy--Schwarz argument in \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\) supplies the sharper explicit inequality
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}-\theta_n|
 \leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\]
Since \(R^*_{n,d,L}\) is the infimum over all such estimators by \({\rm def:minimax-criteria}\),
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{28}
\]
Likewise, \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\) shows that \(\mathcal C_{h^\dagger}\) is admissible for the confidence-set infimum and establishes the explicit expected-length inequality
\[
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{29}
\]
Finally, \({\rm thm:conditional-isolate-wall}\) proves that every graph-only conditional-bias certificate over the unchanged class is pointwise at least
\[
 \frac Bn\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}.
\tag{30}
\]
Equations (28)--(30) are upper and conditional-identification statements; they do not themselves establish a tuple-uniform minimax converse or determine the missing-sign scale from the first moment \(\pi_{m,n}\).  That is exactly the scope recorded in the proposition's closing sentence.
