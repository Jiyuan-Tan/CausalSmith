Assume \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\ne\varnothing\), fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), and put
\[
 \mathcal G=\sigma\!\left(A,(f_i)_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\right),
 \qquad E_h=\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n,
 \qquad m_h=\mathbb E_P(E_h\mid\mathcal G).
\tag{21}
\]
The objective in \({\rm def:exact-optimal-bandwidth}\) has a minimizer: the score and its finite-lattice certificates have only finitely many bandwidth breakpoints, and between consecutive breakpoints the stochastic certificate is constant while the Taylor part of the bias certificate is continuous and nondecreasing.  Hence its infimum over the nonempty compact interval \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\) is attained at one of finitely many included left endpoints.  The smallest minimizer \(h^\dagger\) is consequently well defined and depends on the class, not on the observed sample.

By \({\rm thm:explicit-pc-conditional-certificate}\),
\[
 |m_h|\le\overline b_h(A),
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P\{(E_h-m_h)^2\mid\mathcal G\}
 \le\overline s_h(A)^2.
\tag{22}
\]
Conditional Cauchy--Schwarz and (22) give
\[
 \mathbb E_P(|E_h|\mid\mathcal G)
 \le |m_h|+\{\mathbb E_P((E_h-m_h)^2\mid\mathcal G)\}^{1/2}
 \le\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A).
\tag{23}
\]
Taking expectations, setting \(h=h^\dagger\), taking the supremum over the model class, and applying \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}-\theta_n|
 \le\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{24}
\]

Conditional Chebyshev applied to (22) gives
\[
 P\!\left(
 |E_h-m_h|>\overline s_h(A)/\sqrt\alpha
 \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal G\right)\le\alpha;
\tag{25}
\]
when \(\overline s_h(A)=0\), (22) makes the left side zero.  The triangle inequality and the bias part of (22) turn (25) into coverage by the untruncated interval.  Moreover, the response-derivative envelope and \({\rm def:target}\) give \(|\theta_n|\le B\), so intersection with \([-B,B]\) cannot remove \(\theta_n\).  Integrating and setting \(h=h^\dagger\) proves
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal C_{h^\dagger}\}
 \ge1-\alpha.
\tag{26}
\]

Before intersection with \([-B,B]\), \({\rm def:honest-interval}\) has half-length exactly \(\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A)/\sqrt\alpha\).  Intersection only shortens it, and hence
\[
 \operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_h)
 \le2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}
 \{\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A)\}.
\tag{27}
\]
Expectation, the class supremum, \(h=h^\dagger\), and the definition of \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\) now yield
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_{h^\dagger})]
 \le2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{28}
\]
The degree-lattice quantities in \({\rm def:pc-handle}\) and the isolate summand in \({\rm def:radii}\) show that the certificate includes every realized degree and isolate.  Equations (24), (26), and (28) prove the proposed statement.  None compares \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\) to the analytic envelope \(\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\).
