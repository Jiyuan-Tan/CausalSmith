There is a constant \(c>0\) such that
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Compatible members of the same class must furnish the degree-lattice term, the interior smooth-bump term, the nonlinear honest-length term, and the graph floor.  No tail lower bound expressed solely through \(\pi_{m,n}\), and no lower-bound term of order \(B\pi_{0,n}\), is asserted.
