Condition on \(A\), put \(X_j=Z_j-1/2\), and fix \(i\).  The score is centered by \({\rm lem:exact-score-moments}\).  If \(A_{ij}=1\), the \(N_i\) neighbor contrasts are exchangeable.  Since
\[
 V_i=N_i^{-1}\sum_{k:A_{ik}=1}X_k,
\]
the exact first-moment identity gives
\[
 1=\mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i\mid A]
 =N_i^{-1}\sum_{k:A_{ik}=1}\mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}X_k\mid A]
 =\mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}X_j\mid A].
\]
If \(A_{ij}=0\), then \(q_{i,h}\) is independent of the centered variable \(X_j\), so the last covariance is zero.  Because \(X_j\) takes the two values \(\pm1/2\), every centered function measurable with respect to \(X_j\) is a scalar multiple of \(X_j\); since \(\mathbb E_ZX_j^2=1/4\),
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}\mid X_j,A]=4A_{ij}X_j.
\]
Summing over \(j\) proves, coordinatewise and simultaneously for all admissible \(h\),
\[
 q_h^{(1)}=4AX.
\]

For the second projection, take distinct \(j,k\).  If either coordinate is not a neighbor of \(i\), independence makes the corresponding two-coordinate interaction zero.  If both are neighbors, integrate \(q_{i,h}\) over all contrasts except \((X_j,X_k)\).  Simultaneously changing every neighbor contrast to its negative preserves their conditional joint law by \({\rm ass:bernoulli-design}\) and changes the odd score to its negative.  Thus the conditional function on \(\{-1/2,1/2\}^2\) is odd under \((X_j,X_k)\mapsto(-X_j,-X_k)\).  Its four-term Walsh expansion therefore contains only the degree-one monomials \(X_j\) and \(X_k\): the constant and \(X_jX_k\) are even under simultaneous sign reversal.  Subtracting the two one-coordinate conditional expectations leaves zero.  Hence
\[
 q_h^{(2)}=0.
\]

An \(A\)-measurable linear map preserves Hoeffding order.  Therefore, for every \(A\)-measurable orthogonal projector \(\Pi\),
\[
 \{(I_n-\Pi)q_h\}^{(1)}=4(I_n-\Pi)AX,
 \qquad \{(I_n-\Pi)q_h\}^{(2)}=0.
\]
Finally, conditional independence and positivity of the Bernoulli probabilities in \({\rm ass:bernoulli-design}\) give \(\mathbb E_Z[XX^\top\mid A]=I_n/4\).  Symmetry of \(A\) and idempotence of \(I_n-\Pi\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_Z\!\left[\|4(I_n-\Pi)AX\|_2^2\mid A\right]
 &=16\operatorname{tr}\!\left((I_n-\Pi)AA^\top(I_n-\Pi)\frac{I_n}{4}\right)\\
 &=4\|(I_n-\Pi)A\|_{\mathrm F}^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
