Suppose a future resolution of \({\rm oeq:uniform-feasible-pc}\) supplies the stated conditional inequalities, with conditioning sigma-field \(\mathcal G\) containing \(A\) and the preassignment response functions:
\[
 \left|\mathbb E_P[\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n\mid\mathcal G]\right|\leq\overline b_h(A),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}_P(\widehat\theta_h\mid\mathcal G)\leq\overline s_h(A)^2.
\]
Then conditional Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 \mathbb E_P[|\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n|\mid\mathcal G]
 \leq \overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A).
\]
At \(h=h^\dagger\), taking expectations, then the supremum over \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), gives exactly \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\) by \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\).  Conditional Chebyshev gives
\[
 P\!\left(
 |\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n|>
 \overline b_h(A)+\frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal G
 \right)\leq\alpha.
\]
By \({\rm ass:response-envelope}\) and \({\rm def:target}\), \(|\theta_n|\leq B\), so intersecting the untruncated interval with \([-B,B]\) cannot remove \(\theta_n\).  Integrating the preceding inequality gives coverage at least \(1-\alpha\).  Finally,
\[
 \operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_h)
 \leq 2\left\{\overline b_h(A)+\frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}\right\},
\]
and therefore its worst-case expected length is at most \(2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\).  Thus every deduction in \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\) follows once the missing feasible certificate is constructed, but the deduction is not itself a construction and cannot prove the theorem while that OEQ remains open.
