Assume \(L>0\), and put
\[
 c_{n,d}=n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4},\quad
 g(h)=Lh^2+c_{n,d}h^{-3/2},\quad
 \ell=d^{-1},\quad u=d^{-1/2}.
\tag{1}
\]
By \({\rm ass:log\text{-}degree}\), \(d\to\infty\), so \(d\geq4\) eventually and \({\rm def:bandwidth\text{-}set}\) gives \(\mathcal H_{n,d}=[\ell,u]\).  From \({\rm def:optimal\text{-}bandwidth}\),
\[
 h_0^7=\frac{L^{-2}}{n\sqrt d}.
\tag{2}
\]
Since \(c_{n,d}^2=(n\sqrt d)^{-1}\), positivity and (2) give
\[
 Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}.
\tag{3}
\]
If \(h_0\leq\ell\), then \(g(h)\geq L\ell^2\) for every admissible \(h\), while
\[
 c_{n,d}\ell^{-3/2}\leq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 =Lh_0^2\leq L\ell^2.
\]
Evaluation at \(\ell\) gives
\[
 Ld^{-2}\leq\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)\leq2Ld^{-2}.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(\ell<h_0<u\), then either \(h\geq h_0\), in which case \(Lh^2\geq Lh_0^2\), or \(h\leq h_0\), in which case \(c_{n,d}h^{-3/2}\geq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\).  Evaluation at \(h_0\) gives
\[
 Lh_0^2\leq\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)\leq2Lh_0^2,
 \quad Lh_0^2=L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}.
\tag{5}
\]
If \(h_0\geq u\), then every admissible \(h\) satisfies \(g(h)\geq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}=\sqrt{d/n}\), and
\[
 Lu^2\leq Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 \leq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}.
\]
Evaluation at \(u\) proves
\[
 \sqrt{d/n}\leq\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)
 \leq2\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{6}
\]
Combining (4)--(6) with
\[
 \mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 =\sqrt{d/n}+\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)
\tag{7}
\]
proves all three asserted equivalences with constants independent of \(n,d\).

Fix now an index at which both the bandwidth set and maintained class are nonempty.  By \({\rm thm:honest\text{-}upper}\), the estimator \(\widehat\theta_{\widehat h(A)}\) is a measurable observed-data map and satisfies
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{\widehat h(A)}-\theta_n|
 \leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{8}
\]
It is an admissible competitor in \({\rm def:minimax\text{-}criteria}\), so
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{9}
\]
For the interval criterion set \(a_\alpha=\sqrt{2\log(2/\alpha)}\) and define the untruncated interval
\[
 \mathcal J_{\widehat h(A)}=
 \left[
 \widehat\theta_{\widehat h(A)}-\overline b_{\widehat h(A)}(A)-a_\alpha\overline s_{\widehat h(A)}(A),
 \widehat\theta_{\widehat h(A)}+\overline b_{\widehat h(A)}(A)+a_\alpha\overline s_{\widehat h(A)}(A)
 \right].
\tag{10}
\]
By \({\rm thm:explicit\text{-}pc\text{-}conditional\text{-}certificate}\), its endpoints are finite measurable sample maps.  Since \(\mathcal C_{\widehat h(A)}\subseteq\mathcal J_{\widehat h(A)}\), \({\rm thm:honest\text{-}upper}\) gives
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal J_{\widehat h(A)}\}
 \geq1-\alpha.
\tag{11}
\]
Because \(a_\alpha>1\), its length and the pointwise comparison proved in \({\rm thm:honest\text{-}upper}\) satisfy
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{length}(\mathcal J_{\widehat h(A)})
 &=2\{\overline b_{\widehat h(A)}(A)+a_\alpha\overline s_{\widehat h(A)}(A)\}\\
 &\leq2a_\alpha\{\overline b_{\widehat h(A)}(A)+\overline s_{\widehat h(A)}(A)\}\\
 &\leq2a_\alpha\{\overline b_{h^\dagger}(A)+\overline s_{h^\dagger}(A)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal J_{\widehat h(A)}\) is an admissible interval competitor, and \({\rm def:frontier\text{-}envelope}\) gives
\[
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \leq2\sqrt{2\log(2/\alpha)}\,\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{13}
\]

Along every sequence covered by \({\rm thm:unrestricted\text{-}supported\text{-}converse}\), that theorem supplies \(c>0\), independent of \(n,d\), such that
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L},
 \quad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
\tag{14}
\]
eventually.  Under the compatibility conditions in \({\rm thm:minimax\text{-}lower}\), its explicit block response pairs are members of the maintained class, so their lower bounds transfer upward; the unrestricted theorem proves (14) directly without those block conditions.  Because the maintained class is not nested in the full LiWager2022RandomGraph Theorem~6 regime and \(\theta_n\) is not that source's finite indirect-effect target, neither argument transfers automatically to the source regime.

Finally, \({\rm thm:conditional\text{-}isolate\text{-}wall}\) gives, on every realized graph,
\[
 b(A)\geq\frac Bn\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}
\tag{15}
\]
for every uniformly valid graph-only conditional-bias certificate of the stated kind.  Equations (9), (13), and (14) are the conservative lower--upper bracket.  Its lower side is \(\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\), whereas its upper side is \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\).  No declared result bounds the latter by the former, so (4)--(7) are tuning geometry and a lower benchmark, not a sharp minimax rate or an achieved honest expected-length frontier.
