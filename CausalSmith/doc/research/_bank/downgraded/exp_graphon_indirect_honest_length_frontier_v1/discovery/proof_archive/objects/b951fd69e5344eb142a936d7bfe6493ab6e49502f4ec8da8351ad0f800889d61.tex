On the predeclared class \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\), \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\) implies \(\partial_x^3f_i(a,x)=0\) everywhere.  Hence each map \(x\mapsto f_i(a,x)\) is quadratic.  Substitution of \(L=0\) in \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\) gives
\[
 h_0=\infty,\qquad h_\star=d^{-1/2},
\]
and substitution in \({\rm def:radii}\) deletes the positive-degree Taylor term.  For a quadratic response, its Taylor expansion at \(1/2\) is exact, and the identities
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}\mid A]=0,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i\mid A]=1,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i^2\mid A]=0
\]
show that the exact score recovers its derivative without approximation error for every positive realized degree.  Affine responses are included as the zero-quadratic-coefficient case.  Direct substitution also gives exactly the two displayed complete certificate formulas in the proposition, including isolates and both PC remainders.  These algebraic conclusions do not require the unresolved PC rate.  What remains is the proposition's assertion that the nonremainder score term has order \(\sqrt{d/n}\) uniformly over the whole graphon class: this needs a uniform lower bound for the truncated binomial moments averaged over the random degree sequence, which is not among the frozen results, as well as the still-undefined feasible-PC construction on which the proposition formally depends.
