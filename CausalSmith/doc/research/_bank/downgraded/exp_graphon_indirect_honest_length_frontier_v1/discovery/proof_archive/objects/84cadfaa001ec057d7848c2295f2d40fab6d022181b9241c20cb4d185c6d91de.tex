Put \(a_\alpha=\sqrt{2\log(2/\alpha)}\),
\[
 L_h=\widehat\theta_h-\overline b_h(A)-a_\alpha\overline s_h(A),
 \qquad
 U_h=\widehat\theta_h+\overline b_h(A)+a_\alpha\overline s_h(A),
\]
and define \(\operatorname{clip}_{[-B,B]}(t)=\max\{-B,\min\{B,t\}\}\).  Then
\[
 \mathcal C_h=
 [\operatorname{clip}_{[-B,B]}(L_h),\operatorname{clip}_{[-B,B]}(U_h)].
\]
