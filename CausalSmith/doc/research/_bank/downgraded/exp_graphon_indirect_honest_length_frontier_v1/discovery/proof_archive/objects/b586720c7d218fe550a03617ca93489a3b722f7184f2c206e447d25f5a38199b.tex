On \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\), \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\) implies \(\partial_x^3f_i(a,x)=0\) for every \(i,a,x\).  Hence each map \(x\mapsto f_i(a,x)\) is quadratic.  Substitution of \(L=0\) in \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\) gives
\[
 h_0=\infty,\qquad h_\star=d^{-1/2}.
\]
Substitution in \({\rm def:radii}\) deletes the positive-degree Taylor term.  For a quadratic response, Taylor expansion at one half is exact, and \({\rm lem:exact-score-moments}\) gives
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}\mid A]=0,
 \qquad \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i\mid A]=1,
 \qquad \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i^2\mid A]=0.
\]
Thus the exact score recovers the derivative without approximation error for every positive realized degree; affine responses are the zero-quadratic-coefficient special case.

At \(h_\star=d^{-1/2}\), the two analytic supported-unit contributions in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) are
\[
 (n\sqrt d\,h_\star^3)^{-1/2}=\sqrt{d/n},
 \qquad \sqrt{d/n},
\]
which proves the stated nonremainder order.  Direct substitution of \(L=0\) and \(h=h_\star\) in the corrected \({\rm def:radii}\) yields exactly
\[
 \overline b_{h_\star}(A)=\frac Bn\sum_i\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}
 +\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}
\]
and
\[
 \overline s_{h_\star}(A)^2
 =\frac{C_{\mathrm{pc}}B^2}{n^2}
 \left\{\sum_{i:N_i>0}\mu_{2,i}(h_\star)^{-1}+\sum_iN_i\right\}
 +\widehat V_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}.
\]
Finally, if the maintained class has \(L>0\), the honesty requirement ranges over every response with third derivative bounded by that positive radius.  Observing that one realized response is affine does not change the prespecified class, so it cannot justify replacing its worst-case bias allowance by the \(L=0\) allowance.
