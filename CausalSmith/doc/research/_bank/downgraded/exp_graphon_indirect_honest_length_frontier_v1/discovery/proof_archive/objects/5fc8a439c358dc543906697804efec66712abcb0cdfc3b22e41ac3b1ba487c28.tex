Assume \(L>0\), and put
\[
 c_{n,d}=n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4},\qquad
 g(h)=Lh^2+c_{n,d}h^{-3/2},\qquad
 \ell=d^{-1},\qquad u=d^{-1/2}.
\tag{1}
\]
Along the stipulated triangular array, \({\rm ass:log-degree}\) and the declared condition \(C_{\log}>0\) imply \(d\to\infty\).  Hence, at every sufficiently large index, \(d\geq4\), so \(0<\ell\leq u\leq1/2\).  It follows directly from \({\rm def:bandwidth-set}\) that
\[
 \mathcal H_{n,d}=[\ell,u].
\tag{2}
\]
By \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\), the unconstrained balance point obeys
\[
 h_0^7=\frac{L^{-2}}{n\sqrt d}.
\tag{3}
\]
Since \(c_{n,d}^2=(n\sqrt d)^{-1}\), and all quantities in (3) are positive, taking the positive square root after multiplying (3) by \(L^2\) gives
\[
 Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}.
\tag{4}
\]

Suppose first that \(h_0\leq\ell\).  For every \(h\in[\ell,u]\), nonnegativity of the second summand in (1) gives \(g(h)\geq L\ell^2\).  Moreover, because \(t\mapsto t^{-3/2}\) is decreasing and \(t\mapsto t^2\) is increasing on \((0,\infty)\), (4) yields
\[
 c_{n,d}\ell^{-3/2}
 \leq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 =Lh_0^2
 \leq L\ell^2.
\tag{5}
\]
Evaluating \(g\) at \(\ell\) and using (5), therefore,
\[
 Ld^{-2}
 \leq \inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)
 \leq g(\ell)
 \leq2Ld^{-2}.
\tag{6}
\]

Next suppose that \(\ell<h_0<u\).  If \(h\geq h_0\), then \(Lh^2\geq Lh_0^2\); if \(h\leq h_0\), then \(c_{n,d}h^{-3/2}\geq c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}=Lh_0^2\) by (4).  Thus \(g(h)\geq Lh_0^2\) throughout \([\ell,u]\), whereas evaluation at \(h_0\) and (4) give \(g(h_0)=2Lh_0^2\).  Consequently,
\[
 Lh_0^2
 \leq \inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)
 \leq2Lh_0^2.
\tag{7}
\]
Equation (3) also gives the exact identity
\[
 Lh_0^2=L^{3/7}n^{-2/7}d^{-1/7}
 =L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}.
\tag{8}
\]

Finally suppose that \(h_0\geq u\).  For every \(h\in[\ell,u]\), monotonicity of \(t^{-3/2}\) gives
\[
 g(h)\geq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}
 =n^{-1/2}d^{-1/4}d^{3/4}
 =\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{9}
\]
Because \(u\leq h_0\), (4) and the same two monotonicity facts imply
\[
 Lu^2\leq Lh_0^2=c_{n,d}h_0^{-3/2}
 \leq c_{n,d}u^{-3/2}.
\tag{10}
\]
Evaluation at \(u\), followed by (9)--(10), yields
\[
 \sqrt{d/n}
 \leq \inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h)
 \leq g(u)
 \leq2\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{11}
\]

By the proposed \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\),
\[
 \mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 =\sqrt{d/n}+\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}g(h).
\tag{12}
\]
Adding \(\sqrt{d/n}\) to (6), (7), and (11), and using (8) in the middle case, proves respectively
\[
 \mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 \asymp Ld^{-2}+\sqrt{d/n},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 \asymp L^{3/7}(n^2d)^{-1/7}+\sqrt{d/n},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\asymp\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{13}
\]
The comparison constants may be taken between \(1\) and \(3\), uniformly in \(n\) and \(d\).

For the upper half of the decision-theoretic bracket, fix an index for which both \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\ne\varnothing\).  By the proposed \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\),
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}-\theta_n|
 \leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{14}
\]
The oracle value \(h^\dagger\) is deterministic at the class level, and \({\rm def:estimator}\) together with \({\rm def:empirical-projector}\) makes \(\widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}\) a measurable function of the observed data.  It is therefore admissible in the estimator infimum of the proposed \({\rm def:minimax-criteria}\), so (14) implies
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{15}
\]

For the interval criterion, define the proof-intermediate untruncated interval
\[
 \mathcal J_{h^\dagger}(D)=
 \left[
 \widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}-\overline b_{h^\dagger}(A)
 -\frac{\overline s_{h^\dagger}(A)}{\sqrt\alpha},
 \widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}+\overline b_{h^\dagger}(A)
 +\frac{\overline s_{h^\dagger}(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \right].
\tag{16}
\]
The radii are finite, nonnegative, graph-measurable sample maps by \({\rm def:pc-handle}\) and \({\rm def:radii}\), while the declared range of \(\alpha\) gives \(0<\alpha<1/2\).  Hence (16) is a data-measurable closed interval with finite ordered endpoints, as required by the proposed \({\rm def:minimax-criteria}\).  By \({\rm def:honest-interval}\), \(\mathcal C_{h^\dagger}\subseteq\mathcal J_{h^\dagger}\).  The coverage conclusion of the proposed \({\rm thm:honest-upper}\) therefore implies
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal J_{h^\dagger}(D)\}
 \geq1-\alpha.
\tag{17}
\]
Nonnegativity of the radii gives, pointwise,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{length}(\mathcal J_{h^\dagger})
 &=2\{\overline b_{h^\dagger}(A)
 +\alpha^{-1/2}\overline s_{h^\dagger}(A)\}\\
 &\leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}
 \{\overline b_{h^\dagger}(A)+\overline s_{h^\dagger}(A)\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{18}
\]
Taking expectations and then the supremum over the nonempty model class, the proposed \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) turns (18) into
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\operatorname{length}(\mathcal J_{h^\dagger})]
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{19}
\]
Because (17) makes \(\mathcal J_{h^\dagger}\) admissible in the interval infimum of the proposed \({\rm def:minimax-criteria}\), (19) proves
\[
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{20}
\]

Along every sequence satisfying the hypotheses of \({\rm thm:unrestricted-supported-converse}\), that theorem supplies a constant \(c>0\), independent of \(n\) and \(d\), such that for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 R^*_{n,d,L}\geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \Lambda^*_{n,d,L}(\alpha)
 \geq c\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}.
\tag{21}
\]
Combining (15), (20), and (21) gives the asserted lower--upper bracket.  Separately, \({\rm thm:conditional-isolate-wall}\) proves on every realized graph that every graph-only conditional-bias certificate of the stated uniform kind satisfies
\[
 b(A)\geq\frac{B}{n}
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}.
\tag{22}
\]
The upper endpoints in (15) and (20) involve the oracle conservative envelope \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\), whereas the lower endpoints in (21) involve \(\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\).  No cited definition or result proves \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\lesssim\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\).  Thus (13) is analytic tuning geometry and a minimax lower benchmark, while (15), (20), and (21) form only the stated conservative bracket.
