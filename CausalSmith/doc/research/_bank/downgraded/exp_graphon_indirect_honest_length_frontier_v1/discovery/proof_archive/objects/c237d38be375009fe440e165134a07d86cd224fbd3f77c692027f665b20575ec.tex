At every index for which \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\ne\varnothing\), define
\[
 h^\dagger=\min\operatorname*{argmin}_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A)].
\]
This is the oracle full-class radius-minimizing bandwidth.  Its evaluation requires the full-class supremum and is not an operational data-driven tuning rule.
