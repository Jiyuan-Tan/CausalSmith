Fix a law in the prespecified class \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\).  By \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\) with radius zero,
\[
 \sup_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\sup_{a\in\{0,1\},\,x\in[0,1]}
 |\partial_x^3 f_i(a,x)|\le0,
\]
and hence \(\partial_x^3f_i(a,x)=0\) for every displayed argument.  Since each response is \(C^3\), for \(x,y\in[0,1]\),
\[
 \partial_x^2f_i(a,x)-\partial_x^2f_i(a,y)
 =\int_y^x\partial_t^3f_i(a,t)\,dt=0.
\]
Thus the second derivative is constant, and integrating twice shows that \(x\mapsto f_i(a,x)\) is a polynomial of degree at most two.

Substitution of \(L=0\) into \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\) gives \(h_0=\infty\) and
\[
 h_\star=\min\{d^{-1/2},\max\{d^{-1},\infty\}\}=d^{-1/2}.
\tag{37}
\]
The logarithmic-degree condition in \({\rm def:model-class}\) implies \(d\ge4\) at all sufficiently large indices, so \(d^{-1}\le d^{-1/2}\le1/2\), and (37) belongs to \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\) by \({\rm def:bandwidth-set}\).  The positive-degree Taylor term in \({\rm def:radii}\) is multiplied by \(L\), and is therefore zero.

Fix \(i\) with \(N_i>0\) and \(a\in\{0,1\}\).  By \({\rm lem:exact-score-moments}\), exact support correction gives \(\mu_{2,i}(h)>0\) and
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}\mid A]=0,
 \quad
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i\mid A]=1,
 \quad
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i^2\mid A]=0.
\tag{38}
\]
The exact quadratic expansion is
\[
 f_i(a,1/2+V_i)
 =f_i(a,1/2)+\partial_xf_i(a,1/2)V_i
 +\frac12\partial_x^2f_i(a,1/2)V_i^2.
\]
Multiplication by \(q_{i,h}\), conditional expectation, and (38) yield
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}f_i(a,1/2+V_i)\mid A]
 =\partial_xf_i(a,1/2).
\tag{39}
\]
Thus every quadratic response, including every affine response, has zero positive-degree approximation error.

At (37), the analytic stochastic term in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) is
\[
 (n\sqrt d\,h_\star^3)^{-1/2}
 =(n\sqrt d\,d^{-3/2})^{-1/2}=\sqrt{d/n},
\tag{40}
\]
and the graph term is also \(\sqrt{d/n}\).  Direct substitution of \(L=0\), \(h=h_\star\), the corrected \({\rm def:pc-handle}\), and the corrected \({\rm def:radii}\) gives
\[
 \overline b_{h_\star}(A)
 =\frac Bn\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}
 +\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}
\tag{41}
\]
and
\[
 \overline s_{h_\star}(A)^2
 =\widehat V_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}
 =\frac1{4n^2}\sum_{j\in\mathcal V_n}
 \left(\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\beta_{ij,h_\star}\right)^2.
\tag{42}
\]
The certification in \({\rm thm:explicit-pc-conditional-certificate}\) proves that (41)--(42) are the complete conditional certificates.  No stochastic order for (42) has been used.

All member properties other than the third-derivative radius are identical at radii zero and \(L\), so \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\subseteq\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\).  To see why a realized affine response does not permit a post hoc radius-zero analysis when \(L>0\), retain the graph and uniform latent-type law of any admissible member and replace its response draw by the deterministic function
\[
 g(a,x)=c(x-1/2)^3,
 \qquad c=\frac12\min\{B/3,L/6\}>0.
\tag{43}
\]
This preserves independent and identically distributed preassignment sampling, randomization, the graph law, and anonymous interference.  Moreover,
\[
 \|g\|_\infty\le c/8,\quad
 \|\partial_xg\|_\infty\le3c/4,\quad
 \|\partial_x^2g\|_\infty\le3c,\quad
 \|\partial_x^3g\|_\infty=6c,
\]
which are bounded by \(B,B,B,L\), respectively, by (43).  Hence this alternative remains in \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) but is not in \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\).  For every \(N_i>0\), exact support correction yields
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}g(a,1/2+V_i)\mid A]
 =c\frac{\mathbb E_Z[V_i^4\mathbf1\{|V_i|\le h_i(h)\}\mid A]}
          {\mu_{2,i}(h)}>0.
\tag{44}
\]
The denominator is positive by \({\rm lem:exact-score-moments}\), and the numerator is positive because the truncation contains a nearest nonzero exposure-lattice point of positive binomial probability.  Thus the positive-radius class permits a genuine cubic remainder.  Equations (37)--(44) prove every assertion of the statement.
