At every index for which \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\ne\varnothing\), the oracle bandwidth \(h^\dagger\) is well defined.  For every prespecified \(h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}\), the feasible estimator and interval in \({\rm def:estimator}\) and \({\rm def:honest-interval}\) are polynomial-time reportable and, for every \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), satisfy
\[
 \Pr_Z\{\theta_n\in\mathcal C_h\mid A,(f_i)_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\}\geq1-\alpha
\]
for almost every preassignment realization.  Before intersection with \([-B,B]\), their pointwise half-length is exactly \(\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A)/\sqrt\alpha\), including every realized degree and isolate.  In particular, \(h=d^{-1}\) is admissible whenever \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\ne\varnothing\); it is a prespecified operational choice, not an identity with \(h^\dagger\), and receives no optimizer bound by that identity.  At the oracle bandwidth,
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}\mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}-\theta_n|
 \leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L},
 \qquad
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal C_{h^\dagger}\}\geq1-\alpha,
\]
and
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_{h^\dagger})]
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\]
The first guarantee is finite-sample conditional certification for a reportable prespecified bandwidth; the displays at \(h^\dagger\) are oracle conservative integrated bounds.  No bandwidth oracle inequality and no comparison \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\lesssim\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\) is asserted.
