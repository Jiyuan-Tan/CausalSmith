Fix a law in the prespecified class \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\).  By \({\rm def:model-class}\) and \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\) with radius \(L=0\),
\[
 \sup_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\sup_{a\in\{0,1\},\,x\in[0,1]}
 |\partial_x^3 f_i(a,x)|\leq 0.
\]
Since an absolute value is nonnegative, this implies
\[
 \partial_x^3 f_i(a,x)=0
 \quad\text{for every }i\in\mathcal V_n,
 \ a\in\{0,1\},\ x\in[0,1].
\tag{31}
\]
Each map \(x\mapsto f_i(a,x)\) is \(C^3\) by the declared response-function type.  The fundamental theorem of calculus applied to (31) makes its second derivative constant; integrating that constant twice shows that the map is a polynomial of degree at most two on \([0,1]\).

Substitution of \(L=0\) into \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\) gives \(h_0=\infty\), and hence
\[
 h_\star
 =\min\{d^{-1/2},\max\{d^{-1},\infty\}\}
 =d^{-1/2}.
\tag{32}
\]
The class condition \(C_{\log}\log n\leq d\), incorporated in \({\rm def:model-class}\), and \(C_{\log}>0\) imply that, for every sufficiently large triangular-array index, \(d\geq4\).  Thus \(d^{-1}\leq d^{-1/2}\leq1/2\), so the value in (32) belongs to \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\) by \({\rm def:bandwidth-set}\).  This is the boundary condition needed when the asymptotic order below is invoked.  Moreover, the positive-degree Taylor term in \({\rm def:radii}\) is multiplied by \(L\), so it is identically zero on this class.

Now fix \(i\) with \(N_i>0\) and \(a\in\{0,1\}\).  By \({\rm lem:exact-score-moments}\), exact support correction makes \(\mu_{2,i}(h)>0\), so the division defining \(q_{i,h}\) is valid, and
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}\mid A]=0,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i\mid A]=1,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i^2\mid A]=0.
\tag{33}
\]
Because (31) makes the second-order Taylor formula exact,
\[
 f_i(a,1/2+V_i)
 =f_i(a,1/2)+\partial_xf_i(a,1/2)V_i
  +\frac12\partial_x^2f_i(a,1/2)V_i^2.
\]
Multiplying this identity by \(q_{i,h}\), taking conditional expectation given \(A\), and applying the three identities in (33) yields
\[
 \mathbb E_Z\!\left[q_{i,h}f_i(a,1/2+V_i)\mid A\right]
 =\partial_xf_i(a,1/2).
\]
Thus the exact score has no positive-degree approximation error for any quadratic exposure response on \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\); affine responses are included by taking the quadratic coefficient to be zero.

At the bandwidth in (32), the analytic supported-unit stochastic term in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) is exactly
\[
 (n\sqrt d\,h_\star^3)^{-1/2}
 =(n\sqrt d\,d^{-3/2})^{-1/2}
 =\sqrt{d/n}.
\tag{34}
\]
The graph term in that definition is itself \(\sqrt{d/n}\), while the smoothness term is \(Lh_\star^2=0\).  Hence the two nonremainder contributions both have order \(\sqrt{d/n}\).

Finally, direct substitution of \(L=0\) and \(h=h_\star\) into \({\rm def:radii}\) gives
\[
 \overline b_{h_\star}(A)
 =\frac Bn\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}
  +\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}
\tag{35}
\]
and
\[
 \overline s_{h_\star}(A)^2
 =\frac{C_{\mathrm{pc}}B^2}{n^2}
 \left\{\sum_{i:N_i>0}\mu_{2,i}(h_\star)^{-1}
       +\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}N_i\right\}
 +\widehat V_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}.
\tag{36}
\]
The conditional certification theorem \({\rm thm:explicit-pc-conditional-certificate}\) confirms that these are the complete graph-observable bias and variance certificates; in particular, neither the isolate term nor either feasible-PC remainder has been discarded.

It remains to distinguish class prespecification from a realized special case.  For every declared \(L\geq0\), a law satisfying the radius-zero condition also satisfies \(|\partial_x^3f_i(a,x)|\leq L\), while all other member properties in \({\rm def:model-class}\) are unchanged.  Therefore \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\subseteq\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\).

The distinction is substantive when \(L>0\).  Starting from the graph and latent-type part of any affine law in \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), replace its response draw by the deterministic function
\[
 g(a,x)=c(x-1/2)^3,
 \qquad
 c=\frac12\min\{B/3,L/6\}>0.
\]
This replacement preserves independent and identically distributed preassignment sampling, the graph law, randomization, and anonymous interference.  Moreover,
\[
 \sup_{a,x}|g(a,x)|\leq c/8,\qquad
 \sup_{a,x}|\partial_xg(a,x)|\leq3c/4,\qquad
 \sup_{a,x}|\partial_x^2g(a,x)|\leq3c,\qquad
 \sup_{a,x}|\partial_x^3g(a,x)|=6c.
\]
The choice of \(c\), together with \(B>0\) and \(L>0\), makes the first three quantities at most \(B\) and the last at most \(L\).  Hence the modified law remains in \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), but not in \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\).  For every \(N_i>0\), exact support correction and binomial support also give
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}g(a,1/2+V_i)\mid A]
 =c\,\frac{\mathbb E_Z[V_i^4\mathbf1\{|V_i|\leq h_i(h)\}\mid A]}
              {\mu_{2,i}(h)}
 >0,
\]
where positivity follows because the truncation contains the nearest nonzero lattice points, each having positive binomial probability, and \(\mu_{2,i}(h)>0\) by \({\rm lem:exact-score-moments}\).  Thus the positive-radius class permits a genuine cubic score remainder.  Observing that one realized response is affine does not replace that prespecified class by \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\), and hence does not justify removing the worst-case smoothness allowance from an interval required to be honest uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\).  Equations (31)--(36) establish every assertion of the proposition.
