If \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) is empty, the universal conditional-radius claim is vacuous and the submodel clause is not invoked.  Otherwise fix the graph primitives of an admissible law and define
\[
 f_i(a,x)=\varepsilon_iBx,
 \qquad
 \varepsilon_i\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}{\rm Unif}\{-1,1\},
\tag{54}
\]
with the signs independent of all graph variables.  Because \({\rm ass:unit\text{-}sampling}\) requires independent and identically distributed joint draws and a uniform \(U_i\)-marginal, adjoining independent signs preserves that assumption.  Equation (54) satisfies \(|f_i(a,x)|\leq B\), \(|\partial_xf_i(a,x)|=B\), and has zero second and third derivatives.  It therefore satisfies \({\rm ass:response\text{-}envelope}\) and \({\rm ass:third\text{-}derivative\text{-}bound}\) for every \(L\geq0\), while leaving every graph, rank, support, and randomization condition in \({\rm def:model\text{-}class}\) unchanged.

Fix a realized graph \(A\), and put
\[
 S_0(A)=\{i:N_i=0\},
 \qquad |S_0(A)|=I_0(A).
\tag{55}
\]
Consider two sign configurations in the conditional support of (54) that agree on \(S_0(A)^c\), with all signs on \(S_0(A)\) equal to \(+1\) in the first and \(-1\) in the second.  Each configuration has positive conditional probability.  For an isolate, \({\rm ass:anonymous\text{-}interference}\) gives
\[
 Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}=f_i(Z_i,0)=0,
\tag{56}
\]
and its sign enters no other response.  The complete conditional observed-data laws are therefore identical under the two configurations.  On the other hand, (54) and \({\rm def:target}\) give
\[
 |\theta_n^+-\theta_n^-|=\frac{2B}{n}I_0(A).
\tag{57}
\]
For any measurable estimator \(\widetilde\theta\), let \(m(A)\) be its common conditional mean under these indistinguishable configurations.  The triangle inequality gives
\[
 \max\{|m(A)-\theta_n^+|,|m(A)-\theta_n^-|\}
 \geq\frac12|\theta_n^+-\theta_n^-|
 =\frac BnI_0(A).
\tag{58}
\]
Thus any graph-only conditional-bias radius valid for every response configuration must satisfy the first claimed bound.

Now give an estimator the graph and every sign outside \(S_0(A)\).  This genie can only reduce Bayes risk.  The remaining unknown target contribution is
\[
 T_0(A)=\frac Bn\sum_{i\in S_0(A)}\varepsilon_i.
\tag{59}
\]
Conditional on \(A\), it is independent of all genie-observed data and is symmetric.  If \(S_K=\sum_{k=1}^K\varepsilon_k\), symmetry and the pointwise inequality
\[
 \frac{|s-a|+|-s-a|}{2}\geq |s|
\tag{60}
\]
show that zero minimizes \(a\mapsto\mathbb E|S_K-a|\).  Hence the conditional genie Bayes risk is exactly \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb E|S_{I_0(A)}|\).

For \(K\geq1\), independence and centering give
\[
 \mathbb ES_K^2=K,
 \qquad
 \mathbb ES_K^4=K+6{K\choose2}=3K^2-2K\leq3K^2.
\tag{61}
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz gives \(\mathbb E|S_K|\leq\sqrt K\).  H\"older's inequality, applied as
\[
 \mathbb ES_K^2
 =\mathbb E\{|S_K|^{2/3}|S_K|^{4/3}\}
 \leq(\mathbb E|S_K|)^{2/3}(\mathbb E|S_K|^4)^{1/3},
\tag{62}
\]
and (61) give
\[
 \mathbb E|S_K|
 \geq\frac{(\mathbb ES_K^2)^{3/2}}{(\mathbb ES_K^4)^{1/2}}
 \geq\sqrt{K/3}.
\tag{63}
\]
The same bounds are trivial for \(K=0\).  Substitution of \(K=I_0(A)\) into (59)--(63) proves the displayed conditional Bayes-risk bounds, including the lower constant \(B/(\sqrt3\,n)\).  Averaging over \(A\) gives order \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb E\sqrt{I_0(A)}\).  Since \(\sqrt K\leq K\) for every integer \(K\geq0\), this quantity is no larger than \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb EI_0(A)\).  No step constructs an unconditionally honest interval or transfers (58) to all unconditional procedures, proving the final scope sentence.
