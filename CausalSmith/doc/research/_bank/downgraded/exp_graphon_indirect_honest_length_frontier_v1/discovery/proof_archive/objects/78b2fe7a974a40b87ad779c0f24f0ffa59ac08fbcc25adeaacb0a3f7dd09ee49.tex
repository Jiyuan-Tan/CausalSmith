Assume \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\ne\varnothing\), fix \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), and put
\[
 \mathcal G=\sigma\!\left(A,(f_i)_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\right),
 \qquad E_h=\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n,
 \qquad m_h=\mathbb E_P(E_h\mid\mathcal G).
\tag{21}
\]
The objective in \({\rm def:exact-optimal-bandwidth}\) has a minimizer.  Across all possible degrees, the score and every finite-lattice certificate in \({\rm def:pc-handle}\) have finitely many bandwidth breakpoints.  On each interval beginning at one such included breakpoint, the stochastic and PC-bias certificates are constant and the Taylor part is continuous and nondecreasing.  The supremum over \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) remains nondecreasing there, so its minimum on that interval is attained at the included left endpoint.  Since \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\) is a nonempty compact interval and there are finitely many endpoints, a global minimum is attained.  Thus the smallest minimizer \(h^\dagger\) is well defined and depends on the class, not on the observed sample.

By \({\rm thm:explicit-pc-conditional-certificate}\),
\[
 |m_h|\le\overline b_h(A),
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_P\{(E_h-m_h)^2\mid\mathcal G\}
 \le\overline s_h(A)^2.
\tag{22}
\]
Conditional Cauchy--Schwarz and (22) give
\[
 \mathbb E_P(|E_h|\mid\mathcal G)
 \le |m_h|+\{\mathbb E_P((E_h-m_h)^2\mid\mathcal G)\}^{1/2}
 \le\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A).
\tag{23}
\]
Taking expectations, setting \(h=h^\dagger\), taking the supremum over the model class, and applying \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) proves
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}-\theta_n|
 \le\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{24}
\]

Conditional Chebyshev applied to (22) gives
\[
 P\!\left(
 |E_h-m_h|>\overline s_h(A)/\sqrt\alpha
 \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal G\right)\le\alpha;
\tag{25}
\]
when \(\overline s_h(A)=0\), (22) makes the left side zero.  The triangle inequality and the bias part of (22) turn (25) into coverage by the untruncated interval.  Moreover, \({\rm ass:response-envelope}\) and \({\rm def:target}\) give \(|\theta_n|\le B\), so intersection with \([-B,B]\) cannot remove \(\theta_n\).  Integrating and setting \(h=h^\dagger\) proves
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal C_{h^\dagger}\}
 \ge1-\alpha.
\tag{26}
\]

Before intersection with \([-B,B]\), \({\rm def:honest-interval}\) has half-length exactly \(\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A)/\sqrt\alpha\).  Intersection only shortens it, and hence
\[
 \operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_h)
 \le2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}
 \{\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A)\}.
\tag{27}
\]
Expectation, the class supremum, \(h=h^\dagger\), and \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) now yield
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_{h^\dagger})]
 \le2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{28}
\]
The finite degree-lattice quantities in \({\rm def:pc-handle}\) and the isolate summand in \({\rm def:radii}\) show that the certificate includes every realized degree and isolate.  Equations (24), (26), and (28) prove the proposed statement.  None compares \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\) to \(\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\).
