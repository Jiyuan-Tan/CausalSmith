At every index for which \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\ne\varnothing\), define
\[
 \mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}
 =\sqrt{d/n}+\inf_{h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}}
 \{Lh^2+(n\sqrt d\,h^3)^{-1/2}\}.
\]
At every index for which both \(\mathcal H_{n,d}\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\ne\varnothing\), define
\[
 \mathfrak u_{n,d,L}
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\overline b_{h^\dagger}(A)+\overline s_{h^\dagger}(A)].
\]
The second quantity is the oracle conservative full-class envelope associated with \(h^\dagger\); its definition does not assert \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\lesssim\mathfrak r^{\mathrm{sup}}_{n,d,L}\).
