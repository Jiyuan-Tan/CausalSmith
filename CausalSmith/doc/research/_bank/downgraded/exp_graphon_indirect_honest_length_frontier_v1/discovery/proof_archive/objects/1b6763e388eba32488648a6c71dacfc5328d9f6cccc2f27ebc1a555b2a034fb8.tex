For the explicit construction in \({\rm def:pc-handle}\), all distinct scores over \(h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}\) can be represented and computed in polynomial time.  Conditional on \(A\) and the preassignment response functions, for every \(h\in\mathcal H_{n,d}\),
\[
 \left|\mathbb E_Z(\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n\mid A,(f_i)_i)\right|
 \leq\overline b_h(A),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}_Z(\widehat\theta_h\mid A,(f_i)_i)
 \leq\overline s_h(A)^2.
\]
Consequently \(\inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal C_h\}\geq1-\alpha\).  If \(C_A=\widehat\Pi_r(A)A\) and
\[
 G_A(C_A)=\sum_{i:N_i>0}\frac1{N_i}\sum_{j:A_{ij}=1}(C_A)_{ij}^2,
\]
then the explicit bias certificate also satisfies the deterministic bound
\[
 \widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{pc},h}
 \leq \frac{2B}{n}\|C_A\|_*
      +B\sqrt{\frac{G_A(C_A)}{n}}.
\]
The exact first two Hoeffding projections create no bandwidth-dependent term.  This result does not assert the sharper uniform rates \(\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{pc},h}=O_P(d/n)\) and \(\widehat V_{\mathrm{pc},h}=O_P(d/n)\); the displayed graph norm and conservative variance certificate are the proved computable quantities under the frozen assumptions.
