On the prespecified class \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\), \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\) gives
\[
 \partial_x^3f_i(a,x)=0
 \qquad
 \text{for every }i\in\mathcal V_n, a\in\{0,1\}, x\in[0,1].
\tag{31}
\]
Because each response is \(C^3\), (31) implies that every map \(x\mapsto f_i(a,x)\) is a polynomial of degree at most two.  Substitution of \(L=0\) in \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\) gives
\[
 h_0=\infty,
 \qquad
 h_\star=d^{-1/2}.
\tag{32}
\]
The positive-degree Taylor term in \({\rm def:radii}\) is multiplied by \(L\), and hence vanishes identically.

For a quadratic response, Taylor expansion at one half is exact.  The moment identities in \({\rm lem:exact-score-moments}\) give, for every positive realized degree,
\[
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}\mid A]=0,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i\mid A]=1,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i^2\mid A]=0.
\tag{33}
\]
Multiplying the exact quadratic Taylor formula by \(q_{i,h}\), taking conditional expectation, and using (33) cancels the constant and quadratic coefficients and leaves exactly the derivative at one half.  Thus the exact score has no positive-degree approximation error on \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\); affine responses are the special case with zero quadratic coefficient.

At the bandwidth in (32), the analytic stochastic term in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) is
\[
 (n\sqrt d\,h_\star^3)^{-1/2}
 =(n\sqrt d\,d^{-3/2})^{-1/2}
 =\sqrt{d/n},
\tag{34}
\]
and the graph contribution is already \(\sqrt{d/n}\).  This proves the stated nonremainder supported-unit order.  Direct substitution of \(L=0\) and \(h=h_\star\) in \({\rm def:radii}\) yields
\[
 \overline b_{h_\star}(A)
 =\frac Bn\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}
 +\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}
\tag{35}
\]
and
\[
 \overline s_{h_\star}(A)^2
 =\frac{C_{\mathrm{pc}}B^2}{n^2}
 \left\{\sum_{i:N_i>0}\mu_{2,i}(h_\star)^{-1}
       +\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}N_i\right\}
 +\widehat V_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}.
\tag{36}
\]
The inclusion \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\subseteq\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) follows for \(L\geq0\) because a zero third derivative satisfies the larger bound, with every other member property unchanged.  But when the maintained class has \(L>0\), honesty ranges over every response whose third derivative is bounded by that positive radius.  Merely observing that one realized response is affine does not replace the prespecified class by \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\), so it cannot justify deleting the worst-case smoothness allowance.  Equations (31)--(36) prove the proposition.
