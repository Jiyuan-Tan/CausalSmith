If \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) is empty, the universal conditional-radius claim is vacuous and the submodel clause is not invoked.  Otherwise fix the graph and latent-type marginal of an admissible law and adjoin the independent response law
\[
 f_i(a,x)=\varepsilon_iBx,
 \qquad
 \varepsilon_i\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}{\rm Unif}\{-1,1\},
\tag{45}
\]
independently of the graph variables.  Because the repaired \({\rm ass:unit-sampling}\) has a uniform \(U_i\)-marginal, adjoining independent signs preserves the required independent and identically distributed joint law.  Equation (45) satisfies \(|f_i(a,x)|\le B\), \(|\partial_xf_i(a,x)|=B\), and has zero second and third derivatives.  It therefore satisfies \({\rm ass:response-envelope}\) and \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\) for every \(L\ge0\), while leaving every graph, rank, support, and randomization condition in \({\rm def:model-class}\) unchanged.

Fix a realized graph \(A\) and put \(S_0(A)=\{i:N_i=0\}\), so \(|S_0(A)|=I_0(A)\).  Consider two sign configurations in the conditional support of (45) that agree on \(S_0(A)^c\), with all signs on \(S_0(A)\) equal to \(+1\) in the first configuration and to \(-1\) in the second.  Each configuration has positive conditional probability.  For every isolate, \({\rm ass:anonymous-interference}\) gives
\[
 Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}=f_i(Z_i,0)=0,
\tag{46}
\]
and an isolate's sign enters no other response.  The complete conditional observed-data laws are therefore identical under the two configurations.  On the other hand, (45) and \({\rm def:target}\) give target difference
\[
 |\theta_n^+-\theta_n^-|=\frac{2B}{n}I_0(A).
\tag{47}
\]
For any measurable estimator \(\widetilde\theta\), let \(m(A)\) be its common conditional mean under these indistinguishable configurations.  The triangle inequality gives
\[
 \max\{|m(A)-\theta_n^+|,|m(A)-\theta_n^-|\}
 \ge\frac12|\theta_n^+-\theta_n^-|
 =\frac BnI_0(A).
\tag{48}
\]
Thus any graph-only conditional-bias radius valid for every response configuration must satisfy the first claimed bound.

We now compute the smaller unconditional missing-sign fluctuation without introducing a tail-mass surrogate.  Give an estimator the graph and every sign outside \(S_0(A)\).  This genie can only reduce its Bayes risk.  The remaining unknown target contribution is
\[
 T_0(A)=\frac Bn\sum_{i\in S_0(A)}\varepsilon_i.
\tag{49}
\]
Conditional on \(A\), it is independent of all genie-observed data and is symmetric.  If \(S_K=\sum_{k=1}^K\varepsilon_k\), symmetry and the pointwise inequality
\[
 \frac{|s-a|+|-s-a|}{2}\ge |s|
\]
show that zero minimizes \(a\mapsto\mathbb E|S_K-a|\).  Hence the conditional genie Bayes risk is exactly \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb E|S_{I_0(A)}|\).

For \(K\ge1\), independence and centering give
\[
 \mathbb E S_K^2=K,
 \qquad
 \mathbb E S_K^4=K+6{K\choose2}=3K^2-2K\le3K^2.
\tag{50}
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz gives \(\mathbb E|S_K|\le\sqrt K\).  Hölder's inequality, applied as
\[
 \mathbb E S_K^2
 =\mathbb E\{|S_K|^{2/3}|S_K|^{4/3}\}
 \le(\mathbb E|S_K|)^{2/3}(\mathbb E|S_K|^4)^{1/3},
\]
and (50) give
\[
 \mathbb E|S_K|
 \ge\frac{(\mathbb E S_K^2)^{3/2}}{(\mathbb E S_K^4)^{1/2}}
 \ge\sqrt{K/3}.
\tag{51}
\]
The same bounds are trivial for \(K=0\).  Substitution of \(K=I_0(A)\) into (49)--(51) proves the displayed conditional Bayes-risk bounds.  Averaging over \(A\) gives order \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb E\sqrt{I_0(A)}\).  Since \(\sqrt K\le K\) for every integer \(K\ge0\), this quantity is no larger than \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb EI_0(A)\), the expected pointwise conditional allowance.  No step constructs an unconditionally honest interval or transfers (48) to all unconditional procedures, which proves the final scope sentence.
