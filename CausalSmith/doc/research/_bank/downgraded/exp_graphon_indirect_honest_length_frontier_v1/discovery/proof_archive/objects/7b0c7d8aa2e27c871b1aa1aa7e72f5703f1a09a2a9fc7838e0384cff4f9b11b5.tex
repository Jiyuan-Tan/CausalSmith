If \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) is empty, the assertion quantified over admissible laws is vacuous.  Suppose it is nonempty.  Retain the latent-type and graph law of one admissible member and replace only its response draw by
\[
 f_i(a,x)=\varepsilon_iBx,
 \qquad \varepsilon_i\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}{\rm Unif}\{-1,1\},
\tag{1}
\]
with signs independent of latent types, graph variables, and assignments.  The pairs \((U_i,f_i)\) remain independent and identically distributed with uniform \(U_i\)-marginal, as required by \({\rm ass:unit-sampling}\).  For every displayed argument,
\[
 |f_i(a,x)|\leq B,
 \qquad |\partial_xf_i(a,x)|=B,
 \qquad \partial_x^2f_i(a,x)=\partial_x^3f_i(a,x)=0.
\tag{2}
\]
Thus \({\rm ass:response-envelope}\) and \({\rm ass:third-derivative-bound}\) hold for every \(L\geq0\); all retained graph and randomization properties are unchanged, and \({\rm ass:anonymous-interference}\) supplies the potential outcomes.  Hence (1) is an admissible submodel.

Fix a graph \(A\) in the support of this submodel, and set
\[
 S_0(A)=\{i\in\mathcal V_n:N_i=0\},
 \qquad |S_0(A)|=I_0(A).
\tag{3}
\]
Take two sign vectors in the conditional support which agree on \(S_0(A)^c\), with all isolate signs equal to \(+1\) in the first and \(-1\) in the second.  Each vector has positive conditional probability.  If \(i\in S_0(A)\), anonymous interference and (1) give for every assignment
\[
 Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}=f_i(Z_i,0)=0.
\tag{4}
\]
The nonisolate responses agree because their signs agree.  Therefore the complete conditional observed-data laws, and hence the conditional mean of any data-measurable estimator \(\widetilde\theta\), are identical under the two configurations.  Denote that common mean by \(m(A)\).  By \({\rm def:target}\),
\[
 \theta_n(\varepsilon)=\frac Bn\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\varepsilon_i,
 \qquad |\theta_n^+-\theta_n^-|=\frac{2B}{n}I_0(A).
\tag{5}
\]
If a graph-only radius \(b(A)\) bounds the absolute conditional bias for every response configuration in the conditional support, it bounds both \(|m(A)-\theta_n^+|\) and \(|m(A)-\theta_n^-|\).  The triangle inequality gives
\[
 2b(A)\geq|m(A)-\theta_n^+|+|m(A)-\theta_n^-|
 \geq|\theta_n^+-\theta_n^-|=\frac{2B}{n}I_0(A),
\]
and hence \(b(A)\geq BI_0(A)/n\).

For the Bayes calculation, reveal \(A\) and all signs on \(S_0(A)^c\).  Conditional on this genie information, the known nonisolate part of the target can be subtracted.  By independence in (1) and (4), all remaining data are independent of
\[
 T_0(A)=\frac Bn\sum_{i\in S_0(A)}\varepsilon_i.
\tag{6}
\]
Writing \(K=I_0(A)\) and \(S_K=\sum_{k=1}^K\varepsilon_k\), the conditional genie Bayes absolute-error risk is
\[
 \frac Bn\inf_{a\in\mathbb R}\mathbb E|S_K-a|.
\]
The law of \(S_K\) is symmetric, and \((|s-a|+|-s-a|)/2\geq|s|\); averaging proves that zero is a Bayes action.  Hence the risk equals \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb E|S_K|\).  Independence and centering give
\[
 \mathbb ES_K^2=K,
 \qquad \mathbb ES_K^4=K+6{K\choose2}=3K^2-2K\leq3K^2.
\tag{7}
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz yields \(\mathbb E|S_K|\leq\sqrt K\).  H\"older's inequality gives
\[
 K=\mathbb ES_K^2
 \leq(\mathbb E|S_K|)^{2/3}(\mathbb E|S_K|^4)^{1/3},
 \qquad \mathbb E|S_K|\geq\sqrt{K/3}.
\tag{8}
\]
The same inequalities hold at \(K=0\).  Substitution of \(K=I_0(A)\) proves the displayed conditional Bayes-risk bounds.  Averaging over \(A\) gives unconditional scale \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb E\sqrt{I_0(A)}\); since \(\sqrt K\leq K\) for every nonnegative integer \(K\), it is no larger than \(Bn^{-1}\mathbb EI_0(A)\).  This argument neither constructs an unconditionally honest interval nor lower-bounds every such interval, which proves the final qualification.
