Let \(I_0(A)=\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i=0\}\).  Under the unchanged class, any graph-only radius \(b(A)\) that is required to satisfy
\[
 \left|\mathbb E_Z(\widehat\theta-\theta_n\mid A,(f_i)_i)\right|\leq b(A)
\]
for every response configuration in the conditional support must obey
\[
 b(A)\geq \frac{B}{n}I_0(A)
\]
on every realized graph.  Hence the isolate term in the conditional certificate cannot be replaced by \(B\sqrt{\pi_{0,n}/n}\).  The latter is an unconditional missing-sign fluctuation scale, not a conditional identification radius.  This does not rule out a different, unconditionally honest procedure that abandons the graph-only conditional-bias guarantee; no such procedure is constructed here.
