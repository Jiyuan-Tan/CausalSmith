Put
\[
 a_\alpha=\sqrt{2\log(2/\alpha)},\qquad
 R_h(A)=\overline b_h(A)+a_\alpha\overline s_h(A),
\]
\[
 L_h=\widehat\theta_h-R_h(A),\qquad
 U_h=\widehat\theta_h+R_h(A),\qquad
 \operatorname{clip}_{[-B,B]}(t)=\max\{-B,\min\{B,t\}\}.
\]
Define the unprojected interval \(\mathcal J_h=[L_h,U_h]\) and the reported interval
\[
 \mathcal C_h=
 [\operatorname{clip}_{[-B,B]}(L_h),
  \operatorname{clip}_{[-B,B]}(U_h)].
\]
Thus \(\mathcal C_h\) is the endpointwise monotone projection of \(\mathcal J_h\) onto the known parameter range.
