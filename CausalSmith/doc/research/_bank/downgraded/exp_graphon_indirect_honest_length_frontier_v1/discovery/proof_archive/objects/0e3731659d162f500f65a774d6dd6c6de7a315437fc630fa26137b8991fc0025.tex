Let \(\mathcal G=\sigma(A,(f_i)_{i\in\mathcal V_n})\).  By \({\rm thm:explicit-pc-conditional-certificate}\), for every admissible bandwidth \(h\),
\[
 \left|\mathbb E_P(\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n\mid\mathcal G)\right|
 \leq\overline b_h(A),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}_P(\widehat\theta_h\mid\mathcal G)
 \leq\overline s_h(A)^2.
\tag{16}
\]
Writing the error as its conditional mean plus its centered part, conditional Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_P[|\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n|\mid\mathcal G]
 &\leq
 \left|\mathbb E_P(\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n\mid\mathcal G)\right|
 +\mathbb E_P\!left[
  |\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n-mathbb E_P(\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n\mid\mathcal G)|
  \mid\mathcal G\right]\\
 &\leq\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A).
\end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]
Set \(h=h^\dagger\), take expectations, and then take the supremum over \(P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\).  The definition of \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\) in \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) makes the resulting right-hand side exactly \(\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}\).  Thus
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_{h^\dagger}-\theta_n|
 \leq\mathfrak u_{n,d,L},
\tag{18}
\]
which implies the asserted order bound.

Conditional Chebyshev applied to (16) gives
\[
 P\!\left(
 |\widehat\theta_h-\theta_n|>
 \overline b_h(A)+\frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}
 \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal G
 \right)\leq\alpha.
\tag{19}
\]
By \({\rm ass:response-envelope}\) and \({\rm def:target}\), \(|\theta_n|\leq B\).  Consequently the intersection with \([-B,B]\) in \({\rm def:honest-interval}\) cannot remove the target.  Integrating (19), and then taking the infimum over the model class, proves
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal C_{h^\dagger}\}\geq1-\alpha.
\tag{20}
\]
The interval intersection can only shorten length, so pointwise
\[
 \operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_h)
 \leq2\left\{\overline b_h(A)+\frac{\overline s_h(A)}{\sqrt\alpha}\right\}
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}
       \{\overline b_h(A)+\overline s_h(A)\}.
\tag{21}
\]
At \(h=h^\dagger\), take expectation and the supremum over \(P\), and use \({\rm def:frontier-envelope}\) once more to obtain
\[
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{n,d,L}}
 \mathbb E_P[\operatorname{length}(\mathcal C_{h^\dagger})]
 \leq2\max\{1,\alpha^{-1/2}\}\mathfrak u_{n,d,L}.
\tag{22}
\]
Before intersection, the half-length in (19) is exactly the pointwise certificate \(\overline b_{h^\dagger}(A)+\overline s_{h^\dagger}(A)/\sqrt\alpha\); intersection only reduces it.  Equations (18), (20), and (22) prove every assertion.
