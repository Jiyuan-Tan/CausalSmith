Fix \(0\leq m\leq\lfloor d/2\rfloor\) and write
\[
 K_m(A)=\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf1\{N_i\leq m\}.
\]
For every admissible \((U,A,Z)\)-marginal that remains in \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) after adjoining independent Rademacher response signs, there is an explicit odd \(C^3\) degree-lattice bump whose derivative at one half has magnitude at least \(c a_m\), vanishes at every exposure lattice point of every degree at most \(m\), and yields
\[
 \inf_{\widetilde\theta}\mathbb E_P|\widetilde\theta-\theta_n|
 \geq \frac{c a_m}{n}\mathbb E_P\sqrt{K_m(A)}.
\]
Moreover,
\[
 \frac1n\mathbb E_P\sqrt{K_m(A)}
 \leq\sqrt{\frac{n^{-1}\mathbb E_PK_m(A)}{n}}
 \leq\sqrt{\frac{\pi_{m,n}}{n}}.
\]
Thus the frozen mean-mass quantity \(a_m\sqrt{\pi_{m,n}/n}\) is an upper bound on the scale certified by this missing-sign experiment, not a lower bound without an additional count-concentration argument.  For \(m=0\), the certified isolate scale is \(B n^{-1}\mathbb E_P\sqrt{K_0(A)}\).
