On the predeclared sharp-radius class \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\subseteq\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\), evaluate \({\rm def:radii}\) and \({\rm def:optimal-bandwidth}\) at \(L=0\). Then \(h_0=\infty\), \(h_\star=d^{-1/2}\), and the positive-degree Taylor term in \(\overline b_{h_\star}(A)\) is identically zero. The exact moment equation \(\mathbb E_Z[q_{i,h}V_i^2\mid A]=0\) also cancels every quadratic exposure response, with affine responses as a special case. At \(h_\star=d^{-1/2}\), the nonremainder supported-unit score and graph contributions have order \(\sqrt{d/n}\), while the complete degree-exact certificate retains \(\overline b_{h_\star}(A)=Bn^{-1}\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}\mathbf 1\{N_i=0\}+\widehat\Delta_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}\) and \(\overline s_{h_\star}(A)^2=C_{\mathrm{pc}}B^2n^{-2}\{\sum_{i:N_i>0}\mu_{2,i}(h_\star)^{-1}+\sum_{i\in\mathcal V_n}N_i\}+\widehat V_{\mathrm{pc},h_\star}\). An affine realized law inside \(\mathcal M_{n,d,L}\) with \(L>0\) does not permit setting the honesty radius to zero; the reduction applies only when \(\mathcal M_{n,d,0}\) is fixed before observing the experiment.
