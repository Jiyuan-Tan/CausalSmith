Write \(\mathcal O\) for the relative interior of the attainable support.  In a topological ordering of \(G_0\), the triangular change of variables

Conditioning the displayed product density and cancelling its normalizing constant shows that the conditional density on a nonempty fiber is a quotient of finite products of the factors in Definition \(\ref{def:structural-density}\).  Assumptions \(\ref{ass:smooth-mechanisms}\) and \(\ref{ass:smooth-positive-densities}\) therefore make that density strictly positive and three times continuously differentiable on the fiber's relative interior. Thus distinct reverse factorizations cannot be pasted on disconnected pieces. Fix \((G_0,f,p_N,P)\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{pair}}(d)\).  Independence of the innovations and the matching-component condition imply that the random vectors belonging to distinct weak components of \(G_0\) are mutually independent.  Consider an edge component \(u\to v\).  For any set \(B\) made only of nodes in other components, conditional expectation and independence give
\[
 q(v,B)=q(u,B)=0,
\]
\[
 q(v,B\cup\{u\})
 =\frac{\operatorname{Var}\{f_v(V_u)\}}
 {\operatorname{Var}\{f_v(V_u)\}+\operatorname{Var}(N_v)}
 =\rho^{\to}_{uv},
\]
and
\[
 q(u,B\cup\{v\})
 =\frac{\operatorname{Var}\{\mathbb E[V_u\mid V_v]\}}
 {\operatorname{Var}(V_u)}
 =\rho^{\leftarrow}_{uv}.
\]
The divisions are valid by Assumption \(\ref{ass:variance-nondegeneracy}\), which is a member property of \(\mathcal M_0(d)\).

When \(u\) precedes \(v\), the residual after regressing \(V_v\) on \(B\cup\{u\}\) is \(N_v-\mathbb E N_v\).  It is independent of every predecessor by Assumption \(\ref{ass:anm-independence}\), so characteristicness in Definition \(\ref{def:hsic}\) gives \(D(v,B\cup\{u\})=0\).  Thus the edge component contributes \(\rho^{\to}_{uv}\) to \(S_P\).  When \(v\) precedes \(u\), regression on \(B\cup\{v\}\) is the same as regression on \(V_v\).  If its residual were independent of \(V_v\), it would give the reverse additive-noise representation
\[
 V_u=\mathbb E[V_u\mid V_v]+E,
 \qquad E\perp V_v.
\]
The Condition 19 configuration and Lemma \(\ref{lem:compact-identifiability}\) exclude that representation.  The residual is independent of every member of \(B\), however, because \((V_u,V_v)\) is independent of the other components.  Therefore, if \(a=|B|+1\) is the number of predecessors of \(u\),
\[
 D(u,B\cup\{v\})=1,
 \qquad
 \ell_P(u,B\cup\{v\})
 =\rho^{\leftarrow}_{uv}\frac{a}{a+1}
 \le \frac{d-1}{d}\rho^{\leftarrow}_{uv}.
\]

Let \(\pi\notin\Pi(G_0)\).  Some edge component \(u\to v\) is reversed in \(\pi\).  Move \(u\) from its rank to the rank immediately before \(v\).  Since \(u\) has no descendant other than \(v\), this removes the ancestral reversal \((u,v)\) and creates none, so the move belongs to \(\operatorname{Corr}_{G_0}(\pi)\).  The contribution of this component changes from at most \((d-1)\rho^{\leftarrow}_{uv}/d\) to \(\rho^{\to}_{uv}\).  For each node \(w\) strictly traversed by the insertion, the new predecessor set is its old predecessor set with the independent variable \(V_u\) added.  Its conditional mean, explained risk, and dependence count are unchanged, while
\[
 1-\frac{D(w,A)}{|A|+2}\ge 1-\frac{D(w,A)}{|A|+1}.
\]
All other local terms are unchanged.  Definitions \(\ref{def:gene-score}\) and \(\ref{def:insertion-gain}\) consequently yield
\[
 C_P(\pi;r,s)
 \ge \rho^{\to}_{uv}-\frac{d-1}{d}\rho^{\leftarrow}_{uv}
 \ge\delta_{\mathrm{pair}}>0,
\]
where strict positivity follows from Assumption \(\ref{ass:pair-signal-dominance}\) and finiteness of the edge set.  (If the graph has no edges, every order is topological and the assertion about non-topological orders is vacuous.)  Finally, an insertion-local maximizer cannot be non-topological, because the construction above would supply a strictly improving insertion.  Hence every insertion-local maximizer is topological.

Run the map in Definition \(\ref{def:oracle-recovery}\) from an arbitrary \(\pi\in\mathfrak S_I\).  Every accepted insertion has strictly positive gain, so \(S_P\) strictly increases.  An order can therefore never be revisited.  Since \(|\mathfrak S_I|=d!\), at most \(d!-1\) insertions are accepted. It remains to verify pruning.  In a topological order on a matching graph, a nonparent predecessor \(u\) of \(v\) lies in another independent component.  As long as the current ordered parent set \(A\) contains the true parent when one exists,
\[

 =\mathbb E[V_v\mid V_{\operatorname{pa}_0(v)}],
\]
and its residual is a centered innovation, independent of \(V_u\).  Hence \(H(v,A\setminus\{u\},u)=0\), and every nonedge is deleted.  If \(u\) is the true parent of \(v\), all variables in \(A\setminus\{u\}\) are from independent components, so the tested residual is \(V_v-\mathbb E V_v\).  Were it independent of \(V_u\), the pair would admit both the stated nonconstant forward ANM and an independence factorization, contrary to causal minimality and the Condition 19 identification conclusion in Lemma \(\ref{lem:compact-identifiability}\).  Characteristicness therefore gives \(H(v,A\setminus\{u\},u)>0\), so no true edge is deleted.  This invariant also shows that the conclusion is independent of the deletion order.  The output is exactly \(G_0\).

There are \(d(d-1)\) ordered insertions at each visited order, including the terminal order.  At most \(d!\) orders are visited, giving at most \(d(d-1)d!\) gain comparisons.  Every score is determined by the table
\[
 \{R_{v,A}:v\in I,\ A\subseteq I\setminus\{v\}\},
\]
which has \(d2^{d-1}\) entries, and the score-relevant table
\[
 \{H(v,A,u):v\in I,\ A\subseteq I\setminus\{v\},\ u\in A\},
\]
which has
\[
 d(d-1)2^{d-2}
\]
entries.  Memoization therefore gives the asserted risk and score-HSIC query bounds.  A complete ordered DAG has at most \(d(d-1)/2\) arrows, so pruning requires at most that many additional HSIC checks.

On a held-out fold, define \(\widehat R_{v,A}\) as the average squared residual formed with the off-fold fit.  Conditional on that fit, orthogonality of conditional expectation gives
\[
 \mathbb E[(V_v-\widehat\mu_{v,A}(V_A))^2\mid\widehat\mu_{v,A}]
 =R_{v,A}+\|\widehat\mu_{v,A}-\mu_{v,A}\|_{L^2(P_A)}^2. \tag{12}
\]
Hoeffding's inequality for bounded held-out losses, a union bound, (11), and (12) produce simultaneous intervals containing every \(R_{v,A}\) with half-width
\[
 w_R\le K\left\{\sqrt{\frac{\log(M_d/\alpha)}n}+a_n^2\right\}. \tag{13}
\]

Next compute bounded-kernel HSIC as its usual centered order-four V-statistic on a held-out fold.  Replacing one held-out observation changes it by at most \(K/n\), so the bounded-difference inequality and a union bound give fluctuation at most \(K\sqrt{\log(M_d/\alpha)/n}\).  Its diagonal V-statistic bias is \(O(n^{-1})\), which is absorbed by this term.  If \(e_i\) and \(\widehat e_i\) are the true and fitted residuals, Assumption \(\ref{ass:residual-kernel-lipschitz}\), telescoping the finite product of kernel factors, and Cauchy--Schwarz give
\[
 |\operatorname{HSIC}(\widehat e,V_u)-\operatorname{HSIC}(e,V_u)|
 \le K\mathbb E|\widehat e-e|
 \le K\|\widehat\mu_{v,A}-\mu_{v,A}\|_{L^2(P_A)}.
\]
Together with (11), this gives simultaneous score-relevant HSIC bands with
\[
 w_H\le K\left\{\sqrt{\frac{\log(M_d/\alpha)}n}+a_n\right\}. \tag{14}
\]
The same construction is made for the pruning functionals \(H(v,A,u)\) with \(u\notin A\).  There are at most another \(d(d-1)2^{d-2}\) of them; replacing \(M_d\) by at most \(2M_d\) changes only the universal multiplicative constant in (13)--(14), since \(\log(2M_d/\alpha)\le2\log(M_d/\alpha)\).  Thus every adaptively queried pruning value is covered on the same event.

Suppose now that all queried HSIC values lie in \(\{0\}\cup[\lambda,\infty)\) and \(2w_H<\lambda\).  If the true value is zero, coverage implies that the interval's upper endpoint is below \(\lambda\); if it is at least \(\lambda\), the lower endpoint is strictly positive.  Hence every set-valued decision is the correct singleton.  In particular, the discontinuous indicator is used only after separation; when an interval meets both regions Definition \(\ref{def:simultaneous-bands}\) retains both labels.

Under singleton dependence decisions, interval arithmetic applied to Definitions \(\ref{def:correlation-ratio}\)--\(\ref{def:insertion-gain}\) contains every true gain, because Assumption \(\ref{ass:variance-nondegeneracy}\) keeps all variance denominators away from zero.  At any non-topological order, Theorem \(\ref{thm:pairwise-navigation}\) supplies a correcting gain at least \(\delta_{\mathrm{pair}}\).  Every score table compatible with the bands assigns that same gain a value at least \(\delta_{\mathrm{pair}}-2w_S>0\).  Strict ascent on the finite permutation set therefore makes every compatible terminal order topological.  The singleton pruning decisions are exactly those in Theorem \(\ref{thm:oracle-recovery}\), so every compatible terminal DAG equals \(G_0\).  Their union is \(\widehat{\mathcal G}_n=\{G_0\}\).

If a band meets both \(0\) and \([\lambda,\infty)\), the construction records both labels (and also does so after an empty intersection), so it never converts proximity to the discontinuity into a false binary assertion.  Taking the union over all compatible paths proves the final, deliberately set-valued conclusion.  Such a union can still be a singleton if the ambiguous functional is irrelevant to every terminal pruning decision; therefore no unconditional multiplicity claim is made.
