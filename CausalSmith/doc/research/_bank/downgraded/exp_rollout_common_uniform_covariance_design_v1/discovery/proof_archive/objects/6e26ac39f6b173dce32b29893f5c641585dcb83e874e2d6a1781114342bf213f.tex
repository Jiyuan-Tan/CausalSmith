The attainment part can be closed completely.  For a singleton \(S=\{i\}\), define \(r_j=\sum_{t=j}^kw_t\) and \(r_{k+1}=0\).  Polynomial exactness gives \(\sum_tw_tp_t=1\).  Since \(\sum_tw_t\mathbf1\{U_i\le p_t\}=r_j\) on \((p_{j-1},p_j]\) and is zero on \((q,1]\), exact integration yields
\[
G(p,w)_{S,S}
=\sum_{j=1}^k(p_j-p_{j-1})(r_j-1)^2+(1-q)
\ge\delta\sum_{j=1}^k(r_j-1)^2.
\]
Furthermore,
\[
\lambda_{\max}\{\Omega^{1/2}G(p,w)\Omega^{1/2}\}
\ge\omega_1G(p,w)_{S,S}.
\]
Thus every bounded objective sublevel uniformly bounds all \(r_j\), hence all \(w_j=r_j-r_{j+1}\) for \(j\ge1\), and also \(w_0=-r_1\).  The node polytope is compact, the joint exactness constraints are closed, and the covariance objective is continuous.  A minimizing sequence therefore has a feasible convergent subsequence, proving that \(V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta}\) is attained for every declared feasible parameter tuple.  At any minimizer, the fixed-node semidefinite primal in the proof of thm:exact-covariance-saddle and its ordinary semidefinite dual give a matching certificate for optimization over \(w\) conditional on those nodes.  What remains unresolved is a matching global certificate over \(p\) and the resulting Chebyshev--Lobatto parameter classification.
