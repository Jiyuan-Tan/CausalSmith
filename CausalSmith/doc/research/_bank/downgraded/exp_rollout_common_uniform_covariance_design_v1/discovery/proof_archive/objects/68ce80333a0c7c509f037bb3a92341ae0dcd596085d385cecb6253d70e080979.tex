For every finite \(n\), \(1\le\beta\le n\), positive \(B\), and positive order weights \(\omega\), \(\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)\subsetneq\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}\). Strictness is witnessed by the published-class schedule \(c_{i,\{1\}}=2B\sqrt{\omega_1/n}\) for every \(i\in I_n\), with all other coefficients zero: its nonzero aggregate coefficient satisfies \(a_{\{1\}}=2B\sqrt{\omega_1/n}\) and hence \(a^{\mathsf T}\Omega^{-1}a=4B^2/n>B^2/n\).
