For every declared feasible tuple and every \(p\in\mathcal P_{k,q,\delta}\), \(w\in\mathcal W_\beta(p)\), define \(r_j=\sum_{t=j}^kw_t\).  For every singleton \(S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\),
\[
G(p,w)_{S,S}=\sum_{j=1}^k(p_j-p_{j-1})(r_j-1)^2+(1-q)
\ge\delta\sum_{j=1}^k(r_j-1)^2
\quad\text{and}\quad
G(p,w)_{S,S}\ge\frac{1-q}{q}.
\]
Consequently, if \(0<q<1\), then
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta}\ge
\frac{B^2\omega_1}{n}\frac{1-q}{q}.
\]
At \(q=1\), \(V^*_{n,\beta,k,1,\delta}=0\), attained by \(w_0=-1\), \(w_k=1\), and zero intermediate weights.  If \(\beta=1\), then for every \(0<q\le1\),
\[
V^*_{n,1,k,q,\delta}=\frac{B^2\omega_1}{n}\frac{1-q}{q},
\]
and, for every fixed feasible \(p\), the unique minimizing weights are \(w_0=-1/q\), \(w_k=1/q\), and \(w_t=0\) for \(0<t<k\).
