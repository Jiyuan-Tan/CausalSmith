For every feasible \((n,\beta,k,q,\delta,B,\omega)\) with \(1\le\beta\le n\),
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta}
=\min_{p\in\mathcal P_{k,q,\delta}}
\min_{w\in\mathcal W_\beta(p)}V_{\mathrm{rob}}(p,w),
\]
and at least one joint minimizer exists.  For every fixed \(p\in\mathcal P_{k,q,\delta}\), the minimum over \(w\in\mathcal W_\beta(p)\) is also attained.
