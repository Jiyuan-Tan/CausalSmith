Let \((Y,c)\) belong to the graph union.  Then it has a witnessing static directed graph \(E\), every nonzero coefficient \(c_{i,S}\) is supported on \(S\subseteq\mathcal N_i(E)\), and every displayed nonempty set has cardinality at most \(\beta\).  Extending the graph-supported sum by the zero off-neighborhood coefficients gives
\[
Y_i(z)=c_{i,\varnothing}+
\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}c_{i,S}\prod_{j\in S}z_j.
\]
Thus the graph union is contained in the unrestricted algebraic class, and the same equation verifies Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}low\text{-}order\text{-}schedule}\).

Conversely, let \((Y,c)\) satisfy the unrestricted algebraic display in the statement.  Choose the complete directed graph \(E=I_n\times I_n\).  It contains all self-loops and has \(\mathcal N_i(E)=I_n\) for every \(i\).  Hence every \(S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\) is a subset of every \(\mathcal N_i(E)\); there are no off-neighborhood coefficients to eliminate, and the unrestricted display is exactly the graph-supported expansion in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}published\text{-}low\text{-}order\text{-}class}\).  Therefore \((Y,c)\) belongs to the union.  The two inclusions prove the equality.
