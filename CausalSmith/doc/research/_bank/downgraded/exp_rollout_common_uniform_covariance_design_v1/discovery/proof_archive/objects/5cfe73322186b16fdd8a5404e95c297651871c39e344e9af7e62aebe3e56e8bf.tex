Fix \(0<q<1\), a feasible \(p\), and \(w\in\mathcal W_\beta(p)\).  Because \(\beta\ge1\), \(\{1\}\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\).  For \(A\in\mathbb R\), set
\[
c^{(A)}_{i,\{1\}}=A\quad(i\in I_n),
\qquad
c^{(A)}_{i,R}=0
\quad\text{for }R\ne\{1\},
\]
and let \(Y_i^{(A)}(z)=Az_1\).  This is a fixed degree-one schedule.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}cey\text{-}envelope\text{-}union\text{-}equivalence}\), whose reverse inclusion is witnessed by the complete directed graph, shows that \((Y^{(A)},c^{(A)})\in\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}\) for every finite \(A\).

For this schedule, \(M(r)=AZ_1(r)\), so Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}common\text{-}uniform\text{-}monomial\text{-}covariance}\) and Proposition~\(\mathrm{prop{:}rollout\text{-}budget\text{-}elbow}\) give
\[
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
=A^2G(p,w)_{\{1\},\{1\}}
\ge A^2\frac{1-q}{q}.
\tag{41}
\]
Since \((1-q)/q>0\), letting \(|A|\to\infty\) proves that the inner supremum is \(+\infty\) for every feasible \((p,w)\), and taking the two infima does not change it.

If \(q=1\), choose \(w_0=-1\), \(w_k=1\), and zero intermediate weights.  These weights are polynomial-exact for every order up to \(\beta\).  Under the common-uniform design, \(Z(0)=\mathbf0\) and \(Z(1)=\mathbf1\) almost surely.  Hence, for every fixed finite schedule in the noise-free graph-union envelope,
\[
\widehat\tau_{p,w}=M(1)-M(0)
=\frac1n\sum_{i\in I_n}\{Y_i(\mathbf1)-Y_i(\mathbf0)\}
=\tau
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
The design variance is zero, and nonnegativity proves that the minimax value is zero.  The divergence in (41) uses the absence of a common coefficient or outcome radius in the union.  It says nothing about a uniformly bounded subclass or about the independent Gaussian observation-noise component of the full published model.
