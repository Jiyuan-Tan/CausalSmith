After the fixed-node semidefinite minimization, the remaining value function is a generally nonconvex and nonsmooth function of the ordered nodes: the moment matrix depends polynomially on the nodes, while the maximum over isotypic leading eigenvalues changes active blocks at eigenvalue ties.  Active-block KKT equations certify only stationarity without a global convexity argument.  The frozen primitives provide neither such convexity nor a global polynomial or semidefinite dual certificate.  Moreover, prop:quadratic-witness compares two nodes for one legal schedule, not the supremum over the ellipsoid, so it cannot determine a robust Chebyshev--Lobatto region.
