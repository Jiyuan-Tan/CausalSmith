Let \(h=q/k>0\), so \(\bar p_t=th\).  Every gap of \(\bar p\) is \(h\ge\delta\), while \(\bar p_0=0\) and \(\bar p_k=q\); hence Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}feasible\text{-}node\text{-}class}\) gives \(\bar p\in\mathcal P_{k,q,\delta}\).

For \(0\le t\le\beta\), define
\[
\ell_t(x)=\prod_{\substack{0\le j\le\beta\\j\ne t}}
\frac{x-jh}{(t-j)h}.
\tag{22}
\]
The denominators are nonzero because \(h>0\).  The Lagrange interpolation identity, which follows by observing that the difference of its two sides is a degree-at-most-\(\beta\) polynomial vanishing at the \(\beta+1\) distinct nodes, is
\[
a(x)=\sum_{t=0}^\beta a(th)\ell_t(x)
\quad\text{for every polynomial \(a\) of degree at most \(\beta\)}.
\tag{23}
\]
Taking the difference of (23) at \(x=1\) and \(x=0\) gives
\[
\sum_{t=0}^k\bar w_ta(\bar p_t)=a(1)-a(0).
\tag{24}
\]
For \(a(x)=1\), equation (24) gives \(\sum_t\bar w_t=0\); for \(a(x)=x^d\), \(1\le d\le\beta\), it gives \(\sum_t\bar w_t\bar p_t^d=1\).  Thus Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}unbiased\text{-}weight\text{-}class}\) yields \(\bar w\in\mathcal W_\beta(\bar p)\).

Because \(\ell_t(0)=\mathbf 1\{t=0\}\), the triangle inequality gives
\[
\|\bar w\|_1
\le1+\sum_{t=0}^\beta|\ell_t(1)|.
\tag{25}
\]
The denominator product in (22) has absolute value \(h^\beta t!(\beta-t)!\).  Moreover, \(0\le jh=jq/k\le1\) for \(0\le j\le\beta\), because \(q\le1\) and \(\beta\le k\).  Hence \(|1-jh|\le1\), and
\[
|\ell_t(1)|
\le\frac{h^{-\beta}}{t!(\beta-t)!}.
\]
Summing and using \(\sum_{t=0}^\beta\{t!(\beta-t)!\}^{-1}=2^\beta/\beta!\),
\[
\sum_{t=0}^\beta|\ell_t(1)|
\le\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}q^{-\beta}.
\]
Since \(q^{-\beta}\ge1\), (25) proves
\[
\|\bar w\|_1
\le\left(1+\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}\right)q^{-\beta}.
\tag{26}
\]

Now take any feasible \(p\), nonempty \(S,T\), and stages \(s,t\).  Write the two nonnegative terms in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}exact\text{-}covariance\text{-}operator}\) as
\[
A_{st}=\min(p_s,p_t)^{|S\cap T|}p_s^{|S\setminus T|}p_t^{|T\setminus S|},
\qquad
B_{st}=p_s^{|S|}p_t^{|T|}.
\]
All node coordinates lie in \([0,q]\).  If \(S\cap T\ne\varnothing\), then \(A_{st}\le\min(p_s,p_t)\le q\).  If \(S\cap T=\varnothing\), nonemptiness of both \(S\) and \(T\) gives \(A_{st}\le p_sp_t\le q^2\le q\).  Likewise \(B_{st}\le p_sp_t\le q\).  Therefore
\[
|K_{st}(S,T)|=|A_{st}-B_{st}|
\le A_{st}+B_{st}\le2q.
\tag{27}
\]

Apply (27) to the uniform construction.  Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}exact\text{-}covariance\text{-}operator}\), the triangle inequality, and (26) yield, for all \(S,T\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
|G(\bar p,\bar w)_{S,T}|
&\le\sum_{s,t=0}^k|\bar w_s||\bar w_t||K_{st}(S,T)|\\
&\le2q\|\bar w\|_1^2\\
&\le2\left(1+\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}\right)^2q^{1-2\beta}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{28}
\]
Set \(C=1+2^\beta k^\beta/\beta!\) and \(A=\Omega^{1/2}G(\bar p,\bar w)\Omega^{1/2}\).  Since \(\Omega\) is diagonal, (28) and the definition of \(\omega_{\max}\) imply
\[
|A_{S,T}|\le2\omega_{\max}C^2q^{1-2\beta}.
\tag{29}
\]
For any \(x\in\mathbb R^D\) with \(\|x\|_2=1\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives \(\|x\|_1^2\le D\), so
\[
|x^{\mathsf T}Ax|
\le\max_{S,T}|A_{S,T}|\,\|x\|_1^2
\le2D\omega_{\max}C^2q^{1-2\beta}.
\tag{30}
\]
The matrix \(A\) is symmetric by the displayed kernel formula.  Taking a unit eigenvector for its largest eigenvalue in (30) proves the asserted eigenvalue bound.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}subset\text{-}lattice\text{-}block\text{-}reduction}\) and Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}variance\text{-}certificate}\) therefore give
\[
V_{\mathrm{rob}}(\bar p,\bar w)
\le\frac{2B^2D\omega_{\max}}{n}C^2q^{1-2\beta}.
\]
Finally, the candidate \((\bar p,\bar w)\) is feasible, so Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}exact\text{-}saddle}\) bounds its twofold infimum by this candidate value, proving the final display.
