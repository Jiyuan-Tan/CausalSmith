For the unnormalized published schedule envelope,
\[
\inf_{p\in\mathcal P_{k,q,\delta}}
\inf_{w\in\mathcal W_\beta(p)}
\sup_{(Y,c)\in\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}}
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
=
\begin{cases}
+\infty,&0<q<1,\\
0,&q=1.
\end{cases}
\]
For \(0<q<1\), the divergence is witnessed by the legal singleton schedules \(c_{i,\{1\}}=A\), with all other coefficients zero, as \(|A|\to\infty\).  Thus a common coefficient or outcome radius is necessary for a finite robust precision guarantee below full rollout.  This statement concerns the unnormalized schedule-envelope union, not the full fixed-graph, noisy model of CortezEichhornYu2022 and not a uniformly \(Y_{\max}\)-bounded comparator class.
