At \(n=3\), \(\beta=k=2\), and \(q=1/2\), the legal quadratic witness has risk \(4(1-x)/\{9(1-2x)\}\), so its value is \(14/27\) at \(x=1/8\) and \(2/3\) at \(x=1/4\).
