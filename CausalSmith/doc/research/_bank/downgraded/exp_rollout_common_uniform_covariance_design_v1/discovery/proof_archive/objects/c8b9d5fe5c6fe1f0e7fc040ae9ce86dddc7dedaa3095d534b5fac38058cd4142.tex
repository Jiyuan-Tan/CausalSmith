CortezEichhornYu2022 derives unbiased polynomial-interpolation estimators and spacing-based variance bounds under static-neighborhood low-order outcomes, a common-uniform rollout, per-schedule magnitude and neighborhood-size controls, and independent Gaussian observation noise.  It does not retain the full subset-intersection covariance operator, solve the joint exact-covariance node--weight minimax problem, or provide a legal least-favourable schedule for a common robust class.
