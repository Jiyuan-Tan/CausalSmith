Positivity of the finitely many order weights gives \(\omega_{\min}>0\), so \(B^\dagger\) is well defined.  For each \(S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\), the aggregate coefficient identity and the triangle inequality give
\[
|a_S|\le\frac1n\sum_{i\in I_n}|c_{i,S}|.
\]
Summing this inequality over \(S\), interchanging the two finite sums, and using the uniform bound yield
\[
\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}|a_S|
\le\frac1n\sum_{i\in I_n}\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}|c_{i,S}|
\le L.
\tag{76}
\]
Because \(\Omega\) is diagonal with entry \(\omega_{|S|}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
a^{\mathsf T}\Omega^{-1}a
&=\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}\frac{a_S^2}{\omega_{|S|}}\\
&\le\frac1{\omega_{\min}}\sum_Sa_S^2
\le\frac1{\omega_{\min}}\left(\sum_S|a_S|\right)^2
\le\frac{L^2}{\omega_{\min}}
=\frac{(B^\dagger)^2}{n}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{77}
\]
Membership in the noise-free CEY graph union is assumed, and (77) is exactly the additional ellipsoid condition.  Hence Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ellipsoidal\text{-}coefficient\text{-}class}\) gives \((Y,c)\in\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B^\dagger,\omega)\).  Applying Theorem~\(\mathrm{thm{:}finite\text{-}sample\text{-}robust\text{-}inference}\) with \(B=B^\dagger\) proves unbiasedness, the conservative variance certificate, and the stated coverage.  This is an inclusion-based conservative transfer; it does not assert that the ellipsoidal least-favourable schedule is least favourable for the uniformly bounded subclass.
