For \(q\in(0,1]\), define the \(\beta\)-by-\(\beta\) matrix and scalar
\[
R_\beta(q)_{d,e}=\frac{de\,q^{d+e-1}}{d+e-1}
\quad(1\le d,e\le\beta),
\qquad
\Psi_\beta(q)=\mathbf 1_\beta^{\mathsf T}R_\beta(q)^{-1}\mathbf 1_\beta-1,
\]
whenever the displayed inverse exists, where \(\mathbf 1_\beta\in\mathbb R^\beta\) is the all-ones vector.
