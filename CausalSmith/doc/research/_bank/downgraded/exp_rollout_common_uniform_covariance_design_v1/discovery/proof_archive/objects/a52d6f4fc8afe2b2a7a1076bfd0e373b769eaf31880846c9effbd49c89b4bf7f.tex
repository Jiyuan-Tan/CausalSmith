Under def:quadratic-witness,
\[
M(r)=\frac13 Z_1(r)Z_2(r).
\]
By ass:common-uniform-rollout, or by lem:common-uniform-monomial-covariance with \(S=T=\{1,2\}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Gamma(u,v)
&=\frac19\left[\mathbb E_U\{Z_1(u)Z_2(u)Z_1(v)Z_2(v)\}-u^2v^2\right]\\
&=\frac19\left\{\min(u,v)^2-u^2v^2\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]
Let the weights at \(x\) and \(1/2\) be \(w_x\) and \(w_{1/2}\).  The degree-one and degree-two equations in def:unbiased-weight-class are
\[
xw_x+\frac12w_{1/2}=1,
\qquad
x^2w_x+\frac14w_{1/2}=1.
\]
Their determinant is \(x(1-2x)/4>0\) for \(x\in[1/8,3/8]\), so their unique solution is
\[
w_x=-\frac1{x(1-2x)},
\qquad
w_{1/2}=\frac{4(1-x)}{1-2x}.
\tag{18}
\]
The remaining equation uniquely sets \(w_0=-w_x-w_{1/2}\); it contributes no random term because \(M(0)=0\).

Using (17) at the three required pairs gives
\[
9\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
=w_x^2(x^2-x^4)
+2w_xw_{1/2}\frac{3x^2}{4}
+w_{1/2}^2\frac3{16}.
\]
Substitution of (18) and collection over \((1-2x)^2\) give
\[
9\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
=\frac{1-x^2-6x(1-x)+3(1-x)^2}{(1-2x)^2}
=\frac{4(1-x)}{1-2x}.
\tag{19}
\]
Thus the asserted variance formula follows.  Its derivative on the specified interval is
\[
\frac{d}{dx}\frac{4(1-x)}{9(1-2x)}
=\frac4{9(1-2x)^2}>0,
\]
so it is strictly increasing.  Direct evaluation of (19) gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})\big|_{x=1/8}=\frac{14}{27},
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})\big|_{x=1/4}=\frac23.
\]
This proves the proposition and also verifies the smallest-instance arithmetic over the common-uniform design.
