The paper's design is a noise-free finite-population common-uniform nested Bernoulli rollout: independent \(U_i\sim\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]\) variables are drawn once and \(Z_i(p_t)=\mathbf1\{U_i\le p_t\}\) at every stage.  The noisy observation layer in CortezEichhornYu2022 is comparator context, not part of this note's randomization law.
