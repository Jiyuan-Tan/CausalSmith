Fix \(0<q<1\), a feasible \(p\), and \(w\in\mathcal W_\beta(p)\).  Since \(\beta\ge1\), the singleton \(\{1\}\) belongs to \(\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\).  For each \(A\in\mathbb R\), define the fixed schedule
\[
c^{(A)}_{i,\{1\}}=A\quad(i\in I_n),
\qquad
c^{(A)}_{i,R}=0\quad(R\ne\{1\}),
\qquad
Y_i^{(A)}(z)=Az_1.
\tag{18}
\]
This schedule obeys Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}fixed\text{-}finite\text{-}population}\) and has degree one, so it obeys Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}low\text{-}order\text{-}schedule}\).  The complete-graph direction of Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}cey\text{-}envelope\text{-}union\text{-}equivalence}\) shows that (18) belongs to \(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}\) for every finite \(A\).

For (18), \(M(r)=AZ_1(r)\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}common\text{-}uniform\text{-}monomial\text{-}covariance}\) and Proposition~\(\mathrm{prop{:}rollout\text{-}budget\text{-}elbow}\) therefore give
\[
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
=A^2G(p,w)_{\{1\},\{1\}}
\ge A^2\frac{1-q}{q}.
\tag{19}
\]
The factor \((1-q)/q\) is strictly positive.  Letting \(|A|\to\infty\) in (19) proves that the inner supremum is \(+\infty\) for every feasible \((p,w)\); taking the two infima leaves the value \(+\infty\).

Now let \(q=1\).  For an arbitrary feasible node vector, choose \(w_0=-1\), \(w_k=1\), and \(w_t=0\) for \(0<t<k\).  The weights sum to zero and, for every \(1\le d\le\beta\),
\[
\sum_{t=0}^kw_tp_t^d=-0^d+1^d=1,
\]
so they belong to \(\mathcal W_\beta(p)\).  Under Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}common\text{-}uniform\text{-}rollout}\), \(Z(0)=\mathbf0\) and \(Z(1)=\mathbf1\) almost surely.  Thus, for every fixed finite schedule in the noise-free graph-union envelope,
\[
\widehat\tau_{p,w}
=M(1)-M(0)
=\frac1n\sum_{i\in I_n}\{Y_i(\mathbf1)-Y_i(\mathbf0)\}
=\tau
\quad\text{almost surely}.
\tag{20}
\]
The design variance in (20) is zero.  Since every variance is nonnegative, this feasible endpoint construction proves that the minimax value is exactly zero.  The divergence in (19) uses only the absence of a common coefficient or outcome radius in the noise-free schedule-envelope union; it makes no assertion about a uniformly bounded subclass or about observation noise in the full published model.
