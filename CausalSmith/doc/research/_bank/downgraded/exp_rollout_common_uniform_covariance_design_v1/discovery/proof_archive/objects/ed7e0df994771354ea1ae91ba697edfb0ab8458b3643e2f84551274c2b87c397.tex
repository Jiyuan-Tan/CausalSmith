For every declared feasible tuple, let \(R_\beta(q)\) and \(\Psi_\beta(q)\) be as in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}degree\text{-}moment\text{-}barrier}\).  For \(p\in\mathcal P_{k,q,\delta}\), \(w\in\mathcal W_\beta(p)\), define
\[
f(u)=\sum_{t=0}^kw_t\mathbf 1\{u\le p_t\},
\qquad r_j=\sum_{t=j}^kw_t\quad(1\le j\le k).
\]
Then \(R_\beta(q)\) is positive definite and, for \(d=1,\ldots,\beta\),
\[
\int_0^q d u^{d-1}f(u)\,du=1.
\]
For every singleton \(S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
G(p,w)_{S,S}
&=\int_0^q f(u)^2\,du-1\\
&=\sum_{j=1}^k(p_j-p_{j-1})(r_j-1)^2+(1-q)\\
&\ge \Psi_\beta(q)\ge\frac{1-q}{q},
\qquad
G(p,w)_{S,S}\ge\delta\sum_{j=1}^k(r_j-1)^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Consequently,
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta}\ge
\frac{B^2\omega_1}{n}\Psi_\beta(q).
\]
Moreover, \(\Psi_1(q)=(1-q)/q\); if \(\beta\ge2\) and \(0<q<1\), then
\[
\Psi_\beta(q)>\frac{1-q}{q},
\qquad
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta}>
\frac{B^2\omega_1}{n}\Psi_\beta(q),
\]
and, for each fixed \(\beta\),
\[
\Psi_\beta(q)\sim
\frac{2\beta-1}{\beta^2}
\binom{2\beta-2}{\beta-1}^{\!2}q^{1-2\beta}
\qquad(q\downarrow0).
\]
At \(q=1\), the set of all joint minimizers is exactly
\[
\left\{(p,w):p\in\mathcal P_{k,1,\delta},\quad
w_0=-1,\quad w_k=1,\quad w_t=0\ (0<t<k)\right\},
\]
and \(V^*_{n,\beta,k,1,\delta}=0\).  If \(\beta=1\) and \(0<q<1\), every feasible node vector is jointly minimizing and its unique minimizing weights are \(w_0=-1/q\), \(w_k=1/q\), and \(w_t=0\) for \(0<t<k\); hence
\[
V^*_{n,1,k,q,\delta}=\frac{B^2\omega_1}{n}\frac{1-q}{q}.
\]

Finally, fix \(n,\beta,k,B,\omega\) with \(1\le\beta\le n\), \(k\ge\beta\), \(B>0\), and \(\omega_d>0\), and write
\[
D=|\mathcal S_{n,\beta}|,\qquad
\omega_{\max}=\max_{1\le d\le\beta}\omega_d,\qquad
A_\beta=\frac{2\beta-1}{\beta^2}\binom{2\beta-2}{\beta-1}^{\!2}.
\]
There exists \(q_0>0\), depending only on \(\beta\), such that for every \(0<q\le q_0\) and every \(0<\delta\le q/k\),
\[
\frac{B^2\omega_1A_\beta}{2n}q^{1-2\beta}
\le V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta}
\le \frac{2B^2D\omega_{\max}}{n}
\left(1+\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}\right)^2q^{1-2\beta}.
\]
Consequently, for fixed \((n,\beta,k,B,\omega)\),
\(V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}=\Theta(q^{1-2\beta})\) as \(q\downarrow0\), uniformly over all spacing rules satisfying \(0<\delta(q)\le q/k\).  The constants are not uniform in \(k\), and this assertion does not identify the exact leading constant.
