For every finite \(n\in\mathbb N\) and \(1\leq\beta\leq n\), let
\[
\mathfrak G_n
=
\bigl\{E\subseteq I_n\times I_n:(i,i)\in E
\text{ for every }i\in I_n\bigr\},
\qquad
\mathcal N_i(E)=\{j\in I_n:(j,i)\in E\}.
\]
Define the zero-noise graph-union class
\[
\mathcal U_{n,\beta}
=
\bigcup_{E\in\mathfrak G_n}
\left\{(Y,c):
\begin{array}{l}
c_{i,S}=0\text{ whenever }i\in I_n,\ S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta},
\text{ and }S\nsubseteq\mathcal N_i(E),\\
Y_i(z)=c_{i,\varnothing}+
\displaystyle\sum_{\substack{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\\S\subseteq\mathcal N_i(E)}}
c_{i,S}\prod_{j\in S}z_j
\text{ for every }i\in I_n\text{ and }z\in\{0,1\}^n
\end{array}
\right\}
\]
and the unrestricted algebraic degree-at-most-\(\beta\) class
\[
\mathcal A_{n,\beta}
=
\left\{(Y,c):
Y_i(z)=c_{i,\varnothing}
+\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}c_{i,S}\prod_{j\in S}z_j
\text{ for every }i\in I_n\text{ and }z\in\{0,1\}^n
\right\}.
\]
Then \(\mathcal U_{n,\beta}=\mathcal A_{n,\beta}\).
