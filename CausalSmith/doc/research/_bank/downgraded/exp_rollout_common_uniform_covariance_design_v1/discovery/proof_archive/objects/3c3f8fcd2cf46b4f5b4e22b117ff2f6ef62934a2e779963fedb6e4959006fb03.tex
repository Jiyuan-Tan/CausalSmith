Fix \(S,T\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\) and write
\[
X_S(r)=\prod_{i\in S}Z_i(r).
\]
By Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}common\text{-}uniform\text{-}rollout}\), the coordinates of \(U\) are independent and \(\Pr_U(U_i\le r)=r\).  Hence
\[
\mathbb E_U X_S(p_s)=p_s^{|S|}.
\]
For a coordinate in \(S\cap T\),
\[
\mathbf 1\{U_i\le p_s\}\mathbf 1\{U_i\le p_t\}
=\mathbf 1\{U_i\le\min(p_s,p_t)\}.
\]
Coordinates in \(S\setminus T\) and \(T\setminus S\) occur at only one stage.  Independence across coordinates therefore gives
\[
\mathbb E_U\{X_S(p_s)X_T(p_t)\}
=\min(p_s,p_t)^{|S\cap T|}p_s^{|S\setminus T|}p_t^{|T\setminus S|}.
\]
Subtracting \(\mathbb E_U X_S(p_s)\mathbb E_U X_T(p_t)=p_s^{|S|}p_t^{|T|}\) proves the displayed covariance formula, which is \(K_{st}(S,T)\) by Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}exact\text{-}covariance\text{-}operator}\).

By Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}low\text{-}order\text{-}schedule}\) and the definition of \(a\), finite summation over units yields
\[
M(r)=\bar c_{\varnothing}+\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}a_SX_S(r),
\qquad
\bar c_{\varnothing}=\frac1n\sum_{i\in I_n}c_{i,\varnothing}.
\]
Consequently, Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}rollout\text{-}estimator}\) gives
\[
\widehat\tau_{p,w}
=\bar c_{\varnothing}\sum_{t=0}^kw_t
+\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}a_S\sum_{t=0}^kw_tX_S(p_t).
\]
The first term is nonrandom.  Bilinearity of covariance, the monomial identity just proved, and finiteness of \(\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
&=\sum_{S,T}a_Sa_T\sum_{s,t=0}^kw_sw_tK_{st}(S,T)\\
&=\sum_{S,T}a_SG(p,w)_{S,T}a_T
=a^{\mathsf T}G(p,w)a,
\end{aligned}
\]
where the second equality is Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}exact\text{-}covariance\text{-}operator}\).  This proves both claims.
