Fix \(n\in\mathbb N\), \(1\leq\beta\leq n\), \(B>0\), and \(\omega_r>0\) for every \(r\in\{1,\ldots,\beta\}\).  In particular, \(n\geq1\), so every division by \(n\) below is valid.  If \((Y,c)\in\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)\), the definition of the ellipsoidal common-uniform coefficient class directly includes the condition
\[
(Y,c)\in\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)
\subseteq
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}.
\]

It remains to prove that this inclusion is strict.  The bounds \(1\leq\beta\leq n\) imply that \(1\in I_n\) and \(\{1\}\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\).  Let \(E^*=I_n\times I_n\) be the complete directed graph.  It contains every self-loop, and its in-neighborhood at each \(i\in I_n\) is
\[
N_i(E^*)=\{j:(j,i)\in E^*\}=I_n.
\]
Because \(\omega_1>0\) and \(n\geq1\), the quantity \(\sqrt{\omega_1/n}\) is a well-defined positive real number.  Define coefficients by
\[
c^*_{i,\{1\}}=2B\sqrt{\frac{\omega_1}{n}},
\qquad
c^*_{i,R}=0
\quad
\text{for every }R\in\{\varnothing\}\cup\mathcal S_{n,\beta}
\text{ with }R\neq\{1\},
\]
for each \(i\in I_n\), and define the fixed potential outcomes by
\[
Y_i^*(z)=c^*_{i,\varnothing}
+\sum_{R\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}c^*_{i,R}
  \prod_{j\in R}z_j,
\qquad z\in\{0,1\}^n.
\]
This is the expansion required by the bounded-order polynomial interference assumption.  Moreover, its only nonzero interaction index is \(\{1\}\), which has cardinality one and is contained in \(N_i(E^*)=I_n\).  Thus the schedule is supported on the neighborhoods of the admissible self-looped graph \(E^*\) and has degree at most \(\beta\).  By the definition of the published noise-free graph-union schedule class,
\[
(Y^*,c^*)\in\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}.
\]

For each \(R\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\), the definition of the aggregate coefficient gives
\[
a_R^*
=\frac1n\sum_{i\in I_n}c^*_{i,R}
=
\begin{cases}
2B\sqrt{\omega_1/n},&R=\{1\},\\
0,&R\neq\{1\}.
\end{cases}
\]
Because every \(\omega_r\) is positive, the diagonal matrix \(\Omega\), with diagonal entry \(\omega_{|R|}\) at index \(R\), is invertible and \((\Omega^{-1})_{R,R}=\omega_{|R|}^{-1}\).  Hence
\[
(a^*)^{\mathsf T}\Omega^{-1}a^*
=\sum_{R\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}
  \frac{(a_R^*)^2}{\omega_{|R|}}
=\frac{4B^2\omega_1/n}{\omega_1}
=\frac{4B^2}{n}.
\]
The positivity of \(B\) and \(n\geq1\) yields
\[
\frac{4B^2}{n}>\frac{B^2}{n}.
\]
Therefore \((Y^*,c^*)\) violates the aggregate coefficient ellipsoid assumption \(a^{\mathsf T}\Omega^{-1}a\leq B^2/n\) and, by the definition of \(\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)\), does not belong to that class.  We have thus exhibited an element of \(\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}\) outside \(\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)\), proving
\[
\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)
\subsetneq
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}.
\]
