Proposition~\(\mathrm{prop{:}rollout\text{-}budget\text{-}elbow}\) gives, for every admissible spacing value,
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}
\ge \frac{B^2\omega_1}{n}\Psi_\beta(q),
\qquad
\Psi_\beta(q)\sim A_\beta q^{1-2\beta}.
\]
Since \(A_\beta>0\), the definition of asymptotic equivalence with relative error \(1/2\) supplies \(q_0>0\), depending only on \(\beta\), such that
\[
\Psi_\beta(q)\ge \frac{A_\beta}{2}q^{1-2\beta}
\]
whenever \(0<q\le q_0\).  Substitution in the preceding variance lower bound yields
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}
\ge \frac{B^2\omega_1A_\beta}{2n}q^{1-2\beta}.
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}uniform\text{-}small\text{-}budget\text{-}upper\text{-}certificate}\) applies separately at every \(q\), because its uniform node gaps equal \(q/k\ge\delta(q)>0\), and gives
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}
\le \frac{2B^2D\omega_{\max}}{n}
\left(1+\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}\right)^2q^{1-2\beta}.
\]
The quantities \(n,\beta,k,B,\omega\) are fixed, \(A_\beta>0\), \(D=|\mathcal S_{n,\beta}|<\infty\), and \(0<\omega_1\le\omega_{\max}<\infty\).  Hence both displayed constants are finite and strictly positive, and neither they nor \(q_0\) depends on the admissible spacing function \(\delta\).  The two inequalities are therefore exactly the asserted uniform relation
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}=\Theta\!\left(q^{1-2\beta}\right)
\qquad(q\downarrow0),
\]
with no claim of uniformity in \(k\) or identification of an exact leading constant.
