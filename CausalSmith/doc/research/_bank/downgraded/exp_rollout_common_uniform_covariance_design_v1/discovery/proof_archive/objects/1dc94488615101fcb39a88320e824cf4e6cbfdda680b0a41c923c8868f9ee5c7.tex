Fix \(n\in\mathbb N\), \(1\leq\beta\leq n\), \(B>0\), and \(\omega\in(0,\infty)^\beta\).  If \((Y,c)\in\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)\), then the defining membership condition in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ellipsoidal\text{-}coefficient\text{-}class}\) includes \((Y,c)\in\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}\).  Hence
\[
\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)\subseteq\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}.
\]

It remains to prove that the inclusion is strict.  Because \(1\leq\beta\leq n\), one has \(1\in I_n\) and \(\{1\}\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\).  For every \(i\in I_n\), define coefficients by
\[
c^*_{i,\{1\}}=2B\sqrt{\frac{\omega_1}{n}},
\qquad
c^*_{i,R}=0
\quad\text{for every }R\in\{\varnothing\}\cup\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\text{ with }R\neq\{1\},
\]
and define the associated potential outcomes by the low-order expansion
\[
Y_i^*(z)=c^*_{i,\varnothing}+
\sum_{R\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}c^*_{i,R}
\prod_{j\in R}z_j,
\qquad z\in\{0,1\}^n.
\]
Thus \((Y^*,c^*)\) satisfies Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}low\text{-}order\text{-}schedule}\), and the membership criterion in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}published\text{-}low\text{-}order\text{-}class}\) gives
\[
(Y^*,c^*)\in\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}.
\]

By the definition of the aggregate coefficients, for \(R\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\),
\[
a_R^*=\frac1n\sum_{i\in I_n}c^*_{i,R}
=
\begin{cases}
2B\sqrt{\omega_1/n},&R=\{1\},\\
0,&R\neq\{1\}.
\end{cases}
\]
Every \(\omega_r\) is positive, so the diagonal matrix \(\Omega\) is invertible and \((\Omega^{-1})_{R,R}=\omega_{|R|}^{-1}\).  Consequently,
\[
(a^*)^{\mathsf T}\Omega^{-1}a^*
=\sum_{R\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}
\frac{(a_R^*)^2}{\omega_{|R|}}
=\frac{\left(2B\sqrt{\omega_1/n}\right)^2}{\omega_1}
=\frac{4B^2}{n}.
\]
Since \(B>0\) and \(n\geq1\),
\[
\frac{4B^2}{n}>\frac{B^2}{n}.
\]
The witness therefore violates Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}aggregate\text{-}coefficient\text{-}ellipsoid}\), equivalently the ellipsoid membership condition in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}ellipsoidal\text{-}coefficient\text{-}class}\), and hence
\[
(Y^*,c^*)\notin\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega).
\]
The displayed witness belongs to the published class but not to the ellipsoidal class, which, together with the first inclusion, proves
\[
\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)\subsetneq
\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}.
\]
