The paper derives the exact common-uniform kernel, block-diagonalizes its ellipsoidal support operator, proves a jointly attained node--weight spectral minimum, constructs the legal least-favourable schedule, and derives the computable lower barrier \(\Psi_\beta(q)\), including its strict finite-stage gap and small-budget asymptotic.  It also supplies a fixed-design radius variance bound and finite-sample Chebyshev interval.  A global analytic node certificate and the Chebyshev--Lobatto regime map remain open.
