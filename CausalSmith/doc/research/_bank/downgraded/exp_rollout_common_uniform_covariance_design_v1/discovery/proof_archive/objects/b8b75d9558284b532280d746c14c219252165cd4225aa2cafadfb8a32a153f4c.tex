Fix a feasible \((p,w)\) and a schedule in def:ellipsoidal-coefficient-class.  The expansion used in lem:common-uniform-monomial-covariance and ass:common-uniform-rollout give
\[
\mathbb E_U M(r)=\bar c_{\varnothing}
+\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}a_Sr^{|S|}.
\tag{20}
\]
By def:unbiased-weight-class, \(\sum_tw_t=0\) and
\(\sum_tw_tp_t^d=1\) for every \(1\le d\le\beta\).  Applying these equalities term by term to (20) yields
\[
\mathbb E_U\widehat\tau_{p,w}
=\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}a_S.
\tag{21}
\]
On the other hand, ass:low-order-schedule gives the finite-population causal contrast
\[
\tau
=\frac1n\sum_{i\in I_n}\{Y_i(\mathbf1)-Y_i(\mathbf0)\}
=\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}a_S.
\tag{22}
\]
Equations (21)--(22) establish design unbiasedness without identifying \(\tau\) by definition.

By lem:common-uniform-monomial-covariance,
\[
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})=a^{\mathsf T}G(p,w)a.
\]
Applying the ellipsoid support calculation (7)--(9) from thm:exact-covariance-saddle, followed by lem:subset-lattice-block-reduction and def:variance-certificate, gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
\le\frac{B^2}{n}\lambda_{\max}\{\Omega^{1/2}G(p,w)\Omega^{1/2}\}
=V_{\mathrm{rob}}(p,w).
\tag{23}
\]

Suppose first that \(V_{\mathrm{rob}}(p,w)>0\), and put
\(R=\sqrt{V_{\mathrm{rob}}(p,w)/\alpha}\).  For
\(X=\widehat\tau_{p,w}-\tau\), equations (21)--(22) give \(\mathbb E_UX=0\), while (23) gives \(\mathbb E_UX^2\le V_{\mathrm{rob}}(p,w)\).  The pointwise inequality
\[
X^2\ge R^2\mathbf1\{|X|>R\}
\]
therefore implies
\[
\Pr_U(|X|>R)
\le\frac{\mathbb E_UX^2}{R^2}
\le\alpha.
\]
Taking complements proves the stated probability bound, and def:chebyshev-interval makes it equivalent to coverage by \(\mathrm{CI}^{\mathrm{Ch}}_{1-\alpha}(p,w)\).

If \(V_{\mathrm{rob}}(p,w)=0\), (23) gives \(\mathbb E_UX^2=0\).  Since \(X^2\ge0\), this implies \(X=0\) almost surely.  Hence \(\widehat\tau_{p,w}=\tau\) almost surely and the asserted singleton has coverage one.
