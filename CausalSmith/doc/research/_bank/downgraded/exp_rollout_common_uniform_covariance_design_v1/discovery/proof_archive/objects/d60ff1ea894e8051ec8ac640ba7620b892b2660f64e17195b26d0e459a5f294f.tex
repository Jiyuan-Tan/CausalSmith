Fix \(n,\beta,k,B,\omega\) with \(1\le\beta\le n\), \(k\ge\beta\), \(B>0\), and \(\omega_d>0\) for \(1\le d\le\beta\).  Write \(D=|\mathcal S_{n,\beta}|\) and \(\omega_{\max}=\max_{1\le d\le\beta}\omega_d\).  For every \(q\in(0,1]\) and \(0<\delta\le q/k\), set \(\bar p_t=tq/k\).  On the nodes \(\bar p_0,\ldots,\bar p_\beta\), let \(\ell_t\) be the degree-\(\beta\) Lagrange cardinal polynomial and define \(\bar w_t=\ell_t(1)-\ell_t(0)\) for \(0\le t\le\beta\) and \(\bar w_t=0\) for \(t>\beta\).  Then \(\bar p\in\mathcal P_{k,q,\delta}\), \(\bar w\in\mathcal W_\beta(\bar p)\), and
\[
\|\bar w\|_1\le
\left(1+\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}\right)q^{-\beta}.
\]
For every feasible node vector \(p\), all nonempty \(S,T\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\), and all stages \(s,t\),
\[
|K_{st}(S,T)|\le2q.
\]
For the displayed uniform-node construction,
\[
\lambda_{\max}\!\left\{\Omega^{1/2}G(\bar p,\bar w)\Omega^{1/2}\right\}
\le 2D\omega_{\max}
\left(1+\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}\right)^2q^{1-2\beta},
\]
and consequently
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta}
\le\frac{2B^2D\omega_{\max}}{n}
\left(1+\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}\right)^2q^{1-2\beta}.
\]
