Fix a declared feasible tuple.  Every \(\omega_d\) is positive, so \(\Omega\) is positive definite.  For a fixed feasible \((p,w)\), put
\[
A(p,w)=\Omega^{1/2}G(p,w)\Omega^{1/2},
\qquad b=\Omega^{-1/2}a.
\]
Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}aggregate\text{-}coefficient\text{-}ellipsoid}\) is exactly
\[
\|b\|_2^2=a^{\mathsf T}\Omega^{-1}a\le\frac{B^2}{n}.
\tag{65}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}common\text{-}uniform\text{-}monomial\text{-}covariance}\) gives
\[
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
=a^{\mathsf T}G(p,w)a=b^{\mathsf T}A(p,w)b.
\tag{66}
\]
The matrix \(A(p,w)\) is positive semidefinite: for any real aggregate vector \(a\), take the fixed array \(c_{i,S}=a_S\), \(c_{i,\varnothing}=0\); then the right side of (66) is a variance.  Expanding a vector in an orthonormal eigenbasis of the finite symmetric matrix gives
\[
\sup_{\|b\|_2^2\le B^2/n}b^{\mathsf T}A(p,w)b
=\frac{B^2}{n}\lambda_{\max}\{A(p,w)\}.
\tag{67}
\]

The supremum is attained by a legal schedule.  By Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}subset\text{-}lattice\text{-}block\text{-}reduction}\), choose an active block \(h\), a unit leading eigenvector \(v^*\) of \(H_h(p,w)\), and its unit orthogonal lift \(Q_hv^*\).  Set
\[
a^*=\frac B{\sqrt n}\Omega^{1/2}Q_hv^*.
\tag{68}
\]
Orthogonality gives
\[
(a^*)^{\mathsf T}\Omega^{-1}a^*=\frac{B^2}{n},
\qquad
(a^*)^{\mathsf T}G(p,w)a^*
=\frac{B^2}{n}\lambda_{\max}\{A(p,w)\}.
\tag{69}
\]
For every \(i\in I_n\), define
\[
c^*_{i,S}=a^*_S,
\qquad c^*_{i,\varnothing}=0,
\qquad
Y_i^*(z)=\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}a^*_S\prod_{j\in S}z_j.
\tag{70}
\]
This array is fixed before \(U\) by Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}fixed\text{-}finite\text{-}population}\), and it has degree at most \(\beta\) by Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}low\text{-}order\text{-}schedule}\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}cey\text{-}envelope\text{-}union\text{-}equivalence}\), whose reverse inclusion uses the complete directed graph, places (70) in the corrected noise-free graph-union envelope.  Its aggregate coefficient is \(a^*\), and (69) verifies the ellipsoid condition.  Finally, finiteness of \(n\) and \(\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\) gives
\[
\max_{i\in I_n,\ z\in\{0,1\}^n}|Y_i^*(z)|
\le\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}|a_S^*|<\infty.
\tag{71}
\]
Thus (70) is a legal least-favourable member of \(\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)\) and attains (67).

Combining (66)--(71), Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}subset\text{-}lattice\text{-}block\text{-}reduction}\), and Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}variance\text{-}certificate}\) gives, for every fixed feasible pair,
\[
\sup_{(Y,c)\in\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B,\omega)}
\operatorname{Var}_U(\widehat\tau_{p,w})
=\frac{B^2}{n}\max_{0\le h\le\beta}\lambda_{\max}\{H_h(p,w)\}
=V_{\mathrm{rob}}(p,w).
\tag{72}
\]
Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}joint\text{-}design\text{-}attainment}\) and Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}exact\text{-}saddle}\) replace both infima in (72) by attained minima.  This proves the headline equality and universal joint attainment.

It remains to expose the fixed-node semidefinite epigraph.  Let \(d_\beta=|\mathcal S_{n,\beta}|\), and let \(\mathbb K(p)\) be indexed by pairs \((s,S)\), with block entry \(K_{st}(S,T)\).  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}common\text{-}uniform\text{-}monomial\text{-}covariance}\) identifies \(\mathbb K(p)\) as a covariance matrix, so \(\mathbb K(p)\succeq0\).  Define
\[
D_p(w)b=(w_0\Omega^{1/2}b,\ldots,w_k\Omega^{1/2}b)^{\mathsf T},
\qquad
L_p(w)=\mathbb K(p)^{1/2}D_p(w).
\]
Direct block multiplication gives
\[
A(p,w)=D_p(w)^{\mathsf T}\mathbb K(p)D_p(w)
=L_p(w)^{\mathsf T}L_p(w).
\tag{73}
\]
With \(C(p)_{r,t}=p_t^r\) for \(0\le r\le\beta\) and \(e=(0,1,\ldots,1)^{\mathsf T}\), the weight constraints are \(C(p)w=e\).  Completing the square gives
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho I_{d_\beta}&L_p(w)^{\mathsf T}\\
L_p(w)&I_{(k+1)d_\beta}
\end{pmatrix}\succeq0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\rho I_{d_\beta}-L_p(w)^{\mathsf T}L_p(w)\succeq0,
\tag{74}
\]
because the quadratic form at \((x,y)\) equals
\[
x^{\mathsf T}\{\rho I_{d_\beta}-L_p(w)^{\mathsf T}L_p(w)\}x
+\|y+L_p(w)x\|_2^2.
\]
Thus the fixed-node weight problem is the explicit semidefinite program
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{minimize}_{w,\rho}&\rho\\
\text{subject to}&C(p)w=e,\\
&\begin{pmatrix}
\rho I_{d_\beta}&L_p(w)^{\mathsf T}\\
L_p(w)&I_{(k+1)d_\beta}
\end{pmatrix}\succeq0.
\end{array}
\tag{75}
\]
Its entries are affine in \((w,\rho)\), and (73)--(74) show that its value is \(\lambda_{\max}\{A(p,w)\}\).  It descends to the quotient by the common null space \(\mathcal N_p=\ker\{w\mapsto L_p(w)\}\), because the objective is constant on its cosets.  Fixed-node attainment follows from Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}joint\text{-}design\text{-}attainment}\).  This proves the semidefinite-epigraph and attainment assertions without claiming the still-open global node dual characterization.
