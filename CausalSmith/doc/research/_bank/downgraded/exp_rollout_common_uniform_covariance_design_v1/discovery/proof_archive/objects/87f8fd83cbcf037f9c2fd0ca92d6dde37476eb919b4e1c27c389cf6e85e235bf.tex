Proposition~\(\mathrm{prop{:}rollout\text{-}budget\text{-}elbow}\) gives, for every admissible spacing value,
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}
\ge\frac{B^2\omega_1}{n}\Psi_\beta(q),
\qquad
\Psi_\beta(q)\sim A_\beta q^{1-2\beta}.
\]
Since \(A_\beta>0\), the definition of asymptotic equivalence with relative error \(1/2\) supplies \(q_0>0\), depending only on \(\beta\), such that \(\Psi_\beta(q)\ge(A_\beta/2)q^{1-2\beta}\) whenever \(0<q\le q_0\).  This proves the displayed lower bound.  Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}uniform\text{-}small\text{-}budget\text{-}upper\text{-}certificate}\) applies separately at every \(q\), because its uniform node gaps equal \(q/k\ge\delta(q)\), and gives the displayed upper bound.  Neither constant depends on the choice of the spacing function.  These two inequalities are exactly the asserted uniform \(\Theta(q^{1-2\beta})\) statement.
