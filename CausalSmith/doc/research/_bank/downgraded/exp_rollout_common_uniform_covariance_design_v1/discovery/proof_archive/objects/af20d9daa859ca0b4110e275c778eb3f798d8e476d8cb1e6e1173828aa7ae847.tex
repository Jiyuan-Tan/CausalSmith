Let \(L>0\), put \(\omega_{\min}=\min_{1\le d\le\beta}\omega_d\), and suppose \((Y,c)\in\mathcal C^{\mathrm{CEY}}_{n,\beta}\) satisfies
\[
\max_{i\in I_n}\sum_{S\in\mathcal S_{n,\beta}}|c_{i,S}|\le L.
\]
Then, with \(B^\dagger=\sqrt n\,L/\sqrt{\omega_{\min}}\), one has \((Y,c)\in\mathcal C_{n,\beta}(B^\dagger,\omega)\).  Consequently, for every feasible \((p,w)\), the estimator is design-unbiased for \(\tau\), its variance is bounded by the certificate obtained from \(V_{\mathrm{rob}}(p,w)\) after substituting \(B=B^\dagger\), and the corresponding interval in Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}chebyshev\text{-}interval}\) has design coverage at least \(1-\alpha\).
