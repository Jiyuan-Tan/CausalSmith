%%% FIELD positive-lag-cyclic-cde-adjustment-statement
For every \((G,\mathcal M)\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal S,L,H,p)\), suppose that no summary bidirected edge joins \(Y\) to a component label \(B\) that is either \(Y\) itself or has a directed path to \(Y\) in \(\mathcal S\). Then, without requiring the strongly connected component of \(Y\) in \(\mathcal S\) to be a singleton,
\[
\mathbb E_{\mathcal M}[Y_0\mid\operatorname{do}(X_{-\gamma}=x,Z=z)]
=\mathbb E_p[Y_0\mid X_{-\gamma}=x,Z=z],\qquad x\in\{0,1\},
\]
and consequently \(q(\mathcal M)=A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\).
%%% FIELD positive-lag-cyclic-cde-adjustment-proof
Put
\[
W=\operatorname{PP}_{\mathcal S,L}(Y_0)
=\{X_{-\gamma}\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}Z,
\qquad w_x=(x,z),\quad x\in\{0,1\}.
\tag{1}
\]
The disjoint union in (1) follows from \(\text{ass:focal-positive-lag-parent}\) and the definition of \(Z\). By \(\text{ass:strict-observed-positivity}\), every full-window cell has positive \(p\)-mass. Each assignment \(W=w_x\) has at least one extension to \(\{0,1\}^{\mathcal W_H}\), and its probability is the sum of the positive probabilities of all such extensions. Hence
\[
p(W=w_x)>0,\qquad x\in\{0,1\},
\tag{2}
\]
so both observational conditional expectations in the statement are defined.

We first locate the actual parents of the terminal vertex. If \((B,s)\in\operatorname{pa}_G(Y_0)\), then \((B,s)\to(Y,0)\in E_G^{\to}\). Membership of \(G\) in \(\mathfrak G_{H,L}(\mathcal S)\), together with \(\text{ass:positive-bounded-directed-lags}\), gives
\[
1\le -s\le L.
\tag{3}
\]
The exact projection condition \(\text{ass:exact-summary-projection}\) gives \(B\to Y\in E_{\mathcal S}^{\to}\). Equations (3) and the definition of the positive-lag possible-parent set therefore imply
\[
\operatorname{pa}_G(Y_0)\subseteq W.
\tag{4}
\]
The same positive-lag condition also makes \(Y_0\) terminal in the observed window: an edge \(Y_0\to(C,t)\) would require \(t\ge1\), whereas every \(t\in\mathbb T_H\) satisfies \(t\le0\).

Let \(\mathcal E_Y\) be the set of exogenous-factor indices consisting of the private index \(\{Y_0\}\) and every bidirected-edge index incident to \(Y_0\). We claim that none of the factors \((U_a:a\in\mathcal E_Y)\) occurs in the recursive determination of \(V_W\). Indeed, acyclicity of the ADMG and \(\text{ass:structural-recursion}\) imply, by induction along a topological ordering of \(E_G^{\to}\), that every coordinate of \(V_W\) is a deterministic function only of exogenous factors incident to its directed ancestors. The private factor of \(Y_0\) could reach \(W\) only along a directed path beginning at \(Y_0\), which is impossible because \(Y_0\) is terminal. Now consider an incident pairwise factor with edge
\[
Y_0\leftrightarrow(B,s).
\tag{5}
\]
If this factor occurred in the recursion for a coordinate \((C,-\ell)\in W\), then terminality again excludes the route starting at \(Y_0\); therefore \((B,s)\) would have a directed path in \(G\) to \((C,-\ell)\). Projecting the edges on this path gives a directed walk from \(B\) to \(C\) in \(\mathcal S\). Since \((C,-\ell)\in W\), the definition of \(W\) gives \(C\to Y\in E_{\mathcal S}^{\to}\). Deleting repetitions from the concatenated directed walk shows that \(B\) is a directed ancestor of \(Y\), with the reflexive case \(B=Y\) included. But (5) projects to \(Y\leftrightarrow B\), contradicting the graphical premise. This proves the claim.

Let \(\mathcal E_W\) denote the exogenous-factor indices occurring in the recursive determination of \(V_W\). The preceding paragraph proves \(\mathcal E_Y\cap\mathcal E_W=\varnothing\). By \(\text{ass:independent-edge-latents}\), the finite family of all private and pairwise factors is mutually independent. Direct factorization of its finite joint mass function therefore gives
\[
(U_a:a\in\mathcal E_Y)\mathbin{\perp}V_W.
\tag{6}
\]
This argument permits different pairwise latent factors to overlap at an observed vertex; it uses only that the factors indexed by distinct private vertices or bidirected edges are independent.

Write \(U_Y=(U_a:a\in\mathcal E_Y)\). By \(\text{ass:structural-recursion}\) and (4), there is a deterministic binary table, namely the frozen structural table at \(Y_0\), such that
\[
Y_0=f_{Y_0}(V_{\operatorname{pa}_G(Y_0)},U_Y).
\tag{7}
\]
For either \(x\in\{0,1\}\), equations (2), (6), and (7) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb E_p[Y_0\mid W=w_x]
&=\sum_u f_{Y_0}((w_x)_{\operatorname{pa}_G(Y_0)},u)
   \Pr_{\mathcal M}(U_Y=u\mid W=w_x)\\
&=\sum_u f_{Y_0}((w_x)_{\operatorname{pa}_G(Y_0)},u)
   \Pr_{\mathcal M}(U_Y=u).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
Here the first equality also uses \(\text{ass:observed-law-compatibility}\), which identifies the observational law of \(\mathcal M\) with \(p\). Under \(\operatorname{do}(W=w_x)\), every actual parent in (4) is fixed to its corresponding entry of \(w_x\), while an intervention leaves the exogenous distribution unchanged. The truncated structural recursion and (7) thus give
\[
\mathbb E_{\mathcal M}[Y_0\mid\operatorname{do}(W=w_x)]
=\sum_u f_{Y_0}((w_x)_{\operatorname{pa}_G(Y_0)},u)
   \Pr_{\mathcal M}(U_Y=u).
\tag{9}
\]
Comparing (8) and (9), and then using (1), proves the two displayed equalities in the statement. Subtracting the case \(x=0\) from the case \(x=1\) and applying \(\text{def:micro-cde}\) and the definition of \(A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\) proves \(q(\mathcal M)=A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\).

The removed singleton-SCC premise is genuinely unnecessary in this positive-lag setting. For an exact four-vertex check, take \(\mathcal V=\{X,Y\}\), \(H=L=\gamma=1\), directed edges \(X_{-1}\to Y_0\) and \(Y_{-1}\to X_0\), no bidirected edges, mutually independent nondegenerate private bits, and \(Y_0=X_{-1}\mathbin{\oplus}E_Y\) with \(\Pr(E_Y=1)=\delta\in(0,1/2)\). The summary has the directed cycle \(X\to Y\to X\), every observed cell can be made positive, and both the interventional and conditional contrasts equal \(1-2\delta\).

The no-bidirected-ancestor premise is load-bearing. Retain \(X_{-1}\to Y_0\), add \(X_{-1}\leftrightarrow Y_0\), let the shared edge latent \(U\) be fair, and take independent noises \(E_X\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(e)\) and \(E_Y\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\delta)\), where \(e,\delta\in(0,1/2)\). Set
\[
X_{-1}=U\mathbin{\oplus}E_X,
\qquad
Y_0=(X_{-1}\mathbin{\lor}U)\mathbin{\oplus}E_Y,
\tag{10}
\]
and let the other two window variables be independent nondegenerate private bits. All sixteen observed cells are positive. Direct calculation gives
\[
q(\mathcal M)=\frac{1-2\delta}{2},
\qquad
\mathbb E[Y_0\mid X_{-1}=1]-\mathbb E[Y_0\mid X_{-1}=0]
=(1-2\delta)(1-e),
\tag{11}
\]
which are unequal because \(e\ne1/2\). Thus removing the bidirected-ancestor condition would make the conclusion false.
%%% FIELD positive-lag-fa-extension-collapse-statement
Suppose \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal S,L,H,p)\ne\varnothing\) and no summary bidirected edge joins \(Y\) to a component label that is either \(Y\) itself or has a directed path to \(Y\) in \(\mathcal S\). Then every \((G,\mathcal M)\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal S,L,H,p)\) satisfies \(q(\mathcal M)=A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\), and
\[
\Theta(\mathcal S,L,H,p)=\{A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\},
\qquad
\underline q(p)=\overline q(p)=A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p).
\]
In particular, the conclusion permits directed cycles through \(Y\) in the summary graph; the positive-lag unrolling, not a singleton strongly connected component, supplies the terminal-time argument.
%%% FIELD positive-lag-fa-extension-collapse-proof
Strict observed positivity and \(\text{ass:focal-positive-lag-parent}\) ensure that both events \(\{X_{-\gamma}=x,Z=z\}\), \(x\in\{0,1\}\), have positive \(p\)-probability, as proved in equations (1)--(2) of \(\text{lem:positive-lag-cyclic-cde-adjustment}\). Hence \(A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\) is well defined.

Fix an arbitrary \((G,\mathcal M)\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal S,L,H,p)\). The graphical premise of the present theorem is exactly the graphical premise of \(\text{lem:positive-lag-cyclic-cde-adjustment}\); all of that lemma's structural and compatibility hypotheses follow from membership in the fiber and the listed assumptions. Therefore
\[
q(\mathcal M)=A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p).
\tag{12}
\]
Because the chosen member was arbitrary, the definition of the attainable set gives
\[
\Theta(\mathcal S,L,H,p)\subseteq\{A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\}.
\tag{13}
\]
The nonempty-fiber premise supplies at least one pair \((G,\mathcal M)\) in the fiber, and (12) shows that this pair attains \(A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\). Thus the reverse inclusion in (13) holds and
\[
\Theta(\mathcal S,L,H,p)=\{A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p)\}.
\tag{14}
\]
Finally, by \(\text{def:sharp-envelope}\), \(\underline q(p)\) and \(\overline q(p)\) are respectively the infimum and supremum of the set in (14). The infimum and supremum of a nonempty singleton equal its unique element, proving
\[
\underline q(p)=\overline q(p)=A_{\mathrm{FA}}(p).
\]
No step uses a singleton strongly connected component. The cyclic four-vertex model displayed in \(\text{lem:positive-lag-cyclic-cde-adjustment}\) verifies directly that the enlarged graphical regime is nonempty and can have a summary cycle through \(Y\).
