For \(p\in\mathfrak O\), define
\[
F(p)=\{v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}:Bv=p\},
\qquad
F_b(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)
=\{v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}:K_{\mathrm{blind}}Bv=K_{\mathrm{blind}}p\}.
\]
If \(v\in F(p)\), applying the linear map \(K_{\mathrm{blind}}\) to \(Bv=p\) proves \(v\in F_b(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)\).  Thus
\[
F(p)\subseteq F_b(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p).
\tag{1}
\]
Minimization of \(h_e^{\top}v\) over the larger set in (1) cannot increase its value, and maximization over that set cannot decrease its value.  By \(\text{def:z-blind-projection}\),
\[
L_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)\leq L(p)
\leq U(p)\leq U_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p),
\]
which is equivalent to the asserted interval inclusion.
