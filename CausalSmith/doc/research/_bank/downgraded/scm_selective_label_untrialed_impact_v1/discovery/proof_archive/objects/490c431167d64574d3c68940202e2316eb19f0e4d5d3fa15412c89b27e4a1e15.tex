L(p_0)-L_{\mathrm{blind}}(K^{32}p_0)=\frac{199}{800},
\qquad
U_{\mathrm{blind}}(K^{32}p_0)-U(p_0)=\frac{199}{800}.
\]
By \(\text{thm:general-dual-face-strict-refinement-characterization}\), the strict region is relatively open on the whole observable polytope.
