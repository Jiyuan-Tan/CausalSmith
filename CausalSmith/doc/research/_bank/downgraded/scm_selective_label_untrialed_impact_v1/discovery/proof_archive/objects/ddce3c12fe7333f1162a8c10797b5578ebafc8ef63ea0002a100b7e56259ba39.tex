With the simplex mass multiplier left explicit, define
\[
\mathcal D_L=\{(\alpha,\lambda)\in\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^{|\mathcal O|}:\alpha\mathbf 1+B^{\top}\lambda\leq h_e\},
\qquad
\mathcal D_U=\{(\alpha,\lambda)\in\mathbb R\times\mathbb R^{|\mathcal O|}:\alpha\mathbf 1+B^{\top}\lambda\geq h_e\}.
\]
For \(p\in\mathfrak O\), define the unnormalized full optimal faces
\[
\Lambda_L(p)=\operatorname*{argmax}_{(\alpha,\lambda)\in\mathcal D_L}
\{\alpha+\lambda^{\top}p\},
\qquad
\Lambda_U(p)=\operatorname*{argmin}_{(\alpha,\lambda)\in\mathcal D_U}
\{\alpha+\lambda^{\top}p\}.
\]
Finally, writing \(d_K\) for the number of rows of \(K_{\mathrm{blind}}\), define the embedded blind-dual subspace
\[
\mathcal E_K=\{(\alpha,K_{\mathrm{blind}}^{\top}\mu):
\alpha\in\mathbb R,\ \mu\in\mathbb R^{d_K}\}.
\]
