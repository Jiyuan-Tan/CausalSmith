Define
\[
F(p)=\{v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}:Bv=p\},\qquad
F_b(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)
=\{v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}:K_{\mathrm{blind}}Bv=K_{\mathrm{blind}}p\}.
\]
Applying \(K_{\mathrm{blind}}\) to \(Bv=p\) proves \(F(p)\subseteq F_b(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)\).  Minimization over the larger set cannot increase, and maximization cannot decrease.  Therefore
\[
L_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)\leq L(p)\leq U(p)
\leq U_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p),
\]
which is the asserted inclusion.
