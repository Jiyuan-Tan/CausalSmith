Fix \(p\in\mathfrak O\), put \(\beta=1-\alpha\), write \((D_L,D_U)=\Gamma'_p\), and define \(H(g)=\max\{D_L(g),-D_U(g)\}\).  Let
\[
V_p=\{g:\mathbf 1^{\top}g=0,\ g_o=0\text{ whenever }p_o=0\}.
\]
Suppose the active projection cones and extreme slopes of \(\Lambda_L(p)\) and \(\Lambda_U(p)\), refined by their zero and comparison hyperplanes, yield a finite polyhedral fan on \(V_p\) such that: (i) locally,
\[
\theta(\Pi_{\mathfrak O}(p+x))-\theta(p)=(D_L(x),D_U(x)),
\]
with both coordinates linear on each fan cone; (ii) every facet normal is nonzero on \(V_p\); and (iii) on every full-dimensional cone where \(H\equiv0\), either the slope of \(D_L\) is zero and \(-D_U\leq0\), or the slope of \(D_U\) is zero and \(D_L\leq0\).  Let \(\mathcal A_p\) be the finite closure under row selection and sign changes of matrices whose nonzero rows are the fan facet normals and nonzero endpoint slopes.  For
\[
X_m=m^{-1/2}\sum_{i=1}^m(e_{O_i}-p),
\]
from an independent size-\(m\) categorical sample, define
\[
\delta_m(p)=\max_{A\in\mathcal A_p}\sup_x
\left|\Pr_p\{AX_m\leq x\}-\Pr\{AG_p\leq x\}\right|.
\]
Assume \(G_p\) is nondegenerate on \(V_p\),
\[
m_n\to\infty,\qquad m_n^2/n\to0,\qquad\eta_n\downarrow0,
\]
and
\[
\frac{\delta_{m_n}(p)+m_n/\sqrt n+m_n^2/n}{\eta_n}\to0.
\]
Then \(\delta_m(p)\to0\), atoms of \(H(G_p)\) can occur only at zero, and
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\Pr_p\left\{
L(p)\geq\widehat L-\frac{c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,1-\alpha+\eta_n}}{\sqrt n},\
U(p)\leq\widehat U+\frac{c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,1-\alpha+\eta_n}}{\sqrt n}
\right\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
No projection-cone or LP-basis uniqueness is required.  The fan, the exact multinomial probabilities defining \(\delta_m(p)\), and the Gaussian orthant probabilities are finite and enumerable.

For the thirty-two-type table the conditions are nonvacuous.  In its stated row order, let
\[
p_\star=B^{32}_{\cdot,1}=B^{32}_{\cdot,17}
=\tfrac12(\mathbf e_1+\mathbf e_7).
\]
This is a boundary vertex of \(\mathfrak O^{32}\), and every mixture of types \(1\) and \(17\) is optimal for both endpoint programs.  If \(\zeta\sim\mathcal N(0,1/4)\), then \(G_{p_\star}=\zeta(\mathbf e_1-\mathbf e_7)\), and exact active-cone enumeration gives
\[
D_L(G_{p_\star})=0,\qquad
D_U(G_{p_\star})=\frac45|\zeta|,\qquad
H(G_{p_\star})=0.
\]
Thus the exact Gaussian directional law is the unit atom at zero, and the asserted conservative conclusion holds for every \(m_n\to\infty\) with \(m_n^2/n\to0\) and every decreasing slack dominating the displayed computable error rate.
