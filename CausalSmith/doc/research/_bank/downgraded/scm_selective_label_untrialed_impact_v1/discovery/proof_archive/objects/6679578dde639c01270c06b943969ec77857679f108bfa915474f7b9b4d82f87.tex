Fix \(p\in\mathfrak O\).  By \(\text{lem:finite-incidence-duality-continuity}\), the full lower dual optimum is attained on \(\Lambda_L(p)\), while the blind lower dual is
\[
\max_{\alpha,\mu}\left\{\alpha+\mu^{\top}K_{\mathrm{blind}}p:
\alpha\mathbf 1+B^{\top}K_{\mathrm{blind}}^{\top}\mu\leq h_e\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]
Under the substitution \(\lambda=K_{\mathrm{blind}}^{\top}\mu\), the feasible set in (1) is exactly \(\mathcal D_L\cap\mathcal E_K\), and its objective is \(\alpha+\lambda^{\top}p\).  Thus (1) restricts the full lower-dual objective to the embedded blind subset.  Attainment of both finite programs implies
\[
L_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)=L(p)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Lambda_L(p)\cap\mathcal E_K\neq\varnothing.
\tag{2}
\]
The ordering in \(\text{lem:z-blind-coarsening}\) then gives
\[
L(p)>L_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Lambda_L(p)\cap\mathcal E_K=\varnothing.
\tag{3}
\]

Similarly, the blind upper dual is
\[
\min_{\alpha,\mu}\left\{\alpha+\mu^{\top}K_{\mathrm{blind}}p:
\alpha\mathbf 1+B^{\top}K_{\mathrm{blind}}^{\top}\mu\geq h_e\right\},
\tag{4}
\]
which is the restriction of the full upper dual to \(\mathcal D_U\cap\mathcal E_K\).  Hence
\[
U_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)=U(p)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Lambda_U(p)\cap\mathcal E_K\neq\varnothing,
\]
and the upper ordering in \(\text{lem:z-blind-coarsening}\) gives
\[
U(p)<U_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Lambda_U(p)\cap\mathcal E_K=\varnothing.
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (2)--(5) also prove the advertised computable characterization: after the finite primal programs produce \(L(p)\) and \(U(p)\), each displayed intersection condition is a finite linear feasibility problem in \((\alpha,\lambda,\mu)\).

By the continuity lemma, the gap functions
\[
g_L(p)=L(p)-L_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p),
\qquad
g_U(p)=U_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p)-U(p)
\tag{6}
\]
are continuous on \(\mathfrak O\).  Consequently
\[
\mathcal S_{\mathrm{strict}}
=g_L^{-1}((0,\infty))\cap g_U^{-1}((0,\infty))
\tag{7}
\]
is relatively open in \(\mathfrak O\).

It remains to prove nonemptiness under \(\mathsf{Bracket}_{\mathrm{SLCC}}\).  Fix \(s_*\) and \(q_\ell<q_m<q_h\) supplied by \(\text{def:bracket-stratum-design}\).  Extend every row of \(\text{def:complete-32-type-table}\) to all additional performance values.  For \(z=0\), set the neutral outcome equal to one for every \(q\), and extend the nonincreasing \(r=1\) path by
\[
Y_{(1,q)}=
\begin{cases}
Y_{(1,q_\ell)},&q<q_m,\\
Y_{(1,q_m)},&q=q_m,\\
Y_{(1,q_h)},&q>q_m.
\end{cases}
\tag{8}
\]
For \(z=1\), set the neutral outcome equal to zero for every \(q\) and use (8) for the nondecreasing \(r=1\) path.  The extension agrees at the three distinguished values, is monotone in the required direction, and lies below the neutral value for \(z=0\) and above it for \(z=1\).  Therefore correctness, both performance orders, and neutral invariance hold at every additional \(q\).

Retain the two endpoint action bits and set every action coordinate not read by a trial arm at \(s_*\) to zero.  This includes \(A_{(1,q_m)}\): deployment uses it, but \(e\notin J_0\), and the target reads only \(Y_{(1,q_m)}\).  Legality places no cross-coordinate restriction on actions, so all thirty-two extensions remain legal.

Conversely, restrict any legal type with \(S=s_*\) to \(q_\ell,q_m,q_h\).  Its binary monotone outcome path has one of the canonical observable--target representatives in \(\text{thm:complete-32-type-refinement-certificate}\), after replacing an unread neutral constant by its displayed representative if necessary.  Its endpoint action pair is one of the four binary pairs, and all unread action coordinates may be fixed without changing the trial law or the outcome-only target.  Thus every legal type supported on \(s_*\) has the same observable--target column as one of the extended rows.  Extra performance values and off-stratum configurations create no new column in a law supported on \(s_*\).

Assign the weights \(w^{32}(\pi^{32,\circ})\) to fixed legal extensions of the thirty-two rows and zero mass to types outside \(s_*\); call the resulting mixture \(w_0\).  A trial arm \(j\) using endpoint \(d\in\{\ell,h\}\) contributes \(\rho_j\) times its conditional-cell indicator, whereas the canonical table contributes one half of that indicator.  Thus its block is the canonical endpoint block multiplied by \(2\rho_j>0\).  The bracket condition supplies at least one arm of each endpoint type.  Selecting one such block of each type and dividing by \(2\rho_j\) is a left inverse, so equality of expanded full laws is equivalent to equality of canonical full laws; the same holds after label coarsening.  Moreover, a law with zero mass outside \(s_*\) cannot be generated using a positive weight on another stratum, since every arm probability is positive and every incidence entry is nonnegative.  Duplicate or rescaled endpoint arms and other strata therefore enlarge neither relevant fiber.

The four endpoint programs at \(p_0=Bw_0\) are consequently the canonical programs at \(p^{32,\circ}\), after quotienting duplicate columns.  The exact certificate gives
\[
L(p_0)-L_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p_0)=\frac{199}{800},
\qquad
U_{\mathrm{blind}}(K_{\mathrm{blind}}p_0)-U(p_0)=\frac{199}{800}.
\tag{9}
\]
Thus \(p_0\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{strict}}\).

Because the finite legal class is nonempty, choose \(u\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}\) with \(u_t>0\) for every legal type.  For \(0<\delta<1\), set
\[
w_\delta=(1-\delta)w_0+\delta u,
\qquad
p_\delta=Bw_\delta.
\tag{10}
\]
Every coordinate of \(w_\delta\) is positive.  Aggregate weights of duplicate columns and enumerate the distinct generators as \(b_1,\ldots,b_N\), with aggregate weights \(\beta_k>0\).  On \(\{x:\mathbf 1^{\top}x=0\}\), the map \(T(x)=\sum_kx_kb_k\) surjects onto \(V=\operatorname{span}\{b_k-b_1:2\leq k\leq N\}\).  Choose a linear right inverse \(R\).  If \(V\neq\{0\}\), the finite-dimensional constant \(C=\sup_{\|d\|_2=1}\|Rd\|_\infty\) is finite, and \(\beta+Rd\) remains nonnegative whenever \(\|d\|_2<\min_k\beta_k/C\).  These weights have mass one and generate \(p_\delta+d\).  The case \(V=\{0\}\) is immediate.  Hence \(p_\delta\in\operatorname{relint}(\mathfrak O)\).

By continuity of the two gaps in (6), both remain positive for all sufficiently small \(\delta>0\), since both equal \(199/800\) at \(\delta=0\).  Therefore
\[
p_\delta\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{strict}}\cap\operatorname{relint}(\mathfrak O).
\tag{11}
\]
Equations (7) and (11) prove the required nonempty relatively open region.  The block-rescaling and legal-extension construction explicitly proves robustness to duplicate endpoint arms, arbitrary positive randomization probabilities, extra performance values, other strata, and off-stratum configurations.
