Fix \(p\in\mathfrak O\) and \(n\geq1\).  For each cell \(o\in\mathcal O\), the indicator variables \(\mathbf 1\{O_i=o\}\) are independent Bernoulli variables with success probability \(p_o\) under \(\text{ass:iid-finite-sampling}\).  Hence
\[
N_o=\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf 1\{O_i=o\}\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p_o).
\tag{1}
\]
By the defining inversion of the two-sided Clopper--Pearson interval at error \(\alpha/|\mathcal O|\), each marginal interval obeys
\[
\Pr_p\{p_o\notin[\ell_o(N_o),u_o(N_o)]\}
\leq\frac{\alpha}{|\mathcal O|}.
\tag{2}
\]
Indeed, its lower endpoint is obtained by inverting the upper-tail binomial test and its upper endpoint by inverting the lower-tail test, each at half the displayed marginal error; the two rejection probabilities sum to at most \(\alpha/|\mathcal O|\).  No independence among the counts is used.  The union bound applied to (2) gives
\[
\Pr_p\{p\in\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}\}
\geq1-\sum_{o\in\mathcal O}\frac{\alpha}{|\mathcal O|}
=1-\alpha.
\tag{3}
\]

On the event \(p\in\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}\), we also have \(p\in\mathfrak O\), so \(\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}\cap\mathfrak O\neq\varnothing\).  Every \(v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}\) satisfying \(Bv=p\) is then feasible for the confidence optimization.  By \(\text{prop:sharp-selective-label-interval}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
[L(p),U(p)]
&=\{h_e^{\top}v:v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}},\ Bv=p\}\\
&\subseteq
\{h_e^{\top}v:v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}},\ Bv\in\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}\}
=\mathcal I_{n,\alpha}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
If the intersection is empty, \(\text{def:whole-set-confidence-construction}\) returns \([0,1]\), which contains the identified set because \(h_e\) is binary and the latent weights form a probability vector.  Combining (3)--(4) yields
\[
\Pr_p\{[L(p),U(p)]\subseteq\mathcal I_{n,\alpha}\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
Finally, whenever \(\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}\cap\mathfrak O\neq\varnothing\), the feasible weight set is nonempty because \(\mathfrak O=B\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}\).  It is a closed subset of the compact simplex, since \(B\) is linear and \(\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}\) is closed.  Its linear image under \(v\mapsto h_e^{\top}v\) is therefore a compact interval, so both implementation optima are attained.
