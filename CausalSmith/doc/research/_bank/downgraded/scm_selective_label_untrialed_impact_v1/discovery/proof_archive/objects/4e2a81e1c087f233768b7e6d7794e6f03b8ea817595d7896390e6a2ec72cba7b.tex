In the row order of \(\text{def:complete-32-type-table}\), write \(b_i=B^{32}_{\cdot,i}\) and
\[
p_\dagger=\tfrac12(b_3+b_5)=\tfrac14(\mathbf e_2+\mathbf e_3+2\mathbf e_7).
\]
Write \((D_L,D_U)=\Gamma'_{p_\dagger}\) and \(H(g)=\max\{D_L(g),-D_U(g)\}\).  Then \(p_\dagger\in\mathfrak O^{32}\) is on a nontrivial projection face, both \(\Lambda_L(p_\dagger)\) and \(\Lambda_U(p_\dagger)\) contain distinct slope vectors, and the projection-composed endpoint derivative has a genuine, nondegenerate atom.  More precisely, for
\[
g_0=-\mathbf e_2-3\mathbf e_3+4\mathbf e_7,
\qquad
g_1=\mathbf e_2+\mathbf e_3-2\mathbf e_7,
\]
the exact tangent-cone projections are
\[
z_0=\Pi_{T_{\mathfrak O^{32}}(p_\dagger)}g_0
=\frac{4\mathbf e_1-\mathbf e_2-11\mathbf e_3+4\mathbf e_4+4\mathbf e_5}{5},
\qquad
z_1=\Pi_{T_{\mathfrak O^{32}}(p_\dagger)}g_1
=\frac{-8\mathbf e_7+2\mathbf e_8+2\mathbf e_9+2\mathbf e_{11}+2\mathbf e_{12}}{5},
\]
and
\[
D_L(g_0)=0,\quad D_U(g_0)=\frac65,\quad H(g_0)=0,
\qquad
D_L(g_1)=\frac{12}{5},\quad D_U(g_1)=0,\quad H(g_1)=\frac{12}{5}.
\]
Indeed, writing \(g(x,y)=x\mathbf e_2+y\mathbf e_3-(x+y)\mathbf e_7\), the full-dimensional cone
\[
\mathcal C_0=\{g(x,y):x+y<0,\ x>y,\ 3x-2y>0\}
\]
satisfies \(H(g)=0\).  The Gaussian \(G_{p_\dagger}\) has full support on
\[
V_{p_\dagger}=\{g(x,y):x,y\in\mathbb R\},
\]
and there is also a relatively open neighborhood of \(g_1\) on which \(H>0\).  Hence
\[
0<\Pr\{H(G_{p_\dagger})=0\}<1.
\]
