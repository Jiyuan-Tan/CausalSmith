Fix \(p\in\mathfrak O\), put \(\beta=1-\alpha\), write \((D_L,D_U)=\Gamma'_p\), and define \(H(g)=\max\{D_L(g),-D_U(g)\}\).  Let
\[
V_p=\{g:\mathbf 1^{\top}g=0,\ g_o=0\text{ whenever }p_o=0\}.
\]
Suppose the active projection cones and the extreme slopes of \(\Lambda_L(p)\) and \(\Lambda_U(p)\), refined by their zero and comparison hyperplanes, yield a finite polyhedral fan on \(V_p\) such that: (i) locally,
\[
\theta(\Pi_{\mathfrak O}(p+x))-\theta(p)=(D_L(x),D_U(x)),
\]
with both coordinates linear on every fan cone; and (ii) every retained proper hyperplane normal is nonzero on \(V_p\).  Let \(\mathcal R_p\) be the finite pool of all nonzero restrictions to \(V_p\) of those normals and endpoint slopes.  Let \(\mathcal A_p\) be the finite family of all matrices obtained by stacking any ordered subset of \(\mathcal R_p\cup(-\mathcal R_p)\), without repeated rows.  Thus \(\mathcal A_p\) contains all row stackings, row selections, and rowwise sign changes required by the finite Boolean and inclusion--exclusion lower-orthant events.  For \(M\in\mathcal A_p\), define
\[
\Omega_M=M\Sigma_pM^{\top},\qquad r_M=\operatorname{rank}(\Omega_M),
\]
and, with the maximum over an empty set equal to zero,
\[
C(p)=\max\left\{0,\max_{M\in\mathcal A_p:r_M>0}(42r_M^{1/4}+16)
\sum_{o\in\mathcal O}p_o
\|\Omega_M^{\dagger/2}M(\mathbf e_o-p)\|_2^3\right\}.
\]
If
\[
X_m=m^{-1/2}\sum_{i=1}^m(\mathbf e_{O_i}-p),
\qquad
\delta_m(p)=\max_{M\in\mathcal A_p}\sup_x
\left|\Pr_p\{MX_m\leq x\}-\Pr\{MG_p\leq x\}\right|,
\]
then \(C(p)<\infty\) for fixed \(p\) and \(\delta_m(p)\leq C(p)m^{-1/2}\).  This constant need not be uniform near covariance degeneracy.  Assume Gaussian nondegeneracy on \(V_p\):
\[
v^{\top}\Sigma_pv>0\quad\text{for every nonzero }v\in V_p.
\]
Use the explicit schedules
\[
m_n=\max\{1,\lfloor n^{1/3}\rfloor\},
\qquad
\eta_n=\min\{\alpha/2,n^{-1/12}\}.
\]
Then atoms of \(H(G_p)\) can occur only at zero and the centered without-replacement critical value satisfies
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\Pr_p\left\{
L(p)\geq\widehat L-\frac{c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,1-\alpha+\eta_n}}{\sqrt n},
U(p)\leq\widehat U+\frac{c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,1-\alpha+\eta_n}}{\sqrt n}
\right\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
No projection-cone or LP-basis uniqueness and no additional restriction on fan cones where \(H\) vanishes is required.  In the thirty-two-type table,
\[
p_\dagger=\tfrac12(B^{32}_{\cdot,3}+B^{32}_{\cdot,5})
=\tfrac14(\mathbf e_2+\mathbf e_3+2\mathbf e_7)
\]
has simultaneous projection and lower/upper LP ties.  Exact rational certificates give \((D_L,D_U,H)(g_0)=(0,6/5,0)\) for \(g_0=-\mathbf e_2-3\mathbf e_3+4\mathbf e_7\), and \((D_L,D_U,H)(g_1)=(12/5,0,12/5)\) for \(g_1=\mathbf e_2+\mathbf e_3-2\mathbf e_7\), with \(H=0\) on a full-dimensional fan cone.  Consequently
\[
0<\Pr\{H(G_{p_\dagger})=0\}<1,
\]
so the certificate has a genuine nondegenerate atom.
