Let \(\Phi(x)=\theta(\Pi_{\mathfrak O}x)\).  For \(x_k=p+t_kg_k\), \(t_k\downarrow0\), \(g_k\to g\), put \(q_k=\Pi_{\mathfrak O}x_k\) and \(d_k=(q_k-p)/t_k\).  Projection against feasible \(p\) gives \(\|q_k-x_k\|_2\leq t_k\|g_k\|_2\), so \((d_k)\) is bounded.  The projection variational inequality is
\[
\langle x_k-q_k,v-q_k\rangle\leq0\qquad(v\in\mathfrak O).
\]
Every subsequential limit \(d\) lies in \(T_{\mathfrak O}(p)\).  For a radial \(z\), substitute \(v=p+t_kz\), divide by \(t_k^2\), and pass to the limit.  Closure then gives
\[
\langle g-d,z-d\rangle\leq0\qquad(z\in T_{\mathfrak O}(p)).
\]
This uniquely characterizes \(d=\Pi_{T_{\mathfrak O}(p)}g\).  Hence the projection is Hadamard directionally differentiable with that derivative, including at a proper face.

Now consider
\[
\mathcal P=\operatorname{conv}\{(B_{\cdot,t},h_e(t)):t\in\mathcal T_{\mathrm{SLCC}}\}.
\]
Its lower and upper boundary functions are \(L\) and \(U\).  Its finitely many supporting facets show locally that \(L\) is a maximum of finitely many affine lower supports and \(U\) is a minimum of finitely many affine upper supports.  Their active slopes are precisely the normalized dual faces \(\Lambda_L(p)\) and \(\Lambda_U(p)\).  Therefore, for \(r_k=p+t_kz_k\in\mathfrak O\), \(z_k\to z\),
\[
\frac{L(r_k)-L(p)}{t_k}\to\max_{\lambda\in\Lambda_L(p)}\lambda^{\top}z,\qquad
\frac{U(r_k)-U(p)}{t_k}\to\min_{\lambda\in\Lambda_U(p)}\lambda^{\top}z.
\]
Inactive facets have a strict gap and disappear for large \(k\); this proves the formula without a unique basis.  Composing with the projection derivative yields
\[
\Phi'_p(g)=
\left(
\max_{\lambda\in\Lambda_L(p)}\lambda^{\top}\Pi_{T_{\mathfrak O}(p)}g,\
\min_{\lambda\in\Lambda_U(p)}\lambda^{\top}\Pi_{T_{\mathfrak O}(p)}g
\right)=\Gamma'_p(g).
\]
This is a continuous finite piecewise-linear map.  By \(\text{lem:finite-multinomial-clt}\),
\[
\sqrt n(\widehat p-p)\rightsquigarrow G_p.
\]
Applying \(\text{lem:hadamard-directional-delta-method}\) gives
\[
\sqrt n\{\widehat\theta-\theta(p)\}\rightsquigarrow\Gamma'_p(G_p).
\]
The derivation allowed a projection face and retained the entire optimal dual faces, proving the final assertion.
