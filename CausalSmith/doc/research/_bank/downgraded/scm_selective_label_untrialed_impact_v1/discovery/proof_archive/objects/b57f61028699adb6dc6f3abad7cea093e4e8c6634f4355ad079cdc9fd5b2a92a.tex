Let \(d=|\mathcal O|\), let \(\Phi=\theta\circ\Pi_{\mathfrak O}\), and define
\[
X_n=\sqrt n(\widehat p-p),
\qquad
T_n=\max\{\sqrt n(\widehat L-L(p)),\sqrt n(U(p)-\widehat U)\}.
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\text{lem:finite-multinomial-clt}\), using \(\text{ass:iid-finite-sampling}\),
\[
X_n\rightsquigarrow G_p,
\qquad X_n=O_{\Pr}(1).
\tag{2}
\]
Furthermore, \(X_n\in V_p\) almost surely: its coordinates sum to zero, and if \(p_o=0\), then \(N_o=0\) almost surely.

By the local fan hypothesis, there is \(r>0\) such that
\[
\Phi(p+x)-\Phi(p)=(D_L(x),D_U(x))
\quad\text{for }x\in V_p\text{ with }\|x\|_2<r.
\tag{3}
\]
The two coordinates are continuous, positively homogeneous, and linear on finitely many fan cones.  Hence there is \(C_D<\infty\) such that
\[
|D_L(x)|\vee|D_U(x)|\leq C_D\|x\|_2,
\qquad x\in V_p.
\tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4), consistently with \(\text{thm:projection-composed-directional-limit}\), imply
\[
T_n=H(X_n)
\tag{5}
\]
with probability tending to one and \(H(X_n)\rightsquigarrow H(G_p)\).

Put \(m=m_n\).  Conditional on the full sample, let \(\widehat p_b\) be the empirical law of a uniformly chosen size-\(m\) subsample without replacement, and put
\[
a_n=\sqrt m(\widehat p-p)=\sqrt{m/n}\,X_n.
\tag{6}
\]
When both empirical laws lie in the neighborhood in (3), the random quantity whose lower conditional quantile defines \(c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,1-\alpha+\eta_n}\) equals
\[
S_{n,b}=\max\left\{
D_L\bigl(\sqrt m(\widehat p_b-p)\bigr)-D_L(a_n),
D_U(a_n)-D_U\bigl(\sqrt m(\widehat p_b-p)\bigr)
\right\}.
\tag{7}
\]
Indeed, apply (3) at \(\widehat p_b\) and \(\widehat p\), subtract the two endpoint coordinates, multiply by \(\sqrt m\), and use positive homogeneity.

We next compare (7) with an independent size-\(m\) experiment.  Couple the ordered indices sampled without replacement to \(m\) indices sampled with replacement from \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\).  Coupling can fail only if two with-replacement indices coincide, so the union bound gives conditional failure probability at most
\[
\frac{m(m-1)}{2n}.
\tag{8}
\]
For each with-replacement draw, use a maximal categorical coupling between \(\widehat p\) and \(p\).  Its mismatch probability is
\[
1-\sum_o\min\{\widehat p_o,p_o\}
=\operatorname{TV}(\widehat p,p)
=\frac12\|\widehat p-p\|_1.
\]
Independent copies and another union bound therefore give total conditional mismatch probability at most
\[
\frac m2\|\widehat p-p\|_1.
\tag{9}
\]
On \(\{\|X_n\|_2\leq M\}\), (9) is at most \(m\sqrt d M/(2\sqrt n)\).

On the successful coupling event, \(\sqrt m(\widehat p_b-p)=X_m\) for the independent vector in \(\text{lem:finite-alphabet-fan-berry-esseen-explicit-schedule}\), and (7) becomes
\[
K_{a_n}(X_m),
\qquad
K_a(g)=\max\{D_L(g)-D_L(a),D_U(a)-D_U(g)\}.
\tag{10}
\]
The local-neighborhood failure is exponentially small.  Each coordinate of \(\mathbf e_{O_i}-p\) is centered and belongs to \([-1,1]\).  Applying the exponential Markov inequality to each coordinate and taking a finite union gives constants \(C_0,c_0>0\), depending only on the fixed law and the neighborhood, such that
\[
\Pr_p\{\|X_m\|_2>r\sqrt m/2\}\leq C_0e^{-c_0m}.
\tag{11}
\]
For fixed \(M\), equations (6), (8)--(11) thus bound the conditional coupling and localization error on \(\{\|X_n\|_2\leq M\}\) by
\[
q_{n,M}=\frac{m(m-1)}{2n}
+\frac{m\sqrt d M}{2\sqrt n}+C_0e^{-c_0m}.
\tag{12}
\]

On each pair of fan cones containing \(a\) and \(g\), the event \(K_a(g)\leq x\) is an intersection of finitely many linear inequalities.  Taking the finite union over cone pairs expresses it as a finite Boolean combination of lower orthants whose row matrices are in \(\mathcal A_p\).  Finite inclusion--exclusion and \(\text{lem:finite-alphabet-fan-berry-esseen-explicit-schedule}\) therefore bound the multinomial-to-Gaussian comparison by a fixed finite multiple of
\[
\delta_m(p)\leq C(p)m^{-1/2}.
\tag{13}
\]
The rank-zero members contribute no error because both corresponding linear images are deterministic zero.  This is precisely where the effective-range whitening in that lemma is needed.

Equation (4) also yields
\[
|K_{a_n}(g)-H(g)|
\leq |D_L(a_n)|\vee|D_U(a_n)|
\leq C_D\|a_n\|_2.
\tag{14}
\]
On \(\{\|X_n\|_2\leq M\}\), the last bound is at most \(C_DM\sqrt{m/n}\).  Every nonzero normal retained in \(\mathcal A_p\) has, by Gaussian nondegeneracy on \(V_p\), a centered normal image with positive variance and hence a bounded density.  The Gaussian probability of every slab created by (14) is consequently bounded by a fixed multiple of \(\sqrt{m/n}\), which is at most a fixed multiple of \(m/\sqrt n\).

For the fixed schedules in the statement, \(\text{lem:finite-alphabet-fan-berry-esseen-explicit-schedule}\) gives
\[
\delta_m(p)=O(n^{-1/6}),\qquad
\frac m{\sqrt n}=O(n^{-1/6}),\qquad
\frac{m^2}{n}=O(n^{-1/3}),\qquad
e^{-c_0m}=O(e^{-c'n^{1/3}})
\tag{15}
\]
for some \(c'>0\).  Since \(\eta_n=n^{-1/12}\) eventually, every term in (15) is \(o(\eta_n)\).  Combining (2) and (12)--(15), at every continuity point \(x\) of the distribution of \(H(G_p)\), gives
\[
\Pr^*\{S_{n,b}\leq x\}\longrightarrow
F(x):=\Pr\{H(G_p)\leq x\}
\quad\text{in probability},
\tag{16}
\]
where \(\Pr^*\) denotes conditional probability given the full sample.

On the relative interior of each full-dimensional cone of the fan refined by endpoint-comparison and zero hyperplanes, \(H\) is one homogeneous linear functional.  Boundaries of these cones lie in finitely many proper hyperplanes whose normals are nonzero on \(V_p\), and hence have \(G_p\)-probability zero by Gaussian nondegeneracy.  A nonzero linear functional of \(G_p\) has a continuous Gaussian law; a zero linear functional is identically zero.  It follows that
\[
F\text{ can have an atom only at }0.
\tag{17}
\]

Let \(c_n=c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,\beta+\eta_n}\), using the lower-quantile convention, and let \(q_\beta=\inf\{x:F(x)\geq\beta\}\).  If \(q_\beta\neq0\), then for every sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\), the point \(x_\varepsilon=q_\beta-\varepsilon\) may be chosen as a continuity point and satisfies \(F(x_\varepsilon)<\beta\).  Equation (16), together with \(\eta_n\downarrow0\), implies \(\Pr\{c_n>x_\varepsilon\}\to1\).  Combining this fact with (5), weak convergence, and continuity at the nonzero point \(q_\beta\), and then letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), yields
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\Pr\{T_n\leq c_n\}
\geq F(q_\beta)\geq\beta.
\tag{18}
\]

It remains to treat \(q_\beta=0\), where
\[
F(0-)\leq\beta\leq F(0).
\tag{19}
\]
If \(H(X_n)=0\), positive homogeneity and (6) give
\[
D_L(a_n)\leq0,
\qquad D_U(a_n)\geq0.
\tag{20}
\]
For every \(g\in V_p\), the global sign implication
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_{a_n}(g)<0
&\Longrightarrow D_L(g)<D_L(a_n)\leq0
\ \text{and}\ D_U(g)>D_U(a_n)\geq0\\
&\Longrightarrow H(g)<0
\end{aligned}
\tag{21}
\]
uses neither a selected projection cone nor a unique LP slope.  The event \(\{H(g)<0\}\) is a finite union of intersections of strict half-spaces from the refined fan.  Approximation from below, finite inclusion--exclusion, and the same coupling comparison used in (12)--(13) give, uniformly on \(\{\|X_n\|_2\leq M,H(X_n)=0\}\),
\[
\Pr^*\{S_{n,b}<0\}
\leq F(0-)+O(\delta_m(p))+O_M(m/\sqrt n)
+O(m^2/n)+O(e^{-c_0m}).
\tag{22}
\]
By (15), the error in (22) is \(o(\eta_n)\).  If \(F(0-)<\beta\), it is eventually smaller than the fixed gap to \(\beta\); if \(F(0-)=\beta\), it is smaller than the added slack \(\eta_n\) with probability tending to one.  A lower \((\beta+\eta_n)\)-quantile is nonnegative whenever the conditional mass strictly below zero is less than \(\beta+\eta_n\).  Letting \(M\to\infty\) using (2), we obtain
\[
\Pr\{H(X_n)=0,\ c_n<0\}\longrightarrow0.
\tag{23}
\]

Also \(c_n=o_{\Pr}(1)\).  For every \(\varepsilon>0\), \(F(-\varepsilon)<\beta\), so (16) gives \(c_n> -\varepsilon\) with probability tending to one.  Moreover \(F(\varepsilon)>\beta\): this is immediate if \(F(0)>\beta\); if \(F(0)=\beta\) and \(H\) takes a positive value, continuity, positive homogeneity, and Gaussian full support on \(V_p\) give positive probability to \(0<H(G_p)\leq\varepsilon\); if \(H\) takes no positive value, then \(F(\varepsilon)=1\).  Another use of (16) gives \(c_n\leq\varepsilon\) with probability tending to one.  Hence
\[
c_n=o_{\Pr}(1).
\tag{24}
\]

The zero set of \(H\) is a finite union of interiors of full-dimensional zero cones and subsets of finitely many proper fan hyperplanes.  Its boundary is therefore Gaussian-null by nondegeneracy, so it is a continuity set for (2).  Consequently
\[
\Pr\{H(X_n)=0\}\longrightarrow F(0)-F(0-).
\tag{25}
\]
For each \(\varepsilon>0\), equations (5), (23), and (24) cover, apart from probability tending to zero, the two disjoint events \(\{H(X_n)\leq-\varepsilon\}\) and \(\{H(X_n)=0\}\).  Equations (17) and (25) thus imply
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\Pr\{T_n\leq c_n\}
\geq F(-\varepsilon)+F(0)-F(0-).
\]
Letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) makes the right-hand side \(F(0)\geq\beta\).  Together with (18),
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\Pr\{T_n\leq c_n\}\geq1-\alpha.
\tag{26}
\]
By (1), the event in (26) is exactly the pair of endpoint inequalities in the statement.  The quantile level is well typed because \(0<\eta_n\leq\alpha/2\), whence \(0<1-\alpha+\eta_n<1\).

Finally, \(\text{prop:32-nondegenerate-atomic-tie-certificate}\) supplies the exact early-kill verification at \(p_\dagger\).  Its tangent representations, all-column polar and dual checks, and matching primal directions establish the stated projections and the values
\[
(D_L,D_U,H)(g_0)=(0,6/5,0),
\qquad
(D_L,D_U,H)(g_1)=(12/5,0,12/5).
\]
It also supplies distinct optimal slopes for both endpoint programs, an open zero cone, and an open positive region.  Because the three positive coordinates of \(p_\dagger\) make \(\Sigma_{p_\dagger}\) positive definite on the two-dimensional zero-sum support space \(V_{p_\dagger}\), the corresponding Gaussian has full support there.  The two open regions therefore have positive probability, proving
\[
0<\Pr\{H(G_{p_\dagger})=0\}<1.
\]
This proves the asserted nonvacuity without projection-cone or LP-basis uniqueness.
