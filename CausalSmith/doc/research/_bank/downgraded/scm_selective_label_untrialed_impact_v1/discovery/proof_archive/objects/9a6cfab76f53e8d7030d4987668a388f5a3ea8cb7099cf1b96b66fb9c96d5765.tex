For each \(o\), \(N_o\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p_o)\).  Exact Clopper--Pearson coverage and the union bound give
\[
\Pr_p\{p\in\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}\}
\geq1-\sum_{o\in\mathcal O}\frac{\alpha}{|\mathcal O|}=1-\alpha.
\]
On this event, \(p\in\mathfrak O\cap\mathcal C_{n,\alpha}\).  Every \(v\) with \(Bv=p\) is feasible for the confidence optimization, so sharpness gives
\[
[L(p),U(p)]
=\{h_e^{\top}v:Bv=p,\ v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}\}
\subseteq
\{h_e^{\top}v:Bv\in\mathcal C_{n,\alpha},\ v\in\Delta_{\mathrm{SLCC}}\}
=\mathcal I_{n,\alpha}.
\]
Thus the asserted coverage is at least \(1-\alpha\).  Off this event an empty intersection triggers \([0,1]\), which still contains the identified set.  When the intersection is nonempty, its feasible weights form a nonempty closed subset of the compact simplex; their linear image is a compact interval and both implementation optima are attained.
