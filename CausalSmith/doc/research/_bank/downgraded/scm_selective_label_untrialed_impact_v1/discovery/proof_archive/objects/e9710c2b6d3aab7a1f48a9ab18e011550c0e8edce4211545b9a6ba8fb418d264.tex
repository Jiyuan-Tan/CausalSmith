At \(p_0=p^{32,\circ}\), the certificate gives
\[
L(p_0)-L_{\mathrm{blind}}(K^{32}p_0)=199/800,\qquad
U_{\mathrm{blind}}(K^{32}p_0)-U(p_0)=199/800.
\]
The lower value is convex and the upper value is concave on the observable polytope: convex combinations of feasible weights prove the two inequalities.  Both are bounded in \([0,1]\).

We prove the needed relative-interior continuity.  Let \(f:C\to[0,1]\) be convex and \(x_0\in\operatorname{relint}(C)\).  Choose \(r>0\) such that the relative radius-\(r\) ball about \(x_0\) lies in \(C\).  For \(x=x_0+du\), \(0<d<r\), \(\|u\|_2=1\),
\[
x=(1-d/r)x_0+(d/r)(x_0+ru)
\]
gives \(f(x)\leq f(x_0)+d/r\), while
\[
x_0=\frac r{r+d}x+\frac d{r+d}(x_0-ru)
\]
gives \(f(x)\geq f(x_0)-d/r\).  Hence \(f\) is continuous at \(x_0\); applying this to \(-f\) proves the concave case.

The certificate puts \(p_0\) in \(\operatorname{relint}(\mathfrak O^{32})\).  Its positive generator representation also puts \(K^{32}p_0\) in the relative interior of the blind image.  Hence all four value functions, with the blind pair composed with \(K^{32}\), are continuous at \(p_0\).  The two positive gaps remain positive on a relatively open neighborhood \(\mathcal U\) of \(p_0\), proving the claim.
