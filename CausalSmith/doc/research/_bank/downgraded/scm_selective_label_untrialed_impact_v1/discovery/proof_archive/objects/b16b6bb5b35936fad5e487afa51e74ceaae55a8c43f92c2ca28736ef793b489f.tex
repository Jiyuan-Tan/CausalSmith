Let \(\Phi=\theta\circ\Pi_{\mathfrak O}\).  The fan assumption supplies \(r>0\) for which
\[
\Phi(p+x)-\Phi(p)=(D_L(x),D_U(x))\qquad(\|x\|_2<r).
\]
Put \(X_n=\sqrt n(\widehat p-p)\) and
\[
T_n=\max\{\sqrt n(\widehat L-L(p)),\sqrt n(U(p)-\widehat U)\}.
\]
Since \(X_n=O_p(1)\), the exact local formula gives \(T_n=H(X_n)\) with probability tending to one.

For a size-\(m\) subsample \(b\), put
\[
G_{m,b}=\sqrt m(\widehat p_b-\widehat p),\qquad
a_n=\sqrt m(\widehat p-p)=\sqrt{m/n}\,X_n.
\]
On bounded sets its centered statistic equals
\[
S_{n,b}=H_{a_n}(G_{m,b}),\qquad
H_a(g)=\max\{D_L(a+g)-D_L(a),-[D_U(a+g)-D_U(a)]\}.
\]
Finite-population second moments make the omitted tails uniformly negligible.

Couple sampling without replacement to sampling with replacement from \(\widehat p\); the failure probability is at most \(m(m-1)/(2n)\).  Couple \(m\) draws from \(\widehat p\) to \(m\) draws from \(p\); the failure probability is at most \(m\|\widehat p-p\|_1/2=O_p(m/\sqrt n)\).  Fan events are finite Boolean combinations of half-spaces with normals in \(\mathcal A_p\), so inclusion--exclusion and \(\delta_m(p)\) compare their independent-\(p\) probabilities with Gaussian probabilities.  Translation by \(a_n\) changes a cone only within \(C\|a_n\|_2\) of finitely many facets.  Nondegeneracy bounds those Gaussian tube probabilities by \(C'\|a_n\|_2\), and \(\|a_n\|_2=O_p(\sqrt{m/n})=O_p(m/\sqrt n)\).  Thus the total conditional comparison error is
\[
O_p\!\left(\delta_m(p)+\frac m{\sqrt n}+\frac{m^2}{n}\right).
\]
It follows that the conditional law of \(S_{n,b}\) converges weakly in probability to the law \(F\) of \(H(G_p)\).  Also \(\delta_m(p)\to0\): after deleting any zero rows, every \(A\in\mathcal A_p\) satisfies \(AX_m\rightsquigarrow AG_p\), and every marginal of \(AG_p\) is continuous; hence the associated multivariate distribution functions converge uniformly.

On each full-dimensional refinement cone, \(H\) is linear.  A nonzero linear functional cannot be constant on a set of positive Gaussian volume, and homogeneity forces a constant linear piece to equal zero.  Proper fan boundaries have Gaussian probability zero.  Therefore zero is the only possible atom.

Let \(q_\beta=\inf\{x:F(x)\geq\beta\}\).  If \(q_\beta\neq0\), \(F\) is continuous at the relevant quantile.  Conditional weak convergence and \(\eta_n\to0\) give quantile convergence, and \(T_n\rightsquigarrow H(G_p)\) gives
\[
\liminf_n\Pr\{T_n\leq c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,\beta+\eta_n}\}\geq\beta.
\]

Now suppose \(q_\beta=0\), so \(F(0-)\leq\beta\leq F(0)\).  On a bounded realization \(z=X_n\) with \(H(z)=0\), homogeneity gives
\[
D_L(a_n)=\sqrt{m/n}\,D_L(z)\leq0,\qquad
D_U(a_n)=\sqrt{m/n}\,D_U(z)\geq0.
\]
If \(g\) is in the interior of a zero cone and farther than \(C\|a_n\|_2\) from its facets, then \(g+a_n\) remains there.  If the lower slope is zero, its centered lower increment equals \(-D_L(a_n)\geq0\); if the upper slope is zero, its negative centered upper increment equals \(D_U(a_n)\geq0\).  Thus zero-cone mass cannot move below zero when the full-sample direction is itself in the zero set, except through the already controlled facet tubes.  Uniformly over bounded such \(z\),
\[
\Pr^*\{S_{n,b}<0\}
\leq F(0-)+O_p\!\left(\delta_m(p)+\frac m{\sqrt n}+\frac{m^2}{n}\right).
\]
The rate and quantile slack imply
\[
c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,\beta+\eta_n}\geq0
\]
with conditional probability tending to one on \(\{H(X_n)=0\}\).  Conditional weak convergence also gives \(c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,\beta+\eta_n}=o_p(1)\).

By the stipulated refinement, the zero set is a union of zero-cone interiors plus finitely many proper Gaussian-null hyperplanes, so it is a Gaussian continuity set and
\[
\Pr\{H(X_n)=0\}\to F(0)-F(0-).
\]
For every \(\varepsilon>0\), observations with \(H(X_n)<-\varepsilon\) are covered because the critical value is \(o_p(1)\), and observations with \(H(X_n)=0\) are covered by its nonnegativity.  Hence
\[
\liminf_n\Pr\{T_n\leq c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,\beta+\eta_n}\}
\geq F(-\varepsilon)+F(0)-F(0-).
\]
Letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) gives \(F(0)\geq\beta\).  Finally, \(T_n\leq c^{\mathrm{sub}}_{n,\beta+\eta_n}\) is exactly the conjunction of the two endpoint inequalities, proving the theorem.
