The generic explicit-rate part does close independently.  For each matrix \(M\) in the finite Boolean-event matrix family, let \(\Omega_M=M\Sigma_pM^{\top}\) and \(r_M=\operatorname{rank}(\Omega_M)\).  If \(r_M=0\), then every summand \(M(\mathbf e_{O_i}-p)\) has zero second moment and hence is zero almost surely, so the lower-orthant approximation error is zero.  If \(r_M>0\), restriction to \(\operatorname{range}(\Omega_M)\), followed by whitening with \(\Omega_M^{\dagger/2}\), makes the total covariance the identity on \(\mathbb R^{r_M}\); the inverse image of an original lower orthant is convex.  Rai\v{c}'s convex-set Berry--Esseen theorem therefore yields
\[
\delta_m(p)\leq C(p)m^{-1/2},
\]
where
\[
C(p)=\max_{M:r_M>0}
\left(42r_M^{1/4}+16\right)
\sum_{o\in\mathcal O}p_o
\left\|\Omega_M^{\dagger/2}M(\mathbf e_o-p)\right\|_2^3<\infty.
\]
The finiteness is pointwise for fixed \(p\) and is not uniform near covariance degeneracy.  With \(m_n=\max\{1,\lfloor n^{1/3}\rfloor\}\) and \(\eta_n=\min\{\alpha/2,n^{-1/12}\}\), the four conditional-approximation terms are respectively \(O(n^{-1/6})\), \(O(n^{-1/6})\), \(O(n^{-1/3})\), and \(O(\exp\{-c n^{1/3}\})\), each \(o(\eta_n)\).  This proves the feasible-schedule component but cannot repair the false thirty-two-type projection certificate.
