The primitive bracket-stratum condition \(\mathsf{Bracket}_{\mathrm{SLCC}}\) holds when there are \(s_*\in\mathcal S\) and \(q_\ell<q_m<q_h\) in \(\mathcal Q\) such that
\[
c_e(s_*)=(1,q_m),\qquad
c_j(s_*)\in\{(1,q_\ell),(1,q_h)\}\quad(j\in J_0),
\]
at least one trial arm uses \((1,q_\ell)\), at least one trial arm uses \((1,q_h)\), and \(\rho_j>0\) for every \(j\in J_0\).  No restriction is placed on other strata, on configurations away from \(s_*\), or on additional elements of \(\mathcal Q\).
