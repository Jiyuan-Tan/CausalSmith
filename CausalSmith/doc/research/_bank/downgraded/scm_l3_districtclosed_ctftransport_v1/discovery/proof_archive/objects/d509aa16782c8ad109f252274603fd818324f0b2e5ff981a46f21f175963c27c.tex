Assume that the exact global acquisition instance of \(\mathrm{def:global\text{-}regime\text{-}block\text{-}acquisition}\) is admissible.  Then \(\Lambda_S\ne\varnothing\) for every \(S\subseteq\mathcal U\).  The closed evidence test on \(\Lambda_{\varnothing}\) returns uniform zero evidence or mixed zero and positive evidence exactly in those two cases and then defines no arm-ranking acquisition problem.  In the remaining case there is a number \(\epsilon>0\) such that
\[
\Phi_{E,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\vartheta^\star)\geq\epsilon
\qquad
\text{for every }S\subseteq\mathcal U\text{ and }\vartheta\in\Lambda_S.
\]
In this uniformly positive case the following conclusions hold.

First, for each \(S\subseteq\mathcal U\) and \(\rho\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\), membership \(\rho\in\operatorname{PO}_G(S)\) is decided exactly by one closed existential formula over the reals.  Exhaustive enumeration returns every minimum-cost solution of the global acquisition problem using at most
\[
2^{|\mathcal U|}|\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}|
\]
membership quantifier-elimination calls, in addition to a fixed number of evidence calls and one joint-realizability call.

Second, for every \(S\), \(\operatorname{PO}_G(S)\) is exactly the set of arms that are conditionally optimal in at least one original-alphabet SCM family in \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P_S)\).  Thus this acquisition problem is exact for every finite sparse ADMG, not merely for a district-saturated relaxation.

Third, whenever the same augmented information is represented by the local row system, broad compatibility implies
\[
\operatorname{PO}_G(S)\subseteq\operatorname{PO}(S).
\]
If, for that augmented menu, the checked arm events are focal-district local and the focal projection hypotheses of \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}district\text{-}fiber\text{-}projection}\) hold, then equality holds.  Hence the local program is always an outer pruning relaxation and is exact only under those additional hypotheses.

Finally, the two-experiment Boolean-pushforward instance of \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}layer3\text{-}acquisition\text{-}obstruction}\) satisfies, in the induced response-law coordinates,
\[
\{q_D^\star(\vartheta):\vartheta\in\Lambda_S\}=\mathcal K_S
\qquad(S\subseteq\{e_1,e_2\}).
\]
Consequently its displayed possibly-optimal table also holds with \(\operatorname{PO}_G\), so \(f_G\) is not submodular in general and the unique exact broad-model acquisition solution in that instance buys both experiments at total cost two.
