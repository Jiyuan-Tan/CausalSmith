Fix a \(\mathsf{CheckedLocalArms}_D\) record.  Each \(e\in\mathcal U\) is a realizable finite block of typed descriptors, possibly with deterministic finite postprocessing.  Acquiring \(e\) reveals the complete finite law of every declared output, including every normalization-dependent complementary cell, equivalently a dependent row-value block \((R_e,p_e)\), at cost \(c_e>0\).  Require joint realizability of the whole experiment menu:
\[
\mathcal K_{\mathcal U}
=\{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D):R_eq=p_e\text{ for every }e\in\mathcal U\}
\ne\varnothing.
\]
For \(S\subseteq\mathcal U\), define
\[
\mathcal K_S
=\{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D):R_eq=p_e\text{ for every }e\in S\}.
\]
The checked lower bound \(d_D^{\mathsf T}q\geq\varepsilon_D>0\) persists on every subface.  Hence every local subset is uniformly positive before acquisition, no subset-specific evidence screen is needed, and one may define the local row-face possibly optimal set by
\[
\operatorname{PO}(S)
=\left\{\rho\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}:
\exists q\in\mathcal K_S\ \forall\sigma\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}},\ 
(n_{\rho,D}-n_{\sigma,D})^{\mathsf T}q\geq0\right\}.
\]
Finally set
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{full}}=\operatorname{PO}(\mathcal U),
\qquad
f(S)=|\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}|-|\operatorname{PO}(S)|,
\]
and define the local ex-post acquisition problem by
\[
\min_{S\subseteq\mathcal U}\sum_{e\in S}c_e
\quad\text{subject to}\quad
\operatorname{PO}(S)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{full}}.
\]
This construction is the computationally cheap district row-face layer and is the uniformly-positive special case of subset-screened global acquisition.  It does not declare \(\mathcal K_S\) to be the exact projection of the broad multi-district compatibility fiber: that equality requires the separately stated augmented focal-saturation and baseline-averaged separation hypotheses.
