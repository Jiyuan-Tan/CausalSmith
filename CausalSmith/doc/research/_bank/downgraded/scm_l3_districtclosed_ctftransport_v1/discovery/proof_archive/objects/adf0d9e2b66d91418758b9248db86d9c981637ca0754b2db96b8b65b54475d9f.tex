The normalization row in \(A_D\) and nonnegativity imply \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\subseteq\Delta(\Omega_D)\); hence the nonempty primal feasible set is bounded.  Apply finite linear-programming strong duality with \(c=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\).  Both optima are attained and
\[
\min_{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)}g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q
=\max_{\eta:A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}}
m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta.
\tag{1}
\]
This is the asserted duality identity.

For a broad compatible model, one-district projection gives \(q_D^\star\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\).  The checked target locality judgments and district-cell linearity give
\[
Q_\sigma^\star-Q_\rho^\star
=\frac{g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star}
{d_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star}.
\tag{2}
\]
If \(A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta\le g^{\mathsf T}\), then nonnegativity of \(q_D^\star\) and \(A_Dq_D^\star=m_D\) imply
\[
g^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\ge
\eta^{\mathsf T}A_Dq_D^\star=m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta.
\]
A positive certificate and the positive denominator in (2) therefore prove strict dominance throughout the broad fiber.

Assume now \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  Exact focal projection makes every \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) the target focal law of an original-alphabet compatible model.  Thus uniform strict dominance is equivalent, by (2) and attained compactness, to \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)>0\).  By (1), this holds exactly when an attained dual optimizer has positive value.  If the minimum is negative, a minimizing law lifts and (2) gives the reverse strict ranking.  If the maximum is positive as well, maximizing and minimizing laws lift to two models with opposite rankings.  The focal lifting theorem preserves every nonfocal district.  The stronger sufficient conditions follow from the separation hierarchy.  Without locality, (2) is unavailable; without exact projection, row-face extrema need not lift.
