The normalization row and nonnegativity make the nonempty face \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) a compact subset of the simplex.  Apply finite linear-programming strong duality with cost \(g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) to obtain attainment and
\[
\min_{q\geq0:A_Dq=m_D}g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q
=\max_{\eta:A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta\leq g_{\rho,\sigma,D}}
m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta.
\tag{1}
\]
These are the primal and dual quantities in the statement by the two definitions.

For a broad compatible model, projection puts \(q_D^\star\) in the face.  The checked-local-arms record and the district-cell lemma give
\[
Q_\sigma^\star-Q_\rho^\star
=\frac{g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star}
{d_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star},
\qquad
d_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\geq\varepsilon_D>0.
\tag{2}
\]
For every dual-feasible \(\eta\),
\[
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star
\geq\eta^{\mathsf T}A_Dq_D^\star
=m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus a positive dual value proves broad strict dominance.

Under \(\mathsf{BASep}_D\), every face law lifts to the focal fiber.  Since the minimum in \((1)\) is attained and the denominator in \((2)\) is positive, strict dominance at every lifted law is equivalent to \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)>0\).  Strong duality makes this equivalent to existence of a positive attained dual value.  A negative minimizing law, and when present a positive maximizing law, lift to the asserted original-alphabet reverse-ranking and opposite-ranking witnesses while every nonfocal district remains fixed.  The separation hierarchy gives the simpler sufficient checks.  Without the record, \((2)\) is not defined; without exact focal projection, \((3)\) remains sound but a primal optimizer need not lift.
