Fix an accepted fixing trace \(\tau\), a domain label \(s\) occurring in that trace, and a family \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  Membership in \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), by \(\mathrm{def:compatibility\text{-}fiber}\), implies the recursive finite-state condition \(\mathrm{ass:recursive\text{-}compatibility}\) and the cross-district independence condition \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}factorization}\).  By \(\mathrm{def:district\text{-}response\text{-}profile}\), recursive substitution therefore assigns to each event \(A\) in the trace a deterministic indicator
\[
 I_A:\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_D\longrightarrow\{0,1\}.
\]
The carrier is finite.  Independence of the district response blocks gives the exact expansion
\[
 P_{M^s}(A)
 =\sum_{\omega\in\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_D}
 I_A(\omega)\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^s(\omega_D).
\tag{1}
\]
Thus every unconditional response-cell probability used by \(\tau\) is a polynomial with integer coefficients in the finitely many response-law coordinates.  The same argument applies separately to every source-labelled leaf of the trace, so an expression with several labelled leaves is a polynomial in the union of their finite coordinate families.

We next verify the semantics of the expression constructors specified in \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\).  The false and true events have indicators zero and one.  If \((A_j:j\in J)\) is a finite pairwise-disjoint family, then pointwise \(I_{\bigsqcup_j A_j}=\sum_j I_{A_j}\); substituting this equality into (1) yields
\[
 P_{M^s}\!\left(\bigsqcup_{j\in J}A_j\right)
 =\sum_{j\in J}P_{M^s}(A_j).
\tag{2}
\]
The marginalization constructor is (2) for the finite partition indexed by the marginalized variable's declared state space.  Pointwise \(I_{A^{\mathsf c}}=1-I_A\), so
\[
 P_{M^s}(A^{\mathsf c})=1-P_{M^s}(A).
\tag{3}
\]
An algebraic product constructor preserves equality of already interpreted scalar expressions.  When such a product is annotated as a district-split event probability, its annotation is valid because \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}factorization}\) makes the indicated district blocks independent.  A type-correct substitution is a homomorphism for rational constants, finite sums, differences, and products; hence it carries each previously checked polynomial identity to a valid polynomial identity.

Consider a conditional constructor with numerator event \(A\cap H\) and context event \(H\).  Acceptance requires a separate certificate whose declared conclusion is
\[
 P_{M^s}(H)>0
\tag{4}
\]
throughout its declared compatibility fiber.  Consequently the quotient is defined, and the definition of conditional probability gives
\[
 P_{M^s}(A\mid H)
 =\frac{P_{M^s}(A\cap H)}{P_{M^s}(H)}.
\tag{5}
\]
Equations (1)--(5), by structural induction over the finite expression syntax, identify the semantic value of every subexpression of \(\tau\).

It remains to prove that the final exact trace check entails equality.  Recursively clear all conditional occurrences.  If the distinct context probabilities appearing as divisors are \(H_1(\mathbf M),\ldots,H_J(\mathbf M)\), the clearing multiplier has the form
\[
 \Delta_\tau(\mathbf M)
 =\prod_{j=1}^{J}H_j(\mathbf M)^{k_j},
 \qquad k_j\in\mathbb N_{>0}.
\tag{6}
\]
Every factor in (6) is strictly positive by its separately accepted certificate, so
\[
 \Delta_\tau(\mathbf M)>0.
\tag{7}
\]
Let \(L_\tau\) and \(R_\tau\) be the response-profile expressions at the input and output of the trace.  By the acceptance premise in the statement and the exact-checker clause of \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\), normalization and rational coefficient comparison verify the formal polynomial identity
\[
 \Delta_\tau L_\tau-\Delta_\tau R_\tau=0.
\tag{8}
\]
Substitution of the response-law coordinates of \(\mathbf M\) into (8) is legitimate because all sums and products are finite.  It gives
\[
 \Delta_\tau(\mathbf M)L_\tau(\mathbf M)
 =\Delta_\tau(\mathbf M)R_\tau(\mathbf M).
\tag{9}
\]
Division of (9) by the strictly positive number in (7) yields
\[
 L_\tau(\mathbf M)=R_\tau(\mathbf M).
\]
Since \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) was arbitrary, every accepted fixing trace preserves its represented event probability on the entire compatibility fiber.
