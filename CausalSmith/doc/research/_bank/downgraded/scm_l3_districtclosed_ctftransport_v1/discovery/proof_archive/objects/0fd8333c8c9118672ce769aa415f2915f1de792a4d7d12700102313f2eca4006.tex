Let \(\mathfrak L_D\) be a checked local-arm instance containing \(\rho,\sigma\).  If \(A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) and \(m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}>0\), then removing \(\rho\) cannot remove a maximizing defined arm from \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\).  An arm without the checked locality and common strict-positive evidence certificate is outside this ranking problem; uniformly zero evidence is reported as undefined rather than ranked.
