Fix canonical finite orders on vertices, regimes, districts, assignments, and generated DAG-node identifiers. The event grammar is \[\mathsf{Atom}::=[s]\,V_{\alpha}=v,\qquad \mathsf{Event}::=\top\mid\bot\mid\mathsf{Atom}\mid\mathsf{Event}\wedge\mathsf{Event}\mid\mathsf{Event}\vee\mathsf{Event},\] where \(\alpha\) is a finite well-typed intervention or partial-copy term and canonical normalized events are disjoint normal forms of unnested atoms. Identified expressions have grammar \[\mathsf{Expr}::=\operatorname{Cell}(s,a,u)\mid\operatorname{Rat}(r)\mid\sum_{j\in J}\mathsf{Expr}_j\mid\prod_{j\in J}\mathsf{Expr}_j\mid\operatorname{Quot}(F,G,\nu_G),\] where \(r\in\mathbb Q\) and \(\nu_G\) is an independently checked nonzero certificate. The phase-indexed node grammar is exactly \[\mathsf{Goal}::=\operatorname{Raw}(\tau,e)\mid\operatorname{Normed}(\tau,e,\nu)\mid\operatorname{AncClosed}(\tau,H,e,\alpha)\mid\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,b)\mid\operatorname{NeedFace}(\tau,D,b,A,m,Z,\kappa)\mid\operatorname{Sum}(J,\mathrm{children})\mid\operatorname{Prod}(\mathrm{children})\mid\operatorname{Resolved}(F,\delta),\] with \(\tau\in\{\mathrm{num},\mathrm{den}\}\). A configuration is a finite well-typed acyclic work DAG of these nodes, its numerator and denominator roots, and a checked trace. For every \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), node denotation is defined recursively: the first three event phases denote their target event probability; \(\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,b)\) and \(\operatorname{NeedFace}(\tau,D,b,A,m,Z,\kappa)\) denote \(bq_D^\star(\mathbf M)\); \(\operatorname{Resolved}(F,\delta)\) denotes \(F(\mathcal P)\); and sum and product nodes denote the corresponding finite sum and product of child denotations. Configuration denotation is the ordered pair of root values. Checked derivations are finite syntax trees: normalization derivations use only consistency, unnesting, contradiction, Boolean normalization, and finite disjoint-additivity constructors; ancestor derivations enumerate summed assignments; district-split derivations enumerate the districts of \(G[H]\) and use finite marginalization and semi-Markovian district factorization; extraction derivations record each fixing, marginalization, context lookup, and certified quotient; face certificates are exact rational LP or Farkas certificates; and zero, nonzero, or strict-positivity certificates are checked by exact rational ring normalization together with Farkas certificates for linear face constraints or displayed sum-of-squares preordering identities for the finite response-law constraints. Thus no certificate constructor consists merely of the semantic equality or inequality being certified. The terminals are \[\mathsf{Result}::=\operatorname{ID}(F,\delta)\mid\operatorname{ZERO\_EVIDENCE}(F_E,z)\mid\operatorname{FAIL}(t),\] where a conditioning derivation \(\delta\) records the numerator derivation, denominator derivation, and positivity certificate. Failure constructors include \(\operatorname{SPLIT\_UNDEFINED}(v,\mathrm{reason},t)\), \(\operatorname{EXTRACTION\_UNDEFINED}(s,a,D,h)\), \(\operatorname{FACE\_NOT\_IDENTIFIED}(D,b,A,m,Z,w_{\mathrm{local}},t)\), \(\operatorname{NO\_ADMISSIBLE\_FACTOR}(D,b,\mathrm{retainedRows},t)\), \(\operatorname{DENOMINATOR\_MAY\_VANISH}(E,\mathbf M,t)\), \(\operatorname{DENOMINATOR\_POSITIVITY\_UNCERTIFIED}(E,t)\), and \(\operatorname{STUCK}(\mathrm{phase},\mathrm{reason},t)\).
