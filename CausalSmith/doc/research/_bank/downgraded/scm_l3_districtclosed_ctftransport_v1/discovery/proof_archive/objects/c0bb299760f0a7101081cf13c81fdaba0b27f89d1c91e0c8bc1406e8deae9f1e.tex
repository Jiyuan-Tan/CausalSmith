For every \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), each nonterminal \(\mathsf{NORM}\), \(\mathsf{ANCESTOR}\), \(\mathsf{SPLIT}\), \(\mathsf{EXTRACT}\), \(\mathsf{FACE\_ID}\), \(\mathsf{MARGINALIZE}\), or \(\mathsf{PRODUCT}\) step preserves the ordered pair of root denotations pointwise.  In particular, \(\mathsf{SPLIT}\) uses the exact total-probability sum--product expansion over the original districts \(\mathcal D(G)\), and \(\mathsf{EXTRACT}\) uses only accepted raw rows and the separately proved equations \(N=u^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\) and \(H=v^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\); it never treats \(N/H\) as a linear row value.  The \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) rule keeps the same two root values and divides only under an independent uniform strict-positivity certificate.  The first seven rules decrease respectively the first still-changeable coordinate of \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathrm{lex}}\), \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) decreases the last coordinate after the others vanish, and no rule creates an earlier-phase node.  Fixed priority and the least-applicable-node convention give a unique successor, while every declared partial-operation failure takes its typed \(\operatorname{FAIL}\) branch; hence \(\operatorname{STUCK}\) is unreachable from a well-typed initial configuration.
