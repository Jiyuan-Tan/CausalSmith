By the recursion definition, for every \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) the initial roots denote
\[
\bigl(P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E),P_{M^\star}(E)\bigr).
\]
Induction over the checked trace, applying the transition-preservation lemma at every nonterminal step, proves that every reached configuration has this same ordered pair of root denotations.

The lexicographic order on \(\mathbb N^8\) is well founded: nested induction on the first coordinate, then the second, and so on excludes an infinite sequence that fixes all earlier coordinates and strictly decreases the first coordinate at which it changes. Every nonterminal transition has this property by the transition-preservation lemma. Hence the run is finite. Fixed priority and the least-node convention give at most one successor; the phase analysis in that lemma gives either that successor or a typed terminal. The run is unique, and \(\operatorname{STUCK}\) is unreachable from a well-typed initial configuration.

Suppose the terminal is

\operatorname{ID}(\operatorname{Quot}(F_B,F_E,\pi),\delta_B,\delta_E,\pi).
\]
The expression grammar starts from typed-menu cells and rational constants and is closed under precisely the finite sums, products, and certified quotients used by the transitions. Induction over \(\delta_B\) and \(\delta_E\) therefore gives
\[
F_B,F_E\in\mathscr R(\mathcal P).
\]
Root preservation gives, for every compatible \(\mathbf M\),
\[
F_B(\mathcal P)=P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E),
\qquad
F_E(\mathcal P)=P_{M^\star}(E).
\]
The independent certificate \(\pi\) proves \(F_E>0\) throughout the compatibility fiber. Division is thus well typed, and
\[
\frac{F_B(\mathcal P)}{F_E(\mathcal P)}
=\frac{P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E)}{P_{M^\star}(E)}
=P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\mid E)
=Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M).
\]

If the independent zero calculus proves \(z:F_E=0\) throughout the fiber, evidence has probability zero in every compatible model, so the conditional is undefined and the terminal is \(\operatorname{ZERO\_EVIDENCE}(F_E,z)\).
