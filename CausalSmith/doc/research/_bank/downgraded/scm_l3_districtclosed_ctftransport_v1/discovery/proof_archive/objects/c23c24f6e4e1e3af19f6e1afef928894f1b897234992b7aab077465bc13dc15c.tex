Suppose \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\) and \(d_Dq>0\) throughout \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). For every realizable additional row \(r_D^\dagger\), the feasible set \(I_D\) is a closed interval and \(p\mapsto\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)\) is a finite convex piecewise-affine function. Its pieces are obtained from feasible bases of the row-augmented dual program and have coefficients that are rational affine functions of \(m_D\). Hence \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\) is the complement in \(I_D\) of one closed interval, allowing zero, one, or two sign boundaries, and each boundary has a primal row-face optimizer and a dual menu-weight certificate. If additionally \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds after adjoining the candidate row, every boundary optimizer is realized by an original-alphabet compatible saturated SCM attaining equality. Without these additional hypotheses, the boundary optimizer is only a row-face witness.
