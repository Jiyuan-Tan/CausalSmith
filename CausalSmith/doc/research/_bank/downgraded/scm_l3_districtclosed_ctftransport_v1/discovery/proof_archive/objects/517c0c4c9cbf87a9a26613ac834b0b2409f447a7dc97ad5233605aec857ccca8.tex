Let \(q=(q_1,q_2,q_3,q_4)^\top\) in the stated response-profile order.  The equations \(A_Dq=m_D\) are
\[
 q_1+q_2+q_3+q_4=1,
 \qquad q_3+q_4=\frac12,
 \qquad q_2+q_4=\frac12,
 \qquad q_j\geq0.
\]
Writing \(p=q_4\) gives
\[
 q_2=q_3=\frac12-p,
 \qquad q_1=p,
 \qquad 0\leq p\leq\frac12.
\]
This proves the unique parameterization \(q(p)\).  Since \(d_Dq(p)=1\), direct row multiplication yields
\[
 Q_\rho(q(p))=q_2+q_3=1-2p,
 \qquad Q_\sigma(q(p))=q_2+q_4=\frac12,
\]
and the candidate row gives \(r_D^\dagger q(p)=q_4=p\), so \(I_D=[0,1/2]\).  Likewise,
\[
 \Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q(p)
 =-q_3+q_4=2p-\frac12,
\]
whence \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+=(1/4,1/2]\).

In the augmented row order, multiplication gives
\[
 (0,-1,0,2)
 \begin{bmatrix}A_D\\ r_D^\dagger\end{bmatrix}
 =(0,0,-1,1)=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}.
\]
Thus this dual vector is feasible with equality and has objective
\[
 (0,-1,0,2)(1,1/2,1/2,p)^\top=2p-\frac12.
\]
At \(p=3/8\) it certifies the exact margin \(1/4\).  The three displayed laws
\[
 q(1/4)=(1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4),\quad
 q(0)=(0,1/2,1/2,0),\quad
 q(1/2)=(1/2,0,0,1/2)
\]
belong to the base face.  Their gap values are respectively \(0,-1/2,1/2\); the one-district projection theorem realizes all three on the original binary alphabets.

With no experiment, \(p=0\) makes \(\rho\) strictly better and \(p=1/2\) makes \(\sigma\) strictly better.  Therefore both arms are possibly optimal and
\(
 \operatorname{PO}(\varnothing)=\{\rho,\sigma\}.
\)
Acquiring the sole block imposes \(q_4=3/8\), and the parameterization shows
\[
 \mathcal K_{\{e^\dagger\}}=\{q(3/8)\}.
\]
At this point \(Q_\rho=1/4<1/2=Q_\sigma\).  Since the declared arm set contains no third arm,
\[
 \operatorname{PO}(\{e^\dagger\})
 =\mathcal P_{\mathrm{full}}
 =\{\sigma\}.
\]
