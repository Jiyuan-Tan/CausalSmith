Fix an accepted extraction record, a compatible family \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), and a retained pair \((h,c)\).  By \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus\text{-}soundness}\), the two constructive derivations in the record satisfy
\[
N_{s,a,D,h,c}(\mathcal P)=P_{M^s}(A_{h,c}),
\qquad
H_{s,a,D,h}(\mathcal P)=P_{M^s}(H_h).
\tag{1}
\]
By \(\mathrm{def:typed\text{-}extraction\text{-}judgment}\), the two accepting locality traces have constructed rows \(r_{s,a,D,h,c}\) and \(v_{s,a,D,h}\).  The exhaustive tables in \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\) give, for every complete nonfocal response profile \(\omega_{-D}\),
\[
\mathbf 1_{A_{h,c}}(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})
=r_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D),
\qquad
\mathbf 1_{H_h}(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})
=v_{s,a,D,h}(\omega_D).
\tag{2}
\]
Recursive compatibility makes the evaluated indicators deterministic after all response profiles are fixed.  District factorization makes the nonfocal response law a probability law independent of the focal block.  Summing \((2)\) first over that law and then over \(q_D^s\), finite total probability yields
\[
P_{M^s}(A_{h,c})=r_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s,
\qquad
P_{M^s}(H_h)=v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s.
\tag{3}
\]
The stored certificate \(\pi_h\) is valid by extraction-calculus soundness and proves the second quantity in \((3)\) strictly positive.  Thus the quotient constructor is legal, and \((1)\)--\((3)\) give
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)
=\frac{N_{s,a,D,h,c}}{H_{s,a,D,h}}
=\frac{r_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s}
{v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s}.
\tag{4}
\]
No row equality was assumed in the extraction record; equations \((3)\) are derived from exhaustive locality and total probability.

For a complete supplied output cell, \(\mathrm{ass:typed\text{-}menu\text{-}realizability}\) gives directly
\[
P_a^s(y)=\mathcal L_{M^s}(f_a(T_a))(y)
=P_{M^s}\{f_a(T_a)=y\}.
\tag{5}
\]
Its accepting locality table, followed by the same total-probability calculation as \((2)\)--\((3)\), gives
\[
P_a^s(y)=(r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,y})^{\mathsf T}q_D^s.
\tag{6}
\]
The preimage identity is used only to evaluate the indicator in the locality table; no probability \(P(T_a=t)\) is treated as supplied unless separately declared.
