Suppose a \(\mathsf{CheckedLocalArms}_D\) record is given and \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\).  Then
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=
\max_{\eta\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}}
\{m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta:A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta
\leq g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\}.
\]
Every feasible dual vector with \(m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta>0\) certifies \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) in every model in the broad fiber \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  If additionally \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds, a positive dual value is also necessary for uniform strict dominance on \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\).  Under that hypothesis, \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\) is attained by an original-alphabet focal-district-saturated SCM with the reverse strict ranking, and
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0<
\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
\]
yields two such same-menu SCM families with opposite rankings while preserving every nonfocal district.  Nonemptiness of the focal fiber together with \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\), and hence with \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\), is sufficient for the exact clauses.  Without the total checked-local-arms record the conditional comparison is undefined; without baseline-averaged focal projection, primal optimizers need not lift.
