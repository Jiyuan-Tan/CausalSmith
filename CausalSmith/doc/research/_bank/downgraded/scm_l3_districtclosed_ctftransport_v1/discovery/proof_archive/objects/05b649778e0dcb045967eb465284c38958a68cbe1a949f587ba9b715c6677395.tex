Fix \(s\in\mathcal S\), \(a\in\mathcal A_s\), and \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), and suppose that the extraction judgment succeeds.  Fix \((h,c)\) with a \(\operatorname{ROW\_ELIGIBLE}\) certificate.  Let \(A_{h,c}\) be the numerator event and \(H_h^{\mathrm{ev}}\) the context event reconstructed by its checked extraction tree.  Row eligibility says that both truth values are functions of \(\omega_D\) alone.  By the incidence-row definition,
\[
\mathbf 1\{A_{h,c}\}(\omega_D)=u_{h,c}(\omega_D),
\qquad
\mathbf 1\{H_h^{\mathrm{ev}}\}(\omega_D)=v_h(\omega_D).
\tag{1}
\]
Typed-menu realizability identifies the menu functionals \(N_{h,c}\) and \(H_h\) with the probabilities of these two events in \(M^s\).  Recursive compatibility makes the events deterministic after the complete district response map is fixed.  Since their indicators have no nonfocal argument, finite total probability and (1) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
N_{h,c}
&=P_{M^s}(A_{h,c})
=\sum_{\omega_D\in\Omega_D}u_{h,c}(\omega_D)q_D^s(\omega_D)
=u_{h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s,\\
H_h
&=P_{M^s}(H_h^{\mathrm{ev}})
=\sum_{\omega_D\in\Omega_D}v_h(\omega_D)q_D^s(\omega_D)
=v_h^{\mathsf T}q_D^s.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
The certificate \(\pi_H\) proves \(H_h>0\), so the defining conditionalization step is legal and yields
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)
=\frac{N_{h,c}}{H_h}
=\frac{u_{h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s}{v_h^{\mathsf T}q_D^s}.
\tag{3}
\]
This calculation displays rather than suppresses the conditioning normalizer.  If the context is fixed and nondistrict, \(v_h=\mathbf 1\); normalization of \(q_D^s\) then gives \(H_h=\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}q_D^s=1\).

If \(s=\star\), then \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\) by the domain label.  If instead \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), district-kernel invariance gives equality of the complete district response-map laws, again yielding \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\).  These are the only two transport-admissible cases used by the stack.  Substitution into (2) gives
\[
N_{h,c}=u_{h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star,
\qquad
H_h=v_h^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star.
\tag{4}
\]
The stacking definition retains exactly these two equations, whereas (3), being a quotient, is not a linear equality in the row system.

Finally, suppose a target event \(C\) has a checked single-district certificate at \(D\).  Its recursive indicator is a function \(b_D:\Omega_D\to\{0,1\}\), so total probability gives
\[
P_{M^\star}(C)=b_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star.
\tag{5}
\]
Taking \(C=B_\rho\cap E\) or \(C=E\) gives the stated \(n_{\rho,D}\) and \(d_D\) formulas.  If an indicator depends on \(\omega_{-D}\), the corresponding total-probability formula is instead
\[
\sum_{\omega_D,\omega_{-D}}
\mathbf 1\{C(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\}
q_D^\star(\omega_D)
\prod_{D'\ne D}q_{D'}^\star(\omega_{D'}),
\]
which is not a single zero--one row applied to \(q_D^\star\).  The eligibility checker therefore rejects precisely this case from the local stack.  Failed judgments similarly contribute no asserted equality.
