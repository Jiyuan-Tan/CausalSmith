Suppose the checked locality judgments at one district \(D\) construct \(n_{\rho,D}\), \(n_{\sigma,D}\), and the common evidence row \(d_D\).  If \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\) and \(d_D^{\mathsf T}q>0\) for every \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), then
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=
\max_{\eta\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}}
\{m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta:A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}\}.
\]
Every feasible \(\eta\) with \(m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta>0\) certifies \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) throughout the broad fiber.  Under \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\), that certificate condition is also necessary for uniform strict dominance on the focal-district-saturated fiber; a negative minimum is attained by an original-alphabet compatible SCM with the reverse ranking, and a negative minimum together with a positive maximum yields two such SCM families with opposite strict rankings while all nonfocal restrictions remain fixed.  Nonemptiness together with \(\mathsf{ASep}_D(\mathcal P)\), and hence with \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\), is sufficient for the exact clauses.  Without the three locality judgments or exact focal projection, no local optimizer is asserted to represent the conditional causal comparison.
