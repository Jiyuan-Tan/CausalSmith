For \(P_a^s\in\Delta(\mathcal X_{W_a})\), write
\[
\operatorname{Extract}_{s,a,D}(P_a^s)\Downarrow(K_{s,a,D},\kappa)
\]
when a finite extraction tree in \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\) constructs a typed kernel \(K_{s,a,D}:H_{s,a,D}\to\Delta(C_{s,a,D})\).  For a context \(h\) and cell \(c\), a judgment
\[
\mathsf{RowEligible}_{s,a,D}(h,c)
\]
contains only: reconstructed numerator and context event syntax \(A_{h,c}\) and \(H_h^{\mathrm{ev}}\); checked derivations \(\delta_N:A_{h,c}\Downarrow N_{s,a,D,h,c}\) and \(\delta_H:H_h^{\mathrm{ev}}\Downarrow H_{s,a,D,h}\); accepting \(\operatorname{SupportLocal}_D\) traces; the rows \(u_{s,a,D,h,c}=\operatorname{row}_D(A_{h,c})\) and \(v_{s,a,D,h}=\operatorname{row}_D(H_h^{\mathrm{ev}})\) constructed by those traces; and an independently checked certificate \(\pi_H:H_{s,a,D,h}>0\).  The judgment may not contain \(N_{s,a,D,h,c}=u_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s\), \(H_{s,a,D,h}=v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s\), or any equivalent semantic equality.  Those equalities are the conclusion of \(\mathrm{lem:district\text{-}cell\text{-}linearity}\).  The kernel entry is evaluated only after the derivations and positivity certificate have been checked:
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=\frac{N_{s,a,D,h,c}}{H_{s,a,D,h}}.
\]
An unavailable event reconstruction, an unproved fixing identity, a rejected support-local trace, an incomplete marginalization, or an uncertified denominator yields no row-eligible judgment.
