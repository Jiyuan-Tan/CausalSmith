Fix \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  We verify the denotational invariant rule by rule.

For \(\mathsf{NORM}\), every constructor in the attached trace is consistency, unnesting, contradiction removal, Boolean disjoint-normal-form rewriting, or finite disjoint additivity.  Each is an equality of events or of their probabilities, so replacing the node preserves every root denotation.  For \(\mathsf{ANCESTOR}\), every omitted variable ranges over a complete finite declared alphabet; hence the checked identity is an iterated instance of
\[
P(e)=\sum_xP(e\cap\{X=x\}).
\tag{1}
\]

For \(\mathsf{SPLIT}\), let \(\omega=(\omega_D:D\in\mathcal D(G))\) denote the complete response-profile tuple.  District factorization applies to the original districts and gives
\[
P_{M^\star}(\omega)=
\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^\star(\omega_D).
\tag{2}
\]
Recursive compatibility makes the normalized event a deterministic function \(e(\omega)\).  Finite total probability and (2) therefore give
\[
P_{M^\star}(e)
=\sum_{\omega:e(\omega)=1}
\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}e_{\omega_D}^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star.
\tag{3}
\]
The successor sum--product DAG is exactly the right-hand side of (3).  No factorization across pieces of an original district is used, even if ancestor restriction disconnects its induced bidirected subgraph.

At \(\mathsf{EXTRACT}\), a retained raw row has checked district locality and transport admissibility.  Typed-menu realizability and total probability give
\[
P_a^s(v)=(r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v})^{\mathsf T}q_D^s.
\]
The admissibility proof is either \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\); in the second case district-kernel invariance applies.  Hence the equality holds with \(q_D^\star\).  For a retained conditional cell, district-cell linearity gives
\[
N_{h,c}=u_{h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star,
\qquad
H_h=v_h^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star.
\tag{4}
\]
The rule stacks (4), not the quotient \(K=N/H\).  Together with \(\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star=1\), every literal row checked by \(\kappa_{\mathrm{all}}\) is valid, so
\[
A_Dq_D^\star=m_D.
\tag{5}
\]
Rejected cross-district or ineligible candidates assert no equation.  Both the row-needed node and its face successor denote the unchanged value \(b^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\), proving preservation at this rule.

At \(\mathsf{FACE\_ID}\), the exact certificate supplies
\[
b=\lambda^{\mathsf T}A_D+
\sum_{\omega\in Z_D(m_D)}\zeta_\omega e_\omega.
\tag{6}
\]
By (5), \(q_D^\star\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), and the definition of the forced-zero set gives \(q_D^\star(\omega)=0\) for every \(\omega\in Z_D(m_D)\).  Multiplying (6) by \(q_D^\star\) yields \(b^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star=\lambda^{\mathsf T}m_D\), the resolved-node denotation.  This argument uses only inclusion in the row face and does not assert that every face point is globally realizable.  Ready \(\mathsf{MARGINALIZE}\) nodes preserve denotation by finite additivity.  Ready \(\mathsf{PRODUCT}\) nodes created by (3) preserve it by the original-district product identity (2).

The \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) rule retains the same two resolved root values.  It constructs a quotient only when its independent certificate proves the denominator strictly positive throughout \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\); every zero or failure branch performs no division.

For the operational claims, the first seven rules decrease respectively the first still-changeable coordinate among
\[
r_N,r_A,r_S,r_X,r_F,r_\Sigma,r_\Pi
\]
and fix all earlier coordinates; no rule creates an earlier phase.  Once these vanish, \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) decreases \(r_C\).  Fixed priority, finite canonical orders, and the least-applicable-node convention give at most one successor.  A well-typed nonterminal has a least unresolved phase, and that rule either produces its successor or its specified typed failure.  Hence \(\operatorname{STUCK}\) is unreachable.
