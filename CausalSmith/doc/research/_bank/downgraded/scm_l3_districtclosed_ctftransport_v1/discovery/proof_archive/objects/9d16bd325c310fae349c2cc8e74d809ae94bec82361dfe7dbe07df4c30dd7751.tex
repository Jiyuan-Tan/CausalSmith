Fix canonical finite orders on vertices, regimes, original districts \(\mathcal D(G)\), complete response profiles, assignments, and generated work-DAG identifiers.  The counterfactual event and identified-expression grammars are
\[
\mathsf{Atom}::=[s]\,V_\alpha=v,
\qquad
\mathsf{Event}::=\top\mid\bot\mid\mathsf{Atom}\mid
\mathsf{Event}\wedge\mathsf{Event}\mid\mathsf{Event}\vee\mathsf{Event},
\]
\[
\mathsf{Expr}::=\operatorname{Cell}(s,a,u)\mid\operatorname{Rat}(r)
\mid\sum_{j\in J}F_j\mid\prod_{j\in J}F_j
\mid\operatorname{Quot}(F,H,\pi_H),
\]
where \(\alpha\) is a well-typed intervention or declared partial-copy term, \(r\in\mathbb Q\), every finite index set is explicitly enumerated, and \(\pi_H\) is an independently checked nonzero certificate.  The separate extraction subgrammar is exactly the event/expression calculus of \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\); its \(\mathsf{RowEligible}\) records contain no district-law equality.

The phase-indexed work nodes are
\[
\begin{split}
\mathsf{Goal}::={}&\operatorname{Raw}(\tau,e)
\mid\operatorname{Normed}(\tau,e,\nu)
\mid\operatorname{AncClosed}(\tau,H,e,\alpha)\\
&\mid\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,C,\ell,b)
\mid\operatorname{NeedFace}(\tau,D,C,\ell,b,A,m,Z,\kappa)\\
&\mid\operatorname{Sum}(J,\mathrm{children})
\mid\operatorname{Prod}(\mathrm{children})
\mid\operatorname{Resolved}(F,\delta),
\end{split}
\]
where \(\tau\in\{\mathrm{num},\mathrm{den}\}\) and \(\ell:\mathsf{Local}_D(C)\Downarrow b\) is a checked locality derivation.  Thus \(b\) is not a total field of arbitrary target events.  A configuration is a finite well-typed acyclic work DAG with numerator and denominator roots and a checked trace.  At \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), the first three phases denote the relevant target event probability, row and face nodes denote \(b^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\), resolved nodes denote their expressions evaluated at \(\mathcal P\), and sum and product nodes have their finite arithmetic denotations.

Normalization traces use consistency, unnesting, contradiction removal, Boolean disjoint-normalization, and finite additivity.  Ancestor traces enumerate complete finite alphabets.  A split trace is an exact total-probability expansion over the original districts \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\): it enumerates complete tuples in \(\prod_D\Omega_D\), tests the normalized event by recursive substitution, and forms the corresponding finite sum of products of original-district coordinate factors.  No split across districts of \(G[H]\) is legal unless a separate proved fixing theorem explicitly derives it.  Extraction traces contain the independent event-expression, support-locality, row-construction, and positivity records specified above.  Face certificates are exact row-reduction or Farkas certificates.  Zero and positivity certificates are verified by exact rational normalization plus Farkas certificates on linear faces or displayed real-algebraic sign certificates on the exact finite canonical constraints; a certificate is never the proposition it claims to prove.

The terminal grammar remains
\[
\mathsf{Result}::=\operatorname{ID}(F,\delta)\mid
\operatorname{ZERO\_EVIDENCE}(F_E,z)\mid\operatorname{FAIL}(t).
\]
Failure traces include ill-typed normalization or splitting, extraction failure, rejected target locality, face nonidentification, absence of an admissible factor, a displayed zero-evidence model, uncertified uniform positivity, and a phase-labelled stuck trace.  A local face witness is explicitly typed as a row-relaxation witness rather than an SCM pair.
