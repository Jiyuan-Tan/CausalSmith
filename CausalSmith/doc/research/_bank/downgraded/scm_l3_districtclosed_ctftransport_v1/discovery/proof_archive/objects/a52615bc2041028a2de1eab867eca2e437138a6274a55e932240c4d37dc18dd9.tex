Run \(\mathsf{DCTFTR}\) first.  By
\(\mathrm{thm:recursion\text{-}soundness}\), the run terminates uniquely.  If it
returns an identifying expression \(F\), that theorem gives, according to the query
type,
\[
F(\mathcal P)=P_{M^\star}(T)
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
F(\mathcal P)=P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\mid E)
\tag{1}
\]
for every \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), with every quotient protected by
its stored nonzero or positive certificate.  Definition
\(\mathrm{def:allowed\text{-}rational\text{-}functional}\) gives
\(F\in\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\).  This proves the fast path.

Every other local terminal invokes
\[
S=\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathbb R^N.
\tag{2}
\]
The assumed nonempty broad compatibility fiber and
\(\mathrm{thm:exact\text{-}global\text{-}canonical\text{-}frontier}\) imply that
\(S\) is nonempty, compact and real semialgebraic and that its joint target-cell
ranges equal those of the broad original-alphabet compatibility fiber.  The
effective presentations of the supplied rational or real-algebraic cells give exact
arithmetic and sign comparison in their ordered coefficient ring \(\mathbb D\), and
the stipulated ordered embedding into \(\mathbb R_{\mathrm{alg}}\) represents the
coefficients without restricting the variables in \((2)\).

Let \(K\) be any real-closed field containing an ordered copy of \(\mathbb D\).
Apply
\(\mathrm{lem:basu\text{-}mohammad\text{-}nezhad\text{-}quantifier\text{-}elimination}\)
over \(K\).  Its algorithm terminates and performs only exact coefficient-ring
operations.  Hence the same finite \(\mathsf{QEFormula}\) syntax is equivalent to
the defining formula in every such \(K\), in particular in the ordinary real field.

For an unconditional target put \(p_T=\Phi_{T,\mathrm{can}}^\star\).  Quantifier
elimination applied to
\[
\varphi_T(t)=\exists x\,[x\in S\wedge t-p_T(x)=0]
\tag{3}
\]
returns \(\chi_T\) such that, for every \(t\in\mathbb R\),
\[
\chi_T(t)
\Longleftrightarrow
\exists x\in S\;t=p_T(x)
\Longleftrightarrow
\exists\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\;
t=P_{M^\star}(T).
\tag{4}
\]
The second equivalence is exact global joint range equality.  Eliminating the closed
sentence asserting two distinct realizations decides identification exactly.
Continuity on compact \(S\) gives an attained minimum \(\ell_T\) and maximum
\(u_T\).  Therefore
\[
\chi_T(\ell)\wedge
\forall t\,[\chi_T(t)\Longrightarrow\ell\leq t]
\tag{5}
\]
holds over \(\mathbb R\) exactly for \(\ell=\ell_T\), and the reversed inequality
does the same for \(u_T\).  Quantifier elimination preserves these real
equivalences; it does not require the endpoints or all range points to be algebraic.

For a conditional target set
\[
e(x)=\Phi_{E,\mathrm{can}}^\star(x),
\qquad
n(x)=\Phi_{B_\rho\cap E,\mathrm{can}}^\star(x).
\tag{6}
\]
Probability semantics gives \(e\geq0\) on nonempty \(S\).  Exactly one of
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf Z:&\neg\exists x\in S\;e(x)>0,\\
\mathsf N:&(\exists x\in S\;e(x)=0)\wedge
(\exists x\in S\;e(x)>0),\\
\mathsf P:&\neg\exists x\in S\;e(x)=0
\end{array}
\tag{7}
\]
holds.  The cited quantifier-elimination theorem and exact evaluation of the closed
outputs therefore give the prescribed payload-free status in the first two cases.
Under \(\mathsf Z\), evidence vanishes throughout.  Under \(\mathsf N\), the
conditional is undefined at one compatible law and defined at another, so a
functional on the full fiber must reject division.  Under \(\mathsf P\), continuity
and compactness give
\[
\min_{x\in S}e(x)>0.
\tag{8}
\]
Only then is \(e(x)t-n(x)=0\) equivalent to \(t=n(x)/e(x)\).  Consequently the real
realization of
\[
\exists x\,[x\in S\wedge e(x)>0\wedge e(x)t-n(x)=0]
\tag{9}
\]
is exactly the conditional causal range.  The ratio is continuous by \((8)\), so
the same compactness and endpoint argument as in \((5)\) gives its attained real
extrema, and the closed two-value sentence gives its exact singleton decision.

Only finitely many terminating calls are made.  If either real range is not a
singleton, two distinct attained values exist, and exact canonical range equality
supplies two original-alphabet same-menu SCM families realizing them.  This is an
existence certificate, not witness extraction: the procedure returns no endpoint
number, algebraic sample, cylindrical decomposition trace, canonical parameter, or
paired SCM.  Since every interpretation above is over \(\mathbb R\), no causal
range point was restricted to \(\mathbb R_{\mathrm{alg}}\).
