Given a \(\mathsf{CheckedLocalArms}_D\) record and a realizable additional complete Layer-3 output-cell row \(r_D^\dagger\in\{0,1\}^{\Omega_D}\), define
\[
I_D=\{(r_D^\dagger)^{\mathsf T}q:q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\}.
\]
For \(p\in I_D\), define
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)
=\min_{\substack{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\\
(r_D^\dagger)^{\mathsf T}q=p}}
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q,
\qquad
\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+
=\{p\in I_D:\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)>0\}.
\]
Every object is defined only from the checked arm rows and their common strictly positive evidence row.
