Suppose \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\) and \(d_Dq>0\) for every \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). Then
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=
\max_{\eta\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}}
\{m_D^\top\eta:A_D^\top\eta\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^\top\}.
\]
Every feasible dual vector with \(m_D^\top\eta>0\) certifies \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) in every model in the broad fiber \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\). If additionally \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds, this certificate condition is also necessary for uniform strict dominance on the saturated fiber. Under these additional hypotheses, \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\) is attained by an original-alphabet compatible saturated SCM with \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\), and \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0<\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)\) yields two such SCM families with opposite strict rankings. Without the additional hypotheses, the primal optimizers need not lift from the row face.
