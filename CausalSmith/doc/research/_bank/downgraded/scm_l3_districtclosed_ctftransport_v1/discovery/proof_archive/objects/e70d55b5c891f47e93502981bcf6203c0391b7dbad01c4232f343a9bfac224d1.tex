Define
\[
\begin{split}
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{sat}}(G,\mathcal X,\mathcal S,\{\Delta_s\}_{s\in\mathcal S})
=\bigl\{\mathbf M\in\mathfrak M(G,\mathcal X,\mathcal S,\{\Delta_s\}_{s\in\mathcal S}):{}&
G[D]\text{ is bidirected complete for every }D\in\mathcal D(G);\\
&\text{for every }s\in\mathcal S\text{ and }D\in\mathcal D(G),
\text{ exactly one finite latent block }U_D^s\text{ taking values in }\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\\
&\text{stores one full deterministic response map for every vertex in }D
\text{ at every parent configuration};\\
&\text{each structural equation in }D\text{ reads the corresponding component of }U_D^s;\\
&(U_D^s:D\in\mathcal D(G))\text{ are mutually independent within each domain }s;\\
&\text{the class contains every independent choice of one probability law on }
\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\text{ for each permitted district block},\\
&\text{subject only to equality of the laws of }U_D^s\text{ and }U_D^\star
\text{ whenever }D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\bigr\}.
\end{split}
\]
Thus changed districts may use arbitrary and mutually unrelated response-block laws, unchanged districts use exactly the target response-block law, and no equality with the broad sparse-ADMG class \(\mathfrak M\) is asserted.
