Fix \(s\in\mathcal S\), \(a\in\mathcal A_s\), \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), and a successful judgment
\(
 \operatorname{Extract}_{s,a,D}(P_a^s)\Downarrow(K_{s,a,D},\kappa).
\)
By the canonical response-profile definition, \(q_D^s\) is a probability mass function on the finite set \(\Omega_D\).  For fixed \(h\in H_{s,a,D}\) and \(c\in C_{s,a,D}\), let \(C(h,c)\subseteq\Omega_D\) be the profiles which produce cell \(c\) at context \(h\) under regime \(a\).  By the incidence-row definition,
\[
 r_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega)=\mathbf 1\{\omega\in C(h,c)\}.
\]
The successful extraction certificate uses only consistency, unnesting, fixing at the declared context, and finite marginalization.  Typed-menu realizability therefore identifies its semantic kernel cell with the probability of \(C(h,c)\) under the response law of \(M^s\).  Consequently, by finite additivity,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)
 &=P_{M^s}\{\omega_D\in C(h,c)\} \\
 &=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega_D}\mathbf 1\{\omega\in C(h,c)\}q_D^s(\omega) \\
 &=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^s.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Every quotient used to recover the displayed kernel is legitimate because success of the extraction judgment includes a nonzero certificate for that quotient; no equality is asserted on an unavailable or zero-denominator context.

If \(s=\star\), then \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\) by notation.  If instead \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), district-kernel invariance gives equality of the joint structural-response laws on \(D\), hence again \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\).  Thus, and only after one of these two admissibility facts has been checked,
\[
 K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^\star.
\]
Finally, the target-row definition applies exactly the same finite evaluation to \(B_\rho\cap E\), \(E\), and a generic reduced target cell.  It gives, respectively,
\[
 P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E)=n_{\rho,D}q_D^\star,
 \qquad P_{M^\star}(E)=d_Dq_D^\star,
 \qquad P_{M^\star}(\text{target cell})=b_Dq_D^\star.
\]
By the extraction and stacking definitions, a failed judgment has neither a certified kernel cell nor an incidence equality and therefore contributes no row to \(A_D\).
