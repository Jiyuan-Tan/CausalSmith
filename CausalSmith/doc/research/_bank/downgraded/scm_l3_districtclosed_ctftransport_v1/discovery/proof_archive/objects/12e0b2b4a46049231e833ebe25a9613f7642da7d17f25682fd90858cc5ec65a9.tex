Fix an accepted fixing trace \(\tau\) and a model family \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:recursive\text{-}compatibility}\) and \(\mathrm{def:district\text{-}response\text{-}profile}\), recursive evaluation makes every event occurring in \(\tau\) a zero--one function of a finite complete response-profile tuple.  By \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}factorization}\), in domain \(s\) the probability of an event \(A\) therefore has the finite expansion
\[
 P_{M^s}(A)
 =\sum_{\omega\in\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_D}
 \mathbf 1_A(\omega)
 \prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^s(\omega_D).
\]
In particular, it is a polynomial in the district-law coordinates before any conditional division is performed.

We prove, by structural induction on the expression and trace constructors of \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\), that their evaluators have the asserted probability semantics.  The zero and sure-event constructors evaluate to \(0\) and \(1\).  A response-cell constructor evaluates by the displayed finite expansion.  The product constructor is sound because evaluation is multiplication of the two already interpreted scalar expressions.  For a finite family of pairwise disjoint events \((A_j:j\in J)\), the disjoint-sum constructor is sound by finite additivity:
\[
 P_{M^s}\!\left(\bigsqcup_{j\in J}A_j\right)
 =\sum_{j\in J}P_{M^s}(A_j).
\]
The marginalization constructor is the same identity for the finite partition indexed by the marginalized variable's declared state space.  The complement constructor is sound because
\[
 P_{M^s}(A^{\mathsf c})=1-P_{M^s}(A).
\]
At a conditional constructor, let \(H\) be the context expression.  Acceptance requires a separate checked certificate that \(H(\mathbf M)>0\) throughout the declared compatibility fiber.  Hence
\[
 P_{M^s}(A\mid H)=\frac{P_{M^s}(A\cap H)}{P_{M^s}(H)}
\]
is defined, clearing the denominator is reversible, and the induction hypotheses apply to its numerator and denominator.  A type-correct substitution is sound because substitution is a homomorphism for constants, addition, subtraction, and multiplication; consequently it carries every previously checked polynomial identity to a valid identity.  Finally, each allowed registry identity is admitted only after its two recursively constructed sides have been cleared of their separately certified positive denominators and the exact identity verifier has normalized the resulting rational-coefficient polynomials and compared every coefficient.  Thus every accepted registry use is sound under every assignment of the response-law coordinates.

For completeness of the last step, recursively clearing the finitely many conditional occurrences in \(\tau\) produces a polynomial multiplier
\[
 \Delta_\tau(\mathbf M)=\prod_{j=1}^{J}H_j(\mathbf M)^{k_j},
 \qquad k_j\in\mathbb N_{>0},
\]
where every \(H_j(\mathbf M)>0\) by its accepted positivity certificate.  Therefore \(\Delta_\tau(\mathbf M)>0\).  If \(L_\tau\) and \(R_\tau\) are the input and output expressions, exact coefficient comparison certifies the formal polynomial identity
\[
 \Delta_\tau L_\tau=\Delta_\tau R_\tau.
\]
Substitution of the response-law coordinates of \(\mathbf M\) preserves this identity.  Division by the strictly positive \(\Delta_\tau(\mathbf M)\) gives
\[
 L_\tau(\mathbf M)=R_\tau(\mathbf M).
\]
Since \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) was arbitrary, every accepted fixing trace preserves the represented event probability on the whole compatibility fiber.
