Assume \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\).  A supplied-cell indicator independent of \(\omega_D\) has each focal slice equal to zero or the normalization row.  An indicator depending only on \(\omega_D\) has every slice equal to its accepted raw row.  Both cases lie in \(R_D(m_D)\), proving \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\).

For strictness, write \(h_y(\omega_D)=\mathbf1\{Y_0=y\}\) and \(k_z(\omega_{-D})=\mathbf1\{Z=z\}\).  The marginal menu retains each \(h_y\).  A joint cell has cross-district indicator \(h_yk_z\), so it violates \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\); after fixing the nonfocal profile its slice is zero or \(h_y\), hence belongs to the active row space.  Independent Bernoulli response blocks realize the complete menu on the original binary alphabets.
