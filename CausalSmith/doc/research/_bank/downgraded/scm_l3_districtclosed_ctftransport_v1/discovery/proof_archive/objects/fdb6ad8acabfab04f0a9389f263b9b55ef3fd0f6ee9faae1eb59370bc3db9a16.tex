For the broad compatibility fiber, the membership
\[
b_D\in\operatorname{row}(A_D)+\operatorname{span}\{e_\omega:\omega\in Z_D(m_D)\}
\]
is sufficient for the district-local target \(b_Dq_D^\star\) to be identified, with value \(\lambda^\top m_D\) whenever
\[
b_D=\lambda^\top A_D+\sum_{\omega\in Z_D(m_D)}\zeta_\omega e_\omega.
\]
If additionally \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds, the membership is also necessary for constancy on \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\).  On that saturated separated class, failure yields two compatible SCM families on the original alphabets \(\mathcal X\) that agree on every supplied law and disagree on the target cell.  For a nonsaturated or cross-district-coupled problem, failure is only a local outer-relaxation witness.
