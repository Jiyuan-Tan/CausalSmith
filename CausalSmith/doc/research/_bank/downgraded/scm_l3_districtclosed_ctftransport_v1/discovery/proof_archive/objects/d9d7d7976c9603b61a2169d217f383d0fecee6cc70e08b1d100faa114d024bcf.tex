For every district \(D\),
\[
\mathcal Q_D(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\]
Suppose \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds.  Then
\[
\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)=\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\]
In particular, every boundary point of the row face is induced on the declared alphabets \(\mathcal X\) by a district-saturated compatible SCM family.  Without district saturation or the separation predicate, only the displayed broad inclusion is asserted.
