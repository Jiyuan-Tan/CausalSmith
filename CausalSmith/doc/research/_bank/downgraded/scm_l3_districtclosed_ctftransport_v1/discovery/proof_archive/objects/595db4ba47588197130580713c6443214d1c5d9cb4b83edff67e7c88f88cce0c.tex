Write \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(A=A_D\), \(m=m_D\), \(Z=Z_D(m_D)\), and let \(J=\Omega_D\setminus Z\).  The normalization row in \(A\) implies \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\) for every \(q\in K\), so \(J\neq\varnothing\).  For each \(\omega\in J\), the definition of \(Z\) supplies \(q^{\omega}\in K\) with \(q^{\omega}(\omega)>0\).  The average
\[
 \bar q=\frac1{|J|}\sum_{\omega\in J}q^{\omega}
\]
belongs to \(K\), is zero on \(Z\), and is strictly positive on every coordinate in \(J\).

Define the feasible-direction space
\[
 L=\{v\in\mathbb R^{\Omega_D}:Av=0,\ v(\omega)=0\text{ for every }\omega\in Z\}.
\]
For any \(v\in L\), finiteness of \(J\) and strict positivity of \(\bar q\) on \(J\) allow a number \(\varepsilon>0\) such that
\(
 \varepsilon |v(\omega)|\leq \bar q(\omega)/2
\)
for every \(\omega\in J\).  Then
\[
 \bar q+\varepsilon v\in K,
 \qquad \bar q-\varepsilon v\in K.
\]
It follows that the direction space of the affine hull of \(K\) is exactly \(L\): every difference of two points of \(K\) lies in \(L\), and the preceding two-sided perturbation realizes every direction in \(L\).

Let \(\widetilde A\) be obtained by appending the rows \(e_\omega\), \(\omega\in Z\), to \(A\).  Then \(L=\ker(\widetilde A)\).  Elementary finite-dimensional linear algebra gives
\[
 L^\perp=(\ker\widetilde A)^\perp
 =\operatorname{row}(\widetilde A)
 =\operatorname{row}(A)+\operatorname{span}\{e_\omega:\omega\in Z\};
\]
indeed, row vectors annihilate the kernel, and equality follows because both sides have dimension \(\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde A)\).

By the one-district fiber-projection theorem and district-cell linearity, the target cell on a compatible family with response law \(q\) equals \(b_Dq\), and every \(q\in K\) occurs.  Thus the target is constant on \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) exactly when \(b_Dv=0\) for every \(v\in L\), which by the preceding display is exactly the claimed membership condition.  If
\[
 b_D=\lambda^\top A+\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega e_\omega,
\]
then every \(q\in K\) satisfies
\[
 b_Dq=\lambda^\top Aq+\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega q(\omega)
 =\lambda^\top m.
\]

If membership fails, choose \(v\in L\) with \(b_Dv\neq0\).  The two laws \(q_\pm=\bar q\pm\varepsilon v\) constructed above lie in \(K\) and satisfy
\[
 b_Dq_+-b_Dq_-=2\varepsilon b_Dv\neq0.
\]
The fiber-projection theorem supplies corresponding families \(\mathbf M_+,\mathbf M_-\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) on the original alphabets \(\mathcal X\).  They agree on every supplied law by membership in the same compatibility fiber and disagree on the target cell, which proves the asserted paired-model certificate.
