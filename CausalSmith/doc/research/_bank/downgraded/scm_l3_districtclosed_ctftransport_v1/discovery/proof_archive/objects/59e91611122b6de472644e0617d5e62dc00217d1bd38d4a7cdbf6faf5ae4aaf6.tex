For a complete target-domain counterfactual cell \(C\), the total checker
\[
\mathsf{Local}_D(C)\Downarrow(b_D,\ell_C)
\]
enumerates every \((\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\), accepts exactly when the recursive indicator of \(C\) is independent of \(\omega_{-D}\), and then returns its Boolean row \(b_D=\operatorname{row}_D(C)\); rejection returns a differing nonfocal pair and no row.  A checked local-arm instance
\[
\mathfrak L_D=(\mathcal R_0,E,\{n_{\tau,D}:\tau\in\mathcal R_0\},d_D,\{\ell_{\tau},\ell_E\},\varepsilon_D,\pi_D^+)
\]
consists of a finite \(\mathcal R_0\subseteq\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\), successful judgments \(\mathsf{Local}_D(B_\tau\cap E)\Downarrow(n_{\tau,D},\ell_\tau)\) for every \(\tau\in\mathcal R_0\), one successful \(\mathsf{Local}_D(E)\Downarrow(d_D,\ell_E)\), a rational \(\varepsilon_D>0\), and an exact Farkas certificate \(\pi_D^+\) of
\[
d_D^{\mathsf T}q\ge\varepsilon_D\qquad(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)).
\]
Only for such an instance define
\[
Q_\tau(q)=\frac{n_{\tau,D}^{\mathsf T}q}{d_D^{\mathsf T}q},\qquad
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}=n_{\sigma,D}-n_{\rho,D}
\]
for \(\rho,\sigma\in\mathcal R_0\).  Thus every local target value and gap is a partial typed output; outside a successful locality judgment or without \(\pi_D^+\), these symbols are undefined.
