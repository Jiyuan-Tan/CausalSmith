Given a \(\mathsf{CheckedLocalArms}_D\) record and \(\rho,\sigma\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\), define
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
=\min_{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)}g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q,
\qquad
\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
=\max_{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)}g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q.
\]
The definitions are made only when the checked record exists; its positivity certificate makes every associated conditional comparison well defined on the face.
