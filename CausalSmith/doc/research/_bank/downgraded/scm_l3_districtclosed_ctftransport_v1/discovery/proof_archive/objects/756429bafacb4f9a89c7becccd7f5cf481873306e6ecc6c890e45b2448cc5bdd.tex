Put \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\).  The normalization row of \(A_D\) gives \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\) for every \(q\in K\).  Thus the assumed nonempty set \(K\) is a closed subset of the finite probability simplex, hence compact, and its linear minimum and maximum are attained.

Apply \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}linear\text{-}programming\text{-}strong\text{-}duality}\) with primal variable \(q\), equality constraint \(A_Dq=m_D\), nonnegativity constraint \(q\geq0\), and cost row \(g\).  The equality multiplier \(\eta\) is unrestricted, and the nonnegative primal variables give the coordinatewise dual constraint \(A_D^\top\eta\leq g^\top\).  Consequently
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
=\min_{q\in K}gq
=\max_{\eta:A_D^\top\eta\leq g^\top}m_D^\top\eta.
\]
For later use, weak duality is also the direct identity and inequality
\[
m_D^\top\eta=q^\top A_D^\top\eta\leq gq
\qquad(q\in K, A_D^\top\eta\leq g^\top).
\]

Let \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  Broad projection gives \(q=q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\in K\).  If a feasible \(\eta\) satisfies \(m_D^\top\eta>0\), weak duality gives \(gq>0\).  The assumed denominator condition gives \(d_Dq>0\), so \(\mathrm{lem:district\text{-}cell\text{-}linearity}\) and the definition of \(g\) yield
\[
Q_\sigma^\star(\mathbf M)-Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M)
=\frac{n_{\sigma,D}q-n_{\rho,D}q}{d_Dq}
=\frac{gq}{d_Dq}>0.
\]
Thus every positive dual certificate is sound on the broad compatibility fiber; division is used only after the stated strict positivity condition.

Now also assume \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  The focal projection theorem gives \(\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)=K\).  Uniform strict dominance on this fiber is therefore equivalent to \(gq>0\) for every \(q\in K\).  Since \(K\) is compact and \(gq\) is continuous, this is equivalent to
\[
\min_{q\in K}gq>0.
\]
Dual attainment makes the last inequality equivalent to the existence of a feasible \(\eta\) with \(m_D^\top\eta>0\), proving necessity.

If \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\), an attained minimizer \(q_-\in K\) has \(gq_-<0\).  Focal projection realizes \(q_-\) by an original-alphabet family in \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\), and the positive common denominator gives \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\).  If also \(\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)>0\), an attained maximizer \(q_+\in K\) is realized in the same way and has \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\).  Both families induce \(\mathcal P\), and the focal realization proof changes only district \(D\), so their other districts retain the baseline graph-compatible restrictions.  Without focal exact projection, an optimizer of the outer row face need not be induced by any graph-compatible SCM, and no converse lifting follows.
