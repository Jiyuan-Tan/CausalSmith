The hypotheses are exactly those of transported dominance duality.  The displayed vector is dual feasible with positive value, so that theorem gives
\[
Q_\sigma^\star(\mathbf M)>Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M)
\]
for every compatible model.  Thus whenever \(\rho\) is defined, the available arm \(\sigma\) has strictly larger value; consequently \(\rho\) cannot maximize and its removal cannot delete a maximizing arm.  In the separate zero-evidence case, recursion soundness says the conditional is undefined, so it is excluded from the comparison rather than assigned an artificial low value.
