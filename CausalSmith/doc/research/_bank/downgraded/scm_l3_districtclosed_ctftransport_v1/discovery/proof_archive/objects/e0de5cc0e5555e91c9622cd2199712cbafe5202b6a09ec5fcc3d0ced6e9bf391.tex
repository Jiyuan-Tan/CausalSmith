Let P \(\subset R[X^{[1]},\ldots,X^{[\omega]},Y]_{\le d}\) be a finite set of s polynomials, where \(X^{[i]}\) is a block of \(k_i\) variables, and Y is a block of \(\ell\) variables. Consider the quantified formula \(\Psi(Y)=(Q_1X^{[1]})\cdots(Q_\omega X^{[\omega]})\Phi(X^{[1]},\ldots,X^{[\omega]},Y)\), where \(\Phi\) is a quantifier-free P-formula. Then there exists a quantifier-free formula \(\Theta(Y)=\bigvee_{i=1}^{I}\bigwedge_{j=1}^{J_i}\bigvee_{n=1}^{N_{ij}}\operatorname{sign}(P_{ijn}(Y))=\sigma_{ijn}\) equivalent to \(\Psi\), where \(P_{ijn}(Y)\) are polynomials in the variables Y and \(\sigma_{ijn}\in\{-1,0,1\}\). Furthermore, \(I\le s^{(k_\omega+1)\cdots(k_1+1)(\ell+1)}d^{O(k_\omega)\cdots O(k_1)O(\ell)}\), \(J_i\le s^{(k_\omega+1)\cdots(k_1+1)}d^{O(k_\omega)\cdots O(k_1)}\), \(N_{ij}\le d^{O(k_\omega)\cdots O(k_1)}\), and the degrees of the polynomials \(P_{ijn}(y)\) are bounded by \(d^{O(k_\omega)\cdots O(k_1)}\). Moreover, there exists an algorithm ([5, Algorithm 14.5] (Quantifier Elimination)) to compute \(\Theta(Y)\) with complexity \(s^{(k_\omega+1)\cdots(k_1+1)(\ell+1)}d^{O(k_\omega)\cdots O(k_1)O(\ell)}\) in D, where D denotes the ring generated by the coefficients of the polynomials in P. If D = Z and \(\tau\) denotes an upper bound on bitsizes of P, then the bitsizes of the integers in the intermediate computations and the output are bounded by \(\tau d^{O(k_\omega)\cdots O(k_1)O(\ell)}\).
