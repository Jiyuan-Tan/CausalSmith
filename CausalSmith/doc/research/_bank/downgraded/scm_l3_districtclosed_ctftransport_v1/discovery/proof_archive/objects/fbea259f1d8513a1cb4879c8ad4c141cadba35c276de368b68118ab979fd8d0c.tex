Write \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(r=r_D^\dagger\), and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\).  Since \(K\) is a nonempty compact convex polytope and \(q\mapsto rq\) is linear, its image
\[
 I_D=\{rq:q\in K\}
\]
is a nonempty compact convex subset of \(\mathbb R\), hence a closed interval.  Every \(p\in I_D\) has a nonempty compact fiber \(K(p)=\{q\in K:rq=p\}\), so the minimum defining \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)\) is attained.

For \(p_0,p_1\in I_D\), choose minimizers \(q_0,q_1\).  If \(0\leq t\leq1\), then \(tq_0+(1-t)q_1\in K(tp_0+(1-t)p_1)\), and therefore
\[
 \Gamma(tp_0+(1-t)p_1;m_D)
 \leq t\Gamma(p_0;m_D)+(1-t)\Gamma(p_1;m_D).
\]
Thus the value function is convex.

The row-augmented primal has equality matrix \(\widetilde A=(A_D;r)\) and right side \((m_D;p)\).  Applying the primal--dual equality of the transported-dominance theorem to this augmented one-district face gives
\[
 \Gamma(p;m_D)
 =\max_{(\eta,\beta):A_D^\top\eta+\beta r^\top\leq g^\top}
 \{m_D^\top\eta+\beta p\}.
\]
The feasible polyhedron in \((\eta,\beta)\) is independent of \((m_D,p)\) and is described by finitely many rational inequalities.  Quotienting its lineality space, each relevant zero-dimensional feasible basis solves a nonsingular rational linear system, so its coordinates are rational.  There are finitely many such bases.  On any interval where one basis is optimal, the value is
\[
 m_D^\top\eta_B+\beta_Bp,
\]
whose slope is rational and whose intercept is a rational linear, hence rational affine, function of \(m_D\).  Taking the maximum of this finite list proves that the value function is finite, continuous, convex, and piecewise affine.  Degenerate bases merely repeat a piece and do not change the conclusion.

For a continuous convex function on an interval, the sublevel set
\[
 \{p\in I_D:\Gamma(p;m_D)\leq0\}
\]
is closed and convex, hence is empty, a point, or a closed interval.  Its relative complement is \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\), giving zero, one, or two transitions between nonpositive and strictly positive regimes.  At every such sign boundary \(p_0\), continuity gives \(\Gamma(p_0;m_D)=0\).  Dual attainment supplies \((\eta_0,\beta_0)\) with value zero there, and the same feasible dual basis supplies the displayed affine lower certificate on its adjacent pruning piece.  Primal attainment supplies \(q_0\in K(p_0)\) with \(gq_0=0\); one-district fiber projection realizes \(q_0\) by a compatible SCM on \(\mathcal X\).  Since \(d_Dq_0>0\), that SCM has equal conditional arm values, as claimed.
