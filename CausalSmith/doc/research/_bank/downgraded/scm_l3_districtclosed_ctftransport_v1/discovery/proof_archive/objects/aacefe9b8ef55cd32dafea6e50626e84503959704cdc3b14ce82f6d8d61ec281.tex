For each \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), let
\[
\Omega_D=\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}
\]
be the finite set of all tuples \(\omega_D=(f_V:V\in D)\) of deterministic response maps, one map for every parent configuration allowed by \(G\) and \(\mathcal X\).  For a source-labelled SCM family \(\mathbf M\), let \(q_D^s\in\Delta(\Omega_D)\) be the joint law in domain \(s\) of this complete district response-map tuple.  Thus no target or menu determines the carrier, and every finite counterfactual cell on the declared alphabets is evaluable on \(\prod_D\Omega_D\).
