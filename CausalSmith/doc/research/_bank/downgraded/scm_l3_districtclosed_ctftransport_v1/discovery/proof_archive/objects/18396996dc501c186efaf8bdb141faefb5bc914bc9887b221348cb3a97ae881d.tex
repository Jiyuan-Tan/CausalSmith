Each parameter fiber is a closed subset of a finite product of compact probability simplices and finite one-hot table sets, hence compact.  Its defining constraints are polynomial equalities and inequalities, so it is semialgebraic.  The relevant compatibility hypothesis makes it nonempty.  Fix either exact row \((\mathfrak C,\Lambda,\Xi)\) in the statement.  Every target-cell polynomial is continuous, so it attains its minimum and maximum on \(\Lambda\).  Joint range equality makes these extrema the sharp causal bounds and turns their optimizers into original-alphabet compatible endpoint models.  Equality of the extrema is equivalent to constancy of the cell on the fiber, hence to identification.  If they differ, the two endpoint models differ in target value.  Since every cell probability lies in \([0,1]\), at least one endpoint strictly improves its logical bound exactly when the lower endpoint is positive or the upper endpoint is less than one.  No connectedness has been assumed, so no interval-filling conclusion follows.

Apply the same reasoning to the nonnegative evidence polynomial.  Compactness gives attained values
\[
e_-=\min_{x\in\Lambda}\Xi_E^\star(x^\star),
\qquad
e_+=\max_{x\in\Lambda}\Xi_E^\star(x^\star).
\]
The exhaustive cases are
\[
e_+=0,\qquad e_-=0<e_+,\qquad e_->0.
\tag{3}
\]
They respectively mean uniform zero evidence, coexistence of zero- and positive-evidence compatible models, and uniform strict positivity.  Division on the whole fiber is valid only in the third case.  There the conditional ratio is continuous on compact \(\Lambda\), and hence attains the stated sharp lower and upper bounds.  Furthermore
\[
Q_\sigma^\star-Q_\rho^\star
=\frac{\Xi_{B_\sigma\cap E}^\star-
\Xi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star}{\Xi_E^\star}.
\tag{4}
\]
Because the denominator in \((4)\) is positive, the sign is exactly the sign of the numerator gap.  A continuous gap that is positive at every point of compact \(\Lambda\) has positive minimum, and the converse is immediate.  Likewise, opposite strict rankings exist exactly when its attained minimum is negative and its attained maximum is positive.  This proves the pruning claims.
