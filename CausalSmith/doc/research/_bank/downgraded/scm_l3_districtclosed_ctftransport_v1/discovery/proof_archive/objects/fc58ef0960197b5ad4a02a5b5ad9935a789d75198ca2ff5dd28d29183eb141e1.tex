Given a checked local-arm instance \(\mathfrak L_D\) containing \(\rho,\sigma\), a menu-dual certificate at level \(\gamma\in\mathbb R\) is a vector \(\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}\) satisfying
\[
A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}
\quad\text{coordinatewise},\qquad
m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\ge\gamma.
\]
The certificate is not well typed without the checked numerator rows and the common strict-positive evidence certificate carried by \(\mathfrak L_D\).
