For each complete supplied cell \(C_{a,v}\) in domain \(s\), first check transport admissibility, namely \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\).  If \(\operatorname{SupportLocal}_D(C_{a,v})\) accepts, retain the independently constructed raw pair
\[
\bigl(r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v},P_a^s(v)\bigr).
\]
For each successful extraction and every accepted \(\mathsf{RowEligible}_{s,a,D}(h,c)\), retain the two separate pairs
\[
\bigl(u_{s,a,D,h,c},N_{s,a,D,h,c}\bigr),
\qquad
\bigl(v_{s,a,D,h},H_{s,a,D,h}\bigr).
\]
Repeated identical row--value pairs may be removed by a checked equality test, but no quotient \(N_{s,a,D,h,c}/H_{s,a,D,h}\) is ever stacked as a linear row value.  Prepend \((\mathbf 1^\top,1)\), order all accepted pairs canonically, and stack their rows and values as \((A_D,m_D)\).  Every row carries its admissibility proof, event-expression derivation, accepting total support-local trace, and, for a context row used in conditionalization, the independent positivity certificate.  A failed extraction, rejected locality trace, changed-district source cell, or cross-district complete cell creates no local row; its complete supplied-cell equality remains in the exact global response-profile and canonical fibers.
