Each \(e\in\mathcal U\) is a realizable finite block of typed descriptors, including optional deterministic-postprocessing descriptors.  Acquiring \(e\) reveals the complete laws of their declared outputs, equivalently a dependent row block \((R_e,p_e)\), at cost \(c_e>0\).  All complement and normalization rows of a finite output law are included in the block.  For \(S\subseteq\mathcal U\), set
\[
\mathcal K_S=\{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D):R_eq=p_e\text{ for every }e\in S\}
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{PO}(S)=\left\{\rho:\exists q\in\mathcal K_S, d_D^{\mathsf T}q>0,
\ \forall\sigma\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}},\
(n_{\rho,D}-n_{\sigma,D})^{\mathsf T}q\geq0\right\}.
\]
Thus an undefined common-evidence value is never ranked.  Define
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{full}}=\operatorname{PO}(\mathcal U),
\qquad
\min_{S\subseteq\mathcal U}\sum_{e\in S}c_e
\quad\text{subject to}\quad
\operatorname{PO}(S)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{full}},
\qquad
f(S)=|\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}|-|\operatorname{PO}(S)|.
\]
