Suppose the reduced arm comparison has one district \(D\). If there exists \(\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) with \(A_D^\top\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^\top\) and \(m_D^\top\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}>0\), removing \(\rho\) cannot remove a maximizing arm from \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\); an arm whose common evidence is certified zero is excluded as undefined rather than ranked.
