For every \(S\subseteq\mathcal U\), define
\[
\operatorname{Pos}_G(S)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\neg\exists\vartheta\,
\left[\vartheta\in\Lambda_S\ \wedge\
\Phi_{E,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\vartheta^\star)=0\right].
\]
The predicate is used only when \(\Lambda_S\ne\varnothing\), and its displayed
closed real formula is evaluated by the exact quantifier-elimination semantics of
\(\mathrm{def:hybrid\text{-}result\text{-}grammar}\).
