Define \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{sat}}\) to contain exactly the families in \(\mathfrak M\) for which every \(G[D]\) is bidirected complete and, in every domain, one latent response variable \(U_D^s\in\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\) generates all mechanisms in \(D\) by reading the component maps of \(U_D^s\).  The class ranges over every choice of laws on \(\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\) satisfying the unchanged-district response-law equalities, with the variables \((U_D^s:D\in\mathcal D(G))\) independent.  Thus \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{sat}}\subseteq\mathfrak M\), and equality is not asserted for a sparse non-clique district.
