Given a checked local-arm instance \(\mathfrak L_D\) containing \(\rho\) and \(\sigma\) and with \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\), define
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=\min_{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)}g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q,\qquad
\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=\max_{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)}g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q.
\]
The margins are undefined if the checked local-arm instance, its common strict-positive denominator certificate, or nonemptiness of the face is absent.
