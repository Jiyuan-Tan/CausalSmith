Suppose the reduced arm comparison has one district \(D\), \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\), and \(d_Dq>0\) for every \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). Then \[\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=\max_{\eta\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}}\{m_D^\top\eta:A_D^\top\eta\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^\top\}.\] Consequently, \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) in every compatible SCM if and only if the displayed dual has a certificate with \(m_D^\top\eta>0\). If \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\), a primal minimizer induces an original-alphabet compatible SCM with \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\). If \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0<\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)\), primal minimizers and maximizers induce two original-alphabet compatible SCMs with opposite arm rankings.
