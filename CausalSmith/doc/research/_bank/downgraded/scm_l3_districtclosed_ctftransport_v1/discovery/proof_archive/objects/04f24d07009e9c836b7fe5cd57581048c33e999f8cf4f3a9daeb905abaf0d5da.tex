Fix a domain \(s\in\mathcal S\) and an arbitrary
\(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  For a checked derivation
\(\delta:A\Downarrow F\), let
\(\operatorname{ev}_{\mathbf M}(F)\) denote the evaluator specified in
\(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\).  We prove by
structural induction on \(\delta\) the invariant
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_{\mathbf M}(F)=P_{M^s}(A).
 \tag{9}
\]

For a supplied-cell leaf \(A=C_{a,u}\),
\(\mathrm{ass:typed\text{-}menu\text{-}realizability}\) and the defining
equalities of \(\mathrm{def:compatibility\text{-}fiber}\) give
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_{\mathbf M}(\operatorname{Cell}(s,a,u))
 =P_a^s(u)=P_{M^s}(C_{a,u}).
 \tag{10}
\]
The contradiction and sure-event leaves satisfy (9) because their evaluators
are respectively zero and one.  A rational leaf is accepted as an event value
only through a checked identity, whose soundness is the coefficient-comparison
argument in
\(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}fixing\text{-}calculus\text{-}soundness}\).

Suppose (9) holds for all immediate subderivations.  For the complement
constructor, finite complementarity gives
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_{\mathbf M}(1-F)
 =1-P_{M^s}(A)=P_{M^s}(A^c).
 \tag{11}
\]
For a disjoint-union constructor, the syntactic disjointness checker has
verified that the finite events \((A_j:j\in J)\) are pairwise disjoint, and
finite additivity gives
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_{\mathbf M}\!\left(\sum_{j\in J}F_j\right)
 =\sum_{j\in J}P_{M^s}(A_j)
 =P_{M^s}\!\left(\bigsqcup_{j\in J}A_j\right).
 \tag{12}
\]
The marginalization constructor explicitly enumerates a finite alphabet and
checks that its events form the partition
\(A=\bigsqcup_x A_x\), so (12) proves that case as well.  For a product
constructor used only arithmetically, its evaluator is the product of its child
evaluators.  If its conclusion represents an intersection of events, the
constructor is well typed only when the checked support sets are disjoint
unions of original districts, and it carries the corresponding accepted
district-product fixing trace.  Applying
\(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}fixing\text{-}calculus\text{-}soundness}\) to
that trace and then the induction hypotheses gives
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_{\mathbf M}\!\left(\prod_{j\in J}F_j\right)
 =\prod_{j\in J}P_{M^s}(A_j)
 =P_{M^s}\!\left(\bigcap_{j\in J}A_j\right).
 \tag{13}
\]

At a fixing or registry-substitution constructor, the trace is accepted only
after its response-profile expansion, denominator checks, and exact
cleared-denominator identity have succeeded.  The already proved
\(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}fixing\text{-}calculus\text{-}soundness}\)
therefore equates the input and output evaluations; combining this equality
with the child induction hypotheses proves (9).  Finally, a conditional kernel
constructor contains derivations
\(\delta_1:A\cap H\Downarrow F_1\) and
\(\delta_0:H\Downarrow F_0\), together with an independent strict-positivity
certificate for \(F_0\) on the declared fiber.  The induction hypotheses and
that certificate yield
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_{\mathbf M}(F_0)=P_{M^s}(H)>0,
\]
and consequently
\[
 \operatorname{ev}_{\mathbf M}
 \!\left(\operatorname{Quot}(F_1,F_0,\nu_0)\right)
 =\frac{P_{M^s}(A\cap H)}{P_{M^s}(H)}
 =P_{M^s}(A\mid H).
 \tag{14}
\]
If the conclusion is an ordinary event-probability judgment, the quotient in
(14) remains a kernel intermediate and can enter the conclusion only through
a checked fixing trace; it is never introduced by an uncertified division.
Equations (10)--(14), together with the fixing case, exhaust the finite
derivation constructors and prove (9).

We next prove the assertion about positivity certificates rather than assuming
it.  Each accepted certificate carries a rational
\(\varepsilon>0\) and one of the finite exact witnesses allowed by
\(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\).  For a linear-face
witness, write the displayed constraints as
\[
 Lx=\ell,\qquad Kx-k\ge0.
\]
The checker verifies rational vectors \(\lambda\), \(\mu\ge0\), and the
coefficient identity
\[
 H(x)-\varepsilon
 =\lambda^{\mathsf T}(Lx-\ell)
  +\mu^{\mathsf T}(Kx-k).
 \tag{15}
\]
Every \(x\) in the declared face satisfies its displayed constraints, so
(15) implies \(H(x)\ge\varepsilon>0\).  No completeness of this certificate
form is needed.

For a finite semialgebraic witness, write its displayed constraints as
\(h_i(x)=0\) and \(g_j(x)\ge0\).  The checker verifies, coefficient by
coefficient, an identity of the form
\[
 H(x)-\varepsilon
 =\sum_i\tau_i(x)h_i(x)
  +\sum_{J}\sigma_J(x)\prod_{j\in J}g_j(x),
 \tag{16}
\]
where it also verifies an exact rational sum-of-squares decomposition of every
\(\sigma_J\).  At a point satisfying the displayed constraints, the first sum
in (16) vanishes and every term of the second sum is nonnegative.  Hence again
\(H(x)\ge\varepsilon>0\).  The constant/ring-normalization case is (15) or
(16) with no constraints, after the checker has verified the positive rational
constant and exact coefficient identity.  Checked substitutions preserve these
identities exactly.  The response-law tuple induced by any
\(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) satisfies every displayed simplex,
menu, graph, and invariance constraint by
\(\mathrm{ass:recursive\text{-}compatibility}\),
\(\mathrm{ass:typed\text{-}menu\text{-}realizability}\), and
\(\mathrm{def:compatibility\text{-}fiber}\).  Therefore every accepted
positivity certificate is valid on its declared compatibility fiber.

It remains to prove the exact behavior of the support-local checker.  Fix a
district \(D\) and define the finite nonfocal carrier
\[
 \widehat\Omega_{-D}
 =\prod_{D'\in\mathcal D(G),\,D'\ne D}
   \Omega_{D'}^{\mathrm{all}}.
\]
If there is no nonfocal district, this product is the singleton empty tuple.
By \(\mathrm{ass:recursive\text{-}compatibility}\) and
\(\mathrm{def:district\text{-}response\text{-}profile}\), both
\(\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\) and \(\widehat\Omega_{-D}\) are finite and
nonempty.  Acyclic recursive evaluation computes the truth value
\[
 i_A(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})
 :=\mathbf 1\{A\text{ is true under }(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\}
 \in\{0,1\}
 \tag{17}
\]
in finitely many table lookups.

By its definition, \(\operatorname{SupportLocal}_D(A)\) exhaustively tests
the finite family of equalities
\[
 i_A(\omega_D,\eta)=i_A(\omega_D,\eta')
 \quad\text{for every }\omega_D\in\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}
 \text{ and every }\eta,\eta'\in\widehat\Omega_{-D}.
 \tag{18}
\]
Thus the nested enumeration terminates.  If it accepts, (18) holds, which is
exactly independence of the recursive indicator from every nonfocal response
profile.  Conversely, that independence is the universal equality (18), so
every test succeeds and the checker accepts.  On acceptance, let \(\eta_0\)
be the first nonfocal profile in the fixed finite order.  The constructed row
is
\[
 \operatorname{row}_D(A)(\omega_D)
 =i_A(\omega_D,\eta_0).
 \tag{19}
\]
Combining (18) and (19) yields, for every nonfocal profile \(\eta\),
\[
 \operatorname{row}_D(A)(\omega_D)
 =i_A(\omega_D,\eta).
\]
The enumeration ranges over all profiles, including profiles assigned zero
probability by the current model, so this is the asserted structural locality,
not merely an almost-sure statement.  This proves termination, the if-and-only-if
claim, and correctness of the constructed incidence row, completing the proof.
