Let \(\mathsf{Local}_D(C)\Downarrow b_D\) be checked.  District-cell linearity gives
\[
P_{M^\star}(C)=b_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star
\tag{1}
\]
for every compatible model.  If
\[
b_D=\lambda^{\mathsf T}A_D+
\sum_{\omega\in Z_D(m_D)}\zeta_\omega e_\omega,
\tag{2}
\]
the broad inclusion in the one-district projection theorem gives \(A_Dq_D^\star=m_D\).  By the definition of \(Z_D(m_D)\), every compatible \(q_D^\star\) is zero on \(Z_D(m_D)\).  Multiplying (2) by \(q_D^\star\) and using (1) yields
\[
P_{M^\star}(C)=\lambda^{\mathsf T}m_D,
\]
so membership is sufficient on the broad fiber.

Now assume \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  The one-district projection theorem identifies the focal saturated projection with \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) and gives
\[
R_D(m_D)=\operatorname{span}(K-K)^\perp.
\tag{3}
\]
The map \(q\mapsto b_D^{\mathsf T}q\) is constant on \(K\) if and only if \(b_D\) annihilates every difference \(q-q'\), which by (3) is equivalent to \(b_D\in R_D(m_D)\).  If membership fails, (3) supplies \(q,q'\in K\) with \(b_D^{\mathsf T}q\ne b_D^{\mathsf T}q'\).  Exact focal projection lifts both points to compatible original-alphabet SCM families with the same menu, and (1) makes their target probabilities unequal.  The lifting construction changes only the focal district.  The two stated stronger sufficient checks follow from the separation hierarchy.  If target locality fails, (1) is unavailable; if exact focal projection fails, the two row-face points need not lift.  This proves the final limitation.
