For every finite set \(\mathcal P_0\) of polynomials in \(R[X_1,\ldots,X_k]\), there is a cylindrical decomposition of \(R^k\) adapted to \(\mathcal P_0\), and such a decomposition can be computed in time \((sd)^{2^{O(k)}}\), where \(s=\operatorname{card}\mathcal P_0\) and \(d=\max_{P\in\mathcal P_0}\deg(P)\).
