For every district use the complete carrier \(\Omega_D=\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\).  For a tuple \(\theta=(q_D^s:s\in\mathcal S,D\in\mathcal D(G))\), \(q_D^s\in\Delta(\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}})\), and every finite counterfactual cell \(C\), define
\[
\Psi_C^s(\theta^s)=
\sum_{\omega\in\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}}
\mathbf 1\{C\text{ is true under recursive evaluation of }\omega\}
\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^s(\omega_D).
\]
If \(C\) visits \(k\) original districts, this polynomial is multi-affine in their probability blocks and has total degree at most \(k\).  For complete supplied cells \(C_{a,v}\), define
\[
\begin{split}
\Theta(\mathcal P)=\bigl\{\theta:{}&q_D^s\in\Delta(\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}})
\quad(s\in\mathcal S,D\in\mathcal D(G)),\\
&q_D^s=q_D^\star\quad\text{when }D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing,\\
&\Psi_{C_{a,v}}^s(\theta^s)=P_a^s(v)
\quad(s\in\mathcal S,a\in\mathcal A_s,v\in\mathcal X_{W_a})\bigr\}.
\end{split}
\]
All complete supplied-cell equations are retained, including cross-district cells rejected by the local row checker.  Set
\[
L_T(\mathcal P)=\min_{\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)}\Psi_T^\star(\theta^\star),
\qquad
U_T(\mathcal P)=\max_{\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)}\Psi_T^\star(\theta^\star),
\]
and
\[
\mathcal G_{\rho,\sigma}(\theta)=
\Psi_{B_\sigma\cap E}^\star(\theta^\star)
-\Psi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star(\theta^\star).
\]

For an arbitrary possibly nonsaturated ADMG, let \(\vartheta^s=(\mu^s,p^s)\in\operatorname{Can}(G,\mathcal X)\) retain the graph-compatible one-hot structural tables, canonical latent-factor restrictions, and product law across original districts.  Its coordinates satisfy
\[
p_U^s(u)\ge0,
\quad\sum_up_U^s(u)=1,
\quad\mu_{V,v\mid pa,u_V}^s\in\{0,1\},
\quad\sum_{v\in\mathcal X_V}\mu_{V,v\mid pa,u_V}^s=1.
\]
Let \(\Phi_{C,\mathrm{can}}^s(\vartheta^s)\) be the exact finite recursive cell polynomial and \(q_{D,\mathrm{can}}^s(\cdot;\vartheta^s)\) its induced complete district response-law.  Define
\[
\begin{split}
\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)=\bigl\{\vartheta:{}&
\vartheta^s\in\operatorname{Can}(G,\mathcal X)\quad(s\in\mathcal S),\\
&q_{D,\mathrm{can}}^s(\cdot;\vartheta^s)

\end{split}
\]
The response-profile fiber is exact for the all-district-saturated class; the canonical fiber is exact for the broad graph-compatible class and never drops sparse latent-factor restrictions.
