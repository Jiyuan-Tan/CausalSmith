The initial configuration has root denotation
\[
\bigl(P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E),P_{M^\star}(E)\bigr)\tag{1}
\]
for every \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), by the initialization clause. Induction on the finite checked trace, using transition preservation at each step, shows that (1) is invariant at every reached configuration.

Every nonterminal step strictly lowers \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathrm{lex}}\in\mathbb N^8\). There is no infinite descending lexicographic sequence in \(\mathbb N^8\): the first coordinate can decrease only finitely often; after its last decrease, the same argument applies successively to the remaining seven coordinates. Thus the run is finite. The fixed priority and least-node convention make every successor unique, and the exhaustive typed failure branches make \(\operatorname{STUCK}\) unreachable.

Suppose the terminal is an identification result. Structural induction on its expression derivations shows that the resolved roots use only menu cells, rational constants, finite sums, products, and certified quotients. Hence
\(F_B,F_E\in\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\). Root preservation gives, throughout the compatibility fiber,
\[
F_B(\mathcal P)=P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E),\qquad
F_E(\mathcal P)=P_{M^\star}(E).\tag{2}
\]
The terminal certificate \(\pi\) proves \(F_E(\mathcal P)>0\) for every compatible model. Division is therefore legitimate, and (2) yields
\[
\frac{F_B(\mathcal P)}{F_E(\mathcal P)}
=\frac{P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E)}{P_{M^\star}(E)}
=P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\mid E)
=Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M).\tag{3}
\]
If the independent zero certificate instead proves \(F_E=0\) throughout the fiber, (2) shows that the conditional is undefined everywhere and the zero-evidence terminal is sound. In every other failure branch the algorithm stops before using the unavailable split, extraction, face equality, or denominator positivity statement and performs no division. In particular, a local face witness gives global nonidentification only after the separate one-district projection hypotheses have been verified. A numerical sign without the required certificate cannot trigger (3), by the condition rule itself.
