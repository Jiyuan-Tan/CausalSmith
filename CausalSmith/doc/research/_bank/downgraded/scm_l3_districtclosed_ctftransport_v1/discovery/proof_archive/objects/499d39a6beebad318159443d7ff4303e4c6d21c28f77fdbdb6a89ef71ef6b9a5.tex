S_0=h,\qquad S_1=h',\qquad
S_{i-1}=Q_iS_i-S_{i+1},\qquad \deg S_{i+1}<\deg S_i,
\tag{2}
\]

\[
h(\alpha+\epsilon)=\epsilon h'(\alpha)+o(\epsilon),
\qquad
h(\alpha-\epsilon)=-\epsilon h'(\alpha)+o(\epsilon).
\]
Thus \(V\) decreases by exactly one when \(x\) crosses \(\alpha\) from left to right and is unchanged at every other crossing.  Partitioning a rational interval at the finitely many roots of the \(S_i\) proves directly that
\[
\#\{\alpha\in(l,u):h(\alpha)=0\}=V(l)-V(u).
\tag{3}
\]
