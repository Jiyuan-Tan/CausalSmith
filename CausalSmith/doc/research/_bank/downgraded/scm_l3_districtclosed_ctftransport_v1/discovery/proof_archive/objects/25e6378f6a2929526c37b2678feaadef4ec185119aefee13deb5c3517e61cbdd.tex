Suppose a \(\mathsf{CheckedLocalArms}_D\) record is given.  If there exists \(\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) with
\[
A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}
\leq g_{\rho,\sigma,D},
\qquad
m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}>0,
\]
then removing \(\rho\) cannot remove a maximizing arm from \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\).  A candidate comparison for which the common-evidence checker instead certifies uniform zero is excluded as undefined rather than ranked.
