Let
\[
 \mathcal Q_D=\{q_D^\star(\mathbf M):\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\}.
\]
We prove both inclusions.

First take \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  The normalization row of \(A_D\) gives \(\mathbf 1^\top q_D^\star=1\), and \(q_D^\star\geq0\) because it is a probability mass function.  For every other retained row, district-cell linearity and its checked source-admissibility proof give the corresponding equality
\(
 r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^\star=K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h).
\)
Stacking these equalities in the declared order yields
\[
 A_Dq_D^\star=m_D.
\]
Hence \(q_D^\star\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), and therefore
\(
 \mathcal Q_D\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\)

For the reverse inclusion, nonemptiness of \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) supplies a reference family \(\mathbf M_0\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  Fix any \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\).  Because the reduced targets and every retained menu factor visit only the one district \(D\), a typed-menu cell in a target or transport-admissible source domain is, after consistency and unnesting, a finite disjoint union of fixed-context district cells.  No cross-district quotient is needed.  The successful extraction judgments therefore give all of those cell probabilities as incidence rows, and the equations \(A_Dq=m_D\) preserve their values cell by cell.  Finite additivity then preserves each supplied law in those domains.

We now realize \(q\) without changing any declared endogenous alphabet.  Introduce for district \(D\) a finite response variable \(U_D\) taking values in \(\Omega_D\) with
\[
 P(U_D=\omega)=q(\omega).
\]
At every parent configuration visited by a target or menu, the structural equation of each \(V\in D\) reads the component map \(f_V\) of \(U_D=\omega=(f_V:V\in D)\).  At an unvisited parent configuration, extend the map by the value used in \(\mathbf M_0\).  Acyclicity of \(G\) makes these equations recursive, and their outputs remain in the fixed set \(\mathcal X_V\).  Use this response block in the target domain and in every source domain satisfying \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\).  In a source domain satisfying \(D\subseteq\Delta_s\), retain the reference response block of \(\mathbf M_0\).  This dichotomy is exhaustive by district closure.  Retain all response blocks outside \(D\) from \(\mathbf M_0\), independently across districts.  Hence recursive compatibility and district factorization are preserved, and district-kernel invariance holds in every unchanged district.

In admissible domains the preceding cell calculation and \(A_Dq=m_D\) preserve every supplied menu law; in changed-
\(D\) domains the complete reference response block was retained, so their supplied laws are preserved as well.  Typed-menu realizability therefore gives a family \(\mathbf M_q\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), and construction gives
\[
 q_D^\star(\mathbf M_q)=q.
\]
Thus \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\subseteq\mathcal Q_D\).  The construction permits zero masses in \(q\), so it realizes boundary points as well as relative-interior points on the original alphabets \(\mathcal X\).
