Assume \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\).  A supplied-cell indicator independent of \(\omega_D\) has every focal slice equal to either zero or the normalization row.  An indicator depending only on \(\omega_D\) has every slice equal to its accepted raw row, including when the cell is a deterministic finite preimage.  Both cases lie in \(R_D(m_D)\), proving \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\).

For strictness, let \(Y_0\) and \(Z\) be independent binary response coordinates in distinct singleton districts, take \(D\) to generate \(Y_0\), and supply the complete marginal law of \(Y_0\) and complete joint law of \((Y_0,Z)\).  If \(h_y(\omega_D)=\mathbf1\{Y_0=y\}\) and \(k_z(\omega_{-D})=\mathbf1\{Z=z\}\), the marginal menu retains \(h_y\), whereas a joint cell has cross-district indicator \(h_yk_z\), violating \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\).  After fixing \(\omega_{-D}\), its slice is zero or \(h_y\), hence lies in the active row space.  Independent Bernoulli response blocks realize the menu on the original binary alphabets.
