For every accepted \(\mathsf{RowEligible}_{s,a,D}(h,c)\), define the two zero--one incidence rows
\[
u_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D)=\operatorname{row}_D(A_{h,c})(\omega_D),
\qquad
v_{s,a,D,h}(\omega_D)=\operatorname{row}_D(H_h^{\mathrm{ev}})(\omega_D),
\qquad \omega_D\in\Omega_D.
\]
For a complete supplied cell \(C_{a,v}\), an accepting \(\operatorname{SupportLocal}_D(C_{a,v})\) trace similarly defines
\[
r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v}=\operatorname{row}_D(C_{a,v}).
\]
All three rows are constructed from the accepted exhaustive truth table.  A conditional probability is not an incidence row.
