For a defined one-district comparison and \(\gamma\in\mathbb R\), a menu-dual dominance certificate is a vector \(\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}\) satisfying
\[
A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\leq g_{\rho,\sigma,D}
\quad\text{coordinatewise},
\qquad
m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\geq\gamma.
\]
The certificate is strict when \(\gamma>0\).  Its checker uses exact rational or real-algebraic arithmetic and separately requires the common-evidence positivity premise before converting a numerator sign into a conditional-value order.
