Fix \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\).  Let \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) and set \(q=q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\).  By \(\mathrm{def:district\text{-}response\text{-}profile}\), \(q\geq0\) and \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\).  A raw row retained by \(\mathrm{def:stacked\text{-}menu\text{-}system}\) carries proofs that its cell is district-local and that \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\).  Recursive evaluation and \(\mathrm{ass:typed\text{-}menu\text{-}realizability}\) give
\[
P_a^s(v)=r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v}q_D^s.
\]
If \(s=\star\), then \(q_D^s=q\); otherwise transport admissibility and \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}kernel\text{-}invariance}\) give \(q_D^s=q_D^\star=q\).  Thus every retained raw equality holds at \(q\).  For each retained derived row, \(\mathrm{lem:district\text{-}cell\text{-}linearity}\) and the same admissibility proof give
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q.
\]
Stacking these equations with normalization yields \(A_Dq=m_D\).  Hence
\[
\mathcal Q_D(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\tag{1}
\]

Assume now \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  By \(\mathrm{def:baseline\text{-}averaged\text{-}active\text{-}slice\text{-}menu}\), there exists one baseline \(\mathbf M^0\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\) witnessing the predicate.  Put \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(q_0=q_D^\star(\mathbf M^0)\), and \(Z=Z_D(m_D)\).  Inclusion (1) gives \(q_0\in K\), so \(K\ne\varnothing\).

We first establish the asserted exact direction space.  Let \(J=\Omega_D\setminus Z\).  Since \(A_D\) contains normalization and \(K\ne\varnothing\), \(J\ne\varnothing\).  For each \(\omega\in J\), the definition of \(Z\) gives \(q^\omega\in K\) with \(q^\omega(\omega)>0\).  Therefore
\[
\bar q=\frac1{|J|}\sum_{\omega\in J}q^\omega
\]
belongs to \(K\), is zero on \(Z\), and is strictly positive on \(J\).  Define
\[
L=\{v\in\mathbb R^{\Omega_D}:A_Dv=0,
\ v(\omega)=0\text{ for every }\omega\in Z\}.
\]
Every difference of two points of \(K\) belongs to \(L\).  Conversely, if \(v\in L\), finiteness of \(J\) and positivity of \(\bar q\) on \(J\) permit \(\varepsilon>0\) such that \(\bar q\pm\varepsilon v\geq0\).  Since \(A_Dv=0\), both perturbations lie in \(K\), and
\(v=(2\varepsilon)^{-1}\{(\bar q+\varepsilon v)-(\bar q-\varepsilon v)\}\).  Hence
\[
L=\operatorname{span}(K-K).
\tag{2}
\]
If \(\widetilde A_D\) is obtained by appending the rows \(e_\omega\), \(\omega\in Z\), to \(A_D\), then \(L=\ker(\widetilde A_D)\).  Finite-dimensional Gaussian elimination gives
\[
R_D(m_D)=\operatorname{row}(\widetilde A_D)
=(\ker\widetilde A_D)^\perp
=\operatorname{span}(K-K)^\perp.
\tag{3}
\]

Fix arbitrary \(q\in K\).  Complete each partial profile in \(\Omega_D\) at every unvisited parent configuration by a fixed element of the relevant nonempty alphabet, obtaining an injection \(\iota_D:\Omega_D\to\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\).  In the target domain replace the law of \(U_D^\star\) by \((\iota_D)_\#q\); make the same replacement in every source with \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\); retain the baseline \(D\)-block law in every source with \(D\subseteq\Delta_s\); and hold fixed every response law and structural table in every district \(D'\ne D\).  The two source cases exhaust the domains by \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}closure}\).  Call the resulting family \(\mathbf M^q\).

The replacement uses the unrestricted focal response-block law allowed by \(\mathrm{def:focal\text{-}district\text{-}saturated\text{-}model\text{-}class}\), preserves the original alphabets, and leaves the acyclic recursive structural equations unchanged.  Independence of the new focal block from the fixed nonfocal blocks preserves \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}factorization}\).  Equal target and unchanged-source replacements preserve \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}kernel\text{-}invariance}\); every nonfocal equality remains true because those blocks are fixed.  Thus \(\mathbf M^q\) is in the focal-district-saturated model class before checking the supplied menu.

For a transport-admissible complete supplied cell \(C_{a,v}\) in domain \(s\), the nonfocal product law remains \(\nu_{-D}^{0,s}\).  Finite total probability, district factorization, and \(\mathrm{def:active\text{-}slice\text{-}separated\text{-}menu}\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
P_{M^{q,s}}^a(v)-P_{M^{0,s}}^a(v)
&=\sum_{\omega_{-D}}\nu_{-D}^{0,s}(\omega_{-D})
\chi_{C_{a,v},\omega_{-D}}(q-q_0)\\
&=\bar\chi_{C_{a,v}}^{s,\mathbf M^0}(q-q_0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
The witnessing \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\) condition puts the averaged row in \(R_D(m_D)\), while \(q-q_0\in\operatorname{span}(K-K)\); (3) therefore makes (4) zero.  This is exactly the advertised fixed-baseline preservation identity.  If the cell is not transport-admissible, then \(s\ne\star\) and \(D\cap\Delta_s\ne\varnothing\); district closure gives \(D\subseteq\Delta_s\), and the construction retained that source's focal block as well as every nonfocal block.  Its probability is unchanged.  Thus every complete cell of every supplied law remains equal to \(\mathcal P\), so
\[
\mathbf M^q\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P),
\qquad q_D^\star(\mathbf M^q)=q.
\]
Since \(q\in K\) was arbitrary, this and (1) prove
\(\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)=K\), including every boundary point, with all nonfocal graph-compatible restrictions unchanged.  Finally, \(\mathrm{lem:active\text{-}slice\text{-}implies\text{-}baseline\text{-}averaged}\) gives the stated sufficient corollaries from nonemptiness plus \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\), and \(\mathrm{lem:structural\text{-}separation\text{-}implies\text{-}active\text{-}slice}\) supplies the further \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\) check.
