Fix \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  We check the rules in their declared phase order.

For \(\mathsf{NORM}\), the attached derivation \(\nu\) is built from consistency, unnesting, contradiction removal, Boolean normalization, and finite disjoint additivity.  Each constructor is an equality of events or of their probabilities, hence the replaced node and its successor have the same denotation.  For \(\mathsf{ANCESTOR}\), the omitted finite variables are summed over their complete declared state spaces; therefore
\[
 P(e)=\sum_xP(e,x),
\]
which is the checked derivation \(\alpha\).  For \(\mathsf{SPLIT}\), semi-Markovian district factorization applies to the enumerated districts of \(G[H]\); the finite sum over the enumerated assignments and product over district factors is exactly the original ancestor-closed probability.  These three substitutions therefore preserve every root that reaches the replaced node.

At \(\mathsf{EXTRACT}\), only a successful extraction with a proof of \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\) is retained.  District-cell linearity and district-kernel invariance give every retained equality, and stacking them with normalization gives
\[
 A_Dq_D^\star(\mathbf M)=m_D.
\]
The denotation of both \(\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,b)\) and its \(\operatorname{NeedFace}\) successor is, by the grammar, \(bq_D^\star(\mathbf M)\); rejected or failed candidates create no equation.  Thus extraction preserves denotation.

At \(\mathsf{FACE\_ID}\), the checked representation
\(
 b=\lambda^\top A+\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega e_\omega
\)
and the certified facts \(Aq_D^\star=m\) and \(q_D^\star(\omega)=0\) for \(\omega\in Z\) give
\[
 bq_D^\star=\lambda^\top m.
\]
Thus the resolved expression has the old face-node denotation.  For \(\mathsf{MARGINALIZE}\), finite additivity identifies the parent denotation with the sum of the resolved child denotations.  For \(\mathsf{PRODUCT}\), district factorization identifies it with their product.  Hence each of the seven nonterminal transformation rules preserves both root denotations pointwise.

When both roots are resolved, \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) records those unchanged numerator and denominator values.  It constructs a quotient only when the separate certificate \(\pi\) proves strict positivity of the denominator throughout the compatibility fiber; its zero and failure branches perform no division.

It remains to check operational claims.  Each of the first seven rules decreases its designated coordinate of
\(
 \operatorname{rank}_{\mathrm{lex}}=(r_N,r_A,r_S,r_X,r_F,r_{\Sigma},r_{\Pi},r_C)
\)
and fixes every earlier coordinate, while no rule creates an earlier-phase node.  Condition decreases \(r_C=1\) after the first seven coordinates vanish.  The canonical orders, fixed priority, and least-applicable-node convention select at most one successor.  Conversely, every well-typed nonterminal has a least unresolved phase.  The rule for that phase applies, and every failure of a partial operation is routed to its explicitly declared \(\operatorname{FAIL}\) constructor.  Therefore a well-typed run cannot reach \(\operatorname{STUCK}\).
