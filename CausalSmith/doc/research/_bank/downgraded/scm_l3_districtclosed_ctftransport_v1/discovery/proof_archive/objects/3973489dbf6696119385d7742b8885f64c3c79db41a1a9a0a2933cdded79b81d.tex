Assume that the reduced arm comparison has the single district \(D\), as required by the proposed statement.  
Let \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\).  Feasibility of the stated dual vector and \(A_Dq=m_D\) give
\[
 g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q
 \geq q^\top A_D^\top\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}
 =m_D^\top\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}>0.
\]
Because \(n_{\rho,D}\) and \(n_{\sigma,D}\) are incidence rows for subevents of the common evidence event, coordinatewise
\[
 0\leq n_{\rho,D}\leq d_D,
 \qquad 0\leq n_{\sigma,D}\leq d_D.
\]
In particular,
\[
 d_Dq\geq n_{\sigma,D}q
 =g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q+n_{\rho,D}q
 >0.
\]
Thus every compatible law has positive common evidence, and district-cell linearity gives
\[
 Q_\sigma(q)-Q_\rho(q)
 =\frac{g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q}{d_Dq}>0.
\]
Arm \(\rho\) is strictly dominated by the available comparator \(\sigma\) at every compatible law and therefore cannot be a maximizing arm.  Removing it cannot remove a maximizer.  If the independent zero calculus instead certifies that an arm's conditioning evidence has probability zero, recursion soundness says that its conditional value is undefined; excluding that arm is a domain-of-definition decision, not an ordering of an undefined quantity below a reward.
