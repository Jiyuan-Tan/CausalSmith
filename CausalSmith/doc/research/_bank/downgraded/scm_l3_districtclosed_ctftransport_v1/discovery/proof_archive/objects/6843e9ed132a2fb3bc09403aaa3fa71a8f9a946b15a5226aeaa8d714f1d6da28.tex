Fix \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\).  For a baseline \(\mathbf M^0\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\), let
\[
\nu_{-D}^{0,s}(\omega_{-D})
=\prod_{D'\in\mathcal D(G),\,D'\ne D}q_{D'}^{0,s}(\omega_{D'}),
\qquad
\omega_{-D}\in\prod_{D'\ne D}\Omega_{D'},
\]
where \(q_{D'}^{0,s}\) is the response-profile law induced by \(M^{0,s}\).  For every complete supplied cell \(C_{a,v}\) in a domain \(s\) that is transport-admissible for \(D\), meaning \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), define the baseline-averaged focal row
\[
\bar\chi_{C_{a,v}}^{s,\mathbf M^0}
=\sum_{\omega_{-D}}
\nu_{-D}^{0,s}(\omega_{-D})
\chi_{C_{a,v},\omega_{-D}}
\in\mathbb R^{\Omega_D}.
\]
The baseline-averaged active-slice predicate \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds exactly when there exists one \(\mathbf M^0\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\) such that
\[
\bar\chi_{C_{a,v}}^{s,\mathbf M^0}\in R_D(m_D)
\]
for every transport-admissible complete supplied cell.  The same witnessing baseline must be used for all cells and all feasible replacements of the focal law; a baseline depending on the replacement law is not permitted.
