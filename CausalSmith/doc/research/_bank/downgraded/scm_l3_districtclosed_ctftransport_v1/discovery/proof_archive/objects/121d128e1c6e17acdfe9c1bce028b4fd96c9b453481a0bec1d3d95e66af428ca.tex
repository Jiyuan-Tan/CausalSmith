Suppose \(\operatorname{Extract}_{s,a,D}(P_a^s)\Downarrow(K_{s,a,D},\kappa)\) and its trace contains an accepted \(\mathsf{RowEligible}_{s,a,D}(h,c)\) with numerator and context rows \(u_{s,a,D,h,c}\) and \(v_{s,a,D,h}\).  Then
\[
N_{s,a,D,h,c}=u_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s,\qquad
H_{s,a,D,h}=v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s>0,\qquad
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=\frac{N_{s,a,D,h,c}}{H_{s,a,D,h}}.
\]
After, and only after, a proof of \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), the first two equations hold with \(q_D^\star\).  The stack retains those two equations separately and never the quotient.  If \(\mathsf{Local}_D(C)\Downarrow(b_D,\ell_C)\), then \(P_{M^\star}(C)=b_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\); this gives the numerator and evidence equations only when the corresponding locality checks for \(B_\rho\cap E\) and \(E\) succeed.  Failed extraction or locality produces no incidence equation.
