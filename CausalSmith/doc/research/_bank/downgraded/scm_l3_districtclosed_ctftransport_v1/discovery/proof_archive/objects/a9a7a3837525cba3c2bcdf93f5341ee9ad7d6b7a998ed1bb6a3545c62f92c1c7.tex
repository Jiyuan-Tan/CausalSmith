Fix \(s\in\mathcal S\), \(a\in\mathcal A_s\), and \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), and suppose
\[
\operatorname{Extract}_{s,a,D}(P_a^s)\Downarrow(K_{s,a,D},\kappa).
\]
For \(h\in H_{s,a,D}\) and \(c\in C_{s,a,D}\), let
\[
S(h,c)=\{\omega_D\in\Omega_D:\omega_D\text{ produces }c
\text{ at context }h\text{ under }a\}.
\]
By \(\mathrm{def:incidence\text{-}row}\),
\[
r_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D)=\mathbf 1\{\omega_D\in S(h,c)\}.
\]
The successful judgment in \(\mathrm{def:typed\text{-}extraction\text{-}judgment}\) certifies every consistency, unnesting, fixing, marginalization, context lookup, and nonzero quotient used to form \(K_{s,a,D}\). By \(\mathrm{ass:typed\text{-}menu\text{-}realizability}\), the supplied law is the regime law of \(M^s\); by \(\mathrm{ass:recursive\text{-}compatibility}\), the extracted cell is deterministic conditional on \(\omega_D\). Finite total probability therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)
&=\sum_{\omega_D\in\Omega_D}
\mathbf 1\{\omega_D\in S(h,c)\}q_D^s(\omega_D)\\
&=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^s.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every quotient used here is legal because its nonzero premise occurs in \(\kappa\).

If \(s=\star\), then \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\) by notation. If \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}kernel\text{-}invariance}\) gives equality of the joint structural-response laws on \(D\), hence again \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\). Only after one of these two admissibility facts is established may one conclude
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^\star.
\]

Finally, \(\mathrm{def:target\text{-}arm\text{-}rows}\) evaluates the deterministic indicators of \(B_\rho\cap E\), \(E\), and a generic district-local target cell on \(\Omega_D\). Thus
\[
P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E)=n_{\rho,D}q_D^\star,
\qquad
P_{M^\star}(E)=d_Dq_D^\star,
\qquad
P_{M^\star}(\text{target cell})=b_Dq_D^\star.
\]
By the extraction and stacking definitions, a failed judgment has neither a certified kernel cell nor a certified incidence equality and contributes no derived row.
