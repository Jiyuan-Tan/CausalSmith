compatibility hypothesis gives nonemptiness.  Fix either exact row

\[
\ell_T=\min_{x\in\Lambda}\Xi_T^\star(x^\star),
\qquad
u_T=\max_{x\in\Lambda}\Xi_T^\star(x^\star).
\tag{3}
\]
Joint range equality turns \((3)\) into sharp causal bounds and turns their

No connectedness premise was used, so no interval-filling conclusion is available.

Apply the same compactness argument to the nonnegative evidence polynomial:

The exhaustive cases are

e_+=0,
\qquad

\tag{5}

They mean, respectively, uniform zero evidence, coexistence of zero and positive

maximum is positive.  This proves the safe-pruning characterizations.
