Suppose the reduced arm comparison has one district \(D\), \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\), and \(d_Dq>0\) throughout \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). For every realizable additional row \(r_D^\dagger\), the feasible set \(I_D\) is a closed interval and \(p\mapsto\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)\) is a finite convex piecewise-affine function. Its pieces are obtained from the feasible bases of the row-augmented dual program and have coefficients that are rational affine functions of \(m_D\). Hence \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\) is the complement in \(I_D\) of one closed interval, allowing zero, one, or two sign boundaries; each boundary returns both a dual menu-weight certificate on the pruning side and an original-alphabet SCM attaining equality.
