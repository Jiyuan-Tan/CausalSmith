Fix \(s\in\mathcal S\), \(a\in\mathcal A_s\), \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), \(h\in H_{s,a,D}\), and \(c\in C_{s,a,D}\), and suppose
\[
\operatorname{Extract}_{s,a,D}(P_a^s)\Downarrow(K_{s,a,D},\kappa).
\]
By typed-menu realizability, \(P_a^s\) is the regime-\(a\) law generated by \(M^s\). By recursive compatibility, after a complete district response profile \(\omega_D\in\Omega_D\) and the certified context \(h\) are fixed, every structural equation used by the extraction is deterministic. Hence the extracted output cell is \(c\) precisely when
\[
r_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D)
=\mathbf 1\{\omega_D\text{ produces }c\text{ at }h\text{ under }a\}=1,
\]
by the incidence-row definition. The extraction certificate guarantees that every fixing, marginalization, context lookup, and quotient used to expose this kernel is defined; in particular, the returned object is the Markov kernel obtained by randomizing the deterministic output above with the response-profile law \(q_D^s\). Finite total probability therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)
&=\sum_{\omega_D\in\Omega_D}
 P_{M^s}(\omega_D)\,\mathbf 1\{\omega_D\text{ produces }c\text{ at }h\text{ under }a\}\\
&=\sum_{\omega_D\in\Omega_D}q_D^s(\omega_D)\,
r_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D)
=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^s.
\end{aligned}\tag{1}
\]
This also proves that the row entries are zero or one and that their multiplication by \(q_D^s\) is well typed.

If \(s=\star\), equation (1) already has the target response law. If \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), district-kernel invariance states that the joint law of the structural response maps on \(D\) is the same in \(M^s\) and \(M^\star\), whence \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\). Thus, in exactly the two transport-admissible cases,
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^\star.\tag{2}
\]
No such replacement is made for a changed district.

Finally, the target-row construction evaluates a successfully reduced target-district cell on every \(\omega_D\). Applying the same finite total-probability calculation in the target domain gives
\[
P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E)=n_{\rho,D}q_D^\star,\qquad
P_{M^\star}(E)=d_Dq_D^\star,\qquad
P_{M^\star}(C)=b_Dq_D^\star\tag{3}
\]
for the generic reduced target cell \(C\). By the typed-extraction and stacked-menu definitions, an unsuccessful judgment has no returned kernel or certificate and contributes no row. Hence (1)--(3) use only successful, certified extractions and target reductions.
