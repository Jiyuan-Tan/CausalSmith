Suppose checked locality judgments reduce both arm numerators and their common evidence to one district \(D\), and \(d_D^{\mathsf T}q>0\) throughout \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\).  If there is \(\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) with
\[
A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T},
\qquad m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}>0,
\]
then removing \(\rho\) cannot remove a maximizing defined arm from \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\).  Separately, if the recursion certifies the common evidence to be zero, the corresponding conditional arm value is undefined and is excluded rather than ranked.
