Fix an accepted extraction record.  By \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus\text{-}soundness}\), its two constructive derivations give
\[
N_{s,a,D,h,c}(\mathcal P)=P_{M^s}(A_{h,c}),
\qquad
H_{s,a,D,h}(\mathcal P)=P_{M^s}(H_h).
\tag{1}
\]
The two accepting \(\operatorname{SupportLocal}_D\) tables state pointwise, for every \(\omega_D\) and every complete nonfocal profile \(\omega_{-D}\),
\[
\mathbf1_{A_{h,c}}(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})=r_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D),
\qquad
\mathbf1_{H_h}(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})=v_{s,a,D,h}(\omega_D).
\tag{2}
\]
Recursive compatibility makes these indicators deterministic after all response profiles are fixed.  Summing (2) over the independent nonfocal response law and then over \(q_D^s\) gives, by finite total probability,
\[
N_{s,a,D,h,c}=r_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s,
\qquad
H_{s,a,D,h}=v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s.
\tag{3}
\]
The independent certificate in the judgment gives \(H_{s,a,D,h}>0\); hence the quotient constructor and (3) give the displayed conditional ratio.  Nothing in the row-eligibility record asserted (3); it has been derived from (1), exhaustive locality, and total probability.
