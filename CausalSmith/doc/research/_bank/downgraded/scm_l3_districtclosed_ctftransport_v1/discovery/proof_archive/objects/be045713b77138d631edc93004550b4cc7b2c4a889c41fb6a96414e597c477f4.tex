Write \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(r=r_D^\dagger\), and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\).  Normalization makes \(K\) a nonempty compact convex polytope in a finite probability simplex.  Its image under \(q\mapsto rq\) is a nonempty compact convex subset of \(\mathbb R\), hence a closed interval \(I_D\).  For \(p\in I_D\), the fiber \(K(p)=\{q\in K:rq=p\}\) is nonempty and compact, so the defining minimum of \(gq\) is attained.

For \(p_0,p_1\in I_D\), choose minimizers \(q_i\in K(p_i)\).  If \(0\leq t\leq1\), then \(tq_0+(1-t)q_1\in K(tp_0+(1-t)p_1)\), whence
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(tp_0+(1-t)p_1;m_D)
\leq t\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p_0;m_D)
+(1-t)\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p_1;m_D).
\tag{1}
\]
Thus the value function is convex.

Append \(r\) to \(A_D\) and apply \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}linear\text{-}programming\text{-}strong\text{-}duality}\) to \(K(p)\).  It yields
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)
=\max_{(\eta,\beta):A_D^\top\eta+\beta r^\top\leq g^\top}
\{m_D^\top\eta+\beta p\}.
\tag{2}
\]
The feasible polyhedron is independent of \((m_D,p)\) and is rational because \(A_D\), \(r\), and \(g\) have integer entries.  Quotient its lineality space
\[
L_0=\{(u,t):A_D^\top u+t r^\top=0\}.
\]
For \(q\in K(p)\) and \((u,t)\in L_0\),
\(m_D^\top u+tp=q^\top(A_D^\top u+t r^\top)=0\), so the objective is constant on cosets of \(L_0\).  A rational complement gives a pointed rational quotient polyhedron.  It has finitely many vertices, and every finite attained linear maximum on it is attained at a vertex.  Enumerating those feasible bases gives rational \((\eta_B,\beta_B)\) with
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)
=\max_B\{m_D^\top\eta_B+\beta_Bp\},
\qquad p\in I_D.
\tag{3}
\]
Therefore the function is finite, continuous, convex, and piecewise affine; each slope is rational and each intercept is rational linear, hence rational affine, in \(m_D\).

The set
\[
N=\{p\in I_D:\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)\leq0\}
\]
is closed by continuity and convex by (1).  A closed convex subset of a real interval is empty, a point, or a closed interval, including the whole interval.  Its relative complement is \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\), so there are at most two relative sign boundaries.  At a boundary \(p_\partial\), continuity gives \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p_\partial;m_D)=0\); primal attainment supplies \(q_\partial\in K(p_\partial)\) with \(gq_\partial=0\).  Along a sequence on an adjacent positive component converging to \(p_\partial\), one of the finitely many pieces in (3) recurs.  Its dual pair is feasible independently of \(p\), is positive on that subsequence, and by continuity has value zero at the boundary.  This is the claimed zero certificate and adjacent positive piece.

Augment the supplied menu by \(rq=p_\partial\).  If this augmented system satisfies \(\mathsf{BASep}_D\), \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}district\text{-}fiber\text{-}projection}\) applied to the augmented row system realizes \(q_\partial\) by an original-alphabet compatible SCM saturated only at \(D\).  The assumed positivity gives \(d_Dq_\partial>0\), so division is valid and
\[
Q_\sigma^\star-Q_\rho^\star
=\frac{gq_\partial}{d_Dq_\partial}=0.
\]
All nonfocal graph-compatible restrictions are preserved by the focal construction.  The nonempty-plus-\(\mathsf{ASep}_D\) and nonempty-plus-\(\mathsf{Sep}_D\) sufficient checks follow from the hierarchy lemmas.
