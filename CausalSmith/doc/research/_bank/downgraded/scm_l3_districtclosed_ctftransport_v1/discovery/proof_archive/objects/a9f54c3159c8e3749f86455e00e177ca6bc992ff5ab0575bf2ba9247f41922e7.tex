We prove the two exact finite range equalities separately and then derive their optimization consequences.

Assume first \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and take \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\).  For every domain \(s\) and district \(D\), push the law of \(U_D^s\in\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\) forward to the finite profile coordinates \(\Omega_D\) visited by the declared menus and target cells; call it \(q_D^s\).  District factorization makes the joint profile law the product of these district laws.  Finite recursive evaluation and total probability therefore give, for every declared cell \(C\),
\[
P_{M^s}(C)
=\sum_{\omega\in\prod_D\Omega_D}
\mathbf 1\{C\text{ is true under recursive evaluation of }\omega\}
\prod_Dq_D^s(\omega_D)
=\Psi_C^s(\theta^s).
\tag{1}
\]
If \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), district-kernel invariance gives \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\).  Typed-menu realizability and (1) give
\(\Psi_{C_{a,v}}^s(\theta^s)=P_a^s(v)\) for every complete supplied cell.  Thus \(\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)\), which proves the forward saturated range inclusion simultaneously for all cells in \(\mathcal F\).

Conversely, take \(\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)\).  Because each declared alphabet is finite and nonempty, complete each \(\omega_D\in\Omega_D\) deterministically at unvisited parent configurations, obtaining an injection \(\iota_D:\Omega_D\to\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\).  Push \(q_D^s\) through \(\iota_D\), take distinct district blocks independently, and let each structural equation read its component response map.  Acyclicity gives unique recursive evaluation in \(\mathcal X\).  The unrestricted response-block clause of \(\mathrm{def:district\text{-}saturated\text{-}model\text{-}class}\) admits these laws, and using the same \(\iota_D\) across domains preserves every unchanged-district equality.  The complete-cell equations defining \(\Theta(\mathcal P)\) yield
\[
P_{M_\theta^s}^a(v)=\Psi_{C_{a,v}}^s(\theta^s)=P_a^s(v).
\]
Hence \(\mathbf M_\theta\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\), it uses the original alphabets, and it realizes every corresponding \(\Psi\)-value.  This proves the reverse saturated range inclusion.

For the arbitrary-ADMG row, take \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  Recursive compatibility permits application of \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}canonical\text{-}counterfactual\text{-}equivalence}\) in every domain to the finite joint collection containing all response-map counterfactuals used by the supplied and target cells.  The resulting graph-compatible canonical SCM uses the same observed alphabets and preserves their joint counterfactual law.  In particular it preserves every district response-profile law, so district-kernel invariance implies
\[
q_{D,\mathrm{can}}^s(\cdot;\vartheta^s)
=q_{D,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\cdot;\vartheta^\star)
\quad\text{when }D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing.
\]
Preservation of each complete supplied cell gives
\(\Phi_{C_{a,v},\mathrm{can}}^s(\vartheta^s)=P_a^s(v)\).  Thus \(\vartheta\in\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)\), and preservation of \(\mathcal F\) proves the forward canonical inclusion.

Conversely, \(\vartheta\in\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)\) consists of graph-compatible one-hot structural tables and finite canonical exogenous probability vectors.  The one-hot constraints choose exactly one value in \(\mathcal X_V\) at every structural input, the simplex constraints define probability laws, and the graph-compatible latent restrictions and product law across districts give recursive compatibility and district factorization.  Its response-law equalities give district-kernel invariance; district closure is fixed by the model-class instance; and the complete supplied-cell equations give each entire supplied law.  Hence its canonical realization lies in \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), uses the original alphabets, and realizes the displayed \(\Phi_{\mathrm{can}}\)-values.  This proves the reverse canonical inclusion.

Both parameter fibers are finite-dimensional and bounded.  The saturated fiber is a closed subset of a finite product of simplices cut out by finitely many polynomial equalities.  The graph-compatible canonical fiber is a closed subset of a finite union of products of simplices and finite one-hot sets cut out by finitely many polynomial equalities and inequalities.  Thus both are compact semialgebraic sets; nonemptiness follows from the corresponding assumed SCM fiber and forward inclusion.  Each summand in (1) contains at most one coordinate from each visited district, so \(\Psi_C^s\) is multi-affine and has total degree at most the number of visited districts.

Fix either exact range row \((\mathfrak C,\Lambda,\Xi)\) and a target cell \(T\).  The continuous target polynomial attains its minimum and maximum on the nonempty compact fiber.  Exact range equality makes these the attained sharp SCM bounds.  Since cell probabilities lie in \([0,1]\), at least one endpoint is strictly inside the logical interval exactly when the lower endpoint is positive or the upper endpoint is less than one.  Identification is equivalent to equality of the extrema.  If they differ, attaining minimizers and maximizers give original-alphabet same-menu SCMs with unequal endpoint values; no claim about intermediate values is needed.

The nonnegative evidence polynomial likewise attains its extrema.  If its maximum is zero, evidence is zero throughout the fiber.  If its minimum is zero and maximum positive, a compatible minimizing model has zero evidence, so positivity is not uniform.  In both cases a conditional functional on the whole fiber is rejected before division.  If the evidence minimum is positive, the ratio
\[
x\longmapsto
\frac{\Xi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star(x^\star)}{\Xi_E^\star(x^\star)}
\]
is continuous on the compact fiber and attains its sharp lower and upper bounds.  Exact range equality supplies endpoint SCMs, and unequal extrema supply distinct same-menu models.

For two arms the evidence denominator is common and strictly positive.  Therefore
\[
Q_\sigma^\star(\mathbf M_x)-Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M_x)
=\frac{\Xi_{B_\sigma\cap E}^\star(x^\star)
-\Xi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star(x^\star)}
{\Xi_E^\star(x^\star)}
\]
has the sign of its numerator.  A continuous numerator-gap polynomial is positive everywhere on a compact fiber exactly when its attained minimum is positive.  Its negative minimum and positive maximum are attained by two same-menu original-alphabet models with opposite rankings, and conversely such a pair forces those endpoint signs.  This proves the exact pruning claim.

Finally, the broad part of \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}district\text{-}fiber\text{-}projection}\) gives \(\mathcal Q_D(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D)\).  Under \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\), its exact part gives
\[
\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)=\mathcal K_D(m_D),
\qquad
R_D(m_D)=\operatorname{span}\{q-q':q,q'\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\}^\perp.
\tag{2}
\]
That predicate already supplies one baseline \(\mathbf M^0\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\).  Holding its nonfocal laws fixed, the probability change of an admissible complete cell under \(q_0\mapsto q\) is exactly
\[
\bar\chi_{C_{a,v}}^{s,\mathbf M^0}(q-q_0)=0,
\]
where zero follows from (2) and the defining membership of the averaged row in \(R_D(m_D)\).  This proves why baseline-averaged rather than pointwise slice membership is the exact condition for this fixed-baseline construction.  The hierarchy lemma shows that nonemptiness plus \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\), and therefore nonemptiness plus \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\), is sufficient.  Its strict example shows that the baseline-averaged condition can apply even when individual Boolean slices are outside the active row space.

The focal construction changes only the response block at \(D\) and its unchanged-source copies, retaining every nonfocal graph-compatible restriction.  The first exact global row instead saturates every district so that its full Cartesian multi-affine fiber is exact.  Without focal-district saturation and the single-baseline averaged condition, the local face is only an outer relaxation; allowing a baseline to vary with \(q\) would merely restate projection membership and is not used.  Distinct endpoint optimizers of the appropriate exact global program remain the required original-alphabet paired-model certificate.
