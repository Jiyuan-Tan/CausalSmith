Suppose a \(\mathsf{CheckedLocalArms}_D\) record is given and \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\).  For every realizable additional complete output-cell row \(r_D^\dagger\), \(I_D\) is a closed interval and \(p\mapsto\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)\) is a finite convex piecewise-affine function.  Its pieces are obtained from feasible bases of the row-augmented dual and have coefficients rational affine in \(m_D\).  Consequently \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\) is the complement in \(I_D\) of a closed convex subset, which is empty, a point, or a closed interval, and has zero, one, or two relative sign boundaries.  Every boundary has a zero-valued dual menu certificate and a primal row-face law attaining equality; every adjacent strict-pruning side has a positive dual piece.  If the augmented menu at a boundary satisfies \(\mathsf{BASep}_D\), the equality law is realized by an original-alphabet SCM saturated only at \(D\), with every nonfocal district unchanged.  Nonemptiness of the augmented focal fiber together with \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\), and hence with \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\), is sufficient for this realization.  Without the checked-local-arms record the frontier is undefined; without baseline-averaged focal projection, no causal lifting of a row-face boundary is asserted.
