Write \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(A=A_D\), \(m=m_D\), and \(Z=Z_D(m_D)\).  Let \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  By \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}district\text{-}fiber\text{-}projection}\), \(q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\in K\).  If
\[
b_D=\lambda^\top A+
\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega e_\omega,
\]
then
\[
\begin{aligned}
b_Dq_D^\star(\mathbf M)
&=\lambda^\top A q_D^\star(\mathbf M)
+\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega q_D^\star(\mathbf M)(\omega)\\
&=\lambda^\top m.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1}
\]
The equality \(Aq_D^\star=m\) follows from the broad inclusion, and every coordinate indexed by \(Z\) vanishes by \(\mathrm{def:compatible\text{-}face}\).  Thus the displayed functional identifies the causal target on the broad fiber.  All rows in the coefficient system and \(b_D\) are rational.  Rational Gaussian elimination therefore chooses \(\lambda\) and \(\zeta\) rational whenever the decomposition exists.  The entries of \(m\) are supplied cells or certified extracted kernels by \(\mathrm{def:stacked\text{-}menu\text{-}system}\), and every quotient in an extracted kernel has a certified nonzero denominator by \(\mathrm{def:typed\text{-}extraction\text{-}judgment}\).  Hence \(\lambda^\top m\in\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\); no division is introduced without a certificate.

Assume next \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  The focal projection theorem gives \(\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)=K\), and also
\[
R_D(m_D)=\operatorname{span}(K-K)^\perp.
\tag{2}
\]
The target is constant on the focal compatibility fiber if and only if \(b_D\) is constant on \(K\), by the exact projection.  Constancy on \(K\) is equivalent to
\(b_D(q-q')=0\) for all \(q,q'\in K\), which by (2) is equivalent to \(b_D\in R_D(m_D)\).  This proves necessity.

If membership fails, (2) gives \(q_+,q_-\in K\) with \(b_D(q_+-q_-)\ne0\).  Exact focal projection realizes these laws by \(\mathbf M_+,\mathbf M_-\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\) on the original alphabets.  Membership in the same compatibility fiber makes every supplied law identical, whereas
\[
b_Dq_+-b_Dq_-=b_D(q_+-q_-)\ne0
\]
gives unequal target cells.  The construction in the projection theorem changes only the focal response law, so all nonfocal districts retain the baseline graph-compatible restrictions.  The sufficient \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\) corollaries follow from \(\mathrm{lem:active\text{-}slice\text{-}implies\text{-}baseline\text{-}averaged}\) and \(\mathrm{lem:structural\text{-}separation\text{-}implies\text{-}active\text{-}slice}\), respectively.  Without exact focal projection, failure supplies only variation on the outer row face, not a pair of compatible SCMs.
