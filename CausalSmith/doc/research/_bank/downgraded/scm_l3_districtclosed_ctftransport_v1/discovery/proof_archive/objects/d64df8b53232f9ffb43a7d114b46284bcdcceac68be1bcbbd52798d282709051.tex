Write \(q=(q_{00},q_{01},q_{10},q_{11})^\top\) in the prescribed order. The equations \(A_Dq=m_D\) are
\[
q_{10}+q_{11}=\frac12,\qquad
q_{01}+q_{11}=\frac12,\qquad
q_{00}+q_{01}+q_{10}+q_{11}=1.\tag{1}
\]
Setting \(p=q_{11}\), equations (1) give
\[
q=(p,\tfrac12-p,\tfrac12-p,p)^\top,\tag{2}
\]
and nonnegativity is equivalent to \(0\leq p\leq\tfrac12\). Thus (2) is the unique parameterization of the base face. Since \(d_D=\mathbf1^\top\), the denominator is one. Direct multiplication by the two numerator rows gives
\[
Q_\rho(q(p))=q_{01}+q_{10}=1-2p,\qquad
Q_\sigma(q(p))=q_{01}+q_{11}=\frac12.\tag{3}
\]
The acquired row selects \(q_{11}=p\), and
\[
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q(p)=q_{11}-q_{10}=2p-\frac12.\tag{4}
\]
Equations (2)--(4) prove
\[
I_D=[0,\tfrac12],\qquad
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)=2p-\frac12,\qquad
\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+=(\tfrac14,\tfrac12].\tag{5}
\]

A_{D,\mathrm{aug}}^\top(0,-1,0,2)^\top

The dual value is \(2p-\tfrac12\), and at \(p=\tfrac38\) it is \(\tfrac14\).
