Fix \(s\in\mathcal S\), \(a\in\mathcal A_s\), and \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), and suppose the two displayed judgments succeed.  Let \(A_{h,c}\) and \(H_h^{\mathrm{ev}}\) be the reconstructed numerator and context events.  By \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus\text{-}soundness}\), their expression derivations give
\[
N_{s,a,D,h,c}=P_{M^s}(A_{h,c}),
\qquad H_{s,a,D,h}=P_{M^s}(H_h^{\mathrm{ev}}).
\tag{1}
\]
The two accepting support-local traces construct rows satisfying, for every complete nonfocal response profile,
\[
\mathbf 1\{A_{h,c}(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\}
=u_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D),
\quad
\mathbf 1\{H_h^{\mathrm{ev}}(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\}
=v_{s,a,D,h}(\omega_D).
\tag{2}
\]
Recursive compatibility makes these events deterministic after the complete response maps are fixed.  Finite total probability, followed by summation over the nonfocal probability law in (2), therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
N_{s,a,D,h,c}
&=\sum_{\omega_D}u_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D)q_D^s(\omega_D)
=u_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s,\\
H_{s,a,D,h}
&=\sum_{\omega_D}v_{s,a,D,h}(\omega_D)q_D^s(\omega_D)
=v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
The independent certificate proves \(H_{s,a,D,h}>0\), so conditionalization and (3) yield
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=\frac{N_{s,a,D,h,c}}{H_{s,a,D,h}}
=\frac{u_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s}
{v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s}.
\tag{4}
\]
If \(s=\star\), the law is already \(q_D^\star\).  If \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), district-kernel invariance gives \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\).  These are exactly the admissible cases, so (3) gives the two target-law equalities retained by the stack.  Equation (4) is not linear and is not retained.

Finally, if \(\mathsf{Local}_D(C)\Downarrow b_D\), support-calculus soundness says that the recursive indicator of \(C\) equals \(b_D(\omega_D)\) for every nonfocal profile.  Total probability in the target domain gives
\[
P_{M^\star}(C)=\sum_{\omega_D}b_D(\omega_D)q_D^\star(\omega_D)
=b_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star.
\]
Taking \(C=B_\rho\cap E\) or \(C=E\) proves the claimed specializations.  If locality is rejected, the returned nonfocal pair shows why no such single-district row equation exists; failed judgments assert nothing.
