Put \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\).  The normalization row of \(A_D\) gives \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\) on \(K\).  Thus the assumed nonempty set \(K\) is a closed subset of the finite simplex and is compact; the primal minimum and maximum are attained.

Apply finite linear-programming strong duality to the variables \(q\), equality constraint \(A_Dq=m_D\), nonnegativity constraint \(q\geq0\), and cost column \(g^\top\).  The equality multiplier \(\eta\) is unrestricted, and nonnegative primal variables give \(A_D^\top\eta\leq g^\top\).  Therefore
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
=\min_{q\in K}gq
=\max_{\eta:A_D^\top\eta\leq g^\top}m_D^\top\eta.
\]
For feasible \(q\) and \(\eta\), weak duality is also immediate:
\[
m_D^\top\eta=q^\top A_D^\top\eta\leq gq.
\]

For every model \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), the broad projection theorem gives \(q=q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\in K\).  If \(m_D^\top\eta>0\), weak duality gives \(gq>0\).  Since \(d_Dq>0\), district-cell linearity yields
\[
Q_\sigma^\star(\mathbf M)-Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M)
=\frac{n_{\sigma,D}q-n_{\rho,D}q}{d_Dq}
=\frac{gq}{d_Dq}>0.
\]
Thus every positive dual certificate is sound on the broad fiber.

Now additionally assume \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  Exact saturated projection gives \(\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)=K\).  Uniform strict dominance on that fiber is therefore equivalent, by the positive-denominator display, to \(gq>0\) for every \(q\in K\).  Compactness makes this equivalent to \(\min_{q\in K}gq>0\), and dual attainment makes it equivalent to a feasible \(\eta\) with \(m_D^\top\eta>0\).

If \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\), an attained minimizer \(q_-\) has \(gq_-<0\).  Exact saturated projection realizes it by an original-alphabet family in \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\), and the common positive denominator gives \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\).  If also \(\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)>0\), an attained maximizer \(q_+\) is realized in the same way and has the opposite strict ranking.  Both families induce the supplied menu because they belong to the same compatibility fiber.  Without exact projection, only the positive-certificate conclusion survives: a row-face minimizer need not be a compatible SCM law.
