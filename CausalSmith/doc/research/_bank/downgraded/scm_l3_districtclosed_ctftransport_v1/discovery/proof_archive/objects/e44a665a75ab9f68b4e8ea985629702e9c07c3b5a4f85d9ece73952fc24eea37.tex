Fix \(s,a,D,h,c\) and an accepted row-eligible record.  By \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus\text{-}soundness}\),
\[
N_{s,a,D,h,c}=P_{M^s}(A_{h,c}),\qquad
H_{s,a,D,h}=P_{M^s}(H_h).
\tag{1}
\]
The two exhaustive support-local traces give, for every complete nonfocal profile,
\[
\mathbf1_{A_{h,c}}(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})=u_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D),\qquad
\mathbf1_{H_h}(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})=v_{s,a,D,h}(\omega_D).
\tag{2}
\]
Recursive compatibility makes both indicators deterministic after the complete response maps are fixed.  Finite total probability and (2) therefore imply
\[
N_{s,a,D,h,c}=\sum_{\omega_D}u_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D)q_D^s(\omega_D),\qquad
H_{s,a,D,h}=\sum_{\omega_D}v_{s,a,D,h}(\omega_D)q_D^s(\omega_D).
\tag{3}
\]
The independently checked certificate gives \(H_{s,a,D,h}>0\); hence the definition of the extracted conditional and (3) give the asserted ratio.  If \(s=\star\), no transport is required.  If \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), district-kernel invariance gives \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\).  These are precisely the admitted source cases.

If \(\mathsf{Local}_D(C)\) accepts, the same exhaustive identity as (2), now for \(C\), and target-domain total probability give
\[
P_{M^\star}(C)=\sum_{\omega_D}b_D(\omega_D)q_D^\star(\omega_D)=b_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star.
\tag{4}
\]
Taking \(C=B_\rho\cap E\) or \(C=E\) proves the special cases only when their judgments exist.  Rejection yields no row by construction, so no unproved conditional or nonlocal equation has been used.
