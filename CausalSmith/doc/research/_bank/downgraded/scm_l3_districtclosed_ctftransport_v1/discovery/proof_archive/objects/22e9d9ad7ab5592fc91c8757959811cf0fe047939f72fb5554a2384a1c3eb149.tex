Fix \(s\in\mathcal S\), \(a\in\mathcal A_s\), and \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), and suppose
\[
\operatorname{Extract}_{s,a,D}(P_a^s)\Downarrow(K_{s,a,D},\kappa).
\]
For \(h\in H_{s,a,D}\) and \(c\in C_{s,a,D}\), let
\[
S(h,c)=\{\omega_D\in\Omega_D:\omega_D\text{ produces \(c\) at context \(h\) under \(a\)}\}.
\]
By the incidence-row definition,
\[
r_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D)=\mathbf 1\{\omega_D\in S(h,c)\}.
\]
The successful extraction judgment certifies every consistency, unnesting, fixing, marginalization, context lookup, and nonzero quotient used to form the kernel. Typed-menu realizability identifies the supplied law with the regime law of \(M^s\); recursive compatibility makes the response profile deterministic conditional on \(\omega_D\). Therefore finite total probability gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)
&=P_{M^s}\{\omega_D\in S(h,c)\}\\
&=\sum_{\omega_D\in\Omega_D}
  \mathbf 1\{\omega_D\in S(h,c)\}q_D^s(\omega_D)\\
&=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^s.
\end{aligned}
\]
Every quotient in the first equality is legal because its nonzero premise is part of \(\kappa\).

If \(s=\star\), then \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\) by notation. If \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), district-kernel invariance says that the joint structural-response law on \(D\) in domain \(s\) equals that in the target domain, hence again \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\). Only after one of these two admissibility facts has been proved may one conclude
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^\star.
\]

Finally, the target-row definition evaluates the deterministic indicators of \(B_\rho\cap E\), \(E\), and a generic district-local target cell on the same finite carrier. Thus
\[
P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E)=n_{\rho,D}q_D^\star,
\qquad
P_{M^\star}(E)=d_Dq_D^\star,
\qquad
P_{M^\star}(\text{target cell})=b_Dq_D^\star.
\]
By the extraction and stacking definitions, a failed judgment has neither a certified kernel cell nor a certified incidence equality and consequently contributes no derived row.
