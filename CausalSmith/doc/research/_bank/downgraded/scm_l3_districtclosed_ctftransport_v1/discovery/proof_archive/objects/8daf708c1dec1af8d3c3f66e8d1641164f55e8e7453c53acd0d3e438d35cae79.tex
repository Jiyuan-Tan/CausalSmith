Suppose the reduced arm comparison has one district \(D\), its numerator and evidence locality judgments accept, and \(d_D^{\mathsf T}q>0\) for every \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). If there exists \(\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) with \(A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) and \(m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}>0\), removing \(\rho\) cannot remove a maximizing arm from \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\); an arm whose common evidence is certified zero is excluded as undefined rather than ranked.
