For \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), the predicate \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds when every complete supplied cell \(C_{a,v}\) in a domain satisfying \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\) has, under recursive evaluation on \(\prod_{D'\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_{D'}\), an indicator that is either independent of \(\omega_D\), or depends only on \(\omega_D\).  In the second case its raw row \(r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v}\) and value \(P_a^s(v)\) must occur in \((A_D,m_D)\).  This is a finite syntactic and tabular condition; it excludes an unrecorded cross-district polynomial constraint involving \(q_D^\star\).
