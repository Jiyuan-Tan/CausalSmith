There are two exact finite frontiers with different scopes.

First, assume \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\).  Then \(\Theta(\mathcal P)\) is a nonempty compact semialgebraic set and, for every finite collection \(\mathcal F\) of target-domain counterfactual cells,
\[
\{(P_{M^\star}(C):C\in\mathcal F):\mathbf M\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\}
=
\{(\Psi_C^\star(\theta^\star):C\in\mathcal F):\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)\}.
\]
Every \(\Psi_C^s\) is multi-affine in the district response-law blocks and has total degree at most the number of districts visited by \(C\).  Every point of the right-hand range is realized by a district-saturated SCM family on the original observed alphabets \(\mathcal X\).

Second, for an arbitrary acyclic directed mixed graph, if \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\), then \(\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)\) is a nonempty compact semialgebraic set and
\[
\{(P_{M^\star}(C):C\in\mathcal F):\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\}
=
\{(\Phi_{C,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\vartheta^\star):C\in\mathcal F):
\vartheta\in\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)\}.
\]
The canonical realization also uses the original observed alphabets and retains the internal latent-factor restrictions of \(G\).

For either exact row
\[
(\mathfrak C,\Lambda,\Xi)
\in
\{(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P),\Theta(\mathcal P),\Psi),
(\mathcal C(\mathcal P),\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P),\Phi_{\mathrm{can}})\},
\]
provided \(\mathfrak C\ne\varnothing\), the sharp target-cell range is the attained interval
\[
\left[
\min_{x\in\Lambda}\Xi_T^\star(x^\star),
\max_{x\in\Lambda}\Xi_T^\star(x^\star)
\right].
\]
It is strictly inside \([0,1]\) exactly when the lower endpoint is positive or the upper endpoint is less than one, and it is a singleton exactly when the cell is identified.  Distinct minimizers and maximizers give original-alphabet same-menu SCM families attaining the endpoints.  These are finite semialgebraic characterizations; effective exact decision is claimed only when the menu probabilities have an effective rational or algebraic encoding.

For the same row let
\[
e_-(\Lambda)=\min_{x\in\Lambda}\Xi_E^\star(x^\star),
\qquad
e_+(\Lambda)=\max_{x\in\Lambda}\Xi_E^\star(x^\star).
\]
If \(e_+(\Lambda)=0\), evidence is zero throughout \(\mathfrak C\); if \(e_-(\Lambda)=0<e_+(\Lambda)\), positivity is not uniform.  In either case a conditional functional on all of \(\mathfrak C\) is rejected before division.  If \(e_-(\Lambda)>0\), the sharp conditional range is the attained interval
\[
\left[
\min_{x\in\Lambda}
\frac{\Xi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star(x^\star)}{\Xi_E^\star(x^\star)},
\max_{x\in\Lambda}
\frac{\Xi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star(x^\star)}{\Xi_E^\star(x^\star)}
\right].
\]
Under this positivity condition, \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) throughout \(\mathfrak C\) if and only if
\[
\min_{x\in\Lambda}
\{\Xi_{B_\sigma\cap E}^\star(x^\star)-\Xi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star(x^\star)\}>0,
\]
and opposite strict rankings occur in two same-menu original-alphabet models if and only if the minimum of the displayed gap is negative and its maximum is positive.  Thus this strict minimum is the exact safe-pruning certificate for the chosen fiber.

Finally, for every district \(D\),
\[
\mathcal Q_D(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\]
For a sparse nonsaturated graph or a cross-district-coupled menu, \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) is only an outer relaxation.  Equality for the saturated fiber follows only under \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) by the one-district fiber-projection theorem.  Consequently a failed local face test is not a global nonidentification certificate outside that theorem's scope; distinct optimizers of the appropriate exact global program are the paired-model certificate.
