Under the finite semi-Markovian convention, the restricted carriers in the definitions admit proper typed embeddings
\[
\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{CTFTR}}\hookrightarrow\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{DCTFTR}},
\qquad
\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{CTFIDU+}}\hookrightarrow\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{DCTFTR}}.
\]
The first embedding covers only observational and atomic hard-interventional descriptors representable in the DCTFTR grammar. The second covers only one-domain Layer-3 actions equipped with the declared constructor map into the DCTFTR partial-copy grammar and sets \(\mathcal S=\{\star\}\). On either image, every successful DCTFTR functional and every successful functional returned by the corresponding published calculus agree semantically with the same target query; no syntactic or transition-by-transition equivalence is asserted. The instance \(\mathfrak I_{\mathrm{bin}}\) lies outside both source carriers: its genuine cross-world cell \(p\) is outside the first restricted grammar, while \(P^\star(Z=1)=0\) and \(P^s(Z=1)=1\) cannot arise from one SCM. No embedding of the excluded CTFTR conditional or stochastic-policy constructors, or of CTFIDU+ actions lacking a declared DCTFTR constructor, is claimed.
