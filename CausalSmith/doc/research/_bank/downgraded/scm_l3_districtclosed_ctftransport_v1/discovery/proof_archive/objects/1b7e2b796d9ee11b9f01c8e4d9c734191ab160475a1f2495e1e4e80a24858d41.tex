Fix canonical finite orders on vertices, regimes, original districts of \(G\), assignments, complete response profiles, and generated work-DAG identifiers.  The event and expression grammars are
\[
\mathsf{Atom}::=[s]V_\alpha=v,
\qquad
\mathsf{Event}::=\top\mid\bot\mid\mathsf{Atom}\mid
\mathsf{Event}\wedge\mathsf{Event}\mid\mathsf{Event}\vee\mathsf{Event},
\]
and
\[
\mathsf{Expr}::=\operatorname{Cell}(s,a,u)\mid\operatorname{Rat}(r)
\mid\sum_{j\in J}\mathsf{Expr}_j
\mid\prod_{j\in J}\mathsf{Expr}_j
\mid\operatorname{Quot}(F,G,\nu_G),
\]
where normalized events are canonical disjoint normal forms of unnested atoms, \(r\in\mathbb Q\), and \(\nu_G\) is an independently checked nonzero certificate.  The phase-indexed node grammar is
\[
\begin{split}
\mathsf{Goal}::={}&\operatorname{Raw}(\tau,e)
\mid\operatorname{Normed}(\tau,e,\nu)
\mid\operatorname{AncClosed}(\tau,H,e,\alpha)
\mid\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,b)\\
&\mid\operatorname{NeedFace}(\tau,D,b,A,m,Z,\kappa)
\mid\operatorname{Sum}(J,\mathrm{children})
\mid\operatorname{Prod}(\mathrm{children})
\mid\operatorname{Resolved}(F,\delta),
\end{split}
\]
with \(\tau\in\{\mathrm{num},\mathrm{den}\}\).  A configuration is a finite well-typed acyclic work DAG with numerator and denominator roots and a checked trace.  For every \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), the first three phases denote their target event probabilities, a row or face node denotes \(bq_D^\star(\mathbf M)\), a resolved node denotes \(F(\mathcal P)\), and sum and product nodes have their usual finite denotations.

Checked normalization and ancestor derivations use consistency, unnesting, contradiction removal, disjoint additivity, and finite marginalization.  A checked split derivation never factors districts newly created by restricting \(G\).  It uses the original partition \(\mathcal D(G)\) and verifies the exact finite identity
\[
P_{M^\star}(e)
=\sum_{\omega\in\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_D}
\mathbf 1\{e(\omega)=1\}
\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^\star(\omega_D).
\]
Equivalently, each satisfying profile tuple contributes a product of original-district coordinate leaves \(\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,e_{\omega_D})\).  Extraction derivations retain raw district-local cells and, from a successful conditional extraction, only the separately certified numerator and context equalities carried by \(\operatorname{ROW\_ELIGIBLE}\); a quotient is never treated as a linear row.  Face certificates are exact rational row-space, linear-programming, or Farkas certificates.  Zero, nonzero, and strict-positivity certificates are checked by exact ring normalization together with Farkas certificates for linear faces or displayed sum-of-squares preordering identities for finite response-law constraints; no certificate constructor is merely the proposition being certified.

The fast-path terminals remain
\[
\mathsf{Result}::=\operatorname{ID}(F,\delta)
\mid\operatorname{ZERO\_EVIDENCE}(F_E,z)
\mid\operatorname{FAIL}(t).
\]
Failure constructors include split or extraction undefinedness, row ineligibility, \(\operatorname{FACE\_NOT\_IDENTIFIED}(D,b,A,m,Z,w_{\mathrm{local}},t)\), no admissible factor, a zero-denominator witness, uncertified positivity, and a typed stuck trace.  A local face witness certifies variation only in \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), not global compatible-model variation.
