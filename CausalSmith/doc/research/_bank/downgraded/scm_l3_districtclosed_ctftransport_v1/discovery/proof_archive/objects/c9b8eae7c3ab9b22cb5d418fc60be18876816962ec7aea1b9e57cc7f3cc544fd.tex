Fix a domain and a model family \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), and fix an accepted trace.  Write
\[
\Omega=\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_D,
\qquad
\mu^s(\omega)=\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^s(\omega_D).
\]
Every factor is a probability mass function on a finite nonempty set by \(\mathrm{def:district\text{-}response\text{-}profile}\).  The equality defining \(\mu^s\) is the product law of the original district response blocks, by \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}factorization}\).  Acyclic recursive evaluation, supplied by \(\mathrm{ass:recursive\text{-}compatibility}\), assigns to each finite event expression \(A\) a total indicator \(I_A:\Omega\to\{0,1\}\).  Hence finite total probability gives
\[
P_{M^s}(A)=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}I_A(\omega)\mu^s(\omega).
\tag{1}
\]
This also covers multiworld events: a single complete response-profile tuple fixes every structural response at every intervention appearing in the event, and acyclicity makes the recursive substitution unique.

We prove soundness simultaneously for event traces and their scalar expressions by structural induction over exactly the constructors in \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\).  For \(\mathsf{ZERO}\) and \(\mathsf{ONE}\), equation (1) is respectively the empty sum and \(\sum_{\omega}\mu^s(\omega)=1\).  For \(\mathsf{CELL}\), membership of \(\mathbf M\) in the fiber defined by \(\mathrm{def:compatibility\text{-}fiber}\) gives
\[
\llbracket\operatorname{CellVal}(s,a,v)\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}
=P_a^s(v)
=P_{M^s}(C_{a,v}),
\tag{2}
\]
where the final equality is typed-menu realizability built into that fiber.

For \(\mathsf{DISJOINT\_SUM}\), the checked truth table certifies \(A=\biguplus_{i\in I}A_i\), with \(I\) finite.  Thus, using the induction hypotheses and finite additivity,
\[
P_{M^s}(A)
=\sum_{i\in I}P_{M^s}(A_i)
=\sum_{i\in I}\llbracket F_i\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}
=\left\llbracket\operatorname{Add}_{i\in I}F_i\right\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}.
\tag{3}
\]
The \(\mathsf{MARGINALIZE}\) case is (3) applied to the complete-alphabet partition
\[
A=\biguplus_{x\in\mathcal X_X}\bigl(A\cap\{X=x\}\bigr);
\tag{4}
\]
the index set is finite because every declared alphabet is finite.  For \(\mathsf{COMPLEMENT}\), the premise checker establishes \(A\subseteq H\) pointwise on \(\Omega\).  Therefore
\[
P_{M^s}(H\setminus A)
=P_{M^s}(H)-P_{M^s}(A)
=\llbracket F_H-F_A\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}.
\tag{5}
\]

For \(\mathsf{PRODUCT}\), let its registry record assign the factors \(A_j\) to pairwise disjoint sets \(\mathcal E_j\subseteq\mathcal D(G)\) of original districts and verify from their complete truth tables that \(I_{A_j}\) depends only on \((\omega_D:D\in\mathcal E_j)\).  Mutual independence of the original district blocks, from \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}factorization}\), then yields
\[
P_{M^s}\!\left(\bigcap_j A_j\right)
=\prod_jP_{M^s}(A_j)
=\prod_j\llbracket F_j\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}
=\left\llbracket\operatorname{Mul}_jF_j\right\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}.
\tag{6}
\]
No product identity lacking that checked original-district partition is accepted.

For \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\), the trace separately contains derivations of a numerator event \(N\subseteq H\) and its context \(H\), as well as a certificate whose checked conclusion is \(P_{M^s}(H)>0\).  By the induction hypotheses,
\[
\left\llbracket\operatorname{Quot}(F_N,F_H,\pi_H)\right\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}
=\frac{\llbracket F_N\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}}{\llbracket F_H\rrbracket_{\mathcal P}}
=\frac{P_{M^s}(N)}{P_{M^s}(H)}
=P_{M^s}(N\mid H).
\tag{7}
\]
Thus the only division in the induction is legal.  In particular, a zero or uncertified denominator has no \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) constructor.

For \(\mathsf{REGISTRY}\), every Boolean registry entry is checked by evaluating both event trees at every \(\omega\in\Omega\).  Equality of these finite truth tables gives equality of their indicators and hence, by (1), equality of probabilities.  This proves directly the registered consistency, recursive-substitution, unnesting, contradiction, sure-event, Boolean-normalization, complete-alphabet marginalization, and checked-context complement identities.  The sole non-Boolean registry rule is the product rule already proved in (6).  For \(\mathsf{SUBST}\), the induction hypotheses give equality for every substituted child, and the displayed finite sum, difference, product, or quotient evaluator preserves equality; quotient substitution again requires its already checked positive denominator.  The \(\mathsf{TRANS}\) case follows from transitivity of equality.  These cases exhaust the trace grammar.

It remains to verify that the final algebraic checker cannot introduce an unsound equality.  Let \(g_1,\ldots,g_k\) be all denominator expressions in the two expressions compared by the checker, and let \(m_j\) be the largest power needed to clear occurrences of \(g_j\).  Its side-condition records give
\[
g_j(\mathbf M)>0\quad(1\le j\le k),
\qquad
\Delta(\mathbf M):=\prod_{j=1}^kg_j(\mathbf M)^{m_j}>0.
\tag{8}
\]
After recursive expansion using the displayed evaluators, the checker normalizes both cleared expressions in one fixed monomial order and compares every coefficient exactly.  Acceptance therefore means an identity in the ordinary polynomial ring,
\[
\operatorname{NF}\!\left(\Delta L\right)
=\operatorname{NF}\!\left(\Delta R\right),
\tag{9}
\]
not a numerical comparison at one model.  Polynomial identities remain identities after substituting the response-law coordinates and the supplied cell values induced by \(\mathbf M\).  Equations (8)--(9) consequently give
\[
0=\Delta(\mathbf M)\bigl(L(\mathbf M)-R(\mathbf M)\bigr),
\]
and division by the strictly positive \(\Delta(\mathbf M)\) yields \(L(\mathbf M)=R(\mathbf M)\).  Since \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) and the accepted trace were arbitrary, every accepted finite fixing trace preserves its represented probability throughout the compatibility fiber.
