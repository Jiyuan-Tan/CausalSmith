Write \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(r=r_D^\dagger\), and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\).  Since \(K\) is a nonempty compact convex polytope and \(q\mapsto rq\) is linear, its image
\[
I_D=\{rq:q\in K\}
\]
is a nonempty compact convex subset of \(\mathbb R\), hence a closed interval.  For each \(p\in I_D\), the fiber \(K(p)=\{q\in K:rq=p\}\) is nonempty and compact, so the defining minimum is attained.

For \(p_0,p_1\in I_D\), choose minimizers \(q_0,q_1\).  For \(0\leq t\leq1\), the law \(tq_0+(1-t)q_1\) lies in \(K(tp_0+(1-t)p_1)\); hence
\[
\Gamma(tp_0+(1-t)p_1;m_D)
\leq t\Gamma(p_0;m_D)+(1-t)\Gamma(p_1;m_D).
\]
Thus the value function is convex.

The row-augmented primal has equality matrix \(\widetilde A=(A_D;r)\) and right side \((m_D;p)\).  Applying the primal--dual equality gives
\[
\Gamma(p;m_D)
=\max_{(\eta,\beta):A_D^\top\eta+\beta r^\top\leq g^\top}
\{m_D^\top\eta+\beta p\}.
\]
The feasible polyhedron is independent of \((m_D,p)\) and has finitely many rational inequalities.  After quotienting its lineality space, every relevant basic solution is obtained from a nonsingular rational subsystem and is therefore rational; only finitely many bases exist.  Where a basis \(B\) is optimal the value is
\[
m_D^\top\eta_B+\beta_Bp,
\]
whose coefficients are rational and whose intercept is a rational linear, hence rational affine, function of \(m_D\).  The maximum of the finite relevant list is a finite continuous convex piecewise-affine function.  Degenerate bases may repeat pieces but add none.

The sublevel set
\[
\{p\in I_D:\Gamma(p;m_D)\leq0\}
\]
of a continuous convex function is closed and convex; in a real interval it is empty, a point, or a closed interval.  Its relative complement is \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\), which therefore has zero, one, or two sign boundaries.  At a boundary \(p_0\), continuity gives \(\Gamma(p_0;m_D)=0\).  Dual attainment supplies a row-menu weighting of value zero at \(p_0\), and its affine piece is a lower certificate on the adjacent strict-pruning region.  Primal attainment supplies \(q_0\in K(p_0)\) with \(gq_0=0\).

If the augmented menu satisfies \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\), the corrected projection theorem realizes every such \(q_0\) by an original-alphabet saturated compatible SCM.  Since \(d_Dq_0>0\), its two conditional arm values are equal.  Without those two hypotheses, primal attainment establishes only a boundary point of the row-face relaxation.
