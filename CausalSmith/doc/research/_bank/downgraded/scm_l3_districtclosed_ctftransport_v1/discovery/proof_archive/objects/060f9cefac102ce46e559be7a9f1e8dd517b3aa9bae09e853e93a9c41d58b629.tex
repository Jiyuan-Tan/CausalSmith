For a realizable additional Layer-3 output-cell row \(r_D^\dagger\in\{0,1\}^{\Omega_D}\), define
\[
I_D=\{(r_D^\dagger)^{\mathsf T}q:q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\},
\]
and, for \(p\in I_D\),
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)=
\min_{\substack{q\geq0,\ A_Dq=m_D\\(r_D^\dagger)^{\mathsf T}q=p}}
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q,
\qquad
\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+=
\{p\in I_D:\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)>0\}.
\]
The augmented menu includes the complete binary output law, hence both the row and its normalization-dependent complement; no other coordinate of an underlying returned tuple is silently supplied.
