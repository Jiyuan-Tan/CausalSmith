The algebra \(\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\) is generated by rational constants and the directly supplied complete output-cell probabilities \(P_a^s(y)\), including deterministic-pushforward output cells, under finite sums, differences, products, and marginalization sums.  It contains a quotient \(F/H\) only together with an independently checked certificate that \(H\ne0\) on the relevant compatibility fiber; a conditional causal output requires the stronger stored certificate \(H>0\) throughout that fiber.  A quantifier-free semialgebraic range or endpoint description returned by the canonical quantifier-elimination fallback is represented by \(\mathsf{HybridResult}\), not declared to belong to \(\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\).
