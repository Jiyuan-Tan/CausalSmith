\(\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\) is the typed algebra generated by rational constants and supplied complete-menu cells under finite sums, differences, products, marginalization sums, and quotients carrying independently checked nonzero certificates on the relevant compatibility fiber.  Every member is therefore a rational function of the supplied cell probabilities on its certified domain.  A real-algebraic number, quantifier-free semialgebraic description, isolated endpoint, or canonical parameter sample returned by the exact CAD fallback is represented instead by a \(\mathsf{HybridResult}\) certificate; it is not declared to be a member of \(\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\) merely because its coordinates are algebraic.
