For a target-domain event \(C\), the partial judgment
\[
\mathsf{Local}_D(C)\Downarrow b_D
\]
exists exactly when the total checker \(\operatorname{SupportLocal}_D(C)\) accepts and constructs \(b_D=\operatorname{row}_D(C)\in\{0,1\}^{\Omega_D}\).  Only under displayed judgments
\[
\mathsf{Local}_D(B_\rho\cap E)\Downarrow n_{\rho,D},
\qquad
\mathsf{Local}_D(B_\sigma\cap E)\Downarrow n_{\sigma,D},
\qquad
\mathsf{Local}_D(E)\Downarrow d_D
\]
are the common-evidence arm rows and \(g_{\rho,\sigma,D}=n_{\sigma,D}-n_{\rho,D}\) defined.  A rejected checker returns a nonfocal witness and creates no local target row; that event remains represented only by its exact multi-district polynomial in the global fibers.
