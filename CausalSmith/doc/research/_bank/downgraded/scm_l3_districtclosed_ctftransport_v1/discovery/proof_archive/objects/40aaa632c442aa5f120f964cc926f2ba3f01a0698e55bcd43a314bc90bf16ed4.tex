Fix \(s\in\mathcal S\), \(a\in\mathcal A_s\), and \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), and assume
\(
\operatorname{Extract}_{s,a,D}(P_a^s)\Downarrow(K_{s,a,D},\kappa)
\).
For fixed \(h\in H_{s,a,D}\) and \(c\in C_{s,a,D}\), let \(S(h,c)\subseteq\Omega_D\) be the profiles producing cell \(c\) at context \(h\).  The incidence-row definition gives
\[
r_{s,a,D,h,c}(\omega_D)=\mathbf 1\{\omega_D\in S(h,c)\}.
\]
A successful extraction certificate uses only consistency, unnesting, fixing at the declared context, and finite marginalization, and it carries a nonzero certificate for every quotient.  Typed-menu realizability therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)
&=P_{M^s}\{\omega_D\in S(h,c)\}\\
&=\sum_{\omega_D\in\Omega_D}
\mathbf 1\{\omega_D\in S(h,c)\}q_D^s(\omega_D)\\
&=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^s.
\end{aligned}
\]
If \(s=\star\), then \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\) by notation.  If \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), district-kernel invariance gives equality of the joint structural-response laws on \(D\), hence again \(q_D^s=q_D^\star\).  Thus, and only after one of these admissibility facts is checked,
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^\star.
\]
Finally, the target-row definition applies the same finite indicator evaluation to \(B_\rho\cap E\), \(E\), and a generic district-local target cell, so
\[
P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\cap E)=n_{\rho,D}q_D^\star,
\qquad
P_{M^\star}(E)=d_Dq_D^\star,
\qquad
P_{M^\star}(\text{target cell})=b_Dq_D^\star.
\]
By the extraction and stacking definitions, a failed judgment has neither a certified kernel cell nor an incidence equality and therefore creates no derived row.
