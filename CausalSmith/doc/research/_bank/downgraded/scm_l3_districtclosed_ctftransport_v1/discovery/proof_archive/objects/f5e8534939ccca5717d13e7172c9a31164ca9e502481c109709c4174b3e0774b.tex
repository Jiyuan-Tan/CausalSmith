For a target event \(C\), a district row at \(D\) is formed only after a checked recursive evaluation proves that its indicator depends on \(\omega_D\) and on no response profile outside \(D\).  Under that certificate, evaluation on \(\Omega_D\) gives \(b_D\), and the special cases \(C=B_\rho\cap E\), \(C=B_\sigma\cap E\), and \(C=E\) give \(n_{\rho,D}\), \(n_{\sigma,D}\), and \(d_D\); when both numerator rows exist, set \(g_{\rho,\sigma,D}=n_{\sigma,D}-n_{\rho,D}\).  If a whole numerator, evidence event, or target cell visits more than one original district of \(G\), none of these single-district rows is formed for that whole event.  It is handled instead by the exact original-district sum--product expansion or by \(\Psi\) and \(\Phi_{\mathrm{can}}\).
