Fix canonical finite orders on vertices, domains, descriptors, complete assignments, original districts, response profiles, and work-DAG identifiers.  Events and rational expressions use the finite constructors of \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\).  The phase-indexed local nodes are
\[
\begin{split}
\mathsf{Goal}::={}&\operatorname{Raw}(\tau,e)
\mid\operatorname{Normed}(\tau,e,\nu)
\mid\operatorname{AncClosed}(\tau,H,e,\alpha)\\
&\mid\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,b,\ell)
\mid\operatorname{NeedFace}(\tau,D,b,\ell,A,m,Z,\kappa)\\
&\mid\operatorname{Sum}(J,\mathrm{children})
\mid\operatorname{Prod}(\mathrm{children})
\mid\operatorname{Resolved}(F,\delta),
\end{split}
\]
where \(\tau\in\{\mathrm{num},\mathrm{den}\}\) and every \(b\) is accompanied by an accepted \(\mathsf{Local}_D\) trace \(\ell\).  A configuration is a finite well-typed acyclic work DAG with numerator and denominator roots and a checked transition trace.  Raw, normalized, and ancestor-closed nodes denote their target event probabilities; local nodes denote \(b^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\); resolved nodes denote \(F(\mathcal P)\); sums and products have their ordinary finite denotations.

Normalization and ancestor constructors carry exhaustive truth tables and complete-alphabet partitions.  A split constructor enumerates the original districts \(\mathcal D(G)\), every full response-profile tuple, and the exact expansion
\[
P_{M^\star}(e)=\sum_{\omega\in\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_D}
\mathbf1_e(\omega)\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^\star(\omega_D);
\]
it never factors districts newly created by restricting a graph.  Extraction nodes carry only admissibility proofs, accepted raw support-local traces, accepted \(\mathsf{RowEligible}\) records, and a literal certificate for each separately stacked equation.  Face certificates are exact row-reduction or Farkas certificates.  Quotients carry independent strict-positivity certificates.

The terminal grammar remains
\[
\mathsf{Result}::=\operatorname{ID}(F,\delta)\mid
\operatorname{ZERO\_EVIDENCE}(F_E,z)\mid\operatorname{FAIL}(t).
\]
Failure records include ill-typed or nonlocal split leaves, undefined extraction, local face nonidentification, no admissible factor, a zero-evidence witness, uncertified evidence positivity, and an explicit phase error.  No failure record is itself a global paired-model certificate.  The lexicographic rank has the eight coordinates \((r_N,r_A,r_S,r_X,r_F,r_\Sigma,r_\Pi,r_C)\) of the frozen grammar.
