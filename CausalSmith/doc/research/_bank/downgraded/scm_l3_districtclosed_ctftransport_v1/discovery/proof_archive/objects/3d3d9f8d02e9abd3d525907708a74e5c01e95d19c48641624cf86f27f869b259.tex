Write \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(r=r_D^\dagger\), and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\).  The normalization row makes \(K\) a nonempty compact convex polytope in the finite probability simplex.  Its image under the linear map \(q\mapsto rq\) is therefore a nonempty compact convex subset of \(\mathbb R\), hence a closed interval \(I_D\).  For every \(p\in I_D\),
\[
K(p)=\{q\in K:rq=p\}
\]
is nonempty and compact, so the defining minimum of \(gq\) is attained.

Let \(p_0,p_1\in I_D\), choose minimizers \(q_i\in K(p_i)\), and let \(0\leq t\leq1\).  Then \(tq_0+(1-t)q_1\in K(tp_0+(1-t)p_1)\), whence
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(tp_0+(1-t)p_1;m_D)
\leq t\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p_0;m_D)
+(1-t)\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p_1;m_D).
\]
Thus the value function is convex.

Append \(r\) to \(A_D\) and apply \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}linear\text{-}programming\text{-}strong\text{-}duality}\) to the nonempty bounded fiber \(K(p)\).  It gives
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)
=\max_{(\eta,\beta):A_D^\top\eta+\beta r^\top\leq g^\top}
\{m_D^\top\eta+\beta p\}.
\tag{3}
\]
The dual feasible polyhedron is independent of \((m_D,p)\) and is cut out by finitely many rational inequalities, because \(A_D\), \(r\), and \(g\) have integer entries.  Let
\[
L_0=\{(u,t):A_D^\top u+t r^\top=0\}
\]
be its lineality space.  If \(p\in I_D\) and \(q\in K(p)\), then for \((u,t)\in L_0\),
\[
m_D^\top u+tp
=q^\top(A_D^\top u+t r^\top)=0.
\]
Hence the objective in (3) is constant on cosets of \(L_0\) and descends to the quotient polyhedron.  Choose a rational linear complement to the rational space \(L_0\).  In that complement the quotient is a pointed rational polyhedron with finitely many vertices.  Strong duality says its linear objective has a finite attained maximum for every \(p\in I_D\).  An attained maximum of a linear functional on a pointed polyhedron is attained at a vertex: if an optimal face contained no vertex, it would contain a line, contradicting pointedness.  Enumerating its finitely many vertices gives rational pairs \((\eta_B,\beta_B)\), indexed by feasible bases \(B\), such that
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)
=\max_B\{m_D^\top\eta_B+\beta_Bp\}
\qquad(p\in I_D).
\tag{4}
\]
Thus the value is finite, continuous, convex, and piecewise affine.  Each slope \(\beta_B\) is rational, and each intercept \(m_D^\top\eta_B\) is rational linear, hence rational affine, in \(m_D\).

The sublevel set
\[
N=\{p\in I_D:\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)\leq0\}
\]
is closed by continuity and convex by convexity.  A closed convex subset of a real interval is empty, a point, or a closed interval, with the whole interval allowed in the last case.  Its relative complement is \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\), so there are zero, one, or two relative sign boundaries.  At any such boundary \(p_\partial\), continuity gives
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p_\partial;m_D)=0.
\]
Primal attainment supplies \(q_\partial\in K(p_\partial)\) with \(gq_\partial=0\).  By (4), only finitely many dual pieces occur.  Along a sequence in an adjacent positive component converging to \(p_\partial\), some active piece recurs on a subsequence.  Its affine value is positive on that subsequence and converges to zero at the boundary.  Its feasibility is independent of \(p\), so the same pair \((\eta_B,\beta_B)\) is a zero-valued dual menu certificate at \(p_\partial\) and a positive certificate on the adjacent piece.

Finally augment the supplied menu by the equality \(rq=p_\partial\).  If the augmented menu has nonempty focal-district-saturated compatibility fiber and satisfies \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\), the focal projection theorem for its augmented row system realizes \(q_\partial\) by an original-alphabet compatible SCM saturated only at \(D\).  The stated condition \(d_Dq_\partial>0\) permits division and gives
\[
Q_\sigma^\star-Q_\rho^\star
=\frac{gq_\partial}{d_Dq_\partial}=0.
\]
The realization changes no other district's graph-compatible structure.  The implication \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\Rightarrow\mathsf{ASep}_D\) makes structural separation a sufficient check.  Without the focal saturation and active-slice hypotheses, primal attainment supplies only a boundary point of the row-face relaxation, not an SCM lifting.
