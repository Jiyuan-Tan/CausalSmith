Assume \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\). Then \(\Theta(\mathcal P)\) is a nonempty compact finite-dimensional semialgebraic set, every \(\Psi_C^s\) is multi-affine in the district response-law blocks and has total degree at most the number of districts visited by \(C\), and, for every finite collection \(\mathcal F\) of target-domain cells evaluated on the declared response carriers,
\[
\bigl\{(P_{M^\star}(C):C\in\mathcal F):
\mathbf M\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\bigr\}
=
\bigl\{(\Psi_C^\star(\theta^\star):C\in\mathcal F):
\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)\bigr\}.
\]
Every point of the right-hand range is realized by a district-saturated SCM family on the original observed alphabets \(\mathcal X\).

Assume instead only \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\). Then \(\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)\) is a nonempty compact finite-dimensional semialgebraic set and
\[
\bigl\{(P_{M^\star}(C):C\in\mathcal F):
\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\bigr\}
=
\bigl\{(\Phi_{C,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\vartheta^\star):C\in\mathcal F):
\vartheta\in\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)\bigr\}.
\]
Every point of this right-hand range is realized by a graph-compatible canonical SCM family on \(\mathcal X\).

For the saturated fiber, the explicit target-cell endpoints are
\[
L_T(\mathcal P)=\min_{\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)}\Psi_T^\star(\theta^\star),
\qquad
U_T(\mathcal P)=\max_{\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)}\Psi_T^\star(\theta^\star),
\]
and for the broad sparse-ADMG fiber they are
\[
\min_{\vartheta\in\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)}
\Phi_{T,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\vartheta^\star),
\qquad
\max_{\vartheta\in\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)}
\Phi_{T,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\vartheta^\star).
\]
For either exact fiber, these extrema are attained sharp lower and upper bounds. They are strictly tighter than \([0,1]\) exactly when the lower endpoint is positive or the upper endpoint is below one; the cell is identified exactly when the endpoints agree. If the endpoint values differ, their optimizers induce distinct original-alphabet SCM families that agree on every supplied law and disagree on the target. No assertion is made that every scalar between the endpoints is attainable.

For either exact fiber, let the evidence lower and upper endpoints be the minimum and maximum of the corresponding evidence polynomial. If the upper endpoint is zero, the evidence is null throughout the fiber; if the lower endpoint is zero and the upper endpoint is positive, evidence positivity is not uniform. In either case a conditional functional on the whole fiber is rejected before division. If the evidence lower endpoint is positive, the minimum and maximum of
\[
\frac{\Psi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star}{\Psi_E^\star}
\quad\text{or, respectively,}\quad
\frac{\Phi_{B_\rho\cap E,\mathrm{can}}^\star}{\Phi_{E,\mathrm{can}}^\star}
\]
are attained sharp lower and upper bounds for \(Q_\rho^\star\), without asserting interval filling. If the two conditional endpoint values differ, their optimizers induce distinct original-alphabet same-menu SCM families. Under the same positivity condition, \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) throughout the relevant fiber if and only if the minimum of its numerator-gap polynomial is positive; the reverse strict order holds throughout if and only if the maximum is negative. Two original-alphabet compatible families with opposite strict rankings exist if and only if that minimum is negative and that maximum is positive. Thus the first strict-minimum condition is the exact safe-pruning test on each exact fiber.

For every district \(D\), the broad projection obeys
\[
\mathcal Q_D(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\]
Equality with the row face is available for \(\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\) under \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\), but is not implied for an arbitrary sparse multi-district ADMG. Hence a local \(\mathsf{DCTFTR}\) face-failure trace alone is not a global paired-model certificate; distinct endpoint optimizers of either exact global program are such certificates whenever their objective values differ.
