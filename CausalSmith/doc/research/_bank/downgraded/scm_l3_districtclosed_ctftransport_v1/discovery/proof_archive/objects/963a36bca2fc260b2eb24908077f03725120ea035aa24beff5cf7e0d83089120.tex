Write \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(A=A_D\), \(m=m_D\), \(Z=Z_D(m_D)\), and \(J=\Omega_D\setminus Z\).  The normalization row in \(A\) implies \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\) for every \(q\in K\), so \(J\ne\varnothing\).  For each \(\omega\in J\), the definition of \(Z\) supplies \(q^\omega\in K\) with \(q^\omega(\omega)>0\).  Hence
\[
\bar q=\frac1{|J|}\sum_{\omega\in J}q^\omega
\]
belongs to \(K\), vanishes on \(Z\), and is strictly positive on every coordinate in \(J\).

Define
\[
L=\{v\in\mathbb R^{\Omega_D}:Av=0,\ v(\omega)=0\text{ for every }\omega\in Z\}.
\]
For any \(v\in L\), finiteness of \(J\) and positivity of \(\bar q\) permit \(\varepsilon>0\) such that \(\varepsilon|v(\omega)|\leq\bar q(\omega)/2\) for all \(\omega\in J\).  Then \(\bar q\pm\varepsilon v\geq0\) and
\[
A(\bar q\pm\varepsilon v)=m,
\]
so \(\bar q\pm\varepsilon v\in K\).  Conversely, every difference of two points of \(K\) lies in \(L\).  Thus \(L\) is exactly the direction space of the affine hull of \(K\).

Append the rows \(e_\omega\), \(\omega\in Z\), to \(A\), obtaining \(\widetilde A\).  Then \(L=\ker(\widetilde A)\).  Row reduction gives the finite-dimensional identity
\[
L^\perp=(\ker\widetilde A)^\perp
=\operatorname{row}(\widetilde A)
=\operatorname{row}(A)+\operatorname{span}\{e_\omega:\omega\in Z\}.
\]

For broad sufficiency, let \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  The broad inclusion in the projection theorem gives \(q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\in K\).  If
\[
b_D=\lambda^\top A+\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega e_\omega,
\]
then district-cell linearity and the definition of \(Z\) yield
\[
b_Dq_D^\star(\mathbf M)
=\lambda^\top Aq_D^\star(\mathbf M)
 +\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega q_D^\star(\mathbf M)(\omega)
=\lambda^\top m.
\]
Hence the target is identified throughout the broad fiber.

Under \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\), the projection theorem gives \(\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)=K\).  Therefore the target is constant on the saturated fiber exactly when \(b_Dv=0\) for every \(v\in L\), which is equivalent to \(b_D\in L^\perp\), the displayed active-face row space.  If membership fails, choose \(v\in L\) with \(b_Dv\ne0\).  The laws
\[
q_+=\bar q+\varepsilon v,
\qquad q_-=\bar q-\varepsilon v
\]
belong to \(K\) and satisfy \(b_Dq_+-b_Dq_-=2\varepsilon b_Dv\ne0\).  Exact saturated projection supplies \(\mathbf M_+,\mathbf M_-\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\) on \(\mathcal X\) inducing them.  Membership in the same compatibility fiber makes all supplied laws equal, while their target cells differ.  Outside this exact-projection scope, the argument proves only the broad sufficient direction and local row-face variation.
