Fix canonical finite orders on vertices, domains, descriptors, districts, assignments, response profiles, and generated work-DAG identifiers.  The counterfactual syntax is
\[
\mathsf{Atom}::=[s]V_\alpha=v,
\qquad
\mathsf{Event}::=\top\mid\bot\mid\mathsf{Atom}
\mid\mathsf{Event}\wedge\mathsf{Event}
\mid\mathsf{Event}\vee\mathsf{Event},
\]
where \(\alpha\) is a finite well-typed intervention or partial-copy term.  A descriptor may first return a finite counterfactual tuple \(T_a\) and then apply an explicit deterministic finite map \(f_a\).  Its output-cell parser expands
\[
\{f_a(T_a)=y\}=\bigsqcup_{t:f_a(t)=y}\{T_a=t\}
\tag{1}
\]
and records the finite preimage and pointwise disjointness of its tuple cells.  Normalized events are canonical disjoint normal forms of consistent unnested atoms, together with contradiction and sure-event forms.

Identified expressions have grammar
\[
\mathsf{Expr}::=\operatorname{Cell}(s,a,y)\mid\operatorname{Rat}(r)
\mid\sum_{j\in J}\mathsf{Expr}_j
\mid\prod_{j\in J}\mathsf{Expr}_j
\mid\operatorname{Quot}(F,H,\nu_H),
\]
where \(J\) is finite, \(r\in\mathbb Q\), and \(\nu_H\) is an independently checked nonzero certificate on the declared fiber.  The phase-indexed work-node grammar is
\[
\begin{split}
\mathsf{Goal}::={}&\operatorname{Raw}(\tau,e)
\mid\operatorname{Normed}(\tau,e,\nu)
\mid\operatorname{AncClosed}(\tau,H,e,\alpha)
\mid\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,b)\\
&\mid\operatorname{NeedFace}(\tau,D,b,A,m,Z,\kappa)
\mid\operatorname{Sum}(J,\mathrm{children})
\mid\operatorname{Prod}(\mathrm{children})
\mid\operatorname{Resolved}(F,\delta),
\end{split}
\]
with \(\tau\in\{\mathrm{num},\mathrm{den}\}\).  A configuration \(\mathsf{Config}\) is a finite well-typed acyclic DAG of these nodes, two distinguished roots, and a checked transition trace.

For \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), raw, normalized, and ancestor-closed nodes denote their target-domain event probabilities.  A row or face node at \((D,b)\) denotes \(b^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\); a resolved node denotes \(F(\mathcal P)\); and sum and product nodes denote the finite sum and product of their children.  Configuration denotation is the ordered pair of root denotations.  Normalization derivations check consistency, unnesting, (1), and finite disjoint Boolean identities pointwise.  Ancestor derivations enumerate every marginalized assignment.  Split derivations expand over the original districts \(\mathcal D(G)\), never over districts newly formed in an induced subgraph.  Extraction derivations are finite \(\mathsf{XEvent}\)- and \(\mathsf{XExpr}\)-trees with exhaustive \(\operatorname{SupportLocal}_D\) tables, separate numerator and context rows, and independent positivity certificates; they contain no semantic row equality.  A face node is well typed only after literal verification of all equations in \(A_Dq_D^\star=m_D\), and its row-span and forced-zero certificates use exact rational or real-algebraic linear arithmetic.  Zero, nonzero, and strict-positivity certificates are independently checked polynomial identities, exact linear certificates, or displayed finite preordering identities; none may consist of the conclusion it certifies.

The terminal grammar is
\[
\mathsf{Result}::=\operatorname{ID}(F,\delta)
\mid\operatorname{ZERO\_EVIDENCE}(F_E,z)
\mid\operatorname{FAIL}(t).
\]
Failure traces include ill-typed normalization or deterministic-preimage parsing, undefined splitting, failed locality or extraction, \(\operatorname{FACE\_NOT\_IDENTIFIED}\), \(\operatorname{NO\_ADMISSIBLE\_FACTOR}\), \(\operatorname{DENOMINATOR\_MAY\_VANISH}\), \(\operatorname{DENOMINATOR\_POSITIVITY\_UNCERTIFIED}\), and \(\operatorname{STUCK}\).  For a nonterminal configuration define
\[
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathrm{lex}}
=(r_N,r_A,r_S,r_X,r_F,r_{\Sigma},r_{\Pi},r_C)\in\mathbb N^8,
\]
counting respectively raw, normalized, ancestor-closed, row-needed, face-needed, unresolved-sum, unresolved-product nodes, and one terminalization flag; every terminal has rank zero.
