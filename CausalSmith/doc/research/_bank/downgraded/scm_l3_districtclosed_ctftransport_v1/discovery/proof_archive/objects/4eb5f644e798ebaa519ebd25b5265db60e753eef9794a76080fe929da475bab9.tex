Let \(R\) be real closed, let \(\mathcal P_0\subset R[X^{[1]},\ldots,X^{[\omega]},Y]_{\le d}\) contain \(s\) polynomials, with blocks \(X^{[i]}\) of \(k_i\) variables and \(Y\) of \(\ell\) variables, and let \(\Psi(Y)=(Q_1X^{[1]})\cdots(Q_\omega X^{[\omega]})\Phi\) with \(\Phi\) quantifier free.  Then there is an equivalent quantifier-free sign formula \(\Theta(Y)\), and an algorithm computes it using
\[
s^{(k_\omega+1)\cdots(k_1+1)(\ell+1)}
d^{O(k_\omega)\cdots O(k_1)O(\ell)}
\]
arithmetic operations in the coefficient ring, with the displayed bounds on its numbers and degrees of output polynomials.
