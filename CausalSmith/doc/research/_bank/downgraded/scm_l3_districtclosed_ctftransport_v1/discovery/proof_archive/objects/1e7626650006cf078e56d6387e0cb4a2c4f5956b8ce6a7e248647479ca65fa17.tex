For every district \(D\),
\[
\mathcal Q_D(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\]
Suppose \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds.  This predicate entails \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\), and
\[
\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)=\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\]
Moreover,
\[
R_D(m_D)
=\operatorname{span}\{q-q':q,q'\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\}^{\perp}.
\]
For the single witnessing baseline \(\mathbf M^0\) in \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\), every transport-admissible complete supplied cell is preserved under replacement of its focal law \(q_0\) by an arbitrary \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) because
\[
\bar\chi_{C_{a,v}}^{s,\mathbf M^0}(q-q_0)=0.
\]
Every boundary point of the row face is therefore induced on the declared alphabets \(\mathcal X\) by a compatible SCM family saturated only at \(D\), while every district \(D'\ne D\) retains its original graph-compatible restrictions.  Nonemptiness of \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\) together with \(\mathsf{ASep}_D(\mathcal P)\), and hence also together with \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\), is a convenient sufficient condition for \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  Without baseline-averaged separation or focal-district saturation, only the displayed broad inclusion is asserted.
