Fix \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  We verify the rules in phase order.

For \(\mathsf{NORM}\), the attached derivation is a composition of consistency, unnesting, contradiction removal, Boolean disjoint-normal-form identities, and finite additivity.  Each constructor preserves event probability, so replacing the node preserves every root that reaches it.  For \(\mathsf{ANCESTOR}\), the omitted finite variables range over their complete declared alphabets, and hence
\[
P(e)=\sum_xP(e,x),
\]
which is precisely the checked marginalization derivation.  For \(\mathsf{SPLIT}\), the enumerated finite sum over assignments and product over districts of \(G[H]\) equals the ancestor-closed probability by district factorization.  Thus the first three rules preserve the two root denotations.

At \(\mathsf{EXTRACT}\), a retained raw row has a checked proof of district locality and of \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\).  Typed-menu realizability and finite evaluation of its indicator give
\[
P_a^s(v)
=\sum_{\omega_D\in\Omega_D}r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v}(\omega_D)q_D^s(\omega_D).
\]
The admissibility proof and district-kernel invariance replace \(q_D^s\) by \(q_D^\star\), so the right side is \(r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v}q_D^\star\).  The district-cell-linearity lemma gives
\(
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q_D^\star
\)
for every retained derived row.  Together with \(\mathbf 1^\top q_D^\star=1\), stacking yields
\[
A_Dq_D^\star(\mathbf M)=m_D.
\]
The grammar assigns both \(\operatorname{NeedRows}(\tau,D,b)\) and its \(\operatorname{NeedFace}\) successor the denotation \(bq_D^\star(\mathbf M)\).  Rejected cells create no equality.  Hence extraction preserves denotation.

At \(\mathsf{FACE\_ID}\), the checked representation
\[
b=\lambda^\top A+\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega e_\omega
\]
and the certificates \(Aq_D^\star=m\) and \(q_D^\star(\omega)=0\) for \(\omega\in Z\) give
\[
bq_D^\star=\lambda^\top m.
\]
This is the resolved node's denotation; no surjectivity of the row face is used.  The \(\mathsf{MARGINALIZE}\) rule preserves denotation by finite additivity, and \(\mathsf{PRODUCT}\) preserves it by district factorization.  Thus all seven transformation rules preserve the ordered root pair pointwise.

The \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) rule records the same resolved numerator and denominator.  It creates a quotient only when the independent certificate \(\pi\) proves strict positivity throughout \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\); the zero and failure branches perform no division.

For the operational assertions, each of the first seven rules decreases its named coordinate of
\(
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathrm{lex}}=(r_N,r_A,r_S,r_X,r_F,r_{\Sigma},r_{\Pi},r_C)
\)
while fixing every earlier coordinate, and no rule creates an earlier-phase node.  Condition decreases \(r_C\) after the other coordinates vanish.  Fixed priority, canonical finite orders, and the least-applicable-node convention select at most one successor.  Conversely, a well-typed nonterminal has a least unresolved phase.  Its rule either applies or invokes the explicitly declared failure constructor for the failed partial operation.  Therefore \(\operatorname{STUCK}\) is unreachable.
