Fix \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\).  Let \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) and put \(q=q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\).  By the definition of a district response law, \(q\geq0\) and \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\).  Consider first a raw row retained in \(A_D\).  By \(\mathrm{def:stacked\text{-}menu\text{-}system}\), retention includes proofs that its complete supplied cell is district-local and that either \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\).  Recursive evaluation and \(\mathrm{ass:typed\text{-}menu\text{-}realizability}\) give
\[
P_a^s(v)=r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v}q_D^s.
\]
In the target case \(q_D^s=q\) by definition, and in the unchanged-source case \(q_D^s=q\) by \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}kernel\text{-}invariance}\).  Thus every retained raw equality holds at \(q\).  For a retained derived row, \(\mathrm{lem:district\text{-}cell\text{-}linearity}\), together with the same admissibility proof, gives
\[
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=r_{s,a,D,h,c}q.
\]
Stacking normalization, raw, and derived equalities in their defining order yields \(A_Dq=m_D\).  Hence
\[
\mathcal Q_D(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\]

Now assume \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  The preceding inclusion and \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\) show
\[
\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\subseteq\mathcal K_D(m_D).
\]
For the reverse inclusion, choose a baseline \(\mathbf M^0\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\) and an arbitrary \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\).  Since every observed alphabet is finite and nonempty, each partial profile \(\omega_D\in\Omega_D\) can be completed at every unvisited parent configuration by a fixed element of the corresponding alphabet.  Denote the resulting injection by
\[
\iota_D:\Omega_D\longrightarrow\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}.
\]
In the target domain replace the law of \(U_D^\star\) by the pushforward \((\iota_D)_\#q\).  Make the identical replacement in every source satisfying \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), and retain the entire baseline law of \(U_D^s\) in every source satisfying \(D\subseteq\Delta_s\).  These exhaust the cases by \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}closure}\).  Hold fixed every structural table and every latent law in every district \(D'\ne D\).  Call the resulting family \(\mathbf M^q\).

The component-reading equations at \(D\) are unchanged, so they remain recursive because \(G\) is acyclic and remain valued in \(\mathcal X\).  All graph-compatible mechanisms and latent-factor restrictions at \(D'\ne D\) are literally those of \(\mathbf M^0\).  Independence of \(U_D^s\) from the other district blocks is preserved by taking the new marginal law independently, as required by \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}factorization}\).  Bidirected completeness of \(G[D]\), the full-block representation, and the unrestricted-law clause in \(\mathrm{def:focal\text{-}district\text{-}saturated\text{-}model\text{-}class}\) admit \((\iota_D)_\#q\) without altering the declared graph or alphabets.  Equality of the target block law and every unchanged-source copy proves \(\mathrm{ass:district\text{-}kernel\text{-}invariance}\) at \(D\); all other required equalities were preserved from \(\mathbf M^0\).  Thus \(\mathbf M^q\) remains in the focal-district saturated model class before imposing its menu equations.

It remains to verify every complete supplied cell.  If its domain satisfies \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) says that its recursive indicator is either independent of \(\omega_D\) or depends only on \(\omega_D\).  In the first case its probability is unchanged because every other district law is fixed.  In the second case its raw row occurs in \((A_D,m_D)\), whence
\[
P_{M^{q,s}}^a(v)
=r^{\mathrm{raw}}_{s,a,D,v}q
=P_a^s(v),
\]
the last equality being the corresponding coordinate of \(A_Dq=m_D\).  If the cell's domain is not transport-admissible for \(D\), then \(s\ne\star\) and \(D\cap\Delta_s\ne\varnothing\); district closure gives \(D\subseteq\Delta_s\), and every district block in that source, including \(U_D^s\), was left at its baseline law.  Its probability is therefore unchanged.  Hence all complete cells, and thus all supplied laws, equal \(\mathcal P\).  We have proved
\[
\mathbf M^q\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P),
\qquad q_D^\star(\mathbf M^q)=q.
\]
This proves the reverse inclusion.  The construction used only nonnegativity and \(A_Dq=m_D\), not strict positivity of a coordinate, so it realizes every boundary point on the original alphabets while leaving every district \(D'\ne D\) in its baseline graph-compatible form.
