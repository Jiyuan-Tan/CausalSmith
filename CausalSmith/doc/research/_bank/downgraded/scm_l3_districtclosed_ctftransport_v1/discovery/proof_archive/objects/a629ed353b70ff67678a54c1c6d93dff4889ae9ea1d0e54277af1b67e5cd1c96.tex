Define the district row face and its forced-zero coordinates by
\[
\mathcal K_D(m_D)=\{q\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{\Omega_D}:A_Dq=m_D\},
\qquad
Z_D(m_D)=\{\omega\in\Omega_D:q(\omega)=0\text{ for every }q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\}.
\]
Define the broad and saturated global district projections by
\[
\mathcal Q_D(\mathcal P)=\{q_D^\star(\mathbf M):\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\},
\qquad
\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)=\{q_D^\star(\mathbf M):\mathbf M\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\}.
\]
