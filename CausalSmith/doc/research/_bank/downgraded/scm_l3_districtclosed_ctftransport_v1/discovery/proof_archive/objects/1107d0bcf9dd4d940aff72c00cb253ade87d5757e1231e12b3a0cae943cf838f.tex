For the complete carrier \(\Omega_D=\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\) and corrected raw/row-eligible stack,
\[
\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\Longrightarrow\mathsf{ASep}_D(\mathcal P).
\]
The implication is strict: with two independent binary singleton districts generating \(Y_0\) and \(Z\), supplying the complete marginal of \(Y_0\) and complete joint law of \((Y_0,Z)\) satisfies \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\) for the \(Y_0\)-district but not \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\).
