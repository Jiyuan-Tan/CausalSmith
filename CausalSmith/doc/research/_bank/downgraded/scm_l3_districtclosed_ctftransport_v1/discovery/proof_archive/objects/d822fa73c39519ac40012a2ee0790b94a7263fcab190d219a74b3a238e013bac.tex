Fix \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), a domain \(s\), and a checked derivation \(\delta:A\Downarrow F\).  We prove \(F(\mathcal P)=P_{M^s}(A)\) by structural induction on \(\delta\), using the constructors of \(\mathrm{def:finite\text{-}extraction\text{-}calculus}\).  At a source-cell leaf, \(\mathrm{ass:typed\text{-}menu\text{-}realizability}\) and \(\mathrm{def:compatibility\text{-}fiber}\) give
\[
 \operatorname{Eval}_{s,a}\!\left(P_a^s(v)\right)
 =P_a^s(v)
 =P_{M^s}(C_{a,v}).
\]
The contradiction and sure-event leaves have probabilities \(0\) and \(1\), respectively.  If the induction claim holds for \(A\), finite complementarity gives
\[
 P_{M^s}(A^{\mathsf c})
 =1-P_{M^s}(A)
 =1-F(\mathcal P).
\]
If it holds for pairwise disjoint events \((A_j:j\in J)\), where \(J\) is finite, then finite additivity gives
\[
 P_{M^s}\!\left(\bigsqcup_{j\in J}A_j\right)
 =\sum_{j\in J}P_{M^s}(A_j)
 =\sum_{j\in J}F_j(\mathcal P).
\]
The marginalization constructor enumerates a finite partition \((A_x:x\in\mathcal X_W)\), so this same equality proves that case.  A fixing-event constructor is admitted only with a checked fixing trace and a type-correct substitution.  The trace preserves probability by \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}fixing\text{-}calculus\text{-}soundness}\), and the induction hypotheses identify the substituted leaves, proving the fixing case.  There is no quotient constructor in an event-probability derivation.  A kernel quotient is evaluated only after sound derivations of its numerator event and context event have been supplied and a certificate of strict positivity for the context has been accepted.  Thus its division is defined and
\[
 \frac{F_{A\cap H}(\mathcal P)}{F_H(\mathcal P)}
 =\frac{P_{M^s}(A\cap H)}{P_{M^s}(H)}
 =P_{M^s}(A\mid H).
\]
This completes the induction and proves expression soundness.

It remains to justify the two checker claims.  The positivity verifier accepts only one of its finite certificate forms.  For an exact rational certificate, direct normalization proves the displayed number is strictly positive.  For a displayed linear face, substitution of its rational Farkas multipliers into the listed equality and nonnegativity constraints gives the claimed strict lower bound by finite linear algebra.  For a displayed real-algebraic certificate, the checker verifies every polynomial identity and sign condition in the finite trace by exact integer arithmetic; substitution of any point satisfying the displayed canonical constraints therefore gives the certified strict inequality.  In each case the certificate's declared constraints contain the compatibility fiber under \(\mathrm{def:compatibility\text{-}fiber}\), so the accepted positivity claim holds for every compatible \(\mathbf M\).  No division used above can consequently have a zero denominator.

Finally fix a district \(D\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:recursive\text{-}compatibility}\) and \(\mathrm{def:district\text{-}response\text{-}profile}\), both the focal carrier \(\Omega_D\) and the product of all nonfocal response-profile carriers are finite.  For the recursive event indicator \(I_A\), the support-local checker exhaustively compares
\[
 I_A(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 I_A(\omega_D,\omega'_{-D})
\]
for every \(\omega_D\in\Omega_D\) and every pair \(\omega_{-D},\omega'_{-D}\) of complete nonfocal profiles.  Hence the enumeration terminates.  If it accepts, all displayed pairs are equal, so \(I_A\) is independent of the nonfocal profile.  The constructed coordinate
\[
 \operatorname{row}_D(A)(\omega_D)
 =I_A(\omega_D,\omega^0_{-D})
\]
at the checker's fixed reference profile \(\omega^0_{-D}\) then equals \(I_A(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\) for every nonfocal profile.  Conversely, if \(I_A\) is independent of the nonfocal profile, every comparison in the exhaustive list is equal, and the checker accepts.  On rejection, the first unequal comparison supplies an explicit triple \((\omega_D,\omega_{-D},\omega'_{-D})\) witnessing nonlocality.  This proves termination, the stated if-and-only-if characterization, and correctness of the constructed row.
