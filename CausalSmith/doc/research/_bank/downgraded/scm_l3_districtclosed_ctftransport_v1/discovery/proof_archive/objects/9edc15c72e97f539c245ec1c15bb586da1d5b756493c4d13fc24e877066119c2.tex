For a fixed district \(D\), define its active row space by
\[
R_D(m_D)=\operatorname{row}(A_D)+
\operatorname{span}\{e_\omega:\omega\in Z_D(m_D)\}
\subseteq\mathbb R^{\Omega_D}.
\]
For a complete supplied cell \(C_{a,v}\) in domain \(s\) that is transport-admissible for \(D\), meaning \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), and for
\(\omega_{-D}\in\prod_{D'\in\mathcal D(G),\,D'\ne D}\Omega_{D'}\), define its focal recursive-indicator slice by
\[
\chi_{C_{a,v},\omega_{-D}}(\omega_D)
=\mathbf 1\{C_{a,v}\text{ is true under recursive evaluation of }
(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\},
\qquad \omega_D\in\Omega_D.
\]
The active-slice face-separation predicate \(\mathsf{ASep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds exactly when
\[
\chi_{C_{a,v},\omega_{-D}}\in R_D(m_D)
\]
for every transport-admissible complete supplied cell and every fixed nonfocal response profile \(\omega_{-D}\).  When \(D\) is the only district, the nonfocal product is the singleton empty profile.
