Put \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\).  The normalization row of \(A_D\) gives \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\) for every \(q\in K\).  Hence the assumed nonempty \(K\) is a closed subset of a finite probability simplex, so it is compact and each linear optimum is attained.

Apply \(\mathrm{lem:finite\text{-}linear\text{-}programming\text{-}strong\text{-}duality}\) with equality constraint \(A_Dq=m_D\), coordinate constraint \(q\geq0\), and cost row \(g\).  Its unrestricted equality multiplier \(\eta\) and nonnegative primal coordinates give
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
=\min_{q\in K}gq
=\max_{\eta:A_D^\top\eta\le g^\top}m_D^\top\eta.
\tag{1}
\]
For every primal-feasible \(q\) and dual-feasible \(\eta\), weak duality is also the direct calculation
\[
m_D^\top\eta=q^\top A_D^\top\eta\le gq.
\tag{2}
\]

Let \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  The broad projection theorem gives \(q=q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\in K\).  If \(m_D^\top\eta>0\), (2) yields \(gq>0\).  The assumed denominator condition yields \(d_Dq>0\), and \(\mathrm{lem:district\text{-}cell\text{-}linearity}\) together with \(g=n_{\sigma,D}-n_{\rho,D}\) gives, only after this positivity has been invoked,
\[
Q_\sigma^\star(\mathbf M)-Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M)
=\frac{n_{\sigma,D}q-n_{\rho,D}q}{d_Dq}
=\frac{gq}{d_Dq}>0.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus every positive dual certificate is sound throughout the broad fiber.

Assume also \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  The exact focal projection gives \(\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)=K\).  Since \(d_Dq>0\) on all of \(K\), (3) shows that uniform strict dominance on the focal fiber is equivalent to \(gq>0\) for every \(q\in K\).  Compactness and continuity make this equivalent to \(\min_{q\in K}gq>0\), and dual attainment in (1) makes that equivalent to a feasible \(\eta\) with \(m_D^\top\eta>0\).  This proves necessity.

If \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\), an attained minimizer \(q_-\in K\) has \(gq_-<0\).  Exact focal projection realizes it by an original-alphabet family in \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\), and the positive denominator gives \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\).  If also \(\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)>0\), an attained maximizer \(q_+\in K\) is realized in the same way and gives the opposite strict ranking.  Both families induce \(\mathcal P\), and the projection construction preserves all nonfocal graph-compatible restrictions.  The two easier sufficient premises follow from the two hierarchy lemmas.  Without \(\mathsf{BASep}_D\), (1) remains an exact row-face duality and a sound broad certificate, but its optimizer need not lift to the focal SCM fiber.
