The algebra \(\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\) is generated by complete supplied output-cell probabilities, including deterministic-pushforward cells, rational constants, finite sums, differences, products, and marginalization sums.  It contains a quotient \(F/H\) only together with an independently checked certificate that \(H\ne0\) on the relevant compatibility fiber; a conditional causal output requires the stronger checked certificate \(H>0\) throughout that fiber.  Finite deterministic-preimage expansion introduces no new generator beyond the declared output law.
