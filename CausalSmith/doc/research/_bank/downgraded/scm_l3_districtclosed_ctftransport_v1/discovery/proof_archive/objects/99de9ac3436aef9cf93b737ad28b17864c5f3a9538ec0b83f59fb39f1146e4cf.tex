Use the query-independent complete carrier \(\Omega_D=\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\).  For \(\theta=(q_D^s:s\in\mathcal S,D\in\mathcal D(G))\), with \(q_D^s\in\Delta(\Omega_D)\), define every finite counterfactual cell polynomial by
\[
\Psi_C^s(\theta^s)=
\sum_{\omega\in\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}\Omega_D}
\mathbf 1\{C\text{ is true under recursive evaluation of }\omega\}
\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^s(\omega_D).
\]
If \(C\) visits \(k\) original districts, this polynomial is multi-affine in the district-law blocks and has total degree at most \(k\).  For complete supplied cells \(C_{a,v}\), define
\[
\begin{split}
\Theta(\mathcal P)=\bigl\{\theta:{}&q_D^s\in\Delta(\Omega_D)
\quad(s\in\mathcal S,D\in\mathcal D(G)),\\
&q_D^s=q_D^\star\quad\text{if }D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing,\\
&\Psi_{C_{a,v}}^s(\theta^s)=P_a^s(v)
\quad\text{for all }s,a,v\bigr\}.
\end{split}
\]
For a target cell \(T\), set
\[
L_T(\mathcal P)=\min_{\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)}\Psi_T^\star(\theta^\star),
\qquad
U_T(\mathcal P)=\max_{\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)}\Psi_T^\star(\theta^\star),
\]
and
\[
\mathcal G_{\rho,\sigma}(\theta)=
\Psi_{B_\sigma\cap E}^\star(\theta^\star)-
\Psi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star(\theta^\star).
\]

For an arbitrary, possibly nonsaturated ADMG \(G\), let
\(\vartheta^s=(\mu^s,p^s)\in\operatorname{Can}(G,\mathcal X)\), with the graph-compatible latent-factor restrictions, product law across distinct original districts, simplex constraints
\[
p_U^s(u)\geq0,
\qquad \sum_up_U^s(u)=1,
\]
and one-hot structural-table constraints
\[
\mu_{V,v\mid pa,u_V}^s\in\{0,1\},
\qquad
\sum_{v\in\mathcal X_V}\mu_{V,v\mid pa,u_V}^s=1.
\]
Let \(\Phi_{C,\mathrm{can}}^s(\vartheta^s)\) be its finite recursive cell polynomial and \(q_{D,\mathrm{can}}^s(\cdot;\vartheta^s)\) its induced law on the complete response-map carrier.  Define
\[
\begin{split}
\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)=\bigl\{\vartheta:{}&
\vartheta^s\in\operatorname{Can}(G,\mathcal X)\quad(s\in\mathcal S),\\
&q_{D,\mathrm{can}}^s(\cdot;\vartheta^s)
=q_{D,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\cdot;\vartheta^\star)
\quad\text{if }D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing,\\
&\Phi_{C_{a,v},\mathrm{can}}^s(\vartheta^s)=P_a^s(v)
\quad\text{for every complete supplied cell}\bigr\}.
\end{split}
\]
The first fiber uses district-saturated response blocks on the full carrier; the second retains every internal latent-factor restriction of the sparse graph.
