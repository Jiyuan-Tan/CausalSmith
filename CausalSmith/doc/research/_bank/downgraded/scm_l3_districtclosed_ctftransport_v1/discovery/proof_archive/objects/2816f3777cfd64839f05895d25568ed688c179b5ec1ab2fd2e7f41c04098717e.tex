Let \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). It is nonempty and compact, and the map \(q\mapsto r_D^\dagger q\) is linear. Its image
\[
I_D=\{r_D^\dagger q:q\in K\}\tag{1}
\]
is therefore a nonempty compact convex subset of \(\mathbb R\), hence a closed interval.

For \(p\in I_D\), apply finite linear-programming strong duality to the augmented equality matrix. Writing \(\xi\) for the multiplier of \(r_D^\dagger q=p\) gives
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)
=\max_{\eta,\xi}\left\{m_D^\top\eta+p\xi:
A_D^\top\eta+(r_D^\dagger)^\top\xi\leq g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^\top\right\}.\tag{2}
\]
The feasible polyhedron on the right does not depend on \(p\). Quotient its lineality space; for every feasible \(p\), the objective is constant along that lineality space because \((m_D,p)\) belongs to the column space of the augmented equality matrix. The quotient is a rational pointed polyhedron. Since (2) has a finite attained optimum, an optimal face contains an extreme point. Extreme points are obtained by choosing a linearly independent active set from finitely many inequalities, so there are finitely many relevant bases. Gaussian elimination shows that every associated objective is affine in \((m_D,p)\) with rational coefficients. Consequently (2) is the maximum of finitely many affine pieces on their basis regions. It is therefore finite, convex, continuous, and piecewise affine on \(I_D\).

The nonpositive sublevel set
\[
N=\{p\in I_D:\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)\leq0\}\tag{3}
\]
is closed by continuity and convex by convexity. Thus it is empty, a singleton, or a closed interval, including possibly all of \(I_D\). Its complement is \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\), and a closed interval has at most two relative boundary points. At any such boundary \(p_0\), continuity gives
\(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p_0;m_D)=0\). Primal and dual attainment in (2) provide respectively a law
\[
q_0\in K,\qquad r_D^\dagger q_0=p_0,\qquad
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q_0=0,\tag{4}
\]
and dual menu weights of value zero. On any adjacent component of the strict-positive set, one of the finitely many dual affine pieces is positive and supplies the asserted certificate.

The assumed positivity of \(d_Dq\) converts the numerator-gap signs into conditional arm-order signs. If the augmented menu at \(p_0\) satisfies \(\mathsf{BASep}_D\), the one-district projection theorem applied to the augmented row system lifts (4) to an SCM on \(\mathcal X\) saturated only at \(D\), with all nonfocal restrictions unchanged. Nonempty augmented focal compatibility together with \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\), and hence with \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\), implies that baseline-averaged premise by the separation hierarchy. No lifting is inferred when those premises fail.
