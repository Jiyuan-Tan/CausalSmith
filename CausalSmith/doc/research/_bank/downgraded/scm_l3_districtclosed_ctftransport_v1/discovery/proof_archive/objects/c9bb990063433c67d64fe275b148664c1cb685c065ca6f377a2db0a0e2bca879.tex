The normalization row and nonnegativity make the nonempty face a compact subset of the simplex.  Apply finite linear-programming strong duality with cost \(g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) to obtain attainment and the displayed primal-dual equality.  For a broad compatible model, projection puts \(q_D^\star\) in the face, and accepted locality gives
\[
Q_\sigma^\star-Q_\rho^\star=
\frac{g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star}
{d_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star}.
\tag{1}
\]
Dual feasibility gives
\[
g^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\geq
\eta^{\mathsf T}A_Dq_D^\star=m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta.
\tag{2}
\]
The denominator in (1) is positive by hypothesis, so a positive dual value proves broad strict dominance.

Under \(\mathsf{BASep}_D\), every face law lifts.  Compactness and continuity show that strict dominance at every lifted law is equivalent to the attained minimum \(\Gamma\) being positive; strong duality makes this equivalent to a positive attained dual value.  A negative minimizing law and, when present, a positive maximizing law lift to the asserted original-alphabet ranking witnesses while every nonfocal district remains fixed.  The separation lemmas give the two baseline-free sufficient checks.  Without exact projection, (2) remains sound but a primal optimizer may fail to lift.
