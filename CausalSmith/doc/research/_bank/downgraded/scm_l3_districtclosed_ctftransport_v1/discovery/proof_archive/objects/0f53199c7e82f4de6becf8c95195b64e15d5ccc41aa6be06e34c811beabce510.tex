Let \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). The normalization row in \(A_D\) and nonnegativity imply \(K\subseteq\Delta(\Omega_D)\), so the assumed nonempty face is compact and bounded. Apply finite linear-programming strong duality with \(A=A_D\), \(m=m_D\), and \(c=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^\top\). Both optima are attained and
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
=\min_{\substack{q\geq0\\A_Dq=m_D}}g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q
=\max_{\eta:\,A_D^\top\eta\leq g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^\top}m_D^\top\eta.\tag{1}
\]

For a broad compatible model, the projection theorem gives \(q_D^\star\in K\). District-cell linearity and the common evidence row give
\[
Q_\sigma^\star-Q_\rho^\star
=\frac{n_{\sigma,D}q_D^\star-n_{\rho,D}q_D^\star}{d_Dq_D^\star}
=\frac{g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q_D^\star}{d_Dq_D^\star}.\tag{2}
\]
The denominator is positive by hypothesis. If \(\eta\) is dual feasible, then, because \(q_D^\star\geq0\),
\[
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q_D^\star
\geq \eta^\top A_Dq_D^\star
=\eta^\top m_D.\tag{3}
\]
Thus a feasible dual vector with \(m_D^\top\eta>0\) makes (2) strictly positive in every model in the broad fiber.

Assume now \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\). Exact focal projection identifies the set of attainable focal laws with \(K\). Since \(q\mapsto d_Dq\) is continuous and positive on compact \(K\), its minimum is positive. Therefore \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) at every model in the focal fiber if and only if \(g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q>0\) for every \(q\in K\), which by compactness is equivalent to
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)>0.\tag{4}
\]
By (1), condition (4) is equivalent to existence of an attained feasible dual vector of positive value, proving necessity of the certificate.

If \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\), an attained minimizer \(q_-\in K\) has negative numerator gap. Exact focal projection lifts it to an original-alphabet compatible SCM, and (2) gives \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\). If also
\(\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)>0\), an attained maximizer \(q_+\) similarly lifts and gives the opposite strict ranking. The focal replacement construction leaves every nonfocal district fixed. The active-slice and structural-separation lemmas supply the two advertised sufficient conditions for the exact projection. Without that projection, (1)--(3) remain valid but a row-face optimizer need not be globally realizable.
