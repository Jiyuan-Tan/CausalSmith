The finite extraction calculus has event syntax
\[
\mathsf{XEvent}::=
\operatorname{Cell}(s,a,y)\mid\top\mid\bot\mid
\neg\mathsf{XEvent}\mid
\bigsqcup_{j\in J}\mathsf{XEvent}_j\mid
\operatorname{Marg}_{W}(\mathsf{XEvent})\mid
\operatorname{Fix}(\mathsf{FixTrace},\mathsf{XEvent}),
\]
where \(J\) and every marginalized alphabet are finite, and a disjoint-union node carries a complete pointwise disjointness table.  The cell event is
\[
\operatorname{Cell}(s,a,y)=\{f_a(T_a)=y\}.
\]
Its probability leaf is the supplied output cell \(P_a^s(y)\).  Only its recursively evaluated indicator is expanded by the finite identity
\[
\mathbf 1\{f_a(T_a)=y\}
=\sum_{t:f_a(t)=y}\mathbf 1\{T_a=t\};
\tag{1}
\]
the tuple-cell probabilities on the right of \((1)\) are not menu generators unless an identity-output tuple descriptor is separately supplied.

Menu expressions have syntax
\[
\begin{split}
\mathsf{XExpr}::={}&
\operatorname{CellProb}(s,a,y)\mid\operatorname{Rat}(r)
\mid\sum_{j\in J}\mathsf{XExpr}_j
\mid\mathsf{XExpr}-\mathsf{XExpr}
\mid\prod_{j\in J}\mathsf{XExpr}_j\\
&\mid\operatorname{Quot}(F,H,\pi_H),
\end{split}
\]
where \(r\in\mathbb Q\) and \(\pi_H\) is a separately checkable strict-positivity certificate on its declared compatibility fiber.  A derivation \(\delta:A\Downarrow F\) is generated only by an output-cell lookup, the truth-table identity \((1)\), contradiction or sure-event evaluation, finite disjoint additivity, complementation, finite marginalization, multiplication of independent original-district factors, and a checked fixing trace.

A fixing trace is a finite tree whose leaves are event or expression derivations and whose constructors are identity, typed substitution from a fixed finite registry of sound Boolean or fixing identities, finite disjoint sum, finite product, complement, marginalization, conditional quotient with a separately checked positive denominator, composition, and cleared-denominator comparison.  Every constructor stores all of its children and type data.  A comparison node is accepted only when cross-multiplication is legal under its stored denominator certificates and coefficient-by-coefficient normalization gives the zero polynomial.  The identity registry is finite and contains only pointwise Boolean identities, finite total-probability identities, and the fixing identities explicitly verified by recursive substitution of structural response tables.  Thus no constructor contains the semantic equality it is intended to establish.

Positivity certificates are either a positive rational constant after exact normalization, a rational Farkas identity for a linear face, or a displayed finite preordering identity
\[
p-\varepsilon=
\sum_i\tau_i h_i+
\sum_{J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,t\}}\sigma_J
\prod_{j\in J}g_j,
\qquad \varepsilon>0,
\]
where the declared fiber has equations \(h_i=0\), inequalities \(g_j\geq0\), and every \(\sigma_J\) is checked coefficient by coefficient to be a finite sum of squares.  All polynomial equalities are checked by exact integer or rational coefficient comparison.

For an event \(A\) and a district \(D\), the total procedure \(\operatorname{SupportLocal}_D(A)\) enumerates
\[
(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\longmapsto
\mathbf 1\{A\text{ is true under recursive evaluation of }(\omega_D,\omega_{-D})\}
\]
over the complete finite response carriers.  It accepts exactly when every two entries with the same \(\omega_D\) agree, and then returns their common row \(\operatorname{row}_D(A)\in\{0,1\}^{\Omega_D}\).  Otherwise it returns the first disagreeing pair in the canonical order.  All syntax trees, registries, coefficient vectors, truth tables, and searches are finite and canonically ordered.
