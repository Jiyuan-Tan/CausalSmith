Let \(\mathfrak L_D\) be a checked local-arm instance containing \(\rho,\sigma\), and suppose \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\).  Then
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=
\max_{\eta\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}}
\{m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta:A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\}.
\]
Every feasible dual vector of positive value certifies \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) throughout the broad fiber.  Under \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\), positivity is also necessary for uniform strict dominance on the focal-saturated fiber; a negative minimum lifts to a reversing original-alphabet model, and a negative minimum together with a positive maximum lifts to two models with opposite rankings.  Nonempty focal saturation plus \(\mathsf{ASep}_D\), hence plus \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\), is sufficient for these exact clauses.  Without a checked local-arm instance or exact focal projection, the ratio or lifting conclusion is not asserted.
