Assume \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\). If
\[
b_D\in\operatorname{row}(A_D)+\operatorname{span}\{e_\omega:\omega\in Z_D(m_D)\},
\]
then the district-local target cell is constant throughout the broad fiber \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\). More precisely, every representation
\[
b_D=\lambda^\top A_D+\sum_{\omega\in Z_D(m_D)}\zeta_\omega e_\omega
\]
identifies its value as \(\lambda^\top m_D\). If also \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds, then this active-face membership is necessary and sufficient for constancy on \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\). Under those two additional hypotheses, failure of membership yields two district-saturated SCM families on \(\mathcal X\) that agree on every supplied law and disagree on the target cell. Outside that exact-projection scope, failure certifies variation only on the local row face.
