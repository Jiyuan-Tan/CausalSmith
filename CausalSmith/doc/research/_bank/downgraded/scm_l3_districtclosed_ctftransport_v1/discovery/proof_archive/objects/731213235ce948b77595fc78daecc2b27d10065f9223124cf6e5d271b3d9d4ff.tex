Fix \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  The \(\mathsf{NORM}\) trace is a composition of checked event equalities, so it preserves denotation.  At \(\mathsf{ANCESTOR}\), every eliminated variable ranges over a complete finite alphabet, and repeated use of
\[
P(e)=\sum_xP(e\cap\{X=x\})
\tag{1}
\]
proves preservation.

For \(\mathsf{SPLIT}\), write \(\omega=(\omega_D:D\in\mathcal D(G))\).  District factorization applies to the original districts and gives
\[
P_{M^\star}(\omega)=\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}q_D^\star(\omega_D).
\tag{2}
\]
Recursive compatibility makes the normalized event a deterministic function of \(\omega\).  Total probability and (2) yield
\[
P_{M^\star}(e)=
\sum_{\omega:e(\omega)=1}
\prod_{D\in\mathcal D(G)}e_{\omega_D}^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star.
\tag{3}
\]
The successor work DAG is precisely (3).  No independence across pieces of an original district is invoked.

At \(\mathsf{EXTRACT}\), an accepted raw cell is transport-admissible and support-local, so typed-menu realizability, total probability, and district-kernel invariance give its checked row equation.  For every row-eligible pair, district-cell linearity gives
\[
N=u^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star,
\qquad H=v^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star.
\tag{4}
\]
The rule stacks (4) separately and never stacks their ratio.  Together with normalization these are exactly the checked equations \(A_Dq_D^\star=m_D\).  Rejected candidates assert no equation.  The row and face nodes both denote the same already-checked local target row, so the step preserves the roots.

At \(\mathsf{FACE\_ID}\), its certificate supplies
\[
b=\lambda^{\mathsf T}A_D+
\sum_{\omega\in Z_D(m_D)}\zeta_\omega e_\omega.
\tag{5}
\]
Since \(q_D^\star\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), multiplication of (5) by \(q_D^\star\) gives \(b^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star=\lambda^{\mathsf T}m_D\).  Ready marginalization and product nodes preserve denotation by finite additivity and (2), respectively.  The condition rule retains the resolved numerator and denominator and constructs a quotient only under its independent uniform positivity certificate.

Operationally, each rule decreases the stated first changeable coordinate and fixes every earlier one; later coordinates may increase, which is exactly lexicographic decrease.  The canonical priority and least-node choice give at most one successor.  A least unresolved phase always has either its checked successor or its specified failure branch, so a well-typed run cannot reach \(\operatorname{STUCK}\).
