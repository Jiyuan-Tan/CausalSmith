Assume \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and every supplied output-cell probability is effectively rational or real algebraic.  Let \(\mathbb D\) be the effective ordered coefficient ring they generate, with its effective embedding into \(\mathbb R_{\mathrm{alg}}\) for coefficient representation and exact arithmetic.  The \(\mathsf{QEFormula}\) outputs of \(\mathsf{DCTFTR\text{-}CAN}\) are equivalent to their defining first-order formulas in every real-closed field extension containing \(\mathbb D\), and in particular their realizations over the ordinary real field \(\mathbb R\) are used for the causal conclusions.  For either an unconditional finite target-domain counterfactual cell \(T\) or the conditional query \(Q_\rho^\star=P_{M^\star}(B_\rho\mid E)\), the algorithm terminates and returns a \(\mathsf{HybridResult}\).  A fast-path \(\operatorname{ID\_RAT}(F,\delta)\) has \(F\in\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\) and equals the causal query throughout \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  Otherwise, for the real canonical fiber \(S=\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P)\), the fallback returns quantifier-free formulas whose real realizations are exactly the full target range and its attained lower and upper endpoints, together with an exact singleton decision.  For an unconditional target it returns \(\operatorname{UNCOND\_QE}\), and identification holds exactly when the real range is a singleton.  For a conditional target it evaluates the closed sentences \(\mathsf Z\), \(\mathsf N\), and \(\mathsf P\) from \(\mathrm{def:hybrid\text{-}result\text{-}grammar}\): it returns the payload-free terminal \(\operatorname{ZERO\_EVIDENCE\_QE}\) exactly under \(\mathsf Z\), the payload-free terminal \(\operatorname{NOT\_UNIFORMLY\_DEFINED\_QE}\) exactly under \(\mathsf N\), and only under \(\mathsf P\) forms the conditional real range and returns \(\operatorname{COND\_QE}\). Exact operations in \(\mathbb D\) compute the output syntax and evaluate every closed quantifier-free decision.
