For every \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\), each nonterminal \(\mathsf{NORM}\), \(\mathsf{ANCESTOR}\), \(\mathsf{SPLIT}\), \(\mathsf{EXTRACT}\), \(\mathsf{FACE\_ID}\), \(\mathsf{MARGINALIZE}\), or \(\mathsf{PRODUCT}\) step preserves the ordered pair of root denotations pointwise. The preservation witnesses are respectively the checked normalization derivation, finite ancestor marginalization identity, checked district sum-product derivation, admissible extraction equations \(A_Dq_D^\star=m_D\), active-face calculation, finite additivity, and district factorization. The \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) rule labels its terminal with the same two root values and divides only under an independent strict-positivity certificate. In the rank \(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathrm{lex}}=(r_N,r_A,r_S,r_X,r_F,r_{\Sigma},r_{\Pi},r_C)\), the first seven rules respectively decrease \(r_N,r_A,r_S,r_X,r_F,r_{\Sigma},r_{\Pi}\) while fixing every earlier coordinate, and \(\mathsf{CONDITION}\) decreases \(r_C\) after the others vanish; no rule creates an earlier-phase node. The fixed priority and least-applicable-node convention give a unique successor. From a well-typed initial configuration, \(\operatorname{STUCK}\) is unreachable because every declared partial-operation failure takes its specified \(\operatorname{FAIL}\) branch.
