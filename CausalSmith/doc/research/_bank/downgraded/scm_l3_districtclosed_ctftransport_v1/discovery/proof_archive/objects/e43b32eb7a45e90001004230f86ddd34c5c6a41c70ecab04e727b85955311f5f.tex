Write
\[
K=\mathcal K_D(m_D),\qquad A=A_D,\qquad m=m_D,
\qquad Z=Z_D(m_D),\qquad J=\Omega_D\setminus Z.
\]
For broad sufficiency, let \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\).  By \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}district\text{-}fiber\text{-}projection}\), \(q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\in K\).  If
\[
b_D=\lambda^\top A+
\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega e_\omega,
\]
then
\[
\begin{aligned}
b_Dq_D^\star(\mathbf M)
&=\lambda^\top A q_D^\star(\mathbf M)
+\sum_{\omega\in Z}\zeta_\omega q_D^\star(\mathbf M)(\omega)\\
&=\lambda^\top m.
\end{aligned}
\]
The first term uses \(Aq_D^\star=m\); every term in the sum vanishes by the definition of \(Z\).  The coefficient matrix in the displayed decomposition has entries in \(\{0,1\}\), and \(b_D\) is also a zero--one row.  Rational Gaussian elimination therefore permits \(\lambda\) and the \(\zeta_\omega\) to be chosen rational whenever the decomposition is consistent.  By \(\mathrm{def:stacked\text{-}menu\text{-}system}\), the entries of \(m\) are supplied cells or certified extracted kernels; by \(\mathrm{def:typed\text{-}extraction\text{-}judgment}\), every division used by the latter has a certified nonzero denominator.  Hence \(\lambda^\top m\in\mathscr R(\mathcal P)\).  Thus the target equals an allowed rational functional of the supplied menu throughout the broad fiber.  This proves sufficiency without saturation or a separation hypothesis.

Now assume \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{ASep}_D(\mathcal P)\).  The focal projection theorem gives
\[
\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)=K,
\]
so \(K\ne\varnothing\).  Because \(A\) contains the normalization row, \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\) for every \(q\in K\); hence \(J\ne\varnothing\).  For each \(\omega\in J\), the definition of \(Z\) supplies \(q^\omega\in K\) with \(q^\omega(\omega)>0\).  Therefore
\[
\bar q=\frac{1}{\lvert J\rvert}\sum_{\omega\in J}q^\omega
\]
belongs to \(K\), vanishes on \(Z\), and is strictly positive on every coordinate in \(J\).

Define
\[
L=\{v\in\mathbb R^{\Omega_D}:Av=0,
\ v(\omega)=0\text{ for every }\omega\in Z\}.
\]
If \(v\in L\), finiteness of \(J\) and strict positivity of \(\bar q\) on \(J\) permit \(\varepsilon>0\) such that
\[
\varepsilon\lvert v(\omega)\rvert
\leq\frac12\bar q(\omega)
\qquad(\omega\in J).
\]
Then \(\bar q\pm\varepsilon v\geq0\), both vectors vanish on \(Z\), and
\(A(\bar q\pm\varepsilon v)=m\); hence both belong to \(K\).  Conversely, the difference of any two points of \(K\) lies in \(L\).  It follows that \(L\) is exactly the direction space of the affine hull of \(K\).

Let \(\widetilde A\) append to \(A\) the coordinate rows \(e_\omega\) for \(\omega\in Z\).  Then \(L=\ker(\widetilde A)\).  Finite-dimensional row reduction gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
L^\perp
&=(\ker\widetilde A)^\perp
=\operatorname{row}(\widetilde A)\\
&=\operatorname{row}(A)+
\operatorname{span}\{e_\omega:\omega\in Z\}
=R_D(m_D).
\end{aligned}
\]
By the exact projection, the target is constant on the focal fiber if and only if the linear row \(b_D\) is constant on \(K\).  This holds if and only if \(b_Dv=0\) for every \(v\in L\), equivalently if and only if \(b_D\in L^\perp=R_D(m_D)\).  This proves necessity.

If membership fails, choose \(v\in L\) with \(b_Dv\ne0\) and then choose \(\varepsilon>0\) as above.  The two laws
\[
q_+=\bar q+\varepsilon v,
\qquad q_-=\bar q-\varepsilon v
\]
belong to \(K\) and satisfy
\[
b_Dq_+-b_Dq_-=2\varepsilon b_Dv\ne0.
\]
The focal projection theorem realizes them by \(\mathbf M_+,\mathbf M_-\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\) on the original alphabets.  Membership in the same compatibility fiber proves that these families agree on every supplied law, while the last display proves disagreement on the target cell.  The projection construction changes only the response law at \(D\), so all other districts retain their baseline graph-compatible restrictions.  By \(\mathrm{lem:structural\text{-}separation\text{-}implies\text{-}active\text{-}slice}\), the older \(\mathsf{Sep}_D\) condition remains a sufficient test for the exact clause.  Without the focal exact-projection hypotheses, the construction supplies only two points of the local row-face relaxation, not necessarily two compatible SCMs.
