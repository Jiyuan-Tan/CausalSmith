Put \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\) and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\). The normalization row of \(A_D\) gives \(\mathbf 1^\top q=1\) for every \(q\in K\). Hence the assumed nonempty set \(K\) is a closed subset of the finite probability simplex and is compact; the primal minimum and maximum are attained.

Apply finite linear-programming strong duality with variable \(q\), equality constraint \(A_Dq=m_D\), nonnegativity constraint \(q\geq0\), and cost column \(g^\top\). The equality multiplier \(\eta\) is unrestricted, and the nonnegative primal variables give the dual inequality \(A_D^\top\eta\leq g^\top\). Therefore
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
=\min_{q\in K}gq
=\max_{\eta:A_D^\top\eta\leq g^\top}m_D^\top\eta.
\]
For every feasible pair \((q,\eta)\), weak duality is also the direct calculation
\[
m_D^\top\eta=q^\top A_D^\top\eta\leq gq.
\]

Let \(\mathbf M\in\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\). Broad projection gives \(q=q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\in K\). If a feasible \(\eta\) satisfies \(m_D^\top\eta>0\), weak duality gives \(gq>0\). Since \(d_Dq>0\), district-cell linearity yields
\[
Q_\sigma^\star(\mathbf M)-Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M)
=\frac{n_{\sigma,D}q-n_{\rho,D}q}{d_Dq}
=\frac{gq}{d_Dq}>0.
\]
This proves soundness of every positive certificate on the broad fiber.

Now assume also \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\). Exact saturated projection gives \(\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)=K\). Uniform strict dominance on that fiber is therefore equivalent to \(gq>0\) for every \(q\in K\). Compactness makes the latter equivalent to
\[
\min_{q\in K}gq>0,
\]
and dual attainment makes this equivalent to a feasible \(\eta\) with \(m_D^\top\eta>0\).

If \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\), an attained minimizer \(q_-\) has \(gq_-<0\). Exact saturated projection realizes \(q_-\) by an original-alphabet family in \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\), and positivity of the common denominator gives \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\). If additionally \(\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)>0\), an attained maximizer \(q_+\) is realized in the same way and has the opposite strict ranking. Both families induce the same supplied menu because they belong to the same compatibility fiber. Without exact projection, a row-face optimizer need not be a graph-compatible SCM law, so no converse lifting follows.
