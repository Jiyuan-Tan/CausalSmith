Under the frozen transport assumptions, district closure has no nontrivial strictly weaker graph-only replacement for a partially selected district.  More precisely, omit district closure while retaining district-kernel invariance exactly as stated, and let \(0<|D\cap\Delta_s|<|D|\).  Then every nonconstant binary incidence row on \(\Omega_D\) admits two finite original-alphabet SCM families having identical supplied source laws but different target values of that row.  Hence, based on a source row alone, the universally transportable rows are exactly the constant rows.  If one additionally assumes invariance of the marginal response law on \(I=D\setminus\Delta_s\), then a row transports for every compatible pair if and only if it is constant on every fiber of the coordinate projection \(\Omega_D\to\Omega_I\); this condition is decidable by finite enumeration, and failure gives a two-point leakage certificate.
