Write \(K=\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), \(r=r_D^\dagger\), and \(g=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\). The set \(K\) is a nonempty compact convex polytope. Its image under the linear map \(q\mapsto rq\) is therefore a nonempty compact convex subset of \(\mathbb R\), hence a closed interval \(I_D\). For each \(p\in I_D\),
\[
K(p)=\{q\in K:rq=p\}
\]
is nonempty and compact, so the defining minimum is attained.

For \(p_0,p_1\in I_D\), choose minimizers \(q_0\in K(p_0)\) and \(q_1\in K(p_1)\). If \(0\leq t\leq1\), then \(tq_0+(1-t)q_1\in K(tp_0+(1-t)p_1)\), and consequently
\[
\Gamma(tp_0+(1-t)p_1;m_D)
\leq t\Gamma(p_0;m_D)+(1-t)\Gamma(p_1;m_D).
\]
Thus the value function is convex.

The row-augmented primal has equality matrix obtained by appending \(r\) to \(A_D\) and right side \((m_D,p)\). Strong duality gives
\[
\Gamma(p;m_D)
=\max_{(\eta,\beta):A_D^\top\eta+\beta r^\top\leq g^\top}
\{m_D^\top\eta+\beta p\}.
\]
The feasible dual polyhedron is independent of \((m_D,p)\) and has finitely many rational inequalities. Its lineality directions have zero objective whenever the optimum is finite. After quotienting that lineality, begin with an attained optimum and move within its optimal face until the active constraint normals span the quotient space; otherwise a nonzero feasible direction in their common nullspace permits a further move. The resulting optimum is represented by a feasible basis. There are finitely many subsets of constraint rows, and every basis solves a nonsingular rational subsystem, so each associated \((\eta_B,\beta_B)\) is rational. Consequently
\[
\Gamma(p;m_D)=\max_B\{m_D^\top\eta_B+\beta_Bp\}
\]
over the finite relevant feasible bases. Thus every piece has rational slope and an intercept that is a rational linear, hence rational affine, function of \(m_D\). The value function is finite, continuous, convex, and piecewise affine.

The sublevel set
\[
\{p\in I_D:\Gamma(p;m_D)\leq0\}
\]
is closed and convex. Within a real interval it is empty, a point, or a closed interval. Its relative complement is \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\), which has zero, one, or two relative boundaries. At a boundary \(p_0\), continuity gives \(\Gamma(p_0;m_D)=0\). Primal attainment supplies \(q_0\in K(p_0)\) with \(gq_0=0\). On an adjacent strict-pruning component, choose an affine dual piece active arbitrarily close to \(p_0\); finiteness lets one piece recur along a convergent sequence. Its feasibility is independent of \(p\), and its affine objective converges to zero at \(p_0\). It is therefore a zero-valued boundary certificate and is positive on the adjacent piece.

If the row-augmented menu at \(p_0\) has nonempty district-saturated compatibility fiber and satisfies \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\), exact saturated projection realizes \(q_0\) by an original-alphabet saturated compatible SCM. Because \(d_Dq_0>0\), its two conditional arm values are equal. Without those hypotheses, primal attainment establishes only a boundary point of the row-face relaxation.
