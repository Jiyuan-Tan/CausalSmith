Let \(\mathcal U\) be a finite set of physically realizable candidate experiments.  Each \(e\in\mathcal U\) discloses a finite block of complete typed output laws, including every cell of each declared finite output, whose probabilities are effectively rational or real algebraic, and has an effectively comparable cost \(c_e>0\).  For \(S\subseteq\mathcal U\), let \(\mathcal P_S\) be \(\mathcal P\) augmented by all complete output laws disclosed by experiments in \(S\), and define
\[
\Lambda_S=\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P_S).
\]
An admissible global acquisition instance is required to satisfy \(\Lambda_{\mathcal U}\ne\varnothing\).  For every \(S\subseteq\mathcal U\), use \(\operatorname{Pos}_G(S)\) from \(\mathrm{def:global\text{-}subset\text{-}positivity}\).

Apply the closed zero, mixed, and positive evidence tests of \(\mathrm{def:hybrid\text{-}result\text{-}grammar}\) to the full fiber \(\Lambda_{\mathcal U}\).  Uniform zero evidence or mixed zero and positive evidence returns the corresponding payload-free status and leaves the numerical full-menu conditional arm-ranking benchmark undefined.  Only when \(\operatorname{Pos}_G(\mathcal U)\) holds define
\[
n_\rho(\vartheta)
=\Phi_{B_\rho\cap E,\mathrm{can}}^\star(\vartheta^\star).
\]
For a subset \(S\), define its numerical possibly-optimal set only when \(\operatorname{Pos}_G(S)\) holds, by
\[
\operatorname{PO}_G(S)
=\left\{\rho\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}:
\exists\vartheta\in\Lambda_S\ \forall\sigma\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}},\ 
n_\rho(\vartheta)-n_\sigma(\vartheta)\geq0\right\}.
\]
Set
\[
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{full},G}=\operatorname{PO}_G(\mathcal U),
\qquad
f_G(S)=|\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}|-|\operatorname{PO}_G(S)|
\]
on positive subsets, and define the exact broad-model ex-post acquisition problem by
\[
\min_{S\subseteq\mathcal U}\sum_{e\in S}c_e
\quad\text{subject to}\quad
\operatorname{Pos}_G(S)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\operatorname{PO}_G(S)=\mathcal P_{\mathrm{full},G}.
\]
Thus the acquired full menu, rather than the unaugmented menu, is required to establish uniform conditional definability.
