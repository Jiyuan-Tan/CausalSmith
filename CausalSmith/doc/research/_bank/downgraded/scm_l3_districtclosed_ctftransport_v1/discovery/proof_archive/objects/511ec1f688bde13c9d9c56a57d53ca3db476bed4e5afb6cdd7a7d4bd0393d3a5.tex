Suppose \(\operatorname{Extract}_{s,a,D}(P_a^s)\Downarrow(K_{s,a,D},\kappa)\) and \(\mathsf{RowEligible}_{s,a,D}(h,c)\) is accepted.  Then
\[
N_{s,a,D,h,c}=u_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s,
\qquad
H_{s,a,D,h}=v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s,
\qquad
K_{s,a,D}(c\mid h)=
\frac{u_{s,a,D,h,c}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s}
{v_{s,a,D,h}^{\mathsf T}q_D^s},
\]
where the last division is legal because the row-eligibility certificate proves \(H_{s,a,D,h}>0\).  After, and only after, proving \(s=\star\) or \(D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing\), the first two equations hold with \(q_D^\star\), and the stack retains the two pairs \((u,N)\) and \((v,H)\), never the quotient as a row value.  If \(\mathsf{Local}_D(C)\Downarrow b_D\), then \(P_{M^\star}(C)=b_D^{\mathsf T}q_D^\star\); in particular this gives the \(n_{\rho,D}\) and \(d_D\) formulas only under their displayed locality judgments.  A failed extraction or rejected support-local checker yields no incidence row.
