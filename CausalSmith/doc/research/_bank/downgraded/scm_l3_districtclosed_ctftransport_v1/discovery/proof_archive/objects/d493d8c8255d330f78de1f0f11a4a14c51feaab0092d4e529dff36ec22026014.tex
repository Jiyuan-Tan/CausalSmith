Let the displayed \(\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\) exist. It is feasible for the dominance dual and has positive value. The transported-dominance theorem therefore gives
\[
Q_\sigma^\star(\mathbf M)>Q_\rho^\star(\mathbf M)\tag{1}
\]
for every compatible model on which the one-district common-evidence comparison is defined. Since \(\sigma\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\), equation (1) prevents \(\rho\) from maximizing over \(\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\) in any such model. Removing \(\rho\) consequently cannot remove a maximizing defined arm. If the common evidence is certified zero, recursion soundness returns the zero-evidence terminal and the conditional arm value is undefined; excluding that arm from ranking does not reinterpret undefinedness as a low reward.
