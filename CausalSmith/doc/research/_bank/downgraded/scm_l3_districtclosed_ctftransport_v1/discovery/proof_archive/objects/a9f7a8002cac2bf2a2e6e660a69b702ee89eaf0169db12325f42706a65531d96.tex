Given a \(\mathsf{CheckedLocalArms}_D\) record, \(\rho,\sigma\in\mathcal R_{\mathrm{arm}}\), and \(\gamma\in\mathbb R\), a menu-dual dominance certificate at level \(\gamma\) is a vector \(\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}\) satisfying
\[
A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}
\leq g_{\rho,\sigma,D}
\quad\text{coordinatewise},
\qquad
m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}\geq\gamma.
\]
It is not defined in the absence of the total locality and common-evidence record.
