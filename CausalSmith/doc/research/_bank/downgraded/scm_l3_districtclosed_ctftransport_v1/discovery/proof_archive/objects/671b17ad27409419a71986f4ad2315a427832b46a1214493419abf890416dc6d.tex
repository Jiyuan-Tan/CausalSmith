Suppose the two arm numerators and their common evidence have accepting locality judgments at one district \(D\), \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\), and \(d_D^{\mathsf T}q>0\) for every \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). Then
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=
\max_{\eta\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}}
\{m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta:A_D^{\mathsf T}\eta\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}\}.
\]
Every feasible dual vector with \(m_D^{\mathsf T}\eta>0\) certifies \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) in every model in the broad fiber \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\). If additionally \(\mathsf{BASep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds, this certificate condition is also necessary for uniform strict dominance on the focal-district-saturated fiber. Under this additional hypothesis, \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\) is attained by an original-alphabet compatible focal-district-saturated SCM with \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\), and \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0<\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)\) yields two such SCM families with opposite strict rankings while preserving every other district's graph-compatible restrictions. Nonemptiness of \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\) together with \(\mathsf{ASep}_D(\mathcal P)\), and hence with \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\), is sufficient for the exact clauses. Without baseline-averaged focal projection, the primal optimizers need not lift from the row face.
