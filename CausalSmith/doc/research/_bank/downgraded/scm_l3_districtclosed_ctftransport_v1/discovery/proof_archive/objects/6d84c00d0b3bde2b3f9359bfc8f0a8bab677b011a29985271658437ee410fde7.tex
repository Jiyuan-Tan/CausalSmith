Let \(\mathsf{QEFormula}\) be the finite syntax of quantifier-free Boolean combinations of polynomial sign tests with coefficients in the effectively presented field of real algebraic numbers generated by the supplied menu values.  Its denotation is the corresponding semialgebraic subset of the displayed free real variables.

The hybrid terminal grammar is exactly
\[
\begin{split}
\mathsf{HybridResult}::={}&
\operatorname{ID\_RAT}(F,\delta)
\mid\operatorname{UNCOND\_QE}(\chi_T,\chi_{\min},\chi_{\max},\mathsf{isSingleton})\\
&\mid\operatorname{ZERO\_EVIDENCE\_QE}(\chi_E)
\mid\operatorname{NOT\_UNIFORMLY\_DEFINED\_QE}(\chi_E)\\
&\mid\operatorname{COND\_QE}(\chi_Q,\chi_{\min},\chi_{\max},\mathsf{isSingleton}),
\end{split}
\tag{1}
\]
where every \(\chi\) is a \(\mathsf{QEFormula}\) and \(\mathsf{isSingleton}\) is the exact truth value of the associated closed first-order sentence.

The deterministic procedure \(\mathsf{DCTFTR\text{-}CAN}\) first runs \(\mathsf{DCTFTR}\).  A successful rational identification is returned as \(\operatorname{ID\_RAT}\).  Every other terminal invokes the graph-compatible canonical fiber
\[
S=\Theta_G^{\mathrm{can}}(\mathcal P).
\]
For an unconditional target polynomial \(p_T=\Phi_{T,\mathrm{can}}^\star\), quantifier elimination is applied to
\[
\exists x\,[x\in S\wedge t-p_T(x)=0]
\tag{2}
\]
to obtain the equivalent one-variable range formula \(\chi_T(t)\).  It also decides whether two distinct satisfying values exist.  Quantifier elimination applied to
\[
\chi_T(\ell)\wedge
\forall t\,[\chi_T(t)\Longrightarrow\ell\leq t]
\tag{3}
\]
and its reversed inequality yields the endpoint formulas \(\chi_{\min}(\ell)\) and \(\chi_{\max}(u)\).

For a conditional target put \(e=\Phi_{E,\mathrm{can}}^\star\) and \(n=\Phi_{B_\rho\cap E,\mathrm{can}}^\star\).  Quantifier elimination decides exactly the following mutually exclusive sentences:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\mathsf Z:&\neg\exists x\,[x\in S\wedge e(x)>0],\\
\mathsf N:&\exists x\,[x\in S\wedge e(x)=0]
\ \wedge\ \exists x\,[x\in S\wedge e(x)>0],\\
\mathsf P:&\neg\exists x\,[x\in S\wedge e(x)=0].
\end{array}
\tag{4}
\]
The first two yield the two evidence terminals in \((1)\).  Only in case \(\mathsf P\) is quantifier elimination applied to
\[
\exists x\,[x\in S\wedge e(x)>0\wedge e(x)t-n(x)=0]
\tag{5}
\]
to obtain \(\chi_Q(t)\), followed by the singleton decision and the two endpoint formulas constructed as in \((3)\).  The procedure returns formulas and decisions only: it does not return explicit endpoint values, algebraic samples, CAD traces, or endpoint SCM parameters.
