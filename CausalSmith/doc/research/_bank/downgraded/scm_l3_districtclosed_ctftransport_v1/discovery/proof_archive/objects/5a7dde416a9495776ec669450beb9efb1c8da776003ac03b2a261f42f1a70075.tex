Suppose \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\) and \(d_Dq>0\) for every \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). Then
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
=\max_{\eta\in\mathbb R^{\operatorname{rows}(A_D)}}
\{m_D^\top\eta:A_D^\top\eta\le g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^\top\}.
\]
Every feasible dual vector with \(m_D^\top\eta>0\) certifies \(Q_\sigma^\star>Q_\rho^\star\) in every SCM in the broad fiber \(\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\). If also \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\ne\varnothing\) and \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\) holds, then uniform strict dominance on \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\) is equivalent to the existence of such a positive dual certificate. Under those two additional hypotheses, \(\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0\) yields an original-alphabet district-saturated compatible SCM with \(Q_\sigma^\star<Q_\rho^\star\), and
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)<0<\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)
\]
yields two such SCM families, agreeing on every supplied law, with opposite strict arm rankings. Without exact saturated projection, no converse or primal-SCM lifting claim is made.
