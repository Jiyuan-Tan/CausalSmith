Suppose \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\) and \(d_Dq>0\) throughout \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\). For every realizable additional row \(r_D^\dagger\), the feasible set \(I_D\) is a closed interval and \(p\mapsto\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)\) is a finite convex piecewise-affine function. Its pieces may be obtained from feasible bases of the row-augmented dual program and have coefficients that are rational affine functions of \(m_D\). Hence \(\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^+\) is the complement in \(I_D\) of a closed convex subset, which is empty, a point, or a closed interval, and has zero, one, or two relative sign boundaries. Each boundary has a dual menu-weight certificate of value zero and a primal row-face law attaining equality; the adjacent strict-pruning side has a positive dual piece. If the row-augmented supplied menu at that boundary has nonempty \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat}}(\mathcal P)\) and satisfies \(\mathsf{Sep}_D(\mathcal P)\), the equality law is realized by a district-saturated SCM on the original alphabets \(\mathcal X\). Without those hypotheses, no SCM lifting of the row-face boundary is asserted.
