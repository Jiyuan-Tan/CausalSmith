Given a checked local-arm instance \(\mathfrak L_D\) containing \(\rho,\sigma\), a realizable row \(r_D^\dagger\), and nonempty \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\), define
\[
I_D=\{(r_D^\dagger)^{\mathsf T}q:q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\},
\]
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)=
\min_{\substack{q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\\(r_D^\dagger)^{\mathsf T}q=p}}
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q,\qquad
\mathcal J_{\rho\prec\sigma}^{+}
=\{p\in I_D:\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(p;m_D)>0\}.
\]
These objects are partial typed outputs and are undefined without the locality rows and common-denominator positivity certificate in \(\mathfrak L_D\).
