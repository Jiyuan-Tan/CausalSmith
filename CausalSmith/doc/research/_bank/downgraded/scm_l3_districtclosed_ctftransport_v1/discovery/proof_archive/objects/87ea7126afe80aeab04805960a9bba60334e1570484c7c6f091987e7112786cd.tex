Whenever the common numerator and evidence locality judgments accept at \(D\), define the compact-face endpoints
\[
\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=
\min\{g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q:q\geq0,\ A_Dq=m_D\},
\qquad
\overline\Gamma_{\rho\prec\sigma}(m_D)=
\max\{g_{\rho,\sigma,D}^{\mathsf T}q:q\geq0,\ A_Dq=m_D\}.
\]
They are defined only when \(\mathcal K_D(m_D)\ne\varnothing\); the normalization row makes that face a subset of the probability simplex.
