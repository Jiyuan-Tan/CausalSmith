\[
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{sat}}(G,\mathcal X,\mathcal S,\{\Delta_s\}_{s\in\mathcal S})
=\left\{\mathbf M\in\mathfrak M(G,\mathcal X,\mathcal S,\{\Delta_s\}_{s\in\mathcal S}):
\begin{array}{l}
\text{every district of \(G\) is bidirected-complete, and}\\
\text{\(\mathbf M\) has the district response-block representation}
\end{array}
\right\}.
\]
Thus this is a subclass of the broad district-closed model class; the clique and response-block conditions are saturated-only and are not assumptions on \(\mathfrak M\).
