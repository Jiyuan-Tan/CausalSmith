For a fixed district \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), define
\[
\begin{split}
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{sat},D}(G,\mathcal X,\mathcal S,\{\Delta_s\}_{s\in\mathcal S})
=\bigl\{\mathbf M\in\mathfrak M(G,\mathcal X,\mathcal S,\{\Delta_s\}_{s\in\mathcal S}):{}&
G[D]\text{ is bidirected complete};\\
&\text{for every }s\in\mathcal S\text{ there is exactly one finite latent block }
U_D^s\in\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\\
&\text{storing one full deterministic response map for each vertex in }D
\text{ at every allowed parent configuration};\\
&\text{each structural equation of a vertex in }D
\text{ reads the corresponding component of }U_D^s;\\
&U_D^s\text{ is independent of the exogenous blocks of every }D'\ne D;\\
&\text{every probability law on }\Omega_D^{\mathrm{all}}\text{ is permitted for }U_D^s,
\text{ subject only to equality with the target law}\\
&\text{when }D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing;\\
&\text{for each }D'\ne D\text{, all structural tables, latent-factor restrictions,}
\text{ and cross-domain equalities remain those of }\mathfrak M
\bigr\}.
\end{split}
\]
The phrase ``every probability law is permitted'' includes closure under replacing the law of \(U_D^\star\) and all its unchanged-source copies while holding all mechanisms and latent laws in districts \(D'\ne D\), and all changed-source copies of \(U_D\), fixed. Define
\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)
=\mathcal C(\mathcal P)\cap
\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{sat},D}(G,\mathcal X,\mathcal S,\{\Delta_s\}_{s\in\mathcal S})
\]
and
\[
\mathcal Q_D^{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)
=\{q_D^\star(\mathbf M):\mathbf M\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{sat},D}(\mathcal P)\}.
\]
