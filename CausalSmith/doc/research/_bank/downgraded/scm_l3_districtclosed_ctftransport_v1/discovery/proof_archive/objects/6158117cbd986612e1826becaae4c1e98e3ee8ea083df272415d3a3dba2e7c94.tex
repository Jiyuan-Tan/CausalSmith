Let \(q=q_D^\star(\mathbf M)\) for an arbitrary compatible model.  District-cell linearity and the stacking construction give \(q\in\mathcal K_D(m_D)\).  Feasibility of the stated dual vector gives
\[
g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q
\geq q^\top A_D^\top\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}
=m_D^\top\eta_{\rho,\sigma,D}>0.
\]
Because \(n_{\rho,D}\) and \(n_{\sigma,D}\) are incidence rows for subevents of the common evidence event,
\[
0\leq n_{\rho,D}\leq d_D,
\qquad
0\leq n_{\sigma,D}\leq d_D
\]
coordinatewise.  Hence
\[
d_Dq\geq n_{\sigma,D}q
=g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q+n_{\rho,D}q>0.
\]
Both conditionals are therefore defined, and
\[
Q_\sigma(q)-Q_\rho(q)
=\frac{g_{\rho,\sigma,D}q}{d_Dq}>0.
\]
Thus \(\rho\) is strictly dominated by the available arm \(\sigma\) in every compatible model and cannot be maximizing; removing it cannot remove a maximizing arm.  If instead an independent certificate proves that the common evidence has probability zero, recursion soundness declares the conditional undefined.  Exclusion in that case is a domain-of-definition decision, not a ranking of an undefined reward.
