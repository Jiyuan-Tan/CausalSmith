For each \(s\in\mathcal S\), let \(\theta^s=(\mu^s,p^s)\) range over \(\operatorname{Can}(G,\mathcal X)\), the finite canonical SCM parameterization obtained by enumerating the structural tables compatible with \(G\) and the declared observed alphabets.  Its coordinates obey
\[
 p_{U}^{s}(u)\geq 0,\qquad \sum_{u\in\Omega_U}p_U^s(u)=1,
 \qquad
 \mu_{V,v\mid pa,u_V}^{s}\in\{0,1\},\qquad
 \sum_{v\in\mathcal X_V}\mu_{V,v\mid pa,u_V}^{s}=1.
\]
The canonical exogenous variables are assigned to their graph-compatible district blocks, and blocks belonging to distinct districts have product law.  Thus the displayed constraints include recursive compatibility with \(G\) and district factorization, while retaining the original alphabet \(\mathcal X_V\) at every observed vertex.

For any finite counterfactual event \(C\) in domain \(s\), recursively evaluate the one-hot tables in topological order and define the cell polynomial
\[
 \Phi_C^s(\theta^s)
 =\sum_{u\in\prod_U\Omega_U}
   \mathbf 1\{C\text{ is true under }(\mu^s,u)\}
   \prod_U p_U^s(u_U).
\]
The indicator is itself a finite polynomial in the one-hot coordinates by recursive substitution.  For every \(D\in\mathcal D(G)\), let \(q_D^s(\omega_D;\theta^s)\) be the analogous polynomial obtained by summing the same monomials over the canonical exogenous assignments whose induced structural response profile on \(D\) is \(\omega_D\).

Writing \(C_{a,v}\) for the event that descriptor \(a\) returns \(v\in\mathcal X_{W_a}\), define the exact global canonical fiber by
\[
\begin{split}
 \Theta(\mathcal P)=\bigl\{\theta=(\theta^s:s\in\mathcal S):{}&
 \theta^s\in\operatorname{Can}(G,\mathcal X)\quad(s\in\mathcal S),\\
 &q_D^s(\cdot;\theta^s)=q_D^\star(\cdot;\theta^\star)
   \quad\text{whenever }D\cap\Delta_s=\varnothing,\\
 &\Phi_{C_{a,v}}^s(\theta^s)=P_a^s(v)
   \quad\text{for every }s\in\mathcal S,\ a\in\mathcal A_s,
   \ v\in\mathcal X_{W_a}\bigr\}.
\end{split}
\]
All graph, one-hot, simplex, district-factorization, invariance, and complete supplied-law constraints are retained.  For a target-domain counterfactual cell \(T\), set
\[
 L_T(\mathcal P)=\min_{\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)}\Phi_T^\star(\theta^\star),
 \qquad
 U_T(\mathcal P)=\max_{\theta\in\Theta(\mathcal P)}\Phi_T^\star(\theta^\star),
\]
and for two arms with common evidence define
\[
 \mathcal G_{\rho,\sigma}(\theta)
 =\Phi_{B_\sigma\cap E}^\star(\theta^\star)
  -\Phi_{B_\rho\cap E}^\star(\theta^\star).
