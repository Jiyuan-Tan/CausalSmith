%%% FIELD proof-gram-threshold
Fix a realization of the training-fold fit and an \(x\) in the \(P_X\)-full-measure set on which the projection-Gram gap holds.  Suppress \(x\) from the notation, put \(p=2K^2\), and order the eigenvalues of \(G\) and \(\widehat G\) as
\[
 \lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\lambda_p\geq0,
 \qquad
 \widehat\lambda_1\geq\cdots\geq\widehat\lambda_p\geq0.
\]
Let \(\delta=\|\widehat G-G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\).  Symmetry gives, for every unit vector \(v\),
\[
 \bigl|v^{\mathsf T}(\widehat G-G)v\bigr|\leq\delta. \tag{1}
\]
We first derive the required eigenvalue perturbation bound directly.  For \(1\leq s\leq p\), let \(E_s\) be the span of eigenvectors of \(G\) with indices \(s,\ldots,p\), and let \(\widehat F_s\) be the span of eigenvectors of \(\widehat G\) with indices \(1,\ldots,s\).  Since
\[
 \dim(E_s)+\dim(\widehat F_s)=(p-s+1)+s=p+1,
\]
their intersection contains a unit vector \(u\).  The spectral decompositions then imply
\[
 u^{\mathsf T}Gu\leq\lambda_s,
 \qquad
 u^{\mathsf T}\widehat Gu\geq\widehat\lambda_s.
\]
Consequently, (1) yields
\[
 \widehat\lambda_s-\lambda_s
 \leq u^{\mathsf T}(\widehat G-G)u
 \leq\delta.
\]
Interchanging \(G\) and \(\widehat G\), and intersecting the top \(s\)-dimensional eigenspace of \(G\) with the bottom \((p-s+1)\)-dimensional eigenspace of \(\widehat G\), gives the reverse inequality.  Hence
\[
 |\widehat\lambda_s-\lambda_s|\leq\delta,
 \qquad 1\leq s\leq p. \tag{2}
\]

Write \(r=\operatorname{rank}(G)\).  By positive semidefiniteness and Assumption~\(\mathrm{ass{:}projection\mbox{-}gram\mbox{-}gap}\),
\[
 \lambda_s\geq\varepsilon\quad(1\leq s\leq r),
 \qquad
 \lambda_s=0\quad(r<s\leq p). \tag{3}
\]
On the event \(\delta<\varepsilon/4\), equations (2)--(3), together with \(\tau_G=\varepsilon/2\) from Definition~\(\mathrm{def{:}spectral\mbox{-}thresholded\mbox{-}gram\mbox{-}inverse}\), give
\[
 \widehat\lambda_s>3\varepsilon/4>\tau_G
 \quad(1\leq s\leq r),
 \qquad
 \widehat\lambda_s<\varepsilon/4<\tau_G
 \quad(r<s\leq p). \tag{4}
\]
Thus exactly \(r\) fitted eigenvalues are retained, although \(r\) is not supplied to the construction.

Define the thresholded fitted Gram matrix
\[
 \widetilde G
 =\sum_{s:\,\widehat\lambda_s>\tau_G}
   \widehat\lambda_s\widehat v_s\widehat v_s^{\mathsf T}.
\]
By the spectral definition of the fitted generalized inverse,
\[
 \widehat G_{\tau_G}^{\dagger}=\widetilde G^+ . \tag{5}
\]
On the event in (4), every discarded fitted eigenvalue is at most \(\delta\).  Therefore
\[
 \|\widetilde G-\widehat G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\delta,
 \qquad
 \|\widetilde G-G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq2\delta. \tag{6}
\]
If \(r>0\), (4) says that the smallest nonzero eigenvalue of \(\widetilde G\) exceeds \(3\varepsilon/4\); if \(r=0\), then \(\widetilde G=0\).  In either case, (3)--(4) imply
\[
 \|\widetilde G^+\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq2\varepsilon^{-1},
 \qquad
 \|G^+\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\varepsilon^{-1}. \tag{7}
\]

For completeness, the pseudoinverse perturbation step follows from elementary Moore--Penrose algebra.  For symmetric matrices \(H\) and \(A_0\), let \(P_H=H^+H=HH^+\) and \(P_{A_0}=A_0^+A_0=A_0A_0^+\).  Expanding the right-hand side and using \(A_0(I-P_{A_0})=0\), \((I-P_H)H=0\), \(H^{+2}H=H^+\), and \(A_0A_0^{+2}=A_0^+\) proves the exact identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
 H^+-A_0^+
 ={}&-H^+(H-A_0)A_0^+
     +H^{+2}(H-A_0)(I-A_0A_0^+)\\
    &+(I-H^+H)(H-A_0)A_0^{+2}. \tag{8}
\end{aligned}
\]
Indeed, the three terms on the right of (8) reduce respectively to
\[
 -P_HA_0^+ + H^+P_{A_0},
 \qquad
 H^+(I-P_{A_0}),
 \qquad
 -(I-P_H)A_0^+,
\]
whose sum is \(H^+-A_0^+\).

Apply (8) with \(H=\widetilde G\) and \(A_0=G\).  The matrices \(I-GG^+\) and \(I-\widetilde G^+\widetilde G\) are orthogonal projections and hence have operator norm at most one.  By (6)--(7), the operator norms of the three terms in (8) are bounded in order by
\[
 (2\varepsilon^{-1})(2\delta)(\varepsilon^{-1})
     =4\varepsilon^{-2}\delta,
\]
\[
 (2\varepsilon^{-1})^2(2\delta)
     =8\varepsilon^{-2}\delta,
 \qquad
 (2\delta)(\varepsilon^{-1})^2
     =2\varepsilon^{-2}\delta.
\]
Combining these inequalities with (5) proves the pointwise bound
\[
 \|\widehat G_{\tau_G}^{\dagger}-G^+\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq14\varepsilon^{-2}\delta
 \quad\text{whenever }\delta<\varepsilon/4. \tag{9}
\]

It remains to obtain the unconditional integrated inequality.  Let
\[
 \mathcal E=\{x:\delta(x)<\varepsilon/4\}.
\]
The threshold rule always gives
\[
 \|\widehat G_{\tau_G}^{\dagger}(x)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq\tau_G^{-1}=2\varepsilon^{-1},
\]
while (3) gives \(\|G(x)^+\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\varepsilon^{-1}\).  Hence, on \(\mathcal E^c\),
\[
 \|\widehat G_{\tau_G}^{\dagger}(x)-G(x)^+\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq3\varepsilon^{-1}. \tag{10}
\]
Markov's inequality applied to the nonnegative function \(\delta^2\) gives
\[
 P_X(\mathcal E^c)^{1/2}
 \leq4\varepsilon^{-1}
       \|\delta\|_{L^2(P_X)}. \tag{11}
\]
Using (9) on \(\mathcal E\), (10)--(11) on \(\mathcal E^c\), and the triangle inequality in \(L^2(P_X)\), we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \|\widehat G_{\tau_G}^{\dagger}-G^+\|_{L^2(P_X;\mathrm{op})}
 &\leq14\varepsilon^{-2}\|\delta\|_{L^2(P_X)}
     +3\varepsilon^{-1}P_X(\mathcal E^c)^{1/2}\\
 &\leq26\varepsilon^{-2}
       \|\widehat G-G\|_{L^2(P_X;\mathrm{op})}. \tag{12}
\end{aligned}
\]

Finally, by the definition of the essential-supremum error coordinate,
\[
 r_{G,\infty}=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{x}\delta(x).
\]
On \(\{r_{G,\infty}<\varepsilon/4\}\), equation (4) holds for \(P_X\)-almost every \(x\), so the selected rank equals \(\operatorname{rank}\{G(x)\}\) there.  If \(r_{G,\infty}=o_P(1)\), then
\[
 \Pr\{r_{G,\infty}<\varepsilon/4\}\longrightarrow1,
\]
which proves the asserted high-probability rank-selection conclusion.
