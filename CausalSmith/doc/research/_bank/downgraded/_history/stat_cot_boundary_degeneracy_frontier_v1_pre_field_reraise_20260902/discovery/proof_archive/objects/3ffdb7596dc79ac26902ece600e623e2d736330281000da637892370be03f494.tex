For \(d\ge1\), opposite-fold joint and marginal wavelet projections are conditionally normalized and clipped to fixed positive bounds. Their averaged conditional density enters a zero-outcome-mean ridge Galerkin inverse, which defines the observed Hessian, first-order score, diagonal-free kernel, and foldwise variance estimator on every sample. The point-estimator resolutions balance \(2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y}\), \(2^{-2s_zJ_z}\), and \(2^{dJ_z/2}/n\). Full covariance estimation is used only for an observed spectral proxy. For \(d=0\), the separate branch uses the full empirical quantile processes.
