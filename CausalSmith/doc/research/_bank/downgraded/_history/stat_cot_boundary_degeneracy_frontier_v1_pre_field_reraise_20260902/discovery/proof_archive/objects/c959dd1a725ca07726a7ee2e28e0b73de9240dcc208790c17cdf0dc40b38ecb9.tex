The corrected population theory supplies the deterministic envelope
\[
C_B\{2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y}+\mathbf 1\{d\ge1\}2^{-2s_zJ_z}\},

the growing-rank Gaussian-quadratic approximation and forced null Gaussianization for \(d\ge1\), the separate regular studentized central limit theorem, and the exact \(d=0\) quadratic-process law.  It does not define a common-multiplier statistic, a conditional critical value, or \(\mathcal I_n^{\mathrm{mix}}\).  The population bounded-Lipschitz approximation therefore cannot by itself imply uniform conditional quantile validity.  A solution still requires a joint conditional multiplier coupling for the linear and diagonal-free quadratic summands, shift-weighted pilot-Hessian and covariance control, uniform spectral-tail truncation, anti-concentration as the linear variance vanishes, and inversion after adding the displayed deterministic bias envelope.
