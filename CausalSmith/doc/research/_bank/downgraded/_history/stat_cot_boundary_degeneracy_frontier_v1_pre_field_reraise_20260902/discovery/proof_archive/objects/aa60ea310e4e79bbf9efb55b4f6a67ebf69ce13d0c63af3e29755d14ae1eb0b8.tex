Can the mixed linear--quadratic calibration handle construct \(\mathcal I_n^{\mathrm{mix}}\) such that
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{0\le\rho\le1}\inf_{(P_{0,n},P_{1,n})\in\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)}P\{\theta_n\in\mathcal I_n^{\mathrm{mix}}\}\ge1-\alpha,
\]
with expected half-width, at the corrected resolutions for \(d\ge1\), of order
\[
\rho n^{-1/2}
+n^{-2s_z/(2s_z+d)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{d/(2s_z+d)}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2,
\]
and of order \(\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-1}\) for \(d=0\)?  At each fixed unequal law, can the same construction attain the regular Gaussian width whenever the corrected pilot remainder is \(o(n^{-1/2})\)? The construction must derive from the primitive model class uniform spectral-tail, anti-concentration, common-multiplier coupling, observable pilot-Hessian and covariance control, and a valid allowance for the corrected pilot remainder
\[
C_B\left[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+\mathbf 1\{d\ge1\}2^{-s_zJ_z}\}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
\right].
\]
No centering by \(B^y_{J_y,n}+B^z_{J_z,n}\) is permitted.
