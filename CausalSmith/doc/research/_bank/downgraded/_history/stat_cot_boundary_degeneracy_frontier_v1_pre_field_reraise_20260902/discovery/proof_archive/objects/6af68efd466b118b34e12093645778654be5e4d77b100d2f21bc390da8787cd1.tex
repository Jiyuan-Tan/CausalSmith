For \(d\ge1\), balance the corrected pilot-error projection remainder with the canonical quadratic fluctuation by choosing
\[
2^{J_z^*}\asymp\{n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\}^{2/(2s_z+d)},
\qquad
2^{J_y^*}\asymp
\{n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\}^{2s_z/\{(s_y+1)(2s_z+d)\}}.
\]
Then
\[
\frac{2^{dJ_z^*/2}}n
\asymp
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}2^{-s_zJ_z^*}
\asymp
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}2^{-(s_y+1)J_y^*}
\asymp
n^{-2s_z/(2s_z+d)}
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{d/(2s_z+d)},
\]
and
\[
D_{J_y^*,J_z^*}
=\{n\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\}^{\{2s_z/(s_y+1)+2d\}/(2s_z+d)+o(1)}
=o(n^2).
\]
These resolutions optimize the proved observable pilot-error envelope.  They are not obtained by treating the true-discrepancy energy gaps \(B^y_{J_y,n}\) and \(B^z_{J_z,n}\) as estimator biases.
