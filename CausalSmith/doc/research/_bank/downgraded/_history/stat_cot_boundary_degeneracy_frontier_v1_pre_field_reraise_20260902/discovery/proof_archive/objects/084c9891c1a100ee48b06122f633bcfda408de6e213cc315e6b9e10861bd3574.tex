When \(d=0\), define the empirical distribution function in arm \(a\) by \(\widehat F_{a,n}(y)=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf 1\{Y_{a,i,n}\le y\}\) and its left-continuous generalized inverse by \(\widehat Q_{a,n}(u)=\inf\{y\in[0,1]:\widehat F_{a,n}(y)\ge u\}\). Let \(V^Q_{J_y}\) be the span of all boundary scaling functions and wavelets from \(\Psi_y\) through level \(J_y\), with the constant scaling function retained, and let \(P^Q_{J_y}\) be the \(L^2[0,1]\)-orthogonal projector onto \(V^Q_{J_y}\). Define the full-sample statistic
\[
\widehat\theta^0_{J_y,n}=\int_0^1\left[P^Q_{J_y}\{\widehat Q_{1,n}-\widehat Q_{0,n}\}(u)\right]^2du .
\]
Choose \(J_y^0\) so that \(2^{J_y^0}\to\infty\), \(2^{J_y^0}=o(n)\), and \(2^{-2(s_y+1)J_y^0}=o(n^{-1})\). The square retains its diagonal term. Define \(\mathfrak R_{n,0}(s_y,s_z,\rho)=\min\{\rho^2,\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-1}\}\).
