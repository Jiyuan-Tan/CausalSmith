Put \(a_y=\min\{s_y,1\}\), \(e=\beta/(2\beta+1)\), \(b=4s_z/(4s_z+d)\), and \(\kappa=2/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)\). For \(d\ge1\), uniform absorption of the H\"older-valid pilot remainder over the declared local-radius range means
\[
\sup_{0\le\rho\le1}
\frac{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho^{a_y\kappa}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2}
{\rho n^{-1/2}+n^{-b}}=O(1).
\]
This absorption holds, with strict exponent inequalities forced by the logarithmic pilot factor, if and only if
\[
s_z>\frac{3d}{4}
\quad\text{and}\quad
s_y>
\frac{s_z\!\left[11d+4s_z+\sqrt{(4s_z-d)(100s_z+23d)}\right]}
{2(4s_z-3d)(2s_z+d)}.
\]
Equivalently, the second condition is
\[
(4s_z-3d)(2s_z+d)s_y^2-s_z(11d+4s_z)s_y-12s_z^2>0.
\]
Consequently, throughout exactly this region the Gram-corrected local upper bound in \(\mathrm{thm{:}conditional\text{-}minimax\text{-}frontier}\) attains the intrinsic frontier uniformly in \(0\le\rho\le1\). Thus \(s_z=d/4\) is the intrinsic minimax elbow, whereas uniform sharpness of the present observable pilot construction begins only beyond \(s_z=3d/4\) and the displayed outcome-smoothness threshold.

There are two supplemental global estimators. First, for every fixed \(0<\tau<\min\{s_y,s_z\}\), put \(a_\tau=\tau/(2\tau+d+1)\) and use the explicit blockwise aligned wavelet construction in \(\mathrm{def{:}smoothcot\text{-}median\text{-}estimator}\). Its median satisfies, uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)\),
\[
\left\{E(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{sm,med}}_{\tau,n}-\theta_n)^2\right\}^{1/2}
\lesssim_\tau n^{-a_\tau}(\log n)^{a_\tau}.
\]
Second, when \(s_z\ge1\), choose an odd \(K_n\asymp\log n\), split each arm into \(K_n\) disjoint blocks of size \(N_n=\lfloor n/K_n\rfloor\), and on every block use covariate cells of side \(N_n^{-1/(d+2)}\). Project each covariate to its cell center; at each occupied center form the empirical conditional outcome law; optimally couple the two projected empirical covariate marginals in Euclidean distance; integrate the squared Wasserstein distance between paired empirical conditional outcome laws; and take the median of the block values. Then
\[
\left\{E(\widehat\theta_n^{\mathrm{dir,med}}-\theta_n)^2\right\}^{1/2}
\lesssim n^{-1/(d+2)}(\log n)^{1+1/(d+2)}.
\]
Accordingly, the upper side of the minimax bracket is the minimum of \(\rho^2\), the theorem's Gram-corrected pilot envelope, and the SmoothCOT envelope for every fixed admissible \(\tau\), with the direct-COT envelope inserted when \(s_z\ge1\). These additions sharpen an otherwise pilot-dominated upper bracket but do not enlarge the exact uniform matching region: \(a_\tau<\min\{b,1/2\}\), and, for \(s_z\ge1\), \(1/(d+2)<\min\{b,1/2\}\). Outside the displayed pilot-feasibility region the sharpened bracket is the conclusion, and equality of its two sides is not asserted.
