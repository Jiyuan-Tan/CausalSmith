Under the uniform second-order expansion, use \(S_n=A_{J_y,J_z,n}+\sqrt{nV_{L,n}}\). The primary conclusion is the \(n\)-dependent triangular approximation
\[
d_{\mathrm{BL}}\!\left(\mathcal L\left[\frac{n\{\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n-B^y_{J_y,n}-B^z_{J_z,n}\}}{S_n}\right],\mathcal L\left[\frac{1}{S_n}\sum_{e=1}^2\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}\{(\xi_{e,k}+\eta_{k,n})^2-(1+\eta_{k,n}^2)\}\right]\right)\to0.
\]
The spectrum is repeated twice, \(A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2=4\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}^2\), and \(\eta_{k,n}=2^{-1/2}v_{k,J_y,J_z,n}^{\top}\Gamma_{J_y,J_z,n}^{-1/2}\sqrt n\,\delta_{J_y,J_z,n}\). At equality all shifts vanish; if \(J_z\to\infty\), the anisotropic spectrum proposition gives \(n\mathbb U_{J_y,J_z,n}/A_{J_y,J_z,n}\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1)\). A centered Gaussian-chaos limit is asserted only along subsequences for which the padded ordered arrays \((\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}/A_{J_y,J_z,n})_k\) and \((\eta_{k,n})_k\) converge in \(\ell^2\); without that assumption the triangular proxy is the conclusion. If \(D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m=o(1)\), the observable pilot-control lemma permits replacement of the population kernel in the proxy by the average of the two observed \(\widehat K_{J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}\). In the regular zone, if \(\widehat V_{L,n}/V_{L,n}\to_P1\), \(\sqrt{nV_{L,n}}/A_{J_y,J_z,n}\to\infty\), and the displayed bias and remainder are \(o(\sqrt{V_{L,n}/n})\), then
\[
\frac{\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n}{\sqrt{\widehat V_{L,n}/n}}\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
For the same randomized-arm scalar-outcome specialization, this strictly tightens SmoothCOT Theorem 3.2 and Remark 3.2 by adjoining an equality-local triangular law to their regular CLT and faster-than-root-\(n\) equality statement.
