Under the uniform second-order expansion, suppose additionally that \(\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\to0\) and
\[
2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-2s_zJ_z}=O(S_n/n),
\qquad
S_n=A_{J_y,J_z,n}+\sqrt{nV_{L,n}}.
\]
Then the finite-\(n\) Gaussian-quadratic proxy based on the corrected raw-moment kernel satisfies
\[
d_{\mathrm{BL}}\!\left(
\mathcal L\left[\frac{n\{\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n-B^y_{J_y,n}-B^z_{J_z,n}\}}{S_n}\right],
\mathcal L\left[\frac1{S_n}\sum_{e=1}^2\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}
\{(\xi_{e,k}+\eta_{k,n})^2-(1+\eta_{k,n}^2)\}\right]
\right)\to0,
\]
where \(K_{J_y,J_z,n}=\Gamma_{J_y,J_z,n}^{1/2}B_{J_y,J_z,n}\Gamma_{J_y,J_z,n}^{1/2}\), the spectrum is repeated twice,
\[
A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2=4\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}^2,
\qquad
\eta_{k,n}=2^{-1/2}v_{k,J_y,J_z,n}^{\top}\Gamma_{J_y,J_z,n}^{-1/2}\sqrt n\,\delta_{J_y,J_z,n}.
\]
The same proxy and the normalized estimator are diffuse locally Gaussian:
\[
d_{\mathrm{BL}}\!\left(
\mathcal L\left[\frac{n\{\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n-B^y_{J_y,n}-B^z_{J_z,n}\}}{S_n}\right],
\mathcal N\!\left(0,\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2+nV_{L,n}}{S_n^2}\right)
\right)\to0.
\]
In particular, at equality all shifts and the linear projection vanish and
\[
\frac{n\mathbb U_{J_y,J_z,n}}{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
If \(D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m=o(1)\), the population kernel in the finite-\(n\) proxy may be replaced by the average observed kernel formed from \(\widehat B_{J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}\). Separately, at fixed unequal laws, if \(\widehat V_{L,n}/V_{L,n}\to_P1\), \(\sqrt{nV_{L,n}}/A_{J_y,J_z,n}\to\infty\), and the corrected bias and remainder are \(o(\sqrt{V_{L,n}/n})\), then
\[
\frac{\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n}{\sqrt{\widehat V_{L,n}/n}}\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
No fixed or subsequential non-Gaussian chaos is asserted in this growing-covariate-resolution regime.
