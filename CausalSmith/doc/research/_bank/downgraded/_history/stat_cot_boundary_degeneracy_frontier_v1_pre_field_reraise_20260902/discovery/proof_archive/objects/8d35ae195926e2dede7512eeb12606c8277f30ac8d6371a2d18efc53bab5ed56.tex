Under the proposed uniform second-order expansion, suppose additionally that \(\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\to0\) and
\[
2^{-2(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-2s_zJ_z}=O(S_n/n),
\qquad S_n=A_{J_y,J_z,n}+\sqrt{nV_{L,n}}.
\]
Then the \(n\)-dependent triangular approximation
\[
d_{\mathrm{BL}}\!\left(\mathcal L\left[\frac{n\{\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n-B^y_{J_y,n}-B^z_{J_z,n}\}}{S_n}\right],\mathcal L\left[\frac{1}{S_n}\sum_{e=1}^2\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}\{(\xi_{e,k}+\eta_{k,n})^2-(1+\eta_{k,n}^2)\}\right]\right)\to0
\]
holds, where the spectrum is repeated twice, \(A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2=4\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}^2\), and \(\eta_{k,n}=2^{-1/2}v_{k,J_y,J_z,n}^{\top}\Gamma_{J_y,J_z,n}^{-1/2}\sqrt n\,\delta_{J_y,J_z,n}\). At equality all shifts vanish; if \(J_z\to\infty\), then \(n\mathbb U_{J_y,J_z,n}/A_{J_y,J_z,n}\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1)\). A centered Gaussian-chaos limit is asserted only along subsequences for which the padded ordered arrays \((\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}/A_{J_y,J_z,n})_k\) and \((\eta_{k,n})_k\) converge in \(\ell^2\). If \(D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m=o(1)\), the population kernel may be replaced by the average observed kernel. Separately, at fixed unequal laws, if \(\widehat V_{L,n}/V_{L,n}\to_P1\), \(\sqrt{nV_{L,n}}/A_{J_y,J_z,n}\to\infty\), and the displayed bias and remainder are \(o(\sqrt{V_{L,n}/n})\), then
\[
\frac{\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-\theta_n}{\sqrt{\widehat V_{L,n}/n}}\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1).
\]
Thus the triangular noncentral representation is equality-local, whereas the regular Gaussian conclusion remains valid at fixed unequal laws.
