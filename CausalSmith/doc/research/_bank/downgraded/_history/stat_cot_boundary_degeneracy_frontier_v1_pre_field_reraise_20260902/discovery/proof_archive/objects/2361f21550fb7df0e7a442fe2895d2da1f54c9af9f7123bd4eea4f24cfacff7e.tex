Let \(E_{J,n}=C_B\{2^{-(2s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-2s_zJ_z}\}\), let \(z_{1-\alpha/2}\) be the \(1-\alpha/2\) standard-normal quantile, and suppose the hypotheses of the established mixed-spectral transition hold for \(d\ge1\). Since \[\sigma_n^2=\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2+nV_{L,n}}{\{A_{J_y,J_z,n}+\sqrt{nV_{L,n}}\}^2}\in[1/2,1],\] the normal comparison there and the bounded normal density transfer the centered statistic's quantiles. Therefore the infeasible population-oracle interval \[\mathcal I_n^{\mathrm{or}}=\left[\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}-E_{J,n}-z_{1-\alpha/2}\frac{\sqrt{A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2+nV_{L,n}}}{n},\ \widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}+E_{J,n}+z_{1-\alpha/2}\frac{\sqrt{A_{J_y,J_z,n}^2+nV_{L,n}}}{n}\right]\] has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) whenever \(|B^y_{J_y,n}+B^z_{J_z,n}|\le E_{J,n}\). Its deterministic half-width is \[E_{J,n}+z_{1-\alpha/2}\left\{\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}{n}+\sqrt{\frac{V_{L,n}}{n}}\right\}\] up to fixed factors. At the rate-balancing resolutions, the established spectrum and bias bounds make this \(O\{r_{n,d}(s_z)+\rho_n n^{-1/2}\}\subseteq O\{r_{n,d}(s_z)+\rho n^{-1/2}\}\). At fixed unequal laws the quadratic and bias terms are negligible under the stated regular conditions, recovering the usual Gaussian half-width. The established no-covariate theorem separately supplies the exact uniform-null oracle law \(2\int_0^1\mathbb B(t)^2dt\). What remains unproved is replacing these population-oracle calibrations by one observable common-multiplier interval uniformly through equality, local, critical, and regular regimes.
