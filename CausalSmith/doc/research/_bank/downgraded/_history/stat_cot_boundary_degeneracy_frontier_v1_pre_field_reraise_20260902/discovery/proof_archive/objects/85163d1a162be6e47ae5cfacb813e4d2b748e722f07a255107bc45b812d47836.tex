Put \(p_{a,n}=q_{a,n}r_n\), \(\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\), and \(\bar D_n=2^{\bar J_y+d\bar J_z}\). Products preserve the declared anisotropic H\"older moduli, so the boundary-wavelet approximation bounds at the pilot resolutions are
\[
\max_a\|p_{a,n}-p_{a,n}^{\mathrm{proj}}\|_\infty
+\|r_n-r_n^{\mathrm{proj}}\|_\infty
\lesssim2^{-s_y\bar J_y}+2^{-s_z\bar J_z}\lesssim\varepsilon.   \tag{1}
\]
For every retained coefficient, expanding the exponential moment of its centered bounded empirical summands and using \(e^v-1-v\le v^2e^{|v|}/2\) gives Bernstein's bound. A union bound over \(O(\bar D_n)\) coefficients and finite overlap in the wavelet reconstruction yield, uniformly over the model class,
\[
P\{E_n>C(\varepsilon+x)\}\le C\exp(-cnx^2/\bar D_n),
\quad 0\le x\le1,                                                \tag{2}
\]
where
\[
E_n=\max_e\left\{\|\widetilde r_n^{(-e)}-r_n\|_\infty
+\max_a\|\widetilde p_{a,n}^{(-e)}-p_{a,n}\|_\infty\right\}.
\]
The tuning identities give \(\bar D_n\asymp(n/\log n)^{1/(2\beta+1)}\). Thus (2) implies \(E_n=O_P(\varepsilon)\), and tail integration gives \(\|E_n\|_{L^k}\le C_k\varepsilon\) for each fixed \(k\).

On \(\widetilde r\ge c/2\),
\[
\frac{\widetilde p}{\widetilde r}-\frac pr
=\frac{\widetilde p-p}{\widetilde r}
+p\frac{r-\widetilde r}{r\widetilde r}.                          \tag{3}
\]
Equations (2)--(3) and the density lower bounds prove the conditional-pilot rate and \(P(\cap_e\mathcal E_n^{(-e)})\to1\). The construction normalizes every conditional density, including off this event. Since \(T(\widetilde r)\in[c/2,2C]\),
\[
\frac c{4C}\le\widehat r_n^{(-e)}\le\frac{4C}{c}.              \tag{4}
\]
Lebesgue orthonormality gives the deterministic Gram bounds. Moreover,
\[
\|\widehat G^{(-e)}-G\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\le\|\widehat r_n^{(-e)}-r_n\|_\infty,
\qquad
\widehat G^{-1}-G^{-1}=\widehat G^{-1}(G-\widehat G)G^{-1},      \tag{5}
\]
which proves the Gram perturbation assertions.

Let \(A_z=-\partial_y(\bar q_n\partial_y)\), \(A_z^{\mathrm{op}}=P_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}A_zP_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}\), and use hats for the pilot forms. Normalized clipping gives \(c/(4C)\le\widehat{\bar q}_n\le4C/c\). This, the true density bounds, and the zero-mean inequality \(\|v\|_2\le\|v'\|_2\) give uniform coercivity. The weak forms and (2) imply
\[
\|\widehat A_z^{\mathrm{op}}-A_z^{\mathrm{op}}\|_{H^1/\mathbb R\to H^{-1}}
\lesssim E_n.                                                     \tag{6}
\]
The resolvent identity gives an \(O_P(E_n+\lambda_n)\) perturbation in energy norm. If \(u=A_z^{-1}f\), integration of the Neumann equation gives \(u'=-F/\bar q_n\), where \(F(y)=\int_0^yf(v)dv\). Since \(\bar q_n^{-1}\) is uniformly \(a_y\)-H\"older, boundary-wavelet approximation and Galerkin orthogonality give, for retained \(f,g\),
\[
|\langle f,\{(A_z^{\mathrm{op}})^{-1}P_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}-A_z^{-1}\}g\rangle|
\lesssim2^{-2a_yJ_y^{\mathrm{op}}}\|f\|_2\|g\|_2
\le\varepsilon^{a_y}\|f\|_2\|g\|_2.                           \tag{7}
\]
The last inequality is the declared oversampling rule. Integrating the form against \(\widehat r\), using (2), and expanding both Gram inverses proves
\[
\|\widehat B^{(-e)}-B\|_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_P(\varepsilon^{a_y}+\varepsilon+\lambda_n).                   \tag{8}
\]
The same calculation also gives the energy-relative bilinear estimate
\[
|x^\top(\widehat B-B)y|
\le O_P(\varepsilon+\lambda_n)(x^\top Bx)^{1/2}(y^\top By)^{1/2}
+C\varepsilon^{a_y}\|x\|_2\|y\|_2.                             \tag{9}
\]

Fix fold \(e\), condition on its opposite-fold sigma-field \(\mathscr F_{-e}\), and write \(\mu_a=E\Phi(W_{a,1,n})\), \(d_a=\mu_a-\widehat\mu_a\), \(d=d_1-d_0\), and \(Z_{a,i}=\Phi(W_{a,i,n})-\mu_a\). By \(\mathrm{ass{:}two\text{-}arm\text{-}sampling}\), the evaluation variables are conditionally independent and centered. Direct expansion of the diagonal-free correction yields
\[
\widehat U_e=U_e^{\mathrm{can}}(\widehat B)
+2\bar Z_{1,e}^\top\widehat Bd-2\bar Z_{0,e}^\top\widehat Bd+d^\top\widehat Bd,
\qquad
E(\widehat U_e\mid\mathscr F_{-e})=d^\top\widehat Bd.           \tag{10}
\]

For a conditional distribution function \(F\) perturbed by sup-norm at most \(E\), positivity gives a quantile displacement \(|w|\le E/c\), and the H\"older condition gives
\[
F(x+w)-F(x)-q(x)w
=\int_0^w\{q(x+t)-q(x)\}dt=O(|w|^{1+a_y}).                      \tag{11}
\]
Applying (11) to both arms, multiplying the squared quantile difference, and using exact linearity in the common marginal gives the conditional-mean remainder
\[
|R_{0,e}|\le C\{E_n^{2+a_y}+\|\Delta_n\|_\infty^{a_y}E_n^2\}. \tag{12}
\]
After one differentiation, the residual first-order coefficient \(g_{a,e}\), combining the displayed score and the two linear terms in (10), satisfies
\[
\sum_{a=0}^1\|g_{a,e}\|_{L^2(P_{a,n})}^2
\le C\{E_n^{2+2a_y}+\|\Delta_n\|_\infty^{2a_y}E_n^2\}+o_P(\rho_n^2). \tag{13}
\]
The little-oh term is the energy-relative part of (9) applied to the projected discrepancy.

The anisotropic H\"older modulus converts local sup-norm to inverse-elliptic radius. If \(A=\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\), amplitude \(A\) persists on an outcome interval of length proportional to \(A^{1/s_y}\) and a covariate rectangle of volume proportional to \(A^{d/s_z}\). Zero outcome mass makes the antiderivative have order \(A^{1+1/s_y}\) on a comparable interval. The identity \(\int\Delta_n\mathcal G_n\Delta_n=\int H_n^2/\bar q_n\) and positivity therefore imply
\[
\rho_n^2\gtrsim A^{2+3/s_y+d/s_z},
\qquad
\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\lesssim\rho_n^\kappa,
\quad \kappa=\frac{2}{2+3/s_y+d/s_z}.                            \tag{14}
\]

Conditional independence and (13) give
\[
E\!\left[\left\{m^{-1}\sum_{a,i\in I_{a,e}}g_{a,e}(W_{a,i,n})\right\}^2\middle|\mathscr F_{-e}\right]
\le\frac C m\{E_n^{2+2a_y}+\rho_n^{2a_y\kappa}E_n^2\}+o_P(\rho_n^2/n). \tag{15}
\]
Pair counting for the canonical kernel replacement gives
\[
E[\{U_e^{\mathrm{can}}(\widehat B)-U_e^{\mathrm{can}}(B)\}^2\mid\mathscr F_{-e}]
\le\frac C{m^2}\|\Gamma^{1/2}(\widehat B-B)\Gamma^{1/2}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2. \tag{16}
\]
At outcome level \(j\), (7)--(9) bound the Galerkin part by \(C\min\{2^{-2j},\varepsilon^{a_y}\}\). Hence
\[
\frac{\|\Gamma^{1/2}(\widehat B-B)\Gamma^{1/2}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2}{\|K\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2}
\lesssim O_P((\varepsilon+\lambda_n)^2)
+\sum_{j\ge j_0}2^j\min\{2^{-4j},\varepsilon^{2a_y}\}=o_P(1).  \tag{17}
\]
Equations (12), (14)--(17), the moment bound following (2), and \(\sqrt n\varepsilon\to\infty\) prove
\[
O_P\{\varepsilon^{2+a_y}+\rho_n^{a_y\kappa}\varepsilon^2\}
+o_P(1)\left\{\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n\right\}. \tag{18}
\]
Since \(a_y\kappa=2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)\), (18) is the displayed pilot contribution. Comparing it with the corrected deterministic envelope from \(\mathrm{prop{:}anisotropic\text{-}spectrum\text{-}bias}\) proves the revised negligibility condition.

Finally, compact support gives \(\|\Phi(W)\|_2^2\lesssim D_{J_y,J_z}\). Exponential Markov bounds for scalar quadratic forms on a \(1/4\)-net yield
\[
\max_e\|\widehat\Gamma^{(-e)}-\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_P\!\left(\sqrt{D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m}+D_{J_y,J_z}\log n/m\right)=o_P(1). \tag{19}
\]
Uniform positive definiteness makes the square-root map locally Lipschitz. Equations (8), (17), and (19) prove the operator and relative Hilbert--Schmidt consistency of \(\widehat K\). The condition in (19) is not used in (1)--(18), so it is needed only for the observed spectral plug-in.
