For \(d\ge1\), specialize Definition 4 and Theorem 4 of Lin, Gao, Blanchet, and Glynn to scalar outcomes, \(d\)-dimensional covariates, group sizes \(n_0,n_1\), and \(N=n_0\wedge n_1\).  If the outcome and covariate supports are compact, the transport objective is Lipschitz, both conditional kernels are Wasserstein--1 Lipschitz in the covariate, the two arm samples are independent and i.i.d. within arm, and the estimator's covariate coupling obeys
\[
E\!\left[\int\|z_0-z_1\|_2\,d\widehat\pi_{n_0,n_1}(z_0,z_1)\right]\le C_{\widehat\pi}N^{-1/(d+2)},
\]
then its adapted direct conditional-optimal-transport estimator and population value satisfy
\[
E|\widehat V_{n_0,n_1}-V_{\mathrm c}|\le \overline C N^{-1/(d+2)}\log N.
\]
