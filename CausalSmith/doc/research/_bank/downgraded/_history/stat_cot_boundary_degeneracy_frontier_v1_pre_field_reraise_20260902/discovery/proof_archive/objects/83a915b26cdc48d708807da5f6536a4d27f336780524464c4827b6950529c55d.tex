Fix \(d\ge1\), \(\tau>0\), and a block size \(N_n^{\mathrm{sm}}\). Suppose the two joint laws on \([0,1]^{d+1}\) have densities \(p_{a,n}\) bounded above and away from zero and belonging to \(\mathcal C^{\tau+\epsilon}([0,1]^{d+1})\) for some \(\epsilon>0\). For the squared-distance cost and the aligned wavelet COT block value \(\widehat V_{\ell,\tau,n}^{\mathrm{sm}}\) at resolution \(J_{\tau,n}^{\mathrm{sm}}=\lfloor\log_2(N_n^{\mathrm{sm}})/(2\tau+d+1)\rfloor\), there is a finite constant \(C\), depending on the two laws, their density bound, \(\tau\), and \(d\), such that
\[
E\left|\widehat V_{\ell,\tau,n}^{\mathrm{sm}}-\theta_n\right|
\le C(N_n^{\mathrm{sm}})^{-\tau/(2\tau+d+1)}.
\]
