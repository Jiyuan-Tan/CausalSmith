For \(d\ge1\), opposite-fold joint and marginal wavelet projections are conditionally normalized and clipped to fixed positive bounds.  The marginal pilot defines \(\widehat G_{R,J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}\), and the averaged conditional-density pilot enters an oversampled zero-outcome-mean ridge Galerkin inverse whose deterministic approximation error is below the H\"older-order pilot remainder.  The inverse-elliptic form matrix is converted to \(\widehat B_{J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}=\{\widehat G_{R,J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}\}^{-1}\widehat H_{\mathrm{form},J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}\{\widehat G_{R,J_y,J_z,n}^{(-e)}\}^{-1}\), which defines the diagonal-free kernel and observed spectral proxy.  The first-order score and foldwise variance estimator remain cross-fitted.  For \(d=0\), the separate branch uses the full empirical quantile processes.
