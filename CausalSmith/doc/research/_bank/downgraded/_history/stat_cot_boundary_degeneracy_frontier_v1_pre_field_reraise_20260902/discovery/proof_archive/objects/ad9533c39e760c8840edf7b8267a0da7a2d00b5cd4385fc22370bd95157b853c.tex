Uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)\), there are fixed constants \(0<c_H<C_H<\infty\) and \(0<c_\Gamma<C_\Gamma<\infty\) such that, if \(g=\sum_{(j_y,k_y,\gamma)\in\Lambda_{J_y,J_z}^{(d)}}x_{j_y,k_y,\gamma}\psi^y_{j_y,k_y}\psi^z_\gamma\), then \[c_H\sum_{j_y,k_y,\gamma}2^{-2j_y}x_{j_y,k_y,\gamma}^2\le\int_{\mathcal Z_d}\int_{\mathcal Y}g(y,z)\{\mathcal G_{n,z}g(\cdot,z)\}(y)dydR_n(z)\le C_H\sum_{j_y,k_y,\gamma}2^{-2j_y}x_{j_y,k_y,\gamma}^2,\] and \(c_\Gamma I\preceq\Gamma_{J_y,J_z,n}\preceq C_\Gamma I\). The min--max principle therefore transfers the globally ordered spectrum of the diagonal \(H_y^{-1}\) reference to \(K_{J_y,J_z,n}\): for \(2^{-2J_y}\le u\le1\) and \(1\le k\le D_{J_y,J_z}\), \[N_{J_y,J_z,n}(u)\asymp\min\{D_{J_y,J_z},2^{dJ_z}u^{-1/2}\},\qquad \nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}\asymp\min\{1,(2^{dJ_z}/k)^2\}.\] Consequently, for \(d\ge1\), \[\|K_{J_y,J_z,n}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\asymp1,\qquad \|K_{J_y,J_z,n}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\asymp2^{dJ_z},\qquad \frac{\max_k|\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}|}{\{\sum_k\nu_{k,J_y,J_z,n}^2\}^{1/2}}\lesssim2^{-dJ_z/2}.\] The same quadratic-form comparison and anisotropic approximation give \( |B^y_{J_y,n}|\lesssim2^{-2(s_y+1)J_y}\) and \( |B^z_{J_z,n}|\lesssim2^{-2s_zJ_z}\). When \(d=0\), the Hilbert--Schmidt norm remains bounded above and away from zero as \(J_y\to\infty\).
