If the standard interior condition \(L>1\) is added, the full mixture calculation closes.  Disjoint support and wavelet scaling give \(\|b_{n,\omega}\|_\infty\lesssim c_0 2^{-s_zJ_z}\) and \(\|b_{n,\omega}\|_{C^{s_z}}\lesssim c_0\); hence small fixed \(c_0\) gives positivity and H\"older membership.  Orthonormality gives \(\int b_{n,\omega}^2dz=\gamma_n^2M_{J_z}\), so the cosine lemma gives endpoint separation \(\gamma_n^2M_{J_z}/(8\pi^2)\{1+o(1)\}\) and inverse-elliptic radius \(O(\gamma_nM_{J_z}^{1/2})\le\rho\).  Under the null, the likelihood inner product of sign vectors \(\omega,\omega'\) is bounded by \(\exp\{Cn\gamma_n^2\sum_k\omega_k\omega'_k\}\).  Averaging independent Rademacher signs and using \(\cosh x\le e^{x^2/2}\) yields \(\chi^2\le\exp(Cn^2\gamma_n^4M_{J_z})-1\).  At \(2^{J_z}\asymp n^{2/(4s_z+d)}\), this exponent is bounded and \(\gamma_n^2M_{J_z}\asymp\min\{\rho^2,r_{n,d}(s_z)\}\).  The same likelihood Taylor calculation gives bounded product Hellinger distance for the fixed-frequency \(n^{-1/2}\) increments.
