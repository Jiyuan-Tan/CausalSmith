Put \(p_{a,n}=q_{a,n}r_n\).  Products preserve the declared anisotropic H\"older bounds, with constants depending only on \(C\) and \(L\).  The wavelet approximation bounds at the declared pilot resolutions are
\[
\max_a\|p_{a,n}-p_{a,n}^{\mathrm{proj}}\|_\infty
+\|r_n-r_n^{\mathrm{proj}}\|_\infty
\lesssim2^{-s_y\bar J_y}+2^{-s_z\bar J_z}\lesssim\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}. \tag{1}
\]
For every retained coefficient, the centered compactly supported wavelet summand is bounded and has variance of its natural wavelet order.  Exponential Markov's inequality followed by a union bound over \(O(2^{\bar J_y+d\bar J_z})\) coefficients, and finite overlap in the reconstruction, gives
\[
\max_{a,e}\|\widetilde p_{a,n}^{(-e)}-p_{a,n}\|_\infty
+\max_e\|\widetilde r_n^{(-e)}-r_n\|_\infty
=O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n})                                      \tag{2}
\]
uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{n,d}(s_y,s_z,\rho)\).  On \(\widetilde r\ge c/2\),
\[
\frac{\widetilde p}{\widetilde r}-\frac p r
=\frac{\widetilde p-p}{\widetilde r}
+p\frac{r-\widetilde r}{r\widetilde r}.                                \tag{3}
\]
Thus (2), (3), and \(r_n\ge c\) prove the conditional-pilot rate.  Since the true densities lie strictly inside the clipping bounds, they also give \(P(\cap_e\mathcal E_n^{(-e)})\to1\).  Clipping and normalization are locally Lipschitz there; everywhere, the displayed normalizations enforce positive marginal and unit conditional mass.

We next treat the correction that was absent from the uncorrected coordinate system.  Since \(T(\widetilde r)\in[c/2,2C]\) and its integral lies in the same interval, every realization satisfies
\[
\frac{c}{4C}\le\widehat r_n^{(-e)}(z)\le\frac{4C}{c}.                   \tag{4}
\]
Lebesgue orthonormality of the tensor features therefore gives, with fixed \(0<c_G<C_G<\infty\),
\[
c_GI\preceq G,\widehat G^{(-e)}\preceq C_GI.                             \tag{5}
\]
Furthermore, for every \(x\),
\[
|x^{\top}(\widehat G^{(-e)}-G)x|
\le\|\widehat r_n^{(-e)}-r_n\|_\infty
\int\{x^{\top}\Phi(y,z)\}^2,dy,dz
=\|\widehat r_n^{(-e)}-r_n\|_\infty\|x\|_2^2.                         \tag{6}
\]
Hence \(\|\widehat G^{(-e)}-G\|_{\mathrm{op}}=O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n})\).  The exact inverse identity
\[
(\widehat G^{(-e)})^{-1}-G^{-1}
=(\widehat G^{(-e)})^{-1}(G-\widehat G^{(-e)})G^{-1}                    \tag{7}
\]
and (5) prove the asserted dimension-free inverse perturbation bound.

Let \(A_z=-\partial_y(\bar q_n\partial_y)\), let \(A_z^{\mathrm{op}}=P_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}A_zP_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}\), and define \(\widehat A_z^{\mathrm{op},(-e)}\) with \(\widehat{\bar q}_n^{(-e)}\).  On the zero-mean subspace, Poincar\'e's inequality and ellipticity imply fixed coercivity.  The weak forms and (2) show
\[
\|\widehat A_z^{\mathrm{op},(-e)}-A_z^{\mathrm{op}}\|_{H^1/\mathbb R\to H^{-1}}
\lesssim\|\widehat{\bar q}_n^{(-e)}-\bar q_n\|_\infty.
\]
The resolvent identity
\[
(\widehat A_z^{\mathrm{op},(-e)}+\lambda_nI)^{-1}-(A_z^{\mathrm{op}})^{-1}
=(\widehat A_z^{\mathrm{op},(-e)}+\lambda_nI)^{-1}
\{A_z^{\mathrm{op}}-\widehat A_z^{\mathrm{op},(-e)}-\lambda_nI\}(A_z^{\mathrm{op}})^{-1} \tag{8}
\]
gives an \(O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}+\lambda_n)\) perturbation relative to the population Galerkin inverse.  To compare that inverse with \(\mathcal G_{n,z}=A_z^{-1}\), let \(u=A_z^{-1}f\) for \(f\) in the point-estimator space.  In one dimension, \(u'=-F/q\), where \(F(y)=\int_0^yf(v)\,dv\).  Since \(q^{-1}\) is uniformly \(a_y\)-H\"older and \(F\in H^1\), boundary-wavelet approximation gives
\[
\inf_{v\in P_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}H^1}\|u-v\|_{H^1}
\lesssim2^{-a_yJ_y^{\mathrm{op}}}\|f\|_2.
\]
Galerkin orthogonality and coercivity therefore imply, for retained \(f,g\),
\[
|\langle f,\{(A_z^{\mathrm{op}})^{-1}P_{J_y^{\mathrm{op}}}-A_z^{-1}\}g\rangle|
\lesssim2^{-2a_yJ_y^{\mathrm{op}}}\|f\|_2\|g\|_2
\le\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{a_y}\|f\|_2\|g\|_2.                  \tag{9}
\]
The last inequality is exactly the oversampling rule.  Integrating against \(\widehat r_n^{(-e)}\), and using (2), gives
\[
\|\widehat H_{\mathrm{form}}^{(-e)}-H^{\mathrm{form}}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{a_y}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}+\lambda_n). \tag{10}
\]
Expanding
\[
\widehat B-B
=(\widehat G^{-1}-G^{-1})\widehat H_{\mathrm{form}}\widehat G^{-1}
+G^{-1}(\widehat H_{\mathrm{form}}-H^{\mathrm{form}})\widehat G^{-1}
+G^{-1}H^{\mathrm{form}}(\widehat G^{-1}-G^{-1})                         \tag{11}
\]
and applying (5), (7), and (10) proves the displayed operator bound for \(\widehat B-B\).  Because \(G=I_y\otimes G_z\), the pilot-coefficient, ridge, and Gram parts are relative in the inverse-elliptic energy, while (9) controls the deterministic Galerkin part in Euclidean norm.  Thus, for all \(x,y\),
\[
|x^{\top}(\widehat B-B)y|
\le O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}+\lambda_n)
(x^{\top}Bx)^{1/2}(y^{\top}By)^{1/2}
+C\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{a_y}\|x\|_2\|y\|_2.                  \tag{12}
\]

It remains to establish the nonlinear remainder without assuming derivatives absent from the H\"older class.  Put \(a_y=\min\{s_y,1\}\).  The conditional densities have a uniform \(a_y\)-H\"older modulus.  Positivity makes every conditional distribution function strictly increasing.  If a perturbation of its distribution function has sup-norm at most \(E\), the induced quantile displacement \(w\) obeys \(|w|\le E/c\).  The defining H\"older inequality gives directly
\[
F(x+w)-F(x)-q(x)w
=\int_0^w\{q(x+t)-q(x)\}\,dt
=O(|w|^{1+a_y}).
\]
Substituting \(|w|\le E/c\) into the inverse identity shows that the error after the linear displacement is \(O(E^{1+a_y})\).  Squaring the displacement and integrating therefore leaves \(O(E^{2+a_y})\) after the equality quadratic form.  Arm exchange makes the functional even in \(\Delta\), and the equality quadratic form is the inverse-elliptic form established in \(\mathrm{lem{:}cosine\text{-}second\text{-}variation}\).  Applying the same calculation about a nonequal pair changes the quadratic coefficient by at most \(C\|\Delta_n\|_\infty^{a_y}\).  Consequently
\[
|R_{\mathrm{det}}|
\lesssim E^{2+a_y}+\|\Delta_n\|_\infty^{a_y}E^2.                        \tag{13}
\]

The primitive anisotropic H\"older condition controls the factor in (13).  If \(A=\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\), the H\"older bounds force amplitude of order \(A\) on an outcome interval of length \(A^{1/s_y}\) and a covariate rectangle of volume \(A^{d/s_z}\).  Zero outcome mass forces the antiderivative of \(\Delta_n\) to have size of order \(A^{1+1/s_y}\) on an outcome interval of length of order \(A^{1/s_y}\).  The energy identity for \(\mathcal G_{n,z}\) and the two density lower bounds then imply
\[
\rho_n^2\gtrsim A^{2+3/s_y+d/s_z},
\qquad
\|\Delta_n\|_\infty\lesssim\rho_n^{2/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}.               \tag{14}
\]
Taking \(E=O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n})\) in (13) and using (14) gives exactly \(O_P\{\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}+\rho_n^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\}\).  When \(s_y\ge1\), \(a_y=1\) and this specializes to the earlier cubic-plus-local-quadratic expression.

For completeness, let \(\mu_a=E_{P_{a,n}}\Phi(W_{a,1,n})\), \(\widehat\mu_a=\int\Phi\widehat q_a\widehat r\), \(d_a=\mu_a-\widehat\mu_a\), and \(d=d_1-d_0\).  Conditional on a pilot fold, the expectation of its displayed diagonal-free correction is exactly
\[
d_0^{\top}\widehat Bd_0+d_1^{\top}\widehat Bd_1-2d_1^{\top}\widehat Bd_0
=d^{\top}\widehat Bd.                                                    \tag{15}
\]
By the corrected projection formula, \(d^{\top}Bd\) is precisely the equality inverse-elliptic quadratic form of the projected raw-moment error.  Thus (15), unlike the uncorrected formula, is the quadratic Taylor term.  Replacing \(B\) by \(\widehat B\) costs, by (11)--(12), \(O_P\{(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{a_y}+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}+\lambda_n)\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2\}=O_P(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y})\), because \(\lambda_n=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\).  Conditional centering and sample splitting make the corresponding linear and canonical kernel replacements
\[
o_P(1)\left\{\frac{\rho_n}{\sqrt n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n\right\}.       \tag{16}
\]
Equations (12)--(16) prove the asserted complete pilot, Gram, Galerkin, ridge, and operator remainder.

Finally, compact wavelet support gives \(\|\Phi(W)\|_2^2\lesssim D_{J_y,J_z}\).  Exponential matrix moments for the centered rank-one covariance summands, obtained by expanding their scalar quadratic forms and applying exponential Markov's inequality on a \(1/4\)-net, yield
\[
\max_e\|\widehat\Gamma^{(-e)}-\Gamma\|_{\mathrm{op}}
=O_P\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{D_{J_y,J_z}\log n}{m}}+
\frac{D_{J_y,J_z}\log n}{m}\right)=o_P(1).                              \tag{17}
\]
For the Galerkin part, the levelwise form error is bounded by \(C\min\{2^{-2j_y},\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{a_y}\}\).  Squaring, multiplying by the outcome-level multiplicity, and dividing by the covariate multiplicity gives a summable dominating series \(C2^{-3j_y}\) whose individual terms vanish.  Hence dominated convergence, together with the relative part of (12), yields \(\|\widehat B-B\|_{\mathrm{HS}}=o_P(\|B\|_{\mathrm{HS}})\).  The square-root map is Lipschitz on the fixed spectral interval in (7).  Combining this fact and (17) gives both operator consistency of \(\widehat K\) and
\[
\|\widehat K-K\|_{\mathrm{HS}}
=o_P(\|B\|_{\mathrm{HS}})=o_P(\|K\|_{\mathrm{HS}}).                    \tag{18}
\]
Here (7) transfers the Hilbert--Schmidt norm between \(B\) and \(K\).  Condition (17) is used only for the observed spectral proxy.
