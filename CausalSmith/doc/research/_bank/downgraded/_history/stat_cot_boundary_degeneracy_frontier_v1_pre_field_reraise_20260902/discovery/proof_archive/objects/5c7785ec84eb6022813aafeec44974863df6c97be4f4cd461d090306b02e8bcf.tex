Let
\[
E_{R,n}=C_B\left[
\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}\{2^{-(s_y+1)J_y}+2^{-s_zJ_z}\}
+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^{2+a_y}
+\rho^{2a_y/(2+3/s_y+d/s_z)}\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pil},n}^2
\right].
\]
The proved exact-\(\theta_n\) expansion and the oracle spectral theorem imply the following population-calibrated partial result.  If the oracle theorem's score-approximation condition holds and the little-oh term in the expansion is bounded by a deterministic \(o(S_n/n)\) sequence, then an interval centered at \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{an}}_{J_y,J_z,n}\), with half-width equal to a sufficiently large fixed multiple of \(E_{R,n}\), that deterministic \(o(S_n/n)\) sequence, and the \(1-\alpha/2\) quantile of the population Gaussian-quadratic proxy divided by \(n\), has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\).  This follows by Markov's inequality for the \(L^2\)-controlled remainder and the bounded-Lipschitz approximation for the oracle term.  Its half-width is of order
\[
E_{R,n}+\frac{A_{J_y,J_z,n}}n+\sqrt{\frac{V_{L,n}}n}.
\]
At the corrected resolutions this is the advertised corrected Gram scale.  The result is infeasible because the proxy uses the population Hessian, spectrum, local shifts, and linear variance.  No uniform conditional common-multiplier quantile coupling or anti-concentration bound for their observable analogues has been proved.
