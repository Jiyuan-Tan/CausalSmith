If computation begins from the raw streams rather than their summaries, reading and summarizing the data incurs in addition at least \(\Omega(B_{\mathrm{tot}})\) input-record operations; thus no end-to-end running-time claim polynomial only in logarithmic gap parameters is made.

Let \(Q=8(n^2+2n)\) and \(\beta=e_{\star}/(4Q)\).  In any one restarted transcript, the public program has \(n^2+2n-2\) reached matrix occurrences.  Adding \(n+1\) covariance inversions, \(n\) primitive formations, at most two projector constructions and one product-norm computation per graph comparison, and \(n\) singleton selections is bounded by \(Q\).  Assign the \(j\)-th operation in that restart a numerical enclosure radius
\[
 \varepsilon_j\le\beta,
 \tag{28}
\]
and, for the operation's own decision, reduce it further to the minimum of \(\beta\) and the required fixed fraction of \(g_0\), \(h_0\), \(1/\sqrt n\), or \(\tau/n\).  Then, deterministically,
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^{Q}\varepsilon_j\le Q\beta=e_{\star}/4.
 \tag{29}
\]

We first justify rational covariance algebra.  Clear denominators in an input covariance matrix.  Its integer entries have bit length at most \(O(L)\).  The Leibniz determinant formula has at most \(n!\) products of \(n\) such entries, so every determinant minor has bit length \(O(n(L+\log n))\).  Fraction-free elimination therefore decides positive definiteness and, when the matrix is invertible, computes its adjugate-over-determinant inverse with polynomially many operations on polynomial-bit integers.  Sums and the three-factor primitive formula increase numerator and denominator lengths only polynomially in \(L\) and \(n\).  Outward rounding every entry to \(p\) fractional bits gives operator error at most \(n2^{-p}\), because the operator norm is at most the Frobenius norm.  The min--max formula and the quadratic-form bound used in (9) transfer this operator radius to every ordered eigenvalue.  Thus primitive and eigenvalue enclosure error at most any prescribed \(\xi>0\) is obtained with
\[
 p=O\!\left(L+\log n+\log(1/\xi)\right).
 \tag{30}
\]
This contains, and sharpens for exact rational centers, the prior covariance-to-primitive partial result.  If the input itself is an outward interval rather than an exact rational center, the condition \(\lambda_{\min}(S_{m,r})\ge(2\kappa)^{-1}\) and the resolvent identity give the previously established additional inverse error \(4\kappa^2n2^{-p}\), and the primitive error is at most \(Cn\kappa^4 2^{-p}\); adding \(O(\log\kappa)\) bits in (30) absorbs it.

The nonalgebraic radius terms also have certified rational enclosures at polynomial cost.  A square root of a nonnegative rational is bracketed by bisection, and range reduction writes a positive rational as \(2^jy\) with \(y\in[1,2)\).  With \(z=(y-1)/(y+1)\in[0,1/3]\),
\[
 \log y=2\sum_{\ell=0}^{s}\frac{z^{2\ell+1}}{2\ell+1}+r_s,
 \qquad
 0\le r_s\le\frac{2z^{2s+3}}{(2s+3)(1-z^2)}.
\]
The same series at \(z=1/3\) encloses \(\log2\).  Thus \(O(p+L)\) rational terms suffice for \(p\) output bits, with explicit tail sign and magnitude; outward rounding gives certified values of \(t_r\) and \(a_r\) in polynomial Boolean time.

Rank certification does not require eigenvectors.  For a rational symmetric matrix \(K\) and rational \(x\), exact fraction-free symmetric elimination gives a rational block \(LDL^{\top}\) congruence of \(K-xI\).  Congruence preserves the maximal dimensions of positive and negative quadratic-form subspaces, so the signs of the one- and two-dimensional diagonal blocks determine the inertia and hence the number of eigenvalues below \(x\).  Bisection between rational endpoints gives outward eigenvalue intervals of width \(\xi\) in \(O(\log((1+\|K\|_{\mathrm{op}})/\xi))\) inertia calls.  The determinant-minor bound above proves polynomial bit growth in every call.  At the terminal stage, (18)--(19) from Theorem 1 give, for every reached \(M\),
\[
 b_M\le ne_{r_B}<g_0/112.
 \tag{31}

Choosing the rational rounding radius below \(g_0/112\), the inertia counts certify that every empirical eigenvalue lies either in the zero cluster below \(g_0/28\) or in the positive cluster above \(27g_0/28\).  They consequently certify the population rank and the public forbidden band.  On an off-promise input, failure of these inequalities produces \(\bot\), not a rounded rank.
