%%% FIELD def-full-class-handle-construction
For each \(m\in\{2,\ldots,K-1\}\), parameterize an unrestricted two-batch top-\(m\) design by \(0<\rho<1\), an interior first-batch allocation \(a\in\mathcal W\), and a branch-specific allocation \(b_J\in\operatorname{int}\Sigma_J\) for every \(J\in\mathcal J_m\).  Thus \(D=D(\rho,a,\{b_J\}_{J\in\mathcal J_m})\in\mathfrak D_2^{\mathrm{all,multi}}\).  For a fixed tuple, replace \(q_i(\rho)\) by \(\rho a_i\), \(r_i(J;\rho)\) by \((1-\rho)b_{J,i}\), and \(n_i(J;\rho)\) by \(\rho a_i+(1-\rho)b_{J,i}\) in the survivor-set programs of \(\text{def:branch-programs}\), whose Gaussian objectives depend on the known variance vector \(s\).  This produces, for each best-exclusion or terminal branch, an explicit convex quadratic objective subject to linear inequalities.  The full-class handle jointly optimizes the resulting finite branch minimum over the discrete choice of \(m\), over \(\rho\), \(a\), and \(\{b_J\}\), and against all \(\mu\) having a unique best arm.  Its proposed optimality certificate consists of primal branch witnesses and feasible Lagrange multipliers for the branch polyhedra that give matching bounds on the robust ratio to \(I_w(\mu)\).  Neither a minimax interchange nor the value \(G_{\mathrm{multi}}\) is imposed by this definition.
%%% FIELD oeq-full-two-batch-frontier-statement
Using the branch-dual saddle handle in \(\text{def:full-class-handle}\), characterize
\[
 \sup_{D\in\mathfrak D_2^{\mathrm{all,multi}}}
 \inf_{\mu:\,|\arg\max_i\mu_i|=1}
 \frac{\Gamma_D(\mu)}{I_w(\mu)}.
\]
Is the displayed value \(G_{\mathrm{multi}}\), or can nonproportional first-stage allocations or branch-specific survivor allocations, jointly with the discrete choice of \(m\), achieve a strictly larger certified multiplier?  Derive an attaining design and a matching saddle certificate in either case.  The restriction \(m=K-1\) is the nested unrestricted-allocation eliminate-one subquestion over \(\mathfrak D_2^{\mathrm{all}}\), not the full two-batch frontier.
%%% FIELD oeq-full-two-batch-frontier-partial-result
For every \(m\in\{2,\ldots,K-1\}\) and \(0<\rho<1\), the proportional design \(\mathsf D_{\rho,w,m}\) belongs to \(\mathfrak D_2^{\mathrm{all,multi}}\): take \(a=w\) and, for each \(J\in\mathcal J_m\), take \(b_{J,i}=w_i/W_J\) for \(i\in J\) and \(b_{J,i}=0\) for \(i\notin J\).  Indeed, \(w_i>0\) and \(W_J=\sum_{i\in J}w_i>0\), so \(a\in\mathcal W\), \(b_J\in\operatorname{int}\Sigma_J\), and the first- and second-batch fractions coincide with those in \(\text{def:proportional-design}\).  Since unique best-arm means have positive gaps and \(v_i>0\), \(I_w(\mu)>0\).  Hence the inclusion and \(\text{thm:multi-proportional-frontier}\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sup_{D\in\mathfrak D_2^{\mathrm{all,multi}}}
 \inf_{\mu:\,|\arg\max_i\mu_i|=1}
 \frac{\Gamma_D(\mu)}{I_w(\mu)}
 &\ge
 \max_{2\le m\le K-1}\ \sup_{0<\rho<1}
 \inf_{\mu:\,|\arg\max_i\mu_i|=1}
 \frac{\Gamma_{\rho,w,m}(\mu)}{I_w(\mu)} \\
 &=G_{\mathrm{multi}}.
\end{aligned}
\]
For each fixed \(D(\rho,a,\{b_J\})\), the top-\(m\) branch partition is finite, and substituting \(\rho a_i\) and \((1-\rho)b_{J,i}\) into the Gaussian branch construction gives explicit convex quadratic programs for all best-exclusion and terminal branches.  The missing step is a single adversarial mean construction, or an equivalent dual certificate, that upper-bounds the robust ratio by \(G_{\mathrm{multi}}\) simultaneously for every admissible \(m\), \(a\), and collection \(\{b_J\}\).
