%%% FIELD proof-thm-simultaneous-bands
Put \(N=nm\) and \(d=|\mathcal G_n|\), fix \(P\in\mathfrak P^{\mathrm{pred}}_{n,m}(r,\mu,K,q,B,\lambda)\), and fix a deterministic \(g\in\mathcal G_n\).  By \(\mathrm{def:contrast\mbox{-}process}\), the definition of \(\tau_g\), and \(\mathrm{def:ipw\mbox{-}score}\),
\[
 \widehat\tau_g-\tau_g
 =\sum_{i\in I}\sum_{j\in J}g_{ij}
   \bigl(Z^{\mathrm{ipw}}_{ij}-M_{ij}\bigr)
 =\sum_{i\in I}\sum_{j\in J}g_{ij}E_{ij}.
\]
For the cell \((i,j)\), write \(\mathcal H_{ij}=\mathbb G_{o(i,j)-1}\).  The loading \(g\) is deterministic, while \(\mathrm{lem:ipw\mbox{-}identification}\) gives
\[
 E_P[g_{ij}E_{ij}\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]
 =g_{ij}E_P[E_{ij}\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]=0.
\]
Consequently, in the row-major order from \(\mathrm{def:global\mbox{-}filtration}\), the summands \(g_{ij}E_{ij}\) form a scalar martingale-difference sequence.

We next bound its predictable quadratic variation and increments.  The observed-outcome identity gives
\[
 Z^{\mathrm{ipw}}_{ij}
 =\frac{A_{ij}Y_{ij}(1)}{p_{ij}}
  -\frac{(1-A_{ij})Y_{ij}(0)}{1-p_{ij}}.
\]
The denominators are strictly positive by \(\mathrm{ass:overlap}\), and \(p_{ij}\) is \(\mathcal H_{ij}\)-measurable by the definition of the recorded propensity.  Since \(A_{ij}(1-A_{ij})=0\), iterated conditional expectation and \(\mathrm{ass:sequential\mbox{-}randomization}\) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
 E_P\!\left[(Z^{\mathrm{ipw}}_{ij})^2\mid\mathcal H_{ij}\right]
 &=E_P\!\left[
   \frac{A_{ij}Y_{ij}(1)^2}{p_{ij}^2}
   +\frac{(1-A_{ij})Y_{ij}(0)^2}{(1-p_{ij})^2}
   \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal H_{ij}\right]\\
 &=E_P\!\left[
   \frac{Y_{ij}(1)^2}{p_{ij}}
   +\frac{Y_{ij}(0)^2}{1-p_{ij}}
   \mathrel{\Big|}\mathcal H_{ij}\right].
\end{aligned}
\]
Indeed, the first term in the first conditional expectation can be conditioned once more on \(\sigma(\mathcal H_{ij},Y_{ij}(0),Y_{ij}(1))\), on which \(\mathrm{ass:sequential\mbox{-}randomization}\) makes the conditional mean of \(A_{ij}\) equal to \(p_{ij}\); the second term is identical with \(1-A_{ij}\) and \(1-p_{ij}\).

The variable \(M_{ij}=E_P[Z^{\mathrm{ipw}}_{ij}\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]\) is \(\mathcal H_{ij}\)-measurable by \(\mathrm{lem:ipw\mbox{-}identification}\).  Therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
 E_P[E_{ij}^2\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]
 &=E_P[(Z^{\mathrm{ipw}}_{ij})^2\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]-M_{ij}^2\\
 &\le E_P[(Z^{\mathrm{ipw}}_{ij})^2\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]\\
 &\le \frac{2B^2}{q}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The last line uses \(\mathrm{ass:bounded\mbox{-}outcomes}\) and both inequalities in \(\mathrm{ass:overlap}\).  The same assumptions give
\[
 |Z^{\mathrm{ipw}}_{ij}|\le \frac{B}{q}
\]
and, from the definition \(M_{ij}=\mu^1_{ij}-\mu^0_{ij}\) and conditional Jensen's inequality,
\[
 |M_{ij}|
 \le E_P[|Y_{ij}(1)|\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]
     +E_P[|Y_{ij}(0)|\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]
 \le 2B.
\]
Because \(q<1\), it follows that
\[
 |g_{ij}E_{ij}|
 \le |g_{ij}|\left(\frac{B}{q}+2B\right)
 \le \frac{3B}{q}|g_{ij}|
 \le R_g:=\frac{3BC_\infty}{qN},
\]
where the final inequality is \(\mathrm{ass:dense\mbox{-}loadings}\).  Moreover, for every row-major partial sum ending at step \(k\),
\[
 \sum_{o(i,j)\le k}E_P[g_{ij}^2E_{ij}^2\mid\mathcal H_{ij}]
 \le \sigma_g^2:=\frac{2B^2}{q}\lVert g\rVert_F^2.
\]
The lower bound in \(\mathrm{ass:average\mbox{-}loadings}\), together with \(B>0\) and \(q>0\), ensures \(\sigma_g^2>0\).

Apply \(\mathrm{lem:tropp\mbox{-}matrix\mbox{-}freedman}\) to the \(1\)-dimensional self-adjoint martingale of partial sums and then to its negative.  The deterministic bounds just obtained verify respectively its predictable-variation and increment hypotheses.  A union bound over the two signs gives, for every \(t\ge0\),
\[
 P\!\left(\left|\widehat\tau_g-\tau_g\right|\ge t\right)
 \le 2\exp\!\left\{-\frac{t^2}{2(\sigma_g^2+R_gt/3)}\right\}.
\]
For \(x>0\), define
\[
 t_g(x)=\sqrt{2\sigma_g^2x}+\frac{2R_gx}{3}.
\]
A direct expansion shows
\[
 t_g(x)^2-2x\{\sigma_g^2+R_gt_g(x)/3\}
 =\frac{2R_gx}{3}\sqrt{2\sigma_g^2x}\ge0.
\]
Thus the preceding tail inequality and the displayed values of \(\sigma_g^2\) and \(R_g\) imply
\[
 P\!\left(
 |\widehat\tau_g-\tau_g|>
 2B\sqrt{\frac{\lVert g\rVert_F^2}{q}x}
 +\frac{2BC_\infty}{qN}x
 \right)\le2e^{-x}.
\]

Take \(x=\log(2d/\alpha)>0\).  Positivity follows from \(d\ge2\) in \(\mathrm{ass:polynomial\mbox{-}family}\) and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\).  A union bound over the deterministic family \(\mathcal G_n\) makes the probability of any failure at most
\[
 2d\exp\{-\log(2d/\alpha)\}=\alpha.
\]
On the complementary event, \(|\widehat\tau_g-\tau_g|\le R_{g,\alpha}\) simultaneously for every \(g\in\mathcal G_n\), which is the asserted finite-sample inclusion.

Finally, \(\mathrm{ass:dense\mbox{-}loadings}\) implies uniformly in \(g\) that
\[
 \lVert g\rVert_F^2
 \le N\left(\frac{C_\infty}{N}\right)^2
 =\frac{C_\infty^2}{N}.
\]
Substitution into \(R_{g,\alpha}\), with \(N=nm\), proves
\[
 R_{g,\alpha}
 =O\!\left(
 B\sqrt{\frac{\log(2|\mathcal G_n|/\alpha)}{nmq}}
 +\frac{B\log(2|\mathcal G_n|/\alpha)}{qnm}
 \right)
\]
uniformly in \(g\), with constants depending only on \(C_\infty\).  The family size enters the leading stochastic term through the separate factor \(\sqrt{\log|\mathcal G_n|}\); the upper bound \(|\mathcal G_n|\le n^\kappa\) in \(\mathrm{ass:polynomial\mbox{-}family}\) makes this at most order \(\sqrt{\log n}\).  By \(\mathrm{def:frontier\mbox{-}rate}\), \(b_{nm}\) contains no dependence on \(\mathcal G_n\), so this entropy multiplier is not absorbed into that lower/reference scale.  No step uses \(\widehat s_g\), its positivity or consistency, or the multiplier critical value \(c_{1-\alpha}\).  Hence the proved bands are the stated conservative direct-IPW bands and do not assert feasible multiplier exactness.
