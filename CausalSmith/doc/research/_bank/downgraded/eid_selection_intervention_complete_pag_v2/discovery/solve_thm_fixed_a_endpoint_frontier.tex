Put \(g=\operatorname{gr}(\eta)\).  Because \(\eta\in\mathcal C(\mathcal O)\), the definition of the fixed-\(A\) fiber gives \(\eta\in\mathcal C_A(\mathcal O,\eta)\).  Hence
\[
 \mu_\eta(e)\in\mathcal M_A(e;\mathcal O,\eta),
 \qquad
 \mathcal M_A(e;\mathcal O,\eta)\ne\varnothing.
 \tag{1}
\]
The hypotheses that \(u,v\in A(\eta)\), \(uv\notin H\), and \(uv\) occurs in the regime-\(k\) skeleton are precisely the intervention-created-adjacency hypotheses of \(\mathrm{lem:exact\mbox{-}fixed\mbox{-}a\mbox{-}graphical\mbox{-}frontier}\).  That lemma, together with \(g=\operatorname{gr}(\eta)\), yields
\[
 \mathcal M_A(e;\mathcal O,\eta)
 \subseteq
 \mathcal M_A^{\mathrm{gr}}(e;\mathcal O,g)
 =\mathcal G_A(u,v;k).
 \tag{2}
\]
Combining (1) and (2) proves the asserted nonempty outer bracket.

Suppose first that \(u\notin Z_k\).  By the three-case definition in \(\mathrm{def:fixed\mbox{-}a\mbox{-}mark\mbox{-}sets}\),
\[
 \mathcal G_A(u,v;k)=\{\mathtt{tail}\}.
\]
A nonempty subset of a singleton is that singleton, so (1)--(2) imply
\[
 \mathcal M_A(e;\mathcal O,\eta)=\{\mathtt{tail}\}.
 \tag{3}
\]
Suppose instead that \(u\in Z_k\) and \(\operatorname{Reach}_{A(\eta)}(u,v)=0\).  The same definition gives
\[
 \mathcal G_A(u,v;k)=\{\mathtt{arrow}\},
\]
and therefore (1)--(2) give
\[
 \mathcal M_A(e;\mathcal O,\eta)=\{\mathtt{arrow}\}.
 \tag{4}
\]

It remains to establish the conditional conclusion in the reachability case.  Assume
\[
 u\in Z_k,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Reach}_{A(\eta)}(u,v)=1.
 \tag{5}
\]
By the definition of nonempty reachability in \(L_{A(\eta)}\), there is a directed \(u\)-to-\(v\) path.  Since \(A(\eta)\subseteq V\) and \(V\) is finite, deleting repeated directed cycles produces a simple directed path \(P\).  Let \(B_0\) be the empty directed graph on \(A(\eta)\), and let \(B_P\) contain exactly the directed edges of \(P\).  The reachability part of \(\mathrm{lem:exact\mbox{-}fixed\mbox{-}a\mbox{-}graphical\mbox{-}frontier}\) states that both \(B_0\) and \(B_P\) are admissible and that
\[
 \operatorname{RepGraph}(\eta,B_0),\ 
 \operatorname{RepGraph}(\eta,B_P)
 \in\mathcal C_A^{\mathrm{gr}}(\mathcal O,g).
 \tag{6}
\]
Thus both replacement specifications have ancestral selected set \(A(\eta)\) and preserve the skeleton and the unshielded-collider set of every regime projection on its full domain \(U_j\), including all instances involving \(\zeta_j\).  The same lemma computes their marks at \(e\) as
\[
 \mu^{\mathrm{gr}}_{\operatorname{RepGraph}(\eta,B_0)}(e)
   =\mathtt{arrow},
 \qquad
 \mu^{\mathrm{gr}}_{\operatorname{RepGraph}(\eta,B_P)}(e)
   =\mathtt{tail}.
 \tag{7}
\]
This proves the two asserted common-basal graphical witness statements without promoting either specification to a law-level model.

Now impose the two antecedents in the theorem's displayed implication:
\[
 \operatorname{Real}(\eta,B_0)\ne\varnothing,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Real}(\eta,B_P)\ne\varnothing.
 \tag{8}
\]
Choose
\[
 \eta_{\mathtt{arrow}}\in\operatorname{Real}(\eta,B_0),
 \qquad
 \eta_{\mathtt{tail}}\in\operatorname{Real}(\eta,B_P).
 \tag{9}
\]
By \(\mathrm{lem:replacement\mbox{-}preservation}\), each model in (9) is a fully specified faithful member of \(\mathcal C_A(\mathcal O,\eta)\).  Its graphical reduct is the corresponding replacement specification, so (7) and the definition of \(\mathcal M_A\) imply
\[
 \{\mathtt{tail},\mathtt{arrow}\}
 \subseteq\mathcal M_A(e;\mathcal O,\eta).
 \tag{10}
\]
Under (5), the third case of \(\mathrm{def:fixed\mbox{-}a\mbox{-}mark\mbox{-}sets}\) gives
\[
 \mathcal G_A(u,v;k)=\{\mathtt{tail},\mathtt{arrow}\}.
 \tag{11}
\]
Equations (2), (10), and (11) therefore establish
\[
 \mathcal M_A(e;\mathcal O,\eta)
 =\mathcal G_A(u,v;k)
 =\{\mathtt{tail},\mathtt{arrow}\}.
\]

Finally, \(\mathrm{lem:dai\mbox{-}twin\mbox{-}mag\mbox{-}interface}\) gives
\[
 Z_k=T_k(\eta)\cup
 \bigl((F_k(\eta)\setminus T_k(\eta))\cap A(\eta)\bigr),
\]
so the proof uses \(u\in Z_k\), which allows either direct targeting or indirect affectedness in \(A(\eta)\), and never replaces this condition by \(u\in T_k(\eta)\).  Moreover, \(\mathrm{prop:retained\mbox{-}edge\mbox{-}sanity\mbox{-}reduction}\) exhibits a retained adjacency with an arrowhead endpoint outside \(Z_k\).  Consequently the restriction \(uv\notin H\) is necessary for the stated intervention-created-edge bracket.  This completes the proof.
