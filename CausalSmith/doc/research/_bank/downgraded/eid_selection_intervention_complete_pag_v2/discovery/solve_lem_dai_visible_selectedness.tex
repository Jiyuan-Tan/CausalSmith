%%% FIELD proof-visible-selectedness
Fix \(\eta\in\mathfrak M_{D,K}^{\mathrm{Dai}}\) and write \(A=A(\eta)\) and \(\mathcal O=\mathcal O(\eta)\).  Let \(uv\) be absent from the observational skeleton \(H\) and present in an observed--observed part of an intervention-regime skeleton.  Thus there is some \(k\in[K]\) such that
\[
uv\notin E(H),
\qquad
uv\in E\bigl(\operatorname{skel}(M_k(\eta))[V]\bigr).
\tag{1}
\]
(When \(K=0\), no such \(k\) exists and the implication is vacuous.)  By the regime-adjacency characterization in \(\mathrm{lem:dai\mbox{-}twin\mbox{-}mag\mbox{-}interface}\), the second relation in (1) holds only if either \(uv\in E(H)\), or there is an \(H[A]\)-path
\[
\pi=(v_0=u,v_1,\ldots,v_\ell=v)
\quad\text{with}\quad
v_1,\ldots,v_{\ell-1}\in F_k(\eta)\cap A.
\tag{2}
\]
The first alternative is excluded by the first relation in (1).  Hence (2) holds.  Because \(H[A]\) is the subgraph induced by the vertex set \(A\), every vertex of \(\pi\), including both endpoints, belongs to \(A\).  Consequently
\[
u\in A(\eta)
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
v\in A(\eta),
\]
as claimed.  This argument uses only the graphical interface, whose premises are supplied by \(\mathrm{ass:selection\mbox{-}structure}\), \(\mathrm{ass:selection\mbox{-}parent\mbox{-}domain}\), \(\mathrm{ass:pretreatment\mbox{-}selection}\), and \(\mathrm{def:model\mbox{-}class}\).  In particular, it performs no conditioning, division, inversion, rank argument, or limit operation; selection-event positivity and finite-alphabet support are therefore not additionally invoked.

%%% FIELD proof-visible-selectedness-certificates
Fix \(\eta\in\mathfrak M_{D,K}^{\mathrm{Dai}}\), put \(A=A(\eta)\), and let \(\mathcal O=\mathcal O(\eta)\).  For brevity write
\[
C^{(r)}=C^{(r)}(\mathcal O),\qquad 0\le r\le |V|,
\]
for the sets in \(\mathrm{def:observable\mbox{-}selectedness\mbox{-}closure}\).  We prove by induction on \(r\) that
\[
C^{(r)}\subseteq A.
\tag{3}
\]

For \(r=0\), every \(q\in C^{(0)}\) is, by the defining seed rule, an endpoint of an observed--observed adjacency absent from \(H\) and present in some intervention-regime skeleton.  Applying \(\mathrm{lem:dai\mbox{-}visible\mbox{-}selectedness}\) to that adjacency gives \(q\in A\).  Thus (3) holds at \(r=0\).

Suppose (3) holds for some \(r<|V|\).  An element of \(C^{(r+1)}\setminus C^{(r)}\) is a vertex \(x\) for which there are nonadjacent vertices \(i,j\in C^{(r)}\) such that
\[
i-x-j
\tag{4}
\]
is an unshielded noncollider in the observational MAG \(M_0(\eta)\).  The induction hypothesis yields
\[
i,j\in A.
\tag{5}
\]
Assume, for contradiction, that \(x\notin A\).

Because \(ix\in E(H)\), the observational-adjacency clause of \(\mathrm{lem:dai\mbox{-}twin\mbox{-}mag\mbox{-}interface}\) says that this adjacency is generated either by a basal observed edge or by an observed common child ancestral to selection.  The latter alternative would imply \(x\in A\): indeed, if \(c\) is such a common child, then \(x\to c\) and \(c\) has a directed path to a selection vertex.  By \(\mathrm{ass:selection\mbox{-}structure}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:selection\mbox{-}parent\mbox{-}domain}\), either \(x\) itself is an observed parent of that selection vertex or the path has an observed penultimate vertex \(p\in\operatorname{pa}_{G_0(\eta)}(S_\eta)\); in both cases
\[
x\in\operatorname{An}_{G_0(\eta)}
   \bigl(\operatorname{pa}_{G_0(\eta)}(S_\eta)\bigr)\cap V
=A,
\]
contrary to the supposition.  Hence \(ix\) is a basal observed edge.

That basal edge cannot have direction \(x\to i\).  If it did, (5) and the definition of \(A\) would give
\[
x\in\operatorname{An}_{G_0(\eta)}(i)
\subseteq
\operatorname{An}_{G_0(\eta)}
   \bigl(\operatorname{pa}_{G_0(\eta)}(S_\eta)\bigr),
\]
and therefore \(x\in A\), again a contradiction.  Thus its basal direction is \(i\to x\).  Since this observational adjacency meets \(V\setminus A\), the retained-orientation clause of \(\mathrm{lem:dai\mbox{-}twin\mbox{-}mag\mbox{-}interface}\) implies that its observational-MAG orientation is also \(i\to x\).  Applying the same argument to \(jx\) gives \(j\to x\).  Together with the nonadjacency of \(i\) and \(j\), this makes (4) the unshielded collider
\[
i\to x\leftarrow j,
\]
contradicting the premise that (4) is a noncollider.  Therefore \(x\in A\), and (3) follows at stage \(r+1\).

Induction gives (3) for every \(0\le r\le |V|\).  Taking the union specified in \(\mathrm{def:observable\mbox{-}selectedness\mbox{-}closure}\) yields
\[
\mathsf C_{\mathrm{sel}}(\mathcal O)
=\bigcup_{r=0}^{|V|}C^{(r)}(\mathcal O)
\subseteq A(\eta).
\]
Finally, the seed adjacencies, observational unshielded-noncollider statuses, and recursion are all functions of the full oracle signature \(\mathcal O\) by \(\mathrm{def:oracle\mbox{-}signature}\) and \(\mathrm{def:observable\mbox{-}selectedness\mbox{-}closure}\).  Thus the certificate is observable and never queries \(A(\eta)\) or another compatible model in its computation.  As above, the proof is finite and graphical; it uses no positivity denominator, rank condition, or limiting argument.
