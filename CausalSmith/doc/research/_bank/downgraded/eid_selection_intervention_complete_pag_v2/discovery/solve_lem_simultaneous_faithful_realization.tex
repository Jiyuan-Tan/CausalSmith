%%% FIELD exceptional-reduction-statement
Fix \(\eta\in\mathcal C(\mathcal O)\) and a graph \(B\) admissible for the graphical replacement lemma. Suppose that a nonempty open set \(\Theta\subset\mathbb R^r\) directly parameterizes finite-alphabet ambient tuples \(\eta_\theta\) whose selection-set, basal-graph, and target components jointly equal \(\operatorname{RepGraph}(\eta,B)\). Assume that every \(\eta_\theta\) satisfies every member condition in \(\mathrm{def:model\mbox{-}class}\) except possibly pooled twin faithfulness. In particular, each tuple is already represented by one product law of mutually independent exogenous variables whose same realized vector is used in every world, measurable response functions shared across regimes at every non-target vertex, one basal selection response generated before intervention, and positive basal selection probability; these coupled-response objects are part of the parameterized tuple and are not reconstructed from regime-local conditional tables. For \(k\in[K]\), let \(p_{\theta,k}\) be the mass function of the pooled selected law \(P_{\eta_\theta,k}^{\mathrm{pool}}\), and let the replacement MAG be the latent projection on \(U_k\) determined by \(\operatorname{RepGraph}(\eta,B)\). Assume that every cell of \(p_{\theta,k}\) is strictly positive and rational in \(\theta\), with all denominators positive on \(\Theta\), and that \(m\)-separation in every replacement MAG implies the corresponding conditional independence under \(p_{\theta,k}\) for every \(\theta\in\Theta\). For every \(k\in[K]\), every triple of pairwise disjoint sets \(R_1,R_2,W\subseteq U_k\), and every cell assignment \(a=(r_1,r_2,w)\), clear positive denominators in
\[
 p_{\theta,k}(r_1,r_2,w)p_{\theta,k}(w)
 -p_{\theta,k}(r_1,w)p_{\theta,k}(r_2,w)
\]
and denote the resulting polynomial by \(q_{k,R_1,R_2,W,a}\). There exists \(\theta^\star\in\Theta\) for which all pooled selected laws are simultaneously faithful to their replacement MAGs if and only if, for every query \((k,R_1,R_2,W)\) that is \(m\)-connected in its replacement MAG, at least one polynomial \(q_{k,R_1,R_2,W,a}\) is not the zero polynomial. Every such simultaneously faithful \(\eta_{\theta^\star}\) belongs to \(\operatorname{Real}(\eta,B)\). Thus the conclusion is conditional on the supplied directly coupled rational parameterization and reduces faithful realization within that family to atomic determinant nontriviality; it constructs neither the parameterization nor the atomic certificates.
%%% FIELD exceptional-reduction-proof
Write \(\mathcal Q\) for the set of quadruples
\[
 Q=(k,R_1,R_2,W),
 \qquad k\in[K],\qquad
 R_1,R_2,W\subseteq U_k
\]
such that the three vertex sets are pairwise disjoint and \(R_1\) and \(R_2\) are \(m\)-connected given \(W\) in the replacement MAG. The set \(\mathcal Q\) is finite because \(K\), every domain \(U_k\), and every variable alphabet are finite.

Fix \(Q=(k,R_1,R_2,W)\) and a cell assignment \(a=(r_1,r_2,w)\). Define
\[
 \Delta_{Q,a}(\theta)
 :=p_{\theta,k}(r_1,r_2,w)p_{\theta,k}(w)
   -p_{\theta,k}(r_1,w)p_{\theta,k}(r_2,w).
 \tag{1}
\]
Strict positivity of every full cell implies \(p_{\theta,k}(w)>0\) for every \(\theta\in\Theta\) and every \(w\); when \(W=\varnothing\), this marginal is \(1\). Therefore the definition of conditional independence for a finite table gives
\[
 X_{R_1}\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}X_{R_2}\mid X_W
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \frac{p_{\theta,k}(r_1,r_2,w)}{p_{\theta,k}(w)}
 =
 \frac{p_{\theta,k}(r_1,w)}{p_{\theta,k}(w)}
 \frac{p_{\theta,k}(r_2,w)}{p_{\theta,k}(w)}
 \quad\text{for every }a,
 \tag{2}
\]
and multiplication by the positive number \(p_{\theta,k}(w)^2\) shows that (2) is equivalent to
\[
 \Delta_{Q,a}(\theta)=0
 \quad\text{for every cell assignment }a.
 \tag{3}
\]
All masses and hence all marginals in (1) are rational functions of \(\theta\). By the stipulated denominator positivity, clearing a common denominator produces a polynomial \(q_{Q,a}\) and a function \(d_{Q,a}(\theta)>0\) on \(\Theta\) such that
\[
 q_{Q,a}(\theta)=d_{Q,a}(\theta)\Delta_{Q,a}(\theta).
 \tag{4}
\]
Consequently,
\[
 q_{Q,a}(\theta)=0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \Delta_{Q,a}(\theta)=0
 \qquad(\theta\in\Theta).
 \tag{5}
\]

Suppose first that for every \(Q\in\mathcal Q\) at least one \(q_{Q,a}\) is not the zero polynomial. Choose one such assignment \(a(Q)\) for each \(Q\), and set
\[
 Q_*(\theta):=\prod_{Q\in\mathcal Q}q_{Q,a(Q)}(\theta),
 \tag{6}
\]
with the empty product equal to \(1\). Since a polynomial ring over \(\mathbb R\) is an integral domain, finiteness of \(\mathcal Q\) and the choice of the factors imply that \(Q_*\) is not the zero polynomial.

We record the elementary fact needed to locate a common parameter. A nonzero real polynomial in \(r\) variables cannot vanish on a nonempty open subset of \(\mathbb R^r\). For \(r=0\) the assertion concerns a nonzero constant. For \(r=1\) it follows by repeated division by \(t-t_0\), which bounds the number of distinct roots by the degree. Inductively, write a polynomial in \(r\) variables as
\[
 h(t_1,\ldots,t_r)=\sum_{j=0}^{d}h_j(t_1,\ldots,t_{r-1})t_r^j.
 \tag{7}
\]
If \(h\) vanished on an open box, then for every point in the box's projection the resulting univariate polynomial would vanish on an interval, so every coefficient \(h_j\) would vanish on the projected open box. The induction hypothesis would make every \(h_j\) the zero polynomial, contrary to \(h\ne0\). Since every nonempty open set contains an open box, the fact follows.

Apply this fact to \(Q_*\) and the nonempty open set \(\Theta\). There is \(\theta^\star\in\Theta\) such that
\[
 Q_*(\theta^\star)\ne0.
 \tag{8}
\]
For every \(Q\in\mathcal Q\), equations (6) and (8) give \(q_{Q,a(Q)}(\theta^\star)\ne0\). Equations (3)--(5) then imply
\[
 X_{R_1}\not\!\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}X_{R_2}\mid X_W
 \quad\text{under }P_{\eta_{\theta^\star},k}^{\mathrm{pool}}
 \qquad\text{for every }Q\in\mathcal Q.
 \tag{9}
\]
For a query that is \(m\)-separated in its replacement MAG, the assumed Markov direction gives the corresponding conditional independence. Combining this implication with (9) yields, for every \(k\in[K]\) and every disjoint \(R_1,R_2,W\subseteq U_k\),
\[
 X_{R_1}\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}X_{R_2}\mid X_W
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 R_1\text{ and }R_2\text{ are }m\text{-separated by }W.
 \tag{10}
\]
Thus all pooled laws are simultaneously faithful. The parameter-family premise already supplies one coupled product-exogenous shared-response model satisfying every other member condition of \(\mathrm{def:model\mbox{-}class}\), including non-target response sharing, the common basal selection response, and positive basal selection probability. Equation (10) supplies the only possibly missing condition, pooled twin faithfulness. Hence \(\eta_{\theta^\star}\in\mathfrak M_{D,K}^{\mathrm{Dai}}\). Its selection-set, basal-graph, and target components equal \(\operatorname{RepGraph}(\eta,B)\) by hypothesis, so \(\mathrm{def:replacement\mbox{-}realization\mbox{-}fiber}\) gives
\[
 \eta_{\theta^\star}\in\operatorname{Real}(\eta,B).
 \tag{11}
\]
The role of \(\mathrm{lem:graphical\mbox{-}replacement\mbox{-}preservation}\) is to guarantee that the common graphical specification used here has the declared acyclic selected-DAG structure and the required replacement MAG signatures; no coupling is inferred from that graphical lemma.

Conversely, suppose \(\theta^\star\in\Theta\) makes every pooled selected law faithful to its replacement MAG. For any \(Q\in\mathcal Q\), faithfulness and \(m\)-connection imply failure of the corresponding conditional independence. By (3), there is a cell assignment \(a\) with \(\Delta_{Q,a}(\theta^\star)\ne0\). Equations (4)--(5) give
\[
 q_{Q,a}(\theta^\star)\ne0,
 \tag{12}
\]
so \(q_{Q,a}\) is not the zero polynomial. This proves atomic nontriviality for every \(m\)-connected query and establishes the converse. If \(K=0\), both the collection of pooled faithfulness requirements and \(\mathcal Q\) are empty; the equivalence is vacuous, any point of the nonempty \(\Theta\) is admissible, and (11) follows from the directly parameterized member conditions. This completes the proof.
%%% FIELD faithful-realization-partial
For any supplied nonempty open set \(\Theta\subset\mathbb R^r\) that directly parameterizes finite-alphabet tuples already carrying one product exogenous law, the same realized exogenous vector in every world, response functions shared off target, one basal pre-intervention selection response, positive basal selection probability, and every other model-class condition except possibly pooled twin faithfulness, the remaining genericity step is finite and algebraic. If the pooled selected cell masses are strictly positive rational functions with positive denominators and the Markov direction holds, conditional independence is equivalent to the vanishing of finitely many cleared determinant polynomials. Choosing one nonzero polynomial for each \(m\)-connected query and taking their product yields a point \(\theta^\star\) at which all pooled laws are simultaneously faithful. Conversely, simultaneous faithfulness implies that at least one determinant polynomial for each \(m\)-connected query is nonzero. What remains unproved is the construction of such a directly coupled nonempty open rational parameter family for every admissible replacement and the atomic nontriviality certificate for every \(m\)-connected query on that constrained family.
