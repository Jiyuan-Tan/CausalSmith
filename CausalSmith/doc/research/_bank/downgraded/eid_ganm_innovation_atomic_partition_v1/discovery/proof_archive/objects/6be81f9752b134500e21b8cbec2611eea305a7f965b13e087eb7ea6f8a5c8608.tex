The finite-poset reduction is complete.  For \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\), Definition~\ref{def:refinement-order} makes \(\preceq\) a partial order on coordinate partitions.  If \(\pi_R\ne\pi_{R'}\), exactly one of
\[
 \pi_R\not\preceq\pi_{R'}\text{ and }\pi_{R'}\not\preceq\pi_R,
 \qquad \pi_R\prec\pi_{R'},
 \qquad \pi_{R'}\prec\pi_R
\]
holds.  The conclusion requested by Open-ended Question~\ref{oeq:crossing-rigidity} would exclude the first case by producing a nontrivial directional additive-noise split of a block innovation.  The conclusion requested by Open-ended Question~\ref{oeq:strict-refinement-rigidity} would exclude the second case, and after exchanging \(R,R'\), the third case, by producing such a split in a coarse innovation.  Each produced mechanism and residual has exactly the regularity, independence, and dimension-matched \(\operatorname{SmoothPos}\) law forbidden by Assumption~\ref{ass:innovation-atomicity}.  Hence those two rigidity conclusions imply \(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\).  The already proved Lemma~\ref{lem:empty-dag-common-refinement} closes the subcase in which both graphs are empty, and Theorem~\ref{thm:three-coordinate-crossing} closes the displayed three-coordinate crossed single-edge configuration.  No frozen result closes the arbitrary incomparable or strict-refinement cases.
