Fix \(P\) and \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\).  Suppose first that
\(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}=: \pi_0\).  By
Definition~\ref{def:admissible-class}, each representation satisfies the
mechanism regularity, positive smooth innovation density, mutually independent
block-noise, acyclicity, causal minimality, and conditional Equation~(3)-failure
requirements appearing in Definition~\ref{def:supplied-group-class}.
Innovation atomicity is an additional restriction and is not needed for this
membership implication.  Hence
\[
 R,R'\in\mathfrak G_{\pi_0}(P).
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:gobler-fixed-partition-identifiability}, whose premise fixes the
same supplied labelled blocks, gives \(G_R=G_{R'}\).  This proves the first
claim without comparing unequal partitions.

If both graphs are empty, Lemma~\ref{lem:empty-dag-common-refinement} gives
\(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\), proving the second claim.  The third claim is precisely the
relabelling-invariant single-edge exclusion in
Theorem~\ref{thm:crossing-frontier}.  The fourth claim is
Theorem~\ref{thm:root-parent-closed-refinement-exclusion}; applying the same
theorem after exchanging \(R\) and \(R'\) proves its symmetric form.  These
four deductions exhaust exactly the asserted frontier.  None of the cited or
proved premises applies to an arbitrary incomparable crossing or to a strict
refinement with external fine parents, so no equality outside the displayed
cases is inferred.
