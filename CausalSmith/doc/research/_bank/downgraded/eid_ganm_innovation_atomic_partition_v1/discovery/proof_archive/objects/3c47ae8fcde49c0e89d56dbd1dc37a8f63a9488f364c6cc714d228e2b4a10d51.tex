Fix \(\pi_0\).  Comparing Definition~\ref{def:admissible-class} with Definition~\ref{def:supplied-group-class} gives
\[
\{R\in\mathfrak A(P):\pi_R=\pi_0\}\subseteq\mathfrak G_{\pi_0}(P),
\]
because every supplied-group condition is retained and innovation atomicity is additional.  Proposition~\ref{prop:atomicity-removal-converse} proves strictness over law--representation pairs: the merged empty-DAG representation of \(P_{\mathrm{merge}}\) belongs to the supplied-group fiber, but its bivariate innovation satisfies
\[
\varepsilon_{\{1,2\},2}
=\varepsilon_{\{1,2\},1}+\varepsilon_{\{1,2\},1}^{3}+N_2,
\qquad
N_2\perp\!\!\!\perp\varepsilon_{\{1,2\},1},
\qquad
\mathcal L(N_2)\in\operatorname{SmoothPos}(1),
\]
so it is not innovation-atomic.

On the common singleton subfiber, \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\cap\mathfrak S(P)\) implies \(G_R=G_{R'}\) by Lemma~\ref{lem:peters-scalar-dag-identifiability}.  This is exactly the labelled-DAG conclusion of Theorem~28 of Peters et al.

For every \(\theta\in(0,\infty)^5\), Lemma~\ref{lem:vector-family-atomicity} supplies an empty-DAG member of \(\mathfrak A(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})\), so that fiber is nonempty.  It also excludes additive-noise representations in both directions between \(U\) and \(V\).  To rule out every scalar representation, first note that a probability law on \(\mathbb R\) cannot equal a nonzero translate of itself.  Indeed, if its characteristic function \(\phi\) obeyed \(\phi(t)=e^{itc}\phi(t)\) for \(c\neq0\), continuity at zero and \(\phi(0)=1\) would give a sufficiently small \(t\) with \(\phi(t)\neq0\) and \(e^{itc}\neq1\), a contradiction.

Now suppose a causally minimal scalar additive-noise representation of \((U,V,W)\) existed.  The witness construction gives \(W\perp\!\!\!\perp(U,V)\) and an everywhere positive joint density.  If \(W\) were a parent of \(Y\in\{U,V\}\), then conditional on all other parents the structural conditional law of \(Y\) would be a translate of its innovation by a mechanism depending on \(W\), whereas joint independence makes the same conditional law independent of \(W\).  The translation fact forces the mechanism to be essentially constant in \(W\).  Positivity makes the comparison valid on a full-measure set with full support, and continuity of the \(C^3\) mechanism upgrades essential constancy to pointwise constancy, contradicting causal minimality.  The same argument applied to the conditional law of \(W\) rules out every edge from \(U\) or \(V\) into \(W\).  Hence \(W\) is isolated.

Because \(U\) and \(V\) are dependent, their two-node acyclic scalar graph cannot be empty; it must contain exactly one of \(U\to V\) and \(V\to U\).  The corresponding structural equation is a directional scalar additive-noise representation, contrary to Lemma~\ref{lem:vector-family-atomicity}.  Therefore
\[
\mathfrak A(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})\neq\varnothing,
\qquad
\mathfrak S(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})=\varnothing.
\]
This proves the strict non-scalar frontier and all comparator claims that do not assert the unresolved global unknown-partition theorem.  Conditional on that theorem, Lemma~\ref{lem:gobler-fixed-partition-identifiability} supplies exactly its fixed-partition graph projection; the missing content is only the unconditional assertion that the partition itself is identified from \(P\).
