Let \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\). First, if \(G_R\) and \(G_{R'}\) are both empty, then \(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\). Second, when \(d=3\), there are no distinct \(i,j,k\in V\) such that \(\pi_R=\{\{i,j\},\{k\}\}\), \(G_R\) has the single edge \(\{k\}\to\{i,j\}\), \(\pi_{R'}=\{\{i,k\},\{j\}\}\), and \(G_{R'}\) has the single edge \(\{j\}\to\{i,k\}\). Hence unequal incomparable partitions are excluded in the empty-DAG case and in the relabelled three-coordinate single-edge crossing, but no arbitrary-crossing conclusion is asserted.
