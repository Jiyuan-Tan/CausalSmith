For two unequal finite partitions, the refinement order gives an exhaustive dichotomy: they are incomparable, or one is a strict refinement of the other.  If \(\mathrm{oeq:crossing-rigidity}\) supplied its requested innovation split, atomicity would exclude the first case; if \(\mathrm{oeq:strict-refinement-rigidity}\) supplied its requested coarse-innovation split, atomicity would exclude the second.  This proves the logical reduction in the statement, but neither load-bearing split is presently established in the frozen core, so equality of all admissible partitions is not yet proved.
