For any \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\), Definition~\ref{def:refinement-order} makes \(\preceq\) the ordinary refinement partial order on the finite set of coordinate partitions.  If \(\pi_R\neq\pi_{R'}\), exactly one of the following alternatives holds:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\pi_R\not\preceq\pi_{R'}\ \text{ and }\ \pi_{R'}\not\preceq\pi_R,\\
&\pi_R\prec\pi_{R'},\\
&\pi_{R'}\prec\pi_R.
\end{aligned}
\]
The first alternative would be excluded by a positive resolution of Open-ended Question~\ref{oeq:crossing-rigidity}; the second would be excluded by a positive resolution of Open-ended Question~\ref{oeq:strict-refinement-rigidity}; and the third reduces to the second after interchanging \(R\) and \(R'\).  In each case, the requested conclusion is a nontrivial directional additive-noise split whose mechanism is \(C^3\), whose residual is independent of the conditioning innovation coordinates, and whose residual law lies in the dimension-matched \(\operatorname{SmoothPos}\) class.  Such a split is ruled out verbatim by Assumption~\ref{ass:innovation-atomicity}.  Hence the two rigidity conclusions would imply \(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\).  The already proved Lemma~\ref{lem:empty-dag-common-refinement} closes the subcase in which both graphs are empty.  What is not yet proved is either arbitrary rigidity conclusion.
