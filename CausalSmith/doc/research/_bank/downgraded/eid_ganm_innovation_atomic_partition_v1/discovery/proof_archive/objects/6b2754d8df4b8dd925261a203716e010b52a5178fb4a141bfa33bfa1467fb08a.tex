Let \((S,\mathcal S,\mu)\) and \((T,\mathcal T,\nu)\) be \(\sigma\)-finite measure spaces, and let \(F:S\times T\to[0,\infty]\) be \(\mathcal S\otimes\mathcal T\)-measurable. Then both section-integral maps are measurable and
\[
\int_{S\times T}F\,d(\mu\times\nu)
=\int_S\!\left(\int_TF(s,t)\,d\nu(t)\right)d\mu(s)
=\int_T\!\left(\int_SF(s,t)\,d\mu(s)\right)d\nu(t).
\]
