For every \(P\) and every \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\), the following partition-ambiguity frontier holds. First, \(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\) implies \(G_R=G_{R'}\). Second, if \(G_R\) and \(G_{R'}\) are both empty, then \(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\). Third, when \(d=3\), there are no distinct \(i,j,k\in V\) for which \(\pi_R=\{\{i,j\},\{k\}\}\), \(G_R\) has the single edge \(\{k\}\to\{i,j\}\), \(\pi_{R'}=\{\{i,k\},\{j\}\}\), and \(G_{R'}\) has the single edge \(\{j\}\to\{i,k\}\). Fourth, no such pair can satisfy \(\pi_R\preceq\pi_{R'}\), \(\pi_R\neq\pi_{R'}\), and possess a block \(B\in\pi_{R'}\) that is a root of \(G_{R'}\), contains at least two blocks of \(\pi_R\), and has the property that every \(G_R\)-parent of every block \(A\in\pi_R\) with \(A\subseteq B\) is itself contained in \(B\); the symmetric conclusion holds after interchanging \(R\) and \(R'\). These conclusions do not assert equality of arbitrary admissible partitions.
