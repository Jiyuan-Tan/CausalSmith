The graph-identification step is complete conditional only on partition uniqueness.  Indeed, suppose that all members of \(\mathfrak A(P)\) have the same partition, choose \(R_0\in\mathfrak A(P)\), and put
\[
 \pi^\star(P)=\pi_{R_0}.
\]
Then \(\pi_R=\pi^\star(P)\) for every \(R\in\mathfrak A(P)\).  By Definitions~\ref{def:admissible-class} and \ref{def:supplied-group-class},
\[
 R\in\mathfrak G_{\pi^\star(P)}(P)
 \qquad\text{for every }R\in\mathfrak A(P).
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:gobler-fixed-partition-identifiability} therefore gives \(G_R=G_{R_0}\) for every such \(R\).  Defining \(G^\star(P)=G_{R_0}\) and using Definition~\ref{def:recovery-map},
\[
 \mathcal T(P)
 =\{(\pi_R,G_R):R\in\mathfrak A(P)\}
 =\{(\pi^\star(P),G^\star(P))\}.
\]
Existence uses only \(\mathfrak A(P)\ne\varnothing\); labelled graph equality is exactly the conclusion of the cited fixed-partition lemma.  Thus the sole missing premise is the unresolved general partition-uniqueness theorem.
