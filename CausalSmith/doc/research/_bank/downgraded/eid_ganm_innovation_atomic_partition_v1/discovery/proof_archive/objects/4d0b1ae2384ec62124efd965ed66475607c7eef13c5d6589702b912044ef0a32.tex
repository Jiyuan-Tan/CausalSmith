Let \(n\geq1\), let \(E\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) be open, and let \(F:E\to\mathbb R^n\) be continuously differentiable. If \(a\in E\) and \(DF(a)\) is invertible, then, with \(b=F(a)\), there exist open sets \(U,V\subseteq\mathbb R^n\) with \(a\in U\), \(b\in V\), such that \(F\) is one-to-one on \(U\), \(F(U)=V\), and the inverse map \(G:V\to U\) is continuously differentiable.
