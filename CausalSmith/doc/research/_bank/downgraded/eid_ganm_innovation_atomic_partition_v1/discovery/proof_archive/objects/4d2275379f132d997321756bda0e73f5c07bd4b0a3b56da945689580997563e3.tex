Fix \(\theta=(a,b,c_0,d_0,\lambda)\in(0,\infty)^5\), and abbreviate the coordinates from Definition~\ref{def:vector-atomic-witness} by \((U,V,W)\).  That definition gives the factorization
\[
 p_{U,V,W}(u,v,w)=p_{U,V}(u,v)p_W(w)>0,
\]
so \(W\perp\!\!\!\perp(U,V)\).  Lemma~\ref{lem:vector-family-atomicity} also gives that \(U\) and \(V\) are dependent and excludes both scalar additive-noise relations
\[
 V=h(U)+N_V,\quad N_V\perp\!\!\!\perp U,
 \qquad\text{and}\qquad
 U=k(V)+N_U,\quad N_U\perp\!\!\!\perp V,
\]
whenever the mechanism is \(C^3\) and the residual law belongs to \(\operatorname{SmoothPos}(1)\).

Suppose for contradiction that \(R\in\mathfrak S(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})\).  We first prove that the singleton vertex \(\{3\}\), corresponding to \(W\), is isolated in \(G_R\).  If an edge incident to \(\{3\}\) existed, write it as \(i\to j\), let \(A=X_i\) be its parent, let \(Y=X_j\) be its child, and let \(S=X_{\cup(\operatorname{pa}_{G_R}(j)\setminus\{i\})}\) denote the other parents.  One of \(A,Y\) is \(W\), while every coordinate of the other variable and of \(S\) lies in \(\{U,V\}\).  The displayed density factorization therefore implies
\[
 A\perp\!\!\!\perp Y\mid S.
\]
This conditional independence also covers the case in which \(S\) is empty.

Let \(r_j\) be the strictly positive innovation density at node \(j\), and write the structural equation, with the other parent coordinates displayed, as
\[
 Y=f_j(A,S)+N_j,
 \qquad N_j\perp\!\!\!\perp(A,S).
\]
Definition~\ref{def:scalar-restricted-class} and its member assumptions give \(f_j\in C^3\), \(\int_{\mathbb R}r_j(y)\,dy=1\), and \(r_j(y)>0\) for every \(y\).  Thus, for Lebesgue-almost every \((a,s,y)\),
\[
 p_{Y\mid A=a,S=s}(y)=r_j\bigl(y-f_j(a,s)\bigr).
\]
The conditional independence makes the left side independent of \(a\).  Consequently, for Lebesgue-almost every \((a,a',s,y)\),
\[
 r_j\bigl(y-f_j(a,s)\bigr)
 =r_j\bigl(y-f_j(a',s)\bigr).
\]
Here equivalence with Lebesgue null sets is justified by the everywhere positive joint density of \((U,V,W)\) and its marginals.

By Fubini's theorem, for Lebesgue-almost every \((a,a',s)\), the last equality holds for Lebesgue-almost every \(y\).  Fix such a triple and put \(c=f_j(a,s)-f_j(a',s)\).  If \(c\ne0\), translation of \(y\) gives \(r_j(y)=r_j(y+c)\) for almost every \(y\).  Let \(I\) be a half-open interval of length \(|c|\).  Strict positivity gives \(q=\int_I r_j(y)\,dy>0\), while the almost-everywhere period relation gives
\[
 1=\int_{\mathbb R}r_j(y)\,dy
 \ge \sum_{n=-N}^{N}\int_{I+nc}r_j(y)\,dy
 =(2N+1)q
\]
for every \(N\ge0\), an impossibility as \(N\to\infty\).  Thus \(c=0\) for each triple in the Fubini-conull set, and \(f_j(a,s)=f_j(a',s)\) for almost every \((a,a',s)\).  The complement of the equality set is open because \(f_j\) is continuous.  A nonempty open complement would have positive Lebesgue measure, contradicting the almost-everywhere equality.  Therefore
\[
 f_j(a,s)=f_j(a',s)\quad\text{for all }a,a',s.
\]
The child mechanism is constant in the input coordinates indexed by its parent \(i\), contrary to Assumption~\ref{ass:causal-minimality}.  This proves that \(\{3\}\) is isolated.

After deleting this isolated vertex, acyclicity leaves only three possibilities on \(\{1\},\{2\}\): no edge, \(\{1\}\to\{2\}\), or \(\{2\}\to\{1\}\).  With no edge, mutual innovation independence would imply \(U\perp\!\!\!\perp V\), contradicting Lemma~\ref{lem:vector-family-atomicity}.  The edge \(\{1\}\to\{2\}\) yields
\[
 V=f_{R,\{2\}}(U)+\varepsilon_{R,\{2\}},
 \qquad \varepsilon_{R,\{2\}}\perp\!\!\!\perp U,
\]
where \(f_{R,\{2\}}\in C^3\) and \(\mathcal L(\varepsilon_{R,\{2\}})\in\operatorname{SmoothPos}(1)\) by the smoothness, positivity, normalization, and \(C^3\) log-density clauses of Definition~\ref{def:scalar-restricted-class}.  This is the first relation excluded by Lemma~\ref{lem:vector-family-atomicity}.  The reverse edge analogously yields its excluded reverse relation.  All possible acyclic scalar graphs are impossible, so \(\mathfrak S(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})=\varnothing\).
