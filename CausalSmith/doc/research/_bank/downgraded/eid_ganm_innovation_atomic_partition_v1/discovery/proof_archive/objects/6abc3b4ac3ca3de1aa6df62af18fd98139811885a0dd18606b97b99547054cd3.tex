For every \(P\), put \(\mathcal D_P=\{\pi_R:R\in\mathfrak A(P)\}\). There is a unique function \(\Gamma_P\) on \(\mathcal D_P\) such that \(G_R=\Gamma_P(\pi_R)\) for every \(R\in\mathfrak A(P)\), and hence \[\mathcal T(P)=\{(\pi,\Gamma_P(\pi)):\pi\in\mathcal D_P\}.\] Thus \(\mathcal T(P)\) is the graph of a partial function from coordinate partitions to coordinate-labelled block DAGs. Its domain obeys three proved exclusions: distinct domain points cannot both carry empty DAGs; when \(d=3\), it cannot contain the relabelled crossed single-edge pair \(\{\{i,j\},\{k\}\}\) and \(\{\{i,k\},\{j\}\}\); and it cannot contain a strict refinement pair satisfying the displayed coarse-root and fine-parent-closure conditions of the partition-ambiguity frontier. No claim is made that \(\mathcal D_P\) is a singleton.
