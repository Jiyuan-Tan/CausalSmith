Fix \(\pi_0\).  By \(\mathrm{def:admissible-class}\), every \(R\in\mathfrak A(P)\) with \(\pi_R=\pi_0\) is a GANM on the supplied blocks, has \(C^3\) mechanisms, mutually independent positive smooth block noises, is acyclic and causally minimal, and satisfies the stated conditional Equation~(3)-failure requirement.  These are precisely the membership requirements retained by \(\mathrm{def:supplied-group-class}\); innovation atomicity is additional.  Hence
\[
 \{R\in\mathfrak A(P):\pi_R=\pi_0\}\subseteq\mathfrak G_{\pi_0}(P).
\]
The cited fixed-partition theorem then makes \(G_R\) constant over this fiber, proving the asserted graph-coordinate reduction without identifying the partition.

The inclusion is strict.  Under \(\mathrm{def:merge-witness}\), put \(B=\{1,2\}\).  The change of variables \((N_1,N_2)\mapsto(X_1,X_2)=(N_1,N_1+N_1^3+N_2)\) has determinant one, so the block density is
\[
 p_{X_B}(x_1,x_2)=\phi(x_1)\phi(x_2-x_1-x_1^3),
\]
which is everywhere positive with a smooth log-density.  It is independent of \(X_3=N_3\).  The empty graph on \(\{B,\{3\}\}\) therefore belongs to \(\mathfrak G_{\{B,\{3\}\}}(P_{\mathrm{merge}})\); its edgewise and minimality conditions are vacuous.  But its block innovation satisfies
\[
 \varepsilon_{B,2}=\varepsilon_{B,1}+\varepsilon_{B,1}^3+N_2,
 \qquad N_2\perp\!\!\!\perp\varepsilon_{B,1},
\]
with a \(C^3\) mechanism and \(\mathcal L(N_2)\in\operatorname{SmoothPos}(1)\).  Hence this representation is not in \(\mathfrak A(P_{\mathrm{merge}})\).

On the singleton partition, the cited Peters theorem makes the graph constant over \(\mathfrak S(P)\), while the cited grouped theorem makes it constant over the corresponding supplied-group fiber containing the singleton part of \(\mathfrak A(P)\).  If the common subfiber is nonempty, a representation in the intersection anchors the two constants, so the graph conclusions agree.

It remains to prove strictness beyond the scalar frontier.  The vector-family lemma gives \(\mathfrak A(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})\neq\varnothing\).  Suppose that a causally minimal scalar ANM representation of \((U,V,W)\) existed.  Since \(W\perp\!\!\!\perp(U,V)\), no edge can join \(W\) to \(U\) or \(V\).  Indeed, if \(W\) is a parent of \(Y\in\{U,V\}\), then joint independence implies
\[
 p_{Y\mid X_{\operatorname{pa}(Y)}}(y\mid x_{\operatorname{pa}(Y)})
 =p_{Y\mid X_{\operatorname{pa}(Y)\setminus\{W\}}}(y\mid x_{\operatorname{pa}(Y)\setminus\{W\}})
\]
for almost every parent value.  The ANM formula makes the left side a translate of the positive noise density by \(f_Y(x_{\operatorname{pa}(Y)})\).  Equality for two \(W\)-values forces the translation difference to be zero: otherwise that positive integrable density would have a nonzero period and infinite integral.  Continuity and positivity of the parent density then make \(f_Y\) constant in its \(W\)-argument, contrary to causal minimality.  If instead \(Y\in\{U,V\}\) is a parent of \(W\), joint independence gives \(p_{W\mid X_{\operatorname{pa}(W)}}=p_W\), and the identical translation argument makes \(f_W\) constant in its \(Y\)-argument.  Thus \(W\) is isolated.  Since \(U,V\) are dependent, the remaining two-node scalar DAG must contain either \(U\to V\) or \(V\to U\), and its structural equation is a directional scalar additive-noise representation.  Both directions were excluded by \(\mathrm{lem:vector-family-atomicity}\).  Therefore \(\mathfrak S(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})=\varnothing\).  This proves every asserted inclusion, agreement, and strictness claim, while keeping the vector and scalar differential conditions distinct.
