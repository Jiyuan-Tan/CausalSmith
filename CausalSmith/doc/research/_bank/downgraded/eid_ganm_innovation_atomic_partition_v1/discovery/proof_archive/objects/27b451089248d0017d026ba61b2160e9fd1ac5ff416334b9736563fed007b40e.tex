Let \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\) satisfy \(\pi_R\preceq\pi_{R'}\) and \(\pi_R\neq\pi_{R'}\). Suppose there is a block \(B\in\pi_{R'}\) that is a root of \(G_{R'}\), contains at least two blocks of \(\pi_R\), and is fine-parent-closed: whenever \(A\in\pi_R\), \(A\subseteq B\), and \(Q\in\operatorname{pa}_{G_R}(A)\), one has \(Q\subseteq B\). Then these hypotheses are incompatible. More precisely, choosing a sink fine block \(D\subset B\) and setting \(C=B\setminus D\) yields a \(C^3\) map \(h:\mathbb R^{|C|}\to\mathbb R^{|D|}\) such that \(\varepsilon_{R',D}=h(\varepsilon_{R',C})+\varepsilon_{R,D}\), with \(\varepsilon_{R,D}\perp\!\!\!\perp\varepsilon_{R',C}\) and \(\mathcal L(\varepsilon_{R,D})\in\operatorname{SmoothPos}(|D|)\), contradicting innovation atomicity of \(\varepsilon_{R',B}\). No conclusion is asserted for a coarse nonroot block or when a fine block in \(B\) has an external parent.
