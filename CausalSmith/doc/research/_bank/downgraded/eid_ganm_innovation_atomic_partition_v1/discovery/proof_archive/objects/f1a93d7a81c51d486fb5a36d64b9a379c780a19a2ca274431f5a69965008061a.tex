Because both graphs are empty, \(\varepsilon_{R,B}=X_B\) and \(\varepsilon_{R',B'}=X_{B'}\).  The triangular density lemma and block-noise independence give
\[
 \ell_P(x)=\sum_{B\in\pi_R}\nu_{R,B}(x_B)
 =\sum_{B'\in\pi_{R'}}\nu_{R',B'}(x_{B'}).
\]
Enumerate the cells of \(\rho:=\pi_R\wedge\pi_{R'}\) as \(C_1,\ldots,C_k\).  If \(i\in C_r\), \(j\in C_s\), and \(r\neq s\), then \(i,j\) are separated by at least one of \(\pi_R,\pi_{R'}\): otherwise their blocks in both partitions would have the same nonempty intersection cell.  Differentiating the factorization belonging to a separating partition yields
\[
 \partial_i\partial_j\ell_P(x)=0
 \quad\text{for every }x\in\mathbb R^d.
\]
It follows that the gradient of \(\ell_P\) in the coordinates \(C_r\) is independent of all coordinates outside \(C_r\).  Integrating these block gradients successively along the axis-aligned path from \(0\) to \(x\) gives
\[
 \ell_P(x)=c+\sum_{r=1}^k g_r(x_{C_r}),
 \qquad
 g_r(u):=\ell_P(0_{C_1},\ldots,0_{C_{r-1}},u,0_{C_{r+1}},\ldots,0_{C_k})-\ell_P(0),
\]
after absorbing the resulting constant into \(c\).  Each \(g_r\) is \(C^3\).  Put
\[
 I_r=\int_{\mathbb R^{|C_r|}}e^{g_r(u)}\,du.
\]
Tonelli's theorem and \(\int p_P=1\) imply
\[
 1=e^c\prod_{r=1}^k I_r.
\]
Every \(I_r\) is strictly positive, and the last finite product equality forces every \(I_r<\infty\).  Thus \(r_r=e^{g_r}/I_r\) is an everywhere positive unit-integral density with \(\log r_r\in C^3\), and
\[
 p_P(x)=\prod_{r=1}^k r_r(x_{C_r}).
\]
This proves mutual independence of the common-refinement cells and places every cell law, and every product of cell laws, in the appropriate \(\operatorname{SmoothPos}\) class.

If \(\pi_R\neq\pi_{R'}\), at least one block \(B\) of one partition contains at least two cells of \(\rho\); otherwise both partitions would equal \(\rho\).  Choose one such cell \(C\subsetneq B\) and put \(D=B\setminus C\).  In the corresponding empty-DAG representation,
\[
 \varepsilon_{R,B,C}=X_C,
 \qquad
 \varepsilon_{R,B,D}=0(X_C)+X_D,
 \qquad
 X_D\perp\!\!\!\perp X_C,
\]
or the identical formula in \(R'\).  The zero map is \(C^3\), and the density just constructed shows \(\mathcal L(X_D)\in\operatorname{SmoothPos}(|D|)\).  This is a forbidden nontrivial directional split under \(\mathrm{ass:innovation-atomicity}\).  Hence \(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\).
