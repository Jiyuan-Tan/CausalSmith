Let \(B_1,\ldots,B_m\) be a topological ordering of \(G_R\).  Define \(\Psi_R\) as in the statement.  Acyclicity in \(\mathrm{ass:block-acyclicity}\) implies that every parent of \(B_j\) occurs among \(B_1,\ldots,B_{j-1}\).  Hence, given \(e\in\mathbb R^d\), the equations
\[
 x_{B_j}=f_{R,B_j}\!\left(x_{\cup\operatorname{pa}_{G_R}(B_j)}\right)+e_{B_j},
 \qquad j=1,\ldots,m,
\]
determine \(x_{B_1},\ldots,x_{B_m}\) successively and uniquely.  They define a global inverse \(T_R=\Psi_R^{-1}\).  The mechanisms are \(C^3\) by \(\mathrm{ass:mechanisms-c3}\); induction over \(j\) therefore shows that both \(\Psi_R\) and \(T_R\) are \(C^3\).

In the same topological block order, \(D\Psi_R(x)\) is block lower triangular.  Indeed, the \(B_j\)-output depends only on \(x_{B_j}\) and parent blocks preceding it, and
\[
 D_{x_{B_j}}\Psi_R(x)_{B_j}=I_{|B_j|}.
\]
Thus
\[
 \det D\Psi_R(x)=\prod_{j=1}^m\det I_{|B_j|}=1.
\]
The density formula can also be verified without any local inversion qualification.  For every nonnegative Borel function \(a\), mutual innovation independence and the innovation densities give
\[
 \mathbb E[a(X)]
 =\int_{\mathbb R^d}a(T_R(e))\prod_{j=1}^m p_{\varepsilon_{R,B_j}}(e_{B_j})\,de.
\]
Successively substitute \(x_{B_j}=f_{R,B_j}(x_{\cup\operatorname{pa}(B_j)})+e_{B_j}\) in topological order.  Conditional on the preceding blocks, each substitution is a Euclidean translation and hence preserves Lebesgue measure.  Tonelli's theorem justifies the successive substitutions for nonnegative \(a\).  Therefore
\[
 \mathbb E[a(X)]
 =\int_{\mathbb R^d}a(x)
 \prod_{B\in\pi_R}p_{\varepsilon_{R,B}}\!\left(x_B-f_{R,B}(x_{\cup\operatorname{pa}_{G_R}(B)})\right)dx.
\]
This proves the displayed density formula.  Every factor is strictly positive by \(\mathrm{ass:innovation-positive-density}\), so \(p_P>0\).  Taking logarithms gives the displayed sum.  Each summand is the composition of a \(C^3\) innovation log-density from \(\mathrm{ass:innovation-c3-density}\) with a \(C^3\) residual map, so \(\ell_P\in C^3(\mathbb R^d)\).
