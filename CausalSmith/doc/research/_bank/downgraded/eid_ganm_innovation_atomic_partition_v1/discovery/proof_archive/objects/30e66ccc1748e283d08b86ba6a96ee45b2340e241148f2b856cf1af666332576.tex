Assume first that \(G_R\) and \(G_{R'}\) are empty.  All premises of
Lemma~\ref{lem:empty-dag-common-refinement} hold because
\(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\).  That lemma gives directly
\(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\).

For the second claim, suppose for contradiction that \(d=3\) and that distinct
\(i,j,k\in V\) realize the displayed configuration.  Let
\(\sigma:\{1,2,3\}\to V\) be the bijection with
\(\sigma(1)=i\), \(\sigma(2)=j\), and \(\sigma(3)=k\), and relabel the observed
vector by \(\widetilde X_a=X_{\sigma(a)}\).  Relabel every block, mechanism
input and output, innovation coordinate, and graph vertex by \(\sigma^{-1}\),
obtaining representations \(\widetilde R,\widetilde R'\) of the law
\(\widetilde P\) of \(\widetilde X\).

We verify that this relabelling does not add an assumption.  Coordinate
permutations are linear \(C^\infty\) bijections with determinant of absolute
value one, so strict positivity and \(C^3\) log-density regularity are
preserved.  Composition of a \(C^3\) mechanism with input and output
permutations remains \(C^3\).  Mutual independence of the block innovations,
acyclicity, and nonconstancy along an edge are invariant under bijective
relabelling.  The parent and nondescendant sets correspond bijectively, and
the displayed Equation~(3)-failure condition is the same coordinate-free
Jacobian--Hessian equality after simultaneous row and column permutation.
Finally, every nontrivial coordinate split before relabelling corresponds to
one after relabelling, with the same independence and
dimension-matched \(\operatorname{SmoothPos}\) properties.  Therefore
\(\widetilde R,\widetilde R'\in\mathfrak A(\widetilde P)\).

Their partitions are exactly
\[
 \pi_{\widetilde R}=\{\{1,2\},\{3\}\},\qquad
 \pi_{\widetilde R'}=\{\{1,3\},\{2\}\},
\]
and their respective single edges are
\(\{3\}\to\{1,2\}\) and \(\{2\}\to\{1,3\}\).
Theorem~\ref{thm:three-coordinate-crossing} says that two such admissible
innovation-atomic representations cannot coexist.  This contradiction proves
the relabelled exclusion.  The proof used only the empty-DAG lemma and the
three-coordinate theorem, so it yields no claim for a different incomparable
configuration.
