All comparator claims that do not invoke the unresolved unknown-partition
conclusion can be established.  Fix \(\pi_0\).  The membership comparison in
Definitions~\ref{def:admissible-class} and
\ref{def:supplied-group-class} gives
\[
 \{R\in\mathfrak A(P):\pi_R=\pi_0\}
 \subseteq \mathfrak G_{\pi_0}(P),
\]
because innovation atomicity is additional to, and does not remove, any
supplied-group condition.  The inclusion is strict over law--representation
pairs: Proposition~\ref{prop:atomicity-removal-converse} constructs the merged
empty-DAG representation of \(P_{\mathrm{merge}}\), verifies every
supplied-group condition, and exhibits the forbidden internal split
\[
 \varepsilon_{\{1,2\},2}
 =\varepsilon_{\{1,2\},1}+\varepsilon_{\{1,2\},1}^{3}+N_2,
 \qquad
 N_2\perp\!\!\!\perp\varepsilon_{\{1,2\},1},
 \qquad
 \mathcal L(N_2)\in\operatorname{SmoothPos}(1).
\]
Hence that representation is in the supplied-group fiber but not in the
atomic fiber.

On the common singleton subfiber, if
\(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\cap\mathfrak S(P)\), then
Lemma~\ref{lem:peters-scalar-dag-identifiability} gives \(G_R=G_{R'}\).
Thus the labelled graph conclusion there agrees with Theorem~28 of Peters et
al.

Finally fix \(\theta\in(0,\infty)^5\).  Lemma
\ref{lem:vector-family-atomicity} supplies an empty-DAG representation in
\(\mathfrak A(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})\), so this atomic fiber is nonempty.
It also excludes additive-noise representations in both directions between
\(U\) and \(V\).  To see that no third scalar variable can evade that
obstruction, use the following elementary translation fact.  If a probability
law \(L\) on \(\mathbb R\) were invariant under translation by \(c\ne0\), its
characteristic function \(\phi\) would satisfy
\(\phi(t)=e^{itc}\phi(t)\).  Continuity at zero and \(\phi(0)=1\) give a
sufficiently small \(t\) for which \(\phi(t)\ne0\) and \(e^{itc}\ne1\), a
contradiction.  Thus two translates of a probability law are equal only when
their shifts agree.

Suppose for contradiction that a causally minimal scalar additive-noise
representation of \((U,V,W)\) existed.  The witness construction makes
\(W\perp\!\!\!\perp(U,V)\) and gives a strictly positive joint density.  For
an edge from \(W\) into \(Y\in\{U,V\}\), conditioning on the remaining parent,
if any, makes the structural conditional law of \(Y\) a translate of its
innovation by the mechanism value, while joint independence makes that
conditional law independent of \(W\).  The translation fact forces the
mechanism to be constant in \(W\), contrary to causal minimality.  For an edge
from \(Y\in\{U,V\}\) into \(W\), the conditional law of \(W\) given all its
parents is likewise independent of their values, and the same translation
argument contradicts causal minimality.  Strict positivity makes all these
conditional comparisons valid on full-measure sets, and continuity of the
\(C^3\) mechanisms upgrades essential constancy to constancy.  Therefore
\(W\) is isolated.  Since \(U\) and \(V\) are dependent, their two-node
acyclic scalar graph must contain exactly one of \(U\to V\) and \(V\to U\).
Its structural equation is one of the two directional additive-noise
representations excluded by Lemma~\ref{lem:vector-family-atomicity}.  This
contradiction proves
\[
 \mathfrak A(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})\ne\varnothing,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak S(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})=\varnothing.
\]
What remains uncertified in the exact comparator theorem is only its two
sentences that project or compare the still-unproved atomic
partition-and-DAG theorem itself.
