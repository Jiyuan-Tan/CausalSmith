The graph-identification step is complete conditional on partition
uniqueness.  Assume that
\[
 (\mathrm U)\qquad
 R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\Longrightarrow\pi_R=\pi_{R'}.
\]
Because \(\mathfrak A(P)\ne\varnothing\), choose
\(R_0\in\mathfrak A(P)\) and put
\(\pi^\star(P)=\pi_{R_0}\) and \(G^\star(P)=G_{R_0}\).  By \((\mathrm U)\),
every \(R\in\mathfrak A(P)\) has \(\pi_R=\pi^\star(P)\).  Definition
\ref{def:admissible-class} supplies every condition in Definition
\ref{def:supplied-group-class}, so
\[
 R,R_0\in\mathfrak G_{\pi^\star(P)}(P).
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:gobler-fixed-partition-identifiability} therefore gives
\(G_R=G_{R_0}=G^\star(P)\).  Applying Definition~\ref{def:recovery-map},
\[
 \mathcal T(P)
 =\{(\pi_R,G_R):R\in\mathfrak A(P)\}
 =\{(\pi^\star(P),G^\star(P))\}.
\]
Thus no graph-level gap remains: the only missing premise is \((\mathrm U)\),
which is exactly Theorem~\ref{thm:partition-uniqueness} and presently depends
on the two unresolved rigidity questions.
