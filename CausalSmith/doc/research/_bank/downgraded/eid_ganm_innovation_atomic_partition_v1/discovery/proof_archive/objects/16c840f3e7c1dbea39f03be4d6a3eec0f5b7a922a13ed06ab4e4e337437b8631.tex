For any two finite partitions \(\pi_R\neq\pi_{R'}\), exactly one of the following occurs: they are incomparable under \(\preceq\), \(\pi_R\prec\pi_{R'}\), or \(\pi_{R'}\prec\pi_R\). A positive resolution of Open-ended Question~\ref{oeq:crossing-rigidity} would exclude the first case by producing a forbidden directional split of an innovation. A positive resolution of Open-ended Question~\ref{oeq:strict-refinement-rigidity}, applied after exchanging \(R\) and \(R'\) if necessary, would exclude the other two cases by producing a forbidden split of a coarse-block innovation. Therefore the displayed finite-poset reduction is complete, but the two innovation-splitting premises are not established in the frozen core.
