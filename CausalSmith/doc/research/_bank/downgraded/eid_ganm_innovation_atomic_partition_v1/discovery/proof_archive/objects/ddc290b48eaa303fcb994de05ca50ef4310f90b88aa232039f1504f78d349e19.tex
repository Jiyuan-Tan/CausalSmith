For every \(P\) with \(\mathfrak A(P)\neq\varnothing\), all \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\) have \(\pi_R=\pi_{R'}\). Indeed, unequal finite coordinate partitions are either incomparable or strictly comparable under \(\preceq\): Open-ended Question 1 excludes the incomparable case by forcing a directional split in one innovation, and Open-ended Question 2 excludes the strictly comparable case by forcing a directional split in a coarse-block innovation. Innovation atomicity rules out both splits. Thus the observed law has one coordinate-labelled innovation-atomic causal-unit partition, and this partition is the unique refinement-minimal element of \(\{\pi_R:R\in\mathfrak A(P)\}\).
