Assume \(\mathfrak A(P)\neq\varnothing\). By Theorem~\ref{thm:partition-uniqueness}, every two representations \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\) have the same coordinate partition. Choose \(R_0\in\mathfrak A(P)\) and set
\[
\pi^\star(P):=\pi_{R_0}.
\]
The preceding equality makes this definition independent of the choice of \(R_0\), and for every \(R\in\mathfrak A(P)\),
\[
\pi_R=\pi^\star(P). \tag{1}
\]

We next verify the premise needed for the fixed-partition result rather than identifying the graph by definition. Unfold Definitions~\ref{def:admissible-class} and~\ref{def:supplied-group-class}. A representation \(R\in\mathfrak A(P)\) induces \(P\), is a grouped additive-noise model on its labelled blocks, has \(C^3\) mechanisms and mutually independent innovations with everywhere positive densities and \(C^3\) log-densities, is acyclic and causally minimal, and satisfies the parent--node--nondescendant conditional Equation~(3)-failure requirement. Innovation atomicity is an additional restriction in \(\mathfrak A(P)\) and is not required for membership in the supplied-group fiber. Combining these clauses with (1) gives
\[
R\in\mathfrak G_{\pi^\star(P)}(P)
\qquad\text{for every }R\in\mathfrak A(P). \tag{2}
\]
If \(R,R'\in\mathfrak A(P)\), (2) and the cited Lemma~\ref{lem:gobler-fixed-partition-identifiability} therefore imply
\[
G_R=G_{R'}. \tag{3}
\]
Set \(G^\star(P):=G_{R_0}\). Equality (3) is equality of coordinate-subset-labelled vertices and directed edge sets, as required by Definition~\ref{def:recovery-map}.

Finally, the recovery map is the observable-law functional
\[
\mathcal T(P)=\{(\pi_R,G_R):R\in\mathfrak A(P)\}.
\]
Equations (1) and (3) give
\[
\mathcal T(P)\subseteq\{(\pi^\star(P),G^\star(P))\}. \tag{4}
\]
Conversely, the chosen \(R_0\in\mathfrak A(P)\) contributes \((\pi_{R_0},G_{R_0})=(\pi^\star(P),G^\star(P))\) to \(\mathcal T(P)\), so
\[
\{(\pi^\star(P),G^\star(P))\}\subseteq\mathcal T(P). \tag{5}
\]
The two inclusions (4)--(5) prove the claimed equality. Thus the structural target is shown to equal the recovery functional; it was not defined to equal it.
