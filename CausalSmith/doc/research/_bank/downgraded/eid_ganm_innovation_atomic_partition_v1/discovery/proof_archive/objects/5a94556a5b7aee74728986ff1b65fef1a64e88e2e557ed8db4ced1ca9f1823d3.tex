Write
\[
 Q(u,v)=au^4+bv^4+\lambda u^2v^2+c_0u^2+d_0v^2.
\]
All five parameters are positive, so \(Q(u,v)\geq au^4+bv^4\).  Therefore
\[
 0<Z_{a,b,c_0,d_0,\lambda}
 \leq\left(\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-au^4}\,du\right)
      \left(\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-bv^4}\,dv\right)<\infty.
\]
The normalized density is everywhere positive, and its log is the polynomial \(-Q\) minus a constant, hence belongs to \(C^3(\mathbb R^2)\).  This proves membership in \(\operatorname{SmoothPos}(2)\).  Moreover,
\[
 \partial_u\partial_v\log p_{a,b,c_0,d_0,\lambda}(u,v)=-4\lambda uv
\]
is not identically zero.  Were \(U,V\) independent, their positive density would satisfy the rectangular identity
\[
 p(u,v)p(u_0,v_0)=p(u,v_0)p(u_0,v)
\]
for every \(u,v\) after continuity extends the almost-everywhere product factorization.  Taking logarithms and differentiating in \(u,v\) would make the mixed derivative vanish, a contradiction.  Hence \(U,V\) are dependent.

Conditional on \(U=u\), symmetry gives \(\mathbb E[V\mid U=u]=0\), and the conditional density is
\[
 r_{d_0+\lambda u^2}(v)
 =K(d_0+\lambda u^2)^{-1}\exp\{-bv^4-(d_0+\lambda u^2)v^2\}.
\]
For \(r>0\), quartic domination permits differentiation under the integral on every compact \(r\)-interval.  Thus
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \frac{d}{dr}\mathbb E_r[V^2]
 &=-\mathbb E_r[V^4]+\mathbb E_r[V^2]^2\\
 &=-\operatorname{Var}_r(V^2)<0.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Strictness holds because the everywhere positive density cannot concentrate \(V^2\) at one value.  Hence \(\operatorname{Var}(V\mid U=u)\) is a strictly decreasing function of \(u^2\) and is not essentially constant.  The conditional-covariance diagnostic excludes every \(V=h(U)+\eta_D\) with \(\eta_D\perp\!\!\!\perp U\).  Interchanging \(u,v\), \(a,b\), and \(c_0,d_0\) yields
\[
 \frac{d}{ds}\mathbb E_s[U^2]=-\operatorname{Var}_s(U^2)<0,
\]
so the reverse additive-noise representation is also impossible.  The bivariate law is therefore innovation-atomic.

The density of \(W\) is normalized by the same quartic bound, is everywhere positive, and has a \(C^3\) log-density.  In the specified empty-DAG representation, \((U,V)\perp\!\!\!\perp W\); all mechanisms are zero; acyclicity holds; and causal minimality and the edgewise condition are vacuous because there are no edges.  The scalar \(W\)-block has no nontrivial coordinate split, while the \((U,V)\)-block was just proved atomic.  Thus this representation belongs to \(\mathfrak A(P_{\mathrm{vec},\theta})\) for every \(\theta\in(0,\infty)^5\).
