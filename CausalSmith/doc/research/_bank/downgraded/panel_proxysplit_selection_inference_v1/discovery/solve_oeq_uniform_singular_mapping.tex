The answer is negative on the broad whole-array class.  Fix any \(0\leq D_{\mathrm{gap}}<\infty\), and suppose
\[
 0<\kappa<\frac{6373}{15369},
 \qquad
 0<\nu<\frac{39}{625},
\]
with a finite moment envelope \(C_{\mathrm{mom}}\) large enough for the bounded-moment witness in \(\texttt{thm:maximal-uniform-singular-mapping-failure}\).  Then
\[
 \lim_{n\to\infty}
 \sup_{(P_j)_{j\geq1}\in\mathcal M(D_{\mathrm{gap}})}
 \mathbb E_{P_n}\!\left[
  \sup_{x\in\mathbb R}
  \left|
   \Pr_{P_n}\!\left\{
    \frac{\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{gap}}-\tau}
         {\widehat{\operatorname{se}}_{\widehat J_A}}
    \leq x\,\middle|\,\mathcal F_A
   \right\}-\Phi(x)
  \right|
 \right]
 =1,
\]
rather than zero.  The certificate consists of stationary independent-over-time square proximal arrays with zero population loss gap, constant treatment effect, singular stacked limiting covariance,
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(D)=\frac{39}{625},
 \qquad
 V_s^{\mathrm{gap}}=V_s=1
 \quad(s\in\mathcal S).
\]
Consequently positive limiting marginal variances and coordinatewise scalar studentization do not suffice for the requested whole-array uniform selector-conditional Gaussian approximation; common finite-row stabilization is additionally required.
%%% FIELD theorem-proof
For a whole triangular array \(P_\bullet=(P_j)_{j\geq1}\), define its row-\(n\) expected conditional Kolmogorov error by
\[
 \mathcal K_n(P_\bullet)
 =\mathbb E_{P_n}\!\left[
  \sup_{x\in\mathbb R}
  \left|
   \Pr_{P_n}\!\left\{
    \frac{\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{gap}}-\tau}
         {\widehat{\operatorname{se}}_{\widehat J_A}}
    \leq x\,\middle|\,\mathcal F_A
   \right\}-\Phi(x)
  \right|
 \right].
\]
Both functions inside the absolute value are distribution functions, so
\[
 0\leq\mathcal K_n(P_\bullet)\leq1.
\]
Under the displayed restrictions on \(\kappa\), \(\nu\), and \(C_{\mathrm{mom}}\), \(\texttt{thm:uniform-singular-mapping-fails}\) gives, for every fixed finite \(D_{\mathrm{gap}}\),
\[
 \lim_{n\to\infty}
 \sup_{P_\bullet\in\mathcal M(D_{\mathrm{gap}})}
 \mathcal K_n(P_\bullet)=1.
\]
That theorem obtains the equality from the explicit diagonal arrays of \(\texttt{thm:maximal-uniform-singular-mapping-failure}\): their loss gaps vanish, their selector and rolewise ATT variances equal the displayed positive constants, their stacked limiting covariance is positive semidefinite but singular, and their studentized statistic tends to minus infinity on an event whose probability tends to one.  Thus those arrays satisfy the frozen assumptions yet attain the maximal possible conditional Kolmogorov error.  Since \(1\neq0\), the convergence asked for in \(\texttt{oeq:uniform-singular-mapping}\) is false.  A mapping argument using only the frozen assumptions cannot establish a false conclusion, which proves the asserted negative answer.
