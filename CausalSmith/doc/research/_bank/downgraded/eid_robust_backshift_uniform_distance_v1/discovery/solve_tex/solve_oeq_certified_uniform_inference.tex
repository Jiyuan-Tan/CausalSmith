For arbitrary nonempty positive-semidefinite covariance regions, \(\text{thm:confidence-union-coverage}\) proves the set inclusion
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\]
Therefore, whenever the two events are measurable,
\[
\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq 1-\alpha
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\geq 1-\alpha.
\]
Under the four true-instance bounds
\[
\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0,
\qquad
\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0,
\qquad
\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa,
\qquad
M_H\leq\overline M,
\]
and the sole candidate-side condition-number premise stated in \(\text{thm:non-effective-four-margin-contraction}\), that theorem gives constants
\[
L_0=(p!\,\overline\kappa^{p-1})^{1/p},
\qquad
K_0=\frac{6\overline M}{\gamma_0}
\sqrt{p(p-1)(1+L_0^2)},
\qquad
C_0=16K_0L_0^3,
\]
and asserts the existence of \(r_0>0\) such that, on \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\),
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq 2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\]
Hence along covered outcomes for which the same candidate-side condition-number premise holds and \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\), both set radii converge to zero. This is the strongest established partial result: it is set-theoretic and non-effective and therefore does not provide any of the executable or effective interfaces asked for in the open-ended question.
