Assume the full conditional analytic and stochastic premises stated in \(\mathrm{prop:conditional\text{-}uniform\text{-}margin\text{-}band}\), and specialize that proposition to the fixed confidence level \(\delta=0.05\).  This value lies in \((0,1/4)\), as required there.  The proposition supplies a threshold \(n_0<\infty\) such that, whenever \(n_\ast\ge n_0\), the declared-pair test \(\Psi_n\) satisfies
\[
 \sup_{\mathbf P\in\mathcal M_s:\,\gamma_{pq}\le0}
 \mathbb P_{\mathbf P}(\Psi_n=1)\le0.05
 \tag{1}
\]
and
\[
 \sup_{\mathbf P\in\mathcal M_s:\,\gamma_{pq}\ge3w_n}
 \mathbb P_{\mathbf P}(\Psi_n=0)\le0.05<0.20.
 \tag{2}
\]
Thus the measurable test \(\psi=\Psi_n\) satisfies the two error constraints in Definition \(\mathrm{def:testing\text{-}frontier}\) at the positive separation \(\rho=3w_n\).  Taking the infimum over feasible separations in that definition gives
\[
 \rho_n^\ast\le3w_n.
 \tag{3}
\]

We next compute this upper radius without suppressing its logarithm.  By Definition \(\mathrm{def:simultaneous\text{-}band}\), the definitions of \(r_{H,n}\), \(h_n\), and \(\ell_n\), and the specialization \(\delta=0.05\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 3w_n
 &=3\left(\frac2m+\frac{2M}{m^2}\right)r_{H,n}\\
 &=3\left(\frac2m+\frac{2M}{m^2}\right)C_1h_n^s\\
 &=3\left(\frac2m+\frac{2M}{m^2}\right)C_1
 \left\{\frac{\log(C_0Rn_\ast/0.05)}{n_\ast}\right\}^{s/(2s+d)}.
 \tag{4}
\end{aligned}
\]
All divisions in (4) are legitimate because \(m>0\), \(n_\ast\ge1\), and \(0.05>0\); moreover \(s/(2s+d)>0\) because \(s>0\) and \(d\ge2\).  Put \(A=C_0R/0.05\).  The fixed-constant conditions \(C_0>1\) and \(R\ge2\) imply \(A>1\).  For \(n_\ast\ge\max\{2,A\}\), monotonicity of the logarithm gives \(\log A\le\log n_\ast\), and hence
\[
 \log(C_0Rn_\ast/0.05)=\log(An_\ast)
 =\log A+\log n_\ast\le2\log n_\ast.
 \tag{5}
\]
Combining (3)--(5) yields
\[
 \rho_n^\ast
 \le
 3\left(\frac2m+\frac{2M}{m^2}\right)C_1,2^{s/(2s+d)}
 \left(\frac{\log n_\ast}{n_\ast}\right)^{s/(2s+d)}.
 \tag{6}
\]
The prefactor in (6) is finite and independent of \(n_\ast\); enlarging it if necessary gives the asserted finite constant \(C\) and proves the upper inequality for all sufficiently large \(n_\ast\).

Now also suppose that the existing class constants admit the strict-slack clause of Definition \(\mathrm{def:fixed\text{-}support\text{-}lower\text{-}family}\).  Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:sharp\text{-}separation\text{-}lower}\) then gives a constant \(c>0\) and a threshold \(n_1<\infty\) such that
\[
 \rho_n^\ast\ge c
 \left(\frac{\log n_\ast}{n_\ast}\right)^{s/(2s+d)}
 \qquad(n_\ast\ge n_1).
 \tag{7}
\]
Taking \(n_\ast\ge\max\{n_0,n_1,2,A\}\) and combining (6) with (7) proves the displayed two-sided frontier.  The upper argument used every premise inherited through \(\mathrm{prop:conditional\text{-}uniform\text{-}margin\text{-}band}\), whereas (7) used none of the endpoint-response, wavelet, kernel, influence, increment, or concentration premises; it is unconditional relative to that analytic and stochastic program.
