%%% FIELD feasible-nonempty-proof
Because \(G\) is finite and connected, one may construct a spanning tree \(T_G\) as follows.  Start with any vertex and, while the current vertex set is not all of \(U\), use connectedness to choose an edge having exactly one endpoint in the current set and adjoin that edge and its other endpoint.  Each step adds one vertex and creates no cycle, so the resulting subgraph is a spanning tree.  Since \(2\le K\le R\), delete any \(K-1\) edges of \(T_G\).  Deleting one edge of a tree increases the number of nonempty connected components by exactly one, and hence the remaining forest has exactly \(K\) nonempty components, say \(C_1,\ldots,C_K\).  They are pairwise disjoint and have union \(U\).  Moreover, each \(T_G[C_k]\) is connected, so its supergraph \(G[C_k]\) is connected.  Thus \((C_1,\ldots,C_K)\) satisfies every condition in \(\mathrm{def:connected-partitions}\), proving \(\mathfrak C_K(G)\ne\varnothing\).

%%% FIELD scope-charging-proof
Fix an ordered pair \((i,i')\) with \(\Sigma_{ii'}\ne0\), and put \(S=N_G[i]\cup N_G[i']\).  By \(\mathrm{def:interaction-graph}\), every two distinct vertices of \(S\) form an edge of \(F\); hence \(S\) is a clique of \(F\).

For each \(v\in S\), let
\[
  \mathcal T_v=\{t\in V(\mathcal T):v\in B_t\}.
\]
This set is nonempty by \(\mathrm{ass:valid-tree-decomposition}\) and induces a connected subtree by \(\mathrm{ass:running-intersection}\).  For \(u,v\in S\), either \(u=v\), or \(\{u,v\}\in E_F\); in the latter case \(\mathrm{ass:tree-edge-cover}\) gives a bag containing both.  Thus the finite family \(\{\mathcal T_v:v\in S\}\) is pairwise intersecting.  We now verify the needed Helly fact directly.  Root \(\mathcal T\), and for every nonempty subtree \(A\) let \(r(A)\) be its vertex nearest the root.  Choose \(A_\star\) for which \(r(A_\star)\) has maximum depth.  For any other pairwise-intersecting subtree \(A\), choose \(x\in A\cap A_\star\).  The root-to-\(x\) path contains both \(r(A)\) and \(r(A_\star)\); maximality of the depth of \(r(A_\star)\) puts \(r(A_\star)\) on the subpath from \(r(A)\) to \(x\).  Since \(A\) is connected, that subpath lies in \(A\).  Hence \(r(A_\star)\in A\).  Therefore all the subtrees have a common vertex.

It follows that
\[
  \mathcal T_S:=\bigcap_{v\in S}\mathcal T_v
  =\{t:S\subseteq B_t\}
\]
is nonempty.  An intersection of subtrees of a tree is connected whenever it is nonempty: the unique path between any two vertices of the intersection lies in every intersected subtree.  A nonempty connected subgraph of a rooted tree has a unique vertex nearest the root, since two distinct vertices of minimum depth would have a path passing through a vertex of smaller depth.  This vertex is precisely the node \(t(i,i')\) in \(\mathrm{def:scope-charges}\).  Thus each supported ordered pair belongs to exactly one set \(H_t\).

Finally, because \(S\subseteq B_{t(i,i')}\), the bag labeling determines each of the two sets
\[
  L_i(\mathcal C)=\{\ell_{\mathcal C}(v):v\in N_G[i]\},\qquad
  L_{i'}(\mathcal C)=\{\ell_{\mathcal C}(v):v\in N_G[i']\},
\]
and therefore determines \(m_{ii'}(\mathcal C)\) and \(a_{ii'}(\mathcal C)\) by \(\mathrm{def:exact-risk}\).  The sets \(H_t\) partition all ordered pairs with nonzero covariance entry, while every omitted pair contributes zero.  Consequently every summand of \(V\) is locally determined and is charged exactly once.

%%% FIELD exact-milp-proof
Write \(C_k=\{v:x_{vk}=1\}\) for the projection of a feasible mixed-integer point.  The equations \(\sum_{k=1}^Kx_{vk}=1\), with binary \(x_{vk}\), make the sets \(C_k\) pairwise disjoint with union \(U\).  Also \(s_k=\sum_vx_{vk}\ge1\), so every \(C_k\) is nonempty.

Fix \(k\).  The binary variables \(r_{vk}\) select exactly one vertex \(r_k\), and \(r_{vk}\le x_{vk}\) implies \(r_k\in C_k\).  The constraints on \(h_{vk}\) imply
\[
 h_{r_k k}=s_k,\qquad h_{vk}=0\quad(v\ne r_k).
\]
Indeed, at the selected root the lower bound is \(s_k\) and the upper bound \(h_{vk}\le s_k\) is binding, while at every nonroot \(h_{vk}\le Rr_{vk}=0\).  The flow balance is consequently
\[
 \operatorname{out}_k(v)-\operatorname{in}_k(v)
 =\begin{cases}
 s_k-1,&v=r_k,\\
 -1,&v\in C_k\setminus\{r_k\},\\
 0,&v\notin C_k.
 \end{cases}
\]
Both endpoint-capacity inequalities force \(f^k_{uv}=0\) unless \(u,v\in C_k\).  If a connected component \(A\) of \(G[C_k]\) did not contain \(r_k\), summing the displayed balance over \(A\) would give zero on the left, because internal flows cancel and no positive flow crosses the boundary of \(A\), but would give \(-|A|<0\) on the right.  This contradiction proves that every vertex of \(C_k\) lies in the root component.  Hence \(G[C_k]\) is connected, and the projection lies in \(\mathfrak C_K(G)\) by \(\mathrm{def:connected-partitions}\).

Conversely, fix \(\mathcal C\in\mathfrak C_K(G)\).  Set \(x_{vk}=\mathbf 1\{v\in C_k\}\), set \(s_k=|C_k|\), choose any root \(r_k\in C_k\), and set \(r_{r_k k}=1\), all other \(r_{vk}=0\), \(h_{r_k k}=s_k\), and all other \(h_{vk}=0\).  Because \(G[C_k]\) is finite and connected, choose a spanning tree of it and orient that tree away from \(r_k\).  On an oriented tree arc from a parent to a child, put
\[
 f^k_{uv}=\bigl|\text{the descendant subtree rooted at }v\bigr|,
\]
and put zero on every other oriented edge.  Every such flow is between \(1\) and \(s_k\le R\), so the endpoint capacities hold.  At the root, the outgoing descendant-subtree sizes sum to \(s_k-1\).  At a nonroot vertex, the incoming flow equals the size of its subtree and the outgoing flows sum to that size minus one.  Thus all balance equations and all root constraints hold.

It remains to verify the logical auxiliaries and the objective.  For each \(i,k\), set
\[
 u_{ik}=\mathbf 1\{C_k\cap N_G[i]\ne\varnothing\}.
\]
The lower inequalities and the upper inequality in \(\mathrm{def:exact-milp}\) are then satisfied.  They also show that this is the only possible binary value of \(u_{ik}\) in any extension of the same \(x\): a neighborhood occurrence forces \(u_{ik}=1\), and its absence forces \(u_{ik}=0\).  The three overlap inequalities similarly force
\[
 b_{ii'k}=u_{ik}u_{i'k}
 =\mathbf 1\{k\in L_i(\mathcal C)\cap L_{i'}(\mathcal C)\}.
\]
Therefore
\[
 \sum_{k=1}^K b_{ii'k}=|L_i(\mathcal C)\cap L_{i'}(\mathcal C)|
 =m_{ii'}(\mathcal C).
\]
Set \(z_{ii'q}=\mathbf 1\{q=m_{ii'}(\mathcal C)\}\).  This is feasible.  Conversely, binary one-hot \(z\) together with \(\sum_qqz_{ii'q}=\sum_kb_{ii'k}\) forces exactly this choice.  Hence every auxiliary extension of \(\mathcal C\) has objective
\[
 \frac1{N_{\exp}}\sum_{i,i'\in U}\sum_{q=1}^K
 2^{q+1}\Sigma_{ii'}z_{ii'q}
 =\frac1{N_{\exp}}\sum_{i,i'\in U}
 2^{m_{ii'}(\mathcal C)+1}\Sigma_{ii'}
 \mathbf 1\{m_{ii'}(\mathcal C)>0\}
 =V(\mathcal C;\Sigma),
\]
where the last equality is \(\mathrm{def:exact-risk}\).  The choice of a root and of a feasible flow need not be unique, but neither appears in this equality.

By \(\mathrm{lem:feasible-nonempty}\), the finite set \(\mathfrak C_K(G)\) is nonempty.  Projection maps every feasible mixed-integer point into this set, and the extension construction maps every member of this set to at least one feasible point with the same criterion value.  Thus the two sets of attainable objective values coincide.  Their minima coincide, and the projection of any mixed-integer optimizer is an element of \(\arg\min_{\mathcal C\in\mathfrak C_K(G)}V(\mathcal C;\Sigma)\), as claimed.

%%% FIELD fpt-dp-proof
Root \(\mathcal T\) as supplied.  For a node \(t\), let \(\mathcal T_{\downarrow t}\) be its descendant subtree, and define
\[
 X_t=\bigcup_{s\in V(\mathcal T_{\downarrow t})}B_s,
 \qquad
 E_t^G=\{e\in E:\text{the unique introduce-edge node of }e
                    \text{ lies in }\mathcal T_{\downarrow t}\}.
\]
The uniqueness in the second definition is \(\mathrm{ass:unique-edge-introduction}\).  A partial history at \(t\) is a labeling \(\ell_t:X_t\to\{1,\ldots,K\}\).  Its processed graph for label \(k\) has vertex set \(\ell_t^{-1}(k)\) and precisely the edges in \(E_t^G\) whose endpoints both have label \(k\).

We give an exact semantic invariant for \(q=(\lambda,\pi,c)\).  A partial history realizes \(q\) when: (i) \(\ell_t|_{B_t}=\lambda\); (ii) two bag vertices are in the same block of \(\pi\) exactly when they lie in the same processed component, so \(\pi\) refines the fibers of \(\lambda\); and (iii), for each label \(k\), exactly one of the following holds:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
 k\in\lambda(B_t): & c_k=0\text{ and every processed }k\text{-component meets }B_t;\\
 k\notin\lambda(B_t),\ c_k=0: & \ell_t^{-1}(k)=\varnothing;\\
 k\notin\lambda(B_t),\ c_k=1: & \ell_t^{-1}(k)\ne\varnothing
   \text{ and it is one processed component.}
\end{array}
\]
This is the completed-label invariant of \(\mathrm{def:dp-state}\), with the consequences for absent labels written explicitly.

For a bag labeling \(\lambda\), define the rational local charge
\[
 \gamma_t(\lambda)=\frac1{N_{\exp}}
 \sum_{(i,i')\in H_t}
 2^{|\lambda(N_G[i])\cap\lambda(N_G[i'])|+1}\Sigma_{ii'}
 \mathbf 1\{|\lambda(N_G[i])\cap\lambda(N_G[i'])|>0\}.
\]
This is well defined because \(\mathrm{lem:scope-charging}\) gives
\(N_G[i]\cup N_G[i']\subseteq B_t\) for every \((i,i')\in H_t\).  We prove by induction from the leaves that
\[
 D_t(q)=\min_{\ell_t\text{ realizes }q}
       \sum_{s\in V(\mathcal T_{\downarrow t})}\gamma_s(\ell_t|_{B_s}),
 \tag{1}
\]
with the minimum of the empty set equal to \(+\infty\).

At an empty leaf, the empty history is the unique realizing history and has cost zero.  This is exactly the initialization in \(\mathrm{def:dp-transitions}\).

At an introduce-vertex node \(t\) with child \(s\) and introduced vertex \(v\), running intersection implies that \(v\notin X_s\): otherwise the bags containing \(v\) would have a path from a descendant bag to \(t\) passing through \(s\), contrary to \(v\notin B_s\).  No processed edge below \(t\) is incident to \(v\).  Thus choosing a label \(k\) creates exactly one singleton component and is legal exactly when \(c_k=0\); if \(c_k=1\), it would restart a closed label.  The state update therefore gives a bijection between realizing histories and the legal predecessors prescribed by the recurrence.

At an introduce-edge node, the vertex set and labeling do not change.  If the edge lies in \(E\) and its endpoint labels agree, it joins exactly the two endpoint components, which is represented by merging their blocks.  If the labels differ, it joins no within-label components.  An edge in \(E_F\setminus E\) has no role in \(G\)-connectivity and leaves \(\pi\) unchanged.  These are exactly the stated transitions.

At a forget-vertex node, the processed graph is unchanged and only its intersection with the bag loses \(v\).  If the block containing \(v\) retains another bag vertex, deleting \(v\) from that block preserves the invariant.  If the whole block disappears while another block of the same label remains, a processed component no longer meets the bag while that label is unfinished, so the history must be rejected.  If no other block of that label remains, the disappearing block is the only component of that label by the child invariant; it becomes the unique closed component and the update \(c_k=1\) is necessary and sufficient.  In particular this update prevents any later introduce-vertex node from restarting label \(k\).

Now let \(t\) be a join with children \(s_1,s_2\).  Running intersection gives
\[
 X_{s_1}\cap X_{s_2}=B_t.
\tag{2}
\]
Indeed, a vertex occurring in both descendant subtrees has a bag path through \(t\), so it occurs in \(B_t\); the reverse inclusion follows from the equal join bags.  The processed edge sets of the children are disjoint by unique edge introduction.  Moreover no edge has one endpoint in \(X_{s_1}\setminus B_t\) and the other in \(X_{s_2}\setminus B_t\), since a bag covering such an edge would contradict (2) and running intersection.  Child histories therefore combine exactly when their bag labelings agree.  For a label active in the bag, its components in the union meet the bag, and their bag connectivity is the transitive closure of the two child equivalence relations; both child flags must be zero.  For an absent label, a child flag zero means that the child contains no vertex of that label, whereas a child flag one means that it contains one closed component.  If both flags are one, the two closed components are disjoint and cannot be joined across the separator, so the pair is invalid.  Otherwise their logical disjunction records exactly the unique closed component, if any.  This proves both necessity and sufficiency of the join rule, including its absent-completed-label clause.

In every node case, descendant costs are inherited from one child or added across the two disjoint child subtrees, and then \(\gamma_t\) is added once.  Taking the minimum over the exact predecessor bijections proves (1).

At the empty root \(r\), vertex cover gives \(X_r=U\), and unique edge introduction gives \(E_r^G=E\).  The accepted state \((\varnothing,\varnothing,\mathbf 1_K)\) therefore says that every label is nonempty and induces exactly one connected component of \(G\).  Its histories are precisely the label maps of \(\mathfrak C_K(G)\).  Conversely, fix \(\mathcal C\in\mathfrak C_K(G)\) and restrict its label map to each \(X_t\).  If a processed component of label \(k\) fails to meet \(B_t\), the tree-decomposition separator property used in (2) prevents it from connecting to a same-label vertex outside \(X_t\).  Global connectedness of \(G[C_k]\) then implies that this component is all of \(C_k\); it is unique and is correctly marked completed.  Thus the restrictions satisfy every transition and reach the accepted root.  This proves soundness and completeness.

By \(\mathrm{lem:scope-charging}\), the sets \(H_t\) partition the supported ordered pairs.  Hence, for every complete history \(\ell_{\mathcal C}\),
\[
 \sum_{t\in V(\mathcal T)}\gamma_t(\ell_{\mathcal C}|_{B_t})
 =V(\mathcal C;\Sigma).
\]
Equation (1) at the root consequently gives
\[
 D_r(\varnothing,\varnothing,\mathbf 1_K)
 =\min_{\mathcal C\in\mathfrak C_K(G)}V(\mathcal C;\Sigma).
\]
The minimum is finite and attained by \(\mathrm{lem:feasible-nonempty}\).

Every bag has size at most \(w+1\).  There are at most \(K^{w+1}\) bag labelings, at most \(\operatorname{Bell}(w+1)\) partitions of a bag, and at most \(2^K\) flag vectors.  Discarding the incompatible triples can only decrease their product, so there are at most
\[
 S_{w,K}=K^{w+1}\operatorname{Bell}(w+1)2^K
\]
states per bag.  Leaf, introduce, and forget transitions can be enumerated in \(S_{w,K}\operatorname{poly}(R,K,w)\) operations per node, and a conservative join implementation tests all \(S_{w,K}^2\) child-state pairs.  The supported ordered pairs and their highest bags can be preprocessed in \(R^2S_{w,K}^2\operatorname{poly}(R,K,w)\) operations, which is a deliberately loose upper bound.  Every local value is a sum of rational inputs multiplied by an integer of at most \(K+2\) bits and divided by \(N_{\exp}\).  There are at most \(R^2\) summands, so exact additions, comparisons, and stored values have bit length polynomial in \(R,K,L_{\mathrm{bits}}\).  The asserted bit-operation time and space bounds follow.

For each finite table entry, choose a minimizing legal predecessor, breaking ties deterministically, and record it in \(P_t\).  Starting at the accepted root and following these pointers reaches leaf entries.  At every introduce-vertex occurrence record its label; equality of the bag labelings at joins and running intersection make repeated occurrences consistent.  The resulting map labels all of \(U\), and the soundness induction proves that its fibers form an optimizer \(\mathcal C^\star\).  A verifier of \(\operatorname{Cert}(G,K,\Sigma,\mathcal T)\) recomputes each displayed local rational charge, enumerates the legal predecessor or predecessor-pair candidates from the displayed finite child entries, checks that the displayed value is their minimum and that the pointer attains it, and finally checks the root state and reconstructed labels.  Any unlisted finite entry would be generated by one of those finite predecessor candidates, so this same pass also checks completeness of the displayed finite table.  The work is quadratic at joins and polynomial elsewhere in the displayed table size and rational bit length.

For the stipulated kill test on the path \(a-c-b\), at the join bag \(\{c\}\) with \(\lambda(c)=2\), label \(1\) is absent.  If the left child has closed \(\{a\}\) with label \(1\) and the right child has closed \(\{b\}\) with label \(1\), then both child flags equal one, so the join rule rejects; indeed those two components cannot cross the separator \(\{c\}\).  In the separator-active analogue, label \(1\) occurs on \(c\), both flags are zero, and transitive closure merges the child connectivity through \(c\), so the local join is accepted.  This exactly matches the semantic invariant rather than merely testing a syntactic special case.

%%% FIELD rank-compression-statement
For every finite connected \(G\), every \(2\le K\le R\), every symmetric rational \(\Sigma\), every positive integer \(N_{\exp}\), and every supplied width-\(w\) nice tree decomposition \(\mathcal T\) of \(F\) satisfying the stated decomposition assumptions, the representative-set handle has an affirmative answer.  For each fixed bag labeling and completed-label vector, minimum-weight elimination over \(\mathbb F_2\) retains at most \(2^{|B_t|-|\lambda(B_t)|}\) label-refining connectivity partitions and preserves every legal completion.  Applied after the charge from \(H_t\), these reductions give a deterministic exact algorithm with bit-operation bound
\[
 O\!\left((|V(\mathcal T)|+R^2)
 K^{2(w+1)}2^{2K+4(w+1)}
 \operatorname{poly}(R,K,w,L_{\mathrm{bits}})\right)
 =2^{O(Kw)}\operatorname{poly}(|V(\mathcal T)|,R,L_{\mathrm{bits}}).
\]
The retained rational rows, elimination pivots, and predecessor pointers form a checkable compressed table/backpointer certificate and reconstruct an optimizer \(\mathcal C^\star\); completed-label join compatibility and the exact rational scope charges are preserved.
In particular, the requested \(2^{O(Kw)}\operatorname{poly}(R,L_{\mathrm{bits}})\) bound holds for a size-normalized supplied decomposition satisfying \(|V(\mathcal T)|\le\operatorname{poly}(R)\).  Without such an input-size condition, the running time of an algorithm that reads an explicitly supplied \(\mathcal T\) necessarily also depends on \(|V(\mathcal T)|\).

%%% FIELD rank-compression-proof
Fix a node \(t\), a bag labeling \(\lambda\), and a compatible flag vector \(c\).  Let \(A=\lambda(B_t)\) be the active-label set, let \(a=|A|\), and put \(X_k=\lambda^{-1}(k)\) for \(k\in A\).  Let \(\mathscr P_\lambda\) be the set of partitions of \(B_t\) refining these fibers.  For \(\pi,\rho\in\mathscr P_\lambda\), define
\[
 M_\lambda[\pi,\rho]
 =\mathbf 1\bigl\{(\pi\vee\rho)|_{X_k}
       \text{ has one block for every }k\in A\bigr\},
\tag{3}
\]
where \(\vee\) is transitive closure of the union of the two equivalence relations.  This is exactly the question whether the processed connectivity \(\pi\), together with a future connectivity pattern \(\rho\) attached through the bag, completes every currently active label.

We first prove the rank bound rather than assume it.  In each nonempty fiber \(X_k\), choose one distinguished vertex.  A rooted cut of \(X_k\) assigns every vertex to one of two sides and assigns the distinguished vertex to the first side; the second side is allowed to be empty.  Thus there are \(2^{|X_k|-1}\) rooted cuts.  Let \(\mathscr D_\lambda\) be the Cartesian product of these cut families, and over \(\mathbb F_2\) define
\[
 C_\lambda[\pi,\delta]
 =\mathbf 1\{\text{every block of }\pi\text{ lies on one side of every
 fiber cut in }\delta\}.
\]
If \(r_k\) is the number of blocks of \((\pi\vee\rho)|_{X_k}\), then the number of cut tuples coarsening both \(\pi\) and \(\rho\) equals
\[
 \prod_{k\in A}2^{r_k-1}.
\]
Its parity is one exactly when every \(r_k=1\).  Therefore
\[
 M_\lambda=C_\lambda C_\lambda^{\mathsf T}\quad\text{over }\mathbb F_2,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{rank}(M_\lambda)
 \le |\mathscr D_\lambda|
 =2^{\sum_{k\in A}(|X_k|-1)}
 =2^{|B_t|-a}.
\tag{4}
\]

Consider any finite weighted family \(\mathscr A\subseteq\mathscr P_\lambda\), where the weights are exact rationals.  Sort it by nondecreasing weight with deterministic tie-breaking, scan the row vectors \(C_\lambda[\pi,\cdot]\), and retain a partition precisely when its row is linearly independent of the previously retained rows.  Call the retained family \(\operatorname{red}(\mathscr A)\).  Its size is bounded by (4).  For every query \(\rho\), it preserves the minimum compatible weight:
\[
 \min_{\substack{\pi\in\mathscr A\\M_\lambda[\pi,\rho]=1}}
       \operatorname{wt}(\pi)
 =
 \min_{\substack{\pi\in\operatorname{red}(\mathscr A)\\
                  M_\lambda[\pi,\rho]=1}}
       \operatorname{wt}(\pi).
\tag{5}
\]
To see this, only the inequality from right to left needs proof.  If a compatible minimizer \(\pi\) was discarded, its row is the sum over \(\mathbb F_2\) of earlier retained rows, all of weight at most \(\operatorname{wt}(\pi)\).  Taking the scalar product with the row of \(\rho\) and using (3)--(4), the left side is one, so at least one summand has scalar product one.  That retained partition is compatible with \(\rho\) and has no larger weight.  This proves (5), including for negative rational weights.

It remains to prove that reduction may be interleaved with every recurrence operation.  Introduce-vertex extends a partition by a singleton.  For any completion query on the enlarged bag, deleting the introduced vertex from its query block gives a query on the child bag, and the two compatibility tests agree.  Introduce-edge replaces \(\pi\) by \(\pi\vee\{\{u,v\}\}\) when the endpoint labels agree; compatibility with \(\rho\) after this operation is the same as compatibility of \(\pi\) with \(\rho\vee\{\{u,v\}\}\).  At a nonclosing forget operation, the block of the forgotten vertex contains a surviving bag vertex.  If \(\rho\) is a query on the smaller bag, adjoining the forgotten vertex as a singleton gives a child query; compatibility forces any representative selected for that query also to have the forgotten vertex connected to a surviving vertex, and projection commutes with the compatibility test.  When forgetting closes the last active block of a label, no partition information for that now-absent label remains, so retaining the minimum-weight candidate for the new flag vector is exact.

For a join, let \(\mathscr A_1,\mathscr A_2\) be child families for one legal pair of completed-label vectors.  The candidate partitions are \(\pi_1\vee\pi_2\) with weight \(w_1(\pi_1)+w_2(\pi_2)\).  If \((\pi_1,\pi_2)\) is optimal for a future query \(\rho\), representation of \(\mathscr A_1\) applied to the query \(\pi_2\vee\rho\) supplies \(\pi'_1\) of no greater first weight.  Representation of \(\mathscr A_2\) applied next to \(\pi'_1\vee\rho\) supplies \(\pi'_2\) of no greater second weight.  Then \(\pi'_1\vee\pi'_2\vee\rho\) connects exactly the required fibers.  Thus pairwise join preserves (5).  The flag vectors are handled outside the matrix: active labels have zero flags in both children, and an absent label permits the pairs \((0,0),(1,0),(0,1)\) but not \((1,1)\).  Enumerating precisely these pairs preserves the completed-label rule proved in \(\mathrm{thm:fpt-dp}\); no rank operation can restart or duplicate a closed label.

Finally, \(\gamma_t(\lambda)\) depends on \(\lambda\) but not on \(\pi\), by \(\mathrm{lem:scope-charging}\).  Adding this exact rational constant before reduction therefore preserves (5).  Induction over the nice decomposition now shows that every compressed family represents the corresponding full family in \(\mathrm{def:dp-transitions}\).  At the empty accepted root there is no active fiber and a single completion query, so (5), together with \(\mathrm{thm:fpt-dp}\), proves that the compressed root value is exactly \(\min_{\mathcal C\in\mathfrak C_K(G)}V(\mathcal C;\Sigma)\).

For a bag of size \(b\le w+1\), summing the rank bound crudely over at most \(K^b\) labelings and \(2^K\) flag vectors gives at most
\[
 M_b=K^b2^K2^b
\]
retained weighted partitions.  At a join, at most \(M_b^2\) pairs are generated.  Explicit cut rows have length at most \(2^b\); even charging a quadratic \(2^{2b}\) field-operation overhead for elimination of every generated row yields the conservative factor
\[
 K^{2(w+1)}2^{2K+4(w+1)}.
\]
All field operations for elimination are over \(\mathbb F_2\), while weight additions and comparisons use the exact rationals whose polynomial bit-length bound was proved in \(\mathrm{thm:fpt-dp}\).  Since \(R\ge K\ge2\), the connected graph \(G\subseteq F\) contains an edge; hence \(w\ge1\).  The elementary inequalities \(\log_2K\le K\) and \(w+1\le2w\) then turn the displayed parameter factor into \(2^{O(Kw)}\).  This proves the stated time bound, including its unavoidable linear dependence on the explicit decomposition input.  If \(|V(\mathcal T)|\le\operatorname{poly}(R)\), that term is absorbed into \(\operatorname{poly}(R,L_{\mathrm{bits}})\); absent this size normalization, merely reading the supplied tree rules out a bound independent of \(|V(\mathcal T)|\).

Each retained row stores the generating predecessor or predecessor pair, its rational value, and the elimination pivot that caused its retention.  Backtracking from the retained root row reconstructs the same consistent vertex labels as in \(\mathrm{thm:fpt-dp}\).  A verifier recomputes local charges, legal candidates, cut rows, deterministic Gaussian-elimination pivots, and predecessor equalities; all checks fit within the same bound.  Thus pruning discards only histories proved redundant by (5), while retaining an auditable rational table and a reconstructing backpointer chain.
