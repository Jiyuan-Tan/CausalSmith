Let \(t\) be the join node of \(\mathcal T_{\mathrm{kill}}\), let its children be \(s_a,s_b\), and write their states as \(q_j=(\lambda_j,\pi_j,c^{(j)})\), \(j\in\{a,b\}\). By the join form in \(\mathrm{ass:nice-node-forms}\), \(B_t=B_{s_a}=B_{s_b}=\{c\}\); the two child label maps must agree with the parent label map by \(\mathrm{def:dp-transitions}\). First suppose \(\lambda(c)=2\) and each child has completed label \(1\). Since label \(1\) is absent from the common bag, completion has the precise state representation \(c^{(a)}_1=c^{(b)}_1=1\) by \(\mathrm{def:dp-state}\). The absent-label clause of the join rule in \(\mathrm{def:dp-transitions}\) rejects a predecessor pair whenever the same absent label has completion flag one in both children. Applying that clause with \(k=1\) therefore rejects this pair. This rejection is also forced by the invariant: the processed label-\(1\) component containing \(a\) is closed in the left branch and the processed label-\(1\) component containing \(b\) is closed in the right branch. In \(G_{\mathrm{kill}}\), the only path from \(a\) to \(b\) is \(a-c-b\), while \(c\) has label \(2\); hence no future same-label edge merge can join those two closed label-\(1\) components. Accepting the pair would thus represent a disconnected label class, contrary to the unique-component invariant in \(\mathrm{def:dp-state}\). The value \(\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(1,0,1)\) can affect only rational local charges and not this legality decision, because the join compatibility clauses of \(\mathrm{def:dp-transitions}\) depend only on \(\lambda,\pi,c\).

For the separator-active analogue, take \(\lambda_a(c)=\lambda_b(c)=1\). Label \(1\) is active in the common bag, so the join rule requires \(c^{(a)}_1=c^{(b)}_1=0\); these are precisely the admissible unfinished-label flags under \(\mathrm{def:dp-state}\). In each child, the active label-\(1\) component meets the bag in the block \(\{c\}\): in the underlying processed subgraph the left component may contain the edge \(\{a,c\}\), and the right component may contain \(\{c,b\}\). By \(\mathrm{def:dp-transitions}\), the parent partition is the transitive closure of the union of the two child equivalence relations. The two represented components share the bag vertex \(c\), so this closure puts their union in one component; concretely, \(a\) is joined to \(c\) and \(c\) to \(b\), whence the union is connected in \(G_{\mathrm{kill}}\). No active-label flag condition fails. For label \(2\), if it is absent and unfinished in both local states, the disjunction rule gives parent flag zero and the forbidden case \(c^{(a)}_2=c^{(b)}_2=1\) does not occur. Thus the predecessor pair is locally accepted and its label-\(1\) components are merged by transitive closure. This is a statement about legality at the join; final nonemptiness of both labels is checked separately by the empty-root acceptance condition. The two asserted outcomes follow.
