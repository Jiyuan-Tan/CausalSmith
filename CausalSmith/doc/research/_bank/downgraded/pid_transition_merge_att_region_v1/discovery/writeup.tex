\section*{pid\_\allowbreak{}transition\_\allowbreak{}merge\_\allowbreak{}att\_\allowbreak{}region}

\noindent\textit{Specialization:} v1\par

\paragraph{TL;DR.} On the compact binary four-period latent-transition model, the singular case with distinct treated terminal rows and exactly one collided control terminal row admits an exact observable treatment. Terminal splitting reduces the forty-coordinate existential fiber to displayed ten-variable systems and exactly thirty-two collided-state, orientation, and relative-alignment branches. A Collins-style sign certificate is necessary and sufficient for branch feasibility and aggregate-ATT point identification; the associated exact algorithm returns the attained, possibly disconnected image with Thom-encoded endpoints, latent witnesses, and discarded-branch certificates. An explicit injective one-parameter family of one-collision laws has exactly two separated quotient ATT values throughout, with a dense rational subfamily. This proves positive-dimensional persistence, not relative openness in the full collision variety. The contribution is the structural reduction and causal certificate grammar, not a generic CAD complexity improvement, a completion of the other collision patterns, or an overlap theorem for Ahn--Kasahara's regular class.

\section{Project justification}

\paragraph{Gap.} Ahn--Kasahara's parameter and ATT identification results impose their stated RSM, M, ID, and TI--LT regular conditions, while Higgins and Jochmans's four-observation theorem requires rank, type-separation, and connectivity conditions; neither classifies the one-collided-control-row merger stratum or supplies an explicit positive-dimensional family with two aggregate-ATT values. Geiger--Meek and Collins supply general real quantifier elimination but leave the economically meaningful two-arm branch structure opaque.

\paragraph{Niche.} Exactly one collided control terminal row is the first nonregular stratum on which full-path splitting, rank failure, probability-box contact, and relative treatment-control alignment interact but remain reducible to ten variables. Within it, an explicitly parameterized observed-law path permits a direct persistence proof without a generic or relative-open claim.

\paragraph{Fill.} The project proves the reduction, gives the observable \(\mathscr L_{k_0,\sigma}\) classification, computes the exact attained ATT image on all thirty-two labeled branches, and supplies an injective one-parameter one-collision family whose two quotient ATT values remain separated, including a dense rational subfamily.

\section{Related work}

Ahn--Kasahara is the closest econometric comparator in the same full-path latent-transition architecture: their parameter and ATT results operate under their stated regular conditions. Those results motivate the estimand and architecture here; the compact class \(\mathcal M_\eta\) and its singular one-collision stratum are not asserted to equal, contain, or extend the Ahn--Kasahara identification class, and only the architecture and ATT formula transfer as motivation. Higgins and Jochmans give a four-observation Markov-mixture identification benchmark under rank, separation, and connectivity conditions, but do not classify a two-arm alignment fiber with one collided control terminal row. Critch's binary hidden Markov geometry motivates rational splitting and semialgebraic membership, but has neither two treatment arms nor a causal alignment functional. Geiger--Meek and Collins provide generic real quantifier elimination and exact CAD certification; they do not provide the model-specific ten-coordinate reduction, branch labels, ATT image, or endpoint representations. Basu supplies the relevant worst-case real-algebraic complexity benchmark. Duarte, Finkelstein, Knox, Mummolo, and Shpitser motivate polynomial optimization for discrete causal bounds, while the present object is an exact attained semialgebraic image. Henry, Kitamura, and Salanie study a different exclusion-driven finite-mixture set. Romano--Shaikh motivates set inversion; the finite-sample coverage theorem here is auxiliary and appendix-tier and asserts neither endpoint nor Hausdorff consistency. The persistence evidence is exactly the explicit injective one-parameter family proved here, not a relatively open chamber in a larger collision variety.

\section{Assumptions}

\begin{assumption}[ass:eta-domain]\label{ass:eta-domain}
\(\eta\in\mathbb Q\cap(0,3/20)\)
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Novel.} Any data-generating process with nondegenerate latent shares, assignment probabilities, and transition rows satisfies this compact-interior restriction for some smaller rational \(\eta\). Rationality permits exact input encoding, while the box makes every stratum-restricted image attained. This condition is maintained independently and is not attributed to Ahn--Kasahara's common-support condition.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:path-factorization]\label{ass:path-factorization}
\(P=F(\psi)\)
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (Two-type first-order latent Markov path factorization with treatment-arm transition independence, \cite{AhnKasahara2026}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:compact-box]\label{ass:compact-box}
\(\psi\in\Theta_\eta\)
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Novel.} The common box is credible in applications without nearly deterministic type shares, selection, or transitions. It is stronger than bounding treatment assignment alone and is maintained transparently to define a compact sensitivity model, not to claim coverage of the full published class.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:one-collision-pattern]\label{ass:one-collision-pattern}
\(\varnothing\ne\widetilde\Phi_\eta(P)\subseteq\Theta_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}\)
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Novel.} The exclusive stratum contains observed laws for which every compatible compact-box representation has the same single control-row collision while the other three terminal rows remain distinct. Such pooling is natural when latent groups share one next-state probability but differ elsewhere. Exclusivity makes the named stratum a complete causal model rather than a selected representation and, by compactness of the global fiber, keeps its noncollided gaps bounded away from zero at each law.
\end{assumption}

\begin{assumption}[ass:iid-sampling]\label{ass:iid-sampling}
\(W_1,\ldots,W_n\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}P\)
\par\smallskip\noindent\emph{Standard} (Independent multinomial sampling, \cite{RomanoShaikh2010}).
\end{assumption}

\begin{lemma}[lem:quotient-att]\label{lem:quotient-att}
For every \(P\in\mathcal M_\eta\), \(\psi\in\widetilde\Phi_\eta(P)\), and \(\sigma\in\mathfrak S_2\), one has \(F(\sigma\psi)=P\) and \(\tau(\sigma\psi;P)=\tau(\psi;P)\). The map \(\bar\tau_P([\psi])=\tau(\psi;P)\) is therefore well defined on \(\Phi_\eta(P)\) and on every \(\Phi_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Fix \(P\in\mathcal M_\eta\), \(\psi\in\widetilde\Phi_\eta(P)\), and \(\sigma\in\mathfrak S_2\).  The box restriction gives \(B(e_z;d)\geq\eta\), so
\[
 p_d=\sum_{z=1}^2\pi_zB(e_z;d)\geq\eta>0.
\]
Thus every posterior weight used below is defined.  The interpretation of the transition parameters and iterated expectation give
\[
 \mathbb E_\psi[X_2(0)\mid Z=z,D=1]
 =(1-u_{z1})a_{z0}+u_{z1}a_{z1}=m_{z2},
\]
and, successively,
\[
 \mathbb E_\psi[X_3(0)\mid Z=z,D=1]
 =(1-m_{z2})b_{z30}+m_{z2}b_{z31}=m_{z3},
\]
\[
 \mathbb E_\psi[X_4(0)\mid Z=z,D=1]
 =(1-m_{z3})b_{z40}+m_{z3}b_{z41}=m_{z4}.
\]
Since \(w_{z1}=\mathbb P_\psi(Z=z\mid D=1)\), another application of iterated expectation yields
\[
 \mathbb E_\psi[X_t(0)\mid D=1]=\sum_{z=1}^2w_{z1}m_{zt},\qquad t\in\{3,4\}.
\]
Consistency gives \(O_t(P)=\mathbb E_\psi[X_t(1)\mid D=1]\).  Consequently the structurally defined causal target is related to the observed law by
\[
 \mathbb E_\psi[X_t(1)-X_t(0)\mid D=1]
 =O_t(P)-\sum_{z=1}^2w_{z1}m_{zt}=\tau_t(\psi;P).
\]
This establishes the causal interpretation rather than defining it by the observed law.

Under the simultaneous action, every type-indexed coordinate satisfies \(q_z(\sigma\psi)=q_{\sigma^{-1}(z)}(\psi)\).  Relabeling the finite sum in the definition of \(F\) therefore gives, cell by cell,
\[
 F(\sigma\psi)_{ijkld}
 =\sum_{z=1}^2\pi_{\sigma^{-1}(z)}B(e_{\sigma^{-1}(z)};d)
   B(u_{\sigma^{-1}(z),d};i)B(a_{\sigma^{-1}(z),i};j)
   B(\kappa_{\sigma^{-1}(z),d,3,j};k)B(\kappa_{\sigma^{-1}(z),d,4,k};l)
 =F(\psi)_{ijkld}=P_{ijkld}.
\]
The same relabeling shows
\[
 w_{z1}(\sigma\psi)=w_{\sigma^{-1}(z),1}(\psi),
 \qquad m_{zt}(\sigma\psi)=m_{\sigma^{-1}(z),t}(\psi).
\]
Hence
\[
 \tau_t(\sigma\psi;P)
 =O_t(P)-\sum_{z=1}^2w_{\sigma^{-1}(z),1}m_{\sigma^{-1}(z),t}
 =\tau_t(\psi;P).
\]
The box \(\Theta_\eta\) is itself invariant under this permutation.  Therefore \(\sigma\psi\in\widetilde\Phi_\eta(P)\), and \(\tau\) is constant on every orbit.  This proves that \(\bar\tau_P\) is well defined on \(\Phi_\eta(P)\), and restriction proves the same assertion on \(\Phi_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\).
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The causal image is meaningful only after simultaneous latent labels are removed without changing aggregate ATT. \textit{Gap.} Regular mixture identification is commonly stated up to labels, but the quotient-induced causal functional must still be checked before singular branch images are pooled. \textit{Consumer.} The public \(ak\) package may report endpoint witnesses with arbitrary latent labels without changing the reported ATT set.

\begin{theorem}[thm:dimension-reduction]\label{thm:dimension-reduction}
For every rational \(\eta\in(0,3/20)\), \(k_0\in\mathcal A\), and \(P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}\), terminal splitting induces a surjection from the union of the sixteen feasible \((s,\sigma)\) ten-variable solution sets \(\{y:\mathscr G_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,\eta;y)\}\) onto \(\widetilde\Phi_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\). Thus the forty-coordinate existential description in \((R,v)\in\mathbb R^{32}\times\mathbb R^8\) is exactly reduced to six terminal coordinates and four prefix splits. Across \(k_0\in\mathcal A\) there are exactly thirty-two labeled \((k_0,s,\sigma)\) branches. Rank failures and all \(\eta\)-box contacts occur as zero or weak-sign cells inside these systems and create no omitted branch.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix the displayed \(\eta,k_0,P\).  By \(P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}\), the global fiber is nonempty and every one of its points has the specified single collision.  For \(\psi\) in that fiber define the arm-conditional latent prefix mass
\[
 R_{zd}^{\psi}(i,j,k)
 =w_{zd}B(u_{zd};i)B(a_{zi};j)B(\kappa_{zd3j};k),
 \qquad v_{zdk}^{\psi}=\kappa_{zd4k}.
\]
Summing over \(z\), first without and then with the event \(X_4=1\), gives the two terminal-splitting identities
\[
 H_d(i,j,k)=R_{1d}^{\psi}(i,j,k)+R_{2d}^{\psi}(i,j,k),
 \qquad
 N_d(i,j,k)=v_{1dk}^{\psi}R_{1d}^{\psi}(i,j,k)+v_{2dk}^{\psi}R_{2d}^{\psi}(i,j,k).
 \tag{1}
\]
Every factor in \(F\) lies in \([\eta,U]\).  Moreover \(p_d\leq U\), so
\[
 w_{zd}=\frac{\pi_zB(e_z;d)}{p_d}\geq\frac{\eta^2}{U}>\eta^2,
 \qquad R_{zd}^{\psi}(i,j,k)>\eta^5.
 \tag{2}
\]
Thus all conditional masses in (1) are strictly positive.

At the collided block \((d,k)=(0,k_0)\), the definition of \(\Theta_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}\) gives \(v_{10k_0}^{\psi}=v_{20k_0}^{\psi}\).  Equation (1) then implies
\[
 N_0(i,j,k_0)=v_{10k_0}^{\psi}H_0(i,j,k_0),
 \]
so the three minors \(\chi_{0k_0}^{01},\chi_{0k_0}^{10},\chi_{0k_0}^{11}\) vanish and, since \(H_0(0,0,k_0)>0\),
\[
 v_{10k_0}^{\psi}=v_{20k_0}^{\psi}
 =\frac{N_0(0,0,k_0)}{H_0(0,0,k_0)}.
 \tag{3}
\]
The four numbers \(R_{10}^{\psi}(i,j,k_0)\) remain free splits.  In each of the other three blocks the terminal difference is nonzero.  Solving the two-by-two linear system (1) gives, without any rank inversion,
\[
 R_{1d}^{\psi}(i,j,k)
 =\frac{N_d(i,j,k)-v_{2dk}^{\psi}H_d(i,j,k)}{v_{1dk}^{\psi}-v_{2dk}^{\psi}},
 \qquad R_{2d}^{\psi}(i,j,k)=H_d(i,j,k)-R_{1d}^{\psi}(i,j,k).
 \tag{4}
\]
Choose \(s_{dk}=\operatorname{sgn}(v_{1dk}^{\psi}-v_{2dk}^{\psi})\) in those three blocks.  There are \(2^3\) possible choices of \(s\).

For each \(z,d,j\), the factorization of \(R^{\psi}\) gives
\[
 R_{zd}^{\psi}(0,j,0)R_{zd}^{\psi}(1,j,1)
 =R_{zd}^{\psi}(0,j,1)R_{zd}^{\psi}(1,j,0),
 \tag{5}
\]
which is precisely \(\rho_{zdj}(R^{\psi})=0\).  There are \(2\cdot2\cdot2=8\) equations in (5).  If the arm-local control labels are aligned with the treated labels by \(\sigma\), the shared transition \(a_{zi}\) says
\[
 \frac{T_{\sigma(z),0}^{R^{\psi}}(i,1)}{S_{\sigma(z),0}^{R^{\psi}}(i)}
 =\frac{T_{z1}^{R^{\psi}}(i,1)}{S_{z1}^{R^{\psi}}(i)}.
\]
The denominators are positive by (2), and cross multiplication gives the four equations
\[
 \gamma_{zi}^{\sigma}(R^{\psi})=0,
 \qquad z\in\{1,2\},\ i\in\mathcal A.
 \tag{6}
\]
All reconstructed primitives equal the coordinates of \(\psi\), so their weak box signs hold.  Equations (2)--(6) show that the six noncollided terminal probabilities and four collided-block splits form a solution \(y\) of one of the sixteen systems indexed by \((s,\sigma)\).  Reconstruction returns \(\psi\).  This proves surjectivity in one direction.

Conversely, take a solution \(y\) of \(\mathscr G_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,\eta;y)\), and form \(R,v\) by the displayed splitting formulas.  The imposed signs make every denominator in (4) nonzero, while the imposed lower bounds give \(R_{zd}(i,j,k)>0\).  Put
\[
 T_{zd}(i,j)=\sum_kR_{zd}(i,j,k),\quad
 S_{zd}(i)=\sum_jT_{zd}(i,j),\quad
 w_{zd}=\sum_iS_{zd}(i).
\]
These quantities are positive, and (1), which holds by construction, implies \(\sum_z w_{zd}=1\).  For fixed \(z,d,j\), positivity and \(\rho_{zdj}=0\) imply
\[
 \frac{R_{zd}(0,j,1)}{R_{zd}(0,j,0)}
 =\frac{R_{zd}(1,j,1)}{R_{zd}(1,j,0)}.
\]
After normalizing both sides, the common Bernoulli probability is
\[
 \kappa_{zd3j}=\frac{R_{zd}(0,j,1)}{T_{zd}(0,j)},
 \]
and hence
\[
 R_{zd}(i,j,k)=T_{zd}(i,j)B(\kappa_{zd3j};k).
 \tag{7}
\]
The definitions of \(u_{zd}\) and the arm-local pre-transition give
\[
 T_{zd}(i,j)=w_{zd}B(u_{zd};i)B(a_{zdi};j),
 \qquad a_{zdi}=\frac{T_{zd}(i,1)}{S_{zd}(i)}.
 \tag{8}
\]
Equation (6), again using positive \(S\), says
\[
 a_{\sigma(z),0,i}=a_{z1i}.
 \tag{9}
\]
Declare structural type \(z\) to be treated arm-local component \(z\) and control arm-local component \(\sigma(z)\), and denote the common value in (9) by \(a_{zi}\).  Set
\[
 \pi_z=p_0w_{\sigma(z),0}+p_1w_{z1},
 \qquad e_z=\frac{p_1w_{z1}}{\pi_z}.
 \tag{10}
\]
Because \(p_0,p_1>0\), (10) has a positive denominator and gives
\[
 \pi_ze_z=p_1w_{z1},\qquad
 \pi_z(1-e_z)=p_0w_{\sigma(z),0},\qquad
 \sum_z\pi_z=p_0+p_1=1.
 \tag{11}
\]
All coordinates in (7)--(10), including \(v\), satisfy the imposed weak box signs, so the reconstructed vector lies in \(\Theta_\eta\).  Combining (7), (8), and (11) recovers the prefix factors in \(F\).  The two identities (1) recover respectively every \(X_4=1\) cell and, by subtraction, every \(X_4=0\) cell.  Therefore
\[
 F(\operatorname{rec}_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,y))=P.
\]
The strict orientation signs and (3) give exactly the required one-collision pattern, so reconstruction lies in \(\widetilde\Phi_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\).  This proves the converse and hence exactness of the reduction.

There are six free terminal coordinates because the three noncollided blocks contribute two each; the collided terminal value is fixed by (3).  There are four free prefix splits, and every other one of the original thirty-two \(R\)-coordinates is fixed by (1).  Thus the forty coordinates \((R,v)\) reduce to ten.  For fixed \(k_0\), the inventory has \(2^3\) orientations and two relative alignments, hence sixteen branches; multiplying by the two choices of \(k_0\) gives thirty-two.  No spectral, moment, or coefficient matrix is inverted anywhere.  A rank failure is retained by the polynomial equalities, a vanishing projection coefficient is retained as a zero cell, and every box contact is retained by a weak inequality.  The only divided quantities are the explicitly positive masses or the three orientation-signed nonzero terminal differences.  Therefore none of these cases creates an omitted branch.
\end{proof}

 \exists y\;\mathscr G_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,\eta;y)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \text{the signed lifting table accepts at least one cell above }(P,\eta).
 \tag{12}
\]
Necessity follows because any solution lies in one lifted cell and realizes its recorded signs. \begin{theorem}[thm:exact-one-collision-region]\label{thm:exact-one-collision-region}
For rational \(\eta\) and rational \(P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,0}^{1\mathrm c}\cup\mathcal M_{\eta,1}^{1\mathrm c}\), \(\mathsf{OneCollisionATT}(P,\eta)\) terminates after examining exactly thirty-two labeled \((k_0,s,\sigma)\) branches and returns, for each \(k_0\), exactly the attained joint image \(\mathcal I_{k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\) as a quantifier-free semialgebraic formula. It returns each nonempty \(\mathcal I_{k_0,t}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\) as a finite ordered union of closed intervals and isolated points with Thom-encoded real-algebraic endpoints, a reconstructed feasible latent representation for every endpoint and isolated component, and an exact infeasibility certificate for every discarded branch. The theorem claims equivalence to Collins real quantifier elimination after the proved ten-coordinate reduction and claims no improvement in generic worst-case CAD complexity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Assume that \(\eta\) and the cells of \(P\) are rational and that \(P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,0}^{1\mathrm c}\cup\mathcal M_{\eta,1}^{1\mathrm c}\).  After the positive denominators \(p_d\) are cleared, every branch is a finite sign system with rational, hence integer after common rescaling, polynomial coefficients.  The algorithm enumerates
\[
 2\cdot 2^3\cdot 2=32
\]
labels \((k_0,s,\sigma)\).  The finiteness and termination assertion of the cited cylindrical-algebraic-decomposition lemma, applied to each of these fixed finite systems, proves that this loop terminates.  The image equality proved in the observable-classification theorem shows that the disjunction of the accepted branch projections is exactly \(\mathcal I_{k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\), not an outer approximation.  Introducing free coordinates \((q_3,q_4)\) and adjoining
\[
 q_t=\tau_t(\operatorname{rec}_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,y);P),\qquad t=3,4,
\]
then clearing positive denominators and eliminating \(y\), gives the claimed quantifier-free integer-polynomial sign formula for the joint image.

It remains to justify attainment and the stated one-dimensional format.  The set \(\Theta_\eta\) is compact, \(F\) is continuous, and therefore
\[
 K(P)=\{\psi\in\Theta_\eta:F(\psi)=P\}
\]
is compact.  The exclusive-stratum assumption says \(K(P)\subseteq\Theta_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}\), so \(K(P)=\widetilde\Phi_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\).  Also \(p_1\geq\eta\), and every denominator in \(w_{z1}\) and in the ATT recurrence is positive.  Hence \(\psi\mapsto\tau(\psi;P)\) is continuous on \(K(P)\).  It follows that the joint image and both coordinate projections are compact and every endpoint is attained.

Quantifier elimination makes each marginal a semialgebraic subset of \(\mathbb R\).  To see its exact form directly, collect the finitely many nonzero univariate polynomials in its quantifier-free formula.  Their real roots partition \(\mathbb R\) into finitely many points and open intervals on each of which every polynomial has constant sign.  A compact union of selected pieces is therefore a finite ordered union of closed intervals and isolated points.  Because the input coefficients are rational, every boundary root is real algebraic.  Square-free factorization, rational root isolation, derivative-sign evaluation, and the signed lifting table give its square-free polynomial, rational isolating interval, and Thom sign sequence.

If \(q_t\) is such an endpoint, compactness gives \(\psi\in K(P)\) with \(\tau_t(\psi;P)=q_t\).  The dimension-reduction theorem puts this \(\psi\) in an accepted labeled branch, and exact lifting supplies a real-algebraic \(y\) and hence the reconstructed real-algebraic endpoint witness.  Conversely, an alleged discarded branch would be feasible only if its existential sign formula were true, contradicting its exact CAD rejection; the first failed sign or projection polynomial is therefore an exact infeasibility certificate.  These arguments prove equivalence to real quantifier elimination after the ten-coordinate reduction.  No step changes the generic CAD projection schedule or its worst-case complexity, so no complexity improvement is claimed.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} The theorem turns a singular point-identification failure into an exact attained causal region without convexifying disconnected components. \textit{Gap.} Duarte and coauthors provide successively refined polynomial-program outer bounds and \(\epsilon\)-sharpness, while Collins CAD provides general exact elimination; neither result supplies this reduced transition-alignment system, its fixed branch inventory, or causal endpoint witnesses. \textit{Consumer.} The \(ak\) package receives a terminating exact-arithmetic backend for the completed singular stratum and can display the latent representation attaining each reported endpoint.

\begin{theorem}[thm:finite-sample-coverage]\label{thm:finite-sample-coverage}
Under independent multinomial sampling, for every \(n\ge1\), \(\inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}}P^n\{\mathcal I_{k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\subseteq\mathcal C_{n,k_0}^{\tau}\}\ge1-\alpha\). The event covers both marginal identified sets simultaneously. No endpoint-consistency, Hausdorff-consistency, or branch-persistence conclusion is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a fixed cell \(w\), put \(Y_r=\mathbf 1\{W_r=w\}\) and \(q=P(w)\).  Then the \(Y_r\)'s are independent Bernoulli variables with mean \(q\).  For every real \(\lambda\), elementary one-variable calculus gives the Bernoulli exponential bound
\[
 \mathbb E\exp\{\lambda(Y_r-q)\}
 =(1-q)e^{-\lambda q}+qe^{\lambda(1-q)}
 \leq e^{\lambda^2/8}.
 \tag{27}
\]
For completeness, let \(K(\lambda)\) be the logarithm of the middle expression.  Exponential tilting preserves the Bernoulli form, with some tilted success probability \(q_\lambda\), and therefore
\[
 K(0)=K'(0)=0,
 \qquad K''(\lambda)=q_\lambda(1-q_\lambda)\leq\tfrac14.
\]
Taylor's formula with integral remainder gives \(K(\lambda)\leq\lambda^2/8\), proving (27) without an additional probabilistic premise.  Markov's inequality and independence now imply, for \(\lambda>0\),
\[
 P^n\{\widehat P_n(w)-P(w)\geq\delta\}
 \leq\exp\{-\lambda n\delta+n\lambda^2/8\}.
\]
Choosing \(\lambda=4\delta\), and applying the same calculation to \(-Y_r\), yields
\[
 P^n\{|\widehat P_n(w)-P(w)|>\delta\}\leq2e^{-2n\delta^2}.
 \tag{28}
\]
There are thirty-two cells.  With \(\delta=\delta_n(\alpha)\), (28) and the union bound give, uniformly in \(P\),
\[
 P^n\{\|\widehat P_n-P\|_\infty>\delta_n(\alpha)\}
 \leq32\cdot2\exp\{-2n\delta_n(\alpha)^2\}=\alpha.
 \tag{29}
\]
On the complementary event, the true \(P\) itself belongs to
\[
 \{Q\in\mathcal M_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}:
 \|Q-\widehat P_n\|_\infty\leq\delta_n(\alpha)\}.
\]
The definition of \(\mathcal C_{n,k_0}^{\tau}\) then includes the entire set \(\mathcal I_{k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\), not merely the ATT value of a selected representation.  Taking probabilities in (29) and then the infimum over the maintained law class proves
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}}
 P^n\{\mathcal I_{k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)
 \subseteq\mathcal C_{n,k_0}^{\tau}\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
One event contains the joint set, so projecting it covers both marginal identified sets simultaneously.  No step controls lower hemicontinuity, branch persistence, endpoints, or Hausdorff distance; accordingly no such consistency conclusion follows from this argument.
\end{proof}
\par\smallskip\noindent \textit{Justification.} Finite-sample full-PMF inversion provides honest coverage of every disconnected component without choosing a latent branch. \textit{Gap.} Romano--Shaikh develops general inference for identified sets under stated pointwise and uniform conditions; this auxiliary result uses finite multinomial concentration to cover the entire completed-stratum image and deliberately makes no topology-consistency claim. \textit{Consumer.} Applied analyses can attach a finite-sample honest region to the exact singular identified set while clearly separating coverage from set estimation.

\begin{lemma}[lem:cad-quantifier-elimination]\label{lem:cad-quantifier-elimination}
\texttt{For every finite family of real polynomials,\allowbreak{} a finite sign-\allowbreak{}invariant cylindrical algebraic decomposition adapted to that family can be computed. Its cylindrical lifting decides every Boolean combination of their sign conditions,\allowbreak{} yields an equivalent quantifier-\allowbreak{}free formula after projection,\allowbreak{} and represents sections by continuous semialgebraic real-\allowbreak{}root functions. For rational input,\allowbreak{} real-\allowbreak{}algebraic sample points and boundary roots admit square-\allowbreak{}free polynomials,\allowbreak{} rational isolating intervals,\allowbreak{} and Thom derivative-\allowbreak{}sign encodings.}
