%%% FIELD proof-exact-one-collision-region
Fix rational \(\eta\) and rational \(P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,0}^{1\mathrm c}\cup
\mathcal M_{\eta,1}^{1\mathrm c}\).  Choose
\(\psi^0\in\widetilde\Phi_\eta(P)\), which exists by the definition of this
union.  Since \(\psi^0\in\Theta_\eta\), the mixture weights sum to one and
\(e_z\in[\eta,1-\eta]\).  Therefore
\[
p_1=\sum_{z=1}^2\pi_z e_z
   \geq \eta\sum_{z=1}^2\pi_z=\eta>0,
\qquad
p_0=\sum_{z=1}^2\pi_z(1-e_z)
   \geq \eta\sum_{z=1}^2\pi_z=\eta>0.
\tag{1}
\]
Thus every conditional probability defining \(H_d\) and \(N_d\) is defined.
Because the cells of \(P\) and \(\eta\) are rational, every coefficient in a
cleared branch system is rational.  Multiplying each atomic polynomial by a
positive common integer denominator produces an integer polynomial and
preserves its required equality or sign.

The algorithm in Definition
\(\mathrm{def{:}one\text{-}collision\text{-}algorithm}\) enumerates
\[
2\cdot 2^3\cdot 2=32
\tag{2}
\]
labels \((k_0,s,\sigma)\): two proposed collided states, eight orientations,
and two relative alignments.  For each fixed label, the algorithm uses the
complete projection-and-lifting decision procedure whose correctness is
asserted by Lemma \(\mathrm{lem{:}cad\text{-}quantifier\text{-}elimination}\).
That lemma gives a finite sign-invariant cylindrical decomposition for the
finite branch family and decides every Boolean combination of its atomic
signs.  Hence every branch computation terminates, and the finite loop in
(2) terminates.

There is a unique \(k_\ast\in\mathcal A\) for which
\(P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,k_\ast}^{1\mathrm c}\).  Existence follows from the
assumed union.  If both values worked, then every member of the nonempty
global fiber would have its control terminal row collided at \(k=0\) and
noncollided at \(k=1\), while also having its control terminal row collided at
\(k=1\) and noncollided at \(k=0\).  This is impossible.  Thus uniqueness
holds.

For \(k_0=k_\ast\), Theorem
\(\mathrm{thm{:}dimension\text{-}reduction}\) gives a surjective
reconstruction map
\[
\coprod_{s\in\{-1,1\}^3,\,\sigma\in\Sigma}
 \{y:\mathscr G_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,\eta;y)\}
 \longrightarrow
 \widetilde\Phi_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P).
\tag{3}
\]
Because (3) is a map into the displayed fiber, every accepted point
reconstructs a feasible structural vector; because it is surjective, every
feasible structural vector occurs in at least one accepted chart.  Lemma
\(\mathrm{lem{:}quotient\text{-}att}\) proves that simultaneous label
permutation leaves \(\tau\) unchanged.  Applying \(\tau\) to both sides of
(3) therefore yields exactly, rather than merely as an outer bound,
\[
\mathcal I_{k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)
=\bigcup_{s,\sigma}
 \left\{
 \tau\!\left(\operatorname{rec}_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,y);P\right):
 \mathscr G_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,\eta;y)
 \right\}.
\tag{4}
\]

For \(k_0\neq k_\ast\), suppose that a branch point \(y\) were accepted.
We verify reconstruction soundness directly, because the
dimension-reduction theorem itself is quantified over the correct stratum.
For every \((z,d,j)\), positivity of \(R_{zd}(i,j,k)\) makes
\(T_{zd}^R(i,j)>0\), and \(\rho_{zdj}(R)=0\) gives
\[
\frac{R_{zd}(0,j,1)}{T_{zd}^R(0,j)}
=\frac{R_{zd}(1,j,1)}{T_{zd}^R(1,j)}
=\kappa_{zd3j}^R.
\tag{5}
\]
After expanding the two denominators, equality of the first two fractions is
exactly the rank-one equality.  Consequently
\[
R_{zd}(i,j,k)=T_{zd}^R(i,j)B(\kappa_{zd3j}^R;k).
\tag{6}
\]
The definitions of \(S^R,w^R,u^R,a^R\), again with positive denominators,
give
\[
T_{zd}^R(i,j)
=w_{zd}^R B(u_{zd}^R;i)B(a_{zdi}^R;j).
\tag{7}
\]
For each aligned pair, \(\gamma_{zi}^{\sigma}(R)=0\) and positivity of both
initial-state masses imply
\[
a_{z1i}^R=a_{\sigma(z),0,i}^R.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus (6)--(8) supply the common pre-treatment transition required by the
structural model.  The recovered weights obey \(\sum_z w_{zd}^R=1\).  With
\[
\pi_z=p_0w_{\sigma(z),0}^R+p_1w_{z1}^R,
\qquad
e_z=\frac{p_1w_{z1}^R}{\pi_z},
\tag{9}
\]
one has \(\sum_z\pi_z=p_0+p_1=1\) and
\[
\pi_ze_z=p_1w_{z1}^R,
\qquad
\pi_z(1-e_z)=p_0w_{\sigma(z),0}^R.
\tag{10}
\]
The box atoms put every recovered primitive in \([\eta,1-\eta]\).
Equations (6)--(10), the terminal split identities
\(H_d=R_{1d}+R_{2d}\) and
\(N_d=v_{1dk}R_{1d}+v_{2dk}R_{2d}\), and (10) show cell by cell that the
reconstructed structural vector generates \(P\).  The collision and strict
orientation atoms put it in \(\Theta_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}\).  Hence
\[
\operatorname{rec}_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,y)
 \in\widetilde\Phi_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)
 \subseteq\widetilde\Phi_\eta(P).
\tag{11}
\]
But membership of \(P\) in
\(\mathcal M_{\eta,k_\ast}^{1\mathrm c}\) requires every element of the last
fiber in (11) to lie in \(\Theta_{\eta,k_\ast}^{1\mathrm c}\), whereas the
reconstruction in (11) lies in \(\Theta_{\eta,k_0}^{1\mathrm c}\).  These two
strata are disjoint because one requires collision at \(k_0\) and
noncollision at \(1-k_0\), while the other requires the reverse.  This
contradiction proves that all wrong-state branches are rejected and that
\(\mathcal I_{k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)=\varnothing\) there.  Consequently (4),
with the empty case included, proves exactness for each \(k_0\).

For the joint output, adjoin retained coordinates \((q_3,q_4)\) and the two
equalities
\[
q_t=\tau_t\!\left(
 \operatorname{rec}_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,y);P\right),
\qquad t\in\{3,4\}.
\tag{12}
\]
All denominators cleared in (12) are positive on the branch by construction,
so clearing preserves equality.  Applying the cited quantifier-elimination
lemma to eliminate \(y\) gives a quantifier-free Boolean combination of
integer-polynomial sign conditions in \((q_3,q_4)\).  Disjoining these
formulas over the labels in (2) and using (4) proves that the returned joint
formula defines exactly the attained joint image.

It remains to prove the marginal and witness assertions.  The parameter
space \(\Theta_\eta\) is compact by definition, and \(F\) is polynomial,
hence continuous.  Therefore
\[
K(P)=\{\psi\in\Theta_\eta:F(\psi)=P\}
\tag{13}
\]
is compact.  Exclusivity at \(k_\ast\) gives
\(K(P)=\widetilde\Phi_{\eta,k_\ast}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\).  Equation (1) bounds
the only denominator in \(w_{z1}\) away from zero, and the recursions defining
\(m_{z3}\) and \(m_{z4}\) are polynomial in the primitive probabilities.
Thus \(\psi\mapsto\tau(\psi;P)\) is continuous on \(K(P)\).  Its joint image
and its two coordinate images are compact; in particular, every coordinate
endpoint and every isolated coordinate value is attained by a feasible
\(\psi\).

Each marginal is semialgebraic by (12) and quantifier elimination.  The
finitely many nonzero univariate polynomials in its quantifier-free formula
have finitely many roots.  Those roots partition the real line into finitely
many points and open intervals on each of which every atomic sign is
constant.  Hence the marginal is a finite union of points and intervals.
Its compactness makes it closed and bounded, so after merging adjacent
pieces it is a finite ordered union of closed intervals and isolated points.
For rational input, the cited CAD lemma supplies every boundary root as a
real-algebraic number with a square-free integer polynomial, a rational
isolating interval, and its Thom derivative-sign encoding.

Finally, fix a marginal endpoint or isolated value.  Attainment above gives
a nonempty section of (13), and (3) places an attaining representation in an
accepted labeled chart.  Lifting the endpoint section in (12) supplies a
real-algebraic sample point \(y\); the rational reconstruction formulas then
supply the promised real-algebraic feasible \(\psi\).  If a branch is
discarded, sign-invariant lifting covers its candidate cells and the decision
of its original Boolean formula is false on every one of them.  Recording for
each such cell a first original atom with the wrong sign is therefore an exact
finite infeasibility certificate.  This is precisely Collins real quantifier
elimination after the ten-coordinate reduction; no step changes the generic
projection schedule, so no generic worst-case complexity improvement is
claimed.

%%% FIELD statement-exact-one-collision-region
For rational \(\eta\) and rational
\(P\in\mathcal M_{\eta,0}^{1\mathrm c}\cup
\mathcal M_{\eta,1}^{1\mathrm c}\),
\(\mathsf{OneCollisionATT}(P,\eta)\) terminates after examining exactly
thirty-two labeled \((k_0,s,\sigma)\) branches and returns, for each \(k_0\),
exactly the attained joint image \(\mathcal I_{k_0}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\) as a
quantifier-free semialgebraic formula. It returns each nonempty
\(\mathcal I_{k_0,t}^{1\mathrm c}(P)\) as a finite ordered union of closed
intervals and isolated points with Thom-encoded real-algebraic endpoints, a
reconstructed feasible latent representation for every endpoint and isolated
component, and an exact infeasibility certificate for every discarded branch.
The theorem claims equivalence to Collins real quantifier elimination after
the proved ten-coordinate reduction and claims no improvement in generic
worst-case CAD complexity.

%%% FIELD construction-one-collision-algorithm
\(\mathsf{OneCollisionATT}(P,\eta)\) takes reduced rational encodings of
\(\eta\) and the thirty-two cells of \(P\); enumerates exactly
\(2\cdot2^3\cdot2=32\) labeled branches \((k_0,s,\sigma)\); builds each
ten-variable system \(\mathscr G_{k_0,s,\sigma}\); performs the fixed Collins
projection and signed Sturm--Habicht lifting in
\(\mathrm{def{:}observable\text{-}sign\text{-}certificate}\); and projects
each retained cell through
\(\tau(\operatorname{rec}_{k_0,s,\sigma}(P,y);P)\). The joint image is
returned as a quantifier-free Boolean combination of integer-polynomial sign
conditions in \((\tau_3,\tau_4)\). Each marginal is returned as an ordered
finite union of closed intervals and isolated points whose endpoints have a
square-free integer polynomial, rational isolating interval, and Thom sign
sequence. Every endpoint carries a reconstructed real-algebraic \(\psi\) and
its \((k_0,s,\sigma)\) label. An exact certificate for a discarded branch
serializes the complete original cleared polynomial family, the recursive
all-pairs projection data and their exact identities, the signed
Sturm--Habicht lifting and root-isolation data, and a covering of every lifted
candidate cell by a first violated original atom. Branch dispositions,
quantifier-free outputs, endpoints, and witnesses must be derived from those
records and cannot be accepted as predeclared inputs.

%%% FIELD construction-execution-certificate-contract
At \(\eta=1/10\), \(\vartheta=0\), and \(P=P^\diamond\), a machine-readable
record bearing the external identifier
\(\texttt{art:p-diamond-onecollisionatt-v1}\) is a canonical execution
certificate for \(\mathsf{OneCollisionATT}\) if and only if all of the
following data and checks are present.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The input section contains all thirty-two reduced rational cells
\(P^\diamond_{ijkld}\) literally and verifies by exact arithmetic that their
sum is one, that \(p_0=p_1=1/2\), and that both \(\psi^\diamond\) and
\(\psi^{\diamond,\mathrm{sw}}\) generate every displayed cell. From these
inputs alone it derives every entry of \(H\) and \(N\), every collision minor
\(\chi\), and the complete instantiated original family
\(\mathscr G_{k_0,s,\sigma}\), recording canonical polynomial identifiers and
exact rational values or signs whenever a polynomial is specialized to a
sample.
\item For every one of the thirty-two labels \((k_0,s,\sigma)\), the record
contains every original cleared integer equality, weak inequality, and strict
inequality with a canonical polynomial identifier. At each of the ten
projection coordinates it contains contents, all coefficients and leading
coefficients, discriminants, and the resultants and signed principal
subresultant coefficients for every unordered pair in the complete current
family, together with exact determinant, pseudo-remainder, or Bezout
identities that verify those projected polynomials.
\item The lifting record contains the signed Sturm--Habicht sequences, exact
root-isolating intervals and Thom signs, section and sector sample encodings,
parent--child incidence data, and an algebraically checkable proof that the
children cover every real stack over every retained parent cell.
\item Each rejected branch is derived by evaluating a first violated original
atom on every lifted candidate cell. Each accepted branch, reconstructed
structural vector, cellwise equality \(F(\psi)=P^\diamond\), ATT value,
quantifier-free output, endpoint isolator, and Thom sign is derived from the
accepted lifted cells, rather than supplied as an input constant.
\item A deterministic exact checker begins only with the literal input cells
and the declared polynomial order, verifies every projection and lifting
identity and every coverage relation, derives all branch dispositions and
outputs, verifies the canonical serialization, and rejects a payload with a
predeclared leaf list or output formula that lacks such derivations.
\end{enumerate}
This contract defines validity, not existence: a reduced-rational regression
fixture containing the correct PMF and proposed witness values is not by
itself a canonical execution certificate.
