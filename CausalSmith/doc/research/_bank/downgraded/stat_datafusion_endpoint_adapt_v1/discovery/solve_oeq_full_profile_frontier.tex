For \(v\in\{0,1\}\) and \(j\in\{1,\ldots,m\}\), let \(\widehat h_{v,j}\) be the capped informative-cell mean in \(\mathrm{def:capped-estimator}\), and set
\[
 r^{\mathrm{cap}}_{n,m}
 =\min\left\{2a_m,\sqrt{\frac{32m\log(6m/\delta_0)}{n}}\right\},
\]
\[
 I^{\mathrm{cap}}_{v,j}
 =\left[
 \max\{-a_m,\widehat h_{v,j}-r^{\mathrm{cap}}_{n,m}\},
 \ \min\{a_m,\widehat h_{v,j}+r^{\mathrm{cap}}_{n,m}\}
 \right].
\]
Define the exact image of the product rectangle under the average absolute-value map by
\[
 D^{\mathrm{cap}}_v
 =\left[
 \frac1m\sum_{j=1}^{m}\min_{x\in I^{\mathrm{cap}}_{v,j}}|x|,
 \ \frac1m\sum_{j=1}^{m}\max_{x\in I^{\mathrm{cap}}_{v,j}}|x|
 \right].
\]
The capped-cell coordinate-rectangle endpoint rule is
\[
 C^{\mathrm{caprect}}_{n,m}
 =\left(-b+kD^{\mathrm{cap}}_0,\ b-kD^{\mathrm{cap}}_1\right),
\]
where affine operations on an interval denote its exact set image. For compatibility with the existing regular-reduction notation, set
\[
 r_{n,m}=r^{\mathrm{cap}}_{n,m},\qquad
 I_{v,j}=I^{\mathrm{cap}}_{v,j},\qquad
 D_v=D^{\mathrm{cap}}_v,\qquad
 C^{\mathrm{rect}}_{n,m}=C^{\mathrm{caprect}}_{n,m}.
\]

%%% FIELD oeq-updated-partial-result
The closed part consists of three uniform statements. First,
\[
 \ell_{n,m}(0)\asymp
 k\min\{a_m,m^{1/4}n^{-1/2}\}
 \qquad\text{for every }n,m.
\]
Second, uniformly over \(h\in H_m\),
\[
 \ell_{n,m}(h)\asymp
 k\{2a_m-T_0(h)-T_1(h)+\min(a_m,n^{-1/2})\},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak A_{n,m}\asymp\max\{1,a_m\sqrt n\},
 \qquad
 n\leq\frac{\sqrt m}{20a_m^2}.
\]
Third, the capped-cell coordinate rectangle is one implementable, profile-independent, finite-sample honest rule satisfying
\[
 \sup_{h\in H_m}
 R_{n,m}(C^{\mathrm{caprect}}_{n,m},h)
 \leq
 C_0k\min\left\{
 a_m,\sqrt{\frac{m\log(6m/\delta_0)}{n}}
 \right\}.
\]
Together with \(\mathrm{thm:oracle-positivity}\), this yields the global upper envelope
\[
 \mathfrak A_{n,m}
 \leq
 C_0
 \frac{\min\{a_m,\sqrt{m\log(6m/\delta_0)/n}\}}
      {\min\{a_m,n^{-1/2}\}}
 \leq
 C_0\min\left\{
 \max\{1,a_m\sqrt n\},
 \sqrt{m\log(6m/\delta_0)}
 \right\}.
\]
This is only an upper bound. Its two nonconstant ceilings cross at
\(na_m^2/m=\log(6m/\delta_0)\), hence at
\(na_m^2/m\asymp\log m\) for fixed \(\delta_0\). No matching
higher-information lower bound, sharp heterogeneous-profile formula,
globally oracle-optimal family, or sufficient Bernstein certificate
degree has yet been proved.
