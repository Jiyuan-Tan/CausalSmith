By \(\mathrm{thm{:}constant\text{-}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}set\), the class \(\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))\) is nonempty.  By hypothesis there is \(n_0\) such that \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\neq\varnothing\) for every \(n\geq n_0\).  Fix such an \(n\) and any \((C_m)_{m\geq1}\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))\).  The probability axioms give, for every \(\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)\),
\[
 0\leq P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}\leq1.
\]
Because the local set is nonempty, taking its supremum preserves both endpoints:
\[
 0\leq
 \sup_{\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}
 \leq1. \tag{3}
\]
Taking the infimum in (3) over the nonempty honesty class again preserves the bounds.  A limit inferior of a sequence in \([0,1]\) belongs to \([0,1]\), which proves (1).

For the constant honest construction, equation (2) in the proof of \(\mathrm{thm{:}constant\text{-}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}set\) gives
\[
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=1
\]
for every \(\theta_n\).  Thus the supremum over the nonempty set \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) equals one for every \(n\geq n_0\), and taking the limit superior proves (2).

Suppose now that \((0,0,0,0)\in K\).  The first conclusion of \(\mathrm{thm{:}joint\text{-}collision\text{-}experiment\text{-}obstruction\), applied to this \(\kappa\) and \(K\), supplies two forward, zero-bias, exact-Pareto arrays having the identical coordinate value
\[
 (\lambda_n,h_n,b_{1,n},b_{2,n})=(0,0,0,0)
\]
but asymptotically perfectly distinguishable restrictions to \(\mathcal T_n\).  Therefore these four coordinates do not determine a unique local law for the tail-rank experiment.  The second conclusion of the same theorem states that the two exceedance point measures have order-\(k_n\) overlap and that, as a result, their joint information cannot in general equal the summed marginal form
\[
 I_{rs}=\sum_{a=1}^2\int
 \dot\ell_{a,r}(z)\dot\ell_{a,s}(z)\,dM_{\vartheta_0}^{(a)}(z)
\]
displayed in \(\mathrm{def{:}joint\text{-}experiment\text{-}handle\).  Thus the proposed four-coordinate handle supplies neither a uniquely indexed local experiment nor its joint information form.

Finally, \(\mathrm{def{:}ambiguity\text{-}lower\text{-}bound\text{-}handle\) specifies variables to be perturbed in a desired opposite-orientation Le Cam or Assouad construction, but it asserts no bounded-divergence property and no risk inequality.  A construction handle alone therefore yields no positive numerical lower bound.  Combining this fact with the nonuniqueness and missing-overlap certificates above shows that the four-coordinate summed-marginal proposal does not determine the candidate \(v_\eta(K)\).  The only certified conclusions are the probability bracket (1) and the achieved constant-set value (2); nothing in this argument excludes a nontrivial frontier after augmenting the experiment by the common-index coordinate and a joint overlap intensity.
