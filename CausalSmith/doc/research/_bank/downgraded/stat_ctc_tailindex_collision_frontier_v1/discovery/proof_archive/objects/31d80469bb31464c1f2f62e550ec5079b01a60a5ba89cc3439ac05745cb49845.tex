For the forward scaled exact-Pareto tuple \(\theta_n^{\mathrm{Par}}\), let \(u_n\to\infty\) satisfy
\[
\frac{u_n}{\beta_n c_{1,n}^{1/\alpha_{1,n}}+c_{2,n}^{1/\alpha_{2,n}}}\to\infty,
\qquad |\Delta_n|\log u_n\leq L,
\]
and let \(r_n(u)=\beta_n^{\alpha_{1,n}}(c_{1,n}/c_{2,n})u^{-\Delta_n}\). For every such sequence,
\[
E_{P_{\theta,n}^{\mathrm{Par}}}[F_{1,n}(X_{1,n})\mid X_{2,n}>u_n]
=\frac12+\frac12\frac{r_n(u_n)}{1+r_n(u_n)}+o(1).
\]
If, in addition, \(\Delta_n\log c_{2,n}\to0\), then \(\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n)=1+o(1)\); and if \(\lambda_n\to\lambda\in\mathbb R\), then
\[
D_n(t_n)\to g_{\alpha_0,\beta_0,c_0}(\lambda)
=\{2[1+\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0e^{-\lambda/\alpha_0}]\}^{-1}.
\]
The limiting curve tends to \(1/2\) as \(\lambda\to+\infty\), equals \(\{2(1+\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0)\}^{-1}\) at \(\lambda=0\), and tends to zero as \(\lambda\to-\infty\).
