For the forward scaled exact-Pareto tuple \(\theta_n^{\mathrm{Par}}\), let \(u_n\to\infty\) satisfy
\[
\frac{u_n}{\beta_n c_{1,n}^{1/\alpha_{1,n}}+c_{2,n}^{1/\alpha_{2,n}}}\to\infty,
\]
and let \(r_n(u)=\beta_n^{\alpha_{1,n}}(c_{1,n}/c_{2,n})u^{-\Delta_n}\). Then
\[
E_{P_{\theta,n}^{\mathrm{Par}}}[F_{1,n}(X_{1,n})\mid X_{2,n}>u_n]
=\frac12+\frac12\frac{r_n(u_n)}{1+r_n(u_n)}+o(1).
\]
If, in addition, \(\Delta_n\log c_{2,n}\to0\), then \(\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n)\to1\). If moreover \(\lambda_n\to\lambda\in[-\infty,+\infty]\), then, with \(R(\lambda)=\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0e^{-\lambda/\alpha_0}\) for finite \(\lambda\),
\[
\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n)\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
1, & \lambda=-\infty,\\
\displaystyle \frac12+\frac12\frac{R(\lambda)}{1+R(\lambda)}, & \lambda\in\mathbb R,\\
\frac12, & \lambda=+\infty,
\end{cases}
\]
and
\[
D_n(t_n)\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
0, & \lambda=-\infty,\\
g_{\alpha_0,\beta_0,c_0}(\lambda)
=\{2[1+\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0e^{-\lambda/\alpha_0}]\}^{-1}, & \lambda\in\mathbb R,\\
\frac12, & \lambda=+\infty.
\end{cases}
\]
In particular, the middle expression equals \(\{2(1+\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0)\}^{-1}\) at \(\lambda=0\).
