Fix \(\kappa\in[0,\infty]\) and a compact \(K\subset\mathcal L_\kappa\) for which \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) is nonempty for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Under the coverage class, loss, tail-rank measurability restriction, and limit order in \(\mathrm{def{:}ambiguity\text{-}criterion}\), define
\[
 V_n(K)=
 \inf_{(C_m)\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))}
 \sup_{\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}.
\]
The certified lower-side result is exactly the tautological bracket
\[
 0\leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}V_n(K)\leq1. \tag{1}
\]
For the honest construction in \(\mathrm{thm{:}honest\text{-}adaptive\text{-}direction\text{-}set\text{-}partial\text{-}answer}\),
\[
 \limsup_{n\to\infty}
 \sup_{\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=1. \tag{2}
\]
If \(K\) contains \((0,0,0,0)\), the frozen four-coordinate summed-marginal handle does not determine a collision-specific common value \(v_\eta(K)\): the same four-coordinate point contains tail-rank restrictions that are asymptotically perfectly distinguishable, and the proposed information form omits their nonzero overlap covariance.  Thus (1)--(2) are a partial answer and establish neither a positive minimax lower bound nor a matching ambiguity frontier.  This conclusion is restricted to the declared \(\mathcal T_n\)-experiment and makes no assertion about unrestricted full-distribution causal discovery or about a correctly augmented local experiment.
