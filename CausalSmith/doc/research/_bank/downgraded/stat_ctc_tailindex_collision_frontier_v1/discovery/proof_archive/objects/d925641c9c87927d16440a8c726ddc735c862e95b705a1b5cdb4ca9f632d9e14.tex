Exchange the variable labels exactly by setting
\[
\widetilde X_{1,n}=X_{2,n},\quad
\widetilde X_{2,n}=X_{1,n},\quad
\widetilde\epsilon_{1,n}=\epsilon_{2,n},\quad
\widetilde\epsilon_{2,n}=\epsilon_{1,n},
\tag{1}
\]
and likewise
\[
\widetilde\alpha_{1,n}=\alpha_{2,n},\quad
\widetilde\alpha_{2,n}=\alpha_{1,n},\quad
\widetilde c_{1,n}=c_{2,n},\quad
\widetilde c_{2,n}=c_{1,n}.
\tag{2}
\]
The reverse structural equations in \(\mathrm{ass{:}linear\text{-}scm}\) become the forward equations
\[
\widetilde X_{1,n}=\widetilde\epsilon_{1,n},
\qquad
\widetilde X_{2,n}=\beta_n\widetilde X_{1,n}+\widetilde\epsilon_{2,n}.
\tag{3}
\]
All member conditions in \(\mathrm{def{:}collision\text{-}model\text{-}class}\) are invariant under this simultaneous label exchange. The transformed collision coordinates and tail-constant limit are
\[
\widetilde\Delta_n=-\Delta_n,qquad
\widetilde\lambda_n=-\lambda_n,qquad
\frac{\widetilde c_{1,n}}{\widetilde c_{2,n}}  o\frac1{c_0}.
\tag{4}
\]
The transformed scale-locality condition also holds. Indeed, \(\mathrm{ass{:}tail\text{-}constant\text{-}balance}\) and \(\mathrm{ass{:}local\text{-}tail\text{-}collision}\) give
\[
\Delta_n\log c_{1,n}
=\Delta_n\log c_{2,n}+\Delta_n\log(c_{1,n}/c_{2,n})\to0,
\tag{5}
\]
so
\[
\widetilde\Delta_n\log\widetilde c_{2,n}
=-\Delta_n\log c_{1,n}\to0.
\tag{6}
\]
Apply \(\mathrm{thm{:}class\text{-}level\text{-}collision\text{-}curve}\) to the transformed array. Its propagated-to-idiosyncratic ratio is
\[
\widetilde R(-\lambda)
=\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0^{-1}e^{\lambda/\alpha_0}
=R_{\mathrm{rev}}(\lambda).
\tag{7}
\]
The rank coefficients transform as
\[
\widetilde\Gamma_{12,n}=\Gamma_{21,n},qquad
\widetilde\Gamma_{21,n}=\Gamma_{12,n},qquad
\widetilde D_n=-D_n.
\tag{8}
\]
The forward class limits and (7)--(8) therefore give
\[
\Gamma_{21,n}\to1,qquad
\Gamma_{12,n}\to\frac12+\frac{R_{\mathrm{rev}}(\lambda)}{2\{1+R_{\mathrm{rev}}(\lambda)\}},
\tag{9}
\]
and
\[
D_n\to-g_{\alpha_0,\beta_0,1/c_0}(-\lambda).
\tag{10}
\]
This explicitly accounts for both the sign change of the contrast and the inversion of the tail-constant ratio.

On the reverse exact-Pareto subclass, (1)--(2) turn the model into the forward exact-Pareto tuple. Apply \(\mathrm{thm{:}pareto\text{-}collision\text{-}curve\text{-}extended}\) with transformed limit \(\widetilde\lambda=-\lambda\), and then use (8). If \(\lambda=-\infty\), then \(\widetilde\lambda=+\infty\), so the forward triple \((\widetilde\Gamma_{12,n},\widetilde\Gamma_{21,n},\widetilde D_n)\) tends to \((1,1/2,1/2)\), giving the original triple \((1,1/2,-1/2)\). If \(\lambda=+\infty\), then \(\widetilde\lambda=-\infty\), so the forward triple tends to \((1,1,0)\), and the original triple is also \((1,1,0)\). The finite case is (9)--(10). This proves all three exact-Pareto endpoint claims.
