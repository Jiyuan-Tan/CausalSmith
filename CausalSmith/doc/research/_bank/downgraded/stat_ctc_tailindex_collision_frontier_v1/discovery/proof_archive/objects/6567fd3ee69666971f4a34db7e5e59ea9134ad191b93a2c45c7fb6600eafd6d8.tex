The arbitrary-level assertion is an immediate specialization of \(\mathrm{thm{:}pareto\text{-}collision\text{-}curve\text{-}extended}\): that theorem requires only the displayed support-ratio limit, so the additional condition \(|\Delta_n|\log u_n\leq L\) in the present statement is admissible but unused. Under \(\Delta_n\log c_{2,n}\to0\) and \(\lambda_n\to\lambda\in\mathbb R\), the same extended theorem gives
\[
\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n)\to1,qquad
D_n(t_n)\to g_{\alpha_0,\beta_0,c_0}(\lambda)
=\{2[1+\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0e^{-\lambda/\alpha_0}]\}^{-1}.
\]
At \(\lambda=0\), direct substitution gives \(\{2(1+\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0)\}^{-1}\). Since \(\alpha_0,\beta_0,c_0>0\), the factor \(\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0e^{-\lambda/\alpha_0}\) tends to zero as \(\lambda\to+\infty\) and to \(+\infty\) as \(\lambda\to-\infty\). The displayed reciprocal therefore tends respectively to \(1/2\) and to zero, which proves every assertion in the target statement.
