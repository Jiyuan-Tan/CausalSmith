For every triangular array \((\theta_n)_{n\geq1}\in\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)\) such that \(o_n=\mathsf{forward}\) eventually, \(\Delta_n\log c_{2,n}\to0\), and \(\lambda_n=\Delta_n\log t_n\to\lambda\in\mathbb R\), define
\[
R(\lambda)=\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0\exp(-\lambda/\alpha_0).
\]
Then
\[
\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n)\to1,
\qquad
\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n)\to\frac12+\frac{R(\lambda)}{2\{1+R(\lambda)\}},
\]
and
\[
D_n(t_n)\to\frac{1}{2\{1+R(\lambda)\}}
=g_{\alpha_0,\beta_0,c_0}(\lambda).
\]
These are sequential conclusions for each array satisfying the displayed conditions; no additional uniformity over arrays is asserted.
