Fix an array satisfying the hypotheses of the theorem.  Exchange the two labels exactly by defining
\[
\widetilde X_{1,n}=X_{2,n},\qquad
\widetilde X_{2,n}=X_{1,n},\qquad
\widetilde\epsilon_{1,n}=\epsilon_{2,n},\qquad
\widetilde\epsilon_{2,n}=\epsilon_{1,n}.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(\widetilde P_{\theta,n}\) be the law of \((\widetilde X_{1,n},\widetilde X_{2,n})\), and let \(\widetilde F_{1,n}=F_{2,n}\) and \(\widetilde F_{2,n}=F_{1,n}\) be its marginal distribution functions.  For the innovation-tail objects set
\[
\begin{gathered}
\widetilde\alpha_{1,n}=\alpha_{2,n},\qquad
\widetilde\alpha_{2,n}=\alpha_{1,n},\qquad
\widetilde c_{1,n}=c_{2,n},\qquad
\widetilde c_{2,n}=c_{1,n},\\
\widetilde U_{1,n}=U_{2,n},\qquad
\widetilde U_{2,n}=U_{1,n},\qquad
\widetilde A_{1,n}=A_{2,n},\qquad
\widetilde A_{2,n}=A_{1,n},\\
\widetilde\rho_1=\rho_2,\qquad
\widetilde\rho_2=\rho_1,
\qquad
\widetilde c_0=c_0^{-1}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{2}
\]
By \(\mathrm{ass{:}linear\text{-}scm}\), the reverse structural equations become
\[
\widetilde X_{1,n}=\widetilde\epsilon_{1,n},
\qquad
\widetilde X_{2,n}=\beta_n\widetilde X_{1,n}+\widetilde\epsilon_{2,n}.
\tag{3}
\]
Thus the transformed orientation is eventually forward.  We next verify that the transformed sequence is in the domain of \(\mathrm{thm{:}class\text{-}level\text{-}collision\text{-}curve}\).  By \(\mathrm{def{:}collision\text{-}model\text{-}class}\), independent identically distributed sampling and continuity of the margins are preserved by the coordinate permutation (1); innovation independence and the coefficient limit are unchanged; the two uniform tail-normalization, uniform second-order, bounded-bias, and declared-envelope conditions are merely permuted in \(j\); and the intermediate-sequence condition is unchanged.  The local-index condition is also preserved because
\[
\widetilde\alpha_{1,n}\to\alpha_0,qquad
\widetilde\alpha_{2,n}\to\alpha_0,qquad
\widetilde\Delta_n
:=\widetilde\alpha_{1,n}-\widetilde\alpha_{2,n}
=-\Delta_n\to0,
\tag{4}
\]
where the limits are supplied by \(\mathrm{ass{:}local\text{-}tail\text{-}collision}\).  Moreover, positivity and \(\mathrm{ass{:}tail\text{-}constant\text{-}balance}\) give
\[
\frac{\widetilde c_{1,n}}{\widetilde c_{2,n}}
=\frac{c_{2,n}}{c_{1,n}}longrightarrow c_0^{-1}=\widetilde c_0.
\tag{5}
\]
Consequently the transformed sequence satisfies every member condition of \(\mathrm{def{:}collision\text{-}model\text{-}class}\), with limiting tail-constant ratio \(\widetilde c_0\).

It remains to verify the transformed scale-locality condition, which is not automatic from permutation alone.  From the positive ratio convergence in \(\mathrm{ass{:}tail\text{-}constant\text{-}balance}\),
\[
\log(c_{1,n}/c_{2,n})\longrightarrow\log c_0,
\tag{6}
\]
so this logarithm is bounded.  Combining (6), \(\Delta_n\to0\) from \(\mathrm{ass{:}local\text{-}tail\text{-}collision}\), and the assumed \(\Delta_n\log c_{2,n}\to0\), we obtain
\[
\Delta_n\log c_{1,n}
=\Delta_n\log c_{2,n}
+\Delta_n\log(c_{1,n}/c_{2,n})longrightarrow0.
\tag{7}
\]
Therefore
\[
\widetilde\Delta_n\log\widetilde c_{2,n}
=-\Delta_n\log c_{1,n}\longrightarrow0.
\tag{8}
\]
The transformed collision coordinate satisfies, by the definition of \(\lambda_n\) and (4),
\[
\widetilde\lambda_n
=\widetilde\Delta_n\log t_n
=-\lambda_n\longrightarrow-\lambda.
\tag{9}
\]

Apply the already established \(\mathrm{thm{:}class\text{-}level\text{-}collision\text{-}curve}\) to the transformed array, using (3), (5), (8), and (9).  Its limiting propagated-to-idiosyncratic ratio is
\[
\widetilde R(-\lambda)
=\beta_0^{\alpha_0}\widetilde c_0
\exp\{-(-\lambda)/\alpha_0\}
=\beta_0^{\alpha_0}c_0^{-1}e^{\lambda/\alpha_0}
=R_{\mathrm{rev}}(\lambda).
\tag{10}
\]
The coordinate permutation (1), the identities for the transformed margins, and \(\mathrm{def{:}finite\text{-}threshold\text{-}ctc}\) give the exact equalities
\[
\widetilde\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n)=\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n),
\qquad
\widetilde\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n)=\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n),
\qquad
\widetilde D_n(t_n)=-D_n(t_n).
\tag{11}
\]
Indeed, each conditioning rank and each response rank in the transformed definition is the corresponding oppositely labelled original rank.  The transformed forward limits, (10), and (11) yield
\[
\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n)\to1,
\qquad
\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n)\to
\frac12+\frac{R_{\mathrm{rev}}(\lambda)}{2\{1+R_{\mathrm{rev}}(\lambda)\}},
\tag{12}
\]
and
\[
D_n(t_n)
\to-\frac1{2\{1+R_{\mathrm{rev}}(\lambda)\}}
=-g_{\alpha_0,\beta_0,1/c_0}(-\lambda),
\tag{13}
\]
where the last equality follows directly from the declared formula for \(g_{\alpha_0,\beta_0,1/c_0}\).  This proves the finite-\(\lambda\) reverse class formula.

Now restrict to the reverse scaled exact-Pareto subclass in the statement.  Equations (1)--(2) turn this sequence into a forward scaled exact-Pareto tuple of the form in \(\mathrm{def{:}pareto\text{-}witness}\): its two independent Pareto innovations have indices \((\widetilde\alpha_{1,n},\widetilde\alpha_{2,n})=(\alpha_{2,n},\alpha_{1,n})\), constants \((\widetilde c_{1,n},\widetilde c_{2,n})=(c_{2,n},c_{1,n})\), and the same positive coefficient \(\beta_n\).  Its required scale-locality condition is exactly (8).  Hence the already established \(\mathrm{thm{:}pareto\text{-}collision\text{-}curve}\) applies with extended collision limit \(\widetilde\lambda=-\lambda\).

For finite \(\lambda\), that theorem together with (10)--(11) reproduces (12)--(13).  If \(\lambda=-\infty\), then \(\widetilde\lambda=+\infty\), and the forward exact-Pareto endpoint in \(\mathrm{thm{:}pareto\text{-}collision\text{-}curve}\) is
\[
(\widetilde\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n),
  \widetilde\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n),
  \widetilde D_n(t_n))
\longrightarrow(1,1/2,1/2).
\tag{14}
\]
Applying (11) to (14) gives
\[
(\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n),\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n),D_n(t_n))
\longrightarrow(1,1/2,-1/2).
\tag{15}
\]
If \(\lambda=+\infty\), then \(\widetilde\lambda=-\infty\), and the other forward exact-Pareto endpoint is
\[
(\widetilde\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n),
  \widetilde\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n),
  \widetilde D_n(t_n))
\longrightarrow(1,1,0).
\tag{16}
\]
Equations (11) and (16) yield
\[
(\Gamma_{21,n}(t_n),\Gamma_{12,n}(t_n),D_n(t_n))
\longrightarrow(1,1,0).
\tag{17}
\]
The finite case and (15)--(17) establish all asserted exact-Pareto endpoints.
