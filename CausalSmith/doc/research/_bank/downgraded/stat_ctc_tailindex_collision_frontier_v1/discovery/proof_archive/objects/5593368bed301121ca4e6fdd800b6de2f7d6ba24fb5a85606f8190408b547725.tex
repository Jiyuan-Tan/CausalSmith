Fix any parameter sequence satisfying the hypotheses of the proposition, any intermediate sequence \(k_n\), and any declared envelope \(B_n:\mathcal G_n\to[0,\infty)\).  We verify, one by one, the conditions in the definition of \(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)\).

First consider the scaled-Pareto law in \(\theta_n^{\mathrm{Par}}\).  Its lower endpoint is \(a_{j,n}=c_{j,n}^{1/\alpha_{j,n}}>0\), and its distribution function is
\[
 F_{\epsilon_j,n}(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 0,&0\leq x<a_{j,n},\\
 1-c_{j,n}x^{-\alpha_{j,n}},&x\geq a_{j,n}.
 \end{cases}
\]
The two branches agree at \(a_{j,n}\), because \(c_{j,n}a_{j,n}^{-\alpha_{j,n}}=1\); hence each innovation law is continuous and atomless.  Solving
\(1-c_{j,n}x^{-\alpha_{j,n}}=1-1/s\) gives, for every \(s>1\),
\[
 U_{j,n}(s)=(c_{j,n}s)^{1/\alpha_{j,n}}. \tag{1}
\]
In particular, because \(t_n=n/k_n>1\),
\[
 \frac{U_{j,n}(t_n)^{\alpha_{j,n}}}{t_n}
 =\frac{c_{j,n}t_n}{t_n}=c_{j,n}. \tag{2}
\]
Thus the scale constants used to construct the Pareto laws equal the threshold-normalized constants required in the structural tuple; there is no normalization mismatch.

By the construction in \(\mathrm{def:pareto\text{-}witness}\), \(o_n=\mathsf{forward}\), the innovations are independent, and
\[
 X_{1,n}=\epsilon_{1,n},\qquad
 X_{2,n}=\beta_nX_{1,n}+\epsilon_{2,n}. \tag{3}
\]
Equation (3) is precisely the forward clause of \(\mathrm{ass:linear\text{-}scm}\), while independence is \(\mathrm{ass:innovation\text{-}independence}\).  The assumed limit \(\beta_n\to\beta_0>0\) verifies \(\mathrm{ass:coefficient\text{-}limit}\).  The first margin is continuous by the preceding display.  For every \(x\geq0\), innovation independence and atomlessness give
\[
 \Pr\!\left(\beta_n\epsilon_{1,n}+\epsilon_{2,n}=x\right)
 =E\!\left[\Pr\!\left(\epsilon_{2,n}=x-\beta_n\epsilon_{1,n}\mid\epsilon_{1,n}\right)\right]=0. \tag{4}
\]
Consequently the second margin has no atoms and its distribution function is continuous.  This proves \(\mathrm{ass:continuous\text{-}margins}\).  Taking \(Z_{1,n},\ldots,Z_{n,n}\) under the product law \(\bigl(P_{\theta,n}^{\mathrm{Par}}\bigr)^{\otimes n}\) verifies the independent common-law requirement in \(\mathrm{ass:iid\text{-}triangular\text{-}sampling}\).

Set \(A_{1,n}=A_{2,n}=0\).  From (1), whenever \(s>1\) and \(sx>1\),
\[
 \frac{U_{j,n}(sx)}{U_{j,n}(s)}=x^{1/\alpha_{j,n}}. \tag{5}
\]
For every compact subset of \((0,\infty)\), the restriction \(sx>1\) holds for all sufficiently large \(s\), uniformly in \(x\) in that compact subset.  Therefore the zero-auxiliary branch of \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}second\text{-}order\text{-}variation}\) holds exactly, so its remainder is identically zero and hence uniform over the triangular array.

Next fix \(m>1\).  The hypotheses \(\alpha_{j,n}\to\alpha_0>0\) imply that, eventually, \(0<\alpha_{j,n}\leq2\alpha_0\).  The intermediate-sequence condition gives \(t_n=n/k_n\to\infty\), and hence
\[
 \inf_{s\in[m^{-1},m]}
 \frac{U_{j,n}(t_n)s}{a_{j,n}}
 =m^{-1}t_n^{1/\alpha_{j,n}}
 \geq m^{-1}t_n^{1/(2\alpha_0)}\longrightarrow\infty. \tag{6}
\]
Thus the Pareto survival formula applies throughout the indicated range for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Using (2), for those \(n\),
\[
 \sup_{s\in[m^{-1},m]}
 \left|
 \frac{Q_{j,n}\{\epsilon>U_{j,n}(t_n)s\}}
 {c_{j,n}\{U_{j,n}(t_n)s\}^{-\alpha_{j,n}}}-1
 \right|=0. \tag{7}
\]
This proves \(\mathrm{ass:uniform\text{-}tail\text{-}normalization}\).  The assumed convergence \(c_{1,n}/c_{2,n}\to c_0\in(0,\infty)\) is exactly \(\mathrm{ass:tail\text{-}constant\text{-}balance}\).

The two assumed limits \(\alpha_{1,n}\to\alpha_0\) and \(\alpha_{2,n}\to\alpha_0\) yield
\[
 \Delta_n=\alpha_{1,n}-\alpha_{2,n}\longrightarrow0, \tag{8}
\]
so \(\mathrm{ass:local\text{-}tail\text{-}collision}\) holds.  The chosen \(k_n\) satisfies \(\mathrm{ass:intermediate\text{-}sequence}\) by hypothesis.  Finally, the zero auxiliaries imply
\[
 b_{j,n}=\sqrt{k_n}A_{j,n}(t_n)=0,\qquad
 \sqrt{k}\,|A_{j,n}(n/k)|=0\leq B_n(k)
 \quad(k\in\mathcal G_n). \tag{9}
\]
The first equality in (9) verifies \(\mathrm{ass:bounded\text{-}bias\text{-}coordinates}\), and the inequality verifies \(\mathrm{ass:declared\text{-}bias\text{-}envelope}\); nonnegativity of \(B_n\) is used in the last step.

Equations (2)--(9), together with the structural construction (3), verify every member property listed in \(\mathrm{def:collision\text{-}model\text{-}class}\).  Hence \((\theta_n^{\mathrm{Par}})_{n\geq1}\in\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)\), with \(A_{1,n}=A_{2,n}=0\), for every admissible \(k_n\) and every declared nonnegative \(B_n\), as claimed.
