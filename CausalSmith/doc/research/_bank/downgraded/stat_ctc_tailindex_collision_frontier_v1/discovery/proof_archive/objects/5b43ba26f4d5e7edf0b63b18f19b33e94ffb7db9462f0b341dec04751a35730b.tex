By \(\mathrm{thm{:}honest\text{-}adaptive\text{-}direction\text{-}set\text{-}partial\text{-}answer}\), the class \(\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))\) is nonempty.  For every sequence \((C_m)\) in this class, every sufficiently large \(n\), and every \(\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)\),
\[
 0\leq P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}\leq1. \tag{3}
\]
Nonemptiness of \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) implies that taking the supremum in (3) preserves both bounds.  Taking the infimum over the nonempty honest class again preserves them, so
\[
 0\leq V_n(K)\leq1. \tag{4}
\]
The limit inferior in (4) proves (1).

For the constant construction, equation (3) in the proof of \(\mathrm{thm{:}honest\text{-}adaptive\text{-}direction\text{-}set\text{-}partial\text{-}answer}\) gives, for every sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \sup_{\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=1. \tag{5}
\]
Taking the limit superior in (5) proves (2).

Suppose now that \((0,0,0,0)\in K\).  The first certificate in \(\mathrm{thm{:}joint\text{-}collision\text{-}experiment\text{-}obstruction}\) gives two forward exact-Pareto zero-bias arrays with
\[
 (\lambda_n,h_n,b_{1,n},b_{2,n})=(0,0,0,0)
\]
whose restrictions to \(\mathcal T_n\) have total-variation distance tending to one.  Therefore a single local law indexed only by these four coordinates cannot represent the tail-rank restrictions uniformly over \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\).  Its second certificate gives exceedance counts \(Y_{1,n}\) and \(Y_{2,n}\) for which
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}k_n^{-1}\operatorname{Cov}(Y_{1,n},Y_{2,n})>0. \tag{6}
\]
The summed marginal information in \(\mathrm{def{:}joint\text{-}experiment\text{-}handle}\) has no cross term and therefore does not encode (6).  The opposite-report construction in \(\mathrm{def{:}ambiguity\text{-}lower\text{-}bound\text{-}handle}\) merely names a desired bounded-divergence family; it does not supply such a family or prove its class membership, divergence, or orientation separation.  Hence neither a positive Le Cam lower bound nor a matching upper value follows from that handle.  Equations (4)--(6) prove precisely the stated bracket, the constant-set value, and the obstruction to deriving \(v_\eta(K)\) from the frozen four-coordinate experiment.
