The map \(C_n^*\) is constant.  Hence the inverse image of each subset of its finite range is either the whole sample space or the empty set, both in \(\mathcal T_n\).  Thus \(C_n^*\) is \(\mathcal T_n\)-measurable, and the same is true of every \(C_n^{*,(c)}\).

For every \(\theta\in\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)\), the orientation projection satisfies \(o(\theta_n)\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\).  Since \(C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\) pointwise,
\[
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{o(\theta_n)\in C_n^*\}=1. \tag{1}
\]
Taking the uniform infimum and limit inferior in (1), with the coverage condition understood vacuously if the model class is empty, gives
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\theta\in\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{o(\theta_n)\in C_n^*\}
 \geq1\geq1-\eta.
\]
By \(\mathrm{def{:}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}class}\),
\[
 (C_n^*)\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)). \tag{2}
\]
Replacing \(B_n\) by \(B_n^{(c)}=cB_n\) and repeating (1)--(2) proves
\[
 (C_n^{*,(c)})\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B^{(c)}))
 \qquad(c\in\mathcal C).
\]
Because (1) is independent of the sign and magnitude of \(\Delta_n\), it includes the equality boundary \(\Delta_n=0\).

Let \(\sigma\) exchange the observed-coordinate and orientation labels.  Since the full alphabet is invariant under this exchange,
\[
 C_n^*(\sigma z)=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}
 =\sigma C_n^*(z),
\]
so the construction is equivariant.  If \(c\leq c'\), then both sensitivity sets equal \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\), proving the required nesting.

Fix \(\kappa\) and \(K\) as in the statement.  For every sufficiently large \(n\) and each \(\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)\), constancy gives
\[
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=1. \tag{3}
\]
The local set is nonempty, so its supremum in (3) is one.  This value contains no occurrence of \(\mathcal G_n\), \(B_n(k)\), \(s_{\mathrm{CTC},n}(k)\), or \(s_{\mathrm{idx},n}(k)\), and is therefore grid-independent.

The result is partial because \(\mathrm{ass{:}declared\text{-}bias\text{-}envelope}\) supplies only
\[
 \sqrt{k}|A_{j,n}(n/k)|\leq B_n(k),
 \qquad j\in\{1,2\},\quad k\in\mathcal G_n, \tag{4}
\]
not a stochastic expansion for \(\widehat D_n-D_n\) or an implication from (4) to a bias bound for \(\widehat D_n\).  In addition, \(\mathrm{thm{:}joint\text{-}collision\text{-}experiment\text{-}obstruction}\) proves that the proposed four-coordinate experiment omits a root-\(k_n\) common-index coordinate and the nonzero order-\(k_n\) overlap covariance.  Thus the multiplier calibration named in \(\mathrm{def{:}adaptive\text{-}direction\text{-}handle}\) is not a consequence of the frozen premises.  Equations (1)--(4) prove the exact fallback statement and do not assert impossibility under a correctly augmented experiment.
