Fix \(n\).  A constant map from the sample space to the finite range of direction sets is \(\mathcal T_n\)-measurable: the inverse image of any subset of its range is either the empty set or the whole sample space, both of which belong to the sigma-field \(\mathcal T_n\).  Thus \(C_n^*\) and every \(C_n^{*,(c)}\) satisfy the measurability requirement in \(\mathrm{def{:}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}class}\).

By the definition of the orientation alphabet,
\[
 o(\theta_n)\in\{\mathsf{forward},\mathsf{reverse}\}
 =\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}
\]
for every structural tuple \(\theta_n\).  Consequently, for every \(\theta\in\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)\),
\[
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{o(\theta_n)\in C_n^*\}
 =P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{o(\theta_n)\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=1. \tag{1}
\]
Taking first the infimum over \(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)\) and then the limit inferior in (1) gives one, which is at least \(1-\eta\) because \(\eta\in(0,1/2)\).  This proves \((C_n^*)\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))\).  The identical pointwise calculation with \(B\) replaced by \(B^{(c)}\) proves \((C_n^{*,(c)})\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B^{(c)}))\) for every \(c\in\mathcal C\).  Equation (1) does not depend on \(\Delta_n\), so it also proves validity on the equality boundary \(\Delta_n=0\).

Exchanging the two variable labels permutes \(\mathsf{forward}\) and \(\mathsf{reverse}\), and therefore maps the full alphabet \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\) to itself.  This proves label equivariance.  For \(c\leq c'\), both sensitivity-set values equal \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\), so
\[
 C_n^{*,(c)}\subseteq C_n^{*,(c')}
\]
holds with equality.  Finally, the event \(\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}\) is the whole sample space.  Hence its probability equals one pointwise in \(\theta_n\), and its supremum over a nonempty local set \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) also equals one.

It remains to state exactly why the advertised nonconstant calibration is not part of this result.  Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}declared\text{-}bias\text{-}envelope}\) bounds only
\(\sqrt{k}\lvert A_{j,n}(n/k)\rvert\); it contains no inequality for \(\widehat D_n-D_n\).  More decisively, \(\mathrm{thm{:}joint\text{-}collision\text{-}experiment\text{-}obstruction}\) proves that the four collision coordinates can coincide for tail-rank experiments that are asymptotically perfectly distinguishable and that the candidate sum of marginal information forms omits a nonzero order-\(k_n\) cross-covariance.  Therefore the simultaneous critical value and bias correction called for by \(\mathrm{def{:}adaptive\text{-}direction\text{-}handle}\) are not delivered by the frozen premises.  The argument proves the constant honest construction and its exact ambiguity value; it does not claim that every nonconstant honest procedure is impossible.
