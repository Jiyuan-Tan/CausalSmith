By \(\mathrm{thm{:}constant\text{-}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}set}\), the class \(\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))\) is nonempty.  By hypothesis, there is \(n_0\) such that \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\neq\varnothing\) whenever \(n\geq n_0\).  Fix such an \(n\), an honest sequence \((C_m)\), and \(\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)\).  The probability axioms give
\[
 0\leq P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}\leq1.
\]
Because both optimization domains are nonempty, first taking the supremum over \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) and then the infimum over \(\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))\) preserves these endpoints.  A limit inferior of numbers in \([0,1]\) is again in \([0,1]\), proving (1).

For the constant honest construction, the event \(\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}\) is the full sample space.  Therefore
\[
 \sup_{\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=1
\]
for every \(n\geq n_0\), and the limit superior is one, proving (2).

Suppose now that \((0,0,0,0)\in K\).  The first certificate in \(\mathrm{thm{:}joint\text{-}collision\text{-}experiment\text{-}obstruction}\) supplies two forward, zero-bias, exact-Pareto arrays at the identical coordinate
\[
 (\lambda_n,h_n,b_{1,n},b_{2,n})=(0,0,0,0)
\]
whose restrictions to \(\mathcal T_n\) have total-variation distance tending to one.  Hence those four coordinates do not index a unique local law.  The second certificate in that theorem gives
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}k_n^{-1}
 \operatorname{Cov}(Y_{1,n},Y_{2,n})>0,
\]
whereas the candidate information form in \(\mathrm{def{:}joint\text{-}experiment\text{-}handle}\) contains only the two marginal integrals and no overlap term.  Thus the handle provides neither a uniquely indexed experiment nor its joint information.

Finally, \(\mathrm{def{:}ambiguity\text{-}lower\text{-}bound\text{-}handle}\) names variables for a desired opposite-orientation Le Cam or Assouad family, but it proves no class membership, bounded divergence, orientation separation, or risk inequality.  A construction handle alone cannot supply a positive numerical lower bound.  The only certified conclusions are therefore the probability bracket (1) and the achieved value (2).  This argument does not exclude a nontrivial frontier after adding the missing common-index coordinate and a joint overlap intensity.
