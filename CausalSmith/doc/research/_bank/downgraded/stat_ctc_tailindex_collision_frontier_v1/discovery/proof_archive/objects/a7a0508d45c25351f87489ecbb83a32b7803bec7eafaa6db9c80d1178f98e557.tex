Fix \(\kappa\in[0,\infty]\) and a compact \(K\subset\mathcal L_\kappa\) for which \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) is nonempty for all sufficiently large \(n\).  Under the same coverage class, loss, tail-rank measurability restriction, and limit order as in the ambiguity criterion, the certified lower-side bracket is only
\[
 0\leq
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{(C_n)\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))}
 \sup_{\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}
 \leq1. \tag{1}
\]
For the constant honest construction from \(\mathrm{thm{:}constant\text{-}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}set}\),
\[
 \limsup_{n\to\infty}\sup_{\theta_n\in K_n(K,\kappa;B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=1. \tag{2}
\]
If \(K\) contains \((0,0,0,0)\), the proposed four-coordinate summed-marginal experiment does not determine an ambiguity frontier \(v_\eta(K)\): at that same coordinate it permits asymptotically perfectly distinguishable tail-rank restrictions, and its information form omits the nonzero overlap covariance.  Thus (1)--(2) certify neither a positive collision-specific lower bound nor a matching upper bound.  They do not rule out a frontier after augmenting the local experiment with a common-index coordinate and a joint overlap intensity.
