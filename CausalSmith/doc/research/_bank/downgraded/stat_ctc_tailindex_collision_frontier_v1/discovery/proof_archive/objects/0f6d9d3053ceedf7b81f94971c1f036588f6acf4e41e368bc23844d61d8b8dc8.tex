The class \(\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))\) is nonempty by \(\mathrm{thm{:}constant\text{-}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}set}\).  For every member \((C_n)\) of this class, every \(\theta_n\), and every \(n\), probability axioms give

 0\leq P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}\leq1. \tag{3}

Because \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) is nonempty eventually, taking the supremum over this set preserves both bounds in (3).  Taking the infimum over the nonempty honesty class again preserves the bounds, and taking the limit inferior proves (1).

For the particular constant construction, the event in (3) is the whole sample space.  Its probability is therefore one for every \(\theta_n\).  The eventual nonemptiness of \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) implies that the supremum in (2) is one for every sufficiently large \(n\), and hence its limit superior is one.
