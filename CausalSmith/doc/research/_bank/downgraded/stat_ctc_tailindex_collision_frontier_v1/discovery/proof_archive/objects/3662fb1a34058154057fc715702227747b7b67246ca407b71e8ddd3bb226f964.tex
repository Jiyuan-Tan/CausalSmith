Fix \(n\), and write \(\Omega_n=(\mathbb R_+^2)^n\) for the sample space.  By the displayed construction in the theorem, \(C_n^*\) is the constant map from \(\Omega_n\) to \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\).  For any subset \(E\) of the finite range of direction sets, the inverse image \((C_n^*)^{-1}(E)\) is either \(\varnothing\) or \(\Omega_n\).  Both sets belong to every sigma-field, hence to \(\mathcal T_n\).  Thus \(C_n^*\) is \(\mathcal T_n\)-measurable.  The identical argument applies to every constant map \(C_n^{*,(c)}\), \(c\in\mathcal C\).

Let \(\theta=(\theta_m)_{m\geq1}\in\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)\).  By the definition of the orientation projection and the orientation alphabet,
\[
 o(\theta_n)=o_n\in\{\mathsf{forward},\mathsf{reverse}\}
 =\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}=C_n^*.
\]
Consequently the coverage event is the whole sample space and
\[
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{o(\theta_n)\in C_n^*\}=1. \tag{1}
\]
If \(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)\) is nonempty, taking the infimum of (1) over that class gives one; if it is empty, the coverage inequality in \(\mathrm{def{:}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}class}\) is vacuous.  In either case,
\[
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\theta\in\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B)}
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{o(\theta_n)\in C_n^*\}
 \geq 1\geq 1-\eta,
\]
where the last inequality uses \(\eta\in(0,1/2)\).  Together with the measurability and range properties already proved, this is exactly
\((C_n^*)\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B))\) by \(\mathrm{def{:}honest\text{-}direction\text{-}class}\).

Fix \(c\in\mathcal C\).  Replacing \(B\) by \(B^{(c)}\) changes the model class but not the pointwise inclusion \(o(\theta_n)\in\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\).  The same calculation therefore gives
\[
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{o(\theta_n)\in C_n^{*,(c)}\}=1
 \quad\text{for every }\theta\in\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B^{(c)}),
\]
and hence \((C_n^{*,(c)})\in\mathcal H_\eta(\Theta_{\mathrm{CTC}}(B^{(c)}))\).  Equation (1) did not use the sign or magnitude of \(\Delta_n\), so it applies in particular on the equality boundary \(\Delta_n=0\) and under either strict ordering.

Let \(\sigma\) exchange the two variable labels and simultaneously exchange the two elements of \(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\).  Since \(\sigma(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}})=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\), for every sample \(z\in\Omega_n\),
\[
 C_n^*(\sigma z)=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}
 =\sigma(\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}})=\sigma C_n^*(z),
\]
which proves label equivariance.  If \(c\leq c'\), the definitions give
\[
 C_n^{*,(c)}=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}=C_n^{*,(c')},
\]
so the asserted nesting holds with equality.

Finally, for every \(\theta_n\),
\[
 \{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=\Omega_n,
 \qquad
 P_{\theta,n}^{\otimes n}\{C_n^*=\mathcal A_{\mathrm{dir}}\}=1. \tag{2}
\]
Whenever \(K_n(K,\kappa;B)\) is nonempty, taking the supremum of (2) over that set leaves the value one.

It remains to justify the stated scope of the result.  Assumption \(\mathrm{ass{:}declared\text{-}bias\text{-}envelope}\) asserts only
\[
 \sqrt{k}\,|A_{j,n}(n/k)|\leq B_n(k),
\]
and supplies no bound relating this quantity to the bias of \(\widehat D_n\).  Moreover, \(\mathrm{thm{:}joint\text{-}collision\text{-}experiment\text{-}obstruction}\) proves both that the four displayed collision coordinates fail to index a uniform local experiment and that the summed marginal information omits the order-\(k_n\) overlap of the two exceedance channels.  Hence the frozen premises do not deliver the joint critical value and contrast-bias control requested by \(\mathrm{def{:}adaptive\text{-}direction\text{-}handle}\).  The construction proved above is therefore the certified exactly honest fallback; the argument makes no vanishing-ambiguity claim and does not assert impossibility of every nonconstant procedure.
