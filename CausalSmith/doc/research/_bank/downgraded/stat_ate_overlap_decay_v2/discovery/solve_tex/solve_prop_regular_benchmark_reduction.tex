Fix \(\kappa>0\).  Suppose, toward a contradiction, that
\((P,m_0,m_1,p)\in\mathcal M_n\cap\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\), and define
\[
u_\star=\frac12\min\{\lambda_0,\eta_-\}.
\]
The declared ranges \(\lambda_0\in(0,1)\) and \(\eta_-\in(0,1)\) imply
\(0<u_\star\le\lambda_0\) and \(u_\star<\eta_-\).  Membership in
\(\mathcal M_n\), by \texttt{def:public-weak-overlap-class}, entails
\texttt{ass:uniform-shell}.  Its lower shell inequality, applied at
\(u=u_\star\), gives
\[
P\!\left\{\frac{u_\star}{2}<e(X)\le u_\star\right\}
\ge c_-u_\star^\kappa>0,
\]
where strict positivity follows from \(c_->0\), \(u_\star>0\), and
\(\kappa>0\).  Membership in \(\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\), by
\texttt{def:regular-overlap-class}, entails
\texttt{ass:uniform-overlap-comparator}, and therefore
\[
P\{e(X)\ge\eta_-\}=1.
\]
Since \(u_\star<\eta_-\), however,
\[
\left\{\frac{u_\star}{2}<e(X)\le u_\star\right\}
\subseteq\{e(X)<\eta_-\},
\]
so the same shell has probability zero.  This contradiction proves
\[
\mathcal M_n\cap\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}=\varnothing.
\]

Now fix \(n\in\mathbb N\) and
\((r_{0n},r_{1n},r_{en})\in[0,\bar r]^3\).  In particular, \(n\ge1\) and
the three radii are nonnegative.  For every \(\kappa>1\),
\texttt{def:tail-noise-rate} yields
\[
Q_\kappa(n,r_{1n})=n^{-1/2}.
\]
Furthermore,
\[
\frac{\kappa}{2(\kappa+1)}\longrightarrow\frac12,
\qquad
\frac{\kappa}{\kappa+2}\longrightarrow1
\qquad\text{as }\kappa\to\infty.
\]
Because \(n\ge1\), continuity of \(a\mapsto n^{-a}\) gives
\[
r_{1n}n^{-\kappa/[2(\kappa+1)]}
\longrightarrow r_{1n}n^{-1/2}.
\]
If \(r_{en}>0\), continuity of \(a\mapsto r_{en}^{a}\) gives
\(r_{en}^{\kappa/(\kappa+2)}\to r_{en}\).  If \(r_{en}=0\), then
\(r_{en}^{\kappa/(\kappa+2)}=0=r_{en}\), since
\(\kappa/(\kappa+2)>0\).  Hence in either case
\[
r_{1n}r_{en}^{\kappa/(\kappa+2)}
\longrightarrow r_{1n}r_{en}.
\]
The term \(r_{0n}r_{en}\) is independent of \(\kappa\).  Substituting these
limits into the finite sum in \texttt{def:sharp-rate} proves
\[
\lim_{\kappa\to\infty}\rho_n
=(1+r_{1n})n^{-1/2}+r_{1n}r_{en}+r_{0n}r_{en}.
\]

For the asserted uniform comparison, set
\[
A_n=r_{1n}r_{en}+r_{0n}r_{en}\ge0,
\qquad
B_n=n^{-1/2}+A_n>0.
\]
Since \(0\le r_{1n}\le\bar r\),
\[
B_n
\le (1+r_{1n})n^{-1/2}+A_n
\le (1+\bar r)n^{-1/2}+A_n
\le (1+\bar r)B_n.
\]
The comparison constants \(1\) and \(1+\bar r\) are independent of \(n\)
and of all three radii.  Consequently
\[
(1+r_{1n})n^{-1/2}+r_{1n}r_{en}+r_{0n}r_{en}
\asymp n^{-1/2}+r_{1n}r_{en}+r_{0n}r_{en}
\]
uniformly over the declared radius range, and in particular uniformly under
\(r_{1n}\le\bar r\).

It remains to prove the two algebraic source-class comparisons.  Define
\[
C_n=n^{-1/2}+r_{0n}r_{en}+r_{1n}r_{en}.
\]
By \texttt{lem:jin-syrgkanis-regular-benchmark}, the constant-weight
square-root lower-bound template after the stated interpretive substitutions is
\[
J_n=n^{-1/2}+\max\{r_{0n},r_{1n}\}r_{en}.
\]
Nonnegativity of \(r_{0n}\), \(r_{1n}\), and \(r_{en}\) implies
\[
\max\{r_{0n},r_{1n}\}r_{en}
\le (r_{0n}+r_{1n})r_{en}
\le 2\max\{r_{0n},r_{1n}\}r_{en}.
\]
After adding \(n^{-1/2}\) to the first inequality, and using
\(n^{-1/2}\le2n^{-1/2}\) with the second, we obtain
\[
J_n\le C_n\le2J_n.
\]

Finally, \texttt{lem:bonvini-et-al-regular-benchmark} supplies only its
one-functional lower-rate template.  Under the stated treated-side
interpretive substitutions \(\varepsilon_n=r_{1n}\) and
\(\delta_n=r_{en}\), this becomes
\[
L_{1,n}=n^{-1/2}+r_{1n}r_{en}.
\]
Since \(r_{0n}r_{en}\ge0\),
\[
L_{1,n}\le C_n.
\]
No control-side term or sum of two armwise risks has been inferred from that
cited result.  The two cited lemmas retain their distinct source classes and
lower-bound risk conventions; neither asserts a matching upper bound or a joint
two-arm minimax theorem for \(\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\).  Together with the
proved disjointness of \(\mathcal M_n\) and
\(\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\), these calculations are therefore only
algebraic source-class sanity comparisons.  They establish neither minimax
equivalence for \(\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\) nor any class-inclusion
transfer.
