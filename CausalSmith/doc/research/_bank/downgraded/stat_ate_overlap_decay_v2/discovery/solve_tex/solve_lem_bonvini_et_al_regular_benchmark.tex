Fix \(\kappa>0\). Suppose for contradiction that \((P,m_0,m_1,p)\in\mathcal M_n\cap\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\), and set
\[
u_\star=\frac12\min\{\lambda_0,\eta_-\}.
\]
Because \(\lambda_0,\eta_->0\), we have \(0<u_\star\le\lambda_0\) and \(u_\star<\eta_-\). Membership in \(\mathcal M_n\), by \texttt{def:public-weak-overlap-class}, entails \texttt{ass:uniform-shell}; hence
\[
P\!\left\{\frac{u_\star}{2}<e(X)\le u_\star\right\}
\ge c_-u_\star^\kappa>0,
\]
where the strict inequality uses \(c_->0\), \(u_\star>0\), and \(\kappa>0\). Membership in \(\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\), by \texttt{def:regular-overlap-class}, entails \texttt{ass:uniform-overlap-comparator}, and therefore
\[
P\{e(X)\ge\eta_-\}=1.
\]
But \(u_\star<\eta_-\) implies
\[
\left\{\frac{u_\star}{2}<e(X)\le u_\star\right\}
\subseteq\{e(X)<\eta_-\},
\]
so the same shell has probability zero. This contradiction proves
\[
\mathcal M_n\cap\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}=\varnothing.
\]

Now fix \(n\in\mathbb N\) and \((r_{0n},r_{1n},r_{en})\in[0,\bar r]^3\). In particular, \(n\ge1\) and all three radii are nonnegative. For every \(\kappa>1\), \texttt{def:tail-noise-rate} gives
\[
Q_\kappa(n,r_{1n})=n^{-1/2}.
\]
Moreover,
\[
\frac{\kappa}{2(\kappa+1)}\longrightarrow\frac12,
\qquad
\frac{\kappa}{\kappa+2}\longrightarrow1.
\]
Continuity of \(a\mapsto n^{-a}\) therefore gives
\[
r_{1n}n^{-\kappa/[2(\kappa+1)]}
\longrightarrow r_{1n}n^{-1/2}.
\]
If \(r_{en}>0\), continuity of \(a\mapsto r_{en}^{a}\) gives \(r_{en}^{\kappa/(\kappa+2)}\to r_{en}\). If \(r_{en}=0\), both sides are zero because \(\kappa/(\kappa+2)>0\). Thus, in either case,
\[
r_{1n}r_{en}^{\kappa/(\kappa+2)}
\longrightarrow r_{1n}r_{en}.
\]
Substitution into \texttt{def:sharp-rate} and passage to the limit in its finite sum yield
\[
\lim_{\kappa\to\infty}\rho_n
=(1+r_{1n})n^{-1/2}+r_{1n}r_{en}+r_{0n}r_{en}.
\]

For the uniform comparison, define
\[
A_n=r_{1n}r_{en}+r_{0n}r_{en}\ge0,
\qquad
B_n=n^{-1/2}+A_n>0.
\]
Since \(0\le r_{1n}\le\bar r\),
\[
B_n
\le (1+r_{1n})n^{-1/2}+A_n
\le (1+\bar r)n^{-1/2}+A_n
\le (1+\bar r)B_n.
\]
The comparison constants \(1\) and \(1+\bar r\) are independent of \(n\) and of the radii. Consequently,
\[
(1+r_{1n})n^{-1/2}+r_{1n}r_{en}+r_{0n}r_{en}
\asymp n^{-1/2}+r_{1n}r_{en}+r_{0n}r_{en}
\]
uniformly over \(r_{1n}\le\bar r\).

It remains to verify the stated algebraic comparisons. Put
\[
C_n=n^{-1/2}+r_{0n}r_{en}+r_{1n}r_{en}.
\]
Under the interpretive substitutions recorded in \texttt{lem:jin-syrgkanis-regular-benchmark}, the constant-weight square-root lower-bound form is
\[
J_n=n^{-1/2}+\max\{r_{0n},r_{1n}\}r_{en}.
\]
For nonnegative \(r_{0n},r_{1n},r_{en}\),
\[
\max\{r_{0n},r_{1n}\}r_{en}
\le(r_{0n}+r_{1n})r_{en}
\le2\max\{r_{0n},r_{1n}\}r_{en}.
\]
Adding the nonnegative term \(n^{-1/2}\) proves
\[
J_n\le C_n\le2J_n.
\]

By \texttt{lem:bonvini-et-al-regular-benchmark}, the Bonvini--Kennedy--Dukes--Balakrishnan citation supplies only a single-functional lower-rate form. The purely interpretive treated-side substitutions \(\varepsilon_n=r_{1n}\) and \(\delta_n=r_{en}\) give
\[
L_{1,n}=n^{-1/2}+r_{1n}r_{en}.
\]
Because \(r_{0n}r_{en}\ge0\),
\[
L_{1,n}\le C_n.
\]
No control-side formula or sum of armwise risks is inferred from this citation. Both cited leaves retain their own source classes and lower-bound risk conventions; neither proves a matching upper bound or a joint two-arm minimax theorem for \(\mathcal M_n^{\mathrm{reg}}\). Together with the class disjointness established above, these calculations prove exactly the claimed source-class algebraic sanity comparisons and no minimax-equivalence or class-inclusion transfer.
