**Topic.** For an arbitrary finite randomized design with rational positive assignment probabilities, binary potential-outcome schedule, assignment-specific observed-coordinate sets, and a fixed linear effect estimator, characterize all design-estimable expected variance bounds as the Boolean Mobius space generated by observable subsets. Prove universal conservativeness is finite pointwise domination, derive the observable-margin LP dual and the full Pareto-admissible class, and return exact rational primal-dual and assignment-level implementation certificates. Establish the supplied six-coordinate three-atom experiment's nonnegative quartic optimum 107/72 and exact 1/24 separation from every observable quadratic and cubic bound, explain the quartic threshold by complement symmetry, tensorize the gap under independent replication, and state correctly scaled conservative studentized inference under explicit boundedness, nondegeneracy, and LLN assumptions. Preserve the finite binary scope; do not claim finite-sample coverage, universal interval shortening, efficient large-design computation, or extension to real outcomes. Correctly describe Harshaw--Middleton--Savje as a quadratic characterization and school-data empirical illustration, not a reanalysis of original causal conclusions.
