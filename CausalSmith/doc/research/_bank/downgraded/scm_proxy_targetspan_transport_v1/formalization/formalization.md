### T-block: t5 — The simultaneous requirement of unrestricted uniform cove…
**Statement.** The simultaneous requirement of unrestricted uniform coverage and regular-submodel Wald localization is false on the stated array class. More precisely, fix \(p\in(0,1)\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and one deterministic allocation sequence \((n_s(n),n_t(n))_{n\ge2}\) obeying the triangular-allocation condition. For each \(n\ge2\), let \(\Omega_n=\Omega(n_s(n),n_t(n))\) be the corresponding finite product sample space. There is an admissible instance with binary \(E,U,W,Y\), singleton \(\mathcal X=\{x\}\), a fixed model \(\mathcal M^{(0)}\in\mathcal R_{1,1/4,1/16}\), and an array \(\mathbb M^{(1)}=(\mathcal M^{(1)}_n)_{n\ge2}\in\mathcal A_p\), relative to that same allocation sequence, such that \(\theta_{x,1}(\mathcal M^{(0)})=1/2\), \(\theta_{x,1,n}(\mathcal M^{(1)}_n)=5/8\) for every \(n\), and the two induced two-sample laws on the common row space \(\Omega_n\) converge to one another in total variation. Consequently, for every sequence of set-valued rules \(\mathcal C_n:\Omega_n\to\{C\subseteq[0,1]:C\ne\varnothing\}\), which are measurable because \(\Omega_n\) carries its full finite power-set sigma-field, satisfying \[ \liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\mathbb M\in\mathcal A_p} \mathbb P_{\mathcal M_n}^{\,n_s(n),n_t(n)} \{\theta_{x,1,n}\in\mathcal C_n\} \ge 1-\alpha, \] and every fixed \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/8)\), the constructed fixed regular baseline satisfies \[ \liminf_{n\to\infty} \mathbb P_{\mathcal M^{(0)}}^{\,n_s(n),n_t(n)} \left\{I^W_{n,1-\alpha}\ne\varnothing,\ d_H\!\left(\mathcal C_n,I^W_{n,1-\alpha}\right) \ge\frac18-\varepsilon\right\} \ge1-\alpha. \] In particular, since the Wald interval is nonempty with probability tending to one, the same lower bound holds for \(\{d_H(\mathcal C_n,I^W_{n,1-\alpha})\ge1/8-\varepsilon\}\) under any totalization on empty-interval samples. Thus \(d_H(\mathcal C_n,I^W_{n,1-\alpha})\) does not converge to zero in probability, and the stronger requirement \(\sqrt n\,d_H(\mathcal C_n,I^W_{n,1-\alpha})\longrightarrow_P0\) is impossible a fortiori. Hence no measurable nonempty confidence-set sequence can combine unrestricted uniform coverage with even unscaled Wald localization on \(\mathcal A_p\). The uniform-coverage half alone remains attainable: the row-indexed version of \(\widehat C_{x,y,1-\alpha}\) from the concentration projection theorem has the displayed uniform coverage, including along changing-rank arrays.
