Put \(S=\{a,b\}\).  Since \(\theta\in\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}\setminus\mathcal E_{ab,\pi_0}\), the fixed-pair conclusion of \(\text{thm:generic-calibration-elbow}\) gives
\[
\Pi_S(\mathcal O_S)=\{\pi_0\},
\qquad
\Theta_S(\mathcal O_S)=\{T_{\pi_0}\}.
\tag{1}
\]
This is the identifying step, rather than a definition of the structural target: \(\text{thm:generic-calibration-elbow}\), using the residual characterization, proves that the observable compatibility functional has exactly the displayed structural equivalence class off the exceptional variety.

We first verify the claimed positivity of the feasibility margin.  For each \(\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\setminus\{\pi_0\}\), define the proof-intermediate number
\[
c_\pi
:=
\max_{\{u,v\}\in\binom S2}
\left\|\operatorname{offdiag}(R_\pi^{uv})\right\|_{\max}.
\tag{2}
\]
By (1), \(\pi\notin\Pi_S(\mathcal O_S)\).  The equivalence in \(\text{def:compatible-orders}\) therefore implies that at least one observed view pair has a nonzero off-diagonal residual entry, whence
\[
c_\pi>0.
\tag{3}
\]
Because \(p\ge2\), there are exactly \(p!-1\) wrong permutations, a finite nonempty set.  Substituting (1)--(3) into \(\text{def:feasibility-margin}\) yields
\[
\delta_{\mathrm{feas}}(\mathcal O_S)
=
\min_{\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\setminus\{\pi_0\}}c_\pi
>0,
\tag{4}
\]
since the minimum of a finite nonempty family of strictly positive real numbers is strictly positive.

It remains to check explicitly the positive-definiteness premise needed by the repaired covariance-region theorem.  For \(c\in S\), set \(A_c=I_p-B^{(c)}\).  The definition of \(\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) makes \(B^{(c)}\) strictly lower triangular after simultaneous permutation by \(\pi_0\); hence \(A_c\) is unit lower triangular in that order and is invertible.  Let
\[
D_S:=\operatorname{blockdiag}_{c\in S}(A_c^{-1}).
\tag{5}
\]
Let \(K_S\) be the covariance matrix of the selected disturbance vector \((\varepsilon^{(c)})_{c\in S}\), stacked by view.  After a simultaneous coordinate permutation, component alignment identifies it with
\[
K_S\ \sim\ \bigoplus_{i\in V}\Gamma_i[S,S].
\tag{6}
\]
Every \(\Gamma_i\) is positive definite by \(\theta\in\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}\), so every principal submatrix \(\Gamma_i[S,S]\) is positive definite.  Direct sums and permutation congruences preserve positive definiteness; consequently \(K_S\succ0\).  The covariance formula in \(\text{def:fixed-order-open-parameter-space}\), equivalently the stacked structural equations, then gives
\[
\Sigma^{SS}=D_SK_SD_S^{\mathsf T}\succ0,
\tag{7}
\]
because \(D_S\) is invertible.  The same argument for one view gives, for every \(c\in M\),
\[
\Sigma^{cc}
=A_c^{-1}\operatorname{diag}_{i\in V}(\Gamma_i[c,c])A_c^{-\mathsf T}
\succ0.
\tag{8}
\]
Thus (7) supplies the selected-joint nondegeneracy required for whitening in \(\text{thm:covariance-region-inversion}\), while (8) makes every modified-Cholesky inverse well defined.  The same parameter-space definition also supplies the centered component-aligned Gaussian law.

Now impose \(\text{ass:calibration-sampling}\) and \(\text{ass:marginal-sampling}\).  These state, respectively, that the aligned observations from \(S\) and the observations retained for each marginal are independent and identically distributed samples from the corresponding marginals of the same law \(P\); no independence between the resulting covariance estimators is needed.  The fixed positive-margin hypotheses \(\text{ass:feasibility-separation}\) and \(\text{ass:path-product-separation}\) give
\[
\delta_{\mathrm{feas}}(\mathcal O_S)
\ge \kappa_{\mathrm{feas}}>0,
\qquad
\delta_{\mathrm{path}}(\mathcal O_S)
\ge \kappa_{\mathrm{path}}>0.
\tag{9}
\]
Equation (4) shows in particular that the first such fixed positive margin is available pointwise off \(\mathcal E_{ab,\pi_0}\); (9) records the uniform lower bounds required for the stated asymptotic conclusion.

Let \(\min\{n_S,\min_{c\in M}n_c\}\to\infty\), let \(\alpha_n\to0\), and suppose the radii from \(\text{def:covariance-region}\) vanish.  Along the resulting tail on which the displayed \(\eta\)-conditions hold, (7)--(9) and the two sampling assumptions verify every premise of \(\text{thm:covariance-region-inversion}\).  Its consistency conclusion therefore gives
\[
d_{\mathrm H}^{d_{\mathrm{coef}}}
\left(
\widehat\Theta_{S,n}(\alpha_n),
\Theta_S(\mathcal O_S)
\right)
\xrightarrow{P}0
\tag{10}
\]
and
\[
\Pr\!\left\{
\widehat\prec_{S,n}(\alpha_n)=\prec_S
\right\}
\longrightarrow1.
\tag{11}
\]
Substitution of (1) into (10) is precisely Hausdorff recovery of the singleton \(\{T_{\pi_0}\}\).  Finally, by \(\text{def:essential-poset}\) and (1), for distinct \(i,j\in V\),
\[
i\prec_S j
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
G_{T_{\pi_0}}\text{ contains a directed path from }i\text{ to }j.
\tag{12}
\]
Hence \(\prec_S\) is the ancestral relation induced by the recovered tuple, and (11) proves its exact recovery in probability.
