Fix \(p\ge 2\), \(m\ge 2\), and \(\pi_0\in\mathfrak S_V\).  For every unordered view pair write it uniquely as \(\{a,b\}\) with \(1\le a<b\le m\), and define
\[
Q_{\mathrm{all}}(\theta)
:=
\prod_{1\le a<b\le m}\ \prod_{\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\setminus\{\pi_0\}}
F_\pi^{ab}(\theta),
\qquad
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{all},\pi_0}
:=
\{\theta\in\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}:Q_{\mathrm{all}}(\theta)=0\}.
\]
Every factor \(F_\pi^{ab}\), and hence \(Q_{\mathrm{all}}\), is a nonzero polynomial; specifically, the witness \(\theta_\pi^{ab}\in\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) has a wrong-order residual entry equal, up to transpose orientation, to \(\beta(r_u-r_v)/(1+\beta^2)\ne0\).  Thus \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{all},\pi_0}\) is a proper relatively Lebesgue-null algebraic subset.  Outside it, every view pair simultaneously satisfies
\[
\Pi_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})=\{\pi_0\},
\qquad
\Theta_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})
=\Theta_M(\mathcal O_M)=\{T_{\pi_0}\}.
\]
For each fixed pair \(\{a,b\}\), its exceptional set is exactly its order-nonidentification locus:
\[
\mathcal E_{ab,\pi_0}
=
\{\theta\in\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}:
\Pi_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})\ne\{\pi_0\}\}.
\]
The coefficient-nonidentification locus
\[
\{\theta\in\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}:
\Theta_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})\ne\{T_{\pi_0}\}\}
\]
is contained in \(\mathcal E_{ab,\pi_0}\), and the containment can be strict because distinct compatible orders can have the same numerical tuple.

Let \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{marg}}=(\Sigma^{aa})_{a\in M}\) contain all viewwise marginals and no cross-view block.  Define \(\Pi_{\mathrm{marg}}\) to be the set of permutations for which there exists a full member of \(\mathfrak M_{p,m}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) with that common order and observable \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{marg}}\), and define \(\Theta_{\mathrm{marg}}\) to be the corresponding coefficient-tuple set with numerical duplicates removed.  Then
\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{marg}}=\mathfrak S_V,
\qquad
\Theta_{\mathrm{marg}}=\{T_\pi:\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\}.
\]
For \(C\subseteq M\), let \(\Theta_C^{\mathrm{ext}}=\Theta_{\mathrm{marg}}\) when \(\lvert C\rvert\le1\), and let \(\Theta_C^{\mathrm{ext}}=\Theta_C(\mathcal O_C)\) when \(\lvert C\rvert\ge2\).  Define the extended calibration number
\[
\kappa_{\mathrm{cal}}
:=
\min\{\lvert C\rvert:C\subseteq M,\ 
\Theta_C^{\mathrm{ext}}=\Theta_M(\mathcal O_M)\}.
\]
This minimum is well defined because \(M\) is finite and \(C=M\) is feasible.
For every \(\theta\notin\mathcal E_{\mathrm{all},\pi_0}\), \(\lvert\Theta_{\mathrm{marg}}\rvert\ge2\), whereas any single observed pair gives the full singleton.  Consequently one paired cross-view block is generically necessary and sufficient, \(\kappa_{\mathrm{cal}}=2\), and also \(\lvert S_{\min}\rvert=2\) under the original convention \(\lvert S\rvert\ge2\).  When \(m\ge3\) the identifying pair is a proper subset of the views; when \(m=2\) it is the full view set.  No edge-minimality condition and no diversity condition from Assumption 2 of HeurtebiseEtAl2026LiMVAM is imposed.  Every parameter law absolutely continuous with respect to relative Lebesgue measure avoids \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{all},\pi_0}\) almost surely.

Fix \(\theta\in\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}\).  Put \(A^{(a)}=(I_p-B^{(a)})^{-1}\).  Because \(B^{(a)}\) is strictly lower triangular in the order \(\pi_0\), \(I_p-B^{(a)}\) is invertible.  Moreover, positive definiteness of \(\Gamma_i\) gives \(\Gamma_i[a,a]>0\) for every \(i\in V\) and \(a\in M\).  The covariance formula in \(\text{def:fixed-order-open-parameter-space}\) therefore gives
\[
\Sigma_\theta^{aa}
=A^{(a)}\operatorname{diag}_{i\in V}(\Gamma_i[a,a])(A^{(a)})^{\mathsf T}
\succ0.
\]
Thus every principal submatrix inverted in \(\text{def:modified-cholesky}\) is positive definite and has strictly positive determinant.

Nilpotence of the order-triangular matrix gives
\[
(I_p-B^{(a)})^{-1}=\sum_{q=0}^{p-1}(B^{(a)})^q,
\]
so every entry of \(\Sigma_\theta^{ab}\) is a polynomial in the free coordinates of \(\theta\).  The adjugate identity \(C^{-1}=\operatorname{adj}(C)/\det(C)\), applied to each positive-definite predecessor covariance \(C\), shows that every entry of \(B_\pi^{(a)}\), and hence every entry of
\[
R_\pi^{ab}=(I_p-B_\pi^{(a)})\Sigma_\theta^{ab}
(I_p-B_\pi^{(b)})^{\mathsf T},
\]
is rational with denominator a product of positive principal minors.  Consequently denominator clearing in \(\text{def:pairwise-exceptional-variety}\) preserves the zero or nonzero status of every residual entry.

Fix \(1\le a<b\le m\) and \(\pi\ne\pi_0\).  Two distinct total orders have a discordant pair, so there exist \(u,v\in V\) such that \(u\) precedes \(v\) under \(\pi_0\) and \(v\) precedes \(u\) under \(\pi\).  Consider \(\theta_\pi^{ab}\) from \(\text{def:wrong-order-nonvanishing-witness}\).  For \(w\in\{u,v\}\), after simultaneously permuting view coordinates, \(\Gamma_w\) is the direct sum of
\[
\begin{pmatrix}1&r_w\\ r_w&1\end{pmatrix}
\quad\text{and}\quad I_{m-2}.
\]
Its eigenvalues are \(1-r_w\), \(1+r_w\), and \(1\) with multiplicity \(m-2\), all strictly positive because \(\lvert r_w\rvert<1\).  The other disturbance covariance matrices are \(I_m\), and the sole coefficient \(\beta\) respects \(\pi_0\).  Hence \(\theta_\pi^{ab}\in\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}\), including when \(m=2\).

Coordinates outside \(\{u,v\}\) are independent of this two-coordinate subsystem and have zero covariance with it, so adding them to a predecessor set does not change the following regression coefficients.  In each of views \(a,b\),
\[
X_u=\varepsilon_u,
\qquad
X_v=\beta\varepsilon_u+\varepsilon_v,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_u)=\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_v)=1.
\]
Since \(v\) precedes \(u\) under \(\pi\), the corresponding wrong-order innovations are
\[
\widetilde\varepsilon_v=X_v,
\qquad
\widetilde\varepsilon_u
=X_u-\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X_u,X_v)}{\operatorname{Var}(X_v)}X_v
=X_u-\frac{\beta}{1+\beta^2}X_v.
\]
Cross-variable disturbance orthogonality and the witness covariances give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cov}\!\left(
\widetilde\varepsilon_u^{(a)},
\widetilde\varepsilon_v^{(b)}
\right)
&=
\operatorname{Cov}\!\left(
\frac{\varepsilon_u^{(a)}-\beta\varepsilon_v^{(a)}}{1+\beta^2},
\beta\varepsilon_u^{(b)}+\varepsilon_v^{(b)}
\right)\\
&=\frac{\beta}{1+\beta^2}(r_u-r_v)\ne0.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is an off-diagonal entry of \(R_\pi^{ab}\), up to transpose orientation.  Its cleared numerator is nonzero at \(\theta_\pi^{ab}\); hence \(F_\pi^{ab}\) is not the zero polynomial.

There are finitely many unordered pairs and wrong permutations.  A finite product of nonzero real multivariate polynomials is nonzero: for example, choose a monomial order and observe that the leading coefficient of a product is the product of the nonzero leading coefficients.  Therefore \(Q_{\mathrm{all}}\) is nonzero.  By \(\text{lem:nonzero-polynomial-null-set}\), its ambient zero set has Lebesgue measure zero and empty interior.  The space \(\Omega_{\pi_0}^{\mathrm{CA}}\) is nonempty and relatively open in its free Euclidean coordinates: the point with every \(B^{(a)}=0\) and every \(\Gamma_i=I_m\) belongs to it, and positive definiteness is open.  It follows that \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{all},\pi_0}\) is proper and relatively Lebesgue null.  Absolute continuity then gives probability zero to this set by the definition of absolute continuity.

We next identify the exact failure loci.  Fix \(a<b\).  Since every denominator used to define \(f_{\pi,ij}^{ab}\) is positive,
\[
F_\pi^{ab}(\theta)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
f_{\pi,ij}^{ab}(\theta)=0\ \text{for all }i\ne j
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{offdiag}(R_\pi^{ab}(\theta))=0.
\]
The first equivalence uses that \(F_\pi^{ab}\) is a sum of real squares; the second uses positive denominator clearing.  Moreover, \(R_\pi^{ba}=(R_\pi^{ab})^{\mathsf T}\), by the covariance symmetry \(\Sigma^{ba}=(\Sigma^{ab})^{\mathsf T}\), so one orientation tests the unordered pair.  The true order \(\pi_0\) is compatible by \(\text{lem:fixed-order-residual-characterization}\).  The same lemma and the preceding equivalence yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
\theta\in\mathcal E_{ab,\pi_0}
&\Longleftrightarrow
F_\pi^{ab}(\theta)=0\ \text{for at least one }\pi\ne\pi_0\\
&\Longleftrightarrow
\Pi_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})\ne\{\pi_0\}.
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves the exact order-nonidentification statement.

If \(\theta\notin\mathcal E_{\mathrm{all},\pi_0}\), every factor in \(Q_{\mathrm{all}}(\theta)\) is nonzero.  Applying the preceding equivalence separately to every pair and every wrong order gives, simultaneously for all \(a<b\),
\[
\Pi_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})=\{\pi_0\}.
\]
The complete coefficient characterization in \(\text{lem:fixed-order-residual-characterization}\) then implies
\[
\Theta_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})=\{T_{\pi_0}\}.
\]
Full calibration retains the true order and imposes every pair restriction, so its compatible-order set is contained in the compatible-order set for any one pair.  Hence
\[
\Theta_M(\mathcal O_M)=\{T_{\pi_0}\}.
\]
This step proves, rather than defines, equality between the structural coefficient target and the observable residual-restriction functional.

If the pairwise coefficient set is not \(\{T_{\pi_0}\}\), then its compatible-order set cannot be \(\{\pi_0\}\).  Thus its coefficient-nonidentification locus is contained in \(\mathcal E_{ab,\pi_0}\).  To see that equality need not hold, take the parameter with every \(B^{(a)}=0\) and every \(\Gamma_i=I_m\).  Then \(\Sigma^{aa}=I_p\), while \(\Sigma^{ab}=0\) for \(a\ne b\).  For every order \(\pi\), modified Cholesky regression gives \(B_\pi^{(a)}=0\) and \(R_\pi^{ab}=0\) for \(a\ne b\).  Hence
\[
\Pi_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})=\mathfrak S_V,
\qquad
\Theta_{\{a,b\}}(\mathcal O_{\{a,b\}})=\{(0)_{a\in M}\}=
\{T_{\pi_0}\}.
\]
Because \(p\ge2\), there is a wrong order, so this parameter belongs to \(\mathcal E_{ab,\pi_0}\) but not to the coefficient-nonidentification locus.  The containment is therefore strict for this duplicate-order example.

It remains to prove the lower half of the calibration elbow.  Fix an arbitrary \(\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\) and view \(a\in M\).  The rows of \(L_\pi^{(a)}X^{(a)}\) are the successive least-squares innovations from \(\text{def:modified-cholesky}\).  The normal equations make the innovation at position \(k\) orthogonal to the span of the variables in positions \(1,\ldots,k-1\).  Every earlier innovation belongs to that span.  Thus distinct innovations are orthogonal and
\[
D_\pi^{(a)}:=R_\pi^{aa}=L_\pi^{(a)}\Sigma^{aa}(L_\pi^{(a)})^{\mathsf T}
\]
is diagonal.  It is positive definite because \(\Sigma^{aa}\succ0\) and \(L_\pi^{(a)}\) is invertible; therefore every \(D_\pi^{(a)}[j,j]>0\).

For each \(j\in V\), define
\[
\Gamma_{\pi,j}^{\mathrm{marg}}
:=\operatorname{diag}_{a\in M}\bigl(D_\pi^{(a)}[j,j]\bigr)\succ0.
\]
More explicitly, let \((Z_j)_{j\in V}\) be independent standard Gaussian vectors in \(\mathbb R^m\), and set
\[
\widetilde\varepsilon_j
:=\operatorname{diag}_{a\in M}\!\left(\sqrt{D_\pi^{(a)}[j,j]}\right)Z_j.
\]
Then \(\operatorname{Cov}(\widetilde\varepsilon_j)=\Gamma_{\pi,j}^{\mathrm{marg}}\), and the disturbance stacks are independent across \(j\).  Set
\[
\widetilde X^{(a)}=(I_p-B_\pi^{(a)})^{-1}\widetilde\varepsilon^{(a)}.
\]
The resulting law is jointly centered Gaussian, has common order \(\pi\), satisfies cross-variable disturbance orthogonality, and has positive-definite coordinate disturbance covariances.  Moreover,
\[
\operatorname{Cov}(\widetilde X^{(a)})
=(L_\pi^{(a)})^{-1}D_\pi^{(a)}(L_\pi^{(a)})^{-\mathsf T}
=\Sigma^{aa},
\]
where the last equality is the defining identity for \(D_\pi^{(a)}\).  Hence this is a full component-aligned completion of \(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{marg}}\) for every \(\pi\).  Conversely, for a fixed order and fixed positive-definite marginal covariance, the population normal equations uniquely give the coefficients in \(\text{def:modified-cholesky}\).  Consequently
\[
\Pi_{\mathrm{marg}}=\mathfrak S_V,
\qquad
\Theta_{\mathrm{marg}}=\{T_\pi:\pi\in\mathfrak S_V\}.
\]

Now assume \(\theta\notin\mathcal E_{\mathrm{all},\pi_0}\), and choose any \(\pi\ne\pi_0\), which exists because \(p\ge2\).  Suppose for contradiction that \(T_\pi=T_{\pi_0}\).  Then \(L_\pi^{(a)}=L_{\pi_0}^{(a)}\) for every view \(a\).  Under the data-generating representation and component alignment,
\[
R_{\pi_0}^{ab}
=\operatorname{Cov}(\varepsilon^{(a)},\varepsilon^{(b)})
=\operatorname{diag}_{j\in V}(\Gamma_j[a,b])
\]
is diagonal for every pair.  Equality of residualizers would give \(R_\pi^{ab}=R_{\pi_0}^{ab}\), and hence \(F_\pi^{ab}(\theta)=0\), for every \(a<b\).  This forces \(Q_{\mathrm{all}}(\theta)=0\), a contradiction.  Thus \(T_\pi\ne T_{\pi_0}\), and \(\lvert\Theta_{\mathrm{marg}}\rvert\ge2\).

If \(\lvert C\rvert\le1\), no distinct pair of calibrated views exists and \(\Theta_C^{\mathrm{ext}}=\Theta_{\mathrm{marg}}\ne\Theta_M(\mathcal O_M)\).  If \(\lvert C\rvert=2\), the simultaneous pair result gives \(\Theta_C^{\mathrm{ext}}=\Theta_M(\mathcal O_M)=\{T_{\pi_0}\}\).  Therefore
\[
\kappa_{\mathrm{cal}}=2.
\]
Equivalently, zero observed cross-view pairs are insufficient and any one observed pair is sufficient outside \(\mathcal E_{\mathrm{all},\pi_0}\).  The original definition of \(S_{\min}\) minimizes only over sets of size at least two, so the same argument gives \(\lvert S_{\min}\rvert=2\).  A two-element set is proper in \(M\) exactly when \(m\ge3\).  The proof invoked only order-triangular coefficients, positive-definite coordinate disturbance covariances, Gaussian completion, and component alignment; it did not impose edge minimality or the diversity condition of HeurtebiseEtAl2026LiMVAM.
