Suppose \(W=\varnothing\) and \(p(v)>0\) for every \(v\in\mathcal A_V\). Whenever \(\mathsf{ID}(G,V,T,Y)\) returns \(\phi_{\mathrm{ID}}(p)\), that functional is constant on every observed-law fiber of the fixed-alphabet class and can be represented by a support-guarded rational success certificate whose denominator guards are entailed by strict observational positivity. This implication is one-way: no converse from fixed-alphabet identification to classical ID is asserted. In the further special case \(H=\varnothing\), the certificate reduces to the ordinary truncated g-formula \(Q_M(t,y)=\sum_{a_{D\setminus(T\cup Y)}}\prod_{i\in D\setminus T}p(a_i\mid a_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)})\).
