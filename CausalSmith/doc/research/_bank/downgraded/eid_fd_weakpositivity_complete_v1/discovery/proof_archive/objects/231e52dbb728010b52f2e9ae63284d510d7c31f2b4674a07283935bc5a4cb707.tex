Fix the four-node notation \(\iota=(i_Z,i_A,i_X,i_Y)\), put
\(\mathcal Z=\mathcal A_{i_Z}\), \(\mathcal B=\mathcal A_{i_A}\),
\(\mathcal X=\mathcal A_{i_X}\), and
\(\mathcal Y=\mathcal A_{i_Y}\), and let \(p\) be an arbitrary law on
\(\mathcal Z\times\mathcal X\times\mathcal Y\).  Define
\[
 \mathcal Z_+=\{z\in\mathcal Z:p(z)>0\}.
\]
For \(z\in\mathcal Z_+\), define, under the displayed positive
denominators,
\[
 e_z(x)=\frac{p(z,x)}{p(z)},
 \qquad
 m_{zx}(y)=\frac{p(z,x,y)}{p(z,x)}\quad\text{when }p(z,x)>0,
\]
and put
\[
 \rho_z=\bigl(e_z,(m_{zx})_{x:e_z(x)>0}\bigr),
 \qquad
 \mathcal R(p)=\{\rho_z:z\in\mathcal Z_+\}.
\]
For an integer \(b\geq1\), let \(\Pi_b(p)\) be the set of partitions
\(\pi\) of all of \(\mathcal Z\) into at most \(b\) nonempty blocks
such that any two positive-mass states in the same block have the same
observed profile:
\[
 z,z'\in C\cap\mathcal Z_+\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \rho_z=\rho_{z'}
 \qquad(C\in\pi).
\]
Thus a zero-mass root state may join any block or form a separate block.
For \(\pi\in\Pi_b(p)\), let \(\mathcal K_\pi(p)\) be the set of arrays
\(k=(k(z,x,\cdot))_{z,x}\) of elements of \(\Delta(\mathcal Y)\) for
which, for every \(C\in\pi\) and \(x\in\mathcal X\), there is one row
\(s_{C,x}\in\Delta(\mathcal Y)\) satisfying
\[
 k(z,x,\cdot)=s_{C,x}\qquad(z\in C).
\]
If there exists \(z\in C\cap\mathcal Z_+\) with \(p(z,x)>0\), this
common row is constrained by
\[
 s_{C,x}=m_{zx};
\]
the profile condition makes the right-hand side independent of the
chosen reached \(z\).  If no such reached positive-mass pair exists,
\(s_{C,x}\) is free in \(\Delta(\mathcal Y)\).  Define the
arbitrary-observed-law response-kernel region by
\[
 \mathcal K(p)=
 \bigcup_{\pi\in\Pi_{|\mathcal B|}(p)}\mathcal K_\pi(p).
\]
