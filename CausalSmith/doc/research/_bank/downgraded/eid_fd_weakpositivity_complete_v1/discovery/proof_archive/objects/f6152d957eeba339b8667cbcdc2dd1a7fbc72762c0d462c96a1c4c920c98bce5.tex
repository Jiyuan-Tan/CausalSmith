Suppose that
\(\sqrt N(T_N-\vartheta)\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,\sigma^2)\) with
\(\sigma>0\), and that \(S_N\to_p\sigma\).  Then
\(\sqrt N(T_N-\vartheta)/S_N\Rightarrow\mathcal N(0,1)\), and the
two-sided interval
\[
\left[T_N-z_{1-\alpha/2}S_N/\sqrt N,
T_N+z_{1-\alpha/2}S_N/\sqrt N\right]
\]
has limiting coverage \(1-\alpha\) for every \(\alpha\in(0,1)\).
