Write \(N_V=|\mathcal A_V|\) and \(N_q=|\mathcal A_T||\mathcal A_Y|\). Since \(\mathcal A_V\) and \(\mathcal A_Y\) are coordinate projections of \(\Omega\),
\[
 N_V\le h,
 \qquad
 N_q=J_T|\mathcal A_Y|\le J_Th.
 \tag{1}
\]
For a CPT family \(\theta\), define the observational and interventional network polynomials
\[
 P_\theta(v)
 =\sum_{a_D:\,a_V=v}\prod_{i\in D}
   \theta_i(a_i\mid a_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)}),
 \tag{2}
\]
and
\[
 Q_\theta(t,y)
 =\sum_{a_{D\setminus(T\cup Y)}}
   \prod_{i\in D\setminus T}
   \theta_i(a_i\mid a_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)}),
 \tag{3}
\]
where the coordinates in \(T\cup Y\) are fixed at \((t,y)\) in (3), and all unreported hidden or observed coordinates are summed. Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:causal\text{-}factorization}\) makes (2) equal to the observed marginal \(p_M(v)\), and Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:truncated\text{-}intervention}\) makes (3) equal to the causal target coordinate \(Q_M(t,y)\). Thus the causal target is not defined by any expression below; equality with an observed-law expression must be proved from (2)--(3).

First run the functional-map feasibility enumerator. By Theorem \(\mathrm{thm:feasibility\text{-}certificate}\), this terminates in
\[
 O\!\left(FJ_T(d+m)h+FR\right)
 \tag{4}
\]
table operations. On failure it gives the asserted polynomial contradiction to joint-treatment positivity; on success it gives a rational original-alphabet member of \(\mathfrak M\). This proves the first output and permits us henceforth to assume that the class is feasible.

For each deterministic map tuple \(f\) allowed by Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:qualitative\text{-}functionality}\), enumerate every row-support pattern \(S\) from Definition \(\mathrm{def:support\text{-}stratum}\). Let \(\Theta_{f,S}\) be the relatively open product of row simplexes obtained by fixing the functional rows according to \(f\), setting entries outside \(S\) to zero, requiring entries in \(S\) to be strictly positive, and imposing every row-sum equation. There are at most
\[
 B:=F2^K
 \tag{5}
\]
enumerated pairs \((f,S)\). Discard a pair if some \(t\in\mathcal A_T\) has no compatible complete assignment. By Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:support\text{-}propagation}\), such a pair has \(P_\theta(T=t)=0\) throughout its stratum and is excluded by Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:joint\text{-}treatment\text{-}positivity}\). Conversely, if every \(t\) has a compatible complete assignment, every \(\theta\in\Theta_{f,S}\) has \(P_\theta(T=t)>0\), again by that lemma. Hence the retained strata partition the feasible CPT parameter space.

For a retained stratum define
\[
 \mathcal B(f,S)
 :=\{v\in\mathcal A_V:\text{a compatible complete assignment extends }v\}.
 \tag{6}
\]
Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:support\text{-}propagation}\), applied to (2), gives the equivalence
\[
 v\in\mathcal B(f,S)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 P_\theta(v)>0
 \qquad(\theta\in\Theta_{f,S}).
 \tag{7}
\]
Thus the observable-support branch \(\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal A_V\) is selected by the rational-coefficient guards
\[
 p(v)>0\quad(v\in\mathcal B),
 \qquad
 p(v)=0\quad(v\notin\mathcal B).
 \tag{8}
\]
There are at most \(2^{N_V}\le2^h\) such branches, and reachability decides (6) without division.

We next describe the finite guarded success lane. For observed-cell events \(E_1\subseteq E_0\subseteq\mathcal A_V\), put
\[
 P_\theta(E_j)=\sum_{v\in E_j}P_\theta(v),
 \qquad j\in\{0,1\}.
 \tag{9}
\]
There are at most \(3^{N_V}\le3^h\) ordered pairs \((E_1,E_0)\). If \(E_0\cap\mathcal B\ne\varnothing\), (7)--(8) entail
\[
 P_\theta(E_0)=\sum_{v\in E_0}p(v)>0,
 \tag{10}
\]
so this exact guard licenses division. Parameterize every nonfunctional row of \(\Theta_{f,S}\) by all but one of its supported probabilities and substitute the last probability as one minus their sum. Equations (2), (3), and (9) then become rational-coefficient polynomials in at most \(K\) free coordinates. For a target coordinate \(\ell=(t,y)\), the assertion
\[
 Q_\theta(\ell)
 =\frac{P_\theta(E_1)}{P_\theta(E_0)}
 \tag{11}
\]
on that stratum is equivalent, by the positive denominator (10), to the polynomial identity
\[
 Q_\theta(\ell)P_\theta(E_0)-P_\theta(E_1)\equiv0.
 \tag{12}
\]
Identity (12) is decided by exact coefficient comparison, not by quantifier elimination. If it holds for every retained stratum having observable support \(\mathcal B\), then (2)--(3) prove (11) for every model in that observed-law branch. Choosing one such pair for every \(\ell\) gives a complete rational leaf for \(q\), and (10) licenses every denominator mechanically. In addition, when \(W=\varnothing\), \(\mathcal B=\mathcal A_V\), and classical \(\mathsf{ID}\) succeeds, Proposition \(\mathrm{prop:classical\text{-}id\text{-}embedding}\) supplies its finite guarded rational leaf. Memoizing the classical recursion over pairs of vertex subsets uses at most \(4^d\) states, hence at most \(O(4^d(d+m+h))\) elementary graph and circuit operations. If all feasible branches receive complete leaves, (8), (10)--(12), and the cited proposition give the certificate \(C\) in output (i).

It remains to scan the unresolved strata for an exact support-hole witness. Fix a row \(\rho=(i,u)\) for which the parent event \(\{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)=u\}\) is observationally unreachable according to Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:support\text{-}propagation}\). For states \(a,b\in\mathcal A_i\) and a target coordinate \(\ell\), leave all other rows free in the preceding simplex coordinates and form
\[
 D_{\rho,a,b,\ell}(x)
 :=Q_{\theta(x,\rho=e_a)}(\ell)
   -Q_{\theta(x,\rho=e_b)}(\ell),
 \tag{13}
\]
where \(e_a,e_b\) are simplex vertices. This is an explicitly computable rational-coefficient polynomial of degree at most \(d\). If it is not the zero polynomial, set
\[
 L=h(d+1)+1,
 \qquad
 \mathcal G=\left\{\frac1L,\frac2L,\ldots,\frac{d+1}{L}\right\}.
 \tag{14}
\]
Every Cartesian grid point in \(\mathcal G^K\), restricted to the actually present free coordinates, lies in the relevant product of open simplexes: a row has at most \(h-1\) free coordinates and therefore their sum is at most \((h-1)(d+1)/L<1\). A nonzero polynomial having degree at most \(d\) in each coordinate cannot vanish on all of \(\mathcal G^K\). Indeed, induction on the number of coordinates reduces this assertion to the elementary fact that a nonzero univariate polynomial of degree at most \(d\) has at most \(d\) roots. Hence exact grid evaluation finds \(x^\star\) with
\[
 D_{\rho,a,b,\ell}(x^\star)\ne0.
 \tag{15}
\]
The parent event of \(\rho\) has probability zero, so changing only that row leaves every monomial contributing to (2), and hence the entire observed law, unchanged. Joint-treatment positivity is therefore unchanged as well. Moreover, (3) does not contain a row at an intervened vertex; hence (15) implies \(i\notin T\), as required by the support-hole lemma. Equation (15) and the truncated product (3) show that the two targets differ. Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:support\text{-}hole\text{-}separator}\) now gives two original-alphabet models in \(\mathfrak M\), and the construction (14) makes every unchanged nonfunctional entry rational while \(e_a,e_b\) are rational vertices. Thus output (ii) is an exact rational \(\mathsf{Sep}\), not merely an existence assertion.

Suppose the scan finds no nonzero polynomial (13). The truncated product is affine in the entries of any one row, because a product term contains exactly one entry from that row. Consequently, equality of (13) for every pair of simplex vertices implies, coordinate by coordinate, that \(q\) is constant over the whole row simplex. Observational unreachability already makes \(p\) constant over that row. For a nonfunctional row we may therefore replace it by the uniform distribution on its existing support \(S_{i,u}\); this remains in the same support stratum. A functional row is already a fixed singleton in its map stratum and requires no replacement. Repeating this operation within each stratum yields semialgebraic strata \(\overline\Phi_s\) in which every row that remains free has a strictly positive observational parent event. This is the asserted positive-support qualification; unreachable rows have not been assumed positive but have been fixed only after their target-inertness was proved.

Let \(\mathcal U\) be the canonical unresolved strata whose observable branches did not receive a complete success leaf. The displayed formula \(\Psi_{\mathcal U}(p)\) is obtained by inserting two copies of (2)--(3), two canonical stratum formulas, equality to the same observed-law coordinates, and a finite disjunction of target-coordinate inequalities. Therefore
\[
 \Psi_{\mathcal U}(p)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists M_0,M_1\text{ represented in unresolved strata such that }
 p_{M_0}=p_{M_1}=p,
 \ q_{M_0}\ne q_{M_1}.
 \tag{16}
\]
The forward implication reads the witnesses from the formula. For the reverse implication, canonicalize every unreachable target-inert row in both models; the preceding argument preserves both laws and both targets and places the resulting pair in two members of \(\mathcal U\). This proves (16). If (16) is later shown satisfiable, then \(\mathcal S_{\mathrm{sep}}\ne\varnothing\), and Lemma \(\mathrm{lem:real\text{-}algebraic\text{-}separator}\) supplies an exactly encodable real-algebraic pair. The front end itself only emits (16) and makes neither the positive nor the negative identification inference.

We verify the quantitative claims. A polynomial in (12) is a product or difference of network polynomials with at most \(h\) assignment monomials and degree at most \(2d\); after the simplex substitution it has at most the coarse expansion factor \(4^d\). Using (1), (5), at most \(2^h\) observable branches, and at most \(3^h\) event pairs, all coefficient tests cost at most
\[
 O\!\left(BJ_T(d+m)h^3 4^d6^h\right).
 \tag{17}
\]
There are at most \(R\) rows, at most \(h^2\) ordered state pairs per row, and \(N_q\le J_Th\) target coordinates. Evaluating (13) on at most \((d+1)^K\) grid points costs at most
\[
 O\!\left(BJ_TR(d+m)h^4(d+1)^K\right).
 \tag{18}
\]
For every vertex \(i\), both \(r_i\le h\) and \(|\mathcal A_i|r_i\le h\), because the corresponding products range over subsets of the factors defining \(h\). Hence
\[
 R\le K\le dh.
 \tag{19}
\]
Combining (4), (17)--(19), and the memoized classical lane gives the first two lines of \(\mathcal T\) and their advertised bound in the declared parameters.

Finally, \(\Psi_{\mathcal U}\) has two copies of at most \(K\) raw CPT variables and at most \(N_V\le h\) free coordinates of \(p\). There are at most \(B^2\) stratum pairs. Within one pair, two row-normalization and support/canonical blocks use at most \(2R+4K\) atoms, two joint-positivity blocks use \(2J_T\), equality of each model's observed law to \(p\) uses at most \(2h\), and writing every nonzero target difference as the disjunction of its positive and negative signs uses at most \(2J_Th\). This proves the stated atom count. Equations (2)--(3) have degree at most \(d\). Their uncollected network coefficients are \(0,1\), or \(-1\); collecting assignment terms gives magnitude at most \(2h\). Clearing the denominator of a canonical uniform-row equation introduces an integer coefficient no larger than \(|S_{i,u}|\le h\). Thus every coefficient has bit height at most \(\lceil\log_2(2h+1)\rceil\). Encoding each network atom by at most \(h\) monomials of length at most \(d\) gives the third line of \(\mathcal T\), and all other returned objects have no larger encoding than the work used to construct them. This proves termination, soundness of every decisive output, exactness of the residual characterization, and the claimed elementary output bound. It deliberately does not prove the missing universal fixed-alphabet dichotomy.
