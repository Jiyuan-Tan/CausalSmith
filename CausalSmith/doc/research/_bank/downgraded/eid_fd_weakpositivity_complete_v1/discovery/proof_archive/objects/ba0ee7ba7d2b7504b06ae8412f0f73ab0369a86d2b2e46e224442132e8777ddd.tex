Let \(\phi:D_\phi\subseteq\mathbb R^k\to\mathbb R^m\) be differentiable at
\(\vartheta\), let \(T_N\) take values in \(D_\phi\), and let
\(r_N\to\infty\).  If \(r_N(T_N-\vartheta)\Rightarrow T\), then
\[
r_N\bigl(\phi(T_N)-\phi(\vartheta)\bigr)
\Rightarrow D\phi(\vartheta)T,
\]
and the difference between the left side and
\(D\phi(\vartheta)r_N(T_N-\vartheta)\) converges to zero in probability.
