Let \(D=\{Z,A,X,Y\}\), let \(\mathcal A_Z,\mathcal A_A,\mathcal A_X,\mathcal A_Y\) be arbitrary fixed finite nonempty alphabets, and let \(G\) have precisely the edges \(Z\to A\), \(A\to X\), \(A\to Y\), and \(X\to Y\).  Set \(V=\{Z,X,Y\}\), \(H=\{A\}\), \(W=\{A\}\), \(T=\{Z,X\}\), and take the outcome set to be \(\{Y\}\).  For every \(M\in\mathfrak M(G,\mathcal A,V,W,T)\), every \(z\in\mathcal A_Z\), every \(x\in\mathcal A_X\), and every \(y\in\mathcal A_Y\), joint-treatment positivity entails \(p_M(z,x)>0\), and
\[
 P_M(Y=y\mid do(Z=z,X=x))
 =\frac{p_M(z,x,y)}{p_M(z,x)}
 =p_M(Y=y\mid Z=z,X=x).
\]
Thus the complete joint-intervention distribution is graphwide identified by a guarded conditional-probability circuit for arbitrary fixed finite alphabets.
