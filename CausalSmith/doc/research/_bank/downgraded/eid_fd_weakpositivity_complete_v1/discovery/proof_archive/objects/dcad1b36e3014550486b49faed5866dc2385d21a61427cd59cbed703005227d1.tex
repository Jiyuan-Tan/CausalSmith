Fix a row-support pattern \(S\).  By its definition,
\[
 \theta_i(a\mid u)>0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 a\in S_{i,u}.
\]
Let \(B\subseteq D\) and fix a cell \(B=b\).  Causal factorization expands its observational probability as
\[
 P_M(B=b)
 =\sum_{a_{D\setminus B}}
   \prod_{i\in D}
   \theta_i(a_i\mid a_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)}),
\]
where the sum ranges over complete assignments extending \(b\).  Each product is nonnegative.  A particular product is strictly positive if and only if every one of its finitely many factors is strictly positive, which, by the displayed support equivalence, holds if and only if
\[
 a_i\in S_{i,a_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)}}
 \quad\text{for every }i\in D.
\]
A finite sum of nonnegative terms is positive if and only if at least one term is positive.  This proves the observational assertion.

For an intervention \(do(T=t)\), truncated intervention semantics gives, for any cell \(B=b\) disjoint from the fixed intervention coordinates,
\[
 P_M(B=b\mid do(T=t))
 =\sum_{a_{D\setminus(T\cup B)}}
   \prod_{i\in D\setminus T}
   \theta_i(a_i\mid a_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)}),
\]
where all summands extend both \(a_T=t\) and \(a_B=b\).  The identical nonnegative-sum argument shows that this cell is positive exactly when one compatible complete assignment uses only supported entries in the truncated product.  No division or limiting argument is used.

For row \((i,u)\), there are \(2^{|\mathcal A_i|}-1\) possible nonempty supports, and functionality restricts a declared functional row to singleton supports.  Ignoring that restriction gives the upper bound
\[
 \prod_{i\in D}
 \left(2^{|\mathcal A_i|}-1\right)^{r_i}
 \le
 \prod_{i\in D}2^{|\mathcal A_i|r_i}
 =2^{\sum_{i\in D}|\mathcal A_i|r_i}
 =2^K.
\]
For a specified observational or interventional cell and a specified \(S\), enumerate the at most \(h\) compatible complete assignments and test the finitely many indicated membership relations.  The preceding equivalences show that this reachability test decides the positivity guard exactly.  Enumerating the at most \(2^K\) strata also decides whether such a guard is entailed on any declared finite collection of compatible support strata.
