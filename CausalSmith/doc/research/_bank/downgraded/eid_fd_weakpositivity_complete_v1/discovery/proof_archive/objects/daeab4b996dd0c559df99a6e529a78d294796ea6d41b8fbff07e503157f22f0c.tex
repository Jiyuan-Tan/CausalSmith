Fix \(M=(G,\mathcal A,\theta)\in\mathfrak M(G,\mathcal A,V,W,T)\).  Every sum below is finite because every alphabet in \(\mathcal A\) is finite.  Since \(W=\{A\}\), Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:qualitative\text{-}functionality}\) says that, for each \(z\in\mathcal A_Z\), the probability row \(\theta_A(\cdot\mid z)\) is a point mass on the nonempty finite set \(\mathcal A_A\).  Consequently there is a uniquely determined map \(f:\mathcal A_Z\to\mathcal A_A\) such that
\[
 \theta_A(a\mid z)=\mathbf 1\{a=f(z)\}
 \qquad
 (a\in\mathcal A_A,\ z\in\mathcal A_Z).
\]

The stipulated edge set gives
\[
 \operatorname{pa}_G(Z)=\varnothing,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{pa}_G(A)=\{Z\},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{pa}_G(X)=\{A\},
 \qquad
 \operatorname{pa}_G(Y)=\{A,X\}.
\]
Thus Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:causal\text{-}factorization}\) and the preceding point-mass representation imply, for every \((z,a,x,y)\in\mathcal A_Z\times\mathcal A_A\times\mathcal A_X\times\mathcal A_Y\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P_M(z,a,x,y)
 &=\theta_Z(z)\theta_A(a\mid z)\theta_X(x\mid a)\theta_Y(y\mid a,x)\\
 &=\theta_Z(z)\mathbf 1\{a=f(z)\}\theta_X(x\mid a)\theta_Y(y\mid a,x).
\end{aligned}
\]
By the definition of the observed marginal, \(V=\{Z,X,Y\}\), and \(H=\{A\}\), summation over the hidden state gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 p_M(z,x,y)
 &=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A_A}P_M(z,a,x,y)\\
 &=\theta_Z(z)\theta_X(x\mid f(z))\theta_Y(y\mid f(z),x).
\end{aligned}
\]
Summing this identity over \(y'\in\mathcal A_Y\), and using normalization of the conditional-probability row \(\theta_Y(\cdot\mid f(z),x)\), yields
\[
\begin{aligned}
 p_M(z,x)
 &=\sum_{y'\in\mathcal A_Y}p_M(z,x,y')\\
 &=\theta_Z(z)\theta_X(x\mid f(z))
   \sum_{y'\in\mathcal A_Y}\theta_Y(y'\mid f(z),x)\\
 &=\theta_Z(z)\theta_X(x\mid f(z)).
\end{aligned}
\]
Because \(T=\{Z,X\}\), Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:joint\text{-}treatment\text{-}positivity}\), applied to the joint treatment assignment \((z,x)\in\mathcal A_Z\times\mathcal A_X\), entails
\[
 p_M(z,x)=P_M(T=(z,x))>0.
\]
This is the strict support guard needed for the only division below.  Hence
\[
 \frac{p_M(z,x,y)}{p_M(z,x)}
 =\theta_Y(y\mid f(z),x)
 =p_M(Y=y\mid Z=z,X=x).
\]
The ratio is an observable-law functional; it has not been identified with the causal target by definition.

To establish that identification, apply Assumption \(\mathrm{ass:truncated\text{-}intervention}\).  Under \(do(Z=z,X=x)\), the rows at \(Z\) and \(X\) are replaced by point masses at \(z\) and \(x\), while the mechanisms at \(A\) and \(Y\) remain unchanged.  Marginalizing the remaining non-outcome vertex \(A\) therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 P_M(Y=y\mid do(Z=z,X=x))
 &=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A_A}\theta_A(a\mid z)\theta_Y(y\mid a,x)\\
 &=\sum_{a\in\mathcal A_A}\mathbf 1\{a=f(z)\}\theta_Y(y\mid a,x)\\
 &=\theta_Y(y\mid f(z),x).
\end{aligned}
\]
Combining the last two displays proves, for every \(z\in\mathcal A_Z\), \(x\in\mathcal A_X\), and \(y\in\mathcal A_Y\),
\[
 P_M(Y=y\mid do(Z=z,X=x))
 =\frac{p_M(z,x,y)}{p_M(z,x)}
 =p_M(Y=y\mid Z=z,X=x).
\]

Finally, let \(M,M'\in\mathfrak M(G,\mathcal A,V,W,T)\) have the same observed law \(p\).  The equality just proved, applied once to each model, gives
\[
 P_M(Y=y\mid do(Z=z,X=x))
 =\frac{p(z,x,y)}{p(z,x)}
 =P_{M'}(Y=y\mid do(Z=z,X=x))
\]
for every \(z,x,y\), where \(p(z,x)>0\) follows from joint-treatment positivity in either model.  Thus the complete joint-intervention distribution is constant on every observed-law fiber.  The map \(p\mapsto(p(z,x,y)/p(z,x))_{z,x,y}\) is therefore a graphwide identifying arithmetic circuit, and every one of its divisions is licensed by the mechanically entailed guard \(p(z,x)>0\).
