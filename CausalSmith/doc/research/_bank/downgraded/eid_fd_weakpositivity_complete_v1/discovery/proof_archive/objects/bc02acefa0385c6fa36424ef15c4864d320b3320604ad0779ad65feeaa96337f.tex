For an ordered quadruple \(\iota=(i_Z,i_A,i_X,i_Y)\) of distinct
vertices, \(\mathfrak M_{\mathrm{sep}}(G,\mathcal A,V,W,T,Y;\iota)\) is
the set of recursive models \(M=(G,\mathcal A,\theta)\) for which
\(D=\{i_Z,i_A,i_X,i_Y\}\), the edge set is
\(\{i_Z\to i_A,i_A\to i_X,i_A\to i_Y,i_X\to i_Y\}\),
\(V=\{i_Z,i_X,i_Y\}\), \(W=\{i_A\}\),
\(T=\{i_Z,i_X\}\), and \(Y=\{i_Y\}\); the observational law obeys the
recursive product factorization, interventions obey the truncated product
factorization, every row at \(i_A\) is a point mass, and
\[
 P_M(i_Z=z)>0\quad(z\in\mathcal A_{i_Z}),
 \qquad
 P_M(i_X=x)>0\quad(x\in\mathcal A_{i_X}).
\]
No condition \(P_M(i_Z=z,i_X=x)>0\) is imposed.
