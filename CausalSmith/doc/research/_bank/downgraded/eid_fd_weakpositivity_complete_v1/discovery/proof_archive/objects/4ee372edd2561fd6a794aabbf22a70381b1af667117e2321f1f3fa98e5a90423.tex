Fix the four-node notation \(\iota=(i_Z,i_A,i_X,i_Y)\), put
\(\mathcal Z=\mathcal A_{i_Z}\), \(\mathcal B=\mathcal A_{i_A}\),
\(\mathcal X=\mathcal A_{i_X}\), and
\(\mathcal Y=\mathcal A_{i_Y}\), and let \(p\) be a law on
\(\mathcal Z\times\mathcal X\times\mathcal Y\) with \(p(z)>0\) for every
\(z\).  Define, under the displayed positive denominators,
\[
 e_z(x)=\frac{p(z,x)}{p(z)},
 \qquad
 m_{zx}(y)=\frac{p(z,x,y)}{p(z,x)}\quad\text{when }p(z,x)>0,
\]
and
\[
 \rho_z=\bigl(e_z,(m_{zx})_{x:e_z(x)>0}\bigr),
 \qquad
 \mathcal R(p)=\{\rho_z:z\in\mathcal Z\}.
\]
For an integer \(b\geq1\), let \(\Pi_b(p)\) be the set of partitions
\(\pi\) of \(\mathcal Z\) with at most \(b\) nonempty blocks such that
every block is contained in one set \(\{z:\rho_z=\rho\}\).  For
\(\pi\in\Pi_b(p)\), let \(\mathcal K_\pi(p)\) be the set of arrays
\(k=(k(z,x,\cdot))_{z,x}\) of elements of \(\Delta(\mathcal Y)\) such
that, for every block \(C\in\pi\),
\[
 k(z,x,\cdot)=m_{zx}\quad\text{if }e_z(x)>0,
\]
and, if \(e_z(x)=0\) for \(z\in C\), there is one
\(s_{C,x}\in\Delta(\mathcal Y)\) satisfying
\[
 k(z,x,\cdot)=s_{C,x}\quad\text{for every }z\in C.
\]
The separate-positive response-kernel region is
\[
 \mathcal K(p)=
 \bigcup_{\pi\in\Pi_{|\mathcal B|}(p)}\mathcal K_\pi(p).
\]
