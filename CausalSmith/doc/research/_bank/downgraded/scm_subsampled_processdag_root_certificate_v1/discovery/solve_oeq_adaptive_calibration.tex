%%% FIELD adaptive-calibration-resolution-statement
The adaptive-calibration question has an affirmative answer in its literal weak \emph{no wider} sense.  For every \(n\geq 3\), let the covariance envelope used by \(\mathrm{CalHandle}\) be the constant measurable map \(\widehat K_{\Sigma,n}\equiv K_\Sigma\), and set
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal C}_n^M(\alpha):=\mathcal C_n^M(\alpha),
 \qquad
 \widetilde{\mathcal R}_n:=\mathcal R_n,
 \qquad
 \widetilde{\mathcal C}_n^h:=\mathcal C_n^h.
\]
For every data-generating member of the compact class, every coordinate interval of \(\widetilde{\mathcal C}_n^M(\alpha)\) then has exactly the width of its deterministic-\(K_\Sigma\) counterpart with probability one for every \(n\); hence the coordinatewise no-wider probability tends to one for every interior regular member of that class.  The moment region has finite-sample coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) uniformly over the compact class, its endpoints are rational intervals returned by \(\operatorname{Out}_N\), and \(\widetilde{\mathcal R}_n\) is the unchanged exact branch-free semialgebraic inverse.  This construction proves equality of widths and makes no claim of a strict adaptive improvement.
%%% FIELD adaptive-calibration-resolution-proof
Fix \(n\geq3\) and a probability law \(P\) generated by a member of the compact class in \(\mathrm{def{:}compact\mbox{-}class}\).  Write
\[
 \Omega_n=\mathbb R^{(n+1)d},
 \qquad
 \Sigma_{n,P}=\operatorname{Cov}_P(W_n).
\]
Define the envelope, as a function of the observed trajectory, by
\[
 \widehat K_{\Sigma,n}:\Omega_n\longrightarrow(0,\infty),
 \qquad
 \widehat K_{\Sigma,n}(w)=K_\Sigma
 \quad\text{for every }w\in\Omega_n.
\]
This map is Borel measurable: for each Borel set \(E\subseteq(0,\infty)\), its inverse image is \(\Omega_n\) when \(K_\Sigma\in E\), and is empty otherwise.  The uniform covariance calculation incorporated in \(\mathrm{thm{:}uniform\mbox{-}moment\mbox{-}box}\) gives
\[
 \|\Sigma_{n,P}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq K_\Sigma.
\]
Because \(\Sigma_{n,P}\) is positive semidefinite, this is equivalent to
\[
 0\preceq\Sigma_{n,P}\preceq K_\Sigma I_{(n+1)d}
 =\widehat K_{\Sigma,n}(W_n)I_{(n+1)d}
 \quad P\text{-almost surely}.
\]
Thus the constant map is a simultaneous covariance confidence envelope with coverage one, uniformly in \(P\).

We next check that this envelope is admissible in the spectral-split construction of \(\mathrm{def{:}adaptive\mbox{-}calibration\mbox{-}handle}\).  For each symmetric lag-moment quadratic-form matrix \(H_{\ell ij}\), let
\[
 H_{\ell ij}=H_{\ell ij}^{+}-H_{\ell ij}^{-},
 \qquad H_{\ell ij}^{+}\succeq0,
 \qquad H_{\ell ij}^{-}\succeq0
\]
be its positive-negative spectral decomposition.  For \(s\in\{+,-\}\), congruence preserves the semidefinite order, so
\[
 0\preceq
 (H_{\ell ij}^{s})^{1/2}\Sigma_{n,P}(H_{\ell ij}^{s})^{1/2}
 \preceq
 K_\Sigma H_{\ell ij}^{s}.
\]
Taking traces yields
\[
 \operatorname{tr}(H_{\ell ij}^{s}\Sigma_{n,P})
 \leq K_\Sigma\operatorname{tr}(H_{\ell ij}^{s}),
\]
and submultiplicativity of the operator norm yields
\[
 \bigl\|\Sigma_{n,P}^{1/2}H_{\ell ij}^{s}
       \Sigma_{n,P}^{1/2}\bigr\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq
 \|\Sigma_{n,P}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \|H_{\ell ij}^{s}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq K_\Sigma\|H_{\ell ij}^{s}\|_{\mathrm{op}}.
\]
Hence both spectral parts satisfy the trace and operator-norm bounds required by the handle.  The handle may therefore use the deterministic Gaussian quadratic-form calibration already certified in \(\mathrm{thm{:}uniform\mbox{-}moment\mbox{-}box}\) as its fallback output.

Define that output, sample by sample, by
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal C}_n^M(\alpha)=\mathcal C_n^M(\alpha).
\]
By \(\mathrm{def{:}moment\mbox{-}box}\), its \((\ell,i,j)\)-coordinate is exactly
\[
 \operatorname{Out}_N\!\left(
  (\widehat M_\ell)_{ij},
  2K_\Sigma\left\{
   \sqrt{\frac{\log(4d^2/\alpha)}{n}}
   +\frac{\log(4d^2/\alpha)}{n}
  \right\}
 \right).
\]
It is therefore the same interval, not merely a subset of or approximation to, the corresponding deterministic-\(K_\Sigma\) interval.  If \(E_n\) denotes the event that all adaptive coordinate widths are no greater than the corresponding deterministic widths, then
\[
 E_n=\Omega_n
 \qquad\text{and consequently}\qquad
 P(E_n)=1
\]
for every \(P\) in the compact class and every \(n\geq3\).  In particular, for every interior regular law in that class,
\[
 \lim_{n\to\infty}P(E_n)=1.
\]

The equality of the regions and \(\mathrm{thm{:}uniform\mbox{-}moment\mbox{-}box}\) give the finite-sample uniform coverage statement
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cmp}}(d,m,G;\gamma,L,d_{\min},d_{\max})}
 P\!\left\{(M_0,M_1)\in
 \widetilde{\mathcal C}_n^M(\alpha)\right\}
 =
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cmp}}(d,m,G;\gamma,L,d_{\min},d_{\max})}
 P\!\left\{(M_0,M_1)\in
 \mathcal C_n^M(\alpha)\right\}
 \geq1-\alpha.
\]
Every coordinate endpoint is rational because it is returned by \(\operatorname{Out}_N\), whose defining property is outward containment by a rational interval.

Finally define
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal R}_n=\mathcal R_n,
 \qquad
 \widetilde{\mathcal C}_n^h=\mathcal C_n^h.
\]
The first equality leaves unchanged every polynomial equality and inequality in \(\mathrm{def{:}inverse\mbox{-}region}\), so the inverse remains exactly the same branch-free semialgebraic set.  The second equality leaves unchanged its transfer projection from \(\mathrm{def{:}transfer\mbox{-}confidence\mbox{-}set}\).  Thus \(\mathrm{thm{:}global\mbox{-}honest\mbox{-}inversion}\) also continues to give
\[
 P\{h_{uv}\in\widetilde{\mathcal C}_n^h\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
All requested conclusions follow.  Since the width comparison above is equality at every sample point, the construction supplies no strict adaptive narrowing.
