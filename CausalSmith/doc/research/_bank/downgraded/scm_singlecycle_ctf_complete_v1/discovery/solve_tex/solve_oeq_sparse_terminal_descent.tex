%%% FIELD rational-descent-fails-statement
For a well-typed explicit input \(I\), write
\[
 \mathfrak M(I):=\{\mathcal M_\theta:\theta\in\Theta_{\mathcal L}\}
\]
for the instance-relative family obtained by holding fixed the displayed finite source alphabets, source decoder, structural tables, graph bound, source labels, and source incidence in \(I\), and varying only the independent source masses \(\theta\).  There exists a well-typed instance \(I_\star\) with \(V=\{W,Y\}\), \(\mathcal X_W=\{0,1,2\}\), \(\mathcal X_Y=\{0,1\}\), permitted graph \(W\to Y\to W\), and three independent private binary sources \(s_1,s_2,s_3\), each incident only to \(Y\), having the following properties.  If \(x,y,z\) are the three source probabilities, the supplied complete observational and intervention laws contain the coordinates
\[
 a=xy,\qquad b=xz,\qquad c=yz,
\]
and, for
\[
 A=\{Y_{\operatorname{do}(W=0)}=1\},\qquad
 B=\{Y_{\operatorname{do}(W=2)}=1\},
\]
one has
\[
 H=xyz,\qquad K=yz=c,
 \qquad q=\frac{H}{K}=x=\sqrt{\frac{ab}{c}}
 \quad\text{on }\{c>0\}.
\]
Thus \(q\) is uniformly identified relative to \(\mathfrak M(I_\star)\), and its graph is the positive semialgebraic branch
\[
 c t^2=ab,\qquad t\geq0,
\]
but no rational arithmetic circuit in the supplied regime probabilities represents \(q\) on the full positive feasible image.  On the corresponding explicit legal terminal, the two-copy separator is unsatisfiable and rational function-field membership fails, so \(\mathsf{SATerm}\) emits \(O\).  More generally, no total sound procedure on the declared explicit source-decoder input class can be complete if every positive output must be a rational arithmetic circuit and every negative output must be two genuine positive-denominator countermodels from the same family \(\mathfrak M(I)\).  A third outcome, such as \(O\), is unavoidable.  This settles rational terminal descent negatively; it does not settle universal nonvanishing transmission of a locally separating pair through every recursive boundary interface.
%%% FIELD rational-descent-fails-proof
For clarity, the family in the statement is instance-relative.  Given a fixed explicit input \(I\), each \(\theta\in\Theta_{\mathcal L}\) supplies masses to the source states listed in \(I\), and \(\mathcal M_\theta\) is the SCM obtained from the fixed decoder and those masses.  Thus
\[
 \mathfrak M(I)=\{\mathcal M_\theta:\theta\in\Theta_{\mathcal L}\}
\]
does not range over alternative response decoders or alternative source alphabets compatible merely with the graph bound \(G\).

We construct \(I_\star\).  Let \(V=\{W,Y\}\), ordered as written, with
\[
 \mathcal X_W=\{0,1,2\},\qquad \mathcal X_Y=\{0,1\}.
\]
Let \(G\) contain the two permitted directed edges \(W\to Y\) and \(Y\to W\), and no bidirected edge.  Its sole nontrivial directed strongly connected component is the induced chordless two-cycle, and the equivalence relation generated by directed-SCC membership and bidirected adjacency has one class containing exactly that cycle.  Hence \(\text{ass:chordless-cycle-scc}\) and \(\text{ass:one-cycle-per-district}\) hold.

Take three sources \(S=\{s_1,s_2,s_3\}\), each with alphabet \(\{0,1\}\), and set
\[
 \operatorname{inc}(s_i)=\{Y\},\qquad i\in\{1,2,3\}.
\]
Write their states as \(U_1,U_2,U_3\), give them the product distribution required by \(\text{ass:source-independence}\), and put
\[
 x=P(U_1=1),\qquad y=P(U_2=1),\qquad z=P(U_3=1).
\]
Every source has one child, so \(1\leq |\operatorname{inc}(s_i)|\leq2\), verifying \(\text{ass:sparse-source-incidence}\).  Define the fixed structural decoder by
\[
 W=0,
 \qquad
 Y=
 \begin{cases}
 U_1U_2,&W=0,\\
 U_1U_3,&W=1,\\
 U_2U_3,&W=2.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{1}
\]
Here multiplication is ordinary multiplication of binary variables.  Each source affects the displayed table of \(Y\), so the declared private-source incidence is retained.  The graph is only an upper bound in \(\text{def:model-class}\); accordingly, the equation for \(W\) may ignore its permitted parent \(Y\).  This inactivity will also show that feedback is not load-bearing for the obstruction.

We check subset unique solvability rather than inferring it from the graph.  Fix a source tuple.  If both endogenous equations are retained, the first gives the unique value \(W=0\), after which the first branch of (1) gives a unique value of \(Y\).  If only the \(W\)-equation is retained, it again has the unique solution \(W=0\), for either boundary value of \(Y\).  If only the \(Y\)-equation is retained, each of the three possible boundary values of \(W\) selects exactly one branch of (1).  If neither equation is retained, the retained system has the unique empty assignment.  These are all subsets of \(V\).  Thus \(\text{ass:subset-unique-solvability}\) holds with empty exceptional set.  By \(\text{def:legal-profiles}\), every one of the eight decoded source tuples is legal, and therefore
\[
 \Theta_{\mathcal L}=[0,1]^3.
\]
All alphabets, source tables, response tables, and probability polynomials are finite and have integer entries, so the resulting \(I_\star\) is a well-typed explicit legal input.

Supply the three complete regimes
\[
 \mathcal R=\bigl\{(\varnothing,\varnothing),
 (\{W\},1),(\{W\},2)\bigr\}.
\]
Under observation, \(W=0\) surely and (1) gives
\[
 P(W=0,Y=1)=xy=:a,
 \qquad P(W=0,Y=0)=1-a,
\]
with every cell having \(W\neq0\) equal to zero.  Under the two interventions,
\[
 P(Y=1\mid\operatorname{do}(W=1))=xz=:b,
 \qquad
 P(Y=1\mid\operatorname{do}(W=2))=yz=:c,
\]
and the complementary cells are \(1-b\) and \(1-c\).  Hence every coordinate of \(p=\Phi(x,y,z)\) lies in \(\mathbb Q[a,b,c]\), while \(a,b,c\) themselves occur among the supplied complete-law coordinates.

For the unnested events in the statement, (1) gives
\[
 A=\{U_1U_2=1\},\qquad B=\{U_2U_3=1\}.
\]
Their intersection is \(\{U_1=U_2=U_3=1\}\).  Source independence therefore yields, directly from the causal SCM rather than by definition of an observed functional,
\[
 H(x,y,z)=P(A\cap B)=xyz,
 \qquad
 K(x,y,z)=P(B)=yz=c.
 \tag{2}
\]
On the positive-denominator domain \(K=c>0\), division in the definition of \(q\) is valid, and (2) gives \(q=x\).

We next prove instance-relative uniform identification.  Let \(\theta=(x,y,z)\) and \(\theta'=(x',y',z')\) lie in one fiber \(\mathcal F_+(p)\).  Equality of the complete supplied laws implies equality of their three displayed coordinates:
\[
 xy=x'y'=a,\qquad xz=x'z'=b,
 \qquad yz=y'z'=c.
\]
The definition of \(\mathcal F_+(p)\) and (2) give \(c>0\).  Therefore
\[
 x^2=\frac{(xy)(xz)}{yz}=\frac{ab}{c}
 =\frac{(x'y')(x'z')}{y'z'}=(x')^2.
 \tag{3}
\]
Both \(x\) and \(x'\) are probabilities and hence nonnegative, so (3) implies \(x=x'\).  Using (2),
\[
 H(\theta)K(\theta')=x c^2=x'c^2
 =H(\theta')K(\theta).
\]
Thus the conditional target is constant on every positive source-realizable fiber.  The same calculation identifies it explicitly as the unique \(t\geq0\) satisfying
\[
 c t^2=ab,
\]
namely \(t=\sqrt{ab/c}\).  The boundary case \(x=0\) is included: then \(a=b=0\), while the only division in (3) remains legitimate because \(c>0\).

We prove that this identified map is not rational.  Suppose that a rational arithmetic circuit \(C\) in all coordinates of \(p\) represents \(q\) on the full positive feasible image.  Substitute the complete-law coordinates computed above into the circuit.  Since each such coordinate belongs to \(\mathbb Q[a,b,c]\), flattening the finite circuit gives a rational function \(R(a,b,c)\in\mathbb Q(a,b,c)\).  The circuit's verified divisor condition makes the substitution defined at every point arising from \((x,y,z)\in(0,1)^3\).  Hence
\[
 R(xy,xz,yz)=x
 \qquad\text{for every }(x,y,z)\in(0,1)^3.
 \tag{4}
\]
Write the flattened circuit as \(R=P/Q\), where \(P,Q\in\mathbb Q[a,b,c]\) and the substituted circuit denominator is nonzero on this open cube.  Cross-multiplication in (4) gives
\[
 P(xy,xz,yz)-xQ(xy,xz,yz)=0
\]
throughout that cube.  A polynomial over \(\mathbb Q\) vanishing on an open box is the zero polynomial: for one variable this follows because a nonzero polynomial has only finitely many roots, and induction applies after fixing the remaining coordinates in open intervals.  Thus (4) is an identity in \(\mathbb Q(x,y,z)\).

The substitution homomorphism
\[
 \mathbb Q[a,b,c]\longrightarrow\mathbb Q[x,y,z],
 \qquad (a,b,c)\longmapsto(xy,xz,yz),
\]
is injective.  Indeed, the monomial \(a^i b^j c^k\) maps to the monomial having exponent vector
\[
 (i+j,i+k,j+k),
\]
and one recovers
\[
 i=\frac{(i+j)+(i+k)-(j+k)}2,
 \quad
 j=\frac{(i+j)+(j+k)-(i+k)}2,
 \quad
 k=\frac{(i+k)+(j+k)-(i+j)}2.
\]
Distinct source monomials therefore have distinct images.  The map extends to an embedding \(\mathbb Q(a,b,c)\hookrightarrow\mathbb Q(x,y,z)\).

Now apply the field automorphism
\[
 \sigma:\mathbb Q(x,y,z)\longrightarrow\mathbb Q(x,y,z),
 \qquad \sigma(x,y,z)=(-x,-y,-z).
\]
It fixes \(xy,xz,yz\), hence it fixes the embedded copy of \(\mathbb Q(a,b,c)\) pointwise.  Identity (4) would put \(x\) in that fixed subfield.  Applying \(\sigma\) would then give both \(\sigma(x)=x\) and \(\sigma(x)=-x\), an impossibility for the nonzero indeterminate \(x\) over a field of characteristic zero.  This contradiction proves that no rational arithmetic circuit \(C\) represents the uniformly identified target.

The displayed finite decoder, legal set, empty exterior interface, and polynomials \(\Phi,H,K\) form an explicit source-aware terminal input.  The two-copy separator tested by \(\mathsf{SATerm}\) is unsatisfiable there, because the fiber calculation proves that any two legal positive-denominator points with the same \(\Phi\)-value have zero target cross-product.  On the other hand, the preceding automorphism argument proves that function-field membership in \(\mathbb Q(p)\) fails on its positive component.  By \(\text{def:source-terminal-elimination}\), this is exactly the branch on which \(\mathsf{SATerm}\) emits an obstruction record \(O\).  Thus the claim that \(O\) is impossible at every legal source-factorized terminal is false.

Finally let \(\mathcal A\) be any purported total sound procedure on the same explicit input class whose only admissible outputs are a rational identifying circuit or a genuine pair of same-instance positive-denominator countermodels.  Run \(\mathcal A\) on \(I_\star\).  A positive output contradicts nonrationality, while a negative output contradicts fiber constancy.  Thus \(\mathcal A\) has no sound admissible output on \(I_\star\), contradicting totality and completeness.  This argument depends only on the required output semantics, not on the transition rules of SC-CTFID.

The quantifiers in this conclusion are essential.  They range over \(\mathfrak M(I_\star)\), whose decoder is fixed, not over every mechanism merely bounded by \(G\).  Identification on this restricted family does not imply identification on the larger graph-compatible SCM class.  Nor does the construction refute published acyclic CTFID completeness, which concerns a different model class and input semantics.  Indeed the permitted edge \(Y\to W\) is inactive in (1), so the same algebraic obstruction survives after deleting it: cyclic feedback is not load-bearing in this witness.  Finally, although the single value here has a quantifier-free semialgebraic graph, no general completeness theorem for semialgebraic, algebraic-circuit, piecewise-branch, or Thom-root outputs has been proved.
%%% FIELD universal-boundary-transmission-statement
Consider a terminal reached by the deterministic recursion with two legal source-mass points \(\theta^-\) and \(\theta^+\) from the same explicit source-decoder instance, satisfying every stored boundary primitive-event equality,
\[
 \Phi(\theta^-)=\Phi(\theta^+),\qquad
 K(\theta^-)K(\theta^+)>0,
\]
and having nonzero local cross-product
\[
 \Delta=H(\theta^-)K(\theta^+)-H(\theta^+)K(\theta^-)\ne0.
\]
Must there always exist exterior source masses using only the fixed exterior source alphabets and decoder of that same explicit instance, together with a boundary assignment of positive transmitting weight, such that recursive gluing yields two global same-alphabet, same-source-incidence models agreeing on every supplied law but having unequal conditional targets?  If not, characterize the stored interfaces for which transmission is possible and give a finite source-preserving certificate that every legal exterior attachment annihilates the local contrast.
%%% FIELD universal-boundary-transmission-partial
The proved conditional gluing lemma establishes the following implication: once a certificate contains a boundary assignment with nonzero transmitting weight, holding the exterior mechanisms and sources fixed preserves every supplied-law coordinate and carries a nonzero local target cross-product to unequal global conditional targets.  The missing step is existence of such an assignment for every locally separating pair.  The recursion state records complete boundary primitive-event equalities, but no frozen invariant bounds a transmitting coefficient away from zero or rules out cancellation over all legal exterior attachments.  The rational-descent issue is no longer part of this question: the algebraic-branch theorem answers that issue negatively.
