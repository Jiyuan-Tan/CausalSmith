## Definitions (P)

**P-1 (For each connected component of \(G_n\), take a positive…).** For each connected component of \(G_n\), take a positive unit-norm Perron eigenvector of the component block of \(\mathcal H_n\), assign ranks by decreasing coordinate with label ties, and concatenate components by their smallest label. **P-2 (Independently for \(i\in\mathcal I_n\), draw…).** Independently for \(i\in\mathcal I_n\), draw \(U_{n,i}=1\) and \(U_{n,i}=0\) with probability \((4\lambda_n)^{-1}\) each and draw \(U_{n,i}=*\) with probability \(t_n=1-(2\lambda_n)^{-1}\); set \(E_{n,i,k}=\{U_{n,i}=k,\ U_{n,j}=*\text{ for all }j\in B_{n,i}\}\) and \(q_{n,i}=(4\lambda_n)^{-1}t_n^{|B_{n,i}|}\).

## Theorems (T)

### T-block: t1 — For each fixed \(B\ge1\) and \(0<c\le1\), the constant…
**Statement.** For each fixed \(B\ge1\) and \(0<c\le1\), the constant \(C_{\mathrm N}(B,c)<\infty\) may be chosen so that every member of \(\mathcal M_n(d;B,c)\) satisfies \[d_{\mathrm K}((T_n-\tau_n)/\sigma_n,Z_0)\le\min\left\{1,C_{\mathrm N}(B,c)\left(\frac{D_n^2}{n\lambda_n}+\frac{D_n}{n}\right)^{1/5}\right\}\le\min\left\{1,C_{\mathrm N}(B,c)\left(\frac{2D_n^{3/2}}n\right)^{1/5}\right\}.\] Consequently \(K_n(d;B,c)\le\min\{1,C_{\mathrm N}(B,c)(2d^{3/2}/n)^{1/5}\}\).

### T-block: t2 — For every fixed \(\beta>0\), the construction in…
**Statement.** For every fixed \(\beta>0\), the construction in \(\mathrm{def:repeated\text{-}star\text{-}witness}\) belongs to \(\mathcal M_n(k_n+1;B,c)\) eventually for every fixed \(B\ge1\) and \(0<c\le1\), has \(D_n/n^{2/3}\to\beta\), and satisfies \[(T_n-\tau_n)/\sigma_n\Rightarrow\mathcal L_{\beta}=\mathcal L\left(\sqrt{8/9}Z_0-\frac{N_{r_{\beta}}-r_{\beta}}{3\sqrt{r_{\beta}}}\right).\] The law \(\mathcal L_{\beta}\) has variance one and nonzero third cumulant \(-1/(27\sqrt{r_{\beta}})\), so it is not \(\mathcal N(0,1)\).

### T-block: t3 — For every fixed \(B\ge1\), every fixed \(0<c\le1\), and e…
**Statement.** For every fixed \(B\ge1\), every fixed \(0<c\le1\), and every integer sequence \(1\le d_n\le n\), \[K_n(d_n;B,c)\longrightarrow0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad d_n=o(n^{2/3}).\] No additional slowly varying boundary factor is present.

### T-block: t4 — The universal constant \(C_0<\infty\) may be chosen so th…
**Statement.** The universal constant \(C_0<\infty\) may be chosen so that every \((G_n,Y_n)\in\mathcal M_n(d;B,c)\) obeys \(\mathrm{VB}_n\ge\sigma_n^2>0\) and, for every \(\varepsilon>0\), \[\mathbb P_U\left(\left|\frac{\widehat{\mathrm{VB}}_n}{\mathrm{VB}_n}-1\right|>\varepsilon\right)\le\frac{C_0B\lambda_n}{c^2n\varepsilon^2}.\] Consequently the class supremum is at most \(C_0Bd/(c^2n\varepsilon^2)\), so the ratio converges to one uniformly whenever \(d_n=o(n)\), including the sharp normal regime.

**Statement.** For each fixed \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), the constant \(C_{\mathrm W}(B,c,\alpha)<\infty\) may be chosen so that \[\inf_{(G_n,Y_n)\in\mathcal M_n(d;B,c)}\mathbb P_U(\tau_n\in C_{n,\alpha})\ge1-\alpha-2K_n(d;B,c)-C_{\mathrm W}(B,c,\alpha)(d/n)^{1/3}.\] Hence, if \(d_n=o(n^{2/3})\), then \(\liminf_n\inf_{\mathcal M_n(d_n;B,c)}\mathbb P_U(\tau_n\in C_{n,\alpha})\ge1-\alpha\).

### T-block: t6 — If \(G_n\) is edgeless, then \(D_n=\lambda_n=1\), every \…
**Statement.** If \(G_n\) is edgeless, then \(D_n=\lambda_n=1\), every \(B_{n,i}=\varnothing\), and \(q_{n,i}=1/4\). The fixed \(r=2\) CGD estimator is therefore a sum of independent modified Horvitz--Thompson endpoint contrasts, and \(K_n(1;B,c)\to0\), recovering the classical independent triangular-array central limit theorem under the stated fourth moments and variance floor.

### T-block: t7 — For fixed \(B\ge1\), \(0<c\le1\), and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\),…
**Statement.** For fixed \(B\ge1\), \(0<c\le1\), and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), let \(((G_n,Y_n))_{n\ge1}\) be any sequence such that \((G_n,Y_n)\in\mathcal M_n(D_n;B,c)\) for every \(n\).
