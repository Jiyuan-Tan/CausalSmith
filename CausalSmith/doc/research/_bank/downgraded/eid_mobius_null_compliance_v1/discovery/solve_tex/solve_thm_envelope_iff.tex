\(\mathcal S_3\subseteq\mathcal M\).
%%% FIELD envelope-proposed-statement
Within the present one-sided WARP ambient class \(\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\), for every \(\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\) with \(\mathcal S_3\subseteq\mathcal M\), the vector \((\mu_t^\star)_{t\in\mathcal B}\) is outcome-nonrestrictively identified on \(\mathcal M\) if and only if \(\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal E_3\). Consequently, \(\mathcal E_3\) is the unique inclusion-maximal such support within \(\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\). Here \(\mathcal S_3\) is the eight-map one-sided three-factor specialization, and hence a subclass, of Blackwell--Pashley's coordinate-specific receipt model; it is not that published class itself. Relative to this one-sided three-factor slice, \(\mathcal E_3\) enlarges the response family from eight to 29 maps while preserving identification of the same eight perfect-complier means. No sufficiency claim is made for a broader two-sided or non-WARP class.
%%% FIELD maximal-proposed-statement
For every \(h\in\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\setminus\mathcal E_3\), there exist two finite laws \(P_+\) and \(P_-\), supported on \(\mathcal S_3\cup\{h\}\), with full-support randomized \(Z\), common positive response-type masses, and mutually independent Bernoulli potential outcomes conditional on type, such that \(\mathcal L_{P_+}(Z,D,Y)=\mathcal L_{P_-}(Z,D,Y)\) but \(\mu_t^\star(P_+)\ne\mu_t^\star(P_-)\) for at least one \(t\). The construction keeps every Bernoulli probability in \([1/4,3/4]\). Consequently, for every \(\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\) with \(\mathcal S_3\cup\{h\}\subseteq\mathcal M\), the vector \((\mu_t^\star)_{t\in\mathcal B}\) is not outcome-nonrestrictively identified on \(\mathcal M\). This necessity conclusion is within the present one-sided WARP ambient class and makes no sufficiency assertion for broader two-sided or non-WARP classes.

Choose an ordering \(U=\{j_1,j_2,j_3\}\).  For \(C_0\subseteq U\), put \(\eta_k=1\) when \(j_k\in C_0\) and \(\eta_k=-1\) otherwise, and rank bundles by
\[
u_{C_0}(d)=\sum_{k=1}^3 \eta_k4^k\mathbf 1\{j_k\in d\}.
\]
If \(d\ne d'\), the largest-index term on which they differ has absolute value greater than the sum of all lower-index terms, so \(u_{C_0}(d)\ne u_{C_0}(d')\).  Thus this utility induces a strict total order.  On the menu \(\{d:d\subseteq z\}\), its unique maximizer includes every offered element of \(C_0\) and excludes every element of \(U\setminus C_0\), and hence equals \(z\cap C_0=g_{C_0}(z)\).  Also \(g_{C_0}(z)\subseteq z\).  By the definition of \(\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\), every separable map therefore belongs to that ambient class.

We next verify that the baseline is contained in the candidate envelope.  If \(C_0=U\), then \(g_{C_0}=g^\star\) by the definitions.  Suppose \(C_0\ne U\), and fix \(s\in\mathcal B\).  If \(s\not\subseteq C_0\), then \(g_{C_0}(z)=z\cap C_0\ne s\) for every \(z\), so \(\sigma_s(g_{C_0})=0\).  If \(s\subseteq C_0\), then, among the assignments containing \(s\), the equality \(g_{C_0}(z)=s\) holds exactly when \(z=s\cup R\) for some \(R\subseteq U\setminus C_0\).  The definition of the Möbius signature and the elementary binomial identity give
\[
\sigma_s(g_{C_0})
=\sum_{R\subseteq U\setminus C_0}(-1)^{|R|}
=(1-1)^{|U\setminus C_0|}=0,
\]
where the last equality uses \(C_0\ne U\).  Hence \(g_{C_0}\in\mathcal N_3\) for every \(C_0\ne U\), while \(g_U=g^\star\), and therefore
\[
\mathcal S_3\subseteq\mathcal E_3. \tag{28}
\]

Now let \(\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal E_3\), and consider two laws satisfying the maintained assumptions, supported on \(\mathcal M\), having positive perfect-complier share, and inducing the same observed law \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\).  Full-support randomization makes each conditional cell quantity \(q_{zt}\) and \(r_{zt}\) an observed-law functional.  Thus the signed transforms \(p_t\) and \(m_t\) are also the same under the two laws.  By the signed-identification theorem, for every \(t\in\mathcal B\),
\[
p_t=p^\star>0,
\qquad
m_t=p^\star\mu_t^\star,
\qquad
\mu_t^\star=\frac{m_t}{p_t}. \tag{29}
\]
The division in (29) is justified by the positive-perfect-share assumption.  The right-hand side depends only on \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\), so the two laws have the same vector \((\mu_t^\star)_{t\in\mathcal B}\).  By the definition of outcome-nonrestrictive identification, this proves sufficiency without restricting the conditional distribution of the potential-outcome vector given response type.

Conversely, suppose \(\mathcal S_3\subseteq\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\) but \(\mathcal M\not\subseteq\mathcal E_3\).  Choose \(h\in\mathcal M\setminus\mathcal E_3\).  The maximal-converse theorem gives two admissible finite laws supported on
\[
\mathcal S_3\cup\{h\}\subseteq\mathcal M
\]
that have the same observed law, have positive perfect-complier share, and disagree on at least one component of \((\mu_t^\star)_{t\in\mathcal B}\).  Equivalently, its inclusion corollary says directly that outcome-nonrestrictive identification fails on \(\mathcal M\).  Therefore identification on \(\mathcal M\) implies \(\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal E_3\), proving the equivalence.

Equation (28) shows that \(\mathcal E_3\) is itself among the supports under comparison, and the sufficient direction shows that it identifies the target.  The necessary direction shows that every identifying support in the present ambient class which contains \(\mathcal S_3\) is a subset of \(\mathcal E_3\).  Thus \(\mathcal E_3\) is the greatest, and hence the unique inclusion-maximal, such support within \(\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\).  The Möbius-coefficient lemma further shows that retaining all eight baseline maps uniquely forces the signed isolating coefficients \(a_t(z)\); hence the recovery map in (29) is not chosen by fitting the added interactive maps.  Finally, \(C_0\mapsto g_{C_0}\) is injective because \(g_{C_0}(U)=C_0\), so \(|\mathcal S_3|=2^3=8\), while the classification theorem gives \(|\mathcal E_3|=29\), with 21 interactive maps.  This is precisely an enlargement of the one-sided three-factor slice from eight to 29 maps, not a containment or sufficiency claim about Blackwell--Pashley's full published class or any broader two-sided or non-WARP class.
%%% FIELD maximal-proof
Fix \(h\in\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\setminus\mathcal E_3\).  First note that the nine response types used below are distinct and admissible.  Indeed, each \(g_{C_0}\) is one-sided and is rationalized by the strict utility ranking
\[
u_{C_0}(d)=\sum_{k=1}^3\eta_k4^k\mathbf 1\{j_k\in d\},
\qquad
\eta_k=2\mathbf 1\{j_k\in C_0\}-1,
\]
for any ordering \(U=\{j_1,j_2,j_3\}\): distinct bundles have distinct utilities because the largest differing power dominates all smaller powers, and the unique maximizer among \(d\subseteq z\) is \(z\cap C_0\).  Hence \(g_{C_0}\in\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\).  Moreover, if \(C_0\ne U\), then for every \(s\in\mathcal B\), either \(s\not\subseteq C_0\) and \(g_{C_0}\) never selects \(s\), or
\[
\sigma_s(g_{C_0})
=\sum_{R\subseteq U\setminus C_0}(-1)^{|R|}
=(1-1)^{|U\setminus C_0|}=0.
\]
Thus \(g_{C_0}\in\mathcal N_3\) for \(C_0\ne U\), whereas \(g_U=g^\star\), proving \(\mathcal S_3\subseteq\mathcal E_3\).  Since \(h\notin\mathcal E_3\), it is distinct from all eight separable maps.

Because \(\mathcal E_3=\{g^\star\}\cup\mathcal N_3\), the chosen \(h\) is not \(g^\star\) and is not Möbius-null.  Consequently there exists \(t\in\mathcal B\) with
\[
\sigma_t(h)\ne0. \tag{19}
\]
Set \(V=U\setminus t\) and, for \(S\subseteq V\), define
\[
v_h(S)=\mathbf 1\{h(t\cup S)=t\}.
\]
For \(B\subseteq V\), the subset-inversion construction defines
\[
\beta_{t\cup B}
=\sum_{Q\subseteq B}(-1)^{|B|-|Q|}v_h(V\setminus Q),
\]
and we put \(\beta_C=0\) for \(C\not\supseteq t\).  For each \(S\subseteq V\), interchange the two finite sums to obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{B\subseteq V\setminus S}\beta_{t\cup B}
&=\sum_{Q\subseteq V\setminus S}v_h(V\setminus Q)
  \sum_{B:\,Q\subseteq B\subseteq V\setminus S}(-1)^{|B|-|Q|}\\
&=v_h(S). \tag{20}
\end{aligned}
\]
The inner sum equals \((1-1)^{|(V\setminus S)\setminus Q|}\), so it vanishes unless \(Q=V\setminus S\), in which case it equals one; this proves the second line of (20).  Taking \(B=V\) in the definition and substituting \(S=V\setminus Q\) yields
\[
\beta_U
=\sum_{S\subseteq V}(-1)^{|S|}v_h(S)
=\sum_{z\supseteq t}(-1)^{|z|-|t|}\mathbf 1\{h(z)=t\}
=\sigma_t(h)\ne0, \tag{21}
\]
where the penultimate equality is the bijection \(z=t\cup S\) between supersets of \(t\) and subsets of \(V\), and the last equality uses the definition of \(\sigma_t\) and (19).

On \(\mathcal S_3\cup\{h\}\), set
\[
n_h=1,
\qquad
n_{g_C}=-\beta_C \quad(C\subseteq U).
\]
We claim that this vector is observationally null in receipt cell \(t\).  If \(z\not\supseteq t\), one-sided receipt implies \(g(z)\ne t\) for every type in the support.  If \(z=t\cup S\) with \(S\subseteq V\), then
\[
g_{t\cup B}(z)=z\cap(t\cup B)=t
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
B\cap S=\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
B\subseteq V\setminus S.
\]
All separable coefficients with index not containing \(t\) are zero.  Therefore (20) gives, for every \(z\in\mathcal B\),
\[
\sum_{g\in\mathcal S_3\cup\{h\}}n_g\mathbf 1\{g(z)=t\}
=v_h(S)-\sum_{B\subseteq V\setminus S}\beta_{t\cup B}
=0, \tag{22}
\]
with the first, one-sided case understood separately.  At the perfect-complier type, (21) gives
\[
n_{g^\star}=n_{g_U}=-\beta_U=-\sigma_t(h)\ne0. \tag{23}
\]

Give each of the nine types the common mass \(q_g=1/9\).  Let \(K=\max_g|n_g|\); since \(n_h=1\), one has \(K\ge1\).  With \(\varepsilon=(36K)^{-1}\),
\[
\left|\frac{\varepsilon n_g}{q_g}\right|
=\frac{|n_g|}{4K}\le\frac14. \tag{24}
\]
Choose any assignment probabilities satisfying \(\pi_z>0\) for all \(z\in\mathcal B\), use the same assignment law under both signs, and make \(Z\) independent of \(G\) and all potential outcomes.  Conditional on \(G=g\), let the eight potential outcomes be mutually independent Bernoulli variables, with
\[
P_\pm\{Y(s)=1\mid G=g\}=\frac12 \quad(s\ne t),
\qquad
P_\pm\{Y(t)=1\mid G=g\}
=\frac12\pm\frac{\varepsilon n_g}{q_g}. \tag{25}
\]
Equation (24) puts every success probability in \([1/4,3/4]\), so the finite product construction defines two valid laws with finite moments.  Set \(D=G(Z)\) and \(Y=Y(D)\).  These definitions enforce receipt consistency and outcome exclusion, while the product construction enforces instrument independence and full-support randomization.  The response-type probabilities are common and positive, and
\[
p^\star=P_\pm(G=g^\star)=\frac19>0. \tag{26}
\]

It remains to compare the complete observed laws.  Because the type masses, response maps, and assignment law coincide, the conditional receipt distribution given every assignment is the same.  For a received bundle \(d\ne t\), (25) makes the conditional law of \(Y(d)\) identical under the two signs for every response type.  For \(d=t\), the difference at the success atom, conditional on \(Z=z\), is
\[
\begin{aligned}
&P_+(D=t,Y=1\mid Z=z)-P_-(D=t,Y=1\mid Z=z)\\
&\quad=\sum_{g:\,g(z)=t}q_g
\left[
\left(\frac12+\frac{\varepsilon n_g}{q_g}\right)
-\left(\frac12-\frac{\varepsilon n_g}{q_g}\right)
\right]\\
&\quad=2\varepsilon
\sum_{g\in\mathcal S_3\cup\{h\}}n_g\mathbf 1\{g(z)=t\}=0, \tag{27}
\end{aligned}
\]
where the last equality is (22).  The failure atom also agrees because it is the common receipt probability minus the success-atom probability.  Thus all conditional atoms of \((D,Y)\) given \(Z=z\) agree.  Since the assignment law is common,
\[
\mathcal L_{P_+}(Z,D,Y)=\mathcal L_{P_-}(Z,D,Y). \tag{28}
\]
On the other hand, (23) and (25) imply
\[
\mu_t^\star(P_+)-\mu_t^\star(P_-)
=\frac{2\varepsilon n_{g^\star}}{q_{g^\star}}
\ne0. \tag{29}
\]
Equations (24)--(29) prove the stated finite Bernoulli witness for the arbitrary excluded type \(h\).

Finally, let \(\mathcal M\subseteq\mathcal G^{\mathrm{WARP}}_3\) satisfy \(\mathcal S_3\cup\{h\}\subseteq\mathcal M\).  Both witness laws are then supported on \(\mathcal M\), satisfy the maintained causal assumptions, have the positive share in (26), induce the same \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\) by (28), and disagree on a target component by (29).  The definition of outcome-nonrestrictive identification therefore implies that the vector of perfect-complier means is not identified on \(\mathcal M\).  This inclusion argument establishes necessity only; it does not transfer the 29-map sufficiency theorem outside the present one-sided WARP ambient class.
