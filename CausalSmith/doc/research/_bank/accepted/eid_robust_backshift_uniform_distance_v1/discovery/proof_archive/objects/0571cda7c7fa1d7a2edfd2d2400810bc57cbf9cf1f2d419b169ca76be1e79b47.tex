We separate the representation, finite population classification, and contraction claims.

First fix \(n\geq 1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and a realization of the encoded covariance regions, and write
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}
=\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\}.
\]
By \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\), effective real quantifier elimination applied to the finite formula in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\) terminates and returns a quantifier-free rational-polynomial formula whose realization is exactly \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\).  The same theorem gives
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\]
Taking probabilities and using monotonicity of probability therefore yields
\[
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}
\geq \Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H}),
\]
so \(\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha\) implies the stated finite-sample coverage bound.  The representation theorem also proves that the audit is a universal audit over all full feasible tuples and that the procedure releases the exact code precisely when no feasible tuple violates a threshold; otherwise it returns \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\).

We next establish the asserted population classification explicitly.  Fix rational matrices \(\Sigma_e\), \(e\in E\).  For each \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|=h\), let \(\phi_K(A)\) be the first-order formula
\[
\begin{split}
\phi_K(A)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
&A\in\mathcal D\ \text{ and there exist }\Psi\in\mathbb S_+^p
\text{ and }t_e\in[0,\infty)^p\ (e\in K)\text{ such that}\\
&A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)
\qquad(e\in K).
\end{split}
\]
Define \(\Phi_\Sigma(A)=\bigvee_{K\subseteq E,\,|K|=h}\phi_K(A)\).  By \(\text{def:compatible-set}\),
\[
A\in\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\Phi_\Sigma(A).
\]
Indeed, a witnessing fitted set of cardinality at least \(h\) contains a subset of cardinality exactly \(h\), which proves the forward implication, and a witness for one such subset is itself permitted by \(\text{def:compatible-set}\), which proves the reverse implication.

The formula \(\Phi_\Sigma\) is a first-order formula over the ordered real field with rational coefficients.  To see this without any hidden analytic predicate, invertibility is \((\det A)^2>0\); unit diagonal is given by the equations \(A_{ii}=1\); and, because there are finitely many directed simple cycles, the cycle-product condition is the finite conjunction
\[
1-\pi_C(A)>0,
\qquad
1+\pi_C(A)>0
\]
over those cycles.  Positive semidefiniteness of the symmetric matrix \(\Psi\) is equivalent to nonnegativity of all of its principal minors, nonnegativity of the shifts is coordinatewise polynomial inequality, and every displayed covariance equation is polynomial because each \(\Sigma_e\) has rational entries.  Thus the three closed sentences
\[
\neg\exists A\,\Phi_\Sigma(A),
\]
\[
\exists A\left[
\Phi_\Sigma(A)\ \wedge\
\forall A'\left(
\Phi_\Sigma(A')\Longrightarrow
\sum_{i,j=1}^p(A_{ij}-A'_{ij})^2=0
\right)
\right],
\]
and
\[
\exists A\,\exists A'\left[
\Phi_\Sigma(A)\ \wedge\ \Phi_\Sigma(A')\ \wedge\
\sum_{i,j=1}^p(A_{ij}-A'_{ij})^2>0
\right]
\]
say, respectively, that \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)\) is empty, is a singleton, or contains at least two matrices.  They are decidable by \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), and the three cases are exhaustive and mutually exclusive.

The boundary-abstention statement follows directly from the strict threshold.  If a feasible tuple \(w\) has \(\gamma(w)=0\), then \(\gamma_0>0\) gives
\[
\gamma(w)=0<\gamma_0.
\]
Hence that tuple witnesses the audit sentence asserting a threshold violation.  By \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\), and equivalently by the exact audit characterization in \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\), the procedure returns \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\).

No normalization chart is used in this argument: the entries of \(A\) are the free matrix variables in \(\Phi_\Sigma(A)\), and every normalization constraint above is polynomial in those entries.  For completeness, consider any finite rational-semialgebraic chart description of the same candidate locus.  On each denominator-sign cell, adjoin new chart coordinates \(u\), the polynomial graph equations obtained by clearing the denominators in \(A=q(u)/d(u)\), and the stipulated strict sign of every denominator.  The sign conditions ensure that clearing denominators is reversible.  Therefore projection onto \(A\) has two inclusions: every chart point projects to the matrix represented by that chart and hence satisfies the original matrix-space formula, while every matrix covered by the charts has chart coordinates satisfying one of the graph cells.  Taking the finite union over the charts gives exactly the original matrix-space set.  Applying \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\) to the augmented formula therefore changes only its representation, not its projected realization.

It remains to prove the quantitative release claim.  Work on \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\) and suppose that the universal five-margin audit releases.  In the feasibility formula choose
\[
K=H,
\qquad A=D,
\qquad \Psi=\Omega,
\qquad t_e=s_e,
\qquad \Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\quad(e\in H).
\]
The three structural assumptions cited by \(\text{thm:audited-release-contraction}\), the positive-semidefinite invariant-noise condition in \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\), and the event \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\) verify every feasibility conjunct: in particular, membership in an encoded covariance region makes each \(\Sigma_e\) positive definite, and
\[
D\Sigma_eD^{\mathsf T}=\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e)
\qquad(e\in H)
\]
is equivalent to the honest covariance equation.  Thus the true tuple is among the universally audited feasible tuples.  Since the audit releases, its five margins obey
\[
\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0,
\qquad
\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0,
\qquad
\lambda_H\geq\lambda_0,
\qquad
\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa,
\qquad
M_H\leq\overline M.
\]
Here the equalities between the tuple margins and the displayed true-instance margins follow by substituting \(K=H\), \(A=D\), \(\Psi=\Omega\), \(t=s\), and \(\Gamma=\Sigma\) into \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\).

All hypotheses of \(\text{thm:audited-release-contraction}\) now hold.  That theorem proves that the search in \(\text{def:audited-release-contraction}\) terminates with \(\underline\mu>0\), whence \(r_0>0\), and that
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\]
\[
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
C_0=16K_0L_0^3,
\]
with
\[
L_0=(p!\,\overline\kappa^{p-1})^{1/p},
\qquad
K_0=\frac{6\overline M}{\gamma_0}
\sqrt{p(p-1)(1+L_0^2)}.
\]
The construction of these constants uses only \(p,m,c,\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0,\overline\kappa,\overline M\), so the inequalities are uniform over the audited class.  If the audit releases along a sequence and \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\), the two right-hand sides converge to zero, proving both asserted contractions.  The invoked theorems make no assertion on an abstaining outcome, and the constructions specify symbolic quantifier elimination rather than a numerical or CRAN implementation; hence none of the exclusions in the final sentence has been strengthened into an unwarranted conclusion.
