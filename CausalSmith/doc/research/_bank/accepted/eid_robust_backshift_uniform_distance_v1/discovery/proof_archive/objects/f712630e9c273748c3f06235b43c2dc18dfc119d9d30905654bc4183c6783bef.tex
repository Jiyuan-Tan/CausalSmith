Let \(I\) be a finite index set of cardinality \(r\), let \(Z^\circ\in\{0,1\}^{I\times p}\), and let \(a^\circ\in(0,\infty)^{I\times p}\). Suppose the subarray \((a^\circ_{ek}:Z^\circ_{ek}=1)\) has a joint distribution absolutely continuous on its positive orthant. Define \(P_e^{k\ell}=(Z^\circ_{ek}a^\circ_{ek},Z^\circ_{e\ell}a^\circ_{e\ell})\), \(N_{uv}^{k\ell}=\#\{e\in I:(Z^\circ_{ek},Z^\circ_{e\ell})=(u,v)\}\), and \(M_I^{k\ell}=\max_{L\text{ affine line}}\#\{e\in I:P_e^{k\ell}\in L\}\). Then, simultaneously for all \(k<\ell\), almost surely
\[
 M_I^{k\ell}=\max\{N_{00}^{k\ell}+N_{10}^{k\ell},N_{00}^{k\ell}+N_{01}^{k\ell},N_{00}^{k\ell}+\mathbf 1\{N_{11}^{k\ell}>0\},\min(2,r)\}.
\]
