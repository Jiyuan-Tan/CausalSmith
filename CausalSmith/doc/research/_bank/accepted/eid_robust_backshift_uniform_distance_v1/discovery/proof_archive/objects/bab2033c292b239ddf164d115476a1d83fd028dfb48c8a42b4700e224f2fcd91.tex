Fix \(p\geq2\), \(m\geq1\), and \(0\leq c\leq m-1\), put \(h=m-c\) and \(q=m-2c=h-c\), and fix \(\gamma_0\in\mathbb Q_{>0}\), \(\zeta_0\in\mathbb Q\cap(0,1]\), and \(\overline\kappa,\overline M\in\mathbb Q\cap[1,\infty)\). Define
\[
L_0=\bigl(p!\,\overline\kappa^{p-1}\bigr)^{1/p},
\qquad
K_0=\frac{6\overline M}{\gamma_0}
\sqrt{p(p-1)(1+L_0^2)},
\qquad
C_0=16K_0L_0^3.
\]
Under \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\), and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\), suppose that \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\) and that the true instance satisfies the four bounds
\[
\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0,
\qquad
\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0,
\qquad
\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa,
\qquad
M_H\leq\overline M.
\]
Fix \(n\geq1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and an arbitrary indexed family of nonempty covariance regions \(\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\subseteq\mathbb S_{++}^{p}\). For a set \(K\subseteq E\) of size \(h\) and shifts \((t_e:e\in K)\), define \(\gamma_K(t)=0\) when \(q<3\), and otherwise define
\[
\gamma_K(t)=
\min_{1\leq k<\ell\leq p}
\min_{\substack{S\subseteq K\\|S|=q}}
\max_{e<f<g\in S}
\left|
\det\begin{pmatrix}
1&t_{ek}&t_{e\ell}\\
1&t_{fk}&t_{f\ell}\\
1&t_{gk}&t_{g\ell}
\end{pmatrix}
\right|.
\]
Assume declaratively that every tuple consisting of \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|=h\), \(A\in\mathcal D\), \(\Psi\in\mathbb S_+^p\), \(t_e\in[0,\infty)^p\), and \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\) for \(e\in K\), satisfying
\[
A\Gamma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)
\qquad(e\in K),
\]
also satisfies all four bounds
\[
\gamma_K(t)\geq\gamma_0,
\qquad
1-\operatorname{CP}(I_p-A)\geq\zeta_0,
\qquad
\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\|A^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq\overline\kappa,
\]
\[
1+\|\Psi\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\max_{e\in K}\|\Gamma_e\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\max_{e\in K}\|\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq\overline M.
\]
Then there exists a real number \(r_0>0\), depending only on \(p,m,c,\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\overline\kappa,\overline M\), such that on the event
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}
=\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\},
\]
one has
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\]
\[
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\]
Consequently, along any sequence of covered outcomes satisfying the same declarative candidate-side bounds and \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\), both displayed set radii converge to zero. The existence of \(r_0\) is non-effective: no procedure is asserted for checking the universal candidate premise or for computing \(r_0\). No lower eigenvalue bound on the true or candidate covariances is required.
