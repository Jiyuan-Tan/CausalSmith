Fix integers \(p\geq2\), \(m\geq1\), and \(0\leq c\leq m-1\), put \(h=m-c\) and \(q=m-2c=h-c\), and fix rational bounds \(\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0>0\) and \(\overline\kappa,\overline M\geq1\). Under \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\), and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\), suppose additionally that \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\) and that the true instance satisfies
\[
\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0,\qquad
\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0,\qquad
\lambda_{\mathrm{cov},H}\geq\lambda_0,\qquad
\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa,\qquad
M_H\leq\overline M.
\tag{44}
\]
Set
\[
L_0=\bigl(p!\,\overline\kappa^{\,p-1}\bigr)^{1/p},\qquad
K_0=\frac{6\overline M}{\gamma_0}
\sqrt{p(p-1)(1+L_0^2)},
\qquad C_0=16K_0L_0^3.
\tag{45}
\]
There exists a real \(r_0>0\), depending only on \(p,m,c,\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0,\overline\kappa,\overline M\), with the following property. Fix \(n\geq1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and arbitrary nonempty regions \(\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\subseteq\mathbb S_{++}^p\). Assume declaratively that, for every \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|=h\), every \(A\in\mathcal D\), every \(\Psi\in\mathbb S_+^p\), every \(t_e\in[0,\infty)^p\), and every \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\), \(e\in K\), satisfying
\[
A\Gamma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)
\qquad(e\in K),
\tag{46}
\]
the following five candidate-side bounds hold: for every \(1\leq k<\ell\leq p\) and every \(S\subseteq K\) with \(|S|=q\), some \(e<f<g\) in \(S\) satisfies
\[
\left|\det\begin{pmatrix}
1&t_{ek}&t_{e\ell}\\
1&t_{fk}&t_{f\ell}\\
1&t_{gk}&t_{g\ell}
\end{pmatrix}\right|\geq\gamma_0;
\tag{47}
\]
and
\[
1-\operatorname{CP}(I_p-A)\geq\zeta_0,\qquad
\min_{e\in K}\lambda_{\min}(\Gamma_e)\geq\lambda_0,
\qquad
\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\|A^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\overline\kappa,
\tag{48}
\]
\[
1+\|\Psi\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\max_{e\in K}\|\Gamma_e\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\max_{e\in K}\|\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq\overline M.
\tag{49}
\]
On the simultaneous coverage event
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}=\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\},
\tag{50}
\]
one then has
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\tag{51}
\]
Consequently, along any sequence of covered outcomes satisfying the same declarative candidate bounds and \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\), both set radii in (51) converge to zero. The existence of \(r_0\) is non-effective: no procedure for checking (47)--(49) or computing \(r_0\) is asserted.
