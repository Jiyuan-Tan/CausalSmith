The field-tier radius result is informed by RothenhauslerEtAl2015BackShift, Theorem 1 and its appendix proof, which supplies the all-honest model, pairwise profile reduction, and cycle-product normalization; its generic-amplitude result is Corollary 2(ii). KleinsteuberShen2013NUJDUniqueness, Theorem 1, informs the complete-family uniqueness step through profile collinearity. AkhavanEtAl2019GeometryJD, Section 4.2 and equation (42), informs the robust joint-diagonalization setting by trimming outlier target matrices with a chosen residual threshold, but it does not state a worst-case replacement radius. ShenEtAl2020RJDSEM, Section III.C and equations (27)--(28), defines R-JDSEM through an elementwise \(\ell_1\) joint-diagonalization loss for sparse node- or entry-level outlier components within local covariance matrices, rather than replacement of whole covariance slabs. Its noiseless anchored or known-edge Theorem 1 establishes a sufficient uniqueness result under prior edge information; it gives neither an adversarial whole-environment replacement radius, the criterion \(\delta_H>c\), nor a legal two-explanation BACKSHIFT converse. PerryVonKugelgenScholkopf2022SparseShift, Corollary 4.2, Theorem 5.3, and Corollary 5.4, shows how pairwise environment comparisons and sparse mechanism changes support detection and graph recovery, while PfisterPeters2022SparseIV, Theorem 3, illustrates how a support pattern can control generic causal identifiability. LeNguyen2022HermitianSDC, Theorem 7 and Algorithm 5, supplies a complete-family congruence-feasibility primitive; this proposal uses it only to motivate an oracle reduction and does not claim a terminating exact solver. HeKressner2024RandomizedSDC supplies exact and perturbative results for a complete approximately diagonalizable family, rather than adversarial selection among competing size-\(h\) subsets. Afsari2008JDSensitivity relates local joint-diagonalization error to conditioning and a uniqueness modulus; the present contraction instead combines a global compact residual gap with local affine-minor inversion, using four true-side margins and only candidate conditioning. AndrewsCheng2013SporadicID motivates confidence procedures that remain valid as identification disappears; the present coverage claim is instead a finite-sample set-theoretic implication for arbitrary supplied covariance regions. None of these sources characterizes adversarial positive-definite replacement under BACKSHIFT normalization and nonnegative raw shifts or provides the exact executable program left open here.
