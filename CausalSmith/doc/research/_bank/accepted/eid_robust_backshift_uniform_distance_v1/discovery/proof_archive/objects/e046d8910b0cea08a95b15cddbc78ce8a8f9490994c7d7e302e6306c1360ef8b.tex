For fixed \(n\geq1\) and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), an encoded covariance-region family is a finite list \(\mathfrak C^{(n)}_\alpha=(\varphi^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}:e\in E)\) in which \(\varphi^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}(G)\) is a quantifier-free Boolean combination of sign conditions \(q(G)>0\), \(q(G)=0\), and \(q(G)<0\), with \(q\) a polynomial with rational coefficients in the entries of a symmetric \(p\times p\) matrix \(G\).  Its realization is required to satisfy
\[
 \mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}
 =\{G\in\mathbb S_{++}^p:\varphi^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}(G)\},
 \qquad
 \mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\ne\varnothing
 \quad(e\in E).
\]
The finite formulas, their rational coefficients, and their Boolean syntax constitute \(\operatorname{RegionCode}(\mathcal C^{(n)}_\alpha)\).  This definition equips the existing regions with an effective representation; it does not alter their realized subsets of \(\mathbb S_{++}^p\).
