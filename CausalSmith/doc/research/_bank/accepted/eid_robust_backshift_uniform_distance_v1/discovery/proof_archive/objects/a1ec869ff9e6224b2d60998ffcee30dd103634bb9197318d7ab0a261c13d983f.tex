Fix an outcome in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\). For every \(e\in H\), set \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\). For every \(e\in E\setminus H\), choose one \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\); this finite collection of choices exists because \(E\setminus H\) is finite and each region is nonempty. Define
\[
K=H,\qquad A=D,\qquad \Psi=\Omega,
\qquad t_e=s_e\quad(e\in H).
\tag{1}
\]
By the definition of \(H\), \(|K|=h\). Assumption \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\) gives \(A=D\in\mathcal D\). The theorem-local premise gives \(\Psi=\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\), and \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\) gives \(t_e=s_e\in[0,\infty)^p\). Assumption \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\) and the choice \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\) on \(H\) give, for every \(e\in K\),
\[
\Gamma_e
=D^{-1}\bigl(\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e)\bigr)D^{-\mathsf T}
=A^{-1}\bigl(\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\bigr)A^{-\mathsf T}.
\tag{2}
\]
Thus (1)--(2) are exactly the witnesses required by \(\text{def:compatible-set}\), and hence \(D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\). The selected family \(\Gamma\) belongs to \(\prod_{e\in E}\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\); therefore \(\text{def:confidence-union}\) gives \(D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). The outcome was arbitrary, so
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\tag{3}
\]
If the two events in (3) are measurable, monotonicity of probability yields the stated probability implication. No step used positive definiteness of any \(\Gamma_e\): positive semidefiniteness is exactly the domain required by \(\text{def:compatible-set}\). Finally, the causal target \(B=I_p-D\) is fixed by the structural model, whereas \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\) is an observed-region compatibility functional; inclusion (3), rather than a definition, proves their coverage relationship.
