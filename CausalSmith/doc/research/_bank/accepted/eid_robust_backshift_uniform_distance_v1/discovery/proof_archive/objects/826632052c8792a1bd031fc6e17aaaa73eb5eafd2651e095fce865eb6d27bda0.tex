We separate the coverage statement, the rational population calculation, and the computational obstruction.

\emph{Coverage.}  Fix \(n\ge1\) and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\).  On \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\), set \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\) for \(e\in H\).  For each \(e\in E\setminus H\), choose any \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\); this is possible because every indexed region is nonempty.  By \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(D\in\mathcal D\).  By \(\text{ass:invariant-noise-interior}\), \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_{++}^p\subseteq\mathbb S_+^p\).  By \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\), \(s_e\in[0,\infty)^p\) for \(e\in H\), and by \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\),
\[
\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e=D^{-1}\{\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e)\}D^{-\mathsf T},\qquad e\in H.
\]
Since \(|H|=h\), the witnesses \(K=H\), \(A=D\), \(\Psi=\Omega\), and \(t_e=s_e\) meet every condition in \(\text{def:compatible-set}\).  Thus \(D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\).  The union in \(\text{def:confidence-union}\) contains this choice of \(\Gamma\), so
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\]
Monotonicity of probability now gives
\[
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\ge \Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\ge1-\alpha.
\]
This proves coverage without equating the causal matrix \(D\), or \(B=I_p-D\), with the random set \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\).

\emph{Exact rational population certificates.}  Suppose every entry of every \(\Sigma_e\) is rational.  It is enough to enumerate subsets \(K\subseteq E\) having \(|K|=h\): any witness with more than \(h\) slots restricts to one with exactly \(h\) slots.  For such a \(K\), let \(\mathcal F_K(A,\Psi,(t_e)_{e\in K})\) be the following finite conjunction:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&A_{ii}=1 &&(1\le i\le p),\\
&(\det A)^2>0,\\
&\prod_{(i,j)\in C}A_{ij}^2<1
&&\text{for every directed simple cycle \(C\) of length at least two},\\
&\Psi=\Psi^{\mathsf T},\qquad x^{\mathsf T}\Psi x\ge0
&&\text{for every \(x\in\mathbb R^p\)},\\
&t_{ek}\ge0 &&(e\in K,\ 1\le k\le p),\\
&A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e) &&(e\in K).
\end{aligned}
\]
The universally quantified inequality in the fourth line is the definition of \(\Psi\succeq0\).  For an off-diagonal entry, \((I_p-A)_{ij}=-A_{ij}\); hence the finitely many cycle inequalities are equivalent to \(\operatorname{CP}(I_p-A)<1\).  It follows directly from \(\text{def:compatible-set}\) that
\[
A\in\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\bigvee_{\substack{K\subseteq E\\|K|=h}}
\exists\Psi\,\exists(t_e)_{e\in K}\ \mathcal F_K(A,\Psi,(t_e)_{e\in K}).
\tag{1}
\]
All polynomials in (1), including those under the universal quantifier defining positive semidefiniteness, have rational coefficients.  Define the right-hand side of (1) to be \(\mathcal F(A)\).  The three output cases are expressed by the closed first-order formulas
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{infeasible:}&\neg\exists A\,\mathcal F(A),\\[2mm]
\text{singleton:}&\exists A\,[\mathcal F(A)\wedge
 \forall A'\{\mathcal F(A')\Rightarrow\sum_{i,j}(A'_{ij}-A_{ij})^2=0\}],\\[2mm]
\text{multiple:}&\exists A\,\exists A'\,[\mathcal F(A)\wedge\mathcal F(A')
 \wedge\sum_{i,j}(A'_{ij}-A_{ij})^2>0].
\end{array}
\tag{2}
\]
They are exhaustive because a nonempty set either has exactly one element or contains two distinct elements.  Applying \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\) to (1) gives a quantifier-free rational semialgebraic description of the full set \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)\), and applying it to (2) decides the three cases.  The resulting finite Boolean combination of rational polynomial sign conditions is the exact symbolic certificate; in each closed case of (2), its remaining signs are signs of rational constants and are checked exactly.

For every \(e,f,g\in K\) and \(k<\ell\), adjoin the polynomial
\[
q_{efg;k\ell}(t)=
\det\begin{pmatrix}
1&t_{ek}&t_{e\ell}\\
1&t_{fk}&t_{f\ell}\\
1&t_{gk}&t_{g\ell}
\end{pmatrix}
\tag{3}
\]
to the sign family.  It has integer coefficients and is homogeneous with respect to the determinant calculation, so no ratio is taken.  The signs of (3) record precisely the affine-minor separation of each fitted shift cloud.  Formula (1) shows why these signs alone are not feasibility certificates: none of invertibility, positive semidefiniteness, nonnegative shifts, or the covariance equations follows from (3).

No normalization chart is needed in (1), because unit diagonal and all cycle products are imposed globally.  If a row-permutation calculation is used internally, let \(u_i\ne0\) denote the pivot used to normalize row \(i\).  Replace every division by \(u_i\) with its cross-multiplied polynomial equality, retain the sign condition \(u_i^2>0\), and multiply every rational cycle inequality by the positive product of the relevant \(u_i^2\).  These operations are equivalences on that chart.  There are finitely many row permutations and pivot choices, and a proposed candidate is accepted only after satisfying (1); consequently switching among these certified charts cannot change the returned set.

\emph{Why arbitrary set-valued regions are not computable inputs.}  We give an explicit finite-query indistinguishability witness.  Take \(p=2\), \(m=h=3\), \(c=0\), \(H=E\),
\[
D=I_2,\qquad \Omega=I_2,\qquad
s_1=(0,0),\quad s_2=(1,0),\quad s_3=(0,1).
\]
Then
\[
\Sigma_1=I_2,\qquad \Sigma_2=\operatorname{diag}(2,1),\qquad
\Sigma_3=\operatorname{diag}(1,2),
\]
and the unique affine minor is
\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\1&1&0\\1&0&1\end{pmatrix}=1,
\]
so \(\gamma_H=1\).  Let \(\mathcal C^0_e=\{\Sigma_e\}\).  We first verify directly that \(\mathcal R_0(\Sigma)=\{I_2\}\).  If \(A\in\mathcal R_0(\Sigma)\), unit diagonal gives
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&a\\b&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Subtracting the fitted covariance equations between environments 2 and 1 makes the invariant nuisance cancel and gives a diagonal matrix on the right.  Its off-diagonal entry on the left is
\[
\left[A\operatorname{diag}(1,0)A^{\mathsf T}\right]_{12}=b,
\]
so \(b=0\).  Similarly, environments 3 and 1 give
\[
\left[A\operatorname{diag}(0,1)A^{\mathsf T}\right]_{12}=a,
\]
so \(a=0\).  Hence \(A=I_2\), while the displayed \(D,\Omega,s\) show that \(I_2\) is feasible.  Therefore the confidence union formed from \(\mathcal C^0\) is \(\{I_2\}\).

For a nonzero rational number \(q\), put
\[
A_q=\begin{pmatrix}1&q\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\Gamma_e(q)=A_q^{-1}\{I_2+\operatorname{diag}(s_e)\}A_q^{-\mathsf T}.
\]
Every \(\Gamma_e(q)\) is rational and positive definite.  Moreover, \(A_q\) has unit diagonal, is invertible, and \(\operatorname{CP}(I_2-A_q)=0<1\).  Thus \(A_q\in\mathcal R_0(\Gamma(q))\), witnessed by invariant nuisance \(I_2\) and the displayed nonnegative shifts.

Consider any deterministic purported exact algorithm which is allowed finitely many adaptive membership questions of the form ``is \(Q\in\mathcal C_e\)?'' and then halts.  Run it on \(\mathcal C^0\); only finitely many matrices \(Q\) are queried.  For each fixed queried \(Q\) and environment \(e\), the equality \(Q=\Gamma_e(q)\) excludes at most one \(q\), because
\[
[\Gamma_e(q)]_{12}=-q(1+s_{e2})
\]
and \(1+s_{e2}>0\).  Choose a nonzero rational \(q\) outside this finite exceptional set and define
\[
\mathcal C^1_e=\{\Sigma_e,\Gamma_e(q)\},\qquad e=1,2,3.
\]
Every membership answer along the run is identical for \(\mathcal C^0\) and \(\mathcal C^1\), so determinism forces the same output.  But selecting \(\Sigma_e\) in every slot shows \(I_2\in\mathcal U(\mathcal C^1)\), while selecting \(\Gamma_e(q)\) in every slot shows \(A_q\in\mathcal U(\mathcal C^1)\).  Since \(q\ne0\),
\[
\mathcal U(\mathcal C^0)=\{I_2\}\ne\mathcal U(\mathcal C^1).
\]
This contradiction proves the finite-query impossibility.  The frozen type of \(\mathcal C^{(n)}_\alpha\) supplies not even a membership oracle, much less a finite semialgebraic encoding, so an exact computable implementation cannot be asserted on that type.  Likewise, detecting that some compatible explanation has \(\gamma_H=0\) cannot be required computationally without an effective input representation.  Set-theoretically one may always return the full union or abstain; effectivity is the missing premise.

Thus the coverage implication and the rational population certificate are established, while the conjunction demanded by the question is false under the declared arbitrary-region input class.  This obstruction is computational and does not by itself affirm or refute a separate analytic local-modulus claim for \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\), \(\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}\), or \(\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}\) after an effective region class is specified.
