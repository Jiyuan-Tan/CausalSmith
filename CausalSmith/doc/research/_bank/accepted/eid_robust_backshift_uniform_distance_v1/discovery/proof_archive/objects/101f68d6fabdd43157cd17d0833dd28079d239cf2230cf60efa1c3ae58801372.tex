Fix the integers, probability level, and rational thresholds in the statement. We first prove the unconditional assertions. By \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\), after the sample outcome is fixed, every region is the nonempty realization of a finite Boolean formula in rational-polynomial sign conditions. The formula \(F_K(w)\) in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\) adds only finitely many polynomial equalities and inequalities: inverse relations are represented by \(AV=VA=I_p\), positive semidefiniteness and positive definiteness by principal-minor sign conditions, and the cycle normalization by the two inequalities \(1-\pi_C(A)>0\) and \(1+\pi_C(A)>0\) for each of the finitely many directed simple cycles \(C\). The same definition expressly gives first-order real-polynomial representations of its operator-norm, eigenvalue, and finite minimum--maximum conditions. Thus both its projection formula \(\Phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\) and its closed audit sentence lie in the scope of \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\). The exact projection, termination, and audit equivalence are precisely the unconditional conclusions of \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\). Consequently the projected formula realizes \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\), and the procedure releases it exactly when no feasible tuple violates any of the five inequalities. Since \(\gamma_0>0\), a feasible tuple with \(\gamma(w)=0\) satisfies \(\gamma(w)<\gamma_0\); the audit sentence is then true, and the procedure returns \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\).

For completeness, we give the claimed rational-population classification explicitly rather than folding it into the preceding projection. Fix a rational covariance family \(\Sigma\), and let \(\Phi_\Sigma(A)\) be the compatibility predicate obtained from \(\text{def:compatible-set}\). For every one of the finitely many sets \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|\geq h\), it existentially quantifies \(\Psi\in\mathbb S_+^p\) and \(t_e\in[0,\infty)^p\). Since \(A\in\mathcal D\subseteq\mathrm{GL}_p(\mathbb R)\), multiplication on the left by \(A\) and on the right by \(A^{\mathsf T}\) gives the equivalence
\[
\Sigma_e=A^{-1}\bigl(\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\bigr)A^{-\mathsf T}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e).
\]
Thus the predicate imposes
\[
A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)
\qquad(e\in K),
\]
and imposes the unit-diagonal, determinant, and finite cycle inequalities defining \(A\in\mathcal D\). Because the entries of \(\Sigma\) are rational, \(\Phi_\Sigma\) is a first-order formula with rational-polynomial atoms. The following three closed formulas express the three alternatives:
\[
\mathsf E_0:=\neg\exists A\,\Phi_\Sigma(A),
\]
\[
\mathsf E_1:=\exists A\left[\Phi_\Sigma(A)\wedge
\forall A'\left(\Phi_\Sigma(A')\Rightarrow
\sum_{i,j=1}^p(A_{ij}-A'_{ij})^2=0\right)\right],
\]
\[
\mathsf E_{\geq2}:=\exists A\,\exists A'\left[
\Phi_\Sigma(A)\wedge\Phi_\Sigma(A')\wedge
\sum_{i,j=1}^p(A_{ij}-A'_{ij})^2>0\right].
\]
They say, respectively, that \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)\) is empty, is a singleton, or has at least two elements. Exactly one holds by the elementary trichotomy for a set. Each is decidable by \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), proving the classification claim.

The matrix entries of \(A\) are the free coordinates of \(\Phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\), so this construction uses no normalization chart. If an auxiliary rational chart with coordinate \(u\) represents, on a denominator-sign cell, an entry as \(A_{ij}=P_{ij}(u)/Q_{ij}(u)\), adjoin
\[
Q_{ij}(u)A_{ij}-P_{ij}(u)=0
\]
together with the chosen strict sign condition on \(Q_{ij}(u)\). On that cell these equations are equivalent to the chart relation. Existentially projecting out \(u\) therefore returns exactly the same set of matrices \(A\); the projection is effective by \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\). Taking the finite union over denominator-sign cells proves the asserted chart invariance.

We now impose the three displayed BACKSHIFT assumptions and the theorem-local condition \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\). Consider an outcome in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\). For each \(e\in H\), choose \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\); this is permitted by the definition of \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\). Every region outside \(H\) is nonempty by \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\), and \(E\setminus H\) is finite, so choose one \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\) for each \(e\in E\setminus H\). The structural assumptions give, for every \(e\in H\),
\[
D\Gamma_eD^{\mathsf T}
=D\Sigma_eD^{\mathsf T}
=\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e),
\]
where \(D\in\mathcal D\) by \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\) by the local premise, and \(s_e\in[0,\infty)^p\) by \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\). Since \(|H|=h\), the choice \(K=H\), \(A=D\), \(\Psi=\Omega\), and \(t_e=s_e\) is feasible in \(\text{def:compatible-set}\). Hence
\[
D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)
\subseteq
\bigcup_{\widetilde\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha},\ e\in E}
\mathcal R_c(\widetilde\Gamma)
=\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha.
\]
This proves \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\). Monotonicity of probability then gives
\[
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}
\geq\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha.
\]
This is also the coverage implication in \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\).

It remains to verify that the hypotheses of \(\text{thm:audited-release-contraction}\) follow on a covered outcome when the universal audit releases. Because every encoded region lies in \(\mathbb S_{++}^p\), the covered matrices \(\Sigma_e\), \(e\in H\), are positive definite. Thus the tuple used above, now written
\[
w_0=(H,D,\Omega,(s_e)_{e\in H},(\Sigma_e)_{e\in H}),
\]
is a full feasible candidate for \(F_H\) in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\): the covariance equations were just verified, the inverse and normalization constraints follow from \(D\in\mathcal D\), and the nuisance and shifts satisfy their cone restrictions. A released audit means that every feasible candidate, hence \(w_0\), obeys all five audited inequalities. By the definitions of the candidate margins and the corresponding true-instance margins,
\[
\gamma(w_0)=\gamma_H,
\qquad \zeta(w_0)=\zeta_D,
\qquad \lambda(w_0)=\lambda_H,
\qquad \kappa(w_0)=\kappa_D,
\qquad M(w_0)=M_H.
\]
Therefore
\[
\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0,
\qquad \zeta_D\geq\zeta_0,
\qquad \lambda_H\geq\lambda_0,
\qquad \kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa,
\qquad M_H\leq\overline M.
\]
All model and margin hypotheses of \(\text{thm:audited-release-contraction}\) are now established. That theorem, with the constants from \(\text{def:audited-release-contraction}\), proves that \(\underline\mu>0\), that \(r_0>0\) is effectively computable, and, whenever \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0\),
\[
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\]
Its constants depend only on \(p,m,c,\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0,\overline\kappa,\overline M\), which proves uniformity. Along the stated sequence, the right-hand sides converge to zero because \(C_0\) is fixed and \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\). Every contraction step used both membership in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\) and audit release to establish the true margins, and then used the small-radius premise. Hence the proof yields no contraction claim on abstaining outcomes or beyond \(r_0\), as asserted.
