Under the prespecified support pattern \(Z\) and absolutely continuous active amplitudes, simultaneously for every \(k<\ell\), \(M_{k\ell}=M_{k\ell}^{\mathrm{gen}}\) almost surely. Consequently, the pattern guarantees \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)=\{D\}\) uniformly for all admissible \(D\in\mathcal D\), all \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_{+}^{p}\), and every positive-semidefinite assignment to the \(c\) replacement slots if and only if, for every \(k<\ell\), \(\max\{n_{00}^{k\ell}+n_{10}^{k\ell},n_{00}^{k\ell}+n_{01}^{k\ell},n_{00}^{k\ell}+\mathbf 1\{n_{11}^{k\ell}>0\},\min(2,h)\}<h-c\). Failure of this inequality has an ambiguity witness with positive-definite invariant noise and positive-definite covariance slots; it is not a claim that every fixed singular boundary instance is ambiguous. In particular, any uniformly robustly identifying support pattern satisfies \(m\ge2c+3\), and this environment-count lower bound is attained by support patterns with no larger forced affine incidence.
