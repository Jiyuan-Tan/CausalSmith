By \(\text{ass:support-factorization}\), for every \(e\in H\) and \(k<\ell\),
\[
 (s_{ek},s_{e\ell})=(Z_{ek}a_{ek},Z_{e\ell}a_{e\ell}). \tag{22}
\]
Apply \(\text{lem:generic-affine-occupancy}\) with \(I=H\), \(r=h\), \(Z^\circ=Z\), and \(a^\circ=a\).  Its absolute-continuity premise is exactly \(\text{ass:generic-active-amplitudes}\).  Therefore, on one probability-one event and simultaneously for all \(k<\ell\),
\[
 M_{k\ell}
 =\max\!\left\{
 n_{00}^{k\ell}+n_{10}^{k\ell},
 n_{00}^{k\ell}+n_{01}^{k\ell},
 n_{00}^{k\ell}+\mathbf 1\{n_{11}^{k\ell}>0\},
 \min(2,h)
 \right\}
 =M_{k\ell}^{\mathrm{gen}}. \tag{23}
\]
This application counts indexed observations, so coincident zero-shift points contribute with their full multiplicity.

By \(\text{def:environment-distance}\),
\[
 \delta_H>c
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 M_{k\ell}<h-c\quad\text{for every }k<\ell. \tag{24}
\]
Substitution of (23) into (24) gives exactly the displayed four-term support criterion.  The support factorization, \(Z_{ek}\in\{0,1\}\), and \(a_{ek}>0\) imply \(s_{ek}\ge0\), consistently with \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\).  If the criterion holds, the positive-semidefinite sufficiency part of \(\text{thm:sharp-replacement-radius}\), together with \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\), proves \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)=\{D\}\) for every \(D\in\mathcal D\), every \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\), and every positive-semidefinite replacement assignment.

Conversely, if the criterion fails, then \(\delta_H\le c\).  Choose \(D=I_p\), which belongs to \(\mathcal D\) because it is invertible, has unit diagonal, and \(\operatorname{CP}(0)=0<1\), and choose \(\Omega=I_p\in\mathbb S_{++}^p\).  The honest covariances \(I_p+\operatorname{diag}(s_e)\) are positive definite.  The fixed-interior converse in \(\text{thm:sharp-replacement-radius}\) supplies positive-definite replacements and a second admissible structural matrix.  Hence a uniform guarantee over the full positive-semidefinite class fails.  This proves necessity at the class level through its positive-definite subclass, without making a pointwise assertion at a singular invariant-noise boundary.

The term \(\min(2,h)\) makes the strict inequality in (24) possible only if \(h-c\ge3\).  Since \(h=m-c\),
\[
 m\ge2c+3. \tag{25}
\]
The lower bound is attained.  When \(m=2c+3\), take the all-active pattern \(Z_{ek}=1\).  Then, for every \(k<\ell\),
\[
 n_{11}^{k\ell}=h,\qquad
 n_{00}^{k\ell}=n_{10}^{k\ell}=n_{01}^{k\ell}=0,
 \qquad M_{k\ell}^{\mathrm{gen}}=2<h-c=3. \tag{26}
\]
Thus an all-active support pattern attains the smallest possible environment count almost surely.
