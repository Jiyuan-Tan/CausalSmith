For arbitrary nonempty positive-semidefinite covariance regions, \(\text{thm:confidence-union-coverage}\) proves
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\},
\]
and hence, whenever the displayed events are measurable,
\[
\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
Under the four true-side inequalities
\[
\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0,
\qquad
\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0,
\qquad
\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa,
\qquad
M_H\leq\overline M,
\]
and only the candidate-side condition-number bound in \(\text{thm:non-effective-four-margin-contraction}\), that theorem supplies a non-effective \(r_0>0\) such that, on \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\),
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\]
The candidate covariance, nuisance, and shift scale bounds follow locally from the true scale bound, candidate conditioning, and radius at most one. No covariance-interiority, candidate affine-separation, candidate normalization-slack, or candidate scale assumption is required. These results provide no executable union representation, exact decision routine, or effective radius.
