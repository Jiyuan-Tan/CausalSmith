The answer to \(\text{oeq:certified-uniform-inference}\) is partial and sharp with respect to the presently certified scope. Fix \(p\geq2\), \(m\geq1\), \(c\in\{0,\ldots,m-1\}\), put \(h=m-c\), and fix \(n\geq1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), rational thresholds \(\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0\in\mathbb Q_{>0}\), and rational bounds \(\overline\kappa,\overline M\in\mathbb Q\cap[1,\infty)\).

The following representation and classification conclusions are unconditional on a BACKSHIFT model. For every realized nonempty rational-semialgebraic covariance-region family supplied by \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\), \(\text{CertificateInferenceHandle}\) terminates. Its first quantifier-elimination output is a finite quantifier-free rational-polynomial formula whose realization is exactly the complete confidence union \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). Its universal audit returns that exact full-union code precisely when every full feasible candidate satisfies
\[
\gamma(w)\geq\gamma_0,\qquad
\zeta(w)\geq\zeta_0,\qquad
\lambda(w)\geq\lambda_0,\qquad
\kappa(w)\leq\overline\kappa,\qquad
M(w)\leq\overline M;
\]
otherwise it returns \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\). In particular, any feasible candidate with \(\gamma(w)=0\) forces abstention. For every rational population covariance family, finite rational-polynomial sentences decide whether its compatibility set is empty, is a singleton, or contains at least two matrices. The certificates use the entries of the normalized matrix globally, so no normalization chart is required. An auxiliary finite rational-semialgebraic chart may be introduced by adjoining its polynomial graph and its denominator-sign cells before projection; doing so does not change the resulting matrix-space set.

The coverage and contraction conclusions are model-conditional. Suppose a fixed honest instance satisfies \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\), and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\), together with the theorem-local condition \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\). Define
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}
=\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\}.
\]
Then
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\},
\qquad
\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha
\ \Longrightarrow\ 
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
On a realization in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\), if the universal five-margin audit releases the exact union code, then the residual-gap construction in \(\text{def:audited-release-contraction}\) gives an effectively computable \(r_0>0\), and
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\]
where
\[
L_0=(p!\,\overline\kappa^{p-1})^{1/p},\qquad
K_0=\frac{6\overline M}{\gamma_0}\sqrt{p(p-1)(1+L_0^2)},
\qquad C_0=16K_0L_0^3.
\]
These bounds are uniform for fixed \(p,m,c,\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0,\overline\kappa,\overline M\). Along any sequence of such coverage outcomes on which the audit releases, \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0\), and \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\), both displayed set radii converge to zero. No contraction conclusion is asserted when the audit abstains or when the radius exceeds \(r_0\), and no numerical or CRAN implementation is claimed.
