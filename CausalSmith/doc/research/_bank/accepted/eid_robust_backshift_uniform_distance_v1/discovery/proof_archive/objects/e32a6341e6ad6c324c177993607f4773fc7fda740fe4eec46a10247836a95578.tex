For arbitrary nonempty positive-semidefinite covariance regions, \(\text{thm:confidence-union-coverage}\) proves the set inclusion
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\},
\]
and hence, whenever the displayed events are measurable,
\[
\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq 1-\alpha
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\geq 1-\alpha.
\]
Under the four true-side inequalities
\[
\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0,
\qquad
\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0,
\qquad
\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa,
\qquad
M_H\leq\overline M,
\]
and only the candidate-side normalization-slack and condition-number inequalities stated in \(\text{thm:non-effective-four-margin-contraction}\), that theorem provides a non-effective number \(r_0>0\) such that, on \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\),
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq 2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\]
The proof also derives the needed local overlap bounds on candidate covariance, invariant-noise, and diagonal-shift scales from \(M_H\leq\overline M\), candidate conditioning, and an entry radius at most one; no covariance-interiority assumption and no candidate-side affine-separation or scale assumption is required. These are the strongest closed analytic and set-theoretic conclusions. They neither supply an executable representation of the union nor make the compactness radius effective.
