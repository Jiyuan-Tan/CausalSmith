For each \(n\ge1\) and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}=\bigcup\{\mathcal R_c(\Gamma):\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in E\}\). On the simultaneous honest-slot coverage event \(\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\}\), take \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\) for \(e\in H\) and choose an arbitrary point of the nonempty region \(\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\) for each \(e\in E\setminus H\); then \(D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\subseteq\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}\). Thus simultaneous honest-slot coverage of probability at least \(1-\alpha\) yields \(\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}\}\ge1-\alpha\). If every region is the singleton \(\{\Sigma_e\}\) and \(\delta_H>c\), the sharp replacement-radius theorem gives the unlabeled sanity conclusion \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}=\{D\}\).
