For every \(n\geq1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and indexed family of nonempty sets \(\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\subseteq\mathbb S_{++}^{p}\), define the set-theoretic confidence union
\[
\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}
=\bigcup_{\Gamma\in\prod_{e\in E}\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}}
\mathcal R_c(\Gamma).
\]
Thus \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}\) is the union of the population replacement-compatibility sets over all simultaneous selections from the covariance regions. This definition asserts neither computability nor a coverage property.
