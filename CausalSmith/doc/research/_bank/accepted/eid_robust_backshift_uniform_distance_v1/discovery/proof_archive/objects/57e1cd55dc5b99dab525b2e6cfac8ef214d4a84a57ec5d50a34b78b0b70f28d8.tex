Fix \(n\geq1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and an arbitrary indexed family of nonempty covariance regions \(\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\subseteq\mathbb S_{+}^{p}\), \(e\in E\). Under \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\), and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\), suppose additionally that \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\), and define
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}
=\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\}.
\]
Then
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\]
Consequently, whenever the displayed events are measurable,
\[
\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
This is a set-theoretic coverage implication and makes no representation or computability claim.
