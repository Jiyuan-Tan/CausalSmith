The answer to \(\text{oeq:certified-uniform-inference}\) is partial and sharp with respect to the presently certified scope. For rational population covariances, finite rational-polynomial sentences decide whether the compatibility output is empty, a singleton, or has at least two elements. The certificates are written globally in the entries of the normalized matrix, so no normalization chart is required; auxiliary finite rational-semialgebraic charts may be changed by adjoining their polynomial graph and denominator-sign cells before projection, without changing the resulting matrix-space set.
