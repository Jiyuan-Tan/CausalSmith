\textbf{Coverage under positive-semidefinite invariant noise.}
Fix \(n\geq1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and a sample outcome in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\). For \(e\in H\), put \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\). For each of the finitely many \(e\in E\setminus H\), choose \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\); such a choice exists because nonemptiness is part of \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\). Take
\[
A=D,\qquad K=H,\qquad \Psi=\Omega,\qquad t_e=s_e\quad(e\in H).
\]
The definition of \(H\) gives \(|K|=h\). Assumption \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\) gives \(A=D\in\mathcal D\). The theorem-local condition \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\) gives \(\Psi\in\mathbb S_+^p\), and \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\) gives \(t_e\in[0,\infty)^p\). Finally, membership in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\) and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\) give, for every \(e\in K=H\),
\[
\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e
=D^{-1}\bigl(\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e)\bigr)D^{-\mathsf T}
=A^{-1}\bigl(\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\bigr)A^{-\mathsf T}.
\]
These are exactly the witnesses required by \(\text{def:compatible-set}\), so \(D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\). By \(\text{def:confidence-union}\), \(D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). This proves
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\]
Monotonicity of probability then gives
\[
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}
\geq\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha.
\]
Thus the coverage argument requires only \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\).

\textbf{The exact projected code.}
Fix a realized encoded family and abbreviate its rational formula by \(\rho_e(\Gamma_e)\). For a set \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|=h\), expand the formula \(F_K\) from \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\). All matrix equalities are entrywise polynomial equalities. Invertibility is the polynomial inequality \((\det A)^2>0\). If \(\mathfrak C_p\) is the finite set of directed simple cycles of length at least two and \(\pi_C(A)\) is the product of the corresponding entries of \(I_p-A\), then
\[
\operatorname{CP}(I_p-A)<1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
-1<\pi_C(A)<1\quad\text{for every }C\in\mathfrak C_p,
\]
by the definition of \(\operatorname{CP}\). The right side is expressed by \(1-\pi_C(A)>0\) and \(1+\pi_C(A)>0\). Positive semidefiniteness of the symmetric variable \(\Psi\) is, by the definition of that cone, the first-order condition
\[
\forall v\in\mathbb R^p,\qquad v^{\mathsf T}\Psi v\geq0.
\]
Positive definiteness of a symmetric \(\Gamma_e\) is likewise the first-order condition
\[
\forall v\in\mathbb R^p,\qquad
\left(\sum_{j=1}^p v_j^2=0\right)
\ \vee\ v^{\mathsf T}\Gamma_ev>0.
\]
The region atom \(\rho_e(\Gamma_e)\) is a finite Boolean combination of rational-polynomial signs by \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\). The remaining clauses \(A_{ii}=1\), \(t_{ej}\geq0\), and
\[
A\Gamma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)
\]
are polynomial conditions with integer coefficients. Hence \(F_K\) is a first-order ordered-field formula with rational-polynomial atoms and contains every feasibility requirement listed in the theorem.

Let
\[
\Phi_{\mathcal U}(A)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\bigvee_{\substack{K\subseteq E\\|K|=h}}
\exists\Psi\,\exists(t_e)_{e\in K}\,\exists(\Gamma_e)_{e\in K} F_K.
\]
We prove equality between its realization and \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\) in both directions. First suppose \(A\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). By \(\text{def:confidence-union}\), there is a full family \(\Gamma\), with \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\) for every \(e\in E\), such that \(A\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\). By \(\text{def:compatible-set}\), there are a set \(K_0\subseteq E\) with \(|K_0|\geq h\), a matrix \(\Psi\in\mathbb S_+^p\), and nonnegative shifts \(t_e\) satisfying the covariance equations on \(K_0\). Choose any \(K\subseteq K_0\) with \(|K|=h\). Restriction of these witnesses to \(K\) satisfies \(F_K\), so \(\Phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\) holds.

Conversely, suppose \(\Phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\) holds. A disjunct supplies \(K\) of size \(h\) and witnesses satisfying \(F_K\). The determinant clause permits multiplication of
\[
A\Gamma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)
\]
by \(A^{-1}\) and \(A^{-\mathsf T}\), yielding
\[
\Gamma_e=A^{-1}\bigl(\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\bigr)A^{-\mathsf T}
\qquad(e\in K).
\]
The unit-diagonal, cycle, positive-semidefinite, and nonnegativity clauses therefore give \(A\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\) on \(K\). For every \(e\notin K\), choose an arbitrary \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\), possible by nonemptiness; the values outside \(K\) do not enter the compatibility witness. Thus the completed family is among those in \(\text{def:confidence-union}\), and \(A\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). We have proved
\[
\{A:\Phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\}=\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha.
\]
There are exactly \(\binom mh\) disjuncts. Applying \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\) to their finite disjunction terminates and returns an equivalent quantifier-free formula with rational-polynomial sign atoms. Its realization is therefore exactly the complete confidence union.

\textbf{Polynomial margin audit.}
It remains to show that the five candidate-margin tests in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\) are effective and exact. For \(e<f<g\) and \(k<\ell\), the affine minor \(\Delta^{k\ell}_{efg}(t)\) is a polynomial. When \(h-c\geq3\), the condition \(\gamma(w)\geq\gamma_0\) is exactly the finite Boolean formula
\[
\bigwedge_{1\leq k<\ell\leq p}
\ \bigwedge_{\substack{S\subseteq K\\|S|=h-c}}
\ \bigvee_{e<f<g\in S}
\left(\Delta^{k\ell}_{efg}(t)\geq\gamma_0
\ \vee\ -\Delta^{k\ell}_{efg}(t)\geq\gamma_0\right).
\]
This is precisely the expansion of the finite minimum--maximum defining \(\gamma(w)\). When \(h-c<3\), the condition is false because \(\gamma(w)=0<\gamma_0\). The cycle condition \(\zeta(w)\geq\zeta_0\) is exactly
\[
\bigwedge_{C\in\mathfrak C_p}
\left(\pi_C(A)\leq1-\zeta_0\right)
\wedge
\left(-\pi_C(A)\leq1-\zeta_0\right).
\]
The positive-definiteness condition \(\lambda(w)\geq\lambda_0\) is exactly
\[
\forall v\in\mathbb R^p,\quad
v^{\mathsf T}(\Psi-\lambda_0I_p)v\geq0,
\qquad
\forall e\in K\ \forall v\in\mathbb R^p,\qquad
v^{\mathsf T}(\Gamma_e-\lambda_0I_p)v\geq0,
\]
by the Rayleigh-quotient definition of the least eigenvalue.

For the condition number, define the first-order formula
\[
\mathsf{Cond}_{\overline\kappa}(A)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\exists q>0\ \forall v\in\mathbb R^p,\qquad
q\sum_jv_j^2\leq\sum_i(Av)_i^2
\leq\overline\kappa^2q\sum_jv_j^2.
\]
If it holds, the upper inequality gives \(\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\overline\kappa\sqrt q\). Applying the lower inequality to \(v=A^{-1}u\) gives \(\|A^{-1}u\|_2\leq q^{-1/2}\|u\|_2\), hence \(\|A^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq q^{-1/2}\) and \(\kappa(w)\leq\overline\kappa\). Conversely, if \(\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\|A^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\overline\kappa\), set \(q=\|A^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}^{-2}>0\). The norm inequalities
\[
\|Av\|_2\leq\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\|v\|_2,
\qquad
\|v\|_2=\|A^{-1}Av\|_2\leq\|A^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\|Av\|_2
\]
give both clauses in \(\mathsf{Cond}_{\overline\kappa}(A)\). Thus this polynomial first-order formula is equivalent to the stated condition-number bound.

For scale, let \(\mathsf{NormBd}(X,r)\) abbreviate
\[
r\geq0\quad\wedge\quad
\forall v\in\mathbb R^p,\qquad
\sum_i(Xv)_i^2\leq r^2\sum_jv_j^2.
\]
By the definition of the operator norm, this is equivalent to \(\|X\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq r\). Therefore \(M(w)\leq\overline M\) is equivalent to existence of \(r_\Psi,r_\Gamma,r_t\geq0\) such that
\[
\begin{gathered}
\mathsf{NormBd}(\Psi,r_\Psi),\qquad
\mathsf{NormBd}(\Gamma_e,r_\Gamma)\quad(e\in K),\\
\mathsf{NormBd}(\operatorname{diag}(t_e),r_t)\quad(e\in K),
\qquad
1+r_\Psi+r_\Gamma+r_t\leq\overline M.
\end{gathered}
\]
Indeed, any such bounds dominate the three maxima in \(M(w)\); conversely one may choose the three actual maxima of the finitely many operator norms. This is again a first-order rational-polynomial formula.

All threshold coefficients are rational by hypothesis. Hence the sentence
\[
\mathsf{Bad}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\bigvee_{\substack{K\subseteq E\\|K|=h}}
\exists A\,\exists\Psi\,\exists t\,\exists\Gamma_K
\left[F_K\wedge\neg\mathsf{Good}(w)
\right],
\]
where \(\mathsf{Good}(w)\) is the conjunction of the five displayed margin conditions, is a closed first-order formula over rational polynomial signs. By \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), its truth is decidable in finite time. It is false exactly when every feasible candidate satisfies every threshold. Thus the procedure returns the already-computed exact union code exactly after a clean universal audit and returns \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\) otherwise. If a feasible tuple has \(\gamma(w)=0\), then \(\gamma_0>0\) makes \(\mathsf{Good}(w)\) false, so \(\mathsf{Bad}\) is true and the procedure abstains.

The finite-membership-query necessity clause is exactly \(\text{lem:finite-membership-query-obstruction}\), which the present theorem consumes only to justify the encoded input contract. No step above supplies an inverse perturbation inequality for joint diagonalization, and no bounded candidate envelope was assumed; accordingly the proof establishes no contraction assertion. Finally, \(B=I_p-D\) is fixed as the causal structural target independently of the observed-region set \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). The first part derives the advertised coverage relation between them.
