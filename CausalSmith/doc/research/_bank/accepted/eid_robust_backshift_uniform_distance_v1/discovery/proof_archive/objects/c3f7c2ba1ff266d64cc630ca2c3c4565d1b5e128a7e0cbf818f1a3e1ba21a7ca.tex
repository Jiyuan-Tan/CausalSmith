Fix the integers, probability level, and rational thresholds in the statement. We first establish the assertions that do not use a BACKSHIFT model. By \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\), after the sample outcome is fixed, each region is the nonempty realization of a finite Boolean formula in rational-polynomial sign conditions. The formula \(F_K(w)\) in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\) adds only finitely many polynomial equalities and inequalities. Indeed, inverse relations are expressed by \(AV=VA=I_p\), positive semidefiniteness and positive definiteness are expressed by principal-minor sign conditions, and cycle normalization is expressed by
\[
1-\pi_C(A)>0,
\qquad
1+\pi_C(A)>0
\]
for each of the finitely many directed simple cycles \(C\). The same definition gives first-order real-polynomial representations of the operator-norm, eigenvalue, and finite minimum--maximum conditions that enter the five margins. Hence its projection formula \(\Phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\) and its closed audit sentence satisfy the hypotheses of \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\). The exact projection, termination, and audit equivalence then follow from \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\): the projection realizes \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\), and the procedure releases its code exactly when no feasible candidate violates any of the five inequalities. If a feasible candidate has \(\gamma(w)=0\), then \(\gamma_0>0\) gives
\[
\gamma(w)=0<\gamma_0,
\]
so the audit sentence is true and the procedure returns \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\).

We next prove the rational-population classification. Fix a rational covariance family \(\Sigma\), and let \(\Phi_\Sigma(A)\) be the compatibility predicate obtained by expanding \(\text{def:compatible-set}\). For each of the finitely many \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|\geq h\), that predicate existentially quantifies \(\Psi\in\mathbb S_+^p\) and \(t_e\in[0,\infty)^p\). Since \(A\in\mathcal D\subseteq\mathrm{GL}_p(\mathbb R)\), multiplication on the left by \(A\) and on the right by \(A^{\mathsf T}\) gives, for every \(e\in K\),
\[
\Sigma_e=A^{-1}\bigl(\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\bigr)A^{-\mathsf T}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e).
\]
Together with the unit-diagonal, nonzero-determinant, and finitely many cycle inequalities defining \(A\in\mathcal D\), these are rational-polynomial conditions because every entry of \(\Sigma\) is rational. Thus \(\Phi_\Sigma\) is a first-order formula over the ordered field of real numbers with rational-polynomial atoms. Define the closed formulas
\[
\mathsf E_0:=\neg\exists A\,\Phi_\Sigma(A),
\]
\[
\mathsf E_1:=\exists A\left[\Phi_\Sigma(A)\wedge
\forall A'\left(\Phi_\Sigma(A')\Rightarrow
\sum_{i,j=1}^p(A_{ij}-A'_{ij})^2=0\right)\right],
\]
and
\[
\mathsf E_{\geq2}:=\exists A\,\exists A'\left[
\Phi_\Sigma(A)\wedge\Phi_\Sigma(A')\wedge
\sum_{i,j=1}^p(A_{ij}-A'_{ij})^2>0\right].
\]
By \(\text{def:compatible-set}\), these say respectively that \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)\) is empty, is a singleton, or contains at least two matrices. Elementary set trichotomy shows that exactly one holds, and \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\) decides each formula. This proves the classification.

The free coordinates of \(\Phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\) are the entries of \(A\), so the construction uses no normalization chart. Suppose an auxiliary rational chart with coordinate \(u\) represents an entry, on a fixed denominator-sign cell, by
\[
A_{ij}=\frac{P_{ij}(u)}{Q_{ij}(u)},
\qquad Q_{ij}(u)\neq0.
\]
On that cell, this relation is equivalent to the polynomial graph equation
\[
Q_{ij}(u)A_{ij}-P_{ij}(u)=0
\]
together with the selected strict sign of \(Q_{ij}(u)\). Adjoining these graph equations and existentially projecting out \(u\) therefore returns exactly the same matrices \(A\). Projection is effective by \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), and taking the finite union over denominator-sign cells proves chart invariance.

We now impose \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\), and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\), together with the theorem-local condition \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\). Consider an outcome in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\). For every \(e\in H\), choose \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\), which is permitted by the definition of \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\). Each region indexed by \(e\in E\setminus H\) is nonempty by \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\), and this index set is finite, so choose one \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\) for every such \(e\). For \(e\in H\), the three structural assumptions give
\[
D\Gamma_eD^{\mathsf T}
=D\Sigma_eD^{\mathsf T}
=\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e),
\]
where \(D\in\mathcal D\) by \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\) by the local premise, and \(s_e\in[0,\infty)^p\) by \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\). Since \(|H|=h\), the choices \(K=H\), \(A=D\), \(\Psi=\Omega\), and \(t_e=s_e\) are feasible in \(\text{def:compatible-set}\). Therefore
\[
D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)
\subseteq
\bigcup_{\substack{\widetilde\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\\e\in E}}
\mathcal R_c(\widetilde\Gamma)
=\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,
\]
where the last equality is \(\text{def:confidence-union}\). Thus
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\]
Monotonicity of probability now yields
\[
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}
\geq\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H}),
\]
which proves the stated coverage implication when \(\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha\).

It remains to derive the premises needed for contraction. Every encoded covariance region is contained in \(\mathbb S_{++}^p\), so on \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\) every \(\Sigma_e\), \(e\in H\), is positive definite. In the full candidate formula for \(K=H\), take
\[
A=D,
\qquad V=D^{-1},
\qquad \Psi=\Omega,
\qquad t_e=s_e,
\qquad \Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\quad(e\in H).
\]
The preceding covariance equations, cone conditions, inverse relations, and normalization conditions show that this is a full feasible candidate, call it \(w_0\). If the universal audit releases, then every feasible candidate, and hence \(w_0\), satisfies all five audited inequalities. The margin definitions in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\), evaluated at \(w_0\), agree term by term with the true-instance definitions:
\[
\gamma(w_0)=\gamma_H,
\qquad
\zeta(w_0)=\zeta_D,
\qquad
\lambda(w_0)=\lambda_H,
\qquad
\kappa(w_0)=\kappa_D,
\qquad
M(w_0)=M_H.
\]
Consequently a released audit implies
\[
\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0,
\qquad
\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0,
\qquad
\lambda_H\geq\lambda_0,
\qquad
\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa,
\qquad
M_H\leq\overline M.
\]
These are exactly the five true-instance margin hypotheses of \(\text{thm:audited-release-contraction}\); its other hypotheses are the three structural assumptions already imposed. That theorem therefore yields \(\underline\mu>0\), effective computability of \(r_0>0\), and, whenever \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0\),
\[
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\]
with the displayed values of \(L_0,K_0,C_0\). By that theorem, these constants depend only on \(p,m,c,\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0,\overline\kappa,\overline M\), proving uniformity. On a sequence of covered and released outcomes with \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\), positivity of the fixed \(r_0\) implies \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0\) eventually, and both right-hand sides then converge to zero. The argument invoked coverage, release, and the small-radius inequality before deriving contraction, so it gives no contraction statement on abstaining outcomes or beyond \(r_0\), as claimed.
