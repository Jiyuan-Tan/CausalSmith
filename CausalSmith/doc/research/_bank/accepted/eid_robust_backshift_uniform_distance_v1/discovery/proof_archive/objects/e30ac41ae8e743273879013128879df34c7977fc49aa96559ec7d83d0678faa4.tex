Fix an outcome in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\). For \(e\in H\), set \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\). For each \(e\in E\setminus H\), choose one \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\); these finitely many choices exist because every region is nonempty. Put
\[
K=H,\qquad A=D,\qquad \Psi=\Omega,\qquad t_e=s_e\quad(e\in H).
\tag{41}
\]
The definition of \(H\) gives \(|K|=h\). Assumption \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\) and \(\text{def:admissible-matrices}\) give \(A=D\in\mathcal D\). The theorem-local premise gives \(\Psi=\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\), and \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\) gives \(t_e=s_e\in[0,\infty)^p\). Finally, the coverage event and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\) give, for every \(e\in K\),
\[
\Gamma_e
=\Sigma_e
=D^{-1}\bigl(\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e)\bigr)D^{-\mathsf T}
=A^{-1}\bigl(\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\bigr)A^{-\mathsf T}.
\tag{42}
\]
Thus the tuple in (41) witnesses \(D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\) by \(\text{def:compatible-set}\). Since every selected \(\Gamma_e\) belongs to its region, \(\text{def:confidence-union}\) now gives \(D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). The outcome was arbitrary, so
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\tag{43}
\]
For measurable events, monotonicity of probability applied to (43) yields the displayed probability implication. The structural target \(D\), and therefore \(B=I_p-D\), was fixed by the causal equilibrium model before \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\) was formed; equations (41)--(43) prove the coverage relation between them.
