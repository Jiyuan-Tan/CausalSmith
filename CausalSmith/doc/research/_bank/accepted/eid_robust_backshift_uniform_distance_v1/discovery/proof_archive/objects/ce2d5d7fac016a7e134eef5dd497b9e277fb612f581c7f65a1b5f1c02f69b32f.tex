The proved representation result \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\) already gives exact rational-semialgebraic projection, the coverage implication, chart-independent polynomial certificates, and mandatory release of the full feasible union or \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\) after the five-margin audit.  There is also an explicit local inverse bound, which isolates the remaining issue.

Put \(q=h-c\), fix a set \(S\subseteq H\) of cardinality \(q\), and suppose that a candidate \(A\in\mathcal D\) fits matrices \(\Gamma_e\) for \(e\in S\) with
\[
 \max_{e\in S}\|\Gamma_e-\Sigma_e\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le r.
\]
Write
\[
 Q=AD^{-1}=I_p+R,
 \qquad
 L=\bigl(p!\,\kappa_D^{p-1}\bigr)^{1/p},
 \qquad
 K=\frac{6M_H}{\gamma_H}
 \sqrt{p(p-1)(1+L^2)}.
\]
The determinant expansion of a unit-diagonal matrix is a sum over permutations.  Every nonidentity term is a product of disjoint directed cycle products, each of absolute value less than one by \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\); hence \(|\det D|\le p!\).  If \(\sigma_{max}(D)=\|D\|_{\mathrm{op}}\), then the definition of \(\kappa_D\) gives every other singular value at least \(\sigma_{max}(D)/\kappa_D\).  Therefore
\[
 \frac{\sigma_{max}(D)^p}{\kappa_D^{p-1}}
 \le |\det D|\le p!,
 \qquad\text{so}\qquad
 \|D\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le L.
 \tag{1}
\]

Choose \(e_0\in S\) and set \(x_e=s_e-s_{e_0}\).  For each \(i<j\), the definition of \(\gamma_H\) supplies a triple in \(S\) whose \((i,j)\) affine minor has absolute value at least \(\gamma_H\).  Expressing that affine minor as a sum of three determinants based at \(e_0\) shows, by the triangle inequality, that there are \(e,f\in S\) such that
\[
 |x_{ei}x_{fj}-x_{ej}x_{fi}|\ge \gamma_H/3.
 \tag{2}
\]
The candidate and honest covariance equations have invariant terms that cancel under differences.  With \(E_e=\Gamma_e-\Sigma_e\), their \((i,j)\) off-diagonal entries give
\[
 R_{ij}x_{ej}+R_{ji}x_{ei}
 =-\sum_{k=1}^p R_{ik}R_{jk}x_{ek}
   -[A(E_e-E_{e_0})A^{\mathsf T}]_{ij}.
 \tag{3}
\]
The definition of \(M_H\) and nonnegativity of the shifts imply \(|x_{ek}|\le M_H\).  If \(u=\|R\|_{\mathrm F}\), then (1), (3), and \(\|E_e-E_{e_0}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\le2r\) yield
\[
 |R_{ij}x_{ej}+R_{ji}x_{ei}|
 \le M_Hu^2+2rL^2(1+u)^2.
 \tag{4}
\]
Apply (4) at the two indices in (2) and invert the resulting \(2\times2\) system.  Since all its entries have absolute value at most \(M_H\), Cramer's rule gives, for every ordered pair \(i\ne j\),
\[
 |R_{ij}|
 \le \frac{6M_H}{\gamma_H}
 \{M_Hu^2+2rL^2(1+u)^2\}.
 \tag{5}
\]
The unit diagonals of \(A\) and \(D\) imply
\[
 0=(A-D)_{ii}=(RD)_{ii}
 =R_{ii}+\sum_{k\ne i}R_{ik}D_{ki}.
 \tag{6}
\]
Combining (5), (6), and Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\[
 u\le K\{M_Hu^2+2rL^2(1+u)^2\}.
 \tag{7}
\]
Consequently, whenever
\[
 u\le \rho_{\mathrm{loc}}
 :=\min\left\{1,\frac{1}{2KM_H}\right\},
\]
equation (7) implies
\[
 u\le16KL^2r,
 \qquad
 \|A-D\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \le16KL^3r.
 \tag{8}
\]
Thus the desired linear modulus is proved for every feasible candidate already lying in the explicit identity-chart neighborhood \(\|AD^{-1}-I_p\|_{\mathrm F}\le\rho_{\mathrm{loc}}\).  On the simultaneous honest-slot coverage event, \(D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\) by \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\), so (8), if established for every member of the union, would also give the claimed diameter inequality by the triangle inequality.

What is not proved is a positive \(r_0\), depending only on the five true-instance margins, for which every feasible member of the unrestricted union must enter this identity-chart neighborhood.  That assertion requires a global coercivity or properness theorem for congruence joint diagonalization across candidates with unbounded norm, vanishing determinant, or cycle product tending to one.  The exact uniqueness lemma applies only after the limiting alternative remains an invertible member of the open normalized parameter space, so it does not exclude those boundary sequences.  The candidate-side five-margin audit in \(\text{CertificateInferenceHandle}\) detects them and abstains, but an audit is not an unconditional proof that they cannot occur.  Hence the representation, coverage, boundary abstention, and local linear modulus are established, while the requested unconditional full-union contraction remains open.
