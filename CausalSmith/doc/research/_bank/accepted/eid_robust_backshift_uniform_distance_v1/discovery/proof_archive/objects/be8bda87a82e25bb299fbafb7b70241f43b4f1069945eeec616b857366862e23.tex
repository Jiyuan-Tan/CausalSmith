The strongest closed result is as follows. For arbitrary nonempty positive-semidefinite covariance regions, \(\text{thm:confidence-union-coverage}\) proves \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\). Under the four true-side bounds \(\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0\), \(\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0\), \(\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa\), and \(M_H\leq\overline M\), and only candidate-side normalization-slack and condition-number bounds, \(\text{thm:non-effective-four-margin-contraction}\) gives an existential \(r_0>0\) such that
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\]
The proof derives local bounds \(\|\Gamma_e\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\overline M\) and \(\|\Psi\|_{\mathrm{op}},\|\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq L_0^2\overline M\) on the overlap when the entry radius is at most one. The radius \(r_0\) remains a non-effective positive compactness minimum, and no covariance-interiority margin is used.
