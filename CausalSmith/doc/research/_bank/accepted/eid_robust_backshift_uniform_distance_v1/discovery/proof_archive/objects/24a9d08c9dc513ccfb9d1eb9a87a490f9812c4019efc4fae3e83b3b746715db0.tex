Under the prespecified support pattern \(Z\) and absolutely continuous active amplitudes, simultaneously for every \(k<\ell\), \(M_{k\ell}=M_{k\ell}^{\mathrm{gen}}\) almost surely. Consequently, corruption-robust identification holds almost surely if and only if, for every \(k<\ell\), \(\max\{n_{00}^{k\ell}+n_{10}^{k\ell},n_{00}^{k\ell}+n_{01}^{k\ell},n_{00}^{k\ell}+\mathbf 1\{n_{11}^{k\ell}>0\},\min(2,h)\}<h-c\). In particular, any robustly identifying support pattern satisfies \(m\ge2c+3\), and this environment-count lower bound is attained by support patterns with no larger forced affine incidence.
