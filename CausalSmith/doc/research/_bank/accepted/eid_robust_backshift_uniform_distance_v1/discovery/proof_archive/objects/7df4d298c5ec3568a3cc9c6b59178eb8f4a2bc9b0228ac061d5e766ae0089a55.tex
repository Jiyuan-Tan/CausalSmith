Write a normalized candidate as
\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&u\\v&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Any feasible explanation must congruence-diagonalize both covariance differences. Since
\[
\Sigma_2-\Sigma_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,0),
\qquad
\Sigma_3-\Sigma_1=\operatorname{diag}(0,1),
\]
their transformed off-diagonal entries are, respectively,
\[
\left[A\operatorname{diag}(1,0)A^{\mathsf T}\right]_{12}=v,
\qquad
\left[A\operatorname{diag}(0,1)A^{\mathsf T}\right]_{12}=u.
\]
Feasibility forces both to vanish, so \(u=v=0\) and \(A=I_2\). Conversely, \(I_2\) is feasible. Indeed, for any \(a,b\in[0,2]\), take
\[
\Psi=\operatorname{diag}(a,b),
\]
\[
t_1=(2-a,2-b),\qquad
t_2=(3-a,2-b),\qquad
t_3=(2-a,3-b).
\]
Then \(\Psi\succeq0\), every shift is nonnegative, and \(\Sigma_e=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\). Conversely, once \(A=I_2\), the off-diagonal covariance equations force \(\Psi\) to be diagonal; writing it as \(\operatorname{diag}(a,b)\), PSD feasibility gives \(a,b\geq0\), while nonnegativity of all three shifts gives \(a,b\leq2\). Thus the displayed family is exactly the set of nuisance gauges and \(\mathcal R_0(\Sigma)=\{I_2\}\).

The three shift points are
\[
(2-a,2-b),\qquad(3-a,2-b),\qquad(2-a,3-b),
\]
whose affine determinant is one. Since \(h-c=3\), the definition gives \(\gamma=1\). Also \(\operatorname{CP}(I_2-I_2)=0\), so \(\zeta=1\), and \(\kappa(I_2)=1\). The least covariance eigenvalue is two, hence \(\lambda_{\mathrm{cov}}=2\). Finally,
\[
M
=1+\|\Psi\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\max_e\|\Sigma_e\|_{\mathrm{op}}
+\max_e\|\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq1+2+3+3=9.
\]
Every full feasible tuple therefore passes the five thresholds \((1,1,2,1,9)\). The singleton regions have finite rational-semialgebraic codes. Exactness and termination from \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\), together with the universal bounds just proved, therefore make \(\text{CertificateInferenceHandle}\) release the union \(\{I_2\}\).
