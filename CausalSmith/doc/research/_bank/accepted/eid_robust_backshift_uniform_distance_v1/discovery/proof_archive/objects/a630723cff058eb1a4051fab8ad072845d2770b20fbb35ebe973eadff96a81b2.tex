Fix the integers, probability level, and rational thresholds in the statement. By \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\), after the sample outcome is fixed, each region is the nonempty realization of a finite Boolean formula in rational-polynomial sign conditions. The full formula \(F_K\) in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\) adds only finitely many polynomial equalities and inequalities. In particular, positive semidefiniteness of \(\Psi\), positive definiteness of the covariance variables, invertibility, cycle normalization, and the five candidate margins all have first-order real-polynomial representations. Under the gauge-invariant repair, the third margin is precisely
\[
\lambda_{\mathrm{cov}}(w)=\min_{e\in K}\lambda_{\min}(\Gamma_e),
\]
so its threshold is \(\Gamma_e\succeq\lambda_0I_p\) for every \(e\in K\), while feasibility retains only \(\Psi\succeq0\). The exact projection, termination, and audit equivalence now follow from \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\). A feasible candidate with \(\gamma(w)=0\) violates \(\gamma(w)\geq\gamma_0\) because \(\gamma_0>0\), and therefore forces \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\).

For a rational population covariance family \(\Sigma\), let \(\Phi_\Sigma(A)\) be the compatibility predicate obtained by expanding \(\text{def:compatible-set}\). It is first-order rational-polynomial: multiply every covariance equation by \(A\) and \(A^{\mathsf T}\), and express the normalized admissible set by unit-diagonal equations, a nonzero determinant, and finitely many cycle inequalities. Define
\[
\mathsf E_0:=\neg\exists A\,\Phi_\Sigma(A),
\]
\[
\mathsf E_1:=\exists A\left[\Phi_\Sigma(A)\wedge
\forall A'\left(\Phi_\Sigma(A')\Rightarrow
\sum_{i,j=1}^p(A_{ij}-A'_{ij})^2=0\right)\right],
\]
and
\[
\mathsf E_{\geq2}:=\exists A\,\exists A'\left[
\Phi_\Sigma(A)\wedge\Phi_\Sigma(A')\wedge
\sum_{i,j=1}^p(A_{ij}-A'_{ij})^2>0\right].
\]
By \(\text{def:compatible-set}\), these formulas say that \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)\) is empty, is a singleton, or contains at least two matrices. Elementary set trichotomy makes exactly one true, and \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\) decides each. The projected formula is already in the entries of \(A\), so it uses no chart; replacing a rational chart by its polynomial graph on denominator-sign cells and projecting the chart coordinates preserves the represented matrix set.

Now impose the three structural assumptions and \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\). On an outcome in

G_{n,\alpha,H}=\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\},
\]
choose \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\) on \(H\) and arbitrary region members outside \(H\). The choices

K=H,\qquad A=D,\qquad \Psi=\Omega,\qquad t_e=s_e

\[
D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\subseteq\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,

which proves the event inclusion and, by probability monotonicity, the coverage implication.

\qquad

Thus audit release implies all five true-instance margins required by \(\text{thm:audited-release-contraction}\). Notice that this implication uses no positive lower eigenvalue for \(\Omega\): the nuisance is merely PSD, and the audited eigenvalue threshold concerns the covered covariance matrices \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\).
