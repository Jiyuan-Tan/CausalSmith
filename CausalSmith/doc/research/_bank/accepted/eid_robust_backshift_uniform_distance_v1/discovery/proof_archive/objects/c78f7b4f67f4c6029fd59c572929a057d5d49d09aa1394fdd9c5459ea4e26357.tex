We separate exact symbolic computation, the margin audit, and causal coverage.

\textbf{1. Exact projection of the full confidence union.}
Fix a subset \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|=h\).  Every clause of \(W_K\) in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\) is a first-order formula over the ordered field of real numbers with rational-polynomial atoms.  Indeed, each region formula has this form by \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\).  Symmetry is a finite collection of linear equalities.  Positive definiteness is written as
\[
 \forall v\left[
 \sum_i v_i^2>0\Rightarrow v^{\mathsf T}\Gamma_ev>0
 \right],
\]
and positive semidefiniteness is written as \(\forall v\,v^{\mathsf T}\Psi v\geq0\), so neither condition requires division.  The determinant, unit-diagonal, nonnegative-shift, and covariance constraints are polynomial.  Finally, there are finitely many directed simple cycles, and
\[
 -1<\pi_C(A)<1\quad(C\in\mathfrak C_p)
\tag{5}
\]
is a finite family of strict rational-polynomial inequalities.

We next verify that this formula has the asserted semantics.  The determinant clause gives invertibility.  The unit-diagonal clauses and (5), together with the definition of the maximum cycle product in \(\text{def:admissible-matrices}\), give \(A\in\mathcal D\).  The quadratic-form and coordinatewise inequalities give \(\Psi\in\mathbb S_+^p\) and \(t_e\in[0,\infty)^p\).  Because \(A\) is invertible, the last equality in \(F_K\) is equivalent, by multiplication on the left by \(A^{-1}\) and on the right by \(A^{-\mathsf T}\), to
\[
 \Gamma_e=A^{-1}
  \bigl(\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\bigr)A^{-\mathsf T},
 \qquad e\in K.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus \(F_K\) is exactly the cardinality-\(h\) compatibility formula, including every normalization, stability, covariance, positivity, and support constraint.

For any matrix \(A\), \(\text{def:confidence-union}\) and \(\text{def:compatible-set}\) imply
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha
 &\Longleftrightarrow
 \exists(\Gamma_e:e\in E)\left[
   \bigwedge_{e\in E}\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}
   \ \wedge\ A\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)
 \right]\\
 &\Longleftrightarrow
 \bigvee_{\substack{K\subseteq E\\|K|=h}}
 \exists\Gamma\,\exists\Psi\,\exists t\;
 W_K(A,\Psi,t,\Gamma).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
For the second equivalence, a compatibility witness on a set of size at least \(h\) restricts to any \(h\)-element subset, while a witness on a set of size \(h\) already meets \(\text{def:compatible-set}\).  This is the finite reduction in \(\text{def:subset-consensus}\).  Region membership in (7) is equivalent to \(\mathsf{Reg}_e(\Gamma_e)\) by the realization equality in \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\).  Nonemptiness of every region also ensures that coordinates outside \(K\), although irrelevant to (6), can always be supplied.

The right side of (7) is precisely \(\Phi(A)\).  By \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), applying effective quantifier elimination produces a finite quantifier-free Boolean combination \(\phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\) of rational-polynomial sign conditions equivalent to \(\Phi(A)\).  Hence
\[
 \{A:\phi_{\mathcal U}(A)\}=\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha.
\tag{8}
\]
There are only \(\binom mh\) subset branches and finitely many variables and cycle constraints in each branch.  The cited elimination algorithm terminates on each of the two finite formulas used by the handle.  This proves termination and exact full-union representation, conditional only on the handle releasing the computed code.

\textbf{2. Exact encoding of the candidate margins.}
We show that \(\mathsf{Good}_K\) is equivalent to the five displayed margin inequalities for a witness of \(W_K\).  Set \(q=h-c\).  If \(q<3\), the definition gives \(\widehat\gamma=0<\gamma_0\), and \(\mathsf G_\gamma\) is false.  Suppose \(q\geq3\).  All index families are finite, so
\[
 \widehat\gamma(K,t)\geq\gamma_0
\]
holds if and only if, for every coordinate pair \(k<\ell\) and every \(q\)-element \(S\subseteq K\), at least one triple in \(S\) has
\[
 |\Delta_{efg}^{k\ell}(t)|\geq\gamma_0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 (\Delta_{efg}^{k\ell}(t))^2\geq\gamma_0^2.
\tag{9}
\]
The last equivalence uses \(\gamma_0>0\).  Formula (9), with the finite conjunctions and disjunctions shown in the definition, is exactly \(\mathsf G_\gamma\).

By the finite maximum defining \(\widehat\zeta\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \widehat\zeta(A)\geq\zeta_0
 &\Longleftrightarrow
 |\pi_C(A)|\leq1-\zeta_0
 \quad\text{for every }C\in\mathfrak C_p\\
 &\Longleftrightarrow
 1-\zeta_0-\pi_C(A)\geq0
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 1-\zeta_0+\pi_C(A)\geq0
 \quad\text{for every }C.
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
This is \(\mathsf G_\zeta\), with no denominator or sign ambiguity.

For a real symmetric matrix \(G\), the variational definition of its least eigenvalue gives
\[
 \lambda_{\min}(G)\geq\lambda_0
 \Longleftrightarrow
 v^{\mathsf T}(G-\lambda_0I_p)v\geq0
 \quad\text{for every }v.
\tag{11}
\]
Applying (11) to \(\Psi\) and to every \(\Gamma_e\), \(e\in K\), proves that \(\mathsf G_\lambda\) is equivalent to \(\widehat\lambda\geq\lambda_0\).  The positive threshold forces the candidate invariant matrix into the positive-definite interior; no global assumption that the true \(\Omega\) is positive definite has been used.

Let \(\sigma_{\min}(A)>0\) and \(\sigma_{\max}(A)\) be the extreme singular values, whose positivity follows from the determinant clause.  If \(\widehat\kappa(A)\leq\overline\kappa\), choose \(r=\sigma_{\min}(A)^2\).  Then
\[
 rI_p\preceq A^{\mathsf T}A
 \preceq\overline\kappa^{,2}rI_p,
\]
so \(\mathsf G_\kappa\) holds.  Conversely, any \(r>0\) satisfying these two semidefinite inequalities obeys
\[
 r\leq\sigma_{\min}(A)^2,
 \qquad
 \sigma_{\max}(A)^2\leq\overline\kappa^{,2}r.
\]
Division by the positive number \(\sigma_{\min}(A)^2\) gives
\[
 \widehat\kappa(A)^2
 =\frac{\sigma_{\max}(A)^2}{\sigma_{\min}(A)^2}
 \leq\overline\kappa^{,2}.
\]
Both sides are nonnegative and \(\overline\kappa\geq1\), so \(\widehat\kappa(A)\leq\overline\kappa\).  Hence \(\mathsf G_\kappa\) is exact.

On a feasibility witness, \(\Psi\) is positive semidefinite, every \(\Gamma_e\) is positive definite, and every \(t_{ej}\) is nonnegative.  Consequently their operator norms are their largest eigenvalues, and
\[
 \|\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\|_{\mathrm{op}}=max_j t_{ej}.
\tag{12}
\]
If \(\widehat M\leq\overline M\), choose
\[
 x=\|\Psi\|_{\mathrm{op}},
 \qquad y=\max_{e\in K}\|\Gamma_e\|_{\mathrm{op}},
 \qquad z=\max_{\substack{e\in K\\1\leq j\leq p}}t_{ej}.
\]
The semidefinite and coordinatewise inequalities in \(\mathsf G_M\) follow, as does \(1+x+y+z\leq\overline M\).  Conversely, any \(x,y,z\) satisfying \(\mathsf G_M\) bounds the three norms in (12) by \(x,y,z\), respectively, and therefore bounds \(\widehat M\) by \(\overline M\).  Thus \(\mathsf G_M\) is exact.

All atoms used in these encodings are rational-polynomial atoms: the thresholds are rational, and each semidefinite relation is the universally quantified quadratic-form formula already displayed.  It follows that \(\mathsf{Bad}\) is a closed first-order ordered-field sentence with rational coefficients.  By \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), its truth is decidable.  If it is false, every feasible witness satisfies all five margins, the handle returns \(\phi_{\mathcal U}\), and (8) proves exactness of the returned full-union code.  If it is true, the handle abstains by definition.  A feasible witness with \(\widehat\gamma=0\) makes \(\mathsf G_\gamma\) false because \(\gamma_0>0\); it is therefore a witness to \(\mathsf{Bad}\), proving the stated mandatory abstention conclusion.

\textbf{3. Coverage under positive-semidefinite invariant noise.}
Fix an outcome in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\).  For \(e\in H\), set \(\Gamma_e=\Sigma_e\).  For each \(e\in E\setminus H\), choose any \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\); such choices exist because \(E\) is finite and each region is nonempty.  In \(\text{def:compatible-set}\), take
\[
 A=D,
 \qquad K=H,
 \qquad \Psi=\Omega,
 \qquad t_e=s_e\quad(e\in H).
\tag{13}
\]
The set \(H\) has cardinality \(h\) by its definition.  Assumption \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\) gives \(D\in\mathcal D\).  The explicit theorem premise gives \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\), and \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\) gives \(s_e\in[0,\infty)^p\).  Finally, \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\) and the definition of \(\Gamma_e\) on \(H\) give
\[
 \Gamma_e=\Sigma_e
 =D^{-1}\bigl(\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e)\bigr)D^{-\mathsf T},
 \qquad e\in H.
\tag{14}
\]
Equations (13)--(14) verify every compatibility constraint, so \(D\in\mathcal R_c(\Gamma)\).  Since the chosen \(\Gamma_e\) belongs to its region for every \(e\), \(\text{def:confidence-union}\) gives \(D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\).  Therefore
\[
 G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\tag{15}
\]
Taking probabilities in (15) and using monotonicity proves
\[
 \Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}
 \geq\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha.
\]
Only positive semidefiniteness of \(\Omega\) was used.

The exact witness in \(\text{lem:finite-membership-query-obstruction}\) proves that finite membership-oracle access alone cannot replace \(\operatorname{RegionCode}\).  The present argument supplies no inequality converting covariance-region diameter into the diameter of (8); in particular, the margin audit is a decidable guard, not an inverse joint-diagonalization modulus.  Uniform contraction over unrestricted candidates would require a separately proved coercivity bound or a compact candidate envelope, so no such rate is inferred here.  Finally, \(B=I_p-D\) is the structural causal target fixed by the equilibrium model.  Formula (7) is instead an observed-law compatibility projection, and (15) proves its relation to \(D\); neither definition equates the target with the projection.
