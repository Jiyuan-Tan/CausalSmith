Put \(q=m-2c=h-c\). Because \(\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0>0\), \(\text{def:affine-separation}\) implies \(q\geq3\).

\textbf{Uniform matrix bounds.} Let \(G\) be either structural matrix \(D\) or an audited candidate matrix \(A\). In the Leibniz expansion of \(\det G\), the identity permutation contributes one because \(G\) has unit diagonal. Every nonidentity permutation decomposes into disjoint directed cycles and fixed points. The absolute value of each nontrivial cycle product is at most \(1-\zeta_0<1\), and every fixed-point factor is one. Thus every one of the \(p!\) Leibniz terms has absolute value at most one, whence
\[
|\det G|\leq p!.
\tag{1}
\]
If \(\sigma_1(G)=\|G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\), the condition-number bound makes every other singular value at least \(\sigma_1(G)/\overline\kappa\). Therefore
\[
\frac{\sigma_1(G)^p}{\overline\kappa^{p-1}}
\leq\prod_{j=1}^p\sigma_j(G)=|\det G|\leq p!,
\]
so
\[
\|G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq L_0.
\tag{2}
\]
Since \(G_{ii}=1\), \(\|G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\geq1\), and hence
\[
\|G^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq\overline\kappa/\|G\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq\overline\kappa.
\tag{3}
\]

\textbf{Compactness and the global residual gap.} Every defining condition of \(\mathfrak T\) is closed. Inverses are encoded by \(DU=UD=I_p\) and \(AV=VA=I_p\); cycle slack is a finite family of weak polynomial inequalities; affine separation is a finite conjunction of disjunctions of weak polynomial inequalities; and the PSD conditions \(\Omega,\Psi\succeq0\) and covariance-interiority conditions \(\Sigma_e,\Gamma_e\succeq\lambda_0I_p\) are closed. Equations (2)--(3) bound \(D,A,U,V\). Each scale inequality bounds the PSD nuisance, all covariance matrices, and every nonnegative shift coordinate on its side. Thus every fixed \((H_1,H_2,S)\)-piece is closed and bounded in a finite-dimensional Euclidean space and is compact by \(\text{lem:compact-continuous-minimum}\). Their finite union \(\mathfrak T\) is compact, and its closed far subfamily \(\mathfrak T_{\mathrm{far}}\) is compact.

If \(\mathfrak T_{\mathrm{far}}\neq\varnothing\), continuity of \(\mathfrak r\) and \(\text{lem:compact-continuous-minimum}\) give a minimizer. Suppose its residual were zero. Then on \(S\),
\[
D\Sigma_eD^{\mathsf T}=\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e),
\qquad
A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e),
\qquad e\in S.
\tag{4}
\]
For each \(k<\ell\), the first design's affine-separation constraint supplies a triple in \(S\) with affine minor of absolute value at least \(\gamma_0\); hence its \((k,\ell)\)-cloud on \(S\) is not contained in an affine line.

There are \(|E\setminus S|=m-q=2c\) remaining labels. Partition them into disjoint sets \(C_1,C_2\) of cardinality \(c\). Extend the first explanation to \(S\cup C_1\) by using zero shifts and covariance \(D^{-1}\Omega D^{-\mathsf T}\) on \(C_1\), and extend the second to \(S\cup C_2\) by using zero shifts and covariance \(A^{-1}\Psi A^{-\mathsf T}\) on \(C_2\). The nuisance matrices are only required to be PSD, so these auxiliary covariances are PSD; no positive-definiteness claim is needed. Both added shift vectors are nonnegative. The two fitted sets have size \(q+c=h\), intersect exactly in \(S\), and (4) makes the explanations agree there. The hypotheses of \(\text{lem:overlap-uniqueness}\), which accepts PSD invariant nuisances and covariance slots, now hold. It follows that \(A=D\). Since \(U=D^{-1}\), this gives \(AU=I_p\), contrary to \(\|AU-I_p\|_{\mathrm F}\geq\rho_0>0\). Therefore
\[
\mu_0>0.
\tag{5}
\]
The same conclusion holds by definition when the far family is empty.

All coefficients describing \(\mathfrak T_{\mathrm{far}}\) are rational or effectively specified real algebraic numbers, and the latter have rational polynomial equations with isolating sign conditions. For rational \(d>0\), the assertion that some \(\tau\in\mathfrak T_{\mathrm{far}}\) satisfies \(\mathfrak r(\tau)<d\) is first-order rational-polynomial after squaring the Frobenius-norm inequalities. It is decidable by \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\). By (5), the assertion is false for all sufficiently small dyadic \(d=2^{-j}\). Hence the search terminates and returns
\[
0<\underline\mu\leq\mu_0,
\qquad
r_0=\frac{\underline\mu}{2\sqrt p\,L_0^2}>0.
\tag{6}
\]

\textbf{Entry into the identity chart.} Fix \(A\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). Exactness of the released code from \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\) supplies \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|=h\), matrices \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\), a nuisance \(\Psi\succeq0\), and nonnegative shifts \(t_e\) such that
\[
A\Gamma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e),
\qquad e\in K.
\tag{7}
\]
The clean universal audit supplies the candidate-side affine separation, cycle slack, covariance interiority, condition number, and scale constraints. Inclusion--exclusion gives \(|H\cap K|\geq2h-m=q\); choose \(S\subseteq H\cap K\) with \(|S|=q\). On \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\),
\[
\|\Gamma_e-\Sigma_e\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad e\in S.
\tag{8}
\]
Set \(U=D^{-1}\) and \(V=A^{-1}\). The theorem-local premise supplies \(\Omega\succeq0\); the three structural assumptions supply the normalization, covariance equations, and nonnegative shifts; and the five true-instance bounds supply the remaining true-side constraints. Thus these objects form a tuple in \(\mathfrak T\). From (2), (7), (8), submultiplicativity, and \(\|X\|_{\mathrm F}\leq\sqrt p\|X\|_{\mathrm{op}}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left\|A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}-\Psi-\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\right\|_{\mathrm F}
&=\left\|A(\Sigma_e-\Gamma_e)A^{\mathsf T}\right\|_{\mathrm F}\\
&\leq\sqrt p\,L_0^2r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
If \(\|AD^{-1}-I_p\|_{\mathrm F}\geq\rho_0\), the maximum of the left side of (9) over \(S\) is at least \(\mu_0\geq\underline\mu\); when \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0\), (9) bounds it by \(\underline\mu/2\), a contradiction. Thus, with
\[
Q=AD^{-1}=I_p+R,
\qquad u=\|R\|_{\mathrm F},
\]
one has
\[
u<\rho_0\leq1,
\qquad K_0\overline M u\leq\frac12.
\tag{10}
\]

\textbf{Local inverse inequality.} Fix \(e_0\in S\) and write \(x_e=s_e-s_{e_0}\). For each \(i<j\), affine separation supplies a triple in \(S\) with determinant of absolute value at least \(\gamma_0\). Expanding that determinant into the three pairwise cross-products based at \(e_0\) and applying the triangle inequality supplies \(e,f\in S\) such that
\[
|x_{ei}x_{fj}-x_{ej}x_{fi}|\geq\gamma_0/3.
\tag{11}
\]
Let \(E_e=\Gamma_e-\Sigma_e\). Subtract the candidate equation (7) at \(e_0\) from its equation at \(e\), and likewise subtract the true covariance equations. The first differencing cancels \(\Psi\), and the second cancels \(\Omega\). Taking the \((i,j)\) off-diagonal entry of
\[
Q\operatorname{diag}(x_e)Q^{\mathsf T}
+A(E_e-E_{e_0})A^{\mathsf T}
\]
therefore gives
\[
R_{ij}x_{ej}+R_{ji}x_{ei}
=-\sum_{k=1}^pR_{ik}R_{jk}x_{ek}
-[A(E_e-E_{e_0})A^{\mathsf T}]_{ij}.
\tag{12}
\]
The scale bound and shift nonnegativity imply \(|x_{ek}|\leq\overline M\). Equation (8) gives \(\|E_e-E_{e_0}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq2r_{n,\alpha,H}\), while
\[
\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}=\|QD\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq(1+u)L_0.
\]
Thus (12), Cauchy--Schwarz, and \(\sum_k|R_{ik}R_{jk}|\leq u^2\) imply
\[
|R_{ij}x_{ej}+R_{ji}x_{ei}|
\leq\overline M u^2
+2r_{n,\alpha,H}L_0^2(1+u)^2.
\tag{13}
\]
Apply (13) at the two environments in (11) and solve the resulting two-by-two system. Since every coefficient \(|x_{ek}|\leq\overline M\) and the determinant is at least \(\gamma_0/3\), for every ordered pair \(i\neq j\),
\[
|R_{ij}|\leq\frac{6\overline M}{\gamma_0}
\left\{\overline M u^2
+2r_{n,\alpha,H}L_0^2(1+u)^2\right\}.
\tag{14}
\]
The unit diagonals of \(A\) and \(D\) give
\[
0=(A-D)_{ii}=(RD)_{ii}
=R_{ii}+\sum_{k\neq i}R_{ik}D_{ki}.
\tag{15}
\]
For each \(i\), the squared norm of the \(i\)-th column of \(D\) is at most \(L_0^2\). Summing the squared off-diagonal bounds in (14), applying Cauchy--Schwarz to (15), and taking square roots yields
\[
u\leq K_0\left\{\overline M u^2
+2r_{n,\alpha,H}L_0^2(1+u)^2\right\}.
\tag{16}
\]
By (10), the first term on the right of (16) is at most \(u/2\), and \((1+u)^2\leq4\). Hence
\[
u\leq16K_0L_0^2r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\tag{17}
\]
Finally,
\[
\|A-D\|_{\mathrm{op}}
=\|RD\|_{\mathrm{op}}
\leq uL_0
\leq16K_0L_0^3r_{n,\alpha,H}
=C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\tag{18}
\]
Taking the supremum over \(A\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\) proves the outer-radius bound. On the coverage event, \(D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\) by \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\), so the union is nonempty. For \(A_1,A_2\) in it, the triangle inequality and (18) give \(\|A_1-A_2\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}\), proving the diameter bound. The constants use only the fixed dimensions and five audit thresholds, which proves uniformity and the stated limits.
