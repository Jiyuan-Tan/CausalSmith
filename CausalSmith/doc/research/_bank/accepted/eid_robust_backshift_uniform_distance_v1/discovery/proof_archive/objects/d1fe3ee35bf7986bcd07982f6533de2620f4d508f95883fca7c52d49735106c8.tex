Let every covariance region be supplied by \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\), and let \(\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0\in\mathbb Q_{>0}\) and \(\overline\kappa,\overline M\in\mathbb Q\) satisfy \(\overline\kappa,\overline M\geq1\). Under \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\), and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\), suppose additionally that \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\). For
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}=\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\},
\]
one has
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}.
\]
Consequently, \(\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha\) implies \(\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\geq1-\alpha\).

Pointwise for every realized encoded region family, the procedure in \(\text{def:certificate-inference-handle}\) terminates. Its first quantifier-elimination output is a finite quantifier-free rational-polynomial formula whose realization is exactly the complete confidence union \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). Its audit sentence is true exactly when there exists a full feasible candidate tuple whose affine separation, cycle slack, positive-definiteness margin, condition number, or scale violates, respectively, \(\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0,\overline\kappa,\overline M\). Hence the procedure returns the exact full-union code after a clean universal audit and otherwise returns \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\). In particular, it abstains whenever a feasible candidate has zero affine separation.

The encoded input contract is essential for this exact symbolic conclusion in the finite-membership-query model: \(\text{lem:finite-membership-query-obstruction}\) gives rational positive-definite regions with separated affine minors for which no deterministic procedure using only finitely many membership queries computes the exact union for every arbitrary nonempty region family. No operator-norm contraction or confidence-union rate follows from this representation theorem; such a result additionally requires a proved inverse joint-diagonalization coercivity bound or a compact candidate envelope. The causal target remains \(B=I_p-D\), while \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\) is an observed-region compatibility functional whose coverage relation to that target is proved above rather than imposed by definition.
