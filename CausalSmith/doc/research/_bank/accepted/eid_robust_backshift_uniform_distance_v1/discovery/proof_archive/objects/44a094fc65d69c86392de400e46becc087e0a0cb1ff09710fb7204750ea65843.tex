Whole-environment replacement creates a discrete overlap geometry: two explanations may discard different slots and therefore share only \(m-2c\) covariance matrices. Affine rather than linear collinearity is the invariant obstruction because the common nondiagonal noise covariance disappears only after differencing. For inference, one-sided normalization closure is enough: strict cycle-product slack is needed on the true matrix, while feasible candidates require only a uniform condition-number bound.
