Fix integers \(p\geq2\), \(m\geq1\), and \(0\leq c\leq m-1\), put \(h=m-c\), and fix \(n\geq1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), rational thresholds \(\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0>0\), and rational bounds \(\overline\kappa,\overline M\geq1\). The following certified uniform-inference result is partial and sharp with respect to the presently certified scope. If every covariance region is supplied by \(\text{def:encoded-covariance-regions}\), then, for every realized encoded region family, \(\text{CertificateInferenceHandle}\) terminates and computes a finite quantifier-free rational-polynomial code whose realization is exactly the full union \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\). It returns that code exactly when its universal five-margin audit is clean and otherwise returns \(\mathsf{ABSTAIN}\). The audit is clean exactly when every full feasible candidate has affine separation at least \(\gamma_0\), cycle slack at least \(\zeta_0\), positive-definiteness margin at least \(\lambda_0\), condition number at most \(\overline\kappa\), and scale at most \(\overline M\). In particular, a feasible candidate with zero affine separation forces abstention. For every rational population covariance family \(\Sigma\), finite rational-polynomial sentences decide whether \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)\) is empty, is a singleton, or has at least two elements. The certificates use the entries of the normalized matrix globally, so no normalization chart is required. An auxiliary finite rational-semialgebraic chart may be replaced by its polynomial graph on finitely many denominator-sign cells and then projected without changing the resulting matrix-space set.

Under \(\text{ass:backshift-normalization}\), \(\text{ass:nonnegative-shifts}\), and \(\text{ass:honest-covariance-model}\), suppose in addition that \(\Omega\in\mathbb S_+^p\), and define
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}
=\{\Sigma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\text{ for every }e\in H\}.
\]
Then
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\},
\]
and hence
\[
\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
On every outcome in \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\) on which the universal audit releases the exact code, the constants from \(\text{def:audited-release-contraction}\) satisfy \(\underline\mu>0\), the radius \(r_0>0\) is effectively computable, and
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\]
where
\[
L_0=(p!\,\overline\kappa^{p-1})^{1/p},\qquad
K_0=\frac{6\overline M}{\gamma_0}\sqrt{p(p-1)(1+L_0^2)},
\qquad
C_0=16K_0L_0^3.
\]
These bounds are uniform over fixed \(p,m,c,\gamma_0,\zeta_0,\lambda_0,\overline\kappa,\overline M\). Along any sequence of covered outcomes on which the audit releases and \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\), both displayed set radii converge to zero. No contraction is asserted on an abstaining outcome, beyond the radius \(r_0\), or for an unrestricted confidence union that fails the universal audit, and no numerical or CRAN implementation is asserted.
