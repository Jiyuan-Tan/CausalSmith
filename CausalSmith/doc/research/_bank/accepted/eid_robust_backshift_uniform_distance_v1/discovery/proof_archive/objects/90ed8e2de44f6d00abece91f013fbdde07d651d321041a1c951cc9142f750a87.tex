For each \(n\geq1\), \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), and \(e\in E\), let \(\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\subseteq\mathbb S_{++}^{p}\) be an arbitrary nonempty random covariance region. Define
\[
\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}
=\bigcup_{\substack{\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\\ e\in E}}
\mathcal R_c(\Gamma).
\]
Thus \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}\) is the purely set-theoretic inversion of the simultaneous covariance regions through the population replacement-compatibility map \(\mathcal R_c\).
