The set-theoretic simultaneous-coverage implication for arbitrary nonempty positive-semidefinite covariance regions is established by \(\text{thm:confidence-union-coverage}\). The result \(\text{thm:non-effective-four-margin-contraction}\) establishes a non-effective existential local radius and an explicit linear modulus under four bounds on the true instance---affine separation, normalization slack, structural conditioning, and scale---and only a condition-number bound on every feasible candidate, in addition to its pointwise membership in \(\mathcal D\). Can one additionally build and formally verify an executable real-closed-field quantifier-elimination, sign-determination, and root-isolation substrate which, from finite rational-semialgebraic covariance-region codes, (i) terminates and emits a quantifier-free code whose realization is exactly \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\), (ii) decides a release-or-abstain condition, possibly using a conservative universal four-margin audit to certify unknown true-side margins and forcing abstention within that audit whenever it finds a feasible candidate with zero affine separation, while distinguishing that operational sufficient audit from the sole candidate-side condition-number bound required by the analytic contraction theorem, (iii) decides for every rational population covariance family whether \(\mathcal R_c(\Sigma)\) is empty, is a singleton, or contains at least two matrices, and (iv) computes a certified positive lower bound for the compact far-family residual and hence an effective positive radius \(r_0\)? Can the same verified program automate normalization-chart changes without altering the represented matrix set and support a corruption-aware extension of the CRAN \(\texttt{backShift}\) workflow? Every executable, effective, decision, chart-automation, and software item in this question is unresolved future work. A feasible candidate with zero affine separation or a small normalization slack may trigger that conservative operational audit, but neither is an analytic candidate-side obstruction to contraction around a fixed true instance satisfying \(\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0>0\) and \(\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0>0\).
