Using \(\text{CertificateInferenceHandle}\), can one construct finite rational affine-minor certificates for singleton, multiple-candidate, and infeasible population outputs when every \(\Sigma_e\) is rational, together with a computable implementation of \(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}\) that retains the definition-level coverage implication: every indexed region family having simultaneous honest-slot coverage probability at least \(1-\alpha\) yields \(\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha}\}\ge1-\alpha\)? Quantitatively, can one give explicit functions \(r_0(\gamma_H,\kappa_D,\zeta_D,\lambda_H,M_H,p,m)>0\) and \(C(\gamma_H^{-1},\kappa_D,\zeta_D^{-1},\lambda_H^{-1},M_H,p,m)\in(0,\infty)\) such that, for every \(n\ge1\) and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\), on the simultaneous honest-slot coverage event and whenever \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\le r_0\), \(\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha},D)\le C r_{n,\alpha,H}\), and hence \(\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha})\le2C r_{n,\alpha,H}\)? The coverage implication and inequalities must hold uniformly over classes with fixed \(p,m,c\) and bounds \(\gamma_H\ge\gamma_0>0\), \(\zeta_D\ge\zeta_0>0\), \(\lambda_H\ge\lambda_0>0\), \(\kappa_D\le\overline\kappa\), and \(M_H\le\overline M\). The contraction conclusion \(\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha},D)\to0\) and \(\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_{\alpha})\to0\) is required only along region sequences satisfying \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\to0\). Can normalization charts be switched without invalidating the certificates? Separately, whenever the simultaneous regions admit a compatible BACKSHIFT explanation with \(\gamma_H=0\), can the implementation be required to return the full set-valued feasible output or abstain rather than report a singleton?
