For arbitrary nonempty positive-semidefinite covariance regions, \(\text{thm:confidence-union-coverage}\) proves
\[
G_{n,\alpha,H}\subseteq\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\},
\]
and hence, whenever the events are measurable,
\[
\Pr(G_{n,\alpha,H})\geq1-\alpha
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Pr\{D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\}\geq1-\alpha.
\]
Under the four true-side bounds \(\gamma_H\geq\gamma_0\), \(\zeta_D\geq\zeta_0\), \(\kappa_D\leq\overline\kappa\), and \(M_H\leq\overline M\), and only the feasible-candidate condition-number bound \(\|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}\|A^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\overline\kappa\) beyond \(A\in\mathcal D\), \(\text{thm:non-effective-four-margin-contraction}\) gives an existential \(r_0>0\) such that, on \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\),
\[
r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{outer}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha,D)
\leq C_0r_{n,\alpha,H},
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{\mathrm{op}}(\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha)
\leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\]
The candidate covariance, nuisance, and shift scale bounds are derived locally from the true scale bound, candidate conditioning, and radius at most one. No covariance-interiority, candidate affine-separation, candidate scale, or uniform candidate normalization-slack assumption is required. The union remains set-theoretic and the compactness radius remains non-effective.
