Put \(q=m-2c=h-c\).  The condition \(\gamma_H\geq\gamma _0>0\), together with \(\text{def:affine-separation}\), implies \(q\geq3\).

\textbf{Uniform matrix bounds.}
Let \(G\) be either structural matrix \(D\) or an audited candidate matrix \(A\).  In the Leibniz expansion of \(\det G\), the identity permutation contributes \(1\), because \(G\) has unit diagonal.  Each nonidentity permutation is a product of disjoint directed-cycle entry products.  The absolute value of every such cycle product is at most \(1-\zeta _0<1\), by the cycle-slack condition in \(\text{def:audited-release-contraction}\).  Hence every one of the \(p!\) Leibniz terms has absolute value at most one, and
\[
 |\det G|\leq p!.
 \tag{1}
\]
Write \(\sigma_1(G)=\|G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\) for the largest singular value.  The condition-number bound implies that every singular value is at least \(\sigma_1(G)/\overline\kappa\).  Therefore
\[
 \frac{\sigma_1(G)^p}{\overline\kappa^{\,p-1}}
 \leq\prod_{j=1}^p\sigma_j(G)=|\det G|\leq p!,
\]
so
\[
 \|G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq L_0.
 \tag{2}
\]
Also \(\|G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\geq1\), since \(G_{ii}=1\), and hence
\[
 \|G^{-1}\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq\overline\kappa/\|G\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq\overline\kappa.
 \tag{3}
\]

\textbf{Compactness and the positive global residual gap.}
Every defining condition of \(\mathfrak T\) is closed.  In particular, inverses are encoded by the polynomial equations \(DU=UD=I_p\) and \(AV=VA=I_p\); cycle slack is a finite family of weak polynomial inequalities; affine separation is a finite conjunction of disjunctions of weak polynomial inequalities; and the eigenvalue and operator-norm bounds are first-order semialgebraic conditions.  Equations (2)--(3) bound \(D,A,U,V\).  The two scale inequalities bound \(\Omega,\Psi\), every covariance matrix, and every nonnegative shift coordinate.  Thus each fixed \((H_1,H_2,S)\)-piece is a closed bounded subset of a finite-dimensional Euclidean space and is compact by \(\text{lem:compact-continuous-minimum}\).  There are finitely many index triples.  An open cover of their union restricts to an open cover of each piece; taking the union of the finitely many finite subcovers proves that \(\mathfrak T\) is compact.  The far family is closed in \(\mathfrak T\).  Given an open cover of it, adjoining the open complement of the far family covers \(\mathfrak T\); a finite subcover, after discarding that complement, covers the far family.  Hence \(\mathfrak T_{\mathrm{far}}\) is compact.

If \(\mathfrak T_{\mathrm{far}}\) is nonempty, continuity of \(\mathfrak r\) and \(\text{lem:compact-continuous-minimum}\) give a minimizer.  Suppose, for contradiction, that a minimizer \(\tau\) has \(\mathfrak r(\tau)=0\).  On its overlap set \(S\), the same positive-definite covariances then have the two exact explanations
\[
 D\Sigma_eD^{\mathsf T}=\Omega+\operatorname{diag}(s_e),
 \qquad
 A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e),
 \qquad e\in S.
 \tag{4}
\]
For every \(k<\ell\), the affine-separation constraint on the first design supplies a triple in \(S\) whose affine minor has absolute value at least \(\gamma _0\); its \((k,\ell)\)-cloud on \(S\) is therefore not contained in an affine line.

To apply \(\text{lem:overlap-uniqueness}\) exactly as stated, partition the \(2c\) labels in \(E\setminus S\) into two sets of cardinality \(c\), append one part to \(S\) as the first honest set and the other part as the second honest set, and define the additional covariances from the corresponding structural equation using zero additional shifts.  The two resulting honest sets have cardinality \(q+c=h\), their intersection is exactly \(S\), and all added covariances are positive definite because \(\Omega,\Psi\succeq\lambda _0I_p\).  Equation (4) makes the two extended covariance families agree on their intersection.  The lemma therefore gives \(A=D\).  But then \(AU=AD^{-1}=I_p\), contradicting the defining inequality \(\|AU-I_p\|_{\mathrm F}\geq\rho _0>0\).  Consequently
\[
 \mu _0>0.
 \tag{5}
\]
This also holds by definition when the far family is empty.

All coefficients in the description of \(\mathfrak T_{\mathrm{far}}\) are rational or effectively specified real algebraic numbers.  The latter may be encoded by their rational polynomial equations and isolating sign conditions.  For a positive rational \(d\), the assertion
\[
 \exists\tau\in\mathfrak T_{\mathrm{far}},\qquad \mathfrak r(\tau)<d
 \tag{6}
\]
is a first-order formula with rational-polynomial atoms: replace each Frobenius norm inequality by the corresponding inequality for the sum of squared entries.  By \(\text{lem:effective-real-quantifier-elimination}\), (6) is decidable.  From (5), it is false for every sufficiently small dyadic \(d=2^{-j}\).  Hence the search in \(\text{def:audited-release-contraction}\) terminates and returns
\[
 0<\underline\mu\leq\mu _0,
 \qquad
 r_0=\frac{\underline\mu}{2\sqrt p\,L_0^2}>0.
 \tag{7}
\]

\textbf{Every released candidate enters the identity chart.}
Fix \(A\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\).  Exactness of the released code, proved in \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\), supplies a set \(K\subseteq E\) with \(|K|=h\), matrices \(\Gamma_e\in\mathcal C^{(n)}_{e,\alpha}\), a nuisance \(\Psi\), and nonnegative shifts \(t_e\) such that
\[
 A\Gamma_eA^{\mathsf T}=\Psi+\operatorname{diag}(t_e),
 \qquad e\in K.
 \tag{8}
\]
The clean universal audit gives every candidate-side margin and scale constraint in \(\text{def:audited-release-contraction}\).  Inclusion--exclusion gives
\[
 |H\cap K|\geq |H|+|K|-m=2h-m=q.
\]
Choose \(S\subseteq H\cap K\) of cardinality \(q\).  On \(G_{n,\alpha,H}\), the radius definition gives
\[
 \|\Gamma_e-\Sigma_e\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq r_{n,\alpha,H},
 \qquad e\in S.
 \tag{9}
\]
Set \(U=D^{-1}\) and \(V=A^{-1}\).  The three structural assumptions give the true normalization, covariance equations, and nonnegative shifts.  The definitions of \(\gamma_H,\zeta_D,\lambda_H,\kappa_D,M_H\) turn the five displayed true-instance bounds into the corresponding true-side constraints of \(\mathfrak T\).  Equation (8) and the clean universal audit give the candidate-side constraints.  Thus the two tuples, with this \(S\), belong to \(\mathfrak T\).  By (8)--(9), (2), submultiplicativity of the Frobenius norm under left and right operator norms, and \(\|X\|_{\mathrm F}\leq\sqrt p\|X\|_{\mathrm{op}}\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \left\|A\Sigma_eA^{\mathsf T}-\Psi-\operatorname{diag}(t_e)\right\|_{\mathrm F}
 &=\left\|A(\Sigma_e-\Gamma_e)A^{\mathsf T}\right\|_{\mathrm F}\\
 &\leq\sqrt p\,L_0^2r_{n,\alpha,H}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{10}
\]
If \(\|AD^{-1}-I_p\|_{\mathrm F}\geq\rho _0\), the definition of \(\mu _0\) and (7) make the left side of (10), maximized over \(S\), at least \(\mu _0\geq\underline\mu\).  When \(r_{n,\alpha,H}\leq r_0\), (10) instead bounds it by \(\underline\mu/2\), a contradiction.  Thus, writing
\[
 Q=AD^{-1}=I_p+R,
 \qquad u=\|R\|_{\mathrm F},
\]
one has
\[
 u<\rho _0\leq1,
 \qquad K_0\overline M u\leq\frac12.
 \tag{11}
\]

\textbf{The local inverse inequality.}
Fix \(e_0\in S\) and put \(x_e=s_e-s_{e_0}\).  For each \(i<j\), affine separation supplies a triple in \(S\) with determinant of absolute value at least \(\gamma _0\).  Expanding that determinant using the points based at \(e_0\) writes it as a sum of three quantities
\(
x_{ei}x_{fj}-x_{ej}x_{fi}
\).
The triangle inequality therefore supplies \(e,f\in S\) with
\[
 |x_{ei}x_{fj}-x_{ej}x_{fi}|\geq\gamma _0/3.
 \tag{12}
\]
Let \(E_e=\Gamma_e-\Sigma_e\).  Subtract the candidate equation (8) at \(e_0\) from that at \(e\), use the true covariance equation to cancel \(\Omega\), and take the \((i,j)\) off-diagonal entry.  Since \(Q=I_p+R\), this gives
\[
 R_{ij}x_{ej}+R_{ji}x_{ei}
 =-\sum_{k=1}^pR_{ik}R_{jk}x_{ek}
 -[A(E_e-E_{e_0})A^{\mathsf T}]_{ij}.
 \tag{13}
\]
The true scale bound and nonnegativity imply \(|x_{ek}|\leq\overline M\).  Moreover, (9) gives \(\|E_e-E_{e_0}\|_{\mathrm{op}}\leq2r_{n,\alpha,H}\), while
\[
 \|A\|_{\mathrm{op}}=\|QD\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq(1+u)L_0.
\]
Thus (13), Cauchy--Schwarz, and \(\sum_k|R_{ik}R_{jk}|\leq u^2\) imply
\[
 |R_{ij}x_{ej}+R_{ji}x_{ei}|
 \leq\overline M u^2+2r_{n,\alpha,H}L_0^2(1+u)^2.
 \tag{14}
\]
Apply (14) at the two environments in (12).  If their right sides are \(b_e,b_f\), direct elimination in the resulting two-by-two system gives
\[
 R_{ij}=
 \frac{b_ex_{fi}-b_fx_{ei}}
 {x_{ej}x_{fi}-x_{fj}x_{ei}}.
\]
Every coefficient has absolute value at most \(\overline M\), each of \(|b_e|,|b_f|\) is bounded by the right side of (14), and the denominator has absolute value at least \(\gamma _0/3\).  Therefore, for every ordered pair \(i\neq j\),
\[
 |R_{ij}|\leq\frac{6\overline M}{\gamma _0}
 \left\{\overline M u^2+2r_{n,\alpha,H}L_0^2(1+u)^2\right\}.
 \tag{15}
\]
The unit diagonals of \(A\) and \(D\) give
\[
 0=(A-D)_{ii}=(RD)_{ii}
 =R_{ii}+\sum_{k\neq i}R_{ik}D_{ki}.
 \tag{16}
\]
For each \(i\), the squared Euclidean norm of the \(i\)-th column of \(D\) is at most \(L_0^2\).  Combining (15)--(16) and Cauchy--Schwarz yields
\[
 u\leq K_0\left\{\overline M u^2+2r_{n,\alpha,H}L_0^2(1+u)^2\right\}.
 \tag{17}
\]
By (11), the first term on the right of (17) is at most \(u/2\), and \((1+u)^2\leq4\).  Hence
\[
 u\leq16K_0L_0^2r_{n,\alpha,H}.
 \tag{18}
\]
Finally,
\[
 \|A-D\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 =\|RD\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq uL_0
 \leq16K_0L_0^3r_{n,\alpha,H}
 =C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
 \tag{19}
\]
Because \(A\) was arbitrary, (19) proves the outer-radius bound.  On the coverage event, \(D\in\mathcal U^{(n)}_\alpha\) by \(\text{thm:certified-inference-representation-obstruction}\), so the union is nonempty.  For any \(A_1,A_2\) in it, the triangle inequality and (19) give
\[
 \|A_1-A_2\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq\|A_1-D\|_{\mathrm{op}}+\|A_2-D\|_{\mathrm{op}}
 \leq2C_0r_{n,\alpha,H}.
\]
Taking suprema proves the diameter bound.  The construction of the constants uses only the fixed dimensions and five audit bounds, proving uniformity; (19) then gives both asserted limits whenever the audit releases and the radius tends to zero.
