The primitives are a joint reward-transition kernel \(K(dy,dx',dh'\mid x,h,a)\), a known memoryless behavior policy \(b\) and a known memoryless target policy \(e\); derived from them are the policy-induced joint-state kernels \(P_p\), their stationary laws \(d_p\), the target-policy reward regression \(g_e\), the stationary target value \(\theta(K,e)\), and the observable one-step policy ratio \(\rho_t\). The latent coordinates \(H_t\), the joint states \(S_t=(X_t,H_t)\) and both stationary laws are unobserved; only \(\mathsf O_T\) — the observed states \(X_t\), the actions \(A_t\) and the reward VALUES \(Y_t\in[-1,1]\) — together with \(b\), \(e\) and the observed alphabet is revealed. A trajectory of length \(T\) consists of the joint states \(S_1,\ldots,S_{T+1}\) together with the action-reward pairs \((A_t,Y_t)\) for \(t=1,\ldots,T\): at each epoch \(t\) the action is drawn from \(b(\cdot\mid X_t)\) and the kernel \(K\) generates the pair \((Y_t,S_{t+1})\) from \((S_t,A_t)\), so the terminal state \(S_{T+1}\) belongs to the trajectory while the observation records only the first \(T\) states, and of those only their observed coordinates. An estimator sees each epoch exactly through the triple \((X_t,A_t,Y_t)\): its argument is the sequence of those triples itself, and the reward enters only through its real value in \([-1,1]\). ## Lemmas (L)

**L-1 (For every fixed \(t_0>0\), \(\zeta>0\), and \(C\ge1\), th…).** For every fixed \(t_0>0\), \(\zeta>0\), and \(C\ge1\), there is \(c^\star<\infty\), independent of \(T\), \(|\mathcal X|\), and \(|\mathcal H|\), such that \(\sup_{M\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,C)}E_M[(\widehat\theta_{k_T}-\theta(K,e))^2]\le c^\star T^{-\beta}\) for all sufficiently large \(T\). In particular, the overlapping-history covariance sum is geometric and introduces no logarithmic loss.

**L-2 (For every fixed \(t_0>0\), \(\zeta>0\), \(C>1\),…).** For every fixed \(t_0>0\), \(\zeta>0\), \(C>1\), \(Q\ge1\), and \(v\in\{-1,+1\}\), the signed-depth construction belongs to \(\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,C)\) for every \(T\). Its policy-induced stationary laws satisfy \(d_r(j,u)=w_j(1+u\varepsilon_C r^j)/2\) when action one is taken with probability \(r\), \(\max_{j,u}d_e(j,u)/d_b(j,u)\le1+\varepsilon_C\le C\), and \(\theta(K,e)=vc_0\varepsilon_Cw_Q\).

**L-3 (For the signed-depth alternatives, there exists…).** For the signed-depth alternatives, there exists \(B_0<\infty\), independent of \(Q\) and \(T\), such that \(\operatorname{KL}(\mathbb P_{Q,+1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}\,\|\,\mathbb P_{Q,-1}^{\mathrm{obs},T})\le B_0T\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}\). **L-4 (For every \(T\in\mathbb N\), \(C\ge1\),…).** For every \(T\in\mathbb N\), \(C\ge1\), \(M\in\mathcal M_T(t_0,\zeta,C)\), and integer \(0\le k\le\lfloor T/2\rfloor\), \[ E_M[(\widehat\theta_k-\theta(K,e))^2] \le4q_C^2\alpha^{2k} +\frac2T\left\{L^{k+1}\left(1+\frac4{L-1}\right) +\frac4{1-\alpha}\right\}. \] The bound is uniform in \(C\) and in both state-space cardinalities.

**L-5 (For every \(C>1\), \(Q\ge1\), and \(T\in\mathbb N\), the…).** For every \(C>1\), \(Q\ge1\), and \(T\in\mathbb N\), the signed-depth alternatives satisfy \[ \frac{q_C}{2}\le\varepsilon_C\le q_C \] and \[ \operatorname{KL}(\mathbb P_{Q,+1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}\,\|\, \mathbb P_{Q,-1}^{\mathrm{obs},T}) \le \frac{4c_0^2K_0^2}{1-c_0^2} T\varepsilon_C^2\alpha^{2Q}L^{-Q}. \] The displayed coefficient depends only on \(t_0\), not on \(C,Q,T\), or any alphabet cardinality.

**L-6 (For every fixed \(t_0>0\), \(\zeta>0\), every \(C\ge1\),…).** For every fixed \(t_0>0\), \(\zeta>0\), every \(C\ge1\), and every \(T\in\mathbb N\), \[ R_T(t_0,\zeta,C)\ge\frac{e^{-1/4}}{64T}. \] This lower bound holds on a common singleton-state, equal-policy subexperiment and is therefore uniform in \(C\).

## Theorems (T)

### T-block: t1 — For every fixed \(t_0>0\), \(\zeta>0\), and \(C>1\), ther…
**Statement.** For every fixed \(t_0>0\), \(\zeta>0\), and \(C>1\), there exist \(0<c_\star\le c^\star<\infty\) and \(T_\star<\infty\), depending only on \(t_0,\zeta,C\), such that \(c_\star T^{-2/(2+t_0\zeta)}\le R_T(t_0,\zeta,C)\le c^\star T^{-2/(2+t_0\zeta)}\) for every \(T\ge T_\star\). The upper bound is attained by \(\widehat\theta_{k_T}\); the lower bound holds against every measurable observable-data estimator, already with constant \(X_t\) and \(2(Q+1)\) latent states. Thus the HuWager2023 Theorem 3.1 minimax exponent remains sharp after strictly restricting its POMDP class by \(d_e\le Cd_b\), with no logarithmic factor.

### T-block: t2 — At \(C=1\), \(d_e=d_b\), the immediate estimator…
**Statement.** At \(C=1\), \(d_e=d_b\), the immediate estimator \(\widehat\theta_0\) has cardinality-uniform mean-squared error of order \(T^{-1}\), and \(R_T(t_0,\zeta,1)\asymp T^{-1}\). Consequently the fixed-\(C>1\) theorem has a genuine discontinuous boundary in exponent, although its constants need not be uniform as \(C\downarrow1\).

### T-block: t3 — The refreshed insulin construction…
**Statement.** The refreshed insulin construction \(\mathcal I_{\mathrm{grid}}\) has \(360\) joint states, Dobrushin coefficient at most \(1/2\) under both policies, one-step policy overlap \(L=10/3\), and the analytic stationary-overlap bound \(d_e(s)\le720d_b(s)\) for every state. An interval-certified evaluation of its finite transition matrices satisfies \(-0.0060<\Delta_{\mathrm{imm}}<-0.0058\), so immediate action weighting is biased despite finite latent stationary overlap.

### T-block: t4 — Let \(t_0>0\) and \(\zeta>0\) be fixed, and let…
**Statement.** Let \(t_0>0\) and \(\zeta>0\) be fixed, and let \(\delta_T\ge0\) be any deterministic sequence with \(\delta_T\to0\). With \(\beta=2/(2+t_0\zeta)\), there are constants \(0<c_{\mathrm{loc}}\le C_{\mathrm{loc}}<\infty\), depending only on \(t_0,\zeta\), such that, for all sufficiently large \(T\), \[ c_{\mathrm{loc}}\left\{T^{-1}+T^{-\beta}\delta_T^{2(1-\beta)}\right\} \le R_T(t_0,\zeta,1+\delta_T) \le C_{\mathrm{loc}}\left\{T^{-1}+T^{-\beta}\delta_T^{2(1-\beta)}\right\}. \] An attaining observable estimator is \(\widehat\theta_{\kappa_T}\), where \(\kappa_T=0\) if \(T\delta_T^2\le1\), and otherwise \[ \kappa_T:=\min\!\left\{\left\lfloor T/2\right\rfloor, \left\lfloor\frac{\log(T\delta_T^2)}{2\log(1/\alpha)+\log L}\right\rfloor\right\}. \] Thus the exact threshold is \(\delta_T\asymp T^{-1/2}\): when \(T\delta_T^2=O(1)\), immediate weighting has parametric order, whereas when \(T\delta_T^2\to\infty\), the required history is of order \(\log(T\delta_T^2)\) and the second risk term dominates. There is no logarithmic factor in the risk.

### T-block: t5 — For every fixed \(t_0>0\) and \(\zeta>0\), uniformly over…
**Statement.** For every fixed \(t_0>0\) and \(\zeta>0\), uniformly over all \(C\ge1\), \[ R_T(t_0,\zeta,C) \asymp_{t_0,\zeta} T^{-1}+T^{-\beta}q_C^{2(1-\beta)}, \qquad \beta=\frac{2}{2+t_0\zeta}, \] for all sufficiently large \(T\), with comparison constants and the threshold independent of \(C\) and of both state-space cardinalities. The upper bound is attained by the observable estimator \(\widehat\theta_{\kappa_T^{\mathrm{rad}}(C)}\). Equivalently, the exact elbow is \(q_C\asymp T^{-1/2}\): depth zero has parametric order when \(Tq_C^2=O(1)\), whereas the hidden-state term dominates when \(Tq_C^2\to\infty\). No logarithmic factor occurs.
