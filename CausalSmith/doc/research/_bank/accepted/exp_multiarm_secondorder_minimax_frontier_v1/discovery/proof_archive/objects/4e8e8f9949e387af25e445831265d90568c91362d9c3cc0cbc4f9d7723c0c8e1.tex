\textbf{Proof.}
On \(\{w(\theta)p_\theta(x)>0\}\), define the joint score
\[
 Z(\theta,x)=\frac{w'(\theta)}{w(\theta)}+
 \frac{\partial_\theta p_\theta(x)}{p_\theta(x)}.
\]
The likelihood-score centering condition follows from the displayed differentiation assumption:
\[
 \mathbb E_\theta\!\left[
 \frac{\partial_\theta p_\theta(X)}{p_\theta(X)}
 \right]
 =\int\partial_\theta p_\theta(x)\,dx=0.
\]
Consequently the cross term between the prior score and likelihood score vanishes, and
\[
 \mathbb E\{Z(\theta,X)^2\}
 =J(w)+\mathbb E\{I(\theta)\}.
\]
By the absolute-continuity, integrability, compact-support, and boundary hypotheses, integration by parts is legitimate and has no boundary contribution. Since \(T(x)\) does not depend on \(\theta\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E[\{T(X)-g(\theta,X)\}Z(\theta,X)]
 &=\iint \{T(x)-g(\theta,x)\}
 \partial_\theta\{w(\theta)p_\theta(x)\}\,d\theta\,dx\\
 &=\iint \partial_\theta g(\theta,x)
 w(\theta)p_\theta(x)\,d\theta\,dx\\
 &=\mathbb E\{\partial_\theta g(\theta,X)\}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Cauchy--Schwarz now gives
\[
 \left[\mathbb E\{\partial_\theta g(\theta,X)\}\right]^2
 \leq \mathbb E\{T(X)-g(\theta,X)\}^2
 \left[J(w)+\mathbb E\{I(\theta)\}\right].
\]
The denominator is positive and finite by hypothesis, so division proves the claim. \(\square\)
