For \(K=2\) and \(c=(1,-1)\), let \(\pi_n^\star\) be the least-favorable prior in the published reduced scalar problem. For each \(\vartheta=(p_+,p_-,r_0)\) with \(p_++p_-+r_0=n\), choose uniformly an ordered partition \((P,N,R)\) of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\) with sizes \((p_+,p_-,r_0)\), assign response type \((1,0)\) on \(P\) and \((0,1)\) on \(N\), and, independently over \(i\in R\), draw \(H_i\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) and assign type \((H_i,H_i)\). Mix this kernel under \(\pi_n^\star\), and call the resulting prior on complete labeled schedules \(\widetilde\pi_n^\star\). For every assignment law \(\mathcal D\), including laws dependent across units, chosen independently of the schedule draw, put \(S_i=Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}\) on the positive arm and \(S_i=1-Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}\) on the negative arm, and put \(X=\sum_iS_i\). Conditional on \(\vartheta\), \(X=p_++\operatorname{Binomial}(r_0,1/2)\); \(A\) is ancillary; and, conditional on \(X=x\), \(S\) is uniform over the binary vectors of sum \(x\), with a kernel independent of \(\vartheta\) and \(A\). Consequently every full-data estimator can be Rao--Blackwellized first over \(A\) and then over \(S\) conditional on \(X\), without increasing squared Bayes risk. The prior \(\widetilde\pi_n^\star\) therefore has Bayes risk exactly equal to the scalar minimax value \(\rho_n((1,-1))\) and is an exact least-favorable prior for the unrestricted full two-arm schedule game. These conclusions include every boundary case in which one or more of \(p_+\), \(p_-\), and \(r_0\) is zero.
