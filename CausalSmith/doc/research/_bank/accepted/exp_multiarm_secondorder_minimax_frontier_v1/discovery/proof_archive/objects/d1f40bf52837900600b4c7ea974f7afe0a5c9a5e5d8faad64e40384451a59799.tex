The finite rational optimizer is a mixture over allocation-count orbits and need not equal the multinomial law induced by independent \(q^\star\).  Its support at \(n\leq5\) neither proves that a design correction persists asymptotically nor proves that it disappears.  Without a selected normalization and limiting experiment, no analytic feedback rule or least-favorable prior sequence can yet be shown to attain a multi-arm second-order constant.
