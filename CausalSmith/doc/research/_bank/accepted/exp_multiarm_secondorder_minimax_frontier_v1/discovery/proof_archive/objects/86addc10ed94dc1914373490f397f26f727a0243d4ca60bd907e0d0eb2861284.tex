For \(K=2\) and \(c=(1,-1)\), the published reduced scalar minimax problem has a unique minimizer \(f_n^\star\), which is the posterior mean of \((p_+-p_-)/n\) given \(X_2\) under a least-favorable prior \(\pi_n^\star\) on \((p_+,p_-,r_0)\), and the scalar minimax value is the Bayes risk of \(\pi_n^\star\). A separate published reduction theorem proves that the unrestricted full binary two-arm game has the same minimax value and that balanced Bernoulli assignment with \(f_n^\star(\sum_i S_i)\) is minimax and admissible. The published Airy results give an explicit scalar nonlinear-shrinkage upper sequence and a scalar prior sequence whose Bayes risks attain the matching \(n^{-4/3}\) lower expansion. The cited source does not itself construct \(\pi_n^\star\) as a prior on complete response schedules and does not assert that this scalar prior, without a separate lift, is a Bayes-risk converse against arbitrary full-data procedures.
