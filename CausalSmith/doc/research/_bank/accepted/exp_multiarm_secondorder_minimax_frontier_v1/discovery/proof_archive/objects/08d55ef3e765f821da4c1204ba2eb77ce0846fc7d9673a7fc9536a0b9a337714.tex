The continuous-action squared-loss orbit game is a finite convex quadratically represented decision problem, not literally the rational LP used for computation.  The LP discretizes actions on \(\Gamma_M\).  Exact optimal primal--dual certificates imply a \(1/(4M^2)\) continuous upper--lower gap after barycentering and posterior recomputation.  For \(n=3,4,5\), floating support discovery followed by rational reconstruction produced rigorous but nonzero rational enclosures rather than a single exact value; those enclosure widths are about \(4.1\times10^{-15}\), \(1.7\times10^{-16}\), and \(7.9\times10^{-15}\).  Finite enclosures cannot certify a limiting exponent or continuum boundary problem.
