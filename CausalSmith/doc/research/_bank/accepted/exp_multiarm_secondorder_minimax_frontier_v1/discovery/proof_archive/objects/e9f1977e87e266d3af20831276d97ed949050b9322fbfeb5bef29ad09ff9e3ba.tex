Unrestricted finite-population binary minimax theory has a sharp published two-arm solution, but a delivered multi-arm theorem cannot obtain its own converse by importing that source's Airy expansion or least-favorable posterior-mean saddle. Existing general-contrast design results either restrict the estimator or change the adversary, and the literature does not identify the face-and-corner second-order frontier of the full \(2^K\)-response-type simplex.
