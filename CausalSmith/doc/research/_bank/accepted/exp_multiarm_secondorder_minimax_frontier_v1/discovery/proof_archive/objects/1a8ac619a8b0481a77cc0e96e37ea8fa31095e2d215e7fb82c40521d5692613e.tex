The completed routes are unit-permutation reduction to the exact \(2^K\)-response-type game, the contrast-weighted first-order rule, the support-two embedding and coefficient rescaling, barycentering exact grid-LP primal actions into deterministic orbit rules, and completing the square under exact dual priors to obtain the \(1/(4M^2)\) sandwich. A scalar contrast-score reduction was stress-tested and refuted at \(n=3\). Exact certificates were computed through \(n=5\), but they were not extrapolated into an asymptotic design or feedback formula.
