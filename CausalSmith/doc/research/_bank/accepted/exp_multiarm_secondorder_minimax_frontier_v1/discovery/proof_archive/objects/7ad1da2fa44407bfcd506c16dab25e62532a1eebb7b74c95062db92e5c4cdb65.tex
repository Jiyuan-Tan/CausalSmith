For \(K=2\) and \(c=(1,-1)\), the reduced scalar minimax problem has a unique minimizer \(f_n^\star\), namely the posterior mean of \((p_+-p_-)/n\) given \(X_2\) under a least-favorable scalar prior \(\pi_n^\star\) on \((p_+,p_-,r_0)\), and the scalar minimax value is the Bayes risk of \(\pi_n^\star\). A separate reduction theorem proves that the unrestricted binary finite-population full-game minimax value equals this scalar value and is attained, admissibly, by independent balanced Bernoulli assignment and \(f_n^\star(\sum_i S_i)\). The published Airy theorems further give an explicit second-order nonlinear scalar shrinkage sequence and asymptotically least-favorable scalar prior sequences at scale \(n^{-4/3}\). The cited results do not state that \(\pi_n^\star\), without an additional construction, is a prior on complete two-arm schedules or an exact full-game Bayes-risk converse.
