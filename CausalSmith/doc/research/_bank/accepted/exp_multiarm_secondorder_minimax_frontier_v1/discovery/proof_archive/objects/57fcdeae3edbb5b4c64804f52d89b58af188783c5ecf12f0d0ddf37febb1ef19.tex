For \(K=3\), \(c=c^\dagger=(1,-1/2,-1/2)\), and independent assignment with \(q^\star=(1/2,1/4,1/4)\), define
\[
 Z_i=\operatorname{sign}(c_{A_i}^\dagger)(2Y_i^{\mathrm{obs}}-1),
 \qquad
 \mu_i=t_{i,1}-\frac{t_{i,2}+t_{i,3}}{2}.
\]
Then \(Z_i\in\{-1,1\}\),
\[
 \mathbb E(Z_i\mid t_i)=\mu_i,
 \qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(Z_i\mid t_i)=1-\mu_i^2,
\]
and
\[
 \mathbb E\!\left[\left\{\frac1n\sum_iZ_i-\tau_{c^\dagger}(z)\right\}^2\middle|z\right]
 =\frac1n-\frac1{n^2}\sum_i\mu_i^2.
\]
The exact scalar minimax value at \(n=3\), when only \((Z_1,Z_2,Z_3)\) is retained and each \(\mu_i\) ranges over \(\{-1,-1/2,0,1/2,1\}\), is
\[
 V_3^Z=1-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.
\]
Nevertheless, an explicit deterministic rule based on the full observed arm labels and outcomes under the same independent \(q^\star\) design has maximum risk
\[
 \frac{511653}{4000000}<\frac{18213}{136000}<V_3^Z.
\]
Thus the map from the full data to \((Z_i)_{i=1}^3\) is not minimax-preserving, even at the fixed \(q^\star\) design, and cannot itself be an exact scalar reduction of the unrestricted three-arm orbit game.  In addition, if \(M_\mu=\sum_i\mu_i\) and \(Q_\mu=\sum_i\mu_i^2\), then over feasible half-integer \(M_\mu\),
\[
 \min Q_\mu=
 \begin{cases}
 |M_\mu|/2,&|M_\mu|\leq n/2,\\
 (3|M_\mu|-n)/2,&n/2\leq|M_\mu|\leq n.
 \end{cases}
\]
Hence the coefficient \(\tfrac12|x|\) is the correct local wedge for the scalar relaxation, but this proposition does not establish that the discarded arm-label information is negligible at any second-order scale.
