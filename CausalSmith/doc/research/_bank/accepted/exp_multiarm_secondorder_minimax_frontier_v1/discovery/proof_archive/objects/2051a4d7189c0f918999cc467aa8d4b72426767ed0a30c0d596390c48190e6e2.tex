For \(|S_c|=2\), let the two active coefficients be \(L_c/2\) and \(-L_c/2\). Relabeling the active arms and multiplying the published two-arm posterior-mean rule by \(L_c/2\) yields an exact finite-\(n\) saddle under independent probability-\(1/2\) assignment, which equals \(q^\star\); the lifted least-favorable prior gives the matching converse, and
\[
 d_n(c)=C_0(c)C_A n^{-4/3}+o(n^{-4/3}).
\]
For every nonzero zero-sum contrast, including \(|S_c|\geq3\), the universal second-order rate theorem fixes the order \(n^{-4/3}\). Its independent \(q^\star\) design and explicit clipped-shrinkage rule satisfy, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \max_z R_n(\mathcal D^\star,\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{sh}};z)
 \leq C_0(c)\{n^{-1}-\kappa_c n^{-4/3}\},
 \qquad \kappa_c>0,
\]
where the theorem gives \(\kappa_c\) explicitly. This is a uniform positive second-order improvement by an observed-data procedure, although it need not attain the unknown sharp constant. For \(c=c^\dagger\), every \(n,M\geq1\) admits a computable orbit procedure and rational prior satisfying
\[
 B_n(\nu_{n,M})\leq\rho_n^\dagger\leq U_{n,M}
 \leq\Lambda_{n,M}^\dagger
 \leq B_n(\nu_{n,M})+\frac{1}{4M^2}.
\]
Taking \(M_n=n\) gives
\[
 n^{4/3}\{U_{n,n}-B_n(\nu_{n,n})\}
 \leq\frac{1}{4n^{2/3}}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus the three-arm finite procedures and unrestricted rational-prior converses already match the exact minimax risk to \(o(n^{-4/3})\). What remains open is analytic sharp attainment: second-order optimality of \(q^\star\), the sharp constant or matched subsequential constants, an analytic limiting multivariate rule, and convergence of a least-favorable-prior sequence.
