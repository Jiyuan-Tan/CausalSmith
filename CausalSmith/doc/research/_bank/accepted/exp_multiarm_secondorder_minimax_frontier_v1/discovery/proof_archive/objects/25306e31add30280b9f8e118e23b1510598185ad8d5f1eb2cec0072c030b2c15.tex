When \(|S_c|=2\), lifting the published two-arm procedure and least-favorable prior attains \(\rho_n(c)=C_0(c)\rho_{n,2}\), including its Airy correction. For \(c=c^\dagger\), Theorem \(\text{thm:k3-grid-certificate-sandwich}\) extracts, at every \((n,M)\), a computable randomized allocation, deterministic barycentered orbit rule, and rational prior satisfying
\[
 B_n(\nu_{n,M})\leq\rho_n^\dagger\leq U_{n,M}
 \leq B_n(\nu_{n,M})+\frac{1}{4M^2}.
\]
Thus its upper and lower certificates match at every selected scale \(a_n\) for which \(a_n/M_n^2\to0\). What remains unproved for \(|S_c|\geq3\) is the normalization itself and whether these finite-game optimizers asymptotically agree with independent \(q^\star\) plus an analytic multivariate shrinkage rule.
