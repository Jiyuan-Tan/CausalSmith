The exact orbit game was solved by rational primal and dual enclosures for \(n=1,\ldots,5\) with \(M=n^2\); allocation support, binding risk rows, dual support, posterior-prior lower bounds, and barycentered upper risks were inspected.  The proposed scalar score was analyzed exactly: its moment deficiency and local wedge were derived, its \(n=3\) scalar minimax value was solved, and a rational full-data rule was constructed that strictly beats it.  This falsifies state-by-state and minimax-preserving scalarization but leaves open whether the full-versus-scalar gap is negligible on the eventual asymptotic scale.  Small-sample active sets touch mixed faces and pure corners and have not stabilized sufficiently to justify naming a continuum operator.
