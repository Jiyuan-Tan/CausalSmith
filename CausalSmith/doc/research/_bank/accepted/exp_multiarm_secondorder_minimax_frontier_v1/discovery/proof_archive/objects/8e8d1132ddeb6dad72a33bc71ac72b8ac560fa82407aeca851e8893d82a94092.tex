Optimal grid actions were barycentered into deterministic orbit rules, exact maximum risks were recomputed, complete dual nature multipliers were converted into rational priors, and unrestricted posterior means and design-uniform Bayes lower bounds were derived by completion of the square.  The resulting theorem proves a \(1/(4M^2)\) matched-certificate gap.  The computed \(n=1,\ldots,5\) sequence was then compared with independent \(q^\star\) and the scalar score, but it does not decide the design limit or the analytic form of shrinkage.
