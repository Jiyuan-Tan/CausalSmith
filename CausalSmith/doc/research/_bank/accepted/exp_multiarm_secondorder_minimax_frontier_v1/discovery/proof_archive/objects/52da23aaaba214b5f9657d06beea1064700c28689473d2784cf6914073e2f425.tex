Theorem \(\text{thm:k3-grid-certificate-sandwich}\) supplies the exact finite-\(n\) extraction requested by the directive.  From an exact rational grid-LP primal, it barycenters the grid action into a deterministic multivariate orbit rule \(\delta_{n,M}(r,x)\) and retains the allocation mixture \(\pi^{n,M}\).  From a complete exact rational dual it extracts a prior \(\nu_{n,M}\), recomputes the unrestricted posterior mean, and proves
\[
 B_n(\nu_{n,M})\leq\rho_n^\dagger\leq U_{n,M}
 \leq\Lambda_{n,M}^\dagger
 \leq B_n(\nu_{n,M})+\frac1{4M^2}.
\]
The lower bound is design-uniform because, for fixed \(\nu\), posterior completion of the square reduces Bayes risk at allocation \(r\) to
\[
 \sum_m\nu_m\tau_m^2-
 \sum_{x:D_{r,x}(\nu)>0}\frac{N_{r,x}(\nu)^2}{D_{r,x}(\nu)},
\]
and a mixture over \(r\) cannot improve on the minimum of these values.  Therefore any selected normalizer and grid sequence satisfying \(a_n/M_n^2\to0\) automatically yield \(a_n(U_{n,M_n}-B_n(\nu_{n,M_n}))\to0\).  In particular, \(M_n=n^2\) suffices for every \(a_n=o(n^4)\), including the unproved candidate \(n^{4/3}\).

For the computed \(M=n^2\) certificates at \(n=1,\ldots,5\), the exact continuous certificate gaps \(U_{n,M}-B_n(\nu_{n,M})\) were respectively
\[
 0.25,\quad \frac1{88},\quad 0.002664108,\quad
 0.000684814,\quad 0.000342300,
\]
where the last three decimals are outward summaries of exact rational numbers.  However, no second-order normalizer has yet been selected by proof, the displayed primal supports do not equal the multinomial allocation law generated by independent \(q^\star\), and grid-optimal supports need not identify a unique continuous minimax design.  The finite certificate theorem therefore does not decide second-order optimality of independent \(q^\star\), prove that a design correction is necessary, or furnish an analytic multivariate shrinkage feedback.  Those conclusions require the still-open asymptotic comparison.
