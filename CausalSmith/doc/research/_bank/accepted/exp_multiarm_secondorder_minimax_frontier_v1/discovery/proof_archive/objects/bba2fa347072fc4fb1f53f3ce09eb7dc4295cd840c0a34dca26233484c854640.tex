After relabeling the two active arms and multiplying the published two-arm rule by \(L_c/2\), the embedded two-arm equality and the published construction give an exact finite-\(n\) saddle: independent assignment supported on \(S_c\) with probability \(1/2\) on each active arm, the coefficient-rescaled nonlinear posterior-mean estimator, and the correspondingly lifted least-favorable prior. This assignment is \(q^\star\), its minimax risk is \(\rho_n(c)\), and its coefficient-scaled Airy converse is \(d_n(c)=C_0(c)C_A n^{-4/3}+o(n^{-4/3})\). Consequently, the unresolved construction and attainment question is restricted to nonzero zero-sum contrasts with \(|S_c|\geq3\). Decide whether independent assignment with \(q^\star\) remains second-order optimal; if it does, derive the multivariate nonlinear shrinkage rule, and if it does not, characterize the necessary finite-\(n\) design correction and certify its improvement in \(G_n(c)\).
