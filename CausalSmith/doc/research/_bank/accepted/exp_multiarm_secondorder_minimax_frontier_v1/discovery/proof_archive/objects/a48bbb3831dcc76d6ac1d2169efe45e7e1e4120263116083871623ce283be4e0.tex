For every nonzero zero-sum contrast,
\[
 0\leq d_n(c)\leq C_0(c)\{n^{-1}-\rho_{n,2}\}
 =C_0(c)C_A n^{-4/3}+o(n^{-4/3}),
\]
so \(0\leq\limsup_n n^{4/3}d_n(c)\leq C_0(c)C_A\).  If \(|S_c|=2\), equality holds in the upper bound for every \(n\), which completely selects \(a_n=n^{4/3}\) and the limit \(C_0(c)C_A\).  For \(c^\dagger\), exact rational lower and upper certificates for \(n=1,\ldots,5\) give intervals for \(n^{4/3}d_n(c^\dagger)\) ending at \([0.948937,0.951865]\) when \(n=5\), but these finite values prove no limit.  The scalar-score map is not minimax-preserving at \(n=3\), so scalar asymptotic equivalence cannot be assumed.
