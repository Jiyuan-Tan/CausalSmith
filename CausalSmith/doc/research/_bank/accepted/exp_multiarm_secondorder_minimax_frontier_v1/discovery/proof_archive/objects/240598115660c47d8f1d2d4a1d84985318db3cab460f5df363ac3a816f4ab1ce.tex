Fix a nonzero zero-sum contrast \(c\) with \(|S_c|\geq3\), and condition on whatever second-order normalization and limit, if any, are proved by the genuinely multivariate second-order frontier. Construct a finite-\(n\) procedure and a least-favorable prior sequence whose upper and lower risks match at that normalization. Decide whether independent assignment with \(q^\star\) remains second-order optimal; if it does, derive the multivariate nonlinear shrinkage rule, and if it does not, characterize the necessary finite-\(n\) design correction and certify its improvement in the exact orbit game. The support-two case is excluded because the identity \(\rho_n(c)=C_0(c)\rho_{n,2}\) lifts the published optimal two-arm design, shrinkage estimator, and least-favorable prior. For \(c=c^\dagger\), the exact grid-certificate sandwich already supplies computable finite-\(n\) orbit procedures and unrestricted prior lower bounds whose gap is \(o(a_n^{-1})\) whenever \(a_n/M_n^2\to0\); the remaining obligation is the analytic asymptotic characterization and the comparison with independent \(q^\star\), neither of which follows from the finite certificates alone.
