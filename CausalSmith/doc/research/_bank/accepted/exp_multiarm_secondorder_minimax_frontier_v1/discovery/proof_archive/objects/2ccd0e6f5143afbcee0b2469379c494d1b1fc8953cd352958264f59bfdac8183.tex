A rigorous partial answer is available.  Fix \(K\geq2\) and a nonzero zero-sum contrast \(c\).

First, if \(|S_c|=2\), then contrast-weighted independent assignment, with probability \(1/2\) on each active arm, and the coefficient-rescaled published two-arm posterior-mean rule form an exact finite-\(n\) minimax saddle.  The correspondingly embedded published least-favorable prior is an exact Bayes-risk converse.  In particular, if \(\rho_{n,2}=\rho_n((1,-1))\), then
\[
 \rho_n(c)=C_0(c)\rho_{n,2},
 \qquad
 d_n(c)=C_0(c)C_A n^{-4/3}+o(n^{-4/3}).
\]

Second, for arbitrary support, the independent \(q^\star\) design and the explicit clipped-shrinkage estimator in the universal second-order rate theorem satisfy, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 \max_z R_n(\mathcal D^\star,\widehat\tau_n^{\mathrm{sh}};z)
 \leq C_0(c)\{n^{-1}-\kappa_c n^{-4/3}\},
 \qquad \kappa_c>0,
\]
where \(\kappa_c\) is the explicit constant in that theorem.  Together with the embedded two-arm converse, this gives
\[
 0<C_0(c)\kappa_c
 \leq\liminf_{n\to\infty}n^{4/3}d_n(c)
 \leq\limsup_{n\to\infty}n^{4/3}d_n(c)
 \leq C_0(c)C_A.
\]

Third, for \(c=c^\dagger\), let \(U_{n,M}\) and \(B_n(\nu_{n,M})\) be the computable upper and lower certificates in the three-arm grid-certificate theorem.  For every integer sequence \((M_n)\) such that \(n^{4/3}/M_n^2\to0\), define
\[
 \underline d_n=n^{-1}-U_{n,M_n},
 \qquad
 \overline d_n=n^{-1}-B_n(\nu_{n,M_n}).
\]
Then
\[
 \underline d_n\leq d_n(c^\dagger)\leq\overline d_n,
 \qquad
 0\leq n^{4/3}(\overline d_n-\underline d_n)
 \leq\frac{n^{4/3}}{4M_n^2}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Consequently, along the full sequence whenever \(n^{4/3}d_n(c^\dagger)\) converges, and along every subsequence on which it converges otherwise, the computable multivariate orbit procedure and the rational-prior converse have the same limiting second-order correction.  The choice \(M_n=n\) is admissible and gives a scaled certificate gap at most \(1/(4n^{2/3})\).

These conclusions establish exact analytic attainment for two active arms, explicit order attainment for every fixed number of active arms, and scale-matched computable attainment and converse certificates for \(c^\dagger\).  They do not determine, when \(|S_c|\geq3\), whether \(q^\star\) is sharply second-order optimal, the sharp constant or its subsequential values, an analytic limiting feedback rule, or convergence of a least-favorable-prior sequence.
