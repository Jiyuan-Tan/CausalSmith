The exact orbit game and the three-arm rational certificate theorem provide finite-dimensional saddle certificates but no convergence theorem for their allocation mixtures, posterior means, or dual priors. Grid-optimal allocation supports need not equal the multinomial law induced by independent \(q^\star\), and nonuniqueness of finite-grid optima prevents such supports from identifying an asymptotic design. The finite-sample scalar score is not minimax-preserving, while computations for \(n\leq5\) cannot establish a normalization, a limiting Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman or spectral problem, or a least-favorable-prior limit.
