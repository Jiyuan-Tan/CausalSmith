The projected contrast-weighted centered Horvitz--Thompson procedure is an explicit finite-\(n\) rule with worst-case risk at most \(C_0(c)/n\), and the threshold-prior argument gives a matching unrestricted first-order converse.  The exact finite game also guarantees existence of a finite-\(n\) minimax procedure and a least-favorable prior.  These results do not identify their second-order corrections, prove that independent \(q^\star\) allocation is second-order optimal, or construct the requested multivariate shrinkage feedback.
