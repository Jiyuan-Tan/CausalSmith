For \(|S_c|=2\), relabeling the active arms and scaling the published balanced Bernoulli/posterior-mean saddle by \(L_c/2\) gives the exact finite-\(n\) design, estimator, and lifted least-favorable prior, and the embedded two-arm theorem gives \(d_n(c)=C_0(c)C_A n^{-4/3}+o(n^{-4/3})\). For \(c=c^\dagger\), the proved rational certificate theorem gives, for every \(n,M\geq1\), a computable orbit procedure and rational prior satisfying
\[
 B_n(\nu_{n,M})\leq\rho_n^\dagger\leq U_{n,M}
 \leq\Lambda_{n,M}^\dagger
 \leq B_n(\nu_{n,M})+\frac{1}{4M^2}.
\]
Thus, whenever a normalization \(a_n\) is subsequently proved and a grid-resolution sequence \((M_n)\) satisfies \(a_n/M_n^2\to0\), the certified upper and lower improvements differ by \(o(a_n^{-1})\). This exact finite-\(n\) result neither selects the normalization nor proves that the certificate design converges to independent \(q^\star\), and it does not furnish an analytic multivariate feedback rule.
