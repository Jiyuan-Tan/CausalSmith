For \(K=2\) and \(c=(1,-1)\), the published reduced scalar minimax problem has a unique minimizer \(f_n^\star\), which is the posterior mean of \((p_+-p_-)/n\) given \(X_2\) under a least-favorable prior \(\pi_n^\star\) on \((p_+,p_-,r_0)\), and the Bayes risk of \(\pi_n^\star\) equals the scalar minimax value. A separate published reduction theorem equates the unrestricted full binary two-arm minimax value to that scalar value and proves that balanced Bernoulli assignment with \(f_n^\star(\sum_i S_i)\) attains the full-game value and is admissible. The published Airy theorems give an explicit scalar nonlinear-shrinkage upper sequence and an asymptotically least-favorable scalar prior sequence at scale \(n^{-4/3}\). The cited source does not lift \(\pi_n^\star\) to a prior on complete response schedules.
