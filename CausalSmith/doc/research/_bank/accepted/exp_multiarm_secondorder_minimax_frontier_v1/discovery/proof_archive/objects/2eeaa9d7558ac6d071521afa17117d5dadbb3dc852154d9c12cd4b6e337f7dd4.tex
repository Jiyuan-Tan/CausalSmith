For every nonzero zero-sum contrast, \[0\leq d_n(c)\leq C_0(c)C_A n^{-4/3}+o(n^{-4/3}),\qquad 0\leq\limsup_{n\to\infty}n^{4/3}d_n(c)\leq C_0(c)C_A.\] When \(|S_c|=2\), the embedded comparison is exact and the published Airy theorem gives \(a_n=n^{4/3}\) and \(C_{\mathrm{so}}(c)=C_0(c)C_A\). For \(c=c^\dagger\), the certified intervals for \(n^{4/3}d_n(c^\dagger)\) at \(n=1,\ldots,5\) end at \([0.948937,0.951865]\) for \(n=5\); this is consistent with, but does not prove, \(n^{4/3}\) convergence.
