The exact continuous-action squared-loss orbit game is a finite convex quadratically constrained program, whereas the certified computational rung is a rational grid-action linear program.  Its \(1/(4M^2)\) primal--dual certificate gap makes it usable at any subsequently proved scale satisfying \(a_n/M_n^2\to0\).  The available exact computations stop at \(n=5\); they cannot establish regular variation, a positive limit, stable active faces, separability, or existence and uniqueness for a Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman or cone operator.  The embedded converse gives an upper ceiling on \(d_n(c)\), not a matching positive lower bound when \(|S_c|\geq3\).
