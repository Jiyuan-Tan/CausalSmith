Exact grid actions were barycentered into deterministic orbit rules, and exact dual multipliers were converted into rational priors whose unrestricted posterior means give design-uniform Bayes lower bounds.  This produces a matched finite certificate sandwich with gap at most \(1/(4M^2)\).  The resulting allocations were compared with independent \(q^\star\), but the small-\(n\) supports do not determine their asymptotic relationship.
