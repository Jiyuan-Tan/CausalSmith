The exact score identities and the scalar minimax obstruction are proved in Proposition \(\text{prop:k3-scalar-score-not-minimax-preserving}\).  In particular, the scalar variance deficiency is \(n^{-2}\sum_i\mu_i^2\), and minimizing \(\sum_i\mu_i^2\) at fixed \(\sum_i\mu_i\) gives the local wedge \(\tfrac12|x|\).  Dilation would turn this into the conditional scalar candidate \(2^{-2/3}C_A\) only if the discarded arm-label information were proved negligible at the relevant scale; the exact \(n=3\) risk gap shows that such negligibility cannot be asserted from finite-experiment equivalence.
