When \(|S_c|=2\), the problem is solved exactly by lifting the published two-arm Bernoulli/nonlinear-shrinkage procedure and converse, giving \(\rho_n(c)=C_0(c)\rho_{n,2}\).  For the genuinely three-arm diagnostic, Theorem \(\text{thm:k3-grid-certificate-sandwich}\) gives computable procedures and priors satisfying
\[
 B_n(\nu_{n,M})\leq\rho_n^\dagger\leq U_{n,M}
 \leq\Lambda_{n,M}^\dagger
 \leq B_n(\nu_{n,M})+\frac1{4M^2}.
\]
Thus, once a normalizer \(a_n\) is proved and \(a_n/M_n^2\to0\), the extracted upper and lower certificates match at that scale.  This is a finite-\(n\) multivariate rule and unrestricted converse, but it does not select the scale or settle the design limit.
