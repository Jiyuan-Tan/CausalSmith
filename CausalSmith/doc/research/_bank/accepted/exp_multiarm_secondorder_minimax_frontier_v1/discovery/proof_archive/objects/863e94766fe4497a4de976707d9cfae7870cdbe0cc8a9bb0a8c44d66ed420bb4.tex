Fix \(n\geq 1\), \(K\geq 2\), and a nonzero zero-sum contrast \(c\). Define \(P_c=\{a\in\mathcal A_K:c_a>0\}\) and \(N_c=\{a\in\mathcal A_K:c_a<0\}\). On the subclass of group-homogeneous schedules satisfying \(t_{i,a}=y_i(1)\) for \(a\in P_c\), \(t_{i,a}=y_i(0)\) for \(a\in N_c\), and \(t_{i,a}=0\) otherwise, the contrast obeys \(\tau_c=(L_c/2)\tau_{(1,-1)}\). For the unrestricted minimax risks,
\[
 \rho_n(c)\geq C_0(c)\rho_n((1,-1))
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 0\leq d_n(c)\leq C_0(c)\{n^{-1}-\rho_n((1,-1))\}.
\]
If \(|S_c|=2\), both upper comparisons are exact:
\[
 \rho_n(c)=C_0(c)\rho_n((1,-1)),
 \qquad
 d_n(c)=C_0(c)\{n^{-1}-\rho_n((1,-1))\}.
\]
Consequently, the cited two-arm expansion implies, for every fixed \(c\),
\[
 0\leq d_n(c)\leq C_0(c)C_A n^{-4/3}+o(n^{-4/3}),
\]
with equality in the upper expansion when \(|S_c|=2\).
