Under fixed \(K\), \[\lim_{n\to\infty}n\rho_n(c)=C_0(c)=\frac{1}{4}\left(\sum_{a\in\mathcal A_K}|c_a|\right)^2.\] Independent assignment supported on \(S_c\) with \(q_a^\star=|c_a|/L_c\) and the projected rule \(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{cHT}}^\star\) satisfies \(\max_{z\in\mathcal T_K^n}R_n(\mathcal D,\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{cHT}}^\star;z)\le C_0(c)/n\). Because every target \(\tau_c(z)\) lies in \([-L_c/2,L_c/2]\), projection onto this interval cannot increase pointwise squared loss, so \(\widehat\tau_{\mathrm{cHT}}^\star\) belongs to the same minimax estimator action space. Conversely, every sequence of randomized designs and possibly biased estimators has asymptotic worst-case labeled-schedule risk at least \(C_0(c)/n+o(n^{-1})\).
