The causal target is the fixed finite-population contrast \(\tau_c(z)\); the randomness is only treatment assignment. The minimax functional \(\rho_n(c)\) is a design criterion, not an identification formula. The result says that one can choose a concrete allocation and observed-data shrinkage rule whose worst-case design-based mean squared error improves on \(C_0(c)/n\) by order \(n^{-4/3}\), and that no unrestricted design-estimator pair can improve by a larger order. Exact-rational certificates can audit the finite-population value at this scale. For an ACTG 175-style four-arm binary endpoint, the same construction applies to any prespecified zero-sum regimen contrast, but it does not select the scientific contrast or supply sampling-based inference.
