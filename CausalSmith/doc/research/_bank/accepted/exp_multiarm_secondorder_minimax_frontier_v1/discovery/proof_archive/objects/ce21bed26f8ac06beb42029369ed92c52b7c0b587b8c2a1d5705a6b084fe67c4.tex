Using the active-face boundary-layer handle, first determine whether there exists a regularly varying normalizer \(a_n\to\infty\) for which \(a_nd_n(c)\) converges to a finite positive limit. In that existence branch, define \(C_{\mathrm{so}}(c)=\lim_{n\to\infty}a_nd_n(c)\in(0,\infty)\). In the alternative branch, prove that no regularly varying normalization has a finite positive limit and identify the correct, possibly non-regular, asymptotic behavior. Characterize the corresponding limiting value problem and its unique admissible solution, deciding whether simultaneous simplex faces and corners produce a genuinely multidimensional free-boundary Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman problem, an elliptic cone spectral problem, or a separable collection of scalar layers. For \(c=c^\dagger\), the decision must agree with the certified LP scaling and must replace rather than assume the candidate \(a_n=n^{4/3}\) if the certificates disagree.
