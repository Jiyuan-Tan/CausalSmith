For \(c^\dagger=(1,-1/2,-1/2)\),
\[
 L_{c^\dagger}=|1|+|-1/2|+|-1/2|=2,
 \qquad h_{c^\dagger}=1,
 \qquad C_0(c^\dagger)=1.

Consequently,
\[
 \Gamma_{M,c^\dagger}
 =\{-1+j/M:j=0,\ldots,2M\}
 =\Gamma_M.
\]
After substituting \(K=3\) and \(c=c^\dagger\), every variable, normalization constraint, and response-count risk constraint in the rational-contrast grid program is therefore identical to its counterpart in the three-arm grid program.  The likelihood kernel is the same \(P_m(x\mid r)\), and the target is the same \(\tau_{c^\dagger}(m)\).  Hence
\[
 \Lambda_{n,M}(c^\dagger)=\Lambda_{n,M}^\dagger.
\]

The rational-contrast grid-certificate theorem supplies exact rational optimal primal and dual certificates.  Specialize one such pair at \(c=c^\dagger\), denote its primal variables by \((\pi^{n,M},w^{n,M})\), and denote the response-count component of its dual certificate by \(\nu_{n,M}\).  Under this specialization, the general quantities \(\kappa_{r,x}^{n,M,c}\), \(\delta_{n,M,c}\), \(U_{n,M}(c)\), \(D_{r,x,c}\), \(N_{r,x,c}\), and \(B_{n,c}\) become exactly the quantities displayed in the present theorem.  Since \(C_0(c^\dagger)=1\), the general finite-sample certificate sandwich becomes
\[
 B_n(\nu_{n,M})
 \leq \rho_n^\dagger
 \leq U_{n,M}
 \leq \Lambda_{n,M}^\dagger
 \leq B_n(\nu_{n,M})+\frac{1}{4M^2}.
\]
This specialization also preserves all boundary conventions.  Division by \(\pi_r^{n,M}\) occurs only when \(\pi_r^{n,M}>0\); a value assigned to \(\delta_{n,M}(r,x)\) when \(\pi_r^{n,M}=0\) has zero risk weight; and terms with \(D_{r,x}(\nu)=0\) are omitted from the posterior-mean formula.  No positivity or overlap condition is needed.  Every \(m\in\mathcal M_{n,3}\), including schedules supported on the all-zero or all-one response type, remains a risk constraint.  The upper certificate is implemented by drawing \(r\) according to \(\pi^{n,M}\), assigning uniformly conditional on \(r\), and applying the barycentered rule \(\delta_{n,M}(r,x)\).  The square-completion argument in the general theorem shows that the unrestricted posterior mean \(N_{r,x}(\nu)/D_{r,x}(\nu)\) gives the asserted Bayes-risk lower certificate.

For the scale statement, the finite-sample sandwich gives
\[
 0\leq U_{n,M_n}-B_n(\nu_{n,M_n})
 \leq \frac{1}{4M_n^2}.
\]
Thus \(a_n/M_n^2\to0\) implies
\[
 a_n\{U_{n,M_n}-B_n(\nu_{n,M_n})\}\longrightarrow0.

Because \(C_0(c^\dagger)=1\), the same sandwich also yields
\[
 a_n\{n^{-1}-U_{n,M_n}\}
 \leq a_nd_n(c^\dagger)
 \leq a_n\{n^{-1}-B_n(\nu_{n,M_n})\}.
\]
The two outer sequences differ by at most \(a_n/(4M_n^2)\).  If the middle sequence has a finite limit, squeezing proves that both certificate improvements have that same limit.
