The embedded two-arm converse and the first-order upper construction prove, for every nonzero zero-sum \(c\),
\[
 0\leq d_n(c)\leq C_0(c)\{n^{-1}-\rho_{n,2}\}
 =C_0(c)C_A n^{-4/3}+o(n^{-4/3}).
\]
Equality holds when \(|S_c|=2\), which completely selects \(a_n=n^{4/3}\) and \(C_{\mathrm{so}}(c)=C_0(c)C_A\) in that regime. For \(c=c^\dagger\), the exact rational certificates through \(n=5\) give rigorous intervals for \(n^{4/3}d_n(c^\dagger)\) and exhibit both face and corner support, but a finite prefix proves neither convergence nor an operator limit.
