For \(K=2\) and \(c=(1,-1)\), at every \(n\) the unrestricted binary finite-population minimax risk is attained by independent balanced Bernoulli assignment and the unique estimator that is the posterior mean of \((p_+-p_-)/n\) given \(X_2\) under a least-favorable prior on \((p_+,p_-,r_0)\). The estimator is a nonlinear shrinkage rule, and the same prior attains the exact finite-\(n\) Bayes-risk converse. The published Airy construction further gives an explicit second-order nonlinear shrinkage sequence and an asymptotically least-favorable prior sequence at scale \(n^{-4/3}\).
