**L-1 (For \(K=2\) and \(c=(1,-1)\), let \(\nu\) be any probabil…).** For \(K=2\) and \(c=(1,-1)\), let \(\nu\) be any probability distribution on triples \(\vartheta=(p_+,p_-,r_0)\) of nonnegative integers satisfying \(p_++p_-+r_0=n\). Conditional on \(\vartheta\), choose uniformly an ordered partition \((P,N,R)\) of \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\) with sizes \((p_+,p_-,r_0)\), assign response type \((1,0)\) on \(P\) and \((0,1)\) on \(N\), and, independently over \(i\in R\), draw \(H_i\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) and assign type \((H_i,H_i)\).
