Fix \(p\in(0,1)\) and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\). There is an admissible instance with binary \(E,U,W,Y\), singleton \(\mathcal X=\{x\}\), a fixed model \(\mathcal M^{(0)}\in\mathcal R_{1,1/4,1/16}\), and an array \(\mathbb M^{(1)}=(\mathcal M^{(1)}_n)_{n\ge2}\in\mathcal A_p\) such that \(\theta_{x,1}(\mathcal M^{(0)})=1/2\), \(\theta_{x,1,n}(\mathcal M^{(1)}_n)=5/8\) for every \(n\), and the two induced two-sample laws converge to one another in total variation. Consequently, for every sequence of measurable data-dependent nonempty sets \(\widehat K_n\subseteq[0,1]\) satisfying
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\mathbb M\in\mathcal A_p}
\mathbb P_{\mathcal M_n}^{\,n_s(n),n_t(n)}
\{\theta_{x,1,n}\in\widehat K_n\}
\ge 1-\alpha,
\]
and every fixed \(\varepsilon\in(0,1/8)\), the constructed fixed regular baseline satisfies
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}
\mathbb P_{\mathcal M^{(0)}}^{\,n_s(n),n_t(n)}
\left\{I^W_{n,1-\alpha}\ne\varnothing,\ 
d_H\!\left(\widehat K_n,I^W_{n,1-\alpha}\right)
\ge\frac18-\varepsilon\right\}
\ge1-\alpha.
\]
In particular, since the Wald interval is nonempty with probability tending to one, the same lower bound holds for \(\{d_H(\widehat K_n,I^W_{n,1-\alpha})\ge1/8-\varepsilon\}\) under any totalization on empty-interval samples. Thus \(d_H(\widehat K_n,I^W_{n,1-\alpha})\) does not converge to zero in probability, and \(\sqrt n\,d_H(\widehat K_n,I^W_{n,1-\alpha})\longrightarrow_P0\) is impossible a fortiori. Hence no sequence of measurable data-dependent nonempty confidence sets can combine unrestricted uniform coverage with even unscaled Wald localization on \(\mathcal A_p\). Uniform coverage alone remains attainable: the row-indexed version of \(\widehat C_{x,y,1-\alpha}\) from the concentration projection theorem has the displayed uniform coverage, including along changing-rank arrays.
