Fix \(p\in(0,1)\) and one deterministic allocation sequence \((n_s(n),n_t(n))_{n\ge2}\) satisfying the fixed-proportion triangular-allocation condition. Relative to that one sequence,
\[
\mathcal A_p=\left\{\mathbb M=(\mathcal M_n)_{n\ge2}:\begin{array}{l}
\text{for every \(n\ge2\), the row data have the finite product law }\\
\mathbb P_{\mathcal M_n}^{\,n_s(n),n_t(n)}=P_{O,n}^{\otimes n_s(n)}\otimes b_n^{\otimes n_t(n)}
\text{ on }\Omega_n=\Omega(n_s(n),n_t(n)),\\
\text{and }b_n\in\operatorname{col}(B_{x,n})
\end{array}\right\}.
\]
No restriction is imposed on changes in \(\operatorname{rank}(B_{x,n})\), singularity of the joint multinomial covariance, or divergence of \(d_{x,n}\).
