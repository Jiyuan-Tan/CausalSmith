For every \(n\ge2\), the source observations \(O^s_{1,n},\ldots,O^s_{n_s(n),n}\) form the first finite coordinate block of the row-\(n\) two-sample experiment, whose joint law on \(\Omega_n=\Omega(n_s(n),n_t(n))\) is exactly \(\mathbb P_{\mathcal M_n}^{\,n_s(n),n_t(n)}=P_{O,n}^{\otimes n_s(n)}\otimes b_n^{\otimes n_t(n)}\). This asserts only a finite row-wise product law and no \(\mathbb N\)-indexed i.i.d. source sequence.
