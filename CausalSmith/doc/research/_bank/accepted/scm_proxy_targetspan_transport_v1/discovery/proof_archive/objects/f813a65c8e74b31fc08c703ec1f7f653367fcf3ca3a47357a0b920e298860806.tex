The requested simultaneous conclusion is false on the stated unrestricted array class.  More precisely, fix \(p\in(0,1)\) and \(\alpha\in(0,1)\).  There is an admissible instance with binary \(E,U,W,Y\), singleton \(\mathcal X=\{x\}\), a fixed model in \(\mathcal R_{1,1/4,1/16}\), and an array \(\mathbb M\in\mathcal A_p\) such that the fixed model has \(\theta_{x,1}=1/2\), every row of the array has \(\theta_{x,1,n}=5/8\), and the two induced two-sample laws become arbitrarily close in total variation.  Consequently, no sequence of measurable data-dependent nonempty sets \(\widehat C^{\mathrm{SAR}}_{x,1,n,1-\alpha}\) can satisfy both
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{\mathbb M\in\mathcal A_p}
\mathbb P_{\mathcal M_n}^{\,n_s(n),n_t(n)}
\{\theta_{x,1,n}\in\widehat C^{\mathrm{SAR}}_{x,1,n,1-\alpha}\}
\ge 1-\alpha
\]
and the asserted fixed-regular requirement
\[
\sqrt n\,d_H\!\left(\widehat C^{\mathrm{SAR}}_{x,1,n,1-\alpha},
I^W_{n,1-\alpha}\right)\longrightarrow_P0
\]
for every fixed model in every \(\mathcal R_{r_0,c,\eta}\).  Hence no completion of \(\mathsf{SAR}_{x,y,n}\) can have all the properties in the question without restricting \(\mathcal A_p\).  The uniform-coverage half alone remains attainable: the row-indexed version of \(\widehat C_{x,y,1-\alpha}\) from the concentration projection theorem has the displayed uniform coverage, including along changing-rank arrays, but the impossibility result shows that it cannot also have the demanded Wald equivalence throughout the present class.
