Fix a baseline \(\mathcal M=(\pi,S,M,a,f,q)\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)\), an outcome value \(y\in\mathcal Y\), and \(h\in\mathcal H_x(\mathcal D)\). By Definition~\ref{def:failure-separator},
\[
h^{\top}B_x=0,\qquad h^{\top}b\ne0.
\tag{1}
\]
Let \(v=M^{\top}h\), as in Definition~\ref{def:outcome-null-perturbation}. Lemma~\ref{lem:observable-factorization} and (1) give
\[
v^{\top}R_x=h^{\top}MR_x=h^{\top}B_x=0,
\qquad
v^{\top}q=h^{\top}Mq=h^{\top}b\ne0.
\tag{2}
\]
In particular, \(v\ne0\).

Fix any \(w_{\star}\in\mathcal W\) and any \(y^{\circ}\in\mathcal Y\setminus\{y\}\). These choices exist because \(\mathcal W\) is nonempty and \(\lvert\mathcal Y\rvert\ge2\). Strict primitive positivity gives \(M_{w_{\star}u}>0\), \(f_{x,y}(u,w_{\star})>0\), and \(f_{x,y^{\circ}}(u,w_{\star})>0\) for every \(u\). Since \(\mathcal U\) is finite and \(v\ne0\), the number
\[
\varepsilon
=\frac12\min_{u:v_u\ne0}
\frac{M_{w_{\star}u}\min\{f_{x,y}(u,w_{\star}),
f_{x,y^{\circ}}(u,w_{\star})\}}{|v_u|}
\tag{3}
\]
is well defined and strictly positive. For \(|t|<\varepsilon\), Definition~\ref{def:outcome-null-perturbation} changes the two displayed outcome probabilities by opposite amounts whose absolute value is strictly smaller than half of each original probability. Thus those probabilities remain strictly positive. Every other outcome probability is unchanged, and the opposite changes preserve the sum over \(\mathcal Y\). Hence \(f^{(t)}\) is a strictly positive stochastic kernel. All other primitive kernels and the full-column rank of \(M\) are unchanged, so \(\mathcal M_t\in\mathfrak M_{+}\).

It remains to verify compatibility with the entire supplied law, not only with its balancing moments. For the only possibly changed source cell with outcome \(y\), Assumption~\ref{ass:latent-shift-factorization} and Definition~\ref{def:outcome-null-perturbation} give, for each \(e\in\mathcal E\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
&P_{O,t}(e,w_{\star},x,y)-P_O(e,w_{\star},x,y)\\
&\quad=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}\pi_eS_{ue}M_{w_{\star}u}a_x(u)
\left(t\frac{v_u}{M_{w_{\star}u}}\right)\\
&\quad=t\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}S_{ue}a_x(u)v_u\\
&\quad=t\pi_e\rho_x(e)\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}R_x(u,e)v_u
=0,
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
where the last equality is (2). The change for outcome \(y^{\circ}\) is the negative of (4), hence is also zero. Cells with another outcome, another proxy value, or another treatment value are unchanged by construction. Therefore every cell of \(P_O\) is preserved. Moreover, \(M\) and \(q\) are fixed, so Lemma~\ref{lem:observable-factorization} gives \(b_t=Mq=b\). It follows from Definition~\ref{def:compatible-fiber} that
\[
\mathcal M_t\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)
\qquad (|t|<\varepsilon).
\tag{5}
\]

For the causal query, the perturbation changes \(g_{x,y}(u)\) by
\[
M_{w_{\star}u}\left(t\frac{v_u}{M_{w_{\star}u}}\right)=tv_u.
\tag{6}
\]
The interventional identity in Lemma~\ref{lem:observable-factorization}, followed by (2), therefore yields
\[
\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M_t)-\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M)
=tq^{\top}v
=t h^{\top}Mq
=t h^{\top}b.
\tag{7}
\]
The slope in (7) is nonzero by (1).

Finally, \(b\notin\operatorname{col}(B_x)\) ensures that \(\mathcal H_x(\mathcal D)\) is nonempty. Indeed, each column of \(B_x\) is orthogonal to \(\ker B_x^{\top}\), so \(\operatorname{col}(B_x)\subseteq(\ker B_x^{\top})^{\perp}\). Rank--nullity gives
\[
\dim (\ker B_x^{\top})^{\perp}
=m-\dim\ker B_x^{\top}
=\operatorname{rank}(B_x^{\top})
=\operatorname{rank}(B_x)
=\dim\operatorname{col}(B_x),
\]
and hence the inclusion is equality. If every \(h\in\ker B_x^{\top}\) obeyed \(h^{\top}b=0\), then \(b\) would lie in \((\ker B_x^{\top})^{\perp}=\operatorname{col}(B_x)\), a contradiction. Choosing such an \(h\), equations (5)--(7) show that the compatible values include
\[
\bigl(\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M)-\varepsilon|h^{\top}b|,
      \theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M)+\varepsilon|h^{\top}b|\bigr),
\tag{8}
\]
a nondegenerate open interval around the baseline value.
