Assume first that \(b\in\operatorname{col}(B_x)\).  By the definition of column space, there exists \(\lambda\in\mathbb R^{\lvert\mathcal E\rvert}\) with \(B_x\lambda=b\); equivalently, Definition~\ref{def:balancing-fiber} gives \(\Lambda_x(\mathcal D)\ne\varnothing\).  Fix any such \(\lambda\).  Every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)\) induces the same observed \(B_x\), \(b\), and \(c_{x,y}\).  Lemma~\ref{lem:observable-factorization}, applied within each compatible SCM, gives
\[
\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M)=c_{x,y}\lambda.
\tag{1}
\]
The right-hand side depends only on the supplied observed law and a solution of its displayed linear system.  Thus (1) is constant over \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)\).

The same lemma applies to every \(\lambda'\in\Lambda_x(\mathcal D)\), so
\[
c_{x,y}\lambda=\theta_{x,y}=c_{x,y}\lambda'.
\tag{2}
\]
Solution independence may also be seen directly.  Lemma~\ref{lem:observable-factorization} and proxy-channel injectivity give
\[
MR_x(\lambda-\lambda')=B_x(\lambda-\lambda')=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_x(\lambda-\lambda')=0,
\tag{3}
\]
and hence
\[
c_{x,y}(\lambda-\lambda')
=g_{x,y}^{\top}R_x(\lambda-\lambda')=0.
\tag{4}
\]

Conversely, suppose \(b\notin\operatorname{col}(B_x)\).  The compatible fiber is nonempty by hypothesis, so choose \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)\).  Theorem~\ref{thm:positive-full-law-converse} supplies a separator \(h\) and compatible strictly positive SCMs \(\mathcal M_t\) for all sufficiently small positive and negative \(t\), with
\[
\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M_t)-\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M)
=t h^{\top}b,
\qquad h^{\top}b\ne0.
\tag{5}
\]
Taking any sufficiently small \(t\ne0\) in (5) gives two members of the same observed-data fiber with different causal values.  Thus \(\theta_{x,y}\) is not constant on the fiber.  This proves the equivalence and the stated identifying functional.
