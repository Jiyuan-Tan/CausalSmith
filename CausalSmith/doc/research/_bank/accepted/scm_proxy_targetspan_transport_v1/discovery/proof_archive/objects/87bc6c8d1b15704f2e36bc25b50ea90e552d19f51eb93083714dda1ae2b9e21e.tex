Put \(\widehat T_n=(\widehat H_{x,n},\widehat z_{x,y,n},\widehat b_n)\), and let \(\widehat\Gamma_n\) be the plug-in covariance matrix of \(\sqrt n\,\widehat T_n\), formed from the joint source multinomial covariance and the target multinomial covariance with allocation factors \(p^{-1}\) and \((1-p)^{-1}\). The totalized plug-in variance is
\[
\widehat V_n=
\begin{cases}
D\phi_{r_0}(\widehat T_n)\,\widehat\Gamma_n\,D\phi_{r_0}(\widehat T_n)^{\top},
&\sigma_{r_0}(\widehat H_{x,n})>\sigma_{r_0+1}(\widehat H_{x,n}),\\
0,&\sigma_{r_0}(\widehat H_{x,n})\le\sigma_{r_0+1}(\widehat H_{x,n}),
\end{cases}
\]
where \(D\phi_{r_0}\) is the ordinary derivative of the selector-independent functional on the strict-cutoff-gap region and \(\sigma_{\bar q+1}=0\) for \(\bar q=\min(m,\lvert\mathcal E\rvert)\). The second line is the fixed nonnegative fallback.
