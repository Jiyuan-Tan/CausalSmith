For \(H\in\mathbb R^{m\times\lvert\mathcal E\rvert}\), \(z\in\mathbb R^{\lvert\mathcal E\rvert}\), and \(b\in\mathbb R^m\), define the scalar function \(\phi_{r_0}(H,z,b)=z^{\top}H_{r_0}^{\dagger}b\), where \(H_{r_0}=H^{\star}_{r_0}\) is the selector-based Gram-spectral truncation fixed in the definition of \(H_{r_0}\). Thus \(\phi_{r_0}\) is single valued at tied cutoffs. If \(\sigma_{r_0}(H)>\sigma_{r_0+1}(H)\), the retained subspace is unique, so both \(H_{r_0}\) and \(\phi_{r_0}(H,z,b)\) are independent of the fixed within-tie selector.
