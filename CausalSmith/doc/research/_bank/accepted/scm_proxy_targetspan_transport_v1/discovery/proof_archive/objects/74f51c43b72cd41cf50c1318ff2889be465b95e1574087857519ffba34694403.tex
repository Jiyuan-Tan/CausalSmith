The proved concentration projection has a row-wise version
\[
\widehat C^{\mathrm{conc}}_{x,y,n,1-\alpha}
=\left\{\vartheta\in[0,1]:\exists\kappa,
\ \lVert\widehat H_{x,n}\kappa-\widehat b_n\rVert_\infty
\le r_{s,n}\lVert\kappa\rVert_1+r_{t,n},\ 
|\widehat z_{x,y,n}^{\top}\kappa-\vartheta|
\le r_{s,n}\lVert\kappa\rVert_1\right\},
\]
where \(r_{s,n}=\sqrt{\log(2L/\alpha)/(2n_s(n))}\) and \(r_{t,n}=\sqrt{\log(2L/\alpha)/(2n_t(n))}\). The proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-sample-projection-coverage} applies separately in every row of every \(\mathbb M\in\mathcal A_p\), including changing rank, singular multinomial covariance, and diverging \(d_{x,n}\). Hence
\[
\inf_{\mathbb M\in\mathcal A_p}
\mathbb P_{\mathcal M_n}^{\,n_s(n),n_t(n)}
\{\theta_{x,y,n}\in\widehat C^{\mathrm{conc}}_{x,y,n,1-\alpha}\}
\ge1-\alpha
\]
for every \(n\), and therefore the requested uniform \(\liminf\) coverage inequality holds for this conservative, nonstudentized set. What remains open is a single computable studentized set that also has the two stated regular-branch equivalences.
