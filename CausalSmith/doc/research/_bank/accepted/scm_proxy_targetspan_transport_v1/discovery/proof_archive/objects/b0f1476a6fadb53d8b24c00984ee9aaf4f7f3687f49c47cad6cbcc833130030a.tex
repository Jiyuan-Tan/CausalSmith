Fix \(x\). Lemma~\ref{lem:observable-factorization} gives \(B_x=MR_x\), and therefore
\[
\operatorname{col}(B_x)\subseteq\operatorname{col}(M).
\tag{1}
\]
Assumption~\ref{ass:proxy-channel-injectivity} gives \(\operatorname{rank}(M)=r\). If also \(\operatorname{rank}(B_x)=r\), then both subspaces in (1) have dimension \(r\); hence they are equal. Since \(b=Mq\) by the same lemma, \(b\in\operatorname{col}(M)=\operatorname{col}(B_x)\). Theorem~\ref{thm:target-span-iff} then identifies the intervention law. This proves that the published rank condition implies the target-span condition.

We next verify that the inclusion of identifiable regimes is strict using Definition~\ref{def:rank-deficient-success-witness}. The columns of \(S\) sum respectively to
\[
\frac9{19}+\frac2{19}+\frac8{19}=1,
\qquad
\frac1{12}+\frac23+\frac14=1,
\tag{2}
\]
and all their entries are positive. Likewise, \(\pi=(1/2,1/2)\), each column of \(M\), and each of \(a_0=\mathbf 1-a_1\) and \(a_1\) are strictly positive probability vectors. The target vector is strictly positive and normalized because
\[
1197+2436+2204=5837.
\tag{3}
\]
For \(i,j\in\{0,1,2\}\) and \(x\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
\frac15\le f_{x,1}(u_i,w_j)\le\frac{13}{25},
\qquad
\frac{12}{25}\le f_{x,0}(u_i,w_j)\le\frac45,
\tag{4}
\]
so the outcome kernel is strictly positive. Finally, direct expansion gives
\[
\det(M)=\frac{49}{100}\ne0.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus \(M\) has rank \(3=r\), and all defining conditions of \(\mathfrak M_{+}\) hold for \(\mathcal M^{\star}\).
