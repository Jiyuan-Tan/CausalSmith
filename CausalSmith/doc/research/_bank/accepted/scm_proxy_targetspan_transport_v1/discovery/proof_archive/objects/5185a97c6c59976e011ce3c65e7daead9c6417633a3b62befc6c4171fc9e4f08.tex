Fix \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and \(y\in\mathcal Y\).  Membership in \(\mathfrak M_{+}\), by Definition~\ref{def:positive-latent-shift-class}, makes every primitive entry strictly positive.  Hence, for every \(e\in\mathcal E\),
\[
\rho_x(e)=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}a_x(u)S_{ue}>0.
\tag{1}
\]
All conditionals used below are therefore well defined.  Summing Assumption~\ref{ass:latent-shift-factorization} over \(y\), using normalization of the outcome kernel, gives
\[
P_O(E=e,W=w,X=x)
=\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}S_{ue}M_{wu}a_x(u).
\tag{2}
\]
Summing (2) over \(w\), using column-stochasticity of \(M\), gives
\[
P_O(E=e,X=x)=\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}S_{ue}a_x(u)
=\pi_e\rho_x(e)>0.
\tag{3}
\]
Consequently, the definitions of \(B_x\) and \(R_x\) imply, cell by cell,
\[
\begin{aligned}
B_x(w,e)
&=\frac{\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}S_{ue}M_{wu}a_x(u)}
        {\pi_e\rho_x(e)}\\
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}M_{wu}
  \frac{a_x(u)S_{ue}}{\rho_x(e)}
=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}M_{wu}R_x(u,e).
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
Thus \(B_x=MR_x\).

Summing Assumption~\ref{ass:target-mechanism-invariance} over \(x\) and \(y\), and using normalization of the treatment and outcome kernels, yields
\[
b_w=Q_{\mathcal M}(W=w)
=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}q_uM_{wu},
\tag{5}
\]
so \(b=Mq\).  Retaining the outcome factor in the source calculation instead gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
c_{x,y}(e)
&=\frac{\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}\sum_{w\in\mathcal W}
 S_{ue}a_x(u)M_{wu}f_{x,y}(u,w)}
 {\pi_e\rho_x(e)}\\
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}R_x(u,e)
  \sum_{w\in\mathcal W}M_{wu}f_{x,y}(u,w)\\
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}g_{x,y}(u)R_x(u,e).
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]
Hence \(c_{x,y}=g_{x,y}^{\top}R_x\).

Under \(\operatorname{do}(X=x)\), the SCM intervention replaces the treatment kernel by a point mass at \(x\), while leaving the target latent distribution and the invariant proxy and outcome mechanisms unchanged.  The truncated factorization from Assumption~\ref{ass:target-mechanism-invariance} therefore gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\theta_{x,y}
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}\sum_{w\in\mathcal W}
  q_uM_{wu}f_{x,y}(u,w)\\
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}q_ug_{x,y}(u)
=g_{x,y}^{\top}q.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Equation (7) is an SCM intervention identity, not a definition of the causal target as an observed-law functional.

Now let \(\lambda\in\Lambda_x(\mathcal D)\).  Definition~\ref{def:balancing-fiber} gives \(B_x\lambda=b\).  Equations (4)--(5) yield
\[
M(R_x\lambda-q)=0.
\tag{8}
\]
Definition~\ref{def:positive-latent-shift-class} gives \(\operatorname{rank}(M)=r\); hence \(M:\mathbb R^r\to\mathbb R^m\) is injective.  Thus
\[
R_x\lambda=q.
\tag{9}
\]

For the unconditional identities, put
\[
p_x(e)=P_O(E=e,X=x)=\pi_e\rho_x(e)>0,
\qquad
D_x=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(p_x(e):e\in\mathcal E\bigr).
\tag{10}
\]
Definition~\ref{def:unconditional-balancing-moments} and (4), (6) imply
\[
H_x=B_xD_x=MR_xD_x,
\qquad
z_{x,y}=D_xR_x^{\top}g_{x,y}.
\tag{11}
\]
Division in Definition~\ref{def:unconditional-weight-map} is valid by (10), and that definition gives \(D_x\kappa=\lambda\).  Therefore
\[
H_x\kappa=MR_xD_x\kappa=MR_x\lambda=B_x\lambda=b,
\tag{12}
\]
and
\[
z_{x,y}^{\top}\kappa
=g_{x,y}^{\top}R_xD_x\kappa
=g_{x,y}^{\top}R_x\lambda
=g_{x,y}^{\top}q
=\theta_{x,y}.
\tag{13}
\]
This proves every asserted factorization and balancing identity.
