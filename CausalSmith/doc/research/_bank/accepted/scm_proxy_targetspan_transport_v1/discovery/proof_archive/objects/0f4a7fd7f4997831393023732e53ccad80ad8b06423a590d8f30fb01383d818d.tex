Assume first that \(b\in\operatorname{col}(B_x)\). By the definition of a column space, there exists \(\lambda\in\mathbb R^{\lvert\mathcal E\rvert}\) with \(B_x\lambda=b\); equivalently, Definition~\ref{def:balancing-fiber} gives \(\Lambda_x(\mathcal D)\ne\varnothing\). Fix any such \(\lambda\). Every \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)\) induces the same observed \(B_x\), \(b\), and \(c_{x,y}\) by Definition~\ref{def:compatible-fiber}. Lemma~\ref{lem:observable-factorization}, applied within each compatible SCM, gives
\[
\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M)=c_{x,y}\lambda.
\tag{1}
\]
The right-hand side is a function only of the supplied observed law and the displayed solution of its observed linear system. Thus (1) is constant over \(\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)\), proving identification.

The same lemma applies to every \(\lambda'\in\Lambda_x(\mathcal D)\), and hence
\[
c_{x,y}\lambda=\theta_{x,y}=c_{x,y}\lambda'.
\tag{2}
\]
For completeness, solution independence also follows directly from the injectivity condition contained in \(\mathfrak M_{+}\):
\[
M R_x(\lambda-\lambda')=B_x(\lambda-\lambda')=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
R_x(\lambda-\lambda')=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
c_{x,y}(\lambda-\lambda')=g_{x,y}^{\top}R_x(\lambda-\lambda')=0.
\tag{3}
\]

Conversely, suppose \(b\notin\operatorname{col}(B_x)\). The fiber is nonempty by hypothesis, so choose a baseline \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)\). Theorem~\ref{thm:positive-full-law-converse} supplies strictly positive SCMs \(\mathcal M_t\in\mathfrak F(\mathcal D)\) for all sufficiently small positive and negative \(t\), with
\[
\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M_t)-\theta_{x,y}(\mathcal M)=t h^{\top}b
\tag{4}
\]
for a separator satisfying \(h^{\top}b\ne0\). Thus two compatible SCMs have different target interventional probabilities, so \(\theta_{x,y}\) is not constant on the fiber. This proves the reverse implication and completes the equivalence.
