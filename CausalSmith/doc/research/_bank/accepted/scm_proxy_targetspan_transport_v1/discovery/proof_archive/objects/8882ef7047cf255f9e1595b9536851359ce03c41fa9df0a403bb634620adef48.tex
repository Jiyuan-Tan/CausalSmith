Fix \(\mathcal M\in\mathfrak M_{+}\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and \(y\in\mathcal Y\).  By Assumption~\ref{ass:strict-primitive-positivity}, for every \(e\in\mathcal E\),
\[
\rho_x(e)=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}a_x(u)S_{ue}>0.
\]
Thus all conditionals below are well defined.  Summing the source factorization in Assumption~\ref{ass:latent-shift-factorization} over \(y\), and using that \(f_{x,\cdot}(u,w)\) is a probability kernel, gives
\[
P_O(E=e,W=w,X=x)
=\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}S_{ue}M_{wu}a_x(u).
\]
Summing this identity over \(w\), and using that each column of \(M\) sums to one, gives
\[
P_O(E=e,X=x)=\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}S_{ue}a_x(u)
=\pi_e\rho_x(e)>0.
\]
Consequently, by the definitions of \(B_x\) and \(R_x\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
B_x(w,e)
&=\frac{\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}S_{ue}M_{wu}a_x(u)}
        {\pi_e\rho_x(e)}\\
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}M_{wu}
  \frac{a_x(u)S_{ue}}{\rho_x(e)}
=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}M_{wu}R_x(u,e).
\end{aligned}
\]
This proves \(B_x=MR_x\).

Likewise, summing the target factorization in Assumption~\ref{ass:target-mechanism-invariance} over \(x\) and \(y\), and using normalization of the treatment and outcome kernels, yields
\[
b_w=Q_{\mathcal M}(W=w)
=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}q_uM_{wu},
\]
so \(b=Mq\).  If instead the outcome factor is retained in the source calculation, then
\[
\begin{aligned}
c_{x,y}(e)
&=\frac{\pi_e\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}\sum_{w\in\mathcal W}
 S_{ue}a_x(u)M_{wu}f_{x,y}(u,w)}
 {\pi_e\rho_x(e)}\\
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}R_x(u,e)
  \sum_{w\in\mathcal W}M_{wu}f_{x,y}(u,w)\\
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}g_{x,y}(u)R_x(u,e).
\end{aligned}
\]
Hence \(c_{x,y}=g_{x,y}^{\top}R_x\).

Under \(\operatorname{do}(X=x)\), the SCM intervention replaces the treatment kernel by a point mass at \(x\) and leaves the target latent law and the invariant \(W\)- and \(Y\)-mechanisms unchanged.  Therefore Assumption~\ref{ass:target-mechanism-invariance} and the truncated target factorization give
\[
\begin{aligned}
\theta_{x,y}
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}\sum_{w\in\mathcal W}
  q_uM_{wu}f_{x,y}(u,w)\\
&=\sum_{u\in\mathcal U}q_ug_{x,y}(u)
=g_{x,y}^{\top}q.
\end{aligned}
\]
This is an SCM intervention identity, not a definition of the causal target in terms of the observed law.

Now let \(\lambda\in\Lambda_x(\mathcal D)\).  Definition~\ref{def:balancing-fiber} gives \(B_x\lambda=b\).  Combining this equality with \(B_x=MR_x\) and \(b=Mq\) yields
\[
M(R_x\lambda-q)=0.
\]
By Definition~\ref{def:positive-latent-shift-class} and Assumption~\ref{ass:proxy-channel-injectivity}, \(\operatorname{rank}(M)=r\); hence the linear map \(v\mapsto Mv\) on \(\mathbb R^r\) is injective.  It follows that
\[
R_x\lambda=q.
\]

For the unconditional identities, define
\[
p_x(e)=P_O(E=e,X=x)=\pi_e\rho_x(e)>0,
\qquad
D_x=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(p_x(e):e\in\mathcal E\bigr).
\]
The definitions of \(H_x\) and \(z_{x,y}\) in Definition~\ref{def:unconditional-balancing-moments}, together with the conditional identities already proved, imply
\[
H_x(w,e)=p_x(e)B_x(w,e),
\qquad
z_{x,y}(e)=p_x(e)c_{x,y}(e).
\]
Equivalently,
\[
H_x=B_xD_x=MR_xD_x,
\qquad
z_{x,y}=D_xR_x^{\top}g_{x,y}.
\]
Definition~\ref{def:unconditional-weight-map} is applicable because \(p_x(e)>0\), and it gives \(D_x\kappa=\lambda\).  Therefore
\[
H_x\kappa
=MR_xD_x\kappa
=MR_x\lambda
=B_x\lambda
=b,
\]
and
\[
\begin{aligned}
z_{x,y}^{\top}\kappa
&=g_{x,y}^{\top}R_xD_x\kappa\\
&=g_{x,y}^{\top}R_x\lambda\\
&=g_{x,y}^{\top}q
=\theta_{x,y}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Finally, the same factorization proves the automatic outcome-row property required by the regular branch.  Since \(\operatorname{rank}(M)=r\), one has \(\operatorname{rank}(M^{\top})=r\), so \(M^{\top}:\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R^r\) is surjective.  Thus there exists \(h\in\mathbb R^m\) satisfying \(M^{\top}h=g_{x,y}\).  Using the two unconditional factorizations above,
\[
H_x^{\top}h
=D_xR_x^{\top}M^{\top}h
=D_xR_x^{\top}g_{x,y}
=z_{x,y}.
\]
Hence \(z_{x,y}\in\operatorname{col}(H_x^{\top})=\operatorname{row}(H_x)\).  In particular, outcome-row membership follows from the model class and is not an additional assumption.  All asserted identities have now been established.
