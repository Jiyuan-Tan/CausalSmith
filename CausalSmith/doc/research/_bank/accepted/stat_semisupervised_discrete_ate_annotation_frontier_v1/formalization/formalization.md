**L-9 (There is a positive numerical \(c\), independent of…).** There is a positive numerical \(c\), independent of \(m,d\) and depending on \(\epsilon\) only through the requirement \(\epsilon<1/2\), such that for every \(n\ge1\), \(m\ge0\), and \(d\ge2\), \[ R_\epsilon(n,m,d)\ge c/n. \tag{C39} \] The two hypotheses have the same treatment--covariate marginal, so the bound is entirely label-gated.

**L-10 (There are positive constants…).** There are positive constants \(b_\epsilon,\gamma_\epsilon,c_\epsilon,C_\epsilon\) and \(0<\rho_\epsilon<1\) with the following property. Let \(L\ge2\), let \(u,v\ge0\), put \(M=u+v>0\), and choose \(B>0\), \(a=\gamma_\epsilon B/L^2\), and an integer \(k\le d-1\) such that \[ MB\le b_\epsilon L,\qquad ka\le c_\epsilon. \tag{C42} \] Apply the finite-atom recipe in \(\mathrm{def:common\mbox{-}marginal\mbox{-}prior\mbox{-}handle}\) to \(k\) rare cells at scale \((a,B,L)\). The normalized laws lie in \(\mathcal M_{d,\epsilon}\), the two priors induce exactly the same distribution of the full random table \(P_{XA}\), their raw target centers are separated by at least \(c_\epsilon ka\), and their raw total-mass and target variances are at most \(C_\epsilon kBa\). In the raw Poisson experiment of labeled intensity \(u\) and auxiliary intensity \(v\), the two mixture laws have total variation at most \[ C_\epsilon uka\rho_\epsilon^L. \tag{C43} \]
