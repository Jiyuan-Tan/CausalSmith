The established order results imply, uniformly over every deterministic threshold sequence, that \(0<c\leq L_n^\star/r_n\leq C<\infty\).  Conditional on each realized design, the local optimization is the explicit finite-dimensional affine problem \(\inf_{w\in\mathcal W_{z,x}}\{\operatorname{exactHolderBias}_{z,x}(w)+t_\alpha\|w\|_2\}\), with value zero when \(\mathcal W_{z,x}=\varnothing\).  The total-Gram theorem controls the probability of the latter event and gives order-level weight bounds on its complement.  These facts establish the sharp order but not a common asymptotic constant or the honesty of the zero-totalized candidate interval.
