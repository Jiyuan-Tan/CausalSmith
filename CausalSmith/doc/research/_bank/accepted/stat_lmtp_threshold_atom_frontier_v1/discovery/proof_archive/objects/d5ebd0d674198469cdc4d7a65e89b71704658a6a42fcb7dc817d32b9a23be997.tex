Let \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\), and denote its observed \((X,A,Y)\)-margin by \(P\).  Definition \texttt{def:full-data-model-class} gives \(P\in\mathcal M\), and Proposition \texttt{prop:causal-bridge} gives, for every declared threshold,
\[
 \psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})=\theta_{\delta_n}(P). \tag{31}
\]
Both \(\widehat\theta_n\) and \(\mathrm{CI}_n\) are measurable functions only of \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\).  Their distributions under \((P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}\) therefore equal their distributions under \(P^{\otimes n}\).  The upper-risk conclusion of Theorem \texttt{thm:minimax-risk}, the coverage conclusion of Theorem \texttt{thm:honest-length}, and its expected-length upper bound, applied to the observed margin and combined with (31), prove all three displayed upper bounds uniformly over \(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\).

We explicitly verify that the two observed lower-bound experiments lift into the same full-data class.  For either the global pair or the localized pair in the proof of Theorem \texttt{thm:minimax-risk}, let \(\mu_j(a)\) be the Bernoulli regression under alternative \(j\in\{0,1\}\).  On an extension of that experiment, take \(U\sim\operatorname{Unif}[0,1]\) independent of \((X,A)\), and define
\[
 g_{j,x}(a,u)=\mathbf 1\{u\leq\mu_j(a)\},
 \qquad
 Y_j(a)=g_{j,X}(a,U),
 \qquad
 Y_j=g_{j,X}(A,U). \tag{32}
\]
The space \([0,1]\) is standard Borel, and each \(g_{j,x}\) is jointly Borel measurable because \(\mu_j\) is continuous.  Equation (32) holds simultaneously for every \(a\) with no exceptional diagonal, and it gives observed consistency.  Independence of \(U\) from \((X,A)\) implies the latent-randomization condition \(A\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}U\mid X\).  Finally, for all \(s,t\in[0,1]\),
\[
 \mathbb E[|Y_j(t)-Y_j(s)|\mid X=x]
 =\int_0^1|\mathbf 1\{u\leq\mu_j(t)\}-\mathbf 1\{u\leq\mu_j(s)\}|\,\mathrm du
 =|\mu_j(t)-\mu_j(s)|\longrightarrow0 \tag{33}
\]
as \(t\to s\).  Thus Assumptions \texttt{ass:consistency}, \texttt{ass:exchangeability}, and \texttt{ass:full-data-response-continuity} all hold.  Conditional on \((A=a,X=x)\), (32) is Bernoulli with mean \(\mu_j(a)\), so its observed margin is exactly the original \(P_j\in\mathcal M\).  Hence each lifted law belongs to \(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\).

Applying Proposition \texttt{prop:causal-bridge} to the two lifts makes their causal target separations identical to the observed separations: \(a_0n^{-1/2}\) for the global pair and at least \(a_1\delta_n^{\kappa+1}h_n^\beta\) for the localized pair.  Observed-sample estimators and intervals see exactly the same product distributions as before.  Therefore the two-point inequality (15) in the proof of Theorem \texttt{thm:minimax-risk} yields
\[
 R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star\geq cr_n,
\]
and the two-coverage inequality (29)--(30) in the proof of Theorem \texttt{thm:honest-length} yields
\[
 L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star\geq cr_n.
\]
These lower bounds use full-data members of the very class governing the upper bounds.  Combined with the first paragraph, they prove the theorem.
