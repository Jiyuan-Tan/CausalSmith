For each member of \(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\), let \(P^{\mathrm F}\) denote its packaged probability-law component. For estimators and intervals measurable with respect to the observed sample \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\), define
\[
 R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=\inf_{\widehat\psi}\sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}|\widehat\psi-\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})|
\]
and
\[
 L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=
 \inf_{C_n:\,\inf_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 (P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}\{\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})\in C_n\}\geq1-\alpha}
 \sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}[\operatorname{len}(C_n)].
\]
Thus both optimizations range over one carrier-heterogeneous class, while every sampling probability is evaluated under the law component of the selected member.
