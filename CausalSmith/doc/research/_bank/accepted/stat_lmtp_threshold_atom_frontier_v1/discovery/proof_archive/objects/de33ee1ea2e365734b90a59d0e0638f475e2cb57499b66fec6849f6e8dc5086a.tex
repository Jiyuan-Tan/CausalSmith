\(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) is the set of laws \(P\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X\times[0,1]\times[0,1])\) satisfying \(\texttt{ass:conditional-density-law}\), \(\texttt{ass:stratum-mass}\), and \(\texttt{ass:polynomial-thinning}\), and such that, for every \(x\in\mathcal X\), an almost-everywhere version of \(\mathbb E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=x]\) admits a continuous extension to \([0,\bar\delta]\).  There is no common Hölder exponent, modulus, or radius.
