Let \(P^{\mathrm F}\) satisfy the premises of the proposed statement, and fix a stratum \(x\in\mathcal X\).  Assumption \texttt{ass:stratum-mass} gives \(P^{\mathrm F}(X=x)=p_x\geq p_{\min}>0\), so all conditional laws below are well defined.  Assumption \texttt{ass:consistency} supplies the jointly measurable bounded potential-outcome process.  Under the directed NPSEM replacement of Assumption \texttt{ass:exchangeability}, the conditional law of \((A,U)\) given \(X=x\) is the product of its two conditional marginals, and there is one event of probability one on which, simultaneously for every \(a\in[0,1]\),
\[
 Y(a)=g_X(a,U),
 \qquad
 Y=g_X(A,U).
 \tag{1}
\]
The common null set in (1), rather than a dose-dependent null set, is essential when \(a\) is later replaced by the random variable \(A\).  The map \(g_x\) is jointly Borel measurable and takes values in \([0,1]\), so all integrals below exist and Fubini's identity applies without an additional integrability condition.

Define
\[
 m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)
 =\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[g_x(a,U)\mid X=x].
 \tag{2}
\]
For every Borel set \(B\subseteq[0,1]\), the product conditional law, (1), Assumption \texttt{ass:conditional-density-law}, and Fubini's identity give, equation by equation,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y\mathbf 1\{A\in B\}\mid X=x]
 &=\int_{B\times\mathsf U}g_x(a,u)\,
     P^{\mathrm F}(\mathrm da\mid X=x)
     P^{\mathrm F}(\mathrm du\mid X=x)\\
 &=\int_B\left\{\int_{\mathsf U}g_x(a,u)
     P^{\mathrm F}(\mathrm du\mid X=x)\right\}\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\\
 &=\int_Bm_x^{\mathrm F}(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
Here \(\mathsf U\) denotes the standard Borel state space of \(U\); no density for \(U\) is required.

We next show that (3) selects the already declared continuous observed-regression version.  By (1), (2), and Assumption \texttt{ass:full-data-response-continuity},
\[
 \left|m_x^{\mathrm F}(t)-m_x^{\mathrm F}(s)\right|
 \leq
 \mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}
 \bigl[|g_x(t,U)-g_x(s,U)|\mid X=x\bigr]
 =\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}
 \bigl[|Y(t)-Y(s)|\mid X=x\bigr]
 \longrightarrow0
 \tag{4}
\]
as \(t\to s\).  Thus \(m_x^{\mathrm F}\) is continuous on \([0,1]\).  Assumption \texttt{ass:holder-regression} says that \(\mu_x^P\) is continuous and is an almost-everywhere version of the conditional mean of \(Y\) given \((A,X=x)\).  Consequently, using Assumption \texttt{ass:conditional-density-law} once more, for every Borel \(B\),
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A\in B\}\mid X=x]
 =\int_B\mu_x^P(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da.
 \tag{5}
\]
The observed margin of \(P^{\mathrm F}\) is \(P\), so subtracting (5) from (3) yields
\[
 \int_B\{m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)-\mu_x^P(a)\}\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da=0
 \qquad\text{for every Borel }B\subseteq[0,1].
 \tag{6}
\]
To make the resulting version argument explicit, put \(f=m_x^{\mathrm F}-\mu_x^P\).  For every integer \(j\geq1\), take in (6) first \(B=\{f\geq1/j\}\) and then \(B=\{f\leq-1/j\}\).  The nonnegativity of \(\pi_x\) shows that both sets have zero measure under \(\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\).  Their countable union is \(\{f\neq0\}\); hence \(f=0\) for \(\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\)-almost every \(a\).

Assumption \texttt{ass:polynomial-thinning} gives \(\pi_x(a)\geq c_-a^\kappa>0\) for Lebesgue-almost every \(a\in(0,1]\), because \(c_->0\) and \(\kappa\geq0\).  Therefore (6) implies
\[
 m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mu_x^P(a)
 \quad\text{for Lebesgue-almost every }a\in(0,1].
 \tag{7}
\]
Both sides of (7) are continuous by (4) and Assumption \texttt{ass:holder-regression}.  If their difference were nonzero at any \(a_0\in[0,1]\), continuity would make its absolute value bounded away from zero on a relative neighborhood of \(a_0\) whose intersection with \((0,1]\) has positive Lebesgue measure, contradicting (7).  Thus
\[
 m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mu_x^P(a)
 \qquad\text{for every }a\in[0,1].
 \tag{8}
\]
This includes the boundary dose \(a=0\); no observed conditional mean at a singleton was inserted by definition.

Now fix \(\delta\in[0,\bar\delta]\).  Definition \texttt{def:clamp-policy} and the simultaneous identities (1) imply, on the same probability-one event,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 Y\{d_\delta(A)\}
 &=g_X\{d_\delta(A),U\}\\
 &=g_X(A,U)\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}
   +g_X(\delta,U)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}\\
 &=Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}
   +Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]
Applying the same conditional product-law calculation as in (3), now with the fixed response \(g_x(\delta,U)\) and the Borel set \([0,\delta]\), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}\mid X=x]
 &=m_x^{\mathrm F}(\delta)P(A\leq\delta\mid X=x)\\
 &=\mu_x^P(\delta)q_x(\delta),
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]
where (8) gives the first factor and Proposition \texttt{prop:pushforward-setup} gives the second.  Taking expectations in (9), using that the observed margin is \(P\), summing over the finite stratum space, and invoking the definitions of \(\psi_\delta\) and \(\theta_\delta\), we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F})
 &=\mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}]
   +\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}p_xq_x(\delta)\mu_x^P(\delta)\\
 &=\theta_\delta(P).
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
Proposition \texttt{prop:policy-support} separately verifies the required support condition for every realized \(d_\delta(A)\).  Equations (3), (8), (9), and (11) prove all parts of the proposed statement for every declared threshold, including both boundary cases \(\delta=0\) and \(\delta=\bar\delta\).
