Fix \(P\in\mathcal M\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and \(\delta\in[0,\bar\delta]\).  For every Borel set \(B\subseteq[0,1]\), Definition \texttt{def:clamp-policy} gives the disjoint inverse-image decomposition
\[
 \{d_\delta(A)\in B\}
 =\{A\in B\cap(\delta,1]\}
   \mathbin{\dot\cup}
   \bigl(\{A\in[0,\delta]\}\cap\{\delta\in B\}\bigr).
\]
By Assumption \texttt{ass:conditional-density-law}, conditional on \(X=x\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 P\{d_\delta(A)\in B\mid X=x\}
 &=\int_{B\cap(\delta,1]}\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da
   +\mathbf 1\{\delta\in B\}\int_0^\delta\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\\
 &=\int_B\pi_x(a)\mathbf 1\{a>\delta\}\,\mathrm da
   +q_x(\delta)\delta_\delta(B),
 \end{aligned}
\]
where the last equality is the definition of \(q_x\).  Equality on every Borel set proves the asserted equality of measures.  Finally, Assumption \texttt{ass:polynomial-thinning} and integration of its almost-everywhere inequalities give
\[
 \frac{c_-}{\kappa+1}\delta^{\kappa+1}
 =\int_0^\delta c_-a^\kappa\,\mathrm da
 \le q_x(\delta)
 \le\int_0^\delta c_+a^\kappa\,\mathrm da
 =\frac{c_+}{\kappa+1}\delta^{\kappa+1}.
\]
This also covers \(\delta=0\), when all three quantities vanish.
