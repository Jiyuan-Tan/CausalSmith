The result fixes a finite number of strata, a bounded outcome, a known Hölder radius, and a known thinning exponent. Every full-data member packages its own standard-Borel carrier, latent variable, jointly Borel response maps, and potential-outcome process; no common carrier, and no richness condition on every carrier, is imposed. Exact equality of the causal clamp mean and the observed functional requires simultaneous latent-response consistency, conditional latent randomization, positive exposure density on \((0,1]\), and conditional \(L^1\)-response continuity. The last premise is non-testable and scientifically substantive, but under it the pointwise equality is proved rather than posited. The causal lower bounds use particular admissible members with carrier \([0,1]\); they do not claim the same lower frontier for a model obtained by externally fixing an arbitrary carrier such as a singleton. The proposal proves no longitudinal recursion, no simultaneous band over a continuum of thresholds, and no claim about the validity of McCoy et al.'s estimator for their different stochastic-shift target.
