\(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) is the carrier-heterogeneous class of full-data packages. A member consists of its own standard Borel space \(\mathcal U\), an \(\mathcal U\)-valued variable \(U\), jointly Borel maps \(\{g_x:[0,1]\times\mathcal U\to[0,1]:x\in\mathcal X\}\), a potential-outcome process \(\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\}\), and a probability-law component, denoted \(P^{\mathrm F}\), for \((X,A,Y,U,\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\})\). Its observed \((X,A,Y)\)-margin is some \(P\in\mathcal M\), and the packaged objects satisfy the simultaneous latent-response factorization, latent randomization, and conditional \(L^1\)-response-continuity member conditions. The notation \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) ranges over these packages, and in probabilities and expectations \(P^{\mathrm F}\) denotes the package's probability-law component. No carrier is fixed across members.
