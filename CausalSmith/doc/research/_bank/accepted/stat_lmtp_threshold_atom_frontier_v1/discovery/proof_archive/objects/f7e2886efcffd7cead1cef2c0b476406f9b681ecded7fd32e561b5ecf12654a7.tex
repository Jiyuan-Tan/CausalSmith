Let \(P^{\mathrm F}\) be a member-level full-data structure with observed margin \(P\in\mathcal M\), and let \(\mathcal U\), \(U\), \(\{g_x:x\in\mathcal X\}\), and \(\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\}\) denote the standard-Borel carrier and response objects packaged by this particular member. Suppose \(U\) is \(\mathcal U\)-valued and, for every \(x\in\mathcal X\), \(g_x:[0,1]\times\mathcal U\to[0,1]\) is jointly Borel measurable, such that outside one \(P^{\mathrm F}\)-null set, simultaneously for every \(a\in[0,1]\), \(Y(a)=g_X(a,U)\) and \(Y=g_X(A,U)\), and such that \(A\perp\!\!\!\perp U\mid X\). Suppose also that for every \(x\in\mathcal X\) and \(s\in[0,1]\), \(\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[|Y(t)-Y(s)|\mid X=x]\to0\) as \(t\to s\). Define \(m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[g_x(a,U)\mid X=x]\). Then, for every Borel \(B\subseteq[0,1]\), \(\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y\mathbf 1\{A\in B\}\mid X=x]=\int_Bm_x^{\mathrm F}(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\), and \(m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mu_x^P(a)\) for every \(a\in[0,1]\). Consequently, for every \(0\leq\delta\leq\bar\delta\), \(d_\delta(A)\) belongs to the support of the conditional natural-treatment law almost surely, \(Y\{d_\delta(A)\}=Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}+Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}\) almost surely, and \(\psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F})=\mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}]+\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}p_xq_x(\delta)\mu_x^P(\delta)=\theta_\delta(P)\).
