Specialize the declared parameter to \(\beta=1\), so that \(\ell=0\), and take \(\delta_n\equiv0\).  For the realized design \(Z=\{(X_i,A_i):i\in I_2\}\), define
\[
Q_{n,x}(z)=\operatorname{realizedExactModulus}_{z,x}(t_\alpha),
\qquad
\rho_n(z)=\max_{x\in\mathcal X}Q_{n,x}(z).
\]
For every \(P\in\mathcal M\) and \(x\in\mathcal X\), the maps \(Q_{n,x}\) and \(\rho_n\) are Borel measurable, hence almost everywhere strongly measurable and integrable under the \(n\)-fold observed-design law, with
\[
0\leq Q_{n,x}(z)\leq Lh_n+t_\alpha,
\qquad
0\leq\rho_n(z)\leq Lh_n+t_\alpha.
\]
Consequently every literal real integral \(\operatorname{exactModulusHandle}\) in this specialization is well defined, and the set of integrated handles whose real supremum defines \(\operatorname{worstExactModulus}_n(t_\alpha)\) is nonempty and bounded above.  There are \(P_0\in\mathcal M\), \(x_0\in\mathcal X\), and constants \(c_0>0\) and \(C<\infty\), depending only on the declared constants, such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
h_n=n^{-1/(\kappa+3)},
\qquad
\mathbb E_{P_0}\rho_n(Z)\geq
\mathbb E_{P_0}Q_{n,x_0}(Z)\geq c_0h_n,
\qquad
\operatorname{worstExactModulus}_n(t_\alpha)\geq c_0h_n.
\]
Since \(r_n=n^{-1/2}\) in this specialization,
\[
\frac{2\operatorname{worstExactModulus}_n(t_\alpha)}{r_n}
\geq2c_0n^{(\kappa+1)/(2(\kappa+3))}\longrightarrow\infty,
\qquad
\frac{L_n^\star}{r_n}\leq C.
\]
The worst-case expected full length of an untruncated symmetric interval with realized radius \(\rho_n\) has the same divergent lower-order comparison.  Thus the literal zero-totalized real-integral candidate cannot furnish a single sharp asymptotic minimax-constant theorem uniformly over the full declared \(\beta\)-range and all threshold regimes.  This result does not decide marginal honesty, sharpness for any separately fixed \(\beta>1\), or the general exact minimax constant.
