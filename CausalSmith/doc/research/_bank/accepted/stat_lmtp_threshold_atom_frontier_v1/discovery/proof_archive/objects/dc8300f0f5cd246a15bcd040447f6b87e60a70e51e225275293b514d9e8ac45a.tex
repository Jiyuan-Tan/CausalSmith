\(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) is the class of full-data laws \(P^{\mathrm F}\) for \((X,A,Y,U,\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\})\) whose \((X,A,Y)\)-margin is some \(P\in\mathcal M\) and which satisfy the simultaneous latent-response factorization, latent randomization, and conditional \(L^1\)-response-continuity member conditions.
