Fix \(\delta\in[0,\bar\delta]\).  Definition \texttt{def:clamp-policy} says that \(d_\delta(A)=A\) on \(\{A>\delta\}\) and \(d_\delta(A)=\delta\) on \(\{A\leq\delta\}\).  Assumption \texttt{ass:consistency} therefore yields, almost surely,
\[
 Y\{d_\delta(A)\}
 =Y(A)\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}+Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}
 =Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}+Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}.
\]
All variables here are bounded and jointly measurable by Assumption \texttt{ass:consistency}, so the following conditional expectations exist.  Assumption \texttt{ass:exchangeability}, applied at the fixed treatment \(\delta\), gives for every stratum \(x\)
\[
 \mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}\mid X=x]
 =\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y(\delta)\mid X=x]
   P(A\leq\delta\mid X=x).
\]
The first factor is \(\mu_x^P(\delta)\) by Assumption \texttt{ass:causal-mean-version}; the second is \(q_x(\delta)\) by Proposition \texttt{prop:pushforward-setup}.  Taking expectations in the preceding almost-sure identity, using that the observed margin of \(P^{\mathrm F}\) is \(P\), and summing over the finite stratum space gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F})
 &=\mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}]
   +\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}p_xq_x(\delta)\mu_x^P(\delta)\\
 &=\theta_\delta(P),
 \end{aligned}
\]
where the last equality is the definition of \(\theta_\delta\).  Proposition \texttt{prop:policy-support} verifies that every realized modified treatment is in the conditional natural-treatment support; the displayed calculation additionally uses the explicitly imposed all-treatment mean version, so it does not infer a point value from an almost-everywhere regression version.
