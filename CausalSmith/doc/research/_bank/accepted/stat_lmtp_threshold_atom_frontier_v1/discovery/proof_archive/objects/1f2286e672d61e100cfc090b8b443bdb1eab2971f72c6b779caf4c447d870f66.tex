Let \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\), and let \(P\in\mathcal M\) be its observed \((X,A,Y)\)-margin.  Definition \(\texttt{def:full-data-model-class}\) supplies, for this member, a standard Borel carrier \(\mathcal U\), an \(\mathcal U\)-valued variable \(U\), jointly Borel maps \(g_x\), and the jointly measurable process \(Y(a)\); it also imposes Assumptions \(\texttt{ass:consistency}\), \(\texttt{ass:exchangeability}\), and \(\texttt{ass:full-data-response-continuity}\).  Fix \(x\in\mathcal X\).  By Assumption \(\texttt{ass:stratum-mass}\),
\[
 P^{\mathrm F}(X=x)=P(X=x)=p_x\geq p_{\min}>0,
 \tag{1}
\]
so every conditional distribution below is an ordinary probability distribution on the positive-probability event \(\{X=x\}\).

Assumption \(\texttt{ass:consistency}\) gives a single \(P^{\mathrm F}\)-probability-one event on which, simultaneously for all \(a\in[0,1]\),
\[
 Y(a)=g_X(a,U),
 \qquad
 Y=g_X(A,U).
 \tag{2}
\]
Assumption \(\texttt{ass:exchangeability}\) gives \(A\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}U\mid X\).  Hence, on the fixed stratum from (1),
\[
 P^{\mathrm F}_{(A,U)\mid X=x}
 =P^{\mathrm F}_{A\mid X=x}\otimes P^{\mathrm F}_{U\mid X=x}.
 \tag{3}
\]
Define
\[
 m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)
 =\int_{\mathcal U}g_x(a,u)\,P^{\mathrm F}(\mathrm du\mid X=x).
 \tag{4}
\]
The integrand in (4) is jointly Borel and takes values in \([0,1]\), by Definition \(\texttt{def:full-data-model-class}\); therefore its parameter integral is Borel and all ensuing nonnegative product integrals are finite.  For every Borel \(B\subseteq[0,1]\), equations (2)--(4), followed by Assumption \(\texttt{ass:conditional-density-law}\), give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y\mathbf 1\{A\in B\}\mid X=x]
 &=\int_{B\times\mathcal U}g_x(a,u)\,
   \{P^{\mathrm F}_{A\mid X=x}\otimes P^{\mathrm F}_{U\mid X=x}\}
   (\mathrm da,\mathrm du)\\
 &=\int_Bm_x^{\mathrm F}(a)\,P^{\mathrm F}(\mathrm da\mid X=x)\\
 &=\int_Bm_x^{\mathrm F}(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]
This proves the claimed conditional integral identity.

We next derive the pointwise equality needed by the clamp; it is not a member condition.  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:holder-regression}\) says that the declared \(\mu_x^P\) is continuous on \([0,1]\) and is an almost-everywhere version of \(\mathbb E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=x]\).  Thus Assumption \(\texttt{ass:conditional-density-law}\) implies
\[
 \mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A\in B\}\mid X=x]
 =\int_B\mu_x^P(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da
 \tag{6}
\]
for every Borel \(B\subseteq[0,1]\).  The observed-margin property in Definition \(\texttt{def:full-data-model-class}\), together with (5)--(6), yields
\[
 \int_B\{m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)-\mu_x^P(a)\}\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da=0
 \quad\text{for every Borel }B\subseteq[0,1].
 \tag{7}
\]
Put \(f_x=m_x^{\mathrm F}-\mu_x^P\).  For each integer \(j\geq1\), equation (7) applied to the Borel sets \(B_j^+=\{a:f_x(a)\geq1/j\}\) and \(B_j^-=\{a:f_x(a)\leq-1/j\}\) gives
\[
 0=\int_{B_j^+}f_x(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da
 \geq \frac1j\int_{B_j^+}\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da,
 \qquad
 0=\int_{B_j^-}f_x(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da
 \leq-\frac1j\int_{B_j^-}\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da.
 \tag{8}
\]
Consequently \(f_x=0\) for \(\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\)-almost every \(a\).  By Assumption \(\texttt{ass:polynomial-thinning}\), \(c_->0\), and \(\kappa\geq0\),
\[
 \pi_x(a)\geq c_-a^\kappa>0
 \quad\text{for Lebesgue-almost every }a\in(0,1].
 \tag{9}
\]
It follows that \(f_x=0\) Lebesgue-almost everywhere on \((0,\bar\delta]\).  Assumption \(\texttt{ass:full-data-response-continuity}\) makes \(m_x^{\mathrm F}\) continuous on \([0,\bar\delta]\), while Assumption \(\texttt{ass:holder-regression}\) makes \(\mu_x^P\) continuous there.  If \(f_x(a_0)\neq0\) for some \(a_0\in[0,\bar\delta]\), continuity would give a relative neighborhood of \(a_0\) on which \(|f_x|\) is bounded away from zero.  Because \(\bar\delta>0\), that neighborhood has an intersection with \((0,\bar\delta]\) of positive Lebesgue measure, contradicting the preceding almost-everywhere equality.  Therefore
\[
 m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mu_x^P(a)
 \quad\text{for every }a\in[0,\bar\delta].
 \tag{10}
\]
In particular, equality at the boundary \(a=0\) is obtained by continuity and the positive-density result on every punctured right neighborhood; it is not imposed by a choice of conditional-expectation version.

Fix \(\delta\in[0,\bar\delta]\).  Definition \(\texttt{def:clamp-policy}\) and the simultaneous identities (2) imply, on the same probability-one event,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 Y\{d_\delta(A)\}
 &=g_X\{\max(A,\delta),U\}\\
 &=g_X(A,U)\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}
   +g_X(\delta,U)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}\\
 &=Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}+Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]
This identity also covers \(A=\delta\), because (2) then gives \(Y=Y(\delta)\).  Equations (2)--(4), conditional independence in (3), (10), and Proposition \(\texttt{prop:pushforward-setup}\) give
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}\mid X=x]
 &=m_x^{\mathrm F}(\delta)P^{\mathrm F}(A\leq\delta\mid X=x)\\
 &=\mu_x^P(\delta)\int_0^\delta\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\\
 &=\mu_x^P(\delta)q_x(\delta).
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
Taking expectations in (11), applying (12), using the equality of observed margins for the retained-natural-course term, and summing over the finite stratum space \(\mathcal X\), we obtain
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F})
 &=\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y\{d_\delta(A)\}]\\
 &=\mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}]
   +\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}p_xq_x(\delta)\mu_x^P(\delta)\\
 &=\theta_\delta(P).
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
The first and last equalities in (13) are the definitions of \(\psi_\delta\) and \(\theta_\delta\).  Proposition \(\texttt{prop:policy-support}\) supplies the support assertion for every \(\delta\in[0,\bar\delta]\).  At \(\delta=0\), Assumption \(\texttt{ass:conditional-density-law}\) gives \(q_x(0)=0\) and the clamp is the identity; at \(\delta=\bar\delta<1\), (10) still applies.  Hence all conclusions hold uniformly over the full declared closed threshold range.
