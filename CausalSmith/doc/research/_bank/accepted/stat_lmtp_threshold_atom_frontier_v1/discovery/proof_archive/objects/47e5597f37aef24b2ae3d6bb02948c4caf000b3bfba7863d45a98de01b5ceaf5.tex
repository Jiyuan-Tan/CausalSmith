Proposition \(\texttt{prop:observed-margin-surjectivity}\) proves for every finite \(n\) and every declared deterministic threshold that
\[
 R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=R_n^\star,
 \qquad L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=L_n^\star. \tag{20}
\]
If \(\Gamma(P^{\mathrm F})\) denotes the observed margin, its surjectivity, the target equality in \(\texttt{prop:causal-bridge}\), and equality of the observed-sample laws give, for every observed-sample estimator \(T\),
\[
 \sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}
 |T-\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})|
 =
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}|T-\theta_{\delta_n}(P)|. \tag{21}
\]
Apply (21) to \(T=\widehat\theta_n\).  Theorem \(\texttt{thm:minimax-risk}\), followed by (20), yields constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on the declared model constants, such that for every deterministic threshold sequence and all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 c r_n
 \leq R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star
 \leq
 \sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}
 |\widehat\theta_n-\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})|
 \leq C r_n. \tag{22}
\]
For intervals, (17)--(18) in the proof of \(\texttt{prop:observed-margin-surjectivity}\), with \(C_n=\mathrm{CI}_n\), and \(\texttt{thm:honest-length}\) give
\[
 \inf_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 (P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}
 \{\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})\in\mathrm{CI}_n\}
 =
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M}P^{\otimes n}
 \{\theta_{\delta_n}(P)\in\mathrm{CI}_n\}
 \geq1-\alpha \tag{23}
\]
and
\[
 \sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}[\operatorname{len}(\mathrm{CI}_n)]
 =
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}[\operatorname{len}(\mathrm{CI}_n)]
 \leq C r_n. \tag{24}
\]
Every observed-sample interval honest over \(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) is honest over \(\mathcal M\), and conversely, by the exact coverage identity and surjectivity.  Therefore the lower bound in \(\texttt{thm:honest-length}\) and (20) imply
\[
 L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=L_n^\star\geq c r_n. \tag{25}
\]
Equations (20), (22), and (25) prove \(R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star\asymp L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star\asymp r_n\), while (22)--(24) prove attainment by the displayed total-Gram estimator and bias-aware interval.  The identities in (20) hold for every \(n\); only comparison with \(r_n\) invokes the sufficiently-large-\(n\) conclusions of the two established observed-data theorems.
