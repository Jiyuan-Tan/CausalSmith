\(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) is the collection of full-data law packages \(P^{\mathrm F}\) with the following memberwise structure.  Each member carries its own standard Borel latent-response space \(\mathcal U\), an \(\mathcal U\)-valued random variable \(U\), jointly Borel maps \(g_x:[0,1]\times\mathcal U\to[0,1]\) for \(x\in\mathcal X\), and a jointly measurable process \(\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\}\), together with a probability law for \((X,A,Y,U,\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\})\).  Its \((X,A,Y)\)-margin is some \(P\in\mathcal M\), and, within that member's own carrier, the simultaneous latent-response consistency, latent randomization, and conditional \(L^1\)-response-continuity member conditions hold.  Different members may use different standard Borel carriers; no common carrier and no carrier-richness condition is imposed.
