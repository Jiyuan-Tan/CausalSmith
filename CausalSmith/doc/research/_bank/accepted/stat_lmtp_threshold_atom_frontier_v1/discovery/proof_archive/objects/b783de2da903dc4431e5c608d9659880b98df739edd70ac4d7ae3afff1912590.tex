The headline result is the matched minimax-risk and uniformly honest expected-length frontier \(n^{-1/2}+\delta_n^{\kappa+1}h_n^\beta\) for the exact unsmoothed causal clamp mean \(\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})\), with \(n h_n^{2\beta+1}(\delta_n+h_n)^\kappa\asymp1\). Each member of the full-data class packages its own standard-Borel latent carrier and response map; conditional \(L^1\)-response continuity then proves, rather than assumes, the pointwise bridge \(\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})=\theta_{\delta_n}(P)\). The accepted observed-data total-Gram estimator, atom-specific fallback interval, and global and localized lower bounds therefore lift unchanged. The causal converse uses explicit member packages with carrier \([0,1]\), a uniform latent variable, and Bernoulli threshold response maps; it does not assert the frontier for every externally fixed arbitrary carrier.
