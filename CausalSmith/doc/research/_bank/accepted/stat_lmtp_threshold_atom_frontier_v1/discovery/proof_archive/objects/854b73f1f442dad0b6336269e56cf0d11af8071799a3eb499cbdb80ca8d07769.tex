Let \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\), let \(P\in\mathcal M\) be its observed \((X,A,Y)\)-margin, and interpret \(\mathcal U\), \(U\), \(g_x\), and \(\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\}\) as the carrier components packaged by this member.  Define \(m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[g_x(a,U)\mid X=x]\).  Then, for every \(x\in\mathcal X\) and Borel \(B\subseteq[0,1]\), \(\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y\mathbf 1\{A\in B\}\mid X=x]=\int_Bm_x^{\mathrm F}(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\), and \(m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mu_x^P(a)\) for every \(a\in[0,1]\).  Consequently, for every \(0\leq\delta\leq\bar\delta\), \(d_\delta(A)\) belongs to the support of the conditional natural-treatment law almost surely, \(Y\{d_\delta(A)\}=Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}+Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}\) almost surely, and \(\psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F})=\mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}]+\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}p_xq_x(\delta)\mu_x^P(\delta)=\theta_\delta(P)\).
