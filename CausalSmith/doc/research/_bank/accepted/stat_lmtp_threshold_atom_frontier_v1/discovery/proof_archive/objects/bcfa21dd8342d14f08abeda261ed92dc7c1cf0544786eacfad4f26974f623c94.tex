The established theorems \(\texttt{thm:minimax-risk}\) and \(\texttt{thm:honest-length}\) give constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) such that \(c r_n\leq L_n^\star\leq C r_n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), uniformly over every declared deterministic threshold sequence, and \(\texttt{thm:phase-diagram}\) identifies the four rate regimes. Thus rate-optimal uniformly honest inference is proved; only the literal candidate's higher-order integrability, honesty under zero totalization, and exact asymptotic minimax constant remain open.
