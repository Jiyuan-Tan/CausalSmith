Fix \(P\in\mathcal M\), a split block \(I_2\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), \(\ell\), \(\beta\), \(\kappa\), \(L\), \(c_-\), \(c_+\), \(p_{\min}\), \(\delta_n\), \(h_n>0\), and a test multiplier \(t\geq0\).  For a realized design \(z=\{(X_i,A_i):i\in I_2\}\), put \(u_i=(A_i-\delta_n)/h_n\), \(S_{z,x}=\{i\in I_2:X_i=x,\ 0\leq u_i\leq1\}\), and
\[
\mathcal W_{z,x}=\left\{w=(w_i)_{i\in I_2}\in\mathbb R^{I_2}: w_i=0\ \text{for }i\notin S_{z,x},\quad \sum_{i\in I_2}w_i u_i^j=\mathbf 1\{j=0\}\ \text{for }j=0,\ldots,\ell\right\}.
\]
If \(V_{z,x}\) is the matrix whose row indexed by \(i\in S_{z,x}\) is \(v_\ell(u_i)^\top\), these are equivalently the affine realized-design constraints \(V_{z,x}^\top w_{S_{z,x}}=e_0\), and the associated total Gram is \(G_x=V_{z,x}^\top V_{z,x}\).  Let \(\mathcal H_{\beta,L}\) be the class of functions \(f:[0,1]\to[0,1]\) that are continuous and satisfy
\[
\left|f(a')-\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}f^{(j)}(a)(a'-a)^j/j!\right|\leq L|a'-a|^\beta
\quad\text{for every }a,a'\in[0,1],
\]
where the derivatives are the intrinsic derivatives on \([0,1]\) used in the declared Hölder condition.  For \(w\in\mathcal W_{z,x}\), define the exact realized Hölder bias and conditional radius objective by
\[
\operatorname{exactHolderBias}_{z,x}(w)
=\sup_{f\in\mathcal H_{\beta,L}}
\left|\sum_{i\in I_2}w_i f(A_i)-f(\delta_n)\right|,
\qquad
\mathcal Q_{z,x,t}(w)
=\operatorname{exactHolderBias}_{z,x}(w)+t\left(\sum_{i\in I_2}w_i^2\right)^{1/2}.
\]
The realized exact modulus is the real-valued totalization
\[
\operatorname{realizedExactModulus}_{z,x}(t)=
\begin{cases}
\inf_{w\in\mathcal W_{z,x}}\mathcal Q_{z,x,t}(w),&\mathcal W_{z,x}\neq\varnothing,\\
0,&\mathcal W_{z,x}=\varnothing.
\end{cases}
\]
Thus the empty affine-feasible set has value zero, matching the real \(\operatorname{sInf}\) convention in the verified construction.  Define
\[
\operatorname{exactModulusHandle}(P,I_2,x,\ell,\beta,\kappa,L,c_-,c_+,p_{\min},\delta_n,h_n,t)
=\int \operatorname{realizedExactModulus}_{z,x}(t)\,
\mathrm d(P_{X,A}^{\otimes n})(z),
\]
the real-valued integral under the \(n\)-fold independent observed-design law induced by \(P\).  The inputs \(\kappa,c_-,c_+,p_{\min}\) enter by requiring that \(P\) satisfy the corresponding member conditions of \(\mathcal M\); they do not impose any additional smoothness on \(\pi_x\).  Finally set
\[
t_\alpha=\sqrt{\log(12J/\alpha)/2},\qquad
\operatorname{worstExactModulus}_n(t_\alpha)
=\sup_{P\in\mathcal M}\sup_{x\in\mathcal X}
\operatorname{exactModulusHandle}(P,I_2,x,\ell,\beta,\kappa,L,c_-,c_+,p_{\min},\delta_n,h_n,t_\alpha).
\]
Both \(\operatorname{realizedExactModulus}_{z,x}(t)\) and \(\operatorname{exactModulusHandle}\) are radius, or half-length, quantities.  An untruncated symmetric interval with such radius has full length twice the radius.  The zero value on \(\mathcal W_{z,x}=\varnothing\) only totalizes this candidate handle on singular designs; no validity, nondegeneracy, honesty, or optimality of an interval using that convention is asserted here.
