The proved order theory gives \(0<c\leq L_n^\star/r_n\leq C<\infty\) uniformly over all deterministic threshold sequences.  Conditional on a realized design, the local problem is finite-dimensional and its bias and stochastic radii are explicitly controlled by the total Gram.  What remains unresolved is the exact asymptotic value of the combined regular-plus-local confidence-length modulus.  Under only the measurable two-sided envelope, the rescaled local densities need not converge along a threshold sequence, so neither the normalized Gram nor a least-favorable local experiment has yet been shown to possess a unique uniform limit.
