Proposition \(\texttt{prop:observed-margin-surjectivity}\) proves, for every finite \(n\) and every declared deterministic threshold,
\[
 R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=R_n^\star,
 \qquad
 L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=L_n^\star.
 \tag{20}
\]
Let \(\Gamma(P^{\mathrm F})\) denote the observed margin.  The surjectivity assertion of \(\texttt{prop:observed-margin-surjectivity}\), the target equality in \(\texttt{prop:causal-bridge}\), and equality of the observed-sample law under \(P^{\mathrm F}\) and under \(\Gamma(P^{\mathrm F})\) imply, for every observed-sample estimator \(T\),
\[
 \sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}
 |T-\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})|
 =
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}|T-\theta_{\delta_n}(P)|.
 \tag{21}
\]
Apply (21) to \(T=\widehat\theta_n\).  Theorem \(\texttt{thm:minimax-risk}\), followed by (20), gives constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on the declared model constants, such that for every deterministic threshold sequence and all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
 c r_n
 \leq R_n^\star=R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star
 \leq
 \sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}
 |\widehat\theta_n-\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})|
 \leq C r_n.
 \tag{22}
\]

For intervals, the same proposition proves, for every observed-sample interval \(C_n\), exact equality of its worst-case coverage probability and worst-case expected length in the causal and observed experiments.  Taking \(C_n=\mathrm{CI}_n\) and invoking \(\texttt{thm:honest-length}\) therefore yields
\[
 \inf_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 (P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}
 \{\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})\in\mathrm{CI}_n\}
 =
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M}P^{\otimes n}
 \{\theta_{\delta_n}(P)\in\mathrm{CI}_n\}
 \geq1-\alpha
 \tag{23}
\]
and
\[
 \sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}
 \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}[\operatorname{len}(\mathrm{CI}_n)]
 =
 \sup_{P\in\mathcal M}
 \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}[\operatorname{len}(\mathrm{CI}_n)]
 \leq C r_n.
 \tag{24}
\]
Every interval uniformly \((1-\alpha)\)-honest over \(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) is, by the exact coverage identity, uniformly honest over \(\mathcal M\), and conversely.  The lower bound in \(\texttt{thm:honest-length}\) and (20) consequently give
\[
 L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=L_n^\star\geq c r_n.
 \tag{25}
\]
Equations (20), (22), and (25) prove
\[
 R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=R_n^\star\asymp r_n,
 \qquad
 L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=L_n^\star\asymp r_n.
\]
Equations (22)--(24) show that the displayed total-Gram estimator and bias-aware interval attain the causal upper bounds.  The exact equalities hold for every \(n\); only the common order comparison with \(r_n\) uses the sufficiently-large-\(n\) conclusions of the two established observed-data theorems.
