The established theorems \(\texttt{thm:minimax-risk}\) and \(\texttt{thm:honest-length}\) give matching minimax risk and uniformly honest expected-length order \(r_n\), and \(\texttt{thm:phase-diagram}\) gives the regular, critical, atom-dominated, fixed-threshold, and zero-threshold regimes. They do not assert a general exact asymptotic constant.
