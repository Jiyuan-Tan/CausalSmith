Fix the declared regime constants.  Does there exist a constant \(c>0\) such that, for every admissible deterministic threshold sequence \(\delta_{\bullet}=(\delta_n)_{n\geq1}\) for which \(\delta_n/\delta_{\mathrm{crit},n}\to\lambda\) for some \(\lambda\in[0,\infty]\), there exists a deterministic sequence \(B_{\bullet}=(B_n)_{n\geq1}\) of admissible three-way split blocks, allowed to depend on the entire threshold sequence but not on the realized data, with the following properties?  For a realized observed sample \(z\), let \(\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{TG}}_{n,B_n}(z)\) be exactly the total-Gram estimator \(\widehat\theta_n\) of \(\texttt{def:total-gram-estimator}\) formed with the three blocks in \(B_n\), and interpret the design argument of \(\operatorname{realizedExactModulus}_{z,x}\) as the \((X,A)\)-projection of \(z\), using the third block in \(B_n\).  Put
\[
 R_{n,B_n}(z)=\sup_{x\in\mathcal X}\operatorname{realizedExactModulus}_{z,x}(t_\alpha),
 \qquad
 t_\alpha=\sqrt{\log(12J/\alpha)/2},
\]
and define the explicit candidate interval
\[
 C^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}(z)=
 \left[
 \max\left\{0,\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{TG}}_{n,B_n}(z)-R_{n,B_n}(z)\right\},
 \min\left\{1,\widehat\theta^{\mathrm{TG}}_{n,B_n}(z)+R_{n,B_n}(z)\right\}
 \right].
\]
Is this interval eventually uniformly \((1-\alpha)\)-honest, in the sense that
\[
 \inf_{P\in\mathcal M}P^{\otimes n}
 \left\{\theta_{\delta_n}(P)\in C^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}\right\}
 \geq1-\alpha
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\), while retaining bounded \(Y\) and the measurable envelope \(c_-a^\kappa\leq\pi_x(a)\leq c_+a^\kappa\)?  If
\[
 \ell^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}
 =\sup_{P\in\mathcal M}\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}
 \left[\operatorname{len}\!\left(C^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}\right)\right],
\]
do both sharp-constant limits
\[
 \frac{L_n^\star}{r_n}\longrightarrow c,
 \qquad
 \frac{\ell^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}}{r_n}\longrightarrow c
\]
hold?  Finally, write \(W_{n,B_n}=\operatorname{worstExactModulus}_n(t_\alpha)\) for the worst integrated radius computed with the third block in \(B_n\).  Do there exist a fixed constant \(D>0\), a fixed \(\mathsf{amplitude}\) with \(0<\mathsf{amplitude}\leq1/4\), and sequences \(P_0,P_{\mathrm{reg}},P_{\mathrm{loc}}:\mathbb N\to\mathcal M\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\), the laws \(P_0(n)\), \(P_{\mathrm{reg}}(n)\), and \(P_{\mathrm{loc}}(n)\) satisfy the declared ClampModel member conditions, their \(n\)-sample experiments satisfy \(\texttt{ass:iid-sampling}\), the pair \((P_0(n),P_{\mathrm{reg}}(n))\) satisfies the declared global Bernoulli shift at scale \(n^{-1/2}\), the pair \((P_0(n),P_{\mathrm{loc}}(n))\) satisfies the declared localized Bernoulli perturbation at \(\beta\), \(\delta_n\), \(h_n=\operatorname{infoBandwidth}(n,\delta_n,\beta,\kappa,\bar\delta)\), and \(\mathsf{amplitude}\), and
\[
 \chi^2\!\left(P_{\mathrm{reg}}(n)^{\otimes n}\middle\Vert P_0(n)^{\otimes n}\right)\leq D,
 \qquad
 \chi^2\!\left(P_{\mathrm{loc}}(n)^{\otimes n}\middle\Vert P_0(n)^{\otimes n}\right)\leq D?
\]
For these witnesses define
\[
 \Delta_n^{\mathrm{mix}}
 =\left|\theta_{\delta_n}\!\left(P_{\mathrm{reg}}(n)\right)-
          \theta_{\delta_n}\!\left(P_0(n)\right)\right|
  +\left|\theta_{\delta_n}\!\left(P_{\mathrm{loc}}(n)\right)-
          \theta_{\delta_n}\!\left(P_0(n)\right)\right|.
\]
Do the three matching limits
\[
 \frac{\Delta_n^{\mathrm{mix}}}{2W_{n,B_n}}\longrightarrow1,
 \qquad
 \frac{\ell^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}}{2W_{n,B_n}}\longrightarrow1,
 \qquad
 \frac{2W_{n,B_n}}{L_n^\star}\longrightarrow1
\]
hold?  Here \(W_{n,B_n}\) is a radius, so \(2W_{n,B_n}\) is the candidate untruncated expected full length.  The convention \(\operatorname{realizedExactModulus}_{z,x}(t_\alpha)=0\) when the affine feasible set is empty remains in force.  This question does not assert that the zero-totalized candidate is honest, nondegenerate, or optimal.
