**A-1 (assumption).** The observations \(O_1,\ldots,O_n\) are independent and identically distributed from \(P\).

**A-2 (assumption).** For every \(x\in\mathcal X\) and Borel set \(B\subseteq[0,1]\), \(P(A\in B\mid X=x)=\int_B\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\).

**A-5 (assumption).** For every \(x\in\mathcal X\), \(p_x\geq p_{\min}\).

**A-6 (assumption).** For every \(x\in\mathcal X\) and Lebesgue-almost every \(a\in[0,1]\), \(c_-a^\kappa\leq\pi_x(a)\leq c_+a^\kappa\).

**A-7 (assumption).** For every \(x\in\mathcal X\), an almost-everywhere version of \(\mathbb E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=x]\) has a continuous extension \(\mu_x^P\) satisfying \(\left|\mu_x^P(t)-\sum_{j=0}^{\ell}(\mu_x^P)^{(j)}(s)(t-s)^j/j!\right|\leq L|t-s|^\beta\) for all \(s,t\in[0,1]\).

If \[ \ell^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n} =\sup_{P\in\mathcal M}\mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}} \left[\operatorname{len}\!\left(C^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}\right)\right], \] do both sharp-constant limits \[ \frac{L_n^\star}{r_n}\longrightarrow c, \qquad \frac{\ell^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}}{r_n}\longrightarrow c \] hold? Finally, write \(W_{n,B_n}=\operatorname{worstExactModulus}_n(t_\alpha)\) for the worst integrated radius computed with the third block in \(B_n\). Do there exist a fixed constant \(D>0\), a fixed \(\mathsf{amplitude}\) with \(0<\mathsf{amplitude}\leq1/4\), and sequences \(P_0,P_{\mathrm{reg}},P_{\mathrm{loc}}:\mathbb N\to\mathcal M\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(n\), the laws \(P_0(n)\), \(P_{\mathrm{reg}}(n)\), and \(P_{\mathrm{loc}}(n)\) satisfy the declared ClampModel member conditions, their \(n\)-sample experiments satisfy \(\texttt{ass:iid-sampling}\), the pair \((P_0(n),P_{\mathrm{reg}}(n))\) satisfies the declared global Bernoulli shift at scale \(n^{-1/2}\), the pair \((P_0(n),P_{\mathrm{loc}}(n))\) satisfies the declared localized Bernoulli perturbation at \(\beta\), \(\delta_n\), \(h_n=\operatorname{infoBandwidth}(n,\delta_n,\beta,\kappa,\bar\delta)\), and \(\mathsf{amplitude}\), and \[ \chi^2\!\left(P_{\mathrm{reg}}(n)^{\otimes n}\middle\Vert P_0(n)^{\otimes n}\right)\leq D, \qquad \chi^2\!\left(P_{\mathrm{loc}}(n)^{\otimes n}\middle\Vert P_0(n)^{\otimes n}\right)\leq D? \] For these witnesses define \[ \Delta_n^{\mathrm{mix}} =\left|\theta_{\delta_n}\!\left(P_{\mathrm{reg}}(n)\right)- \theta_{\delta_n}\!\left(P_0(n)\right)\right| +\left|\theta_{\delta_n}\!\left(P_{\mathrm{loc}}(n)\right)- \theta_{\delta_n}\!\left(P_0(n)\right)\right|. \] Do the three matching limits \[ \frac{\Delta_n^{\mathrm{mix}}}{2W_{n,B_n}}\longrightarrow1, \qquad \frac{\ell^{\mathrm{mod}}_{n,B_n}}{2W_{n,B_n}}\longrightarrow1, \qquad \frac{2W_{n,B_n}}{L_n^\star}\longrightarrow1 \] hold? Here \(W_{n,B_n}\) is a radius, so \(2W_{n,B_n}\) is the candidate untruncated expected full length. The convention \(\operatorname{realizedExactModulus}_{z,x}(t_\alpha)=0\) when the affine feasible set is empty remains in force. This question does not assert that the zero-totalized candidate is honest, nondegenerate, or optimal.

## Lemmas (L)

**L-1 (For every \(P\in\mathcal M\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and…).** For every \(P\in\mathcal M\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and \(0\leq\delta\leq\bar\delta\), the conditional pushforward law satisfies \(P\{d_\delta(A)\in\mathrm da\mid X=x\}=\pi_x(a)\mathbf 1\{a>\delta\}\,\mathrm da+q_x(\delta)\,\delta_\delta(\mathrm da)\), with \(c_-\delta^{\kappa+1}/(\kappa+1)\leq q_x(\delta)\leq c_+\delta^{\kappa+1}/(\kappa+1)\).

**L-2 (For every \(P\in\mathcal M\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and…).** For every \(P\in\mathcal M\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and \(0\leq\delta\leq\bar\delta\), \(d_\delta(A)\) lies in the support of the conditional natural-treatment law given \(X=x\) almost surely. At \(\delta=0\) this follows from the identity map; at \(\delta>0\) every neighborhood of \(\delta\) has positive conditional probability by conditional absolute continuity and the lower polynomial envelope.

**L-3 (Uniformly over deterministic…).** Uniformly over deterministic \(\delta_n\in[0,\bar\delta]\), the bandwidth satisfies \(n h_n^{2\beta+1}(\delta_n+h_n)^\kappa\asymp1\) and the effective local count satisfies \(n h_n(\delta_n+h_n)^\kappa\asymp h_n^{-2\beta}\). If \(\delta_n/\delta_{\mathrm{edge},n}\to\infty\), then \(h_n\asymp(n\delta_n^\kappa)^{-1/(2\beta+1)}\); if \(\delta_n/\delta_{\mathrm{edge},n}=O(1)\), then \(h_n\asymp n^{-1/(2\beta+\kappa+1)}\).

**L-4 (The constant \(\lambda_\star\) is strictly positive).** The constant \(\lambda_\star\) is strictly positive. There are constants \(C,c>0\), depending only on the declared model constants, such that uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal M\), \(x\in\mathcal X\), and deterministic \(\delta_n\), \(P(\Omega_x^c)\leq C\exp\{-c n h_n(\delta_n+h_n)^\kappa\}\). On \(\Omega_x\), the exact intercept weights reproduce polynomials through degree \(\ell\) and obey \(\sum_{i\in I_2}|w_{ix}|\leq C\) and \(\sum_{i\in I_2}w_{ix}^2\leq C/N_x\). No continuity or differentiability of \(\pi_x\) beyond two-sided polynomial comparability is required.

**L-5 (For every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) and…).** For every \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) and \(x\in\mathcal X\), there is exactly one continuous function on \([0,\bar\delta]\) that is an almost-everywhere version of \(\mathbb E_P[Y\mid A=a,X=x]\). Hence \(\mu_{x,\mathrm{cont}}^P\) is well defined.

## Theorems (T)

### T-block: t1 — Let \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\), let…
**Statement.** Let \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\), let \(P\in\mathcal M\) be its observed \((X,A,Y)\)-margin, and interpret \(\mathcal U\), \(U\), \(g_x\), and \(\{Y(a):a\in[0,1]\}\) as the carrier components packaged by this member. Define \(m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[g_x(a,U)\mid X=x]\). Then, for every \(x\in\mathcal X\) and Borel \(B\subseteq[0,1]\), \(\mathbb E_{P^{\mathrm F}}[Y\mathbf 1\{A\in B\}\mid X=x]=\int_Bm_x^{\mathrm F}(a)\pi_x(a)\,\mathrm da\), and \(m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mu_x^P(a)\) for every \(a\in[0,\bar\delta]\). Consequently, for every \(0\leq\delta\leq\bar\delta\), \(d_\delta(A)\) belongs to the support of the conditional natural-treatment law almost surely, \(Y\{d_\delta(A)\}=Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}+Y(\delta)\mathbf 1\{A\leq\delta\}\) almost surely, and \(\psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F})=\mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}]+\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}p_xq_x(\delta)\mu_x^P(\delta)=\theta_\delta(P)\).

### T-block: t2 — There exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on…
**Statement.** There exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on the declared model constants, such that for every deterministic threshold sequence and all sufficiently large \(n\), \(c r_n\leq R_n^\star\leq\sup_{P\in\mathcal M}\mathbb E_P|\widehat\theta_n-\theta_{\delta_n}(P)|\leq C r_n\). The lower bound is the maximum, and therefore the same order as the sum, of a same-class global Bernoulli shift yielding \(n^{-1/2}\) separation and a same-class localized Bernoulli regression perturbation of width \(h_n\) yielding \(\delta_n^{\kappa+1}h_n^\beta\) separation with bounded product divergence.

### T-block: t3 — For every \(n\) large enough,…
**Statement.** For every \(n\) large enough, \(\inf_{P\in\mathcal M}P\{\theta_{\delta_n}(P)\in\mathrm{CI}_n\}\geq1-\alpha\), and \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal M}\mathbb E_P[\operatorname{len}(\mathrm{CI}_n)]\leq C r_n\). Conversely, every confidence interval with coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) uniformly over \(\mathcal M\) satisfies \(L_n^\star\geq c r_n\). The interval adapts the bias-aware construction of Armstrong and Kolesár to the distinct polynomially thinned random-design clamp functional and remains honest on singular-Gram realizations through the atom fallback.

### T-block: t4 — The phase boundary satisfies…
**Statement.** The phase boundary satisfies \(\delta_{\mathrm{crit},n}/\delta_{\mathrm{edge},n}\to\infty\). If \(\delta_n/\delta_{\mathrm{crit},n}\to0\), then \(r_n\asymp n^{-1/2}\) (regular regime). If \(\delta_n/\delta_{\mathrm{crit},n}\to c\in(0,\infty)\), both summands of \(r_n\) have order \(n^{-1/2}\) (critical regime). If \(\delta_n/\delta_{\mathrm{crit},n}\to\infty\) and \(\delta_n\to0\), then \(r_n\asymp\delta_n^{\kappa+1}(n\delta_n^\kappa)^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}\) (atom-dominated regime). If \(\delta_n\to\delta_0\in(0,\bar\delta]\), then \(r_n\asymp n^{-\beta/(2\beta+1)}\) (fixed-threshold regime). At \(\delta_n=0\), \(q_x(0)=0\), \(\theta_0(P)=\mathbb E_P[Y]\), and the construction reduces to bounded-mean inference at rate \(n^{-1/2}\).

### T-block: t5 — In the one-stratum model with \(\beta=\kappa=1\) and…
**Statement.** In the one-stratum model with \(\beta=\kappa=1\) and \(\pi(a)=2a\), whenever \(\delta_n\gg n^{-1/4}\), \(h_n\asymp(n\delta_n)^{-1/3}\), the localized Bernoulli alternatives have product Kullback--Leibler divergence of order \(n\delta_n h_n^3\), and their target separation is of order \(\delta_n^2h_n\). The regular-to-atom transition is exactly \(\delta_n\asymp n^{-1/10}\).

### T-block: t6 — There exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on…
**Statement.** There exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on the declared model constants, such that for every deterministic threshold sequence and all sufficiently large \(n\), \[ c r_n\leq R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star \leq\sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}} \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}|\widehat\theta_n-\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})| \leq C r_n. \] Moreover, \[ \inf_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}}(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n} \{\psi_{\delta_n}(P^{\mathrm F})\in\mathrm{CI}_n\}\geq1-\alpha, \qquad \sup_{P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}} \mathbb E_{(P^{\mathrm F})^{\otimes n}}[\operatorname{len}(\mathrm{CI}_n)]\leq Cr_n, \] and every uniformly \((1-\alpha)\)-honest observed-sample interval over \(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) has worst-case expected length at least \(cr_n\). Thus \(R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star\asymp L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star\asymp r_n\), attained by the same total-Gram estimator and bias-aware interval as in the observed-data experiment.

### T-block: t7 — For every \(P\in\mathcal M\), there exists…
**Statement.** For every \(P\in\mathcal M\), there exists \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) whose observed \((X,A,Y)\)-margin is exactly \(P\). Thus the observed-margin map from \(\mathcal M^{\mathrm F}\) onto \(\mathcal M\) is surjective. Moreover, for every \(n\) and every declared deterministic \(\delta_n\), \[ R_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=R_n^\star \qquad\text{and}\qquad L_{n,\mathrm F}^\star=L_n^\star. \]

### T-block: t8 — Let…
**Statement.** Let \(P^{\mathrm F}\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\) have observed margin \(P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\). Then \(m_x^{\mathrm F}(a)=\mu_{x,\mathrm{cont}}^P(a)\) for every \(x\in\mathcal X\) and every \(a\in[0,\bar\delta]\), and, for every \(0\leq\delta\leq\bar\delta\), \[ \psi_\delta(P^{\mathrm F}) =\mathbb E_P[Y\mathbf 1\{A>\delta\}] +\sum_{x\in\mathcal X}p_xq_x(\delta)\mu_{x,\mathrm{cont}}^P(\delta) =\theta_{\delta,\mathrm{cont}}(P). \]

### T-block: t9 — The observed-margin map from…
**Statement.** The observed-margin map from \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\) onto \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\) is surjective. For every \(n\) and every declared deterministic \(\delta_n\), the observed-sample clamp-target criteria satisfy \[ R_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star=R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star, \qquad L_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star=L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star. \]

### T-block: t10 — There exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on…
**Statement.** There exist constants \(0<c<C<\infty\), depending only on the declared model constants and on \(\alpha\), such that, for every deterministic \(\delta_n\in[0,\bar\delta]\) and all sufficiently large \(n\), \[ c s_n\leq R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star \leq\sup_{P\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}} \mathbb E_{P^{\otimes n}}|\widehat\theta_{n,\mathrm{cont}}-\theta_{\delta_n,\mathrm{cont}}(P)| \leq Cs_n. \] The interval \(\mathrm{CI}_{n,\mathrm{cont}}\) has coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) uniformly over \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}\), has worst-case expected length at most \(Cs_n\), and every uniformly \((1-\alpha)\)-honest interval has worst-case expected length at least \(cs_n\). The same estimator and interval obey the corresponding causal statements over \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cont}}^{\mathrm F}\), and \[ R_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star=R_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star, \qquad L_{n,\mathrm{cont},\mathrm F}^\star=L_{n,\mathrm{cont}}^\star. \] Consequently the continuity-only elbow is \(\delta_n\asymp n^{-1/[2(\kappa+1)]}\); at \(\delta_n=0\) the rate is \(n^{-1/2}\), whereas if \(\delta_n\to\delta_0\in(0,\bar\delta]\), neither uniform risk consistency nor vanishing uniformly honest expected length is possible.
