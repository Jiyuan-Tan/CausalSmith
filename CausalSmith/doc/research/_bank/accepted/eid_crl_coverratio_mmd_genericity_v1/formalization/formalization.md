## Assumptions (A)

**A-1 (assumption).** For every \(i\in[n]\), the mechanism \(p_i\) and intervention density \(q_i\) are strictly positive normalized \(C^3\) densities on their closed cubes.

**A-2 (assumption).** The observational density \(p^0\) is faithful to the DAG \(G\).

**A-3 (assumption).** The same map \(f\) is a \(C^2\) diffeomorphism of \([0,1]^n\) onto \(\mathcal X\) in every environment.

**A-4 (assumption).** Besides the known observational law \(P^0\), the laws \(P^1,\ldots,P^n\) arise from distinct unknown targets and replace exactly one \(p_i\) by the corresponding parent-independent density \(q_i\).

**A-5 (assumption).** For every \(i\in[n]\), \(s_i\,\partial_{v_i}\log\{q_i(v_i)/p_i(v_i\mid v_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)})\}>0\) throughout the closed cube.

**A-6 (assumption).** Within each environment \(e\), the \(N_e\) observations are independent draws from \(P^e\), and the training and evaluation folds are independent.

**A-7 (assumption).** Every \(p_i\), \(q_i\), and latent-coordinate log-ratio \(L_i\circ f\) has Holder norm of order \(\beta\) at most \(M\).

**A-8 (assumption).** Every \(p_i\) and \(q_i\) is bounded below by \(c\) on its closed cube.

**A-9 (assumption).** For every \(i\in[n]\), \(s_i\,\partial_{v_i}L_i(f(v))\ge c\) throughout the closed cube.

**A-10 (assumption).** The evaluation domain satisfies \(\operatorname{dist}(f^{-1}(K),\partial[0,1]^n)\ge\rho\).

**A-11 (assumption).** The shared mixing map obeys \(\max\{\|f\|_{C^2([0,1]^n)},\|f^{-1}\|_{C^2(\mathcal X)},\sup_{v\in[0,1]^n}|\det Df(v)|,\sup_{x\in\mathcal X}|\det Df^{-1}(x)|\}\le M\).

**A-12 (assumption).** The observed log-ratios obey \(\max_{i\in[n]}\|L_i\|_{C^2(K)}\le M\).

**A-13 (assumption).** For every \(i\in[n]\), the conditioning-coordinate density obeys \(c\le g_i(z)\le M\) for all \(z\in\mathcal Z_i(K)\).

**A-14 (assumption).** For every edge \(j\to i\) of \(G\), the observational law \(p^0\) does not satisfy \(V_i\mathbin{\perp\!\!\!\perp}V_j\mid V_{\operatorname{pa}_G(i)\setminus\{j\}}\).
