Fix \(x\in\mathcal X\) with \(p_x>0\).  Instrument overlap makes conditioning on \((Z=z,X=x)\) well defined for both \(z\).  Treatment consistency, selection exclusion-consistency, and outcome exclusion-consistency give
\[
P_{\mathrm{obs}}(Y=i,S=1,D=0\mid Z=z,X=x)
=P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,D_z=0\mid Z=z,X=x).
\]
Conditional instrument independence removes \(Z=z\) from the right side.  Hence, by the definition of \(\ell_i(x)\),
\[
\ell_i(x)=P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,D_0=0\mid X=x)-P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,D_1=0\mid X=x).
\]
No defiers gives \(D_1\ge D_0\), so \(\{D_1=0\}\subseteq\{D_0=0\}\), and the difference is
\[
P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,D_0=0,D_1=1\mid X=x)=P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,C\mid X=x).
\]
The symmetric calculation uses \(\{D_0=1\}\subseteq\{D_1=1\}\) and yields
\[
h_j(x)=P(Y_1=j,S_1=1,C\mid X=x).
\]
Summing over the finite support \(\mathcal Y\) proves
\[
q_0(x)=P(S_0=1,C\mid X=x),\qquad q_1(x)=P(S_1=1,C\mid X=x),
\]
and subtraction gives the asserted expression for \(\Delta q(x)\).

Under weak selection monotonicity, conditional on \((C,X=x)\), either \(S_1\ge S_0\) almost surely or \(S_1\le S_0\) almost surely.  In the first case,
\[
P(S_0=S_1=1,C\mid X=x)=P(S_0=1,C\mid X=x)=q_0(x)=m(x),
\]
and
\[
\Delta q(x)=P(S_0=0,S_1=1,C\mid X=x)\ge0.
\]
In the second case, the survivor mass is \(q_1(x)=m(x)\) and
\[
\Delta q(x)=-P(S_0=1,S_1=0,C\mid X=x)\le0.
\]
Thus a positive gap forces \(d(x)=+1\), a negative gap forces \(d(x)=-1\), and a zero gap does not identify the label.  Indeed, at zero gap either permitted monotonicity direction makes its one-sided selection stratum have probability zero.
