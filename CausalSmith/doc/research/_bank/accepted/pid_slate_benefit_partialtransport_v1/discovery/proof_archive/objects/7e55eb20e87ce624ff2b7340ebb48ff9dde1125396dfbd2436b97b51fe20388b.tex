Choose a baseline compatible law \(\bar P\in\mathcal M\), which exists by the premise.  In every cell with \(m(x)>0\), the sharp-threshold theorem and compactness of the finite polytope provide couplings \(\gamma_x^L,\gamma_x^U\in\Gamma_x\) satisfying
\[
b_x(\gamma_x^L)=B_L(x),\qquad b_x(\gamma_x^U)=B_U(x).
\]
For a zero-survivor cell take the zero matrix for both.  Apply the latent-completion part of the threshold-flow construction separately to the two collections.

Here are the mass checks that make that construction a full-law argument.  If \(\Delta q(x)>0\), then exact rows give total survivor mass \(q_0(x)\), and the residual selected-only masses
\[
r_j=h_j(x)-\sum_i\gamma_{ij,x}
\]
are nonnegative and sum to \(q_1(x)-q_0(x)\).  Allocate them to compliers with \((S_0,S_1)=(0,1)\); allocate the remaining complier probability \(P(C\mid X=x)-q_1(x)\ge0\) to \((0,0)\).  If \(\Delta q(x)<0\), exact columns give survivor mass \(q_1(x)\), the residuals \(r_i=\ell_i(x)-\sum_j\gamma_{ij,x}\) sum to \(q_0(x)-q_1(x)\), and they are allocated to \((S_0,S_1)=(1,0)\), with the remainder in \((0,0)\).  If the gap is zero, the tie-face proposition makes both margins exact, and all nonsurviving compliers receive \((0,0)\).  These allocations are nonnegative by the defining inequalities of \(\Gamma_x\) and by capacity identification.

For each of the lower and upper constructions, retain the baseline conditional probabilities of \(X\), of the three no-defier compliance types, and of every never-taker and always-taker latent component.  Replace only the conditional complier selection/outcome allocation by the preceding one.  Retain \(P(Z=1\mid X=x)=\pi(x)\) and draw \(Z\) conditionally independently of all potential variables.  Then set \(D=D_Z\), \(S=S_D\), and \(Y=Y_D\) when \(S=1\).  This proves conditional instrument independence and all three consistency/exclusion restrictions by construction; the retained propensity proves overlap; the retained compliance types prove no defiers; and the three displayed allocations prove weak selection monotonicity.  The selected complier outcome masses are exactly \(\ell\) and \(h\), while all noncomplier components are unchanged.  The observed decomposition under \(Z=0\) and \(Z=1\) therefore agrees cell by cell with the baseline law, so both completed laws induce exactly \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\).

Calling the two laws \(P^L\) and \(P^U\), respectively, their common target-stratum mass is \(M>0\), and
\[
P^L(Y_1>Y_0,S_0=S_1=1,C)=\sum_xp_xB_L(x),
\]
\[
P^U(Y_1>Y_0,S_0=S_1=1,C)=\sum_xp_xB_U(x).
\]
Division by \(M\), justified by positive aggregate survivor mass, gives \(\theta_L\) and \(\theta_U\).  Zero-survivor and observable-tie cells were handled explicitly above, so no boundary cell is omitted.
