We prove the affirmative, conservative conclusion.  First note that \(\sqrt n\eta_n\to\infty\) implies \(n\eta_n\to\infty\), so
\[
a_n=(n\eta_n)^{-1/4}\downarrow0,
\qquad
\sqrt{n\eta_n}\,a_n=(n\eta_n)^{1/4}\to\infty,
\qquad
\sqrt n\,a_n\to\infty.
\tag{1}
\]

We establish a uniform deterministic-stochastic bound for the plug-in endpoints.  Represent the observed law by its finite vector \(v(P)\) of joint cell probabilities and let
\[
\delta_n=\|\widehat v_n-v(P)\|_\infty.
\]
Because every coordinate indicator is bounded by one, Chebyshev's inequality and a union bound over the fixed finite support give, uniformly in \(P\in\mathcal P_n\),
\[
\delta_n=O_P(n^{-1/2}).
\tag{2}
\]
On cells with \(p_x\ge 4\delta_n/\varepsilon_Z\), overlap and elementary ratio algebra give
\[
\max_{i,j}\left{left|\widehat p_x\widetilde\ell_i(x)-p_x\ell_i(x)\right|,
\left|\widehat p_x\widetilde h_j(x)-p_xh_j(x)\right|\right}
\le \frac{16\delta_n}{\varepsilon_Z^2}.
\tag{3}
\]
Indeed, write each empirical conditional probability as an empirical joint cell divided by the empirical \((X,Z)\) cell, multiply by \(\widehat p_x\), and use
\(P(X=x,Z=z)\ge\varepsilon_Zp_x\); the stated lower bound on \(p_x\) makes the empirical denominator at least half its population value.  Cells failing this inequality have total probability at most \(4|\mathcal X|\delta_n/\varepsilon_Z\), so their aggregate contribution is bounded by the same order.

The positive-part projection is one-Lipschitz.  Every prefix, tail, mass minimum, and threshold cut is a maximum or minimum of linear forms with coefficients in \(\{-1,0,1\}\).  Thus, summing (3) over at most \(K\) coordinates and over the finite cell set, the unscreened aggregate numerator and denominator errors are bounded by
\[
\frac{64K|\mathcal X|}{\varepsilon_Z^2}\delta_n.
\tag{4}
\]
The direction margin fixes the estimated sign on every cell whose contribution is not absorbed by the right side of (4), with probability tending to one uniformly: a wrong sign requires a conditional-capacity error at least \(\kappa_\sigma/2\), while retained cells have \(p_x\ge\eta_n+o_P(1)\), and (1)--(3) make that probability vanish.

Screening changes any one numerator by no more than it changes survivor mass, because \(0\le B_L(x)\le B_U(x)\le m(x)\).  Except on an event whose probability tends to zero uniformly by (2), every cell classified differently from its population positive-support status has unconditional survivor mass at most \(2\eta_n+32K\delta_n/\varepsilon_Z^2\).  Hence, after summing cells, screening adds at most
\[
2|\mathcal X|\eta_n+\frac{32K|\mathcal X|}{\varepsilon_Z^2}\delta_n
\tag{5}
\]
to the aggregate error.  Combining (4)--(5), using \(M\ge m_\star\), and applying
\[
\left|\frac{N'}{M'}-\frac NM\right|
\le \frac{2}{m_\star}\{|N'-N|+|M'-M|\}
\quad\text{whenever }M'\ge m_\star/2,
\tag{6}
\]
gives
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_n}P\left(
\|\widehat\Psi_n-\Psi(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\|_\infty>
\frac{256K|\mathcal X|}{m_\star\varepsilon_Z^2}
\{\eta_n+delta_n\}
\right)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{7}
\]
The fallback case \(\widehat M_n=0\) lies in the exceptional event in (7), since (4)--(5) and \(M\ge m_\star\) imply \(\widehat M_n\ge m_\star/2\) with probability tending to one uniformly.

By (1)--(2), \(\delta_n/a_n\to_P0\) uniformly.  Therefore (7) and the definition of \(g_n\) imply
\[
\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_n}P\left(
\|\widehat\Psi_n-\Psi(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\|_\infty>g_n
\right)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{8}
\]
This bound is insensitive to how many threshold cuts tie: maxima and minima remained Lipschitz in (4).  It is also insensitive to local nonnegativity faces and to cells with vanishing survivor mass: these are precisely the perturbations bounded in (4) and (5).  The estimated face envelope is computable because it contains finitely many cuts and finitely many choices of the two coordinate rules.  Its conditional quantile is well defined, and \(c_{1-\alpha,n}^+\ge0\) by construction.  Thus the confidence set contains
\[
[\widehat\Psi_{n,L}-g_n,\widehat\Psi_{n,U}+g_n]\cap[0,1].
\]
On the event in (8), \(\theta_L\ge\widehat\Psi_{n,L}-g_n\) and \(\theta_U\le\widehat\Psi_{n,U}+g_n\).  The sharp-threshold theorem gives \(\Theta_I(P_{\mathrm{obs}})=[\theta_L,\theta_U]\), so this event implies full-set containment.  Consequently
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n}
P\{\Theta_I(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\subseteq\mathcal C_{1-\alpha,n}\}=1,
\]
which is stronger than the required lower bound \(1-\alpha\).  Since \(a_n+\eta_n\to0\), the guard vanishes.  The argument deliberately claims uniform validity, not first-order exactness of the unguarded directional multiplier; the latter would require additional uniform Gaussian and anti-concentration work not imposed by the requested coverage inequality.
