On the stated submodel, capacity identification gives, in every cell with positive complier mass,
\[
q_0(x)=q_1(x)=P(C\mid X=x)=m(x)>0.
\]
Therefore \(\Delta q(x)=0\), and exact mass makes both capacity margins bind, so \(\Gamma_x=\Gamma_x^{\ast}=\Gamma_x^0\).  Define
\[
F_0(t)=\frac{\ell_{\le t}(x)}{m(x)},\qquad F_1(t)=\frac{h_{\le t}(x)}{m(x)},\qquad F_0(-1)=0.
\]
Division is valid because this cell has \(m(x)>0\).  Since \(\min\{\Delta q(x),0\}=0\), the branch-free formulas become
\[
\frac{B_L(x)}{m(x)}=\max\left\{0,\max_t\{F_0(t)-F_1(t)\}\right\}
\]
and
\[
\frac{B_U(x)}{m(x)}=\min_t\{F_0(t-1)+1-F_1(t)\}.
\]
The mass cap is redundant because the threshold \(t=0\) is at most one after normalization.  The established fixed-marginal strict-benefit lemma, transcribing Lu--Ding--Dasgupta Proposition 2, equation (6), identifies these as the sharp lower and upper probabilities for the strict-benefit event with the two normalized ordinal marginals.  Hence the exact-mass construction reduces cell by cell to the published complete-marginal formula; no converse claim about a larger structural class is used.
