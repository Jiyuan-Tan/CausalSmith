Set \(a_n=(n\eta_n)^{-1/4}\).  In each retained cell, form the finite face envelope consisting of every lower and upper cut within \(a_n\) of its empirical optimum and, for each projected-capacity coordinate at most \(a_n\), both the boundary rule \(g\mapsto\max\{g,0\}\) and the interior rule \(g\mapsto g\); coordinates larger than \(a_n\) use the interior rule.  Propagate every such face choice, with the estimated sign branch, through the two numerators and the common-denominator quotient.  Let \(c_{1-\alpha,n}\) be the conditional \((1-\alpha)\)-quantile of the supremum, over this finite face envelope, of the outward multiplier score \(\max\{T_L,-T_U\}\) generated by \(\mathbb G_n^\xi\), and put \(c_{1-\alpha,n}^+=\max\{c_{1-\alpha,n},0\}\).  Define
\[
g_n=\frac{512K|\mathcal X|}{m_\star\varepsilon_Z^2}(a_n+\eta_n)
\]
and
\[
\mathcal C_{1-\alpha,n}=
\left[\max\left\{0,\widehat\Psi_{n,L}-\frac{c_{1-\alpha,n}^+}{\sqrt n}-g_n\right\},
\min\left\{1,\widehat\Psi_{n,U}+\frac{c_{1-\alpha,n}^+}{\sqrt n}+g_n\right\}\right].
\]
Then
\[
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n}
P\{\Theta_I(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\subseteq\mathcal C_{1-\alpha,n}\}\ge1-\alpha.
\]
This conclusion permits arbitrary simultaneous threshold-cut ties and arbitrary cells with survivor mass approaching zero.  The latter cells may be omitted at \(\eta_n\).  The deterministic term \(g_n\downarrow0\) is an explicit uniform localization-and-screening guard; consequently the stated result is uniformly valid and consistent but may be conservative, and it does not assert first-order exact uniform calibration after deleting that guard.
