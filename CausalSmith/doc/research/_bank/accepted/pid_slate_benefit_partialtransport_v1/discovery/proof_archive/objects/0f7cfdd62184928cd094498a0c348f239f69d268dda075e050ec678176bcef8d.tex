Let \(\mathcal E\) be the fixed finite collection of observed atomic and aggregate cell events needed for all empirical conditional probabilities, and define
\[
\delta_n=\max_{E\in\mathcal E}|\widehat P_n(E)-P_{\mathrm{obs}}(E)|.
\]
For each indicator, independence gives variance at most \(1/(4n)\).  Since \(\mathbf 1\{|W|>u\}\le W^2/u^2\), taking expectations and summing over \(\mathcal E\) yields, uniformly in \(P\),
\[
P(\delta_n>u)\le\frac{|\mathcal E|}{4nu^2}.
\tag{1}
\]
Now \(a_n=(n\eta_n)^{-1/4}\downarrow0\) and
\[
na_n^2=\sqrt{n/\eta_n}\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
so (1) implies \(\delta_n/a_n\to_P0\) uniformly.

We compare unconditional capacities.  Put \(L_i(x)=p_x\ell_i(x)\), \(H_j(x)=p_xh_j(x)\), \(\widehat L_i(x)=\widehat p_x\widehat\ell_i(x)\), and \(\widehat H_j(x)=\widehat p_x\widehat h_j(x)\).  On a cell satisfying \(p_x\ge4\delta_n/\varepsilon_Z\), overlap gives population denominators at least \(\varepsilon_Zp_x\), while their empirical counterparts are at least half that value.  Expanding each empirical ratio, using that every numerator is bounded by its denominator, and then taking the difference of the two instrument arms gives
\[
\max_{i,j}\{|\widehat L_i(x)-L_i(x)|,|\widehat H_j(x)-H_j(x)|\}
\le\frac{16\delta_n}{\varepsilon_Z^2}.
\tag{2}
\]
The coordinatewise positive-part projection does not enlarge this bound because \(p_x\ell_i(x)\) and \(p_xh_j(x)\) are nonnegative and \(\widehat p_x\max\{u,0\}=\max\{\widehat p_xu,0\}\).  If \(p_x<4\delta_n/\varepsilon_Z\), both population unconditional capacities are at most \(p_x\), both empirical unconditional capacities are at most \(\widehat p_x\le p_x+\delta_n\), and (2), with its larger constant, still holds.  Thus (2) is valid for every cell on the event defining \(\delta_n\).

The branch-free cut functionals are positively homogeneous.  If every unconditional capacity coordinate changes by at most \(e\), each prefix or tail changes by at most \(Ke\), \(\Delta q\) by at most \(2Ke\), and the lower and upper cut values by at most \(4Ke\).  Therefore the unscreened aggregate numerator and denominator errors are each at most
\[
A_{0,n}=\frac{64K|\mathcal X|}{\varepsilon_Z^2}\delta_n.
\tag{3}
\]
This Lipschitz calculation uses no sign classification and is valid at every simultaneous threshold tie and every zero-gap face.

Screening removes only cells with estimated unconditional survivor mass \(\widehat r_x\le\eta_n\).  Since \(0\le\widehat B_L(x)\le\widehat B_U(x)\le\widehat m(x)\), deleting all such cells changes each empirical numerator and the empirical denominator by at most \(|\mathcal X|\eta_n\).  Consequently, for \(e=L,U\),
\[
|\widehat N_{e,n}-N_e|\le A_n,
\qquad
|\widehat M_n-M|\le A_n,
\qquad
A_n=A_{0,n}+|\mathcal X|\eta_n.
\tag{4}
\]
This argument directly covers cells whose survivor mass approaches zero and does not require uniform recovery of their support.

Because \(M\ge m_{\star}\), on \(A_n\le m_{\star}/2\) one has \(\widehat M_n\ge m_{\star}/2\), so the fallback is not used.  Since \(0\le N_e\le M\), elementary quotient algebra and (4) give
\[
\left|\frac{\widehat N_{e,n}}{\widehat M_n}-\frac{N_e}{M}\right|
\le\frac{|\widehat N_{e,n}-N_e|}{\widehat M_n}
+\frac{N_e|\widehat M_n-M|}{M\widehat M_n}
\le\frac{4A_n}{m_{\star}}.
\tag{5}
\]
On the event \(\delta_n\le a_n\), the right side of (5) is bounded by
\[
\frac{256K|\mathcal X|}{m_{\star}\varepsilon_Z^2}a_n
+\frac{4|\mathcal X|}{m_{\star}}\eta_n
\le g_n.
\tag{6}
\]
Equation (1) makes this event have probability tending to one uniformly, and \(A_n\le m_{\star}/2\) there eventually because \(a_n+\eta_n\to0\).  Equations (5)--(6) prove the asserted uniform consistency.

On \(\|\widehat\Psi_n-\Psi(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\|_\infty\le g_n\), one has \(\theta_L\ge\widehat\Psi_{n,L}-g_n\) and \(\theta_U\le\widehat\Psi_{n,U}+g_n\).  The sharp-threshold theorem identifies \(\Theta_I(P_{\mathrm{obs}})=[\theta_L,\theta_U]\), so the guarded interval contains the entire identified set.  Uniform containment probability therefore tends to one.  No multiplier quantile is used in this proof; calibration of an unguarded face-aware multiplier region remains an open strengthening.
