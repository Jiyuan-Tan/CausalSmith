Choose a baseline compatible law \(\bar P\in\mathcal M\), whose existence is part of compatibility.  The sharp-threshold theorem and compactness give, for each cell, \(\gamma_x^L,\gamma_x^U\in\Gamma_x^{\ast}\) such that
\[
b_x(\gamma_x^L)=B_L(x),\qquad b_x(\gamma_x^U)=B_U(x).
\]
When \(m(x)=0\), both matrices are the zero matrix.  We apply the same completion separately to the lower and upper collections.

Fix one selected matrix \(\gamma_x\).  Its total mass is \(m(x)\).  If \(q_1(x)>q_0(x)\), the row-slack sum is \(q_0(x)-m(x)=0\), so every row binds.  The column residuals
\[
r_j=h_j(x)-\sum_i\gamma_{ij,x}
\]
are nonnegative and sum to \(q_1(x)-q_0(x)\).  Allocate them to compliers with \((S_0,S_1)=(0,1)\) and \(Y_1=j\), and allocate the remaining complier mass \(P(C\mid X=x)-q_1(x)\ge0\) to \((0,0)\).  If \(q_0(x)>q_1(x)\), every column binds; allocate the row residuals
\[
r_i=\ell_i(x)-\sum_j\gamma_{ij,x}
\]
to \((S_0,S_1)=(1,0)\) compliers with \(Y_0=i\), and put the remaining mass \(P(C\mid X=x)-q_0(x)\) in \((0,0)\).  At equality both margins bind and all nonsurviving compliers use \((0,0)\).  These checks also cover zero-survivor cells.  Assign arbitrary potential outcomes whenever the corresponding selection indicator is zero.

For each endpoint construction retain from \(\bar P\) the law of \(X\), the conditional probabilities of the three no-defier compliance types, and every never-taker and always-taker latent component.  Replace only the conditional complier selection/outcome allocation by the preceding one.  Retain \(P(Z=1\mid X=x)=\pi(x)\), draw \(Z\) conditionally independently of all potential variables, and set \(D=D_Z\), \(S=S_D\), and \(Y=Y_D\) on \(\{S=1\}\).  Conditional instrument independence, all consistency and exclusion restrictions, overlap, and no defiers then hold by construction.  The allocation uses only \((1,1),(0,1),(0,0)\) when \(q_1>q_0\), only \((1,1),(1,0),(0,0)\) when \(q_0>q_1\), and only \((1,1),(0,0)\) at equality, so weak selection monotonicity also holds.

The selected complier outcome masses under treatment zero and one are exactly \(\ell\) and \(h\); all noncomplier observed components are unchanged.  The resulting laws therefore induce exactly \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}\).  Call the lower and upper completions \(P^L\) and \(P^U\).  Their common target-stratum mass is \(M>0\), while
\[
P^L(Y_1>Y_0,S_0=S_1=1,C)=\sum_xp_xB_L(x),
\]
and
\[
P^U(Y_1>Y_0,S_0=S_1=1,C)=\sum_xp_xB_U(x).
\]
Division by \(M\) gives \(\theta_L\) and \(\theta_U\), respectively.  This is a full-law attainment argument, not merely attainment inside the projected transport polytope.
