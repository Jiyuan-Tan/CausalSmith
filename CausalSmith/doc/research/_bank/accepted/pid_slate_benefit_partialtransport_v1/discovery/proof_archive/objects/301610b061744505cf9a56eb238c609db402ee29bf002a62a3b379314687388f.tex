\noindent\textit{Proof.}
The displayed latent masses are nonnegative and sum to
\[
0.075+0.10+0.075+0.25+0.50=1.
\tag{1}
\]
Every unit has \((D_0,D_1)=(0,1)\), so every unit is a complier and there
are no defiers.  The only selection types are
\((S_0,S_1)=(1,1),(0,1),(0,0)\), so weak selection monotonicity holds in
the increasing direction.  The fair instrument is independent of all
potential variables.  Treatment and selection consistency and outcome
exclusion therefore give, conditional on \(Z=0\), selected outcome masses
\((0.075,0.10,0.075)\) at \(D=0\) and unselected mass \(0.75\).  Given
\(Z=1\), the selected masses at \(D=1\) are
\[
(0.075,0.10,0.075)+(0.025,0.15,0.075)
=(0.10,0.25,0.15),
\tag{2}
\]
with unselected mass \(0.50\).  Thus \(P^{\star}\in\mathcal M\) and it
induces exactly \(P_{\mathrm{obs}}^{\star}\).

Proposition \(\mathrm{prop{:}capacity\text{-}identification}\) gives
\[
\ell=(0.075,0.10,0.075),\qquad
h=(0.10,0.25,0.15),
\tag{3}
\]
so \(q_0=m=0.25\), \(q_1=0.50\), and \(\Delta q=0.25\).  The prefix
differences \(\ell_{\le t}-h_{\le t}\), for \(t=0,1,2\), are

\(\mathrm{def{:}threshold\text{-}cuts}\) gives \(B_L=0\).  The upper
threshold values \(\ell_{<t}+h_{>t}\) are
\[
0.40,\qquad 0.225,\qquad 0.175.
\tag{4}
\]
Taking their minimum together with the mass cap \(m=0.25\) gives
\(B_U=0.175\).  Theorem
\(\mathrm{thm{:}sharp\text{-}exact\text{-}mass\text{-}threshold\text{-}interval}\)
therefore yields
\[
\Theta_I(P_{\mathrm{obs}}^{\star})
=\left[\frac{0}{0.25},\frac{0.175}{0.25}\right]=[0,0.7].
\tag{5}
\]

For direct endpoint certificates, the displayed diagonal survivor
allocation has row margin \(\ell\), column usage

the lower endpoint after its column residuals are assigned to
