Let \(\mathcal E\) be the finite collection of observed events used to form the empirical cell probabilities, and let
\[
\delta_n=\max_{E\in\mathcal E}\left|\widehat P_n(E)-P_{\mathrm{obs}}(E)\right|.
\]
Set
\[
b_{n,\alpha}=\sqrt{\frac{|\mathcal E|}{4n\alpha}},\qquad
A_{n,\alpha}=\frac{64K|\mathcal X|}{\varepsilon_Z^2}b_{n,\alpha}+|\mathcal X|\eta_n,
\qquad
g_{n,\alpha}=\min\left\{1,\frac{4A_{n,\alpha}}{m_{\star}}\right\},
\]
and define
\[
\mathcal C_{1-\alpha,n}
=\left[\max\{0,\widehat\Psi_{n,L}-g_{n,\alpha}\},\
\min\{1,\widehat\Psi_{n,U}+g_{n,\alpha}\}\right].
\]
For every \(P\in\mathcal P_n\),
\[
P\{\delta_n\le b_{n,\alpha}\}\ge 1-\alpha.
\]
On this event, if \(g_{n,\alpha}<1\), then \(A_{n,\alpha}<m_{\star}/4\) and
\[
\|\widehat\Psi_n-\Psi(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\|_\infty\le g_{n,\alpha};
\]
if \(g_{n,\alpha}=1\), then \(\mathcal C_{1-\alpha,n}=[0,1]\).  Consequently, for every \(n\),
\[
\inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n}P\{\Theta_I(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\subseteq\mathcal C_{1-\alpha,n}\}\ge1-\alpha.
\]
For fixed \(\alpha\), \(g_{n,\alpha}=O(n^{-1/2}+\eta_n)\).  In particular, choosing \(\eta_n=n^{-1/2}L_n\), where \(L_n\to\infty\) and \(L_n=o(n^{1/2})\), respects \(\sqrt n\eta_n\to\infty\) and gives guard padding \(O(n^{-1/2}L_n)\).  The tolerance \(a_n=(n\eta_n)^{-1/4}\) is used only for the diagnostic near-active-face multiplier envelope.  No first-order exact calibration of an unguarded face-aware multiplier confidence set is asserted.
