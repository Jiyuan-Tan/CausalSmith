Suppress \(x\) and write \(q_0=\sum_i\ell_i\), \(q_1=\sum_jh_j\), and \(m=\min\{q_0,q_1\}\).  The exact-mass definition gives, for every \(\gamma\in\Gamma_x^{\ast}\),
\[
\sum_i\left(\ell_i-\sum_j\gamma_{ij}\right)=q_0-m,
\qquad
\sum_j\left(h_j-\sum_i\gamma_{ij}\right)=q_1-m.
\]
Every summand is nonnegative.  If \(q_0<q_1\), the first sum is zero, so every row constraint binds and \(\Gamma_x^{\ast}=\Gamma_x^+\).  If \(q_1<q_0\), every column constraint binds and \(\Gamma_x^{\ast}=\Gamma_x^-\).  If \(q_0=q_1=m\), both sums vanish, whence
\[
\Gamma_x^{\ast}=\Gamma_x^+=\Gamma_x^-=\Gamma_x^0.
\]
Conversely, if \(q_0\ne q_1\), a matrix cannot have both exact margins, because its total mass cannot equal both unequal totals.  The smaller-margin directional polytope is nonempty by a greedy complete-bipartite allocation, whereas \(\Gamma_x^0\) is empty.  Therefore \(\Gamma_x^+=\Gamma_x^-=\Gamma_x^0\) holds if and only if \(\Delta q(x)=0\), and on that face it also equals \(\Gamma_x=\Gamma_x^{\ast}\).

By capacity identification and weak selection monotonicity, a zero gap makes the probability of the permitted one-sided selection stratum equal to zero.  Hence \(S_0=S_1\) almost surely among compliers in the cell and
\[
P(S_0=S_1,C\mid X=x)=P(C\mid X=x).
\]
Finally, when \(q_0=q_1\), for every \(t\),
\[
\ell_{\le t}-h_{\le t}
=(q_0-\ell_{>t})-(q_1-h_{>t})
=h_{>t}-\ell_{>t}.
\]
Also \(\min\{\Delta q,0\}=0\).  Thus the branch-free lower formula equals both legacy directional formulas, and the upper formula is common to all presentations.
