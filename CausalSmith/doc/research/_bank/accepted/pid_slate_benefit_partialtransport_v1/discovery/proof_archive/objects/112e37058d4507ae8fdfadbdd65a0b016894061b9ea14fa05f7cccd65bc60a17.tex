On the stated submodel, capacity identification gives, for every relevant cell,
\[
q_0(x)=q_1(x)=P(C\mid X=x)=m(x)>0.
\]
Hence \(\Delta q(x)=0\), and the tie-face proposition gives \(\Gamma_x=\Gamma_x^0\) with both margins exact.  Define the normalized distribution functions
\[
F_0(t)=\frac{\ell_{\le t}(x)}{m(x)},\qquad
F_1(t)=\frac{h_{\le t}(x)}{m(x)},\qquad F_0(-1)=0.
\]
Division is valid because the cell is restricted to positive complier mass.  The threshold formulas become
\[
\frac{B_L(x)}{m(x)}=max\left\{0,\max_t\{F_0(t)-F_1(t)\}\right\},
\]
and
\[
\frac{B_U(x)}{m(x)}=min_t\{F_0(t-1)+1-F_1(t)\}.
\]
The cited fixed-marginal result of Lu, Ding, and Dasgupta, Proposition 2 and equation (6), states these sharp lower and upper formulas for the strict-benefit probability after the harmless reversal of their row/column indexing.  Thus the partial-transport formulas reduce exactly, cell by cell, to the published complete-marginal formulas rather than merely implying them.
