Fix a cell \(x\) with \(p_x>0\).  Instrument overlap makes both conditional laws below well defined.  By treatment consistency, selection exclusion-consistency, and outcome exclusion-consistency,
\[
 P_{\mathrm{obs}}(Y=i,S=1,D=0\mid Z=z,X=x)
 =P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,D_z=0\mid Z=z,X=x).
\]
Conditional instrument independence removes \(Z=z\) from the right-hand side.  Therefore, using no defiers so that \(\{D_1=0\}\subseteq\{D_0=0\}\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\ell_i(x)
&=P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,D_0=0\mid X=x)
  -P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,D_1=0\mid X=x)\\
&=P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,D_0=0,D_1=1\mid X=x)\\
&=P(Y_0=i,S_0=1,C\mid X=x).
\end{aligned}
\]
The symmetric calculation, now using \(\{D_0=1\}\subseteq\{D_1=1\}\), gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
h_j(x)
&=P(Y_1=j,S_1=1,D_1=1\mid X=x)
  -P(Y_1=j,S_1=1,D_0=1\mid X=x)\\
&=P(Y_1=j,S_1=1,C\mid X=x).
\end{aligned}
\]
Summing these identities over the finite support \(\mathcal Y\) yields
\[
q_0(x)=P(S_0=1,C\mid X=x),\qquad
q_1(x)=P(S_1=1,C\mid X=x),
\]
and hence the asserted formula for \(\Delta q(x)\).

It remains to use weak selection monotonicity.  Conditional on \((C,X=x)\), either \(S_1\ge S_0\) almost surely or \(S_1\le S_0\) almost surely.  In the first case,
\[
P(S_0=S_1=1,C\mid X=x)=P(S_0=1,C\mid X=x)=q_0(x)=m(x),
\]
whereas in the second case the same probability is \(q_1(x)=m(x)\).  Moreover, the first case implies \(\Delta q(x)=P(S_0=0,S_1=1,C\mid X=x)\ge0\), and the second implies \(\Delta q(x)=-P(S_0=1,S_1=0,C\mid X=x)\le0\).  Thus a strictly positive gap forces \(d(x)=+1\), and a strictly negative gap forces \(d(x)=-1\).  When the gap is zero, both weak directions imply equality of \(S_0\) and \(S_1\) among compliers, so the observable gap does not distinguish the label \(d(x)\).  Every equality used here follows from the separately declared consistency, independence, overlap, no-defier, and weak-selection-monotonicity assumptions.
