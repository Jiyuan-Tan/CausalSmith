\[
g_n=\frac{512K|\mathcal X|}{m_{\star}\varepsilon_Z^2}(a_n+\eta_n).
\]
Define
\[
\mathcal C_{1-\alpha,n}
=\left[\max\{0,\widehat\Psi_{n,L}-g_n\},\ \min\{1,\widehat\Psi_{n,U}+g_n\}\right].
\]
Uniformly over \(P\in\mathcal P_n\),
\[
P\!\left\{\|\widehat\Psi_n-\Psi(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\|_\infty\le g_n\right\}\to1
\]
and therefore
\[
\inf_{P\in\mathcal P_n}P\{\Theta_I(P_{\mathrm{obs}})\subseteq\mathcal C_{1-\alpha,n}\}\to1.
\]
This uniformly consistent conservative conclusion permits arbitrary simultaneous threshold-cut ties, positive-survivor zero-gap cells, and survivor masses approaching zero that are omitted at \(\eta_n\).  Its validity is supplied entirely by the deterministic guard; it does not assert first-order exact calibration of an unguarded face-aware multiplier confidence set.
