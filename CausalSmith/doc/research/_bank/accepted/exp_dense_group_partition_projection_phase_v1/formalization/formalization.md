## Theorems (T)

### T-block: t1 — For every \(n\ge2M\), \(\mathsf K_n\) acts on…
**Statement.** For every \(n\ge2M\), \(\mathsf K_n\) acts on \(\mathcal H_{n,k}\) by \(\lambda_{n,k}=(-1)^k(M)_k/(n-M)_k\). Consequently, for centered \(f,g\in L^2(\Omega_{n,M})\) and a uniform ordered disjoint pair \((A,C)\), \(\operatorname{Cov}(f(A),g(C))=\sum_{k=1}^M\lambda_{n,k}\langle\Pi_{n,k}f,\Pi_{n,k}g\rangle_n\), and \(C_{\tau\tau,n}=\sum_{k=1}^M\lambda_{n,k}\|\Pi_{n,k}(h_{1,n}-h_{0,n})\|_n^2\).

### T-block: t2 — Under the two-stage design,…
**Statement.** Under the two-stage design, \(\mathbb E[\widehat\tau_n]=\tau_n\) and the exact finite-sample variance is \[\sigma_n^2=\frac{R_n}{G_n}+C_{\tau\tau,n}-\frac{C_{11,n}}{G_{1n}}-\frac{C_{00,n}}{G_{0n}}.\] Moreover, \(\mathbb E[\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}]=(V_{1,n}-C_{11,n})/G_{1n}+(V_{0,n}-C_{00,n})/G_{0n}\), so \(\mathbb E[\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}]-\sigma_n^2=-C_{\tau\tau,n}\) exactly.

### T-block: t3 — For every array satisfying group-count growth, stable tre…
**Statement.** For every array satisfying group-count growth, stable treatment and sampling fractions, and bounded potential outcomes, \[G_n\sigma_n^2=R_n-\rho E_{\tau,1,n}+o(1).\] More precisely, \(G_nC_{\tau\tau,n}+\rho E_{\tau,1,n}\to0\) and \(C_{11,n}/p_n+C_{00,n}/(1-p_n)\to0\); all Johnson degrees \(k\ge2\) vanish at the \(G_n\)-scale uniformly over the bounded schedules.

### T-block: t4 — For every array in \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{dense}}\),…
**Statement.** For every array in \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{dense}}\), \[ G_n\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}-R_n\xrightarrow{p}0 \quad\text{and}\quad G_n\bigl(\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}-\sigma_n^2\bigr) -\rho E_{\tau,1,n}\xrightarrow{p}0. \] Consequently, CR2 is asymptotically conservative in the sense that, for every \(\varepsilon>0\), \[ \Pr\!\left\{ \widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}/\sigma_n^2<1-\varepsilon \right\}\longrightarrow0, \] and it is ratio-consistent if and only if \[ \frac{\rho E_{\tau,1,n}}{R_n-\rho E_{\tau,1,n}}\longrightarrow0. \] These conclusions are unconditional over \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{dense}}\) and use no predictable quadratic-variation premise.

### T-block: t5 — For every array in \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{dense}}\) with \…
**Statement.** For every array in \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{dense}}\) with \(\rho=0\), equivalently \(N_n/n\to0\), \[ \frac{\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}}{\sigma_n^2}\xrightarrow{p}1. \] No condition \(N_n^2/n\to0\) is imposed. In particular, the conclusion applies whenever the remaining defining conditions of \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{dense}}\) hold with \(N_n=M\lfloor n^{3/4}/M\rfloor\); these grouped-unit counts satisfy \(N_n/n\to0\) but \(N_n^2/n\to\infty\).

### T-block: t6 — Suppose \(G_{1n}\ge 2\) and \(G_{0n}\ge 2\)
**Statement.** Suppose \(G_{1n}\ge 2\) and \(G_{0n}\ge 2\). Let \({\tt fit}={\tt lm}(Y^{\mathrm{obs}}\sim Z)\) be unweighted, with only an intercept and numeric binary group treatment, equal group size \(M\), and clusters equal the realized groups. With the default identity target, the treatment-coordinate entry of \({\tt clubSandwich::vcovCR}({\tt fit},\ {\tt cluster}={\tt group},\ {\tt type}={\tt 'CR2'})\) equals \(\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}=s_{1,n}^2/G_{1n}+s_{0,n}^2/G_{0n}\) exactly.

### T-block: t7 — For the balanced \(n=N_n=8\), \(M=2\), \(G_n=4\),…
**Statement.** For the balanced \(n=N_n=8\), \(M=2\), \(G_n=4\), \(G_{1n}=2\) witness, \(V_{1,n}=E_{\tau,1,n}=3/7\), \(C_{11,n}=-1/7\), \(\sigma_n^2=1/7\), and \(\mathbb E[\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR2},n}]=2/7\). Exhaustive enumeration over the \(105\) perfect matchings and the \(6\) balanced treatment allocations, totaling \(630\) design points, reproduces both moments.

### T-block: t8 — Under \(\mathbb H_n^{\mathrm{same}}\) and…
**Statement.** Under \(\mathbb H_n^{\mathrm{same}}\) and \(\mathbb H_n^{\mathrm{ind}}\), the induced laws of \(\mathcal O_n\) are exactly equal for every \(n\). Suppose \(G_n\to\infty\), \(p_n\to p\in(0,1)\), and \(N_n/n\to\rho\in(0,1]\). With prior probability tending to one, \(V_{1,n}\to1/M\), \(V_{0,n}\to1/M\), and \(E_{\tau,1,n}\to0\) under \(\mathbb H_n^{\mathrm{same}}\), whereas \(E_{\tau,1,n}\to2/M\) under \(\mathbb H_n^{\mathrm{ind}}\). Hence \(G_n\sigma_n^2\to1/(Mp(1-p))\) under the first prior and \(G_n\sigma_n^2\to1/(Mp(1-p))-2\rho/M\) under the second. The limit gap is \(2\rho/M>0\), and the smaller limit satisfies \(1/(Mp(1-p))-2\rho/M\ge2/M\). Consequently, for every fixed \(c_\sigma\) with \(0<c_\sigma<1/(Mp(1-p))-2\rho/M\), the probability of \(G_n\sigma_n^2\ge c_\sigma\) tends to one under each prior; the separated-limit claim is therefore confined to dense rows \(\rho>0\) that satisfy scaled-variance nondegeneracy with high prior probability.

### T-block: t9 — Fix \(M\ge2\), \(p\in(0,1)\), \(\rho\in(0,1]\), \(B\ge1\)…
**Statement.** Fix \(M\ge2\), \(p\in(0,1)\), \(\rho\in(0,1]\), \(B\ge1\), and design sequences satisfying the three fraction and growth assumptions. Put \[ d_{\mathrm{same}}=\frac1{Mp(1-p)},\qquad d_{\mathrm{ind}}=\frac1{Mp(1-p)}-\frac{2\rho}{M}, \] and suppose \(0<c_\sigma<d_{\mathrm{ind}}\). There are high-prior-probability sets of deterministic Rademacher schedule rows whose Cartesian diagonal selections all belong to \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{dense}}\); after conditioning \(\mathbb H_n^{\mathrm{same}}\) and \(\mathbb H_n^{\mathrm{ind}}\) on these sets, their induced one-realization mixture laws have total-variation distance tending to zero, while \(G_n\sigma_n^2\) converges uniformly on the respective supports to \(d_{\mathrm{same}}\) and \(d_{\mathrm{ind}}\). Consequently there exists \(\varepsilon>0\) such that every measurable \(\widehat S_n(\mathcal O_n)\) obeys \[ \liminf_{n\to\infty}\sup_{Y\in\mathcal C_{\mathrm{dense}}} \Pr_Y\left(\left|\frac{\widehat S_n}{\sigma_n^2}-1\right|>\varepsilon\right) \ge\frac12. \] Thus no estimator from one realization is uniformly ratio-consistent for the exact variance over the full dense class. The deterministic support arrays, rather than a generally unavailable pair of deterministic arrays with close laws, are the valid diagonal certificate.
