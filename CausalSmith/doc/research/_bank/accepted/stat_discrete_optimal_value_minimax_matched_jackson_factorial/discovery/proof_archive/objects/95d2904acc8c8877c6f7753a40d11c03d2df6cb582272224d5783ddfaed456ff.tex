\(\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) is the set of all laws \(\mathbb P\) on \([d]\times\{0,1\}^2\) for which there are \(p=(p_1,\ldots,p_d)\in\Delta_d\), \(\pi_x\in[\epsilon,1-\epsilon]\), and \(\mu_{ax}\in[0,1]\) satisfying, for every \(x\in[d]\) and \(a\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
 q_{a1,x}=p_x\pi_x^a(1-\pi_x)^{1-a}\mu_{ax},\qquad
 q_{a0,x}=p_x\pi_x^a(1-\pi_x)^{1-a}(1-\mu_{ax}),
\]
where \(q_{ay,x}=\mathbb P(X=x,A=a,Y=y)\).  Thus the support contains at most \(d\) covariate cells; the parameters on a null cell may be chosen arbitrarily within their displayed ranges.
