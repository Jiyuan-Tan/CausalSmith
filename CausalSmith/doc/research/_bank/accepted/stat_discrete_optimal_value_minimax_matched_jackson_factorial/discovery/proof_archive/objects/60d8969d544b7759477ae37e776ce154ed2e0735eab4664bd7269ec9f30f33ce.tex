For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there is \(C_\epsilon<\infty\) such that the explicit all-data estimator \(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}\) satisfies \(\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}E_P[(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}-V^\star(P))^2]\leq C_\epsilon\min\{1,d/[n\log(ed)]\}\) for every \(n\geq1\) and \(d\geq2\). The bound permits unknown unequal propensities, arbitrary occupied-cell masses, null cells, outcome means at zero or one, and exact treatment-effect ties.
