By \(\mathrm{thm:jackson\text{-}factorial\text{-}upper}\),
\[
 \sup_{\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}}
 E_{\mathbb P}[(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}-\Psi(\mathbb P))^2]
 \leq C_\epsilon\min\{1,d/[n\log(ed)]\}.
 \tag{32}
\]
By \(\mathrm{def:minimax\text{-}risk}\), \(\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\) is the infimum of the same worst-case loss over all measurable estimators.  Substituting the particular measurable estimator \(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}\) therefore gives
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \leq\sup_{\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}}
 E_{\mathbb P}[(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}-\Psi(\mathbb P))^2].
 \tag{33}
\]
The all-measurable-estimator converse \(\mathrm{thm:all\text{-}estimator\text{-}lower}\) gives
\[
 \mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}
 \geq c_\epsilon\min\{1,d/[n\log(ed)]\}.
 \tag{34}
\]
Concatenating (32)--(34) proves the displayed matched frontier for every \(n\geq1\) and \(d\geq2\).

For the causal conclusion, \(\mathrm{prop:identification\text{-}and\text{-}extension}\) proves that the observed-margin map from \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\) onto \(\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) is surjective, and \(\mathrm{prop:causal\text{-}optimal\text{-}value\text{-}corollary}\) proves, rather than defines,
\[
 V^\star(P)=\Psi\{\operatorname{Obs}(P)\}.
\]
Since the data in both formulations are the same observed variables \((X,A,Y)\), every measurable estimator has identical risk at an observed law and at any of its completions.  Surjectivity and target equality make the two suprema, and hence their minimax risks, equal.  This proves the stated causal transfer without changing the statistical target.
