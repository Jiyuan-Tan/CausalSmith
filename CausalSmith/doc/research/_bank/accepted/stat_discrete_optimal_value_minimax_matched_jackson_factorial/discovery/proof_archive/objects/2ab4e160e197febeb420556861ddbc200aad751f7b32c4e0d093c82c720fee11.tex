There are positive universal constants such that, whenever \(d\geq2\), \(n\) is at least a constant multiple of \(d/\log(ed)\), and \(\log(en)\) is at most a constant multiple of \(\log(ed)\), the minimax mean-squared error for estimating \(\lVert\mathbf P-\mathbf Q\rVert_1\) from two independent samples of respective sizes \(\operatorname{Pois}(2n)\), one from each of \(\mathbf P,\mathbf Q\in\Delta_d\), is bounded below by a positive constant multiple of \(\min\{1,d/[n\log(en)]\}\).
