Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\) and put \(\mathbb P=\operatorname{Obs}(P)\).  By \(\mathrm{def:model-class}\), \(\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  For an occupied cell \(x\), its overlap probabilities are positive, and \(\mathrm{ass:conditional-exchangeability}\) and \(\mathrm{ass:consistency}\) give, separately for each \(a\in\{0,1\}\),
\[
 E_P\{Y(a)\mid X=x\}
 =E_P\{Y(a)\mid A=a,X=x\}
 =E_P(Y\mid A=a,X=x)=\mu_{ax}.                                     \tag{6}
\]
The first equality in (6) is armwise conditional independence; the second is consistency on \(\{A=a\}\).  By \(\mathrm{def:oracle-value}\), finite-support conditioning and (6) imply
\[
 V^\star(P)=\sum_{x:p_x>0}p_x\max_a\mu_{ax}.
\]
Null-cell summands vanish, so \(\mathrm{def:observed-optimal-value}\) identifies the last display with \(\Psi(\mathbb P)\).  This proves the causal equality rather than placing it in either definition.
