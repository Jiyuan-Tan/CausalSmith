Fix \(\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  By \(\mathrm{def:observed-model-class}\), choose parameters \(p_x,\pi_x,\mu_{ax}\) in the displayed ranges.  Summing the two outcome probabilities in arm \(a\) gives
\[
 s_a(q_x)=p_x\pi_x^a(1-\pi_x)^{1-a},\qquad s(q_x)=p_x,
 \qquad q_{a1,x}=s_a(q_x)\mu_{ax}.                                  \tag{1}
\]
If \(p_x>0\), the bound \(\epsilon\leq\pi_x\leq1-\epsilon\) in \(\mathrm{def:observed-model-class}\) implies
\(\epsilon s(q_x)\leq s_1(q_x)\leq(1-\epsilon)s(q_x)\); if \(p_x=0\), then \(q_x=0\) and the same inequalities hold.  Hence \(q_x\in\mathcal C_\epsilon\) by \(\mathrm{def:overlap-cone}\).  On this cone, \(s_a(q_x)\geq\epsilon s(q_x)\), so (1) and \(\mathrm{def:global-extension}\) yield
\[
 g_{a,\epsilon}(q_x)=\frac{s(q_x)q_{a1,x}}{s_a(q_x)}=p_x\mu_{ax},
 \qquad \bar f_\epsilon(q_x)=p_x\max_a\mu_{ax}                    \tag{2}
\]
on occupied cells.  Both sides of (2) are zero on null cells.  Summing (2) and invoking \(\mathrm{def:observed-optimal-value}\) proves \(\Psi(\mathbb P)=\sum_x\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)\).

For the global bounds, put \(D_a(u)=\max\{s_a(u),\epsilon s(u)\}\).  Because \(0\leq u_{a1}\leq s_a(u)\leq D_a(u)\),
\[
 0\leq g_{a,\epsilon}(u)=\frac{s(u)u_{a1}}{D_a(u)}\leq s(u),        \tag{3}
\]
and (3) passes to the maximum over \(a\).  On \(s_a(u)\leq\epsilon s(u)\), one has \(g_{a,\epsilon}(u)=u_{a1}/\epsilon\), whose coordinate derivatives have absolute value at most \(\epsilon^{-1}\).  On \(s_a(u)\geq\epsilon s(u)\), write \(S=s(u)\), \(t=s_a(u)\), and \(z=u_{a1}\).  The derivatives of \(Sz/t\), respectively in a coordinate outside arm \(a\), the success coordinate in arm \(a\), and the failure coordinate in arm \(a\), are
\[
 \frac zt,\qquad \frac zt+\frac St\left(1-\frac zt\right),
 \qquad \frac zt-\frac St\frac zt.                                \tag{4}
\]
Here \(0\leq z/t\leq1\) and \(S/t\leq\epsilon^{-1}\), so every derivative in (4) has absolute value at most \(1+\epsilon^{-1}\).  The two formulas agree on \(t=\epsilon S\).  A line segment in \(\mathbb R_+^4\setminus\{0\}\) is divided by this hyperplane into at most two nontrivial pieces, and integration of the coordinate-gradient bound along those pieces gives
\[
 |g_{a,\epsilon}(u)-g_{a,\epsilon}(v)|
 \leq(1+\epsilon^{-1})\lVert u-v\rVert_1.                           \tag{5}
\]
At the origin, (5) follows by continuity and (3), since \(s(u)=\lVert u\rVert_1\).  Finally,
\[
 |\max_a g_{a,\epsilon}(u)-\max_a g_{a,\epsilon}(v)|
 \leq\max_a|g_{a,\epsilon}(u)-g_{a,\epsilon}(v)|,
\]
which proves the asserted Lipschitz bound, including coordinate faces and tie surfaces.

It remains to prove equivalence of the experiments.  For the chosen parameters of \(\mathbb P\), draw \(X\) with masses \(p_x\); conditional on \(X=x\), draw \(Y(0)\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\mu_{0x})\) and \(Y(1)\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\mu_{1x})\) independently, draw \(A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(\pi_x)\) independently of their joint vector, and set \(Y=Y(A)\).  This construction satisfies consistency, satisfies the armwise conditional independence in \(\mathrm{ass:conditional-exchangeability}\), and has its observed margin equal to \(\mathbb P\) by the two formulas in \(\mathrm{def:observed-model-class}\).  Its observed margin also has the required overlap, so \(\mathrm{def:model-class}\) places the completion in \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\).  Conversely, if \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\), then \(\mathrm{def:model-class}\) states that its observed margin belongs to \(\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).  This proves both directions, including arbitrary null cells and boundary means.
