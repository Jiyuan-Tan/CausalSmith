For every \(\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), one has \(q_x\in\mathcal C_\epsilon\) and \(\Psi(\mathbb P)=\sum_{x=1}^d\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)\).  Moreover, \(0\leq\bar f_\epsilon(u)\leq s(u)\) and \(\lvert\bar f_\epsilon(u)-\bar f_\epsilon(v)\rvert\leq(1+\epsilon^{-1})\lVert u-v\rVert_1\) for all \(u,v\in\mathbb R_+^4\).  Every \(\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) has a completion \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\) with \(\operatorname{Obs}(P)=\mathbb P\), obtained by drawing conditionally independent Bernoulli potential outcomes with means \(\mu_{0x},\mu_{1x}\), then drawing \(A\) independently of their joint vector given \(X\), and setting \(Y=Y(A)\); conversely, \(\operatorname{Obs}(P)\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) for every \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\).
