For \(\theta\in[-1/2,1/2]^d\), define \(P_\theta^{\mathrm{dense}}\) by \(p_x=1/d\), \(\pi_x=1/2\), \(\mu_{1x}=(1+\theta_x)/2\), and \(\mu_{0x}=(1-\theta_x)/2\). Complete the observed law by drawing \(Y(0)\) and \(Y(1)\) conditionally independent Bernoulli variables with these means, drawing \(A\mid X=x\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\) independently of the potential outcomes, and setting \(Y=Y(A)\). For even \(K\), let \(E_K=\inf_{p:\deg(p)\leq K}\sup_{t\in[-1,1]}\lvert\lvert t\rvert-p(t)\rvert\), and obtain symmetric probability measures \(\nu_{0,K}\) and \(\nu_{1,K}\) by taking the negative and positive parts of twice a symmetrized norm-one Hahn--Banach measure that annihilates every polynomial of degree at most \(K\) and integrates \(\lvert t\rvert\) to \(E_K\). Thus their moments agree through degree \(K\) and \(\int\lvert t\rvert\,d\nu_{1,K}-\int\lvert t\rvert\,d\nu_{0,K}=2E_K\). Put \(K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}=2\lceil4L_d\rceil\), \(\lambda_{n,d}=2n/d\), and \(a_{n,d}=\sqrt{K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}/(64\lambda_{n,d})}\). Let \(\Pi_{0,n,d}\) be the pushforward of \(\nu_{0,K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}}^{\otimes d}\) under the coordinatewise scaling map \(t\mapsto a_{n,d}t\), and define \(\Pi_{1,n,d}\) analogously from \(\nu_{1,K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}}^{\otimes d}\). Let \(\mathbb M_{0,n,d}\) and \(\mathbb M_{1,n,d}\) be the respective observation mixtures when the total sample size is independent \(\operatorname{Pois}(2n)\). Finally, define \(\mathfrak R^{\mathrm{Pois}}_{2n,d,\epsilon}:=\inf_{\widehat V}\sup_{P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}}E_P[(\widehat V-V^\star(P))^2]\) in that Poissonized experiment and \(\Delta_{n,d}^{\mathrm{dense}}:=E_{\Pi_{1,n,d}}V^\star(P_\theta^{\mathrm{dense}})-E_{\Pi_{0,n,d}}V^\star(P_\theta^{\mathrm{dense}})\).
