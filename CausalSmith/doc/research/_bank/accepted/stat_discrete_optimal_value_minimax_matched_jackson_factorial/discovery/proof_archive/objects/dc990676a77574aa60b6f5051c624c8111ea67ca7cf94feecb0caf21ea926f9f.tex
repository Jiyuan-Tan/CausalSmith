Fix \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\).  By \(\mathrm{ass:consistency}\), \(Y(a)=Y\) on \(\{A=a\}\), and by \(\mathrm{ass:conditional-exchangeability}\), for every occupied cell \(x\),
\[
 E_P\{Y(a)\mid X=x\}=E_P\{Y(a)\mid A=a,X=x\}=E_P(Y\mid A=a,X=x)=\mu_{ax}.
\]
The conditioning event in the middle has positive probability because \(p_x>0\) and \(\epsilon\leq\pi_x\leq1-\epsilon\) by \(\mathrm{ass:fixed-overlap}\).  Taking the maximum over \(a\), then expectation over the finite support \([d]\), proves
\[
 V^\star(P)=\sum_{x:p_x>0}p_x\max_a\mu_{ax}.
\]
Equivalently the sum may be written over all \(x\in[d]\), after assigning arbitrary versions to \(\mu_{ax}\) on null cells; every such added summand is zero.

For an occupied \(x\), the definitions of the observed cell masses give
\[
 s(q_x)=p_x,\qquad s_1(q_x)=p_x\pi_x,\qquad
 s_0(q_x)=p_x(1-\pi_x),qquad q_{a1,x}=s_a(q_x)\mu_{ax}.
\]
Fixed overlap therefore implies \(q_x\in\mathcal C_\epsilon\).  On this cone \(s_a(q_x)\geq\epsilon s(q_x)\), so \(\mathrm{def:global-extension}\) yields
\[
 g_{a,\epsilon}(q_x)
 =\frac{s(q_x)q_{a1,x}}{s_a(q_x)}
 =p_x\mu_{ax},
 \qquad
 \bar f_\epsilon(q_x)=p_x\max_a\mu_{ax}.
\]
For a null cell \(q_x=0\), both sides are zero by definition.  Summation proves the last representation in \(\mathrm{def:oracle-value}\).

It remains to verify the global analytic assertions.  Write \(D_a(u)=\max\{s_a(u),\epsilon s(u)\}\).  Since \(0\leq u_{a1}\leq s_a(u)\leq D_a(u)\),
\[
 0\leq g_{a,\epsilon}(u)=s(u)u_{a1}/D_a(u)\leq s(u),
\]
and the same bounds hold for the maximum \(\bar f_\epsilon\).  On the half-space \(s_a(u)\leq\epsilon s(u)\), \(g_{a,\epsilon}(u)=u_{a1}/\epsilon\), whose coordinate derivatives have absolute value at most \(\epsilon^{-1}\).  On the half-space \(s_a(u)\geq\epsilon s(u)\), put \(t=s_a(u)\), \(z=u_{a1}\), and \(S=s(u)\).  The derivatives of \(Sz/t\) in coordinates outside arm \(a\), in the success coordinate of arm \(a\), and in the failure coordinate of arm \(a\), respectively, are
\[
 z/t,\qquad z/t+(S/t)(1-z/t),\qquad z/t-(S/t)(z/t).
\]
Here \(0\leq z/t\leq1\) and \(S/t\leq\epsilon^{-1}\), so every displayed derivative has absolute value at most \(1+\epsilon^{-1}\).  The two formulas agree on \(t=\epsilon S\).  Any line segment in \(\mathbb R_+^4\setminus\{0\}\) is split by that hyperplane into at most two pieces; integrating the coordinate-gradient bound along those pieces gives
\[
 |g_{a,\epsilon}(u)-g_{a,\epsilon}(v)|
 \leq(1+\epsilon^{-1})\|u-v\|_1.
\]
At the origin this follows by continuity and \(0\leq g_{a,\epsilon}(u)\leq s(u)=\|u\|_1\).  Finally,
\[
 |\max_a g_{a,\epsilon}(u)-\max_a g_{a,\epsilon}(v)|
 \leq\max_a|g_{a,\epsilon}(u)-g_{a,\epsilon}(v)|,
\]
which proves the asserted Lipschitz bound for \(\bar f_\epsilon\), including overlap boundaries, coordinate faces, and the origin.
