Work first in the uncapped Poisson experiment.  Poisson thinning from \(M\sim\operatorname{Pois}(n/4)\) and the fair marks \(B_i\) gives, with \(m=n/8\), mutually independent counts
\[
 N'_{\jmath,x}\sim\operatorname{Pois}(mq_{\jmath,x}),\qquad
 N_{\jmath,x}\sim\operatorname{Pois}(mq_{\jmath,x})
\]
over all \((\jmath,x)\), with the pilot array independent of the evaluation array.  For a scalar \(N\sim\operatorname{Pois}(mq)\), the definition of \(U_h\) gives the exponential generating function
\[
 \sum_{h\geq0}U_h(N;z)\frac{w^h}{h!}=e^{-zw}(1+w/m)^N.
\]
Taking the expectation of one copy and of the product of copies at \(w,v\), respectively, gives
\[
 E e^{-zw}(1+w/m)^N=e^{(q-z)w},
\]
and
\[
 E\!\left[e^{-z(w+v)}(1+w/m)^N(1+v/m)^N\right]
 =\exp\{(q-z)(w+v)+(q/m)wv\}.
\]
Coefficient extraction proves both factorial identities in the statement.

Set \(L=L_d\), \(\tau=L/m\), \(c_\jmath=N'_{\jmath,x}/m\), and
\[
 G_x=\bigcap_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}\bigl\{|c_\jmath-q_{\jmath,x}|\leq h_\jmath/4\bigr\},
 \qquad v_x=\sqrt{p_x\tau}+\tau.
\]
The following bounds are now derived from the displayed pilot construction.  On \(G_x\), \(q_x\in Q_x\), \(h_\jmath/2\leq r_\jmath\leq h_\jmath\), and solving the quadratic inequality between \(c_\jmath\) and \(q_{\jmath,x}\) yields
\[
 c_\jmath\leq C(q_{\jmath,x}+\tau),\qquad
 q_{\jmath,x}\leq C(c_\jmath+\tau).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \sum_\jmath r_\jmath\leq Cv_x,\qquad
 \frac{q_{\jmath,x}}{mr_\jmath^2}\leq\frac C L.                     \tag{4}
\]
For completeness, these conclusions also hold when \(q_{\jmath,x}=0\), because then the corresponding Poisson count is zero almost surely.

To control the complement, for \(W\sim\operatorname{Pois}(\lambda)\), exponential Markov applied to
\(E e^{t(W-\lambda)}=\exp\{\lambda(e^t-1-t)\}\) gives, for \(z\geq1\),
\[
 \Pr\{|W-\lambda|>\sqrt{2\lambda z}+2z\}\leq2e^{-z}.                \tag{5}
\]
Choose the single numerical constant \(H_0\) large enough that the event in (5) with \(z=100L\) implies
\[
 |W/m-q|\leq\frac{H_0}{4}\left\{\sqrt{(W/m)L/m}+L/m\right\}.
\]
This follows by considering \(\lambda\geq C L\), where \(W\geq\lambda/2\), and \(\lambda<CL\), where the additive \(H_0L/m\) term dominates.  Integrating (5) above \(100L\), and using \(W/m\leq q+|W/m-q|\), proves for \(t=1,2,4\)
\[
 E\left[\left\{|W/m-q|+\sqrt{(W/m)\tau}+\tau\right\}^t
       \mathbf1_{\mathrm{bad}}\right]
 \leq C e^{-40L}(\sqrt{q\tau}+\tau)^t.                              \tag{6}
\]
Independence across the four coordinates, a union bound, and Cauchy--Schwarz with the fourth moment in (6) give
\[
 E\left[\left\{\|b_x-q_x\|_1+\sum_\jmath r_\jmath\right\}^t
       \mathbf1_{G_x^c}\right]
 \leq C e^{-20L}v_x^t,\qquad t=1,2,                                  \tag{7}
\]
and (4), together with the corresponding unconditional second moments, gives
\[
 E\left[\left(\sum_\jmath r_\jmath\right)^2\mathbf1_{G_x}\right]
 \leq Cv_x^2.                                                         \tag{8}
\]
Thus the failure cost retains the local \(p_x\)-scale.

Apply \(\mathrm{lem:simultaneous-jackson-certificate}\) on \(G_x\).  If \(\ell_\jmath=0\), then \(c_\jmath\leq h_\jmath\), whence \(c_\jmath+ h_\jmath\leq C\tau\) and
\[
 \sqrt{(q_{\jmath,x}-\ell_\jmath)(u_\jmath-q_{\jmath,x})}
 \leq C\sqrt{q_{\jmath,x}\tau},\qquad r_\jmath\leq C\tau.
\]
If \(\ell_\jmath>0\), then \(c_\jmath>h_\jmath\), so \(q_{\jmath,x}\geq3c_\jmath/4\) and \(r_\jmath\leq C\sqrt{q_{\jmath,x}\tau}\).  Since \(\sum_\jmath\sqrt{q_{\jmath,x}}\leq2\sqrt{p_x}\), the pointwise polynomial bias is
\[
 |P_{x,K_d}(q_x)-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)|
 \leq C_\epsilon\left\{\frac{\sqrt{p_x\tau}}{K_d}+\frac{\tau}{K_d^2}\right\}. \tag{9}
\]

Conditional on the pilot, unbiasedness of the factorial lift gives \(E(Z_x\mid N')=P_{x,K_d}(q_x)\).  From the second factorial identity and (4),
\[
 \frac{E\{U_h(N;q_0)^2\}}{r^{2h}}
 \leq\sum_{\ell=0}^h\binom h\ell^2\ell!(C/L)^\ell
 \leq\exp(Ch^2/L)                                                     \tag{10}
\]
whenever \(|q-q_0|\leq r\).  Combine (10), coordinate independence, Minkowski's inequality, and the coefficient envelope from \(\mathrm{lem:simultaneous-jackson-certificate}\).  With \(S_x=(1+\epsilon^{-1})\sum_\jmath r_\jmath\),
\[
 E\{(Z_x-\bar f_\epsilon(b_x))^2\mid N'\}
 \leq S_x^2\exp(C_1K_d+C_2K_d^2/L).                                  \tag{11}
\]
Choose \(\kappa>0\) sufficiently small and then \(D_0\) sufficiently large.  Since \(K_d=\max\{2,\lfloor\kappa L\rfloor\}\), (11) is at most \(C S_x^2d^{1/16}\) for \(d\geq D_0\); increasing \(C\) covers the remaining finite values.

Write \(W_x=Z_x-\bar f_\epsilon(b_x)\), \(t_x=d^{1/4}S_x\), and note the elementary projection inequalities
\[
 |\Pi_{[-t,t]}(w)-w|\leq w^2/t,\qquad |\Pi_{[-t,t]}(w)|\leq|w|.       \tag{12}
\]
On \(G_x\), equations (9)--(12), \(|\bar f_\epsilon(b_x)-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)|\leq S_x\), and \(d^{-3/16}\leq C/L^2\) give
\[
 |E(T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}\mid N')-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)|
 \leq C_\epsilon\left\{\sqrt{\frac{p_x}{mL}}+\frac1{mL}\right\}, \tag{13}
\]
and
\[
 E\{(T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x))^2\mid N'\}
 \leq C S_x^2d^{1/16}.                                                 \tag{14}
\]
On \(G_x^c\), clipping and global Lipschitzness give the deterministic inequality
\[
 |T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)|
 \leq(1+\epsilon^{-1})\|b_x-q_x\|_1+d^{1/4}S_x.
\]
Its first two moments are controlled by (7); because \(e^{-20L}d^{1/2}\leq Cd^{1/16}\), they fit respectively into the right side of (13) and the expectation of (14).  Integrating (13)--(14) and using (8), \(v_x^2\leq2(p_xL/m+L^2/m^2)\), proves
\[
 |E T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)|
 \leq C_\epsilon\left\{\sqrt{\frac{p_x}{mL_d}}+\frac1{mL_d}\right\},
\]
\[
 \operatorname{Var}(T_x^{\mathrm{JF}})
 \leq C_\epsilon d^{1/16}\left\{\frac{p_xL_d}{m}+\frac{L_d^2}{m^2}\right\}.
\]
This proves the stated control, including pilot failure, null coordinates, boundary means, and ties.
