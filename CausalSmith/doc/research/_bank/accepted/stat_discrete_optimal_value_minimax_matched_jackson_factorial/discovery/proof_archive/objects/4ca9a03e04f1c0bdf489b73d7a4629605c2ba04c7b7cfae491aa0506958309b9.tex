For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there is \(c_\epsilon>0\) such that \(\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\geq c_\epsilon\min\{1,d/[n\log(ed)]\}\) for every \(n\geq1\) and \(d\geq2\). The proof must cover all measurable estimators by splicing an overlap-preserving Poisson moment-matching experiment when \(n>d^2\), the exact normalized \(L_1\) reduction when \(d/\log(ed)\leq n\leq d^2\), and null-cell padding plus a regular two-point submodel in the saturated and bounded-alphabet regimes.
