## Lemmas (L)

**L-1 (Let…).** Let \(Q_x=\prod_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}[\ell_{\jmath,x},u_{\jmath,x}]\) have center \(b_x\) and positive radii \(r_{\jmath,x}\). The single polynomial \(P_{x,K_d}\) in \(\mathrm{def:jackson-kernel}\) has coordinate degree \(2(K_d-1)\) and satisfies, for every \(v\in Q_x\), \(\lvert P_{x,K_d}(v)-\bar f_\epsilon(v)\rvert\leq C_\epsilon\sum_{\jmath\in\mathcal J}\{\sqrt{(v_{\jmath}-\ell_{\jmath,x})(u_{\jmath,x}-v_{\jmath})}/K_d+r_{\jmath,x}/K_d^2\}\). If \(P_{x,K_d}(v)-\bar f_\epsilon(b_x)=\sum_\alpha b_{x,\alpha}((v-b_x)/r_x)^\alpha\), then \(\sum_\alpha\lvert b_{x,\alpha}\rvert\leq A^{K_d}(1+\epsilon^{-1})\sum_{\jmath}r_{\jmath,x}\) for a universal \(A<\infty\). Both bounds hold for the same polynomial when \(Q_x\) touches the origin, a coordinate face, or a treatment-effect tie surface.

**L-2 (In the uncapped Poisson comparison experiment induced by…).** In the uncapped Poisson comparison experiment induced by \(M\) and \(B_i\), conditional on the pilot counts the statistics \(U_h(N;z)\) satisfy \(E[U_h(N;z)]=(q-z)^h\) and \(E[U_h(N;z)U_t(N;z)]=\sum_{\ell=0}^{h\wedge t}\binom h\ell\binom t\ell\ell!(q/m)^\ell(q-z)^{h+t-2\ell}\). With the pilot rectangles and clipping in \(\mathrm{def:jackson-factorial-estimator}\), uniformly over \(\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), \(\lvert E[T_x^{\mathrm{JF}}]-\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)\rvert\leq C_\epsilon\{\sqrt{p_x/(mL_d)}+1/(mL_d)\}\) and \(\operatorname{Var}(T_x^{\mathrm{JF}})\leq C_\epsilon d^{1/16}\{p_xL_d/m+L_d^2/m^2\}\). The contribution of pilot failure obeys these same local \(p_x\)-dependent scales.

**L-3 (Choose the universal cutoff \(D_0\) sufficiently large).** Choose the universal cutoff \(D_0\) sufficiently large. For every \(d\geq D_0\) and \(n>d^2\), the construction in \(\mathrm{def:dense-moment-matching-construction}\) has even degree \(8L_d\leq K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}<8L_d+2\), amplitude \(a_{n,d}^2=K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}d/(128n)\leq1/4\), and, for every \(\theta\in[-a_{n,d},a_{n,d}]^d\), an observed margin \(\operatorname{Obs}(P_\theta^{\mathrm{dense}})\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) satisfying \(p_x=1/d\), \(\pi_x=1/2\), \(\mu_{1x}=(1+\theta_x)/2\), \(\mu_{0x}=(1-\theta_x)/2\), and \(\Psi\{\operatorname{Obs}(P_\theta^{\mathrm{dense}})\}=1/2+(2d)^{-1}\sum_{x=1}^d\lvert\theta_x\rvert\). The symmetric measures \(\nu_{0,K}\) and \(\nu_{1,K}\) exist for every even \(K\), match moments through degree \(K\), and have absolute-moment gap \(2E_K\) with \(E_K\asymp K^{-1}\). Consequently \(\Delta_{n,d}^{\mathrm{dense}}=a_{n,d}E_{K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}}\asymp a_{n,d}/K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}\). Writing \(\lambda_{n,d}=2n/d\), the one-cell likelihood identity under \(\theta_x=0\) is \(E_0[L_{\theta_x}L_{\theta_x'}]=\exp(\lambda_{n,d}\theta_x\theta_x')\); hence moment matching and tensorization give \[ \operatorname{TV}(\mathbb M_{0,n,d},\mathbb M_{1,n,d}) \leq d\left\{\sum_{r=K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}+1}^{\infty}\frac{(\lambda_{n,d}a_{n,d}^2)^r}{r!}\right\}^{1/2} \leq d\left\{\frac{(e/64)^{K_d^{\mathrm{lb}}+1}}{1-e/64}\right\}^{1/2}\leq\frac1{16}. \] Under either product prior, \(\operatorname{Var}[\Psi\{\operatorname{Obs}(P_\theta^{\mathrm{dense}})\}]\leq a_{n,d}^2/(4d)\), and \(D_0\) can be chosen so that each prior assigns probability at most \(1/8\) to a deviation of the target from its prior mean exceeding \(\Delta_{n,d}^{\mathrm{dense}}/4\). The two fuzzy hypotheses therefore imply \(\mathfrak R^{\mathrm{Pois}}_{2n,d,\epsilon}\geq c_\epsilon(\Delta_{n,d}^{\mathrm{dense}})^2\geq c_\epsilon d/[nK_d^{\mathrm{lb}}]\). Finally, applying any fixed-sample estimator to the first \(n\) observations whenever an independent \(\operatorname{Pois}(2n)\) total count is at least \(n\) yields \(\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\geq\mathfrak R^{\mathrm{Pois}}_{2n,d,\epsilon}-P\{\operatorname{Pois}(2n)<n\}\geq c_\epsilon d/[n\log(ed)]\), after decreasing \(c_\epsilon\).

## Theorems (T)

### T-block: t1 — For every…
**Statement.** For every \(\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\), one has \(q_x\in\mathcal C_\epsilon\) and \(\Psi(\mathbb P)=\sum_{x=1}^d\bar f_\epsilon(q_x)\). Moreover, \(0\leq\bar f_\epsilon(u)\leq s(u)\) and \(\lvert\bar f_\epsilon(u)-\bar f_\epsilon(v)\rvert\leq(1+\epsilon^{-1})\lVert u-v\rVert_1\) for all \(u,v\in\mathbb R_+^4\). Every \(\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) has a completion \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\) with \(\operatorname{Obs}(P)=\mathbb P\), obtained by drawing conditionally independent Bernoulli potential outcomes with means \(\mu_{0x},\mu_{1x}\), then drawing \(A\) independently of their joint vector given \(X\), and setting \(Y=Y(A)\); conversely, \(\operatorname{Obs}(P)\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) for every \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\).

### T-block: t2 — For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there is…
**Statement.** For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there is \(C_\epsilon<\infty\) such that the explicit all-data estimator \(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}\) satisfies \(\sup_{\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}}E_{\mathbb P}[(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}-\Psi(\mathbb P))^2]\leq C_\epsilon\min\{1,d/[n\log(ed)]\}\) for every \(n\geq1\) and \(d\geq2\). The bound permits unknown unequal propensities, arbitrary occupied-cell masses, null cells, outcome means at zero or one, and exact treatment-effect ties.

### T-block: t3 — Every \((\mathbf P,\mathbf Q)\in\Delta_d^2\) mapped by…
**Statement.** Every \((\mathbf P,\mathbf Q)\in\Delta_d^2\) mapped by \(\mathrm{def:l1-embedding}\) has an observed margin in \(\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) with \(p_x=(P_x+Q_x)/2\), \(\pi_x=1/2\), and \(\Psi=1/2+\lVert\mathbf P-\mathbf Q\rVert_1/4\). From \(n\) draws from each of \(\mathbf P\) and \(\mathbf Q\), an explicit Markov kernel produces exactly \(n\) independent observations from that margin, so \(\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\) is at least one sixteenth of the corresponding normalized two-sample \(L_1\) minimax mean-squared error.

### T-block: t4 — For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there is…
**Statement.** For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there is \(c_\epsilon>0\) such that the observed-law minimax risk satisfies \(\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon}\geq c_\epsilon\min\{1,d/[n\log(ed)]\}\) for every \(n\geq1\) and \(d\geq2\). This covers all measurable estimators over \(\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) by splicing the dense Poisson moment-matching subclass when \(n>d^2\), the exact normalized \(L_1\) subclass when \(d/\log(ed)\leq n\leq d^2\), and null-cell padding plus a regular two-point subclass in the saturated and bounded-alphabet regimes.

### T-block: t5 — For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are constants \…
**Statement.** For every fixed \(0<\epsilon<1/2\), there are constants \(0<c_\epsilon\leq C_\epsilon<\infty\) such that, for every \(n\geq1\) and \(d\geq2\), \[ c_\epsilon\min\{1,d/[n\log(ed)]\}\leq\mathfrak R_{n,d,\epsilon} \leq\sup_{\mathbb P\in\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}}E_{\mathbb P}[(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}-\Psi(\mathbb P))^2] \leq C_\epsilon\min\{1,d/[n\log(ed)]\}. \] Through \(\mathrm{prop:causal-optimal-value-corollary}\), the same risk frontier applies to the causal optimal-treatment value over all causal completions whose observed margins range over \(\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).

### T-block: t6 — The matched frontier recovers the archived downgraded pre…
**Statement.** The matched frontier recovers the archived downgraded predecessor as a corollary on the identical class: uniformly over \(n\geq1\) and \(d\geq2\), the new upper rate is at most a constant multiple of \(d/n\), while its lower rate is at least a constant multiple of \(\min\{1,\sqrt d/n+d/[n\log(en)]\}\). For every \(2\leq d<D_0\), \(\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{JF}}=\widehat V_{n,d}^{\mathrm{ER}}\). Separately, for every fixed bounded sequence of alphabets \(d=d_n\), \(\mathfrak R_{n,d_n,\epsilon}=\Theta_{\epsilon,d_\bullet}(n^{-1})\), where the comparison constants may depend on that sequence and its finite alphabet bound.

### T-block: t7 — Along arbitrary sequences \(n\to\infty\) with…
**Statement.** Along arbitrary sequences \(n\to\infty\) with \(d=d_n\geq2\), uniform consistency for estimating \(\Psi(\mathbb P)\) over \(\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\) is possible if and only if \(d=o(n\log(en))\). Moreover, along each fixed sequence, \(\mathfrak R_{n,d_n,\epsilon}=\Theta_{\epsilon,d_\bullet}(n^{-1})\) if and only if \(d_n=O(1)\), with comparison constants allowed to depend on that sequence and its finite alphabet bound. By \(\mathrm{prop:causal-optimal-value-corollary}\) and observed-margin surjectivity, the same two equivalences hold for estimating \(V^\star(P)\) over \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\).

### T-block: t8 — For every \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\), consistency an…
**Statement.** For every \(P\in\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\), consistency and armwise conditional exchangeability imply \[ V^\star(P)=\Psi\{\operatorname{Obs}(P)\} =\sum_{x=1}^d p_x\max_{a\in\{0,1\}}\mu_{ax}. \] Thus the observed-law target is the unrestricted optimal-treatment value for every causal completion in \(\mathcal P_{d,\epsilon}\), while the minimax theorems themselves require only the observed law to lie in \(\mathcal D_{d,\epsilon}^{\mathrm{obs}}\).
