Equivalently, \(Q\in\mathfrak M_e(P)\) if and only if \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\) for some \(R\ll P\), or if and only if \(Q\sim P\) and \(dP/dQ\le1/e\) \(Q\)-almost surely. Every admissible \(R\) is realized by a latent construction with \(U=(A,Y(0),Y(1))\).

### T-block: t2 — Fix \(0<e<1\) and \(c=1/e\)
**Statement.** Fix \(0<e<1\) and \(c=1/e\). For every finite continuous convex \(f:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R\) with \(f(1)=0\), the following are equivalent: \(\mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)=\mathfrak L_e(P)\) for every probability law \(P\) on every standard Borel space; this equality holds for every full-support binary \(P\); and \(f\in\mathfrak H_c\). Thus, modulo the divergence-invariant affine term \(b(t-1)\), exactness holds if and only if the generator vanishes on \([0,1/e]\) and is strictly positive on \((1/e,\infty)\).

### T-block: t3 — Fix \(0<e<1\), let \(c=1/e\), and suppose…
**Statement.** Fix \(0<e<1\), let \(c=1/e\), and suppose \(f\notin\mathfrak H_c\). There is a nonempty Euclidean-open set \(\mathcal O_f\subset(0,1)^2\) of binary pairs \((p,q)\), interpreted as \(P=\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p)\) and \(Q=\operatorname{Bernoulli}(q)\), such that \(Q\in\mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)\setminus\mathfrak L_e(P)\) throughout \(\mathcal O_f\). More precisely, either no affine representative of \(f\) is affine on \([0,c]\), in which case \(\mathcal O_f\) can be chosen with \(D_f(P\|Q)<B_f(e)\), or an affine representative vanishes on \([0,c]\) and on \([0,d]\) for some \(d>c\), in which case an open illegal set is contained in the zero-divergence face. This dichotomy includes nondifferentiable and piecewise-linear generators.

### T-block: t4 — Let \(\mathcal Y=\mathbb R\)
**Statement.** Let \(\mathcal Y=\mathbb R\). For every threshold \(y\), write \(p=F_P(y)\). The exact bow-SCM identified interval is \(\{F_Q(y):Q\in\mathfrak M_e(P)\}=[L_e(p),U_e(p)]\). Each endpoint is attained by a bow-ADMG SCM satisfying all maintained support conditions, including when \(P\) has an atom at \(y\), \(p=0\), or \(p=1\). For \(0<p<1\), the witnesses choose residual laws supported on \(({-}\infty,y]\) or its complement; the boundary formulas force the unique value \(0\) or \(1\).

### T-block: t5 — For every admissible generator \(f\),…
**Statement.** For every admissible generator \(f\), \(\mathfrak B^{\mathrm{law}}_{f,e}(P)=\mathfrak L_e(P)=\mathfrak M_e(P)\). For each \(h\in L^\infty(P)\), the exact mutual-AC support-function endpoints are \(\underline\theta_h=eE_P[h]+(1-e)\operatorname*{ess\,inf}_P h\) and \(\overline\theta_h=eE_P[h]+(1-e)\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_P h\). On a finite alphabet restricted to \(\operatorname{supp}(P)\), adding the cap constraints \(q_j\ge ep_j\) to a divergence program yields the exact mutual-AC class, weakly tightens every linear bounded-query optimization, and strictly tightens it whenever a unique relaxed optimizer violates the cap. For every \(f\notin\mathfrak H_c\), a bounded binary query has a strict legality gap on a nonempty open region of observational laws.

### T-block: t6 — Under independent observational sampling, the band…
**Statement.** Under independent observational sampling, the band \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\alpha}\) satisfies \(\Pr\{(L_e(F_P(y)),U_e(F_P(y)))\in\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\alpha}(y)\text{ for every }y\in\mathbb R\}\ge1-\alpha\) for every \(n\), including \(n_a=0\), arbitrary atoms, and contact with \(F_P(y)\in\{0,1\}\). The guarantee follows by combining a propensity Hoeffding interval with a conditional Dvoretzky--Kiefer--Wolfowitz band and propagating their joint rectangle through the full piecewise endpoint map.

### T-block: t12 — Let \(e_1,e_2\in(0,1)\) with \(e_1\ne e_2\)
**Statement.** Let \(e_1,e_2\in(0,1)\) with \(e_1\ne e_2\). ### T-block: t18 — Fix \(0<e<1\) and \(0<\alpha<1/2\)
**Statement.** Fix \(0<e<1\) and \(0<\alpha<1/2\). Consider any sequence of rectangular simultaneous bands that has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) uniformly over a local class. Suppose the class contains a boundary observed-data law with \(F_P(y_0)=1\) and a sequence of alternatives with the same arm propensity \(e\) such that \(F_{P,n}(y_0)=1-\delta_n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), where \(\delta_n>0\), \(n\delta_n\to0\), and the total-variation distance between the corresponding \(n\)-sample observed-data laws is \(o(1)\). Under the boundary law, with probability at least \(1-2\alpha-o(1)\), the lower-endpoint projection of the band at \(y_0\) has diameter at least \(1-e\). The analogous assertion holds for the upper-endpoint projection when the class contains a boundary law with \(F_P(y_0)=0\) and alternatives with the same propensity such that \(F_{P,n}(y_0)=\delta_n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), under the same \(n\)-sample total-variation condition. Such alternative sequences, with the displayed CDF identities holding for all sufficiently large \(n\), exist within the bow-SCM class whenever \(\delta_n>0\) and \(n\delta_n\to0\). Consequently, \(\mathsf H_{\mathrm{dir}}\) cannot yield a nonconservative shrinking band uniformly over such a total-variation-local tie-inclusive class; derivative-aware inference is restricted to strata separated from these contacts, with \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\alpha}\) retained as the honest fallback. The extension of \(L_e,U_e\) to \(e=0\) is algebraic and used only by the clipped finite-sample construction; this impossibility result assumes \(0<e<1\).
