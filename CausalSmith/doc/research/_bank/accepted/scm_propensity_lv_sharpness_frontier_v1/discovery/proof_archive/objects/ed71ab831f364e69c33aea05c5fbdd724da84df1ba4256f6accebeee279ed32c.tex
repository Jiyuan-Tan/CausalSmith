Let \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\) be standard Borel, let \(\mu_X\) be a probability law, let \(e_a:\mathcal X\to[\kappa,1-\kappa]\) be measurable with \(0<\kappa\le1/2\) and \(e_0+e_1=1\), and let \(P_a\) be Markov kernels.  Then
\[
\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
=\mathfrak L^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
=\left\{\boldsymbol Q:Q_a(x,\cdot)\ge e_a(x)P_a(x,\cdot)\ \text{for both }a,\ \mu_X\text{-almost everywhere}\right\}.
\]
Equivalently, membership is characterized by \(P_a(x,\cdot)\ll Q_a(x,\cdot)\) and \(dP_a(x,\cdot)/dQ_a(x,\cdot)\le1/e_a(x)\), \(Q_a(x,\cdot)\)-almost surely, for both arms and \(\mu_X\)-almost every \(x\).  Every admissible pair of residual kernels is realized for both arms and all nonexceptional \(x\) by one global SCM with \(U=(X,A,Y(0),Y(1))\); all exceptional strata are filled with one fixed probability law.
