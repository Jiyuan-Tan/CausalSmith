By \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}sided\text{-}bow\text{-}mixture\text{-}completeness}\), every unrestricted compatible law has a representation \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\) with arbitrary \(R\), and every such representation is realized.  Consistency identifies \(P=\mathcal L(Y(a)\mid A=a)\).  The maintained mutual-absolute-continuity assumption is therefore exactly the additional condition \(Q\ll P\), since \(P\ll Q\) already follows from \(Q\ge eP\).  For the displayed representation,
\[
 Q\ll P\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad R\ll P:
\]
the forward implication follows from \((1-e)R\le Q\) and \(1-e>0\), and the reverse implication follows by summing two measures absolutely continuous with respect to \(P\).  Hence
\[
 \mathfrak M_e(P)=\{eP+(1-e)R:R\ll P\}=\mathfrak L_e(P).
\]
The one-sided cap characterization in the cited theorem, intersected with \(Q\ll P\), gives equivalently \(Q\sim P\) and \(dP/dQ\le1/e\), \(Q\)-almost surely.  The cited latent construction has \(U=(A,Y(0),Y(1))\); the preceding equivalence shows that it satisfies the mutual support assumption exactly for \(R\ll P\).  This proves the theorem as stated.
