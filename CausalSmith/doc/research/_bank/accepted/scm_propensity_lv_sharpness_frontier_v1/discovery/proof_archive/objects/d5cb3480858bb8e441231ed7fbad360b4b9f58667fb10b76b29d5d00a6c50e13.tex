Fix \(0<e<1\) and \(0<\alpha<1/2\).  Consider any sequence of rectangular simultaneous bands that has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) uniformly over a local class containing a law with \(F_P(y_0)=1\) and alternatives with \(F_{P,n}(y_0)=1-\delta_n\), where \(\delta_n>0\) and \(n\delta_n\to0\).  Under the boundary law, with probability at least \(1-2\alpha-o(1)\), the lower-endpoint projection of the band at \(y_0\) has diameter at least \(1-e-o(1)\).  The analogous assertion holds for the upper-endpoint projection when the class contains a law with \(F_P(y_0)=0\) and alternatives with \(F_{P,n}(y_0)=\delta_n\).  Consequently, \(\mathsf H_{\mathrm{dir}}\) cannot yield a nonconservative shrinking band uniformly over such a tie-inclusive local class; derivative-aware inference is restricted to strata separated from these contacts, with \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\alpha}\) retained as the honest fallback.
