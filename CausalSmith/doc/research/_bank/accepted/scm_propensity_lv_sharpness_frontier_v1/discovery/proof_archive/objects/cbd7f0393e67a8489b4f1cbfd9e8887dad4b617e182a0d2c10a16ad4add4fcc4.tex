The class \(\mathfrak M_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\) consists of those \(\boldsymbol Q\in\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\) that admit a witnessing global bow SCM satisfying \(Q_a(x,\cdot)\ll P_a(x,\cdot)\) for both arms and \(\mu_X\)-almost every \(x\).  Within the one-sided class, \(P_a(x,\cdot)\ll Q_a(x,\cdot)\) is automatic, so this is exactly conditional mutual absolute continuity.
