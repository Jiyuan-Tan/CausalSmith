Let \(C_n^L(y_0)\) and \(C_n^U(y_0)\) denote the lower- and upper-endpoint coordinate projections of the rectangular band at \(y_0\).  Write \(\mathbb P_0^{(n)}\) for the boundary observed-data experiment and \(\mathbb P_n^{(n)}\) for the stated alternative.  Set
\[
 t_n=\|\mathbb P_0^{(n)}-\mathbb P_n^{(n)}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}=o(1).
\]
By the definition of total variation, every measurable event \(E\) satisfies
\[
 \mathbb P_0^{(n)}(E)\ge\mathbb P_n^{(n)}(E)-t_n. \tag{34}
\]
Uniform asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) supplies a deterministic \(r_n\downarrow0\) such that coverage under every law in the local class is at least \(1-\alpha-r_n\).

Consider first the lower endpoint.  At the boundary \(F_P(y_0)=1\), the mutual-AC endpoint map gives \(L_e(1)=1\).  At the alternative, \(F_{P,n}(y_0)=1-\delta_n<1\) eventually, because \(\delta_n>0\) and \(n\delta_n\to0\), and hence
\[
 L_e(1-\delta_n)=e(1-\delta_n). \tag{35}
\]
These are causal endpoints by \(\mathrm{thm:bow\text{-}mixture\text{-}completeness}\): if \(B=(-\infty,y_0]\), then every mutual-AC compatible law obeys
\[
 Q(B)=eP(B)+(1-e)R(B),\qquad R\ll P. \tag{36}
\]
When \(P(B)=1\), absolute continuity forces \(R(B)=1\).  When \(P_n(B)=1-\delta_n\in(0,1)\), the residual laws \(P_n(\,\cdot\mid B^c)\) and \(P_n(\,\cdot\mid B)\) attain zero and one, so (35) is the exact lower endpoint.

Define
\[
 E_{0,n}=\{1\in C_n^L(y_0)\},
 \qquad
 E_{1,n}=\{e(1-\delta_n)\in C_n^L(y_0)\}.
\]
Uniform simultaneous coverage implies
\[
 \mathbb P_0^{(n)}(E_{0,n})\ge1-\alpha-r_n,
 \qquad
 \mathbb P_n^{(n)}(E_{1,n})\ge1-\alpha-r_n. \tag{37}
\]
Using (34) on \(E_{1,n}\) and then the union bound under \(\mathbb P_0^{(n)}\),
\[
 \mathbb P_0^{(n)}(E_{0,n}\cap E_{1,n})
 \ge1-2\alpha-2r_n-t_n=1-2\alpha-o(1). \tag{38}
\]
On this intersection the interval \(C_n^L(y_0)\) contains both values, so rectangularity gives
\[
 \operatorname{diam}C_n^L(y_0)
 \ge1-e(1-\delta_n)=1-e+e\delta_n\ge1-e. \tag{39}
\]

For the upper endpoint, the boundary value \(F_P(y_0)=0\) gives \(U_e(0)=0\), while an alternative value \(F_{P,n}(y_0)=\delta_n>0\) gives
\[
 U_e(\delta_n)=1-e+e\delta_n. \tag{40}
\]
Equation (36) verifies exactness: at the boundary \(R(B)=0\), and under the alternative conditioning \(P_n\) on \(B\) or \(B^c\) attains both residual extremes.  Repeating (37)--(39) for \(C_n^U(y_0)\) yields
\[
 \mathbb P_0^{(n)}\left\{\operatorname{diam}C_n^U(y_0)\ge1-e+e\delta_n\right\}
 \ge1-2\alpha-o(1). \tag{41}
\]

It remains to verify that the assumed alternatives exist inside the bow-SCM class.  For the lower face, choose a boundary arm law \(P_0\) supported on \(({-}\infty,y_0]\), choose a probability law \(S\) supported on \((y_0,\infty)\), and put
\[
 P_n=(1-\delta_n)P_0+\delta_nS.
\]
Then \(F_{P_n}(y_0)=1-\delta_n\) and \(\|P_n-P_0\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\le\delta_n\).  Keep the propensity equal to \(e\) and the other-arm conditional law fixed.  The single-observation laws satisfy
\[
 \|\mathbb P_n-\mathbb P_0\|_{\mathrm{TV}}\le e\delta_n.
\]
For any probability laws \(\mu,\nu\), expanding the difference of product measures one coordinate at a time and applying the triangle inequality gives
\[
 \|\mu^{\otimes n}-\nu^{\otimes n}\|_{\mathrm{TV}}
 \le n\|\mu-\nu\|_{\mathrm{TV}}.
\]
Consequently \(t_n\le ne\delta_n=o(1)\).  Realize each law by drawing \(A\) with probability \(e\), drawing \(Y(a)\) independently of \(A\) with law \(P_n\), drawing the other potential outcome from the fixed other-arm law, and setting \(Y=Y(A)\).  Then \(Q=P_n=eP_n+(1-e)P_n\), so the residual law is \(P_n\ll P_n\); consistency, positivity, the bow structure, and mutual absolute continuity all hold.  Interchanging the two half-lines gives the upper-face sequence.

Since \(\alpha<1/2\) and \(1-e>0\), (38)--(41) exclude convergence of the relevant projection diameter to zero under the boundary law.  Every uniformly honest rectangular construction based on \(\mathsf H_{\mathrm{dir}}\) is subject to this indistinguishability argument, so derivative-aware shrinking bands can be justified only on strata separated from the support contacts.  The finite-sample rectangle \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\alpha}\) remains honest there and at the contacts by \(\mathrm{thm:finite\text{-}sample\text{-}simultaneous\text{-}band}\).  The proof uses \(0<e<1\); the value \(e=0\) appears only in the algebraically clipped finite-sample construction.
