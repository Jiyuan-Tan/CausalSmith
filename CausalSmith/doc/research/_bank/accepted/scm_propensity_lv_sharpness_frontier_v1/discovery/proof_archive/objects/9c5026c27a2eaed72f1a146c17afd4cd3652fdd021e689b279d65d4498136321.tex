Under the same standard-Borel and overlap setup, \(\mathfrak L^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\) is the set of pairs \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\) of Markov kernels for which there are Markov kernels \(R_a:\mathcal X\rightsquigarrow\mathcal Y\) satisfying
\[
 Q_a(x,\cdot)=e_a(x)P_a(x,\cdot)+(1-e_a(x))R_a(x,\cdot),\qquad a\in\{0,1\},
\]
for \(\mu_X\)-almost every \(x\).
