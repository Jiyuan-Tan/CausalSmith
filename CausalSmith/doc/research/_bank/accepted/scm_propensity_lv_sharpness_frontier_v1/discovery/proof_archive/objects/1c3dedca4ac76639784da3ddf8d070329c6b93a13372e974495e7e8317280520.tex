Let \(Q\in\mathfrak L_e(P)\).  The likelihood-ratio characterization gives \(Q\sim P\) and \(r=dP/dQ\le c=1/e\).  Convexity of \(f\) on \([0,c]\) and \(E_Qr=1\) give the chord bound
\[
 D_f(P\|Q)
 \le \left(1-\frac1c\right)f(0)+\frac1c f(c)
 =(1-e)f(0)+ef(1/e)=B_f(e). \tag{10}
\]
Thus \(\mathfrak L_e(P)\subseteq\mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)\) for every finite continuous convex admissible generator.  By the definition of the lawful correction and \(\mathrm{thm:bow\text{-}mixture\text{-}completeness}\),
\[
 \mathfrak B^{\mathrm{law}}_{f,e}(P)
 =\mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)\cap\mathfrak L_e(P)
 =\mathfrak L_e(P)=\mathfrak M_e(P). \tag{11}
\]

For \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\), linearity gives
\[
 E_Q[h]=eE_P[h]+(1-e)E_R[h]. \tag{12}
\]
Let \(m=\operatorname*{ess\,inf}_P h\) and \(M=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_P h\), which are finite because \(h\in L^\infty(P)\).  Every \(R\ll P\) satisfies \(m\le E_Rh\le M\).  Conversely, for every \(\varepsilon>0\), the sets \(\{h<m+\varepsilon\}\) and \(\{h>M-\varepsilon\}\) have positive \(P\)-probability by the definitions of essential infimum and supremum.  Normalizing \(P\) on these sets produces admissible residual laws whose expectations are at most \(m+\varepsilon\) and at least \(M-\varepsilon\), respectively.  Taking \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) in (12) proves
\[
 \underline\theta_h=eE_P[h]+(1-e)m,\qquad
 \overline\theta_h=eE_P[h]+(1-e)M. \tag{13}
\]

For the software claim, an empirical \(\texttt{itbound}\) program has finitely many probability-weight variables \((q_j)_j\).  Adding \(dP/dQ\le1/e\) is exactly the collection of closed linear inequalities \(q_j\ge ep_j\); by (11) it replaces the relaxed feasible set by the lawful one.  Set inclusion makes every lower-bound minimization weakly increase and every upper-bound maximization weakly decrease.  The lawful polytope is nonempty and compact, so a linear bounded-query objective attains its lawful optimum.  If the relaxed optimum is attained uniquely at a point violating the cap and the corrected optimum had the same value, the lawful attaining point would be a second relaxed optimizer, contradicting uniqueness.  Hence the correction is strict under the stated uniqueness condition.

Finally take the nonempty open binary illegal set from \(\mathrm{thm:open\text{-}illegal\text{-}dichotomy}\).  Illegality of a binary pair is exactly
\[
 q<ep\quad\text{or}\quad q>ep+1-e.
\]
At least one of these two open alternatives contains a nonempty open subset of \(\mathcal O_f\).  On that subset, for the bounded query equal to the success indicator, the relaxed ball contains the displayed \(q\), whereas (13) gives the lawful endpoint \(ep\) or \(ep+1-e\).  The corresponding relaxed lower or upper endpoint is therefore strictly outside the lawful endpoint.  Projection of this open subset onto the \(p\)-coordinate is nonempty and open, proving the asserted open region of observational laws.
