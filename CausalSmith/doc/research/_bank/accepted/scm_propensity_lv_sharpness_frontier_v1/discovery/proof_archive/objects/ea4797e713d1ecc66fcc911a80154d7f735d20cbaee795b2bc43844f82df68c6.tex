By \(\mathrm{ass{:}positivity}\), \(0<e<1\).  Since \(p\in(0,1)\), the Bernoulli law \(P=\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p)\) has full support on \(\{0,1\}\), and \(q_0=ep\) belongs to \((0,1)\).  The convention \(0\log 0=0\) gives \(f_{\mathrm{KL}}(0)=0\), whereas
\[
 f_{\mathrm{KL}}(1/e)=\frac1e\log(1/e).
\]
Consequently, by the definition of \(B_f(e)\),
\[
 B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(e)
 =e f_{\mathrm{KL}}(1/e)+(1-e)f_{\mathrm{KL}}(0)
 =\log(1/e)=-\log e.
\]

Let \(Q_0=\operatorname{Bernoulli}(q_0)\).  Both \(P\) and \(Q_0\) have full support, so \(P\sim Q_0\), in particular \(P\ll Q_0\), as required in the definition of \(D_f(P\|Q)\).  On the atoms \(1\) and \(0\), respectively, the likelihood ratio \(dP/dQ_0\) is \(p/q_0\) and \((1-p)/(1-q_0)\).  Therefore that definition and \(q_0=ep\) yield
\[
\begin{aligned}
 D_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(P\|Q_0)
 &=q_0\frac{p}{q_0}\log\!\left(\frac{p}{q_0}\right)
   +(1-q_0)\frac{1-p}{1-q_0}
       \log\!\left(\frac{1-p}{1-q_0}\right)\\
 &=p\log(1/e)+(1-p)\log\!\left(\frac{1-p}{1-ep}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
The gap from the radius can be computed without an inequality relaxation:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(e)-D_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(P\|Q_0)
 &=(1-p)\left\{\log(1/e)-
       \log\!\left(\frac{1-p}{1-ep}\right)\right\}\\
 &=(1-p)\log\!\left(\frac{1-ep}{e(1-p)}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(e(1-p)>0\), and
\[
 (1-ep)-e(1-p)=1-e>0.
\]
Thus \((1-ep)/(e(1-p))>1\); strict monotonicity of the logarithm and \(1-p>0\) show that the displayed gap is strictly positive.  This proves the asserted strict inequality at \(q_0\).

For \(q\in(0,1)\), put
\[
 K_p(q)=p\log\!\left(\frac p q\right)
       +(1-p)\log\!\left(\frac{1-p}{1-q}\right).
\]
The logarithm is continuous on \((0,\infty)\), so \(K_p\) is continuous on \((0,1)\).  If
\[
 s=B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(e)-K_p(q_0)>0,
\]
continuity at \(q_0\) supplies \(\delta>0\) such that \(|q-q_0|<\delta\) and \(q\in(0,1)\) imply \(|K_p(q)-K_p(q_0)|<s/2\).  Hence, for every
\[
 0<\varepsilon<\min\{\delta,ep\},
 \qquad q=ep-\varepsilon,
\]
one has \(q\in(0,1)\) and
\[
 D_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}\!\left(P\middle\|\operatorname{Bernoulli}(q)\right)
 =K_p(q)<K_p(q_0)+\frac{s}{2}<B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(e).
\]
The two Bernoulli laws again have full support and hence are mutually absolutely continuous.  The definition \(\mathrm{def{:}jk\text{-}ball}\) now gives
\[
 \operatorname{Bernoulli}(q)\in\mathfrak B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}},e}(P).
\]

It remains to prove incompatibility with the mixture class rather than merely assert it.  If \(Q\in\mathfrak L_e(P)\), then by \(\mathrm{def{:}mixture\text{-}class}\) there is a probability law \(R\ll P\) such that \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\).  Evaluating this equality on the success atom \(\{1\}\) gives
\[
 Q(\{1\})=ep+(1-e)R(\{1\})\ge ep,
\]
where \(1-e>0\) follows from \(\mathrm{ass{:}positivity}\) and \(R(\{1\})\ge0\) because \(R\) is a probability law.  Our candidate instead has \(Q(\{1\})=q=ep-\varepsilon<ep\), equivalently
\[
 \frac{p}{q}>\frac1e,
\]
where division is legitimate because \(e,p,q>0\).  Therefore
\[
 \operatorname{Bernoulli}(ep-\varepsilon)
 \in\mathfrak B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}},e}(P)
 \setminus\mathfrak L_e(P).
\]

Finally, the claimed neighborhood statement is also strict.  Fix any \(\varepsilon\) in the preceding range and write \(q=ep-\varepsilon\).  On the open square \(\Omega=(0,1)^2\), define
\[
 \Phi(p',q')=p'\log\!\left(\frac{p'}{q'}\right)
 +(1-p')\log\!\left(\frac{1-p'}{1-q'}\right).
\]
Both maps
\[
 (p',q')\longmapsto B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(e)-\Phi(p',q')
 \quad\text{and}\quad
 (p',q')\longmapsto ep'-q'
\]
are continuous on \(\Omega\), and both are strictly positive at \((p,q)\).  Their positive inverse images have an open intersection \(\mathcal U\subseteq\Omega\) containing \((p,q)\).  For every \((p',q')\in\mathcal U\), the full-support laws \(P'=\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p')\) and \(Q'=\operatorname{Bernoulli}(q')\) satisfy \(D_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(P'\|Q')<B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}}}(e)\) and \(q'<ep'\).  The first strict inequality and \(P'\sim Q'\) put \(Q'\) in \(\mathfrak B_{f_{\mathrm{KL}},e}(P')\); the event calculation above excludes it from \(\mathfrak L_e(P')\).  Thus the illegal certificate persists throughout a binary open neighborhood, as required.
