The stronger \(\mathrm{thm:support\text{-}regime\text{-}open\text{-}illegal\text{-}dichotomy}\) constructs a nonempty open set of full-support binary pairs that is illegal in both support regimes.  Its mutual-absolute-continuity component is exactly
\[
 Q\in\mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)\setminus\mathfrak L_e(P).
\]
The same theorem gives the strict-interior alternative when \(f\) is nonaffine on \([0,c]\), the zero-divergence-face alternative when the normalized zero interval extends beyond \(c\), and covers finite continuous nondifferentiable and piecewise-linear generators.  These are precisely the assertions of the present theorem.
