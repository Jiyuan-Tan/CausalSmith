Let \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\) be standard Borel spaces, \(\mu_X\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\), \(0<\kappa\le1/2\), let measurable \(\boldsymbol e=(e_0,e_1)\) satisfy \(e_a(x)\in[\kappa,1-\kappa]\) and \(e_0(x)+e_1(x)=1\), and let \(\boldsymbol P=(P_0,P_1)\) and \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\) denote pairs of Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\).  Define the jointly indexed adaptive hinge \(f_{a,x}(t)=(t-1/e_a(x))_+\).  The map \((a,x,t)\mapsto f_{a,x}(t)\) is Borel measurable, each fiber is finite, continuous, and convex with \(f_{a,x}(1)=0\), and \(B_{f_{a,x}}(e_a(x))=0\).  The fiberwise, nonintegrated adaptive divergence classes are exactly the globally realizable conditional causal classes:
\[
 \mathfrak B^{\mathrm{ad},\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
 =\mathfrak L^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
 =\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P),
\]
and, after imposing \(Q_a(x,\cdot)\ll P_a(x,\cdot)\) for both arms on the same common \(\mu_X\)-conull set,
\[
 \mathfrak B^{\mathrm{ad}}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
 =\mathfrak L_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
 =\mathfrak M_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P).
\]
These equalities hold on one common measurable \(\mu_X\)-conull set and make no claim about a prescribed null-stratum version.
