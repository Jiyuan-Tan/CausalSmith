Fix \(0<e<1\) and \(0<\alpha<1/2\). Consider any sequence of rectangular simultaneous bands that has asymptotic coverage at least \(1-\alpha\) uniformly over a local class. Suppose the class contains a boundary observed-data law with \(F_P(y_0)=1\) and a sequence of alternatives with the same arm propensity \(e\) such that \(F_{P,n}(y_0)=1-\delta_n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), where \(\delta_n>0\), \(n\delta_n\to0\), and the total-variation distance between the corresponding \(n\)-sample observed-data laws is \(o(1)\). Under the boundary law, with probability at least \(1-2\alpha-o(1)\), the lower-endpoint projection of the band at \(y_0\) has diameter at least \(1-e\). The analogous assertion holds for the upper-endpoint projection when the class contains a boundary law with \(F_P(y_0)=0\) and alternatives with the same propensity such that \(F_{P,n}(y_0)=\delta_n\) for all sufficiently large \(n\), under the same \(n\)-sample total-variation condition. Such alternative sequences, with the displayed CDF identities holding for all sufficiently large \(n\), exist within the bow-SCM class whenever \(\delta_n>0\) and \(n\delta_n\to0\). Consequently, \(\mathsf H_{\mathrm{dir}}\) cannot yield a nonconservative shrinking band uniformly over such a total-variation-local tie-inclusive class; derivative-aware inference is restricted to strata separated from these contacts, with \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\alpha}\) retained as the honest fallback. The extension of \(L_e,U_e\) to \(e=0\) is algebraic and used only by the clipped finite-sample construction; this impossibility result assumes \(0<e<1\).
