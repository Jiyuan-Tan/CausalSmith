Let \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\) be standard Borel spaces, \(\mu_X\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\), \(0<\kappa\le1/2\), let measurable \(\boldsymbol e=(e_0,e_1)\) satisfy \(e_a(x)\in[\kappa,1-\kappa]\) and \(e_0(x)+e_1(x)=1\) for every \(x\), and let \(\boldsymbol P=(P_0,P_1)\) be Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\).  Candidate pairs \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\) and residual pairs \(\boldsymbol R=(R_0,R_1)\) below are also Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\).  Then
\[
 \mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
 =\mathfrak L^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
 =\{\boldsymbol Q:Q_a(x,\cdot)\ge e_a(x)P_a(x,\cdot)\text{ for }a=0,1\text{ on one measurable }\mu_X\text{-conull set}\}.
\]
Equivalently, on that common conull set, \(P_a(x,\cdot)\ll Q_a(x,\cdot)\) and \(dP_a(x,\cdot)/dQ_a(x,\cdot)\le1/e_a(x)\), \(Q_a(x,\cdot)\)-almost surely, for both arms.  Every admissible pair of residual Markov kernels is realized jointly by one global SCM compatible with the conditional latent architecture.  The scalar theorem \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}sided\text{-}bow\text{-}mixture\text{-}completeness}\) is the corresponding abstract fixed-fiber statement, not evaluation of kernel versions at a prescribed \(\mu_X\)-null covariate value.
