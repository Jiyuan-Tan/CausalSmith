Let \(E_e=\{e\in I_e\}\).  Apply \(\mathrm{lem:hoeffding\text{-}bounded\text{-}mean}\) to \(Z_i=\mathbf 1\{A_i=a\}\).  With \(\delta_e=\sqrt{\log(4/\alpha)/(2n)}\),
\[
 \Pr(E_e^c)\le2e^{-2n\delta_e^2}=\alpha/2. \tag{14}
\]
Conditional on the complete treatment vector and on \(n_a>0\), independence of the sampled pairs implies that the \(n_a\) selected outcomes are independent with common conditional distribution \(P=\mathcal L(Y\mid A=a)\).  Therefore \(\mathrm{lem:dkw\text{-}massart\text{-}cdf\text{-}band}\), applied conditionally with sample size \(n_a\), gives
\[
 \Pr\left\{\left.\sup_y|\widehat F_{P,n}(y)-F_P(y)|>\delta_F\ \right|\ (A_i)_{i=1}^n\right\}
 \le2e^{-2n_a\delta_F^2}=\alpha/2. \tag{15}
\]
Let \(E_F\) denote the complementary simultaneous-CDF event when \(n_a>0\), and take \(E_F\) to hold automatically when \(n_a=0\).  Averaging (15) over the treatment vector yields \(\Pr(E_F^c)\le\alpha/2\).  The union bound with (14) gives
\[
 \Pr(E_e\cap E_F)\ge1-\alpha. \tag{16}
\]

On \(E_e\cap E_F\) and \(n_a>0\), the actual pair \((e,F_P(y))\) belongs to \(I_e\times I_F(y)\) simultaneously for every \(y\).  Hence the image point
\[
 (L_e(F_P(y)),U_e(F_P(y)))
\]
belongs to the set whose coordinatewise rectangular hull defines \(\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\alpha}(y)\).  This argument requires neither continuity nor differentiability of the piecewise endpoint map, so atoms and the values zero and one cause no exception.  If \(n_a=0\), the band is \([0,1]^2\) by definition and contains the endpoint pair automatically.  The corrected endpoint definition at \(e=0\) also makes the image set in the construction well typed whenever the clipped interval \(I_e\) contains zero.  Combining these observations with (16) proves the simultaneous finite-sample guarantee.
