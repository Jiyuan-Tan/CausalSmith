The one-sided frontier \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}sided\text{-}generator\text{-}iff\text{-}frontier}\) proves that \(f\in\mathfrak H_c\) is equivalent to universal one-sided equality and to equality on every full-support binary law.  If \(f\in\mathfrak H_c\), intersect the universal equality
\[
 \mathfrak B^{\to}_{f,e}(P)=\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)
\]
with \(\{Q:Q\ll P\}\).  The support identities in \(\mathrm{prop:mutual\text{-}support\text{-}intersection}\),
\[
 \mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)=\mathfrak B^{\to}_{f,e}(P)\cap\{Q:Q\ll P\},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak L_e(P)=\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)\cap\{Q:Q\ll P\}
\]
then give \(\mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)=\mathfrak L_e(P)\) for every \(P\).  Conversely, suppose the mutual classes are equal for every full-support binary \(P\).  Every full-support binary \(P\) and \(Q\) are mutually absolutely continuous, so the one-sided and mutual classes coincide on this subclass.  The assumed binary mutual equality is therefore the binary one-sided equality, and the cited frontier yields \(f\in\mathfrak H_c\).  Universal mutual equality trivially implies binary mutual equality.  This proves all three equivalences and the stated affine-invariant description.
