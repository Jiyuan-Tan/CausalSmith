For \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\) with \(R\ll P\), the cap characterization gives \(Q\sim P\) and \(dP/dQ\le1/e\).  The convex chord calculation in \(\mathrm{thm:one\text{-}sided\text{-}query\text{-}legality\text{-}correction}\) therefore gives \(Q\in\mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)\), so
\[
 \mathfrak B^{\mathrm{law}}_{f,e}(P)=\mathfrak L_e(P)=\mathfrak M_e(P). \tag{23a}
\]
For \(h\in L^\infty(P)\),
\[
 E_Q[h]=eE_P[h]+(1-e)E_R[h].
\]
If \(m=\operatorname*{ess\,inf}_P h\) and \(M=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_P h\), every \(R\ll P\) has expectation between \(m\) and \(M\).  Conversely, normalizing \(P\) on the positive-probability sets \(\{h<m+\varepsilon\}\) and \(\{h>M-\varepsilon\}\) gives admissible residual laws approaching the two values.  Letting \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\) yields
\[
 \underline\theta_h=eE_P[h]+(1-e)m,
 \qquad
 \overline\theta_h=eE_P[h]+(1-e)M. \tag{23b}
\]
On a finite alphabet restricted to \(\operatorname{supp}(P)\), the cap inequalities \(q_j\ge ep_j\) are equivalent to \(q_j=ep_j+(1-e)r_j\) with \(R\ll P\), and hence give exactly (23a).  Adding them weakly tightens every linear optimization.  If the relaxed optimizer is unique and violates a cap inequality, equality of the corrected and relaxed optimum would produce a distinct cap-feasible relaxed optimizer with the same value, a contradiction.

Finally, \(\mathrm{thm:open\text{-}illegal\text{-}dichotomy}\) supplies a full-support binary open set when \(f\notin\mathfrak H_c\).  On a Bernoulli alphabet illegality is \(q<ep\) or \(q>ep+1-e\).  One alternative holds on a nonempty open subset, and the success-indicator query then has a relaxed lower endpoint below \(ep\) or a relaxed upper endpoint above \(ep+1-e\).  Projection onto the observational coordinate \(p\) is open and nonempty.  This proves the mutual-AC query gap and all assertions of the proposed statement.
