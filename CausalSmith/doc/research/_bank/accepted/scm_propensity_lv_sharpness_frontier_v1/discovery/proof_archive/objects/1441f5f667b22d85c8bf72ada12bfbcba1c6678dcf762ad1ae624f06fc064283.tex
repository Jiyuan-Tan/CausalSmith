For every integer \(m\ge1\), if \(X_1,\ldots,X_m\) are independent real random variables with common distribution function \(F\), and \(\widehat F_m\) is their empirical distribution function, then for every \(t>0\),
\[
 \Pr\left(\sup_{y\in\mathbb R}|\widehat F_m(y)-F(y)|>t\right)\le2\exp(-2mt^2).
\]
