Let \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\) be standard Borel spaces, \(\mu_X\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\), \(0<\kappa\le1/2\), let measurable \(\boldsymbol e=(e_0,e_1)\) satisfy \(e_a(x)\in[\kappa,1-\kappa]\) and \(e_0(x)+e_1(x)=1\), and let \(\boldsymbol P=(P_0,P_1)\), \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\), and \(\boldsymbol R=(R_0,R_1)\) denote pairs of Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\).  In the reverse-support regime, which requires \(Q_a(x,\cdot)\ll P_a(x,\cdot)\) for both arms on one measurable \(\mu_X\)-conull set,
\[
 \mathfrak M_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)=\mathfrak L_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P).
\]
Equivalently, membership holds precisely when, for both arms on one measurable \(\mu_X\)-conull set, \(P_a(x,\cdot)\sim Q_a(x,\cdot)\) and
\[
 \frac{dP_a(x,\cdot)}{dQ_a(x,\cdot)}\le\frac1{e_a(x)}\qquad Q_a(x,\cdot)\text{-almost surely}.
\]
Every such pair is realized jointly by the single product-kernel SCM in \(\mathrm{thm:conditional\text{-}one\text{-}sided\text{-}bow\text{-}mixture\text{-}completeness}\), with residual kernels satisfying \(R_a(x,\cdot)\ll P_a(x,\cdot)\).  The scalar theorem \(\mathrm{thm:bow\text{-}mixture\text{-}completeness}\) is the corresponding abstract fixed-fiber statement and does not identify a prescribed null-stratum version.
