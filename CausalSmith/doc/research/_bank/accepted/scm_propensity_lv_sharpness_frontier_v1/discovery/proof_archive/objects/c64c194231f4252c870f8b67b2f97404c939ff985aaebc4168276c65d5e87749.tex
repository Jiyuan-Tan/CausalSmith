Let \(E_e=\{e\in I_e\}\).  Apply \(\mathrm{lem:hoeffding\text{-}bounded\text{-}mean}\) to \(Z_i=\mathbf 1\{A_i=a\}\).  With \(\delta_e=\sqrt{\log(4/\alpha)/(2n)}\),
\[
 \Pr(E_e^c)\le2\exp(-2n\delta_e^2)=\alpha/2. \tag{30}
\]
Condition on the complete treatment vector.  On \(n_a>0\), independent sampling implies that the selected outcomes \((Y_i:A_i=a)\) are independent and have common conditional distribution \(P=\mathcal L(Y\mid A=a)\).  Therefore \(\mathrm{lem:dkw\text{-}massart\text{-}cdf\text{-}band}\), applied conditionally with sample size \(n_a\), gives
\[
 \Pr\left(\left.\sup_{y\in\mathbb R}
 |\widehat F_{P,n}(y)-F_P(y)|>\delta_F\ \right|\ (A_i)_{i=1}^n\right)
 \le2\exp(-2n_a\delta_F^2)=\alpha/2. \tag{31}
\]
Let \(E_F\) be the complementary simultaneous-CDF event when \(n_a>0\), and declare \(E_F\) to hold when \(n_a=0\).  Averaging (31) over the treatment vector gives \(\Pr(E_F^c)\le\alpha/2\).  The union bound with (30) yields
\[
 \Pr(E_e\cap E_F)\ge1-\alpha. \tag{32}
\]

On \(E_e\cap E_F\) and \(n_a>0\), the true pair \((e,F_P(y))\) belongs to \(I_e\times I_F(y)\) simultaneously for every \(y\).  By the definition of the coordinatewise rectangular hull,
\[
 (L_e(F_P(y)),U_e(F_P(y)))
 \in\widehat{\mathcal C}_{n,\alpha}(y)
 \quad\text{for every }y. \tag{33}
\]
No continuity of the endpoint map is used in this set-inclusion argument, so atoms and contacts at zero or one are covered.  If \(n_a=0\), the band equals \([0,1]^2\) by definition and (33) holds automatically.  The algebraic extension of the endpoint map to \(e=0\) makes the image set well typed when the clipped interval \(I_e\) contains zero; the true SCM still obeys \(0<e<1\).  Combining (32)--(33), and using \(\mathrm{thm:cdf\text{-}endpoints\text{-}sharp}\) to identify these maps as the mutual-AC causal endpoints, proves the finite-sample simultaneous guarantee.
