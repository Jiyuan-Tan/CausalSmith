Measurability of \(e_a\), the lower bound \(e_a\ge\kappa>0\), and continuity of \((u,t)\mapsto(t-1/u)_+\) on \([\kappa,1-\kappa]\times[0,\infty)\) prove joint Borel measurability.  Put \(c_{a,x}=1/e_a(x)>1\).  Then
\[
 f_{a,x}(1)=0,
 \qquad
 B_{f_{a,x}}(e_a(x))
 =e_a(x)(c_{a,x}-c_{a,x})_+
 +(1-e_a(x))(0-c_{a,x})_+=0. \tag{1}
\]
For \(P_a(x,\cdot)\ll Q_a(x,\cdot)\), with density \(r_{a,x}\),
\[
 D_{f_{a,x}}(P_a(x,\cdot)\|Q_a(x,\cdot))
 =\int (r_{a,x}-c_{a,x})_+\,dQ_a(x,\cdot). \tag{2}
\]
The integrand is nonnegative, so (2) is at most the zero radius in (1) if and only if \(r_{a,x}\le c_{a,x}\), \(Q_a(x,\cdot)\)-almost surely.  The conditional one-sided completeness theorem identifies these cap constraints with \(\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\).  Adding reverse support and invoking the conditional mutual completeness theorem gives the mutual class.  Every identity is conditional and almost everywhere; no integration over \(\mu_X\) enters the constraint.

Finally, if two nonnull strata have propensities \(e_1\ne e_2\) and a fixed generator were universally exact at both fibers, \(\mathrm{thm:heterogeneous\text{-}propensity\text{-}elbow}\) would give a contradiction.  Equations (1)--(2), together with the single simultaneous SCM construction in the conditional completeness theorem, prove that the jointly Borel adaptive family avoids this obstruction.
