Let \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\) be standard Borel spaces, \(\mu_X\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\), \(0<\kappa\le1/2\), let measurable \(\boldsymbol e=(e_0,e_1)\) satisfy \(e_a(x)\in[\kappa,1-\kappa]\) and \(e_0(x)+e_1(x)=1\), and let \(\boldsymbol P=(P_0,P_1)\) and \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\) be pairs of Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\).  Let \(\mathcal S_{\mu_X}(\boldsymbol P)\) denote the pairs \(\boldsymbol Q\) for which \(Q_a(x,\cdot)\ll P_a(x,\cdot)\) for both arms on one measurable \(\mu_X\)-conull set.  Then
\[
 \mathfrak M_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)=\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\cap\mathcal S_{\mu_X}(\boldsymbol P),\qquad
 \mathfrak L_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)=\mathfrak L^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\cap\mathcal S_{\mu_X}(\boldsymbol P),
\]
and
\[
 \mathfrak B^{\mathrm{ad}}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)=\mathfrak B^{\mathrm{ad},\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\cap\mathcal S_{\mu_X}(\boldsymbol P).
\]
Forward support \(P_a(x,\cdot)\ll Q_a(x,\cdot)\) is automatic in each one-sided mixture or adaptive-hinge class.
