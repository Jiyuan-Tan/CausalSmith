Let \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\) be standard Borel spaces, let \(\mu_X\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\), let \(0<\kappa\le1/2\), let \(\boldsymbol e=(e_0,e_1)\) satisfy \(\mathrm{ass:conditional\text{-}overlap}\), and let \(\boldsymbol P=(P_0,P_1)\) be Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\).  Then \(\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\) is the set of pairs \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\) of Markov kernels for which one SCM satisfying \(\mathrm{ass:conditional\text{-}bow\text{-}architecture}\), \(\mathrm{ass:conditional\text{-}overlap}\), and \(\mathrm{ass:conditional\text{-}consistency}\) has \(X\sim\mu_X\) and admits one measurable \(\mu_X\)-conull set \(S\) on which, simultaneously for \(a=0,1\), \(\Pr(A=a\mid X=x)=e_a(x)\), \(\mathcal L(Y\mid A=a,X=x)=P_a(x,\cdot)\), and \(\mathcal L(Y(a)\mid X=x)=Q_a(x,\cdot)\).  Conditional kernels are versions and no restriction is placed on \(S^c\).  No reverse-support condition is imposed.
