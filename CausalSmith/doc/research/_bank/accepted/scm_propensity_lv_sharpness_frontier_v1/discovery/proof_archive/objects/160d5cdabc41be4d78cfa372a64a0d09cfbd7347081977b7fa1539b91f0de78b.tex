Under the same self-contained setup of standard Borel \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\), \(\mu_X\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\), \(0<\kappa\le1/2\), measurable \(\boldsymbol e=(e_0,e_1)\) satisfying \(\mathrm{ass:conditional\text{-}overlap}\), and Markov kernels \(\boldsymbol P=(P_0,P_1)\), the class \(\mathfrak L_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\) consists of pairs \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\) for which there are Markov kernels \(\boldsymbol R=(R_0,R_1)\) and one measurable \(\mu_X\)-conull set \(S\) such that \(Q_a=e_aP_a+(1-e_a)R_a\) and \(R_a(x,\cdot)\ll P_a(x,\cdot)\) for both arms and every \(x\in S\).  No restriction is imposed on the versions over \(S^c\).
