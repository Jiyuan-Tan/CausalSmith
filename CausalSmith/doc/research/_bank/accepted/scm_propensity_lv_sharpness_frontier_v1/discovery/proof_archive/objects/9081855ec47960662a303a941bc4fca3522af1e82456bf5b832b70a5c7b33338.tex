Under the conditional standard-Borel and overlap setup,
\[
\mathfrak M_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
=\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
\cap\left\{\boldsymbol Q:Q_a(x,\cdot)\ll P_a(x,\cdot)\ \text{for both }a,\ \mu_X\text{-almost everywhere}\right\},
\]
and the identical reverse-support intersection formula holds with \(\mathfrak L\) in place of \(\mathfrak M\).  Inside either one-sided class, \(P_a(x,\cdot)\ll Q_a(x,\cdot)\) is automatic.  Thus the added reverse-support condition is exactly conditional mutual absolute continuity.
