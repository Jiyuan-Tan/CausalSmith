Let \(\mathcal X,\mathcal Y\) be standard Borel spaces, let \(\mu_X\in\mathcal P(\mathcal X)\), let \(0<\kappa\le1/2\), let measurable \(\boldsymbol e=(e_0,e_1)\) satisfy \(\mathrm{ass:conditional\text{-}overlap}\), and let \(\boldsymbol P=(P_0,P_1)\) be Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\).  With \(f_{a,x}(t)=(t-1/e_a(x))_+\), the class \(\mathfrak B^{\mathrm{ad}}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\) consists of pairs \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\) of Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\) for which there is one measurable \(\mu_X\)-conull set \(S\) such that, for every \(x\in S\) and \(a=0,1\), \(P_a(x,\cdot)\sim Q_a(x,\cdot)\) and
\[
 D_{f_{a,x}}(P_a(x,\cdot)\|Q_a(x,\cdot))\le B_{f_{a,x}}(e_a(x)).
\]
The constraints are fiberwise, not integrated over \(x\), and no assertion is made at a specified point in \(S^c\).
