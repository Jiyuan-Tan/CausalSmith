Fix a standard Borel space \(\mathcal Y\), an arm \(a\), a probability law \(P\), and \(0<e<1\).  If \(Q\in\mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)\), consistency gives
\[
 \mathcal L(Y(a)\mid A=a)=\mathcal L(Y\mid A=a)=P.
\]
Let \(R=\mathcal L(Y(a)\mid A\ne a)\).  The law of total probability and \(\Pr(A=a)=e\) give, for every measurable \(B\subseteq\mathcal Y\),
\[
 Q(B)=eP(B)+(1-e)R(B). \tag{1}
\]
Thus \(Q\in\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)\).

Conversely, suppose \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\) for a probability law \(R\).  Draw \(A\) with \(\Pr(A=a)=e\); conditionally on \(A=a\), draw \(Y(a)\) with law \(P\), and conditionally on \(A\ne a\), draw \(Y(a)\) with law \(R\).  Draw the other potential outcome from any fixed law on \(\mathcal Y\), conditionally independently given \(A\).  With \(U=(A,Y(0),Y(1))\), let the treatment equation return the first coordinate and set \(Y=Y(A)\).  The common latent response type supplies \(A\leftrightarrow Y\), while the explicit treatment argument in the outcome equation supplies \(A\to Y\).  Hence the construction belongs to the nonparametric bow class in \(\mathrm{ass:bow\text{-}structure}\); it satisfies \(\mathrm{ass:consistency}\), has arm probability \(e\), observed arm law \(P\), and, by (1), interventional law \(Q\).  Therefore
\[
 \mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)=\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P). \tag{2}
\]

If (1) holds, then \(Q\ge eP\), hence \(P\ll Q\).  For \(r=dP/dQ\), the measure inequality is equivalent to \(er\le1\), \(Q\)-almost surely, so \(r\le1/e\).  Conversely, if \(P\ll Q\) and \(r\le1/e\), define
\[
 R(B)=\frac{Q(B)-eP(B)}{1-e}.
\]
The numerator has nonnegative \(Q\)-density \(1-er\), and its total mass is \(1-e\).  Thus \(R\) is a probability law and \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\).  Combining this with (2) proves
\[
 \mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)=\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)
 =\left\{Q:P\ll Q,\ \frac{dP}{dQ}\le\frac1e\quad Q\text{-almost surely}\right\}.
\]
