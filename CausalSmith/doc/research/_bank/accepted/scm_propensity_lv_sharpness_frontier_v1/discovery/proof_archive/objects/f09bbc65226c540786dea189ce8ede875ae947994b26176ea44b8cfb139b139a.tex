Let \(\mathcal S_{\mu_X}(\boldsymbol P)\) denote the reverse-support class defined in \(\mathrm{prop:conditional\text{-}mutual\text{-}support\text{-}intersection}\).  Conditional one-sided completeness and that proposition give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathfrak M_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
 &=\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
   \cap\mathcal S_{\mu_X}(\boldsymbol P)\\
 &=\mathfrak L^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)
   \cap\mathcal S_{\mu_X}(\boldsymbol P)\\
 &=\mathfrak L_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P).
 \end{aligned} \tag{64}
\]
The one-sided theorem supplies \(P_a(x,\cdot)\ll Q_a(x,\cdot)\) and
\[
 \frac{dP_a(x,\cdot)}{dQ_a(x,\cdot)}\le\frac1{e_a(x)}
 \quad Q_a(x,\cdot)\text{-almost surely}, \tag{65}
\]
for both arms on a common conull set.  Membership in \(\mathcal S_{\mu_X}(\boldsymbol P)\) supplies the reverse absolute continuity, after taking the finite intersection of conull sets.  Thus (64) implies the stated mutual-support likelihood-ratio characterization.

Conversely, mutual support and (65) make the signed kernel \(Q_a-e_aP_a\) nonnegative.  Since \(1-e_a(x)\ge\kappa>0\), the explicit formula
\[
 R_a(x,B)=\frac{Q_a(x,B)-e_a(x)P_a(x,B)}{1-e_a(x)}
\]
defines the residual Markov kernels on the common conull set, with a fixed fallback law off it, exactly as in the conditional one-sided theorem.  Reverse support \(Q_a\ll P_a\) then implies \(R_a\ll P_a\) because \((1-e_a)R_a\le Q_a\).  The product-kernel SCM constructed in that theorem is one simultaneous two-arm witness, which proves the realization assertion.  The result concerns conditional-law versions on a common \(\mu_X\)-conull set and therefore makes no claim at a prescribed null covariate value.
