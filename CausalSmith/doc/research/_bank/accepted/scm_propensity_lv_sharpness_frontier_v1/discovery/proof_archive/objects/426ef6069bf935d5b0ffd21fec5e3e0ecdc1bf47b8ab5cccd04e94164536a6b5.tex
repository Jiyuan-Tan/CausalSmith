Fix \(0<e<1\), set \(c=1/e\), and let \(f\) be admissible.  If \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\) for an arbitrary probability law \(R\), then \(Q\ge eP\).  Hence \(P\ll Q\), \(r=dP/dQ\le c\), \(Q\)-almost surely, and \(\int r\,dQ=1\).  Convexity on \([0,c]\) gives
\[
 D_f(P\|Q)
 \le\int\left\{\left(1-\frac rc\right)f(0)+\frac rc f(c)\right\}dQ
 =(1-e)f(0)+ef(c)=B_f(e).
\]
Thus \(\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)\subseteq\mathfrak B^{\to}_{f,e}(P)\), and the definition of the one-sided lawful correction gives
\[
 \mathfrak B^{\mathrm{law},\to}_{f,e}(P)
 =\mathfrak B^{\to}_{f,e}(P)\cap\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)
 =\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P).
\]
The one-sided bow-mixture completeness theorem identifies the last class with \(\mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)\).

On a finite alphabet, including cells with \(p_j=0\), a probability vector \(q=(q_j)_j\) belongs to \(\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)\) exactly when
\[
 q_j\ge ep_j\qquad\text{for every }j. \tag{19}
\]
One direction follows from \(q_j=ep_j+(1-e)r_j\).  Conversely, (19) makes \(r_j=(q_j-ep_j)/(1-e)\) a nonnegative probability vector and yields \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\).  It also ensures \(P\ll Q\).  The preceding divergence inequality shows that the divergence constraint is redundant after (19) is imposed.  Hence the capped program has exactly the unrestricted lawful class.  The capped set is compact; restricting to it weakly tightens every linear maximum or minimum.  If an attained relaxed optimizer is unique and violates (19), equality of optimal values would make a capped optimizer a distinct relaxed optimizer, which is impossible.  Thus the tightening is strict in the stated case.

For \(f\notin\mathfrak H_c\), the construction in \(\mathrm{thm:support\text{-}regime\text{-}open\text{-}illegal\text{-}dichotomy}\) supplies a nonempty open set of full-support binary pairs with \(q<ep\) that are feasible for the one-sided divergence ball.  After shrinking to a rectangle, its projection contains a nonempty open interval of observational probabilities \(p\), and for each such \(p\) some feasible \(q<ep\) remains.  For the success indicator, the lawful lower value over (19) is \(ep\), while the relaxed lower value is at most \(q<ep\).  This proves the strict one-sided legality gap.
