Let \(\mathcal X\) and \(\mathcal Y\) be standard Borel spaces, let \(\mu_X\) be a probability law on \(\mathcal X\), let \(e_a:\mathcal X\to[\kappa,1-\kappa]\) be measurable for \(a\in\{0,1\}\), where \(0<\kappa\le1/2\) and \(e_0(x)+e_1(x)=1\), and let \(P_a\) be Markov kernels from \(\mathcal X\) to \(\mathcal Y\).  The class \(\mathfrak M^{\to}_{\mu_X,\boldsymbol e}(\boldsymbol P)\) consists of pairs \(\boldsymbol Q=(Q_0,Q_1)\) of Markov kernels for which one nonparametric latent-variable SCM on the covariate-augmented bow graph \(X\to A\), \(X\to Y\), \(A\to Y\), \(A\leftrightarrow Y\) satisfies \(X\sim\mu_X\), \(\Pr(A=a\mid X=x)=e_a(x)\), \(\mathcal L(Y\mid A=a,X=x)=P_a(x,\cdot)\), \(\mathcal L(Y(a)\mid X=x)=Q_a(x,\cdot)\) for both arms \(\mu_X\)-almost everywhere, and \(Y=Y(A)\) almost surely.  No reverse absolute-continuity restriction is imposed.
