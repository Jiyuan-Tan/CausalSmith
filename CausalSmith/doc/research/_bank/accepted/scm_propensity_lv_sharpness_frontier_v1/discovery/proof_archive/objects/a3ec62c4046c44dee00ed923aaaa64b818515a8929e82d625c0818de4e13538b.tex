For divergence balls, the definition of mutual absolute continuity gives directly
\[
 \mathfrak B_{f,e}(P)
 =\mathfrak B^{\to}_{f,e}(P)\cap\{Q:Q\ll P\}. \tag{1}
\]
For a mixture \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\), positivity gives
\[
 Q\ll P\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad R\ll P, \tag{2}
\]
because on every \(P\)-null set \(E\), \(Q(E)=(1-e)R(E)\).  Thus
\[
 \mathfrak L_e(P)=\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)\cap\{Q:Q\ll P\}. \tag{3}
\]
If a mutual-support SCM witnesses \(Q\in\mathfrak M_e(P)\), dropping reverse support witnesses membership in \(\mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)\), and consistency identifies \(P=\mathcal L(Y(a)\mid A=a)\), so \(Q\ll P\).  Conversely, if \(Q\in\mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)\) and \(Q\ll P\), the one-sided completeness theorem gives \(P\ll Q\); the same SCM witness then satisfies the mutual-support condition.  Therefore
\[
 \mathfrak M_e(P)=\mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)\cap\{Q:Q\ll P\}. \tag{4}
\]
On a two-point alphabet, full support of \(P\) implies \(Q\ll P\) for every probability law \(Q\).  Hence all three intersections in (1), (3), and (4) are vacuous there.
