Fix a standard Borel space \(\mathcal Y\), an arm \(a\), a probability law \(P\), and \(0<e<1\).  Let \(Q\in\mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)\).  By consistency and the defining observed-arm restriction,
\[
 \mathcal L(Y(a)\mid A=a)=\mathcal L(Y\mid A=a)=P.
\]
Put \(R=\mathcal L(Y(a)\mid A\ne a)\), which is a probability law because \(\Pr(A\ne a)=1-e>0\).  The law of total probability gives, for every measurable \(B\subseteq\mathcal Y\),
\[
 Q(B)=eP(B)+(1-e)R(B). \tag{1}
\]
Thus \(Q\in\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P)\).

Conversely, suppose \(Q=eP+(1-e)R\) for a probability law \(R\).  On a standard Borel probability space draw \(A\) with \(\Pr(A=a)=e\); conditionally on \(A=a\), draw \(Y(a)\sim P\), and conditionally on \(A\ne a\), draw \(Y(a)\sim R\).  Draw \(Y(1-a)\), conditionally independently given \(A\), from the fixed law \(P\), and set \(U=(A,Y(0),Y(1))\).  Let the treatment equation return the first coordinate of \(U\), and let the outcome equation be \(Y=Y(A)\).  The shared latent input supplies the permitted bidirected dependence and the outcome equation supplies \(A\to Y\), so this is a nonparametric latent-variable SCM for the bow ADMG.  It obeys consistency, has propensity \(e\), observed arm law \(P\), and, by (1), interventional law \(Q\).  Hence
\[
 \mathfrak M^{\to}_e(P)=\mathfrak L^{\to}_e(P). \tag{2}
\]

If (1) holds, then \(Q\ge eP\) as measures.  Hence \(P\ll Q\).  With \(r=dP/dQ\), the measure inequality is equivalent to \(er\le1\), \(Q\)-almost surely: if \(Q\ge eP\), integrate over \(\{er>1\}\); conversely, \(er\le1\) makes the \(Q\)-density of \(Q-eP\) nonnegative.  Therefore \(r\le1/e\), \(Q\)-almost surely.  Conversely, if \(P\ll Q\) and \(r\le1/e\), define
\[
 R(B)=\frac{Q(B)-eP(B)}{1-e}.
\]
Its numerator has nonnegative \(Q\)-density \(1-er\), it is countably additive, and its total mass is \((1-e)/(1-e)=1\).  Thus \(R\) is a probability law and (1) holds.  Combining this fact with (2) proves every asserted equality and the stated latent realization.
