=== CLUSTER DEFINITIONS (the six research clusters; qid prefix in parentheses) ===

- `panel` (`panel_`) — a causal result exploiting repeated observations of the same units over time (DiD, event study, TWFE, synthetic control, factor / interactive effects, dynamic panels), whatever the technique.
- `exactid` (`eid_`) — point identification: a causal effect, parameter, or the causal structure itself is a unique functional of the observed law under stated assumptions, in potential-outcome / model language. Use `exactid` for a structure/mechanism recovery map and its identifiable / nonidentifiable frontier under named restrictions, and keep a structure-identification kernel in `exactid` when its minimax error, sample complexity, or attaining detector is the estimation rung rather than a separate causal-effect estimand.
- `partialid` (`pid_`) — an identified set or a valid / sharp bound for a causal quantity the assumptions do not pin down, including sensitivity and breakdown analysis.
- `stat` (`stat_`) — estimation-and-inference theory for a causal estimand whose identification is given, under sampling-based (super-population) inference whatever the dependence structure: the minimax rate, efficiency bound, limit law, concentration or coverage result, size–power theorem, policy-learning regret — or a nontrivial estimator for a novel causal estimand — IS the headline.
- `experimentation` (`exp_`) — the theory of randomized experiments, where the experimenter's assignment mechanism is the object of study: design-based inference (fixed potential outcomes, the assignment is the randomness) or model-based inference about an experiment, optimal / adaptive / sequential design, and interference or network / spatial dependence arising from the experiment.
- `scm` (`scm_`) — a causal query over a DAG / ADMG / selection diagram with the graph first-class — interventional, counterfactual, path-specific / mediation, or transport / data-fusion: a new identifying functional under stated assumptions, or a valid graphical bound.

Tie-breakers: a new identification or bound claim routes to `exactid` / `partialid` / `scm` even when it carries an estimator; a result stated purely in potential-outcome language with no graph is `exactid` / `partialid`, not `scm`; dependence created by an experiment's design goes to `experimentation`; a run's cluster is fixed by its qid prefix (no prefix ⇒ `panel`). A problem straddling clusters takes the dominant one.
