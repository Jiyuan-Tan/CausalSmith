Fix \(n,\beta,k,B,\omega\) satisfying the conditions in the proposition, and fix an arbitrary spacing function \(\delta\) such that \(0<\delta(q)\le q/k\) for every \(q\in(0,1]\).  By Proposition~\(\mathrm{prop{:}rollout\text{-}budget\text{-}elbow}\), for every \(q\in(0,1]\),
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}\ge \frac{B^2\omega_1}{n}\Psi_\beta(q).
\]
The same proposition gives
\[
\frac{\Psi_\beta(q)}{A_\beta q^{1-2\beta}}\longrightarrow 1\qquad(q\downarrow0),
\quad\text{where}\quad
A_\beta=\frac{2\beta-1}{\beta^2}\binom{2\beta-2}{\beta-1}^{\!2}.
\]
Because \(\beta\ge1\), every factor in the displayed formula for \(A_\beta\) is strictly positive, so \(A_\beta>0\).  Applying the definition of this limit with tolerance \(1/2\), and decreasing the resulting threshold to at most \(1\) if necessary, there is \(q_0\in(0,1]\), depending only on \(\beta\), such that
\[
\Psi_\beta(q)\ge \frac{A_\beta}{2}q^{1-2\beta}
\qquad\text{whenever }0<q\le q_0.
\]
Substitution into the preceding variance lower bound yields
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}\ge\frac{B^2\omega_1A_\beta}{2n}q^{1-2\beta}.
\]
For the reverse inequality, Lemma~\(\mathrm{lem{:}uniform\text{-}small\text{-}budget\text{-}upper\text{-}certificate}\) applies at each \(q\), since its equally spaced nodes have gap \(q/k\), which is at least the required gap \(\delta(q)\).  With \(D=|\mathcal S_{n,\beta}|\) and \(\omega_{\max}=\max_{1\le d\le\beta}\omega_d\), that lemma gives
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}\le\frac{2B^2D\omega_{\max}}{n}\left(1+\frac{2^\beta k^\beta}{\beta!}\right)^2q^{1-2\beta}
\qquad(0<q\le1).
\]
The finite set \(\mathcal S_{n,\beta}\) is nonempty because \(1\le\beta\le n\), hence \(1\le D<\infty\).  Also \(B>0\), \(n\ge1\), and every \(\omega_d>0\); therefore the lower and upper multiplicative constants in the last two displays are finite and strictly positive.  They and \(q_0\) depend only on the fixed tuple \((n,\beta,k,B,\omega)\), and none depends on the chosen spacing function \(\delta\).  Thus the two inequalities hold simultaneously, with common constants and a common neighborhood \((0,q_0]\), for every admissible spacing function.  This is precisely
\[
V^*_{n,\beta,k,q,\delta(q)}=\Theta\!\left(q^{1-2\beta}\right)\qquad(q\downarrow0)
\]
uniformly over that class of spacing functions.  The displayed upper constant depends on \(k\), and the two bounds do not assert equality of their constants, so neither uniformity in \(k\) nor an exact leading constant has been claimed.
